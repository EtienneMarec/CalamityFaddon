'''Eternity''' is a craftable post-[[Moon Lord]] [[developer]] [[spell tome]]. When used, it will create a large circle, which inside will have five crystals, a beam, and a group of projectiles that create a flower shape while traveling, all of which will be centered around a nearby enemy. The beam will travel in an infinity shape. The crystals will circle the targeted enemy. The flower-shaped projectiles will also harm nearby enemies. If the attack button is being held, the beam's path will slowly grow in size as the beam will change to a darker blue color. Eventually, if the attack is held for long enough, the beam will disappear and the crystal circle will collapse in on the target, causing massive flower-shaped explosions that inflict massive damage.
