The '''1.4.2.103''' update...

== Additions ==
=== [[Buffs]] and [[Debuffs]] ===
*Added [[Astral Infection (debuff)]], [[Shadowflame]], and [[Time Distortion]].
*Added [[Blood Bound]], [[Resurrection Butterfly (buff)]], and [[Third Sage (buff)]].

=== [[Items]] ===
*Added rogue variants for [[Galaxy Smasher]], [[Nanoblack Reaper]], and [[Stellar Contempt]]
*Added [[Angelic Shotgun]], [[Bloody Vein]], [[Corpus Avertor]], [[Deep Sea Dumbbell]], [[Devil's Sunrise]], [[Dodu's Handcannon]], [[Karasawa]], [[Kelvin Catalyst]], [[The Last Mourning]], [[Mad Alchemist's Cocktail Glove]], [[Nadir]], [[Resurrection Butterfly]], [[Roxcalibur]], [[Time Bolt]], and [[Yateveo Bloom]].
*Added [[Hermit's Box of One Hundred Medicines]].

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*Added [[Fearless Goldfish Warrior]].

=== Worldgen ===
*Added Roxcalibur background object.

== Changes ==

=== Items ===
*Replaced most instances of [[God Slayer Inferno]] with Astral Infection:
**[[Hide of Astrum Deus]] now provides immunity to and inflicts Astral Infection onto enemies instead of God Slayer Inferno.
**[[Astral Arcanum]] and [[Astral Bulwark]] now provide immunity to Astral Infection instead of God Slayer Inferno.
*Buffed [[Abyssal Tome]]'s damage from 40 to 42.
*Nerfed [[Aero Stone]]'s jump speed boost from 200% to 100% without auto jump, and 40% to 25% with auto jump.
*Changed [[Anti-materiel Rifle]]'s use sound from PlasmaBlast to LargeWeaponFire.
*Decreased the amounts of [[Cosmilite Bar]]s, [[Darksun Fragment]]s, [[Endothermic Energy|Endothermic Energies]], [[Nightmare Fuel]]s, and [[Phantoplasm]] needed to craft [[Apotheosis]] from 77 to 33 each, and decreased the amount of [[Staff of the Mechworm|Staffs of the Mechworm]] needed from 3 to 2.
*Buffed [[Asteroid Staff]]'s damage from 130 to 180.
*[[Astral Blade]], [[Astral Hamaxe]], [[Astral Scythe]], [[Astral Pickaxe]], [[Astral Pike]], and [[Titan Arm]] now inflict Astral Infection.
*Buffed [[Augur of the Elements]]' damage from 74 to 150, and decreased its reuse delay from 10 to 5.
*Buffed [[Cosmic Kunai]]'s damage from 75 to 150, but nerfed its use animation from 5 to 10.
*Items previously made purely with [[Cosmilite Bar]]s at [[Draedon's Forge]] now require varying amounts of seasonal energy at an [[Ancient Manipulator]]:
**[[Vanquisher Arrow]]s now require 1 seasonal energy.
**[[Essence Flayer]] and [[Executioner's Blade]] now require 5 seasonal energy each.
**[[Cleansing Blaze]] and [[Soul Piercer]] now require 6 seasonal energy.
**[[The Enforcer]] and [[Stream Gouge]] now require 7 seasonal energy.
*Buffed [[Crystyl Crusher]]'s damage from 255 to 500]] and gave it a 25% chance to land a critical strike.
*Renamed Daedalus Circlet to [[Daedalus Mask]].
*Buffed [[Devastation]]'s damage from 114 to 115.
*Decreased the amounts of [[Auric Ore]], Darksun Fragments, Endothermic Energies, and Nightmare Fuel needed to craft [[Drataliornus]] from 80 to 30 each, decreased the amount of [[Effulgent Feather]]s needed from 160 to 60, and decreased the amount of [[Yharim's Gift]]s needed from 8 to 3.
*Buffed [[Eldritch Tome]]'s damage from 32 to 36, and its critical strike chance from 3 to 5.
*[[Elysian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Wings]] now use the {{rare|12}} rarity instead of {{rare|expert}}.
*Buffed [[Empyrean Knives]]' damage from 650 to 1100.
*[[Ethereal Talisman]]'s [[Mana Flower]] effect can now be toggled with visibility.
*[[Exoblade]] no longer uses Baleful Harvester in its recipe.
*Increased [[Face Melter]]'s use animation from 5 to 10, and increased its mana cost from 5 to 10.
*Buffed [[Grand Gelatin]] and [[Vital Jelly]]'s jump speed boosts from 100% to 200%, but they are now 75% less affective if the player has auto jump.
*Buffed [[Greatsword of Blah]]'s damage from 280 to 480.
*Nerfed [[Heart of the Elements]]' jump speed boost from 200% to 100% (220% to 110% when the elementals are hidden).
*Nerfed [[Infernal Blood]], [[Mushroom Plasma Root]], and [[Red Lightning Container]]'s Rage Mode damage boosts in Death Mode from 60% to 50% each.
*Buffed [[Lucrecia]]'s damage from 75 to 90.
*Buffed [[Murasama]]'s shot speed from 15 to 24.
*Nerfed [[Neptune's Bounty]]'s damage from 300 to 250.
*Nerfed [[Obsidian armor]]'s rogue damage and critical strike chance boosts from 10% to 5%, its defense from 3 to 2, and its lava invulnerability from 5 seconds to 3.
*Buffed [[P90]]'s use animation from 2 to 1 and it now fires 2 bullets per use.
*Increased [[Photoviscerator]]'s use animation from 5 to 10, and it now fires 2 streams of fire per use instead of 1.
*[[Pumpkin armor]] no longer boosts rogue damage. 
*[[Revengeance]]'s tooltip no longer states that Rage and Adrenaline are new stats. <!---"Which is a new stat"--->
*Nerfed [[Rouge Slash]]'s damage from 90 to 70.
*Buffed [[Scourge of the Cosmos]]' damage from 1500 to 2200.
*Decreased [[SDFMG]]'s use animation from 4 to 2.
*Increased [[Shadethrower]]'s chance to not consume [[Gel]] from 33% to 66%.
*Buffed [[Shadowbolt Staff]]'s damage from 170 to 200.
*Decresed [[Soma Prime]]'s use animation from 3 to 2, and it now fires 2 bullets per use.
*[[Spear of Paleolith]] is now crafted at any Hardmode anvil instead of any pre-hardmode one.
*Stellar Contempt now requires an Ancient Manipulator to craft.
*Buffed [[Terror Blade]]'s damage from 250 to 350.
*Buffed [[Ultra Liquidator]]'s damage from 96 to 145.
*Buffed [[Venusian Trident]]'s damage from 225 to 450.
*Buffed [[Vesuvius]]' damage from 70 to 75.
*[[Vivid Clarity]] now uses [[Helium Flash]] in its recipe instead of Venusian Trident.

=== Other ===
*Changed [[Charred Ore]]'s light color and changed its map color from ({{color|#800000 ●|#800000}}) to ({{color|#11101A ●|#11101A}}).
*The [[Drunk Princess]] can now mention "Christopher Pham", "DemoN K!ng", "Malik Ciaramella", "Ryan Baker-Ortiz", "Aleksanders Denisovs", "TheSilverGhost", "Lucazii", "Shay", "Prism", "BobIsNotMyRealName", "Guwahavel", "Azura", "Joshua Miranda", "Doveda", "William Chang", "Arche", "DevilSunrise", "Yanmei", "Chaos", "Ryan Tucker", "Fish Repairs", "Melvin Brouwers", "Vroomy Has -3,000 IQ", "The Goliath", and "DaPyRo".
*Rogue damage is now internally counted as throwing damage instead of ranged.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited Rage and Adrenaline meters.
*Resprited [[The Evolution]], [[Fabled Tortoise Shell]], [[Giant Tortoise Shell]], Hide of Astrum Deus, [[Samurai Badge]], and [[Void of Extinction]].
*Resprited [[Amidias' Trident]], [[Arbalest]], [[Conference Call]], [[Corroslime Staff]], [[Hell Burst]], [[Icicle Staff]], [[Impaler]], [[Lead Wizard]], [[Lunarian Bow]], [[Marnite Rifle Spear]], [[Marnite Spear]], [[Pearl God]], [[Plasma Rifle]], [[The Reaper]], Stellar Contempt, [[Swordsplosion]], [[Thunderstorm]], [[Vesuvius]], [[The Wand]], and [[Warblade]]. 
*Resprited [[Marnite Obliterator]].
*Resprited [[Charred Ore]].
*Resprited The Leviathan's meteor vomit, and Vesuvius' meteors.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Heart of Darkness]], [[Laudanum]], and [[Stress Pills]] dropping much more often than intended.
*Fixed [[Soul of Cryogen]] being unobtainable. <!---"SoulfofCryogen"--->
*Fixed Vesuvius always dropping from [[Betsy]].

==Other Videogames==
===Borderlands Series===

*{{item|Evil Smasher}}, a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Smasher weapon of the same name] from Borderlands 2.
*{{item|Carnage}}, shares the same name as the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Carnage_(Borderlands_2) Carnage weapon] from Borderlands 2.
*{{item|Swordsplosion}}, a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/SWORDSPLOSION!!! weapon of the same name] from Borderlands 2.
*{{item|Conference Call}}, a reference to a [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Conference_Call weapon of the same name] from Borderlands 2.
*{{item|Infinity}}, a reference to a [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Infinity weapon of the same name] from Borderlands 2.
*{{item|Norfleet}}, a reference to a [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Norfleet weapon of the same name] from Borderlands 2.
