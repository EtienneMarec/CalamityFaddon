==Pre-[[Mechanical Bosses]] [[File:Map Icon Retinazer (first form).png|link=The Twins|30px]][[File:Map Icon The Destroyer.png|link=The Destroyer|30px]][[File:Map Icon Skeletron Prime.png|link=Skeletron Prime|30px]]==
Upon entering [[Hardmode]], you now have access to a wide variety of equipment for each class. You can now start harvesting [[Life Fruit]]s for the upcoming battles to increase your survivability. Though one can fight [[Anahita and Leviathan]] at this point in the game, it is heavily reccomended the player does not do so, and therefore will not be counted.

==Pre-[[Calamitas]]/[[Plantera]] [[File:Calamitas map.png|link=Calamitas|30px]][[File:Map Icon Plantera (first form).png|link=plantera|30px]]==
After killing the Mechanical Bosses you should now have access to [[Hallowed Bar]]s, [[Chlorophyte Bar]]s, [[Unholy Core]]s and [[Cryonic Bar]]s, allowing you to get more powerful equipment. You may have already fought the [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Aquatic Scourge]], and [[Cryogen]] as well. At this point you should specialize a single class, as the armor set bonuses become much stronger.
