'''Light sources''' are items that produce light. Some are brighter than others and produce varying colors of light. Note that several [[weapons]] and [[armor]] types also generate light, but are not listed on this page for consistency purposes.

=== [[Light Pet]]s ===
These items summon a secondary floating light-emitting character that follows the player. They are infinitely reusable, cost no [[mana]], remain indefinitely, and work in liquids. Light Pets have their own equipment slot in the player's inventory.
