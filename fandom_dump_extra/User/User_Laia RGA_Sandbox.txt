'''The Calamity Mod,''' like the base game, adds a number of references and allusions to other media and pop culture.

The effect of the bullet is also a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Conference_Call Conference Call] weapon, found in Borderlands 2.

===The Binding of Isaac===

*{{item|Lead Wizard}}, a reference to [https://bindingofisaacrebirth.fandom.com/The_Wiz The Wiz] from ''The Binding of Isaac'', which also makes your shots fire at a 30-degree angle away from wherever you are aiming.

===Undertale===

*{{item|Determination Breaker}}, a reference to an overall important concept of the game, [https://undertale.fandom.com/wiki/Determination determination], and the Sans' iconic line "you're gonna have a bad time."

===FromSoftware Games===

==Popular Culture==
===Memes===

*{{item|Grand Dad}}, the name and the weapon damage and knockback is a reference to a meme known as "7 GRAND DAD," a Chinese bootleg copy of ''The Flintstones,'' popularized by Vargskelethor a.k.a Joel from Vinesauce.
*{{item|Burden Breaker}}, the tooltip is a reference to the popular [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/gotta-go-fast "Gotta go fast"] meme catchphrase, popularised by Sega character Sonic.
*{{item|Trash Can (pet)}}, is based on a famous episode from the show ''[[wikipedia:It's_Always_Sunny_in_Philadelphia|It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia]]'', in which Danny DeVito, playing as Frank Reynolds, becomes a wrestler known as The Trash Man.
*One of the quotes of the Drunk Princess NPC if Moon Lord has been defeated will read "There's chemicals in the water...and it's turning the frogs gay!", a [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/alex-jones-gay-frogs-conspiracy reference] to American radio show host and conspiracy theorist Alex Jones.
*One of the quotes of the Merchant NPC during a solar eclipse will read "Are you daft?! Turn off those lamps!.", a reference to the [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/moth-lamp "moth lamp"] meme, mothron being a giant moth.
*The Devourer of Gods' quote "Nothing personal, kid." is likely a reference to the [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/teleports-behind-you-nothing-personal-kid "''*Teleports Behind You* Nothing Personal, Kid"''] meme.
