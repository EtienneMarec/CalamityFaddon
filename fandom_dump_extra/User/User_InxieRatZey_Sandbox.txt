== Hunger and enemy interactions ==
All of the Sky Abyss enemies have a limited amount of time they can spend without attacking and trying to eat other ones. Each enemy spawns with {{dv|50||35|20}}-{{dv|100|||75}}% of its hunger bar and loses 24 hunger points each second afterwards. Additionally, enemies lose 1 hunger point for each {{dv|5|||1}} damage recieved. Each enemy can restore a certain amount of hunger by killing and then eating another enemy.

Almost all of the Sky Abyss enemies can fight each other: the hungrier an enemy gets, the more likely it will start to attack other enemies.
