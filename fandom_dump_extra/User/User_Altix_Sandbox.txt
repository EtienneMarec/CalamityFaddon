The '''1.4.3.001''' update added numerous equipments, especially for the [[Rogue]] and Summoner classes. It also included many AI changes in [[Revengeance Mode]], balance changes, general changes, resprites, and fixes. It is especially notable for the addition of Environmental Hazards, changes to [[Bosses]] and Enemy AIs, and other miscellaneous changes that are exclusive to [[Death Mode]].

== Changes ==
=== AI ===

=== Balance and Reworks ===
==== Armor and Accessories ====
Shield of the Ocean now grants 10% movement speed and +2 life regen while equipping Victide Armor
Ornate Shield now grants 8% DR and +20 max life while equipping Daedalus Armor
The Bee now bases its damage from half of player DR and has an ability to half damage taken when full health
Permafrost Concoction’s DR calculation is separate and reduced Encased time from 5 seconds to 3 seconds. It now additionally grants 15% less mana cost.
Vampiric Talisman now has a lifesteal cap of 6
Necklace of Vexation now increases damage by 20% instead of 15% while under 50% life. It also passively imbues Cursed Inferno and Venom to all attacks while wearing Reaver Armor.
Badge of Bravery now grants 10% melee damage, 5% melee crit, and 10 melee penetration while wearing Tarragon Armor.
Affliction stats buffed to Rev+ status, no longer scales.
Nanotech’s Raiders Talisman effect stacks up to 10% instead of 25%. Passive damage boost decreased from 10% to 5%. Stealth strike armor penetration decreased from +30 to +20. Stealth strike damage boost decreased from 10% to 5%

==== Weapons ====
Brittle Stars and Aquatic Stars now travel through tiles freely
Nerfed Winter's Fury, base damage decreased from 40 to 28
Frostspark Bullet's damage decreased from 11 to 8. It now explodes correctly.
Sand Sharknado Staff is now a Post-Plantera upgrade to the Forgotten Apex Wand. It no longer requires the Tempest Staff. Its damage is decreased from 98 to 35 for compensation
P90's damage decreased from 11 to 6. It now does 50% damage while using Acid Rounds.
Acid Round's damage decreased from 28 to 20
Angelic Shotgun's damage decreased from 180 to 90, and is nearly halved its speed (changed use time from 14 to 26)
Cosmilamp's damage decreased from 180 to 130
Alluvion's damage decreased from 90 to 70
Phangasm's damage decreased from 112 to 86
Onyxia's damage decreased from 235 to 210
Dragon Pow's damage decreased from 11800 to 3900
Gael's Greatsword damage decreased from 5660 to 4069
Vivid Clarity's damage increased from 350 to 550 and mana cost decreased from 150 to 69. It now fires a beam aiming towards the cursor every 4 ticks for a total of 5 times before a 20 tick delay. Upon hitting an enemy or a tile, the main beam no longer pierces nor bounces but creates a massive explosion, 8 fast homing energy orbs, or bouncing piercing lasers from the sky which spawns additional hitscan lasers when enemies are near.
ESpray also inflicts 15 seconds of ichor instead of 20.
ULiquid now inflicts all debuffs for 20 seconds instead of 10.
