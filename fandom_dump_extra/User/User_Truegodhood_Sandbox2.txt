'''[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]]''' is usually the fourth, fifth, or sixth Post-[[Moon Lord]] boss that you will face in your playthrough. Signus is summoned by using the [[Rune of Kos]] in [[The Underworld]] biome at any time.

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
Signus requires a lot of room within the Underworld in order to avoid his devastating contact damage. 
== Gearing Up ==
===Armor===
[[Tarragon armor]] is the preferred choice for all classes.
However, Lunar armors are likely still effective. 
*[[Solar Flare armor]] for Melee
*[[Vortex armor]] for Ranged
*[[Nebula armor]] for Mage
*[[Stardust armor]] for Summoner
*[[Xeroc armor]] for Rogue

===Accessories===
*[[Tarragon Wings]] (with [[Angel Treads]]) or [[Seraph Tracers]] are the preferred wings. 
*The [[Elysian Aegis]] is vital for its [[Elysian Guard]] (increasing stats and reducing movement acceleration for more 
controlled movement
**Its highly recommended to disable the accessory's visibility in order to not accidentally dash towards Singus' Cosmic Lanterns that spawn to the side of the player.
*[[The Absorber]] is important for survivability by increasing damage reduction as well as giving increased jump acceleration.
*[[The Community]] is a great all-around accessory that boosts offensive and defensive stats as well as boosting flight time.
*The [[Heart of the Elements]] provides many all-around boosts including damage, damage reduction, life regen, and jump acceleration.
*The [[Deific Amulet]] is an excellent option providing additional [[immunity frames]] and defense penetration.
*Melee players should use the [[Bloody Worm Scarf]] for its strong melee boosts as well as increased damage reduction.
*Ranged players should use the [[Sniper Scope]] or [[Artemis Emblem]], which both help in boosting damage output.
**Artemis Emblem additionally gives some life regen and ammo conservation.
*Magic players should use the [[Sigil of Calamitas]] for its strong magic boosts.
*Summoners can boost their minion count and damage with the [[Statis' Blessing]] and [[Statis' Curse]].
**Summoners can also use the [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] to provide a direct DPS boost through other minions.
*Rogue users will also find the [[Statis' Ninja Belt]] useful to boost their damage output in addition to the aformentioned boosts.
===Weapons===
The weapons made from [[Uelibloom Bar]]s or [[Bloodstone Core]]s are typically the best options for the fight.
====Melee====
*The [[True Biome Blade]]'s range, homing, and DPS should make the fight fairly straight-forward.
*The [[Lacerator]], [[Solar Flare]], or [[Verdant]] are all options that can deal good DPS towards Signus and his Comis Lantersn.

====Ranged====
*[[Seadragon]] is an excellent option with homing and good DPS.
**Along with [[Holy Fire Bullet]]s can decimate Signus and his Cosmic Lanterns
*The [[Shredder]] or even the [[S.D.M.G.]] can deal solid damage.

====Mage====
*Although DPS is lower, [[Nuclear Fury]] is the all-around best option being able to simultaneously handle lanterns and Signus with its homing.
*The [[Sanguine Flare]] and [[Mistlestorm]] is a decent choice dealing decent damage.
*[[Everglade Spray]] is an excellent secondary weapon for applying [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] for long periods of time.

====Summoner====
*[[Elemental Axe]]s is an excellent option being able to easily target Phase 1, handle the probes, and maintain good DPS.
**If [[Signus]] has been defeated, [[Cosmilamp]]s perform even better at the cost of a bit less range.
*The [[Energy Staff]] provides additional DPS to aid your minions.

====Rogue====
*[[Molten Amputator]]s are excellent for the entire fight due to it stacking on top of each other.
**[[Elemental Disk]] can perform a similar task although with lower DPS.
*[[Tarragon Throwing Dart]]s are a decent option dealing reasonable damage.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*Farm [[Blood Orb]]s to easily craft many helpful [[potions]] that will increase your survivability.
*If in multiplayer, [[Wormhole Potion]]s are a cheap and effective tool to escape death.
