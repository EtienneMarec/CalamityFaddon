Tom Nook is a character who plays the most important role in all of the games. He is termed a raccoon in the western versions of the game but a tanuki in the Japanese version. In all games leading to , he is the manager of the town shop. After the final expansion, Nookington's, he will employ his "nephews" Timmy and Tommy, who work on the second floor in every  game except and . In , Nook has taken his interest in real estate to a whole new level, opening Nook's Homes, whilst Timmy and Tommy run the town shop. In , Nook acts as the head of Nook Incorporated. He acts as the leader on the island, acting as task giver and island upgrader.

In , he will give out his picture if the player gives him the safe from the Golden Axe Trading Event.

Tom Nook is a [[Wikipedia:Tanuki|tanuki]] (a [[Wikipedia:Raccoon|raccoon]] in Western versions) with brown fur. He has half-closed, blue eyes with a dark brown, mask-like patch of fur around them. The tip of his nose, his paws, tail, and ears are dark brown.

From  through , as the town's shop owner, he will wear different uniforms for each upgrade of his store.

*For Nook's Cranny, he wears a blue apron with his leaf symbol on it (in }, it is the kanji , meaning "shop"). The blue apron is inferred to be for modesty, referencing the legends of tanuki.
*For Nook 'N' Go, he wears blue, orange, and white-striped shirt and pillbox hat (in , this uniform was red, green, and white).
*For Nookway, he wears a white apron with a purple leaf and green details on it (blue and orange in ).
*For Nookington's, he wears a bright blue suit jacket and pink tie.
*For Lottery|the lottery, he wears a red, white and blue apron and sports visor. In , this was a red and black [[Wikipedia:Haori|haori]] in addition to his usual apron, and a white [[Wikipedia:Hachimaki|hachimaki]] tied around his head.

In , Nook wears a white shirt with a red necktie and a green argyle sweater vest over it. His sweater has a left-sided pocket protector with a pen sticking out. He also wears black pants. Whenever outside of Nook's Homes, such as the player selecting their new home spot or at The Roost, Nook is seen with a yellow jacket over his sweater.

In , if his house is designed, the player can visit him and find him in casual wear. He wears a white polo shirt with his leaf logo on it, and khaki pants.

Tom Nook makes an appearance in ; however, he is merely a special furniture item. He also appears on the 1,200 Leaf Ticket GUI.

In , he now wears a white and teal hawaiian-style shirt and khaki shorts. He will wear a dark teal jacket from late Fall to just before Spring.
