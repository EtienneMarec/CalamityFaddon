The '''Acid Rain''' is a weather event added by the Calamity Mod. It has a 1/360,000 chance of occuring every tick once the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] has been defeated, or can be summoned through the use of a [[Caustic Tear]]. While the Event is active, Sulphuric Rain droplets will rain from the sky and inflict [[Irradiated]] onto the player.

The Event has multiple levels of difficulty that are encountered at different stages in progression similar to the [[Old One's Army]], with each tier unlocking after the player has defeated a specific boss. The first tier of the event will be unlocked after the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] has been defeated, the next tier will unlock after the [[Aquatic Scourge]] has been defeated and the final tier will unlock after the [[Polterghast]] has been defeated.

To complete the event, the player must use a Caustic Tear or kill 180 enemies. In Multiplayer, the players must kill the amount of enemies calculated by the equation (floor(180 * ln(total players + E - 1))) based on how many players are in the world at the start of the event.

== Notes ==
*While the Acid Rain event is active, Rain will be active across the rest of the world.
*The events enemies will override the [[Sulphurous Sea]]s enemies, similarly to [[Meteor Head]]s.
*As it is a weather event, other events such as the [[Goblin Invasion]] may happen simultaneously.

== Trivia ==
*The Acid Rains [[Music|theme]] is Acidic Downpour, which was composed by wiki editor [https://calamitymod.gamepedia.com/UserProfile:RoverdriveZ RoverdriveZ].
*It is the first event ever added to the Calamity Mod.
*Some of the events enemies are based on extinct aquatic lifeforms, such as the [[Orthocera]] and [[Trilobite]].
*The event used to be a variation of the [[Rain]] event in the [[Sulphurous Sea]], similar to [[Blizzard]]s in the [[Snow]] Biome.

== Tips ==
*This event is very dangerous for early game players due to the high spawnrates of enemies.
