This is a simple template providing standardized formatting for entries in the [[Template:Curse wiki staff list|Curse wiki staff list]] and [[Template:Community_admins_list|community admin list]]. 

'''Usage''':
:{{tlx|Wiki admin|account name}}

'''Note''': The template must be enclosed within a table and does not include a row break character.

'''Example''':
:{{tlx|Wiki admin|Game widow}}

'''Result''':
{|
{{Wiki admin|Game widow}}
|}
