This template is used to display additional crafting requirements of items through the use of [[Schematics]].

== Usage ==
<code>{{(}} schematic | ''&lt;item category&gt;'' | plural = 1 ('''optional''') }}</code>

; List of item categories

{| class="terraria lined"
! Parameter value !! Description
|-
| {{code|tier1}} || Items that require the Sunken Sea schematic to be found.
|-
| {{code|tier2}} || Items that require the Space/Planetoid schematic to be decrypted.
|-
| {{code|tier3}} || Items that require the Jungle schematic to be decrypted.
|-
| {{code|tier4}} || Items that require the Hell/Underworld schematic to be decrypted.
|-
| {{code|tier5}} || Items that require the Ice schematic to be decrypted.
|-
| {{code|tooltip}} || Items that include extra tooltips while locked.
|}

== Example ==
<code><nowiki>{{schematic|tooltip|plural=1}}</nowiki></code> → {{schematic|tooltip|plural=1}}
