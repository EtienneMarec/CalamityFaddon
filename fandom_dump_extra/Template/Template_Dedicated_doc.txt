== Usage ==
Add this to the tops of pages to indicate that the item is dedicated to somebody for their role, such as being a developer, or for completing a task, such as making a donation or succeeding in an organized challenge.

Enter the name of the dedicatee as a parameter to indicate for which person the item was made, and if applicable, enter their reason for the dedication as a second parameter. ({{tlx|dedicated|''Dedicatee Name''|''Reason''}})
