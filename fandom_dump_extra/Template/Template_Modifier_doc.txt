This template is used to display a weapon's or tool's best [[modifier]].

== Usage ==
<code>{{(}} modifier | ''&lt;item category&gt;'' | plural = 1 ('''optional''') }}</code>

; List of item categories

{| class="terraria lined"
! Parameter value !! Description
|-
| {{code|melee}} || Most melee weapons.
|-
| {{code|meleealt}} || Weapons that cannot get size modifiers. For example: yoyos, flails, and spears.
|-
| {{code|speedy}} || Weapons that can receive modifiers to speed, but not to some other stat like mana cost or knockback.
|-
| {{code|harvest}} || Melee "weapons" that are mainly used for harvesting tiles.
|-
| {{code|harvestalt}} || Weapons listed on [[Mining speed]] where Legendary is equivalent to Light in speed boost.
|-
| {{code|harvestmech}} || Harvesting tools that do not benefit from Mining speed.
|-
| {{code|ranged}} || Most ranged weapons.
|-
| {{code|rangedalt}} || Weapons that cannot get speed modifiers.
|-
| {{code|rangednokb}} || Ranged weapons with no knockback.
|-
| {{code|nokb}} || Weapons with no knockback.
|-
| {{code|nokbalt}} || Weapons with no knockback that do not benefit from higher speed.
|-
| {{code|magic}} || Most magic weapons.
|-
| {{code|magicalt}} || Magic weapons with a base use time lower than 5.
|-
| {{code|magicalt2}} || Magic weapons with both base use time and mana cost lower than 5.
|-
| {{code|magicsummon}} || Magic weapons that continue dealing damage after the user switches weapons (and have non-zero knockback).
|-
| {{code|magicnokb}} || Magic weapons with no knockback.
|-
| {{code|magiclow}} || Magic weapons with a base mana cost lower than 5.
|-
| {{code|summon}} || Most summon weapons.
|-
| {{code|summonnokb}} || Summon weapons with no knockback.
|-
| {{code|summonalt}} || Summon weapons with low base damage.
|-
| {{code|summonactive}} || Summon weapons that don't use up minion slots and can be used in quick succession, e.g. [[Borealis Bomber]].
|-
| {{code|spy}} || Most spy weapons.
|-
| {{code|lowdamage}} || Most low base damage weapons that cannot receive damage modifiers due to rounding.
|-
| {{code|none}} || Any weapon that can't be reforged.
|}

== Example ==
<code><nowiki>{{modifier|melee|plural=1}}</nowiki></code> → {{modifier|melee|plural=1}}
