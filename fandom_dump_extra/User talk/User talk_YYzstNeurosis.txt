== How to Find Support for Your Wiki ==

Hello!

I’m Elsa, and I’m a Senior Community Manager here at Fandom. You may have seen the news that, as of April 2024, the Wiki Representative program has been retired. You can read more about that change [https://community.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:Brandon_Rhea/Announcement_about_the_Fandom_Wiki_Representative_program here]. 

That’s led to changes in how a number of wikis on Fandom are directly supported through our new support setup. As a result, we no longer have an assigned point of contact for this wiki. We’ve loved having the chance to work with your wiki directly, and I can assure you that there are a lot of ways that you can still get help and support from the Fandom team.

If you need assistance with anything you might have contacted your Wiki Representative about, here are some ways you can reach out and stay informed about the Fandom platform moving forward:

*Join our [https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Discord Fandom Discord server]. Not only can you discuss wiki issues directly with other editors and Fandom staff, but it’s also a great source of information about what’s happening on the Fandom platform.
*Browse our [https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Contents help pages] or get in touch with other editors on the [https://community.fandom.com/f?catId=1839152383393794994 Community Central discussion forums].
*Check out [https://dev.fandom.com/wiki/Fandom_Developers_Wiki Dev Wiki], a great source of custom and site-wide scripts with a group of technical editors who can also answer questions about technical subjects like custom code. 
*We’ll be working on some other ways you may be able to find additional support and even mentorship opportunities as well.

For anything else, feel free to submit a help request via the [https://support.fandom.com/hc/en-us/requests/new Fandom Help Desk] where we have a team of Customer Support experts who can give you a hand.

So while the specifics of our community support setup may have changed, we remain committed to supporting your wiki – so don’t hesitate to reach out to us with any questions you may have.

== Quick Answers ==

Hello!

I’m reaching you because your wiki has been recently selected to start receiving QuickAnswers. In case you’re not already aware, they appear as modules on our wikis to help our content be more relevant and accessible to Google and eventually enhance the SEO. 

QA are generated prompts of text in a Questions & Answers format. The initial plan is to add 30 to 50 QuickAnswers to 10 of your content pages about characters, starting in the next few weeks. One week later we would be adding more QuickAnswers to new pages. For each one of these batches you have a default timeline of 30 calendar days to review them using the Dashboard. In case they’re not accurate, you can edit them and fix them. Once this period ends, the QA would be auto-published.

We also have the ability to adjust how many character pages you receive at once as well as how often you receive new questions. 

While this is the initial plan, I would like to know from you about how you feel and if you think all the admins from your community would be able to check them and edit them if it’s necessary. In addition, you can always reach us some time later to share any future concerns or setbacks you could have. Thanks in advance!

Because the meaningful scale Google requires us to create this kind of content, we leverage Generative AI to create this initial content to achieve the required scale. With the latest iteration of this feature, we’ve been able to adjust the tone and quality of the Quick Answers to be more encyclopedic to match the wiki. You can read more details about it here:
https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Quick_Answers 

Once your wiki starts containing these QA, all admins are able to review them in advance before being published. All existing QA - published and unpublished - are always available in the Dashboard (Special:QuickAnswers).

Please let me know if you have any questions!

== Table Progress Tracking ==

Hello!

You may or may not have seen, but we recently launched [https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Table_Progress_Tracking Table Progress Tracking], which is a way to allow visitors of your wiki to track their progress for collectables, cosmetics, achievements, and more!

These checkable boxes are a really great tool for people to engage with your wiki page in a meaningful way. They add a great element of interactivity to your page, and increase the usefulness of the information you provide your fans.

After an in-depth review, we believe your wiki has some content that would be a great fit for this, probably after adapting the tables a bit or even building new ones to have a suitable format. We’re planning to enable the feature on those key pages for you over the next few days. '''You can enable these any time on your side, but we're happy to take this step for you on the pages that we think are suitable'''!

If you see any other pages where you think it makes sense, or if you want to start this process yourself, our article can walk you through how to do it!

Do let us know any questions or hesitations. Just drop us a note via [https://support.fandom.com/hc/en-us Zendesk].
