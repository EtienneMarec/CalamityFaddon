Yharim Worm (Japanese: ヤハリムワーム ''Yaharimuwāmu'') is a secret Godseeker Mode [[boss]] that can be fought anywhere. The player should be very prepared as the boss deals very high damage along with fast speed and projectiles. Defeating it will provide the player with a large amount of Coins and the ability to fight [[Godseeker, Yharim]]. The Yharim Worm has 100 segments in total.

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
Due to the extreme danger of this boss. It is recommended you place some important items down in a standard platform arena.

- The [[Arc of the Toilets]] is a very helpful item to sit on

- [[tgc:Hallowed_Bar|Hallowed Bars]] are a great alternative to platforms, As they are easily farm able and can be passed through just like platforms.

- The [[Budget Draedon Plush]] may distract Yharim Worm

== Gearing Up ==

=== Armor ===

* Lunar Armors may seem like the best option. However they are simply not good enough for Yharim Worm
* All classes will want to wear the [[tgc:Maid_set|Maid set]]. Along with having their character named "Draedon" and making their character resemble Draedon within the character editor
** This part may be tricky. Players will want to spend hours ensuring their character looks like Draedon, in order to stand a chance against Yharim Worm.
** If the Player has defeated [[Exo Mechs]], the [[Boss Masks|Draedon Mask]] can be worn as well, providing a special boost

=== Accessories ===

* Ensure none of the accessories used mess with how your player looks.
** Accessory visibility can be toggled off in order to hide most accessories from appearing
*** However accessories such as [[wings]] and the [[tgc:Flying_Carpet|Flying Carpet]] cannot have visibility be toggled off. Making these items useless

=== Weapons ===
Any post Moon Lord weapon will get the job done.

==== Melee ====

* [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]] may remind Yharim Worm how easy it is to craft their own sword, leading Yharim Worm into depression
* The [[tgc:Copper_Shortsword|Copper Shortsword]] is disabled for this fight.

==== Ranged ====

* A [[wikipedia:Dan_Wesson_M1911_ACP_pistol|Dan Wesson M1911 ACP pistol]] is very effective, but may be risky to use.
** A [[wikipedia:Nerf_Blaster|Nerf Blaster]] Is a much weaker safe alternative.

==== Mage ====

* The [[tgc:Tome_of_Infinite_Wisdom|Tome of Infinite Wisdom]] is a great choice due to its massive amount of pierce.

==== Summoner ====

* Lining the entire hot bar with Whips and alternating between all of them in the fight will enrage Yharim Worm. Causing the Calamity Mod to be updated to Terraria 1.4.5 where this strategy is no longer viable.
** However this enragement allows for many new whip options and summons to be available. Players may want to consider this option when fighting.

==== Thrower ====

* The [[tgc:Bone_Glove|Bone Glove]] Is the best option due to its pierce
**

==== Classless ====

* Your fists make for a good backup option if one of the segments split and it is [[Malice Mode]]

=== Miscellaneous ===

* [[Calamitea]] is a great buff choice
* As always [[Delicious Meat]] provides powerful boosts
** However the delicousness of the meat may cause Yharim Worm to smell it through [[Yharim Bar|Yharim Bars]] and gain increased mobility.

== General Tips ==

* Throwing [[Yharim Bar]]<nowiki/>s everywhere around your arena helps predict Yharim Worms attacks, and blocks Yharim worm from seeing you. Forcing Yharim worm to guess your location.

* Be careful when attacking Yharim Worm's segments as single segments will summon [[Godseeker, Yharim]]. 
** If this happens in [[Malice Mode]] refer to the ranger weapon recommendations above for ways to deal with this.
** It is possible to summon up to 50 [[Godseeker, Yharim|Godseeker, Yharims]] in a single fight.

* If Yharim Worm escapes the game window. The player will also have to escape the game window in order to continue the fight.
** You will want to build a 2nd arena on your desktop if this occurs.

* Giving up is a good way to preserve your sanity. Uninstalling the Calamity mod may allow you to touch grass and take a shower. Which is vital for being happy.
