'''[[Calamitas]]''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is usually fought after defeating all [[Mechanical Bosses]] alongside [[Cryogen]], the [[Brimstone Elemental]], and the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. Additionally, Calamitas receives a buff after defeating [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] and begins to drop [[Bloodstone]]. Calamitas can be spawned by using an [[Eye of Desolation]] on the [[Surface]] at [[night]].

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
Calamitas requires a lot of room. Two to three long sets of platforms should be spaced out roughly 20-35 blocks apart. Having platforms above the ground will help as it will prevent you from running into Catastrophe's bouncing fireballs later in the fight. In the case that you end up needing to go near the ground, the ground of the arena should be leveled to prevent crevices and holes obstructing your movement.

Strong vertical mobility is a great asset for this fight as well as good horizontal mobility. As such, it would not be a bad idea to invest in some [[Weightless Candle]]s. Additionally, placing [[Campfire]]s and [[Heart Lantern]]s can help with recovery from hits.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Titanium armor]] is an all-around decent option for Melee, Ranged, and Mages due to its high survivability from [[Shadow Dodge]].
*[[Frost armor]] is a decent option for Melee and Ranged players.
*[[Forbidden armor]] is a decent option for Mage and Summoner players.
*[[Hallowed armor]] and [[Chlorophyte armor]] can be utilized for their good all-around stats.
*[[Spider armor]] is generally preferred for Summoners due to the high damage bonuses for minions.
*[[Titan Heart armor]] is considered the best option for Rogue, specially if using [[rogue|stealth]].
**[[Forbidden Circlet]] is also a decent option for rogue players that don't use stealth strikes much and prefer faster attacking.
*[[Daedalus armor]] is another well-rounded armor set for all classes.
*[[Mollusk armor]] can be used for its great amount of stats at the cost of movement speed.
*[[Squire armor]], [[Monk armor]], [[Huntress armor]] and [[Apprentice armor]] are also decent mixed class or summoner options as well.

=== Accessories ===

==== Movement ====
*Wings are generally preferred over the [[Bundle of Balloons]] or the [[MOAB]] in this fight, as the smoother movement and the higher amount of control will prove valuable when dodging Calamitas' general attacks or the bullet hells.
**[[Bat Wings]] are the strongest wings for this fight, as it boosts damage and mobility stats during the nighttime.
**[[Starlight Wings]] can also be used when paired with Daedalus armor.
**Other alternatives include [[Harpy Wings]]. [[Fairy Wings]], and [[Flame Wings]].
*Note that the Bundle of Balloons or the MOAB can be used to great effect during the brothers phase of the fight, as the high speed of the jumps greatly helps in evading their dashes and Cataclysm's flamethrower, especially in higher difficulties.
*A [[Frog Leg]], [[Aero Stone]], or [[Grand Gelatin]] can be utilized with wings to give an extra boost to movement speed.
**The [[MOAB]] is another alternative set of wings but is generally more difficult to use effectively.
*[[Angel Treads]] can help with keeping up with Calamitas’ speed.
**Note that reforging some accessories to Quick allows the player to accelerate and sprint at the same speed as Angel Treads, saving an accessory slot. 
*The [[Evasion Scarf]] and the [[Shield of Cthulhu]] can be used to make quick dashes to escape deadly situations.
**The [[Ornate Shield]] can also be used when paired with Daedalus armor.

==== Defensive ====
*The [[Ambrosial Ampoule]] provides high life regeneration, increasing survivability.
*[[Permafrost's Concoction]] is a good option because of its added life regen and revival ability. Additionally, it provides offensive bonuses to mages.
*The [[Crown Jewel]] can be used to help reduce the effects of [[Brimstone Flames]] and [[Abyssal Flames]].
**Alternatively, the [[Abaddon]] reduces the damage over time of the Brimstone Flames debuff by 50% whereas the [[Margarita]] [[Alcohol]] halves the duration of both at the cost of some defense and life regeneration.
*The [[Charm of Myths]] can be used its decreased potion cooldown.

==== Offensive ====
*A class emblem can provide a good increase to DPS.
*Melee players should use the [[Mechanical Glove]] and the [[Bloody Worm Scarf]] for their strong melee boosts and the latter's increased damage reduction.
**If the player is utilizing Yoyos, then the [[Yoyo Bag]] should be prioritized.
*Ranged players should use the [[Ranger Emblem]] and [[Magic Quiver]] to help boost damage output and arrow velocity.
*Magic players should use the [[Mana Flower]] and/or the [[Celestial Cuffs]] to quickly recover mana. A [[Celestial Emblem]] will help boost damage and increases mana star grab range as well.
**Note that using the quick mana button or [[Mana Regeneration Potion|Mana Regeneration Potions]] can remove the need for a Mana Flower, saving an accessory slot.
*Summoners should use the [[Hallowed Rune]] for the stat boosts it provides alongside secondary damage with its stars, [[Nuclear Rod]] and [[Jelly-Charged Battery]] for its additional minion effects.
**A [[Apprentice's Scarf|Sentry accessory]] is also helpful if using sentries.
*Rogue users will find the [[Rogue Emblem]], [[Old Die]], [[Corrosive Spine]] and the [[Raider's Talisman]] useful to boost their damage output.
**The [[Electrician's Glove]], [[Mirage Mirror]] and [[Ruin Medallion]] should be used for stealth-focused builds.

== The Fight ==
Calamitas alternates between hovering over the player and firing two accelerating Brimstone Hellfireballs, and leveling with the player and firing 4 acceleration Brimstone Hellblasts. Run horizontally to avoid the Hellfireballs, and fly up to avoid the Hellblasts. 

At 70% health, Calamitas will become invulnerable and summon a bullet hell. Focus on the projectiles and do not be afraid to use the Rod of Discord if you are in a tight spot. Using [[Binoculars]] will also help with seeing the projectiles.

At 40% health, Calamitas will summon her brothers, Cataclysm and Catastrophe. Cataclysm rotates between breathing a brimstone flamethrower and charging at the player, and Catastrophe rotates between firing bouncing brimstone fireballs and charging at the player. Because of the brother's high speed and the long range of Cataclysm's flamethrower, it is important to stay far away from them. In Revengeance mode or above, using Adrenaline or Rage to quickly dispatch them is a good idea.

At 35% health, Calamitas will summon five [[Soul Seeker]]s that rotate around her, periodically firing brimstone darts and summoning Life Seekers. Piercing weapons will make short work of the seekers.

At 10% health, Calamitas will summon a second bullet hell, this time with the addition of Brimstone Fireblasts fired from offscreen which home in on the player and explode into rings of Brimstone Darts. Moving around is especially important, as the darts from the Fireblasts are much harder to dodge if you are close to the explosion. 

=== In [[Expert Mode]] ===
The brothers must be destroyed in order for Calamitas to take damage again. Calamitas and her brothers also attack more aggressively.

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
Calamitas and her brothers are generally more aggressive. Calamitas gains a new attack below 75% health, where she charges at the player twice following her fireball and hellblast attacks. This attack cannot be used if the brothers are alive. Calamitas will also charge at the player nonstop below 10% health. Because she positions herself above the player in the second bullet hell and immediately starts charging after it ends, the player might want to save the Rod of Discord to prevent cheap hits.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===
Calamitas attacks even more aggressively and summons 10 Soul Seekers instead of 5 at 35% health. 

=== In [[Malice Mode]] ===
Calamitas and her brothers charge much faster and further. Cataclysm's flamethrower range is increased, and Cataclysm shoots fireballs more rapidly. This makes prioritizing speed during the brothers phase even more important. The bullet hells also contain more projectiles, and Calamitas' charges gain predictiveness below 10% health. In addition, Calamitas and her brother's projectiles are all affected by Malice Mode's general 25% projectile velocity increase.

== General Tips ==
*The [[Rod of Discord]] (or the [[Normality Relocator]] for the Post-Providence fight) is a very useful tool as it allows instantaneous teleporting to avoid the brothers.
