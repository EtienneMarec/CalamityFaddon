

King Slime is the usually the first boss the player will face in terraria. It is summoned with a Slime Crown and can be summoned anywhere at any time.

This guide will be for the changes that Calamity Mod brings to King Slime.

== Terrain Preparation ==
Build a single set of platforms above the desert and preferably level the ground of the arena. The platforms should be about 20 blocks above the ground. Great vertical and horizontal mobility is a must for this fight, considering the high mobility of the boss itself. [[Campfire|Campfires]] and [[Heart Lantern|Heart Lanterns]] are useful assets to boost health regeneration, so they should be placed throughout the arena.

== Gearing Up ==

=== Armor ===

* [[Wulfrum armor]] is an easy set to obtain, and can be used well.
* [[Platinum armor]] is an option for melee players, due to it providing the highest available defense.
* [[Desert Prowler armor]] can make ranged players quite strong.
* [[Snow Ruffian armor]] is an amazing armour set for rogue players.

=== Coverings ===

* [[Hermes Boots]] and any of its upgrades are extremely useful in keeping up with King Slime's jumps.
* Any double jump, preferably the [[Sandstorm in a Bottle]] or [[Blizzard in a Bottle]] can allow the player to jump over King Slime and circle back.
** The [[Bundle of Balloons]] combines three double jumps into one, making it very useful, though it requires more effort to obtain.
* The [[Frog Leg]] is another option that gives a sizable jump speed boost.
** The [[Shiny Red Balloon]] and its upgrades provide a lesser jump boost, as does the [[Vital Jelly]], but may be easier to obtain.
* [[Luxor's Gift]] is a great covering which provides powerful damage boosts for all classes.
* The [[Shark Tooth Necklace]] provides armor penetration, increasing the damage of your weapons. This is especially helpful for low damage and fast firing weapons such as the [[Minishark]].
* Melee players using [[yoyos]] should equip a [[White String]] and/or a [[Counterweight]] to increase the range and effectiveness of the yoyo.
* Magic players can make use of the [[Magic Cuffs]] and/or [[Mana Flower]] along with [[Mana Potion|Mana Potions]] to recover their mana during the fight.
* Summoners can boost their minion damage and durability with the [[Spirit Glyph]].

=== Weapons ===
Weapons made of [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap|Wulfrum Metal Scraps]] should suffice, although there are many other available options found early in the game.

==== Melee ====

* Any weapon with a projectile, such as the Enchanted Sword will work well.
* Yoyo users should use the [[Amazon]] or [[Rally]], which can deal respectable damage.

==== Ranged ====

* The [[Minishark]] is a powerful option as a gun. Any ammo will suffice, with Flash Rounds or SIlver/Tungsten bullets being the best.
* Anlmost any bow should be viable, but Platinum Bow or Mandible Bow are the best choices, with Frostburn Arrows.

==== Mage ====

* The [[Frost Bolt]] is a good option, as its piercing ability to deal with the slimes spawned plus its capability to inflict [[Frostburn]] can deal high damage to King Slime and deal with it quickly.
* Gem Staves, particularly the [[Ruby Staff]] or [[Diamond Staff]], are good choices, providing high damage with the higher tier staves providing additional piercing.
* Should the player get lucky with [[Dungeon]] generation, the [[Water Bolt]] is also an option, providing high base damage and piercing.

==== Summoner ====

* The [[Stormjaw Staff]] is extremely good for damage output and is probably the best weapon choice.
* The [[Belladonna Spirit Staff]] can inflict [[Poisoned]], which can deal extra damage to King Slime.

==== Rogue ====

* the [[Crystalline]] is great for its raw damage when all of the daggers split, and can even provide a head start to the fight with its stealth strike.
* [[Wulfrum Knife|Wulfrum Knives]] are another good  and easy to obtain weapon.
* The [[Gilded Dagger]] and [[Gleaming Dagger]] have long range and good damage.
* The [[Bouncing Eyeball]] is also a great option if you can last through a [[Blood Moon]].
* [[Molotov Cocktail|Molotov Cocktails]] are an excellent weapon that have a large area of effect and can inflict the On Fire! debuff.

=== Miscellaneous ===

* The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all around stat boost for all classes.
* there are many guides to make an area that can kill King Slime without using a weapon, but having lava below an arena can make the fight significantly easier.
* [[Rage Potion|Rage Potions]] or [[Wrath Potion|Wrath Potions]] provide a considerable increase to damage.
* [[Swiftness Potion|Swiftness Potions]] and [[Regeneration Potion|Regeneration Potions]] can provide useful buffs.
* [[Bounding Potion|Bounding Potions]] are especially useful in [[Malice Mode]], but can help in other modes too.
* [[Calcium Potion|Calcium Potions]] prevent fall damage without using an covering slot.

== The Fight ==
When King Slime spawns, he will be off screen. As he has no attacks besides bouncing at and on the player, he is quite simple to defeat.

In [[Death Mode]], King Slime is larger, and at half health lets out a Crown Jewel, which shoots 3 ruby bolts at the player. This Jewel can be killed but it is not reccommended as it has quite a lot of health and is difficult to hit, as well as it not affecting King Slime's damage or defence.

In Malice Mode, King Slime move much faster, and it has much more exponential speed as its health lowers. The Frog Leg and Bundle of Balloons are especially important as they allow you to manoeuvre around the boss with ease.

King Slime will fall down platforms if you do too, and if you use a Grappling Hook to climb back up quickly, you can temporarily trap him below you.

As King Slime takes more damage, it will get exponentially faster and will eventually be able to pass any player with Lightning Boots.

== General Tips ==

* Disabling backgrounds can help players who have trouble focusing on all the different things that are going on during the fight.
* In Revengeance mode and higher, using [[Adrenaline]] or [[Rage]] can remove a large chunk of King Slime's health when it is low to swiftly end the fight.
* Using the Overlay [[Minimap]] and changing its the transparency with [[Game controls#Page%20Up|<kbd>Page Up</kbd>]] and [[Game controls#Page%20Down|<kbd>Page Down</kbd>]] keys can provide the player with icon of bosses that are too far to see without the Minimap, making it a useful tactic to aim at bosses.
