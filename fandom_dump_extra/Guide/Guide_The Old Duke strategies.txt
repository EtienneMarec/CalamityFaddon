'''[[The Old Duke]]''' is a post-[[Moon Lord]] [[boss]] than can be fought in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]] after defeating [[Polterghast]]. It is summoned by finishing the third tier of the [[Acid Rain]] [[Events|event]] or by fishing with [[Bloodworm]]s in the Sulphurous Sea.

This guide will mention [[Expert Mode]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] exclusive coverings with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
A large open area with either 3 layers of platforms 40-50 blocks apart or several rows of stacked [[Minecart Track]]s 30 blocks apart can help with dodging several of the Old Duke's attacks.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Bloodflare armor]] is the preferred choice for all classes in this stage of the game.
*[[Omega Blue armor]] may also be utilized because of its immense offensive stats, but severely lacks at defensive stats in turn.
*[[Tarragon armor]] can be viable, but is considerably low tier for the current stage of progression.

=== Coverings ===
*The [[Seraph Tracers]] or [[Tarragon Wings]] can be useful because of their mobility stats.
*[[The Absorber]] can be potent for its healing and speed.
**Alternatively the [[Ambrosial Ampoule]] can be used as a defensive option and to negate the effects of the [[Poisoned|Poison]] and [[Venom]] debuffs.
*[[The Community]] is a useful covering for its progressive but powerful scaling stats.
*[[Statis' Ninja Belt]] provides a dash and a chance to dodge attacks, which can help with avoiding contact damage.
*The [[Flesh Totem]] can prove particularily useful against the Old Duke to halve contact damage from its charges.
*[[Asgard's Valor]] provides a dash, extra defense, immunity to knockback, and immunity to several debuffs, particularly the debuffs caused by the [[Feral Bite]] debuff.
**It is also possible to use the [[Ursa Sergeant]] to counter the Feral Bite debuff entirely, in such case [[Elysian Aegis]] may replace Asgard's Valor.
* The [[Reaper Tooth Necklace]] can be used to boost the damage dealt when The Old Duke is not exhausted.
*Melee users can use the [[Fire Gauntlet]] or [[Yharim's Insignia]] for their increases to melee stats along with the [[Celestial Shell]].
*Ranged users can deal extra damage with the [[Artemis Emblem]] and [[Sniper Scope]].
*Mages should utilize the [[Sigil of Calamitas]] and, optionally, the [[Mana Flower]].
*Summoners can use [[Statis' Curse]] and the [[Star-Tainted Generator]] for an increase to minion damage and slots, the [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] can also be utilized.
*Rogues will benefit from the [[Dark God's Sheath]] and the [[Vampiric Talisman]].

=== Weapons ===
==== Melee ====
*The [[Soul Edge]] and [[Galileo Gladius]] are effective homing weapons that can deal great damage and help with focusing on dodging.
*The [[Crescent Moon]] can deal great damage to the Old Duke, especially in its "tired" phase.
*[[The Last Mourning]], [[Lion Heart]] and the [[Gilded Proboscis]] are options for [[true melee]] damage.

==== Ranged ====
*[[Dodu's Handcannon]] can deal great damage to the Old Duke at the cost of having to aim.
*The [[Handheld Tank]] can tear through the Old Duke's "tired" phase with the Bloodflare armor bonus.
*The [[Monsoon]] can deal tremendous amounts of damage with its fast use time when paired with [[Elysian Arrow]]s.

==== Magic ====
*The [[Ghastly Visage]] is a great homing weapon that can allow focusing on dodging.
*The [[Shadowbolt Staff]] and [[Viscera]] can prove effective with walls while the Viscera can also provide [[lifesteal]].
*[[Clamor Noctus]] and the [[Stratus Sphere]] can be used to fry the Old Duke when it is in its "tired" phase.
*The [[Thunderstorm]] ([[RIV]]) can be extremely viable thanks to its damage output, however it may be difficult to obtain due to its rarity.
*The [[Phantasmal Fury]] can also be used as a viable option, due to the homing capability of the trailing bolts and large damage output.

==== Summoner ====
*The [[Ethereal Subjugator]] and [[Sirius]] can work in conjunction by using half of a player's minion slots each.
*The [[Calamari's Lament]] can also be utilized with full slots.
*The [[Gamma Heart]] is also useful, especially with the Gamma Deathray attack.

==== Rogue ====
*The [[Time Bolt]] can be a powerful weapon if its copies can also land hits.
*The [[Night's Gaze]] can be potent for damage.
*The [[Phantasmal Ruin]] is a very powerful and potent weapon with excellent homing capabilities.
*The [[Valediction]] is an excellent homing weapon.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all around stat boost, especially when paired with [[Lore items|Aquatic Scourge lore]].
*Mages can utilize [[Mana Regeneration Potion]]s or [[Aureus Cell]]s for quick mana regeneration.
*[[Slimy Saddle]] or [[Suspicious Looking Jelly Bean]] can be used for quick downwards acceleration.

== The Fight ==
=== Phase 1 ===
Once the Old Duke has been summoned, the fight will begin in its first phase. Throughout the fight after every ten attacks the Old Duke performs it will lose stamina, become "exhausted" and pause for 5 seconds, making a "huffing" sound as it does this. [[Rage Meter|Rage]] and [[Adrenaline]] should be saved for when the Old Duke does this as it will have no [[defense]] or [[Damage Reduction|DR]]. In the first phase the Old Duke follows a set attack pattern, repeating in this order:

*Charges three times with brief breaks in between them, with the Old Duke's charge direction taking a player's velocity into account, attempting to head for their predicted destination, these charges occur faster and with more velocity in [[Revengeance Mode]] and Death Mode. Take care in carefully dodging these to avoid massive damage. Dashing after the Old Duke charges can also help greatly when avoiding them.
*Spits 5 invincible Tooth Balls that target players for 8 seconds, before becoming stationary and vulnerable after 10 more seconds, killing, making contact, or letting these Tooth Balls live for 15 seconds will cause them to explode into 4 or 8 homing teeth in an outward cross formation, along with spawning a remaining sulphur cloud where the initial explosion took place. Tooth Balls move quickly but have trouble turning around, so they can be dodged by quickly turning a different direction on them.
*Charges three times, again taking player velocity into account.
*Pulses and summons two groups of vulnerable Sulphurous Sharkrons left and right of itself, these Sharkrons inflict the [[Venom]], [[Poisoned]], [[Irradiated]], and [[tgc:Feral_Bite|Feral Bite]] debuffs upon contact damage for three seconds, they will also explode into two damaging Sulphurous Gore chunks when killed or making contact with tiles, these inflict the Poisoned and Irradiated debuffs for three seconds.

Players can generally fly around the area and drop down far away from the Old Duke to avoid most of its attacks, grappling onto Minecart tracks can provide extra mobility for outrunning the Old Duke.

=== Phase 2 ===
After the Old Duke is reduced to {{dv|50%||70%|80%}} of its health, it will transition to this phase, as the Old Duke becomes invulnerable and visibly gains yellow glowing eyes, it will fire 6 Sulphurous Sharkrons upwards as it does this and goes on a modified attack pattern that also repeats itself:

*The Old Duke will now only charge twice, however these charges are faster and still take velocity into account.
*It will fly in a circle, spitting out five Tooth Balls and forming a Sulphurous Vortex that will draw players around and into it if close enough, these linger around for 30 seconds until vanishing.
*The Old Duke will charge twice again.
*It will summon two groups of five Sulphurous Sharkrons and vomit a large amount of gravity-affected Sulphurous gore chunks.

Players can still fly around the area and drop down far away to avoid most of these attacks, but it might become necessary to dodge the Old Duke's charges a lot more as it may catch up to players easier. Remember that the Old Duke will still become tired after every ten attacks, so saving burst damage for these is recommended. Avoiding the vortexes the Old Duke spawns can be a nuisance when they take up space in the area so avoiding them entirely should be prioritized. Alternatively, you can try to lure the Old Duke into a corner when he is going to spawn the vortex, so that it is out of the way.

=== Phase 3 ===
Once reduced to {{dv||20%|35%|50%}} of its health, the Old Duke will start to become more translucent and green, becoming invulnerable for its first two charges and a Sulphurous Sharkron attack. After its first initial teleportation, it becomes vulnerable again.

The attack pattern it follows is the exact same as phase 2, however its charges are even faster, it will charge three times before summoning the Sulphurous Vortex instead of twice, and it gains the ability to teleport to a location near a player before it begins its charging attacks, which do not count towards the Old Duke losing stamina.

Priority should now be on avoiding the Old Duke's charges at all costs as its teleport ability allows it to catch up to players significantly, dashing out of the way after the Old Duke charges or grappling onto Minecart tracks for extra mobility are still extremely helpful for this phase. It is also useful to being falling when the Old Duke does teleports and charges, and he will always teleport above you.

== General Tips ==
*It is most optimal to be positioned above or below the Old Duke at all times, as dodging its charges is significantly more difficult in a horizontal alignment.
*Try to avoid the Tooth Balls at all costs, as they can sometimes damage you several times at once, and potentially lead to an unexpectedly quick death.
*[[Bloodfin]]s are an effective healing item for this boss.
*The [[Rod of Discord]] or the [[Normality Relocator]] are useful tools for avoiding the Old Duke's attacks.
*The [[Bobbit Hook]] is extremely good for mobility because of its fast retraction time and launch speed.
