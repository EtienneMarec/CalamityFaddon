The '''Silva armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set. It is the Magic/Summoner counterpart to [[God Slayer armor]] and the last armor sets needed to craft the [[Auric Tesla armor]] for those classes. It cannot be obtained until after defeating several enemies from the upgraded [[Solar Eclipse]], [[Pumpkin Moon|Pumpkin]] and [[Frost Moon]]s as its recipe calls for [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]]. If the entire set is visible (whether equipped or in social slots), the wearer will have afterimages.

It consists of the '''Silva Armor''' and '''Silva Leggings''', as well as having two different headpieces suited to the Magic and Summoner classes, being: the '''Silva Masked Cap''' and '''Silva Horned Hood'''. All helmets share some common set bonuses, but also provide set bonuses specific to their respective class as well.

The '''Silva Armor''' provides 44 defense, 12% increased damage and 8% increased critical strike chance, and +80 max life. The '''Silva Leggings''' provide 39 defense, 12% increased damage and critical strike chance, and 10% increased movement speed.
*The '''Silva Masked Cap''' gives 21 defense, 23% increased magic damage and 13% magic critical strike chance, +100 max mana, and 19% reduced mana cost
*The '''Silva Horned Hood''' gives 13 defense and 10% increased minion damage.

== Set Bonuses ==
Regardless of the headpiece chosen, equipping a full set of Silva armor will provide the following set bonuses:
*Occasionally spawns healing leaf orbs upon enemy hits.
*Boosts maximum run speed and acceleration by 5%.
*If the wearer is reduced to 0 HP, they will receive the [[Silva Immunity]] [[buff]], which makes them [[Death Prevention|unable to die]] for 8 seconds.
**This effect has a 5 minute cooldown before it can trigger again. The cooldown does not decrement during boss fights or events.
**If the wearer is wearing [[Silva Wings]] or is under the effects of the [[Draconic Surge]] buff when this activates, they will be healed to half health.

In addition to the above, each headpiece provides its own class-specific set bonus:

'''Silva Masked Cap''':
*All magic projectiles have a 100% chance to cause a massive explosion upon hitting an enemy every 5 seconds. This explosion deals four times the damage of the projectile that triggered it. 
**The explosion have a softcap of 720 damage, with any damage exceeding the limit dealing 66% less damage.

'''Silva Horned Hood''':
*Increases minion damage by 65% and +5 max minons.
*Summons a Silva Crystal above the wearer's head, which blasts enemies with pulses of life energy similarly to the [[Rainbow Crystal Staff|Rainbow Crystal]] sentry.
**The bursts of light deal 600 damage.
