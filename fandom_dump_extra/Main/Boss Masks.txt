'''Boss Masks''' are [[vanity items]] dropped by most [[bosses]] added in the Calamity Mod. They are worn in the social head slot, like [[tgc:Mask|vanilla masks]]. Each boss has a 14.29% (1 in 7) chance to drop its own mask. In total, there are 34 boss masks.
