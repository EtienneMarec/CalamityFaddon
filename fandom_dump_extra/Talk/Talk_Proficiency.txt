:For starters, the best place to ask questions regarding the Mod is the Calamity Mod Discord server. With that out of the way, due to the way Proficiency is coded, its meant to take time to grind, and not be a simple feat to be completed in 10 minutes. But the best way to gain Proficiency is to use a high-fire rate weapon with low damage on bulky enemies, such as enemies found in the [[Jungle]] or the [[Astral Infection]]. It is also important to remember to equip a [[Level Meter]] for your specific class to help gain Proficiency at a faster pace.
