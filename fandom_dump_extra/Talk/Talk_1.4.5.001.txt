== Name ==
Where is this referred to as "1.4.5.001"? The [https://github.com/MountainDrew8/CalamityMod/releases GitHub page] states that it's "v1.4.5.1". — [[User:Godslayerchickennugget|Godslayerchickennugget]] ([[User talk:Godslayerchickennugget|talk]] | [[Special:Contributions/Godslayerchickennugget|contribs]]) 20:53, 23 August 2020 (UTC)

:The official release name for Calamity mod updates follows the naming pattern "X.X.X.XXX". Officially, Rust and Dust was update "v1.4.5.001". The "v" is missing from this wiki due to the "v" standing for version, and the updates are under the "Calamity Mod Versions" template. "v1.4.5.1" is just a simplified format of the ".001" pattern.
