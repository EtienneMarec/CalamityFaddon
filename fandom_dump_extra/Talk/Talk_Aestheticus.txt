==Message from DevAesthetic==
Hey everyone, this is DevAesthetic. Yes the original Patreon owner and everything. I just want to say this.
Back in 2018, I first came across Calamity mod via the music, Just like how everyone else did. Yeah, The music is fantastic and it's probably one of the driving forces to me to try out this mod. And when I first played through it, was amazed about the amount of bosses and content that the mod provided, Even then some of the items were fantastic. However, As time went on and everything, I guess I had more of a taste for other mods. If anything, I kinda wished that I started out with Thorium as the content in that mod is actually really cool and more unique. These days there's a bunch of really cool mods out there, Some of them I also donated to Like Split, and Fargos (I think plenty of people knows about the Devious Aestheticus, Which fun fact, The design was something I didn't really want but I couldn't care less because I wanted to get away from Fargos after the Terry N. Muse situation for what had come out). I know that to many people, "Calamity should be part of Vanilla Terraria!" "Terraria is just a base for the game Calamity", Says these dumbass things that people tends to forget that a good portion of players actually play Vanilla still. I just want to say, You're actually fucking stupid and you forgot what made Terraria, Terraria. All these mods out there really compliments the games' nature, The more lighthearted funny nature alongside the cool looking boss fights with it's own unique Music style. The mods I can think of that are like that is Spirit, Split, Thorium (Especially thorium). If Calamity was part of Terraria, Most people would actually feel like it would be unplayable or unnecessary, People would not be happy because what Vanilla Terraria has to offer is perfect for them. If you don't believe me, Go on Steam Charts and look up how many people are playing Terraria and how many people are Playing Tmodloader. And let me rephrase this, Calamity probably takes up a good portion of the players in Tmod, But not everyone is playing Calamity.

Now also let me talk about this, I could not stand being called the "Aestheticus guy" when I join other discord servers related to Terraria. I personally liked being considered more of the guy who has the Shortstack OC than that. The many times people come to me and keep telling me that my weapon sucks, And having no fucking control over what I can do with the weapon, It's just heartbreaking to me. It really was putting me down a downward spiral for many months to the point where I couldn't really be around the modding scene anymore. I know, It may sound really dumb, But it's like a pebble that hits bigger rocks going to a mountain to the point where it become a huge boulder crashing down on me the more I went through it. So, I decided that I'm just not even gonna be DevAesthetic anymore, A name I've had for quite a while and been my longest Alias for many years.

However with the recent news that I got from someone, I'm actually glad Calamity is finally dying out, Sure it's not "Dead" But maybe, Just maybe, People will become motivated to actually try out other mods without using Calamity. Trust me when I say this, A lot of these other mods out there are actually really good, They also have really good OSTs, Bossfights, Items, Etc. And they would be a lot more original and unique experience for people to try out. People just needs to learn that they can't live in comfort forever and need to reach out and try something new instead. 

I've also want to say towards the Developers of the Calamity mod. I've seen many times where someone suggests something for the Aestheticus to have something that sounds really fucking cool, But instead you had to say "Ermm well, It's a patreon item owner and like we would have to get approval from the original creator and all that". Litterally fucking do it anyways, Legit this shit actually pisses me the fuck off everytime i see it.

Anyways, I think after everything, Aestheticus really needs a buff. Infact, We need to push it to even higher grounds and try to make it the best Prehardmode item in the game. I know that making it the best weapon in the game Would actually be silly, But for some of the things I've heard in which people have pushed the Aestheticus towards, Is astonishing. Whether it gets used for a new weapon, or just get buffs would be great. Also, I noticed that someone made fanart of the Aestheticus and made even a person that represents that. I actually got emotional a bit when I saw it cause I was so Awestruck about it.
Whoever you are that made the image, Thank you.

Anyways, I just want to say thank you to everyone in the community who actually was really cool, I've made several friends along the way and got to know better to some of the devs. To those who left recently, I hope to see your works out there elsewhere.

-DevAesthetic(Formally) 12-5-2022
