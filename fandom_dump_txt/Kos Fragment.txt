The '''Kos Fragment''' is a Pr-Hardmode Mode non-consumable [[Summoning items|summoning item]] that is dropped by [[Skeletron]]. It is used to summon 3 [[bosses]] at various locations in the world. Each location summons a different boss.
*When used in [[Space]], it summons the [[Weaver|Cloud Pusher]].
*When used in the [[Dungeon]], it summons the [[Ceasing Void]].
*When used in [[The Underworld]], it summons [[Sigmund]].


== Trivia ==
*The name of the item is NOT a reference to the character Kos, from the video game ''[[wikipedia:Bloodborne|Bloodborne]]''.
