'''Darukum, Tormented Plane''' is a subworld added in [[Voidmaster's Threnody]] that houses several [[biome]]s and [[boss]]es.

== Biomes ==

=== [[Ethereal Forest]] ===

The Ethereal Forest is the main biome in Darukum. It hosts trees which bear [[Ghostwood]].

=== [[Dark Caves]] ===

The Dark Caves are the main underground biome in Darukum. It hold ores exclusive to Darukum.

=== [[Dark Depths]] ===

The Dark Depths spawn where the [[Underworld]] would normally be. It contains the [[Dark City]] subbiome.
