The '''1.4.3.001''' (or '''1.4.3.1''') update added numerous equipment, especially for the [[Spy]] and Summoner classes. It also included many AI changes in [[Revengeance Mode]], balance changes, general changes, resprites, and fixes. It is especially notable for the addition of Environmental Hazards, changes to [[Bosses]] and Enemy AIs, and other miscellaneous changes that are exclusive to [[Death Mode]].
