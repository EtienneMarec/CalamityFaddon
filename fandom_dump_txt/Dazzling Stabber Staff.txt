The '''Dazzling Stabber Staff''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. It summons Holy Blade minions that rally in a cone-formation above the player’s head. When enemies are nearby, the blades will dash into enemies and move in an ellipse between hits while leaving behind a purely visual glowing trail.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Notes ==
*[[Storm Weaver]] takes only 50% damage from the Dazzling Stabber Staff.

== Trivia ==
*"En garde" is French for "On [your] guard", originally used as a warning term in the sport of [[wikipedia:fencing|fencing]].
