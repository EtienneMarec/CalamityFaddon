The '''Pearl of Enthrallment''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is dropped by the [[Leviathan and Anahita]]. It summons a friendly Water Elemental which remains above the player's head. It fights similarly to the [[Anahita]] boss, attacking with water spears, frost mist, and magical notes.
