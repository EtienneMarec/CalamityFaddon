The '''Soul of Phytogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Phytogen]].

When equipped, mining [[ores]] boost's the player's ''pickaxe's'' damage by 20% for 10 seconds.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.

* Thank you, it must have been difficult with the sheer density of wildlife in the [[Jungle]]. Was the construct protecting this soul delicious? It's the impression I get from this soul's aroma.
* Plants tend to know what the soil needs best, and sometimes that means letting the forest burn. We as people chose to maintain our power instead, and decided to rewrite the ecosystem. For the Onyx Kinsmen who feigned balance, they continue to reap the planet's treasures gluttonously.
* The remaining outcomes I see are [[Secret world seeds|Jungle consuming the world]] or the [[Goozma|Kinsmen reaching absolute power]]. Both are rather calamitous.

After this, Permafrost will be able to raise stone pillars to absorb enemy projectiles and deal knockback.
