The '''Elder Berry''' is a [[permanent power-up]] item that drops from [[Scorn Eater]]s, [[Impious Immolator]]s, [[Profaned Energy|Profaned Energies]], and the [[Yggdrasil Ent]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] after the player has used all 3 [[Stamina Meter|Stamina Upgrades]]. It is the only permanent consumable in the game that gives the player a dash.

== Notes ==
*The Elder Berry's dash will be disabled by any covering that provides its own dash.
*When dropped, it will float in place with a unique flying animation.
