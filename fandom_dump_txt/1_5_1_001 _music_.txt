The '''1.5.1.001''' (or '''1.5.1.1''') Music mod update changed [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s music boxes.

This Music Mod version is compatible with Main mod version [[1.5.1.001]] & [[1.5.1.002]].

== Changes ==
*"Infernal Catharsis (Rebirth)" now contains the entire track, instead of cutting to the second half. It is now used for the first phase of Jungle Dragon, Yharon instead of "Infernal Catharsis".
*Renamed Music Box (Yharon, Post-dark sun) to [[Music Box (Infernal Catharsis)]].
*Renamed Music Box (Yharon, Rebirth) to [[Music Box (Yharon)]].
*Renamed Music Box (Yharon, Roar of the Jungle Dragon) to [[Music Box (Yharon Phase 2)]].
*Music Box (Infernal Catharsis) can now be crafted with 5 [[Darksun Fragment]]s and 3 [[Cosmilite Bar]]s at a [[Cosmic Anvil]].
*Music Box (Yharon) can no longer be crafted.
