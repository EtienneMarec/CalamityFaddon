==Trivia ==

*"Bloop", one of the Calamity devs, is the name of the creator of the Sloom.
*The Sloome mostly speaks in a type of broken English.
*Sloome has a unique death message that says "Bloop the Sloome ran away..."

* If Exodygen spawns, A different death message will appear saying "Bloop the Sloome left..." in turquoise.

*''"Beware the killer fishy in San Diego"'' is a reference to the Killer fish from San Diego meme.
*"''If big robot for big worm. then why no big robot for big brain?"'' Is a reference towards the [[The Derellect|Derellect]] and [[XP-00 Hypnos]].
**This has actually been officially confirmed by John Derellect and John XP-00 Hypnos themselves
