The '''1.1.5.0948''' update added several new weapons and a variety of resprites, most notably including [[Calamitas]] and [[Cryogen]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Astreal Defeat]], [[The Ballista]], [[Forsaken Saber]], [[Harvest Staff]], [[Pumpler]], [[Rare Waifu in a Bottle]], [[Storm Surge]], [[Tome of Fates]], [[Trinity]], and [[Winter's Fury]].

== Changes ==
*Resprited [[Calamitas]], [[Cryocore]]s, and [[Cryogen]].
*Resprited [[Abyssal Warhammer]], [[Bittercold Staff]], [[Crystal Piercer]], [[Darkecho Greatbow]], [[Effluvium Bow]], [[Glacial Crusher]], [[Ice Star]], [[Nuclear Fury]], [[Shimmerspark]], [[Snowstorm Staff]], [[Starnight Lance]], [[Sun God Staff]], [[Sun Spirit Staff]], [[Temporal Floe Sword]], and [[Verdant]].
*Resprited [[Bar of Life]], [[Cryo Bar]], [[Meld Blob]], and [[Verstaltite Bar]].
*Resprited [[Shardlight Pickaxe]].
*Resprited [[Harpy Ring]], [[Ornate Shield]], [[Soul of Cryogen]], [[Starlight Wings]], and [[Statis' Ninja Belt]].
*Resprited [[Treasure Bag (Cryogen)]].
*Resprited [[Cryo Key]].
*Resprited [[Seabound Staff]]'s Brittle Star minion.
