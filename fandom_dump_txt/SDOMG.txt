The '''SDOMG''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] which is the upgraded version of [[SDFMG]]. When left click is held, it rapidly fires a spread of uneven bullets which slowly becomes more accurate over the course of 10 seconds. If the player has [[Plasma Grenade]]s and is standing still, the item's right click function can be used which shoots a large laser beam which inflicts the player with [[On Fire!]], [[Slow]], and [[Oiled]] while used. Each 5 seconds used will consume one Plasma Grenade.
