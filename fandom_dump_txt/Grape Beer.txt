'''Grape Beer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Grape Beer''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces [[defense]] and movement speed, as well as healing 100 life and mana. It also inflicts the [[Potion Sickness]] debuff for 1 minute and the [[Mana Sickness]] debuff for 5 seconds.
