The '''Omega Healing Potion''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[potion]] that heals the player for 300 health, and inflicts the [[Potion Sickness]] [[debuff]] upon use. It is also dropped from the [[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], each individual member of [[The Fabricational Quartet]], [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]].
