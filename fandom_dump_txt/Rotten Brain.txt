The '''Rotten Brain''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[The Hive Mind]]. It will grant a 10% increase to all damage types when below 75% hp, decrease movement speed by 5% when below 50% health and makes shade rain down on you when you are hit. It is also sold by the [[Dryad]] after [[The Perforators]] are defeated.
