The '''Magna Cannon''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] that fires a blue charge. Despite not being able to penetrate enemies like the [[Space Gun]], it can still be considered a direct upgrade due to the fact that it fires 3 stacked projectiles per shot, effectively tripling its damage output against single targets.
