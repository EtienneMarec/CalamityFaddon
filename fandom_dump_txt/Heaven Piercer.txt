The '''Heaven Piercer''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Drill]] and [[Axe]] combination. It can mine blocks up to {{item|Pitchblende}} and [[Primordial Ore]]. Right clicking with the drill will cause it to fire a laser projectile which breaks any tiles it touches.

== Trivia ==
*The tool's name is a reference to ''[[wikipedia:Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann|Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann]].''
**The name also parallels [[The Devourer of Gods]]' will to destroy gods, while the Heaven Piercer's name expresses the motivation to destroy heaven, which is where gods are typically depicted as living at.
