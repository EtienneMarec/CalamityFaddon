The '''1.4.1.003''' update cleaned up most [[item]] [[rarity|rarities]] and sell prices, along with [[enemy]] coin drop counts. It also added several new [[weapons]] dropped by [[Astral Infection]] enemies, a few new [[classless weapons]] with special [[ammo]] dropped by [[bosses]] on their first defeats, and nerfs to the [[Rage Meter|Rage]] and [[Adrenaline Meter]]s. The update was also notable for splitting off the mod's [[music]] into a separate mod.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Astral Scythe]], [[Astralachnea Staff]], [[Bazooka]], [[Elephant Killer]], [[Hive Pod]], [[Lightning Hawk]], [[Magnum]], [[Stellar Cannon]], and [[Titan Arm]].
*Added [[Grenade Shells]] and [[Magnum Rounds]].
