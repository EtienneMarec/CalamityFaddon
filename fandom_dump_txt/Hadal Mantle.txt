The '''Hadal Mantle''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allows the player to fly and glide. If these wings are worn along with [[Hydrothermic armor]] and any Hydrothermic headpiece, the player's damage is increased by 5%.
