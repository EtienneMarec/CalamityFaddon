The '''Blissful Bombardier''' is a Godseeker Mode [[launcher]] dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. It rapidly fires rockets that explode in a large radius. As the rockets travel, they periodically emit small explosive bursts, which are capable of dealing damage as well; these midair bursts always inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] on hit enemies.
