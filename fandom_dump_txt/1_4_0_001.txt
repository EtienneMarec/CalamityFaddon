The '''1.4.0.001''' update, also known as the '''Astral Expansion''', overhauled the [[Astral Meteor]] into a full biome known as the Astral Infection. It also reworked [[Astrageldon Slime]] into [[Astrum Aureus]], added two new [[Town NPC]]s, made a large amount of [[covering]] nerfs, and added the [[Boss Rush]] event.
