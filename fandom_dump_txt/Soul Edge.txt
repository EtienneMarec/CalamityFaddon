The '''Soul Edge''' is a Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Eidolon Wyrm]]s after [[Polterghast]] is defeated, as well as from the [[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]]. The Soul Edge's projectiles inflict [[Crush Depth]]. When swinging the blade, 2-3 [[Phantom Spirit]]s will be released and seek out nearby enemies. If no enemies are near they will hover around the player until an enemy gets in range, similar to the [[Exoblade]].
