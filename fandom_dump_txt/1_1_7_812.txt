The '''1.1.7.812''' update made a couple of small fixes and changed [[The Absorber]]'s recipe to not be unobtainable in certain worlds.

== Changes ==
*The Absorber now uses either 1 [[Craw Carapace]] or 1 [[Giant Shell]] in its recipe instead of both.
*[[Cosmic Kunai]] now internally uses the {{rare|9}} rarity, meaning it can no longer be destroyed in [[Lava]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed some transparency errors on [[Majestic Guard]]'s sprite.
