'''Defiled One''' is a [[debuff]] that removes the player's wing flight and multi-jumps. It is inflicted onto the player by [[DWN-∞, Zero]] when alive, and lasts until the [[Challenges|challenge]] is defeated.

== Notes ==
*If the player has the Calamity: Community Remix add-on enabled, the double jump given to the player will override Defiled One's multi-jump disable, essentially granting the player a triple jump.

== Trivia ==
*Defiled one is a reference to the now-removed [[Defiled Feather]] effect, which also had the ability to disable wing flight.
