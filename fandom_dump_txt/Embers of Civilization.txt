'''Embers of Civilization''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by [[The Wall of Bronze]], the [[Weapons]] it drops, and bronze [[enemies]] in [[Frozen Hell]], {{dei}} as well as enemies and weapons related to the [[Horseslimes of the Goopocalypse]] and armageddon. It causes the player to lose 15 health per second, deal 15% less damage, use 30% more [[Mana]], and lose 3 minion slots while the duration is active. This can despawn active minions, but the count will not be reduced below one.

If the debuff is inflicted onto an enemy, it will lose 50 health per second and 15% damage reduction.
