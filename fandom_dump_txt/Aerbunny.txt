The '''Aerbunny''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Critters|Critter]] that spawns in [[The Aether]] during the day. Aerbunnys have a 2.5% chance to drop [[tgc:White_String|White String]] when slain, although they don't drop money.

== Trivia ==
*The Aerbunny was the first NPC to be teased for the "''Aether Update''"
*The texture used for the Aerbunny is a modified version of the vanilla [[tgc:Bunny|Bunny]]
