'''Ataraxia''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Swords|broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung, Ataraxia fires three rocket-like projectiles, two small and one large, towards the cursor. The larger projectile explodes on impact with a tile or enemy. The smaller projectiles split into several needles on impact, which fly a short distance from the impact point and rapidly damage enemies. When the blade itself strikes a foe, five additional homing projectiles are produced, flying around the target in a flower-like pattern. The homing projectiles may choose to home on a different enemy if many are close by.

Ataraxia, along with all of its projectiles and their explosions, inflict the [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.

== Trivia ==
*The word ''ataraxia'' refers to a state of emotional tranquility and peace of mind.
*The word ''equanimity'' refers to a state of mental calmness and evenness of temper.
**This word is also used for the name of a [[Equanimity|rogue boomerang]].
*The projectiles for the Ataraxia are named 'Not [[Exoblade]]', 'Also Not Exoblade', 'Definitely Not Exoblade', and 'Still Not Exoblade'.
