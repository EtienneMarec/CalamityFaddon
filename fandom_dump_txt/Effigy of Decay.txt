The '''Effigy of Decay''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[effigy]] that can also be found in [[Rusty Chest]]s. When placed down, all nearby players are granted water-breathing outside of the [[Abyss]], along with immunity to the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]].
