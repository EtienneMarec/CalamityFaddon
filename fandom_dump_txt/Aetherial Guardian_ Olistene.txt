'''Aetherial Guardian, Olistene''' (Japanese: イセリアルガーディアン オリステネ ''Iseriarugādian orisutene'') is a [[boss]] that is intended to be fought shortly before the first mechanical boss.

Defeating her will allow the [[Radiant Paladin]] [[Town NPC]] to move in.

==Spawn==
Aetherial Guardian, Olistene does not spawn on her own, and requires the player to summon her manually by inserting a [[Rotting Eyeball]] into the [[Luminous Altar]] found near the center of [[Caelus]].

== Behavior ==
The Boss will fly around the player and use a variety of attacks.

=== Attacks ===
==== Phase 1 ====
*Flies around the player for {{dv|3||1.5}} seconds.
*Dashes directly at the player while creating a series of mines in her direction that will explode into three evenly spaced Light Bolts after {{dv|3||2}} seconds.

==== Phase 2 ====
*Transitions at {{dv|80||85%|}} health.
These attacks are added to her arsenal:
*Throws an Incandescent Light Spear at the player, which will split into {{dv|4|6}} smaller homing spears after 1.5 seconds.
*Attempts to get close to the player while making short ranged Slashes.

==== Phase 3 ====
*Transitions at {{dv|60%||70%|}} health.
These attacks are added to her arsenal:
*Creates a {{item|link=|Lightning Orb|ext=gif|size=20px}} that will fire {{dv|3||2}} bolts of lightning towards the player.
*Launches {{dv|3||4}} Light Spheres that will orbit the player then explode into four slow moving Light Blots in a cross pattern.

==== Phase 4 ====
*Transitions at {{dv|40%||55%|}} health.
This attack are added to her arsenal:
*Dashes directly at the player and creates a Light Portal, the portal will spawn a variety of enemies until killed.
**The Boss will stop attacking the player while a portal is active.
**When a portal is killed, all enemies it spawns will be killed with it.

==== Phase 5 ====
*Transitions at {{dv|15%||30%|}} health.
All other attacks are removed and Olistene only uses two attacks:
*Tries to get close to the player while making medium range slashes
*Fires a semi circle of 3 Light Spears 2 times

==== Desperation Phase ====
*Once Olistene's health is at 1%, she will cease all attacks, and transform into a planetoid similar to the ones in Caelus, before ramming at the player
**Once she hits a block, the fight will end and she will drop her items


=== In [[Expert Mode]] ===
*Creates a spread of Light Spears every {{dv||1|0.75|0.5}} seconds while flying around the player.
*When a Portal is killed, it will explode into {{dv||6||8|14}} evenly spaced Light Bolts.
*All attacks are 15% faster

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*Portals will create an even spread of 5 Light Bolts every {{dv|||2.5|2}} seconds.
*She will launch two Lightning Orbs perpendicular to the player instead of one.
*All attacks are 15% faster, adding up with the Expert buff to 30%.
