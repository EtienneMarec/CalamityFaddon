'''Verdantium Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Ores|ore]] which generates in [[Dirt Block|Dirt]], [[Mud Block|Mud]], and [[Stone Block]]s in the [[Jungle]] and [[Baron Strait]] after [[Duke Fishron]] has been defeated, and is used to craft [[Verdantium Bar]]s at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or a [[Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least an [[Seismic Hampick]] to mine. It is also dropped by [[Verdant Slime]]s.
