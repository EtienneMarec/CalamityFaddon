The '''Joyful Heart''' is a [[pet]] summoning item that drops from [[Shark]]s. It summons a white shark pet to fly around the player, occasionally spinning around itself. It has a 1 in 10000 chance every frame to squeak and causes all players, [[enemies]], [[critters]], and [[Town NPCs]] within a 15-block radius to emit heart particles similarly to a [[Love Potion]].

== Notes ==
*If the player is named "Aleksh" or "Shark Lad", the player can obtain the Joyful Heart from the [[Starter Bag]].
*Lad Shark behaves the same way as the Baby Skeletron Head summoned by the [[Bone Key]], and the two will tend to overlap if both are summoned.
