The '''Light Wraith''' is a [[Town NPCs|Town NPC]] that will spawn once provided suitable housing.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will generate light orbs that shoot projectiles at enemies and inflict the [[Purified]] debuff.

==Functions==
Upon choosing the "Next Target" dialogue option, the Light Wraith will give dialogue about the next boss in normal progression

==Names==
*The Light Wraith will always be named Guarian. <!-- Are we sure it's not just a misspelling of Guardian? -->
