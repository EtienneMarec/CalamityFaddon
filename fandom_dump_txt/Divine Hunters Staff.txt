The '''Divine Hunters Staff''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]] have been defeated last. It summons 2 minimature clones of [[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]] named Artemilite and Apollite, respectively. Artemiltie will shoot orange pulse lasers, and Apollite will shoot plasma blasts that explode into plasma bolts. There can only be one pair of hunters at a time. It requires 6 minion slots to use.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Ruthless'''. The Mythical modifier provides the widest array of stat bonuses, but these primarily affect the initial summon rather than the resulting minion. Additionally, minions cannot deal [[Critical hit|critical hits]]. The only lasting advantage a Mythical Divine Hunters Staff has over a Ruthless one is [[knockback]].
