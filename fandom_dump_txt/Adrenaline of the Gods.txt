The '''Adrenaline of the Gods''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[permanent power-up]] item. It increases the damage of the [[Adrenaline Mode]] [[buff]] by +45% and increases how much damage reduction the player gains when hit while the meter is full by +15%. It can only be used once.
