The '''Primal Aspid''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns in the [[Snow biome]]. It slowly pursues the player in an attempt to ram them, occasionally firing a spread of 3 projectiles. It will emit dust from its mouth shortly before firing, telegraphing the attack. The projectile harmlessly bursts upon contact with tiles. Killing 20 Primal Aspids will summon [[Cryogen]]. Once found the Player it will chase it relentlessly until It has been slain Naturally or by the Player.
{{-}}

== Trivia ==
* The Primal Aspid is a direct homage to an enemy of the same name and similar apperance from the game, ''[https://store.steampowered.com/app/367520/Hollow_Knight/ Hollow Knight]''.
** The fact that it spawns in the Snow biome is a reference to an ash-filled area in Hollow Knight's called Kingdom's Edge, which is where Primal Aspids are found.
** They drop [[Essence of Chaos]] because of how much it is filled with rage to murder you.
