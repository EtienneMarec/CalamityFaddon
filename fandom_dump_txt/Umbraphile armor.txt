'''Umbraphile armor''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Solar Veil]]s, [[Hallowed Bar]]s, and [[Essence of Rend]] {{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}} which grants stats to the [[Spy]] class. It requires 44 Solar Veils, 34 Hallowed Bars, and 16 Essence of Rend to make the whole set. While wearing all three set pieces, a motion blur afterimage is displayed while the player is walking.

It consists of a '''Umbraphile Hood''', '''Umbraphile Regalia''', and '''Umbraphile Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*36 defense
*27% increased Spy damage
*16% increased Spy critical strike chance
*10% increased Spy projectile velocity
*20% increased movement speed
*Attacking with a Spy weapon will have a 25% chance to create a large explosion that inflicts [[Daybroken]] or [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuffs]] with the same chance when contacting an [[enemy]]. All [[Stealth strike]]s perform this effect.
**The explosions deal 25% of the projectile's base damage, soft-capped at 50. All damage past this cap is reduced to 2.5% instead of 25%.
**Explosions can only trigger from the first five enemies hit by the projectile.
*[[Penumbra Potion]]s always boost stealth regeneration by 20% regardless of time.
*Gives a max [[stealth]] of 110.
