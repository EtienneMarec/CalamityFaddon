'''Grarble coverings''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] ranged [[Covering]] set. It requires 28 [[Gold Bar]]s or [[Platinum Bar]]s,  22 [[Marble Block]]s and 22 [[Granite Block]]s to craft the entire set.

It consists of a '''Grarble Weighted Helm''', '''Grarble Platemail''', and '''Grarble Greaves'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*8-18 defense
*5% increased ranged damage
*3% increased ranged critical strike chance
*7% increased movement speed
*Depending on how long the player has been dealing damage without taking damage, the player's defense will increase up to 10. This additional defense will be lost upon taking damage.
