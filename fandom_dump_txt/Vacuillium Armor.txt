'''Vacuillium Armor''' is an armor set made up of [[Apocalyptic Bar]]s, [[Lihzahrd Brick]]s, and [[Solar Veil]]s. It can be considered a Post-[[Golem]] version of the [[Reaver armor]]. Each armor piece gives its own effects and boosts to the player's stats, but mainly upgraded versions of the Reaver armor boosts.
