The '''Flask of Plague''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[flask]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It grants the user the '''Weapon Imbue: Plague''' [[buff]] when consumed, which causes [[melee weapons|melee]], [[whip]], and [[rogue weapons]] to inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.

== Notes ==
*1-2 Flasks of Plague can be obtained from [[Jungle Crate|Jungle]] and [[Bramble Crate]]s at a 15% chance once [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] has been defeated.
