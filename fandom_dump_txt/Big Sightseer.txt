The '''Big Sightseer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns in the [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. It slowly pursues the player in an attempt to ram them, occasionally firing a projectile with a double-helix trail. It will emit dust from its mouth shortly before firing, telegraphing the attack. The projectile harmlessly bursts upon contact with tiles or any [[weapon]].
