The '''Frigidemon''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in [[Frozen Hell]]. They attack by summoning 5 sets of 3 hailberds near the player. These projectiles accelerate over time, and can move through walls. Upon reaching {{dv|25%|60%}} HP, they will power up, increasing their speed and increasing their number of attacks from 5 to 8.
