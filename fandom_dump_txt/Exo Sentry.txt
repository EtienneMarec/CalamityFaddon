The '''Exo Sentry (Japanese: エクソセントリー Ekusosentorī)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. It hovers at a distance away from the player, slowly moving away from them if they get too close. It attacks by firing three Tesla Bolts in rapid succession.

It can be supercharged by the [[Exo Obelisk]] enemy, causing its lights to turn bright yellow. It will now stay much closer to the player, firing bolts far more frequently.
