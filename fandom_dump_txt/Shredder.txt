The '''Shredder''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. The primary fire (left-click) shoots a barrage of fast-moving blue energy bolts which split off in many directions and speeds upon impacting any enemy or tile and deal 85% damage. The secondary fire (right-click) shoots an equivalent barrage of normal bullets. Both firing modes shoot an identical number of projectiles and have the same firing speed and spread pattern. Each shot has a use time of 3.

== Notes ==
*The Shredder's secondary fire (right-click) can use any type of bullet ammo and will ''not'' change used bullets into [[Musket Ball]]s.
*This weapon can cause extreme lag when fired continuously. The secondary fire will cause more lag with bullets that create many particles, such as [[Chlorophyte Bullet]]s, [[Holy Fire Bullet]]s or [[Acid Round]]s.

== Trivia ==
*While the weapon was originally meant as a reference to the Shredder from ''[[wikipedia:Turok 2: Seeds of Evil|Turok 2: Seeds of Evil]]'', the item underwent changes in development and is now mainly a reference to the Shredifier (and the Infinity) from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
*Its tooltip is in reference to the processor-intensive effects of the primary attack, causing frame rate drops on many players' devices.
