The '''1.3.2.001''' update contained a large amount of balance changes to the mod's [[enemies]], [[coverings]], and [[Covering]]. The update also reworked [[Ravager]] and [[Astrageldon Slime]], and resprited four of the mod's bosses. The update was also notable for removing [[THE LORDE]].

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Eidolist]], [[Flame Pillar]], [[Rock Pillar]].
*Added [[Celestial Jewel]].
*Added [[Auric Ore]], [[Divine Geode]].
*Added throwing variants for [[Empyrean Knives]] and [[Scourge of the Cosmos]].
*Added [[Star Cannon EX]].
*Added [[Charred Relic]].
*Added Astrageldon Slime [[Treasure Bag]].
*Added [[Lore items]] for [[Calamitas]], [[The Destroyer]], [[Plantera]], [[Skeletron Prime]], [[The Twins]].
*Added [[Weak Petrification]].
