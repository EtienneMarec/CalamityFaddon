The '''Flahr''' is a [[grab bag]]-type [[fish]] that can be caught in water in the [[Brimstone Crag]] after [[Calamitas]] and [[Plantera]] have been defeated during a [[Blood Moon]] or a [[Solar Eclipse]] with a 10% chance. When opened, the player will recieve between 1 to 5 [[Flah]] and 5 to 15 [[Flah Seeds]].
