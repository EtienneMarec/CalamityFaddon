'''Blood Orbs''' are a [[crafting material]] dropped by [[enemies]] during a [[Blood Moon]]. They can be used to create any vanilla or Calamity [[Buff Potions|buff potions]] as well as upgrade [[Supreme Healing Potion]]s into [[Omega Healing Potion]]s, and craft [[Bloodstone Core]]s. Wearing [[Bloodflare armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]] grants an extra, independent chance for enemies to drop Blood Orbs.

== Notes ==
*All Potions crafted with Blood Orbs can only be crafted at an [[Alchemy Table]] except the [[Omega Healing Potion]] and [[Calamitas' Brew]], which require a [[Cosmic Anvil]] and the [[Imbuing Station]] respectively.
*[[Xerocodile]]s also provide 5-15 Blood Orbs when opened.
*Blood Orbs will not be dropped from enemies spawned by [[Statues]].

== Tips ==
*Killing multiple [[King Slime]]s can be an extremely efficient way to farm Blood Orbs, especially after defeating the [[Moon Lord]].
*By stacking the effects of [[Death Mode]]'s mob spawn rate multiplier during a Blood Moon with the [[Water Candle]], [[Battle Potion]], [[Zerg Potion]], and/or [[Chaos Candle]], Blood Orbs can be farmed incredibly quickly; one Blood Moon can yield 500-700 Blood Orbs with the right setup, potentially negating the need to farm the basic herbs required for potion brewing.
**The [[Blood Relic]] and [[Cosmolight]] can be used to further expedite this process by allowing Blood Moons to constantly be summoned to farm Blood Orbs.
*Blood Orbs allow certain potions to be crafted in Pre-Hardmode, despite the fact that they normally require [[Hardmode]] materials. These include:
**[[Cadance Potion]]
**[[Lifeforce Potion]]
