The '''1.4.3.001''' (or '''1.4.3.1''') Music mod update added support for the "Boss Checklist" mod.

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod versions [[1.4.3.001]] and [[1.4.3.002]].

== Changes ==
*Renamed Music Box (Yharon, Pre-dark sun) to [[Music Box (Yharon, Post-dark sun)]].
*Replaced "Heaven's Hell-Sent Gift" with "The Heaven-Sent Abomination" as the music track for the [[Astral Infection]].
*Music Boxes can now be tracked with the "Boss Checklist" mod.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed an oversight that caused [[Music Boxes]] to occasionally become unloaded tiles when updating the mod.
