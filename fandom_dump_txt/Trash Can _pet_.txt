'''Trash Can''' is a [[pet]] summoning item dropped by [[Trasher]]s. It summons a little [[wikipedia:Danny DeVito|Danny Devito]] to follow the player. If the player moves too fast, Danny Devito will use a little red carpet to fly towards them.

== Trivia ==
*This item is based on a famous episode from the show ''[[wikipedia:It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia|It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia]]'', in which [[wikipedia:Danny DeVito|Danny DeVito]], playing as [[wikipedia:Frank Reynolds|Frank Reynolds]], becomes a wrestler known as The Trash Man.
**The item itself is dedicated to Uncle Danny, the owner and head administrator of the Calamity Mod Discord who is heavily involved in Calamity work and part of the developer team, who uses Danny Devito as his online persona.
