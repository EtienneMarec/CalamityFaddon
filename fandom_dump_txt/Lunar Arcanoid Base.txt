'''Lunar Arcanoid Base''' is a Godseeker Mode upgrade to [[Astral Arcanoid Base]]. It summons a Lunar Arcanoid Orb that doesn't move by itself and requires the player to move it manually, The Orb creates an aura that damages and weakens your enemies. The Orb itself deals 260 contact damage, the aura deals 60 damage per second, and your enemies deals 20% less damage.
