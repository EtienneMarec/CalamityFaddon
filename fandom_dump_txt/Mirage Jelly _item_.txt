The '''Mirage Jelly''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Mirage Jelly|Mirage Jellies]]. It cuts the [[stealth]] cost required for performing a stealth strike by 5%. This stacks with the [[Coin of Deceit]] and its upgrades. It can be combined with [[Life Jelly]], [[Mana Jelly]] and [[Vital Jelly]] in order to craft the [[Grand Gelatin]].
