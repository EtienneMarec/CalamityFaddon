The '''Infernum Kris''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a possessed human holding a knife to attack enemies by rapidly throwing knives at them. The minion is mostly stationary, but will teleport above enemies and strike downwards if its target gets too far, also leaving behind a radial spray of 8 knives upon teleporting.
