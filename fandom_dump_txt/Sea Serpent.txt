'''Sea Serpents''' are [[Hardmode]] [[enemies]] that spawn in the [[Sunken Sea]] biome. They are initially passive, and swim in a loop underneath the player. After they take damage, they become aggressive and try to swim directly towards the player. Sea Serpents can swim through blocks and do not require water to move.
