'''Death''' is an item found within the [[Starter Bag]] in [[Expert Mode]]. When used in [[Revengeance Mode]], it activates or disables [[Death Mode]], further increasing difficulty. Attempting to use it while not in Revengeance Mode or during a boss battle will have no effect.

It can be crafted at an [[Altar]] and does not have any ingredients.
