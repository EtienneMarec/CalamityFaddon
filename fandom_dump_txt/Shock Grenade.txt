'''Shock Grenades''' are craftable consumable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[bombs]] that can also drop from the majority of [[enemies]] that spawn during the [[Martian Madness]] [[Events|event]]. It throws a shock grenade that explodes upon striking a tile or enemy, releasing several small lightning bolts that can go through tiles. If the grenade's explosion was caused by striking a tile, all of the lightning bolts travel away from the tile in question.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with a Shock Grenade causes an electrifying aura, which continually damages enemies within it, to be left at the resulting grenade's point of impact; additionally, it causes the lightning bolts the grenade spawns to home in on the nearest enemy.
