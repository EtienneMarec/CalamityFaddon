'''Exo Prisms''' are Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]s that drop from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], [[XP-00 Hypnos]], [[XB-10 Nortazzo]], and the various [[Exo Enemies]]. Their main use is to craft [[Miracle Matter]]s and to craft the [[Draedon's Forge]]. When dropped, the item will pulse, glow, and emit bright yellow dust.
