The '''1.5.0.002''' (or '''1.5.0.2''') Music mod update decreased the volume of the "Inferior Fabrications" in game.

This Music Mod version is compatible with Main mod versions [[1.5.0.001]] through [[1.5.0.003]].

== Changes ==
*Decreased the volume of the "Inferior Fabrications" music track that plays during the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] boss fight by 17.5%.

== Notes ==
*This version was never properly distributed, and is identical to the [[1.5.0.003 (music)]] version.
