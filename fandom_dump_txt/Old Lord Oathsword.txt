The '''Old Lord Oathsword''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Bone Serpent]]s in [[The Underworld]]. Critical hits with this weapon summon a fiery explosion on the target, dealing an extra hit of damage and inflicting the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] for 10 seconds.
