[[File:Terratomere (demo).gif|frame|The Terratomere's projectiles homing in on and hitting a [[Super Dummy]]. Notice how only true melee strikes slightly heal the player.]]The '''Terratomere''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It fires a barrage of 4 homing beams when swung, which deal half damage, and can pass through blocks. The sword and projectiles will inflict [[Glacial State]] onto enemies. In addition to this, striking enemies with true melee hits will [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 2 to 4 HP. It is also used to summon [[The Hive Mind]].
