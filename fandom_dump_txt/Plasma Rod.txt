The '''Plasma Rod''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] obtained from the [[Goblin Sorcerer]] and [[Tim]]. It shoots a beam that inflicts [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]] to hit [[enemies]]. If it hits a tile instead, it will explode into eight smaller beams with higher damage. Due to the random spread of smaller beams, some may find it difficult to aim smaller beams at the enemy.

The direct hit of the wand can inflict [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]] for 10 seconds.
