<big>''"...Home to many hideous creatures, spawned from the pumping blood and lurching organs deep within.''"</big>

'''The Crimson''' [[biome]] features two new [[enemies]], one of which summons [[The Perforators]] [[boss]] when killed. A [[shrine]] structure, which contains the [[Crimson Effigy]], a unique piece of [[furniture]], can be found somewhere within this biome.

In [[Death Mode]], [[thorny bushes]] inflict the [[Bleeding]] and [[Weak]] debuffs.
