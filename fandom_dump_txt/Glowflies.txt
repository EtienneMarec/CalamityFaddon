'''Glowflies''' are [[Enemies]] that spawn in [[The Void]]. They spawn in groups of 5, and fly erratically around until they locate a player, which they will then swarm around. Glowflies do not deal damage, and instead give off light, which can attract other enemies found in The Void.

== Notes ==
* Glowflies will not attract certain enemies until they are locked onto a player.
