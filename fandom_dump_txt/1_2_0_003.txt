The '''1.2.0.003''' update made changes to the buy prices for most late [[Hardmode]] and Godseeker Mode [[summoning items]]. It also made a few balance changes and resprited several weapons and Calamity's [[Permanent Power-ups|permanent health boosting items]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*[[???]] now despawns if any boss is active instead of becoming invincible if [[Duke Fishron]] is active.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' body segments now set the piercing of all non-minion piercing projectiles to 1 instead of only projectiles that pierce infinitely.
*The [[Lunatic Cultist]] now causes players to take 1.5x more damage from all sources instead of 1.3500000238418579x.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] can now spawn 1-3 Shades at once instead of 1-2.
*Tweaked the [[Ebonian Slime God]]'s movement.
*Decreased [[Spazmatism]]'s health multiplier from {{revengeance|1.45x}} to {{revengeance|1.25x}}.
*Decreased Spazmatism's charging speed.
*Increased [[Wall of Flesh]]'s health multiplier from {{revengeance|1.3x}} to {{revengeance|1.8x}}.

=== Items ===
*[[Alpha Ray]] now uses the {{rare|12}} rarity instead of {{rare|11}}.
*[[Crimsaber]]'s tooltip no longer ends with an exclamation point.
*Nerfed [[Exoblade]]'s damage from 3280 to 1500.
*[[Nullification Pistol]] now uses Item33 as its sound instead of a custom sound.
*[[Primordial Earth]]'s main projectile now pierces 4 times instead of 8, and its split projectiles now pierce 2 times instead of infinitely.
*Decreased the damage boost that [[The Swarmer]]'s bees gain from bee damage boosting accessories such as the [[Plague Hive]].

=== Other ===
*The [[Cyborg]] now sells [[Cosmic Worm]]s for {{pc|40}} instead of {{pc|20}}.
*The [[Dryad]] now sells [[Necroplasmic Beacon]]s for {{pc|30}} instead of {{pc|10}}.
*The [[Witch Doctor]] has had some of his prices adjusted:
**[[Abombination]]s are now sold for {{gc|50}} instead of {{gc|40}}.
**[[Ancient Medallion]]s are now sold for {{gc|50}} instead of {{gc|40}}.
**[[Birb Pheromones]] are now sold for {{pc|20}} instead of {{pc|2}}.
**[[Dragon Egg (summoning)|Dragon Egg]]s are now sold for {{pc|50}} instead of {{pc|30}}.
*The [[Wizard]] has had some of his prices adjusted:
**[[Celestial Sigil]]s are now sold for {{pc|3}} instead of {{gc|50}}.
**[[Profaned Core]]s are now sold for {{pc|30}} instead of {{pc|5}}.
**[[Profaned Shard]]s are now sold for {{pc|10}} instead of {{pc|1}}.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Aftershock]], [[Crimsaber]], [[Earthen Pike]], [[Ichor Spear]], [[Majestic Guard]], [[Pwnagehammer]], [[Red Sun]], and [[Slag Magnum]].
*Resprited [[Hemagrape|Blood Orange]], [[Dragonfruit]], [[Sambucus Fruit|Elderberry]], and [[Miracle Fruit]].
*Resprited hearts granted by the Hemagrape|Blood Orange, Dragonfruit, Elderberry, and Miracle Fruit.
*Resprited [[Mourningstar]]'s projectile.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Calamitas]] phase one not producing dust correctly when hit.
*Fixed a bug that could occur when Calamitas transitions to her second phase.
*Removed an erroneous transparent pixel on [[Catastrophe]].
