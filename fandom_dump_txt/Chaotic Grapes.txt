The '''Chaotic Grapes''' are a [[consumable]] dropped by the [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]]. It permanently gives the player an eighth [[covering]] slot. This effect does not stack and may only be used once per player.


== Notes ==
*It does nothing if an eighth covering slot (or more) has already been obtained through the use of other mods.
