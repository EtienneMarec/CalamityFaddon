'''The Wall of Gifts''' (Japanese: プレゼントの壁 ''Purezento no kabe'') is a Godseeker Mode [[boss]]. It is a seasonal version of the [[The Wall of Bronze]].

The Wall of Gifts can be summoned manually by throwing the General Voodoo Doll into lava while in the [[Frozen Hell]] biome during the Christmas seasonal event. Doing so will kill [[The General]] before spawning the boss.

==Behavior==
The Wall of Gifts has the exact same AI and attacks as its counterpart, The Wall of Bronze, the only change being that each of its attacks inflict the [[Debuffs|Frozen]] debuff for 60 minutes.

== Tips==
If the player equips the Hand Warmer, they will become immune to the Wall of Gifts frozen debuff, making the fight significantly easier.

If you had named your player: HoHoHo, Santa Claus, or PresentsForAll 10 more presents will be dropped from the Wall of Gifts.
