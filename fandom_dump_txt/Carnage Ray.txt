The '''Carnage Ray''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]]. It creates a red particle beam which deals much more damage the farther away the enemy is from the player, up to a maximum of 3x its base damage. It is made with [[Blood Sample]]s in [[Crimson]] worlds and is the Crimson counterpart to the [[Night's Ray]].
