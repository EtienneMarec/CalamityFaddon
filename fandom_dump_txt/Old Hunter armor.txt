'''Old Hunter armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[vanity]] set. It requires 3 [[Black Ink]] and [[Desert Prowler armor]] to craft the entire set.

It consists of a '''Old Hunter Hat''', '''Old Hunter Shirt''', and '''Old Hunter Pants'''.
