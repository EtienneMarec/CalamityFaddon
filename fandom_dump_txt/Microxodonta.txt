The '''Microxodonta''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[Nuclear Terror]]s. When equipped, it increases the player's movement speed by 92%. While flying, a [[Microbial Cluster]] is released every 0.5 seconds which deals a flat 300 damage to enemies that come in contact with it.

== Trivia ==
*The item is based on the [[wikipedia:Elephant's Foot (Chernobyl)|Elephant's Foot]], a mass of radioactive degrees created by the [[wikipedia:Chernobyl disaster|Chernobyl disaster]].
**The item's name is a combination of the term "Micro Oxygen", a powerful fictional compound capable of eradicating any organic life and Loxodonta, the scientific name for elephants.
*The movement boost of 92% is in reference to the atomic number of [[wikipedia:Uranium|Uranium]], 92.
