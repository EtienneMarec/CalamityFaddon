The '''Voidragon''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[gun]] which is the upgraded version of [[Megaskeet]] and the final upgrade to the [[Minishark]]. It rapidly fires a stream of bullets, which have a 50% chance of not being consumed. Every other shot, it will instead shoot explosive void blasts, which do not consume ammo.
