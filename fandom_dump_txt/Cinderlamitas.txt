''Cinderlamitas'' is a secret [[Hardmode]] [[mini boss]] encountered in the [[Hallow]] as part of the [[Falconer]]'s 300 {{item|Essence of Babil}} quest, or alternatively after completing 5 other quests. It is a [[Calamitas]] prototype converted to the religion of [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], and as such uses similarly themed moves.

{{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}} It can also spawn as a miniboss in the [[Martian Madness]] event after [[The Starborn Moth]] is defeated.

Cinderlamitas will attack the player with different patterns of Holy Flares, telegraphed by prayers and chanting which will appear in the player's chat:
* A volley of torpedo projectiles which ignite platforms and blocks with screen-wide flames.
* Two radial rotating bursts of Holy Flames from Cinderlamitas.
* Three dashes which increase in acceleration. Cinderlamitas ignites and bursts after the third dash.
* Revolving around the player and leaving flames which slowly home in on the player.

=== In Death mode: ===
Cinderlamitas will release gravity affected Holy Flame projectiles when injured. (These do not travel through blocks)

==Trivia==
*The metal plating's pattern is based on [[Cinderplate]].
