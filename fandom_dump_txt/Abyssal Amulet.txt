The '''Abyssal Amulet''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that can be caught while [[fishing]] in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] or  [[Abyss]]. While it is equipped, the player gains 10% increased max life while in the [[Abyss]], inflicts the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]] on all attacks, and gains immunity to the Crush Depth debuff.

== Notes ==
*The Abyssal Amulet can only be caught with a 1/25 (4%) chance if fishing with between 110 and 239 [[fishing power]].
*There is a 25% chance for Crush Depth to be inflicted for 6 seconds, a 37.5% chance to be inflicted for 4 seconds, and a 37.5% chance to be inflicted for 2 seconds.
