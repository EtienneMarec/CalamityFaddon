The '''Heat Spirit''' is a [[pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. They charge around at high speeds and attempt to ram the player for high damage, and once they leave a small area around the player, they turn to charge back again.

== Trivia ==
*The Heat Spirit's appearance resembles the [https://doom.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_soul/Doom_(2016) Lost Soul] Demon from the game ''DOOM (2016)''.
*It is the only enemy in the Brimstone Crag that does not gain an increase in stats or drop [[Bloodstone]] after [[Providence]] is defeated.
