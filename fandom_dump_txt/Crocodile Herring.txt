The '''Crocodile Herring''' is a [[critter]]. It is found in the [[Ocean]] after defeating [[Leviathan and Anahita]] which passively swims back and forth, gaining a speed boost when hitting a wall. It attempts to swim away from the player and will drop [[Crocodile Scale]]s when killed. It can also be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].
