'''NO U''' was a [[debuff]] inflicted solely by [[THE LORDE]]. This debuff removed all buffs and disabled the effects of all [[Covering]]s and [[coverings]].

There was no means of immunization against this debuff. [[Minions]] instantly despawned when NO U was applied. Revival effects such as the set bonuses of [[God Slayer armor]] and [[Auric Tesla armor]] would fail while the player was under the effect of NO U. The [[Invincible]] [[Buffs|buff]] applied by drinking [[Purified Jam]] would not protect the player against NO U. Additionally, any increased life not granted by some of the [[Persistent Augmentations]]  was removed; the player is limited to 500 life at most. Even the [[Lul|lol]] was disabled while this debuff was active, as it cannot protect the player from debuffs.

All effects of NO U were reverted when the debuff wore off or the player died.
