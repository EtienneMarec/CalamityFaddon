The '''1.0.1, 1.0.2, and 1.0.3''' updates added a description and homepage link to the mod, as well as adding [[Falcon Boots]] with some recipes to help make obtaining them easier. 1.0.3 was the final version in the 1.0 series of updates.

== Additions ==
*Added mod description.
*Added mod homepage.
*Added [[Falcon Boots]].
*[[Lava Charm]] can now be crafted with 3 [[Iron Bar]]s, 5 [[Lava Bucket]]s, and 25 [[Obsidian]].
*[[Water Walking Boots]] can now be crafted with 5 [[Leather]] and 8 [[Water Walking Potion]]s.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed some item gravity bugs.
