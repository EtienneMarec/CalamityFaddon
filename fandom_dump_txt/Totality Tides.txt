The '''Totality Tides''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When used, the weapon appears on the user's cursor and fires an assortement of exo projectiles in the direction that the ball is relative to the player. For example, if the player's cursor is to the right of the player, the ball will fire its slew of projectiles right. Perfectly centering the ball on the player's center will cause it to deal 200% more damage and spray projectiles in random directions.
