The '''Bloatfish''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in lowest layer of the [[Abyss]]. While it is not aggressive and will not chase the player to attack them, it can still slightly hurt them via its low contact damage. It serves primarily as a meat-shield for other enemies, as it absorbs projectiles and gets larger as it takes damage, potentially reaching 3 times its original size.

== Notes ==
*The Bloatfish nullifies the piercing capabilities of any projectiles that come in contact with it, effectively absorbing them.

== Trivia ==
*Although the Bloatfish is based on the [[wikipedia:Ocean sunfish|ocean sunfish]], which lives in the warmer and higher places of the water column, the Bloatfish only spawns at the very bottom of the Abyss.
