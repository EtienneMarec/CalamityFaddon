'''Calamitas' Brew''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. It grants the user the '''Abyssal Weapon''' [[buff]] when consumed, which gives 5% increased movement speed and causes [[melee weapons|melee]] and [[rogue weapons]] to inflict the [[Abyssal Flames]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.
