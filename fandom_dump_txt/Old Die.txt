The '''Old Die''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that can be purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|40}} at any time. When equipped, the random damage deviation of each attack is increased. The minimum damage that can be rolled is -7.85% damage and the highest damage that can be rolled is +12.05% damage instead of the ±15% damage deviation that vanilla Terraria has. This means that non-[[Spy]] attacks deal 2.1% more damage on average.

If the player is holding a [[Spy weapon]] or wearing a Spy [[Covering]], the damage roll is rolled twice, with the higher damage value of the two becoming the damage increase applied to the weapon. This means that Spy attacks deal 5.42% more damage on average. <!-- For future reference: expected value of the higher of two random samples from the same uniform distribution is the 2/3 mark of that distribution -->
