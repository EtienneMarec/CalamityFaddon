The '''Ashes of the Sunken Sea''' are [[Hardmode]] buff potions dropped by enemies from the [[Sunken Sea]]. It grants the user the '''Sunken Power''' [[buff]] when consumed, granting the following effects:
*+20% movement speed
*+10% damage
*-50% crit chance
*-20 defense

The Sunken Power buff also applies a blur effect onto the screen when being used.
When the Sunken Power buff runs out of time, the '''Sunken Withdrawal''' [[debuff]] is applied onto the player for 2 hours. It grants the following effects:
*+10 defense
*-30% movement speed
*-20% damage
*-30% crit chance
*-50% mining speed

Sunken Withdrawal can be cured using more Ashes- however, doing so will replenish the debuff to it's full duration once the Sunken Power buff expires. As a result, it is ill advised to use it outside of combat situations, and it must be micromanaged to avoid the debuff during them.
