'''Skyline Attractor''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[fishing pole]].It has 2 modes:<br>
Left Click (small) throws 8 small bobbers, they can only catch 1 item but the line never snaps
Right Click (big) throws out 1 large bobber that catches things 12x times faster and can hold up to 90 different items, but it has a small chance to snap.

It is obtained from [[Aerial Abysscent Crate]]s

== Notes ==
Fishing during a [[Blood Moon]] with this pole will spawn 3 instances of [[Leviathan and Anahita]], if all 3 of them are defeated before midnight, they will be awarded with 69 [[Essential Essence Bar]].
