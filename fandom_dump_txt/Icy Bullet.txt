'''Icy Bullets''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bullets]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{cc|80}} each after the defeating all three [[minibosses]] in the [[Frost Moon]] [[event]]. The bullets are able to pierce into enemies twice until they are broken upon hitting the third enemy. Each time a bullet breaks, it releases three to five ice shards that deal 50% less damage to everything.

== Notes ==
*The damage penalty the Ice Shards possess takes the gun's base damage into account.
