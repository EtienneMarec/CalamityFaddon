The '''Infected Dagger Misses No Skeptics''' are a [[Hardmode]] [[Daggers|Dagger]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon being thrown, it homes in to the nearest enemy, being able to pierce infinitely, and is capable of bouncing to any nearby enemy.

Performing a [[Spy|Stealth Strike]] throws out 3 more daggers, while boosting all the dagger's velocity.
