The '''Burnt Sienna''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Tomb Crawler]]s. When swung, the blade releases cosmetic light particles. Upon killing an enemy, three small, bright-yellow orbs will shoot out, which will [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 3 HP when picked up.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The orbs spawned by upon killing an enemy will home-in on the player if they have the [[Heartreach]] [[buff]].

== Trivia ==
*"Burnt sienna" is a reddish-brown color, which this sword approximately features.
*The Burnt Sienna was formerly used to craft the [[Terra-cotta]].
