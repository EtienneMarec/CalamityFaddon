{{dablink|For the vanilla explosive, see [[Bomb]]}}
'''Bombs''' are [[weapons]] belonging to the [[Spy]] class. They are projectiles thrown out away from the player that explode after traveling a short distance or after contacting a [[block]] or [[enemy]], usually with an area of effect. Most bombs are non-consumable.
