'''Arcanoid Orb''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] dropped by [[Cosmic Elemental|Cosmic Elementals]]. While equipped, it summons an '''Arcanoid Orb''' minion.

While normally stationary, the player can move the Orb by pressing the {{key|Arcanoid Orb}} key. This action uses 5 mana and causes the Orb to move to the cursor's location. If the key is pressed down for 1.5 seconds, the Orb will start to track the cursor, draining the player's mana at a rate of 15 points per second.

The Arcanoid Orb deals 120 damage, which scales with the summon damage bonuses, grants enemies half of a second of static [[immunity frames]] and inflicts the [[Confused]] debuff on hit.
