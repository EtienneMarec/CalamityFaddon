The '''1.4.2.002''' update made a variety of adjustments based on the recently added [[Sunken Sea]] biome and its content, including various fixes. It also made fixes and tweaks to other things, including some tooltips and dialogue.

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Astrum Aureus]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]]' lasers now use sprites instead of drawcode.
*[[Clam]]s can now spawn if the player has the [[Clamity]] debuff.
*Increased the spacing between The Devourer of Gods' laser walls.
*Removed The Devourer of Gods' extra damage taken multiplier from Boss Rush.
*Nerfed [[Eutrophic Ray]]'s damage from {{dv|40|80}} to {{dv|20|40}} in Pre-Hardmode, and {{dv|100|200}} to {{dv|50|100}} in Hardmode.
*Doubled the spawn rate for [[Giant Clam]]s.
*Changed [[Halibut Cannon]] drop rates:
**Reduced the drop rates for most enemies from {{revengeance|0.1%}} to {{revengeance|0.0001%}}.
**Reduced the drop rates for minibosses from {{revengeance|1%}} to {{revengeance|0.01%}}.
**Increased the drop rate for the [[adult Eidolon Wyrm]] from {{revengeance|10%}} to {{revengeance|100%}}.
*Boosted [[Hive Cyst|Hive]] and [[Perforator Cyst]] spawn rates.
*[[The Hive Mind]] changes:
**Nerfed phase 1's health from {{dv|2000|3200|5760|10,560}} to {{dv|1200|1920|2880|5280}}.
***Nerfed Boss Rush health from {{dv|700,000|1,120,000|0|1,280,000}} to {{dv|350,000|560,000|0|640,000}}.
**Nerfed phase 2's health from {{dv|3000|4800|6912|11,520}} to {{dv|5800|9280|12,096|20,000}}.
**Phase 1 now spawns 3-6 enemies at once instead of 5-8.
**Phase 1 can now only spawn up to 3 [[Eater of Souls|Eaters of Souls]] at a time instead of 8, and 1 [[tgc:Devourer|Devourer]] at a time instead of 2.
**Decreased the speed of Hive Blobs and tweaked the dust spawned by their Vile Clot projectiles.
*Increased the duration of [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s homing fireball attack in Death Mode, and his border tornadoes can no longer spawn on platforms.
*[[Sea Serpent]]s can no longer spawn in towns or if the player has the Clamity debuff.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] now drops [[Omega Healing Potion]]s instead of [[Supreme Healing Potion]]s.

=== Items ===
*Changed some [[bar]] recipes:
**[[Chaotic Bar]]s now use 5 [[Chaotic Ore]] for 1 bar instead of 12 Chaotic Ore and 1 [[Core of Chaos]] for 4 bars.
**[[Cryonic Bar]]s now use 5 [[Cryonic Ore]] for 1 bar instead of 16 Cryonic Ore for 4 bars.
**[[Meld Bar]]s now use 6 [[Meld Blob]]s for 3 bars instead of 5 Meld Blobs for 5 bars.
**[[Perennial Bar]]s now use 5 [[Perennial Ore]] for 1 bar instead of 15 Perennial Ore and 1 [[Essence of Sunlight]] for 5 bars.
**[[Uelibloom Bar]]s now use 5 [[Uelibloom Ore]] for 1 bar instead of 12 Uelibloom Ore and 1 [[Unholy Essence]] for 4 bars.
*[[Bazooka]], [[Elephant Killer]], [[Hydra]], [[Lightning Hawk]], and [[Magnum]] can now only be used for set amounts of times during boss fights.
*[[Effulgent Feather]]s now use the {{rare|12}} rarity instead of {{rare|14}}.
*Increased [[Hydra]]'s spread.
*[[Giant Pearl]]'s tooltip now mentions that it doesn't work on bosses, and reduced its effectiveness.
*[[Guide Voodoo Doll]]s are now crafted with 2 [[Bloodletting Essence|Bloodletting]] or [[Fetid Essence]]s and 2 [[Leather]] instead of 10 [[Blood Orb]]s and 5 Leather.
*[[Halibut Cannon]] now has flavor text saying, "This weapon is overpowered, use at the risk of ruining your playthrough".
