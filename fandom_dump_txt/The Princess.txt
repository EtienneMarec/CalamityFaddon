'''The Princess''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] which is an upgrade to the [[Arch Amaryllis]]. When used, it casts a gravity-defying holy fireball. It explodes into six smaller homing flames after hitting four enemies, colliding with a solid tile, or after 0.58 seconds. The flames last four seconds before fading away, and deal 66% of the original damage. After exploding, the main fireball slows down and dissipates in 0.42 seconds.
