'''Coverings''' are equippable items that can provide defense, extra damage and/or special abilities, such as limited flight. Up to five coverings may be equipped at any time; furthermore, up to five coverings may be placed in vanity slots, which will show the items on the player (if possible) but will not apply any of their effects. Additionally, if the [[Celestial Onion]] is consumed, a sixth (seventh if the [[Demon Heart]] is also consumed) slot is available, though the seventh slot will only be functional during Expert Mode and above.

{{tota}} In [[Revengeance Mode]], the player gets an eighth covering slot post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Umbrus]] as well.

{{tota}} Defeating [[Godseeker, Yharim]] and entering goodseeker mode will allow for a tenth covering slot to be used. However, this can only be equiped with [[Bee Wings]], which are essential for fighting any endgame [[Bosses]].
