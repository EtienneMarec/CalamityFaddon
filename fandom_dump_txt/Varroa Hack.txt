The '''Varroa Hack''' is a [[Hardmode]] consumable [[Classless weapons|classless weapon]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. When thrown, upon hitting an enemy, it latches on to them and deals constant damage to them while latched on, inflicting the [[Plague]] and [[Bleeding]] debuff in the process. After some time has passed, or it has hit a tile instead, it explodes, dealing 150% of its damage.
