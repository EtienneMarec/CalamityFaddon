The '''1.1.5.0931''' update lowered the volume of the mod's [[music]] and included some minor changes.

== Changes ==
*All Calamity music tracks have had their volume lowered.
*[[Calamitas]] now drops {{dv|1|1-2}} [[Blighted Lens]]es.
*Increased the speed of [[The Eye of Calamitas]] and its lasers now deal 50% of the weapon's damage instead of 35%.
*Resprited [[Opal Striker]].
