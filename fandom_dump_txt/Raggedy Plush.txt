The '''Raggedy Plush''' is a Godseeker Mode quest [[List of items|Item]]. It can be bought from the [[Snowman]] while both he and the [[Bandit]] are moved in. The Raggedy Plush can be given to the Bandit if it is in the players inventory when they speak to her. Completing the quest will reward the player with the [[Bandits Gift]].

== Notes ==
*The item will be removed from the Snowmans shop if it has been bought.
**It will re-appear in the shop if it has been removed from the world without having been given to the Bandit yet.

== Trivia ==
*The item seems to be a plush of the [[Piggy]].
