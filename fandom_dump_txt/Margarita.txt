'''Margarita''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Margarita''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but halves the duration to most [[debuffs]]. It also heals the player for 200 life and mana, inflicts the [[Potion Sickness]] debuff for 1 minute and the [[Mana Sickness]] debuff for 5 seconds.

While the effects of the Margarita debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
