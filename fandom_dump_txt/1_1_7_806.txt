The '''1.1.7.806''' update made a few Godseeker Mode bosses force damage taken to them to certain amounts. It also changed a few throwing projectiles.

== Changes ==
*[[Ceaseless Void]] now forces all damage taken to 1.
*[[Cosmic Freeze]]'s dust is now less transparent.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] and [[Storm Weaver]]'s body segments now take 0 damage from all sources.
*[[Eradicator]] now passes through blocks instead of bouncing off them.
*Increased the size of the thrown [[Ice Star]]s, [[Pwnagehammer]]s, [[Rot Ball]]s, and [[Tooth Ball]]s.
*Increased the spread of [[Plague Staff]]'s fangs.
