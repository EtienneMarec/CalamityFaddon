'''Levi''' is a Godseeker Mode, [[Death Mode]]-exclusive [[pet]] summoning item dropped by [[Supreme Calamitas]] that summons a miniature [[Leviathan]] that follows the player.

== Trivia ==
*This item is a reference to the YouTuber [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCgzWiRT3thPJBu-veBa0y3A Leviathan], known for his trailers, challenges, and documentation about the Calamity Mod. He was also the first person to defeat all bosses on Revengeance and Death Mode without taking damage.
*The Levi's buff tooltip is the same as the [[Strange Orb|Ocean Spirit's]].
