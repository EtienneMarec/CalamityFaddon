'''Gehenna''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. It occasionally causes fireballs to fall from the sky. These fireballs act in a manner almost identical to the [[Inferno Fork]], leaving behind a lingering red explosion after dying.

== Notes ==
*The brimstone fire and fireballs deal typeless damage. They are boosted by the average of player's class bonuses which stacks additively with bonuses to 'all damage', such as the [[Avenger Emblem]].
*Brimstone fireballs have a base damage of 54.

== Trivia ==
*The item is named after the area [[wikipedia:Gehenna|Gehenna]], a small valley in Jerusalem where initially some of the kings of Judah sacrificed their children by fire. Thereafter, it was deemed to be cursed.
