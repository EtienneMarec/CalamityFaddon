'''Ancient God Slayer armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[vanity]] set which consists of the '''Ancient God Slayer Helm''', '''Ancient God Slayer Chestplate''' and '''Ancient God Slayer Leggings'''. The set is collectively dropped from the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]] at a 5% chance.

== Notes ==
*Unlike the other similar Ancient armor sets from vanilla Terraria, this armor set gives no benefits and is intended purely for vanity.
:*Despite this, killing [[The Devourer of Gods]] with this in the normal armor slots will give you the [[Cosmic Plushie]] if [[Death Mode]] is active.

== Trivia ==
*The Ancient God Slayer vanity set is based off of an older sprite for the [[God Slayer armor]] set.
