The '''1.2.1.002''' update made further expansions and some rebalances to the [[Supreme Calamitas]] fight rework from [[1.2.1.001]]. It also rebalanced many Godseeker Mode weapons, and a few changes to [[Revengeance Mode]] vanilla [[bosses]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Astrum Deus]]' body segments now take 66% damage from projectiles when under 50% health, and causes all projectiles except for minions to penetrate a maximum of 1 time.
*Astrum Deus' body segments now take 50% damage from [[Bee Cloak]], [[Holy Arrow]], [[Star Cloak]], and [[Star Veil]]'s stars instead of 33%.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Head and tail now take 1.5x damage from all sources instead of 2x. The decreasing amounts as its life lowers has been reduced from 1.25x minimum to 1.05x minimum.
**Head and tail no longer kill the piercing of all projectiles that contact it.
**Head and tail now take 50% damage from [[Life Drain]].
*Increased [[Golem]]'s Revengeance Mode health multiplier from {{revengeance|3x}} to {{revengeance|4x}}.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] now transitions into his phases at higher percentages of life. From {{dv|70%|0|85%}}, {{dv|40%|0|50%}}, {{dv|15%|0|20%}}, and 3% to {{dv|75%|0|85%}}, {{dv|50%|0|60%}}, {{dv|25%|0|30%}}, and 10%.
*[[Plaguebringer]]s now shoot [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s buffed stingers instead of its normal ones, and no longer despawn if the player is within the [[Jungle]].
*The Plaguebringer Goliath changes:
**No longer spawns a [[Plaguebringer]] for every 33% health lost in [[Expert Mode]].
**Stingers now constantly accelerate horizontally, and now last 5 seconds instead of 10.
**Buffed missile barrage damage from {{dv|138|240}} to {{dv|164|260}}.
**[[Plague Mine]]s now automatically detonate after 15 seconds if not triggered or destroyed.
*Increased Revengeance Mode [[Plantera]]'s contact damage multiplier.
*Tweaked [[Polterghast]]'s specialized damage resistance formula.
*Buffed [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s Holy Blast damage from {{dv|140|260}} to {{dv|184|312}}, her Holy Fire damage from {{dv|108|200}} to {{dv|150|240}}, her Molten Blast damage from {{dv|144|260}} to {{dv|184|312}}, her Holy Bomb damage from {{dv|122|220}} to {{dv|168|264}}, and her Holy Spear damage from {{dv|122|220}} to {{dv|168|264}}.
*[[Ravager]] changes:
**Buffed body's health from {{dv|40,000|64,000|83,200}} to {{dv|61,000|97,600|104,000}}. Post-[[Providence]] health buffed from {{dv|540,000|864,000}} to {{dv|580,000|928,000}}.
**Buffed head's health from {{dv|20,000|32,000}} to {{dv|25,000|40,000}}.
**Buffed flying claw laser damage from 100 to 120.
**Flying claws now shoot more often.
**Fireballs shot from broken claw areas have velocity raised from 14 to 18.
**Head now fires projectiles more frequently.
**Body parts can no longer despawn while the player is alive.
*[[The Siren]] and [[The Leviathan]] can no longer despawn while the player is alive.
*[[Storm Weaver]] can now despawn if the player is too far away.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**Buffed bullet hell 2 projectile damages from {{dv|300|400}} to {{dv|320|440}}.
**Buffed bullet hell 3 projectile damages from {{dv|300|400}} to {{dv|340|480}}.
**Buffed bullet hell 4 projectile damages from {{dv|300|400}} to {{dv|360|520}}.
**Buffed bullet hell 5 projectile damages from {{dv|300|400}} to {{dv|380|560}}.
**Projectiles from intermediate phases are now despawned when bullet hell segments begin.
**Bullet hell projectiles now greatly increase in speed if the player goes past the border tornadoes.
**Now heavily reduces spawn rates while active.
**Increased fire rate after bullet hell 5 ends.
**Nerfed [[Soul Seeker]]'s health from {{dv|10,000|14,000}} to {{dv|7500|10,500}}.
**Slightly expanded the border tornado positions.
**Now takes 0 damage from any attack that would take over 10% of her max health.
**Tweaked specialized damage resistance formula.
**Now has unique dialogue based on how many times she has been killed prior.
*In Revengeance Mode, whichever one of [[The Twins]] enters its second phase first will now become invulnerable until the other has entered its second phase as well.
*Increased [[Retinazer]]'s Revengeance Mode health multiplier from {{revengeance|1.1x}} to {{revengeance|1.3x}}.
*Decreased [[Spazmatism]]'s Revengeance Mode health multiplier from {{revengeance|1.35x}} to {{revengeance|1.3x}}.
