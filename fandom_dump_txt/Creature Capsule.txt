The '''Creature Capsule''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that drops from the [[Glitch]]. It fires a capsule that, upon exploding, releases a ghost that flies around in a fairly random manner, and fires projectiles at nearby enemies. If the weapon gets a critical hit, and collides with any non boss enemy, it will instantly kill them.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Creature Capsule will cause 5 ghosts to exit the capsule instead of 1, and will increase the crit chance of the capsule by 25%.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Trivia ==
*The Creature Capsule is a reference to the Master Ball item from Pokemon.
**The ghosts the weapon spawns are very similar in appearance to the ghost from Pokemon Red and Blue.
**The crit rate is a reference to the critical capture rate from the same games.
**It is common for players to use the MISSINGNO glitch to duplicate master balls, which may explain why the Glitch enemy drops this weapon.
