The '''Steel Kunai''' is a Godseeker Mode [[dagger]] dropped by [[Sigmund]]. It rapidly fires a stream of quick-moving, short-range kunai that pass through tiles and are not affected by gravity. The kunai turn transparent as they travel; however, this does not affect their damage and is only an aesthetic addition. The kunai also very slightly curves toward nearby enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] causes the dagger to home in on enemies and explode on contact.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Tips ==
*The Steel Kunai's incredibly high single-target damage has limited usefulness against fast bosses like [[Weaver]] and [[The Devourer of Flesh]]; conversely, the Steel Kunai is quite effective versus [[Blood Moon]]s and other buffed Calamity events due to its strong single-target damage.

== Trivia ==
*In the real world, a [[wikipedia:Kunai|kunai]] is a 14th century Japanese farming tool. Since then, the knife-like shape of the kunai has led to its common depiction as a weapon used by ninjas and assassins.
*This weapon functions very similarly to the [[Mycoroot]].
