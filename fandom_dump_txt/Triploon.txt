The '''Triploon''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Dualpoon]]. It can be obtained either through crafting or by receiving it from the [[Agent]]. It rapidly fires three harpoons that are unaffected by gravity. The harpoons travel a short distance before returning to the player.
