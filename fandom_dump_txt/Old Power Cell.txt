The '''Old Power Cell''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[summoning item]] that, when used deep within the [[Jungle Temple]], summons the [[Golem]] boss.

Unlike [[Lihzahrd Power Cell]]s, the Old Power Cell is not usable at a [[Lihzahrd Altar]] and can be used before [[Plantera]] has been defeated.
