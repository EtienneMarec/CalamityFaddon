The '''Rose Bow''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]] that auto-fires. It rapidly fires arrows in a small random spread. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as ammo, arrows are converted into special energy arrows which create slashes on hit enemies that deal 30% of the arrow's damage three times.
