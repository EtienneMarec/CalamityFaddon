'''Exo Freeze''' is a [[debuff]] that makes the target completely unable to move for its duration. Almost all [[enemies]] are susceptible to this debuff, as well as some [[bosses]]. It is inflicted by several [[Miracle Matter|Exo weapons]] along with [[Apotheosis]], [[Drataliornus]], [[Endogenesis]], [[Ice Barrage]], and the [[Elemental Excalibur]].

Exo Freeze does nothing while the [[Boss Rush]] is active.
