The '''Omega Ray''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]] with two firing modes. The left-click mode has five main parallel laser beams and also shoots laser projectiles rapidly. The right-click mode shoots a beam that splits into five. All projectiles fired from this weapon travel through blocks and all particle beams pierce indefinitely. The five particle beams deal 80% of the weapon's damage, the secondary-fire's beam deals 166.25% of the weapon's damage, and the additional laser projectiles deal 40% of the weapon's damage with 40% of the weapon's knockback.

== Notes ==
*The left-click mode's main beams deal Omega Ray's actual listed damage while the secondary laser projectiles deal only half of it. On the other hand, right-click mode beams deal double the weapon's damage. Despite that, left-click mode actually reaches higher DPS due to faster fire rate and additional fired projectiles.
