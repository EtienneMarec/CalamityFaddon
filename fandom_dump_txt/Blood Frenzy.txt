'''Blood Frenzy''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player during their 15th true melee strike while wearing the Melee [[Bloodflare armor|Bloodflare]] or [[Auric Tesla armor|Auric Tesla]] armors. It increases melee damage and critical strike chance by 25%, and reduces the player's contact damage by 50%. This buff has a [[Blood Frenzy Cooldown|cooldown]] of 30 seconds after it runs out.
