The '''Elementary Orbit''' is a post-[[tgc:Moonlord|Moonlord]] [[coverings|covering]], it increases the players max health and mana by 10% and creates minion's with one representing each essence around you, Each consuming 1 minion slot. They fire fast moving energy bolts at enemy's that inflict Vaporfied for 1.5 seconds and Deal ~55 DPS that ignores enemy defense.
