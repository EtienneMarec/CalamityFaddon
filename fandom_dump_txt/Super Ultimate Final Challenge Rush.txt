The '''Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush''' (Japanese: 超究極ゴッドラッシュ殲滅 Chō kyūkyoku fainarucharenjirasshu) is an Epilogue [[Invasions|invasion]], the final tier of the [[Challenge Rush]] series, and the final non-joke [[challenge]] of both the Calamity Mod and tModloader as a whole. The invasion is activated by left-clicking with the [[Sealed Nexus]] and can only be done so after the Ultimate Challenge Rush has been completed. Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush can also be deactivated at any time by using the [[Sealed Nexus]] again. Unlike both prior tiers of the Challenge Rush series, the invasion features both old and new challenges. The challenges summoned have impossibly high amounts of HP and damage and use attacks and gimmicks that are even more difficult to track than even those within the Ultimate Challenge Rush. The invasion punctuates with the sole challenge of Tier 11: The Toxic Community.

Defeating the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush rewards the player with the [[Calamitous Infinity Crown]] and [[Interdimensional Fanny Pack]].
==Spare Lives==
Due to the nature of the invasion, 0 spare lives are available to the player. When a player dies without any [[death prevention]], the player will die as normal.

 
== Progression==
Upon starting the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush, challenges can spawn in a random order as some waves within the tier will choose a challenge out of a defined set. Only one challenge will spawn at a time; the next challenge will spawn once the prior one is defeated. A total of 51 challenges will spawn during the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush. The Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush is divided into 11 Tiers, with each new Tier signified by a [[Status messages#Challenge Rush|status message]].

==Notes ==    
*If the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush ends due to all players dying, the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush will start 2 challenges before the one which all players died to.
*Any effects that change the color of the background will be overridden by the custom background for the invasion. This includes those for day/night.  
** Only tiles and water are affected, challenges and projectiles remain unaffected by color changes.
*All damage taken is increased (where applicable) to a minimum value that increases as the player progresses through the invasion:  
**Contact damage has a minimum of {{dv|480|820}} which increases by 2 for each challenge.
** Projectile damage has a minimum of {{dv|880|1330}} which increases by {{dv|1|2}} for each challenge.
*Challenges defeated during Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush do ''not'' drop any items or currency.
* The waiting time between each tier is 5 seconds.
*All wire-activated mechanisms are disabled during the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush.
*The stats of the challenges during the invasion only scales up with 2 people in multiplayer.

==Tips==    
*Due to the absurd difficulty of the challenges and the invasion’s reputation as the final non-joke challenge of both the Calamity Mod and tModloader as a whole, many mods are encouraged to be used to survive as well as a lot of free time to complete the invasions.
*Many health and buff [[Pets]]]]s, as well as [KROMER] for mages should be brought, as the Super Ultimate Final Challenge Rush can not  take over fourty seconds.
*[[Wulfrum armor]]]] is a good choice as it provides a wide array of set bonuses and  effects.
*Contact damage with any of the challenges and their projectiles/minions should NOT be avoided as they cant  deal over 1 damage, even with no gear.
