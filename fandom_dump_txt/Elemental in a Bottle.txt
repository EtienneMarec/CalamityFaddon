The '''Elemental in a Bottle''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a {{dv|14.29%|20%}} chance to be dropped by [[Sand Elemental]]s. Equipping it summons a Sand Elemental to fight for you, which deals summon damage and casts sandstorms in a similar fashion to their enemy counterpart.

== Notes ==
*The Sand Elemental grants enemies 30 local [[immunity frames]] on contact damage, and the tornadoes it spawns grant 20.

== Trivia ==
*This item used to be called "Waifu in a Bottle", while the Sand Elemental herself used to be called "Sand Waifu". After that, they were known as "Lady in a Bottle" and "Miss Sandy", respectively.
*If the {{fantag|Tranquility Mod}} [[Tranquility Mod]] is enabled, this item becomes unobtainable, due to being replaced with [[Elemental Souls|Sand Elementals' Soul]].
