'''Phantoplasm''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] dropped by [[Phantom Spirit]]s, [[Overloaded Soldier]]s, [[Phantom Debris]], [[Polterghast]] and [[Minnows Prime]]s {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. It is mostly used to craft post-[[Devourer of Gods]] items, as well as [[Bloodstone Core]]s and [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]]s.

{{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}} Phantoplasm can also be placed.
