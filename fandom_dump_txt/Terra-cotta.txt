The '''Terra-cotta''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Dune Splicer]]s after [[Plantera]] has been defeated, and is the indirect upgrade to the [[Burnt Sienna]]. Upon killing an enemy, a lingering explosion is created and small, bright-yellow globes will spawn, which [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 9 HP when picked up.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The orbs spawned by upon killing an enemy will home-in on the player if they are inflicted with the [[Heartreach]] [[buff]].

== Trivia ==
*"Terra cotta" is a reddish-orange color (named after a type of clay ceramic) which this sword approximately features.
*The Terra-cotta was formerly crafted using the Burnt Sienna and was an ingredient in the [[Exoblade]]'s crafting tree, but it is now independent of both.
