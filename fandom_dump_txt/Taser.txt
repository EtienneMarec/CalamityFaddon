The '''Taser''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. When used, a slow hook is fired from the edge of the weapon, attempting to attach to the first enemy it hits and inflicting the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]]. After a short time has passed, the hook returns to the player, hitting any targets it contacts without sticking to them.

The weapon consumes 0.05 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 50.
