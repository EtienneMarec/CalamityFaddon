'''Sigmus, Envoy of the Devourer''' (Japanese: 貪り食う者の使者シグナス ''Musabori kuu mono no shisha shigunasu'') is a Godseeker Mode [[boss]] and is one of the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]]. It drops [[Twisting Nether]] which is a [[crafting material]] required to craft the [[Cosmic Worm]].

== Spawn ==
Sigmus is summoned by using the [[Rune of Kos]] in [[The Underworld]] at any time.

== Behavior ==
Sigmus has 3 phases, each with their own unique behavior, appearance, and methods of attack. Sigmus cycles through these phases randomly; the same phase never appears twice in a row. Sigmus will always start the fight in Phase 1. Sigmus will do {{dv|4|3|2|1}} attack/s per phase before cycling to the next.

Throughout the fight, it summons Cosmic Lantern [[enemies]] that chase the player at high speeds in an attempt to deal contact damage. A maximum of 10 Lanterns can be summoned at a time.

=== Attacks ===
==== Phase 1 ====
*Sigmus flies towards the player and teleports, each to random nearby locations. During this phase, Sigmus ''will'' take knockback, which can slow its advance. Below 50% HP, however, Sigmus will become completely immune to knockback.
**As its health gets lower, it teleports more quickly and moves toward the player more quickly.
*Below 30% health, Sigmus will not enter this phase.

==== Phase 2 ====
*Sigmus flies very quickly back and forth above the player and launches 3 bursts of 3 scythes that inflict [[Whispering Death]].
**The lower Sigmus' health, the faster it shoots these bursts.
**Scythes eventually turn around in an attempt to hit the player again.

==== Phase 3 ====
*Sigmus flies above the player and lunges down at them extremely quickly.
**As Sigmus' health gets lower, its lunges become even faster.
*Sigmus occasionally spawns a wall of five Cosmic Lanterns in front of the player to block them.
*Below 75% health, it summons trails of essence dust behind it while lunging. The essence dust explodes after a short delay.

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*After it teleports, Sigmus summons two pink Cosmic Mines, which chase the player and explode if in close proximity to them or after a short time.
**The Cosmic Mine grows up to three times of its size before exploding.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===
*Sigmus is generally more aggressive and cycles through phases faster as its health decreases.

== Tips ==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating Signus, Envoy of the Devourer, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Signus, Envoy of the Devourer strategies]].}}
*Building a large arena to dodge Sigmus' attacks is a good idea in order to give more room to move around.
*Avoid traveling in a straight horizontal line while fighting Sigmus to avoid Cosmic Lanterns that spawn on each side of the player.
*The [[Lacerator]] is great for destroying Cosmic Lanterns and for doing large amounts of damage to Sigmus while staying at a good distance.
*Using the [[Seadragon]] along with [[Holy Fire Bullet]]s can decimate Sigmus.
*The [[Nuclear Fury]] is a great weapon for the Cosmic Lanterns as its area of effect can easily keep them at bay.
**However, options such as [[Mistlestorm]] and [[Sanguine Flare]] will have much higher direct damage.
*[[Elemental Axe]]s paired with the [[Golden Gun]] and minion slot boosting [[coverings]] can be a deadly combination for defeating Sigmus.
**Additionally, the life aura from summoner [[Tarragon armor]] instantly kills Cosmic Lanterns as they draw near.
*The [[Molten Amputator]] is an excellent [[Spy]] option that deals great damage when the boomerangs stack on top of one another.
*Sigmus' Cosmic Fireballs can be difficult to evade, thus using the [[Normality Relocator]] or a [[Rod of Discord]] is highly recommended.
*The [[Ark of the Elements]] is still a decent option for Melee users.
*coverings such as the [[Flesh Totem]] and [[Elysian Aegis]] are extremely useful for dealing with Sigmus' massive contact damage.
*If not already equipped, the [[Seraph Tracers]] are very useful for keeping up with Sigmus' speed.
*Sigmus' AI is very similar to [[Brimstone Elemental]]'s AI. Practicing her before fighting Sigmus may result in an easier fight.
*It is possible to lure Sigmus out of The Underworld and on to the [[Surface]]. This can give the player much more vertical space to fight in, and will not change its behavior.
*While Sigmus can be fought before any other post-Moon Lord boss, it is not recommended to fight until [[Providence]] is defeated, its high speed, damage, and unpredictable nature make the fight very challenging.

== Trivia ==
*Sigmus' [[Music|theme]] is a segment from [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dt30jWRMjLk '''Servants of the Scourge'''], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO].
**This theme contains a motif from [[The Devourer of Gods]]' theme.
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Boss 4''' will play instead.
*Sigmus' name was inspired by community member CygnusPrawn's username.
*Sigmus, along with [[Storm Weaver]] and [[Ceaseless Void]], can be summoned right after the Moon Lord is defeated, as their summoning item only requires Solar Fragments, Luminite Bars, and [[Unholy Essence]]s.
*Signus was formerly referred to as 'CosmicWraith' in the mod's source files.
**This was due to Cosmic Wraith being the working name for Sigmus during development.
*If the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist Mod] is installed, its unique despawn message will be "The Devourer's assassin has finished its easy task."
