The '''Sanguined Catarachedrae''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] that is obtained by throwing [[Catarachedrae]] into [[Sanguine Shimmer]]. While equipped, hitting an enemy will cause it to bleed for 4 seconds, dealing 6 flat damage every second, similarly to most [[debuffs]]. This effect stacks up to 5 times, dealing maximum of 30 DPS. If the player hits the bleeding enemy a 6th time, it will instead create a bloody explosion with a radius of 2 blocks. The explosion deals 50 typeless damage, which scales with the most powerful class, pierces infinite amount of enemies, although dealing damage only once per target, and inflicts the [[tgc:Obstructed|Obstructed]] debuff for 5 seconds, slowing down enemies considerably and making them unable to target the player.

==Notes==
*If the player strikes an enemy with a weapon that deals [[True melee|true melee]] damage, the explosion will deal 80 base damage instead.
*If the player strikes an enemy with a [[Minions|minion]], the explosion will deal 20 base damage instead.
*Sanguined Catarachedrae has a good synergy with fast weapons that deal low damage, because they can trigger the explosion much more often.
*Since the DoT effect isn't considered a debuff, it cannot be empowered or weakened, and can fully damage enemies that are immune to all debuffs or have high [[defense]] and [[Damage Reduction|damage reduction]], such as [[tgc:Skeletron|Skeletron]].
*Unlike other variants of Catarachedrae, Sanguined one doesn't have a use besides being a covering.

==Trivia==
*Sanguined Catarachedrae is used to represent the true melee damage type.
