The '''Spear of Liberty''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[javelin]] that also has a small chance of being dropped by enemies in the corruption (1.4%). When fired, it throws out a projectile identical to the Cursed Spear, alongside two rapidly moving spears which will aggressively home in onto enemies. Each spear will inflict the [[Cursed Inferno]] [[debuff]] and pierce once. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart is the {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} [[Spear of Destiny]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Spear of Destiny will cause all three spears to aggressively home, ignore tiles, and pierce two additional times.
