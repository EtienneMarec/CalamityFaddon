{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.4.5.001 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.4.5.001''' (or '''1.4.5.1''') Music mod update, also known as '''"Rust and Dust"''' or '''"Draedon's Arsenal"''' resprited the Astrum Deus music box and gave several music tracks improved sound quality.

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod versions [[1.4.5.001]] and [[1.4.5.002]].

== Changes ==
*Renamed Music Box (Calamity) to [[Music Box (The Tale of a Cruel World)]].
*Resprited [[Music Box (Astrum Deus)]].
*The "Universal Collapse" music track now loops properly during [[The Devourer of Gods]] second phase.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed several audio quality issues with "The Tale of a Cruel World", [[Acid Rain]], [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], [[Sunken Sea]], [[The Slime God]], and [[The Old Duke]] music tracks.
