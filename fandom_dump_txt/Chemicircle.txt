The '''Chemicircle''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[yoyo]]. It fires a spinning yoyo that constantly releases damaging gasses, the gasses deal 120% of the damage and inflict the Cancer debuff, the debuff halves a random stat of your enemy and has a {{dv|5%||8%}} to deal from 550 to 900 damage.
