'''Cnidrion''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that can spawn in the [[Surface]] [[Desert]] [[biome]]. It will attempt to follow the player while firing a burst of four water blasts every ten seconds. Its firing rate doubles while below {{dv|33%|||60%}} health, and triples while below 10% health. Cnidrion drops the [[Desert Medallion]], a [[summoning item]] used to summon the [[Desert Scourge]].

== Notes ==
*Cnidrion is one of the few enemies that can be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].
*While passing through bodies of water, its projectiles are almost invisible.
*It can be a dangerous enemy for Pre-Hardmode players exploring the [[Desert]], due to its large hitbox.
*In [[Death Mode]], they can fire water blasts while moving.

== Tips ==
*Cnidrion can swim through blocks in order to reach the player. Its projectiles, however, cannot pierce blocks; barricading with blocks while it shoots and attacking it while it approaches is a good strategy to take one down, provided the player either has high enough DPS or multiple barriers to fall back to.

== Trivia ==
*The name Cnidrion is derived from "Cnidaria", a term used for individuals of the [[wikipedia:Cnidaria|cnidaria]] [[wikipedia:Phylum|phylum]], which contains organisms such as anemones, corals, and jellyfish.
**Despite this, Cnidrion's design takes after seahorses, who are instead members of the extremely distantly related [[wikipedia:Chordate|chordate]] phylum.
*Cnidrion's [[Music|theme music]] was featured at the beginning of the official music video of [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=opvuEVpl_PM UNIVERSAL COLLAPSE], theme of the [[Devourer of Gods]]' second form as a joke.
*Cnidrions were originally considered a [[mini boss]], but were demoted due to their maximum health not reaching the threshold required for the [[Boss Health Bar]] to appear.
