The '''Rune of Kos''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode non-consumable [[Summoning items|summoning item]] that is also dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. It is used to summon the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]] [[bosses]] at various locations in the world. Each location summons a different sentinel.
*When used in [[Space]], it summons the [[Storm Weaver]].
*When used in the [[Dungeon]], it summons the [[Ceaseless Void]].
*When used in [[The Underworld]], it summons [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]].

== Trivia ==
*The name of the item is a reference to the character Kos, from the video game ''[[wikipedia:Bloodborne|Bloodborne]]''. 
**The symbol inside the centre of the sprite is a reference to the Caryll Rune "Moon" from the same game.
*Prior to the [[1.3.3.001]] update, this item could ''only'' be obtained as a drop from Providence, the Profaned Goddess, meaning that she had to be defeated before the player could access the Sentinels.
