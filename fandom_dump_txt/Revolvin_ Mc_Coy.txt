The '''Revolvin' Mc'Coy''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Dedicated content|Developer]] [[Guns|Gun]] that can be fired 6 times before needing to reload. Its damage multiplies if they are fired in quick successions, going from 1.2x, 1.4x, 1.8x, 2.2x, 2.8x, and 3.5x, respectively.

Upon firing with any bullets, it is transformed into a Cactus Bullet that explodes into spines that deals 45% its weapon's base damage. At the last 2 shots however, all bullets are instead turned into stone bullets, being able to inflict [[Armor Crunch]], and exploding into spiky rocks that deals 150% its base damage. 

{{Key|Open}} will reload the weapon, making it able to fire up to 6 bullets again. This only takes effect after it depletes its ammo, and is accompanied by a sound effect.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity alternates between Yanagisusutake ({{color|#4d4b3a ●|#4D4B3A}}), Metallic Green ({{color|#296e01 ●|#296E01}}) and Tetsuonando ({{color|#2b3736 ●|#2B3736}}) colors.
*Its name is a portmanteau of [[wikipedia:Revolver|Revolver]], a real-life pistol, and [https://cactusmccoy.fandom.com/wiki/Cactus_McCoy Cactus McCoy], a character from his flash game series that bears his name.
**Its bullets' effect reflects the curse of the [https://cactusmccoy.fandom.com/wiki/Thorned_Emerald Thorned Emerald], wherein the person who touches it becomes a walking cactus, and then finally petrified if not returned to its place.
