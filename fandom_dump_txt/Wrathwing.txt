The '''Wrathwing''' is a Godseeker Mode [[javelin]] that is dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. It automatically throws fiery javelins which home in on enemies while releasing gravity-affected fireballs as they travel, explode violently on contact with them and apply the [[Daybroken]] debuff.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Wrathwing will cause it to erupt into thirteen gravity-affected fireballs on enemy hits.
