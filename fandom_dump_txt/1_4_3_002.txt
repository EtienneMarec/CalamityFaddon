The '''1.4.3.002''' (or '''1.4.3.2''') update included a few additions, more changes to [[Death Mode]], balance changes, resprites, and minor bug fixes.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}
== Additions ==
*Added [[Abandoned Slime Staff]], [[Deific Thunderbolt]], [[Prideful Hunter's Planar Ripper]], and [[Squirrel Squire Staff]].
*Added [[Ancient God Slayer Chestplate]], [[Ancient God Slayer Helm]], and [[Ancient God Slayer Leggings]].
*Added [[Astral Jelly (buff)|Astral Jelly]] and [[Squirrel Squire (buff)|Squirrel Squire]] [[buffs]].
*Added [[Bobbit Hook]].
*Added [[Brimstone Jewel]].
*Added [[Fearless Goldfish Warrior Banner]] and [[Radiator Banner]].
*Added the [[Galvanic Corrosion]] [[debuff]].
*Added unsafe [[Brimstone Slag Wall]] <!-- Naturally generating only -->, and [[Sulphurous Sand Wall]].
*Added [[Sulphurous Sandstone]], and [[Sulphurous Sandstone Wall]] as [[unobtainable items]].
*Added [[Mod Calls]] for detecting and setting [[Abyss]] light levels, and Death Mode heat and cold immunity.
