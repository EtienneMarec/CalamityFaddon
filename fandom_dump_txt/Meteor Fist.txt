The '''Meteor Fist''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that fires a high-velocity projectile which leaves a particle trail behind. The projectile is slightly affected by gravity, and explodes on contact with enemies or solid blocks. The explosion ignores [[enemy]] [[immunity frames]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Meteor Fist will cause the next fist thrown to bounce between enemies up to four times before exploding.
