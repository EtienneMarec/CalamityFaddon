'''Godslayerlings''' are a rare Godseeker Mode [[critter]] that spawns in the [[Sky]]. They fly around aimlessly and do not engage with the player.  Unlike most critters, Godslayerlings pose a great threat when killed. When dead, a small purple orb stays still in the air and a few seconds after will then rapidly spam [[File:Essence Fireball.gif|42x42px]] god slayer fireballs in all directions in rapid speed in which fills the screen.

== Tips. ==

* Be careful when using your weapon carelessly. 
* If you accidently kill one, use a teleportation item to get away as the chance of dodging every [[File:Essence Fireball.gif|42x42px]] god slayer fireball is impossible.
