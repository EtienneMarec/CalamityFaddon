'''Godterminal Arcanoid Base''' is an upgrade to [[Lunar Arcanoid Base]]. It summons a Lunar Arcanoid Orb that doesnt move by itself and requires the player to move it manually, The Orb creates an aura inflicts God Slayer Inferno. The orb itself deals 320 contact damage, It also shoots out Cluster Projectiles, That deal 80 damage, after they collide with something they shatter into 4 projectiles 30 damage each
