The '''Giant Skeleton''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns underground. It flies slowly towards players, passing through blocks to reach them.

Giant Skeletons will only spawn during a rare hardmode event, in which they will swarm the player en-masse for 2-5 minutes. Said event has a 1/750,000 chance to trigger every frame the player spends underground, the chance increasing by 50,000 every 5 minutes spent below surface level. The event will arrive with the message "A terrible chill runs up your spine!".
