The '''Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It will shoot out a red blade which homes in on enemies and gradually slows to a stop, similar to the blades created by Pumpking. Upon hitting an enemy with the sword itself, it will have a 50% chance to give the [[Tyrant's Fury]] [[buff]] to the player, increasing melee damage by 30% and melee critical hit chance by 10% for a short period of time. The sword also inflicts the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]] on enemy hits.
