The '''Phantasmal Rune''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is an upgrade to the [[Ghast Relic]] . Whenever a [[minion]] hits an enemy it has a 5% chance to do one of these things:

* spawns a ghost that chases your enemies and walks through them dealing 100 damage per second
* inflicts the enemy with a fire that deals 15 damage per second and periodically launches orbs that spread the fire on other enemies
