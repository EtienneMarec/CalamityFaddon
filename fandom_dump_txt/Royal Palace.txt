The '''Royal Palace''' is the second of two [[Subworlds|subworlds]] introduced by the Tranquility Mod. The only way the player can enter there is having the [[Nightmare Mode]] enabled in their world. The final boss of the addon, The Tyrant King, Yharim is intended to be fought here.
