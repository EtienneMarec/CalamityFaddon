The '''Evil Animatronic''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns during the [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. It attacks by throwing a pizza like a boomerang. If it cannot reach the player, it will periodically emit a laugh which inflicts the player with the Blackout debuff.

== Trivia ==
*The Evil Animatronic is a direct reference to Freddy Fazbear from the game Five Nights at Freddys.
**The enemies behavior of laughing to cause a blackout is similar to a mechanic found in said series.
