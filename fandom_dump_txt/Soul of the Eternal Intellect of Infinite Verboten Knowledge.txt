The '''Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge''' (Japanese: 無限の禁断の知の永遠の知性の魂 Mugen no kindan no chi no eien no chisei no tamashī) is a Godseeker Mode [[NPC]] who randomly visits the player's town for one day. 
In order for the Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge to spawn, [[Moon Lord]] must be defeated.

When threatened by [[enemies]], it will defend itself with Aergia Neurons.

*The Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge will always be named Hypnos.

==Pray==
The Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge is capable of giving the player indulgences for {{gc|1}}. Once the praying animation is done, the player will be gived an [[File:Indulgence.png|100px|alt=Indulgence]] [[Indulgence]], and the following stuff will happen:
* The player gains {{gc|1-2}} for 33% chance.
* The player gains 3-6 {{item|Exo Prism}} for 10% chance if [[The Fabricational Quartet]] or [[XP-00 Hypnos]] has been defeated.
* The player gains {{item|Golden Delight}} for 2% chance.
* The player gets [[Webbed|temporary hypnotized]] for 4 seconds for 1/666 chance.
* The player instantly dies for 1/6666 chance.
These effects are applied in the given order. While it is possible for a player to trigger all of them at once upon praying, it is also possible that none of them are triggered.

==Trivia==
* The Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge will always call the player "Zakō Player", regardless of the player's name. Zakō is the Rōmaji transcription of 雑魚, which means worthless fishes. Frequently used by mesugakis.
* Its appearance is based from a fan art design which takes inspiration from vaporwave and denpa-kei. It has a brain swimming ring and Exo Prism headphones.
* The Bloody vein voiceline is a reference to ULTRAKILL's second prime sanctum boss.
