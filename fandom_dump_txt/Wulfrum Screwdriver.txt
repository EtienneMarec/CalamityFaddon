'''Wulfrum Screwdriver''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Wulfrum Screwdriver''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen, but makes every player projectile pierce an additional time.

While the effects of the Screwdriver debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
