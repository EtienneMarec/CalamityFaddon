The '''1.1.4.473''' update added music tracks to [[The Devourer of Gods]] and [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] fights.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Waraxe]].
*Added [[Armor Crunch]].

== Changes ==
*[[Crawlers]], [[Cultist Assassin]]s, [[Heat Spirit]]s, [[Ice Clasper]]s, [[Pitbull]]s, [[Sand Tortoise]]s, and [[Sun Bat]]s now have gores.
*The Devourer of Gods now uses [[Music#Last Battle (Ballos Mix)|Last Battle (Ballos Mix)]] as its theme.
*The Plaguebringer Goliath now uses [[Music#Boss 1 Remix|Boss 1 Remix]] as its theme.
*[[Enemies]] no longer spawn in towns.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Stasis Probe]].

== Fixes ==
*Small optimizations to multiplayer.
