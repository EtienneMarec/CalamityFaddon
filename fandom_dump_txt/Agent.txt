The '''Agent''' is a unique [[NPC]] vendor that will randomly spawn for one night, giving the player random items based on their progression. He is not a [[Town NPC]] and does not require his own house to spawn, and will instead spawn near [[The Codebreaker]].

In order for the Agent to spawn, the player must have placed the [[The Codebreaker#Long Ranged Sensor Array|Long Ranged Sensor Array]] on [[The Codebreaker]], and a Codebreaker must exist in the world. Once this criterion has been met, the Agent has a 5% chance of spawning each night at 8:00 PM, provided no [[events]] are active (apart from a [[Blood Moon]], in which the chances of him spawning are increased to 10%.) He will leave the next day at 2:00 AM.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will defend himself with the [[Triploon]]. After defeating the [[Moon Lord]], he upgrades to the [[Twentloon]], and after the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], the [[Twenty-Twloon]].

== Trivia ==
*All of the Agent's possible names reference assassinated presidents of the United States, which are frequently alleged to have been killed by the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation FBI] in conspiracy theories, other people associated with American intelligence agencies, or fictional characters who work for the United States government.
*The Agent's spawn condition and dialogue implies that his main goal is to arrest [[Draedon]], though the exact crimes he committed are unknown.
*The Agent is referred to as "FBIGUY" in the mod's source files, confirming that the Agent is a reference to the FBI.
