The '''1.4.4.002''' (or '''1.4.4.2''') update added several expansions to the [[Sulphurous Sea]] along with a few other items and balance changes. The update is also notable for the name change of the Bumblebirb into [[The Dragonfolly]].

{{toc limit|3|right="yes"}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Rusty Medallion]].
*Added [[Effigy of Decay]], [[Hardened Sulphurous Sandstone]], [[Hardened Sulphurous Sandstone Wall]], [[Profaned Guardian Trophy]], [[Rusty Chest]], and [[Supreme Calamitas Trophy]].
*Added [[Corroded Caustibow]], [[Gamma Heart]], [[Rusty Beacon Prototype]], and [[Star Struck Water]].
*Added naturally-generated Locked Rusty Chests, [[ambient objects]], [[Microbial Cluster]]s and [[Sulphurous Vines]] to the Sulphurous Sea.
*Added [[Sulphurskin Potion]].
*Added [[Plague Caller]].
*Added [[Broken Water Filter]] and [[Rusty Lockpick]].
*Added [[Effigy of Decay (buff)|Effigy of Decay]], [[Gamma Hydra (buff)|Gamma Hydra]], [[Plaguebringer Bab (buff)|Plaguebringer Bab]], [[Rusty Drone (buff)|Rusty Drone]], and [[Sulphurskin]] [[buffs]].
*Added player-equipped sprites for [[Counter Scarf]] and [[Iron Boots]].

== Changes ==
=== Renames ===
*Renamed Birb Pheromones to [[Exotic Pheromones]].
*Renamed Brimflame Scowl to [[Brimflame Cowl]].
*Renamed Bumblebirb to [[The Dragonfolly]].
*Renamed Bumblebirb (clone) to [[Draconic Swarmer]].
*Renamed The Bumblebirbs (Lore) to [[The Dragonfolly (Lore)]].
*Renamed Bumblebirb Mask to [[Dragonfolly Mask]].
*Renamed Bumblebirb Trophy to [[Dragonfolly Trophy]].
*Renamed Pride Hunter's Planar Ripper to [[Prideful Hunter's Planar Ripper]].

=== Bosses ===
*Toxic Bubbles from the Sulphurous Sea no longer appear while a [[boss]] is alive.

==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*Now takes 99% less damage from the Brimstone Sword's projectiles and explosions.
*No longer drops [[Soul of Sight|Souls of Sight]].
*Tier 2 [[Acid Rain]] is now forced 10 seconds after defeating Aquatic Scourge.

==== [[Astrum Aureus]] ====
*No longer drops [[Lunar Fragments]].

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Increased worms' resistances to the [[Aerial Bane]] from 80% to 90%.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Changed laser telegraph from dusts to a line.
*No longer drops [[Soul of Fright|Souls of Fright]].

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Now charges twice after every set of fireballs and lasers fired in her second phase in [[Revengeance Mode]].

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*No longer drops [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]].

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Reduced Frozen and Chilled time from frost lasers.
*Decreased resistance to the [[Spent Fuel Container]] from 66.67% to 62%.
*Now takes 90% less damage from the [[Brimstone Sword]]'s projectiles and 99% less damage from its explosions.

==== [[The Dragonfolly]] ====
*Red Lightning Aura no longer deals damage until fully materialized.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Now takes 35% more damage from the [[Resurrection Butterfly]].

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Now takes 25% less damage from the Resurrection Butterfly.

==== [[The Old Duke]] ====
*Tooth Ball teeth now have less lifespan.
*Sulphurous Sharkrons no longer deal damage until fully visible.
*Updated resistances:
**Increased [[Galileo Gladius]] resistance from 60% to 80%.
**Increased [[Crescent Moon]] resistance from 40% to 80%.
**Increased [[Soul Edge]] resistance from 25% to 40%.

==== [[Profaned Guardians]] ====
*Added a 5 second grace period to their enrages when outside [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]].

==== [[Providence]] ====
*Now takes 50% more damage from the [[Elemental Axe]].

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Now takes 90% less damage from the [[Stardust Dragon Staff]]'s dragon minion.

=== Other NPCs ===
*[[Charred Slime]]s now spawn after the Brimstone Elemental has been defeated instead of after [[Plantera]] has been defeated.
*[[Flak Crab]]s can now only spawn underwater.
*[[Nuclear Toad]]s now spawn less frequently.
*[[Town NPCs]] now have an icon above them when new items are available in shops. They can be turned off via configuration options.
*The Abomination from the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/fargos-mutant-mod-summons-and-souls.42291/ Fargo's Mutant Mod] can now cancel the Acid Rain event and sells the consumable [[Caustic Tear]] after it has been defeated.
*The [[Sea King]] now sells the consumable Caustic Tear after the Acid Rain is defeated.
*Reduced the amount of kills to clear the first two tiers of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]] from 180 kills to 90 in Tier 1 and 125 in Tier 2.
*Nerfed Tier 1 Water Leech's damage from {{dv|30|51}} to {{dv|20|34}}.

=== Recipes ===
*Any [[Lore item]] can now be crafted with a respective boss [[trophy]] and 10 [[Victory Shard]]s.
*The consumable variant of the [[Caustic Tear]] can now be crafted with 4 [[Sulphuric Scale]]s at any [[water]] source.
*[[Astral Brick]]s are now crafted with [[Astral Stone]] instead of [[Stone Block]]s.
*[[Axe of Purity]] now requires double the [[Pixie Dust]] and [[Crystal Shard]]s. It is now crafted at a [[Hardmode Anvil]].
*[[Broken Biome Blade|Biome Blade]] now requires double the materials.
*[[Celestus]] now requires the [[Molten Amputator]] instead of [[Executioner's Blade]].
*[[The Dance of Light]] now requires a [[Sky Fracture]] to craft.
*[[Eternity]] now requires the [[Slithering Eels]] instead of [[Tome of Fates]].
*[[Fathom Swarmer Visage]] now requires an additional 3 [[Depth Cells]].
*[[Fathom Swarmer Breastplate]] now requires an additional 5 Depth Cells.
*[[Fathom Swarmer Greaves]] now require an additional 4 Depth Cells.
*Adjusted the [[Hadal Stew]] recipe. 5 [[Depth Cells]] are replaced with 10 [[Abyss Gravel]] or [[Voidstone]]. The amount of [[bowl]]s needed and Hadal Stews obtained from one craft is doubled.
*[[Kelvin Catalyst]] now requires a [[Frozen Key]] instead of 30 [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]].
*Adjusted the [[Metal Detector]] recipe. It now requires 5 additional [[Gold Dust]]s and [[Hallowed Bar]]s are replaced with [[Any Iron Bar]]s.
*[[Profaned Soul Crystal]] can now be crafted anywhere but instead requires only half of some materials if crafted in [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]].
*[[Sigil of Calamitas]] now uses the [[Any Evil Water]] recipe group.
*[[Snow Ruffian armor]] now uses the [[Any Snow Block]] and [[Any Ice Block]] recipe groups.
*[[Svantechnical]] now requires the [[SDFMG]] instead of [[S.D.M.G.]]
*[[True Caustic Edge]] now uses the [[Any Evil Flask]] recipe group.
*Increased the number of [[Vanquisher Arrow]]s crafted per set from 250 to 333.

=== Balance and Reworks ===
==== Balance Changes ====
*Added a [[stealth strike]] effect to [[Prismalline]] and [[Snap Clam]].
*[[Flails]] no longer count as [[true melee]] weapons.
*Nerfed [[Barinautical]]'s use time from 6 to 12, and its use delay from 15 to 20.
*Buffed [[Brimlance]]'s damage from 75 to 100.
*Nerfed [[Clam Crusher]]'s damage from 140 to 100.
*Buffed [[Darklight Greatsword]]'s damage from 55 to 75.
*[[Endo Hydra Staff]] now uses local [[immunity frames]]. Nerfed damage from 2563 to 800.
*[[Entropy's Vigil]] now requires 2 minion slots to summon the whole trio. Having 1 minion slot left will only summon Calamitamini. Buffed damage from 27 to 35.
*Nerfed [[Eutrophic Scimitar]]'s projectile damage from 100% base damage to 70%.
*Buffed [[Excalibur Shortsword]]'s damage from 55 to 70.
*Nerfed [[Flarewing Bow]]'s damage from 25 to 20, and its use time from 28 to 29.
*Nerfed [[Forsaken Saber]]'s projectile damage from 100% base damage to 80%.
*Buffed [[Frostbrand]]'s damage from 53 to 81.
*Nerfed [[Hallowed Rune]], stars now deal 33% damage and have a 1 second cooldown before re-appearing.
*Buffed [[Inferna Cutter]]'s damage from 85 to 100, and critical strike chance from 4% to 14%.
*Buffed [[Phoenix Blade]]'s damage from 95 to 120, and its use time from 28 to 26.
*[[Resurrection Butterfly]] now requires 2 minion slots to summon the whole duo. Having 1 minion slot left will only summon the Pink Butterfly. Buffed damage from 25 to 65.
*Nerfed [[Roxcalibur]]'s damage from 200 to 120.
*Nerfed [[Snap Clam]]'s debuff damage from 17.5 per second per clam to 7.5 per second per clam.
*Nerfed [[Spent Fuel Container]]'s damage from 69 to 50, and its use time from 28 to 35.
*Buffed [[Starnight Lance]]'s damage from 68 to 95, and its beam now pierces twice instead of once.
*Buffed [[Storm Saber]]'s damage from 42 to 68. It now additionally fires another beam from above.
*Nerfed [[Submarine Shocker]]'s damage from 70 to 60.
*Buffed [[True Caustic Edge]]'s damage from 42 to 100, and its projectile now gains a 25% increase to damage per bounce.
*Nerfed [[Vile Feeder]], minions now spawn 1-2 mini-eaters instead of 1-3.

==== Reworks ====
*[[The Dance of Light]] now fires a flurry of light blades instead of white splitting flares, causing a flash of light to erupt after 300 hits. Buffed damage from 700 to 2077, critical strike chance from 4% to 24%, and mana cost from 9 to 6. Decreased velocity from 30 to 14.
*Nerfed [[Draedon's Heart]]'s passive damage boost from 10% to 5%. It now inherits the reworked effects of [[Laudanum]] and [[Stress Pills]] below:
**Laudanum no longer grants stat boosts or immunity to the [[Horror]] debuff. It now causes Horror to last twice as long, but provide the player with several positive buffs instead of harming the player.
**Stress Pills no longer grant damage boosts and nerfed defense boost from 8 to 4. It now makes hits during a boss fight only remove half the [[Adrenaline Meter]] instead of the full meter.

==== Tier Changes ====
*Several items sold by the [[Archmage]] have increased in tier. As a result, several items have been rebalanced.
**[[Arctic Bear Paw]], [[Cryogenic Staff]], and [[Frosty Flare]] are now sold after all [[Mechanical Boss]]es have been defeated. Their prices have been raised from {{gc|36}} to {{gc|48}} and [[rarity]] increased from {{rare|5}} to {{rare|6}}.
***Buffed Arctic Bear Paw's damage from 70 to 100.
***Nerfed Cryogenic Staff's damage from 56 to 50.
**[[Absolute Zero]], [[Eternal Blizzard]], [[Icicle Arrow]], [[Icy Bullet]], and [[Winter's Fury]] are now sold after the [[Frost Moon]] has been defeated.
***The price of Icicle Arrows and Icy Bullets raised from {{cc|15}} to {{cc|80}} and rarity increased from {{rare|5}} to {{rare|6}}.
***The price of Absolute Zero, Eternal Blizzard, and Winter's Fury raised from {{gc|36}} to {{gc|80}} and rarity increased from {{rare|5}} to {{rare|8}}.
***Buffed Absolute Zero's damage from 42 to 120.
***Buffed Eternal Blizzard's damage from 32 to 73.
***Buffed Winter's Fury's damage from 28 to 69. 
*[[Charred Ore]] may now only be mined after the [[Brimstone Elemental]] has been defeated. Its pickaxe power requirement is reduced from 200% to 180%. Most items crafted from [[Unholy Core]]s have been rebalanced.
**Nerfed [[Brimblade]]'s damage from 32 to 28.
**Greatly increased the range of [[Brimrose Staff]].
**Nerfed [[Brimstone Fury]]'s damage from 40 to 35.
**Buffed [[Brimstone Sword]]'s damage from 61 to 75.
**Nerfed [[Burning Sea]]'s damage from 79 to 72.
**Nerfed [[Igneous Exaltation]]'s damage from 39 to 34. Launched Igneous Blades now pierce twice instead of infinitely.
*Most [[Drunk Princess' Drinks]] have increased in tier. None of the drinks have changed in terms of their boosted stats.
**[[Screwdriver]], [[Vodka]], and [[White Wine]] are now sold after all Mechanical Bosses have been defeated.
**[[Caribbean Rum]], [[Evergreen Gin]], and [[Margarita]] are now sold after [[Plantera]] has been defeated.
**[[Bloody Mary]] and [[Everclear]] are now sold after [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated.
**[[Cinnamon Roll]], [[Moonshine]], [[Moscow Mule]], and [[Tequila Sunrise]] are now sold after [[Golem]] has been defeated.

=== Additional Changes ===
*Added an electric animation to the [[Adrenaline]] and [[Rage Meter]]s when fully charged and a shake effect when used. The meters now drop smoothly when used instead of abruptly.
**The shake effect can be customized in the [[configuration options]].
*[[Aestheticus]] now swings like a [[broadsword]] instead of being held like a [[wand]].
*[[Astral armor]]'s fallen stars now use a new projectile that deals ranged damage and decreases its damage by 6.25% for each enemy it strikes.
*[[Bloodflare armor]]'s souls and [[Plague Reaper armor]]'s cinders are now affected by ranged damage boosts.
*Added a status message after defeating the Brimstone Elemental to inform the player they can now mine Charred Ore.
*[[Devil's Sunrise]], [[Diseased Pike]], [[Fulguration Halberd]], [[Gilded Proboscis]], [[Heart Rapier]], [[Marnite Spear]], [[Murasama]], [[Sausage Maker]], [[Vulcanite Lance]] and [[Yateveo Bloom]] now benefit from [[true melee]] bonuses.
*Calamity [[Beds]] and [[Bathtubs]] can now be placed on [[platforms]].
*[[Harpy Wings]] now give a chance for most attacks to create a feather while a [[Harpy Ring]] or [[Angel Treads]] are equipped.
*The [[Oiled]] debuff now increases the damage over time for [[Abyssal Flames]], [[Brimstone Flames]], [[Demon Flames]], [[God Slayer Inferno]], and [[Holy Flames]].
*Slightly adjusted the Profaned Soul Artifact challenge to allow for damage up to 5% of Providence's max health instead of 1%.
*[[Skyline Wings]] now grant the player a 25% jump speed boost when wearing [[Aerospec armor]].
*Rage is no longer internally named "[[Stress]]".
*Reduced the amount of life regeneration reduction from the Sulphuric Poisoning debuff from 24 to 6.
*[[Sulphurous armor]] now reduces the effect of the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] debuff.
*Sulphurous armor jump now has a cooldown of 0.33 seconds.
*Changed the Sulphurous Sea world generation to include islands.
*The [[Astral Meteor]] now avoids the Sulphurous Sea completely.
*Afterimages can now be turned off via configuration options instead of lighting settings.
*Added tooltips to [[Abombination]], [[Charred Idol]], and [[Cryo Key]].
*Added custom [[rarity]] to several Developer items:
**These are: [[Crystyl Crusher]], [[Draconic Destruction]], [[Endogenesis]], [[Eternity]], [[Gael's Greatsword]], [[Scarlet Devil]] and [[Temporal Umbrella]].

*Resprited [[Perforator Cyst]].

*Fixed the [[Cosmolight]] not working in Multiplayer.
*Fixed invalid sounds made by [[The Dragonfolly]] that cause it to despawn.
*Fixed [[Enchanted Butterfly]] tooltip referring to Sparks eating dragonflies instead of butterflies.
*Fixed a tile-alignment crash with the [[Eutrophic Shelf]] when the Ancients Awakened Mod is enabled.
*Fixed lag issues from the [[Great Sand Shark]] in Multiplayer.
*Fixed [[Hydrothermic armor]] displaying the player's hair when equipped.
*Fixed an oversight where [[Inferna Cutter]] and [[Ultimus Cleaver]] were still considered [[true melee]] weapons.
*Fixed an oversight where [[Picksaw]]’s tooltip still referred to [[Scoria Ore]] as Chaotic Ore.
*Fixed [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]’s hitbox.
*Fixed a bug where the [[Mechworm]] would randomly attach on targets and disappear from losing any minion slots.
*Fixed the Sulphurous Sea floating above the surface layer.
*Fixed the [[Water Leech]] teleport-leeching to players and spawn-killing them.
