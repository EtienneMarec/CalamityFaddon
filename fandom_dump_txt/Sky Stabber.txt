The '''Sky Stabber''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws up to 4 gravity-ignoring spiky balls, with the amount of balls thrown depending on stack size. The balls move very slowly and decelerate to a stop after travelling a short distance. The balls bounce off tiles and will despawn after landing 20 enemy hits or after a long amount of time. Right-clicking with the weapon will delete all the existing spiky balls.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Sky Stabber will cause the next spiky ball thrown to rain down a spread of 4 homing feathers for each enemy hit. The feathers released are similar to the Set Bonus of the [[Aerospec armor]].
