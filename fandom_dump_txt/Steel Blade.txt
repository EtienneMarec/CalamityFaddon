The '''Steel Blade''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It is dropped by the [[Ceasing Void]]. It fires projectiles that resemble steel. Projectiles move based on player movement.

If an enemy is struck by the blade, the amount of contact damage that enemy deals is added to the weapon's damage. It cannot deal more than 5 damage.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*Though it initially deals noticeably less DPS than most other weapons of its tier, striking an enemy with very high contact damage (such as [[The Hive Mind]]) will significantly increase its damage output.
