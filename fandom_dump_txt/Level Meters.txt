'''Level Meters''' are [[coverings]] purchased from the [[Goblin Tinkerer]] that tell the player their current levels of [[proficiency]] with a certain class, as well as the exact stats gained depending on the class. In addition to this, if they are equipped, they give the player a 10% chance to earn double proficiency if their current proficiency is not divisible by 100.

== Trivia ==
*The sprite used for all five Level Meters is based on [https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Scouter Scouters] from ''[[wikipedia:Dragon Ball Z|Dragon Ball Z]]''.
