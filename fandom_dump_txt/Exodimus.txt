'''Exodimus''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[dagger]] that is an upgrade to the [[Seraphim]]. When used it throws an even spread of 5 infinitely piercing daggers, with the second and fourth daggers inflicting [[Vulnerability Hex]] if [[Supreme Calamitas]] has been defeated. After half a second has passed, all five daggers will rapidly dart towards enemies or the cursor if no enemies exist, and stick onto them, dealing continous damage. A maximum of 20 daggers can be latched onto an enemy at once. After 10 seconds have passed, each dagger explodes into a cross of laser beams.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Exodimus modifies the laser beam pattern to have 8 beams in a star pattern instead of a 4 beamed cross.
