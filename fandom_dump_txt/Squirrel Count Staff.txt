The '''Squirrel Count Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and shoots homing fireballs which explode on impact. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
