'''Armor Crunch''' is a [[debuff]] that decreases target's defense by 15. It is inflicted by the [[Broken Biome Blade]] and any of its upgrades (in certain biomes), melee and [[Spy]] attacks with the [[Armor Crumbling]] or [[Armor Shattering]] [[buff]]s, the [[Aftershock]], [[Earthen Pike]], [[Slag Magnum]], [[Spear of Paleolith]], [[The Ballista]], and [[Apotheosis]]. It is additionally inflicted by [[Golem]], [[The Leviathan]] and several enemies in the [[Dungeon]]. If inflicted on the player, damage reduction is also decreased by 67%. All enemies that are vulnerable to this debuff are also vulnerable to [[War Cleave]].
