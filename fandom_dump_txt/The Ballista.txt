'''The Ballista''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]] that auto-fires. It converts [[Wooden Arrow]]s into special arrows that can pierce one enemy and shatter into fossil shards upon hitting a second, as well as upon hitting solid blocks. Fossil shards deal 20% of the weapon's base damage. All projectiles will inflict the [[Armor Crunch]] [[debuff]].
