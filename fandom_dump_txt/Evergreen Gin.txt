'''Evergreen Gin''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Evergreen Gin''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen, but boosts damage reduction and the damage per second of sickness and water-related debuffs by 50%.

While the effects of the Evergreen Gin debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
