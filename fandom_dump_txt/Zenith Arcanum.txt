The '''Zenith Arcanum''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. When equipped it has the following effects:
*20% increased summon damage and defense.
*15% increased mining speed.
*4 life regeneration.
*8 max minions.
*Summons a Brimstone Elemental, Sand Elemental, Cloud Elemental, Plague Elemental, Sulphur Elemental, Water Elemental, Young Duke, Gladiator Swords, and Howl Trio to attack enemies, each with a base damage of 290.
*Summons a Rare Sand Elemental to heal you.
*Minions inflict [[Demon Flames]], [[Electrified]], [[Temporal Sadness]], [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]], [[Irradiated]], [[Shadowflame]], [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] and [[Holy Flames]] for 3 seconds each.
*Skeletal hands are summoned on enemy hits which deal 660 damage each and inflict [[Vulnerability Hex]].

== Notes ==
*All of the world's attacks grant enemies 20 local [[immunity frames]], except for the Sand Elemental that grants 30 local immunity frames with contact damage.

== Tips ==
*This covering can be very helpful when performing less combat-oriented tasks such as [[Fishing|fishing]], as the elementals will keep most normal enemies at bay and allow the player's full attention to be focused on what they're doing.
*[[Potions]] such as [[Heartreach Potion]] and [[Cadance Potion|cadence]] [[Cadance Potion|potion]] effectively grant homing to the Rare Sand Elemental's healing orbs.

== Trivia ==
*The item's name refers to the concept of [[wikipedia:Food chain|food chain]]s, the cycle of how energy is created then consumed, with the top having no competition.
