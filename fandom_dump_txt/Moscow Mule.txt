'''Moscow Mule''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Moscow Mule''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen, but boosts damage, knockback and critical strike chance.

While the effects of the Moscow Mule debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
