The '''Quiver of Nihility''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. While equipped, any weapon that fires arrows will have said arrows trailed by a second shadow arrow and some purple void particles. The shadow arrow will start to aggressively home after 2.67 seconds, and does the same amount of damage as the base arrow. The purple void particles will very slowly home in on enemies, and dissipate after 16 seconds. Additionally, arrow damage is increased by a multiplicative 30%.
