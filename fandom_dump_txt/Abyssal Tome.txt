The '''Abyssal Tome''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that drops from [[The Slime God]]. It fires a slow moving Abyss Ball that can travel through blocks and is not affected by gravity, and will slowly decelerate until stopping in place. After hitting an enemy twice or after 5 seconds from being shot, it will explode, dealing damage to anything caught in the explosion. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Eldritch Tome]].
