'''Alcohol Poisoning''' is a [[debuff]] that is inflicted on the player when they have more than 3 debuffs active from the drinks sold by the [[Drunk Princess]]. It prevents any positive life regen and decreases the player's life regen by 3 for every drink debuff currently active.

== Notes ==
*[[Everclear]] counts as 2 alcohols for the purposes of alcohol poisoning.
*If the player gets killed by the debuff, the custom [[death message]] "''<PlayerName>'s liver failed.''" or "''<PlayerName> downed too many shots.''" is used.
