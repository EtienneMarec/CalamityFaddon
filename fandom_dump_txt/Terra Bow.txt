The '''Terra Bow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]] and a direct upgrade to the [[Lunarian Bow]]. It fires three arrows. Similarly to the Lunarian Bow, when [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], it fires three parallel bolts that are not affected by gravity; unlike other projectiles, the bolts tend to slide on tiles they collide with instead of bouncing off of them or disappearing.
