The '''Virid Vangar''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Igneous Exaltation]]. When used, it summons a singular gar that sticks to the player's back. When an enemy comes within a 10 block range of the player, the gar will violently slash at the enemy, inflicting [[Poisoned]].
