The '''1.1.6.15''' update added a new duo of bosses known as [[Siren Lure and Leviathan]], along with a handful of new [[weapons]]. The update also modified dust on hit effects from most of the mod's [[enemies]], and resprited the elemental weapon set.

== Additions ==
*Added [[The Leviathan]] and [[Siren Lure]].
*Added [[???]], [[Aquatic Aberration]], [[BOHLDOHR]], and [[Parasea]].
*Added [[Astral Blaster]], [[Atlantis]], [[Brackish Flask]], [[Brimlash]], [[Dragon Rage]], [[Flarefrost Blade]], [[Grax]], [[Greentide]], [[Leviatitan]], [[Lure of Enthrallment]], [[Profaned Trident]], [[Shroomer]], [[Siren's Song]], and [[Titanium Railgun]].
*Added [[Alchemical Flask]], [[Enchanted Pearl]], and [[Leviathan Ambergris]].
*Added [[Leviathan Mask]].
*Added [[Leviathan Trophy]].
*Added [[Grax Boost]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Armored Digger]]'s body segments, [[Calamitas]] and her brothers, [[Cloud Elemental]], all of [[Cryogen]]'s phases, [[Cultist Assassin]]s, [[Desert Scourge]]'s body segments, [[Devourer Spawn]]'s body segments, [[Dried Seeker]]'s body segments, [[Heat Spirit]]s, [[Hive Mind]]'s first phase, [[Ice Clasper]]s, [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]], all three [[Perforators]]' body segments, [[Perforator Mini]]'s body segments, [[Pitbull]]s, all components of [[The Slime God]], and [[Sun Bat]]s now produce less dust when hit.
*Armored Digger's head and tail, [[Blighted Eye]]s, [[Calamity Eye]]s, [[Corrupt Slime Spawn]]s, [[Crimson Slime Spawn]]s, [[Crimulan Blight Slime]]s, Desert Scourge's head and tail, [[The Devourer of Gods]], Devourer Spawn's head and tail, Dried Seeker's head and tail, [[Earth Elemental]]s, [[Ebonian Blight Slime]]s, [[Life Seeker]]s, all three Perforators' heads and tails, [[Plague Charger]]s, [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Plaguebringer Shade]]s, [[Sand Tortoise]]s, [[Shockstorm Shuttle]]s, [[Spiked Crimson Slime Spawn]]s, [[Stasis Probe]]s, and [[Stormlion]]s now produce dust when hit.
*[[Aurora Spirit]]s, Blighted Eyes, Calamitas and her brothers, Calamity Eyes, Cloud Elementals, wingless Corrupt Slime Spawns, [[Cosmic Elemental]]s, all [[Crawlers]], Crimson Slime Spawns, Crimulan Blight Slimes, [[Cryocore]]s, all of Cryogen's phases, Cultist Assassins, [[Dank Creeper]]s, [[Dark Heart]]s, all of Desert Scourge's segments, all of The Devourer of Gods' segments, all of Devourer Spawn's segments, all of Dried Seeker's segments, Earth Elementals, Heat Spirits, both phases of The Hive Mind, Ice Claspers, Jungle Dragon, Yharon, Life Seekers, all segments of all three Perforators, Pitbulls, Plague Chargers, The Plaguebringer Goliath, Plaguebringer Shades, Sand Tortoises, Shockstorm Shuttles, The Slime God core, split Slime Gods, Spiked Crimson Slime Spawns, Stasis Probes, [[Stormlion]]s, and Sun Bats now produce dust when killed.
*Desert Scourge body segments no longer produce [[water]] tiles when hit.
*Desert Scourge and all three Perforators no longer take 50% damage from projectiles which pierce more than 3 times in [[Expert Mode]].
*The Devourer of Gods now has gores.
*All three Perforators now drop [[Healing Potion]]s and are referred to in their death messages by their respective sizes. <!---The Medium Perforator has been defeated--->

=== [[Items]] ===
*[[Ark of the Elements]] now has an orange beam variant.
*[[Elemental Lance]]'s beams now split one more time.
*Renamed [[Lashes of Chaos]]' Brimstone Hellfire projectile to Brimstone Hellfireball. <!---Only matters for the sake of death messages--->
*[[Storm Surge]] now uses 4 [[Aerialite Bar]]s in its recipe.
*Nerfed [[True Caustic Edge]]’s damage from 75 to 47, buffed its knockback from 5.25 to 5.75, and it no longer used a [[Broken Hero Sword]] in its recipes.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Dragon Egg (summoning)]].
*Resprited [[Ark of the Elements]], [[Augur of the Elements]], [[Elemental Axe]], [[Elemental Disk]], [[Elemental Eruption]], [[Elemental Shiv]], [[Sun God Staff]], and [[Sun Spirit Staff]].
*Resprited [[Skyline Wings]].
*Resprited Ark of the Elements' beams, Elemental Lance's beams, and Elemental Shiv's beam.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Spectralstorm Cannon]]'s Spectre projectiles dealing magic damage instead of ranged damage.
