The '''Pizza Wheel''' is a Godseeker Mode magic weapon dropped by [[Evil Animatronic | Evil Animatronics]]. When used, pizzas will begin to roll onto the screen from the left and right. The pizzas roll in straight lines, and ignore both gravity and blocks.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Notes ==
*Worm type enemies take 90% less damage from this weapon.

== Trivia ==
*Pizza Wheel is an attack from the video game Five Nights at Freddys World, and functions almost identically to its counterpart from said game.
