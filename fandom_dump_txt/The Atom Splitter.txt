'''The Atom Splitter''' is a Godseeker Mode [[javelin]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]] have been defeated last. It throws out a gravity-defying javelin that travels through tiles and emits light. Upon contact with an enemy, 15 rainbow javelins randomly appear from all sides of the target, each dealing 70% damage and pierces twice but have a very short lifespan.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with The Atom Splitter will cause the resulting javelin to spawn 100 rainbow javelins with 14.4% damage each.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Trivia ==
*The item's name refers to how, in real life, the act of splitting an atom has the potential to release excessive amounts of heat, or even create large explosions.
