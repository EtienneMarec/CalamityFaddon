The '''Broken Water Filter''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spawn-blocking]] [[tool]] that can also be found in [[Rusty Chest]]s. While the item is favorited in the player's inventory, natural [[Acid Rain]] spawns are disabled.
