The '''Soul of Ionogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Ionogen]].

When equipped, the player is occasionally stuck with lightning which damages on-screen NPCs around the player. The damage dealt is equal to the base damage of of the item currently held. Lightning strikes do not occur if the player is underground.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.

* I am relieved you were able to locate this particular soul... Choosing to control electricity and sunlight through magic is commendable, however without refinement it stays weak against our oppressors' technology.
* The archmage who practiced this magic must have been impressively reclusive. It is unfortunate their soul could only channel its power through batteries, it has the side effect of being mildly toxic for me. 

After this, Permafrost will be able to summon lightning strikes from the sky to electrocute enemies.
