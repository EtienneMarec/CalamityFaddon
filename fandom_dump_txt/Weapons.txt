'''Weapons''' are useful items used for combat against [[enemies]], [[bosses]], and even other players during PvP games. Most weapons are crafted at any crafting Station, while others can only be found in Chests, as enemy/boss drops, or purchased from [[NPCs]].
The Calamity Mod adds a number of weapons for players to use. Many are upgrades to existing [[tgc:Weapons|weapons]] and others are wholly unique. Despite being very frequent within the mod, weapons are very weak and are not recommended for use. Instead, using traps are the preferred method for combat.
