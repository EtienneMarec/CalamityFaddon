The '''Vampiric Talisman''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. While equipped, it increases [[Spy weapons|Spy]] damage by 12% and [[Lifesteal|heals]] the player on Spy weapon critical hits. The amount healed is 1.5% the damage dealt, rounded down and up to a maximum of 6, meaning a minimum of 68 damage (a crit of 34) must be dealt in order to receive any healing. The lifesteal is indicated by a red streak travelling from the hit enemy back the player.
