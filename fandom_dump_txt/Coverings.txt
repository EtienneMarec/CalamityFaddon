'''Coverings''' is a set of clothes people wear to stop them from getting cold outside and the defense items that reduce damage taken from [[enemies]] and most other damage sources. Coverings pieces are equipped by placing them in the coverings slots of the player's inventory. Coverings can also be placed in vanity slots to change the player's appearance without affecting stats. Coverings pieces are either crafted, purchased from [[NPCs]], or dropped from enemies.

Each piece of covering has a [[defense]] statistic that reduces inflicted damage by half / {{expert|3/4}} its value, rounded up. For example, a full set of [[Wulfrum Coverings]] (Melee) provides a total of 9 defense, reducing damage by 5/{{expert|6}} points. [[Statigel Coverings]] (Ranged) grant a total of 25 defense, reducing damage by 13/{{expert|19}}, after having been rounded up. An attack cannot be reduced to zero; all attacks will always deal at least 1 damage (or 2 damage for a [[critical hit]]), regardless of the quality of a player's coverings.

In addition to the defense provided by individual covering pieces, most covering types provide a '''Set Bonus'''<!-- bolded because the page [[Set Bonus]] redirects here and it's an important part of the coverings topic --> when all three pieces of the same covering type are worn simultaneously. For the basic [[Pre-Hardmode]] coverings, e.g. [[Wulfrum Coverings]], this is an additional defense bonus. More advanced or unique coverings can provide other specialized set bonuses (e.g., the [[Omega Blue coverings]] will summon 6 tentacles to attack enemies and heal the player).

The Calamity Mod adds 28 new covering sets. 12 sets can be obtained [[Pre-Hardmode]], 11 sets in [[Hardmode]], and 10 sets Godseeker Mode. Most covering sets have 5 different helmets for each main class (Melee, Ranged, Mage, Summoner, and [[Spy]]), though four covering sets grant universal bonuses and twelve covering sets are class-specific. In addition, several covering sets are rebalanced or given new effects.
