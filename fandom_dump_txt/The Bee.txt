'''The Bee''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Queen Bee]]. When the player is hit at full health, damage dealt by the player will be increased by half of their [[Damage Reduction]], excluding separated damage reduction boosts. Additionally, taking a hit while at full health will have the incoming damage be halved, with a 10-second cooldown.

== Trivia ==
*This covering is a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/The_Bee item of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
*The covering's design is based off of [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_Sectonia Queen Sectonia], the bee-themed main antagonist of ''[[wikipedia:Kirby: Triple Deluxe|Kirby: Triple Deluxe]]''.
*The damage-halving effect when at full health is a reference to the [https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Multiscale_(Ability) "Multiscale" ability] from the ''[[wikipedia:Pokémon|Pokémon]]'' game series.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Bee Cloak]].
