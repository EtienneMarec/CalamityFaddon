The '''Cosmic Guardian''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns rarely underground. A [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] variant is also spawned during the fight with [[The Devourer of Gods]]. In [[Revengeance Mode]], it spawns when the Devourer of Gods reaches 60% health, and an additional two appear at 20% health. Another three will spawn in the Devourer of Gods' final phase at 30% health in all difficulties. It will fly up high and despawn once the Devourer of Gods is defeated in its first phase. It can also be spawned by the [[Chibii Devourer]] pet.

It behaves almost identically to a [[Wyvern]], albeit with significantly increased speed, health, and damage. After [[The Devourer of Gods]] is defeated, it will begin dropping [[Cosmilite Slag]].

{{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}} Cosmic Guardians can also spawn around [[Distortion Essence Sphere]]s during the post-[[The Starborn Moth|Starborn Moth]] [[Martian Madness]] event.

== Notes ==
*The Devourer of Gods spawns one Cosmic Guardian when reduced to 60% life. At 20% life, it will spawn two more Cosmic Guardians. In the final phase three Cosmic Guardians spawn at 30% life.
*The Cosmic Guardian cannot damage or be damaged by the player for 3 seconds after spawning.
*Due to lacking a special resistance to piercing weapons like most worm bosses, the Cosmic Guardian is highly vulnerable to weapons such as [[Valediction]] or [[Blissful Bombardier]].
*If all three Cosmic Guardians spawn at once while the Devourer of Gods is alive, some Cosmic Guardians may spawn without a few segments or lacking their entire body besides the head. This is the result of the many worms' segments reaching the NPC cap, preventing additional segments from spawning.
*The Cosmic Guardian can also be spawned by getting stabbed by the [[Cosmic Plushie|Chibii Devourer]] Pet. If the player's name is Terry, getting stabbed will instead summon the Devourer Of Gods' final phase.
*Just like The Devourer of Gods, the Cosmic Guardian cannot be hurt by any attack that would deal more than 1/2 of their health in one hit, such as another mod's butcher function or an incredibly powerful weapon. When hit by such an attack, the damage is reduced to 0.
*Guardians spawned by the Devourer of Gods, or by the Chibi Devourer pet, do not drop money or items.

== Trivia ==
*The Cosmic Guardian used to be called "Another worm boss" as a reference to the prevalence of [[Worm AI|worm]] [[bosses]] in the mod. After that, it was known as "Devourer of Thots", in reference to the memetic slang term "thot" to refer to a promiscuous woman.
*Before the Cosmic Guardian was introduced, a second Devourer of Gods would spawn in its place.
**The player would have to defeat the newly spawned Devourer of Gods first because if the new Devourer reached 25% life after the old Devourer had been defeated, a ''third'' Devourer of Gods would spawn, and the process would begin anew.
**Only two Devourers of Gods could exist at once, so by defeating the newly spawned Devourer of Gods first, the original Devourer of Gods (which would already be below 25% life) could be defeated without worry of another spawning.
*Due to the Cosmic Guardian previously being only a boss minion, it is one of the few enemies that do not have [[Banners]].
