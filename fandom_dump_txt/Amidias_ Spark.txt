'''Amidias' Spark''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Cnidrion]]s in the [[Underground Desert]]. When equipped, the player will release 8 gravity-affected sparks around them when harmed by any source of damage. These sparks linger for 2 seconds, partially ignore [[immunity frames]], and deal 6 damage.
