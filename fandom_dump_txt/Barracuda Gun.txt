The '''Barracuda Gun''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Piranha Gun]]. Holding left-click will fire two barracudas that latch on to [[enemies]] and deal continuous damage as long as left-click is held down. After an enemy dies, the barracudas will automatically latch onto other enemies in range. The two barracudas cannot latch onto the same enemy.
