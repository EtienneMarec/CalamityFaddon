'''Jake''' is a craftable post-[[Supreme Calamitas]] [[Swords|broadsword]] that autoswings. It is considered the true final upgrade to the [[Ark of the Cosmos]] and the [[Exoblade]], requiring one of the most complex crafting trees in the Calamity Mod. Unlike the weapons required to craft it, it has no unique animations and shoots no projectiles, its power manifesting only in the form of its incredibly high damage. Upon killing an enemy with the sword, a unique message chosen at random from a list of set messages appears above the player's head.
