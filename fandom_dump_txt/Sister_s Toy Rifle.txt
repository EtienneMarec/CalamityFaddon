The '''Sister's Toy Rifle''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]] that is an upgraded version of the [[tgc:Red Ryder|Red Ryder]]. Upon use, it converts any bullets fired into Foam Bullets, which inherits used bullet's effect. It can only fire 6 times, after which it requires a reload. 

{{Key|open}} causes the bar to appear. Right-clicking again the moment bar is about to be full will reload the gun, causing the first 3 shots to deal 1.5x the damage and be able to bounce off enemies up to 5 times. Depending if the reload failed or is successful, it either plays the "Boo-womp" sound, or the "Ding!" sound, respectively.
