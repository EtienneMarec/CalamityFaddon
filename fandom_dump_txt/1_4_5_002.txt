The '''1.4.5.002''' (or '''1.4.5.2''') update added two new items, fixed numerous bugs, and adjusted various [[enemies]] and [[bosses]].

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Aero Dynamite]].
*Added [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Anahita and Leviathan]] ====
*Nerfed [[The Leviathan]]'s health from {{dv|69,000|110,400|145,120}} to {{dv|55,200|88,320|116,096}}.

==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*Now fires several barrages of sand blasts in its spin attack instead of summoning enemies.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ==== 
*Reduced top speed in its second phase in all difficulties.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Now fires blue lasers below 70% health which inflict [[Electrified]] in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Green lasers are now fired below 85% health and inflict [[Cursed Inferno]] instead of homing onto the player.
*All lasers can fire regardless of health in [[Death Mode]].
*Reduced maximum body defense buff from 30 to 10.
*[[Probe]]s now fire a less amount of lasers in a set time.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]]====
*Lasers in Phase 1 can now pass through tiles between them and the player as well as inflicting [[God Slayer Inferno]] for 2 seconds.

==== [[The Dragonfolly]] ====
*Can now have up to 3 Draconic Swarmers alive at once in Revengeance Mode.
*Phase 2 no longer limits the maximum Swarmers to 1.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Freed head in Revengeance Mode no longer deals contact damage.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Now burrows more often in Phase 1 and even more often in higher difficulties.
*The amount of Hive Blobs and Dank Creepers that spawn during the fight now scales with world difficulty.
*Hive Blobs stay much closer to the Hive Mind itself and fire vile clots slightly more often, but with reduced velocity.
*Buffed Hive Blob's health from 75 to 100 for normal blobs and 150 for the variants that can spawn when the Hive Mind takes damage.
*Shaderain projectiles now fall much slower.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Nerfed fast charge telegraph and bullet hell damage reduction from 75% to 40%.
*No longer deals contact damage during bullet hell spins.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Ancient Doom projectiles and clone Fireballs now inflict [[Shadowflame]] in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Reduced top speed in Revengeance Mode.
*Phantasmal Spheres now start out with a slower velocity and accelerate over time.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Can no longer naturally spawn in Death Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Reduced tile enrage requirement by 25%.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] ====
*Now immune to [[Nightwither]].
*Now drops both [[Elysian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Wings]] when defeated entirely at nighttime, regardless of the biome the player is in.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Can no longer naturally spawn in Death Mode.

==== [[Skeletron]] ====
*Hands cannot deal damage for 3 seconds after spawning.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Limbs cannot deal damage for 3 seconds after spawning.
*Rockets in Revengeance Mode are now slightly slower.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Reduced speed and slimes summoned.
*Reduced [[Distorted]] duration from the Core from 4 seconds to 3, and increased speed when possessing another slime.

==== [[Wall of Flesh]] ====
*Lasers while enraged now start out with a slower velocity and accelerate over time.

=== Enemies ===
*[[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]]s no longer drop [[Lumenyl]] pre-[[Calamitas]] or [[Ectoplasm]] pre-[[Plantera]].
*All bosses besides the [[Profaned Guardians]] and the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]] are now guaranteed to drop at least one weapon.
*[[Armored Digger]]s and [[Draedon's Remote]] [[Mechanical Bosses]] now drop [[Dubious Plating]] and [[Mysterious Circuitry]].
*Nerfed [[Draconic Swarmer]]'s health from {{dv|20,000|40,000|50,000}} to {{dv|12,000|24,000|30,000}}.
*Decreased the spawn rate of [[Piggy|Piggies]] and [[Wulfrum Drone]]s.
*Decreased the speed of Wulfrum Drones and [[Wulfrum Hovercraft]]s.
*[[Blue Jellyfish|Blue]] and [[Green Jellyfish]] can no longer spawn in any Calamity Mod biome.
*Increased spawn rates for the [[Giant Clam]], [[Wulfrum Gyrator]], Wulfrum Hovercraft, [[Wulfrum Pylon]], and [[Wulfrum Rover]].
*[[Wulfrum Pylon]]s can now occasionally spawn in the inner third of the world.
*Wulfrum Pylons now boost enemy spawn rates if there are less than 4 other Wulfrum enemies nearby.

=== Recipes ===
*All of the following recipes that use [[Endothermic Energy]], [[Nightmare Fuel]], and/or [[Phantoplasm]] have been replaced with Ascendant Spirit Essence(s):
**[[The Amalgam]], [[Core of the Blood God]], [[Draedon's Heart]], [[Eclipse Mirror]], [[Elemental Gauntlet]], [[Elemental Quiver]], [[Ethereal Talisman]], [[Nanotech]], [[Nucleogenesis]], [[Silva Wings]], and [[The Sponge]].
**[[Fearmonger armor]], [[God Slayer armor]], and [[Silva armor]].
**[[Alluvion]], [[Apotheosis]], [[Ataraxia]], [[Cosmic Shiv]], [[Devil's Devastation]], [[Face Melter]], [[Galaxia]], [[Galaxy Smasher]], [[God's Paranoia]], [[Greatsword of Blah]], [[Light God's Brilliance]], [[Minigun]], [[Nadir]], [[Phangasm]], [[Primordial Ancient]], [[Recitation of the Beast]], [[SDFMG]], and [[T1000]].
**[[Auric Tesla Bar]], [[Dragonfruit]], and [[Silva Crystal]].
*Changed the number of [[Cosmilite Bar]]s in several recipes:
**The Sponge now requires 5 bars instead of 15.
**Galaxy Smasher, Primordial Ancient, and [[Starmada]] now require 5 bars instead of 10.
**SDFMG now requires 5 bars instead of 4.
*Removed Phantoplasm from [[Asgardian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Tracers]]' recipes.
*[[Clock Gatlignum]] now requires [[Clockwork Assault Rifle]] in its recipe.
*[[Draedon's Heart]] and Starmada are now crafted at a Draedon's Forge instead of the Ancient Manipulator.
*[[Laboratory Shelf]] is now crafted and crafted into [[Laboratory Plating]] instead of [[Laboratory Panels]].
*Reduced the number of Forbidden Fragments in Primordial Ancient's recipe from 10 to 5.
*[[Rusted Pipes]] now use the [[Any Iron Bar]] recipe group.
*[[Shredder]] requires [[Frostbite Blaster]] to craft instead of Clockwork Assault Rifle.
*Starmada now requires an Ascendant Spirit Essence to craft.

=== Renames ===
*Renamed Anahita's Song to [[Anahita's Arpeggio]].
*Renamed Omega Blue Leggings to [[Omega Blue Tentacles]].
*Renamed Wulfrum Hood to [[Wulfrum Visage]].

=== Balance and Reworks ===
*All Spy coverings that grant special on-hit effects can only proc on the first 5 hits of a Spy projectile.
*[[Anti-materiel Rifle]] projectiles now have 10% of the weapon's knockback instead of the full amount.
*Reworked [[Caustic Staff]], now inflicts debuffs on a randomized timer between 1 and 3 seconds.
**It now only inflicts one debuff at a time, and cycles through its list of debuffs.
**Debuffs are now only applied if the player is holding a [[Summon weapon]] or [[Tool]].
**Debuffs must time out and cannot be refreshed before the next debuff can be applied.
*Reworked [[Celestus]]' stealth strike, now fires a single scythe that bounces off and homes into enemies after returning to the player once.
*[[Desert Prowler armor]] no longer boosts the damage of ammunition.
*Greatly reduced how often [[Elemental Disk]] splits and how many sub projectiles it can make.
*Nerfed [[Elemental Excalibur]]'s shoot speed from 12 to 6.
*Adjusted [[Entropy's Vigil]], Cataclymini and Catastromini minions now uses ID-static iframes.
*Reworked [[Hypothermia]]'s stealth strike, now fires an ice chunk that splits into more ice shards after traveling a short distance.
*The player can now receive the [[Life Drain]] buff from the following NPCs:
**[[Anahita and Leviathan]], [[Astrum Aureus]], [[Brimling]], [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], [[Cataclysm]], [[Catastrophe]], [[Cryogen]], and [[Soul Seeker]].
*[[Lunarian Bow]] projectiles can no longer pierce.
*Adjusted [[Napalm Arrow]]s, now use ID-static iframes.
*Adjusted [[Optic Staff]], Spazmamini minions now use ID-static iframes.
*[[Profaned Soul Crystal]] now only gives [[Holy Flames]] immunity during the day and [[Nightwither]] immunity during the night.
*Profaned Soul Crystal minions now only inflict Holy Flames at day and Nightwither at night.
*Profaned Soul Crystal's safety damage net is now 75 damage instead of 0.1% of the target's max health.
*[[Pulse Dragon]]'s electrospheres now use local iframes and only last for 0.2 seconds. Now spawns 8 electrospheres per flail instead of 6. The flail itself now deals extra damage to inorganic targets. Increased electrosphere damage from 40% base damage to full damage.
*[[Sealed Singularity]] projectiles no longer use LocalNPCImmunity.
*Cosmic Scythes summoned from the [[Shadow Potion]] now inflict [[Shadowflame]] instead of [[God Slayer Inferno]].
*[[Spectralstorm Cannon]]'s lost soul projectiles now have a 30% projectile deviation. Lost soul projectiles now start on a randomized frame instead of the first frame.
*[[Spirit Glyph]], [[Hallowed Rune]], and heart/star spawns from [[Bloodflare armor]] can now trigger on bosses that have no contact damage.
*[[Sulphurous armor]]'s toxic bubble projectile now only spawns on double jumps.
*Nerfed [[Vanquisher Arrow]]s, decreased the overall amount of secondary arrows that spawn.
*[[Venerated Locket]] knife damage is now affected by Spy damage boosts. Nerfed knife's base damage from 200 to 150.
*[[Yanmei's Knife]] can now trigger its on-hit effects when damaging the following:
**[[Ancient Doom]], Ancient Light, [[Cosmic Mine]], Cultist Clone, [[Dark Heart]], [[Detonating Bubble]], Moon Leech Clot, [[Plaguebringer]], [[Plague Charger]], Probe, [[Ravager|Ravager's Right Leg]], [[Sepulcher|Sepulcher Heart]], [[Sharkron]], and [[Soul Seeker]].

=== Additional Changes ===
*Several sound effects have been added:
**Added new sound effects for the Leviathan.
**Added several new sound effects for [[Brimflame armor]], Omega Blue armor, and [[Plague Reaper armor]].
*[[Acidwood]] trees can now grow on [[Sulphurous Sandstone]] and [[Hardened Sulphurous Sandstone]].
*Anahita's Arpeggio projectile sounds now vary in pitch and velocity based on cursor position.
*Increased Auric Tesla Bar's sell price from {{gc|45}} to {{gc|60}}.
*Biome [[Shrine]]s have an increased chance to spawn on world generation.
*All [[Cryogen]] projectiles now have glowmasks to make them more visible.
*Reworded [[Deific Amulet]], [[Exodus Wings]] and [[Rampart of Deities]]' tooltips.
*[[Draedon's Charging Station]]s can now be found in [[Arsenal Lab]] chests.
*Draedon Furniture can no longer be destroyed by [[Lava]] or [[Explosive]]s.
*Decreased [[Draedon Power Cell]] use time from 14 to 9, doubled the [[Extractinator]] use rate, and they now have a sell price of {{cc|20}}.
*[[Draedon's Security Turret]]s now have a minimum pick requirement of 55% instead of 65%.
*Removed the [[Yoraiz0r's Spell]] effect from [[Fearmonger armor]].
*Reworded the tooltips of [[Grape Beer]] and [[Margarita]].
*Added Cameron and Woffle as names for the [[Guide]].
*The [[Holographic Display Box]] is now obtainable and now has a sell price of {{gc|1}}.
*Updated various Providence, the Profaned Goddess aurora visuals.
*Updated Star Cannon visuals to be akin to how the visuals look in [[tgc:1.4.0.1|Journey's End]].
*The [[Sea King]]'s help quotes are now spoken in a rotation.
*The Sea King now has a quote regarding the [[Arsenal Labs]], and moved the Acid Rain quote to post-Polterghast.
*Increased [[Shadowspec Bar]]'s sell price from {{gc|70}} to {{gc|85}}.
*Added custom jump effects to [[Statigel armor]] and Sulphurous armor.
*[[Vigorous Candle]]'s effect is now disabled while [[Iron Heart]] is active.
*[[Voidstone]] can no longer be blown up by explosives.
