The '''True Forbidden Oathblade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings and is the upgrade to the [[Forbidden Oathblade]]. When swung, it will fire 3 demonic scythes that collide with terrain and pierce up to two targets, each dealing 50% of the blade's damage. Both the sword and the fired projectiles inflict the [[On Fire!]] and [[Shadowflame]] [[debuffs]]. Critical hits additionally summon purple explosions on the target.
