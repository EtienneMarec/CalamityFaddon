The '''Reaver Mask''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] headpiece that acted as the magic headpiece for the [[Reaver armor]] set, granting a total of 40 defense. It was crafted from [[Essence of Sunlight|Essences of Sunlight]], [[Jungle Spores]], and [[Perennial Bar]]s. The helmet provided 10% increased movement speed and free movement in liquids, along with 7 defense, 15% increased magic damage, 12% reduced mana cost, 5% increased magic critical strike chance, and +80 max mana.
<br/>Its set bonus granted an additional 5% magic damage and caused enemies to release spore clouds when hit. Spore clouds dealt 15% damage of the attack that spawned them, capped at 25 damage.
