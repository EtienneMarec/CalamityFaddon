The '''Grand Dad''' was a Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that dropped from the Moon Lord in [[Malice Mode]]. Whenever the Grand Dad contacted a non-[[boss]] enemy, their defense was reduced to zero and their knockback resistance was set to -600%. It also had an exceptionally high knockback stat and size.

Its best [[modifier]] was '''Legendary'''.

== Notes ==
*Grand Dad dealt 75% of its original damage on [[Polterghast]] and [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]].
*The Grand Dad's knockback resistance removal could affect enemies that are immune to knockback.
*The defense and knockback resistance removal did not affect enemies with no contact damage or most event minibosses.

== Tips ==
*The knockback made this weapon a good tool to keep an enemy with high health away.

== Trivia ==
*Its name and tooltip, as well as the "7" on its sprite (and the 777 base damage and 77 knockback), were a reference to a meme known as "7 GRAND DAD," a Taiwanese copy of ''[[wikipedia:The Flintstones: The Rescue of Dino & Hoppy|The Flintstones: The Rescue of Dino & Hoppy]]'' for the Famicom system and popularized by Vargskelethor a.k.a Joel from Vinesauce, and SiIvagunner.
*The word "Yeet", which is present in the tooltip, is internet slang that means "to throw at high velocity".
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Grand Guardian]].
