The '''Bloody Rupture''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is also dropped by [[The Perforators]]. Upon killing an enemy with it, it will release orbs of blood that home in on other enemies, and upon impact, [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 2 life. It functions identically to the [[Leeching Dagger]].

Like every [[shortsword]], it has an incredibly short range, but unlike others, it swings automatically.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemy hits spawn bloodsplosions that deal 120% of the weapon's damage.
