The '''1.1.7.5''' update added two new bosses known as [[Brimstone Elemental]], and [[Bumblebirb]]. It also added several new [[potions]] and [[ammunition]] types, and made several balance changes to late game bosses.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Bumblebirb]], Bumblebirb (clone), and [[Scryllar]] (rage).
*Added [[Ark of the Cosmos]] and [[Asteroid Staff]].
*Added [[Abaddon]], [[Gehenna]], and [[Void of Extinction]].
*Added [[Arctic Arrow]], [[Enhanced Nano Round]], [[Mortar Round]], [[Napalm Arrow]], [[Rubber Mortar Round]], [[Superball Bullet]], and [[Terra Arrow]].
*Added [[Cadence Potion]], [[Calamitas' Brew]], [[Supreme Healing Potion]], [[Titan Scale Potion]], and [[Yharim's Stimulants]].
*Added [[Charred Idol]], [[Effulgent Feather]], and [[IOU an item]].
*Added [[Abyssal Flames]], [[Abyssal Weapon]], [[Cadence]], [[Titan Scale]], and [[Yharim's Power]].
*Added Boss Checklist support for Calamity bosses.
