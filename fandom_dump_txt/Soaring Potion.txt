The '''Soaring Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Soaring''' [[buff]], which increases wing flight time and horizontal flight speed by 10%. In addition, it restores a fraction of the player's wing time whenever an enemy is hit by a [[true melee]] weapon. The amount of flight time restored scales with the player's melee stats and weapon swing speed.
