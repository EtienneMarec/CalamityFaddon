The '''Mana Jelly''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Blue Jellyfish]] and [[Mirage Jelly]]. It increases the player's maximum [[mana]] by 20 and boosts mana regeneration by +4 while standing still. It can be combined with [[Life Jelly]] and [[Vital Jelly]] in order to craft the [[Grand Gelatin]].
