'''Goozmarie''' is a Godseeker Mode [[NPC]] that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:

*There is an empty house.
*Every [[Town Slimes|Town Slime]] exists in the world.


When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself by blasting them with a chromatic beam from her eye.

== Transformation ==
Goozmarie has the ability to transform into any enemy or boss. Bosses can be spawned at any phase, and at any difficulty level, regardless of the worlds difficulty. Enemies spawned by her do not drop items, and do not count towards [[Banners]] or the [[Tally Counter]]. Once the enemy is defeated or despawns, it will transform back into Goozmarie.

The player is also able to select buffs and debuffs that will be applied to them for as long as Goozmarie is transformed. If the [[Community modifications|Calamity: Community Remix]] mod is enabled, the player can also select [[Boss Rush|Curses]] to be applied as well. All modifiers will be removed after Goozmarie ends her transformation.

== Notes ==
*Bosses cannot be summoned, and enemies will not spawn, while Goozmarie is transformed.

== Trivia ==
*Goozmarie seems to be a humanoid version of Goozma.
**Furthermore, her unique titles are a reference to the generated titles Goozma uses in the Hunt of the Old God mod.
*Her quote after defeating astrum deus likely refers to the removed boss [[Astrageldon Slime]].
*It is implied that the town slimes are all citizens of Goozmarie's kingdom.
*She and the Collector are the only npcs that are 4 tiles tall.
**This means it is impossible for her to leave through regular [[Doors]], as they are only 3 tiles tall.
*One of her quotes references Statis, a prominent character in Calamity [[Lore]].
