The '''Burden Breaker''' was a [[Hardmode]] [[Legendary]] [[covering]] dropped from [[Skeletron Prime]] in [[Malice Mode]]. When equipped, once the player reached a horizontal speed of 25 mph, it accelerated the player by a factor of 2.5% per frame (roughly 4.4x speed per second), until either a solid block was hit or they achieved a speed of 2557 mph, at which point the player's speed was reset to zero.

== Notes ==
*Without any obstacles on their path or when using a flying mount, a player could move a horizontal distance of roughly 2600 feet with this covering before losing all speed.
*Players could find this covering useful in larger worlds due to the immense travel speed, which could get the player to the edge of the world within seconds.
*The item's effect is disabled during bossfights.
*If the player unequipped the item after getting up to speed, they would still be able to maintain their speed, although no longer gaining acceleration.
