The '''Slimy Wisdom Tooth''' is a craftable '''Epilogue''' [[Coverings|covering]]. It grants the following while equipped:
*+25% Spy damage
*+50 armor penetration
*Performing a [[Spy|Stealth Strike]] fires out 4 wisdom teeth that inherits 150% of the main weapon's damage. These teeth can inflict the '''Infectious Breaks''' debuff, making all DOT deal 2x more damage. The teeth also makes the enemy take 15% more damage, and heals the player 25% of its damage, capping at 120 health healed.
