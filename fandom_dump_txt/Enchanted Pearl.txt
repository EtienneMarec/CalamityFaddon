The '''Enchanted Pearl''' is craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It increases fishing skill by 10 and the chance to catch [[Crates]] by 10%.

When used in conjuction with the [[Supreme Bait Tackle Box Fishing Station]] and the [[Crate Potion]], it also increases the chance to catch [[Fishing#Rare Items|rare items]].
