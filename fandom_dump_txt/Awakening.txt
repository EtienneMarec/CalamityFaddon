The '''Awakening''' is an item obtained from the [[Starter Bag]]. It summons [[The Calamity]] when right clicked within the inventory. 

== Notes ==
*The Awakening is non-renewable, meaning that player's should take caution to not lose the item as The Calamity and the Unstable Calamity are required encounters to progress through the rest of the game.
