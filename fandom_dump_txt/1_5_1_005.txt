The '''1.5.1.005''' (or '''1.5.1.5''') update fixed a few remaining issues and oversights from previous Calamity updates, and removed random boss spawns from [[Death Mode]].

== Changes ==
*[[Cadance Potion]]s are no longer crafted using [[Calming Potion]]s.
*Removed Death Mode random boss spawns.
*Increased [[The Devourer of Gods]] aggression during its first phase in all world difficulties. 
*The [[Eidolon Staff]] can no longer drop from the [[Eidolon Wyrm]] unless [[Polterghast]] has been defeated.
*Increased [[Frost Staff]]'s use animation from 20 to 50 to match its use time value.
*Increased [[Ravager]]'s despawn range in [[Malice Mode]] by 150%.
*Seraphim's projectiles are now classified as [[Daggers]].
== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug that prevented augmented vanilla leg armor from giving the player their bonuses.
*Fixed an issue that caused the cooldown rack icon sizes to scale incorrectly on higher screen resolutions due to the UI scaling being applied twice.
*Fixed an issue that caused the mod to unload on startup due to cooldown rack shader not being compiled correctly for all devices.
*Fixed an issue that caused The Devourer of Gods to despawn while below 60% health.
*Fixed an issue that with the [[Broken Biome Blade]] weapon line tooltips that caused the game to crash when using a [[Community modifications#Translations|Translation mod]].
