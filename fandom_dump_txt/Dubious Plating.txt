'''Dubious Platings''' are [[crafting material]]s obtained by smelting [[Arsenic Ore]] found within the [[Dungeon]], as well as by killing various Draedon related enemies. They are used to craft various [[weapons]] and [[furniture]] alongside [[Mysterious Circuitry]].

Crafting all of the weapons that require Dubious Plating (in other words, Draedon's Arsenal weapons) costs 296 Platings; additionally, crafting every decoration that requires Dubious Plating requires an additional 31 Platings for a total of 327 Platings.
