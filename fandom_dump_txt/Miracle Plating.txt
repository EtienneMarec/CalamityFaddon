Miracle Platings are post-Moon Lord [[Crafting material|crafting materials]] that drop exclusively from [[XB-10 Losbaf]]. Their main use is to craft the Exo Armors. When dropped or in the inventory, the item will pulse and glow. The item will also emit bright yellow dust when dropped.
