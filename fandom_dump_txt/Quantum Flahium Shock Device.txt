The '''Quantum Flahium Shock Device''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. While equipped, It provides the effects of the [[Fungal Symbiote]], [[Warbanner of the Sun]] and the [[Yoyo Bag]], creating a flahium counterweight when any yoyo is thrown. The player also receives a 40% increase to melee speed and damage, a 35% increase to [[true melee]] damage, a 15% increase to melee critical strike chance, and +10 armor penetration. 

While the Quantum Flahium Shock Device is equipped, any enemy struck by a melee attack will be inflicted with [[Flahmes]].
