The '''Viral Sprout''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a flower-shaped spirit that floats around the player and attacks nearby enemies by firing gravity-affected needles. When moving or attacking, the spirits will move in a group with individual circular motions, and launch an even spread of three needles upwards. Needles inflict the '''Sage Poison''' [[debuff]] which deals a constant 45 damage per second, with higher damage per tick the more spirits are present.
