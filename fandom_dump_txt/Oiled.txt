'''Oiled''' is a vanilla [[debuff]] that increases the effectiveness of fire debuffs. It also affects flame debuffs within the Calamity Mod by increasing the damage per second of the following debuffs by 25%:
*[[Abyssal Flames]]: 16 health per second (total 78.5)
*[[Banishing Fire]]: 187.5 health per second (total 2187.5)
*[[Brimstone Flames]]: 5 health per second (total 25)
*[[Demon Flames]]: 312.5 health per second (total 1562.5)
*{{dei}} [[Embers of Duality]]: 9 health per second (total 44)
*[[God Slayer Inferno]]: 31.5 health per second (total 156.5)
*[[Holy Flames]]: 6.5 health per second (total 31.5)
