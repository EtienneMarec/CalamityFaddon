'''Brimflame armor''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set. It requires 17 [[Ashes of Calamity]], 9 [[Unholy Core]]s, and an [[Obsidian Rose]] to craft the entire set. The armor mainly grants bonuses to the mage class. If the full set is visible (whether equipped or in social slots), the player will have a tiny pulsating aura around them.

It consists of a '''Brimflame Cowl''', '''Brimflame Robes''', and '''Brimflame Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*41 defense
*30% increased magic damage
*25% increased magic critical strike chance
*5% increased movement speed
*+70 max mana
*10% decreased mana usage
*Grants the effect of the [[Obsidian Rose]]
*Immunity to [[On Fire!]], [[Brimstone Flames]], and [[Frostburn]]
*Pressing {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} will activate '''Brimflame Frenzy''' for 10 seconds, temporarily multiplying the player's magic projectile damage by 1.3x. However, the player’s life regen is reduced by 42 (causing heavy damage over time) and mana regeneration is disabled for the duration. Brimflame Frenzy has a 30 second cooldown after its usage.
