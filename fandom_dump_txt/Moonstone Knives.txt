The '''Mooonstone Knives''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] and are the counterpart to the [[Monstrous Knives]]. When used, the player throws 4-5 knives in quick succession. Each knife will restore 3 mana on hit. The knives shatter after a short distance.
