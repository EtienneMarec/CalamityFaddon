'''Flight Curse''' is a [[Debuffs|debuff]] inflicted on the player during the fight with some [[Bosses|bosses]] while in the [[Nightmare Mode]]. This debuff disables the whole functionality of any [[Wings|wings]] type, as well as disabling any other forms of flight, like these of [[tgc:Terraspark Boots|Terraspark Boots]] and so on.
