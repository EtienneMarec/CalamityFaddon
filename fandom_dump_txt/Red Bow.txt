The '''Red Bow''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is [[fishing|fished]] up in the [[Dungeon]] before [[Skeletron]] has been defeated. When equipped, the player's maximum [[Spy stealth]] is increased by 10, [[defense]] is increased by 3 and [[aggro]] is decreased by 200.

== Trivia ==
*The Red Bow is a reference to the [https://undertale.fandom.com/wiki/Faded_Ribbon?file=Faded_Ribbon_item.png Faded Ribbon] from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertale Undertale].
**The method of obtaining alludes to the Faded Ribbon being found in the Ruins.
**The defense and value stats are identical to its stats from the game.
