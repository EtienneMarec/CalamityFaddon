The '''1.5.0.003''' (or '''1.5.0.3''') update adjusted the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] boss fight, buffed several Pre-Hardmode weapons, and fixed several issues from the [[1.5.0.001]] and [[1.5.0.002]] versions.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Supreme Cataclysm Trophy]] and [[Supreme Catastrophe Trophy]]. <!-- 10% drop chance from their corresponding bro. - Merkalto -->

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*Teleportation effects are now only disabled while the player has the [[Boss Effects]] debuff instead of while any boss is alive.
*[[Brain of Cthulhu]] Creepers and [[Eater of Worlds]] segments now have a 50% damage resistance to the [[Demon Scythe]] projectiles.
*Brain of Cthulhu Creepers and Eater of Worlds segments can now drop [[Heart]]s when they are killed on [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Eater of Worlds segments now have a 40% damage resistance to the feathers from the [[Sky Glaze]] and [[Feather Crown]].
*[[The Hive Mind]] shaderain now inflicts [[Shadowflame]] for 1 second instead of [[Cursed Inferno]] for 1.5 seconds.
*Decreased [[Skeletron]]'s [[Reactive Damage Reduction]] timer from 2 minutes and 30 seconds to 2 minutes.
*[[The Slime God]]'s Abyss Ball projectiles now accelerate over time. Increased their duration from 4 seconds to {{dv|5|8|9|10|13}} seconds for the Corrupt variant and {{dv|4|6.5|7.33|8.17|10.67}} for the Crimson variant.
*Nerfed [[XF-09 Ares]] Gauss Spark damage from {{dv|300|451|472|484}} to {{dv|250|376|394|404}}.
*Decreased XF-09 Ares Tesla Sphere projectile velocity by 16.67% while it is firing Exo Overload Beams or when all three mechs are alive.
*Nerfed XF-09 Ares Exo Overload Beam damage from {{dv|500|752|788|808}} to {{dv|450|676|712|732}}, Exothermal Laser damage from {{dv|450|676|712|732}} to {{dv|400|600|632|648}}, Pulse Laser, Plasma Blast, and Tesla Sphere damages from {{dv|350|528|556|572}} to {{dv|320|480|504|532}}, and Exoplasma Bolt damage from {{dv|280|422|444|457}} to {{dv|256|384|403|425}}.
*Nerfed [[XM-05 Thanatos]] Exo Pulse Laser damage from {{dv|350|528|556|572}} to {{dv|320|480|504|532}}.
*[[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]] player prediction ability on Malice Mode has been decreased slightly.
*XS-01 Artemis and XS-03 Apollo now swap phases slightly less often on all difficulties.
*XS-01 Artemis and XS-03 Apollo fire their Pulse Laser and Plasma Blast projectiles less frequently.
*Decreased XS-01 Artemis Exo Pulse Laser projectile velocity by 25%.
*Nerfed XS-01 Artemis Exothermal Beam damage from {{dv|450|676|712|732}} to {{dv|400|600|632|648}} and Pulse Laser damage from {{dv|350|528|556|572}} to {{dv|320|480|504|532}}.
*Nerfed XS-03 Apollo Plasma Blast damage from {{dv|350|528|556|572}} to {{dv|320|480|504|532}}, Plasma Rocket damage from {{dv|450|676|712|732}} to {{dv|400|600|632|648}}, and Exoplasma Bolt damage from {{dv|280|422|444|457}} to {{dv|256|384|403|425}}.

=== Enemies ===
*[[Cnidrion]]s can now only spawn on the surface [[Desert]] biome, decreased how fast it can swim through tiles by 50% and its spawn rate by 60%.
*[[Lava Slime]]s can no longer spawn lava when killed on Expert Mode or higher.
*[[Mimic]]s can no longer drop the [[Compass]].
**This was due to an oversight.
*Decreased [[Wulfrum Drone]] velocity by 25%, and its fly away timer from 8 seconds to 6. Increased its tile detection range for the player from 57.5 tiles to 60.
*Decreased [[Wulfrum Gyrator]] movement speed by 25% and jump speed by 11.11%.
*Decreased [[Wulfrum Hovercraft]] velocity while searching and swooping by 20%. Increased its tile detection range for the player from 57.5 tiles to 60 and its fly away timer from 3 seconds to 6.
*Decreased [[Wulfrum Rover]] movement speed by 25%.
