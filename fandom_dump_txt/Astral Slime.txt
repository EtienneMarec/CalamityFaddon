The '''Astral Slime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. After defeating [[Astrum Aureus]], Astral Slimes will begin to drop the [[Abandoned Slime Staff]] and after defeating [[Astrum Deus]] they will start dropping [[Astral Ore]].

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions of the mod, the slime was able to fire lasers at players flying above it.
*The removed [[Boss]] [[Astrageldon Slime]] used to summon Astral Slimes during the fight.
