The '''Bloody Burrower''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns during the [[Ogswarm]] in a [[The Crimson|Crimson]] world. It slowly floats through the air toward the player while teleporting randomly, similar to the [[Brain of Cthulhu]]. While in the air, it will randomly fire a slow-moving laser at the player.
