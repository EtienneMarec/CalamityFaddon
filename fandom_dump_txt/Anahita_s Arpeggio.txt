The '''Anahita's Arpeggio''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] that is dropped by the [[Leviathan and Anahita]] and is the indirect upgrade to the [[Magical Harp]]. It fires treble clefs in random directions within about 30 degrees of the cursor. The clefs will ricochet off of blocks and pierce through all enemies while traveling, inflicting the [[Confused]] [[debuff]].
