'''Tree-Crown Wings''' are a Godseeker Mode [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allow the player to fly and glide, rarely dropped by [[Ceaseless Void]]. They have far superior wing stats to other wings of their tier, however they have a major drawback in the form of increasing all damage taken by 30%.

== Notes ==
*These wings can be considered similarly to the [[Dimensional Soul Artifact]], where they grant both a large upside and downside. Due to this they are recommended to be worn by experienced players who can take advantage of the vastly increased mobility and more successfully evade attacks, reducing the drawbacks.
*These wings are preferred for nohitting bosses due to the drawback losing its effect.

== Trivia ==
*The Tree-Crown Wings are a reference to the [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Master_Crown Master Crown]'s true form from the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirby_(series) Kirby series], which is also known as the tree crown without a ruler, or Mistilteinn.
