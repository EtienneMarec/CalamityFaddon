The '''1.3.4.102''' update added a new mechanic to the [[Adrenaline Meter]] known as Adrenaline Burnout, and resprited several [[Buffs]], items, and projectiles.

== Changes ==
=== Items ===
*Renamed the Profaned Crag [[Lore item]] from Profaned Crag to Brimstone Crag.
*Added the line "If you hit max adrenaline and don't use it within 5 seconds your adrenaline damage will drop gradually." to [[Revengeance]]'s tooltip.

=== Other ===
*Adrenaline now has a burnout mechanic that causes the player to gradually lose adrenaline damage if they do not activate it after 5 seconds.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Asgard's Valor]], [[Daedalus Emblem]], [[Enchanted Pearl]], [[Ethereal Talisman]], [[Eye of the Storm]], [[Heart of the Elements]], [[Psychotic Amulet]], [[Sigil of Calamitas]].
*Resprited [[Abyssal Weapon]], [[Afflicted]], [[Grax Boost]], [[Reaver Orb (buff)]], [[Silva Crystal (buff)]], [[Tyrant's Fury]].
*Resprited [[Reaver armor]]'s Reaver Orb and [[Silva armor]]'s Silva Crystal summons.
*Resprited [[Iron Heart]]'s heart icons.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a sheeting error on [[Aerospec Helm]].
*Fixed a bug with map drawing after Calamity's world expansion.
*Fixed a bug with large worlds being unable to load.
