The '''Venusian Trident''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Inferno Fork]]. It shoots out a fiery trident which erupts into a giant explosion with a radius slightly larger than [[Explosives]] upon impacting any tile or enemy. Each explosion emits four to nine cinders which fall to the floor and deal contact damage. The explosion lingers for about 2 seconds and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]. Each cinder deals 75% of the weapon's damage.
