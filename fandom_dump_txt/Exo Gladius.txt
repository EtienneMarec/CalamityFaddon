The '''Exo Gladius''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[shortsword]] that is auto-swung. It is the final upgrade to the Night's Saber. When swung, it stabs enemies omnidirectionally, causing a powerful explosion on hit. When right-clicked, the shortsword does an alternate parry fire, that does 4x the damage, knockback and crit chance of the standard attack and deflects projectiles, but has a longer re-use cooldown.

Its best modifier is '''Legendary'''.

== Tips ==
*This weapon is very useful for destroying Brimstone Hearts in the [[Supreme Calamitas]] fight due to its high base damage and effect. Combined with the fact parry can aim projectiles at enemies, it is especially useful during the 2nd [[Sepulcher]] phase, as it allows the player to accurately hit the Brimstone Hearts while not taking damage from projectiles hit at them.
**In addition, if the player hits the Sepulcher with the blade of the weapon, it is possible to intentionally stall the fight to regen health and build up [[Adrenaline]] due to the immunity frames it provides.
*The Tainted [[Enchantment]] is useful for boosting this weapon's true melee damage.
