The ''' 1.2.1.004''' update featured many balance changes and other adjustments to both [[bosses]] and items, including a rework of the [[Astrum Deus]] fight, significant changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]]'s fight, and further expansion of the [[Supreme Calamitas]] fight.

== Additions ==
*Added [[The Devourer of Thots]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
*Adjustments to boss sky shaders.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Overhauled the battle; no longer one long worm, instead the battle takes place against a swarm of nine smaller worms.
*Worm segment length reduced from 52 to 8. Nerfed health of each worm from {{dv|150,000|240,000|264,000}} to {{dv|18,500|29,600|32,000}}.
*Nerfed head damage from {{dv|120|300}} to {{dv|100|160}}, and body damage from {{dv|95|152}} to {{dv|85|136}}.
*Nerfed Astrum Deus' mine duration from 50 seconds to 20.
*A total of 5 [[Astrum Deus Probe]]s can be active at a time instead of 6.
*Body segments now take 66% damage and kill piercing when below 33% health instead of 50%.

==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*The main Bumblebirb can now only spawn up to {{dv|4|0|8}} clones instead of {{dv|10|0|30}}.
*Bumblebirb clones can now only spawn up to {{dv|3|0|5}} clones instead of {{dv|5|0|30}}.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Nerfed health from {{dv|14,000|22,400|25,600}} to {{dv|12,500|20,000|22,400}}.
*Buffed base Ice Bomb damage from {{dv|40|80|92}} to {{dv|44|88|100}}.
*Buffed base Ice Blast damage from {{dv|44|72}} to {{dv|52|80}}.
*Buffed base Ice Rain damage from {{dv|60|120}} to {{dv|64|128}}.
*Decreased speed.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*Nerfed Revengeance Mode health from {{revengeance|8320}} to {{revengeance|7200}}.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|610,000|976,000|1,000,000}} to {{dv|1,400,000|2,240,000|2,400,000}}.
*In Revengeance Mode, now summons [[The Devourer of Thots]] at 25% health instead of a second Devourer of Gods at 20% health. The player no longer needs to kill the newly-spawned Devourer first in order to progress through the battle. Horizontal lasers only spawn if no Devourers of Thots are active.
*Now fires beam portals from its tail at all times, instead of alternating between its head or tail depending on phase.
*Nerfed head damage in Expert Mode from {{expert|1400}} to {{expert|875}}.
*All body projectiles now deal {{dv|280|360}} damage instead of having individual damage values, and decreased their speeds.
*Body segments no longer take 0 damage from all sources. Instead, they take 15% of the damage.
*Body segments no longer kill projectile piercing.
*No longer shrinks to 62 segments in Revengeance Mode, remaining at 102 segments long in all modes.
*Can now spawn up to 8 [[Stasis Probe]]s instead of 3.
*Body segments now take 0 damage from any hit that would deal over 50% of its health.
*Body segments fire projectiles less often.
*Gravity bomb projectiles now inflict [[Distorted]] for 2 seconds instead of 5, produce dust on death, and have set custom transparency rather than dynamic time-based transparency.
*Nebula orb projectiles now last for 15 seconds instead of 30, and have set custom transparency rather than dynamic time-based transparency.
*Head and tail segments now use NPCHit4 as their hit sound instead of NPCHit13.
*Body and tail segments now inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]] for 1 second and 0.833 seconds respectively, instead of 2.5.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Buffed Revengeance Mode health multiplier.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ==== 
*Now spawns [[Detonating Flame]]s with slower vertical velocities.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Now despawns faster when the player leaves the [[Jungle]].

==== [[Profaned Guardians]] ====
*Nerfed the Main Profaned Guardian's overall velocity.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Nerfed health from {{dv|235,000|376,000|400,000}} to {{dv|215,000|344,000|368,000}}.
*Nerfed the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess#Summons|Offense Profaned Guardian]]'s overall velocity.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Nerfed health from {{dv|310,000|496,000|512,000}} to {{dv|260,000|416,000|448,000}}.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Nerfed health from {{dv|250,000|400,000|440,000}} to {{dv|230,000|368,000|400,000}}.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Added more [[status message]] quotes based on how many attempts it took to defeat her.
*While she is alive, the player's life regen is now cut by half (if it is positive).
*[[tgc:Mechanisms|Mechanisms]] are now deactivated during the battle.
*Bullet hell Brimstone Hellblasts no longer make a sound when killed.

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Mothron]]s after Yharon Phase 1 now drop 5-8 [[Darksun Fragment]]s instead of 3-5.
*Nerfed armored Stasis Probe's health from {{dv|12,000|16,800}} to {{dv|6000|8400}}, and its laser damage from {{dv|0|288}} to {{dv|0|240}}.
*Nerfed naked Stasis Probe's health from {{dv|15,000|21,000}} to {{dv|7500|10,500}}, and its laser damage from {{dv|0|288}} to {{dv|0|240}}.
*Nerfed both Stasis Probes' laser velocities by half.

=== Items ===
*The [[Cosmic Worm]] is no longer consumable, now stacks to 1 instead of 20, and the [[Cyborg]] no longer sells it after The Devourer of Gods has been defeated.
*The [[Dragon Egg (summoning)|Dragon Egg]] is no longer consumable, its recipe now requires 10 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]], 10 [[Cosmilite Bar]]s, and 15 [[Effulgent Feather]]s instead of 3 of each bar and 5 feathers, it now stacks to 1 instead of 20, and the [[Witch Doctor]] no longer sells it after Yharon has been defeated.
*The [[Eye of Extinction]] is no longer consumable, its recipe now requires 150 Cosmilite Bars, 300 [[Darksun Fragment]]s, 3000 [[Endothermic Energy|Endothermic Energies]], 3000 [[Nightmare Fuel]], and 150 [[Phantoplasm]], instead of 5 Cosmilite Bars, 1 Darksun Fragment, 3 Endothermic Energies, 3 Nightmare Fuel, and 5 Phantoplasm, and it now stacks to 1 instead of 20.
