'''Genesis''' is a non-consumable [[summoning item]] dropped by [[Vision of the Tyrant]] during the [[Boss Rush]] [[Events|event]], and by [[Birdest Up, Kulu-Yharon-Ku]]. It is used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]], after the [[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]] has been defeated, and a [[Subnautical Plate]] has been used to activate it.

==Trivia==
*Genesis is a counterpart of [[Terminus]] due to its similar usage effects and nearly identical sprite.
**The word "genesis" means the beginning, the source, or the root, contrary to the definition of "terminus".
*Genesis has a form of 'cheat detection'; it will not drop if the player has used qualifying mods- such as Cheat Sheet- during their playtime in the world.
**Furthermore, it is not possible to give it to oneself, as it will not display in give menus. 
***Obtaining the item despite anti-cheat flags being set will cause the player to explode violently with the message '(name) has failed the challenge at hand.' until removed from the inventory.
***This occurs the moment your invincibility frames wear off, much like [[Lul]] in prior versions of the mod.
*Genesis is a reference to the hit song by Justice. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKzWLUQizz8 [[File:Holy (element).png|Holy (element).png]]
