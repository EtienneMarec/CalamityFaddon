The '''Box Jellyfish''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Ocean]] and the 1st layer of the [[Abyss]]. It will remain passive until the player gets too close, it will then attempt to attack the player by swimming towards them.

== Trivia ==
*The Box Jellyfish acts similarly to [[wikipedia:Box jellyfish|its real-life counterpart]]; they both remain passive until they detect something nearby that may be edible.
