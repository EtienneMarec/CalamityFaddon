The '''Starbuster Core''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Stellar Culex]] upon intersecting with a [[Unicorn]], exploding into a crater of [[Astral Ore]] with the core in the middle (after [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated). While equipped the player gains +1 minion slots and all minion attacks spawn a small astral explosion on enemy hits with a one-second cooldown, dealing 40 summon damage.
