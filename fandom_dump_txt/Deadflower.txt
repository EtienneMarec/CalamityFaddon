The '''Deadflower''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] that is obtained by throwing [[Flower of Happiness]] into [[Sanguine Shimmer]], and is considered its side-grade. While equipped, it increases the player's damage by 5% and permanently grants the [[tgc:sunflower|Happy!]] [[Buffs|buff]]. Additionally, hitting an enemy will emit a blood particle, that homes back onto it. The blood projectiles deal 33% damage of the attack that summoned it, and do not pierce nor go through tiles.
