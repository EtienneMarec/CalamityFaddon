The '''Soul Slurper''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. It continuously faces the player while attempting to ram into them. It also fires Brimstone Darts at the player, which inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]].

Its stats receive a significant boost and starts dropping [[Bloodstone]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated.
