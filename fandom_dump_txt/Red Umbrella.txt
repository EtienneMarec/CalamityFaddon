The '''Red Umbrella''' is a [[spear]] dropped by [[Goozma]]. Upon hitting enemies, 5-7 blood droplets downwards, dealing 20% of the weapon's damage each. After defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]], the weapon gains the ability to spew out a large blast of 20-30 blood gloplets upon right clicking once the spear reaches its maximum range.
