The '''Soma Prime''' is a craftable Epilogue [[gun]]. When [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], it fires high-damage [[High Velocity Bullet]]s. The Soma Prime fires very rapidly and can inflict the '''Shred''' [[debuff]] on hit. This debuff deals 750 damage per second over its duration.

The Soma Prime has a 50% chance to not consume ammo.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon itself is a reference to the [http://warframe.fandom.com/wiki/Soma_Prime Soma Prime] from the video game ''[[wikipedia:Warframe|Warframe]]''. In ''Warframe'', the Soma Prime was a very popular assault rifle known for having a high rate of fire, very high critical hit chance, and consistently inflicting a bleed effect on enemies.
*In previous versions of the mod, the Soma Prime was renamed to "THIS WEAPON IS A REFERENCE TO WARFRAME OH MAH GAWD" and was also later renamed to "Gun". However, its name was changed back to "Soma Prime" later.
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity alternates between rarity colors of white ({{color|#FFFFFF ●|#FFFFFF}}) and greenish beige ({{color|#D1CC6F ●|#D1CC6F}}).
