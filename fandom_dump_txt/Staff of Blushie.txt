The '''Staff of Blushie''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[wand]]. Upon holding the {{key|use}} button with the weapon in hand, five blue gears will appear on the screen which are connected by a series of chains, and a row of blue fire appears some distance below. These effects are purely aesthetic, and will follow the player as they move. After 2 seconds, orange fire will start spawning everywhere around the player, causing an invisible aura roughly within the attack's radius to begin rapidly damaging [[enemies]] over a large area. The longer the attack is held, the more damage it deals. The weapon stops attacking after 60 seconds, regardless of whether the player is still holding the {{key|use}} button.

Its best modifier is '''Zealous''' due to its unique way of damaging enemies.

== Notes ==
*The amount of damage dealt is equal to the amount of frames the projectile has lasted for, minus 120, multiplied by 4.5, and rounded down.
**As a result, the maximum damage dealt per frame is 15,660, not accounting for critical strikes.
**Damage is further increased by twice the target's defense.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity appears as a blue ({{color|#0000FF ●|#0000FF}}) color.
*This weapon's tooltip refers to "blue magic", which is a reference to the Bluemagic mod, now known as [https://terrariamods.fandom.com/wiki/Elemental_Unleash Elemental Unleash], developed by Blushiemagic.
**This weapon functions similarly to the [https://terrariamods.fandom.com/wiki/Elemental_Unleash/Charm_of_Blushie Charm of Blushie] from the same mod.
* Similar to [[The Wand]], it humorously states a damage value of 1 despite being an extremely powerful weapon.
