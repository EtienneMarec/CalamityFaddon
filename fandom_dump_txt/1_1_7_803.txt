The '''1.1.7.803''' update changed some visuals in the [[Ravager]] fight to be more fitting with its sprite introduced in [[1.1.7.802]], and several other small changes.

== Changes ==
*The Elemental minions from [[Heart of the Elements]] now stay at set positions relative to the player instead of gathering near them.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s Phoenix Fires now produce less dust.
*Changed [[Ravager]]'s dusts and laser color filters.
*[[Scorpio]]'s rockets no longer have knockback.
