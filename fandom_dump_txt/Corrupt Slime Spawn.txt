'''Corrupt Slime Spawn''' is a [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] [[slime]] summoned during [[The Slime God]] battle, when the [[Ebonian Slime God]] takes damage. It is identical to the [[Slimer]] enemy, which chases the player while flying until it loses health and reverts to a normal slime form.
