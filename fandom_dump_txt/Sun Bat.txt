The '''Sun Bat''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] found in the [[Underground]] and [[Cavern]] layers. It flies around with the typical [[tgc:AI|bat AI]], but it inflicts the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]] on contact, making it dangerous to get hit by. It always emits a bright yellow glow, however, making it easy to spot.

It can drop [[Essence of Sunlight]] when killed, being one of the few non-[[Space]] enemies to do so.
