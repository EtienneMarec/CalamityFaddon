The '''Heavy Laser Rifle''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[ranged weapon]]. When used, two small orange beams are fired that each create a small dust explosion when contacting a block or enemy. The beam is incredibly accurate.

The weapon consumes 0.125 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 190.
