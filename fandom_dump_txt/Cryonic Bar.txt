'''Cryonic Bars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bars]] that are made with 5 [[Cryonic Ore]] at a [[Hardmode Forge]] and are additionally dropped from the [[Fleshy Geode|Fleshy]] and [[Necromantic Geode]]. They are mainly used in the crafting of crystal-themed items, as well as being used to craft [[Life Alloy]]s.
