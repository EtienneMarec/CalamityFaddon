The '''Midas' Handshake''' is a Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that can be purchased from the [[Snowman]]. It functions similarly to the [[Coin Gun]], as it uses [[Coins]] for ammo, with its damage dependant on the coin used. It rapidly fires coins with a somewhat large spread. The weapon has a 95% chance not to consume ammo, and defeated enemies will drop extra Coins upon death.

Shooting enemies with the Midas' Handshake will cause a purely visual burst of coins around them from each shot.

The Midas' Handshake cannot receive Modifiers

== Tips ==
*It may be wise to turn Platinum Coins into Gold Coins, as sustained damage from 100 gold coins will deal more total damage then a single Platinum Coin.

== Trivia ==
*The item is a reference to the Chicago Typewriter from the Resident Evil series.
**Both weapons are extremely powerful guns that can be purchased for obscene amounts of money.
**Both weapons are also based off of the same real world gun, the Thompson submachine gun, commonly known as the Tommy Gun.
*This weapon has a unique rarity, but is referred to here as Hot Pink for consistency between items.
**Its unique rarity alternates between gold ({{color|#ccb548 ●|#ccb548}}), and silver ({{color|#c2d5d7 ●|#c2d5d7}}).
**It is one of very few items with the Hot Pink rarity that is not crafted using [[Shadowspec Bar|Shadowspec Bars]]
