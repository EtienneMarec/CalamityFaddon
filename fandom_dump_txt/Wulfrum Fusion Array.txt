The '''Wulfrum Fusion Array''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] dropped by [[The Inventors]] and the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. When used, it shoots a quick burst of fusion bolts that can hit multiple targets. Enemies hit causes minions to focus on the last-struck enemy and increases their damage against the target, similar to [[tgc:Whips|Whips]]. All fusion bolts fired have a default armor penetration of 6.
