'''Yharon Soul Fragments''' are a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] after defeating his second phase. They are used to make highly powerful endgame equipment, including [[Auric Bar]]s.

Crafting all items (excluding any items that solely require Auric Bars in later tier recipes) requires 29 Yharon Soul Fragments (requiring Yharon to be defeated 2 times on all difficulties).
