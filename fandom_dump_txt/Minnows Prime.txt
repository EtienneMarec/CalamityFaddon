The '''Minnows Prime''' is a [[critter]]. It is found in the [[Dungeon]] after defeating [[Moon Lord]] which passively swims back and forth, gaining a speed boost when hitting a wall. It attempts to swim away from the player and will drop a sizeable amount of [[Phantoplasm]] and [[Ectoplasm]] when killed. It can also be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].
