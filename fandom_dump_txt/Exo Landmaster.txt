The '''Exo Landmaster (Japanese: エクソランドマスター Ekusorandomasutā)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. It attacks by sliding towards the player in order to deal contact damage. It will also occasionally stand still and fire 3 homing Exo Rockets, before continuing its pursuit.

It can be supercharged by the [[Exo Obelisk]] enemy, causing its lights to turn bright yellow, and gaining a permanent energy shield which increases its defense, as well as an extra 2 rockets to its barrage.
== Trivia ==
*The name Landmaster is essentially a direct copy of the vehicles of the same name from the Starfox series.
**Both landmasters are treaded vehicles that launch projectiles.
*The sprite bears a resemblance to real world battlebots.
