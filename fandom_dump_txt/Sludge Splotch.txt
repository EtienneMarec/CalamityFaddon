The '''Sludge Splotch''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] consumable [[rogue weapon]]. It throws a gravity-affected sludge ball that inflicts the [[Slow]] debuff on enemies. The sludge ball dissipates upon hitting a tile or striking an enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with a Sludge Splotch causes the resulting ball to split into several smaller sludge blobs on impact. Just like the main ball, these dissipate upon hitting a tile or striking an enemy.
