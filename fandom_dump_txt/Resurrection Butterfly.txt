The '''Resurrection Butterfly''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapon|summon weapon]]. Upon use with two or more minion slots, the Resurrection Butterfly summons a purple and pink butterfly. The purple butterfly will ram into enemies, while the pink butterfly will fire three petals towards enemies that only deal 50% of the summon's base damage. Although the staff summons two minions per use, each cast only uses one minion slot.

== Notes ==
*One pair of butterflies counts as one minion toward the maximum minion count, similar to the [[Optic Staff]].
*The two butterflies do 135% damage to [[Duke Fishron]], and only 75% damage to the [[Lunatic Cultist]] and [[Astrum Deus]].

== Trivia ==
*The name of the weapon, "Resurrection Butterfly", and the weapon's tooltip are references to a spell-card used by the character [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Yuyuko_Saigyouji Saigyouji Yuyuko] from the ''[[wikipedia:Touhou Project|Touhou Project]]'' series.
**The buff icon is a reference to the [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Saigyou_Ayakashi Saigyou Ayakashi], a giant cherry blossom tree, while the tooltip references [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Reimu_Hakurei Hakurei Reimu] from the same series.
**The texture of the weapon in question is similar to the fan that Yuyuko herself is often depicted with.
**Additionally, the recipe requiring 2003 golden butterflies references the initial full release year of [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Perfect_Cherry_Blossom Perfect Cherry Blossom], the game that Yuyuko came from.
