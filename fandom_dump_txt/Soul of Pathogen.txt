The '''Soul of Pathogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Pathogen]].

When equipped, a stream of blood will be siphoned from the nearest player or [[Town NPC]], regenerating the host's health until that character dies.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.
