The '''Aftershock''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Earth Elemental]]s. Upon hitting an enemy, it spawns a boulder that falls from the sky and pierces five enemies.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Tips ==
*The Aftershock is very useful for rapidly annihilating worm-like enemies, since the falling rocks can hit several segments.

== Trivia ==
*Unlike most on-hit effects, the Aftershock's effect can be triggered by [[Target Dummy|Target Dummies]].
**This will not work during boss fights.
