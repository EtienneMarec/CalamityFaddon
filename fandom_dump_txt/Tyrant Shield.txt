The '''Tyrant Shield''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[classless weapon]]. When used it holds out a corpse of [[Yharim]] in front of the player. If an enemy is moving horizontally in the direction of the corpse and intersects with it, the enemy will take a 5980 classless damage scaling with the player's damage stats. If a projectile is moving horizontally towards the corpse and intersects, it will be reflected and the projectile will become friendly and deal 5980 damage to any enemies it hits. When either event happens, the corpse gets put on cooldown and can't be held out for 1 minute.
