'''"You've gone mad in your attempts to satiate your hunger. You now challenge me, with want for more bloodshed." -Seeker'''

Seeker is an optional post-game epilogue boss that is fought at the end of the true boss rush.

== Fight ==
Seeker has no known form as it is too powerful to have a singular form. Seeker is spawned after beating the true boss rush which requires beating all bosses in the base game and epilogue as listed on the wiki. All of its attacks instantly kill the player.

==== Phase 2 ====
After this it will fight itself, in which it creates divine lightning to strike the player which will spawn on the players position every second. While doing this it will summon holy fire around the player in a bullet hell scenario.

==== Phase 3 ====
After getting down to 90% it will attack using three sentries around the player that shoot divine bolts at the player. It will also clone itself and have it and its clone dash at the player. During this phase Seeker is invincible and can't be damaged until all three sentries are killed. Each sentry has 10,000,000 HP. Once this phase is finished it will start cycling between 5 attacks. The first creates lasers all around the player in a random area. The second attack has it duplicate itself 5 times and start lunging at the player. The third has it create a device at the bottom that has 15,000,000 HP and will keep on attacking until killed. It will shoot divine bolts to shoot above at the player. Seeker will continue to attack while this is around. The fourth attack has it create divine bolts constantly at the player. The fifth attack has it create a shadow clone of a random boss that goes around for a minute and can't be killed. Seeker will use its other attacks while this happens. 

==== Phase 4 ====
Once it gets to 50% HP then it creates 2 permanent clones of itself that can't be damaged. Their will now be a device on the bottom and top of the screen shooting at the player that can't be damaged. There will also always be a shadow boss attacking the player and when one goes another spawns. All three Seekers will use different attacks at the same time. Once defeated the player is given the option to become a god. If they decline, then nothing changes. If they accept then they can now fly without wings spawn anything and give themselves any buffs and have them stack.

==== After Boss ====
After beating seeker the player enters the post-epilogue or post-post-post base game or post-post-post-post pre-hardmode.
