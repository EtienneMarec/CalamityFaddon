The '''Braxaxe''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Pickaxe]], [[Axe]], and [[Hammer]] combination. When used with the left mouse button, it will quickly mines tiles and [[trees]]. And when used with the right mouse button, it will quickly mine trees and [[walls]]. It has a range twenty tiles larger than most other tools.

Mining [[Primordial Ore]] with the tool will drop the ore in its direct item form.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Godly''' or '''Demonic'''. Both modifiers increase its average damage output by the same amount.

{{tota}} With the Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic addon Enabled, its pickaxe power gets changed to 300% making it capable of mining [[Esseric Ore]]
