'''Gem Tech armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set. It requires 38 [[Exo Prism]]s, 12 [[Galactica Singularity|Galactica Singularities]], and 9 [[Core of Calamity|Cores of Calamity]] to craft the entire set. The armor grants bonuses to all classes with its special set bonus.

It consists of a '''Gem Tech Headgear''', '''Gem Tech Body Armor''', and '''Gem Tech Schynbaulds'''.

The full set, excluding the set bonus, only grants 69 defense and no other stats.

== Set Bonus ==
Most of the stats provided by the Gem Tech armor are from the set bonus effect. When the player equips the full set, six Trireme's Gems spawn and orbit around the player.
*The {{item|Yellow Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Yellow Gem|#EDAA2B}} increases melee damage by 45%, melee speed by 26%, and melee critical strike chance by 12%. Upon hitting an enemy with melee damage, seven gem shards are released outwards from the target, which each deals 825 melee damage. The on-hit effect has a cooldown of 5.5 seconds, but holding a true melee weapon causes the cooldown to pass twice as quickly.
*The {{item|Green Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Green Gem|#25BC6C}} increases ranged damage by 50% and ranged critical strike chance by 16%. Upon hitting an enemy with ranged damage, {{item|Gem Tech Flechette|s|link=}} spawn from the player which aim towards enemies. The flechette deals 32% of the original projectile's damage with a softcap of 400. Flechettes will not spawn if more than eight flechettes are present.
*The {{item|Violet Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Violet Gem|#C83AD1}} increases magic damage by 50%, magic critical strike chance by 16%, and maximum mana by 100. Holding any item that is not a magic weapon grants the player increased mana regeneration by 8.
*The {{item|Blue Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Blue Gem|#2577CE}} increases summon damage by 72% and minion slots by 4. It also reduces the [[Vanilla changes#Minions|summon damage penalty]] from -50% damage to -25% damage.
*The {{item|Red Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Red Gem|#E01800}} increases Spy damage by 50%, Spy critical strike chance by 16%, and maximum [[Spy stealth]] by 130. Using any item that is not a Spy weapon will not consume stealth.
*The {{item|Pink Gem|link=|mode=image}} {{color|Pink Gem|#FF73CE}} increases [[defense]] by 75, [[damage reduction]] by 12%, life regeneration by 2, movement speed by 40%, and jump speed by 8%.

When all gems are present, any hit increases life regeneration by 2 for 8 seconds. If the player switches to another class while this buff is active, the player's life regeneration is further increased by 1 for 2.5 seconds.

If the player is hit for over 100 damage, a gem launches upward and homes into nearby targets. The gems that are launched deal 40,000 damage with the class type based on the type of the gem (Pink taking the average of all damage boosts). The type of gem lost is based on the previous class item that the player held. If the player has not held a weapon with any of the five classes or held a weapon of the class with the lost gem, the base pink gem is lost instead. Gems do not grant boosts while they are lost, and respawn 30 seconds after disappearing.
