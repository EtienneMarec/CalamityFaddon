The '''Rubico Prime''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Pestilent Defiler]]. When used, a high-velocity round is fired every half-second for a total of 10 times. The bullets pierce all tiles and enemies, dealing 25% more damage during a critical strike.
