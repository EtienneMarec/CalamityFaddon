'''Ranged weapons''' are [[weapons]] which deal ranged damage, usually on contact with a projectile shot by the weapon, and consume [[ammunition]] when used. They come in the form of [[bows]], [[guns]], [[launchers|rocket launchers]], [[dart guns]] and [[flamethrowers]], which use [[arrows]], [[bullets]], [[rocket]]s, darts and [[Gel]] respectively, among other unclassified weapons which use specialized ammo or do not require ammo.
