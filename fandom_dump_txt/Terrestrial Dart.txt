'''Terrestrial Darts''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[darts]]. Upon hitting an enemy, the dart wil stuck in it, dealing 10% of its base damage every half of a second. Additionally, every 3rd hit, a life energy projectile is released, dealing 30% of the dart's base damage. After being stuck in an enemy for 3 seconds, the dart will disappear.
