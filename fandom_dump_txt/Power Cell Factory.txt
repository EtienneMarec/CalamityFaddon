'''Power Cell Factory''' is a unique piece of [[furniture]] found in [[Arsenal Lab]]s. They are naturally found inactive, but can be broken and replaced by the player to make them functional. Every 15 seconds the Power Cell Factory generates a [[Draedon Power Cell]], which can be retrieved by right-clicking the factory. These Power Cells can be used to [[charge]] certain [[weapons]]. Each Fuel Factory can hold up to 9999 Power Cells, which is equivalent to roughly 41.66 real time hours of generation.

{{Fantag|Calamity Community Remix}}The Power Cell Factory can also be supercharged with certain materials, speeding up Power Cell production. A supercharged Power Cell Factory cannot be supercharged for 90 seconds after its supercharge expires. A list of superchargeable materials is shown below.
