'''Incandescent Nox''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[javelin]] that is dropped by [[Aetherial Guardian, Olistene]]. When used, it automatically throws a javelin that inflicts [[Vaporfied]] on enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Abyssal Incandescence will cause the next javelin thrown to release lasting embers upon contact with an enemy or a block that will inflict [[Holy Flames]].
