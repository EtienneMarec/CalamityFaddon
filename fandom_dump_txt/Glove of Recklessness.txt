The '''Glove of Recklessness''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]] purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|60}} after [[Plantera]] has been defeated. When equipped all Spy weapons thrown by the player have 12% increased usetime but do 10% less damage. Spy weapons thrown also have a large deviation based on how much velocity the weapon thrown has; the faster the initial velocity, the higher the deviation is.

== Notes ==
*This covering is the direct counterpart to the [[Glove of Precision]].

== Tips ==
*This covering synergizes very poorly with [[stealth]] due to the fact that stealth strikes do not benefit from the decrease in usetime, effectively causing stealth strikes to deal 10% less damage.
*Using this covering with homing weapons can help negate the inaccuracy penalty.
