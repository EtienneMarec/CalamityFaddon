The '''Apocalyptic Slime Statue''' is a Hardmode [[furniture]] item. It is obtained by gathering the [[Famine Slime]], [[Pestilent Slime]], [[War Slime]], and [[Death Slime]] within a 15 block radius of eachother, they will then begin to pull each other in and implode, spawning the Apocalyptic Slime Statue where they imploded on top of [[Platforms|Statigel Platforms]]. When right-clicked while holding a [[Gooz Sayer's Coin]] during the night, it is used to summon the [[Horseslimes of the Goopocalypse]]. For the Horseslimes to be summoned, a specific structure must be built around the Apocalyptic Slime Statue made up of four [[Slime Statue]]s:

*The first [[Slime Statue]] must be entirely [[tgc:Paints|painted]] Deep Green and be placed on top of four [[Platforms|Plagued Shelves]] with a [[tgc:Torches|Jungle Torch]] on each side of it.
*The second [[Slime Statue]] must be entirely [[tgc:Paints|painted]] Deep Red and be placed on top of four [[Platforms|Ashen Platform]] with a [[tgc:Torches|Demon Torch]] on each side of it.
*The third [[Slime Statue]] must be entirely [[tgc:Paints|painted]] Deep Yellow and be placed on top of four [[Platforms|Acidwood Platforms]] with a [[tgc:Torches|Coral Torch]] on each side of it.
*The final [[Slime Statue]] must be entirely [[tgc:Paints|painted]] Deep Blue and be placed on top of four [[tgc:Platforms|Brass Shelves]] or [[tgc:Platforms|Metal Shelves]] with a [[tgc:Torches|Bone Torch]] on each side of it.


[[File:Apocalyptic Slime Statue Complete.png|400px]]
<small>A functional Apocalyptic Slime Statue.</small>

Each statue must be within a 30 tile radius of the Apocalyptic Slime Statue. Upon successfully summoning the boss, four colored stars will spiral out of the Apocalyptic Slime Statue towards their respective slime statues and the [[Horseslimes of the Goopocalypse]] will be spawned shortly after, with each Horseslime spawning at their respective statue and the Goopocalypse Core spawning at the Apocalyptic Slime Statue itself.

== Notes ==
*The Apocalyptic Slime Statue cannot be destroyed by [[explosive]]s.
