The '''Arcturus Astroidean''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[bait]] obtained randomly from [[Astral Crate]]s that has a base bait power of 40%. While in the [[Astral Infection]], [[Sulphurous Sea]], or [[Abyss]] biomes, the player's [[Fishing Power]] is increased by 10%.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Arcturus|Arcturus]] is the name of the brightest star in the [[wikipedia:Boötes|Boötes]] constellation. "Astroidean" refers to [[wikipedia:Asteroidea|Asteroidea]], the biological class that starfish belong to.
