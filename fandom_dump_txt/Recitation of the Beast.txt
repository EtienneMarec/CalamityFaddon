The '''Recitation of the Beast''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Demon Scythe]]. It causes a ring of six homing Beast Scythes to appear around the player that then fly off in the direction of the player's cursor. Beast Scythes inflict several [[debuffs]]:
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
*[[Shadowflame]] (33.33% chance for 1.5 seconds)
*[[Brimstone Flames]] (33.33% chance for 2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (33.33% chance for 2.5 seconds)
**Exactly one of Shadowflame, Brimstone Flames or Frostburn will be applied each hit.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon is a reference to the spell card ''Great Magic "Devil's Recitation"'', used by the character [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Byakuren_Hijiri Byakuren Hijiri] from the ''[[wikipedia:Touhou Project|Touhou Project]]'' series. In turn, the spell card itself is a reference to the Demon God [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Shinki Shinki] from a much earlier game in the series, as it resembles the final bullet pattern from her boss fight.
**The tooltip of the weapon reflects this, as Byakuren was sealed in [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Makai Makai] for a thousand years.
