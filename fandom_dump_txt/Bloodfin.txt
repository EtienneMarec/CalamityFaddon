'''Bloodfin''' is a Godseeker Mode [[food item]] that is [[fishing|fished]] up in the [[Brimstone Crag]] once [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated, at a 10% chance. It heals 240 health and grants the player the '''Bloodfin Boost''' [[buff]] on consumption which boosts the player's life regen by 5, and an additional 5 while the player is suffering from a damage over time [[debuff]]. Once consumed, the player receives [[Potion Sickness]] for 60 seconds. If the player is below 75% life, they receive an additional 4 life regen.

== Notes ==
*Bloodfins can be considered an indirect upgrade to [[Honeyfin]]s, given similar names and obtaining methods.
*Bloodfins can be considered the alternative to [[Supreme Healing Potion]]s, as both are obtainable around the same time, and heal the player for a similar amount. However, the Bloodfin is arguably superior due to its life regen boosts, especially at low life, with only slightly less immediate healing.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Angiogenesis|Angiogenesis]] in its tooltip refers to the process by which blood vessels are formed.
