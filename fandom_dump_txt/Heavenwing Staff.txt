The '''Heavenwing Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. Upon use, five non-piercing holy flaming bats are summoned near the cursor, which are fired in a semi-random, generally downward, direction. These bats can travel through tiles and inflict enemies with the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]] on hit.
