The '''Primrose Keepsake''' is a craftable [[pet]] summoning item combining [[Bear's Eye]] and the [[Romajeda Orchid]], allowing both pets to be summoned at once. It summons a guardian angel cat named Bear, and a dog named Kendra to follow the player. If the player is moving too fast, Bear will use blue angel wings to fly towards the player, and Kendra will use black dragon wings to fly towards them.
