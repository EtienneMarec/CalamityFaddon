The '''Celestial Jewel''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]].

It boosts [[defense]] by 8 and life regeneration by 2, even while the player is under the effects of a damaging debuff. When worn while being damaged by a debuff, the player's defense will increase by an additional 11 (for a total of 19) and life regen will increase by an additional 2 (for a total of 4). Pressing the {{key|Astral Teleport}} key while no bosses are alive will teleport the player to a random location in the same manner as a [[Teleportation Potion]], and has no cooldown.

== Notes ==
*If the player is under the effect of one of the alcohols sold by the [[Drunk Princess]], the covering's life regen bonuses will be reduced to 1 regardless of other debuffs.
*Pairing this item with the [[Spelunker's Amulet]] or [[Spelunker Potion]]s can help the player find rare ores.

== Trivia ==
*This covering can be considered a successor to the [[Crown Jewel]], due to their shared unique debuff regen and defense function. However, Crown Jewel can only be obtained using [[Malice Mode]], whereas Celestial Jewel is available in all modes.
**In previous updates, Celestial Jewel was a direct upgrade to the Crown Jewel, and both were exclusive to [[Revengeance Mode]].
