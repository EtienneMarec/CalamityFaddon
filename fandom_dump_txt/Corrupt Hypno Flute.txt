The '''Corrupt Hypno Flute''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Magic weapons|Magic weapon]] dropped by [[Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch]]. It fires red music notes that ricochets and pierce through enemies infinitely until 5 seconds has passed. 

When the notes hits an enemy, it inflicts a '''Corrupt Hypnotization''' debuff, causing inflicted enemies to lose 60 health per second. 
Enemies killed with this debuff turns into a hypnotized ghost, keeping the same AI, but deals 75% of the weapon's base damage, have more sluggish movement speed, and trades taking damage for a 80 second timer that instantly kills them after said timer expires.

==Notes==
*Bosses, Minibosses, and all Godseeker mode enemies are immune to this effect.
