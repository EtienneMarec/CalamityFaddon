The '''Pulse Dragon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flail]]. When used, the player swings two glowing electric dragon heads which moves in a whip-like manner. As they converge, up to eight short-lasting electrosphere projectiles are spawned per each flail. The whip then returns after converging or upon tile contact.

The weapon consumes 0.32 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 190.

Using it in the desert during a sandstorm summons [[Yharon]]
