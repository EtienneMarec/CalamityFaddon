The '''Conclave Crossfire''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] and an upgrade to the [[Tactical Shotgun]]. It fired two shotgun blasts; one was fired from the gun itself while the other rained from the sky towards the cursor. Shots raining from the sky would not pass through tiles.

*This weapon was a reference to the [http://borderlands.wikia.com/wiki/Conference_Call Conference Call] gun from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]'', and was referred to by that name in the mod's source files. The weapon directly named [[Conference Call]] is referred to as "TrueConferenceCall" internally.
*This item used to be known as "Apocalyptic Expulsor", and required [[Hyperius Bullet]]s to fire.
