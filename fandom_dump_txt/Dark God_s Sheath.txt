The '''Dark God’s Sheath''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]]. When equipped:
*The player’s [[Spy stealth]] cap is increased by 20.
*The player receives 20% increased critical strike chance in the space layer of the world.
*All [[stealth strike]]s launch dark meteor blasts at the cursor’s location.
*The player's Spy projectile velocity is doubled.
