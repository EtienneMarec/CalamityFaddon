== Notes ==
*Due to only requiring early-[[Hardmode]] materials to craft, this item can be used to fight Plantera earlier than normal. This is intentional, to give players more freedom as to how they progress through their playthrough.

== Trivia ==
*Its name is a portmanteau of the words "portable" and "bulb", which are also the words that made up the item's previous name.
*Internally, this item is referred to as "Bulb of Doom".
