== Notes ==
*Unlike most critters, the Piggy has a much higher health stat and drops a hefty amount of gold coins.
*If the player is lucky, they can encounter and catch a Piggy very early on in their playthrough, which will help immensely due to the large amount of money it sells for.
*Despite having the same sell value as a [[tgc:Critters#Gold critters|Gold Critter]] ({{gc|10}}), the Piggy cannot be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].

== Trivia ==
*Piggy is a reference to [https://www.youtube.com/user/xBaumProductions BaumProductions] and his ''Terraria Necro Mod'' series on YouTube.
*In prior versions of the mod, the Piggy used to drop a much larger amount of money (around {{gc|10-20}}).
