'''Terminus''' is a non-consumable [[summoning item]] that is used to open a menu that has 2 options: one to start the [[Boss Rush]] [[Events|event]], and one to summon the [[Vision of the Tyrant]] once the vision has been encountered once. It can only be obtained by defeating [[La Ruga]]. Using the Terminus while the Boss Rush is active will not display the menu and cancel the Boss Rush. After defeating [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] and [[Exodygen]], a third option becomes available to summon [[The Fallen Ascended Master, Xeroc]].

== Notes ==
*Using Terminus while a boss is alive will instantly despawn the boss and trigger the Boss Rush.
*Using Terminus to deactivate the Boss Rush will not display a [[status message]].
*Vision of the Tyrant will ''not'' drop any loot if summoned with the item's right click.

== Trivia ==
*The word "terminus" means the end, the limit, or the final goal.
*In the [[Get fixed boi]] seed, the item will instead look like an [[Ogscule]].
*In previous versions of the mod, the item could alternatively be obtained as a drop from [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s second phase.
*This item can be obtained earlier than intended with a combination of the [[Abyssal Diving Gear]] and the [[Aquatic Emblem]]. While the Boss Rush can be triggered at any time, the player will find themselves at a very great disadvantage if they use it before defeating [[Godseeker, Yharim]].
