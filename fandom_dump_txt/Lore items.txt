'''Lore Items''' are items with a piece of lore written into them, and are dropped by their respective [[boss]] on their first kill. They are also able to be crafted using [[Victory Shard]]s and the boss's respective [[trophy]]. The descriptions of these items are insights and commentary on both locations the player has been to and bosses the player has slain. They are implied to be written from the point of view of the Jungle Tyrant, Yharim.

==Trivia== 

*The lore items used to give a variety of effects, for example:
**Crabulon's lore item granted the player a single [[Delicious Meat]] every day.
** The Hive Mind's and The Perforators' lore items prevented [[Hive Tumor|Hive Tumors]] and [[Perforator Cyst|Perforator Cysts]] respectively from spawning.
** Cryogen's lore item gave the player the [[Frozen]] debuff permanently.
* Strangely, [[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]] does not have a lore item.
**{{fantag|Calamity: Communiy Remix}}This is likely because its highly praised fight dialogue is more than enough to reveal several lore elements, and therefore has no need for a lore item.
