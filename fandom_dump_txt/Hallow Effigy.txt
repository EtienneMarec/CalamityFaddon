The '''Hallow Effigy''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[effigy]] found in the Hallow [[shrine]]. When placed down, all players nearby will receive a 25% damage, critical strike chance, and movement speed boost, a 20% health boost, 8% [[damage reduction]], and 20 [[defense]], but will permanently be inflicted with [[Chaos State]] as long as they are within the effigy's range.

== Notes ==
*The Hallow Effigy is an indirect upgrade to the [[Corruption Effigy|Corruption]] and [[Crimson Effigy|Crimson Effigies]], combining both of their effects and increasing them, but with a new added downside.
*The range of the Hallow Effigy is approximately 203 units east and west, while 154 units up and down. This makes a total area on 406 X 308 units (203 X 154 blocks).
