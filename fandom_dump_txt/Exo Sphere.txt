The '''Exo Sphere (Japanese: エクソスフィア Ekusosufia)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. it attacks by rolling quickly and jumping towards the player, causing a small explosion upon landing.

It can be supercharged by the [[Exo Obelisk]] enemy, causing its lights to turn bright yellow. This will cause it to roll much faster, on top of gaining a permanent energy shield which increases its defense.
