The '''Deadlock''' is a [[Debuffs|debuff]] that makes the target completely unable to move and attack, by simply changing the target's AI to "No AI". All [[Enemies|enemies]] and [[Bosses|bosses]], but [[Crazed Mechanic]] are susceptible to this debuff. This debuff is inflicted by [[Dead Star's Laser Rifle]], [[Void Man]]'s attacks and the [[Crazed Mechanic]].

This debuff has its effects even while the [[Boss Rush]] is active.

== Trivia ==
*This is the only debuff that has a "duration cap" - it's maximum duration is capped at 1.5 seconds in any difficulty, and is capped at 1 second in the [[Nightmare Mode]].
*When the debuff's duration is over the target's AI changes back to the AI it had prior to the debuff was inflicted.
*If the debuff is inflicted on player, the entire player's UI will disappear. It is unknown whether this is a bug or not.
