The '''Spore Knife''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]]. Upon hitting an enemy with this sword, they will release spore clouds, which damage enemies.

While it has an incredibly short range like most [[shortsword]]s, it also has auto-swing, rendering it slightly more effective.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemies hit by it and its spores inflict [[Poisoned]] for 2 seconds. Additionally, [[Vilethorn]]-like thorns sprout out in random directions from hit enemies, spanning 4 tiles each.
