The '''Mantis Leg Plate''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[Mount]] summoning item dropped by [[Reactive Guardian, Sodis]]. When used, it changes the player's legs into legs similar to the ones used by Sodis. The legs can reach the speed of 170 mph/125 tiles per second. If the player holds space for 2 seconds, the player jumps 100 tiles into the air leaving reactive flames behind.
