'''Undine's Retribution''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. It summons several aqua javelins from the ground that home in on enemies and inflict the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]]. The spears can also go through blocks, and will disappear after a moderate time if no enemy is hit. If no enemies are nearby, the spears will instead home in on the player.
