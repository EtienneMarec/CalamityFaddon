'''Haunted Bars''' are Godseeker Mode [[bars]] made with 3 [[Phantoplasm]], 1 [[Ruinous Soul]], and 5 [[Exodium Cluster|Exodium Clusters]] per bar. They are primarily used to create [[Polterghast|Polterghast-themed]] gear and are additionally used to craft upgrades to weapons, such as the [[Wrath of the Eldritch]].
