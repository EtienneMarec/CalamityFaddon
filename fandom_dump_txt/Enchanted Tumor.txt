The '''Enchanted Tumor''' is a [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]]. While equipped, it increases the amount of [[Scrolls]] that can currently be active by 1. However, the negative effects of scrolls will be boosted by 25% if two scrolls are active once.

Any [[Boss]] [[Treasure Bag]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] has a 2% chance of containing this covering.
