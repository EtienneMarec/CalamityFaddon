The '''Starborn Princess''' is a Godseeker Mode [[NPC]] vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty house.
*[[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated.

When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself with holy spears.

== Trivia==  
*The Starborn Princess was guest created by artist, everquartz.
*It is heavily implied that the Starborn Princess is the daughter of [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]].
**Supporting this is that the [[Rune of Kos]], the boss summon for the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]], is dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]].
**Furthermore, her interaction quotes display her familial connection to [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]].
*If the player is willing to spend enough coins, they can obtain the event exclusive items for the [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]] while avoiding fighting the harder variants of the [[Pumpkin Moon]], [[Frost Moon]] and [[Solar Eclipse]] [[events]].
*The Starborn Princess has only one possible name, sharing this trait with [[Santa Claus]], the [[Archmage]], the [[Drunk Princess]], the [[Brimstone Witch]], the [[Sea King]], and the [[Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge]].
*In the mod's source files, the Starborn Princess is referred to as 'Rosalina'. This is an unlikely but possible reference to Princess Rosalina from the Super Mario Galaxy game series.
*Everquartz revealed that the name 'Deimos' apparently is a reference the Greek god [[wikipedia:Deimos (deity)|Deimos]], who is the god of dread and terror. They said it was a reference to how the community reacted to the creation of The Starborn Princess; dread and terror. It is also a subtle reference to the 'type D' part of [[The Devourer of Gods]] nonstop mix.
*It was also confirmed by everquartz that The Starborn Princess is self aware about her own existence and how she understands that she is an abomination that shouldn't exist.
*Upon killing The Starborn Princess, [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] will spawn.
*The quote "See the horns on my head? They're from goddesses. GODDESSES." from her during a blood moon is a reference to a lyric in the song called EVIL, by Melanie Martinez.
