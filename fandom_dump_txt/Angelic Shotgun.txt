The '''Angelic Shotgun''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. Each time the weapon is fired, it shoots eight bullets and summons a radiant beam from the sky towards the cursor. The beam deals five times the damage of an individual bullet and pierces infinitely through both enemies and walls. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], the bullets are replaced with special illuminated bullets. Both the illuminated bullets and the beam inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.
