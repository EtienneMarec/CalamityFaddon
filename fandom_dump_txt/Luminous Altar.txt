The '''Luminous Altar''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[furniture]] item that can be found around the center of [[Caelus]] on the top of the Aetherial Diamond. It is used to summon [[Aetherial Guardian, Olistene]] by inserting a [[Rotting Eyeball]] into it. Inserting a [[Bloody Vein]] into the altar will instead summon as many [[Barocraw]]s as can be spawned. It also functions as a [[Demon Altar]] and a [[Light sources|Light Source]].

== Notes ==
*Luminous Altars cannot be broken until [[Aetherial Guardian, Olistene]] has been defeated.
*Luminous Altars cannot be destroyed by [[explosive]]s.
