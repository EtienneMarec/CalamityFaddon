

This page documents content that is affiliated with the Calamity Mod that has been created by members of the community. The games listed are '''unofficial''' and should be considered non-canon to the Calamity Mod.

== Playable/In-Development Games==
===Calamity Dating Sim===
A turn-based RPG, formerly on Scratch, acting as the fourth installment of the '''BA Series.''' The game continues following Jeff Cuphead into a fusion universe of Calamity and various others. The game has typical RPG elements such as party members with stats and skills, except there is no level system and skills are instead progressively gained. The wiki for the game can be found [https://belectonic-arts-official.fandom.com/wiki/Calamity_Dating_Sim here].
===Calamari High===
A visual novel with humanoid female calamity bosses in a high school setting developed by Terry N. Muse, Kerubii, and others. The game has been in production since late 2017, and currently has a public demo that can be found [http://www.mediafire.com/file/hvoad1et66z8pvp/calamity2-1.02-pc.zip here].
===Distorted Memories===
A 3D game that utilizes both hack and slash and shooter game mechanics for class combat. The game served as an indirect "port" of Calamity to the 3D space and carries over most Calamity exclusive items and enemies, even removed content. 
=== [[File:Calamity_Reign_Of_Yharim_Icon.png|60px]] Reign of Yharim ===
A standalone action-adventure game focusing on the world of Calamity with modified lore and new gameplay elements. The game aims to portray Calamity distinct from Terraria with examples such as the evil biomes and Hallow being entirely replaced or removed and resource collection without the sandbox aspect. The game is currently in-development, but is planned to feature most of calamity’s content with some modifications to fit the genre and gameplay. The game will also have new bosses and lore to fill the gaps left by the removal of terraria elements.

===Rise and Downfall ===
A top-down RPG game focusing on the world of Calamity and also contains elements from ''Reign of Yharim''. The game has a combat system based off of ''Undertale'' and ''Deltarune'' with attacks and bosses able to affect the character's "soul". There are also multiple routes that can be followed with their own corresponding endings.
===Calamity's Insanity===
An RPG Maker game starring several Calamity Mod characters.

==Scrapped Games==
===Downfall of a Cruel World ===
A story based game focusing on the player going through the various challenges to bring justice to Yharim. It utilises the calamity progression and lore in combination with its own storyline to bring to light all boss and character lore in an enjoyable way. It has combat and a 2D style very similar to terraria.
===Five Days of Calamity===
A visual novel that involved all of the bosses from the Calamity Mod in somewhat humanoid forms, and not just girls. The main goal of the game was to build friendships with any of the bosses, but not everything was as it seemed as there are many endings depending on your actions.

===Jharim's Quest===
An RPG game that drew inspiration from the Final Fantasy series. The game focused on Jharim, the protagonist of the story, and also includes elements from Calamity Paint and the Community Remix Mod.
