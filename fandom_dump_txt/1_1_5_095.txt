The '''1.1.5.095''' update added a variety weapons, reduced material costs for several recipes, and resprited various [[items]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Cloud Elemental]] and [[Earth Elemental]].
*Added [[Augur of the Elements]], [[Barinade]], [[Celestial Claymore]], [[Clock Gatlignum]], [[Crushsaw Crasher]], [[Draconic Destruction]], [[Dragon's Breath]], [[Effervescence]], [[Elemental Shiv]], [[Excelsus]], [[Eye of the Storm]], [[Fulguration Halberd]], [[Infernal Rift]], [[Lazhar]], [[Lucrecia]], [[Nullification Pistol]], [[The Obliterator]], [[Onyx Chain Blaster]], [[Permafrost]], [[Phoenix Flame Barrage]], [[Red Sun]], [[Speed Blaster]], [[Storm Ruler]], [[Storm Saber]], [[Storm Spray]], [[Svantechnical]], [[Terra Shiv]], [[True Forbidden Oathblade]], and [[Vortexpopper]].
*Added [[The First Shadowflame]], [[Statis' Curse]], [[Yharim's Gift]].
*Added [[Cloudy Waifu (buff)]].
*Added [[Torrential Tear]].

== Changes ==
*[[Animus]] no longer requires any [[Luminite Bar]]s to craft.
*Buffed [[Azathoth]]'s damage from 150 to 250, and it now requires 1 [[Terrarian]] and 5 [[Shadowspec Bar]]s instead of 10 [[Meldiate Bar]]s, 20 [[Nebula Fragment]]s, and 5 [[Yharon Soul Fragment]]s.
*[[Baleful Harvester]] now uses 30 [[Spooky Wood]] and 200 [[Pumpkin]]s in its recipe instead of 100 and 500 of each respectively.
*[[Barracuda Gun]] no longer uses any [[Iron Bar]]s in its recipe.
*Nerfed [[Catastrophe Claymore]]'s damage from 60 to 55, buffed its use time from 26 to 24, and nerfed its shot speed from 16 to 7.
*Catastrophe Claymore now uses 15 [[Crystal Shard]]s, 10 [[Hallowed Bar]]s, 5 [[Ichor]], and 5 [[Soul of Night|Souls of Night]] in its recipe, instead of 35 Crystal Shards, 20 Hallowed Bars, 10 Ichor, 50 [[Rain Cloud]]s, and 10 Souls of Night.
*Buffed [[Demonshade armor]]:
**Buffed [[Demonshade Breastplate]]’s defense from 22 to 35, and increased all of its melee speed boosts.
**Buffed [[Demonshade Greaves]]’ defense from 18 to 28, their movement speed boost from 0.5 to 1, and their acceleration from 10 to 25.
**Buffed [[Demonshade Helm]]’s defense from 20 to 30, its melee damage boost from 20% to 50%, its melee critical strike chance boost from 20% to 50%, and its melee speed boost from 20% to 50%.
**Shadow beams now deal 300 damage and 7 knockback instead of 60 and 5 respectively.
**Demon Scythes now deal 500 damage and 7 knockback instead of 100 and 5 respectively.
*[[Flare Bolt]] now uses 6 [[Hellstone Bar]]s and 9 [[Obsidian]] in its recipe instead of 10 and 20 of each respectively.
*[[Frost Bolt]] now uses 20 [[Ice Block]]s and 10 [[Snow Block]]s instead of 30 and 40 respectively.
*[[Gallant Pickaxe]] now requires 1 of [[Any Lunar Pickaxe]] in its recipe.
*Buffed [[Gamma Fusillade]]'s mana cost from 3 to 2, and it now uses 10 [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]] in its recipe instead of 3 Bars of Life, 1 Core of Calamity, and 50 Souls of Might.
*[[Gelitic Blade]] now uses 35 [[Gel]] in its recipe instead of 55.
*Buffed [[Hellkite]]'s damage from 85 to 98, and its knockback from 6.5 to 7.
*[[Illustrious Knives]] changes:
**Buffed damage from 45 to 115, and its use time from 13 to 11.
**Now requires 5 [[Core of Calamity|Cores of Calamity]] and 3 [[Shadowspec Bar]]s instead of 10 Bars of Life, 10 Luminite Bars, 15 [[Stardust Fragment]]s, and 15 [[Yharon Soul Fragment]]s.
*[[Mariana]] now requires 5 [[Coral]], 3 [[Seashell]]s, and 3 [[Starfish]] to craft instead of 20, 10, and 10 respectively.
*Renamed Nettleline Greatbow to [[Nettlevine Greatbow]].
*[[Nuclear Fury]] now uses 10 [[Soul of Sight|Souls of Sight]] and 5 [[Nebula Fragment]]s in its recipe instead of 15 Souls of Sight and 6 [[Meldiate Bar]]s.
*Several [[pickaxes]] now have increased range:
**[[Flamebeak Hampick]] now has 2 extra tiles of range.
**Gallant Pickaxe now has 4 extra tiles of range.
**[[Blossom Pickaxe]] now has 6 extra tiles of range.
*Nerfed [[Seabound Staff]]'s damage from 13 to 11.
*Renamed Shadecrystal Tome to [[Shadecrystal Barrage]], and nerfed its damage from 33 to 27.
*[[Shadow Essence]]s, Shadowspec Bars, and Yharon Soul Fragments are now [[Expert Mode]]-exclusive.
**Shadow Essence and Yharon Soul Fragment now use the {{rare|rainbow}} rarity instead of {{rare|8}}.
**Shadowspec Bars now use the {{rare|rainbow}} rarity instead of {{rare|10}}.
*Shadowspec Bars now require 3 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]] and 1 Yharon Soul Fragment to craft instead of 1 Bar of Life, 2 [[Ectoplasm]], and 5 Yharon Soul Fragments.
*[[Starfleet]] no longer uses any Cores of Calamity or Meldiate Bars in its recipe.
*[[Temporal Floe Sword]] now requires 1 [[Broken Hero Sword]], 1 [[Frostbrand]], 5 Ectoplasm, and 2 [[Frost Core]]s in its recipe instead of 1 [[Ice Blade]], 1 [[True Excalibur]], 25 Ectoplasm, 750 Ice Blocks, and 500 Snow Blocks.
*[[Terratomere]] now longer uses any Cores of Calamity, [[Cruptix Bar]]s, Draedon Bars, Meldiate Bars, [[Verstaltite Bar]]s, and [[Ueliace Bar]]s in its recipe.
