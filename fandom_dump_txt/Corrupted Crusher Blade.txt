The '''Corrupted Crusher Blade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it emits glowing purple particles and inflicts the [[Cursed Inferno]] [[debuff]] upon striking enemies. While no [[bosses]] are alive, critical strikes caused by this weapon reduce the target's base defense by 10, which cannot stack with itself.
