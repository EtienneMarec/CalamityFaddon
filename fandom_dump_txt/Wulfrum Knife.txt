'''Wulfrum Knives''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[daggers]] made from [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]]s. They function identically to the [[Throwing Knife|Throwing Knives]], but with weaker stats and dealing rogue damage instead. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Wulfrum Knives causes the resulting knife to strike a target twice on hit and travel farther before falling.
