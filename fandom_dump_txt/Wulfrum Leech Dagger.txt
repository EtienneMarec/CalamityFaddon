The '''Wulfrum Leeching Dagger''' is a consumable Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] crafted with [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]]s. It always deals 1 damage to enemies, regardless of any modifiers or attack boosts the player may have, and it cannot inflict critical hits. Whenever an enemy is struck with this weapon, their health is decreased by 0.5% of their max health, ignoring all defense and damage reduction. Upon hitting an enemy with the weapon, it breaks. 

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, the weapon becomes unconsumable for the next 3 seconds.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Light''', as the Wulfrum Leech Dagger has minimal knockback and does not benefit from increased damage or critical strike chance.

== Notes == 
*The enemy or boss must be vulnerable to damage for this weapon to be effective. The Wulfrum Leech Dagger cannot damage enemies that are completely unable to be hit. 
*Its life reduction effect applies to the head segment of worm enemies, but not body or tail segments. 
*Its life reduction effect is not affected by [[rage]] or [[adrenaline]] damage increases. 

== Tips == 
*It benefits most from equipment and covering modifiers that maximize melee speed. 
**It also benefits from melee size buffs, if only to make it easier to land hits. 
*This weapon can become excessively powerful against enemies with very high health, due to the percentage-based life drain effect. 
*This weapon can be useful against bosses which enter periods of very high defense and/or DR, such as [[Brimstone Elemental]] or [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]'s encased phases despite their higher tiers, as the player can continue to deal decent damage to them when they would normally be dealing very little. Due to the weapon's very short range, caution should be taken to avoid excessive contact damage from the boss. 

== Trivia == 
*The Wulfrum Leech Dagger does not work on [[Target Dummies]], but does work on [[Super Dummies]].
