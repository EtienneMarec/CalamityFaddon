The '''Sky Lacian''' is a post-[[Umbrus]] [[Enemies|enemy]] that spawns in the Lower Layer of the [[Sky Abyss]]. It dashes towards Skyline Sharks dealing contact damage, if a player or any enemy attacks it the player is also considered a Shark.

In death mode its much faster and can cast Evercold flames at its enemies, if it gets lower than 30% life the flames start orbitting the fish providing 30 Defense, similar to the [[Coldant Firestarter]].
