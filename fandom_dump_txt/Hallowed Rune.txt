The '''Hallowed Rune''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and the upgrade of the [[Spirit Glyph]]. While equipped, whenever a minion hits an enemy, the player will gain one of the following buffs:

*'''Hallowed Power''', which increases minion damage by 10%. Additionally, while this buff is active, a hallowed star rains from the sky on hit which deals 30 summon damage. This effect has a 3-second cooldown.
*'''Hallowed Defense''', which increases [[defense]] by 8 and damage reduction by 4%.
*'''Hallowed Regen''', which grants +3 life regeneration.
