'''Auric Ore''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ore]] found on the first and second islands of the [[Dragon's Aerie]]. Its main use is to craft [[Auric Bar]]s, which are used in creating some of the most powerful gear in the ''Calamity Mod''. It requires at least a [[Blossom Pickaxe]] to mine. It also drops from [[Auric Slime]]s.

Unlike most other ores, Auric Ore spawns in enormous clusters. The largest veins can easily have 150 blocks of ore or more; to compensate, Auric Bars require a significantly number of Auric Ore to craft compared to other bars.
