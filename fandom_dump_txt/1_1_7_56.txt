The '''1.1.7.56''' update added new changes for Calamity [[bosses]] in [[Prepare to Cry]] mode, and adjusted the stat boosts that the mode, as well as Prepare to Die mode, already gave. The update also buffed most of the mod's [[spears]], made several bosses despawn when brought out of their respective [[biomes]], and resprited The Devourer of Gods.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Extreme Gravity]] and [[Warped]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Increased all Prepare to Die/Cry mode health multipliers:
{{collapse top}}
**Buffed [[King Slime]], [[Eater of Worlds]], [[Brain of Cthulhu]], [[Creeper]], [[Queen Bee]], [[Skeletron]], the unsplit [[Ebonian Slime God]] and [[Crimulan Slime God]], [[Wall of Flesh]], [[The Hungry]], and [[The Hungry II]]'s Prepare to Die ([[Demon Trophy]]) health multipliers from 5x to 7x, their Prepare to Die ([[Celestial Onion]]) health multipliers from 6x to 9x, and their Prepare to Cry health multipliers from 10x to 13x.
**Buffed [[Eye of Cthulhu]]'s Prepare to Die (Celestial Onion) health multiplier from 7x to 9x, and its Prepare to Cry health multiplier from 10x to 13x.
**Buffed [[Desert Scourge]], and [[The Hive Mind]]'s Prepare to Die (Demon Trophy) health multipliers from 6x to 7x, their Prepare to Die (Celestial Onion) health multipliers from 8x to 9x, and their Prepare to Cry health multipliers from 10x to 13x.
**Buffed [[The Perforators]]' Prepare to Die (Demon Trophy) health multipliers from 5x to 7x, their Prepare to Die (Celestial Onion) health multipliers from 7x to 9x, and their Prepare to Cry health multipliers from 10x to 13x.
**Buffed [[Cryogen]]'s Prepare to Die (Demon Trophy) health multiplier from 5x to 7x, and its Prepare to Cry health multiplier from 10x to 13x.
**Buffed [[The Destroyer]], [[The Twins]], [[Skeletron Prime]], [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], Calamitas' brothers, [[Plantera]], [[Plantera's Tentacle]], [[Siren Lure]], all parts of [[Golem]], [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Plaguebringer Shade]], [[Duke Fishron]], and [[Lunatic Cultist]]'s Prepare to Die (Demon Trophy) health multipliers from 3x to 4x, their Prepare to Die (Celestial Onion) health multipliers from 4x to 5x, and their Prepare to Cry health multipliers from 6x to 8x.
**Buffed all of [[Moon Lord]]'s parts, [[Profaned Guardians]], [[Providence, the Profaned God]], Providence's Guardians, [[Storm Weaver]], [[Ceaseless Void]], [[Dark Energy]], [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]], [[Cosmic Lantern]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], [[Bumblebirb]], Bumblebirb's clones, [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]], [[Supreme Calamitas]], and Supreme Calamitas' brothers' Prepare to Die (Demon Trophy) health multipliers from 2x to 3x, their Prepare to Die (Celestial Onion) health multipliers from 3x to 4x, and their Prepare to Cry health multipliers from 4x to 5x.
*[[Hive Blob]]s, [[The Perforator Hive]], [[Cryogen's Shield]], and Siren Lure's [[Ice Shield]] now inherit their respective bosses' Prepare to Die/Cry health multipliers.

*Buffed [[Crystalline]]'s damage from 14 to 16, and it now has a 4% critical strike chance boost.
*[[Dragon Egg (summoning)]] now requires 3 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]] and 5 [[Effulgent Feather]]s to craft instead of 5 and 10 respectively.
*[[Elemental Lance]], [[Gilded Proboscis]], [[Stream Gouge]], and [[Terra Lance]] now have auto swing.
*Nerfed [[Firestorm Cannon]]'s alt fire's use time from 22 to 26.

**Buffed Goldplume Spear's shot speed from 4 to 5.
