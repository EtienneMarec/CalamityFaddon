'''Classless weapons''' are weapons that belong to no particular type of damage. There are two types of classless weapons available. Damaging classless weapons require special [[ammo]] to be fired and are not boosted by damage increasing [[coverings]], [[potions]], and [[debuffs]]. Classless support weapons are affected by damage increases. All classless weapons cannot receive modifiers.

== Notes ==
*All classless weapons, when held, will not trigger the [[Vanilla changes#Minions|Calamity summoner damage penalty]].

== Trivia ==
*The finite ammo classless weapons (such as the [[Lightning Hawk]]) are often collectively referred to as the "[[wikipedia:Resident Evil|Resident Evil]] guns" or "RE guns" by the community, referencing their original inspiration.
