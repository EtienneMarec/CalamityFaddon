The '''Mycelial Claws''' are a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that drops from [[Crabulon]]. The claws feature an extremely fast swing speed and very short range, a similar functionality to the [[Fetid Baghnakhs]].

{{modifier|melee}}

== Tips ==
*When paired with the [[Fungal Symbiote]] this weapon can decimate enemies and even some [[Pre-Hardmode]] bosses extremely quickly.
*The [[Mandible Claws]] are technically better for real enemies because they have slightly reduced knockback but it doesn't really matter.
