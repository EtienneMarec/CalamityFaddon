The '''1.1.7.851''' update made reworks to Calamity's [[Hardmode]] [[ore]] sets by splitting [[Daedalus armor|Daedalus]] and [[Reaver armor|Reaver]] into multiple classes with different helmets, and retiering [[Chaotic Ore|Chaotic]] and [[Uelibloom Ore]] to later in progression. It also changed various projectiles to ignore immunity frames, made a few recipe changes, and added two [[weapons]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Carnage Ray]] and [[Minigun]].
*Added [[Daedalus Hat]], [[Daedalus Headgear]], [[Daedalus Helm]], [[Daedalus Visor]], [[Reaver Cap]], [[Reaver Helmet]], [[Reaver Mask]], and [[Reaver Visage]].
*Added [[Chaos Spirit]], [[Daedalus Crystal]], and [[Reaver Orb]].

*[[Ceaseless Void]] now drops 2-3 [[Dark Plasma]] instead of 1.
*Redid the visuals for [[The Devourer of Gods]]' nebula shots and increased their speed.
*[[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s Holy Blasts, Molten Blasts, and Molten Blobs now collide with liquids.

==== [[Covering]] ====
*[[Daedalus armor]] changes:
**Daedalus Helm now provides melee stat boosts instead of ranged.
**Buffed Daedalus Helm's defense from 13 to 21.
**Daedalus Breastplate now provides damage and critical strike chance bonuses to all types of damage instead of just ranged.
**Daedalus Breastplate no longer gives a 20% chance to not consume ammo.
**Buffed Daedalus Breastplate's defense from 16 to 17.
**Daedalus Leggings now provide a 4% critical strike chance boost to all damage types but summoner, instead of a 5% critical strike chance boost for ranged damage.
**Nerfed Daedalus Leggings' movement speed boost from 11% to 10%, but buffed its defense from 12 to 13.
**Removed [[Shroomite armor]] stealth effect from its set bonus.
**Melee Daedalus armor now grants a 50% chance for hostile projectiles to be deflected while healing the player for half of the projectile's damage.

*[[Reaver armor]] changes:
**Reaver Scale Mail now provides damage and critical strike chance bonuses to all types of damage instead of just melee.
**Nerfed the amount of health that Reaver Scale Mail grants from 100 to 50, but buffed its defense from 15 to 19.
**Reaver Cuisses now provide a critical strike chance boost to all damage types but summoner, instead of only for melee.
**Buffed Reaver Cuisses' defense from 12 to 14.
**Nerfed Reaver Helm's melee damage boost from 12% to 10%, its melee critical strike chance boost from 9% to 5%, its melee speed boost from 8% to 5%, decreased its damage reduction penalty as health lowers, its movement speed boost from 8% to 5%, and buffed its defense from 24 to 25.
**Melee explosions now penetrated once instead of infinitely.

*[[Tarragon armor]] changes:
**Buffed Tarragon Helm's defense from 17 to 27, but no longer provides 100 extra health.
**Tarragon Helm's tooltip now mentions that it makes the wearer immune to [[Lava]] and several debuffs.
**Buffed Tarragon Breastplate's defense from 27 to 30.
**Buffed Tarragon Leggings' defense from 8 to 26.
**All pieces now have the {{rare|12}} rarity instead of {{rare|8}}.
**Nerfed set bonus' defense boosts as health lowers from intervals of 7 each to 4 each.
**Nerfed set bonus damage reduction boost when under 50% health from 5% to 2.5%, and when under 25% health from 7.5% to 5%.

*[[Xeroc armor]] changes:
**Nerfed Xeroc Mask's defense from 23 to 20.
**Buffed Xeroc Plate Mail's defense from 22 to 27.
**Buffed Xeroc Cuisses' defense from 17 to 24, nerfed its critical strike chance boost from 10% to 7%, and nerfed its movement speed boost from 30% to 20%.
**Set bonus changes:
***Now affects throwing damage and critical strike chance, and summon damage.
***Removed mana cost penalty once the player is below 40% health.
***Nerfed set mana cost boost from -15% to -10%.
***Final set of stat changes now activate when under 20% health instead of 25%.
***Nerfed critical strike chance boost when under 85% health from 10% to 5% and its damage boost when under 75% health from 15% to 5%.
***Decreased damage penalty when under 20% health from -25% to -10%, and its critical strike chance penalty from -20% to -5%.
***Explosions and fire projectiles now penetrate once instead of infinitely.
***Explosions now last for 0.8 seconds instead of 0.17.
