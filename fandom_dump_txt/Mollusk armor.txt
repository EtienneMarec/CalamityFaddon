'''Mollusk armor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set crafted from several [[Sunken Sea]] materials. A total of 31 [[Mollusk Husk]]s and 60 [[Sea Prism]]s are required to craft a full set.

It consists of the '''Mollusk Shellmet''', '''Mollusk Shellplate''', and '''Mollusk Shelleggings'''.

== Stats ==
The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*55 defense
*37% increased damage
*14% increased critical strike chance
*22% decreased movement speed
*Can move freely through liquids.
*Summons two [[Shellfish Staff|Shellfishes]].
*Decreases dash length and running speed by 33%.

== Notes ==
*The Shellfish minions deal 70 base damage upon latching onto an enemy and inflict '''Shellfish Claps'''.
*The Shellfish minions are the same minions summoned by the [[Shellfish Staff]].

== Tips ==
*Despite the large stat increases provided by this armor, the movement speed reduction is substantial, making it impractical for bosses such as [[The Twins]] where fast speed is required.
*This set bonus largely negates the sprinting effect of the [[Hermes Boots]], as well as its upgrades and alternatives, allowing you to choose another covering to use in its place.
*Items such as [[Harpy Wings]], [[Vital Jelly]], [[Swiftness Potion]]s, [[Asphalt Block]]s, and [[Sunflower]]s can be combined to combat the movement speed reduction while keeping the high stat boosts.
*This armor can be paired with the helmets of [[Titanium armor]] and [[Adamantite armor]] for the strongest offensive stats possible while avoiding the dash and sprinting reduction from the set bonus.

== Trivia ==
*The armor set itself and its minions resemble the [[Giant Clam]].
