The '''Sea's Searing''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that drops from the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. It has two modes of attack: its primary fire (left-click) will shoot a barrage of sea bubbles that summon several additional bubbles from the sides of the screen upon contacting an [[enemy]] that inflict the [[Wet]] [[debuff]]. Its secondary fire (right-click) launches a sea blast that creates a water spout on contact with blocks and enemies, the sea blast itself inflicts the [[Glacial State]] debuff. All attacks inflict the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] debuff onto enemies.

The Sea's Searing does not require [[ammo]] to fire.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The primary fire has a fixed use time of 15 ticks, and the secondary fire has a fixed use time of 30 ticks; as a result, no modifier can increase or decrease the firing rate of the weapon.
