'''Deicide''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When used, the player will perform slow short distanced punches, with the rate at which punches are thrown increasing very quickly until roughly 20 punches are thrown a second after 8 seconds of continuous punching. After 10 seconds of punching, homing fists will start shooting outwards that deal 20% of the weapon's damage. After 30 seconds, a dark wreathe surrounds the player which boosts their melee damage by 30%. 

Overtime, the player will become increasingly red, and eventually start fuming once 50 seconds have passed. Once a minute has passed, the player is instantly killed and bursts into harmless orange liquid. The player must stop using any weapons in order for the redness to go away. 

If the [[Rage Meter]] is used while using the weapon, a [[22 Hornet Round|.22 Hornet Round]] is fired at the cursor with a damage modifier of 22x, ricocheting off of 21 enemies.

*Deicide is the act of killing a god.
