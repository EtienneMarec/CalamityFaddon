The '''Cragmaw Mire''' is a [[Hardmode]], post-[[Aquatic Scourge]] [[mini boss]] that spawns during the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. They spawn on the [[Sulphurous Sea]] floor and cannot move.

== Behavior ==
The Cragmaw Mire is gravity-affected and completely stationary enemy. It randomly spawns enemies around nearby liquid and cycles between two attacks every 7 seconds and oddly tastes like cheese.
*For 4 seconds, it fires acid bubbles consistently which float upwards, splitting into four crosses after 2-3 seconds or upon tile collision once fully materialized. Crosses explode upon impact with tiles, dealing damage to a small area.
*It pauses for 1 second to charge up a laser beam attack, and a column of harmless dust is formed. Then, a laser beam fires upwards and lingers for 2 seconds.

=== Tier 3 ===
*Bubbles are fired every 0.4 seconds instead of 0.83 seconds
*While the laser is fired, acid droplets are also rapidly fired.
*Transforms into its second phase at {{dv|70%||85%}} health, changing its appearance. In this phase, it loses the ability to spawn enemies and fire bubbles. Instead, it summons a tether that reels the player into itself, and fires a randomized spread of 16 gravity-affected spikes every 10 seconds.

== Notes ==
*The Cragmaw Mire has 50% DR in its first phase, 30% if the player is submerged. In its second phase, its DR is dropped to 0% / {{revengeance|12.5%}}.
*Once the bubble fully materializes after 1.67 seconds, the damage dealt drops to {{dv|40|80}} in all tiers. This damage value is also inherited to the crosses and its explosions.

== Trivia ==
*The Cragmaw Mire's appearance was inspired by a submission for a sulphurous sea design contest that was held on the Calamity art server. The [https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/424301622878011402/551911012878778376/AzufrePiercer.png original artwork], made by Ox#8375, used the name "Azufre Piercer".
*The tether is referred to as 'CragmawVibeCheckChain' in the mod's source files.
*The Cragmaw Mire tastes like cheese as its been proven by many scientists like draedon
*The jaw of the Cragmaw Mire was inspired by a [[Wikipedia:bear_trap|bear trap]].
*Cragmaw Mire could likely reference the popular Family Guy character Quagmire.
*In the Japanese translation, the name is "クラッグマウの泥沼 ''Kuraggumau no doronuma''"
