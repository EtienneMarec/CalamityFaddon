The '''Unstable Calamity''' (Japanese: 不安定な災い ''Fuanteina wazawai'') is a [[Challenges|Godseeker Mode]] boss required to be fought as the first freaky boss after the Moon Lord, even before the [[Profaned Guardians]]. Since it is the first Godseeker boss, it is likely a very difficult challenge due to its one hit abilities.
==Spawn==
The freaky Calamity can be spawned by right-clicking the [[Awakening]] [[lore item]] after defeating the [[tgc:Moon_Lord|Moon Lord]].

Alternatively, the Unstable Calamity can be summoned by calling FreakyBob while at least 50 [[Depressed Cephalopod|Depressed Cephalopods]] have been defeated.
==Behavior==
The battle is unique as The Unstable Calamity will only be defeated after all players have survived 3 minutes. During the fight, The Unstable Calamity is immune to any form of damage and will remain stationary during phase 1, while dashing and teleporting during phase 2.

In its 1st phase, The Unstable Calamity summons 1 {{item|Singularity|nolink=yes|ext=png|maxsize=42px|Singularity}} periodically from above the player's location that fire a semi circle of death lasers at the player.

It then fires Calamity Lasers from it's eye 3 times in a 8 way pattern before firing a circle of death lasers.

The Unstable Calamity transitions into it's second phase at 1 minute and 30 seconds. During it, the Unstable Calamity will utilize harder attacks to kill the player:

It will teleport near the player and dash towards them. The teleport will also create Calamity Lasers in an 8 way pattern. This is repeated 5 times.

It summons 5 {{item|Darkscule|ext=png|maxsize=42px|Darkscules}} that home in on the the player for 5 seconds,  before exploding in a 4 way pattern of death lasers.

It summons 3 Singularities that act the same as they did in phase 1, except that they shoot Calamity Lasers instead. This will repeat 3 times before the Unstable Singularity cycles back to dashing.

At 2 minutes and 45 seconds, the Unstable Calamity will teleport near the player and make a border of Calamity Lasers near it, before firing an 8 way pattern of spinning Giga Beams for 10 seconds. Surviving this attack will end the battle.

== Aftermath ==

* All other Godseeker mode bosses are able to be summoned.
* The lore item will revert to spawning [[The Calamity]]. 
* To fight the '''Unstable Calamity''' a second time, dial '''FreakyBob''' while at least 50 [[Depressed Cephalopod|Depressed Cephalopods]] have been defeated in the current save file.

== Notes ==

* All damage inflicted bypasses invincibility frames, death prevention, and even godmode.

*The Calamity's [[Music|theme]] for phase 1 is '''Otherworldly Space,''' which was composed by the artist '''John Calamity'''.
**The phase 2 [[Music|theme]] is '''Otherworldly Calamity,''' which was composed by the artist '''Jane Calamity'''.
*According to the mod creators, The Unstable Calamity was created after the essence of many souls was absorbed into The Calamity, giving it godly power, yet giving it an unstoppable bloodlust. 
*If the Boss Checklist Mod is installed, its unique despawn message will be "The Unstable Calamity leaves, its bloodlust remaining eternal."
