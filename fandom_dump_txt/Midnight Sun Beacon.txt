The '''Midnight Sun Beacon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Xeno Staff]] and the [[Lunar Portal Staff]]. When used it throws out a Midnight Sun Beacon that summons a Midnight Sun UFO after a few seconds. The Midnight Sun UFO will track an enemy and fire small lasers towards them. After a few seconds of consecutively firing, it will circle above an enemy and fire a concentrated laser beam thats length is determined by the size of the enemy it's targeting. After firing its concentrated laser beam, it will return to firing lasers.
