'''The Codebreaker''' is a piece of constructible [[furniture]] that consists of six different components scattered throughout progression: '''Codebreaker Base''', '''Decryption Computer''', '''Long Ranged Sensor Array''', '''Advanced Display''', [[Honey Cooling Cell]], '''Voltage Regulation System''', and '''Auric Quantum Cooling Cell'''. It is used to decrypt [[schematics]] to unlock various items, to initiate the battle with [[The Fabricational Quartet]], [[XP-00 Hypnos]], and [[XB-10 Losbaf]] [[bosses]], to unlock the [[Murasama]] once the Voltage Regulation System has been installed, by inserting the Murasama into the Schematic slot and the desired gem color in the Power Cell slot, and to communicate with '''Draedon'''.

==Communicate==
*After defeating either [[The Fabricational Quartet]], or [[XP-00 Hypnos]], the Contact icon will appear in the codebreaker's UI, therefore allowing the player to communicate with '''Draedon'''.

== Crafting ==
=== Recipe ===
Most components of The Codebreaker are required to be unlocked through decryption. {{schematic|tooltip|plural=1}}

The requirements are listed as follows:
*'''Codebreaker Base''' has no special requirements.
*'''Decryption Computer''' requires the Sunken Sea schematic to be found.
*'''Long Ranged Sensor Array''' requires the Planetoid schematic to be decrypted.
*'''Advanced Display''' requires the Jungle schematic to be decrypted.
*'''Voltage Regulation System''' requires the Hell schematic to be decrypted.
*'''Auric Quantum Cooling Cell''' requires the Ice ({{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} Exosphere) schematic to be decrypted. However, it can also be crafted if the world was created before the [[1.5.0.001]] update.

== Notes ==
*The Codebreaker cannot be destroyed by [[explosive]]s.
*Although the codebreaker base is only a 5x3 model, it still requires enough space for the 5x8 fully upgraded code breaker to be placed down.
*UI scaling breaks the menu, if you are having issues with the menu try setting the UI scale to 150%.
*Although the Honey Cooling Cell doesn't unlock any new recipes, it is still required in order to complete The Codebreaker and summon Draedon.
