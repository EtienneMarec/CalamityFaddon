'''Frozen Armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Melee]] [[Covering]] set crafted from materials found in [[Frozen Hell]]. It is intended as an upgrade to the [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Molten_armor| Molten Armor] set. While the full set is worn, decorative snow particles will fall off of the player.

It consists of the '''Frozen Visor''', '''Frozen Chestplate''', and '''Frozen Greaves'''.

== Stats==
The full set grants the following stats and bonuses:

200 defense
60% increased melee speed
20% increased true melee size
+75 max life
inflicts Frostburn on true melee hit
Grants immunity to all cold-based debuffs

== Set bonus==
Press {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} to create a small ice shield around the player which can parry attacks. This shield has a 5 second cooldown
Parrying enemies will deal 100 melee damage and will give the player a second of invulnerability
Parrying projectiles will halve their damage
Parrying nothing will freeze the player for a second, and cause their ice shield to be disabled for 10 seconds
