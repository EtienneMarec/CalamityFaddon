'''Cinderplate''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[block]] that can also be found in the core of the [[Space Bio-center Lab]] of a world. It can be used as a building material, and is also required to craft the [[Normality Relocator]].

== Trivia ==
*Cinderplate's name references [[Essence of Sunlight]]'s older name, which was Essence of Cinder.
*Before the introduction of the other 'plate' blocks ([[Chaosplate]], [[Navyplate]], [[Plagueplate]] and [[Elumplate]]), Cinderplate was used to craft all of the Soul Artifacts as well as the Normality Relocator and resembled Chaosplate.
