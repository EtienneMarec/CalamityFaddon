The '''Absolute Zero''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings, and is sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{gc|80}} after defeating all three [[minibosses]] in the [[Frost Moon]] [[event]]. When swung, it fires a dark ice projectile which explodes upon impact with any [[enemy]] or surface, potentially hitting twice. Uniquely, its projectiles deal 50% less damage if they critically hit. Striking an enemy directly also creates large frost explosions. Both the blade and its projectiles always inflict the [[Frostburn]] and [[Glacial State]] [[debuffs]] on enemies.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Absolute zero|Absolute zero]] is the scientific term for 0 Kelvin, the asymptotic lowest possible temperature for anything to reach.
*The projectiles fired by Absolute Zero are visually identical to the projectiles fired by the [[Archmage]]; however, the Absolute Zero's ice crystals explode and deal additional damage on hit.
