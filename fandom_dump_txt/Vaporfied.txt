'''Vaporfied''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by various light, rainbow, mediocre matter, and glitch themed enemies and weapons. It causes the player to lose 10 health per second, 2 defense, 10% movement speed, and their vision is reduced similar to the [[Darkness]] debuff. The player will also gain afterimages that will persist until the debuffs duration ends. If the debuff is inflicted onto an enemy, it will lose 25 health per second and 15% movement speed. The debuff will also cause enemies and the player to emit glowing, pastel rainbow flames and flash a variety of colors.
