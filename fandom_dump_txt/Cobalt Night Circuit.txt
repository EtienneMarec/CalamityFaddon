The '''Cobalt Night Circuit''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that turns the player into a night mech. The mech form increases the max hp by 40, defence by 10 and movement speed by 50%, It also creates a 5 block damaging aura that deals 120 damage per second. It is a counterpart to [[Palladium Light Circuit]]
