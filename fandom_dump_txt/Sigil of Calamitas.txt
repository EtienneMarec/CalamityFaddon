The '''Sigil of Calamitas''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It grants the user a variety of bonuses to magic damage and max mana, these being an additional 15% increased magic damage, and additional 100 maximum mana, and a 10% decrease in mana usage. The Sigil will also increase pickup range for mana stars.
