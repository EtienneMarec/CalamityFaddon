The '''1.4.2.108''' update expanded upon [[Fishing]] by adding in several new items that the player can hook as well as continued to lay down the groundwork for the Spy expansion update.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Baby Ghost Bell]] and [[Radiator]].
*Added [[Bloodstained Glove]], [[Electrician's Glove]], [[Fantasy Talisman]], [[Filthy Glove]], and [[Vampiric Talisman]].
*Added [[Dragoon Drizzlefish]], [[Sea's Searing]], [[Spadefish]], and [[Sparkling Empress]].
*Added [[Aldebaran Alewife]], [[Auric Tesla Bar]], [[Bloodfin]], [[Brimlish]], [[Brimstone Crate]], [[Brimstone Fish]], [[Chaotic Fish]], [[Coastal Demonfish]], [[Coralskin Foolfish]], [[Crag Bullhead]], [[Glimmering Gemfish]], [[Greenwave Loach]], [[Prismatic Guppy]], [[Scarred Angelfish]], [[Shadowfish]], [[Slurpfish]], [[Solar Veil]], [[Stat Meter]], [[Stuffed Fish]], [[Sunken Crate]], and [[Sunken Sailfish]].
*Added [[Abyssal Madness]], [[Abyssal Madness Cooldown]], [[Blood Frenzy]], [[Blood Frenzy Cooldown]], [[Bloodflare Soul Cooldown]], [[Elysian Guard]], [[Tarragon Cloak]], [[Tarragon Cloak Cooldown]], [[Tarragon Immunity]], and [[Tarragon Immunity Cooldown]].
*Added [[Serpent's Bite]].
*Added [[Radiating Crystal]] and [[Rusted Jingle Bell]].
*Added [[Astral Clay]], [[Astral Snow]], [[Celestial Remains]], and [[Novae Slag]].
*Added a Snow variant for the [[Astral Monolith]] tree.
*Added three new quests to the [[Angler]] [[Town NPC]].
*[[Bouncing Betty]] and [[Hell's Sun]] are now obtainable.
*Added [[Gael's Greatsword]], [[Insidious Impaler]], [[Septic Skewer]], and [[Totality Breakers]] as unobtainable items.
*Added [[Umbraphile armor]] as an unobtainable armor set.

== Changes ==
=== Balance and Reworks===
*Reworked [[Astreal Defeat]], no longer fires arrows that move in a rectangular grid pattern. It now fires a stream of arrows that move erratically in an arc.
*Buffed [[Blizzard Staff]], it now inflicts the [[Frostburn]] debuff for 4 seconds on hit.
*The following weapons are no longer boosted by [[Cinnamon Roll]] and [[Fireball]]:
**[[Brand of the Inferno]], [[The Empyrean]], [[Flarefrost Blade]], [[Frigidflash Bolt]], [[Photoviscerator]], [[Sky Dragon's Fury]], [[Sleepy Octopod]], and [[Tears of Heaven]].
*Buffed [[Deep Sea Dumbbell]], flexing now powers up the Spy weapon's stealth strikes dramatically.
*The following items are now boosted by [[Evergreen Gin]]:
**[[Clam Crusher]], [[Clamor Rifle]], [[Coral Cannon]], [[Gacruxian Mollusk]], [[Lionfish]], [[Magical Conch]], [[Polaris Parrotfish]], [[Sand Dollar]], [[Serpentine]], [[Shellfish Staff]], [[Shellshooter]], [[Snap Clam]], [[Urchin Flail]], and [[Yateveo Bloom]].
*Buffed [[Frost Daggerfish]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 3 seconds on hit.
*Buffed [[Frostbrand]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 10 seconds on true melee hits and 8 seconds with its projectile.
*Buffed [[Frost Staff]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 8 seconds on hit.
*[[Heartreach]] now increases the grab range of all healing projectiles by 50%.
*Nerfed [[Heavenly Gale]], teal exo arrows now deal 16% of the weapon's damage and green exo arrows now deal 77% of the weapon's damage.
*Nerfed [[Kelvin Catalyst]]'s damage from 100 to 70.
*Buffed [[Ice Blade]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 10 seconds on true melee hits and 4 seconds with its projectile.
*Buffed [[Ice Boomerang]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 4 seconds on hit.
*Buffed [[Ice Bow]]'s damage from 46 to 61, and it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 8 seconds on hit.
*Buffed [[Ice Sickle]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 10 seconds on true melee hits and 8 seconds with its projectile.
*Buffed [[Marrow]]'s damage from 40 to 53, and it now inflicts the [[Armor Crunch]] debuff for 5 seconds on hit.
*[[Molten armor]]'s set bonus now grants the effects of the [[Lava Charm]] and [[Obsidian Skull]].
*Buffed [[North Pole]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 10 seconds on true melee hits, 8 seconds with its clone projectile, and 4 seconds with its snowflake projectiles.
*[[Profaned Soul Artifact]] now always gives +1 max minions even while the player is [[Burnt Out]].
*Buffed [[Snowball]], it now inflicts the Frostburn debuff for 2 seconds on hit.
*[[Statis' Belt of Curses]] and [[Statis' Ninja Belt]] can now have their dashes toggled using visibility.
*Nerfed [[Throwing Brick]]'s damage from 20 to 14.

=== NPCs ===
*An audio cue is now played when an [[adult Eidolon Wyrm]] targets and attacks a player.
*[[Astrum Aureus]], [[Brimling]]s, [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], [[Cataclysm]], [[Catastrophe]], [[Great Sand Shark]], [[Melter]]s, [[Pestilent Slime]]s, [[Plague Charger]]s, [[Plaguebringer]]s, [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Plagueshell]]s, [[Ravager]], [[Siren and Leviathan]], and [[Viruling]]s are no longer immune to [[Frostburn]].
*[[Ceaseless Void]] now has a unique hit sound.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] now has unique Cosmic Worm summoning and hit sound effects.
*[[Eidolist]]s now have a unique death sound and no longer automatically aggro a player while a boss is alive.
*[[Irradiated Slime]]s are now immune to all debuffs.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] no longer becomes fully invisible when teleporting, and swapped the two music tracks that play during the first and second phase of the fight. <!---This is NOT a music mod change, this is a Main mod source code edit. Merkalto -->
*[[King Slime]] in Revengeance Mode can now only spawn [[Rainbow Slime]]s during [[Hardmode]].
*All enemies that spawn during the [[Old One's Army]] event except [[Etherian Wyvern]]s and [[Kobold Glider]]s are now immune to [[Slow]] and [[Webbed]].
*[[Overloaded Soldier]]s and [[Phantom Debris]] now have mirrored AI.
*[[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] now has unique spawn, hit, laser, and death sound effects.
*All [[Sand Shark]] variants can now tunnel through Astral Sand and Astral Sandstone blocks.
*[[Scorn Eater]]s now have unique hit, death, and jumping sounds, and can now pass through tiles while super jumping.
*All Solar Eclipse enemies now have a {{dv|4%|6%}} chance to drop 1 [[Darksun Fragment]] after a buffed Mothron has been defeated.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]]' Brimstone Monster projectiles now emit a droning sound effect when near the player.
*Voodoo Demon's drop rates are now affected by the [[Defiled Rune]].

=== Recipes ===
*All instances of [[Auric Ore]] in recipes have been replaced with Auric Tesla Bars with a 20:1 ratio.
*[[Asgard's Valor]] recipe now requires 1 [[Core of Calamity]] instead of 3 Cores of Chaos, Eleum, and Sunlight to be crafted.
*[[Astral Breastplate]] recipe now also requires 6 [[Meteorite Bar]]s to be crafted.
*[[Astral Helm]] recipe now also requires 9 Meteorite Bars to be crafted.
*[[Astral Leggings]] recipe now also requires 8 Meteorite Bars to be crafted.
*[[Ataxia Armor]] recipe now also requires 3 [[Core of Chaos|Cores of Chaos]] and 8 [[Hellstone Bar]]s to be crafted.
*[[Ataxia Headgear]], [[Ataxia Helm]], [[Ataxia Helmet]], [[Ataxia Hood]], and [[Ataxia Mask]] recipes all now also require 1 Core of Chaos and 4 Hellstone Bars to be crafted.
*[[Ataxia Subligar]] recipe now also require 2 Cores of Chaos and 5 Hellstone Bars to be crafted.
*[[Daedalus Breastplate]] recipe now also requires 3 [[Essence of Eleum|Essences of Eleum]] to be crafted.
*[[Daedalus Facemask]], [[Daedalus Headgear]], [[Daedalus Helm]], [[Daedalus Hood]], and [[Daedalus Mask]] recipes all now also require 1 Essence of Eleum to be crafted.
*[[Daedalus Leggings]] recipe now also require 2 Essences of Eleum to be crafted.
*[[Elemental Excalibur]] recipe now requires 10 Shadowspec Bars instead of 10 Nightmare Fuel, Endothermic Energy, Calamitous Essences, Darksun Fragments, and Yharon Soul Fragments.
*[[Gravity Normalizer Potion]] recipe now requires an Aldebaran Alewife to be crafted.
*[[Hadal Stew]] recipe now requires 1 Coastal Demonfish to be crafted.
*[[Hermes Boots]] recipe now also requires 4 [[Swiftness Potion]]s to be crafted.
*[[Magic Quiver]] recipe can now be crafted with an [[Endless Quiver]], 5 [[Blighted Lens]]es, 10 [[Pixie Dust]], and 8 [[Soul of Light|Souls of Light]] at a [[Crystal Ball]].
*[[Reaver Cuisses]] recipe now also require 2 [[Essence of Sunlight|Essences of Sunlight]] and 8 [[Jungle Spores]] to be crafted.
*[[Reaver Headgear]], [[Reaver Helm]], [[Reaver Helmet]], [[Reaver Mask]], and [[Reaver Visage]] recipes all now also require 1 Essence of Sunlight and 6 Jungle Spores to be crafted.
*[[Reaver Scale Mail]] recipe now also requires 3 Essences of Sunlight and 12 Jungle Spores to be crafted.
*[[Revivify Potion]] recipe now requires a Scarred Angelfish to be crafted.
*[[Shadowspec Bar]] recipe now requires 1 Auric Tesla Bar and 1 Calamitous Essence to be crafted.
*[[Soaring Potion]] recipe now requires a Sunken Sailfish to be crafted.
*[[Titan Scale Potion]] recipe now requires a Coralskin Foolfish to be crafted.
*[[Dragon Pow]] recipe now requires 4 Yharon Soul Fragments instead of 5 Cosmilite Bars.

== Additional Changes ==
*Abaddon and Abyssal Diving Gear now work when equipped as either a helmet or an covering.
*[[Acceleration Round]]s, [[Acid Round]]s, Astral Round projectile, [[Enhanced Nano Round]]s, [[Flash Round]]s, [[Frostspark Bullet]]s, [[Holy Fire Bullet]]s, [[Hyperius Bullet]]s, [[Superball Bullet]]s, and [[Verium Bullet]]s now have more afterimages.
*Added a glowmask to [[Ectoheart]].
*Added a mod compatibility call to turn Calamity's various difficulty modes on or off. Unlike when using the item, this will not kill anyone, and does work while bosses are alive.
*Added a tooltip to Dragon Pow.
*Added an internal listing of ice-themed weaponry.
*Added new progress messages to Calamity's improved Lihzahrd Temple generation to make it obvious that it has been modified.
*Added numerous recipe groups to Calamity's recipe code. This has no visible effect in-game.
*Added several items to the drop pools of vanilla fishing crates:
**[[Wooden Crate]]s now have a 25% chance to yield 3-5 Wulfrum Shards.
**[[Iron Crate]]s now have a 25% chance to yield 5-8 Wulfrum Shards, and a 25% chance to yield 5-8 Ancient Bone Dust.
**[[Corrupt Crate]]s now have a 25% chance to yield 5-8 Fetid Essences.
**[[Crimson Crate]]s now have a 25% chance to yield 5-8 Bloodletting Essences.
**Corrupt and Crimson Crates now have a 25% chance to yield 5-8 Blighted Gel, and a 25% chance to yield 1-3 Murky Sludge.
**[[Jungle Crate]]s now have a 20% chance to yield 1-3 Murky Paste, a 20% chance to yield 1-3 Maneater Bulbs, a 20% chance to yield 1-3 Trapper Bulbs in Hardmode, a 25% chance to yield 5-10 Perennial Bars post-Plantera, a 20% chance to yield 3-6 Plague Cell Canisters post-Golem, and a 25% chance to yield 5-10 Uelibloom Bars post-Providence.
**[[Sky Crate]]s now have a 25% chance to yield 5-10 Aerialite Bars post-Hive Mind/Perforators, a 20% chance to yield 5-15 Essences of Sunlight in Hardmode, and a 10% chance to yield 1-3 Galactica Singularities post-Moon Lord.
**[[Dungeon Crate]]s now have a 30% chance to yield 5-10 Ectoplasm post-Plantera, and a 20% chance to yield 5-10 Phantoplasm post-Polterghast.
**[[Hallowed Crate]]s now have a 20% chance to yield 5-10 Unholy Essences post-Providence.
*All Spy armor sets now explain how to use Stealth Strikes in their set bonus tooltip.
*All grappling hooks now show their stats in their tooltips.
*Astral Crates now have a 25% chance to drop 5-10 Meld Blobs post-[[Astrum Deus]].
*Astral Sand is no longer valid ammunition for the Sandgun.
*Caribbean Rum and Warped's vertical velocity effects no longer work while mounted.
*[[Code 1]] no longer counts as true melee.
*[[Flamebeak Hampick]] can now be used as a hammer as its alternative fire.
*Heavenly Gale's Exo Tornadoes are now more transparent.
*Increased the item stack limit of Cinnamon Roll and Evergreen Gin from 1 to 30.
*Increased the lifesteal cap from {{dv|60|50|40|20}} to {{dv|80|70|60|50}}.
*Iron Franciscas and Lead Tomahawks are now only recovered 25% of the time instead of 50% of the time.
*Nanotech and Raider's Talisman's tooltips will now list the player's current stacked crit boost.
*Reduced the catch chance of Fish of Light and Fish of Night from 3% to 2%.
*Removed unnecessary light produced by Silva healing orbs.
*Removed unnecessary sounds from the Biome Blade projectiles.
*Increased Shadowspec Bar sell price from {{value|0|27|50}} to {{gc|70}}.
*Significantly reduced the number of Souls granted by opening Soul Fish.
*The Steampunker now sells Astral Solution outside of the Astral Infection.
*The Sulphurous Sea has a new map background.
*Reduced the amount of dust produced by main Terra Bullets by 33%, and split Terra Bullets by 50%.
*The [[Blast Barrel]], [[Consecrated Water]], [[Epidemic Shredder]], [[Luminous Striker]], [[Slick Cane]], [[The Syringe]], and [[Utensil Poker]] now have flavor tooltips.
*Using any non-weapon item will no longer consume Spy stealth.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where Celestial Reaper could damage Town NPCs or the Eternia Crystal.
*Fixed a bug where Golem was dropping Essences of Sunlight directly in Expert Mode.
*Fixed a bug where Lunar Kunai hitboxes were much larger than intended.
*Fixed a bug where Skeletron's magic attacks were dealing way too much damage post-Moon Lord.
*Fixed a bug where Yharon was permanently enraged in multiplayer.
*Fixed a bug where defeating Astrum Aureus would crash the game in multiplayer.
*Fixed a bug where most Calamity mod compatibility calls didn't work.
*Fixed a bug where the Bandit sold Bear's Eye randomly instead of when her name was Laura.
*Fixed a bug where the Traveling Merchant would literally say "[Name of Merchant]".
*Fixed a bug where you could continue to accrue negative lifesteal points (blocking lifesteal for extended periods of time) by attacking enemies, even those immune to lifesteal.
*Fixed a bug with Calamity's biome syncing code.
*Fixed a bug with Calamity's improved Lihzahrd Temple generation which caused other mod worldgen to break.
*Fixed a bug with Ethereal Subjugator where it couldn't summon its last minion (e.g. only 3 ghosts instead of 4 if you have 2 minion slots).
*Fixed a despawning problem with enraged Plaguebringer Goliath.
*Fixed an issue that prevented Amidias' party hat from being properly placed.
*Fixed an issue where Aquatic Scourge wouldn't drop its weapons outside of Hardmode on Normal difficulty.
*Fixed an issue where Galeforce's arrows didn't go the right way.
*Fixed an issue with the immunity frames of Heavenly Gale's green Exo Arrows (and the Exo Tornadoes they spawned).
*Fixed miscellaneous harmless tile framing errors.
*Fixed the held positions of many Calamity wands and staves.
*Fixed the tooltip of Alluring Bait listing its fishing power in percents.
*Forced Terraria's netcode into providing more sync packets for Rev+ Eye of Cthulhu, Queen Bee and Spazmatism. This should help ease desync issues with these bosses.
*Hopefully fixed a bug with the adult Eidolon Wyrm's multiplayer targeting.
*Improved the visuals of The Syringe and fixed minor hitbox errors.
*[[Moon Lord]] will no longer unintentionally drop its loot during Boss Rush.
*Eidolists, Providence, the Profaned Goddess, and Scorn Eaters are all now forcibly classified as organic for the purposes of finite use weaponry.
*Fixed Radiant Star and Quasar's falling stars not dealing Spy damage.
