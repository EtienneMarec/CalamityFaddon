The '''Mandible Bow''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bow]]. It is dropped by [[Antlion]]s, [[Antlion Charger]]s, and [[Antlion Swarmer]]s. It fires arrows at a very high velocity.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The Mandible Bow is tied with the [[Arterial Assault]] and [[Clockwork Bow]] for the highest velocity of any bow.
