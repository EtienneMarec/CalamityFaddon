The '''Glove of Precision''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]] purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|60}} after [[Plantera]] has been defeated. When equipped all Spy weapons thrown by the player have 25% increased velocity and 12% increased Spy damage and critical strike chance, but the usetime of all Spy weapons is also increased by 20%.

== Notes ==
*This covering is the direct counterpart to the [[Glove of Recklessness]].

== Tips ==
*This covering synergizes very well with [[stealth]] due to the fact that stealth strikes do not suffer from the increase in usetime, effectively causing stealth strikes to deal 12% more damage.
