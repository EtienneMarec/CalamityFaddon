'''Frozen Hell''' is a [[biome]] added by the Calamity Mod which spawns upon the defeat of the [[The Wall of Bronze]]. It generates in a 600x600 block square from the point where the Wall of Bronze died, replacing any [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/The_Underworld?so=search Underworld]  tiles with their frozen equivalents. Any lava will also be replaced with thin ice.

Lava placed manually inside of the biome will instantly turn into [[tgc:Ice_Rod|temporary ice blocks]].

Since the Frozen Hell biome counts as both an [[tgc:Ice_biome|Ice Biome]] and [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/The_Underworld?so=search The Underworld], any of the bosses able to be spawned in said biomes can be spawned here. [[tgc:Rain|Blizzards]] are also able to occur in the biome.

==Notes==
*Both natural and placed Underworld blocks can be converted into Frozen Hell blocks.
*Frozen Hell blocks placed outside of The Underworld will not create an artificial Frozen Hell biome.
*[[Brimstone Crag]] blocks can not be converted into Frozen Hell blocks.
**This includes the special lava found within the crags.
==Tips==
*It may be a good idea to defeat [[The Wall of Bronze]] away from any builds containing Underworld blocks, as they will be converted to Frozen Hell blocks upon its death.
*Freezing areas of hell can be an easy way to drain the lava, as it will all be converted into relatively harmless thin ice blocks.
*Almost all of the enemies in frozen hell are weak to the Fire and Holy [[Elemental System|Elements]], making weapons that deal both damage types very effective.
**Conversely, weapons that deal Ice or Unholy damage should be avoided.

==Trivia==
*Frozen Hell's [[Music|theme]] is [[Music|The Long Dream]], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
**The biomes previous song was "Antarctic Reinforcement (Structured Mix)". This was only to be a placeholder until The Long Dream was finished.
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Eerie''' will play instead.
*Frozen Hell has the most biome specific bosses out of any biome in the game at 6, narrowly beating out the [[Jungle]] at 5.
*The biome has existed as a concept for a fairly long time alongside its signature boss, [[The Wall of Bronze]].
