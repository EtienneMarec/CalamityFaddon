The '''1.1.5''' update added [[Cryogen]], [[The Hive Mind]], [[The Perforators]], and a large assortment of new [[weapons]] and resprites.

== Additions ==
*Added Cryogen, The Hive Mind, The Perforators.
*Added [[Aurora Spirit]], [[Cryocore]], Cryocore (buffed), [[Dank Creeper]], [[Dark Heart]], [[Perforator Mini]].
*Added [[Accretion Disk]], [[Aorta]], [[Ark of the Elements]], [[Bittercold Staff]], [[Blood Clot Staff]], [[Cosmic Bolter]], [[Cosmic Rainbow]], [[Dank Staff]], [[Effluvium Bow]], [[Elemental Eruption]], [[Elemental Ray]], [[Eviscerator]], [[Firestorm Cannon]], [[Floodtide]], [[Glacial Crusher]], [[Ice Star]], [[Meowthrower]], [[Perfect Dark]], [[Planetary Annihilation]], [[Rot Ball]], [[Shaderain Staff]], [[Shadethrower]], [[Shadowdrop Staff]], [[Snowstorm Staff]], [[Spatial Lance]], [[Spectre Rifle]], [[Sun Spirit Staff]], [[Terra Flameburster]], [[Terra Ray]], [[Tooth Ball]], [[True Ark of the Ancients]], [[Vein Burster]], and [[Warblade]].
*Added [[Axe of Purity]] and [[Inferna Cutter]].
*Added [[Bloody Worm Tooth]], [[Rotten Brain]], and [[Soul of Cryogen]].
*Added [[Blood Clot (buff)|Blood Clot]], [[Burning Blood]], [[Dank Creeper (buff)|Dank Creeper]], [[Reaver Rage]], [[Solar Spirit (buff)]], [[Tarra Life]], [[Xeroc Rage]], and [[Xeroc Wrath]].
*Added [[Bloody Worm Food]], [[Cryo Key]], and [[Teratoma]].
*Added [[Blood Sample]], [[Cryo Bar]], [[Galactica Singularity]], and [[True Shadow Scale]].

== Changes ==
*[[Desert Scourge]] now only has a 4% chance to spawn a tile of [[Water]] upon hit instead of 50%.
*Decreased the effectiveness of Reaver armor's set bonus' thorns effect by 75%.
*[[Black Lens]]es can now be crafted with 1 [[Lens]] and 1 [[Black Dye]] at a [[Dye Vat]].
*Nerfed [[Catastrophe Claymore]]'s damage from 60 to 55, and reduced projectile range.
*Reworked [[Coral Spout]]'s projectiles to resemble water instead of coral. <!---Visual only--->
*Buffed [[Exsanguination Lance]]'s shot speed from 5 to 8.
*[[Frost armor]] can now be crafted:
**[[Frost Helmet]] can now be crafted with 6 Cryo Bars and 1 [[Frost Core]].
**[[Frost Breastplate]] can now be crafted with 10 Cryo Bars and 1 Frost Core.
**[[Frost Leggings]] can now be crafted with 8 Cryo Bars and 1 Frost Core.
*[[Galactica Blade]] now uses 5 Galactica Singularities in its recipe.
*[[Gallant Pickaxe]] now uses 1 Galactica Singularity in its recipe.
*[[Ice Machine]]s and [[Lava Charm]]s can now be crafted with [[Lead Bar]]s instead of just [[Iron Bar]]s.
*[[Reaver armor]]'s set bonus now grants the Reaver Rage buff when damaged instead of providing [[Titanium armor]]'s shadow dodge effect.
*[[Tarragon armor]]'s set bonus' [[Heartreach]] and [[Calm]] buffs are now active at all times, and replaced its regeneration boost with the Terra Life buff which activates on enemy hits.
*[[Xeroc armor]]'s set bonus now grants the Xeroc Rage and Xeroc Wrath buffs when damaged, and spawns different projectiles based on what damage is being inflicted.
