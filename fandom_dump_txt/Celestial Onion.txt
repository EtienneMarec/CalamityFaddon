The '''Celestial Onion''' is a [[consumable]] dropped by the [[Moon Lord]]. It permanently gives the player a sixth ({{expert|seventh}}) [[covering]] slot. This effect does not stack and may only be used once per player.

If the player does not have a sixth covering slot yet, the Celestial Onion can be used, and it will grant the sixth covering slot. If in [[Expert Mode]], the seventh slot will not be granted until the player also consumes the [[Demon Heart]].

== Notes ==
*If the Demon Heart has also been consumed, the seventh slot does not appear in the player's inventory while in Normal Mode, whether there's an item inside it or not.
*It does nothing if a seventh covering slot (or more) has already been obtained through the use of other mods.

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions of the mod, the onion toggled the [[Prepare to Die/Cry]] mode instead.
**Additionally in previous versions of the mod, the slot granted by the Celestial Onion was only functional in Expert Mode worlds, like the one granted by the Demon Heart.
**In the mod's source files, the onion is referred to as 'MLGRune2' in reference to its previous function.
