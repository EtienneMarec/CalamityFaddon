The '''Illuminal Crystalfish''' is a post-[[Umbrus]] [[Enemies|enemy]] that spawns in the Lower Layer of the [[Sky Abyss]]. It rapidly swims towards its enemies dealing massive amounts of contact damage.

In death mode it creates a Ghost clone every 5 seconds, the clone has infinite health and only dies when the original fish is dead.
