The '''Tenebreus Tides''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spear]]. When swung, it launches a water spear that deals 65% of the spear’s damage. When an enemy is struck with the spear or its main projectile, three swords or spears appear around the struck target. The secondary wave of swords and spears deal half the damage of the projectile that spawned them, and will disappear and return to attack an enemy up to two more times before disappearing, similarly to the [[Influx Waver]].
