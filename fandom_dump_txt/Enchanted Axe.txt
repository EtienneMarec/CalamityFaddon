The '''Enchanted Axe''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spy]] [[boomerang]] that is the direct upgrade to both the [[Iron Francisca]] and [[Lead Tomahawk]]. When used, it throws an enchanted axe that travels a medium distance and acts similarly to a boomerang. After reaching its maximum distance or hitting a block, it will fire a magical axe at an enemy that passes through walls and pierces once, dealing about 15% more than the weapon's base damage. The main axe cannot pass through blocks and pierces indefinitely.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Enchanted Axe will cause the next axe thrown to release eight additional magical axes in a circular pattern around the main axe. This effect takes place up to twice along the axe’s path outwards, if it doesn't hit a block. Additionally, the main axe is thrown further.
