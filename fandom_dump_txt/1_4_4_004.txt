The '''1.4.4.004''' (or '''1.4.4.4''') update added several new items, most notably [[Dyes]] and [[Water Fountains]], along with many balance and boss changes.

{{toc limit|3|right="yes"}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Forbidden Circlet]].
*Added [[Abyss Water Fountain]], [[Astral Water Fountain]], [[Sulphuric Water Fountain]], and [[Sunken Water Fountain]].
*Added [[Lion Heart]], [[Relic of Deliverance]], [[Tundra Flame Blossoms Staff]], and [[Valkyrie Ray]].
*Added [[Astral Blue Dye]], [[Astral Orange Dye]], [[Astral Swirl Dye]], [[Ceaseless Dye]], [[Defiled Flame Dye]], [[Dragon Soul Dye]], [[Elemental Dye]], [[Endothermic Dye]], [[Nightmare Dye]], [[Reaver Dye]], [[Shadowspec Dye]], and [[Stratus Dye]].
*Added [[Adrenaline Hair Dye]], [[Rage Hair Dye]], [[Stealth Hair Dye]], and [[Wing Time Hair Dye]].
*Added [[Joyful Heart]].
*Added [[The Father]] and [[Tundra Flame Blossom (buff)|Tundra Flame Blossom]] [[buffs]].
*Added [[Energy Shell Cooldown]] and [[Profaned Weakness]] [[debuffs]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*Added the [[Reactive Damage Reduction]] system.
*Reduced anti-butcher trigger from 10% of boss health to 50% of boss health.
*Forced some boss drops to not appear outside the world bounds.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Slightly separated the worms from each other.
*Now drops {{dv|80-150|100-180}} Fallen Stars.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Buffed passive [[damage reduction]] from 0% to 15%.
*Nerfed Brimling damage reduction from 20% to 15%.
*Now fires an arc of hellblasts rather than a random shotgun.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Added a small delay to the charge attack in [[Revengeance Mode]].

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Now forces the rain to stop.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Increased damage reduction in Revengeance Mode from 0% to 10%.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Removed a status message when defeated.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Increased damage reduction in Revengeance Mode from 5% to 10%.

==== [[The Old Duke]] ====
*Now forces the rain to stop.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Buffed damage reduction in Revengeance Mode from 0% to 15%.
*Buffed tentacles' damage reduction in Revengeance Mode from 0% to 10%.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Now gains 8 defense per limb alive in Revengeance Mode instead of 9999.
*In Revengeance Mode, if the head's health is reduced to 66%, all limbs are instantly killed and it moves to the next phase.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Increased fire rates of the core’s mines in the final phase, but decreased the teleportation frequency.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Slowed down Brimstone Monsters.
*Brimstone Gigablasts now explode on tile contact at all times.

==== [[Wall of Flesh]] ====
*Now triggers the "A bloody red inferno lingers in the night..." status message when defeated instead of Brimstone Elemental or Skeletron Prime.

=== Enemies ===
*Nerfed Tier 3 [[Acid Eel]] health from {{dv|6,650|10,640}} to {{dv|5,500|8,800}}, damage from {{dv|160|272}} to {{dv|120|204}}, and damage reduction from 30% to 5%. Reduced max speed and acceleration for all tiers.
*Nerfed Tier 3 [[Flak Crab]] health from {{dv|7,500|12,000}} to {{dv|7,000|11,200}}.
*Nerfed [[Gamma Slime]] health from {{dv|6,410|10,256}} to {{dv|5,915|9,464}}, damage from {{dv|145|246}} to {{dv|130|221}}, and damage reduction from 25% to 15%.
*Nerfed Tier 3 [[Orthocera]] health from {{dv|5,700|14,820}} to {{dv|4,500|7,200}}, and damage from {{dv|180|432}} to {{dv|160|272}}. Nerfed Tier 2 health from {{dv|605|1,573}} to {{dv|605|968}}, and damage from {{dv|75|180}} to {{dv|75|127}}. Reduced max speed and acceleration for Tier 3.
*Nerfed Tier 2 [[Skyfin]] damage reduction from 10% to 5%. Nerfed Tier 3 health from {{dv|6,300|10,080}} to {{dv|1,001|1,601}}, damage from {{dv|175|297}} to {{dv|150|255}}, and damage reduction from 20% to 5%. Reduced max speed in all tiers.
*Nerfed Tier 3 [[Sulphurous Skater]] health from {{dv|6,600|10,560}} to {{dv|5,000|8,000}}, and damage from {{dv|140|238}} to {{dv|100|170}}. Reduced max speed in all tiers.
*Nerfed Tier 3 [[Trilobite]] health from {{dv|7,200|11,520}} to {{dv|4,900|7,840}}, and damage from {{dv|160|272}} to {{dv|120|204}}. Reduced max speed for Tier 3.
*Nerfed Tier 2 [[Water Leech]] damage from {{dv|100|170}} to {{dv|80|136}}. Nerfed Tier 3 damage from {{dv|172|292}} to {{dv|120|204}}.

=== Recipes ===
*[[Barracuda Gun]] now uses 1 additional [[Life Alloy]].
*[[Clock Gatlignum]] now uses 3 Life Alloys instead of 5 [[Hallowed Bar]]s.
*[[Crystal Flare Staff]] now uses 3 [[Core of Eleum|Cores of Eleum]], 8 [[Cryonic Bar]]s, and a [[Frost Core]] instead of 3 [[Essence of Eleum|Essences of Eleum]] and 15 [[Crystal Shard]]s.
*[[Eye of Desolation]] no longer requires Souls of Fright.
*Increased [[Fantasy Talisman]]s crafted per set from 100 to 150.
*[[Lazinator]] now uses 1 [[Soul of Fright]], [[Soul of Might|Might]], and [[Soul of Sight|Sight]].

=== Renames ===
*Renamed Daedalus Emblem to [[Artemis Emblem]].
*Renamed Mana Overloader to [[Mana Polarizer]].

*[[Storm Ruler]]'s tornado now scales with melee damage boosts. Nerfed damage from 95 to 80.

*Nerfed [[Fallen Paladin's Hammer]]'s damage from 101 to 81.
*Nerfed [[Subduction Slicer]]'s damage from 100 to 90.

==== Other ====
*Removed Calamity's melee speed hard-cap of 100%.
*Most [[true melee]]-weapon based effects such as the [[Fungal Symbiote]] can now be triggered by most melee weapons on true melee strikes.
*[[Artemis Emblem]] no longer grants minion knockback.
*[[Mana Polarizer]] now grants the player [[lifesteal]] at all times, with the amount of healing depending on mana ratio.

=== Additional Changes ===
*Added some new patron NPC names.
**Dazren for the [[Angler]].
**Finchi for the [[Arms Dealer]].
**Joeseph Jostar for the [[Clothier]].
**Farsni for the [[Nurse]].
*Changed [[Death Mode]] hazards in various ways:
**Reduced the speed of icicle attacks.
**Telegraphed the lightning and geyser attacks.
**Added a [[configuration option]] to customize the frequency of hazards.
*[[Astral Crate]]s now have a 50% chance to contain 10-20 Meteorite, and a 25% chance to contain 5-10 Meteorite Bars.
*[[Baby Ghost Bell Jar]] now counts as a light source.
*[[Bloodworm]] now shows up on the [[Lifeform Analyzer]] and its upgrades.
*[[Boss Rush]] no longer disables [[mounts]].
*Changed [[Gael's Greatsword]]'s tooltip.
*[[Harpy|Harpies]] can now drop [[Essence of Sunlight|Essences of Sunlight]] in [[Hardmode]].
*The [[Hermit's Box of One Hundred Medicines]] blessing can now be deactivated.
*Increased [[Lead Core]]'s drop chance from 1% to 3.33%.
*[[Lead Wizard]] now drops from [[Golem]] instead of [[Armored Digger]]s.
*Added [[Marine Kelp Planter Box]]es in [[Planetoid]] [[chests]] if the [http://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/the-thorium-mod.40788/ Thorium Mod] is enabled.
*The [[Merchant]] now sells [[Angel Statue]]s if the [[Bandit]] is present.
*[[Relic of Resilience]] no longer has health and instantly breaks from any damage. In return, any enemy near the shards are inflicted with the [[Profaned Weakness]] debuff.
*[[Stuffed Fish]] now include herbs from the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/shadows-of-abaddon-mod.46605/ Shadows of Abaddon Mod] if it is enabled.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed the [[Adrenaline]] and [[Rage Meter]]s disappearing in dedicated Multiplayer servers.
*Fixed a bug where [[Dark Energy|Dark Energies]] do not despawn.
*Fixed a bug where [[The Devourer of Gods]] respawned if it was killed too quickly.
*Fixed a tooltip error with the [[Draconic Elixir]] where it displayed outdated numbers.
*Fixed [[Sulphurous Sand]] being placed when the player tries to place down [[Hardened Sulphurous Sandstone]].
*Fixed an oversight where player-placed [[Sulphurous Sand]] didn't count towards an artificial [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]].
*Fixed the [[Super Dummy]] disappearing in Multiplayer.
*Fixed an oversight where the custom [[rarity]] for the [[Svantechnical]] wasn't properly used.
