'''Wulfrum Bars''' are [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[crafting material]]s dropped by [[Wulfrum Enemies]]. They are used solely to craft the Wulfrum-tier items.

== Notes ==
*3-5 Wulfrum Bars can be obtained from [[Wooden Crate]]s and 5-8 Wulfrum Bars can be obtained from [[Iron Crate]]s at a 25% drop chance.
*Large quantities of Wulfrum Bars can be obtained from the [[The Scrapyard Ensemble]].
*Extra Wulfrum Bar are best turned into Wulfrum Knives for profit (5 silver per bar, rather than 80 copper)

== Trivia ==
*The word "Wulfrum" is a modification of "wolfram", the alternate name for Tungsten.
*Wulfrum Bars were previously named "Wulfrum Metal Scraps".
**Before this they were called "Wulfrum Shards".
