'''Hornet Round armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[22 Hornet Round]]s, [[Klepticoin]]s, [[Soul of Plight]]s and [[Scoria Bar]]s. 

It consists of a '''Hornet Round Helm''', '''Hornet Round Mantleplate''', and '''Hornet Round Leggins'''.

== Stats ==
The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*122 defense
*100% increased ranged damage
*100% increased minion damage
*50% increased movement speed
*+220 max life and mana
*+6 max minions
*all bullets convert into 22 hornet rounds.
*Standing still grants +2 life regen.
*Spawns 22 auric dwarfs that attack enemies. Pressing {{key|Armor set Bonus}} makes the dwarfs disappear but causes the player to become a hornet that can fly indefinitely with drastically increased movement speed.
**Short dwarfs ram the enemy while taller dwarfs shoot the enemy with 22 hornet rounds
