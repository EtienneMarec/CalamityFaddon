The '''Angry Dog''' is a rare [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Snow biome]]. It charges and rams into the player at high speeds, although it is slow at turning around. When damaged to {{dv|50%|||90%}} health, it will curl up into a ball and roll towards the player with increased maneuverability. It also inflicts the [[Bleeding]] debuff on contact. It gets significantly stronger after [[Cryogen]] has been defeated, with boosts to its damage, maximum life, and defense.
