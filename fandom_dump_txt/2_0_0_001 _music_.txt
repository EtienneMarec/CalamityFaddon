{{dablink|This is the page for the '''2.0.0.001 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''2.0.0.001''' (or '''2.0.0.1''') update, also known as the '''1.4 Progress Update''', upgraded the mod to Terraria [[tgc:1.4.3.6|1.4.3.6]] (tModLoader v2022.5.103+). This means the Calamity Music Mod now supports many features that were released alongside the Vanilla Terraria 1.4.0.1 series of updates.

This Music Mod version is compatible with the Main mod version [[2.0.0.001]]

== Changes ==
*Added [[Journey Mode]] research support to all Calamity [[Music Boxes]] except those that are unobtainable. <!-- Boss Rush T1-5 boxes -->
*Resprited the mod icon.
*Rewrote the mod description

== Removals ==
*Removed the Music Mod configuration file. <!-- Title theme was baked into the new "Calamity Style" main menu theme -->
