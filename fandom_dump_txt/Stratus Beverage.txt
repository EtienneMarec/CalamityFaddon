'''Stratus Beverage''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[potion]]. It grants the '''Stratus Beverage''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces damage by 33%, but causes projectile shooting weapons to fire out two extra homing stratus stars that deal the weapon's damage. These stars scale with the player's highest damage stat.

While the effects of the Everclear debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
