The '''Twisted Netherite Shovel''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[pickaxe]] that digs out squares of 10 by 10 tiles centered around where the player is clicking. It inflicts the [[Wither]] debuff on hit. It is important to note that the shovel's pickaxe power is incredibly low, and can only effectively clear out lower tier blocks.
