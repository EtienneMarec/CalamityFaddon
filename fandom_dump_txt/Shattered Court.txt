The '''Shattered Court''' is an unique structure that generates in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. It covers a middle-sized area and resembles a half-destroyed house with something like garden on its backyard. The Shattered Court always generates in the end of the [[Brimstone Crag]], very close to the edge of the map.

If the [[Nightmare Mode]] is active in the current world, during the [[Hardmode]] the player will encounter the [[Brimstone Elemental]] there. If the [[Nightmare Mode]] isn't enabled, the Shattered Court structure will be desolate.
