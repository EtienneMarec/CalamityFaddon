The '''Envoidment''' is an uninflictable debuff that nullifies the [[Defense|defense]] and damage reduction of whoever this debuff is inflicted on. After 3 seconds since this debuff has been inflicted, the target will lose 1 HP for the first second, but then the damage amount will increase by 1 every frame, capping at 900 HP per second after 15 seconds with this debuff inflicted has passed.  <!---This debuff will become inflictable in some of the future updates.--->

== Trivia ==
*This debuff's tooltip, "Omnia ad nihilum" roughly translates from Latin as "all to nothing".
