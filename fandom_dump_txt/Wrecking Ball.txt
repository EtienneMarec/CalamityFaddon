The '''Wrecking Ball''' is a Godseeker mode [[flail]] purchased from the [[Collector]] after its respective [[Blueprint]] has been turned in. It swings a metal ball like a broadsword that spawns more metal balls that fall from the sky when striking enemies. The additional balls inflict either rogue or ranged damage.

{{modifier|meleealt}}

== Notes==
The [[Blueprint]] for this item can be obtained by killing the Guardian Defender if it is the last [[Profaned Guardian]] alive.
