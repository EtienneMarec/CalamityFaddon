The '''Anahita Clone''' was a [[Hardmode]] [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] that used to spawn during the [[Leviathan and Anahita]] boss fight, summoned by the Anahita at {{dv|66%|||100%}} and 33% of her health. They launched water spears at the player that would be fired at an increased rate in Death Mode, and were invincible.
