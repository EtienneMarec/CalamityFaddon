The '''Sulphurskin Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Sulphurskin''' [[buff]], which reduces the damage over time dealt by the water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]].

== Notes ==
*The Sulphurskin's effect of reducing severity of the water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] stacks with the [[Sulphurous armor]]'s set bonus in the following manner:
**While the buff is active without the set bonus, the amount of minimum life regen lost is 2 instead of 8, and further reduced by 1 every 5.83 seconds instead of 4.17 seconds.
**With both the armor and the Sulphurskin buff, the amount of minimum life regen lost is 1, and further reduced by 1 every 8.33 seconds.
**Both reduce the amount of life regen lost from the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]] by 2 and can stack with each other.
