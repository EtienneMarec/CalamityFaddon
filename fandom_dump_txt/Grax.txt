The '''Grax''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[hamaxe]] that has a range five tiles larger than most other tools, and is the final upgrade of the [[Silver Axe]] or [[Tungsten Axe]]. Striking an enemy with it will grant the player the '''Grax Boost''' [[buff]] for 10 seconds, which increases [[defense]] by 30, damage reduction by 10% and melee damage by 20%. Right clicking with the item causes the weapon to swing without the ability to cut down [[trees]] or destroy [[walls]].
