The '''Cosmic Plushie''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Death Mode]]-exclusive [[pet]] summoning item that summons a miniature [[The Devourer of Gods|Devourer of Gods]] gijinka. It is obtained by opening The Devourer of Gods' [[Treasure Bag]] with a [[Hardcore]] character while [[Death Mode]] is active.

The Chibii Devourer will follow the player on foot. If the player moves or flies far away, it will create and fly on a miniature [[Cosmic Guardian]] to keep pace. Whenever it summons or dispels the worm, the pet will generate a small burst of red light akin to the player summoning or dispelling their own mount. Chibii Devourer emits a bright pink aura trail as it moves.

When the player and Chibii Devourer are in very dark areas, Chibii Devourer may meow, scream, or stab the player for 500 damage. If it stabs the player, it will additionally spawn a [[Cosmic Guardian]]. If the player character has the name "Terry" then Chibii Devourer will instead spawn [[The Devourer of Gods]]' final form when it stabs the player.

The Chibii Devourer also has the unique effect of disabling the [[Demonshade armor]]'s friendly Red Devil set bonus. With the full set equipped and Chibii Devourer active, the Red Devil buff and minion will not take effect.

== Trivia ==
*Its name and appearance are a reference to [https://www.deviantart.com/kerubii Kerubii], an artist that creates fan work of Calamity and depicts the bosses as female humans.
*It was programmed by and implemented for Terry N. Muse, one of the main lore writers for the mod, as a reward for defeating all the [[bosses]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] without getting hit.
*Chibii Devourer has a top running speed of just over 66 miles per hour.
*Whenever the Cosmic Plushie is used, it makes the same meowing sound effect as [[Meowmere]]'s projectiles.
*Upon being stabbed by the Chibii Devourer, it will trigger the same response as to bringing down [[The Devourer of Gods]]'s HP to 25%.
*The tooltip 'Sharp objects possibly included' refers to the fact that the Chibii Devourer might stab the player in the dark.
**This characteristic is shared with the [[Companion Cube]] pet.
*The pet can also be obtained by placing the Treasure Bag into a chest and then opening it with a new Hardcore character.
*Talking to the [[Drunk Princess]] with the Chibii Devourer active will display a unique quote.
