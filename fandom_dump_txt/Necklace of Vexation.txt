The '''Necklace of Vexation''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that increases damage by 20% when the player has under 50% life. While wearing [[Reaver armor]] with any headpiece, all attacks will inflict the [[Cursed Inferno]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]] onto enemies for two seconds.
