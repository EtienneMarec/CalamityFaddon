'''Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch''' (Japanese: 催眠術にかかった魔女パンドラ ''Saimin-jutsu ni kakatta majo Pandora'') is an optional [[Hardmode]] [[Challenges|boss]] fought anywhere. While she can be fought at anytime in Hardmode, she is generally intended to be fought after the Mechanical Bosses.

==Spawn==
Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch can be summoned by putting a [[Skeletron Mask]] in an [[Chests|Ashen Chest]], and renaming that Chest "Infernal_Fire.exe", while there is a [[Events|Blood Moon]]. This will consume the Mask inside it, and Pandora will appear.

==Behavior==
When summoned, Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch will appear above the player. She tends to teleport near the player upon attacking. After reaching a certain threshold, she will conjure {{dv|1|2}} Nebula Blazes per attack. There can only be 10 nebula blazes at a time, and upon hitting that limit, will be fired out in all directions, homing onto the  player after a given time.

===Attacks===

====Phase 1====
*Fires out a barrage of {{dv|1|2|3}} Shadow Balls and {{dv|2|3|4}} Fireballs at the player, both of them aggressively homing at the player for a certain time before expiring.
*After {{dv|5|4}} rounds of Shadow Balls and Fireballs, fires 2 laser walls at the player. The order goes from Left-Right, Up-Down, Left-Right, Up-Down, and finally in all 4 cardinal directions.
*After the 2nd barrage, Fires out a volley of {{dv|3|6}} Focused lasers in a spread. These lasers can come in 2 colors: Purple and Red. These are merely visuals.
*After the 3rd barrage, Conjures a raining barrage of falling stars for 5 seconds. While this is happening, she begins summoning {{dv|2|4}} more nebula blazes. They share the same limits as ones summoned in between her attacks, and functions the same as one conjured by the former.

====Phase 2====
Upon reaching {{dv|50%|60%|70%}} health, She transitions into Phase 2, turning all red and black in the process. Her Phase 1 attacks remains, but she gains a new attack in conjunction with the starfall attack.

*She now rains falling stars while performing her Laser Wall and Focused Laser attack.
*After the 3rd barrage, she now teleports and follow below the player, and then fires a spread of Focused lasers {{dv|2|3}} times, with the shot predicting the player's movement.

===In Expert Mode===
*Laser walls are much faster, and now repeats 2 times.
*Focused lasers are much faster.

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===
*In Phase 2, after the 1st barrage, she summons a [[Baby_Skeletron_Head.exe]] to aid her in the fight.
**Only one can be out at a time.
*In a Laser Wall attack, She periodically fires a focused laser at the player.


===In [[Death Mode]]===
*Two Baby_Skeletron_Head.exe can now be out at a time.
