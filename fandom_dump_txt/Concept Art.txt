'''Concept Art''' refers to a drawing or sketch of a concept within a video game that has not yet been implemented. It is most often used in the early stages of game development to give artists and programmers an idea of how certain aspects of the game should look and feel. There have been many concept drawings released to the public by the Calamity mod team over the years.
