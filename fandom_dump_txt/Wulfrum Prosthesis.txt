The '''Wulfrum Prosthesis''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] dropped by [[The Inventors]] and the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it fires high-velocity blue energy bolts which cannot pierce but can curve towards nearby enemies. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), mana is not consumed and nearby enemies within a 25-tile radius emit blue particles which move towards the player. In turn, the player temporarily gains the [[Mana Regeneration]] effect and 35 bonus mana regeneration.
