The '''Phyg''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode|Hardmode]] [[Critters|Critter]] that rarely spawns in [[The Aether]] during the day. They will wander around until a player approaches. If a player approaches, they will attempt to fly away, making them hard to catch and/or kill. The fourth Phyg built its house out of Etherium, and the Big Bad Wolf for all its wailing could not tear down its walls.

== Trivia ==
*The Phyg is the Aether alternative to the [[Piggy]].
**It is also much better than the Piggy, having a higher Bacon and Coin yeild, and a higher sell value.
*The Phyg was added due to popular demand for more content revolving the Piggy.
