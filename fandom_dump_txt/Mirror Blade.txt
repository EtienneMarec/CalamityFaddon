The '''Mirror Blade''' is a Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It is dropped by the [[Ceaseless Void]]. It fires projectiles that resemble a mirror. Projectiles move based on player movement.

If an enemy is struck by the blade, the amount of contact damage that enemy deals is added to the weapon's damage. It cannot deal more than 750 damage.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*Though it initially deals noticeably less DPS than most other weapons of its tier, striking an enemy with very high contact damage (such as [[The Devourer of Gods]] or [[Polterghast]]) will significantly increase its damage output.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon used to have a mana cost of 5 on previous versions of the mod.
