'''Tequila''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Tequila''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen, but boosts damage, critical strike chance, knockback, [[defense]] and damage reduction during the day.

While the effects of the Tequila debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
