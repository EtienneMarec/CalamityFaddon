The '''Wulfrum Digging Turtle''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]]. When used with left click, a Wulfrum Turtle is thrown out in an arch above the player. While no enemies are alive, it will walk back and fourth. When it detects an enemy, it will hops towards them and explode, killing itself and dealing 200 damage. When used with right click, the Wulfrum Turtle hides in its shell and stays stationary on the ground. Nearby enemies will try to target the turtle rather than players, and cause the turtle to explode on contact. Only 4 turtles can be active at once.
