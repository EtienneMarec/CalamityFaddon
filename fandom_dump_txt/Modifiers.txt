A '''Modifier''', also known as a '''Prefix''', applies permanent changes to an item's statistics. It changes the name of an item by adding a prefix to the item's name. The Calamity Mod adds several weapon and covering modifiers to benefit the [[Spy]] class, as well as augmenting pre-existing modifiers for balancing.

All modifiers added by the Calamity Mod can be obtained through normal means, including Crafting, [[Grab bag]]s, [[NPC]] shops, and natural world generation. All added modifiers can also be reforged onto weapons and coverings at the [[Goblin Tinkerer]].

== Notes ==
*The '''Stealth Strike damage''' modifier can affect Spy weapons that do not have a stealth strike, as the modifier increases the overall damage of the weapon while at full stealth.

== Trivia ==
*The '''Sharp''' and '''Pointy''' modifiers are shared with [[Melee weapons]].
