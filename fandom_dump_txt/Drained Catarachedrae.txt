The '''Drained Catarachedrae''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] that is obtained by throwing [[Catarachedrae]] into [[Shimmer]]. While equipped, it creates a sanguine aura around the player's cursor, the radius of which is 4 blocks. Clicking will exsanguinate any enemy in the aura, dealing 12 typeless damage to them, which scales with the most powerful class, and inflicting the [[Slow]] debuff for 2 seconds.

Similarly to Catarachedrae, its drained variant has another use — throwing it into [[Sanguine Shimmer]] will absorb the blood, transforming it back to [[Shimmer]] prematurely. Additionally, the player will get the normal variant of Catarachedrae.

==Notes==
*Drained Catarachedrae will not be affected by Shimmer.

*Drained Catarachedrae is extremely useful for crowd control during [[events]], although it can still deal good single target damage to [[Challenges|bosses]].
