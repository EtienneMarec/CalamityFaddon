The '''Blobfish Plushie''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy weapons]] obtained by fishing in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] with a 5% chance. Upon being thrown, these plushies can pierce up to 2 enemies before disappearing. Additionally, it inflicts the [[tgc:Stinky|Stinky]] debuff.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Blobfish Plushie instead throws the plushie harder, causing them to explode into a bunch of lingering cottons upon hitting enemies/tiles. These cottons have 15 armor penetration, can pierce up to 3 enemies, and falls down slowly.
