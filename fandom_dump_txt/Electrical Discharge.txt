The '''Electrical Discharge''' is a Godseeker Mode [[flail]] dropped by the [[Wulfrum Megamechwyrm]]. It throws out a gravity-defying spike ball that travels through solid tiles. Upon travelling far enough or on contact with 4 targets, the flail returns to the player. On hit, the flail inflicts the '''Wither''' [[debuff]] which reduces enemy [[defense]] by 20. The first five hits spawn a blood explosion which deals 50% of the weapon's damage and provides the player with the [[Cosmic Freeze]] buff.
