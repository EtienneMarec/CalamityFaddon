'''Grandfather Clocks''' are [[furniture]] pieces that tell the in-game [[tgc:time|time]]. The Calamity Mod currently adds sixteen types of Grandfather Clocks to the game, each corresponding to specific [[Furniture sets|sets of furniture]].
