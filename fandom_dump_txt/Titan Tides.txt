The '''Titan Tides''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When used, the player channels a small spinning purple aura. The aura grows in size and spins faster over the duration of the channeling, capping at 300% of its initial size, and 5x its initial speed. As it increases in power, the weapon's damage increases, and begins inflicting [[Shred]] when roughly 4.5 seconds have passed.
