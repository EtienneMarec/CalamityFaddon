'''Cyber Armor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set. While lacking the same high stats that many early Hardmode armors provide, it gives the player several movement-based abilities.

It consists of the [[Cyber Head]], [[Cyber Booster]], and [[Cyber Skates]].

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):

*20 defense
*10% increased crit chance
*Double tap a direction to dash
*Gives the player the ability to run and slide down walls, similarly to the [[Hermes Boots]] and [[Climbing Claws]] respectively
*Hold a direction while dashing to boost, charging forward quickly while lowering your boost meter
**Waiting 5 seconds after stopping your boost will cause it to slowly recharge
*Allows the player to grind on [[Minecart Track|Tracks]]
**Pressing down will dismount the player from the track
*Press {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} to perform a trick
**Tricks will increase the players boost slightly, and will increase their crit chance by 5%, up to a maximum of 25%
**Their crit chance will return to 0% if they are not grinding or in the air
