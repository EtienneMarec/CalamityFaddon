The '''Yharon's Kindle Staff''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It is dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. When used, it summons a draconid to attack enemies by slamming into them and rebounding away, and shooting homing fire balls. The draconid and its projectiles can pass through blocks. Each draconid requires 5 minion slots to summon.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Notes ==
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] and her brothers take only half damage from draconids.

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions of the mod, the tablet used to summon a "Son of Yharon", which is now a pet summoned by the [[McNuggets]] item.
*The minion's sprite is referred to as "AngryChicken" in the code.
*The item is based off of an [https://www.amazon.com/Amazon-Fire-Tablet-Family/ Amazon Kindle Fire Tablet]
