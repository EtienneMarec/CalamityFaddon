The '''Squirrel Duke Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and attack enemies by constantly shooting streams of homing bubbles. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
