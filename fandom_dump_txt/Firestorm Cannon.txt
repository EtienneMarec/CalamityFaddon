The '''Firestorm Cannon''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that is a weaponized version of the [[Flare Gun]]. Like the Flare Gun, it uses [[Flare]]s as ammunition.

The Firestorm Cannon has two firing modes, both of which [[Autoswing|have auto-use]]:
*Left click will fire a constant but inaccurate stream of piercing flares; sometimes firing two flares at once. These flares disappear very quickly after sticking to a tile.
*Right click fires an inaccurate spread of four or five flares. These flares do not pierce but last a bit longer when sticking to tiles. These flares deal 140% of the weapon's damage.
