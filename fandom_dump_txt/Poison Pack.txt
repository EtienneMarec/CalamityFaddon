The '''Poison Pack''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws up to 3 gravity-affected spiky balls, with the amount of balls thrown depending on stack size. The balls inflict the [[Poisoned]] [[debuff]], bounce and slide on contact with solid blocks, and decelerate to a stop as it travels. The balls will despawn after a long amount of time, or after right-clicking with the weapon.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Poison Pack will cause the next spiky ball thrown to release spore clouds as it travels.
