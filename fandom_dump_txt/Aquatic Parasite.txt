The '''Aquatic Parasite''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Sulphurous Sea]]. It swims towards the player and can jump out of the water very high in an attempt to catch them.

== Trivia ==
*Its original sprite was intended to be a resprite of the [[Desert Medallion]].
