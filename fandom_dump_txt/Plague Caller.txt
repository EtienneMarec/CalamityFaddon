The '''Plague Caller''' is a [[pet]] summoning item that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It summons a miniature Plaguebringer that is similar to the ones spawned by the [[Infected Remote]] to follow the player.

== Trivia ==
*The miniature Plaguebringer sprite is taken from an unused asset in the code for a scrapped summoner weapon of the same name and with a similar appearance. The Plague Caller summon weapon eventually got redesigned into the [[Fuel Cell Bundle]].
**The miniature Plaguebringer is also used for one of the [[Infected Remote]]'s attacks.
*This pet was added to the [[Community modifications#Calamity's Vanities|Calamity's Vanities]] fan mod, prior to it being added to Calamity proper.
**The pet item from the vanity mod is called the ''[https://terrariamods.gamepedia.com/Calamity%27s_Vanities/Plaguebringer_Power_Cell Plaguebringer Power Cell]''. This is referenced in the Fuel Cell Bundle, which was the redesign of the original Plague Caller.
