The '''Flame Boy Popsicle''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Spy weapons|Spy Weapon]] that throws out a gravity-affected Popsicle. Upon hitting an enemy, it bounces off, being able to be picked up again. Picking it up empowers the weapon, alongside increasing the rank they currently have. The rank mechanic are as follows:

== Trivia ==
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity appears as a Lusty Orange ({{color|#EEB2AA ●|#EEB2AA}}) color.
*The weapon's mechanic is directly inspired by the [https://soul-knight.fandom.com/wiki/%22Lollipop%22 "Lollipop"] from the game [https://soul-knight.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Knight_Wiki Soul Knight].
*The weapon is based off the dedicatee's OC, Miracle Boy, and the popsicle he is sometimes depicted with.
