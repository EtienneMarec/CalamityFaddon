The '''1.1.7.861''' update added [[Bloodstone]] and a set of [[weapons]] themed around it. The update also buffed [[The Devourer of Gods]] and focused on reducing the amount of dust created by many of the mod's [[bosses]] and [[enemies]] in order to reduce lag.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Bloodstone]] and [[Bloodstone Core]].
*Added [[Arterial Assault]], [[Bloodfire Arrow]], [[Lacerator]], [[Mourningstar]], [[The Mutilator]], [[Phangasm]], [[Sanguine Flare]], [[Solar Flare]], [[Telluric Glare]], and [[Viscera]].
*Added [[Bloodflare Core]] and [[Psychotic Amulet]].
*Added [[Arid Artifact]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*Defeating [[Providence, the Profaned God]] will now cause the following bosses and enemies to gain increased health, damage, defense, and coin drops along with dropping varying amounts of Bloodstone:
**[[Brimstone Elemental]].
**[[Calamitas]].
**[[Cultist Assassin]].
**[[Despair Stone]].
**[[Ravager]].
**[[Scryllar]].
**[[Soul Slurper]].
*Brimstone Elemental and [[Bumblebirb]] now have gores.
*Calamitas changes:
**Lasers and [[Catastrophe]]'s fireballs now produce less dust.
**[[Cataclysm]]'s flamethrower projectiles now last 1.5 seconds each instead of 1.
**All projectiles during the fight no longer gain increased damage in [[Prepare to Die]] and Prepare to Cry modes.
**Brothers are now immune to all debuffs.
*Cultist Assassins now drop 1-2 [[Essence of Chaos|Essences of Chaos]] instead of 1, and now spawn more frequently.
*Despair Stones, Scryllars, and Soul Slurpers now drop 1-2 Essences of Chaos.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Buffed head's contact damage from {{dv|330|650}} to {{dv|350|750}}.
**Buffed tail's contact damage from {{dv|55|71}} to {{dv|200|320}}.
**Now has 122 segments instead of 102.
**Head now takes 4 times more damage.
**Decreased base speed.
**Now gains increases to its speed as its health lowers.
**Now burrows into the ground and despawns if all players are dead.
**Head and tail now take 1 damage from any attack that would deal over half of its max health,  accompanied by the message, "You think...you can butcher...ME!?" if its the head that's hit.
**Head and tail now create less dust when hit.
**Head no longer inflicts [[Chilled]].
*[[The Hive Mind]]'s Vile Clot projectiles now produce less dust.
*All of [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s projectiles and [[Detonating Flame]]s now produce less or no dust.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] now produces less dust when hit and her missiles now explode on blocks and produce less dust.
*All of Providence, the Profaned God's projectiles now produce less or no dust.
*All of Providence, the Profaned God's projectiles except her Holy Spears now inflict [[Holy Flames]] for 2 seconds instead of 1.
*Ravager can now only naturally spawn on the [[Surface]], and its spawn rate has been slightly reduced.

=== Items ===
*Buffed [[Alluvion]]'s damage from 125 to 140.
*Buffed [[Ark of the Cosmos]]' use time from 18 to 15.
*[[Asgardian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Aegis]]' explosion now hits every 4 frames.
*[[Azathoth]]'s yoyo and orbs now hit every other frame and its orbs now deals the weapon's full damage instead of 75%.
*[[The Burning Sky]]'s meteors now last for 2 seconds instead of 5.
*[[Counter Scarf]]'s [[Scarf Cooldown]] now lasts for 5 seconds instead of 2.
*[[Effluvium Bow]]'s arrows now collide with blocks.
*Nerfed [[Galaxia]]'s use time from 15 to 17.
*Altered [[Heart of the Elements]]' lighting and made it no longer passively damage enemies that get near the player.<!---Done to reduce lag--->
*All [[legendary weapons]] now use the vanilla game's built in bools to alter their stats instead of custom ones.
*[[The Obliterator]]'s yoyo now hits every 3 frames.

=== Other ===
*[[Aerialite Ore]] can now be blown up after the [[Eater of Worlds]] or [[Brain of Cthulhu]] have been defeated.
*[[Chaotic Ore]] can now be blown up after [[Golem]] has been defeated, and no longer appears on the [[Metal Detector]] before Hardmode.
*[[Charred Ore]] can now be blown up after [[Plantera]] has been defeated, and no longer appears on the Metal Detector before Hardmode.
*[[Cryonic Ore]] can now be blown up after [[Cryogen]] has been defeated, and no longer appears on the Metal Detector before Hardmode.
*[[Perennial Ore]] can now be blown up after [[Plantera]] has been defeated, and no longer appears on the Metal Detector before Hardmode.
*[[Uelibloom Ore]] can now be blown up after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated, and no longer appears on the Metal Detector before Hardmode.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Ambrosial Ampoule]] and [[Honey Dew]] not providing protection against The Plaguebringer Goliath's large [[Plague Charger]]s.
