The '''Frostflow Edge''' is a unique Epilogue [[broadsword]]. It always deals 1 damage to enemies, regardless of any modifiers or attack boosts the player may have, and it cannot inflict critical hits. Whenever an enemy is struck with this weapon, their health is decreased by 4% of their max health, ignoring all defense and damage reduction. Additionally, the secondary fire (right-click) will cause the player to lose 50% of their maximum health and all of their current mana, in exchange for imbuing the sword for one minute. During this time, swings will unleash longer-ranged swarms of blades that will decrease enemy health by 2%.

Being submerged in water with less than 40 defense will start draining 20 life per second from the player.
Its best [[modifier]] is '''Light''', as the Frostflow Edge has minimal knockback and does not benefit from increased damage or critical strike chance.

== Notes ==
*The enemy or boss must be vulnerable to damage for this weapon to be effective. The Frostflow Edge cannot damage enemies that are completely unable to be hit.
*Its life reduction effect applies to the head segment of worm enemies, but not body or tail segments.
*The [[Invincible]] buff's effects will not apply when this weapon is held.
*Its life reduction effect is not affected by [[rage]] or [[adrenaline]] damage increases.

== Tips ==
*It benefits most from equipment and covering modifiers that maximize melee speed.
**It also benefits from melee size buffs, if only to make it easier to land hits.
*This weapon can become excessively powerful against enemies with very high health, due to the percentage-based life drain effect.
*As the empowering right click cannot be used when mana is not full, it may be worth using [[Mana Regeneration Potions]] or [[Astral Injection]] even on a melee build.
*This weapon can be useful against bosses which enter periods of very high defense and/or DR, such as [[XC-39 Oizys]], as the player can continue to deal decent damage to them when they would normally be dealing very little. Due to the weapon's very short range, caution should be taken to avoid excessive contact damage from the boss.

== Trivia ==
*The Rivers of Frost does not work on [[Target Dummies]], but does work on [[Super Dummies]].
*Dying to the self inflicted damage will display a unique death message "<PlayerName> overflowed with water"
**This is a reference to [[Sky Abyss]] enemies' insides being constructed of materials that dissolve in water.
