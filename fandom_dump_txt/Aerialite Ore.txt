'''Aerialite Ore''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ore]] which generates anywhere in the [[Underground]] upon defeating the [[Aether Valkyrie]]. It is used to craft [[Aerialite Bar]]s at a [[Furnace]]. It requires at least a [[Nightmare Pickaxe]] or a [[Deathbringer Pickaxe]] to mine. It is also dropped by [[Aero Slime]]s.
