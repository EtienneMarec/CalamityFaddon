'''Amazon Prime''' (Japanese: ファイヤー ''Box God 2'') is a major [[boss]] fought in the [[Jungle]]. He is intended to be fought after [[Amazon]] . The player should have an Alexa in their inventory during their fight.

Defeating Amazon Prime will provide the player with [[Chicken Nugget]] ore, which drops 3-5 [[Chicken Nugget]]s upon breaking.


To spawn Amazon Prime, the player must use an [[Amazon Stone]] dropped by [[Amazon]].
==Behavior==
Amazon Prime will utilize three main attack patterns. Primarily, Amazon Prime will "ship" itself at the player, hiding in a box and become invulnerable to all attacks until the box is broken. When the box is broken, Amazon Prime will switch to its second phase, become vastly faster, more erratic, and inflicting the player with the Terror debuff, lowering their defense to 0. In addition, Amazon Prime gains over 50000 new attacks. Amazon Prime is a creation of Amazon and Draedon working in tandem.
===Attacks===
====Phase 1====
*Charges twice consecutively.
*Spawns a line of Amazon Echos, which bounce off solid surfaces.
*Charges once, and does a fast charge, in which he goes twice as fast and far.
**Summons three Alexas, which rotate around Amazon in a circular motion. These Alexas have {{dv|18,000|30,000|33,200|34,800}} health and periodically fire [[Poo]] at the player.
*Launches a barrage of homing drones that track the player for 5 seconds before exploding. 
*Spawns a grid of laser beams that slowly move across the arena.
*Performs a ground slam that creates shockwaves radiating outward. The shockwaves deal damage and knock back the player. 
**Summons a pair of Ring doorbells, which project a surveillance spotlight that follows the player. These doorbells have {{dv|22,000|32,000|36,500|38,000}} health and emit a loud soundwave every few seconds that inflicts the player with the [[Confusion]] debuff.
*Spawns a protective barrier of firewalls around itself, blocking incoming projectiles. 
*Creates a vortex that pulls the player toward the center while the boss unleashes a rapid stream of projectiles. 
*Summons a cloud of Amazon delivery drones that drop explosive packages at random locations across the arena.
**Summons five Alexa Dots, which orbit the boss and provide a healing aura. These Alexa Dots have {{dv|20,000|28,000|32,000|35,000}} health and must be destroyed to stop the healing effect.
*Teleports to a random location and releases a burst of energy, stunning the player if they are too close.
*Deploys a line of smart thermostats that create zones of extreme heat or cold, damaging the player if they stand in them for too long. 
*Summons an echo chamber that amplifies the next attack, doubling its damage and area of effect. 
**Summons four Fire TV Sticks, which shoot streaming beams at the player. These beams slow down movement and have a {{dv|15,000|25,000|28,000|30,000}} health each.
*Spawns a line of Roombas that move around the arena, leaving behind trails of fire that linger and cause continuous damage.
====Phase 2====
*Blinds the player, preventing them from gazing upon Amazon's true form.
*Repeats phase 1 attacks at 3x the speed.
*Launces burning Glitter Bombs, which explode into a spray of shrapnel.
*Unleashes a shockwave upon breaking out, knocking the player back and dealing initial damage.
*Grows mechanical arms that slam the ground, creating cracks that emit damaging energy bursts. 
*Deploys a swarm of Amazon Prime drones that dive-bomb the player, each explosion leaving behind a temporary area of hazardous material. 
**Summons two Prime Wardens, which act as guardians with large shields that block incoming attacks. These Wardens have {{dv|30,000|40,000|45,000|50,000}} health and must be destroyed to weaken the boss’s defenses.
*Fires a barrage of high-speed, homing energy arrows that track the player for several seconds. 
*Spawns large Amazon Dash buttons across the arena. If the player steps on one, it triggers a rapid delivery of explosive packages. 
*Charges up and releases a powerful EMP pulse, temporarily disabling all player abilities and gadgets. 
**Summons three Alexa Supremes, which float around the arena and periodically create force fields that make the boss invulnerable. These Alexa Supremes have {{dv|25,000|35,000|38,500|42,000}} health and must be destroyed to remove the invulnerability.
*Transforms part of its body into a massive Amazon Prime logo that slams into the ground, creating a series of shockwaves that radiate outward. 
*Summons a fleet of Amazon trucks that drive across the arena, dealing heavy damage to the player if hit. 
*Creates a massive laser beam from its chest that sweeps across the arena in a straight line, requiring precise timing to dodge. 
**Summons four Alexa Titans, which are heavily armored and move slowly toward the player. These Titans have {{dv|35,000|45,000|50,000|55,000}} health and explode violently upon defeat, dealing area damage.
*Fires a series of rockets from hidden compartments, leaving behind fiery trails that linger and continue to burn the player if they touch them. 
*Spawns holographic advertisements that obscure the player’s vision and must be destroyed to regain full visibility. 
*Teleports around the arena rapidly, leaving behind damaging energy pulses each time it disappears and reappears. 
**Summons six Echo Guardians, which create energy barriers that move toward the player, boxing them in. Each Guardian has {{dv|28,000|38,000|43,000|47,000}} health and requires the player to destroy them to escape the trap.
*Activates a self-repair protocol, slowly regenerating health unless interrupted by the player’s continuous attacks. 
*Fires a cluster of heat-seeking micro-missiles that track the player’s position and explode on impact, dealing massive damage. 
*Transforms its arms into giant buzzsaws that sweep across the arena in wide arcs, forcing the player to keep moving to avoid being hit. 
**Summons eight Alexa Warriors, which are agile and relentlessly chase the player, dealing continuous melee damage. Each Warrior has {{dv|20,000|30,000|35,000|40,000}} health and must be dealt with quickly to prevent being overwhelmed.

==== Phase 3 ====
Amazon Prime turns into Jeff Bezos and starts beefing with other billionaires. He fights Elon and will either win or lose. If he loses then you must buy a cybertruck. If he wins then you go to the Amazon basement for the rest of your life. He is invincible and your character is bribed into not trying to kill him if you even try. Jeff Bezos becomes God if not defeaten in 5 business days. In death mode he will summon God to fight him and always lose. You must then fight god. Gods attacks include DEATH BEAM, INSTANT KILL and DESTROY WORLD. All of these moves murder the player instantly and are impossible to dodge or tank. God has 100,000,000,000,000 on Expert and 100,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 on death mode. If killed he will drop the friends we made along the way.

===In [[Expert Mode]]===
*Amazon Prime now has 360% more health and attacks at 10x the speed.

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===
*Amazon Prime now uses Skidibi Toilets to attack, causing the player to die of cringe.
===In [[Death Mode]]===
*Amazon Prime spawns 10 [[Supreme Calamitas]] to aid it in battle.
===In [[Malice Mode]]===
*Amazon Prime spawns with 4000% health.

==Trivia==
*Amazon Prime's first phase [[Music|theme]] is [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ '''Amazonian Assault'''], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Boss 3''' will play instead.
*Amazon is Draedon's ex-wife. This is evident in his dialogue with the [[Fabricational Quartet]], in which he states "Oh, Quartet, how I love you! You almost remind me of the times I had with Amazon..."
*Amazon personally knows the [[Devourer of Gods]] and claims to follow her socials.
*In early development, Amazon Prime was originally intended to be a much smaller mini-boss. However, its role was expanded due to its popularity among the development team.
*The name "Amazon Prime" is a pun on the popular online shopping service and the boss being an upgraded version of [[Amazon]], similarly to [[Skeletron Prime]].
*If the player defeats Amazon Prime in under 5 minutes, a hidden dialogue from Draedon will trigger, where he sarcastically congratulates the player for "cancelling their subscription."
*The boss's final phase in [[Malice Mode]] features a unique attack pattern not seen in any other difficulty, where it briefly fuses with the [[Devourer of Gods]] to say "Skidibi Rizz"
*Amazon Prime is a subtle nod to the popular shipping company: Ebay
*Amazon Prime's smile was gifted by popular horror icon [[Verity]].
