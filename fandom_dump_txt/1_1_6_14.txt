The '''1.1.6.14''' update made a few nerfs to [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] and her boss servants, as well as respriting several [[items]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
*Decreased [[Desert Scourge]]'s speed.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] changes:
**Decreased the frequency at which she spawns [[Plague Charger]]s and [[Plaguebringer Shade]]s.
**Plaguebringer Shades are now more visible.
**Nerfed Plague Charger’s health from {{dv|400|520}} to {{dv|300|390}}.
**Nerfed large Plague Charger’s health from {{dv|450|585}} to {{dv|350|455}}.

=== [[Items]] ===
*[[Cursed Capper]]'s dusts are now larger.
*[[Leather]] can now be crafted with 5 [[Vertebrae]].
*[[Storm Spray]]’s split streams now last for 1.33 seconds.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Aeries]] and [[Conclave Crossfire]].
*Resprited [[Drew's Wings]] (item) and [[Mana Overloader]].
*Resprited [[Cryonic Ore]].
*Resprited [[Nebulous Core]]'s Star and Dust projectiles.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a visual error with [[Core of Eleum]].
*Fixed [[The Slime God]] core's map icon not appearing.
