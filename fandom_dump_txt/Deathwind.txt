'''Deathwind''' is a Godseeker Mode [[bow]] that is dropped by [[The Devourer of Gods]]. Upon use, the Deathwind turns [[Wooden Arrow]]s into a spread of 4 blue and pink nebula arrows. Nebula arrows do not pierce tiles nor enemies and inflicts [[On Fire!]] and [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]s. Unconverted arrows deal 80% damage.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Tips ==
*Having the ability to maintain relatively high DPS makes the Deathwind a good bossing weapon.
*Pay attention to the projectiles being fired by the Deathwind, as this can make fighting certain enemies easier.
*Due to the Deathwind's average knockback, it would be advised to stay away from tight spaces when using the bow, as one may find themselves overwhelmed by hordes of enemies.
