The '''1.2.4.001''' update reworked [[Astrum Deus]], added some new weapons, and added a variety of resprites, including some for 2 of the mod's bosses.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Aether's Whisper]], [[Apotheosis]], [[Cosmic Immaterializer]], [[Disseminator]], [[Mistlestorm]], [[Molecular Manipulator]], [[Seadragon]].
*Added [[Purified Jam]].
*Added [[Cosmic Energy (buff)|Cosmic Energy]], [[Invincible]].
*Added [[Astrum Deus Mask]].

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] ===

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*The fight now includes a larger "spectral" worm which is invincible and cannot hurt the player until all of the small worms have been defeated. This worm does not inherit the [[Astrum Deus Probe]] ring.
*Nerfed small worm health from {{dv|18,500|29,600|32,000|40,000}} to {{dv|11,000|17,600|20,800|28,800}}.
*Decreased small worm length from 8 to 6.
*Small worms no longer drop Coins and [[Lesser Healing Potion]]s.
*Astrum Deus Probes now fire mines half as often.
*Mines now last for 20 seconds instead of 10.
*Now inflicts the [[Extreme Gravity]] debuff for the entire fight.
*Increased velocity on mines.
*Can now drop an [[Astrum Deus Mask]].

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Increased damage during enrage by 33%.
*No longer invincible outside of the [[Profaned Crag]], and instead gains 9999 defense.
*Can no longer harm players when fading in or out.
*Massively lowered speed of phase 3's brimstone barrage projectiles, and lowered the amount of barrages fired.
*Lowered the amount of dust she spawns idly. 

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Buffed phase 1's brimstone laser damage from {{dv|60|88|92|132}} to {{dv|70|100|105|150}}, and her rapid laser damage from {{dv|50|72|75|108}} to {{dv|60|88|92|132}}.
*Buffed phase 2's health from {{dv|19,000|30,400|34,400|40,000}} to {{dv|27,000|43,200|48,000|56,000}}, her contact damage from {{dv|50|80|84|120}} to {{dv|70|112|117|168}}, her hellfireball damage from {{dv|108|148|155|222}} to {{dv|118|168|176|252}}, and her brimstone laser damage from {{dv|60|92|96|138}} to {{dv|70|104|109|156}}.
*Buffed [[Cataclysm]]'s contact damage from {{dv|78|124|130|186}} to {{dv|88|140|147|210}} and his brimstone flamethrower damage from {{dv|52|92|96|138}} to {{dv|64|104|109|156}}.
*Buffed [[Catastrophe]]'s contact damage from {{dv|90|144|151|216}} to {{dv|100|160|168|240}}, and his fireball damage from {{dv|60|100|105|150}} to {{dv|72|116|121|174}}.
*Buffed [[Soul Seeker]]'s health from {{dv|1500|2100}} to {{dv|7500|12,000}}, and its brimstone dart damage from {{dv|48|84|88|126}} to {{dv|66|100|105|150}}.
*The brothers now have a higher despawn range.
*[[Bloodstone]], [[Chaos Stone]], and [[Animosity]] now drop from the Treasure Bag instead of directly from the boss in Expert Mode.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*[[Cryo Stone]], [[Frost Flare]], and [[Frozen Key]] now drop from the Treasure Bag instead of directly from the boss in Expert Mode.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Now limits projectile piercing to 2 instead of 3.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Head no longer instantly kills the player in Revengeance Mode, and now only does so in Death Mode.
*Lowered piercing resistance on body segments.
*[[Murasama]] and [[Fabsol]] now drop from the Treasure Bag instead of directly from the boss in Expert Mode.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Now fires Cthulhunadoes in phase 3 in Death Mode.

==== [[Eye of Cthulhu]] ====
*[[Teardrop Cleaver]] and [[Victory Shard]]s now drop from the Treasure Bag instead of directly from the boss in Expert Mode.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Buffed phase 2's health from {{dv|2000|3200|4800|11,200}} to {{dv|4000|6400|8060|11,200}}.
*No longer deals contact damage in phase 1.
*Phase 2 is no longer invincible outside of [[The Corruption]], and instead gains 9999 defense.
*Phase 2 can no longer harm players when fading in or out.
*[[Dark Heart]]s now hover higher above the player.
*Changed vile clot projectile color from purple to green, and made them inflict [[Cursed Inferno]].
*Now spawns [[Dank Creeper]]s and [[Devourer]]s 25% more often.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Any damage dealt to Yharon which would take over 10% of his health will now deal no damage.
*[[Drew's Wings]] now drop from the Treasure Bag instead of directly from the boss in Expert Mode.

==== [[THE LORDE]] ====
*Now becomes near invincible upon reaching 1% life for a minute before becoming vulnerable again.
*Now inflicts [[NO U]] constantly if summoned before [[Supreme Calamitas]] has been defeated.
*Despawn distance decreased.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Revengeance Mode projectile rain can now go through blocks.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Buffed [[Plague Mine]] damage in Death Mode from {{death|560}} to {{death|600}}.
*Now spawns up to 2 [[Plaguebringer]]s in Death Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Now immune to [[Exo Freeze]].

==== [[Profaned Guardians]] ====
*Flares now spawn at 7 second intervals instead of 10 second intervals.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Renamed A Dunkin' Donut to A Profaned Guardian.
*Defensive guardian spears now spawn at 10 second intervals instead of 7 second intervals.
*Removed the Healer guardian's negative healing projectiles.
*Offensive guardian flares now spawn at 7 second intervals instead of 10 second intervals.
*Increased vertical acceleration speed.
*[[Icarus' Folly]] now cuts horizontal wing speed.
*Now disables wiring.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Nerfed [[Cosmic Lantern]] health from {{dv|4000|5600|5880|8400}} to {{dv|2000|2800|2940|4200}}.
*No longer invincible outside of [[The Underworld]], and instead gains 9999 defense.
*Can no longer harm players when fading in or out.
*Now switches between attack modes much more quickly.
*Now charges more violently.
*Reduced dust spawned idly.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*The Siren now cycles between phases quicker.
*The Siren now has a 50% chance to spawn with an alternate, more green, sprite.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Now drops Purified Jam in Revengeance Mode if it has never been defeated before in the same world and/or by the same player.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Lowered hitcap from {{dv|40|35|30|15}} to {{dv|30|25|20|10}}.
*Now stops [[Slime Rain]] if it's occurring when she's summoned.
*Supreme Soul Seekers now produce less dust.
*Supreme Soul Seekers now fire less often.
*Completely rewrote dialogue.

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Aquatic Aberration]]s are now rarer.
*[[Parasea]]s are now more common.
*Nerfed Death Mode [[Plaguebringer]]'s health from {{death|29,400}} to {{death|28,000}}, and buffed Death Mode hive bomb damage from {{death|150}} to {{death|180}}.
*Plaguebringers can now only spawn a maximum of 5 large Plague Chargers at a time.
*[[Sun Bat]]s no longer emit light themselves, and now only use dust for lighting.

=== Items ===
*[[Asgardian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Aegis]]' dashes are now quicker.
*[[Counter Scarf]]'s [[Scarf Cooldown]] now lasts for 11 seconds instead of 8.
*[[Crystal Blade]]'s dust now penetrates 3 times instead of 2.
*[[Daedalus Stormbow]] now only gets nerfed in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*[[Death]] now requires a [[Crimson Altar]] or a [[Demon Altar]] to craft.
*[[Draedon's Heart]] now gives immunity to [[Horror]].
*[[Dualpoon]] and [[Triploon]]'s harpoon projectiles no longer last forever.
*[[Duke Fishron]]'s weapons now only get stat nerfs pre-[[Golem]] in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*[[Elemental Quiver]]'s split projectiles no longer recover ammo.
*Elemental Quiver's split projectiles now last shorter.
*[[Exoblade]]'s explosions now last for 0.5 seconds instead of 1, and have a localNPCHitCooldown of 1 instead of 3.
*Vanilla [[Flails]] now only gain a buff in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*Reduced [[God Slayer Chestplate]]'s thorns effect by 50%.
*[[Megafleet]] now uses 1 [[Seadragon]] in its recipe instead of 1 [[Megalodon]].
*[[Nanotech]]'s nanoblades are no longer affected by gravity and can home in on enemies.
*[[Nebulash]] now has a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 2 instead of 4.
*[[Revengeance Mode|Revengeance]]-exclusive items now have a tooltip stating "Revengeance drop".
*[[Siren's Heart]]'s Ice Shield Cooldown now lasts for 60 seconds instead of 30.

==== Balance ====
*Buffed [[Anti-materiel Rifle]]'s damage from 3000 to 4500.
*Decreased [[Ataxia Headgear]]'s chaos flare damage.
*Increased Ataxia Hood's chaos flame damage.
*[[Crystal Bullet]]s now only do 16.67% damage in Revengeance Mode.
*Buffed [[Exoblade]]'s damage from 800 to 1800.
*Buffed [[Flame Scythe]]'s damage from 83 to 98.
*Nerfed [[Greatbow of Turmoil]]'s damage from 70 to 52.
*Buffed [[Hell Burst]]'s damage from 34 to 36.
*Nerfed [[Helstorm]]'s damage from 27 to 24.
*Buffed Megalodon's damage from 27 to 32, and increased its accuracy.
*Nerfed [[Seashell Boomerang|Seashell Boomerang (Melee)]]'s use time from 11 to 15, and knockback from 6.5 to 5.5.
*Nerfed [[Storm Dragoon]]'s damage from 150 to 110.
*Nerfed [[Superball Bullet]]'s damage from 10 to 6.
*[[Terratomere]]'s projectiles now only deal 33% of the weapon's base damage instead of 50%.
*Nerfed [[Vanquisher Arrow]]'s damage from 32 to 18.
*Nerfed [[Verium Bullet]]'s damage from 12 to 11.

=== Other ===
*All Calamity buffs from [[Potions]] now save upon leaving worlds.
*Death Mode now boosts enemy damage by {{death|50%}} instead of {{death|40%}}.
*Extreme Gravity now slightly decreases the player's vertical speed.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed improper cropping on the [[Abyssal Warhammer]].
*Fixed a bug where Bumblebirb would furiously ram into the ground and rebound repeatedly when trying to spawn clones.
*Fixed a bug where [[Clockwork Bow]]'s arrows would drop upon collision.
*Fixed a bug where Draedon's Heart would not give its defense boost while standing still with [[Demonshade armor]].
*Fixed a typo in [[Frostspark Bullet]]'s tooltip which said "Electicity" instead of "Electricity".
*Fixed an oversight where the [[Heart of the Elements]]' components could also be equipped at the same time, resulting in duplicate summons.
*Fixed a bug with modded dash accessories that would cause players to move more slowly occasionally.
*Fixed hold offsets for the Megafleet, Megalodon, [[Nullification Pistol]], and [[Pumpler]].
*Fixed multiplayer desync bugs for most bosses.
*Fixed a bug where Supreme Calamitas would display her 50 death chat message at 49 deaths.
*Fixed Yharon's hitbox becoming small during phase 2.

== Removals ==
*Removed Death Mode boss checks for the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist] mod.
