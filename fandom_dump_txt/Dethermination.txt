The '''Dathermination''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[Dart guns|dart gun]] that is crafted from [[Waterfreeze Reactive Essence]]s. the shot dart has insanely fast speed and knockback, and leaves slashes as it moves. The slashes deal 200% of the weapon's damage and inflict [[Reactive Flames]]. The slashes can go through tiles, and exist for 5 seconds.
