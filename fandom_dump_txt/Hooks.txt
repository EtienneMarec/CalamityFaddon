'''Hooks''' are [[tools]] which aid the player in traversing terrain. When used, hooks fire a chain that latches to a surface or [[platform]] and pulls the player towards it. The ''Calamity Mod'' adds three new hooks, and also augments the descriptions of vanilla hooks by adding various stats regarding tile reach, launch, and pull velocities.
