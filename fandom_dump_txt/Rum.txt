'''Rum''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Rum''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces [[defense]], but boosts life regen and movement speed.

While the effects of the Rum debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
