The '''Poseidon''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that drops from the [[Giant Clam]] [[mini boss]]. It shoots a circular Poseidon typhoon sigil that homes in on [[enemies]] and lingers on top of them for a short duration, repeatedly dealing damage until it dissipates. Each Poseidon typhoon will pierce 3 to 8 times. Poseidon typhoons will also rebound off of blocks 9 times before disappearing.

== Notes ==
*Poseidon can additionally be obtained from [[Sunken Crate]]s in Hardmode at a 7% drop chance after the Giant Clam has been defeated.
*Due to the slow-moving nature of the projectiles fired from the weapon, it is not recommended to use the Poseidon against fast moving bosses, such as [[The Twins]]. It is best utilized against large groups of enemies, multiple segmented enemies, or singular slow-moving targets.

== Trivia ==
*The name of the weapon is a reference to [[wikipedia:Poseidon|Poseidon]], the Greek god of the Sea and other waters.
**The shape depicted on the item and its projectiles resembles a [[wikipedia:Trident|trident]], Poseidon's signature weapon.
