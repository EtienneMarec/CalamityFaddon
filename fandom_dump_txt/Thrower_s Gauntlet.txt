The '''Thrower's Gauntlet''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is the final upgrade to the [[Titan Glove]] and [[Feral Claws]]. It provides a 30% increase to melee speed and damage and a 25% increase to melee critical strike chance, a 100% increase to [[true melee]] damage, increases melee knockback, and gives the player temporary immunity to lava. The covering also inherits the effects of the [[Yoyo Bag]], giving two extra yoyos, 5 Counterweights, and four times the yoyo range.

While wearing the Thrower's Gauntlet, any enemy struck by a melee attack will be inflicted with these [[debuffs]]:
*[[Demon Flames]] for 5 seconds
*[[On Fire!]] for 5 seconds
*[[Frostburn]] for 5 seconds
*[[Holy Flames]] for 5 seconds
