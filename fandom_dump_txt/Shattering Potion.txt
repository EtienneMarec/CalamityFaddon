The '''Shattering Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Crumbling Potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Armor Shattering''' [[buff]], causing all melee and [[Spy]] attacks to inflict the [[Armor Crunch]] [[debuff]] as well as increasing melee and Spy damage and critical strike chance by 8%. The player will also become immune to the [[Armor Crumbling]] buff.
