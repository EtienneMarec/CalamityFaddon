The '''Stormfront Razor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[dagger]] that is an upgrade to the [[Cinquedea]]. When used, it automatically throws a dagger that releases three gravity-affected sparks as it travels. The dagger can pierce four enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Stormfront Razor causes the resulting dagger to move much faster and release a lot more sparks.
