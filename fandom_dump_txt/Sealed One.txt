The '''Sealed One''' is an [[enemy]] that will appear the first time player reaches the 4st level of the [[Abyss]]. It will remain stationary at the bottom of the 4st layer. When the player is near enough, a specific [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] will trigger. After the cutscene is over, the Sealed One disappears and will never appear anymore, becoming an unencounterable enemy.

== Notes ==
*Upon finishing the cutscene, the [[Void Man]] will have a new dialogue line.

== Trivia ==
*The Sealed One is referred as "voids" in the mod's source files, which is an another nickname of the addon mod's owner, but used previously.
**The [[Void Man]]'s quote after finishing the cutscene also implies that.
