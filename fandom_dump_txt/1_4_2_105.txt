The '''1.4.2.105''' update reworked the [[Revengeance Mode]] AIs of most of the vanilla bosses that hadn't received AI reworks in [[1.4.2.104]], along with some Revengeance Mode changes to other bosses. The update also made various changes to the mod's explosive projectiles, rebalanced various weapons, resprited a variety of items and [[The Devourer of Gods]], and fixed a slew of bugs.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Crown Jewel (enemy)]].
*Added [[Cinquedea]] and [[Glaive]] as unobtainable items.
*Added [[Mod Calls]] that allow for other mods to detect Calamity armor set bonuses.

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] ===
==== [[Aquatic Scourge]]  ====
*Now takes 50% damage from all non-minion piercing projectiles instead of taking 20% damage from infinitely piercing projectiles, and dividing the damage of other piercing projectiles by the amount of times they pierce.
*Head now has a circular hitbox.
*Homing projectiles can now home into body segments.
*Decreased how long clouds last, but they now last longer in Death Mode, fade slower, and produce light.

==== [[Astrum Aureus]] ====
*Can now jump further horizontally.
*Now falls more quickly while using its stomp attack and if the player is directly below it.

*[[Aureus Spawn]]s now glow in the dark.
*Lasers now pierce through the player.
*Flame crystals now fade in faster and have a significantly smaller explosion.
