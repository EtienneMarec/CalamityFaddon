The '''Violet Heart of Calamity''' is a [[Pets|pet]] summoning item that summons a Belling. It is obtained by opening [[Supreme Calamitas]]' [[Treasure Bag]] in [[Revengeance Mode]].

The Belling will float behind the player and follow them. If the player outruns it, it will quickly dash towards the player until they get close enough.

== Trivia ==
* The pet is [[User:EiramC|EiramC's]] OC, it was added to the mod because she is a very good Calamity Wiki contributor that helps others.
