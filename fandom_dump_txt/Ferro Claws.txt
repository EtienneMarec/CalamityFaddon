'''Ferro Claws''' is a post-Moonlord [[Hardmode]] [[True melee]] [[covering]] obtained from throwing a Mechanical Glove into [[Shimmer]] after [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated. 

The '''Ferro Claws''' summons 2 homing shrapnel projectiles upon [[True melee]] swings. These projectiles reach 30 blocks out from the original swing before dissipating and deal 25% of the swing's original damage.

The '''Ferro Claws''' also increases [[melee]] damage by 17%, at the cost of decreasing [[melee]] speed by 10%.

it also enables autoswing for all [[melee]] weapons and whips.

== Notes ==
*Throwing a [[Mechanical Glove]] into [[Shimmer]] before [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated will result in the covering being de-crafted into the [[Power Glove]] and [[Ranger Emblem]].
*Whips will not summon projectiles when [[Ferro Claws]] are used.

== Tips ==
*This covering greatly increases the viability of short-ranged [[True melee]] weapons such as the [[Lion Heart]] as the distance the shrapnel projectiles travels is always the same.
