The '''Sand Cloak''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. While equipped, it increases [[defense]] by 1 and movement speed by 5%. Pressing the {{key|Sand Cloak Effect}} key will create a large, stationary damaging dust veil around the player that lingers for 15 seconds at the cost of 25% of the player's [[stealth]]. The player gains an additional 6 defense and 2 life regen while staying in the ring. The cloak has a 30 second cooldown after deploying the dust veil before being able to use again.
