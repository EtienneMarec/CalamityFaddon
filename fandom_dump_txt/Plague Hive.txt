The '''Plague Hive''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. Equipping it grants the following benefits:
*All of the wearer's attacks inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]].
*All of the wearer's attacks spawn homing plague seekers upon hitting an enemy.
*Surrounds the wearer in a plague aura that is approximately 40 tiles in diameter. It inflicts Plague and deals 20 damage per tick.
**Plague seekers deal 25% damage of the attack that spawned them, capped at 30 damage.
*When the wearer takes damage, several bees are spawned that will seek out and attack nearby enemies.
