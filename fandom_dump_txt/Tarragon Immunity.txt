'''Tarragon Immunity''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player during their 25th Spy critical strike while wearing the Spy [[Tarragon armor|Tarragon]] or [[Auric Tesla armor|Auric Tesla]] armors. It grants the player immunity to damage for 3 seconds, in a similar manner to the [[Shadow Dodge]]. This buff has a [[Tarragon Immunity Cooldown|cooldown]] of 25 seconds after it is used.
