'''Demon Cores''' are craftable Pre-[[Hardmode]] consumable [[Spy weapon]]s. They automatically throw dark demon cores that inflict [[Shadowflame]] on any enemy within a 4 block radius of the thrown core.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Demon Cores will cause the next cores thrown to explode into a lingering cloud of gas that also inflicts Shadowflame upon collision with an enemy.
