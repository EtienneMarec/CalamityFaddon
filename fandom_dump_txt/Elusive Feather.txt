The '''Elusive Feather''' is a Godseeker Mode [[classless weapon]] that is sold by the [[Sloome]]. It spawns an aura around the player that last half a second. Any enemy that comes in contact with the aura will receive almost every damaging [[Debuffs|Debuff]] In the mod, Including Exo Annihilate.

==Notes==
This weapon is completely useless against [[Enemies]] that are immune to every [[Debuffs|Debuff]].

==Trivia==
The sprite of the Elusive Feather is a recolor of the [[Defiled Feather]].
