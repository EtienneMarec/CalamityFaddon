The '''Terra Shiv''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that autoswings. On each swing it will fire a green shortsword projectile that will pierce three times before stopping on the fourth enemy. The projectile only moves horizontally to the left or right.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, short travelling beams are shot out which split into 3 more beams. These beams inflict [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] for 20 seconds and heal the player for 5 health per hit. The player also gains 15% increased damage reduction and melee speed.
