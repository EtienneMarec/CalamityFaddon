'''Dihydrogen Monoxide''' (Japanese: 一酸化二水素 ''Issankanisuiso'') is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is fought in the [[More Sunkener Sea]], and an optional part of the [[Elemental route]]. It is a secret boss, intended to be the fought after [[Hydrogen]].

Defeating Dihydrogen Monoxide will drop the Soul of Dihydrogen Monoxide, which must be sold to the [[Archmage]] to complete the secret part of the quest.

==Spawn==
Dihydrogen Monoxide is summoned in the [[More Sunkener Sea]] if the player continuously swings their weapon for five minutes (instead of the one minute for Hydrogen), as well as destroys several projectiles within the area. The gunfire will awaken Dihydrogen Monoxide and begin the fight.

== Behavior==
Dihydrogen Monoxide has the longest fight of the structures (unlike Hydrogen), and the projectiles it uses are extra extraordinarily extremely destructive.

===Attacks===
====Phase 1====
*Dihydrogen Monoxide fires bombs from its shield, which have varying sizes of explosions and deal various amounts of damage. These explosions can also destroy tiles easily, so it is recommended to not invest too much in building an arena. Rusted bombs and shells are more likely to not explode and simply sink.
*Dihydrogen Monoxide will also randomly target any living NPCs in the area, except for enemies which spawn in the Sunken Sea, and the Angler, because he's friends with Dihydrogen Monoxide (he calls him DieMox and they drink tea and play chess every thursday).
