The '''Shattered Pistol''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] dropped from [[The Forbidden Lantern]] boss. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[Ammunition|ammo]], it fires a seashatter bullet that shatters into 6 smaller ones with a slight spread after travelling about 8 tiles. The smaller bullets deal 25% of the weapon's damagae each. Both the seashatter and the smaller bullets do not pierce through enemies nor go through walls, but inflict the [[Suffocation]] and [[Wet]] [[debuffs]] for 2 seconds.
