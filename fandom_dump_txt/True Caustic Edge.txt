The '''True Caustic Edge''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung, it fires a high-velocity, yellow sword beam that can bounce or pierce up to two times, and deals 75% of the weapon's damage. It inflicts the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]] to hit enemies.
