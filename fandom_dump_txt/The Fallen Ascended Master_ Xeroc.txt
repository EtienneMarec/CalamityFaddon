'''The Fallen Ascended Master, Xeroc''' (Japanese: 堕落したアセンデッドマスター ゼロク ''Daraku shita asendeddomasutā zeroku'') is A [[Master Template Items/Godseeker Mode NPCs|Godseeker-Mode]] [[Superbosses|Superboss]] that is required to be beaten in order to summon Challenge-rush as well as  Zeratros, Noxus, Yald'xoroath, and the Nameless Deity. He is recommended to be fought before the [[The Devourer of Gods|Devourer Of Gods]] has been defeated for the first time as he gets a large health buff (1.5x health). Xeroc will not spawn in on its own and requires the [[Terminus]] while having a [[tgc:Cups#Purchased|Chalice]] in your hotbar at night. 

== Spawn==
Upon summoning, A 120 Block wide circle of flames will spawn around Xeroc, attempting to leave the area during the fight will deal Massive Damage to the player. A swirling cloud of mist will appear 25 blocks in front of the player and slowly dissipate, revealing Xeroc facing toward the player. Xeroc will then reach his hand in the sky and manifest his Guan Dao into his hand. After waiting one second after the animation is finished, The fight begins. Xeroc is invulnerable during this time. The player also gains infinite flight during this fight. 

== Phase 1==
Xeroc will summon a Binding Whisp that follows him during the fight that can preform a number of attacks. This cant be done again.

Xeroc will preform the following attacks in a random order, (The same attack can't be preformed twice in a row.)

*Quickly charges toward the player 6 times every 1 second while the Binding Whisp creates a shockwave in A 100 block radius after each swing.
*Teleports around the player 1-3 times and either charges 20, 40 or 60 Blocks toward the player, while Xeroc and the wisp leaves behind clouds of smoke that expand outwards and last 1.5 seconds.
*Follows the player and swings his Guan Dao and A Shadowspec Slash toward the player while the wisp shoots at the opposing side of Xeroc while it shoots Shadowspec Bolts in a 35 degree spread that sometimes curve in toward the player. Xeroc can change sides at anytime. This lasts 9 Seconds
*Stays stationary for a moment before charges toward the player 6 times in a quick secession (This can be preformed twice anytime during the cycle)
*Xeroc summons 25 Angry Spirits 110 blocks away from him while they occasionally shoot fast moving Shadowspec Bolts toward the player while the Binding Whisp that follows the players movement at a slight delay, If it get within 7 blocks from the player, it creates an Shadow explosion that is 14 blocks wide. Xeroc has 95% Damage Reduction while the spirits are alive (This attack is always done last and preforms another cycle once the spirits are defeated)

==Phase 2==
Upon reaching 67% HP, a second Binding Whisp is summoned. This can't be done again. 

Xeroc preforms the following attacks in a random order (The same attack can't be preformed twice in a row.)

*Appears on the right side of the player and charges at the player horizontally 90 blocks and shoots evenly spaced Shadowspec Bolt's Vertically in both directions 6 blocks apart from each other while the Binding Wisp's shoot 3 or 4 Shadowspec Meteors toward the player. He then the same thing vertically with the bolts moving horizontally while the wisps preform the same action again. (This attack is always done first in this cycle)

*The whisp's orbit around Xeroc 1 full rotation every 2 seconds and and can despawn projectiles and damage the player, Xeroc can either charge quickly at the player 8 times traveling 30 block with each attack or charge 6 times traveling 100 blocks each attack leaving smoke behind him that dissipates after 0.4 seconds
*Indicators Appear randomly above the player and Purple Lightning Bolts strike downward while the whisp's follow Xeroc and shoot 2 or 3 Shadowspec Bolts at the player every 1.5 seconds (This Lasts For 12 seconds)
*Xeroc can also preform stronger version's of attacks Stated in Phase 1.

== Phase 3 ==
Upon Reaching 33% HP, a third Binding Whisp is summoned. This can't be done again. Xeroc will develop a staticky multicolored aura that leaves flames of the same property behind him, This gets more intense the lower his HP is.

At 33% HP, The 3 Binding Whisps circle around the player 120 blocks away while they shoot Shadowspec Bolts and Shadowspec Meteors that can explode. If one of the whisps HP reaches 0 it retreats to Xeroc.

At 22% HP, The whisps create A Black Hole that Xeroc gets enveloped In that gets smaller and slower the less HP it has. (Until 10% HP)

At 10% HP, Xeroc becomes invulnerable while losing HP and becomes a black silhouette and slowly moves upward while sending Static Deathrays downward that get bigger overtime. (This lasts for 15 seconds)

Before the fight ends the screen becomes more staticky while Xeroc erupts into flames while the Eye Of Xeroc manifests in the static one second before he disappears and ends the fight.
