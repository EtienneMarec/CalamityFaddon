[[File:Perfect Dark (demo).gif|frame|The Perfect Dark's swing animation. Notice how the vile balls don't travel in a straight line, but are affected by gravity.]]
The '''Perfect Dark''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that also drops from [[The Hive Mind]]. When swung, it fires a small spherical projectile that deals damage to the target hit and sticks to the first surface it touches. After sticking onto a surface for 4 seconds, it explodes - emitting purple particles and dealing damage to all enemies in a small radius. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Vein Burster]].
