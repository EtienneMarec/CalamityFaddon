}}The '''Fishbone Boomerang''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[boomerang]]. When used, it throws up to only one boomerang which remains airborne up to four seconds. Unlike most boomerangs, it has a brief throwing animation, and it decelerates when it reaches its maximum distance as opposed to turning around instantly. It is capable of piercing infinitely, but returns to the player extremely quickly when it hits a surface or an enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Fishbone Boomerang causes the next boomerang thrown to ricochet to another nearby enemy before returning to the player. If no enemies are nearby, it returns to the player as usual.
