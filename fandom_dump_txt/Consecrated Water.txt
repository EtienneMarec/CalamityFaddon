The '''Consecrated Water''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]]. When used, a gravity affected flask is thrown that breaks upon contact with [[enemies]] and blocks. Upon breaking, the flask releases a 20 block high flame pillar that damages enemies and dissipates after three seconds.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Consecrated Water will cause the next flask thrown to spawn two additional flame pillars to the left and right of the main pillar.
