'''Armageddon''' was an item found within the [[Starter Bag]] that can be activated and deactivated. When active, it had multiple effects on gameplay:

*While a [[boss]] is alive (either from Calamity Mod or vanilla), any damage to the player (such as enemy or projectile contact, fall damage, or [[Grenade]]s) would result in instant death.
*The player's respawn time was much shorter.
*Bosses would drop an additional 5 [[Treasure Bag]]s even in Normal Mode if said boss has never been defeated previously in the world.

Right clicking with the item toggled whether or not dodges bypass the mode's instant death effect. 

It could be crafted at an [[Altar]] and does not have any ingredients.

While active, the Armageddon icon would be shown on the bottom-right side of the Difficulty Indicator.

== Notes ==
*The instant death effect while the item is active bypasses [[Death prevention|revival effects]] from [[God Slayer armor]], [[Silva armor]] and [[Auric Tesla armor]], as well as the [[Invincible]] buff from [[Purified Jam]].
*Debuffs such as [[Holy Inferno]] will not kill the player instantly. They decrease the health as usual.
*Attempting to use Armageddon while a boss is alive will have no effect, and display a status message that reads, "You cannot change the rules now."
*Only bosses from Calamity and Vanilla that drop Treasure Bags normally, are affected by the Armageddon Treasure Bag drop increase.
*This was the only way to obtain treasure bags in Normal Mode.
**This allowed the player to obtain Expert Mode exclusive items in Normal Mode.
*If a boss from a different mod is alive, the insta-kill effect still applies, but no extra treasure bags will be dropped.
*Killing the [[Wall of Flesh]] with Armageddon enabled would not cause it to drop treasure bags in normal mode, to prevent players from obtaining the [[Demon Heart]].
