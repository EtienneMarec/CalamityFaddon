}}{{historical content}}{{npc infobox|type=NPC|environment=prison|ai=pretty bad ngl have you seen the boss fights|life=20,000|defense=15|knockback=50|image2=Drunk Princess map.png|image2caption=Map Icon|hurt=NPC_Hit_1.wav|killed=NPC_Killed_6.wav|listexclude=yes}}The '''Spiteful Candle''' is a [[Hardmode]] piece of [[furniture]] that is purchased from the [[Drunk Princess]] for {{gc|50}}. The candle emits light and grants the '''Spite''' [[buff]] to all players within 120x86 rectangular area, which causes [[enemies]] to take 5% more damage from all sources, regardless of their damage reduction stat.

== Notes ==
*This does not affect enemies with more than 99% DR such as [[Sepulcher]] and [[adult Eidolon Wyrm]]s.
*Since a player is 2 tiles wide and just under 3 tiles tall and the candle buffs a 120x86 area, covering a large area with no gaps can be done by placing candles every 243 blocks horizontally and 176 blocks vertically.
**Covering the entire accessible area of a large map with the candle's buff would require 385 candles, costing {{value|192|50}}.
***For medium worlds, it would take 260 candles, costing {{pc|130}}. For small worlds, it would take 119 candles, costing {{value|59|50}}.
***Covering just a single horizontal stretch (for instance, on a skybridge) would take 35 candles on a large world, 26 on a medium world, and 17 on a small world.
*Unlike the [[Water Candle|Water]] and [[Peace Candle]]s, it cannot be held by the player and is required to be placed down in order to receive its effects.
*Like all items sold by the [[Drunk Princess]], a [[Discount Card]] or other similar items will not reduce the candle's price.
*A placed candle will be dropped if it comes in contact with lava (but not water or honey).
