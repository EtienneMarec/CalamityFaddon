The '''Thunder Bolt''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that auto-fires a ball of lightning that inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]]. The projectile travels in a straight line, is unaffected by gravity, emits a dim light, and can damage 4 enemies or bounce off of 4 walls before dissipating.
