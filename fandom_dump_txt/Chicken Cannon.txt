The '''Chicken Cannon''' is a Godseeker Mode [[launchers|launcher]] that can be dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. It rapidly fires chicken rockets that bounce up when hitting the ground, then explode with a very large radius. The explosion lingers for a few seconds and ignores [[immunity frames]]. Right clicking while any rockets are still in the air causes them to instantly explode.
