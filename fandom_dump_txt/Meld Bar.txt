'''Meld Bars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[Bars]] that are crafted with [[Stardust]] and [[Meld Blob]]s. They are primarily used to craft powerful [[Hardmode]] and Godseeker Mode gear which function as alternatives to the gear provided by the [[Celestial Pillars]]; additionally, they are used in the crafting of [[Empyrean armor]], a post-Moon Lord [[Covering]] that functions as the [[spy]] counterpart to the Celestial armors.
