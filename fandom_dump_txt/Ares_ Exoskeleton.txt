'''Ares' Exoskeleton''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XF-09 Ares]] has been defeated last. When used, the player throws out a box which opens up a control panel. The control panel features four weapon choices on top and weapon slots on the bottom. Each weapon slot takes up three minion slots and summons a cannon attached to an arm outwards from the player. The order of the weapon's positions are fixed onto the player. From the leftmost slot to rightmost slot, the cannons appear at the bottom right, bottom left, top right, and top left of the player respectively.
