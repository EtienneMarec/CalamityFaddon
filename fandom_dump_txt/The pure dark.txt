<big>[[THE PURE DARK]]

(DISCLAIMER:i am not the person behind the idea of the void and i only made this for fun and its still a WIP)

<small><small> the true dark is a area located in the [[The Void]]

APPEARANCE: 
it looks vastly different and without a light source of any kind it is near impossible to traverse and has the highest [[secretary]] spawn rate in [[The Void]] highly discouraging any kind of mining if it wasnt discouraged enough already.
*this means its nearly impossible to do anything other than look around.
*theres some ruined houses rarely found around the area that tend to have [[chests]] in them
*some areas have some blocks of a sand block that sometimes have [[Angry Bones]] and other skeletons (usually post plantera skeletons if your at that progression) that spawn there

ENEMIES: the main enemies here are [[Angry Bones]], [[Skeleton Archer]]s, [[Skeleton Sniper]]s, [[Skeleton Commando]]s  and if your foolish [[Secretary]]s too
*the skeletons here dont drop [[Ectoplasm]] nor [[Necroplasm]].
*most skeletons have 2x hp and higher drop rates due to still being oddly rare ([[Angry Bones]] having 4x the hp usually)
*zombies spawn rarely and entirely replace skeletons if you somehow manage to get past the void early and get here pre skeletron.

Chests:

the chests also tend to contain [[Meteorite Bar]]s and some of the usual stuff like [[Torch]]es and [[Healing Potion]]s
