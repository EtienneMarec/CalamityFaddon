'''Unsafe Eutrophic Sand Wall''' is a background [[wall]] that generates naturally in the [[Sunken Sea]] [[biome]], alongside [[Unsafe Navystone Wall]]. Eutrophic Sand Walls will release water whenever the nearby tiles update. It can be removed using any hammer, but only after [[Desert Scourge]] is defeated.
