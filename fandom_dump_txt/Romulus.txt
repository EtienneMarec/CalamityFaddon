'''Romulus''' is a unique [[Hardmode]] [[fishing rod]] dropped by the [[Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Deploying Romulus will cause him to leave the player's inventory, sit down, and fish automatically in the closest [[Water]]. Romulus is completely autonomous and does not need to be given [[bait]] to fish. Left clicking Romulus will pick him up as an item. Right clicking Romulus will withdraw all his caught fish.

If Romulus takes at least 500 total damage, he will die.

==Trivia==
*Despite the tooltip, Romulus is not a bee.
