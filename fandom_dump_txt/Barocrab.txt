The '''Barocrab''' is a rare [[critter]]. It is found on the [[Ocean]] floor and passively walks back and forth. When killed, Barocrabs drop 2 [[Gold Coin]]s as well as the [[Baroclaw]], a powerful Pre-Hardmode covering and weapon.

It can also be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]]. It can be detected with the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].

Releasing a Barocrab in a [[Glowing Mushroom biome]] while carrying 2,147,483,647 [[Delicious Meat]] will summon a more powerful version of [[Crabulon]].
Doing this is necessary to achieve the [[Delicious Meat Ending]].
