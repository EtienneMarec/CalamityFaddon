== Trivia ==
*The [[Draconic Swarmer]], [[Twinkler]], and the [[Super Dummy]] are the only non boss-exclusive NPCs without a banner. This is due to Twinkler being a similar [[Critter]] to the [[Firefly]], the Draconic Swarmer is normally a boss summon, and the Super Dummy being a utility NPC.
