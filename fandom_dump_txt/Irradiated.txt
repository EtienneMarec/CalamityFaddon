'''Irradiated''' is a [[debuff]] with different effects depending on what it has inflicted. If inflicted on the player, they receive various stat increases and decreases. The [[Lead Core]] [[covering]], dropped by [[Irradiated Slime]]s and [[Gamma Slime]]s, provides immunity to this debuff. If inflicted on an enemy, their health decreases over time. All enemies immune to either [[Poisoned]] or [[Crush Depth]] are also immune to Irradiated.
