'''Aegisalt Ore''' is a Hardmode [[ore]] that can be found in the Underground Ice biome after the [[Fatal Circuit]] has been defeated. It is used to craft [[Refined Aegisalt]] at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or a [[Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least a [[Pickaxe Axe]] or higher to mine.
