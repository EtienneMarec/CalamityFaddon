The '''1.3.4.001''' update added the [[Boss Health Bar]], reworked [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], and overhauled the [[Profaned Crag]] [[biome]]. It also added 9 new [[lore items]], adjusted the health values and resistances of most of the game's bosses, and buffed the [[Cursed Inferno]] debuff.

== Additions ==
*Added Boss Health Bar.
*Added [[Halibut Cannon]].
*Added [[Brimling]] and [[Plague Homing Missile]].
*Added [[Revivify Potion]].
*Added [[Revivify]].
*Added [[Iron Heart]].
*Added [[Brimstone Slag]] (item) and [[Smooth Brimstone Slag]].
*Added [[Brimstone Slag Wall]] and [[Smooth Brimstone Slag Wall]].
*Added [[Music Box (Crabulon)]], [[Music Box (Desert Scourge)]], and [[Music Box (Slime God)]].
*Added [[Astrageldon Trophy]], [[Astrum Deus Trophy]], and [[Brimstone Elemental Trophy]].
*Added [[Lore items]] for [[Aquatic Scourge]], [[Astrageldon Slime]], [[Astrum Deus]], [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Golem]], [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Ravager]], [[Blood Moon|The Red Moon]], and [[Siren and Leviathan]].
<!---Duke Fishron and Cultist got lore items which replaced Death of DS and EoC respectively--->
*Added a unique map background for the [[Sulphurous Sea]].
