'''Perdition''' is a Godseeker Mode [[sentry]] that drops from [[Supreme Calamitas]]. When used, a floating beacon sentry follows the player around. When an enemy is targeted via right-clicking with most summon weapons, a cross appears on the marked target, a large area lights up with fire dust, and various types of homing souls appear from below.

{{Modifier|summon}}

[[File:Perdition (demo).gif|frame|left|The Perdition in use.]]

== Trivia ==
*Perdition is a state of eternal punishment originating from Christianity, that sinful people are sentenced to after death.
