'''Snow Ruffian armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[Covering]] set. It requires 60 [[Snow Block]]s, 30 [[Ice Block]]s, and 90 [[Boreal Wood]]  to craft the entire set.

It consists of a '''Snow Ruffian Mask''', '''Snow Ruffian Chestplate''', and '''Snow Ruffian Leggings''', and '''Snow Ruffian Greaves'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*4 defense
*7% increased Spy damage
*3% increased Spy critical strike chance
*5% increased movement speed
*The player can glide by holding the {{key|Jump}} key, which negates fall damage.
*A max [[stealth]] of 50
