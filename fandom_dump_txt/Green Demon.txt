The '''Green Demon''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[mini boss]] that rarely spawns during a [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[Leviathan and Anahita]] has been defeated. This menacing creature is a real test of a player's skill and courage, and can take lots of mental preparation to defeat. Fighting the Green Demon is not for everyone, but it rewards the player with the dazzling [[Green Demon Head|Green Demon Head.]]

==Appearance==
The [[Green Demon]] has a distinct and unsettling appearance. It is a giant green creature with big arms and legs, and an unnerving smile. It has beady eyes which constantly stare at the player.

==Behavior==
This malevolent entity is no pushover when it comes to combat. It has a few tricks up its spectral sleeves:

* '''Chasing Prey:''' The [[Green Demon]] relentlessly pursues the player, moving quickly and with determination. You'll need to stay on your toes to avoid its advances. It moves at a whopping '''150 MPH''', so you'll need a [[Momentum Capacitor]] to escape it.
* '''Projectile Attacks:''' As if its pursuit weren't enough, the [[Green Demon]] also launches deadly projectiles towards its target. These projectiles can deal significant damage, so dodging them is essential.
* '''Ground Slam:''' The nefarious [[Green Demon]] will often slam the ground, propelling the player into the air. This will instantly kill the player as soon as they hit a block, so be careful.
* '''Glock-19:''' The devious [[Green Demon]] will pull out a Glock-19 when at 50% HP, and shoot the player, ignoring defense and dealing a whole '''1000 / 1500 / 2000 / 3000''' damage.
* '''Health Drain:''' In a chilling twist, the [[Green Demon]] can leech health from its victims, restoring its own vitality as it damages the player. This makes it an even tougher adversary.
