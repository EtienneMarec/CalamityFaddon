The '''Ifflamator''' is a craftable post-[[Umbrus]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It shoots yellow flames that rapidly orbit around the cursor and inflict the [[Ancient Astral Blight]] [[debuff]]. The flames dissapear once they get 10 tiles away from the cursor

Note that, unlike other flamethrowers, it lacks a chance to not consume gel meaning the player should make sure they do not run out of ammo at an inopportune time.
