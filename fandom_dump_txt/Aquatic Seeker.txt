The '''Aquatic Seeker''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]]. It is a horned fish that flies through the air towards the player, attempting to hurt them via contact damage and inflicting the [[Venom]] and [[Bleeding]] [[debuff]]s as it does so.
