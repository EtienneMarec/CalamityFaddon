The '''Momentum Capacitor''' is a [[Hardmode]] item purchased from [[Bandit]] for {{gc|60}} after all three [[Mechanical Bosses]] have been defeated. When holding left-click, all summoned minions start gaining speed with a factor of +1.02 per frame. This caps at 500% of the minion's original speed, causing most minions to either fling themselves offscreen or become significantly more aggressive. Minion speeds reset to normal once the player stops using the item.
