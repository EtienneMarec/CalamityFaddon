The '''Emergency Door''' is a piece of [[furniture]] that can be opened and closed to allow or prevent entry respectively. It is sold by the [[Collector]] after being brought its respective [[blueprint]]. After being placed for fifteen seconds it will automatically break.

== Notes ==
*The blueprint for this item can be obtained by shimmering a cosmilite [[door]].
