The '''Clockwork Bar''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bar]] that is used in creating various [[Ranged weapons]] , it also grants bonuses to ranged weapons when located in inventory. It is the only bar able to have modifiers, which will affect every ranged weapon when the bar is in the inventory
