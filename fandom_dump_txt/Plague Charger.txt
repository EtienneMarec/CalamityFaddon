'''Plague Chargers''' are [[Hardmode]] [[enemies]] that spawn in the [[Jungle]] after the [[Golem]] has been defeated. They come in two different variations, normal and large, with varying size, health, damage, and loot.

== Trivia ==
*It appears to be a bee or [[Hornet]] inflicted by the plague.
*Several Plague Chargers of both sizes were also summoned by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] [[boss]] and the [[Plaguebringer]] [[miniboss]]. This was removed in version [[1.5.1.001]].
