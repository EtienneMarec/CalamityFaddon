The '''Overloaded Blaster''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that is dropped by [[The Slime God]]. It fires a spray of 5 small projectiles in a wide, random spread. The projectiles bounce once off of tiles, then fall to the ground once they bounce.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The Overloaded Blaster noticeably differs from other flamethrowers, lacking a well-defined stream or the typical sound effect. However, it is alike them in that it consumes [[Gel]] as ammo and is affected by the [[Shroomite Visage]].

== Trivia ==
*The weapon's design is similar to that of the [https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/Splattershot Splattershot] type weapon from the ''[[wikipedia:Splatoon|Splatoon]]'' franchise.
