The '''1.2.0.002''' update made various rebalances and changes to the [[Stress]] mechanic, [[Polterghast]], and [[The Devourer of Gods]]. The update also added fishing item drops to [[Desert Scourge]] and [[Siren and Leviathan]], added [[summoning items]] to [[Town NPC]] shops after their respective bosses have been defeated, and fixes to content introduced in [[1.2.0.001]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*Increased the drop rate for [[legendary items]] from normal enemies from 0.002% to 0.001% during pre-Hardmode,  0.0004% to 0.002% in Hardmode, and 0.001% to 0.004% Godseeker Mode.
*Increased the drop rate for legendary items from specific bosses from 0.01% to {{dv|0.2%|0.22%}} in pre-Hardmode, {{dv|0.02%|0.022%}} to {{dv|0.33%|0.4%}} in Hardmode, and {{dv|0.1%|0.13%}} to {{dv|2%|2.5}}% post-Moon Lord.
*Various bosses and enemies now inflict [[Marked for Death]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] for varying amounts of time.
*[[Angry Dog]] changes:
**Nerfed health from {{dv|500|600}} to {{dv|100|110}}, its damage from {{dv|25|35}} to {{dv|12|13}}, and its defense from 6 to 4.
**Now gains additional health, damage, and defense after [[Cryogen]] has been defeated. 
**Now inflicts [[Bleeding]] for 1.33 seconds instead of {{dv|2.67|5}}.
*[[Astrum Deus]]' body segments no longer fire more lasers if the player has high [[Stress]].
*[[Brimstone Elemental]]'s Hellfireballs' speeds no longer increase with Stress.
*Both phases of [[Calamitas]] no longer fire more brimstone lasers if the player has high Stress.
*Nerfed [[Cnidrion]]'s health from {{dv|1300|1560}} to {{dv|900|1080}}.
*[[Desert Scourge]] changes:
**The main scourge, smaller scourges, and [[Dried Seeker]]s now gain addition health, contact damage, and defense after [[Skeletron]] has been defeated.
***Main scourge gains 20 body segments.
***Main scourge gains extra speed.
***Smaller scourges gain 10 body segments.
**Now drops [[Angler Earring]]s, [[Angler Hat]]s, [[Angler Pants]], [[Angler Tackle Bag]]s, [[Angler Vest]]s, [[Crate Potion]]s, [[Fisherman's Pocket Guide]]s, [[Fishing Potion]]s, [[High Test Fishing Line]]s, [[Sextant]]s, [[Sonar Potion]]s, [[Tackle Box]]es, and [[Weather Radio]]s.
**Now drops [[Golden Bug Net]]s after Skeletron has been defeated.
*[[The Destroyer]] no longer fires lasers more often if the player has high Stress.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Nerfed health from {{dv|750,000|1,200,000|1,280,000}} to {{dv|650,000|1,040,000|1,120,000}}.
**The second Devourer of Gods now spawns once The Devourer of Gods reaches 20% health instead of 50%.
**The second Devourer of Gods now spawns after a 1 second countdown instead of instantly.
**Now gains 150,000 base health when under 600,000 health and only one Devourer is alive.
**Orb projectiles now gradually slow down.
**Now spawns an additional Beam Portal.
**Lessened amount of lasers shot by body segments.
**Head and tail now use NPCHit13 instead of NPCHit4 for their hit sounds.
**Tail now has an icon on the minimap. 
*[[Eater of Worlds]] no longer fires cursed flame projectiles more often if the player has high Stress.
*[[Golem]] no longer gets more aggressive with high Stress.
*Increased [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s enrage distance and the distance at which his border Infernadoes spawn.
*Yharon's border Infernadoes now have a rainbow filter, spawn quicker, instantly kill the player on contact, and are larger.
*[[Lunatic Cultist]] now causes the player to take 1.3500000238418579x damage while active instead of 1.4500000476837158x damage.
*[[Queen Bee]] no longer becomes more aggressive if the player has high Stress.
*[[Phantasm Dragon]] now has a health multiplier of {{revengeance|15x}}.
*Buffed [[Phantom Spirit]]'s damage from {{dv|110|176}} to {{dv|250|400}}.
*Reduced the amount of dust produced by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] when hit.
*Reduced the amount of dust produced by The Plaguebringer Goliath and its [[Plaguebringer Shade|Shades]]' missiles.
*[[Polterghast]] changes:
**Buffed base contact damage from {{dv|100|160}} to {{dv|125|200}}, buffed Necroghast's contact damage from {{dv|120|192}} to {{dv|150|240}}, and buffed Necroplasm's contact damage from {{dv|220|352}} to {{dv|250|400}}.
**Nerfed Necroplasm's defense from 600 to 400.
**Buffed blue phantom shot damage from {{dv|100|200}} to {{dv|110|220}}, and blue phantom blast damage from {{dv|120|240}} to {{dv|130|260}}.
**Buffed red phantom shot damage from {{dv|120|240}} to {{dv|130|260}}, and red phantom blast damage from {{dv|130|260}} to {{dv|140|280}}.
**Buffed [[Polterghast Hook]]'s damage from {{dv|190|266}} to {{dv|250|350}}.
**Buffed Phantom's projectile damage from {{dv|124|200}} to {{dv|204|320}}.
**Buffed blue [[Phantom Turret]]'s phantom shot damage from {{dv|90|180}} to {{dv|120|240}}.
**Buffed red Phantom Turret's phantom shot damage from {{dv|106|212}} to {{dv|140|280}}.
**Phantom Turrets now shoot phantom shots more often.
**Now takes up 50 NPC slots instead of 25.
**Increased resistance to [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]].
*[[Providence, the Profaned God]] now uses [[Music#Unholy Insurgency|Unholy Insurgency]] as her<!---Referred to as female in the lore with the "God" title being due to her theme of oxymorons---> theme instead of [[Music#Siren's Call (Seafoam Mix)|Siren's Call (Seafoam Mix)]].
*Providence, the Profaned God's attack pattern is now modified if any [[Profaned Guardians|Profaned Guardian]] is alive.
*[[Plantera]] no longer gets more aggressive with high Stress.
*[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]]'s fire balls' speeds no longer increase with Stress.
*[[Siren and Leviathan]] changes:
**The Leviathan no longer fire more meteors if the player has high Stress.
**The Siren's projectiles no longer increase in speed with Stress.
**Now drop [[Bottomless Water Bucket]]s, Crate Potions, Fishing Potions, [[Hotline Fishing Hook]]s, Sonar Potions, and [[Super Absorbant Sponge]]s.
*[[Skeletron Prime]] no longer spins more often if the player has high Stress.
*Skeletron Prime's Prime Cannon no longer fires more often if the player has high Stress.
*[[Stasis Probes]] are now immune to [[coverings]] which have a chance to instantly kill enemies such as the [[Elemental Gauntlet]].
*[[Storm Weaver]]'s body segments now classify as bosses.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] can now drop [[Crystyl Crusher]] or [[Svantechnical]].
*Supreme Calamitas' brothers now slowly move back and forth horizontally instead of staying in a set position.
*Supreme Calamitas' brothers now instantly despawn once Supreme Calamitas is defeated or despawns, instead of having their defenses set to 0 and damage taken multiplied by 100.

**Increased LocalNPCHitCooldown from 10 initially, 8 post-Golem, 4 post-Moon Lord, 2 post-Devourer of Gods, and 1 post-Yharon to 20 initially, 15 post-Golem, 10 post-Moon Lord, 6 post-Devourer of Gods, and 3 post-Yharon.
**Tornado marks now have a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 30 initially, 25 post-Golem, 20 post-Moon Lord, 16 post-Devourer of Gods, and 13 post-Yharon.
**Increased tornado LocalNPCHitCooldown from 15 initially, 9 post-Golem, 7 post-Moon Lord, 5 post-Devourer of Gods, and 3 post-Yharon to 20 initially, 15 post-Golem, 10 post-Moon Lord, 6 post-Devourer of Gods, and 3 post-Yharon.

=== Other ===

**[[Cosmic Worm]]s are sold by the [[Cyborg]].

*Revengeance Mode now grants the player 5% extra damage instead of 10% after any boss has been defeated.
*Stress changes:
**Talking to female Town NPCs now decreases Stress.
**Decreased the amount of Stress gained during a Blood Moon or being in the [[Dungeon]].
**[[Solar Eclipse]]s now increase Stress.
**Decreased the stat boosts gained with Stress by 50%.
**[[Blackout]] is now inflicted for 2 seconds instead of 6.
**The player is now able to be inflicted with [[Obstructed]] for 2 seconds instead of taking damage at over 300 Stress.
**The player now has a smaller chance to fall off [[Ropes]] at over 400 Stress, and the chance no longer scales if the player has Horror.
**Talking to the [[Nurse]] with over 500 Stress no longer inflicts [[Potion Sickness]].
