The '''2.0.0.001''' (or '''2.0.0.1''') update upgraded the mod to Terraria [[tgc:1.4.3.6|1.4.3.6]] (tModLoader v2022.5.103+). This means the Calamity Mod now supports many features that were released alongside the Vanilla Terraria 1.4.0.1 series of updates.

{{toc limit|3}}
== Additions ==
*Added [[Bestiary]] support to all Calamity [[enemies]] and [[bosses]].
*Added the "Calamity Style" Main Menu theme. <!-- Custom cal manu music only plays on this theme w/ music mod on. - Merkalto -->
*Added Defense Damage UI indicator.
*Added [[Adamantite Particle Accelerator]] and [[The Maelstrom]].
*Added [[Aquatic Scourge Relic]], [[Astrum Aureus Relic]], [[Astrum Deus Relic]], [[Brimstone Elemental Relic]], [[Calamitas Clone Relic]], [[Calamitas Relic]], [[Ceaseless Void Relic]], [[Crabulon Relic]], [[Cragmaw Mire Relic]], [[Cryogen Relic]], [[Desert Scourge Relic]], [[Devourer of Gods Relic]], [[Draedon Relic]], [[Dragonfolly Relic]], [[Giant Clam Relic]], [[Great Sand Shark Relic]], [[Hive Mind Relic]], [[Leviathan and Anahita Relic]], [[Mauler Relic]], [[Nuclear Terror Relic]], [[Old Duke Relic]], [[Perforators Relic]], [[Plaguebringer Goliath Relic]], [[Polterghast Relic]], [[Profaned Guardians Relic]], [[Providence Relic]], [[Ravager Relic]], [[Signus Relic]], [[Slime God Relic]], [[Storm Weaver Relic]], and [[Yharon Relic]].
*Added [[Journey Mode]] research support to all Calamity items except those in relation to [[Arsenal Labs]], the [[Rock]], [[Starter Bag]] and unobtainable items in the Main and Music mods.
*Added map backgrounds for the [[Abyss]].
**Layers 2 and 3 share a background with each other, whereas Layers 1 and 4 each use a unique background.
**These are currently only viewable as bestiary backgrounds. However, as no enemies are exclusive to layer 1, its background is currently unviewable.

=== Reworks ===
*Reworked Bladecrest Oathsword. It now has a custom swing animation and fires three scythes per swing instead of one. Decreased use time from 25 to 21.
*Reworked Cloud Elemental AI. She now hovers around and randomly performs one of three attacks: casting a ring of 5 cloud tornadoes, summoning 5 [[Angry Nimbus|Angry Nimbi]], and teleporting. Below 50% health, she casts 8 tornadoes, and summons 6 Angry Nimbi at a faster rate. Additionally, she gains two new attacks: creating 7 clouds which fire lightning bolts and performing four charges at the player in quick succession. She cannot summon Angry Nimbi while 10 or more Angry Nimbi are alive.
*Reworked [[Cnidarian]], it no longer functions as a yoyo and instead causes the player to hold out a fishing rod. While deployed, an electric jellyfish hook, attached to a string, will follow the mouse cursor. If an enemy is damaged by the hook will, it causes active minions to target that enemy. Only one enemy can be targeted at a time. Additionally, sparks will periodically fire from the jellyfish hook when swung. It now does summon damage instead of melee damage.
*Reworked [[Coral Spout]], it no longer continuously fires out gravity-affected coral spike projectiles, and instead, when used, will fire out a spread of five pink coral spikes that travel a short distance before exploding, dealing damage to enemies. Additionally, Coral Spout can also be charged up to decrease the spread of the coral spikes. When charged for 1.25 seconds, the coral spike projectiles will be replaced with a light blue coral chunk. Coral chunks travel much farther and at a higher velocity. When a coral chunk hits an enemy, it will stick to that enemy for 3 seconds before falling off. After falling off of an enemy, coral chunks can be recovered by the player to restore 100 mana. Coral chunks will only linger after falling off of an enemy for 5.5 seconds. Sound cues will also play when charging up the Coral Spout, when it is fully charged, and when coral chunks fall off of enemies.
*Reworked [[Draedon's Heart]], standing still no longer increases the player's life regeneration and damage reduction, and instead adds the Nanomachines meter. The Nanomachines meter will gradually charge over time during boss encounters. When the meter is full, it can be activated, which will cause the player to heal for 360 HP over two seconds while hindering the player's movement. Activating the Nanomachines meter will also cause the player to dismount if they are using one. Getting hit while the meter is charging will cause the meter to stop charging for 1 second. If the player is on Revengeance Mode or higher, while equipped this covering will replace the [[Adrenaline Meter]] with the Nanomachines meter. Additionally, Draedon's Heart now grants the player 48 defense.
*Reworked [[Fishbone Boomerang]], it now has a hitsound that increases in pitch per enemy hit. Once 10 enemies have been hit in a single throw, the hitsound is replaced with a drum. Its stealth strike now gives the boomerang the ability to ricochet between enemies instead of spawning seashell projectiles.

Additionally, it can be used while running, causing the player to hold the Redtide spear steadily forward, which increases the knockback dealt to enemies. When releasing the attack button, ending the run, or after jumping, the player will slash the spear upwards, causing any enemies at the spear's end not immune to knockback to be thrown into the air, and receiving up to 50 fall damage when they hit the ground. Increased its damage from 28 to 33. Decreased the duration the spear inflicts the [[Poisoned]] debuff from 10 seconds to 3 seconds.

Charging up Urchin Mace will cause it to spin rapidly around the player, generating a large whirlpool. While charging up, the mace itself will deal damage to enemies, the whirlpool will not. Once charged, the player can release the whirlpool towards the direction of their mouse cursor by letting go of the charge. The whirlpool will travel a medium-length distance before exploding, dealing damage to all enemies it hits while traveling. Decreased damage from 35 to 20.
*Reworked the [[Victide Mask]] set bonus minion, it no longer summons a sea urchin above the player, firing stingers at nearby enemies. It instead spawns a sea snail that will remain next to the player's side, firing a spread of projectiles towards enemies. While moving, the sea snail will spin above the player and continue to fire projectiles towards nearby enemies.
