The '''1.2.1.007''' update reworked [[Cryogen]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]], along with adding a few weapons.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Death]].
*Added [[Anti-materiel Rifle]], [[Aquatic Dissolution]], [[Photoviscerator]], [[Voltaic Climax]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Stats:
**Buffed health from {{dv|12,500|20,000|22,400}} to {{dv|13,500|21,600|24,000}}.
**Nerfed contact damage from {{dv|65|104|130}} to {{dv|50|80|100}}.
**Buffed ice bomb damage from {{dv|44|88|125}} to {{dv|50|100|125}}.
**Buffed phase 5's ice blast damage from {{dv|60|92|115}} to {{dv|70|100|125}}.
**Nerfed Cryogen's Shield's damage from {{dv|30|60|75}} to {{dv|25|50|62}}.
*AI:
**Transitions into its phases at higher percentages of life. From 74%, 52%, 35%, and 10% to 83%, 66%, 49%, and 15%.
**Now creates ice bombs half as often.
**Now spawns 8-11 minions per 17% health lost instead of 1-3 per 2% health lost.
**Lowered chance to shoot ice blasts in phase 3.
**Phase 4 now exclusively uses its ice shard spread attack instead of exclusively using its ice blast shotgun attack.
**Phase 4 will now attempt to hover above the player and rain ice shards down on them.
**Phase 5 can no longer use its ice blast shotgun attack.
**Phase 6 can no longer use any projectile attacks.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Lowered body's defense.
*Reduced resistance against [[Bee Cloak]], [[Holy Arrow]], [[Star Cloak]], and [[Star Veil]] stars from 83% to 75%.
*Now sets the piercing of projectiles which pierce 2 or more times to 3 instead of 2.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Now becomes invulnerable when there are 3 [[Cosmic Lantern]]s active instead of 2.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*The player can no longer steal her life.
*The player now loses or gains immunity frames depending on how much damage an attack from Supreme Calamitas does.
*Buffed Brimstone Fireblast damage from {{dv|300|400|420}} to {{dv|400|500|625}}.
*Buffed Brimstone Hellblast damage in Normal Mode from 360 to 400.
*Supreme [[Soul Seeker]]s no longer deal contact damage.
*Lowered the chance for Supreme Soul Seekers to fire a laser.
*Buffed Supreme Soul Seeker's laser damage from {{dv|224|400|500}} to {{dv|500|600|750}}.

=== Items ===
*[[Mirror Blade]]'s projectile no longer creates a sound.
*All Calamity [[Wings]] can now have their appearances on the player toggled with visibility.

=== Other ===
*Reduced the [[Revengeance Mode]] damage reduction multiplier from 0.5x to 0.4x, and the multiplier in all other modes from 0.6x to 0.5x.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Arcanum of the Void]].
*Resprited [[Astreal Defeat]], [[The Ballista]], [[Brimblade]], [[Brimrose]], [[Brimstone Fury]], [[Burning Sea]], [[Flarefrost Blade]], Mirror Blade, [[Profaned Sword]], [[Terra-cotta]], and [[Vanquisher Arrow]].
*Resprited [[Bloodstone]], [[Bloodstone Core]], [[Profaned Core]], [[Profaned Shard]], [[Unholy Core]].
*Resprited Astreal Defeat's arrow.
