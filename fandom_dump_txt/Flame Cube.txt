The '''Flame Cube''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Mount]] summoning item, and is the [[exhumed]] form of the [[Exo Box]]. When used, it summons a flying chair mount that behaves similar to the UFO mount summoned by the [[Cosmic Car Key]]. It has no cap to its top movement speed, however, colliding with tiles while traveling over 80 mph will cause the player to crash, killing them.

The Brimthrone has a top speed of 51 mph both vertically and horizontally, for an overall max speed of 62 mph. Holding the {{key|Exo Chair Speed up}} key will cause the chair to build up speed exponentially.

== Notes ==
*The Brimthrone can transport the player across even a large world within seconds, making it ideal for long distance travel, granted there are no blocks obstructing the players path.

== Trivia ==
*The name of the item is a reference to the video game console [[wikipedia:GameCube|the GameCube]].
*The flavor tooltip is a reference to the slogan of DPS Gaming Chair brand.
*The Flame Cube, surprisingly, does not come with UNO.
