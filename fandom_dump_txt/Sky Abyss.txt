The '''Sky Abyss''' is an Epilogue [[Biomes|biome]] added in the Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic Update. It generates in the sky above the [[Abyss]] during the world generation, but they are only visible after [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] has been defeated. When generating all the blocks in that part of the sky get destroyed. The territory completely Fills with water when generating. The border of it has a line of [[Skylac Sand]] that can't be destroyed or teleported through until [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] is defeated. Wings cannot be used in the biome.

It is divided into 2 layers:
'''Lower layer''' is the bottom-left/bottom right half of the biome, it consists of structures similar to the [[Sunken Sea]] and is made of [[Skylac Stone]] and [[Skylac Sand]]. It is much safer than the upper layer in all modes.

'''Upper layer'' is the top-right/top left half of the biome, it consists of small islands made out of [[Esseric Ore]], The [[Esserwyrm]] can spawn there after [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] is defeated. During April First, 5% of the Esseric Islands are replaced with [[Esseric Cake]]



Enemies are able to go to other layers or other biomes when the player is nearby, but most of them despawn once they get too far from the Sky Abyss/[[Sulphurous Sea]] or Normal [[Abyss]].

The water in the bottom layer has the {{color|sky blue|#00BBFF}} color and has the {{color|dark blue|#000644}} color in the upper layer.

== Breath, Light and Detection==
Breath and Light levels work the same as in the normal [[Abyss]], with the lower level being counted the same as the layer 2 of the abyss, and the upper level the same as layer 4.
