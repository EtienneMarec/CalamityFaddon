'''Life Alloys''' are [[Hardmode]] [[crafting material]]s crafted with [[Cryonic Bar|Cryonic]], [[Perennial Bar|Perennial]], and [[Scoria Bar]]s, additionally being dropped from [[Necromantic Geode]]s at a 50% chance. They are primarily used in the crafting of many powerful upgrades. Additionally, they are used in the crafting of multiple Elemental items, and one of the components of [[Miracle Matter]].
