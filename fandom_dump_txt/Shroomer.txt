The '''Shroomer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] and an upgrade to the [[Sniper Rifle]]. It fires bullets at a high velocity along with a translucent mushroom that homes in on and pierces enemies, and deals massive damage.

Holding right click will use its "zoom" ability, which scrolls the viewing area of the screen in the direction of the cursor.
