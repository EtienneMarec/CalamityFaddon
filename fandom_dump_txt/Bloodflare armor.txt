'''Bloodflare armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Bloodstone Core]]s and [[Ruinous Soul]]s. 40 Bloodstone Cores as well as 9 Ruinous Souls are required to craft a set utilizing one headpiece. 84 Bloodstone Cores and 17 Ruinous Souls are needed to make a set including all the headpieces. If the Body Armor, Cuisses, and one of the headpiece varieties are visible (whether equipped or in social slots), the player will have a very faint vibrating shadow behind them.

It consists of the '''Bloodflare Body Armor''' and '''Bloodflare Cuisses''', as well as having five different headpieces suited to each class, being: the '''Bloodflare Ram Mask''', '''Bloodflare Horned Helm''', '''Bloodflare Hydra Hood''', '''Bloodflare Wyvern Helm''', and '''Bloodflare Imp Mask'''. All helmets share a set bonus, but also provide set bonuses specific to their respective class as well.

The '''Bloodflare Body Armor''' gives 35 defense, 12% increased damage, and 8% increased critical strike chance, and +40 max life. It also gives 10 extra life regeneration and +30 defense while submerged in lava. The '''Bloodflare Cuisses''' gives 29 defense, 10% increased damage, 7% increased critical strike chance, and 17% increased movement speed.
*The '''Bloodflare Ram Mask''' gives 49 defense, 10% increased melee damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''Bloodflare Horned Helm''' gives 34 defense and 10% increased ranged damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''Bloodflare Hydra Hood''' gives 22 defense, 10% increased magic damage and critical strike chance, +100 mana, and 17% reduced mana cost.
*The '''Bloodflare Wyvern Helm''' gives 16 defense and 5% increased minion damage.
*The '''Bloodflare Imp Mask''' gives 28 defense, 10% increased Spy damage and critical strike chance, and 5% increased movement speed.

== Set Bonuses ==
Regardless of the headpiece chosen, equipping a full set of Bloodflare armor will provide the following set bonuses:
*Greatly increased life regeneration (equivalent to the [[Crimson armor]]).
*Enemies below 50% life will drop a [[heart]] upon being struck every 5 seconds.
*Enemies killed during a [[Blood Moon]] will have a 50% chance to drop [[Blood Orb]]s upon death.

In addition to the above, each headpiece provides its own class-specific set bonus:

'''Bloodflare Ram Mask:'''
*+900 to [[Aggro]].
*Causes true melee strikes to [[Lifesteal|heal]] on hit.
*Grants the player the [[Blood Frenzy]] [[buff]] for five seconds after landing 15 true melee strikes.
**This effect has a 30 second [[Blood Frenzy Cooldown|cooldown]].
*These bonuses only occur hitting enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher, with the exception of enemies spawned from [[statues]].

'''Bloodflare Horned Helm:'''
*Pressing {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} allows the player to unleash sixteen Bloodflare Souls to damage enemies.
**The souls deal 300 damage and are affected by ranged damage bonuses.
**This effect has a 30 second [[Bloodflare Soul Cooldown|cooldown]].
*Ranged weapons fire bloodsplosion orbs every 2.5 seconds.
**The explosions deal 80% of the weapon's damage and are softcapped at 150 damage.

'''Bloodflare Hydra Hood:'''
*Magic weapons fire ghostly bolts every 1.67 seconds.
**Ghostly bolts deal 130% of the attack's damage but are softcapped at 250 damage.
*Magic critical strikes cause flame explosions every 2 seconds.
**The fireballs have a 50% chance to inflict [[On Fire!]] for 5 seconds and deal 50% of the damage of the attack that summoned them, though they are softcapped at 120 damage.
**Fireballs are only generated from enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher, with the exception of enemies spawned from statues.

'''Bloodflare Wyvern Helm:'''
*50% increased minion damage and +3 max minions.
*Summons three of Polterghast's mines to circle around the player, which explode upon contact with enemies.
**The Polterghast Mines deal 3500 damage.
*If the player is at 90% of their life or above, they will gain 10% increased minion damage.
*If they are at 50% of their life or below, they will gain 20 defense and +2 life regen.

'''Bloodflare Imp Mask:'''
*Spy critical strikes have a 50% chance to [[lifesteal|heal]] the player.
**Heals only occur when hitting enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher, with the exception of enemies spawned from statues.
*If the player is above 80% of their life, their defense is boosted by 30 and their Spy critical strike chance is boosted by 5%.
*If the player is below 80% of their life, their Spy damage will be boosted by 10%.
*Gives a maximum [[Spy stealth]] of 120.
