The '''Regulus Riot''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] dropped by [[Astrum Deus]]. It fires a disk that flies to where the cursor was when it was used and homes in on enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Regulus Riot will cause it to explode into energy stars on death.
