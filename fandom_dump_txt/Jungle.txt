The '''Jungle''' [[biome|book]] features seven new [[enemies]], including a [[mini boss]]. Most of the enemies spawn after the [[Golem]] is defeated and a "[[plague]]" is released into the Jungle, and they are therefore related to [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] [[boss]], which is summoned and fought in this biome. During Godseeker Mode, [[Draconic Swarmer]]s begin spawning in the biome, and [[The Dragonfolly]] and [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] bosses must be fought here as well.

There is also a unique [[Jungle Bio-center Lab]], containing the [[schematic]] used to craft the third tier of Draedon's arsenal and decrypt the fourth schematic.

In [[Death Mode]], the player will be inflicted with the [[Bleeding]] debuff when touching water. [[Thorny bushes]] will also inflict [[Poisoned]].
