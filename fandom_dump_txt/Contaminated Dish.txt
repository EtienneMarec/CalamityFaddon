The '''Contaminated Dish''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Bomb]] that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon hitting enemies/tiles, it explodes into harmless glass shards, releasing nanobot clouds that inflicts the [[Plague]] Debuff in the process. Said clouds deal 65% of the weapon's base damage, but grants a shorter invincibility frame, at 5 instead of the usual 10 i-frame.

Performing a [[Spy|Stealth Strike]] causes the dish to be thrown much harder, exploding into damaging glass shards that sticks into struck enemy instead, dealing periodic damage for a while before disappearing.
