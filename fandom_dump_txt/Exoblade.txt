The '''Exoblade''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that auto-swings. It has one of the most complex crafting trees of all swords in the Calamity Mod.

On swing, it fires a spread of colorful projectiles with different effects related to its components. These include:
*[[Terratomere|A homing green exo beam]] that explodes into multiple homing swords with lingering slashs on enemy hits.
*[[Flarefrost Blade|A fast red exo beam]] that chooses an enemy then orbits around them while firing fireballs inwards.
*[[Flarefrost Blade|A slow blue exo beam]] that chooses an enemy then orbits around them while firing icicles inwards.
*[[Stellar Striker|A mint green exo beam]] that summons lunar flares above hit enemies.
*[[Anarchy Blade|An orange exo beam]] that creates a colossal flaming explosion on enemy hits.

True melee hits will cause all of the onhit effects from the above projectiles, but with a 230% damage modifier. If an enemy is killed by the Exoblade's true melee strikes, a large colorful explosion occurs that inflicts [[Exo Freeze]] onto all enemies within a 40 tile radius for 30 seconds. 

When Exoblade and its projectiles hit an enemy, they will inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
*[[Plague]] (3 seconds)
*[[Ichor]] (3 seconds)
*[[Cursed Flames]] (3 seconds)
*[[Brimstone Flames]] (3 seconds)
