The '''1.4.2.102''' update retiered [[Astrum Deus]] from post-[[Plantera]] to post-[[Lunatic Cultist]], along with giving it a new spawn method. The update also reworked the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]] and [[Calamitas]], along with editing most of the mod's weapon tooltips and debuff durations. The update also reworked some of Calamity weapons to have more interesting effects, most notably on some of the mod's [[spears]], [[bows]], and [[swords]].

== Additions ==
*Added a [[Configuration option]] to buff bosses from the Thorium Mod in [[Revengeance Mode]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*Now despawns quicker, and will despawn while not at either [[Ocean]].
*Changed most Normal Mode drop chances:
**Decreased the chance for [[Aero Stone]] from 20% to 11.11%.
**Changed the chances for [[Angler Tackle Bag]]s, [[High Test Fishing Line]]s, and [[Tackle Box]]es from 6.67% in pre-Hardmode and 12.5% in Hardmode, to 8.33% at all times.
**Changed the chances for [[Angler Earring]]s, [[Fisherman's Pocket Guide]]s, [[Sextant]]s, and [[Weather Radio]]s from 10% in pre-Hardmode and 20% in Hardmode, to 11.11% at all times.
**Changed the chances for [[Angler Hat]]s, [[Angler Vest]]s, [[Angler Pants]], [[Fishing Potion]]s, [[Crate Potion]]s, and [[Sonar Potion]]s from 20% in pre-Hardmode and 33% in Hardmode, to 25% at all times.
**Changed the chance for the [[Golden Bug Net]] from 5% in pre-Hardmode and 10% in Hardmode, to 6.67% at all times.
