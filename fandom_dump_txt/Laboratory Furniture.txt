'''Laboratory Furniture''' are unique pieces of [[furniture]] that are usually found in [[Arsenal Lab]]s. Aged and normal Laboratory variants can be crafted using [[Dubious Plating]] and either [[Laboratory Plating]]s or [[Rusted Plating]]s. Some types have Exo variants which can be crafted with [[Exo Plating]]. The following types exist, listed with their respective dimensions:
*Consoles (2 wide × 3 high)
*Containment Boxes (3 wide × 2 high)
*Displays (4 wide × 3 high)
*Electric Panels (2 wide × 2 high)
*Reinforced Crates (2 wide × 2 high)
*Screens (3 wide × 2 high)
*Servers (2 wide × 4 high)
*Terminals (4 wide × 3 high)
*Tablets (3 wide x 2 high)

== Notes ==
*Electric Panels and Screens may only be placed on walls, similar to [[tgc:Paintings|paintings]].
*Unlike other [[crates]], Reinforced Crates can not be placed on top of each other.
*As of now Exo Tablets and Aged Laboratory Tablets currently are not in the game.
*As of now Laboratory Tablets are not crafted (but they will in the future)

== Trivia ==
*The Design of the laboratory furniture was inspired by various furniture items from the game [[wikipedia:Starbound|Starbound]].
