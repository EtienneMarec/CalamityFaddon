The '''Reaver Helmet''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] headpiece that acted as the summoner headpiece for the [[Reaver armor]] set, granting a total of 36 defense. It was crafted from [[Essence of Sunlight|Essences of Sunlight]], [[Jungle Spores]], and [[Perennial Bar]]s. The helmet provided 3 defense, 5% increased minion damage, +2 max minions, and increased minion knockback.
<br/>Its set bonus granted an additional 16% minion damage and also summoned a Reaver Orb to fight for the player. The Reaver Orb floated above the player's head and fired spore clouds in multiple directions, dealing 80 damage.
