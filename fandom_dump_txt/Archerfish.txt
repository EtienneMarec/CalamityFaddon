The '''Archerfish''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]] obtained from [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as ammo, bullets are replaced with a stream of water in a similar fashion to the [[Water Gun]], albeit much faster and damage-dealing, and can pierce up to 2 enemies before dissipating upon hitting a third.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Unreal'''.
