The '''Disgusting Seawater''' is a [[pet]] summoning item obtained by using a [[Bug Net]] or one of its upgrades on a [[Microbial Cluster]]. It summons a Microbial Cluster to follow the player and provide a small amount of light.

== Trivia ==
*The flavor text is a reference to how the Microbial Cluster has no sprite, and can only be identified by the dust it produces.
