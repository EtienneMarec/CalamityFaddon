The '''Ice Tomb''' was a structure located in the [[Ice biome]] that was composed of [[Ice Block]]s. It looked and behaved similarly to [[Desert]] [[Pyramid]]s. Only one could generate in the world.

The Ice Tomb contained an unlocked [[Frozen Chest]] with one of three possible different valuable items related to ice. Only one valuable item could appear in the chest.

The Ice Tomb has been effectively replaced with the [[Ice Bio-center Lab]].

== Notes ==
*Despite having a Frozen Chest in the tomb, it '''did not''' contain a [[Staff of the Frost Hydra]].

== Tips ==
*Seeing as this structure could be found and looted at any point in the game, it was possible to acquire the [[Frozen Turtle Shell]] before entering [[Hardmode]].

== Trivia ==
*Before the Calamity 1.3 update, the tomb was made out of [[Snow Brick]]s.
