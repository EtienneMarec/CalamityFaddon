The '''1.4.2.003''' update added several new [[furniture]] sets, along with making various changes to most of the mod's existing [[blocks]], [[walls]], and furniture. It also added several [[configuration options]], and fixed various bugs.

== Additions ==
*Added configuration options for disabling [[Adrenaline]] and [[Rage]], moving the Adrenaline and Rage meters, undoing the [[Expert Mode]] [[Chilled]] [[Water]] changes, undoing the Expert Mode [[debuff]] duration reducal, [[Proficiency]], undoing the [[Lunar Events]] enemy wave nerf, disabling enemy spawns near towns, and boosting mining speed.
*Added Botanic furniture:
**Added [[Botanic Bathtub]], [[Botanic Bed]], [[Botanic Bench]], [[Botanic Bookcase]], [[Botanic Candelabra]], [[Botanic Candle]], [[Botanic Chair]], [[Botanic Chandelier]], [[Botanic Chest]], [[Botanic Clock]], [[Botanic Door]], [[Botanic Dresser]], [[Botanic Lamp]], [[Botanic Lantern]], [[Botanic Piano]], [[Botanic Planter]], [[Botanic Platform]], [[Botanic Sink]], [[Botanic Table]], and [[Botanic Work Bench]].
*Added Eutrophic furniture:
**Added [[Eutrophic Bathtub]], [[Eutrophic Bed]], [[Eutrophic Bench]], [[Eutrophic Bookcase]], [[Eutrophic Candelabra]], [[Eutrophic Candle]], [[Eutrophic Chair]], [[Eutrophic Chandelier]], [[Eutrophic Chest]], [[Eutrophic Door]], [[Eutrophic Dresser]], [[Eutrophic Lamp]], [[Eutrophic Lantern]], [[Eutrophic Piano]], [[Eutrophic Platform]], [[Eutrophic Shelf]], [[Eutrophic Sink]], [[Eutrophic Table]], and [[Eutrophic Work Bench]].
*Added Otherworldly furniture:
**Added [[Otherworldly Bathtub]], [[Otherworldly Bed]], [[Otherworldly Bookcase]], [[Otherworldly Candelabra]], [[Otherworldly Candle]], [[Otherworldly Chair]], [[Otherworldly Chandelier]], [[Otherworldly Chest]], [[Otherworldly Clock]], [[Otherworldly Door]], [[Otherworldly Dresser]], [[Otherworldly Lamp]], [[Otherworldly Lantern]], [[Otherworldly Piano]], [[Otherworldly Platform]], [[Otherworldly Sink]], [[Otherworldly Sofa]], [[Otherworldly Table]], and [[Otherworldly Work Bench]].
*Added Plagued Plate furniture:
**Added [[Plagued Plate Basin]], [[Plagued Plate Bathtub]], [[Plagued Plate Bed]], [[Plagued Plate Candelabra]], [[Plagued Plate Candle]], [[Plagued Plate Chair]], [[Plagued Plate Chair]], [[Plagued Plate Chandelier]], [[Plagued Plate Chest]], [[Plagued Plate Clock]], [[Plagued Plate Door]], [[Plagued Plate Dresser]], [[Plagued Plate Lamp]], [[Plagued Plate Lantern]], [[Plagued Plate Piano]], [[Plagued Plate Piano]], [[Plagued Plate Platform]], [[Plagued Plate Sink]], [[Plagued Plate Sofa]], [[Plagued Plate Table]], [[Plagued Plate Work Bench]], and [[Plague Infuser]].

== Changes ==
*Increased the width of the [[Abyss]].
*[[Asphalt]]'s speed nerf now only applies to [[Revengeance Mode]] and higher.
*[[Astrum Deus]] changes:
**Now enrages if gravity is flipped.
**Now despawns slower.
**Now despawns at daytime, and is no longer able to despawn due to distance from the player.
*Bosses fought after nighttime bosses during [[Boss Rush]] now change the time back to day.
*[[Chicken Cannon]]'s rockets now ignore [[Water]]. <!---Otherwise they would just infinitely stay still in Water--->
*[[Clam Crusher]] no longer inflicts [[Silva Stun]] on bosses.
*[[Cosmic Immaterializer]] no longer causes projectiles to deal 25% less damage.
*[[Crystalline]] now requires 50 [[Wulfrum Knife|Wulfrum Knives]] instead of 50 [[Throwing Knife|Throwing Knives]].
*Buffed [[Dungeon Guardian]]'s damage reduction from 99.99% to 99.9999%, and it now shoots skulls every second in Revengeance Mode.
*[[Exoblade]]'s beams no longer heal the player.
*[[Plasma Rifle]] changes:
**Nerfed shot speed from 20 to 12.
**Both projectiles no longer produce light, now have 10 extra updates, and have had their dust tweaked.
*Buffed [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]' Boss Rush health from {{dv|10,000,000|16,000,000|0|19,200,000}} to {{dv|12,500,000|20,000,000|0|24,000,000}}, and she can no longer be targeted while firing her Holy Ray.
*Enraged Revengeance [[Skeletron]] now shoots skulls every second.
*Increased the post [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] [[Solar Eclipse]]'s contact damage modifier from 300% to 350%.
*[[Lethal Lava Burn]] is now exclusive to Revengeance Mode and higher.
*[[Starcore]] is now non-consumable and stacks to 1 instead of 20.
*Buffed [[Starnight Lance]]'s damage from 50 to 60, and it now hits twice instead of once.
*[[Stellar Striker]]'s flares now deal 50% of the weapon's damage instead of 70%.
*[[Silva armor]] now requires [[Tenebris]] to craft:
**All Silva Helmets now require 6 Tenebris.
**Silva Armor now requires 12 Tenebris.
**Silva Leggings now require 9 Tenebris.
*[[Terminus]] now has a tooltip stating that it activates Boss Rush.
*Rebalanced most Calamity pre-Hardmode [[tools]]:
**Nerfed [[Aerial Hamaxe]]'s damage from 25 to 20, its use time from 19 to 25, increased its axe power from 20 to 25, decreased its hammer power from 75 to 65, and increased its size.
**Nerfed [[Gelpick]]'s damage from 20 to 12, its use time from 14 to 19, and its knockback from 4 to 2.5.
**Nerfed [[Greatbay Pickaxe]]'s damage from 13 to 9, its use time from 14 to 15, and increased its size.
**Nerfed [[Reefclaw Hamaxe]]'s use time from 18 to 28, and buffed its knockback from 4 to 6.
**Nerfed [[Skyfringe Pickaxe]]'s damage from 17 to 12, its use time from 8 to 15, its use animation from 14 to 15, its knockback from 4 to 2.5, and increased its size.
**Nerfed [[Wulfrum Axe]]'s damage from 7 to 5, its use time from 7 to 25, its use animation from 15 to 25, increased its knockback from 3 to 4.5, and increased its size.
**Nerfed [[Wulfrum Hammer]]'s use time from 7 to 28, its use animation from 15 to 28, and increased its knockback from 4 to 5.5.
**Nerfed [[Wulfrum Pickaxe]]'s use time from 6 to 15, its use animation from 13 to 15, and increased its knockback from 1 to 2.
*Buffed [[Vivid Clarity]]'s damage from 350 to 500.
*Defeating the [[Wall of Flesh]] for the first time now displays a status message signifying that the Sunken Sea has been buffed.
