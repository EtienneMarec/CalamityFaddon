The '''1.5.1.001''' (or '''1.5.1.1''') update added and removed many items, notably adding many [[dedicated items]] and two [[furniture sets]], and removing several secondary difficulty modes, leading to changes in the obtainment methods of former [[Challenge items]]. [[Debuffs]] received new synergy and vulnerability mechanics, and cooldown debuffs are replaced with custom separated icons. This update was also notable for reworking the [[Ark of the Cosmos]] along with all of the weapons in its crafting tree, beginning with the [[Broken Biome Blade]] and [[Fractured Ark]].
