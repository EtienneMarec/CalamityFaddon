The ''' 1.1.5.08''' update removed several items from the mod, made some balance changes, and resprited a handful of items.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Charged Dart Blaster]], [[Crystal Flare Staff]], [[Plasma Rifle]], [[Scorpio]], and [[Sun God Staff]].
*Added [[Solar God Spirit (buff)]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Frost beams now deal 40 damage at night, 50 at day, and 60 after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
**Nebula lasers now deal {{dv|30|46}} damage at night, {{dv|35|70}} at day, and {{dv|45|50}} after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**Lightning bolts now deal {{dv|100|120}} damage at night, {{dv|150|300}} at day, and {{dv|120|140}} after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**Nebula piercers now deal {{dv|40|52}} damage at night, {{dv|45|90}} at day, and {{dv|60|70}} after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**Gravity spit now deals {{dv|35|42}} damage at night, {{dv|40|80}} at day, and {{dv|43|56}} after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**Now has dialogue based on if the player is hit by its tail or head.
**Now has screen shaders.
**All segments now inflict [[Darkness]] and [[Frostburn]].
**Reduced [[Chilled]] duration from Head contact from {{dv|6.67|16.67}} seconds to 3.33.
**Body and tail segments no longer inflict Chilled.
*Blighted Gel can now drop from [[Crimulan Blight Slime]]s.
*[[The Perforators]] and [[Desert Scourge]] now have movement similar to The Devourer of Gods.
*Increased [[Plantera]]'s [[Living Shard]] drop count from {{dv|4-6|6-8}} to {{dv|6-8|8-10}}.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s damage now lowers as her health does.
*[[Wyvern]]s no longer drop [[Wyvern Feather]]s.

=== Renames ===
*Renamed Apocalyptic Expulsor to [[Conclave Crossfire]].
*Renamed Braelor's Shield to [[Asgard's Valor]].
*Renamed Calamitas' Scroll to [[Sigil of Calamitas]].
*Renamed Ebonian Gel to [[Blighted Gel]].
*Renamed Falcon Boots to [[Angel Treads]].

=== Items ===
*Broke up the tooltips for [[Ataxia Armor]], [[Ataxia Subligar]], [[Calamity Ring]], [[Daedalus Breastplate]], [[Daedalus Emblem]], [[Daedalus Leggings]],  [[Mana Overloader]], [[Monsoon]], [[Nebulous Core]], [[Necklace of Vexation]], [[Reaver Cuisses]], [[Reaver Scale Mail]], [[Statigel Armor]], [[Statigel Greaves]], [[Statis' Ninja Belt]], [[Xeroc Cuisses]], [[Xeroc Plate Mail]], and [[Yharim's Insignia]] into separate lines instead of having their entire tooltips take up one line.
*Lowered the amount of dust created by [[Night's Ray]].
*Nerfed [[Conclave Crossfire]]'s shot speed but increased bullet size.
*[[Aeries]] changes:
**Nerfed damage from 45 to 32.
**Nerfed knockback from 6.5 to 5.5.
**Rarity lowered from {{rare|9}} to {{rare|7}}.
*[[Core of Calamity|Cores of Calamity]] now stack up to 69 instead of 5.
*[[Darklight Greatsword]]'s projectiles now deal 75% of the weapon's damage instead of 50%.
*Buffed [[Discordian Wings]]’ horizontal speed from 9 to 10, but nerfed its horizontal acceleration from 3 to 2.5.
*Thrown [[Eradicator]]s now last for 4 seconds instead of 10.
*[[Galactica Singularity|Galactica Singularities]] now stack up to 69 instead of 1.
*Buffed [[Galeforce]]'s damage from 14 to 16.
*[[Flarewing Bow]] now converts [[Wooden Arrow]]s into flaming obsidian bats.
*Flarewing Bow now lowers all arrow damage by 50% except for Wooden Arrows.
*Flarewing Bow now buffs Wooden Arrow damage by 25%.
*[[The God's Gambit]] now produces less slime.
*[[Hyperius Bullet]]s can now be used by all guns.
*Hyperius Bullets and [[Verium Bullet]]s are now completely invisible aside from their dusts they emit.
*[[Lunarian Bow]]'s projectiles now emit half as much dust.
*Monsoon changes:
**Now only has a 1 in 25 chance of shooting a typhoon arrow.
**Tornadoes are now smaller and last for less time.
**Now has a 1 in 12 chance to shoot a shark.
*[[Overloaded Blaster]] changes:
**Nerfed damage from 22 to 16, and its use time from 11 to 16.
**Projectiles now pierce 2 times instead of 3.
**Now has a 33% chance to not consume [[Gel]].
*All Calamity [[wings]] now give a description of their flight times.
*Simplifed [[Tarragon Breastplate]]’s tooltip.

=== Recipes ===
*[[Aerospec Breastplate]] no longer uses 1 [[Emerald]] in its recipe.
*[[Aerospec Helm]] no longer uses 1 Emerald in its recipe.
*Aeries now requires 1 [[Phoenix Blaster]], 5 [[Shroomite Bar]]s, and 5 [[Spectre Bar]]s instead of 1 [[Venus Magnum]], 3 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]], 1 Core of Calamity, and 6 [[Core of Cinder|Cores of Cinder]] in its recipe.
*Conclave Crossfire no longer uses 1 [[Xenopopper]], 5 Bars of Life, and 1 Core of Calamity in its recipe, and instead uses 7 Cores of Chaos.
*Darklight Greatsword now requires 5 [[Fallen Star]]s, 1 [[Soul of Light]], and 1 [[Soul of Night]].
*[[Devastation]] now requires 10 [[Stardust]] instead of 5 Crystallite, 8 Fallen Stars, 3 Souls of Light, 3 Souls of Night, and 20 Stardust.
*Monsoon now requires 5 [[Shark Fin]]s and 10 [[Vortex Fragment]]s in its recipe instead of 20 [[Ectoplasm]] and 9 [[Verstaltite Bar]]s.
*Replaced [[Chaos Serpent Shard]] with 1 Core of Chaos in [[Cruptix Bar]]'s recipe.
*[[Cobalt Shield]] can now be crafted with 15 [[Cobalt Bar|Cobalt]] or [[Palladium Bar]]s.
*Core of Calamity now uses 1 Core of Chaos in its recipe instead of 1 [[Essence of Chaos]].
*Replaced [[Crystallite]] with 4 [[Stardust]] in [[Verstaltite Bar]]'s recipe.
*Replaced [[Ancient Feather]] with 1 Essence of Cinder in [[Draedon Bar]]'s recipe.
*Removed [[Energy Orb]] from [[Ueliace Bar]]'s recipe.
*Replaced [[Diamond Dust]] with [[Feather]]s in the recipes for [[Blizzard in a Bottle]], [[Cloud in a Bottle]], and [[Sandstorm in a Bottle]].
*[[Muramasa]] can now be crafted with 15 Cobalt or Palladium Bars.
*[[True Paladin's Hammer]] now requires 5 Cruptix Bars in its recipe instead of 7 Cruptix Bars, 25 [[Soul of Fright|Souls of Fright]], and 10 Souls of Night.
*All items made at the [[Particle Accelerator]] are now crafted at a [[Mythril Anvil|Hardmode anvil]] or [[Bookcase]].

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Caustic Edge]], Conclave Crossfire, [[Firestorm Cannon]], Flarewing Bow, [[Megafleet]], [[Megalodon]], [[Meowthrower]], Monsoon, [[Shredder]], [[Spectre Rifle]], [[True Caustic Edge]], [[True Tyrant's Ultisword]], and [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]].
*Resprited Megafleet and [[Starfleet]]'s plasma blast projectiles.
*Animated [[The Hive Mind]]'s sprite.
*Resprited [[Skyline Wings]].
*Resprited Aerospec Breastplate, Aerospec Helm, and [[Aerospec Leggings]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a capitalization error in [[Mana Overloader]]'s tooltip.

== Removals ==
*Removed [[Particle Accelerator]].
*Removed Ancient Feather, Chaos Serpent Shard, [[Crimulan Gel]], Crystallite, Diamond Dust, Energy Orb, [[Living Dew Droplet]], [[Spectral Feather]], [[Twisted Tendril]].
