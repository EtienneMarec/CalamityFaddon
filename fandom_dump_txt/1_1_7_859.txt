The '''1.1.7.859''' update reworked the [[Siren Lure and Leviathan]] boss fight along with making changes to several other [[bosses]]. 

== Changes ==
*[[Crabulon]] now takes up 8 NPC slots instead of 7.
*Changed [[Cryogen]]'s 10% life message from "Cryogen's core is all that remains!" to "Cryogen is derping out!".
*[[Desert Scourge]] now takes up 7 NPC slots instead of 5 and is now immune to all debuffs.
*[[Harpy|Harpies]] now only drop [[Sky Glaze]] after the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] has been defeated.
*Buffed [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s contact damage from {{dv|440|484}} to {{dv|450|585}} and he now has an increased DPS cap after he has been defeated previously.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] changes:
**Buffed health from {{dv|48,000|56,160}} to {{dv|50,000|58,500}}, her contact damage from {{dv|130|156}} to {{dv|190|228}}, and her defense from 40 to 55.
**Buffed missile damage from 80 to {{dv|114|180}}.
**Increased projectile damage counts as her health lowers.
**Altered pattern of when she shoots missiles versus stingers.
**Now despawns quicker.
**Now takes up 12 NPC slots instead of 10.
**Buffed [[Plaguebringer Shade]]'s contact damage from {{dv|110|132}} to {{dv|130|156}} and it now shoots less stingers.
*[[Profaned Guardians]] changes:
**Buffed main guardian's damage from {{dv|130|156}} to {{dv|175|227}}.
**Main guardian now takes 1 damage from any attack that would take over half of its max health.
*Buffed [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s contact damage from {{dv|110|132}} to {{dv|140|182}} and gave her an increased DPS cap after she has been defeated previously.
*[[Ravager]] no longer drops [[Ueliace Bar]]s.<!---Dropped 3-6--->
*[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] changes:
**Buffed health from {{dv|250,000|300,000}} to {{dv|280,000|336,000}}, contact damage from {{dv|280|336}} to {{dv|310|372}}, and its defense from 50 to 70.
**Buffed fireball damage from {{dv|200|240}} to {{dv|240|360}}.
**Now takes up 15 NPC slots instead of 5.
**Now despawns if the player gets too far away from it.
**Increased charging speed.
**Cosmic Lanterns no longer take up any NPC slots, nerfed their damage from {{dv|220|264}} to {{dv|210|252}}, and increased their knockback resistance.
*[[Siren Lure and Leviathan]] changes:
**Nerfed Siren Lure's health from {{dv|38,000|41,800}} to {{dv|30,000|33,000}} and its defense from 35 to 30.
**Nerfed The Leviathan's health from 999,999 to {{dv|60,000|66,000}}, and her defense from 99,999 to 50.
**The Leviathan is now able to be damaged and killed.
***She will no longer be instantly defeated once the Siren Lure is killed. 
***Killing her first will cause the Siren Lure to enrage and vice versa.
***Now drops loot if killed after the Siren Lure has.
**Siren Lure now drops {{gc|30}} instead of {{gc|18}}.
**[[Aquatic Aberration]]s and [[Parasea]]s are now larger.<!---Removed size multiplier--->
**Buffed Aquatic Aberration's health from {{dv|800|960}} to {{dv|900|1080}}, and nerfed its damage from {{dv|80|96}} to {{dv|75|90}}.
**Buffed Parasea's damage from {{dv|200|240}} to {{dv|350|420}}.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**Nerfed health from {{dv|2,354,000|2,589,400}} to {{dv|2,000,000|2,200,000}}.
**Now spawns her brothers at 55% health instead of 70% and enters phase 2 at 25% health instead of 10%.
**All projectiles now collide with blocks.
**Now has an increased DPS cap after she has been defeated previously.
**Now inflicts [[Vulnerability Hex]] for 15 seconds on contact instead of 10.
**Brothers no longer create dust when hit.

*Optimized the alt fire code for [[Aegis Blade]], [[Blossom Flux]], [[Briny Baron]], [[Firestorm Cannon]], [[Malachite]], [[Plasma Rifle]], [[Scorpio]], [[SHPC]], and [[Vesuvius]].
