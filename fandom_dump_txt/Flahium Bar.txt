'''Flahium Bars''' are Godseeker Mode [[bars]] that are made from holding [[Uncharged Flahium]] in the inventory for 60 in-game minutes. They can be used in creating some very powerful items that can be used against the various [[Superboss]]es. Flahium bars will become [[Decayed Flahium]] within 72 minutes of being crafted if not used in anything.
