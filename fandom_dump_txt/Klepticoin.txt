'''Klepticoins''' are a [[Coins|currency item]] used to store excessive amounts of [[Cosmilite Coin]]s. When used as ammo by the [[Coin Gun]] or any similar items, they deal a base of 6000 damage. Completing the [[22 Bullet Theory]] event yields a single Klepticoin upon defeat and the [[Challenge Rush]] event while using Curses (described on the Boss Rush page) will net varying amounts of Klepticoins.

== Notes ==
*As the second-highest value coin in the game, one Klepticoin is equivalent to {{value|0|0|0|0|100}} (one hundred), or {{value|10000|0|0|0|0}} (ten thousand), or {{value|0|1000000|0|0|0}} (one million), or {{value|0|0|100000000|0|0}} (one hundred million), or {{value|0|0|0|10000000000|0}} (ten billion).
*If Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic addon is enabled, it can be used as ammo for [[The Ones Striker]].

== Trivia ==
*The item is themed after the Greed boss from the Ancients Awakened mod, whose owner is the item's dedicatee.
**The fact that it upgrades from Cosmilite Coins is a reference to how Greed was intended to be a member of [[The Devourer of Gods]]' species.
**The item was implemented as a memorial to Ancients Awakened, due to its original owner's leaving.
*It is technically possible to obtain a Klepticoin in [[Pre-Hardmode]]. However, it would require farming for a very, very long time.
