The '''Megaskeet''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Guns|gun]] that is an upgrade to the [[SDOMG]]. It rapidly fires bullets, with every other shots turning into a stream of infinitely piercing light that does not consume ammo. Every 17 shots, it will instead fire a predictively homing [[Sunskater]] that explodes into 2-3 cinder shards, which also does not consume ammo.
