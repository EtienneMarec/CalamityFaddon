The '''Lumenous Amulet''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. While it is equipped, the player's attacks inflict the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]], and, while in the [[Abyss]], they gain 25% increased max life and increased Abyss light level by 2. It also grants immunity to the Crush Depth debuff.
