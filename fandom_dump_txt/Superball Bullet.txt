'''Superball Bullets''' are a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bullet]] type made from [[Parched Scale|Parched Scales]] and [[Meteor Shot]]s. They start off with a slow velocity, which increases each time they bounce on a block or pierce an enemy, to a maximum of 5 times.
