The '''1.2.4.201''' update added the [[Evil Island]] and [[Ice Tomb]] structures. It also changed the health values of most of the mod's [[enemies]] and made some changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]].
{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}
== Additions ==
*Added [[Evil Island]] and [[Ice Tomb]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Increased spectral worm's speed.
*Decreased laser spread of small worms. 
*The small worms no longer count as bosses. 
*Now spawn [[Astrum Deus Probe]]s at a 4% chance when hit if there are no more than 5 active. 
*Changed free Astrum Deus Probe damage from {{dv|100|140}} to {{dv|80|160}}, and their health from {{dv|2500|3500}} to {{dv|2000|4000}}. 

==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*Nerfed clone's health from {{dv|50,000|75,000}} to {{dv|35,000|70,000}}, and its damage from {{dv|300|450}} to {{dv|200|400}}.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Now takes up 64 NPC slots instead of 32.
*Now teleports more slowly. 
*Now only spawns dust when not teleporting.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Lowered the time it takes for it to despawn when it has no target.
*Nerfed [[Dark Energy]] health from {{dv|28,000|44,800}} to {{dv|22,000|44,000}}, and changed their damage from {{dv|190|304}} to {{dv|160|320}}.
*Dark Energies no longer emit dust when hit. 

==== [[Crabulon]] ====
*Nerfed [[Crab Shroom]] health from {{dv|80|112}} to {{dv|25|50}}, and changed their damage from {{dv|35|49}} to {{dv|25|50}}.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Lowered the amount of lasers and fireballs Calamitas fires in [[Death Mode]].
*Changed Cataclysm's base health from {{dv|3500|5600|8800|17,600}} to {{dv|3000|6000|9000|18,000}}, and nerfed his base contact damage from {{dv|88|140}} to {{dv|70|140}}.
*Changed Catastrophe's base health from {{dv|3000|4000|7680|16,000}} to {{dv|2500|5000|7600|16,000}}, and nerfed his base contact damage from {{dv|100|160}} to {{dv|80|160}}.
*Nerfed [[Soul Seeker]] health from {{dv|7500|10,500}} to {{dv|5000|10,000}}, but buffed their contact damage from {{dv|60|84}} to {{dv|60|120}}.
*Nerfed [[Life Seeker]] health from {{dv|300|420}} to {{dv|200|400}}, and changed their contact damage from {{dv|45|63}} to {{dv|35|70}}.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Buffed contact damage from {{dv|50|80}} to {{dv|60|96}}.
*Now has 10 defense.
*Can no longer fire projectiles unless fully opaque.
*Now teleports more slowly. 
*Now shoots ice blasts more often.
*No longer gains additional damage and defense based on its remaining health.
*Nerfed [[Aurora Spirit]] health from {{dv|360|504}} to {{dv|220|440}}, and changed their damage from {{dv|70|98}} to {{dv|60|120}}. 
*Nerfed Cryocore variant 1's health from {{dv|400|560}} to {{dv|220|440}}, and changed its damage from {{dv|55|77}} to {{dv|40|80}}.
*Nerfed Cryocore variant 2's health from {{dv|500|700}} to {{dv|300|600}}, and changed its damage from {{dv|65|91}} to {{dv|55|110}}.

*The smaller Desert Scourges no longer count as bosses.

***Buffed tail damage from {{dv|10|14}} to {{dv|10|20}}.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Now shoots lasers more often in Revengeance Mode.
*Movement speed and laser speed are now faster in Revengeance Mode.
*Rain projectiles such as those from [[Nimbus Rod]] and [[Shaderain Staff]] now deal 35% damage to its body segments instead of 65%.

=== Items ===
*[[Silva armor]] pieces now use [[Effulgent Feather]]s in their recipes instead of [[Yharon Soul Fragment]]s.
*[[Auric Tesla armor]]'s [[Flying Carpet]] now has a unique sprite.
*Adjusted the recipes of Auric Tesla armor's individual pieces to require less materials:
**Auric Tesla Helm now requires 10 [[Life Alloy|Bars of Life]], 7 [[Core of Calamity|Cores of Calamity]], 15 [[Darksun Fragment]]s, 100 [[Endothermic Energy]], 5 [[Galactica Singularity|Galactica Singularities]], 100 [[Nightmare Fuel]], and 35 [[Phantoplasm]] instead of 20 Bars of Life, 15 Cores of Calamity, 30 Darksun Fragments, 200 Endothermic Energy, 10 Galactica Singularities, 200 Nightmare Fuel, and 70 Phantoplasm.
**Auric Tesla Body Armor now requires 20 Bars of Life, 15 Cores of Calamity, 30 Darksun Fragments, 200 Endothermic Energy, 10 Galactica Singularities, 200 Nightmare Fuel, and 70 Phantoplasm instead of 40 Bars of Life, 30 Cores of Calamity, 60 Darksun Fragments, 400 Endothermic Energy, 20 Galactica Singularities, 400 Nightmare Fuel, and 140 Phantoplasm.
**Auric Tesla Cuisses now require 15 Bars of Life, 10 Cores of Calamity, 20 Darksun Fragments, 150 Endothermic Energy, 7 Galactica Singularities, 150 Nightmare Fuel, and 50 Phantoplasm instead of 30 Bars of Life, 20 Cores of Calamity, 45 Darksun Fragments, 300 Endothermic Energy, 15 Galactica Singularities, 300 Nightmare Fuel, and 105 Phantoplasm.
*[[Hermes Boots]] can now be crafted with 10 [[Silk]].

== Other ==
*The [[Adrenaline Meter]] now resets whenever a boss is killed or spawned.
*[[Brimstone Slag]]'s pickaxe requirement has been lowered from 199 to 189, allowing it to be mined with an [[Adamantite Pickaxe|Adamantite]] or [[Titanium Pickaxe]].
*Lowered the [[Profaned Crag]]'s spawnrate multiplier.
*Revengeance Mode now multiplies all damage taken by {{revengeance|15%}} instead of {{revengeance|25%}}.
*[[Water]] in the [[Snow biome]] now drains the player's breath meter quicker in [[Expert Mode]] instead of inflicting [[Chilled]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed Brimstone Elemental, Cryogen, The Hive Mind, and Signus' contact damage going to Normal Mode values after teleporting in Expert Mode.
*Fixed Ravager's head still drawing on its body after detaching.
*Fixed an error in [[Revengeance]]'s tooltip where "activate" was spelt as "activae".
