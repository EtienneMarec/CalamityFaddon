'''Frostspark Bullets''' were [[Hardmode]] [[bullets]] made from [[Cryonic Bar]]s. They inflicted the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[On Fire!]] (8 seconds, 100% chance)
*[[Frostburn]] (8 seconds, 100% chance)
*[[Glacial State]] (4 seconds, 100% chance)

On enemies, these combined debuffs caused the target to be unable to regenerate life, and lose 12 health per second for 8 seconds. Additionally, for 4 seconds, they would be completely unable to move as well as having their defense cut in half.

If the target was immune to the Glacial State [[debuff]], the bullet inflicted 20% extra damage.
