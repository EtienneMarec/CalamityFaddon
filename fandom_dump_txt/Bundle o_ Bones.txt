'''Bundles o' Bones''' are Pre-[[Hardmode]] consumable [[rogue weapon]]s found inside [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Dungeon]]. They automatically throw clusters of bones that bounce off of three enemies or tiles before falling apart into lingering bones that deal damage. The lingering bones deal 5% of the weapon's damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Bundles o' Bones will cause the next cluster thrown to shoot a bone at the nearest enemy every time it bounces. These bones deal 30% of the main cluster's damage.
