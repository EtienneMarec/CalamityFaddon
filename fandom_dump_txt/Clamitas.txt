'''Clamitas (Japanese: クラミタス <nowiki>''</nowiki>Kuramitasu<nowiki>''</nowiki>)''' is a [[Mini Bosses|mini boss]] that rarely spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]], and is the final boss of Pre-[[Hardmode]]. During Hardmode, it gains a significant buff and starts dropping its own weapon set, as well as the [[Eye of Desolation]] used to summon [[Calamitas]]. It is initially passive and closed up, but once the player hits it 5 times, the player receives the '''Clamity''' debuff, which boosts the spawn rate of [[Clam]]s and making them aggressive while reducing the other enemies' spawn rate to 0, and Clamitas becomes aggressive.

While mostly immobile, it can perform one of 2 moves every 4 seconds. Its most common move is to teleport over the player's head and fall down on them, causing a spread of {{dv|8|10|12|14}} brimstone darts to travel outwards on impact. The other, less common move is to close itself up for a short while, then summon a wall of Brimstone Hellblasts from offscreen that move horizontally towards the player.

In Hardmode, it gains 2 new attacks which have a timer of 3 seconds, unrelated to its primary attacks, on top of making its tele-stomp attack faster. Its first attack is to summon a Brimstone Gigablast from above that explodes into brimstone darts downwards. Its other attack is shooting two Brimstone Fireblasts travelling horizontally away from it in both directions.

It can be detected with the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].
