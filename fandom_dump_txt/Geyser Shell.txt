'''Geyser Shells''' are Pre-hardmode [[bullets]] that have a chance to drop from [[Baby Flak Crab]]s when hit with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]]. They release Baby Flak Crabs which start off slow but quickly accelerate by propelling themselves with steam. They attempt to home towards enemies, but due to their ever increasing speed, they often miss if not aimed carefully.
