The '''1.1.4.43''' update added a variety of [[yoyo]]s, throwing weapons, and summoner weapons. It also resprited all of the mod's bosses.

== Additions ==
=== Accessories ===
*Added [[Daedalus Emblem]], [[Statis' Blessing]], [[Statis' Ninja Belt]].

=== Weapons ===
*Added [[Air Spinner]], [[Azathoth]], [[Chaotrix]], [[Cnidarian]], [[The Eye of Calamitas]], [[The God's Gambit]], [[Quagmire]], [[Shimmerspark]], and [[Verdant]].
*Added [[Blighted Eye Staff]], [[Brimstone Flamesprayer]], [[Corroslime Staff]], [[Crimslime Staff]], [[Deathhail Staff]], [[Eradicator]], [[Flame Scythe]], [[Gelitic Blade]], [[Goobow]], [[Mangrove Chakram]], [[Monsoon]], [[Primordial Earth]], [[Royal Knives]], [[Seabound Staff]], [[Staff of the Mechworm]], [[True Paladin's Hammer]], and [[True Tyrant's Ultisword]].

=== Tools ===
*Added [[Gelpick]].

=== Buffs ===
*Added [[Blighted Eyes (buff)|Blighted Eyes]], [[Brittle Star (buff)|Brittle Star]], [[Corro slime (buff)]], [[Crim slime (buff)]], [[Mechworm (buff)|Mechworm]].
*Added [[Glacial State]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Blight Slime]]s now only spawn after [[Skeletron]] has been defeated.
*Blight Slimes now drop 17 of their respective gels instead of 15.
*[[Calamitas]] changes:
**Nerfed phase 1 health from {{dv|27,000|44,500}} to {{dv|14,000|23,100}}.
**Nerfed phase 2 health from {{dv|25,000|41,250}} to {{dv|15,000|24,750}}.
**Speed increased.
**Now inflicts [[Brimstone Flames]] for only {{expert|3.3}} seconds instead of {{expert|5}} in Expert Mode.
**Can now drop Blighted Eye Staff, Brimstone Flamesprayer, and The Eye of Calamitas.
**[[Catastrophe]] now charges like Expert Mode [[Eye of Cthulhu]].
**Brothers now have increased range for their flamethrowers.
**Brothers' speed increased.
*Cleaned up [[Desert Scourge]] and [[Dried Seeker]] AI.
*Desert Scourge can now drop Seabound Staff.
*Buffed [[The Devourer of Gods]]' head and body damage.
*The Devourer of Gods now has heavy resistance against [[Daedalus Stormbow]].
*The Devourer of Gods now drops Deathhail Staff, Eradicator, and Staff of the Mechworm.
*True Slime God can now drop Corroslime Staff and Crimslime Staff.

=== Items ===
*Each piece of [[Aerospec armor]] has had its defense increased by 1.
*[[Desert Medallion]] recipe now requires higher amounts of materials, along with [[Coral]].
*Nerfed [[Galeforce]]'s damage from 19 to 14, and its use time from 12 to 19.
*All Calamity pickaxes now mine much faster.
*Repurposed [[Reefclaw Axe]] into a Hamaxe, and renamed it to Reefclaw Hamaxe.
*Nerfed [[Seabow]] use time from 14 to 25.
*Nerfed [[Tarragon armor]] set bonus defense from 10 to 7.
*[[Temporal Floe Sword]], [[Terratomere]], and their projectiles now inflict Glacial State.
*Nerfed [[Xeroc Mask]] defense from 28 to 25.
*Nerfed [[Xeroc Plate Mail]] defense from 27 to 23.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Calamitas]], [[Desert Scourge]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], [[Devourer Spawn]], [[Slime God]], True Slime God.
*Resprited [[Aerial Hamaxe]], Galeforce, [[Goldplume Spear]], [[Skyfringe Pickaxe]], [[Tradewinds]], [[Wind Blade]].
*Resprited [[Greatbay Pickaxe]], [[Redtide Sword]], Reefclaw Hamaxe, [[Seabow]], [[Urchin Spear]].
*Resprited [[Titanium Shuriken]].
*Resprited [[Tarragon Wings]].

== Fixes ==
*Slime God and True Slime God now despawn when the player dies.
