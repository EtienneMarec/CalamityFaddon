The '''Arch Amaryllis''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Gleaming Magnolia]]. It fires a red flower that pierces infinitely and slows down horizontally more quickly than it does vertically, causing it to travel in an upwards or downwards curve depending on the direction it's fired. After two seconds, or upon hitting a block, the main projectile splits into six homing petals. The petals last two seconds before fading away, and deal 75% of the main damage.
