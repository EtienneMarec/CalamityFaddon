'''Howl's Heart''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is purchased from the [[Wizard]] NPC for {{gc|12}}. While equipped, three pets assist the player:
*'''Howl''' fires blue flames towards enemies that have a base damage of 42.
*'''Calcifer''' provides light around the player.
*'''Turnip-Head''' follows the player around.

== Trivia ==
*Howl, Calcifer, and Turnip-Head are all references to the characters of the same name from the ''[[wikipedia:Howl's Moving Castle|Howl's Moving Castle]]'' book series.
**The designs of the characters summoned are based on their appearances in the 2004 film [[wikipedia:Howl's Moving Castle (film)|Howl's Moving Castle]].
