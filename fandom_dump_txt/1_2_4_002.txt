The '''1.2.4.002''' update made a lot of changes to vanilla bosses in Calamity's difficulty modes, made some bosses more aggressive, and slightly reworked the [[Cryogen]] and [[Bumblebirb]] fights.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Changes ==

===[[Bosses]] ===

==== [[Astrageldon Slime]] ====
*Nerfed Godseeker Mode [[Revengeance Mode]] health from {{dv|0|0|1,800,000}} to {{dv|0|0|800,000}}.
*Buffed post-Moon Lord Revengeance Mode laser damage from {{dv|0|0|630}} to {{dv|0|0|6300}}.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*Buffed [[Brain of Cthulhu]]'s health modifier from 1.5x to 1.8x.
*Lowered Brain of Cthulhu's knockback resistance.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Increased damage during enrage by 33%.
*Enrage now causes her to cycle through her phases much faster, and shoot barrages in quicker succession.

==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*Nerfed the main Bumblebirb's health from {{dv|350,000|525,000|600,000|750,000}} to {{dv|250,000|375,000|450,000|525,000}}, but buffed its contact damage from {{dv|350|525|551|787}} to {{dv|400|600|630|900}}.
*Buffed clone damage from {{dv|250|375}} to {{dv|300|450}}.
*Now switches between phases more often.
*Now spawns clones much more often.
*Now charges less often.
*Clones can no longer spawn mores clones.
*Greatly increased speed on clones.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Buffed phase 2's health from {{dv|19,000|30,400|34,400|40,000}} to {{dv|27,000|43,200|48,000|56,000}}.
*Increased the brothers' charging speeds.
*Buffed [[Soul Seeker]]'s health from {{dv|2500|4000}} to {{dv|7500|12,000}}.

==== [[Crabulon]] ====
*Nerfed health from {{dv|5000|8000|9600|13,600}} to {{dv|3800|6080|7200|8800}}.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|13,500|21,600|24,000|32,000}} to {{dv|22,500|36,000|40,000|44,800}}.
*Buffed Cryogen's Shield's health from {{dv|500|600}} to {{dv|2000|2000}}.
*Buffed [[Aurora Spirit]]'s health from {{dv|180|252}} to {{dv|360|504}}.
*Buffed the normal variant of the [[Cryocore]]'s health from {{dv|200|280}} to {{dv|400|560}}.
*Buffed the buffed variant of the Cryocore's health from {{dv|250|350}} to {{dv|500|700}}.
*Replaced Cryogen's phase 5 where it spins in place while firing frost bolt barrages, ice shards, and star-shaped frost bolt patterns, while also summoning Cryocores and Aurora Spirits. Now Cryogen teleports near the player from time to time while continuing its other attacks.
*Cryogen's Shield now fires extra ice blasts in addition to Cryogen's.
*Cryocores and Aurora Spirits now spawn in pairs randomly instead of in large groups upon phase transitions.
*Reduced dust idly spawned from both Cryogen's Shield and Cryogen itself.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*Buffed head damage in [[Death Mode]] from {{death|99}} to {{death|121}}.
*Now only takes 65% damage from [[Molotov Cocktail]] flares in Expert Mode.
*Now only takes 20% damage from explosives in Expert Mode.
*Can now periodically fire blasts of sand in Revengeance Mode.
*Small Desert Scourges no longer drop Coins.
*Now becomes invincible while outside of the [[Desert]].

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Nerfed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.6x}} to {{death|1.2x}}, buffed its Death Mode damage modifier from {{death|1.25x}} to {{death|1.5x}}, and nerfed its health modifier in other modes from 1.3x to 1.1x.
*The Destroyer now gains a 1.25x damage modifier in all modes.
*Body now forces all projectiles which pierce more than twice to pierce twice.
*Body in Death Mode now cuts all infinitely penetrating projectile damage by 75%, and any projectiles that pierce more than once have their damage divided by how many times they pierce.
*Probes no longer drop hearts in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Changed phase 1's health from {{dv|600,000|960,000|1,000,000|1,200,000}} to {{dv|600,000|960,000|1,040,000|1,120,000}}.
*Buffed phase 2's head damage from {{dv|600|960}} to {{dv|750|1200}}.
*Buffed phase 2's body damage from {{dv|300|480}} to {{dv|375|600}}.
*Renamed Another worm boss to [[Devourer of Thots]].
*Buffed Devourer of Thots' damage from {{dv|250|400}} to {{dv|400|640}}.
*Now starts shooting bolts at 90% health instead of 80%.
*Now starts shooting star projectiles at 80% health instead of 60%.
*Now starts shooting gravity bombs at 70% health instead of 40%.
*Now starts shooting mines at 35% health instead of 20%.
*Speed increased for both phases.
*Devourer of Thots' speed increased.
*The black screen shader is now applied at all times in Death Mode.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Nerfed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|2.3x}} to {{death|2.2x}}, and buffed its health modifier in other modes from 1.65x to 1.9x.
*Sharkrons now gain a 30x health modifier in all modes.

==== [[Eater of Worlds]] ====
*Head now reflects projectiles in Death Mode.
*No longer resists [[Spear]]s and projectiles which act like them, such as [[Arkhalis]].
*Buffed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.25x}} to {{death|1.35x}}.

==== [[Eye of Cthulhu]] ====
*Buffed health modifier from 1.5x to 1.8x.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Nerfed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|7x}} to {{death|6x}}.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Nerfed phase 2's health from {{dv|4000|6400|8000|11,200}} to {{dv|3000|4800|5760|7200}}.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Buffed contact damage from {{dv|600|960}} to {{dv|650|1040}}.
*Increased [[Yharim's Crystal]]'s drop chance from {{revengeance|0.1%}} to {{revengeance|10%}}.

==== [[King Slime]] ====
*Buffed health modifier from 1.4x to 1.7x.

==== [[THE LORDE]] ====
*Now uses the Moon Lord's theme.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Nerfed Core's Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.6x}} to {{death|1.5x}}, and buffed its health modifier in other modes from 1.3x to 1.4x.
*Nerfed Hand's Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.4x}} to {{death|1.2x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 1.2x to 1.1x.
*Nerfed [[True Eye of Cthulhu]]'s [[Revengeance Mode]] health modifier from 250% to 200%.
*Now opens its forehead eye more often.

==== [[The Perforators]] ====
*Increased the max time it takes for the Perforator Hive to shoot volleys of projectiles.
*Buffed blood geyser damage from {{dv|30|48|50|67}} to {{dv|44|60|63|84}}.
*Worms now strictly spawn at every 30% of the Hive's health lost.
*Worms no longer spawn directly from the Hive, and now spawn offscreen.
*Worms' distance before flying decreased.
*No longer spawns [[Perforator Mini]]s.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Plague nuke barrage can now occur upon spawning in Death Mode.
*Plague nuke barrage now begins up to 5 seconds after its dedicated status message instead of up to 20.
*Up to 4 [[Plague Mine]]s can now spawn in Expert Mode, instead of 3 in Revengeance Mode.
*Lowered the max amount of large Plague Chargers from 4 to 3.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Buffed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.8x}} to {{death|2.6x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 1.4x to 2.3x.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|205,000|0|220,000|265,000}} to {{dv|285,000|0|300,000|324,000}}.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Buffed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.5x}} to {{death|1.9x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 1.25x to 1.7x.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Nerfed health in Death Mode from {{death|512,000}} to {{death|480,000}}.
*Nerfed [[Cosmic Lantern]]'s health from {{dv|2000|2800|2940|4200}} to {{dv|150|210|220|315}}.
*Cosmic Lanterns now fade in and are invincible for a few seconds when spawning.
*No longer spawns Cosmic Lanterns randomly when hurt, and now spawns them in columns of 5 on either side of the player during its charging phase.
*Increased Cosmic Lantern limit from 5 to 10.
*Dust explosions now last shorter.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*Buffed the Leviathan's health from {{dv|52,000|83,200|88,000|104,000}} to {{dv|79,000|126,400|140,800|152,000}}.
*The Siren now cycles through phases quicker.
*Reduced dust on the Siren when hit.
*[[Siren Clone]]s now have a 33% chance to fire frost mist as opposed to just water spears.
*Frost mist now emits particles twice as often.

==== [[Skeletron]] ====
*Nerfed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|1.6x}} to {{death|1.2x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 1.3x to 1.1x.
*Buffed Death Mode Skeletron's Hand's health modifier from {{death|1.4x}} to {{death|2.5x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 1.2x to 2.2x.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Nerfed health modifier from 1.15x to 1.1x.
*Nerfed Prime Laser's health modifier from 1.3x to 1.2x.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Nerfed Core health from {{dv|1000|1600|2560|3520}} to {{dv|800|1280|1600|2240}}.
*Core now inflicts [[Marked for Death]] for 5 seconds instead of 3.
*Core is now more aggressive.
*Ebonian Slime God now fires abyss mines 2x more often.
*Split Ebonian Slime God now fires mines 4x more often.
*Crimulan Slime God now fires mines 3x more often.
*Increased flying [[Corrupt Slime Spawn]]'s velocity slightly.
*Buffed flying Corrupt Slime Spawn's health from {{dv|50|70}} to {{dv|300|420}}.
*Buffed wingless Corrupt Slime Spawn's health from {{dv|70|98}} to {{dv|120|168}}.
*Buffed [[Crimson Slime Spawn]]'s health from {{dv|70|98}} to {{dv|200|280}}.
*Buffed [[Spiked Crimson Slime Spawn]]'s health from {{dv|140|196}} to {{dv|250|350}}.
	
==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Now summons lightning orbs.
*Nerfed armored [[Stasis Probe]]'s health from {{dv|4000|5600}} to {{dv|2000|2800}}.
*Nerfed naked Stasis Probe's health from {{dv|5000|7000}} to {{dv|2500|3500}}.
*Reduced Stasis Probe cap from 10 to 3.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Increased [[Vulnerability Hex]] duration on contact from 5 seconds to 10.
*No longer inflicts [[NO U]] in Death Mode.
*Projectiles now inflict Vulnerability Hex.
*Completely rewrote dialogue:

*Reduced laser frequency in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*Nerfed Death Mode health modifier from {{death|2.5x}} to {{death|2.2x}}.
*Nerfed Death Mode The Hungry II's health modifier from {{death|3.5x}} to {{death|3.1x}}.
*Buffed Death Mode [[Leech]]'s health modifier from {{death|2.5x}} to {{death|3.5x}}, and its health modifier in other modes from 2.1x to 3.1x.

=== Items ===
*[[Apotheosis]] now includes 2 [[Excelsus]]es and 77 [[Phantoplasm]] in its recipe.
*Apotheosis now inflicts [[Armor Crunch]] for 10 seconds.
*[[Cosmic Immaterializer]] now requires 12 minion slots to use instead of 8.
*[[Crystal Bullet]]s now only do 12.5% damage in Revengeance Mode instead of 16.67%.
*[[Floodtide]] can now be obtained 3x more commonly from fishing.
*Nerfed [[Helstorm]]'s damage from 27 to 24.
*[[Icebreaker]] can now only last for 2 seconds instead of 5.
*Buffed [[Megalodon]]'s damage from 29 to 32.
*Nerfed [[Superball Bullet]]'s damage from 10 to 6.
*Superball Bullets now pierce 5 times instead of 7.
*[[Terra Arrow]]s and [[Terra Bullet]]s now emit less dust.
*Removed the line, "It's basically cheating top kek lel" from [[Yharim's Crystal]].
*Yharim's Crystal's name whitelist changes:
**Grox, Vlad, Spoopyro, and Echo are no longer whitelisted.
**Grox the Great, MineCat, gamagamer64, and Leviathan are now whitelisted.
*Yharim's Crystal can no longer drop from any enemy.
*Nerfed [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]]'s damage from 300 to 250.

=== Other ===
*Put a max damage boost cap of 75% (100% when wearing Demonshade armor) on players in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*Put a max critical strike chance boost of 75% on players in Revengeance and Death Mode.

*Resprited [[Arch Amaryllis]], [[Charred Idol]], [[Cursed Capper]], [[Earth]], [[Sigil of Calamitas]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a multiplayer desync bug with Astrum Deus.
*Fixed a sheeting error on [[Brimstone Elemental]].
*Fixed a sheeting error on [[Xeroc Wings]].
*Fixed a bug with Calamity's dashing coverings which prevented the player from dashing again if they held the movement key down after dashing.
*Fixed a bug where the [[damage reduction]] cap wasn't taking certain [[coverings]] and [[Buffs]] into account.
*Fixed a bug where Perforator worms would sometimes attach heads to each other.
