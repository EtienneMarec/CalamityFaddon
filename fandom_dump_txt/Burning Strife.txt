The '''Burning Strife''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[Spy]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]] that is dropped by [[Goblin Summoner]]s. It throws a gravity-affected shadowflame spiky ball that bounces off of solid blocks. Upon contact with an enemy, the spiky ball will explode and inflict the [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]] to all nearby enemies, as well as dealing 33% damage to them.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Burning Strife causes the next spiky ball thrown to deal 130% and pierce through four enemies before being destroyed on contact with a fifth. Whenever the spiky ball strikes an enemy, it releases an explosion that is larger than the non-stealth version and deals 42.9% damage.
