'''Water Fountains''' are [[furniture]] items that change the color of all nearby water. They can be activated via right-click or wiring. The Calamity Mod currently adds four different fountains to the game, all of which are purchased from the [[Witch Doctor]] for {{gc|4}}. Abyss and Astral Water Fountains are only available in [[Hardmode]].

== Notes ==
*Unlike vanilla Water Fountains, these are automatically activated when placed.
*The effects of the Fountains are purely cosmetic. They have no effect on [[Fishing]], etc.
*Water Fountains will ''not'' color [[Waterfall Block]]s, or their respective wall.
*Water Fountains will change the color of water when the player is within about 100 blocks of the fountain.
