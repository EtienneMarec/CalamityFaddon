The '''Slurper Pole''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[fishing pole]] dropped by [[Soul Slurper]]s. Unlike most fishing rods, the Slurper Pole can be used to fish in [[Lava]].

== Notes ==
*It can be considered an earlier and weaker version of the [[Hotline Fishing Hook]] as it has much weaker stats than the Hotline Fishing Hook, which has 45% [[fishing power]]. However, it is much easier to obtain than said pole and may prove useful until another hook that can fish in lava is acquired.

== Trivia ==
*The tooltip is a quote by [[wikipedia:Charles Spurgeon|Charles Spurgeon]].
