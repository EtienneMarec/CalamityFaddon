The '''1.3.3.001''' update focused on making the game a less linear experience, with various recipe tweaks to move [[summoning items]] lower in progression, along with removing various anti-cheat features from boss fights. The update also reworked [[Polterghast]], split most Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] sets into several classes with multiple helmets, and resprited [[The Hive Mind]], [[The Perforators]], and various weapon projectile sprites.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Dark Sun Ring]].
*Added [[Auric Tesla Hooded Facemask]], [[Auric Tesla Plumed Helm]], [[Auric Tesla Wire-Hemmed Visage]], [[Bloodflare Helm]], [[Bloodflare Helmet]], [[Bloodflare Horned Helm]], [[Bloodflare Horned Mask]], [[God Slayer Helmet]], [[God Slayer Horned Helm]], [[God Slayer Mask]], [[God Slayer Visage]], [[Silva Horned Helm]], [[Silva Mask]], [[Silva Masked Cap]], [[Tarragon Helmet]], [[Tarragon Horned Helm]], and [[Tarragon Visage]].
*Added [[Armageddon]].
*Added [[Blood Relic]], [[Draedon's Remote]], and [[Eidolon Tablet]].
*Added [[Cosmic Mine]].
*Added [[Silva Stun]].
