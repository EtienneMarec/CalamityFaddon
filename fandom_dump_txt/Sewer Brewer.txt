The '''Sewer Brewer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Melee weapons|melee weapon]] that autoswings. It is dropped from the [[The Plaguebringer Goliath|Plaguebringer Goliath]].

When the weapon is used, the player will hold the weapon out in front of them, charging it up slowly. Releasing the attack button launches a drill bit with the size, speed, and damage varying based on the charge of the weapon, with a fully charged up drill bit dealing 600% of the weapon's base damage. Drill bits slow down while hitting enemies, getting more hits on them before despawning. Fully charged drill bits home in towards enemies.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Godly'''. It cannot get modifiers that affect size.
