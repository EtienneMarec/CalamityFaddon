The '''Stained Mountains''' are an Epilogue [[Biomes|biome]] that spawn after the defeat of [[Hydrabots Invasion]] if it was defeated in [[Revengeance Mode]]. It consists of very high peaks made of [[Titanic Shale]], [[Titanic Snow]], and [[Thorium Ore]]. The [[Stained Mountains Arsenal Lab]] generates there.

During the day more snow generates blocking both the ways to the Arsenal Lab, it dissapears in the night letting the player enter. Wings also dont work during Nighttime.
