'''Alluring Bait''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is caught while [[fishing]] in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] or [[Abyss]]. It increases the player's fishing power by 30 during day, 45 during night, and 60 during a [[Solar Eclipse]]. It also increases the chance to catch fish that are materials in [[Potion]]s.

Alluring Bait increases the chance to catch potion fish through the formula (Fishing Power/10)%, capping the chance at 33.33%. In order to reach this cap, the player must have 333 fishing power or greater.
