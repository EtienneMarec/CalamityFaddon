The '''Phantasmal Ruin''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode non-consumable [[javelin]]. It is the upgrade of the [[Luminous Striker]] and the final upgrade to the [[Spear of Paleolith]]. It automatically throws a gravity-ignoring spear that leave trails of floating lost souls. The lost souls float around and home onto nearby enemies, dealing 25% of the weapon's base damage. Upon contact with an enemy, the spear breaks into four phantasmal spirits outward from the spear which start homing in after a short time and deal 10% of the weapon's damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Phantasmal Ruin will cause the next spear thrown to release a trail of translucent spear clones instead of lost souls and at a faster pace. The spear clones home onto enemies and fade away over time.
