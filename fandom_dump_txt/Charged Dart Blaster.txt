The '''Charged Dart Blaster''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Dart guns|dart gun]] and an upgrade to the [[Dart Rifle]]/[[Dart Pistol]]. It fires an energy dart that travels very fast and splits into other darts when hitting an [[enemy]] or a block alongside a spread of 2 to 4 darts. Right clicking will fire a bouncy explosive dart blast with double damage.
