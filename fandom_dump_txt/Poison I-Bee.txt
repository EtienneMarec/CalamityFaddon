The '''Poison I-Bee''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[boomerang]] dropped by the [[The Plaguebringer Goliath|Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Its primary fire rapidly throws disks that deal rogue damage and release thorns when striking enemies. The thorns deal 35% of the disk's damage, but ignore enemy [[immunity frames]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] causes the resulting disk to fire seeds in all directions inaccurately.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Flawless'''.

== Trivia ==
The Poison I-Bee is a reference to [[wikipedia:Poison_ivy|poison ivy]], a type of plant that is infamous for causing a rash due to urushiol in the plant's sap.
