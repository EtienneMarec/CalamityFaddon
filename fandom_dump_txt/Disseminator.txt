The '''Disseminator''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] and a direct upgrade to the [[Conference Call]]. It fires three shotgun blasts; one is fired from the gun, the second is fired at the cursor from above, and the third is fired at the cursor from below. Unlike the Conclave Crossfire, the extra bullets can pass through tiles.
