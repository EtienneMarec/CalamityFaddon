The '''Orthocera''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy|aquatic enemy]] that spawns during tiers 2 and 3 of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. It floats through water, charging at the player when possible, flops on the surface and fires streams of acid.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Orthoceras|Orthoceras]] is an extinct genus of [[wikipedia:nautiloid|nautiloid cephalopods]] that lived during the Middle [[wikipedia:Ordovician|Ordovician]] period.
**The Orthocera's magic vulnerability is a reference to how real Orthocerases went extinct due to cosmological events. 
*Their holy weakness and unholy resistance is a reference to how real Orthocerases are that of the devil, and how them and the dinosaurs where snuffed by god.
