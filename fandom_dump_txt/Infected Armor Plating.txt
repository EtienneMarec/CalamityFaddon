'''Infected Armor Plating''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] and the [[Plague Emperor]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. Similarly to [[Plague Cell Canister]]s, they are used to craft Plague-themed items such as [[Plague Keeper]], [[Tactical Plague Engine]], and [[Plaguebringer armor]]. However, it is also used for other items, such as the [[True Forbidden Oathblade]] and the third tier of weapons from Draedon's Arsenal.
