'''Shadow's Descent''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Spy]] [[Spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws up to 5 gravity-defying shadow balls that move slowly and decelerate to a stop after travelling a short distance. Once the ball comes to a stop, it will emit homing shadow spirits and create an aura that deals increasing damage to enemies lingering inside it. The balls will despawn after landing 50 enemy hits or after a long amount of time. Right-clicking with the weapon will delete all existing shadow balls. Shadow balls go through tiles and inflict the [[Shadowflame|Shadowflame debuff]] for 10 seconds.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Shadow's Descent causes the next shadow ball to also emit dark pulses when it comes to a stop. The Shadow's Descent stealth strike pulse inflicts several debuffs:
*[[Demon Flames]] (5 seconds)
*[[God Slayer Inferno]] (10 seconds)
*[[Nightwither]] (20 seconds)
*[[Shadowflame]] (30 seconds)
