<big> ''"What? You thought it's a joke that it's utterly useless?..."'' </big>


The '''Compressed Void Chunk''' is an item with a sole purpose: it's the one of the two requirements for the [[Void Man]] to spawn.

==Trivia==
*Despite its tooltip, it's not utterly useless, as it has at least one purpose.
*Compressed Void Chunk spawns the exact same way as the [[tgc:Gems|gemstones]] do: spawns at any side of an any tile upon the worldgen stage, the message when a Compressed Void Chunk generates will show up: "Bringing something utterly useless to this world."
*Upon the first interaction with the [[Void Man]] and if the player has this item in their inventory, it will disappear from their inventory.
