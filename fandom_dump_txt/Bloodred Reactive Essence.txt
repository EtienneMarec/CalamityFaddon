'''Bloodred Reactive Essence''' is a Post-[[The Entropic God, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Crafting materials|crafting material]]. It is dropped from every enemy in the [[Desert]], [[Crimson]], [[Brimstone Crag]] and [[Pot of Pain]] with a 10% chance. It is used to craft various Post-Umbrus blood-themed melee equipment.
