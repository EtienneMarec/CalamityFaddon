'''Evil Island''' is a unique sky island that contain content of the [[Evil Biome]] that was not chosen.

== Crimson Evil Island ==
[[File:Crimson Island.png|300px|thumb|A Crimson Island.]]

If the player chose [[The Corruption]], the island will generate in the ''right'' half of the world and be made of and contain [[Crimson]]-themed content. The usual sky house is located on top of the island, and the house has a locked [[Crimson Chest]] which contains the [[Vampire Knives]].

== Corruption Evil Island ==
[[File:Corruption Island.png|300px|thumb|A Corruption Island.]]

If the player chose [[The Crimson]], the island will generate in the ''left'' half of the world and be made of and contain [[Corruption]]-themed content. The usual sky house is located on top of the island, and the house has a locked [[Corruption Chest]] which contains the [[Scourge of the Corruptor]].

== Notes ==
*The islands are made of their respective evil's [[tgc:Sandstone Block|sandstone]] variant, meaning that evil mushrooms (for [[tgc:Vile Powder|Vile Powder]]/[[tgc:Vicious Powder|Vicious Powder]]) do not grow unless grass is manually placed on the island.
*[[Space]] enemies will spawn instead of the evil's enemies if the island generates high enough.
*Meteors may land on the edges of these islands. They will not land in the middle, however, due to the presence of the chest.
*Because the ore is above ground, it can be mined with any pickaxe at any point in time.
*It will not spawn if the player chose '''random evil biome''', only if they actually selected one of the 2 options.
