The '''Brimstone Witch''' is a Godseeker Mode [[NPC]] vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty house.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] has been defeated in the world (that is intended to house her) for the first time.

Compared to her boss form, Supreme Calamitas is replaced with a deer due to the effects of her own final attack.

When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself with brimstone hellblasts.
*The Brimstone Witch will always be named Calamitas.

== Enchant ==
{{dablink|For a full explanation on this mechanic, see [[Enchantment]].}}
For an expensive price, the Brimstone Witch is capable of [[enchant]]ing the player's items, granting them new power with some drawback. She is also capable of "exhuming" certain items, transforming them into entirely new items.

== Curses {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} ==
With the [[community modifications|Calamity: Community Remix]] addon enabled, the Brimstone Witch gains a secondary chat option for enabling ``Curses`` for the [[Boss Rush]] event. Curses yield varying amounts of [[Klepticoin]]s {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} when beaten and grant permanent buffs to the player.
