'''The Dreaming Ghost''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Summon weapon|summon weapon]]. Upon use with four or more minion slots, The Dreaming Ghost summons a purple and pink butterfly. The purple butterfly will visciously ram into enemies while spawning crystal shards on enemy hits, while the pink butterfly will remain stationary and fire a swarm of sakura bullets. Each purple butterfly active increases the player's damage by 10%, while each pink butterfly increases the player's life regeneration by 2.
