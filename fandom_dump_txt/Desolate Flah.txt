'''Desolate Flah''' is a Godseeker Mode [[item]]. It is only obtained through throwing [[Merge Flah]], [[Energy Core]]s, [[Yharon Soul Fragment]]s, [[Alloy Bar]]s, and [[Meld Bar]]s together into [[Lava]]. It is further processed into [[Reclaimed Flah]] by decoding it in a finished [[Codebreaker]] with 5000 [[Draedon Power Cell]]s.

== Notes ==
*Trying to throw [[Flah]], [[Washed Flah]], [[Blessed Flah]], or [[Cosmic Flah]] instead of Merge Flah into lava will destroy all the items you threw in instead.
