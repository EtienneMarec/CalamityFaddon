'''Esseric Cake''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ore]] that generates in the [[Sky Abyss]] biome after defeating [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] if he was defeated on April First, replacing 5% of the Generating [[Esseric Ore]]

Once a Player picks up Esseric Cake, every item in their inventory's Display name, tooltip and asset change to the ones of Esseric Cake and the player gets inflicted with [[Evercold]] until they dispose of the real Esseric Cake from their inventory

Esseric Cake is the only item in the mod to have a negative sell price, having the player lose money when trying to sell it.

=== Trivia ===
* The Item's tooltip is a reference to a popular trend on video sharing platforms where people create cakes that resemble everyday objects
