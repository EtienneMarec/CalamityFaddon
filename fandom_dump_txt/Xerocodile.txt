The '''Xerocodile''' is a [[grab bag]]-type [[Fishing|fish]] that can be caught on the [[Surface]] during a [[Blood Moon]] with a 6.67% chance. When opened, the player will receive between 5 to 15 [[Blood Orb]]s.

*The Xerocodile's name is a portmanteau of the words "crocodile" and "Xeroc", a figure in the mod's [[lore]], and also a planned [[Superboss]].
