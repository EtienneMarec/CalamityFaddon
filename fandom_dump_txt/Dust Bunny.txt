The '''Dust Bunny''' is a [[permanent power-up]] item obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped from [[Deerclops]]. It increases the player's [[Stamina]] by 100. There are 2 other items that function identically to this: the [[Magma Booster]] and the [[Ever Ink]]. It can only be used once.

== Trivia ==
*The Dust Bunny is based on the dust bunnies found within chapter 3 of the game, ''[[wikipedia:Celeste|Celeste]]''.
