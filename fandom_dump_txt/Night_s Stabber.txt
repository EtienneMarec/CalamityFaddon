The '''Night's Stabber''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]]. Upon hitting an enemy with it, the enemy will release a homing projectile.

While it has an incredibly short range like most [[shortsword]]s, it also has auto-swing, rendering it slightly more effective.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemy hits heal the player by 3 health, and have nightshade vines spawned on them that sprawl outwards for 5 tiles. Additionally, enemies are inflicted with [[Cursed Inferno]], and the player's melee speed is increased by 5%.
