The '''Wulfrum Pylon''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime. It is also summoned by the [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Wulfrum_Excavator Wulfrum Excavator] [[boss]] in [[Expert Mode]] upon it transitioning to a new phase. It is completely stationary, and emits a field around it that will supercharge nearby [[Wulfrum Enemies|Wulfrum Robots]] (this includes the [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Wulfrum_Excavator Wulfrum Excavator] [[boss]]) and increase their spawn rates by 5.5x. If a player enters this field, it spawns {{dv|1|||2}} wulfrum enemies. It spawns more frequently at the outer two-thirds of the world. Hitting a Wulfrum Pylon with any [[pickaxe]] will cause the Wulfrum Excavator to spawn (provided the player has an [[Ogscule]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} in their inventory).
