The '''Abysscent Pistol''' is a Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that is an upgrade to the [[Aquarel Pistol]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[Ammunition|ammo]], it fires a reaper shark that slightly homes and explodes into 4 homing teeth with a slight spread after travelling about 12 tiles. Teeth deal 25% of the weapon's damage each. Both the reaper sharks and teeth do not pierce through enemies nor go through walls, but inflict the [[Crush Depth]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 2 seconds.
