The '''Ace's High''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. It automatically fires a line of 3 cards with a mild delay between each set, randomly choosing one of four types, each with varying effects. All cards inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] upon contact with enemies.
