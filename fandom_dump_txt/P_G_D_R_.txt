The '''P.G.D.R.''' (or '''Portable Gamma Disintegration Ray''') is a Godseeker Mode [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XM-05 Thanatos]] has been defeated last. It charges a deathray that deals more damage the more it is charged, for a maximum of 3000% damage after 3 seconds. After it reaches the maximum amount of charge, it will fire.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifer]] is '''Unreal'''.
