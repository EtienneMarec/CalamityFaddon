The '''Star Flare''' is an Epilogue [[yoyo]] that drops from the [[Starborn Princess]] [[Town NPC]] after [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] has been defeated. While in use, the yoyo will constantly release fast-moving homing celestial heads which move through tiles, deal 200000% of the yoyo's damage, and will always critically hit. The heads will also move through tiles and inflict the [[Holy Flames]] and [[God Slayer Inferno]] debuffs.
