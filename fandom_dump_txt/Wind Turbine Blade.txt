The '''Wind Turbine Blade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]]. When used, the weapon is thrusted towards the player's cursor. After using the weapon 3 times, it will go on a 1 second cooldown before being usable again. If the player right clicks during this cooldown period, the side of the weapon grows by 200% and launches a homing tornado that ignores immunity frames.
