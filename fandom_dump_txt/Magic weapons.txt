'''Magic weapons''' are [[weapons]] that consume [[mana]]. They mostly come in the form of [[Wands|staves]], [[spell tomes]] and [[magic guns]]. Magic weapons have a wide variety of attacks that are usually not seen in other types of weapons. (i.e. firing lasers, raining down stars, creating explosions, etc.)
