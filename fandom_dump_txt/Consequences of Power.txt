'''Consequences of Power''' is a craftable Epilogue  [[summon weapon]]. It is the final upgrade to the [[Black Hawk Remote]]. It summons an advanced jet that swaps between launching homing missiles in high quantities, launching a fast piercing railgun in bursts, and dashing at the nearest enemy while deploying mines with a damaging aura.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is part of a crossover between the Calamity Mod and the game [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Wingman Project Wingman].
**The frequent references in Project Wingman's lore to the "Calamity" is in fact the same one as the Calamity Mod's lore, confirming they take place in the same universe.
*This weapon is a reference to Mission 15 in Project Wingman "[https://projectwingman.fandom.com/wiki/Consequence_of_Power Consequences of Power]" and the PW-MK.1 from Mission 21 "[https://projectwingman.fandom.com/wiki/Kings Kings]"
*This weapon's tooltip formerly read "Calamity is away" it was changed for unknown reasons.
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity appears as a crimson red({{color|#7D0A0a●|#7D0A0A}}) color.
