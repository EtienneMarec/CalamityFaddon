The '''Rods from God''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Dart guns|dart gun]] and an upgrade to the [[Supercharged Dart Blaster]] and the final upgrade of the [[Dart Rifle]] and [[Dart Pistol]]. It rains down barrages of sun darts onto the user's cursor that pierce through 3 enemies. Right clicking creates a sun at the tip of the gun which slowly expands over time. After 10 seconds have passed, the sun can be launched, creating a screen-sized explosion that inflicts [[On Fire!]].

== Trivia ==
*The item is a reference to Sun God from the Bloons franchise.
*The right click is a reference to the Z-Move "Light that Burns the Skies" used by Ultra Necrozma from [[wikipedia:Pokemon|Pokemon]].
*The name "Rods from God" refers to a codename for dropping tungsten rods from the atmosphere to deal massive damage using the concept of [[wikipedia:Kinetic bombardment|kinetic bombardment]].
