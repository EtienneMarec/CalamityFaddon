The '''Bloodstained Glove''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]] that can also be dropped by [[The Perforators]]. When equipped, the player's [[stealth strike]]s gain +10 armor penetration and [[lifesteal|heal]] the player for 1 HP.

== Notes ==
*This covering is the counterpart to the [[Filthy Glove]] dropped by [[The Hive Mind]].
*This covering can synergize quite well with [[Sticky Spiky Ball]]s, due to the fact that their stealth strike effect throws 7 balls that each can hit up to 5 times. If all of the balls hit enemies, they can heal the player for a total of 35 health, as every hit will heal 1 HP. Furthermore, the extra armor piercing can potentially cause them to deal up to 175 extra damage compared to a stealth strike without the covering, if the enemy's defense is over 10 but not so high that the damage is reduced to 1 per hit even with the extra pierce.
