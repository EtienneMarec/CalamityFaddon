The '''Elemental Mix''' is not a single [[debuff]], but rather a set of debuffs that are always inflicted by several [[weapons]] within the Calamity Mod.

It consists of:
*{{item|Brimstone Flames}}
*{{item|Frostbite}}
*{{item|Plague}}
*{{item|Hellfire}}

The combined effects of the debuffs cause the target to lose 103 health per second and lose 4 defense.
