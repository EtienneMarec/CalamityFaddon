'''Waterfrost Darts''' are craftable Epilogue [[darts]]. When fired, the dart will quickly decelerate and stop entirely, traveling about 16 blocks total. Afterwards, the dart will "leap" every 1.5 seconds, travelling about 12 blocks and leaving behind an icicle each time. Upon hitting an enemy, the dart will cause a small ice explosion, dealing additional 30% of its base damage to the enemy and inflicting the [[Glacial State]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 2 seconds. Icicles will also be quickly fired at the hit enemy, dealing 10% of the dart's base damage. After doing 5 leaps, the dart will explode as if it hit an enemy, but icicles will be fired at the closest enemy. Both the dart and icicles inflict the [[Reactive Flames]] debuff for 4 seconds and 2 seconds respectively. Additionally, both of them can predict the enemy's movement, but do not home, and while the dart can pierce through walls, the icicles cannot.
