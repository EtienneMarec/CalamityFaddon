'''Fluorescent Vodka''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Fluorescent''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which gives a +8% boost to all damage but lowers [[defense]] by 20 in exchange. While affected by the buff, the player receives 10 extra [[immunity frames]] when struck for more than one damage and 5 when struck for one damage. This effect is not boosted by the [[Cross Necklace]] or its upgrades but will stack with the extra immunity frames of the [[Deific Amulet]] or [[Rampart of Deities]].

While the effects of the Fluorescent debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.

{{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} killing [[The General]] {{fantag|Apotheosis and Friends}} while under the effects of Fluorescent Vodka initiates the [[22 Bullet Theory]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} [[event]].
