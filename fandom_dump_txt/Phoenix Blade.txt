The '''Phoenix Blade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. Like the [[Breaker Blade]], it has a very large range, but it also causes explosions and spawns healing orbs when killing [[enemies]]. The healing orbs [[Lifesteal|restore]] 10 health when touched, and disappear after about 3 seconds or if the orb hits a solid tile. The sword itself is extremely large, larger than the Breaker Blade and many other swords in the game.
