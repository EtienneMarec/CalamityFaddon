'''Vodka''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Vodka''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but boosts damage and critical strike chance.

While the effects of the Vodka debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
