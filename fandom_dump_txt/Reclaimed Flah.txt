'''Reclaimed Flah''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]. It is only obtained through decoding [[Desolate Flah]] in a complete [[Codebreaker]] with an [[Auric Quantum Cooling Cell]], This requires 5000 [[Draedon Power Cell]]s which are consumed, giving 3 Reclaimed Flah when complete. It is used to further craft into [[Flahium Slag]].
