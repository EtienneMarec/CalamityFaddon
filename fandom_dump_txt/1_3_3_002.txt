The '''1.3.3.002''' update added the [[Defiled Rune]], new summoner helmets for [[Silva armor|Silva]] and [[Auric Tesla armor]], and a few new [[enemies]] in the [[Abyss]]. It also gave [[Bumblebirb]] a new attack and made some non-linearity changes.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Auric Tesla Space Helmet]] and [[Silva Helmet]].
*Added [[Defiled Rune]], [[Old Power Cell]], and [[Starter Bag]].
*Added [[Fish Alert]] and [[Silva Crystal (buff)]].
*Added [[Luminous Corvina]], [[Mirage Jelly]], and [[Toxic Minnow]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*Now fires lightning feather projectiles occasionally.
*Nerfed Bumblebirb clone health from {{dv|70,000|140,000}} to {{dv|50,000|100,000}}.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Decreased the time it takes for it to switch phases during its final phase in Death Mode.
*Now shoots flames more often when under 10% health in Revengeance Mode.
*No longer takes reduced damage if any of the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]] are currently active.
*Beam portal lasers now last 16 seconds instead of 8.
*Beam portal lasers are now slightly slower.
*Sentinel phase now uses [[Music#Scourge of the Universe|Scourge of the Universe]] instead of [[Music#Sentinels of the Scourge|Sentinels of the Scourge]]. 
*[[Extreme Gravity]] now cuts the player's flight time by 66% instead of 75%.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Slightly increased the vertical speed of the second phase.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Second phase can now be fought if a buffed [[Mothron]] has been defeated instead of needing specific equipment.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Tweaked holy blasts' dust. 

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*Can now drop their normal loot if [[Calamitas]] has been defeated.

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Second phase is now invincible for 3 seconds after spawning.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|2,500,000|4,000,000|5,000,000|10,000,000}} to {{dv|5,500,000|8,800,000|10,000,000|20,000,000}}.
*Buffed Cataclysm and Catastrophe's healths from {{dv|300,000|600,000|700,000|1,000,000}} to {{dv|600,000|1,200,000|1,400,000|2,000,000}}.
*Buffed [[Brimstone Heart]] health from {{dv|50,000|100,000|120,000|200,000}} to {{dv|100,000|200,000|240,000|400,000}}.
*Now changes color when she's about to charge.

=== Enemies ===
*[[Corrupt Slime]]s, [[Crimslime]]s and [[Mother Slime]]s now drop [[Murky Sludge]] at a {{dv|25%|33%}} chance instead of {{dv|20%|25%}}.
*[[Dark Energy|Dark Energies]] are now invincible for 3 seconds after spawning. 
*[[Sea Snail]]s now drop [[Sea Shell]]s at a {{dv|33%|50%}} chance instead of {{dv|25%|33%}}.

=== Items ===
*[[Ancient Medallion]] no longer requires 2 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]].
*[[Armageddon]]'s tooltip now mentions that bosses instantly despawn if a player dies. 
*Melee [[Auric Tesla armor]]'s set bonus now multiplies the player's melee damage the higher their health is. 
*Throwing Auric Tesla armor's set bonus now makes throwing critical hits deal 3.5x damage when the player is above 90% health.
*[[Auric Tesla Hooded Facemask]] now gives the player a 30% ranged damage and critical strike chance boost instead of a 20% boost. 
*Ranged [[Bloodflare armor]]'s souls now deal 800 damage instead of 400.
*The shards from [[Crystal Bullet]]s no longer deal 25% damage in Revengeance Mode.
*[[Depths Charm]]'s protection against [[Crush Depth]]'s damage has been increased by 25%.
*Magic [[God Slayer armor]]'s set bonus' souls now deal 1200 damage instead of 900.
*The stars spawned by [[Holy Arrow]]s no longer deal 50% damage in Revengeance Mode.
*Nerfed Throwing [[Empyrean Knives]]' damage from 800 to 600.
*Buffed [[Heavenly Gale]]'s damage from 210 to 550.
*Heavenly Gale now fires 5 arrows instead of 10.
*Renamed lol to [[lul]].
*[[Silva Invulnerability]] now lasts for 10 seconds instead of 15.
*Ranged [[Tarragon armor]]'s set bonus' projectiles now deal 33% damage instead of 20%.
*[[Temple Key]]s can now be crafted with 15 [[Jungle Spores]], 15 [[Rich Mahogany]], 15 [[Soul of Light|Souls of Light]], and 15 [[Soul of Night|Souls of Night]] at a Hardmode Anvil. 
*Buffed [[Vivid Clarity]]'s damage from 50 to 150, but nerfed its use time from 22 to 33.

=== Other ===
*The player now respawns quicker if Armageddon is on.
*New players now start with a [[Starter Bag]].

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited lul, [[Mourningstar]], and [[Shadowspec Bar]].
*Resprited Mourningstar's projectile.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Armageddon]] and [[Astral Chunk]] having their sprites switched. 
*Fixed magic Auric Tesla armor's set bonus saying it inherits throwing bonuses from its ingredients instead of magic bonuses.
*Fixed [[Auric Tesla Plumed Helm]] and [[Auric Tesla Wire-Hemmed Visage]] having swapped recipes.
*Fixed The Devourer of Gods' final phase not spawning correctly.
*Fixed "liquidation" being spelt as "luiquidation" in [[Ultra Liquidator]]'s tooltip.
*Fixed [[Profaned Energy]]'s lanterns drawing incorrectly.
*Fixed [[Waifu in a Bottle]]'s Sand Elemental animating incorrectly.
