The '''Destinary''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[axes|chainsaw]]. It rapidly fires out exo disks which circle back after passing through the cursor or past a set distance. The disks completely ignore i-frames and deal 10% of the weapon's damage, while the chainsaw itself deals full contact damage. Additionally, disk projectiles will instantly remove trees.
