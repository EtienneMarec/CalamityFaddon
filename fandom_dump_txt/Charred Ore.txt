'''Charred Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ore]] that generates in the [[Brimstone Crag]] upon world creation. It is used to craft [[Unholy Core]]s at a [[Hellforge]]. It requires at least a [[Mythril Pickaxe|Mythril]] or [[Orichalcum Pickaxe]] to mine. It can also be obtained from [[Charred Slime]]s.
