The '''1.4.4.003''' (or '''1.4.4.3''') Music mod update fixed a few oversights and bugs.

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod version [[1.4.4.003]].

== Changes ==
*Renamed Music Box (Bumblebirb) to [[Music Box (The Dragonfolly)]].
*Renamed the <code>Title Screen - Toggling this requires a reload to take effect</code> [[Configuration option]] to <code>TitleScreenOverride</code>.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed an issue that would cause dedicated multiplayer servers to crash when a Music mod track would play.

== Trivia ==
*This update was incorrectly labled as '''1.4.5.002''' internally.
