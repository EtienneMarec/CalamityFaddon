<big> ''"The Gogscules are in full bloom!"'' </big>

The Go'''gswarm''' is an [[Challenges|event]] added by the Calamity Mod where [[Ogsculian Burrower|Ogspawn]] can spawn in massive numbers. An Gogswarm has a random chance of occurring after the [[tgc:Blood Moon|Blood Moon]] has happened at least once. It can also be [[Summoning items|triggered]] manually using an [[Ogbait]].

The event has multiple levels of difficulty that are encountered at different stages of progression similar to the [[tgc:Old One's Army|Old One's Army]] and the [[Acid Rain]], with each tier unlocking after the player has defeated a specific [[Challenges|boss]], or survived through a specific [[Challenges|event]]. The first tier of the Gogswarm will unlocked after the [[tgc:Blood Moon|Blood Moon]], the next tier after the [[Pandemic Panic]], and the last tier after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated.

== Chances ==
* After the spawn criteria have been first met, there will be a 1/3 chance every [[tgc:Day|day]] for the Gogswarm to first invade.
* Further invasions (after the first has been defeated) have a 1/30 chance of occurring each day before the next tier has been unlocked.
* After another tier has been unlocked, there will be a 1/3 chance every [[tgc:Day|day]] for the next tier of the Gogswarm to first invade.
