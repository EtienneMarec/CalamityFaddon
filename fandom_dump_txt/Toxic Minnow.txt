The '''Toxic Minnow''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd layers of the [[Abyss]]. It swims in one direction and turns around when it hits a wall, dealing contact damage if the player touches it. It never moves to attack the player or fires projectiles of any kind, even if it is approached or hit.

Once killed, it bursts into roughly 10 toxic green skull clouds, which deals damage and inflicts the [[Poisoned]] [[debuff]] onto anything they touch, including enemies. These clouds linger for 10 seconds before vanishing.
