The '''Pumplerfaust''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. When used, a white pumpkin appears at the barrel of the gun. As the player channels the weapon, the pumpkin will slowly increase in size for 5 seconds until lighting up, indicated with a sound effect. Upon releasing, the pumpkin is launched towards the cursor and causes a colossal explosion upon hitting a tile or an enemy, leaving behind a damaging white mist. Releasing before the pumpkin is fully charged will cause the pumpkin to create a significantly smaller explosion with no lingering mist. This weapon uses no [[ammo]]. The weapon deals extra damage to [[Diamond Crawler]]s.
