The '''Opal Striker''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]] that is an upgrade of the [[Flintlock Pistol]]. It fires four projectiles in quick succession, consuming four [[bullets]] with pauses between volleys. Each bullet has a use time of 5.

== Tips ==
*Although the projectiles appear to grow in size as they travel, their hit profile does not change from that of a standard bullet.
*Unlike the [[Clockwork Assault Rifle]], the Opal Striker actually consumes one bullet per projectile.
*Using a [[modifier]] that increase firing speed may enable this weapon to shoot more bullets in one volley, but will still consume a bullet for each shot.

== Trivia ==
*The Opal Striker was implemented based on a community suggestion made by Gouvod on the mod's Terraria Forums post.
