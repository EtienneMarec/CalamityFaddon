'''The Scrapyard Ensemble''' (Japanese: スクラップヤードアンサンブル ''Sukurappuyādoansanburu'') is a gang of post-[[The Slime God|Slime God]] [[Wulfrumechs]] that are intended to be fought around the same time as the [[tgc:Wall_of_Flesh|Wall of Flesh]]. The battle consists of the four Exo Mechs: '''XS-1B Wulfrartemis''' and '''XS-3B Wulfrapollo''', '''XM-5B Wulfranatos''', and '''XF-9B Wulfrares'''. They are the meant to be the final boss before [[tgc:Hardmode|Hardmode]].

Defeating them will provide the player with a large amount of [[Wulfrum Bar]]s, a [[crafting material]] for various earlygame equipment.

==Spawn==
'''The Scrapyard Ensemble''' do not spawn on their own, and require the player to construct [[The Codebreaker]] and a [[The Codebreaker|Decryption Computer]]. Once constructed, the player can interact (right-click) with [[The Codebreaker]]. The '''Contact''' option is supposed to appear later on by default when an [[The Codebreaker|Auric Cooling Cell]] is placed. To summon the Scrapyard Ensemble, the player must insert an [[Energy Core]] into the [[Draedon Power Cell]] slot, then click the '''Contact''' button.

Once summoned, Draedon will allow the player to choose a Wulfrumech that will be spawned first.

*{{item|Wulfranatos Head Map|link=|mode=image}} Wulfranatos, a intimidating land excavator with gigantic jaws, impenetrable armor, and cores of energy.
*{{item|Wulfrares Map|link=|mode=image}} Wulfrares, a hooded amalgamation of many wulfrum enemies, bearing 4 charged blasters.
*{{item|Wulfrapollo Map|link=|mode=image}}Wulfrartemis and Wulfrapollo, a pair of surveillance robots with hidden superweapons.

== Trivia ==

* The theme heard when Draedon is summoned to give the player the choice of which mech to fight first is an ambient theme composed by PinpinNeon, only officially known in-game as '''Draedon's Ambience'''.
** Unlke other soundtracks, this theme is present in the base Calamity Mod content, and it does not require the music addon to be installed.
* The theme of the Scrapyard Ensemble fight is '''Scrapped Fabrications''', which was composed by '''CDMusic'''.
** If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Boss 3''' will play instead.
* The designs of the Wulfrumechs are comprised of many Wulfrum items and enemies.
** Wulfrares is made up of Wulfrum Pylons, Wulfrum Batteries, Wulfrum Rovers, the now replaced Wulfrum Shards, and an Energy Core.
** Wulfrapollo and Wulfrartemis are made up of  Wulfrum Gyrators and the now replaced Wulfrum Shards.
** Wulfranatos is made up of the now replaced Wulfrum Shards and Energy Cores.
