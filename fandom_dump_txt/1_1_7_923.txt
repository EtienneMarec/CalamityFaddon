The '''1.1.7.923''' update added the [[Potion of Omniscience]], fixed a couple of bugs, and made a couple of other small changes.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Potion of Omniscience]].
*Added [[Omniscience]].

== Changes ==
*Resprited [[Briny Baron]].
*Nerfed [[Crabulon]]'s mushroom projectile damage from {{dv|46|60}} to {{dv|26|44}} and decreased their gravity.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Ancient Medallion]] and [[Decapodita Sprout]] not correctly spawning [[Ravager]] and [[Crabulon]] respectively.
*Fixed the default vanilla hearts not appearing due to Calamity's [[Permanent Power-ups|health upgrades]].
