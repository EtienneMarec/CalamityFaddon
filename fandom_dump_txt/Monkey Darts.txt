'''Monkey Darts''' are consumable [[Hardmode]] [[daggers]] purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{sc|4}} each after [[Plantera]] has been defeated. When used, they throw a gravity affected dart that has a 50% chance of breaking upon contact with an [[enemy]] or [[block]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Monkey Darts will cause three bouncing darts to be thrown instead of one. Only one dart will be consumed when this occurs.
