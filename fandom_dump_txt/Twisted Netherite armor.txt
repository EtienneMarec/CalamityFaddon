'''Twisted Netherite armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set. It requires 24 [[Twisted Netherite Bar]] to craft the entire set. If the entire set is visible (regardless of whether it is equipped or in social slots), the wearer will have a faint pulsing aura.

It consists of a '''Twisted Netherite Helmet''', '''Twisted Netherite Chestplate''', '''Twisted Netherite Leggings''', and '''Twisted Netherite Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*175 defense
*50% damage reduction
*200% increased damage
*2% increased movement speed
*Attacks inflict the [[Wither]] debuff.
*All dropped items are intended to never despawn, even when reloading worlds.
**Due to a bug, this currently does not work.
