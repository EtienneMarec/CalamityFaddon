The '''Shadecrystal Barrage''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]]. When used it fires an inaccurate storm of purple crystals that inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] to any enemies hit. Each use has a chance to fire two crystals instead of one. Unlike [[Crystal Storm]], the crystals produced by this weapon will never ricochet off walls.
