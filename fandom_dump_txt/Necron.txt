'''Necron''' is a Hardmode [[boss]]. While it can be fought at any point after [[Skeletron]] is defeated, it is recommended to be fought after [[Lunatic Cultist]].

==Spawn==
Necron does not spawn on his own and requires the player to summon him by defeating the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] in the [[Dungeon]].
