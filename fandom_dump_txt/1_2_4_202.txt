The '''1.2.4.202''' update fixed some world generation issues regarding the new world generation added in [[1.2.4.201]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Zen Potion]].
*Added [[Zen]].

== Changes ==
*[[Asgardian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Aegis]] now have a dedicated hotkey to toggle their buff instead of being toggled with visibility.
*All of [[The Devourer of Gods]]' final phase's segments now show up on the minimap.
**Head segment's icon now rotates based on the head segment's rotation.
*[[Evil Island]]s now generate on set sides in the world.
*[[Ice Tomb]]s are now more common.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed some generation issues with the Ice Tomb.
