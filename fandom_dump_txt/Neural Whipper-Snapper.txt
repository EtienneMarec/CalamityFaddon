The '''Neural Whipper-Snapper''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Whips]] that can be dropped by the [[Destroyer of Sanity]]. When a [[Minions|minion]] or [[Minions|sentry]] hits an enemy marked by this weapon, it reduces their defense by 3 and a small shock will occur, dealing 1.75x the damage from the minion/sentry's attack. This can be repeated 5 times before needing to be reapplied. If the whip hits an enemy with 0 defense or below, it heals the player for 10 health.

Hitting an enemy fills the excitement meter by 1, capping off at 150. Once it reaches that cap, it automatically empties, and grants the player the '''Manic Seeker''' buff, increasing the player's whip speed by 70%.
