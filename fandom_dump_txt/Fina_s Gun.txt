'''Fina's Gun''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Guns|Gun]] that can be dropped from [[Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch]]. It fires 3-5 tightly parallel bullets at an incredibly quick rate, matching that of the [[P90]], while consuming only 1 unit of ammo.

{{Modifier|lowdamage}}

== Tips ==
*because of its minimal ammo reservation, it can very easily consume bullets at an extraordinary speed.
*This weapon perform best with [[Bullets]] that has high damage or splits into multiple particles, and gears that reduces enemy defense and/or grants armor penetration.
