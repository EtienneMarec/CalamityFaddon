'''The Hallow''' [[biome]] features four new [[enemies]], most of which spawn during Godseeker Mode. Two new [[boss]]es, the [[Profaned Guardians]] and [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], must be summoned and fought in this biome or in [[The Underworld]]. A [[shrine]] structure, which contains the [[Hallow Effigy]], a unique [[furniture]] item, can be found somewhere within this biome.
