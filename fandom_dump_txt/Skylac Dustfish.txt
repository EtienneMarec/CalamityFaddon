The '''Skylac Dustfish''' is an Epilogue [[enemy]] that spawns in the lower layer [[Sky Abyss]]. It swims left to right until it gest hit, After getting hit it releases clouds of Skylac Sand that damage the player.

In [[Death Mode]], it also gets invisible for 4 seconds and swims to the player when getting hit.
