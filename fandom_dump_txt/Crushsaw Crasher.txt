The '''Crushsaw Crasher''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] that has a 8.33% ({{expert|10%}}) chance to drop from [[Catastrophe]]. When used it auto-fires a homing, spinning axe that can pierce one enemy or bounce once before disappearing on its next impact. The axe inflicts [[Brimstone Flames]] upon any enemy it hits.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Crushsaw Crasher will cause five axes to be thrown instead of one.
