'''Worst Prism''' is a craftable [[magic weapon]]. It attacks by absorbing energy for 5 seconds then firing an uneven and high spread of laser beams that reach a base 100 blocks each then randomly and rapidly pulse in length up to 100 blocks in either direction. Each beam inflicts a different debuff depending on the color of the beam.

== Notes ==
*The weapon itself also deals contact damage to enemies. This trait is shared with the [[Hellborn]], [[Helstorm]], [[Cruel Jewel]], and [[Marnite Bayonet]].
*Worst Prism's beams disappear after 1 minute of continuous use, similarly to Last Prism.
*The Worst Prism only deals 85% damage to [[Aquatic Scourge]].

== Trivia ==
*The item is a parody of the [[Last Prism]].
