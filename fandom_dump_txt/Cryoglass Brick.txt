'''Cryoglass Brick''' was a [[block]] that replaced Obsidian Brick in [[Frozen Hell]]. It also had a furniture set and a platform.

== Trivia ==
*Its sprite, as well as the sprites of its furniture and platform, were all recolors of the base games Obsidian Brick set.
