''Nystagmus'' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapon]]. It is dropped by [[Polyphemalus]], when Cataractacomb  is defeated last. When used, it will channel a mini-polyphemalus that will follow the mouse cursor, similar to a magic missile. Once released, the mini-polyphemalus will explode after half a second into four homing eyes. It will also explode upon contact with a tile or enemy.
