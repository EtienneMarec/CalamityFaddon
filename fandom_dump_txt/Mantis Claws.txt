The '''Mantis Claws''' are a [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that autoswings. They are dropped from [[Mantis Shrimp]]s once [[Plantera]] is defeated and act similarly to the [[Fetid Baghnakhs]], having a very short range but extremely fast speed. Mantis Claws additionally create a small explosion on enemy hits. However, the explosion deals no damage and is purely visual.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon only benefits from 25% of melee swing speed bonuses.

== Trivia ==
*The explosion effect is a reference to real life [[wikipedia:Mantis_shrimp|Mantis shrimps]] and how they attack their prey with such speed and force that it creates a tiny vacuum called a cavitation bubble between the claw and the striking surface, the sudden collapse of which creates a shockwave as water rushes to fill in this gap. This explosion causes their targets to be hit twice: once from the claw itself and then a second time from the following explosion, similarly to the in-game weapon (although the in-game explosion does not deal any damage).
