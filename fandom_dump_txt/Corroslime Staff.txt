The '''Corroslime Staff''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] that is dropped by [[The Slime God]]. It summons a miniature [[Ebonian Blight Slime]], which follows the player and attacks [[enemies]]. It deals contact damage, similarly to other slimes. If the player gets too far, it grows two wings and flies towards the player.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Notes ==
*The Corroslime Staff is the [[Corruption]] counterpart for the [[Crimslime Staff]].
