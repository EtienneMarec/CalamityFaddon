'''Crawlers''' are a group of primarily-[[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemies]] that are either found in the [[Underground]] layer, the [[Cavern]] layer, or a specific underground [[biome]], depending on their type. They currently come in eight varieties:
*One for each of the six [[gem]] types, found in either the Underground or Cavern layers depending on gem value.
*One for [[Amber]], exclusive to the [[Underground Desert]].
*One for [[Crystal Shard]]s, exclusive to the [[Underground Hallow]] and therefore [[Hardmode]].

Crawlers spawn hiding in their gem shells. When damaged, they begin to run away from the player.

== Notes ==
*The existence of the crawlers makes farming for gems and crystals a lot easier and allows [[Amber]] to be obtained without needing to use the [[Extractinator]].

== Trivia ==
*Their damage and defense are sorted from "cheapest" gem to "most expensive" gem.
*In previous versions of the mod, the Crawlers would attack the player with standard Fighter [[AI]] instead of hiding and fleeing.
