The '''1.1.4.4''' update featured some changes to the [[Catastrophe Claymore]].

== Changes ==
*Catastrophe Claymore changes:
**Now requires 3 of each mechanical boss soul to craft.
**Can now be crafted with [[Ichor]] instead of just [[Cursed Flame]]s.
**Nerfed damage from 70 to 60.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Brimstone Flames]] and [[Holy Light]] debuffs being mixed up.
