The '''Spectral Veil''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]].  the player is a granted a guaranteed dodge that can only occur every 70 seconds. The item also increases movement speed by 10% and makes stealth generate 20% faster while moving.
