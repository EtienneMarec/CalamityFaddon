The '''Bloom Stone''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a 20% chance to drop from [[Plantera]]. While it is equipped, the player's life regen is increased by 3 if they are standing on any ground, this effect is doubled during daytime.

The Bloom Stone also gives the player an augmented [[Flower Boots]] effect, allowing flowers and plants to grow on grass whilst also occasionally allowing random dye plants to appear on dirt that does ''not'' have grass upon it. This makes it ideal for farming not only seeds and bait, but also various [[dyes]] and [[Strange Plant]]s, as virtually any vanilla dye can be obtained in this manner.
