'''Warglaives''' are [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]]s dropped by [[Goblin Thief|Goblin Thieves]]. When used, it throws out a gravity-affected glaive which pierces through 3 enemies before dissipating on the fourth while inflicting [[War Cleave]] on every hit enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Warglaives causes the glaive to stick to tiles. The stuck glaive will despawn after 10 seconds or after damaging an enemy four times.
