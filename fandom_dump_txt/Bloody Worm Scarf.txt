The '''Bloody Worm Scarf''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that provides a 7 defense, 7% increase to damage reduction and increases melee damage and speed by 7%. This covering can be considered the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Amalgamated Brain]], even though they both can be crafted regardless of the world evil.

== Tips ==
*If the player lives in a Crimson world, defeating the [[Brain of Cthulhu]] will make the [[Dryad]] sell the Worm Scarf.
*If the player lives in a [[Corruption]] world, defeating [[The Hive Mind]] will make the Dryad sell the Bloody Worm Tooth.
*The damage reduction also stacks with the Worm Scarf.
*While the Amalgamated Brain is geared towards all users, the Bloody Worm Scarf is geared primarily towards melee users, as it increases damage for melee weapons.
