'''The Syringe''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[buff potions]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It grants the user the '''Plagued Wrath''', granting the user:
*+10% all damage
*+5% critical strike chance
*+20% movement speed
*Immunity to [[Irradiated]], [[Plague]], [[Poisoned]], and [[Venom]].
*-4 life regen
*-5% [[damage reduction]]
*-8% defense

== Trivia ==
*This item is directly based on [[wikipedia:Steroid|Steroids]] and Vaccines from real life.
*Its debuff's tooltip references [[wikipedia:Bo_Burnham|Bo Burnham]]'s 2021 release "Welcome to the Internet".
