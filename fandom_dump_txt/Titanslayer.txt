The '''Titanslayer''' is an Epilogue [[Bows|bow]] dropped by [[XB-10 Nortazzo]]. It fires a stream of 8 spectral bolts. Every three uses, the stream will be supercharged, increasing the damage and size of the bolts by 500% and 200% respectively, and causing them to explode into souls that deal 25% damage. Supercharged bolts have an additional 20% critical strike chance.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Unreal'''.
