The '''Calamitous Soul Artifact''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is a combination of all four soul artifacts. When worn, the player gains the following effects:
*All negative effects are removed from other coverings, such as negative healing from the [[Mana Overloader]], increased damage taken from the [[Reaper Tooth Necklace]], and the reduced movement speed from [[Asgardian Aegis]]' guard.
*When an enemy is killed, they drop a Brimstone Heart projectile. The hearts create a small explosion after 10 seconds which deals a flat 6666 damage to enemies caught within. Any projectile that hits the heart will be deleted for a maximum of 3 projectiles blocked. If the player touches the heart, it is collected, healing the player for 10 health.
*For every minion summoned which takes up 2 or more minion slots, a new minion slot is gained.
*20% increased melee speed.
*35% increased ranged velocity.
*45% increased Spy damage.
*8 increased max minions.
*Mana cost reduced by 75%.
*All damage increases as the player gets near enemies for a cap of 700% damage. Damage starts to increase upon reaching a 40 tile range of an enemy.
