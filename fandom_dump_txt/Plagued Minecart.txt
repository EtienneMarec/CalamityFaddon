The '''Plagued Minecart''' is a [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive [[Hardmode]] [[Mount]] summoning item that drops from the [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. When used on [[Minecart Track]]s, it summons a rideable cart that can dash for a base distance of 60 tiles. Upon dashing, it fires off a barrage of homing plague rockets that deals 360 base damage. A purely aesthetic flame also comes out of its thruster while dashing. This has a cooldown of 5 seconds, and an accompanying sound effect plays upon expiry.

The Plague Cart has a top running speed of 68 mph with an acceleration of 15 tiles/second<sup>2</sup>. Similar to other minecarts, the Plague Cart is able to jump with a similar height and acceleration.
