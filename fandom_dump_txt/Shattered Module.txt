The '''Shattered Module''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] that drops from [[The Forbidden Lantern]] [[Challenges|boss]]. While equipped, it decreases the player's damage by 8% and causes all of their attacks to inflict the '''Shattered Mechanism''' [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 1 second. This debuff decreases enemy's knockback resistance by 50% and stuns them for the whole duration of the debuff, preventing it from moving or attacking, similarly to the [[Glacial State]] debuff. However, the debuff will not decrease bosses' knockback resistance and will only stun them for up to 0.5 seconds. Additionally, the debuff cannot be reapplied before it has fully run out.

== Notes==
*It is advised to use weapons with high attack speed to reapply the debuff as soon as possible.
