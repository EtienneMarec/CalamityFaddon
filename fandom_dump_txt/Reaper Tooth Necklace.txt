The '''Reaper Tooth Necklace''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is an upgrade to the [[Sand Shark Tooth Necklace]]. While equipped, all of the player's stats will increase by 15%, and their armor penetration is increased by 15. Additionally, a Reaper Shark minion is summoned.
Uniquely among minions, this Reaper Shark will follow the cursor when a summoning weapon is held, allowing the player to directly control it. 

Pressing the Shark Recall key will make the Reaper Shark return to your side, even when a summoning weapon is held.
