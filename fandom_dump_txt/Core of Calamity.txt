'''Cores of Calamity''' are [[Hardmode]] [[crafting materials]]. They can also be obtained from [[Necromantic Geode]]s, a type of [[grab bag]] dropped by the [[Ravager]].

Crafting all items (excluding any items crafted from [[Miracle Matter]] and itself included) requires 52 Cores of Calamity (requiring 156 [[Core of Chaos|Cores of Chaos]], [[Core of Sunlight|Sunlight]], [[Core of Eleum|Eleum]] and 52 [[Ashes of Calamity]]).
