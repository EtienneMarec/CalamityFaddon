The '''Hive''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns in the [[Underground]] [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. It is stationary, and attacks by spawning '''Hivelings''' which attempt to fly into the player, it will also spawn Hivelings when damaged.
