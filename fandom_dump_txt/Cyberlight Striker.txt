The '''Cyberlight Striker''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Dart guns|dart gun]]. It fires a cyberlight dart that fires bullets towards a random direction every 0.1 of a second. If a bullet strikes a bullseye created by right-clicking it converts into 5 Homing darts that deal 80% of the damage.
