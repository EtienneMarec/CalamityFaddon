'''God Slayer armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Cosmilite Bar]]s and [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]]. Making a set with one headpiece requires 55 Cosmilite Bars which requires 275 [[Cosmilite Slag]]. It is the Melee/Ranged/[[Spy]] counterpart to [[Silva armor]]. If the Chestplate, Leggings, and any of the three headpieces of the set are visible (whether equipped or in social slots), the player will have afterimages.

It consists of the '''God Slayer Chestplate''' and '''God Slayer Leggings''', as well as having three different headpieces suited to the Melee, Ranged, and Spy classes, being: the '''God Slayer Horned Greathelm''', '''God Slayer Helmet''', and '''God Slayer Mask.''' All helmets share a set bonus, but also provide set bonuses specific to their respective class as well.

The '''God Slayer Chestplate''' provides 41 defense, 11% increased damage and 6% increased critical strike chance, a thorns effect, and +60 max life. The '''God Slayer Leggings''' provides 35 defense, 10% increased damage and critical strike chance, and 5% increased movement speed.

*The '''God Slayer Horned Greathelm''' gives 48 defense and 14% increased melee damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''God Slayer Helmet''' gives 35 defense and 14% increased ranged damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''God Slayer Mask''' gives 29 defense, 14% increased Spy damage and critical strike chance and 5% increased movement speed.

== Set Bonuses ==
Regardless of the headpiece chosen, equipping a full set of God Slayer armor will provide the following set bonuses:
*Pressing {{key|God Slayer Dash}} allows the player to dash a great distance in 8 directions depending on what movement keys are held.
**The player is invulnerable and immune to most debuffs while dashing.
**Dashes have 3000 base damage and inflict enemies with the [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]] for 10 seconds.
**This effect has a 35 second [[God Slayer Cooldown|cooldown]].
In addition to the above, each headpiece provides its own class-specific set bonus:

'''God Slayer Horned Greathelm''':
*+1,000 to [[Aggro]].
*Taking over 80 damage in one hit will cause the wearer to release a swarm of eight high-damage god killer darts.
**The darts deal 900 typeless damage and can not be increased by any means.
**The darts inflicts God Slayer Inferno for 3 seconds.
*Enemies that touch you have 2.5x their contact damage reflected back at them.
*Attacks that would deal 80 damage or less to the wearer are decreased to 1.
'''God Slayer Helmet''':
*Every 2.5 seconds, firing a ranged weapons also fires a god-killing shrapnel round.
**The shrapnel has 675 base damage. The round spawns 4-6 pieces of shrapnel which deal 30% of the set bonus round's damage.
'''God Slayer Mask''':
*While at full health, Spy damage, crit chance, and velocity are boosted by 10%.
*If the wearer takes over 80 damage in one hit, they will be given 30 extra [[immunity frames]].
*Gives a maximum [[Spy#Stealth|Spy stealth]] of 120.
