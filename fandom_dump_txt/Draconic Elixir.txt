The '''Draconic Elixir''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Draconic Surge''' buff which increases the flight time of [[Wings]] by 20%, increases maximum flight speed and acceleration by 15%, and grants 16 [[defense]].

If the player is wearing [[Silva armor]] or Auric Tesla armor and takes fatal damage, [[Silva Immunity]] will also heal them to half their health. If either revive effect is triggered, the player loses the Draconic Surge buff, disallowing them from drinking another Draconic Elixir for 1 minute, in addition to adding 30 seconds of [[Potion Sickness]].
