The '''Lunatic Beats''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Dedicated content|Developer]] [[Boomerangs|Boomerang]]. It can pierce 10 times before going back. While travelling, it conjures various celestial projectiles, as listed here:
*2 - 4 Solar Fireballs that travels quickly and homes aggressively, creating a big explosion on contact.
*Vortex Portals that appears near where the disc was, firing out powerful lightning at enemies.
*3 Swirling Nebulas identical to that of the Nebula Arcanum, albeit faster and bigger. These are spread out in random directions.
*Stardust Lights that hits struck enemies from above where the disc hit them.

Additionally, the disc and its projectiles deals more damage when returning back to the player.
