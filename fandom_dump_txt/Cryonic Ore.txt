'''Cryonic Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ore]] which generates in the [[Ice biome]] upon [[Cryogen]]'s defeat. It is used to craft [[Cryonic Bar]]s at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or [[Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least an [[Adamantite Pickaxe]] or [[Titanium Pickaxe]] to mine. It is also dropped by [[Cryo Slime]]s. This ore glows slightly which makes it easy to spot through walls.

== Notes ==
*Cryonic Ore cannot be destroyed by explosives, even after Cryogen has been defeated.
*Unlike most other ores, it is visible without the use of a [[Spelunker Potion]], as it naturally glows a dark blue color.
**Though, it is the dimmest glowing ore in the mod, and the glow can be overpowered by other nearby glowing ores.
