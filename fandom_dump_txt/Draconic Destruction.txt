The '''Draconic Destruction''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung, the sword shoots out a singular Draconic Beam in the direction of the cursor. When this beam hits an enemy or tile, it causes 8 secondary beams to shoot outwards from the location of impact in a radial shape. After reaching a certain distance, the secondary beams will disappear and create a wave of tile-ignoring beams to go down, then another wave up, then one final wave down again. All sword beams ignore NPC [[immunity frames]].
