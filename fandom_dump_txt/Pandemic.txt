The '''Pandemic''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[yoyo]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Similar to the [[Oblivion]], the Pandemic constantly fires plague seekers at nearby enemies; plague seekers deal 75% of the yoyo's damage. Both the yoyo and its plague seeker projectiles inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]].
