The '''Solar Flare''' is a Godseeker Mode [[yoyo]] dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. Every impact with an enemy will produce a large explosion of embers that deal 75% of the weapon's damage and inflict the [[Holy Flames]] debuff. Unlike other yoyos, the Solar Flare ignores NPC [[immunity frames]], allowing it to rapidly bounce off of enemies and inflict much more damage than its damage stat would indicate.
