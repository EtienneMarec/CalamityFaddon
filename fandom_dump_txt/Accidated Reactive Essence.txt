'''Accidated Reactive Essence''' is a post-[[The Entropic God, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Crafting materials|crafting material]]. It is dropped from every enemy in the [[Jungle]], [[Glowing Mushroom biome]] and [[The Exosphere]] with a 10% chance. It is used to craft various Post-Umbrus acid-themed summoner equipment.
