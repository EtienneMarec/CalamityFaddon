The '''Exorcism''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]]. It automatically throws a gravity-affected hallowed cross that explodes into a large explosion which deals more damage to enemies closer to it. The amount of damage increases as the cross falls, releasing white particles when fully charged.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Exorcism will cause the next cross thrown to be fully charged with 115% damage, and hallowed stars additionally fall from the sky when the cross explodes, each dealing 57.5% damage.
