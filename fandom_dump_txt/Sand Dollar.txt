The '''Sand Dollar''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] that is purchased by the [[Sea King]] at any time. This weapon can be stacked up to two. The amount of boomerangs thrown is dependent on stack size.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Sand Dollar causes two sand dollars to be thrown out regardless of stack size. They travel farther before returning to the player and spawns a gravity-affected coral shard on enemy hits.

{{Modifier|none}}

== Trivia ==
*In real life, [[wikipedia:Sand dollar|sand dollars]] are a type of burrowing sea urchin, known for possessing a skeleton reminiscent of the old [[wikipedia:Spanish dollar|Spanish dollar]].
