'''The Rot''' (Japanese: 腐敗 ''Fuhai''), also known as Daddy Long Legs, is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that can be fought in the [[Five Pebbles]] biome. The Rot possesses many tentacles that it uses against the player.

Defeating Cryogen will generate [[Rotting matter]] in the [[Five Pebbles]] biome.

== Spawn ==
{{fantag|Catalyst}} The Rot is able to be summoned by killing 30 [[Neuron Flies]] which spawn in the Five Pebbles biome at any time.
== Behavior ==
The Rot has two phases.

The Rot is blind, and instead relies on hearing and touch to locate enemies.

Leaving the Five Pebbles biome will cause Cryogen to enrage, almost doubling its speed and acceleration. Entering the [[Looks To The Moon]] biome will triple its speed and acceleration.

=== Attacks ===
==== Phase 1 ====
*Slowly chases and levels with the player.
*Attempts to grab players with its Tentacles
If Successful, Consumes the player.

==== Phase 2 ====
*Transitions at {{dv|20%||40%|}} health.
*Rapidly and loosely accelerates towards the player, charging at them when it gets close.

=== In [[Expert Mode]] ===

*Has one additional tentacle

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*Transitions into Phase 2 at 40%.
*The Rot and its Tentacles are slightly faster.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===

*The Rot and its tentacles are even more aggressive and faster.

=== During the [[Boss Rush]] ===

* Replaced with [[Terror Rot|Terror Long Legs]] instead

== Aftermath ==
*Once The Rot is defeated for the first time, [[Rotting Matter]] spawns in the Five Pebbles biome.
*If [[Five Pebbles (Boss)]] has been defeated, Brother Long Legs will begin to spawn in the cavern biome.

== Notes ==
*While The Rot is alive, the sky above the player is filled with a dark blue pulsating wall. It cannot be toggled off by lighting options in the Settings Menu.
*If The Rot is close to the player when it enters Phase 2, it will briefly spin in place to give the player time to get away from its dash.

== Tips ==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating The Rot, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:The Rot strategies]].}}

*It is easier to see The Rot during the day.
*A wide arena with several levels of platforms can help in dodging The Rot's attacks.
**Be careful: The Rot uses walls and platforms as its grip to move.
*Take advantage of the fact that The Rot relies on hearing.
