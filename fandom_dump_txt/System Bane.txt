The '''System Bane''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. When used, the player throws a gravity-affected electric machine that continuously deals damage to targets that come into contact with it. Occasionally if there's a target nearby, the device releases lightning to triple-strike it. Up to 5 machines can be out at a time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the System Bane causes the resulting machine to emit a large pulsating electric field that releases lightning into enemies within it.

The weapon consumes 0.085 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 135.
