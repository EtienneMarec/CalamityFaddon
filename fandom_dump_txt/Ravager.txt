'''Meatmetal Sans''' (Japanese: メタルサンズ ''Metarusanzu''), known as '''Ravager''' prior to the Sans Undertale update, is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is found in the Judgement Hall structure, which is located in the [[Bloody Corn Maze]]  under the largest silo. On top of dropping a unique set of [[weapons]] and [[coverings]], its purpose is to act as an efficient, though not necessarily easy, means of acquiring [[Life Alloy|Life Alloys]], [[Core of Calamity|Cores of Calamity]], and other materials via [[Fleshy Geode|Fleshy Geodes]].

After [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated, Meatmetal Sans will not become any stronger. However, he will mention, "wait, wasn't that the kid that fell from the light and genocided us monsters? that's kinda messed up... the fact that they just kept going, genociding all in their path...". He will also start dropping [[Necromantic Geode|Necromantic Geodes]], Souls of Fright, and Papyrus Scarabs, providing the player an alternative means of obtaining these resources.

== Spawn ==
Meatmetal Sans can be found inside of the Judgement Hall, a structure that can be found under the large silo at the ocean side of the Bloody Corn Maze. When entering the Judgement Hall, Meatmetal Sans will be in a deactivated state. Attempting to use a [[Spy]] weapon on Meatmetal Sans while he is deactivated will initiate the fight. He will begin by saying "Terrarian... I remember you're genocides..."

Meatmetal Sans won't respawn in the Judgement Hall after being defeated. Instead, a player needs to find the [[Ness]] NPC, and harvest their soul, which can be combine with [[tgc:Lihzahrd_Brick|Lihzahrd Brick]] and [[Alloy Bar|Alloy Bars]] to make the [[Suspicious Looking Fan Theory]].

When summoned, Meatmetal Sans will play a sped up version of Megalovania instead of the regular boss roar.

== Behavior ==
During the fight, Meatmetal Sans will stand in place and dodge all attacks you attempt to land on them. Their primary form of attack is via bone projectiles and Gaster Blasters. All attacks from Meatmetal Sans only deal 1 damage, but inflict the [[Poisoned]] debuff and ignore the player's invulnerability frames. This debuff cannot be removed by the [[Nurse]].

Meatmetal Sans also has a special mechanic called '''"''Blue Dyed Soul Mode''" (BDSM)''' that allows them to manipulate the gravity of the player, disabling the player's [[Wings|flight]]. This mode is a very subtle reference to the blue soul from Undertale that is used by Sans Undertale. 

The only way to progress through the fight is to attempt to attack Meatmetal Sans with any weapon, which will not deal any damage to the boss but instead increase an invisible counter that progresses the phases of the fight. No matter how much damage an attack might deal, it will only increment the counter by 1. Every time Meatmetal Sans dodges the player, he will use BDSM to launch the player away, forcing the player to run back to Meatmetal Sans. When the player exits Phase 1, Meatmetal Sans will also teleport to a new location to create more distance from the player, preventing cheesing of the second phase.

Meatmetal Sans will also begin talking after every hit, causing a dialogue box to appear on top of the screen. While Sans is talking, the player will be inflicted with the Listening debuff (preventing them from using any attacks).

Throughout the entirety of the fight, the player will be inclicted with the Aichmomania debuff. This debuff only allows the player to use [[True melee|True Melee]] and [[Spy]] weapons. This debuff is a subtle reference to Undertale, a game where one of the weapons is a knife.

Many attacks throughout the fight are known as '''Special Attacks'''. Special Attacks are only triggered after a player attempts to attack Meatmetal Sans or 30 seconds after the last Special Attack. These differ from usual attacks in that only one Special Attack can happen at a time.

==== Attacks ====
===== Phase 1 =====
Once the Player has hit Meatmetal Sans 12 times, all attacks will cease, and a transition between Phase 1 and Phase 2 will take place
* '''''Regular Attacks'''''
** Every few seconds, a small portion of the ground will be marked with exclaimation marks, which telegraph areas where bones will shoot out of the ground.
*** Ocassionally, Meatmetal Sans will give the player BSDM, and throw them in a cardinal direction. Once the player hits a wall, floor, or ceiling, the attack will be performed (although it will orient to the surface you are thrown against).
** Meatmetal Sans will create Gaster Blasters every 20 seconds. These Gaster Blasters will take 2 seconds to charge up before firing a lazer in their direction that lasts for 5 seconds.
*** There is a 6.25% chance for the Gaster Blaster to be a Big varient. Bigger Gaster Blasters take 4 seconds to charge up and only last for 4 seconds.
**Every few seconds, a Metal Cog will be spit out of Meatmetal Sans' mouth. This Cog will bouce off surfaces a maximum of 4 times.
**Meatmetal Sans will sometimes shoot short Lazers out of his eyes. They are slow moving and can either be Orange, Blue, Burgundy, Forest Green, or Brown.
***If the Lazer is Blue, the Attack will damage the player if they are moving.
***If the Lazer is Orange, the Attack will not damage the player if they are moving.
***If the Lazer is Burgundy, the attack might damage the player if they are moving.
***If the Lazer is Forest Green, the attack might not damage the player if they are moving.
***If the Lazer is Brown, then the rules that apply to the lazers will be randomly swapped.
**Fireballs can alternativly be shot instead of lazers, which inflict the On Fire debuff.
* '''''Special Attacks'''''
** Long Vertical Bones move horizontally towards the player. Each Bone is spaced apart from eachother a fair amount, with a gap in the center of the Bone for the player to dodge them through. The gaps position is random on the Bone. The attack ends after the player has passed through 10 Bone gaps. The Bones can come either come in from the left or right of the screen.
** A box is created around the player, with 3 rows of platforms that scroll horizontally and loop around the box with randomly placed gaps. Horizontal Gaster Blasters will continuously appear on the left or right of the box, and will shoot into the space created between a row of platforms. This attack will go on until 10 Gaster Blasters have been shot.
** A box will form around the player, and a platforming segment will start. Starting from the left will be 3 platforms atop a sea of bones. Near the top of the third platform is a set of three bones, and at the end of the sea of bones is a long bone coming from the top.
** A box will form around the player, and a platforming segment will start. Starting from the right is a long platforming segment with 7 platforms. The fourth, fifth, and sixth platforms have a bone above each platform. The last platform has long bones appearing from the top left and bottom right sides.
** A box will form around the player and a segment with moving, long platforms and bones that can block a path will start. The bottom platforms appear from the right, and the higher platforms appear from the left.
** A box will form around the player, and a small platform that moves left and right will form below the player. The bottom of the box is filled with a sea of bones, and 3 sets of levitating bones cover the near center. The center line of bones descends, while the outer two lines rises.
** Sets of bones appear on the bottom, from the left, and large bones appear at the top, from the right. The attack will last for 15 seconds.

While many in the Calamity Community will count this transition as part of Phase 1.5, there is a subset of the Calamity Community that like to group this as either Phase 1 or Phase 2.

No attacks will take place during this Phase, instead of attacking the player, Meatmetal Sans will try to reason with the player (which will have the previously mentioned [[Listening]] debuff applied until he's done talking). Metal Sans will say the following:

* ''"or will you choose to use you're fists?"''
* ''"i know your not truly evil, you don't need to kill us all."''
* ''"i know you might be homicidal and have a taste..."''
* ''"and maybe you might even want to wipe out the calamitous monster race..."''
* ''"but i think you need some patience and resolve."''
* ''"you don't have to slaughter or avoid the dialouge."''
* ''"i know you slaughtered duke fishron, and you waste who you choose" (This dialogue will only play if Duke Fishron has been defeated in the current world)''
* ''"and with all those accessories there's no way that'd you loose..."''
* ''"because i'd seen you move."''
* ''"but i really think you should stop it's kinda rude."''
* ''"Yharim is shaking, he hears you're approach."''
* ''"he heard about how you squashed my bro skeletron like a roach..."''
* ''"don't let calamity be you're coach."''
* ''"there is no reason for you to poach."''
* ''"i know that i may be hard to make the decision..."''
* ''"but i know there is still some good in you"''

Note: All spelling and grammar mistakes have been clarified by the Calamity Dev Team to be intentional. This is a subtle nod to the "I remember you're Genocides" meme from the game '''Undertale''', which this boss is based on.



Once Meatmetal Sans finishes his speech there are 2 different actions you can take. You can either hit him again to begin Phase 2, or you can avoid attacking him for 15 seconds to "spare" him. When spared, Metal Sans will reassure the player that they made the right choice in preperation for a betrayal kill:

* ''"..."''
* ''"your sparing me?"''
* ''"finally"''
* ''"buddy chum pal friend buddy pal chum bud friend fella bruther amigo pal buddy friend chummy chum chum pal"''
* ''"i know how hard it must be..."''
* ''"to make that choice"''
* ''"but..."''
* ''"i still remember you're genocides"''


Once Meatmetal Sans finishes talking, he will cover the entire screen with projectiles, which quickly kill the player. Some custom Death Messages will show up if this happens:
* [Player] was dunked on.
* [Player] had their Genocides remembered.
* [Player] was killed by an Intentional Game Design.
* [Player] faced judgement for their crimes.
* [Player] was flayed down to the bone.

===== Phase 2 =====
During Phase 2, Meatmetal Sans changes his appearance drastically. Rather than have a similar appearence to the player, it will turn into a fleshy, mech-like being. Despite the texture being much bigger, Meatmetal Sans doesn't change their hitbox. Phase 2 ends once the player is able to land 10 (23 if including hits from phase 1 and 1.5) hits on Metal Sans, killing the boss.
* '''''Regular Attacks'''''
** Phase 2 has all the regular attacks from Phase 1, with some small tweaks
*** The chance of Meatmetal Sans Summoning a Big Gaster Blaster increases from 6.25% to 50%
*** Gaster Blasters are created every 10 seconds instead of every 20
*** The frequency of Meatmetal Sans trying to use BDSM to throw you into bones coming out of the ground increases greatly.
*** Lazers fired by Meatmetal Sans will randomize their effect everytime they're shot.
*** Cogs fired by Meatmetal Sans will split into 2 Smaller Bouncing Cogs when hitting walls.
**Meatmetal Sans will ocassionally Create a long bone in the center of the screen that rotates 3 times before dissappearing.
**Meatmetal Sans will teleport to a new location every 10 seconds, with the position being entirely random (as apposed to his dodge teleport, which moves him away from the player).
**Every few seconds, explosive metal bones will be fired into the air, which explode on the ground after 5 seconds of being on the ground

* '''''Special Attacks'''''
** Meatmetal Sans will ocassionally blackout the screen for a breif moment, before bringing the screen back. Once the screen is back, the player will be in the middle of a random Phase 1 attack. The attack will last 5 seconds before Meatmetal Sans repeats the blackout with another random Phase 1 Special Attack. This loop will repeat until 1 minute has elapsed.
** A box is formed around the player, and Gaster Blasters continuously circle the Box aimed at the center. The Gaster Blasters form five full circles before the attack ends.
** If there is available space, Meatmetal Sans will use BDSM to throw you into the air, and then once again to toss you in a horizontal direction. While "falling" in the direction, an assortment of bones will get in your way. The attack lasts for 30 seconds.

=== [[Expert Mode]] ===
*The speed of Meatmetal Sans' attacks increase on Phase 2.
*The fireballs and lasers have an increased rate of fire

=== [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*Meatmetal Sans teleports more frequently.
*Meatmetal Sans has a 10% to fire [[wikipedia:Armour-piercing_fin-stabilized_discarding_sabot|105mm Armor-Piercing Discarding Sabot Fin Stabilized rounds]] out of a [[Handheld Tank]]. Unlike most of Meatmetal Sans' attacks, these projectiles don't avoid invincibility frames or inflict [[Poisoned]]; however, they cut the player's current Health in half.

=== [[Death Mode]] ===
*When when Phase 2 is 50% complete, Meatmetal Sans will speed up all attacks by 25%
*Sans now deals 2 damage rather than 1.
*Meatmetal Sans has a 0.01% to fire an [[wikipedia:LGM-30 Minuteman|Minuteman-III Intercontinental Ballistic Missile]] every frame, which kills the player, Meatmetal Sans, and every other entity in the current world. This does not count as defeating the boss, and it will not drop anything.

== Notes ==
*There is a rare bug that makes it so Meatmetal Sans sometimes resets the hit counter, which restarts the fight from the beginning.

== Tips ==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating Ravager, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Ravager strategies]].}}

* Using [[Spy]] weapons help a lot, as the Knife-based weapons allow the player to maintain distance from Meatmetal Sans.
* The player should obtain the Hopes and Dreams item from the [[Taming|Calamity Pacifist Route]] if they have completed said route on a different file. Doing this will enable the player to resurrect during the fight, effectively breezing through it.
* As Meatmetal Sans's attacks often involve a Box, a Rod of Discord can be used to teleport out of said box. Doing this more than 3 times during the fight, however, will initiate Meatmetal Sans' betrayal kill.
* The Lilith's Necklace, which, with the [[Calamity Mod Wiki|Calamity Mod]] installed, will transform the player into an Annoying Dog, will make the fight significantly easier by granting immunity to [[Poisoned]].
** While these are the most common strategies, many other calamity players recommend to "git gud" and learn the boss's attack patterns.
