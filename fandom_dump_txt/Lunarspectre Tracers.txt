'''Lunerspectre Tracers''' are Godseeker Mode [[Equipment|covering]] that is an upgrade to [[Shatterspectre Tracers]]. They are considered a side-grade to [[Seraph Tracers]]. While equipped, they provide the following effects:

*12 [[defense]]
*5 increased life regen (2.5 HP/s)
*12% increased movement speed
*32% increased jump speed
*The player's max horizontal movement speed is increased to 30 miles per hour (~22 tiles per second).
*While running, a fairy minion is spawned every 2 seconds.
**The fairy minion homes onto the closest target, exploding upon contant and dealing 120 classless damage, which scales with the strongest class and cannot critically hit.
*The player's attacks inflict the [[Moon Burnt]] [[Debuffs|debuff]].
*All debuffs deal 33% more damage and are 50% longer.
*[[Wings|Flight time]] is increased by 80 frames (1.33 seconds)
*Allows auto-jumping
*Immunity to fall damage

==Notes==
*Does the player runs or not at the moment is determined by checking if they have hit at least 30 MPH.

==Trivia==
*The fairy's color will change depending on the time of the day: 
**From 4:30AM to 12PM, the fairy will be pink.
**From 12PM to 7:30PM, the fairy will be green.
**From 7:30PM to 4:30AM, the fairy will be blue.
