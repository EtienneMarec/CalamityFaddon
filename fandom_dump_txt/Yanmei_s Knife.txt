'''Yanmei's Knife''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]]. When swung, a short-ranged green slash appears in front of the player towards the mouse cursor. After the swing, the player is temporarily unable to use the item for three seconds. Striking a [[boss]] or its servants causes the user to gain the '''Kami Injection''' [[buff]] while the target is inflicted with the '''Kami Flu''' [[debuff]]. Kami Injection grants the player 15% movement speed and damage, while Kami Flu reduces the enemy’s [[damage reduction]] by 20% and depletes 125 health per second. Players with the Kami Injection will trail green afterimages, while targets that are inflicted with the Kami Flu debuff will emit a green glow.

== Notes ==
*The knife's effects only trigger on bosses, mini-bosses, and/or boss servants, making it hard to properly test against dummies.
*When the knife successfully hits a vulnerable NPC, it will play the same sound as activating [[Adrenaline Mode]].
*The lowered damage reduction effect of Kami Flu does not work on [[The Dragonfolly]], [[Polterghast]], and [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]].

== Tips ==
*Due to the weapon's low direct damage and the significant stats of the buff and debuff, Yanmei's Knife should not be used as a primary damage-dealer and instead functions better when used periodically, to refresh the buff and debuff.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon is a reference to Automaid, a work-in-progress comic series being illustrated and written by the dedicatee. The knife is the signature weapon of the main character, Yanmei, with the Kami buff and debuffs playing a key role in the comic.
*The tooltip is part of a poem by Piky, one of the beta testers for the mod, written to describe Yanmei:

Memories of the Days of Old <br/>
Yet, profoundly reassuring <br/>
Her voice calls to the fire <br/>
Dancing until the break of dawn <br/>
Of her deeds alone, instilling within her friends <br/>
A heart of Valor <br/>
