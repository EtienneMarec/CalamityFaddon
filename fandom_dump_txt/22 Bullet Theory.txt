The '''22 Bullet Theory''' is a Godseeker Mode [[event]] which occurs when using the [[Phosphorescent Gauntlet]] on [[The General]] {{fantag|Apotheosis and Friends}} while under the effects of [[Fluorescent Vodka]], or by consecutively firing 2,222 [[22 Hornet Round|22 Hornet Rounds]] with any gun. 

The event consists of 22 possessed items that each need to be stunned and collected, closed off with a fight against [[Deicide]]. Items cannot be damaged by the player and can only be stunned through specific methods unique to each item.

Upon being defeated, the player is rewarded a [[Klepticoin]] and a [[22 bullet theory (lore)|unique lore item]].

== Tips ==
*It is ''highly'' advised to have a plan for dealing with each item ahead of time instead of trying to access stun methods on the go.
*Using any gun with [[22 Hornet Round]]s against an item (aside from Divine Retribution and Rover Drive) will stagger them for a 2 seconds with a 20 second cooldown.

== Trivia ==
*The 22 Bullet Theory's [[Music|theme]] is '''[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hsxqvb3s8JU THE SHOT HEARD 'ROUND THE WORLD]''', which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@sploopo Sploopo]. The theme uses motifs from the original theme, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uA3--qzsDCQ Stinging Onslaught], which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@ENNWAY Turquoise].
*Each item, its behavior, and its defeat method are all references to the "Gods" killed during the original 22 Bullet Theory.
**The order in which the items appear is the same order that each represented god was killed by the bullet chronologically.
