The '''Tomatus''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that auto-swings. Hitting an enemy will make a small explosion.

Like every shortsword, it has an incredibly short range. However, this [[shortsword]] can auto-swing.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemies hit by it and its explosions are inflicted with [[Hellfire]].
