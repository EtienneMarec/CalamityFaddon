The '''Corrosive Spine''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. It increases [[defense]] by 4 and movement speed by 10%. It additionally causes all Spy weapons to inflict the [[Venom]] [[debuff]] and occasionally release green toxic clouds in random directions which inflict the [[Irradiated]] debuff to enemies they hit. For stealth strikes, this effect only triggers for the first five enemies hit. The clouds are also released from the player while their [[immunity frames]] are active.
