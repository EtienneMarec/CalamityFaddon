The '''Aegis Core''' is a craftable post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[covering]] with a wide array of defensive and mobility effects. It provides:

* 34 defense (74 in liquids)
* immunity to knockback
* Immunity to most debuffs
* +50 max life and increased life regeneration by 1
* 20% increased jump speed
* Ability to climb walls
* Ability to dodge attacks with a 40 sec cooldown
* A 50 tile dash that deals 400 damage and leaves scythes behind. The scythes infict Evercold and Reactive Flames
* constant jumping
* {{key|Abysscent Guard}} to activate buffs to all damage, crit chance, and defense but reducing your movement speed and increases enemy aggro
