The '''Plagued Fuel Pack''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Plaguebringer]]s. While equipped, Spy damage and projectile velocity are passively increased by 5% and 15% respectively. The player's [[stealth]] also generates 10% faster.

Pressing the {{key|Booster Dash}} key causes the player to dash diagonally upwards in the player's direction at the cost of 25% of the player's stealth, leaving a trail of damaging clouds that inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]]. The dash has a 3 second cooldown before being usable again.
