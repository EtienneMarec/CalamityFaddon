'''Shrines''' are structures found in certain areas of the world, and contain a wide variety of accesories. Most shrines are generated during world creation with the exceptions of the Hallow and Astral shrines which generate as the player enters [[Hardmode]]. The Calamity Mod currently adds 10 types of shrines, each generating within a different biome. These include the [[Surface]]/[[Underground]], [[Cavern]], [[Desert]], [[Snow biome|Snow]], [[Granite Cave]], [[Marble Cave]], [[Corruption]], [[Crimson]], [[Glowing Mushroom biome]], [[Hallow]], [[Astral Infection]], and [[Abyss]]. Each shrine contains a chest which contains a special item depending on the shrine type, alongside normal chest loot.
