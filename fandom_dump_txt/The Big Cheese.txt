|postml=True}}{{NPC}}'''The''' '''Big Cheese''' is a [[NPC]] vendor that sells multiple [[Cheese]] related items, as well as allowing the player to use the [[Culturing]] minigame.

The Big Cheese will move into an available NPC house if the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty house.
*The player has at least 5 [[Deep Brie]] in their inventory.
When threatened by [[enemies]], The Big Cheese will defend Himself with [[Cheese Cutters]].{{inventory

==Culturing==
{{main|Culturing}}When the player selects The Big Cheese's "Culture" option after obtaining any  [[Culturing|Bacteria Samples]], it will open the window for the [[Culturing|Culture Mini-game]]. Culturing is a minigame in Calamity based on growing bacterial cultures, offering many unlockable cheeses that provide various positive and/or negative effects.

==Trivia==
*The Big Cheese's quotes contain many references
**The deaf, Gouda, Brie, Bleus, and Cheese to choose quotes are all references to "shop a pop opera" by [[wikipedia:Jack_Stauber|Jack Stauber]].
**The "I wish I could fly?" and "I'm not dabbing, I'm looking on the ground for cheese!" quotes are references to "[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u_LWodDWPGQ The Quest for Cheese]" by Zach Fuller
*The Culturing minigame is heavily based off of the Garden minigame from [[wikipedia:Cookie_Clicker|Cookie Clicker]]
*The names "Max" and "Alfa" are references to the youtuber Alfaoxtrot
