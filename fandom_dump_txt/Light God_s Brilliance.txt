'''Light God's Brilliance''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]]. When used, it rapidly fires a spray of light beads alongside larger light balls. All of Light God's Brilliance's projectiles emit a dim white light.

The light beads home in on enemies and, upon impact with them or tiles, releases a tiny non-homing shard which settles some distance away. These shards will damage any enemy that touches them and will disappear upon dealing damage or if untouched for long enough.

The light balls travel straight forward at a high speed and explode in a large radius upon impact with tiles or enemies. The light balls do not home, however they and their explosions deal double damage.
