'''Vision of the Tyrant''' (Japanese: 暴君のビジョン ''Bōkun no bijon'') is a large ghastly Epilogue [[Superbosses|Superboss]] that is fought at the end of the [[Challenge Rush]]. It cycles between a variety of different buffed AIs, along with utilizing Boss Rush’s curses for more unique attacks. It drops the [[Rock]] and [[Genesis]] upon death, along with the amount of [[Klepticoin]]s the player should be rewarded with based on their active Curses.

== Spawn ==
Vision of the Tyrant spawns after [[Supreme Calamitas]] in the Boss Rush, or by right clicking [[Terminus]] if it has been encountered already, though it will not drop any items if summoned this way.

In addition to these AI states, Vision of the Tyrant gains different passive abilities based on active Curses:
*The Curse of Blight causes Vision of the Tyrant to regenerate health at a rate of 0.1% per second.
*The Curse of Blundering causes Vision of the Tyrant’s dashes to be 30% quicker.
*The Curse of Karma gives Vision of the Tyrant 5 frames of immunity when hit.
*The Curse of the Tainted causes the Brimstone Monster to fire eight brimstone darts outwards every time Vision of the Tyrant’s AI changes.
*The Curse of Binding causes the player’s speed to reset every third time Vision of the Tyrant’s AI changes.
*The Curse of TIme causes Vision of the Tyrant to instantly kill the player if not killed under X minutes.
*The Curse of Joker causes Vision of the Tyrant to disable a random item in the player’s inventory for 10 seconds every 8 AI changes.
*The Curse of Pacifism causes Vision of the Tyrant to become invincible for the duration of an AI state every 5 AI changes. Its Golem head and Skeletron Prime arms become invincible.
*The Curse of Encumbering causes Vision of the Tyrant to release a cross pattern of deathrays each time it changes AI.

== Resistances ==
*Vision of the Tyrant takes 50% damage from [[Murasama]], [[Nanoblack Reaper]], [[Vermillion Angel]], and [[Apotheosis]].

Also, THE LORDE does not spam projectiles in every direction regardless of AI. While this comparison may lead you to believe that THE LORDE sounds better, Delirium can easily be nuked within seconds in a god run. However, when a comparison is decided based on the fact you can do one faster, i'd say neither side is winning.</ref>. Vision of the Tyrant's sprite seems to be based on the similarity between the two.
