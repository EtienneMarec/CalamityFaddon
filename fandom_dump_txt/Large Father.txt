The '''Large Father''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Drill]] that drops from the [[Wall of Bronze]]. It is the second fastest [[Pickaxe]] in the game, and has a unique attack. Upon right clicking, the player will swing the Large Father in an arc, dealing heavy damage, knockback, and mining a significant area ahead of the player.

== Notes ==
*While the swing attack is useful for destroying a large amount of blocks relatively quickly, its slow swing speed makes this function inefficient for mining tunnels.
*Although primarily a tool, the Large Fathers alt attack can make it a somewhat decent [[Melee weapons|Melee Weapon]].

==Trivia==
*The weapon is a reference to the Big Daddy enemies from the video game Bioshock, which are equipped with large drills on their right hands.
