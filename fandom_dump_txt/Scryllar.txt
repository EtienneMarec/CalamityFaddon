The '''Scryllar''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. It is a ghostly figure that can travel through blocks. It will swoop around the player before moving in to attack. It also has two variations; the normal variant and the rage variant. While they have different stats, they both drop the same loot.

Its stats receive a significant boost and starts dropping [[Bloodstone]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated.

== Trivia ==
*Three Scryllars are depicted in the Underworld's [[Draedon's Logs#Gallery|Draedon's Log]].
*Scryllar is derived from the word "[[wikipedia:Scylla|Scylla]]", which is the name of a legendary aquatic monster in Greek mythology.
*Scryllar's sprite is based on [[wikipedia:Gigan|Gigan]] from the ''[[wikipedia:Godzilla (franchise)|Godzilla]]'' franchise.
