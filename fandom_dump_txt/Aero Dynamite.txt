'''Aero Dynamite''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] consumable [[explosive]]. When thrown, it explodes following a 5-second delay, breaking most nearby blocks, similarly to its predecessor [[Dynamite]]. Unlike other dynamites, the Aero Dynamite is not affected by gravity and smoothly bounces off surfaces. The explosion will also not harm the player.
