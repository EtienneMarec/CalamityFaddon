'''Banana Clown Coverings''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set that drops from [[Banana Clown]]s.

It consists of a '''Banana Clown Mask''', '''Banana Clown Sleeves''', '''Banana Clown Pants''', and '''Banana Clown Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*8 defense
*6 magic damage
*14% increased movement speed
*Consuming [[tgc:Fruit|Fruits]] grants unlimited mana and 10% increased magic damage for 10 seconds with a 1 minute cooldown.
