The '''Unsealed Singularity''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bombs|bomb]] that hurls 5 circular, void-containing flasks. Each flask stops moving after a short time, shattering if enough time has passed or upon any collision with solid tiles or enemies. Once shattered, a black hole is formed at the center of the impact, along with 3 gravity-affected shards which each deal 25% damage. The black hole lasts 5 seconds and pulls in nearby enemies, gaining more strength the more black holes are present at a time. The black hole additionally summons damaging purple squares from roughly 300 blocks away from it which are dragged in and deal 25% damage to any enemies that cross their paths.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Unsealed Singularity throws a single flask which creates a larger, stronger black hole which pulls in purple squares that deal 100% damage and are 5 times larger.
