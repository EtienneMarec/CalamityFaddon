'''Reaper Teeth''' are Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]s obtained by opening [[Front Gar]]s fished up from the [[Sulphurous Sea]] at a 10% chance after [[Polterghast]] has been defeated. They are used to craft the [[Reaper Tooth Necklace]], [[Omega Blue armor]], and several weapons.
