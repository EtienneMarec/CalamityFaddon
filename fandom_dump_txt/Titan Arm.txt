The '''Titan Arm''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that is dropped from [[Atlas]]es after [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated. It has an abnormally high knockback, similar to the [[tgc:Slap Hand|Slap Hand]], and is guaranteed to inflict critical hits. Otherwise, it functions as a normal [[broadsword]].

{{modifier|melee}}

== Tips ==
*This weapon, having the knockback it does, is almost guaranteed to send an enemy off-screen and despawn it, so it is not recommended for fighting for the loot. However, it can be used when exploring, as enemies can get in your way and cause minor annoyance.

== Notes ==
*This weapon can also be obtained from the [[Astral Crate]] after Astrum Aureus has been defeated.

== Trivia ==
*The Titan Arm has the highest base critical strike chance of any weapon in either Calamity or vanilla Terraria.
*Its base damage value of 69 likely alludes to the common internet joke and the fact that the weapon itself is more of a joke weapon.
*With a critical strike chance of 100%, the Titan Arm's DPS can be better expressed as a 138 base damage weapon which is unable to inflict critical hits.
