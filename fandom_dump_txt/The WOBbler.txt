'''The WOBbler''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Hamaxe]] that drops from the [[Wall of Bronze]]. Pressing right click will cause the player to toss out a small glacier. The glacier is affected by gravity, and will slide across the ground, bouncing off of walls and despawning after 6 seconds.

== Notes ==
* The glacier allows the player to get up close to enemies whilst they're frozen, and attack with the WOBblers main attack.

==Trivia==
*The weapon is a reference to the Ice Climbers, specifically their appearance in the Super Smash Bros series.
**The glacier attack is identical to one performed by the Ice Climbers.
**The name WOBbler is a portmanteau of WOB, which is an acronym for Wall of Bronze, and wobble, which is a technique used by the Ice Climbers in Super Smash Bros Melee.
