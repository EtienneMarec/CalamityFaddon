The '''Tranquility Candle''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] piece of [[furniture]] that emits light and grants the Tranquility Candle [[buff]], which reduces enemy spawn rates by 28.57% and maximum spawn count by 60%.

== Trivia ==
*The Tranquility Candle is the exact opposite of the [[Chaos Candle]], which greatly ''increases'' spawn rate.
*Similar to the [[Peace Candle]], the player can receive the effects of the candle while holding it.
*The Tranquility Candle and its counterpart were implemented based on a community suggestion made by Celest on the mod's Discord server.
