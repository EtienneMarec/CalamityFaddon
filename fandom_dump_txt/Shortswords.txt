'''Shortswords''' are a variety of [[swords]] which are stabbed in front of the player. Some shortswords also release a projectile when used, increasing their normally limited range, and many have on-hit effects.

All shortswords, both in Calamity and Vanilla now build up a mechanic called [[Shortstrike]], which allows for shortswords to unleash special attacks when right click is used at full Shortstrike power. More details can be found on its dedicated page.
