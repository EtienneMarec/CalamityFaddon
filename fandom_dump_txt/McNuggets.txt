The '''McNuggets''' is a [[pet]] summoning item that drops from [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. It summons a Son of Yharon that follows the player. If the player stands still for a while, the Son of Yharon will sit near the player.

== Trivia ==
*The pet is based off the old version of the minions spawned by [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]], which were Sons of Yharon.
**This minion was commonly referred to as a "chicken nugget" by the community in reference to its rather rotund stature.
*The pet has a roughly 3.9% chance per second to play an animation where it shakes its head then wags its tail.
*''[[wikipedia:Chicken_McNuggets|McNuggets]]'' are a type of chicken nuggets sold by the international fast food restaurant chain McDonald's. They consist of small pieces of reconstituted boneless chicken meat that have been battered and deep fried.
