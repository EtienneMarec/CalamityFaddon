The '''Bloody Edge''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. It is the [[Crimson]] variant of the [[Night's Edge]] and uses the same crafting recipes. Upon striking enemies, the player has a 50% chance to [[Lifesteal|recover]] 2 to 3 HP.
