'''Rubber Mortar Rounds''' are a Godseeker Mode [[bullet]] type and are a variant of the [[Mortar Round]]. They can bounce up to two times, creating an explosion that destroys tiles on each bounce. They can be used by all guns that do not shoot custom bullets.
