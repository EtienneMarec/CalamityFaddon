The '''1.1.7.51''' update made balance tweaks to a sizable amount of the mod's [[weapons]], along with a few other smaller tweaks.
{{tocright}}
== Additions ==
*Added [[Profaned Energy]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Scorn Eater]]s and [[Ungodly Serpent]]s no longer spawn if the [[Profaned Guardians]] or [[Providence, the Profaned God]] are currently being fought. 
*[[Storm Weaver]] now despawns twice as quickly.
