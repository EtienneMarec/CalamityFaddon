'''Elderstone armor''' is a craftable post-[[Boss Rush]] multiclass [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Rock|rocks]]. The full set requires 3 rocks to be crafted.

It consists of an '''Elderstone Headplate''', '''Elderstone Chestplate''', and '''Elderstone Legplate'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*200 defense
*50% decreased movement speed
*100% increased damage
*Grants the player immunity to knockback
*Creates the Xeroc's Eye that fights for you
