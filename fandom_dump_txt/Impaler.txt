The '''Impaler''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[repeater]] and an upgrade to the [[Stake Launcher]]. It rapidly fires fire [[stake]]s with a 33.33% chance to fire an exploding stake instead. Exploding stakes pierce 7 enemies and cause explosions on hit, while fire stakes pierce 9 enemies and inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]. Instead of having bonus damage against [[Vampire]]s like the [[Stake Launcher]], the Impaler will instantly kill Vampires.
