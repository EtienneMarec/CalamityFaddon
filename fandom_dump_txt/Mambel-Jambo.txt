The '''Mambel-Jambo''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Spy weapons|Spy weapon]] dropped by [[Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch]]. It can travel a modest distance before homing to a targeted enemy. Once it begins homing, one or two of its pieces breaks off and flies into a random direction, dealing 50% of the weapon's base damage. Each pieces that is broken off has a unique effect, as shown here:
*The Iron Sword blade can pierce through multiple enemies and deal a [[Bleeding]] debuff to said enemies.
*The Sickle blade can home into the nearest target and inflicts [[Armor Crunch]].
*The Cobalt Dagger blade can stick to an enemy and deal constant damage to said enemy for some time before disappearing.
*The Gold Pick blade can inflict [[Midas]] and [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] debuff to struck enemy.

Stealth Strike immediately breaks off all the pieces of the weapon and begin homing at the nearest enemy.
