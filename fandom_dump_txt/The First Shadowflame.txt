'''The First Shadowflame''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Goblin Summoner]] during a [[Goblin Invasion]]. It grants all [[minion]]s the ability to inflict the [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]], which prevents health regeneration and deals 15 damage per second, and lasts for 3 seconds.

== Tips ==
*Although it lacks any minion damage boosts, this covering allows minions to inflict its debuff, as opposed to the [[Jelly-Charged Battery]] and [[Nuclear Rod]] which only grant a chance to inflict their debuffs.

== Trivia ==
*This item's name is likely a reference to the [http://darksouls.fandom.com/wiki/First_Flame First Flame] from the ''[[wikipedia:Dark Souls|Dark Souls]]'' series.
*The tooltip mentions the Greek mythological figure [[wikipedia:Prometheus|Prometheus]].
*It is the counterpart to [[The Last Lightice]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} with both being Spy drops from early Hardmode events.
