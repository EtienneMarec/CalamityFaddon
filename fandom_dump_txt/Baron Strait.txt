The '''Baron Strait''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[biome]] that spawns if [[Duke Fishron]] moves past the border of the world. The biome will then instantly generate, taking over the entire [[Ocean]]. The biome servers no functional purpose besides vanity.

The biome is littered with tall [[Tanzanite Glass]] crystals and small pockets of [[Baron Brine]]. The top corner of the biome which intersects with the world border consists of several vertical stripes of blocks.

== Trivia ==
*The [[Music|theme]] heard while in the Baron Strait is '''The End Zone''', which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Ocean Day''' will play instead.
*The biome was concepted following a dream by one of the developers, where the removed [[Old Duke]] boss collided with the right border of the world, creating the Baron Strait.
*The Baron Strait's name is based off of the real life [[wikipedia:Bering Strait|Bering Strait]], though resemblances end there.
*The biome's generation is inspired by the Far Lands from ''[[wikipedia:Minecraft|Minecraft]]''.
**The tile banding on the top corner is based on the Corner Far Lands.
