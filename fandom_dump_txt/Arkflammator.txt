The '''Arkflammator''' is a craftable Epilogue [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It fires purple Scythes infinetely pierce enemies and tiles and create arcs that inflict the [[Noctactricity]] debuff

Note that, unlike other flamethrowers, it lacks a chance to not consume gel meaning the player should make sure they do not run out of ammo at an inopportune time.

It can be considered a counterpart to [[Ifflamator]]
