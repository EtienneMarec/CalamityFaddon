The '''Endogenesis''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[summon weapon]]. It summons an ascended ice construct that has four different modes:
*Endo Cooper will fire five to seven lasers that will infinitely pierce in the direction of nearby enemies.
*Endo Cooper will launch out several homing icicle shards.
*Endo Cooper will aggressively ram back and forth into enemies.
*Endo Cooper will fire endothermic flamethrowers in all directions.

Each mode is toggled by resummoning the Endo Cooper. All attacks except the laser inflict the [[Exo Freeze]], [[Glacial State]], and [[Frostburn]] [[debuffs]]. Each summon requires 10 minion slots to be summoned and only one can be active at once.
