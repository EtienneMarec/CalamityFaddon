'''Provi's Blessing''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player when they feed the [[Profaned Birb]] a Gummy Worm.




== Effects ==

=== On its own ===
* +10% damage increase
* +8 Defense
* +2 Minion slots
* +4 Wing time



=== With [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] equipped ===

[[File:Profaned Soul Artifact.gif|frameless]] ''<span style="color:#FEB10D;">"Resonating with the Profaned Soul Artifact, becoming stronger!'</span>''

''These effects stack with the buff's base stat boosts.''

* +5% damage increase (15% total)
* +8 Defense (+16 total)
* +2 Minion slots (+4 total) *
* Increased health regeneration
<nowiki>*</nowiki>Excluding the increases granted by the Profaned Soul Artifact.




=== With [[Profaned Soul Crystal]] equipped and [[Exo Mechs]]/[[Supreme Calamitas]] both defeated ===

[[File:Profaned Soul Crystal.gif|frameless]] ''<span style="color:#FEB10D;">"Thanks to the Soul Crystal becoming purified, the blessing has become even more powerful!"</span>''

''These effects stack with the buff's base stat boosts.''

* +15% damage increase (25% total damage)
* +4 Minion Slots (+6 total slots) *
* +16 Defense (+24 total) 
* Increased health regeneration

<nowiki>*</nowiki>Excluding the increases granted by the Profaned Soul Crystal. 


== Trivia ==

* If the player has yet to defeat both the [[Exo Mechs]] or [[Supreme Calamitas]], and tries to equip the Profaned Soul Crystal, the tooltip will read <span style="color:#FF0000;">''"The Soul Crystal is too defiled to even remotely affect this blessing..."''</span>
** If only one of the two bosses have been defeated, the tooltip will display <span style="color:#FF0000;">''"The Soul Crystal is still too defiled to empower this blessing..."''</span>

* The buff's name is displayed using the '''{{color|#F7FF00 ●|#F7FF00}}'''
* The buff's icon will shake if the player has either soul item equipped.
