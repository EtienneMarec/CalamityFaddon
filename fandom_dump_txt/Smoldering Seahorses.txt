The '''Smoldering Seahorses''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spell tome]]. It casts several flaming seahorses which home in onto enemies. If no enemies are close by, the seahorses will be effected by gravity, and will fall to the ground and disappear.

== Notes ==
*Smoldering Seahorses can be a useful weapon against more mobile foes such as [[The Slime God]]'s core, as it is one of very few pre-hardmode weapons that are homing.
