The '''Baby Flak Crab''' is a Pre-hardmode [[critter]] found within the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]] after the [[Aquatic Scourge]] has been defeated. Similarly to [[Flak Crab|its adult counterpart]], it disguises itself as a geyser. If the player gets too close to one, it will reveal itself and run away until they are far enough from the player. Baby Flak Crabs can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]], and will rarely drop a few dozen [[Geyser Shell]]s when captured.

== Trivia ==
*The Baby Flak Crab along with the pet version are both inspired by a submission for a sulphurous sea design contest that was held on the Calamity art server. The [https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/404776393117925376/548223155446087691/Toxic_Hermit.gif original sprite], made by TheBlackHand#2549, was based on a more garbage wastes-like vision of the sulphurous sea.
