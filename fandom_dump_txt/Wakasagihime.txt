The '''Wakasagihime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Harpy|Harpies]] after [https://i.redd.it/4foypwzrrgl71.jpg Duke Fishron] has been defeated. Whenever the Wakasagihime contacts a non-[[boss]] enemy, their defense and knockback resistance is reduced. If a hit enemy is knocked into a block, they are dealt damage proportional to their horizontal speed. If killed by this damage, the enemy leaves behind a stationary bubble that deals 500% of the weapon's damage and lasts for 20 seconds. The weapon also has an exceptionally high knockback stat and size.

The Wakasagihime does not require Calamity as a dependency, however using Calamity unlocks more of its features 

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Legendary'''.

==Summons==
When killing enemies in an ocean biome, The Wakasagihime charges up ''Karma''. After killing 10 enemies, a boss will spawn depending on the biome.

===Vanilla===
*Killing an enemy in the [[Ocean]] biome with the Wakasagihime summons Duke Fishron.

===Calamity===
*Killing an enemy in the [[Ocean]] biome with the Wakasagihime summons [[Leviathan and Anahita]] instead.
*Killing an enemy in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] biome with the Wakasagihime summons [[The Old Duke|Old Duke]].

==Notes==
*Wakasagihime deals 75% of its original damage on [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] and [[Empress of Light]].
*Wakasagihime deals 500% of its original damage on [https://i.redd.it/4foypwzrrgl71.jpg Duke Fishron] and 1500% on [[The Old Duke|Old Duke]].
*The Wakasagihime's knockback resistance removal can affect enemies that are immune to knockback.
*The defense and knockback resistance removal doesn't affect enemies with no contact damage or most event minibosses.

==Tips==
*The knockback makes this weapon a good tool to keep an enemy with high health away.

==Trivia==
*The weapon is a reference to a [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Wakasagihime character of the same name] from the ''[[wikipedia:Touhou Project|Touhou Project]]''.
*The drop from Harpies is a reference to how the name [[Siren]] was originally used for harpy-like creatures in Greek mythology.
*The weapon is internally referred to as "FishStick", while its bubbles are "FishBubble".
*The weapon's cost and crafting recipe heavily features ⑨, probably a reference to something.
