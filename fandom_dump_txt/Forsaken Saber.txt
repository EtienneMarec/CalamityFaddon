The '''Forsaken Saber''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. Every swing releases a spread of three whirling blade projectiles, which remains in place for about 1 second after launch and then rapidly moves in the direction of the cursor, damaging the first enemy hit. The sand blade deals 80% of the sword's damage.
