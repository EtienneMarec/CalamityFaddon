Many new enemies are added to the '''Martian Madness''' event, which spawn after defeating [[The Starborn Moth]]. These new enemies can drop [[Strange Matter]], which are used to craft a variety of equipment.

Unlike the pre-[[The Starborn Moth|Starborn Moth]] Martian Invasion, the buffed invasion consists of 12 waves.

== Notes ==
*During the Event, a minor red filter will be applied to the background like during [[Blood Moon]]s, making the sky a slight purple-blue color.
*A maximum of 10 enemies can be active at once during this event.
**Only one [[miniboss]] can be active at once and a miniboss can only spawn outside of its wave if there are less than three enemies active.
*During Boss waves, other enemies will stop attacking the player and will flee. 
*Enemies that can spawn in the pre-[[The Starborn Moth|Starborn Moth]] Invasion will have their health and damage buffed.
**[[Martian Saucer]]s have their total health increased to {{dv|47,250|94,500}} and all damage increased by {{dv|30|boost=0.25}}.
**[[Cinderlamitas]] has its health increased to {{dv|60,000|120,000}} and its damage increased to {{dv|75|boost=0.125}}.
**All other enemies have their health increased by 1.5x and all damage increased by {{dv|10|20}}.
***[[Tesla Turret]]s are excluded from this damage increase.
