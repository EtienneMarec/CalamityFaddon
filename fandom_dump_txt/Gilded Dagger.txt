The '''Gilded Dagger''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] non-consumable [[dagger]]. It throws a fast moving golden dagger that ricochets towards the nearest target after hitting an enemy. The dagger pierces once and breaks on contact with blocks.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Gilded Dagger will cause the next dagger thrown to pierce three times and home in on enemies after ricocheting, as well as granting it 20% increased damage per ricochet.
