The '''Onyx Minigun''' is a craftable Epilogue [[gun]] that is the direct upgrade of the [[Onyxia]] and the true final upgrade to the [[Onyx Blaster]]. It rapidly fires bursts of eight bullets at a time alongside an onyx blast that deals 170% the damage of the fired [[bullets]]. The bullets from the Onyx Minigun are fired in 4 symmetric pairs surrounding the onyx blast. It has a 70% chance to not consume ammo when fired.
