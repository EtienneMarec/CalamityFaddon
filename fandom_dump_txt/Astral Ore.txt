'''Astral Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ore]] which generates in the world after defeating the [[Wall of Flesh]] or [[Astrum Aureus]], converting many blocks around it into the [[Astral Infection]]. A [[Picksaw]] or a pickaxe of a higher power can mine it, but only after [[The General]] {{fantag|Apotheosis and Friends}} has been spoken to. Additionally, after Astrum Deus' defeat, [[Astral Slime]]s will begin to drop Astral Ore.

Similarly to how [[Hellstone]] and [[Meteorite]] inflict [[Burning]], Astral Ore inflicts the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]] on contact. Up to 60-70 bars can be mined from one astral meteorite. {{fantag|Catalyst}} Mining 20 blocks of Astral Ore will cause the player's [[Stamina Meter]] to deplete by 1 point.

Astral Ore is also used in order to obtain [[tgc:Shimmer|Shimmer]], via a special ritual performed in the [[The Aether]]. The player must create a 2x3 outline made out of [[Astral Ore]], and place a bucket of water in the center. This will cause the Astral Ore and water to disappear, leaving behind a small puddle of shimmer. This ritual will not work if the world is still in pre-hardmode.
