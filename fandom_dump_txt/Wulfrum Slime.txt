The '''Wulfrum Slime''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[slime]]. It spawns in a semi-transparent stealth mode, and won't move until approached by a player, which will cause it to activate, and gain a shield. It is immune to knockback.

It can be supercharged by the [[Wulfrum Pylon]] enemy, causing its lights to turn from green to blue, and causing its jumps to end in a small explosion.

== Trivia ==
*It had previously been removed from the mod during the update which added several [[Wulfrum Enemies]], but was added back into the mod in its reworked form later.
**At the time of its removal, it did not spawn naturally, the only way of it existing in a world was through manual spawn options from other mods. (e.g. Cheat Sheet)
