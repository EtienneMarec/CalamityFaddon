'''Sinks''' are pieces of [[furniture]] that also function as minor crafting stations. The Calamity Mod currently adds sixteen types of Sinks to the game, each corresponding to specific [[Furniture sets|sets of furniture]]. Ashen, Ancient and Profaned Sinks count as a source of lava, Botanic Sinks count as a source of honey, and Exo Sinks count as water, lava and honey; the other sinks count as a source of water.
