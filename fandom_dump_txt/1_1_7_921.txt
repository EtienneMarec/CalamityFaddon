The '''1.1.7.921''' update added the [[Phantoplasm]] [[crafting material]] to the mod, dropped by a new enemy know as the [[Phantom Spirit]]. It added a few new items using the material as an ingredient, as well as incorporating it into various existing recipes. The update also resprited the [[Cosmilite]] weapon set.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Phantom Spirit]].
*Added [[Blood Orb]] and [[Phantoplasm]].
*Added [[Fabstaff]] and [[T1000]].
*Added [[Bloodflare Body Armor]], [[Bloodflare Cuisses]], and [[Bloodflare Mask]].
*Added [[Omega Healing Potion]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*Enemies in the [[Dungeon]] after [[Providence, the Profaned God]] has been defeated now have buffed stats and have a {{dv|10%|16.67%}} chance to spawn a Phantom Spirit on death.
*[[Revengeance Mode]] [[The Hive Mind|Hive Mind]]'s first phase now spawns [[Hive Blob]]s every 5 seconds instead of 7.
*Defeating Providence, the Profaned God for the first time will now broadcast a status message to chat informing the player of new enemies dropping Bloodstone.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**[[Soul Seeker]]s are now twice as likely to fire Brimstone Barrage shots every second.
**Brothers now occasionally fire their projectiles directly downwards.
**Now drops [[Vehemence]] in Revengeance Mode.
*Revengeance Mode [[Wall of Flesh]] no longer drops Vehemence.
