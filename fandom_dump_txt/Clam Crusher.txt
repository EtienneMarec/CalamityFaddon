The '''Clam Crusher''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[flail]] that is dropped by the [[Giant Clam]] [[mini boss]] in the [[Sunken Sea]]. It throws out a giant gravity-affected clam that will inflict the [[Eutrophication]] [[debuff]] onto [[enemies]]. The Clam will deal 4x as much damage if it is first fired into the air, then falls and lands onto an enemy. Once the clam hits a block or an enemy, it will retract back to the player.
