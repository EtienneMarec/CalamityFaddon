The '''Quiver of Madness''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is an upgrade to the [[Elemental Quiver]]. It increases all ranged damage by 30% and critical strike chance by 25%, and provides all effects the Deadshot Brooch provides; 100% reduced ammo usage, 15 [[defense]], +2 life regen, and 35% increased pick speed. The covering also summons four brimstone portals to orbit the player. When a ranged projectile intersects with one, the projectile gains triple damage and gains a drastic homing ability. Skull targets will also appear on nearby enemies (with bosses prioritized) which can be shot at to deal 500% damage.
