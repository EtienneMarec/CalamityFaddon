The '''Elemental Ray''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Terra Ray]]. It fires four unique beams behind the player, all aiming towards the mouse cursor, each of which behave differently:
*{{color|Solar|#fE9E23}} beams explode on every enemies hit.
*{{color|Nebula|#FE7EE5}} beams initially aim towards the mouse, but they immediately sweep in a short clockwise arc before disappearing.
*{{color|Vortex|#00F2AA}} beams resemble lightning bolts. They do not pierce and are slightly inaccurate, but they inflict the [[Electrified]] debuff.
*{{color|Stardust|#00AEEE}} beams spawn 2 slow, slightly homing Stardust Bolts upon being fired, and have a chance to spawn another 4 every time it hits an enemy.
