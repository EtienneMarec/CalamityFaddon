}}The '''Voidseer Mole''' is a friendly NPC that spawns at the Void layer of [[Abyss|The Abyss]]. It is invulnerable to all damage, unless the damage comes from a hammer-like weapon, listed below. Right clicking the Mole will cause it open an NPC menu with a quote, however it does not provide any services unlike other NPCs. On death, it will drop the [[Void Biscuit]], and not spawn again.

== Damage Sources ==

* Any tool that can destroy walls
* [[Fallen Paladin's Hammer]]
* [[Pwnagehammer]]
* [[Triactis' True Paladinian Mage-Hammer of Might]]
* Paladin's Hammer
