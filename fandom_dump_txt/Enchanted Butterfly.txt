The '''Enchanted Butterfly''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[light pet]] summoning item. It summons a tiny dragonfly pet to follow the player and provide a small amount of light. It will actively fly towards [[butterflies]] to consume them. It additionally will fly towards nearby items, which will be placed in the player's inventory. When the player is below 75 percent health, the dragonfly turns blue, and when the player is below 50 percent health, the dragonfly turns green.
