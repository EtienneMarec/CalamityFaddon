The '''Magna Core''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]], which is an upgrade to the [[Ethereal Talisman]] and the final upgrade to the [[Sorcerer Emblem]]. It provides a 30% increase to magic damage, a 25% increase to magic critical strike chance, a 100% decrease in mana usage, +250 max mana, and increases pickup range for mana stars. When any [[Mana Potion]] is used, a burst of 8 large granite energy spheres will launch outwards from the player and violently home in on enemies. The energy spheres deal 20,000 magic damage and pierce 5 times each. This ability has a cooldown of 20 seconds.
