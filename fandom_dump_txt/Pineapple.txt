The '''Pineapple''' is a [[pet]] summoning item that drops from [[Goldfish]] with a 0.2% drop rate. It summons a spinning pineapple that hovers over the player.

== Trivia ==
*The buff tooltip is a reference to a popular debate on whether or not pineapple is a suitable topping for pizza.
*The [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|pineapple]] is a tropical fruit native to South America. Pineapple plants in real life do not grow as trees, but instead as small shrubs.
