{{dablink|For defense mechanics, refer to [[tgc:Defense|Defense]] on the Terraria Wiki}}

'''Defense''' is a player statistic that determines how much damage can be taken per hit. The damage the player takes is reduced by {{dv|half|three quarters}} of their defense value. Defense can be increased by equipping [[Covering]] and [[coverings]], or temporarily boosted by various [[buffs]].

Some forms of damage are impossible to reduce through defense. Damage from health-draining [[debuffs]] like [[Plague]] or drowning in water are not reduced by defense. Damage taken from lava, however, can be reduced through defense.

== Damage Reduction ==
{{dablink|Main Article: [[Damage Reduction]]}}
Damage Reduction (DR) is a stat alongside defense that decreases damage that both the player and other NPCs take based on a percentage. The player can boost their damage reduction with certain [[coverings]], although it scales diminishingly as more damage reduction is gained.

== Armor Penetration ==
Armor penetration is a statistic that reduces the defense of the player's targets. For instance, a [[tgc:Purple Slime|Purple Slime]] normally has 6 defense. If the player has 5 Armor penetration and attacks the slime, then the slime will take damage as if it only had 1 defense instead.

== [[Vanilla changes]] ==
Defensive covering [[modifier]]s now also increase damage reduction and gain more defense in Hardmode and Post-Moon Lord, with rarer defense modifiers granting a larger bonus.
*Hard provides 0.25% damage reduction; and increases 2 defense in Hardmode and 3 defense in Post-Moon Lord instead of 1.
*Guarding provides 0.5% damage reduction; and increases 3 defense in Hardmode and 4 defense in Post-Moon Lord instead of 2.
*Armored provides 0.75% damage reduction; and increases 4 defense in Hardmode and 6 defense in Post-Moon Lord instead of 3.
*Warding provides 1% damage reduction; and increases 6 defense in Hardmode and 8 defense in Post-Moon Lord instead of 4.

Several items had their defense values changed.

*A full set of [[Gladiator armor]] now provides 21 defense instead of 7.
*A full set of [[Obsidian armor]] now provides 19 defense instead of 16.
*The [[Hallowed armor|Hallowed Plate Mail]] now provides 18 defense instead of 15 and the Hallowed Greaves now provide 13 defense instead of 11.
*A full set of [[Solar Flare armor]] now provides 94 defense instead of 78.
