The '''Mirage Mirror''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]]. When equipped the player can build [[Spy stealth]] 30% faster while standing still and 20% faster while moving. Enemy [[aggro]] is also reduced by 200, as long as the player is not within the [[Abyss]] biome.
