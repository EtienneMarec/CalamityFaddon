The '''Phlegm''' is a  [[Hardmode]] [[javelin]] dropped by the [[Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon firing, it creates a trail of phlegm which inflicts [[Plague]] and [[Slow]] on all enemies and players underneath it.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Phlegm will cause the player to sneeze.
