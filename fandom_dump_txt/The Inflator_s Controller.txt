'''The Inflator's Controller''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. When held out, The Bloonarius will spawn above the player and hover at that position. When holding {{Key|use}}, it begins charging up a spewing attack, storing 30 bloons in the process. At full charge (indicated by its shaking), when released, will spew out those stored Bloons. These bloons are as follows:
*The Ceramics deals 65% of the weapon's damage, and upon impact, splits into various colored bloons that deals 50% of the weapons damage.
*The MOABs deals 125% of the weapon's damage, and splits into 4 ceramics upon impact.
*The BFBs deals 200% of the weapon's damage, and homes into an enemy, splitting into 2 MOABs on impact.
*The ZOMGs deals 150% of the weapon's damage, and explodes into a plagued clouds, dealing 80% of the weapon's damage, and inflicting the [[Plague]] and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] Debuff. Additionally, it fires out 2 BFBs after exploding.
*The DDTs deals the same damage and split into 4 ceramics as the MOABs, can home into an enemy like the BFBs, and can increase minion damage against struck target by 45.
*The BADs deals 300% of the weapon's damage, and explodes into various shrapnels, which lingers and deals 90% of the weapon's damage. It additionally splits into 2 ZOMGs and 3 DDTs upon impact.

The bloons' chance to be spewed out are determined by the player's current amount of life and has a limit to how many they can be around. They have a set chance of replacing a ceramic bloons, as it follows:
