'''Extreme Gravity''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by certain [[bosses]] that disables both infinite flight and the player's wing time, except for the flight granted by [[Bee Wings]]

== Notes ==
It is adviced to bring Bee Wings to most bossfights, as they are not affected by this debuff.
