The '''Viruling''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Jungle]] surface during the day after [[Golem]] has been defeated. It hovers through the air towards the player, and inflicts the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] on contact.

== Tips ==
*The Viruling cannot fire any projectiles, so any non-melee attacks are useful for killing it.

== Trivia ==
*It appears to be a [[Derpling]] afflicted with the plague, although its behavior has changed as a result.
**The Plague also seems to have made it much more machine-like.
