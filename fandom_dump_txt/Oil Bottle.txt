{{dedicated|Kenadrice|Malice Mode No-Hit}}The '''Oil Bottle''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a chance to be dropped by most enemies with the [[List of NPCs by Hit Sound|NPCHit4 hit sound]]. It summons Cyn to protect you, which repels all nearby non-boss enemies by using her absolute translate ability.
