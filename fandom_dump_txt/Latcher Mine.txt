'''Latcher Mines''' are [[Hardmode]] consumable [[Spy]] [[bombs]] that are sold by the [[Bandit]] for {{sc|3}} each after defeating one of the [[Mechanical Bosses]]. They automatically throw tiny flat mines that stick to enemies and detonate 3 seconds after latching onto them. Upon detonation or contact with blocks, a burst of 6 gravity-affected flame projectiles are flung up in the air which deals damage and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Latcher Mines will cause the next mine thrown to be able to stick to the ground and explode into shrapnels. The mine thrown on the ground lasts 14 seconds and can stick onto enemies contacting it.
