The '''Life Jelly''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Pink Jellyfish]] and [[Mirage Jelly]]. It gives the player 20 more HP and boosts life regeneration by +4 while standing still. It can be combined with [[Mana Jelly]] and [[Vital Jelly]] in order to craft the [[Grand Gelatin]].
