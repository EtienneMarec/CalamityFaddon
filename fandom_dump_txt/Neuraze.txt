The '''Neuraze''' is a Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]] dropped by [[XP-00 Hypnos]]. When used it starts charging up energy. Upon being released, it shoots a laser beam, with size and damage scaling with the amount of time charged, up to a full minute. A fully charged laser beam deals approximately 2000x damage, with the beam itself reaching approximately 10 blocks in width.
