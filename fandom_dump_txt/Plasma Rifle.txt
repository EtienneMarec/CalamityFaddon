The '''Plasma Rifle''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]]. Using the left mouse button fires an explosive plasma blast that creates toxic clouds upon contact with an enemy or a block, while using the right mouse button rapidly fires plasma-like bullets.
