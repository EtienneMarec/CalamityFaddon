The '''Ravenous Soul Artifact''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. When worn, [[aggro]] is boosted by 500%. It allows the player to perform a homing dash with a short cooldown, then bounce off enemies dealing 500 damage. Dashing into projectiles reduces damage taken by 90%.
