The '''Hadal Stew''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[food item]] that can also be crafted and heals the player for 120 life and restores 150 mana, and also grants the [[Well Fed]] [[buff]] for 60 minutes. It applies a shortened version of the [[Potion Sickness]] [[debuff]] upon use, allowing the player to heal more often.

It has a chance to be obtained from [[Abyssal Treasure]]s, which drop from [[pot]]s in the [[Abyss]].
