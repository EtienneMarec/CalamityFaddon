The 'Gloomy Trident' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spears]] that autoswings. On use, it performs a 3-hit combo, first swinging at an upward arc, then again on a downward arc, then finally being thrusted. The first 2 swings fires out a volley of black pearls that inflicts the [[Crush Depth]] debuff. Upon thrusting, it casts out a Maelstrom that lingers for a bit, casting down lightning bolts on its path.

In [[Revengeance Mode]], Rage builds up slowly as you hold the weapon. Hitting the {{Key|Rage Mode}} key conjures a mirage of the Sovereign of the Duskgloom Sea, leaving behind a far stronger maelstrom that sucks up any enemies and inflicts the 'Terror of the Abyss' debuff to all enemies on-screen, reducing their DR by 50%, Damage by 30%, and Defense by 60, along with casting greater amount of lightning bolts and dealing up to 2x the weapon's base damage, all while setting the player's rage to 0. The size of the mirage, and therefore, the power of said maelstrom, is proportional to the amount of rage the player had upon activation, and is also affected by melee bonus. This effect has a 60 second cooldown. If the player stops holding the weapon, their Rage is set to 0 and its mechanics returns to normal functionality.
