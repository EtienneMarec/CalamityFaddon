The '''Twin Cranes''' are a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spell tomes]] that, upon being held, conjures 2 photo-negative squares between the player. Upon using it, it fires out 4 monochrome rays that diverges from each other in a spread. At the same time, the squares fires out both the circle and diamond bullets to the direction of the cursor. The circle bullets will be fired in 2 volleys, travelling at a straight line and dealing 75% of the weapon's base damage. Meanwhile, the diamond bullets will move in a wavy pattern, are fired out in a group of 5 in 4 volleys, and will deal 45% of the weapon's damage.

{{Key|open}} will create a barrier that covers the user, granting them 15 defense and increasing their damages dealt by 25%. Additionally, upon getting hit by a projectile, it will reflect them, making them deal 1.5x more damage than usual. However, this barrier can not regenerate, and once broken, all its effect will be taken away, and the player must wait 2 minutes before they can conjure another one.

== Notes ==
*This item could be considered a more offensive, weaponized version of [[The Sponge]], trading half the defense, 10% Damage reduction, and its ability to regenerate while active for 25% more damage dealt, and gaining the ability to reflect enemy projectiles.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is a references to the spell card "「番いの鶴」"The Twin Cranes", used by [https://lenen.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Tenkai_Zuifeng Tenkai Zuifeng] from the [https://lenen.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Len%27en_Wiki Len'en Project]. Its design is based on the fact that they are sometimes likened to that of a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yubari_King Yubari King] melon. In turn, its design also vaguely references [https://lenen.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Tsurubami_Senri Tsurubami Senri] from the same franchise.
**Its tooltip reflects this, as Tenkai was a former right-hand man of Tsurubami, who both ruled [https://lenen.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Mugenri Mugenri] with an iron fist, until Tsurubami decided to leave for the outside world.
**Its unique way of attacking, along with its barrier effect also reflects their ability to manipulate barriers, being used in various ways.
