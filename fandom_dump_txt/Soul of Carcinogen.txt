The '''Soul of Carcinogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Carcinogen]].

When equipped, the player will have a cigarette in their mouth.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.

*Thank you... it's tragic to see what has become of the Asbestos Archmage. They were gifted with such excellent control over earth and stone, and were completely unscathed against fire of any kind. Not even I knew their power would eventually embody disease and poison. If only I had seen them in a fight against [[Calamitas]]!

After this, Permafrost will occasionally smoke.
