'''Armageddon Wings''' are a Godseeker Mode [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allow the player to fly and glide and are also an upgraded version of the [[Static Wings]]. It grants 15% increased movement speed and increases damage and critical strike chance by 7% and the player gains 2 minion slots while a full set of [[Horseslime armor]] is equipped.
