The '''1.1.4.44''' update added [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], a variety of new [[weapons]] and items, and put buffs on bosses as the game progressed.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Calamity Eye]], [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Plaguebringer Shade]], and [[Plague Charger]].
*Added [[Hellcaster Fragment]], [[Plague Cell Cluster]], [[Shadow Essence]], [[Shadowspec Bar]], and [[Uelibloom Ore]].
*Added melee variants for [[Eradicator]], [[Flame Scythe]], [[Mangrove Chakram]], [[Royal Knives]], [[Seashell Boomerang]], and [[True Paladin's Hammer]].
*Added [[Aeries]], [[Animus]], [[Barracuda Gun]], [[Devastation]], [[The Hive]], [[Impaler]], [[Malevolence]], [[Mephetic Sprayer]], [[Pestilent Defiler]], [[Phoenix Blade]], and [[Virulent Katana]].
*Added [[Infinity Boots]] and [[Toxic Heart]].
*Added [[Demonshade Breastplate]], [[Demonshade Helm]], and [[Demonshade Greaves]].
*Added [[Abomination]].
*Added [[Plague]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*All bosses will now gain boosted stats once the world reaches Hardmode and when the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
**[[Desert Scourge]] gains a 600% health boost and a 200% damage boost in Hardmode, and an additional 300% health boost and 200% damage boost upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***Head is set from 0 defense to 10 defense in Hardmode and 25 upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***Body is set from 8 defense to 20 defense in Hardmode and 45 upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***Tail is set from 18 defense to 40 defense in Hardmode and 75 upon defeating the Moon Lord.
**Both forms of the [[Slime God]] gain a 300% health boost and a 200% damage boost in Hardmode, with an additional 300% health boost and 200% damage boost upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***Phase 1's defense is set from 10 to 25 in Hardmode, and 55 once the Moon Lord has been defeated.
***Phase 2's Defense is set from 25 to 35 in Hardmode, and 65 once the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**[[The Devourer of Gods]] gains a 200% health boost and a 200% damage boost once the Moon Lord has been defeated.
***Head is set from 10 defense to 25.
***Body is set from 35 defense to 55.
***Tail is set from 75 defense to 125.
**[[Calamitas]] phase 1 gains a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and is set from 25 defense to 55 upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***[[Calamitas]] phase 2 gains a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and is set from 35 defense to 50.
***[[Cataclysm]] gains a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and is set from 35 defense to 55.
***[[Catastrophe]] gains a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and is set from 20 defense to 35.
**[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] gains a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and is set from 45 defense to 65 upon defeating the Moon Lord.
***[[Plaguebringer Shade]]s gain a 300% health boost, a 200% damage boost, and are set from 30 defense to 35.
**All pre-Hardmode vanilla bosses, [[Creeper]]s, and [[Skeletron]]'s hands gain a 300% health boost and a 200% damage boost in Hardmode, with an additional 300% health boost and 200% damage boost upon defeating the Moon Lord.
**All other vanilla bosses, [[Skeletron Prime]]'s arms, and [[Golem]]'s fists gain a 300% health boost and a 200% damage boost upon defeating the Moon Lord.
*[[Slime God]] changes:
**Increased hitbox size.
**Given a variant of the mine projectile which will linger in place.
**Now uses custom dust instead of [[King Slime]]'s.
**Can now spawn during a Hardmode [[Slime Rain]].
**Now drops [[Healing Potion]]s instead of [[Lesser Healing Potion]]s.
**[[Crimson Slime Spawn]]s can now drop [[Nazar]].
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Now more resistant to explosive and piercing projectiles.
**Can now spawn while the player is near the [[Nebula Pillar]], and will drop [[Meld Blob]]s if spawned this way.
**Now drops [[Greater Healing Potion]]s instead of Lesser Healing Potions.
*[[Desert Scourge]] changes:
**Now more resistant to explosive and piercing projectiles.
**Can now spawn in the [[Desert]] once Skeletron has been defeated.
*[[Calamitas]] changes:
**Can now spawn during a [[Blood Moon]] or [[Solar Eclipse]] after Golem has been defeated.
**Now uses "[[Music#Boss 2 Remix|Boss 2 Remix]]" by [https://www.youtube.com/user/silentzorah silentzorah] as its theme.
**Now drops Greater Healing Potions instead of Lesser Healing Potions.
*[[Solar Pillar]] enemies now drop Hellcaster Fragments.
*Post-Plantera [[Dungeon]] enemies now drop Shadow Essences.

=== Items ===
*All Calamity armors have had their damage, movement speed, and defense boosts decreased.
*[[Calamity Ring]] now has a chance to rain down brimstone fireballs from the sky when the player is hurt.
*[[Mana Overloader]] now heals the player when their mana is low.
*[[Terratomere]]'s projectiles now have greater homing capabilities.
*[[Nebulous Core]] now spawns homing nebula balls when equipped.
*[[Ancient Fossil]] can now be crafted with [[Slush Block]]s.
*[[Royal Knives]] now require 10 [[Luminite Bar]]s and 10 [[Hellcaster Fragment]]s to craft.
*[[Galactica Blade]] now only requires 5 Luminite Bars to craft.
*Nerfed [[Calamitas' Inferno]]'s damage from 90 to 80.
*Nerfed [[Overloaded Blaster]]'s damage from 30 to 22.
*Nerfed [[Shredder]]'s damage from 25 to 15.
*Nerfed [[Forbidden Sun]]'s damage. <!---How much?-->

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Abyssal Warhammer]], [[Darklight Greatsword]], [[Shardlight Pickaxe]], and [[Starnight Lance]].
*Resprited [[Seabound Staff]]'s Brittle Star minion.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where projectiles would gain ludicrous speed when they started homing onto an enemy.
*[[Aerospec armor]] set bonus tooltip spelling error fixed.
*[[Gamma Fusillade]]'s projectiles no longer have a chance to stop midair.
*Fixed a bug with [[Verium Bullet]]s.
*Slight fixes to [[Staff of the Mechworm]].
