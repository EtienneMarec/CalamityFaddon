The '''1.1.6.16''' update added several new features to the [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] fight, and added a few new [[items]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Abyss Blade]], [[Apoctosis Array]], [[The Burning Sky]], [[Depth Blade]], [[Pandemic]], and [[Terra Lance]]. 
*Added [[lol]].
*Added [[Crush Depth]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Buffed [[The Hive Mind]] phase 1’s Vile Clot damage after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated from {{expert|100}} to {{expert|104}}.
*[[Earth Elemental]] changes:
**Nerfed its health from {{dv|5000|6000}} to {{dv|4000|4800}}, and its damage from {{dv|55|71}} to {{dv|50|65}}.
**Decreased its spawnrate.
**Only one can spawn at a time.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] changes:
**Buffed health from {{dv|600,000|720,000}} to {{dv|800,000|960,000}}, and his defense from 68 to 130.
**Buffed flarenado damage from {{dv|330|400}} to {{dv|400|480}}, his infernado damage from {{dv|500|600}} to {{dv|760|960}}, and his Flare Explosion damage from {{dv|180|200}} to {{dv|260|320}}.
**Now transitions to phases at higher amounts of health.
**Now becomes invincible during phase transitions.
**Flarenadoes last for 6.67 seconds instead of 12.
**Added a new phase where he teleports near the player and becomes invisible, then becomes visible and charges at player.
**Now creates a burst of high damaging fireballs when defeated.
**Now gains different boosts based on the damage type of the last projectile to hit him. 
*Decreased [[Pitbull]]'s spawnrate.

=== [[Items]] ===
*Nerfed [[Bladecrest Oathsword]]’s damage from 36 to 32.
*Buffed [[Darklight Greatsword]]’s use time from 28 to 23.
*[[Deathwind]] now shoots 4-8 lasers instead of 2-4 per shot.
*Buffed [[Devastation]]’s damage from 90 to 120, and its projectiles now deal 250 damage instead of the weapon’s damage.
*[[Elemental Lance]] now uses Terra Lance in its recipe instead of [[Starnight Lance]], [[Hellion Flower Spear]], and [[Exsanguination Lance]].
*[[Excelsus]] now uses 1 [[Influx Waver]], 5 [[Armored Shell]]s, and 6 [[Luminite Bar]]s in its recipe instead of 9 Armored Shells and 8 Luminite Bars, it now also shoots an Influx Beam on swing.
*Exsanguination Lance’s beam no longer inflicts [[Bleeding]]. <!---10 seconds--->
*Buffed [[Flarewing Bow]]’s damage from 24 to 26.
*Buffed [[Galactus Blade]]’s shot speed from 18 to 23.
*Nerfed [[Gelitic Blade]]’s damage from 38 to 36, and its Gel Wave projectile now pierces 3 times instead of 5.
*Buffed [[Hellfire Flamberge]]’s damage from 70 to 79, its use time from 31 to 26, and its knockback from 5.5 to 7.5.
*Buffed [[Ichor Spear]]’s use time from 25 to 19.
*[[Ion Blaster]]’s projectiles now fade quicker.
*Buffed [[Magna Striker]]’s damage from 38 to 43.
*Buffed [[Red Sun]]’s damage from 275 to 375.
*Changed [[Scorpio]]’s tooltip from “Fires rapid-fire mini rockets” to “Rockets”.
*Buffed [[Storm Ruler]]’s use time from 29 to 24.
*Buffed [[Trinity]]’s damage from 50 to 57, its use time from 29 to 24, and it now shoots a spread of 3 bolts instead of 1.
*Nerfed [[Vortexpopper]]’s damage from 50 to 40.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited Excelsus and [[Spectralstorm Cannon]].
