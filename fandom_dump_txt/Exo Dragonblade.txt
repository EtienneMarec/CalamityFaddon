The '''Exo Dragonblade''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Melee weapons|melee weapon]] dropped by [[XB-10 Nortazzo]]. It uses a custom swing animation and performs a three-swing combo of an upward slash followed by a larger downward slash, and then following up with a thrust that deals 150% of the weapon's damage and causes the player to dash towards the blade, gaining a bright flaming aura that upon contact with an enemy, creates a large explosion granting the player a sixth of a second worth of invincibility frames, and dealing 120% of the weapon's damage.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Legendary'''.

* The draconic phoenix mentioned in the item's tooltip is [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon, Dragon of Rebirth]].
