'''Astral Bars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bars]] made with 2 [[Astral Ore]] and 3 [[Stardust]] per bar. They are primarily used to create [[Astral Infection|Astral-themed]] gear and are additionally used to craft upgrades to weapons, such as the [[Radiant Star]]. As well as being used to craft weapons, Astral Bars are used to craft the [[Ethereal Core]], a [[consumable]] that can permanently raise the player's maximum [[mana]].
