The '''Terra Flameburster''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It fires a long-range stream of green flames that pierces through infinite enemies but can not go through blocks. The flames inflicts the [[On Fire!]] and [[Cursed Inferno]] [[debuffs]] to enemies hit by them.
