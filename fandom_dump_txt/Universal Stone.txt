The '''Universal Stone''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. Equipping it summons a Cosmic Conflict Construct to fight for you, which deals summon damage. The covering passively summons a spiky cosmic shield which inflicts [[God Slayer Inferno]] onto enemies caught within it. When the item's visibility is toggled on, consuming [[Mana Potion]]s inflicts [[Mana Burn]] which hurts the player instead of reducing their magic damage. Additionally, the player gains immunity to God Slayer Inferno, and any [[Cosmichid]] plants collected will temporarily boost the player's damage by 1%, capping at 30%. On top of all of these effects, the player also gains 50% increased flight time.

The construct cycles between the following attacks:
*Dashing at enemies while trailed by homing crystal bolts.
*Summoning a ring of cosmic crystals around enemies which then shoot outwards and start homing.
*Dashing horizontally over enemies while summoning Cosmichid spikes which grow upwards.
*Shooting slow large balls of cosmic matter using its arm.
