The '''Astral Pickaxe''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[pickaxe]] capable of mining blocks up to {{item|Lihzahrd Brick}} and {{item|Scoria Ore}}. It has a range bonus of 3 tiles and has a higher than average critical strike chance. It releases light emitting particles when swung, and inflicts the [[Astral Infection]] [[debuff]] onto enemies upon contact.
