'''Security Chests''' are [[chests]] found only in the [[Arsenal Lab]]s that contain rare and unique items. Each Security Chest will contain two or more unique items depending on the type of lab, along with other standard loot.

== Loot ==

=== Standard Laboratory Loot ===
The following set of loot can be found in all Abandoned Workshops and Bio-center Labs. Primary items can only be found in one chest per Bio-center Lab, despite each labs containing two chests.

If the [http://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/the-thorium-mod.40788/ Thorium Mod] is enabled, {{item|Marine Kelp Planter Box|es}} can also be found alongside other Planter Boxes.
