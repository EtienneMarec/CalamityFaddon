The '''Night's Gaze''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[javelin]]. It throws a rapid stratus spear that breaks upon hitting a tile and explodes upon striking an enemy, releasing 4 stratus stars that inflict [[Moon Burnt]]. These stars can pierce for a short amount of time and the explosion from the spear will not be triggered if the spear doesn't strike an enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Night's Gaze will summon stars that travel along parallel to the trajectory of the spear and will home in if an enemy is near.
