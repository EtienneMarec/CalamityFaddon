The '''Dread Bone''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[tgc:Blood Zombie|Blood Zombies]] after defeating [[tgc:Eater of Worlds|Eater of Worlds]] or [[tgc:Brain of Cthulhu|Brain of Cthulhu]]. While equipped, it increases the player's summon damage by 10%. Additionally, it grants the player's [[minions]] the ability to critically hit enemies, dealing 300% of the attack's damage. However, after the minion critically hits or attacks 5 times, it will violently explodes into red blood and gores used by Blood Zombies. The minion will reappear after 3 seconds have passed.
