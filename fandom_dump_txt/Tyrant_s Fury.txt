'''Tyrant's Fury''' is a [[buff]] that has a 50% chance of being applied when the player strikes an [[enemy]] with the [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]] or the [[True Tyrant's Ultisword]]. It grants the player 30% increased melee damage and 10% increased melee critical strike chance.
