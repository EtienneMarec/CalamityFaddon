'''The Codseeker''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[fishing pole]]. When swung, a corpse of [[Yharim (disambiguation)|Yharim]] is deployed as the fishing pole's bobber. It can be used to fish in [[Lava]]. Fishing lines will only have a 1 in 222 chance of snapping with each cast, and when it does, The Codseeker converts itself back into a [[tgc:Fishing_poles#Types|Wood Fishing Pole]] followed by a shrill scream similar to the one heard when [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Ravager Ravager] is summoned. In addition to this, the odds of The Codseeker snapping will remain unaffected even if the player equips the [[High Test Fishing Line]].
