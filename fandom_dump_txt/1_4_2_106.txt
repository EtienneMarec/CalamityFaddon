The '''1.4.2.106''' update added a variety of new [[weapons]] and [[coverings]] for the [[Spy]] class, along with a dedicated [[Town NPC]] known as the [[Bandit]]. The update also reworked the [[defense]] and [[damage reduction]] systems, made several changes to the [[Boss Rush]] event, added new [[fishing]] items to the [[Astral Infection]], and made a heavy amount of optimizations to the mod's performance.

=== [[Weapons]] ===
*Added [[Blast Barrel]], [[Blazing Star]], [[Bloodsoaked Crasher]], [[Bouncing Eyeball]], [[Celestial Reaper]], [[Consecrated Water]], [[Eclipse's Fall]], [[Enchanted Axe]], [[Gacruxian Mollusk]], [[Iron Francisca]], [[Kylie]], [[Lead Tomahawk]], [[Monkey Darts]], [[Scourge of the Seas]], [[Slick Cane]], [[Stormfront Razor]], [[Throwing Brick]], and [[Utensil Poker]]. <!---Spy--->

*[[Cinquedea]] and [[Glaive]] are now obtainable.
