The '''Arid Temple''' is an unique structure that generates in the [[Desert]]. It features a giant building resembling a temple, its entrance is located in the surface [[Desert]], this structure somehow also acts as sort of an "upgraded" version of the Desert Pyramid. The player can enter the Temple only if the [[Nightmare Mode]] is activated in the world. 

Additionally, the temple has several [[tgc:Desert Chest|Desert Chests]] containing various [[Desert]]-themed loot in its inner rooms.

== Trivia ==
*Due to the Arid Temple's size, the Tranquility Mod modifies the [[Desert]] biome's width to make it wider, the biome overlap is possible if the created world has a small size.
*If the player will try to enter the Temple without the [[Nightmare Mode]] enabled in the world, a [[Deniquyne, the Sand Elemental|Sand Elemental]]'s dialogue box will appear, and she will say:
**"Sorry, human, i can not let you in. You must prove that you're worthy enough to enter."
**However, if the <code> Dialogue System </code> configuration option is disabled, only the text will appear above the player on screen.
*Despite the fight with the Sand Elemental beginning inside of the Temple, she will teleport herself and the player to the Temple's entrance, where's a large, sparse area is present.
