The '''Slime Gun EX''' is a [[ranged weapon]] that drops from [[Goozma]]. At first it slowly fires out balls of ebonian goup which create medium sized explosions upon hitting enemies or tiles. After defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]], the weapon's fire rate is greatly increased, and globs will stick to enemies, dealing damage over time instead of immediately exploding. Right clicking causes all stuck globs to detonate, dealing a base of 2x the weapon's damage and increasing by 5% for each glob stuck.
