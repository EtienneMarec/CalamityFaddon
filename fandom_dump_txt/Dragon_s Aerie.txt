''"The forgotten home of the dragons."''

The Dragon's Aerie is a [[Subworlds|subworld]] added by the Calamity mod where [[Godseeker, Yharim]] is intended to be fought. It is accessed through the gateway, which opens up after defeating [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon]].

It is a serene set of floating islands of quiet forests, yellow flora and the ruins of those who enhabited it. Upon entering you are located in a cave on a seperate island connect to the main island with a large decaying bridge. The first island is a place meant to be explored by the player post-[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon]] for [[Auric Ore]] and various other things useful for the two upcoming boss fights. The second island is sprinkled with small ruins where the player can learn more about the dragons. The third is a set of gigantic ruins that act as a prebuilt arena, you gain creative shock on it and you will be unable to exit the island if you are fighting Yharim.

The subworld is mostly barren of all NPCs with the occasional critter and under normal circumstances Where it started plays, except for when you return to fight [[Godseeker, Yharim]], which in that case no music plays and there are no critters to be found.
