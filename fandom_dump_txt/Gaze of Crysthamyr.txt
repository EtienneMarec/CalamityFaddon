'''Gaze of Crysthamyr''' is a [[Moon Lord]] [[Mount]] summoning item. When used it summons a rideable Shadow Dragon mount which can run and fly at high speeds.

The Shadow Dragon is capable of running at a speed of 77 mph while on the ground and can reach 69 mph while flying vertically. The Shadow Dragon mount cannot double jump but is capable of 12.5 seconds of flight, boasting incredibly quick horizontal movement but weak upwards acceleration. Like the [[Hoverboard]], holding {{key|down}} and {{key|jump}} while in the air will make the mount hover.
