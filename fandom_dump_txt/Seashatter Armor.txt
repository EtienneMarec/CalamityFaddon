'''Seashatter armor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] set that is a direct upgrade to [[Mollusk armor]]. 

It consists of the '''Seashatter Conch Helmet''', '''Seashatter Snail Mask''', '''Seashatter Breastplate''', and '''Seashatter Leggings'''.

==Stats==
The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus): 
*+45 [[defense]]
* +15% damage
*+13% critical strike chance
*+12% movement speed
*Allows to move freely through liquids.
*Allows to breathe indefinitely underwater and increases breath loss rate in the [[Abyss]] by 10.
*Pressing {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} will summon a '''Sunken Lantern''' minion.
**While the minion is present, the player will act as if they were underwater. This will also trigger the submerged effects, like the ones granted by headpieces of the '''Seashatter Armor''' set.
**The Sunken Lantern minion will produce a blue-ish light wave once every 2-3 seconds, dealing 130 typeless damage with no ability to deal critical hits. Additionally, the minion's attacks inflict the [[Shine Potion|Shine]] [[Buffs|buff]] for 1 second, causing the hit enemies to glow.
**The Sunken Lantern minion produces light, as powerful as the one produced by [[tgc:Wisp in a Bottle|Wisp in a Bottle]]. However, it also reduces the player's vision, leaving clear only a small circle, the radius of which is about 6 blocks.
*'''With {{item|Seashatter Conch Helmet|Conch Helmet|link=|size=20px}}:'''
**+9 defense (54 total)
**+16% damage (30% total)
**+1% critical strike chance (14% total)
**+15% attack speed
**Grants following stats while submerged in liquid:
***+15% multiplicative damage
***+10% attack speed (25% total)
***-8 defense (46 total)
***-2% critical strike chance (12% total)
**The player will quickly launch 3 sound waves in rapid succession in nearby enemies every 4 seconds, dealing 80 typeless damage each with no ability to deal critical hits.
**Critical strike chance is multiplied by 0.75, rounded up to the nearest number.
*'''With {{item|Seashatter Snail Mask|Snail Mask|link=|size=20px}}:'''
**+32 defense (77 total)
**+6% damage (21% total)
**+3% critical strike chance (16% total)
**+12% flat [[Damage Reduction|damage reduction]]
** +800 [[Aggro]]
**-50 max health
** Grants following stats while submerged in liquid:
***+20 defense (97 total)
***-2 life regen (1 HP/s)
***-6% damage (15% total)
***-3% critical strike chance (13% total)
**All projectiles and enemies will deal 15% less damage from the player's back.
