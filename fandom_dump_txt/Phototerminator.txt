The '''Phototerminator''' is a craftable Epilogue [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that  uses [[gel]] as [[ammunition]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it fires a piercing beam, similarly to [[Hyperhavoc]]. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), it rapidly fires sparks with a 90° innacuracy that deal 10% of the weapon damage.
