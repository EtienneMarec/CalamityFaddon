The '''Hellwing Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]]. Upon use, four non-piercing flaming bats are summoned near the cursor, which are fired in a semi-random, generally downward, direction. These bats can travel through tiles and inflict enemies with the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] on hit.
