This page documents modding content that is affiliated with the Calamity Mod that has been created by members of the community.

== Add-on Mods ==

=== [[File:Calamity_Vanities_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/calamitys-vanities.97575/ Calamity's Vanities] ===
''Calamity's Vanities'' is a vanity mod created by Hypera and currently owned by YuH, with sprites, code, and designs from various other contributors. The mod adds over 80 new [[pets]] based on the mod's content, several [[mounts]], [[critters]], and an assortment of other vanity items. The mod's Discord invite link can be found [https://discord.gg/Qgx8eRA here] and its own wiki can be found [https://terrariamods.gamepedia.com/Calamity%27s_Vanities here].

=== [[File:Catalyst_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/CatalystMod Catalyst] ===
''Catalyst'' is an expansion mod made by Heart Plus Up! and several other developers. It reimplements an overhauled version of the [[Astrageldon Slime]] as a [[superboss]], with more superbosses and some additional [[biomes]] planned for the future. The mod's Discord link can be found [https://discord.gg/Z6Pdm53mTs here], and its wiki can be found [https://terrariamods.fandom.com/wiki/Catalyst here].

=== [[File:Cataclysm_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/CataclysmMod Cataclysm] ===
''Cataclysm'' is a quality-of-life mod by Tomat (a.k.a. Convicted Tomatophile) that adds various changes to the mod's content to increase quality of life. Such changes include, but are not limited to: additional text below enemies to indicate whether they are [[List_of_NPCs_by_Hit_Sound|organic or inorganic]], granting new drops to certain bosses and enemies, and several recipe changes. The mod also adds a few new items, such as a [[rare item variant]] of the [[Cursed Dagger]] and an item that allows players to instantly teleport to the [[Sulphurous Sea]] similarly to the [[Magic Conch]]. Tomat's Discord server can be found [https://discord.gg/Y8bvvqyFQw here].

=== [[File:Infernum_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/InfernumMode Infernum] ===
''Infernum'' is a combat-oriented mod by Lucille Karma that adds a brand new difficulty mode. Infernum Mode adjusts the behaviors of vanilla and Calamity bosses, with the intention of making bosses more difficult. The mod's Discord invite link can be found [https://discord.gg/cKh6nyrSE7 here].

<big>Calamity Noxus Boss</big>

Made by the infernum dev. Fought after boss rush [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2995193002 here]

=== [[File:Apotheosis_and_Friends_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/apotheosis-friends.69009/ Apotheosis & Friends] ===
''Apotheosis & Friends'' is a joke mod created by Terry N. Muse. The mod contains several overpowered weapons with excessive crafting trees based on the weapon [[Apotheosis]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]], a few pets, combinations of the mod's [[coverings]], items which reset [[boss]] flags, and a re-implementation of [[THE LORDE]] alongside its own theme. Its Terraria forum page can be found [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/apotheosis-friends.69009/ here].

=== [[File:Calamity_Community_Remix_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2956324268 Calamity: Community Remix] ===
''Calamity: Community Remix'' is an expansion mod made by the TH group. It adds various new items including new endgame [[coverings]], along with a few new structures, new [[coins|coin]] types, and a couple [[bosses]]. The mod's Discord server can be found [https://discord.gg/rtq3jRDsrA here].

=== [[File:Hypnos_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2865345084 Hypnos in Calamity Mod] ===
''Hypnos in Calamity Mod'' is a boss mod made by YuH. It adds a new endgame boss known as [[XP-00 Hypnos]] which acts as an alternate route for the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] fight. The mod's Discord server can be found [https://discord.gg/rtq3jRDsrA here].

===[[File:SLOOME.png|left|60x60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2825058895 Sloome calamity]===
''Sloome (calamity addon)'' is a content mod made by Jelly Bloop. It adds and modifies content that was previously removed or scrapped in Calamity and currently adds 1 Regular Boss and adds a super boss that is fought after the The Fabricational Quartet and Supreme Calamitas have been defeated.

===[[File:Deimos,_the_Starborn_Princess_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3003980015 Deimos, the Starborn Princess]===
'''Deimos, the Starborn Princess''' is an addon mod that introduces the [[Starborn Princess]] and the [[Soul of the Eternal Intellect of Infinite Verboten Knowledge]], as well as some extra content. The mod was created by Everquartz and was also formerly owned by Jester Romut. The mod is currently owned by Yez.

===[[File:Calamity_Loot_Swap_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/CalamityLootSwap Calamity Loot Swap]===
''Calamity Loot Swap'' is a quality-of-life mod created by Potato Person and currently owned by YuH. It adds "manipulator" items as drops from bosses, which allows the player to switch between any of the boss's loot at will to obtain the desired drop.

=== [[File:Bad_Apple_(Goozma_Boss)_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3015555486 Bad Apple (Goozma Boss)]===
''Bad Apple (Goozma Boss)'' is a boss mod made by MrGoodLuck. The mod's Discord server can be found [https://discord.gg/rtq3jRDsrA here].

===[[File:Wakasagihime_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/app/1281930/workshop/ Wakasagihime]===
''Wakasagihime'' is a content mod made by Artienia. It adds the eponymous [[Wakasagihime]] broadsword into the progression.


===[[File:Unofficial_Calamity_Mod_Music_Mod_Icon.png|60px]][https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/UnCalamityModMusicLite Unofficial Calamity Mod Music]===
''Unofficial Calamity Mod Music'' is a music mod by apotofkoolaid and Heart Plus Up! that aims to replace the entire vanilla soundtrack with new songs that better fit with Calamity's OST. Currently, the mod adds 40 new [[music]] tracks for various areas, each with their own [[music box]]. The mod is split into two versions due to mod browser limitations: the lite version and full version. The lite version can be found on the mod browser and contains only boss tracks, while the full version can be found on the mod's Discord server and contains all of the tracks created for the mod. The mod's Discord invite link can be found [https://discord.gg/WBb7XFuwKk here] and its soundtrack can be found [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuo0XyZGCmUDlfz6xqet4FFyqPe7WjhFt here].

===[[File:Bags mod.png|61x61px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2829862017&searchtext=Bags Bags]===
Bags is a QoL mod that adds loot bags that scale with boss progression. It also introduces two new town NPCs, one of which is the Bag Expert, which gives you information about bags, and the other being the Bag Manipulator, who sells potions that allow you to downgrade the bags dropped for 5 minutes, in case you want post-king slime loot instead of post-eye of cthulhu loot. It's a pretty cool mod. It does this... 

===[[File:Calamity Aquatic.png|60x60px]] Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic===
Tales of the Aquatic is an upcoming addon mod that adds a load of new bosses, enemies, biomes and items, which was originally created by InxieRatZey and later owned by eq for less than a week, and is now owned by InxieRatZey again. It requires Community Remix and Catalyst installed because most of the content in this addon is unlockable after defeating Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus from Catalyst.

== Historical Add-On Mods==

=== [[File:Calamity_Souls_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/CalamitySouls Calamity's Souls] ===
''Calamity's Souls'' was an item mod by Plantare. It added dozens of new coverings which combine Calamity's own armors and coverings, along with adding unique new effects themed around them on top. It also added a new difficulty mode, some new [[grab bag]] [[fishing]] drops, and a few weapons. The mod requires [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/CalamityPlantation Calamity's Plantation] in order to be enabled.

=== [[File:Legendaries_Plus_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://mirror.sgkoi.dev/Mods/Details/ZLegendarys Legendaries+] ===
''Legendaries+'' was an item mod by RandomZachOfKindness, with some sprites from other contributors. It added several Legendary items, extremely rare [[weapons]] and [[ammunition]] dropped from bosses in [[Revengeance Mode]]. The mod's Discord invite link can be found [https://discord.gg/xS2BEjr here].

===[[File:Polyphemalus_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] [https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2874171708 Polyphemalus]===
''Polyphemalus'' was a boss mod made by Doze Zoze. It added a new boss known as [[Polyphemalus]] which is fought during mid-[[Hardmode]], Polyphemalus is now included in Calamity: Community Remix.[https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2825058895]

===[[File:Empathic_Untamed_Calamity_Mod_Icon.png|60px]] Emphatic Untamed Calamity===
''Emphatic Untamed Calamity'' was an expansion mod by Seraph. It added new facets to the [[lore]] as well as more Godseeker Mode content. The add-on contained several new items, a [[town NPC]], lore alterations, a [[Plague]] themed minibiome, and a new boss known as Goozma (old lore version of Noxus, being the final form of slime god). The mod has been discontinued, and is no longer able to load on Calamity Mod versions [[1.4.5.001]] and higher.

==[[File:Calamity_Texture_Pack_Icon.png|80px]] [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/the-calamity-texture-pack.76813/ Calamity Texture Pack]==
The ''Calamity Texture Pack'' is a texture pack created by Niorin which seeks to improve vanilla Terraria's sprites to match the quality and stylings of Calamity's. The texture pack currently contains more than 1,000 sprites, including several bosses, tiles and weapons, contributed from an assortment of different spriters. The texture pack's Discord invite link can be found [https://discord.gg/HYayJQZ here].
