The '''Hadarian''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns in the [[Desert]] [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. It behaves like the [[Vulture]], initially waiting in place for a player to come near, then takes off once approached and begins hovering above the player, periodically swooping down to deal damage. It is much faster than a Vulture and is able to hover and pursue the player at a better pace.

== Trivia ==
*This enemy's name refers to the [[wikipedia:Beta Centauri|Hadar]] star system, also known as Beta Centauri.
