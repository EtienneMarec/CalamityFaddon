The '''Devourer Spawn''' was a Godseeker Mode [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] [[enemy]] that was spawned during the fight with [[The Devourer of Gods]]. It behaved almost identically to [[Wyvern]]s and spawned periodically as the Devourer of Gods was damaged, in a similar manner to the [[Probe | Probes]] in the [[The Destroyer | Destroyer]] fight.
