The '''Core of the Blood God''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It boosts the wearer's maximum health by 10%, causes [[Recovery Potions|Health Potions]] to heal 15% more HP, and provides an 8% increase to all damage and damage reduction. All received contact damage is halved from enemies; this effect has a 20 second cooldown.

== Notes ==
*While it is crafted from the [[Blood Pact]], it does ''not'' give enemies the ability to perform critical strikes on the player. In compensation, the player's maximum health is increased by 10% rather than doubled.
*If the player dashes through an enemy using a shield (such as the [[Elysian Aegis]] or [[Asgardian Aegis]]), the effect of halved contact damage will be put on cooldown despite the fact that no damage was taken.
*While it is equipped, Healing Potions will have their tooltips updated to reflect on their boosted effects.
*The health potion increasing effect stacks additively with that of the Blood Pact.

== Trivia ==
*Its name may be a reference to the memetic phrase "Blood for the Blood God", from the ''[[wikipedia:Warhammer 40,000|Warhammer 40,000]]'' game series.
*It can be considered the counterpart to [[The Amalgam]].
