The '''Calamitous Certificate''' is an item that is dropped by [[The Calamity]]. When used right-clicked in the inventory, a PNG file will be sent to your documents folder of the full certificate image.
==Notes==
*If you already have a certificate file already in your documents folder, text will pop up stating "You were already mailed a certificate".
==Trivia==
*It's successor, the [[Calamitous Certificate EX]], does not have a full version.
