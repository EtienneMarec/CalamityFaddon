'''The Devil of Vehemence''' is an Epilogue Mode [[boss]]. Defeating it unlocks the [[Seal of Vehemence]].

==Spawn ==
The Devil of Vehemence does not spawn on its own and requires the player to sacrifice a [[Vehement Prayer]] at the [[Altar of the Accursed]] in the underworld, Once summoned, Wings and Mounts will be disabled.

==Behavior==
The Devil of Vehemence will normally hover above the player and slowly moves toward the players X-Position, Occasionally dashing or floating in place. She summons {{dv|10|12|15|20}} Calamity Lanterns that orbit around her during the fight and can preform A large variety of attacks. If you try to escape the underworld she instantly kills you.

==Attacks==
The Devil of Vehemnce will preform the following attacks in order. 

*(Lanterns) Fires homing, accelerating Brimstone Darts at the player, (DOV) and fires A Brimstone Energy Sphere that follows the player and explodes into Lightning Projectiles.

*(Lanterns) Hovers above the player At their X-Position in an eye formation and sometimes fire Small Brimstone Deathrays downwards after A short delay or more unlikely Brimstone Hellblasts, (DOV) occasionally fires A Medium Brimstone Deathray at the player.
*(DOV) Fires Brimstone Fireballs at the player that can curve in or out.

===At 90% Health (Invulnerable)===
The Brimstone Lanterns create A circle around the boss and can be damaged while the Devil of Vehemence preforms the following attacks. The Devil of Vehemence Is invulnerable Until all of the lanterns HP reach zero.   

*(Lanterns) Creates A small damaging aura that can randomly move around and disappear after a few seconds while.
*(Lanterns) Sometimes lights up and creates A small shockwave from it.
*(Lanterns) May release wandering spirits that wander around the arena.
*(DOV) fires {{dv|8|10|12|14}} Brimstone Bombs that can explode into an X or + formation.
*(DOV) Fires Brimstone Hellblasts that explode into 8 Spiraling Shots
*(DOV) pulls in the player for A few seconds and unleashes A shockwave shortly after and releases Brimstone bombs in random directions and explode shortly after.
*(DOV) Throws the Vehemence at the player what explode into homing skulls.

==== After Becoming Vulnerable====

* (DOV) Summons 2 Brimstone Energy Spheres that can bounce off each other and explode into lightning projectiles. (Lanterns) Fires 1 Or 2 Brimstone Darts at the player
*(Lanterns) Hovers at the top of the arena and moves in random positions and fires Small Brimstone Deathrays downward and repositions them (This attack repeats itself {{dv|4|5|6|7}} Times)
* (Lanterns) Move Into 2 circular formations that orbit around the Devil of Vehemence and fires Brimstone Plasma Spheres that home in on the player and explode into thunder projectiles, (DOV) Follows the player and sometimes dashes toward the player and creates an explosion at the end of the dash.
*(DOV) Fires {{dv|6|8|10|12}} Brimstone Bombs around the arena and explode in A ⁕ Formation and explode one at A time
*(Lanterns) Move to A random positions in the arena and releases A small shockwave and moves to another spot.
*(DOV) Dashes toward the player {{dv|3|4|5|6}} times in A Row, and sends A Shockwave afterwards, sends multiple Brimstone Energy Spheres Outward. (Lanterns) Can light up and fire lightning projectiles after the energy spheres explode.
*(DOV) Throws the Vehemence At The Player and explodes into homing skulls.

===At 60% Health===
Lightning can strike the arena at random from now on. Vehement Tendrils Sprout From her body and can strike the player.

*(DOV) Creates A large Brimstone energy sphere and fires lightning in random directions and explodes across the whole screen (Parry Accessories can save you from the screen wide explosion)
*(DOV) Follows the player and preforms A slow dash releasing Brimstone Darts in random directions.
*(Lanterns) Fires gravity affected Brimstone Darts upwards for {{dv|10|12|13|14}} seconds. (DOV) Throws the Vehemence at the player every {{dv|5|4.5|4|3.5}} seconds.
*(DOV) Fires Brimstone Hellblasts that explode into 8 Spiraling Shots and then dashes toward the player.
*(DOV) pulls in the player for A few seconds and unleashes A shockwave shortly after and releases {{dv|6|8|10|12}} Brimstone bombs in random directions and explode shortly after, (Lanterns) Moves into random positions and creates A small shockwave after the bombs explode.
* (DOV) Dashes toward the player 3 times in A Row, and sends A Shockwave afterwards, sends multiple Brimstone Energy Spheres Outward. (Lanterns) Can light up and fire lightning projectiles after the energy spheres explode.

===At 30% Health (Invulnerable)===
The Brimstone Lanterns create A circle around the boss and can be damaged while the Devil of Vehemence preforms the following attacks. The Devil of Vehemence Is invulnerable Until all of the lanterns HP reach zero
(DOV) Creates A summoning circle around herself and summons {{dv|10|12|15|20}} Vehement Imps, (Lanterns) Follows one (For Each Imp) behind them and gives them health regeneration. (Always Done First And Cant Be Done Again)    (Lanterns) Moves toward the center of the arena and outwards again. (Also Done During This Phase)  

*(Lanterns) Creates A small damaging aura that can randomly move around and disappear after a few seconds while.
*(Lanterns) Sometimes lights up and creates A medium sized shockwave from them but less often and fires Brimstone Darts in ⁕ Formation
*(Lanterns) May release wandering spirits that wander around the arena.
*(DOV) fires {{dv|6|8|10|12}} Brimstone Bombs that can explode into an X or + formation.
*(DOV) pulls in the player for A {{dv|5|7|10|13}} seconds and unleashes A shockwave shortly after and releases {{dv|6|8|10|12}} Brimstone bombs in random directions and explode shortly after and explode one at a time.
*(DOV) Throws the Vehemence at the player what explode into homing skulls.

====After Becoming Vulnerable (At 30% HP)====
(Lanterns) Fires gravity affected Brimstone Darts upward. (DOV) Fires Energy Spheres in random directions. 

====At 15% Health.====
(DOV) Moves to the center of the arena and pulls in the player for A for a while damaging charred blocks are pulled into it. The Tendrils Expand outward and chases the player.

===Desperation Phase===
When the Devil Of Vehemence reaches 1% HP, The arena brakes and she absorbs the Calamity Lanterns and becomes A massive ball of energy and chases the player while firing Energy Spheres at the player for 15 seconds. and Explodes.
