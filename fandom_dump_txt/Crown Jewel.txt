The '''Crown Jewel''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It is dropped by [[King Slime]].

It boosts life regeneration by 2, even while the player is under the effects of a damaging debuff. When worn while being damaged by a debuff, the player's [[defense]] will increase by 8 and life regen will increase by an additional 4 (for a total of 5).

== Notes ==
*If the player is under the effect of one of the alcohols sold by the [[Drunk Princess]], the covering's life regen bonuses will be nullified.
