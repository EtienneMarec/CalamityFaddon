The '''Void Man''' is a [[Town NPCs|Town NPC]] that will spawn after a random amount of time has passed in-game.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will generate the void orbs that suck enemies in and inflict the [[Nullified]] debuff.

==Functions==
Upon chosing the "Accompany" dialogue option, the dialogue will be shown:
:*"Want me to accompany you, eh? Well, i either way have nothin' better to do..."

The Void Man will join the player as an ally that will attack the enemies with the void orbs, inflicting the [[Nullified]] debuff.

==Names==
*The Void Man will always be named eq or mere eq.

==Trivia==
*The Void Man is a self-insert [[Town NPCs|Town NPC]] of the mod's owner, mere eq (or just eq), thus the dialogues variants that appear if the player has a certain name.
*The Void Man can spawn even if there's no empty house. He is the only [[Town NPCs|Town NPC]] that has no spawning conditions, meaning that he's guaranteed to spawn.
*Some dialogue variants are a direct reference to the mod's owner's real-life stuff:
**Phrase "I'm a void man [music]" is a reference to the song [https://youtu.be/Hy8kmNEo1i8 Scatman].
**Phrase about "And remember: the blood of the ancient schizs is flowing in you." is a reference to the mod's owner being a schizophrenic, and this phrase is something he came up with randomly. This quote most of times has no context at all.
**Phrase about hate about "The unholy shall be consigned to the sacred fire" is a kinda self-explanatory, is it not.
**Phrase about the "red dawn" is a reference to the mod's owner quote "when the red dawn will rise": "mortui illae resurgent; sicut rufus aurora resurget...", which refers that when the Great Revolution will happen, the red sun will rise, and all the deadmen will rise from dead. <!---and after it they say a communism isn't a religion...--->
**Phrase about "massacre..." is a reference to the russian meme [https://www.google.com/search?q=%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B7%D0%BD%D1%8F+%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BC&sxsrf=APwXEdeQKnmdPDyCOhe7utUw3XZ7TBt5_g:1682016771864&source=lnms&tbm=isch&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwiausXhkLn-AhXQuYsKHe9TD5kQ_AUoAXoECAEQAw&biw=1920&bih=937#imgrc=k7bmlWbTBAEW8M massacre] (this is due to mod's owner being a russian), phrase about "KILL KILL KILL ..." is an another russian meme.
*The Void Man is invulnerable to all sorts of damage, even to [[tgc:Lava|lava]] and [[tgc:Traps|traps]], but still can play a sound upon dying.
*The Void Man ally is an unique entity which is not a projectile, nor is really a creature, thus he cannot be killed, and only despawns when the player dies or leaves the world.
**If despawned, he will return to his Town NPC form.
*The damage dealt by the Void Man ally will always be 1, this is due to the main focus is the [[Nullified]] debuff his attacks inflict.
