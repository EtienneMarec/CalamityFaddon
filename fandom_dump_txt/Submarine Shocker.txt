The '''Submarine Shocker''' is a Pre-hardmode [[shortsword]] that drops from the [[Aquatic Scourge]]; it can be considered an indirect upgrade to the [[Aquatic Discharge]]. It unleashes 1-2 sparks upon hitting an enemy, which are affected by gravity and fall through platforms. These sparks linger for 1 second and can hit up to 4 times.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, [[Sulphuric Poisoning]]-inflicting clouds are spawned which linger in place.
