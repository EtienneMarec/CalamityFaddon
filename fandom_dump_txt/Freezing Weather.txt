'''Freezing Weather''' was a [[debuff]] caused by being in a [[blizzard]] in [[Death Mode]] without any form of [[Death Mode#Temperature Protective Gear|Cold Protection]]. It reduced the player's movement speed and acceleration by 0.0167% every second the player was active with this debuff. After 30 seconds, the player would be inflicted with the [[Frozen]] debuff, and would be killed 3 seconds later.

The timer raised twice as fast while the player contacted [[water]]. The debuff was removed as soon as the player exited the [[Snow biome]] or obtained Cold Immunity. However, the decreased movement speed would slowly be removed over time rather than instantly.
