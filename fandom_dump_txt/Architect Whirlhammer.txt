'''Architect Whirlhammer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] covering . It lets you do 2 things:
left click: Launches a hammer to the mouse position, the hammer spins for 4 seconds and vanishes.
right click: Creates a 3x3 box of temporary ice blocks at the mouse position, the blocks last for 15 seconds before vanishing.
