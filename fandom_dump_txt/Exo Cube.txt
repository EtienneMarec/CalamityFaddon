The '''Exo Cube (Japanese:エクソキューブ Ekusokyūbu)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. It is completely stationary, and does not attack. It also cannot be supercharged by the [[Exo Obelisk]].
==Trivia==
*The [[Soma Prime]] is Blockaroz's dedicated weapon
*The Exo Cube was introduced during an Official crossover between Calamity and Geometry
**The Exo Cube is confirmed to be Mr. Geometry Dash himself
