'''The Exosphere''' is a [[subworld]] added by the Calamity Mod which is used for the [[Mechanical Horde]] [[Events|event]] and the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] [[boss]] fight. It can be accessed by building a 4 tile wide by 5 tile tall portal made of [[Rusted Pipes]] and throwing an [[Auric Bar]] onto it while a [[Flint]] is in the player's inventory. The subworld is devoid of any blocks or NPCs aside from several rows of [[Exo Platform]]s. Shortly after entering the subworld, [[Draedon]] will spawn in, give some dialogue lines, then allow the player to choose which Exo Mech they want to fight first. However, failing to choose a mech within 14.5 minutes will summon all of the Exo Mechs to fight the player

Liquids can be placed in The Exosphere by players, but will inflict the [[Electrified]] debuff onto them when entered, regardless of any immunities.

{{fantag|Polyphemalus}} In the bottom left corner of the world is a very small vein of [[Scoria Ore]] with [[La Ruga]] on top of it, eating some of the ore.

{{tota}} The bottom border of the exosphere is moved a lot deeper, with the [[Abyssphere]] biome generating in the new area.

==Notes==
*If all players are killed, the subworld will be closed and all players will be sent back to the main world.
*The subworld will ''not'' save when exited, and will go back to its default state the next time it is entered.
*The high amount of Exo Platforms can allow the player to access [[Exo Plating]] before the The Fabricational Quartet have been defeated.
*Any NPCs aside from Draedon, and the The Fabricational Quartet that are spawned in the subworld will be instantly despawned.
