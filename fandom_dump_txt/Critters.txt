'''Critters''' are non-hostile characters that cannot hurt players. All critters (excluding the [[Aerwhale]]) from the mod can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]], with the exception of the [[Repair Unit]]. This page lists all critters that can be encountered in the ''Calamity Mod''.
