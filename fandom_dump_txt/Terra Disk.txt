The '''Terra Disk''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]]. It automatically fires up to 3 disks that have a chance to create 6 smaller disks outwards from the disk while an enemy is near.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Terra Disk will cause the next disk thrown to travel slower, but also continuously generate smaller disks that will rotate around it.
