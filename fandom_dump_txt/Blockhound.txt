The '''Blockhound (Japanese: ブロックハウンド Burokkuhaundo)''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in the Exosphere after [[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]] have been defeated. It has the same AI as the [[Unicorn]], charging and ramming into the player at ludicrously high speeds. This, along with their ability to inflict [[Sunscreamed]], makes them a dangerous enemy for any and all players.

== Trivia ==
*The Blockhound is the direct offspring of Blockaroz after he absorbed a fragment of an [[Ogscule]] and shattered into a thousand pieces.
