The '''Ethereal Talisman''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]], which is an upgrade to the [[Sigil of Calamitas]]. It provides a 15% increase to magic damage, a 5% increase to magic critical strike chance, a 10% decrease in mana usage, +150 max mana, increases pickup range for mana stars, and will automatically use mana potions when needed. Toggling this covering’s visibility allows you to disable the [[Mana Flower]] effects.
