The '''1.5.0.003''' (or '''1.5.0.3''') Music mod update fixed an issue preventing the mod from being downloaded on the tModLoader Mod Browser.

This Music Mod version is compatible with Main mod versions [[1.5.0.001]] through [[1.5.0.004]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed an issue that prevented the mod from being installed on tModLoader or its mirror site due to the mod's large file size.

== Notes ==
*This version only exists on the tModLoader Mod Browser and is identical to the [[1.5.0.002 (music)]] version.
