The '''Diviner Valkyrie''' is a secret [[miniboss]] encountered via [[Falconer]]'s 30 {{item|Essence of Babil}} quest. Upon activation of the quest, she will perch on a [[Floating Island]] and will remain neutral towards the player until directly attacked. 

Diviner Valkyrie has the following attacks:
*A torrent of spears which chase the player similarly to a fast [[Wyvern]].
*Gusts of non-homing wind which blow in linearly from around the player, similar to those from the [[Turbulance]].
*A flurry of claw scratches telegraphed by dialogue in chat.
*If the player moves too far from the Diviner, rapid lightning strikes will appear ahead of the direction the player is moving.

{{dei}} Diviner Valkyries can also spawn as a regular enemy in Godseeker Mode at around the height [[Floating Island]]s are found.

== Notes ==
*{{dei}} Diviner Valkyries that spawn outside of the quest will not drop the [[Spear of Liberty]] or [[Sky Fracture]].
