The '''Bounding Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Bounding''' [[buff]], increasing the player's jump height by 10, their jump speed by 5%, and fall resistance increases by 25 blocks, allowing the player to fall 50 blocks before taking fall damage.
