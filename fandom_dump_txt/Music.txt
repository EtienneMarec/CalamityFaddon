The Calamity Mod adds many new '''tracks''' or '''music''' to the game. Almost all tracks are made by composer [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO], with three featuring [https://www.youtube.com/user/badoosh12 SixteenInMono], four tracks composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@sploopo Sploopo], four tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@pinpinneon9353 PinpinNeon], four tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@CDMusicYT CDMusic], two tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@roverdrivex9816 RoverdriveX], two tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@gummyleeches GummyLeeches], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/@Thriplerex Thriplerex], one track by [https://soundcloud.com/lilqual lilquail], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/@BrimstonPremium Brimston Premium], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC_PQMsCnlqn1uIMJhLwTEtA Ridiculon], two tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@HeartPlusUp Heart Plus Up!], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/@seeksery seeksery], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCXemm0lOxCrW_pRD1lhLhQg Perturbator], three tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@theexiiedfeiiow4748 The ExiIed FeIIow], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCO5wbMq64o-ruoKCYcQu_-Q mudeth], one track by [https://www.youtube.com/@StarvoXD StarvoXD],  one track by [https://www.youtube.com/@JonnyAtma GaMetal], one track by [[wikipedia:Chief_Keef|Chief Keef]], and twenty eight tracks by [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh]. So far, there are 96 tracks currently in-game. Almost every track composed by DM DOKURO in this mod is featured in an album known as [https://dmdokuro.bandcamp.com/album/the-tale-of-a-cruel-world The Tale Of A Cruel World].

Calamity's music tracks can only be heard in-game if the music add-on mod, known as ''Calamity Mod Music'', is installed and enabled. This mod can be downloaded from the official Calamity Mod Discord server, the front page of this wiki, or alternatively installed through the tModLoader Mod Browser. This music do be hittin' different tho. If this add-on isn't installed, all Calamity themes will be replaced with vanilla Terraria tracks.

==== [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MT6HTpY_OyE Stained, Brutal Calamity] ====
A variable track: in Grief, it is slow and menacing but then fast and frantic; during Lament, it's a little calmer and slower, then gets faster and more hectic; for Epiphany, the song climaxes into a dramatic and intense finish, with a brief choir-exclusive section; finally, Acceptance is somber and monotonic. The track plays during the battle with [[Supreme Calamitas]]. Different sections of the song play as the fight progresses. Each section is meant to represent a specific viewpoint (Grief represents Supreme Calamitas' perspective, Lament represents the player's perspective, Epiphany represents an unknown third voice's perspective, and Acceptance, fittingly enough, represents Supreme Calamitas accepting that her defeat is drawing close). This theme has lyrics sung by a choir as well as DM DOKURO himself.

The track has motifs from Raw, Unfiltered Calamity at 0:49, 6:31, 7:58, and 9:26, and a motif from The Step Below hell at 4:12. It shares a motif with some post-Moon Lord boss tracks, at 5:37 and 7:13 (all with the intro).
