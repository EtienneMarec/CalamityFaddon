The '''Rusty Lockpick''' was a craftable item which allowed the player to access locked [[Rusty Chest]]s while in the player's inventory. A Rusty Lockpick was consumed upon use, so unlocking each Rusty Chest would require an additional key. Additionally, it could also craft items that would otherwise be found in Rusty Chests.
