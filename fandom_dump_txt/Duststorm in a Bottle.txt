The '''Duststorm in a Bottle''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that hurls a jar filled with sand. The jar explodes into a large number of swirling dust clouds upon hitting an enemy or a block. The dust clouds gradually spread out, damaging any enemy that touches them and passing through walls before slowly fading away.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Duststorm in a Bottle causes more dust clouds to be spawned, as well as increasing the duration of the clouds.
