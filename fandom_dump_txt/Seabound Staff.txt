The '''Seabound Staff''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that is dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. It summons a starfish-like minion, which follows the player and attacks [[enemies]] with short dashes, dealing damage on contact.

{{Modifier|summonalt}}

== Tips ==
*The Seabound Staff is rather slow to attack, but lunges similarly to the [[Eye of Cthulhu]]'s second phase in [[Expert Mode]]. It pierces and is very swift when attacking.
