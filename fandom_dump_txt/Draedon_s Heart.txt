'''Draedon's Heart''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], [[XP-00 Hypnos]], and [[XB-10 Losbaf]]. It provides bonuses to defense and life regeneration while the player is standing still.

== Notes ==
*While standing still, using an item prevents the defense and life regeneration buff.

== Trivia ==
*The covering is a reference to an ability Senator Armstrong from ''[[wikipedia:Metal_Gear_Rising:_Revengeance|Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance]]'' has where he can stand still to become near invincible and rapidly regenerate health similarly to the bonuses the player gains when standing still with the covering equipped.
**Its flavor text is a direct quote from Armstrong.
**It is also similar in the way that the source of Armstrong's ability was his cybernetic heart, hence the item's name.
*Before the [[1.5.0.001]] update, the covering was a combination of three [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive coverings: the [[Heart of Darkness]], [[Laudanum]], and [[Stress Pills]].
**This functionality has been replaced with the [[Occult Skull Crown]].
