'''Astral Infection''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by enemies found in the [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]] and [[Weapons]] themed around it. It functions as a weaker version of the [[God Slayer Inferno]] debuff; damaging both players and enemies it afflicts, as well as reducing defense of both enemies and players by 6 and reducing their movement speed by 15%.
