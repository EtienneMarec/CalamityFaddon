| postml = yes}}'''Hadopelagic armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Luminite Bar|Luminite Bars]], [[Scoria Bar|Scoria Bars]], [[Uelibloom Bar|Uelibloom Bars]], and [[Mollusk Husk|Mollusk Husks]]. It requires 20 Luminite Bars, 25 Scoria Bars, 10 Uelibloom Bars, and 35 Mollusk Husks to craft one full set.
Abyssal armor
It consists of the '''Hadopelagic Helmet''', '''Hadopelagic Body Armor,''' and '''Hadopelagic Greaves'''.

The Hadopelagic Helmet gives 33 defense and reduces breath loss in the [[Abyss]]. It also reduces the amount of breath taken away by the Abyss by 30%.

The Hadopelagic Body Armor gives 34 defense and reduces breath loss in the Abyss. It also removes the Bleeding effect from Layers 1-3 in the Abyss. It also grants the effect of [[Anechoic Plating]].

The Hadopelagic Greaves gives 33 defense and grants the ability to swim freely through water and improves mobility on ice. It also allows the player to fall faster in liquids.

The full set reduces the damage taken when out of breath in the abyss by 60 damage per second, the player's movement speed underwater is increased by 5%, but on land is reduced by 60%. The amount of breath taken away by the Abyss is fully restored. It also grants immunity to [[Irradiated]] and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]]. 

The full set also possesses "armored plates" that [[Damage Reduction#Alternative Damage Reduction|reduce damage]] dealt to the player by 15% (lowered by 3% for each missing durability point on the armored plates). However, the plates will be damaged if the player takes more than 50 damage in one hit. The plates can sustain 3 of these hits before breaking. Once broken, they will take 3 minutes to regenerate. While the plates are recharging, the player will not benefit from their damage reduction bonus.
