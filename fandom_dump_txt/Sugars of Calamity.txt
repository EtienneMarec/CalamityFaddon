The '''Sugars of Calamity''' are [[pre-Hardmode]] buff potions crafted from [[Demonic Bone Ash]], [[Glowing Mushroom]]s, and [[Gel]]. It grants the user the '''Calamitous Euphoria''' [[buff]] when consumed, granting the following effects:
*+30% movement speed
*+50% damage
*-30% crit chance
*-10 defense

When the Calamitous Euphoria buff runs out, the player will receive the '''Calamitous Dysphoria''' debuff, granting:
*-10% movement speed
*-20% damage
*-20% crit chance
*-40% mining speed
*-10 defense

The game will track how many Sugars of Calamity the player has used in a row (used while the Calamitous Dysphoria or Calamitous Euphoria buff/debuff is active) and if the value exceeds 2, they will receive the '''Calamitous Anxiety''' debuff, granting the following effects:
*+15% movement speed
*+5 defense
*-50% damage
*-90% crit chance

Calamitous Dysphoria can be cured using more Sugars- however, doing so will replenish the debuff to it's full duration once the Calamitous Euphoria or Calamitous Anxiety buff expires. As a result, it is ill advised to use it outside of combat situations. Worse of all, using too much of it outside of combat will give you the Calamitous Anxiety debuff.
