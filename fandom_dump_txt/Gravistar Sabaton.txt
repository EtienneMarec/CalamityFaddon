The '''Gravistar Sabaton''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Astrum Aureus]]. If equipped, when the {{key|down}} key is pressed while in mid-air, it increases the player's fall speed for 5 seconds, allowing them to create an astral explosion upon hitting the ground, dealing 50 Spy damage to nearby enemies and inflicting the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]]. This effect has an 8 second cooldown.
