'''Abysscent Derailer'''  is an Epilogue [[Launchers|launcher]] that is either crafted or dropped from the [[Esserwyrm]]. lt converts rockets into a very fast bombs. After thebomb hits an enemy or a wall, it explodes into 5 4-pointed stars that slowly home onto enemies, deal 150% of the damage, and inflict the [[Evercold]] Debuff.
