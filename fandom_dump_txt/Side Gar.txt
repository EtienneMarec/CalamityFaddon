The '''Side Gar''' is a [[grab bag]]-type [[Fishing|fish]] that can be caught on the [[Planetoid]]s or [[tgc:Sky Island|Sky Island]]s after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated with a 10% chance. When opened, the player will receive between 5 to 15 [[Galactica Singularity|Galactica Singularities]].
