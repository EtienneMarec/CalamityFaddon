*Due to how the fishing catch chance is coded, the chance to catch the Brimstone Fish depends on a few variables:
**The base chance is 40%:
***If the player is under the effects of a [[Crate Potion]], subtract 10%
***If the player has not yet killed [[Providence]], add 10%
***If the world is in [[Pre-Hardmode]], add 11%
