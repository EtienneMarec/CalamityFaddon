The '''Red Lightning Container''' is a [[permanent power-up]] item obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped from [[The Dragonfolly]]. It increases the [[Rage Mode]] duration by one second. There are 2 other items that function identically to this: the [[Mushroom Plasma Root]] and the [[Infernal Blood]]. It can only be used once.
