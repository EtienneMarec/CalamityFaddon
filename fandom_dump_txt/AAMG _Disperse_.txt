The '''AAMG (Anti Aircraft Machine Gun) "Disperse"''' is a craftable Post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[gun]]. It fires Tungsten Crosses that are able to go through tiles. It deals additional damage to enemies that aren't in contact with a solid block.
