}}The '''Ultimate Boss Rush''' is a special Epilogue [[Events|event]] acting as the "second tier" of the [[Boss Rush]] that is activated by left-clicking with the [[Lore items|Awakening]] lore item and can only be done so after defeating [[The Fallen Ascended Master, Xeroc]], [[The Unimaginable Deity, Yald'xoroath]], and [[The Avatar Of Emptiness, Umbrus]]. The Boss Rush can also be deactivated at any time by using the [[Lore items|Awakening]] lore item again. Unlike the normal Boss Rush, the event features new bosses never fought before. The bosses summoned have extremely high HP and damage and use attacks and gimmicks that are more difficult to track than even those of the [[Superbosses]]. The event punctuates with the sole boss of Tier 6: [[Earthrending Universemover, XO-182 Andromeda Prime]].

Defeating the Boss Rush rewards the player with the [[Otherworldly Log]].

==Progression ==
Upon starting the Ultimate Boss Rush, bosses will spawn in a predetermined order that never changes. Only one boss will spawn at a time; the next boss will spawn once the prior boss is defeated. A total of 21 bosses will spawn during the Ultimate Boss Rush. The bosses are divided into six Tiers, with each new Tier signified by a [[Status messages#Boss Rush|status message]] in the chat and a brief rest period.

If a boss cannot be summoned due to an improper time, the time will be changed to accommodate the boss.

== Notes ==
*If the Boss Rush ends due to all players dying, the Ultimate Boss Rush will next time start after the last completed Tier.
**During Tier 6, dying while inside the Andromedan Interior allows for players to resume that same phase instead.
*Any effects that change the color of the background will be overridden by the custom background for the event. This includes those for day/night.
**Only tiles and water are affected, bosses and projectiles remain unaffected by color changes.
*All damage taken is increased (where applicable) to a minimum value that increases as the player progresses through the event: 
**Contact damage has a minimum of {{dv|240|400}} which increases by 2 for each boss.
**Projectile damage has a minimum of {{dv|220|360}} which increases by {{dv|1|2}} for each boss.
*Bosses slain during Ultimate Boss Rush do ''not'' drop any items or currency.
*The waiting time between the prior boss's death and the next boss spawning is 1 second during Tiers 1 through 3, 6 seconds during Tiers 4 through 5, and 20 seconds right before Tier 6
*All wire-activated mechanisms are disabled during the Ultimate Boss Rush.
* The stats of the bosses during the event only scales up with 2 people in multiplayer. 
**Only health remains the same and never scales.

==Tips== 
*Many health and buff [[potion]]s, as well as mana for mages should be brought, as the Ultimate Boss Rush can easily take over twenty minutes.
*[[Auric Tesla armor]] is a good choice as it provides a wide array of set bonuses and regenerative effects.
*[[Death prevention]] items are strongly recommended as damage can reach up to the thousands, killing most players.
*The player should definitely have end-game weapons and equipment, likely with other mods enabled as well, before facing it if they hope to succeed.
* Contact damage with any of the bosses and their projectiles/minions should be avoided as they can easily deal over 200 damage even with endgame armor.

*The [[Music|themes]] heard throughout the Boss Rush were composed by various artists, with each theme faster and more intense than the last. They were originally for the normal Boss Rush, but scrapped in favor of music by CDMusic.
**[https://youtube.com/watch?v=sYeEGTaPkxc Judgement of Dragons] plays throughout the entirety of Tier 1.
**[https://youtube.com/watch?v=azl4HC6oGo8 Trial of Conquerors] plays through Tier 2.
**[https://youtube.com/watch?v=Omss2LHZRjE Arena of Gods] plays throughout the entirety of Tier 3.
**[https://youtube.com/watch?v=qNUV2RVmEOM Omniscience of Gods] plays through Tier 4.
**[https://youtube.com/watch?v=BVxAJvqJ2-c Descent of Divinity] plays through Tier 5.
**War without Reason plays through Tier 6.
**If the Calamity Music Mod is disabled, the vanilla boss themes will play instead. 
***'''Boss 1 (Otherworldly)''' will play through Tier 1.
***'''Boss 2 (Otherworldly)''' will play through Tier 2.
***'''Invasion (Otherworldly)''' will play through Tier 3.
***'''The Towers (Otherworldly)''' will play through Tier 4.
***'''Lunar Boss (Otherworldly)''' will play through Tiers 5 and 6.
**During each intermission, '''Eerie''' will play.
*All bosses are inspired by other mods, games, and scrapped content along with existing bosses
*Ultimate Boss Rush contains important lore: 
** All bosses came from alternate universes in which they attained absolute supremacy over all beings within their universe.
**It was confirmed by a developer that the narrator is [[The Calamity]] or an entity related to it. 
***This ties into how Ultimate Boss Rush is started, via the Awakening lore item.
