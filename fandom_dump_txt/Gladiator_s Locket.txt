The '''Gladiator's Locket''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] found in the Marble [[shrine]]. When equipped, two Sword Spirits will circle the player and will damage enemies that they contact. The swords orbit in opposite directions, at slightly different speeds. Each sword has a base damage of 30 and ignores enemy [[immunity frames]]. The swords deal classless damage, boosted by an average of each of the player's class damage bonuses.
