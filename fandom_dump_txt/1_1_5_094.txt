The '''1.1.5.094''' update made several balance changes, reworked [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]], and added [[Trophies]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Cryogen Trophy]], [[Desert Scourge Trophy]], [[Devourer of Gods Trophy]], [[Hive Mind Trophy]], [[Perforator Trophy]], [[Plaguebringer Goliath Trophy]], and [[Yharon Trophy]].
*Added [[Marnite Obliterator]], [[Marnite Rifle Spear]], [[Marnite Spear]], [[Night's Stabber]], [[Proporse Pistol]], and [[True Night's Stabber]].
*Added [[Ectoblood]].
*Added [[Brimstone Crag Rock]] and [[Brimstone Slag]] as unobtainable [[block]]s.

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Armored Digger]]s now drop Ectoblood. <!---50% chance for 3-5--->
*[[Calamitas]] can now drop a Calamitas Trophy.
*[[Cryogen]] can now drop a Cryogen Trophy.
*[[Desert Scourge]] can now drop a Desert Scourge Trophy.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] can now drop a Devourer of Gods Trophy.
*The Devourer of Gods now divides projectile damage based on how many times they pierce:
**Projectiles which pierce twice and thrice have their damage divided by 2 and 3 respectively.
**Projectiles which pierce four or more times have their damage divided by 4.
**Infinitely piercing projectiles have their damage divided by 5.
*[[The Hive Mind]] can now drop a Hive Mind Trophy.
*Jungle Dragon, Yharon changes:
**Reduced life to 600,000 for Normal Mode.
**Reduced speed at which phoenix fire from giga flares moves.
**Reduced frequency of giga flares.
**Reduced damage of sky flares.
**Made infernadoes smaller and last a lot less time.
**Made one of the two flares that spawn flarenadoes home in on the player.
**Yharon and his projectiles no longer inflict [[Holy Flames]].
**All projectiles except Sky Flares do much more damage.
**Now does much more contact damage.
**Now gets monstrous defense when outside the [[Jungle]].
**Now gets just slightly faster when outside the Jungle.
**No longer moves at light speed, but only gets slightly faster in his second and third phases to encourage a Jungle-only battle.
**Can now drop a Yharon Trophy.
*[[The Perforators]] can now drop a Perforator Trophy.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] can now drop a Plaguebringer Goliath Trophy.
*[[Slime God|True Slime God]] can now drop a Slime God Trophy.

=== Balancing ===
*[[Ark of the Elements]]' projectiles now last shorter.
*Nerfed [[Deathwind]]'s damage from 155 to 145.
*Nerfed [[Impaler]]'s damage from 105 to 82, and its use time from 19 to 23.
*Nerfed [[Lunarian Bow]]'s damage from 27 to 25, and its arrows now only pierce twice.
*Nerfed [[Malevolence]]'s damage from 60 to 51.
*[[Megalodon]] changes:
**Nerfed damage from 61 to 28, its use time from 2 to 5, and its knockback from 3 to 2.5.
**Decreased accuracy.
**Decreased rarity from {{rare|10}} to {{rare|8}}.
*Nerfed [[Pestilent Defiler]]'s damage from 145 to 135.
*Seraph Tracers no longer have infinite flight.
*Buffed [[Shredder]]'s damage from 22 to 24.
*Nerfed [[Skyline Wings]]' flight time from 50 to 30.
*Nerfed [[Spectre Rifle]] damage from 160 to 150.
*Nerfed [[Terra Flameburster]]'s damage from 105 to 70.
*Nerfed [[Terra Ray]]'s damage from 57 to 54.
*[[Terratomere]]'s beams now last for 4 seconds instead of 10.

=== Other ===
*[[Ectoplasm]] can now be crafted with 3 Ectoblood.
*[[Harpy Ring]] no longer increases flight time.
*Impaler no longer uses a [[Core of Calamity]] in its recipe.
*Megalodon no longer uses 1 [[Chain Gun]], 5 [[Bar of Life|Bars of Life]], and 1 Core of Calamity in its recipe, and now uses 7 [[Core of Eleum|Cores of Eleum]].
*Shredder now uses 5 [[Galactica Singularity|Galactica Singularities]] and 5 [[Luminite Bar]]s in its recipe instead of 1 Core of Calamity.
*Removed [[Sniper Rifle]] from Spectre Rifle's recipe.
*Renamed Angry Chicken Staff to [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]].
*Renamed Galactica Blade to [[Galactus Blade]].
*Renamed Infinity Boots to [[Seraph Tracers]].
*Resprited [[Cosmic Rainbow]], [[Floodtide]], Galactus Blade, Impaler, [[Raider's Glory]], [[Shockstorm Shuttle]], [[Terra Ray]], Terratomere, and Yharon's Kindle Staff.
*Added "The myth, the legend, the weapon that drops more frames than any other" to Shredder's tooltip.
