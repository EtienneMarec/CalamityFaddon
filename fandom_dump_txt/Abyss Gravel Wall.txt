'''Abyss Gravel Walls''' are background [[wall]]s that are naturally generated in the [[Abyss]]. Naturally-generated walls cannot be destroyed or collected and spawn water in front of them whenever a nearby tile updates. Safe versions of Abyss Gravel Walls can be crafted from [[Abyss Gravel]], which have the same appearance but do not release water.

After defeating [[Polterghast]], Natural Abyss Gravel Walls can rarely be found orbiting player-placed [[tgc:Torches#Jungle_Torch_2|Jungle torches]] a sinusoidal pattern, replacing other natural walls in the process.

== Notes ==
*The walls' own water generation counts as a tile update for adjacent walls so that they generate water as well. This may lead to a chain reaction, which is useful for filling sections of the Abyss that may have generated without water.
*Because the recipe for Abyss Gravel Walls post [[Polterghast]], is expensive, [[tgc:Torches#Jungle_Torch_2|Jungle torches]] are the best way to create Natural Abyss Gravel Walls. [[tgc:Torches#Jungle_Torch_2|Jungle torches]] are also the best way to quickly fill an area with water. Because of this, speedrunners often place [[tgc:Torches#Jungle_Torch_2|Jungle torches]] when fighting [[Supreme Calamitas]] in order to drown them.
