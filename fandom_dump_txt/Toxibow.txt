The '''Toxibow''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bow]]. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], it fires toxic arrows that emit a tiny explosion upon contact with tiles or enemies, dealing an additional 50% of the weapon’s damage and has a small chance to inflict the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]].
