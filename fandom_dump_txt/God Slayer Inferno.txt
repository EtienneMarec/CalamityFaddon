'''God Slayer Inferno''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by various sources. It causes the player to lose 15 health per second, 10 defense, and 15% movement speed, or 125 health and 10 defense if the debuff is inflicted to an enemy instead. It also prevents natural life regeneration and inflicts the [[Blackout]] debuff effect on the player. It can be considered a stronger version of the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] debuff.

If the player has the [[Profaned Soul Crystal]] equipped, they lose 17.5 health per second instead of 15. If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they lose 156.5 health per second instead of 125.
