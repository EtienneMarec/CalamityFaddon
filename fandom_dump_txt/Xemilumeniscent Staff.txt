The '''Xemilumeniscent Staff''' is a [[Nightmare Mode]]-exclusive [[wand]] that can only be obtained through breaking the Bulb Trees in the 4th layer of the [[Abyss]] at any time. It has a 1/250 chance to drop upon breaking a Bulb Tree. Upon use it conjures 3 chemilumeniscent orbs that fire laser beams at nearby enemies every 12 frames. When an enemy is killed by an orb, it spawns 3 more orbs and they move faster and deal more damage, every killed enemy spawns 3 more orbs, up to maximum of 12. Using the right-click attack will make you teleport at the cursor's position and spawn 10 chemilumeniscent orbs that do not fire lasers, but aggressively home into nearby enemies, dealing 150% of the weapon's damage, this attack will also give you 30 immunity frames. Such attack can only be used once per 20 seconds.
