The '''Astral Beacon''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[furniture]] item that can be crafted or found naturally on a pillar of [[Astral Monolith]] after the [[Astral Infection]] has generated. When right-clicked during the [[night]], it is used to summon [[Astrum Deus]] while a [[Titan Heart]] or [[Starcore]] is in the player's inventory. Upon successfully summoning the boss, one Titan Heart is removed from the player's inventory if the player does not have a Starcore, and two stars rise up from the beacon, summoning Astrum Deus shortly after. If used during the day, the player is given a [[status message]] "The god of the stars rejects your offering. The ritual can only be performed at night." The beacon can also be right clicked with the [[Bloody Vein]], which will cause the usual spawn animation to play, but with the last collision spawning an [[Ethereal Core]].
