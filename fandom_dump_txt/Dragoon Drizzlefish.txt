The '''Dragoon Drizzlefish''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that is rarely [[Fishing|fished]] up in the Brimstone Crags. It shoots one of two flame projectiles:
*A gravity affected fireball that lingers for a short amount of time after halting.
*A fireball that splits into three fireballs after traveling a short distance.

Both projectiles have a 50% chance to be either orange or red. Orange projectiles inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] onto [[enemies]] while the red projectiles inflict [[Brimstone Flames]].

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*Unlike other flamethrowers, this weapon does not consume [[ammo]], also lacking an equipped "tank." This is likely intentional, as referenced by the weapon's description with the line "The brimstone sac appears to contain fuel".
