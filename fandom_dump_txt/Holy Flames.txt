'''Holy Flames''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by various sources. It causes the player to lose 8 health per second and emit glowing, yellow flames. If inflicted onto enemies, 25 health is lost per second instead.

If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they lose 31.5 health per second instead of 25.

== Causes ==
=== From player ===
In addition to the causes listed below, items that inflict [[Elemental Mix]] will inflict Holy Flames as a part of that.
