The '''Majestic Gar''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. While it does not shoot a projectile, it has a very large range and enough knockback to keep most [[Hardmode]] [[enemies]] away from the player. Every time it strikes an enemy, its defense will be lowered by 1. If the hit enemy's defense is less than or equal to 0 when struck, the player is [[Lifesteal|healed]] for 3 HP. This effect works on any enemy, including bosses.
