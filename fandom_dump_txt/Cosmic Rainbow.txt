The '''Cosmic Rainbow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]]. When fired, it spawns 3 rainbows in the direction of the cursor in a similar manner as an arrow flying out of a bow. Additionally, 2 more rainbows fall from the sky at random locations within the vicinity of the cursor. These rainbows deal constant damage to any entity that collides with them.
