The '''1.1.7.809''' update changed various projectile interactions with [[blocks]]. It also lowered the spawn rates for a few enemies, changed the internal rarities of a few items, and tweaked [[The Devourer of Gods]]' projectiles' dusts.

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Calamitas]]' lasers and [[Cataclysm]]'s fire no longer collide with blocks.
*[[Crabulon]] no longer spawns directly on the player when using the [[Decapodita Sprout]].
*The Devourer of Gods' gravity bombs now emit less dust, and its orbs and Beam Portals no longer emit dust.
*Lowered [[Profaned Energy]]'s spawn rate and made them take up 3 NPC slots.
*Buffed [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s offensive guardian's contact damage from {{dv|0|180}} to {{dv|0|230}}.
*Lowered [[Scorn Eater]]'s spawn rate.
*Armored [[Stasis Probe]]s now take up 2 NPC slots instead of 1.
*[[Ungodly Serpent]] changes:
**Now take up 5 NPC slots instead of 3.
**Lowered spawn rate.
**Now flee if the player is not in [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]].

=== [[Items]] ===
*The following [[weapons]]' projectiles will no longer collide with blocks:
**[[Blight Spewer]]'s flames.
**[[Catastrophe Claymore]]'s bolts.
**[[Cosmilamp]]'s Cosmilamp minions.
**[[Demonic Pitchfork]]'s pitchforks.
**[[The Empyrean]]'s flames.
**[[Entropy's Vigil]]'s lasers and fire.
**[[Everglade Spray]]'s spray.
**[[Eviscerator]]'s Blood Clots.
**[[Havoc's Breath]]'s fireballs.
**[[Impaler]]'s exploding stakes.
**[[Meowthrower]]'s flames.
**[[Oblivion]]'s lasers.
**Thrown [[Tarragon Throwing Dart]]s.
**[[Terra Flameburster]]'s flames.
**[[True Bloody Edge]]'s blade projectiles.
**[[True Excalibur Shortsword]]'s beams.
**[[True Night's Stabber]]'s beams.
**[[True Tyrant's Ultisword]] and [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]]'s Blazing Phantom Blades.
*Thrown [[Crystal Piercer]]s and [[Xeroc Pitchfork]]s no longer bounce.
*Resprited [[Effulgent Feather]].
*[[Elysian Aegis]], [[Profaned Core]], [[Rune of Kos]], [[Treasure Bag (Brimstone Elemental)]], [[Twisting Nether]], and [[Unholy Essence]] now internally use the {{rare|9}} rarity, meaning they can no longer be destroyed in [[Lava]].
*Buffed [[Neptune's Bounty]]'s damage from 330 to 380.
*[[Triactis' True Paladinian Mage-Hammer of Might]] now uses 1 [[True Paladin's Hammer]] in its recipe instead of 1 [[Paladin's Hammer]].
