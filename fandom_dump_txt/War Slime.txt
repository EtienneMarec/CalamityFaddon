The '''War Slime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]] after [[Ravager]] has been defeated. It attacks by jumping towards the player and shooting high-velocity brimstone darts whenever they get too close to it or travel above it.

The War Slime is one of the required slimes to summon [[Astrum Aureus]].
