The '''Magna Striker''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]]. Each shot consumes 4 [[bullet]]s and fires magna blasts in 4-round bursts. Each shot has a use time of 5. The opal shots deal 75% of the weapon's damage, and the magna shots deal full damage.
