The '''Fleshly Megaphone''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Magic weapons|Magic Weapon]] dropped by the [[Destroyer of Sanity]]. When used, it fires out the letter "A" that bounces off tiles, pierces 10 times, and lasts for a moderate time before dissipating. This letter inflicts the '''Irritated''' Debuff, making the inflicted enemy take and deal 50% more damage. The letter also slows down by 5% upon hitting an enemy.

{{Modifier|magiclow}}

==Notes==
*The projectile it fires grants half the usual I-Frames.

==Tips==
*Its debuff's effect serves as a high risk, high reward type, making it riskier to get hit via enemies that already hits hard.
