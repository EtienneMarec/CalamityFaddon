The '''Severed Eye.exe''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Flails]] that, while being swung, fires out blood streams from its eye periodically, dealing 50% of the weapon's damage. Additionally, when thrown, its eye detaches, being able to pierce through enemies, bounce off tiles, and firing out said blood streams onto the closest enemies for some time before expiring. Said detached eye has 85% of the weapon's base damage.

{{modifier|meleealt}}

== Tips ==
*Because of its ability, it is most effective in dealing with large clumps of enemies in a tight space.
