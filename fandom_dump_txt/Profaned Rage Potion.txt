The '''Profaned Rage Potion''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] made from [[Unholy Essence]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Profaned Rage''' [[buff]], boosting the players critical strike chance by 12% and flight speed by 5%. The player will also become immune to the [[Rage Potion|Rage]] buff. It can be considered the upgrade to the vanilla Rage Potion.
