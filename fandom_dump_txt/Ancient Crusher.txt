The '''Ancient Crusher''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]]. When enemies were hit, it created spikes that dealt additional damage for a few seconds. Spikes that critically hit dealt 50% damage. The spikes appeared wherever the enemy was hit, making this more effective on stationary or slow-moving targets.

Its best [[modifier]] was '''Legendary'''.
