The '''Soul of Oxygen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Oxygen]].

When equipped, swinging any [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] will summon gusts of wind from the player which deal high knockback but no damage.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.

* Intriguing, thank you. It is relieving to know civilization still survives in the skies above despite the onslaught of the [[Wyverns]]. It seems this soul sealed alongside violent wind used for divination.
* Perhaps they used this to imagine alternate timelines which they preferred to our own. It is best if we do not waste its remaining power.

After this, Permafrost will be able to summon [[tgc:Ecto Mist|Ecto Mist]].
