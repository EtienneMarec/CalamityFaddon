'''Tenebris''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[block]] found naturally generated within the [[Abyss]]. After [[Calamitas]] has been defeated, it can also convert nearby [[Planty Mush]] into more Tenebris blocks. It requires at least a [[Pickaxe Axe]] or [[Drax]] to mine.

It is used in crafting many aquatic items, typically in conjunction with [[Depth Cells]] and [[Lumenyl]].

== Notes ==
*Tenebris may also be obtained from [[Abyssal Crate]]s after [[Calamitas]] has been defeated.
*This block can be incredibly hard to find, as it can blend in with the walls of the Abyss and can be mistaken for other blocks, such as [[Abyss Gravel]].

== Trivia ==
*In Latin, the word "tenebris" means "dark" or "darkness".
