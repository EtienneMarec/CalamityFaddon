The '''Striatic Walkie''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Enemies|enemy]] which spawns naturally in the [[Cavern]] layer after [[Esserwyrm]] has been defeated.It attacks by either running at the player, or standind at one place and shooting 2 lasers on a random direction close to the player. They exist to drop [[Stiriatic Ore]], so that the player cannot use up all the ore available to them.
