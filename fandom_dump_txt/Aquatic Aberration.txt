The '''Aquatic Aberration''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Ocean]] after [[Plantera]] or [[Calamitas]] has been defeated. It flies after the player when far away, then circling them when near. After 5 seconds of circling them, it will dash at the player, leeching on and dealing constant damage if the attack hits.

If any player is not in water while fighting an Aquatic Aberration, it will become “angered” and rapidly chase after the player. It also has double damage and defense while angered.

== Tips ==

* The Aquatic Aberration’s leech does half of its dash damage every second. Therefore, it is advised to stay underwater when fighting one of these, as even one can be overwhelming if it’s angered.
