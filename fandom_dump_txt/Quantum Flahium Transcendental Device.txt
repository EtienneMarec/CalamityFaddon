The '''Quantum Flahium Transcendental Device''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It provides a 40% increase to magic damage, a 20% increase to magic critical strike chance, grants mana regen that increases as the wearer's health lowers, +250 max mana, and a 35% decrease in mana usage. The covering grants the effects of the [[Ethereal Talisman]] and [[Chaos Stone]]. Toggling this covering’s visibility allows you to disable the [[Mana Flower]] effects.
