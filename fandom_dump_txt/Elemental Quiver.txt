The '''Elemental Quiver''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is an upgrade to the [[Artemis Emblem]]. It increases all ranged damage by 15% and critical strike chance by 5%, and provides all effects the Artemis Emblem provides; 20% reduced ammo usage, 5 [[defense]], +2 life regen, and 15% increased pick speed. It also inherits the arrow speed increase from the [[Magic Quiver]].
