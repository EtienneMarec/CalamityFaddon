'''Artificial Sentience System, AX-P03''' (Japanese: 人工感覚システム、AX-P03 ''Jinkō kankaku shisutemu, AX - P 03'') is a secret [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is meant to be fought before the [[Moon Lord]] is defeated.

== Spawn ==

In order to summon AX-P03, you must use the [[Emergency Transfer Machine]], which has a recipe that can be obtained by following these steps:

* Equip a [[Dimensional Communication Transfer Device]] in your accessories.
* Enter any [[Arsenal Labs | Arsenal Lab]] that isn't a Bio-Center.
** A GUI element that looks like a command prompt should appear on your screen that says {{code|inline=y|data sent to 'drdn@exOS-hell2' from 'AXP03@exOS-xsphr3'}}.
*** If you have defeated the Moon Lord, you will not see this.
** The next line will contain a short message {{code|inline=y|success! placed at index:}} and then a hexidecimal code (ex. {{code|inline=y|0x12AB34CD56EF}}). Write it down, as it is random for each world.
* Go to the Underworld Bio-Center (quickly, as you only have 6 minutes to do so).
** One of the computers should be green instead of its normal blue color. Right click on it.
*** If the computer has been destroyed, placing another one back inside the lab will fix it.
** It will display a GUI element that looks like a command line.
*** Typing the 'HELP' command will display a list of commands - 'JUMP', 'EXEC' and 'HELP'.
** Type 'JUMP', add a space, then the code unique to your world.
*** If successful, it will display {{code|inline=y|VALID INDEX}}. Otherwise, it will display {{code|inline=y|INVALID INDEX, RETRY}}
** Now type 'EXEC'. The computer will now display 'ADDING PERSONAL BLUEPRINTS TO USER... SUCCESS'.
* You can now craft the [[Emergency Cortex Transfer Machine]].

Upon summoning AX-P03 with the Emergency Cortex Transfer Machine, the screen will flash white as AX-P03 appears onscreen. 

== Behavior ==

=== Phase 1 ===

In AX-P03's first phase, it will summon 10 Nano Repair Drones in a circular formation around itself. If these drones are not destroyed within 30 seconds, they will heal 5% of AX-P03's health per drone.

AX-P03 will cycle through a multitude of attacks, in order: 

* Rapid fire fast-moving Attack Missiles in a circular formation
* Shoot slow-moving Attack Missiles in a radial, alternating formation
* Shoot five Attack Drones that slowly home in on the player

=== Phase 2 ===

Upon reaching 50% health, AX-P03 will enter Phase 2 and shoot an unavoidable Hyper Neutralizer Drone that homes toward the player and inflicts them with the 'HyperNeutralized' debuff which will cause the player to die in a single hit. AX-P03 will cycle through upgraded versions of its phase 1 attacks:

* Rapid fire fast-moving Attack Missiles a circular formation, this time moving a sine wave
* Shoot slow-moving Attack Missiles in a much more packed radial, alternating formation
* Shoot ten Attack Drones that slowly home in on the player
