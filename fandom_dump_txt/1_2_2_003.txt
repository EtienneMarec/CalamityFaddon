The '''1.2.2.003''' update made a couple of changes to the mod's bosses and other content.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' body in phase 2 will no longer take damage from attacks which deal 6000 or more damage.
*The Devourer of Gods' tail in phase 2 will no longer take damage from attacks which deal 5000 or more damage.
*[[Plantera]]'s tentacles now gain a {{revengeance|1.25x}} health and damage modifier in Revengeance Mode.
*Buffed [[Supreme Calamitas]]' contact damage from {{dv|300|480|600}} to {{dv|500|800|1000}}.

=== Items ===
*Cut [[Triactis' True Paladinian Mage-Hammer of Might]]'s projectile damage in half.
