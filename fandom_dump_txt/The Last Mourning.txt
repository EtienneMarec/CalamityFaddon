'''The Last Mourning''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Baleful Harvester]]. Upon striking an enemy, Mourning Skulls and Flaming Pumpkins will spawn from all directions off-screen and move in towards the enemy that was struck. Mourning Skulls explode into two fire orbs upon hitting an enemy. The sword, the Mourning Skulls and their fire orbs inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]].
