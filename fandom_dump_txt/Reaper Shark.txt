The '''Reaper Shark''' (Japanese: 死神ザメ ''Shinigami zame'') is a [[Mini Bosses|mini boss]] that spawns in the 3rd and 4th layers of the [[Abyss]]. It is initially stealthed as it hunts for prey, growling to indicate its presence. If approached or attacked by the player, the Reaper Shark will roar and enter an enraged state. Once enraged, it attempts to hurt the player by swimming directly into them.

At 50% HP, the Reaper Shark will briefly go invincible and roar, enraging further. It will then perform a series of faster, angled dash attacks, before resuming its prior behavior. Upon being defeated, the Reaper Shark will burst into several [[Front Gar]]s.

If you right click the '''Reaper Shark''' with 22 [[Scoria Bar|Scoria Bars]] in your hand, it will make an extra loud roar, swim to layer 3 of the abyss, and become the [[Volcanic Reaper Shark]], a buffed scoria-themed version of the Reaper Shark.

It can be detected with the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].
