The '''Holy Mackerel Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] and upgrade of the [[Herring Staff]]. It summons a Holy Mackerel which charges at enemies in order to rapidly attack them while inflicting the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]]. Unlike most minions, the mackerel only takes up half a minion slot, allowing the player to summon twice as many mackerels as one-slot minions.
