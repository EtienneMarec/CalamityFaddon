The '''Barocraw''' is an [[enemy]] that appears in the 3rd and 4th layers of the [[Abyss]]. They behave similarly to the [[Mantis Shrimp]] and when a player is hit they will be inflicted with the following [[debuffs]] for 1.5 ({{dv|||30}}) seconds:
*[[Abyssal Flames]]
*[[Armor Crunch]]
*[[Brimstone Flames]]
*[[Burning]]
*[[Burning Blood]]
*[[Crush Depth]]
*[[Cursed Inferno]]
*[[Fish Alert]]
*[[Glacial State]]
*[[God Slayer Inferno]]
*[[Holy Flames]]
*[[Moon Burnt]]
*[[Noxus Fumes]]
*[[Omnislime]]
*[[Plague]]
*[[Shadowflame]]
*[[Venom]]
*[[Vulnerability Hex]]
*[[Warped]]
*[[Weak Petrification]]
*[[Whispering Death]]


After defeating [[Calamitas]], they drop [[Baronacre]]; a versatile hardmode [[crafting material]].
