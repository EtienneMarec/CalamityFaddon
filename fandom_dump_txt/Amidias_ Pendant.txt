'''Amidias' Pendant''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is dropped by the [[Giant Clam]] [[mini boss]] after the [[Desert Scourge]] has been defeated. It causes three different Prism Shards to fall from the sky every 2 seconds: 2 small functionally identical shards that deal 15 damage, and a large shard that deals 30 damage and homes in onto enemies.
