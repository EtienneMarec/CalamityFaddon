The '''Gel Sword''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it releases a wide wave projectile with both it and the projectile emitting purple slime particles and inflicting the [[Slime (debuff)|Slime]] [[debuff]] upon striking enemies. The projectile pierces once, passes through tiles, and slows down after hitting one enemy. It is possible for the projectile to hit the same enemy twice.
