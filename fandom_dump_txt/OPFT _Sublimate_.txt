'''OPFT "Sublimate"''' (Ozone Powered FlameThrower) is a craftable post-[[Noxus]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It shoots a beam that can pierce through 8 tiles/enemies.

When attacking with right-click, OPFT "Sublimate" changes sprite, the player's outline and eyes get a green glow, and the weapon gets Cyanocharged.  In this form the beam can pierce through infinite tiles and enemies, deals 500% damage, but is harder to aim up and down. Cyanocharging has a 60 second cooldown and lasts 10 seconds.
