'''The Sevens Striker''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[ranged weapon]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), the slot machine on the gun spins for two seconds, rolling one of four attacks at a random chance based on the type of coin. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), a burst of 10 coins are inaccurately fired, each coin having a 20% chance to be consumed.

{{modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The type of coin chosen differs for each type of fire:
**For the primary fire, the coin prioritized is the one at the highest slot, regardless of value.
**For the secondary fire, the coin prioritized is the one with the highest value. If no Gold Coins are available, Platinum Coins are automatically consumed by breaking down into Gold Coins on use.
*Coins can only be taken from the player's inventory and not from the player's [[Piggy Bank]], [[Safe]], [[Defender's Forge]], or [[Void Vault]].
