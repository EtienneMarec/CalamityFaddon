'''Pine's Penetrator''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[sword]] sold by [[The General]]. When used, it fires ornaments projectiles that are affected by gravity after 2 seconds. Its projectiles do as much damage as the sword itself, similar to the [[Chlorophyte Claymore]].

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*The item's name is a tribute to a user named "Pine" who was a popular server owner on ''[[wikipedia:The_Blockheads_(video_game)|The Blockheads]]'', and good friend of one of the mod's developers.
*The weapon is functionally and statistically identical to the [[Christmas Tree Sword]].
