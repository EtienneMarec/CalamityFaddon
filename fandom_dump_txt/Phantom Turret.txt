'''Phantom Turrets''' were Godseeker Mode [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] [[enemies]] that used to spawn during the [[Polterghast]] boss fight.

Once spawned, Phantom Turrets would attempt to ram the player while shooting Phantom Bolts. The red variants would fire red Phantom Bolts and moved slightly faster than their blue counterparts.
