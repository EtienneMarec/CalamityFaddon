The '''Divine Intervention''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that is dropped rarely by [[Meteor Head]]s. Upon striking an enemy with the blade, 3-5 gravity-affected sparks fling outwards from the hit enemy, and a meteor head is summoned from offscreen that homes in on enemies. The sparks deal 10% of the weapon's damage each, and the meteor head does 200% of the weapon's damage. 

In addition to these effects, the player is granted the '''Holy Flame''' buff which increases melee speed by 15% and causes [[stealth]] to generate 5% faster. Enemies hit by the blade or any of its projectiles are inflicted with '''Hell Judgement''' which deals 3 damage per second to enemies and reduces their defense by 3.

Uniquely amongst other melee weapons, the Divine Intervention is able to perform a [[stealth strike]] in which the sword is thrown towards the cursor.
