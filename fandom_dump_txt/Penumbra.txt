The '''Penumbra''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[Bombs|bomb]]. It fires shadow bombs that charge in the direction of the player's cursor and explode into homing souls.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Penumbra will cause a shadow bomb to appear at the location of the cursor, quickly exploding into many homing souls.
