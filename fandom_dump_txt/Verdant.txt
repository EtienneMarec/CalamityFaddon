The '''Verdant''' is a Godseeker Mode [[yoyo]] crafted from [[Uelibloom Bar]]s. If enemies are nearby, the Verdant will randomly fire leaf crystals at them, which deal 80% of the yoyo's damage. The leaves can pass through tiles but cannot pierce enemies.
