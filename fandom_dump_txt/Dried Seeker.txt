The '''Dried Seeker''' was a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] [[enemy]] that was summoned by the [[Desert Scourge]]. It was a small worm that attacked the player by digging through the ground towards them in order to deal contact damage.
