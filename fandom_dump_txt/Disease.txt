The '''Disease''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon killing an enemy with it, it will release homing plague seekers.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemies hit by it will automatically spawn plague seekers regardless of living status.

Like every [[shortsword]], it has an incredibly short range, but unlike most other vanilla shortswords, it has autoswing.
