The '''A M S''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Magic weapons|Magic Weapon]] that drops from the [[Wall of Bronze]]. It acts as a reusable [[Bomb]], and shares the same properties. Its stats cannot be altered, and it cannot gain modifiers.

== Notes ==
*The A M S's stats are identical to a regular [[Bomb]], although it can be reused indefinitely, as it only costs mana to use.
*Due to its low stats and low speed, the A M S is not recommended to be used as a weapon.
