The '''Lacescent Pistol''' is a Post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[gun]] that is an upgrade of [[Abysscent Pistol]] and the final upgrade to [[Shattered Pistol]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[Ammunition|ammo]], it fires a skylac stone that shatters into 3-5 smaller pieces with a slight spread after travelling about 5 tiles. The skylac stone deals 150% of the weapon's damage, while the pieces deal 25% each. Both the skylac stone and the pieces do not pierce through enemies nor go through walls, but inflict the [[Evercold]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 2 seconds.
