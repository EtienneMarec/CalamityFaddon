The '''Epidemic Shredder''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]]. It automatically throws a returning plague disk that pierces five times and inflicts the [[Plague]] [[debuff]]. The disk can bounce on tiles indefinitely until it reaches the player or despawns. The disk also releases homing plague seekers on each bounce and on enemy hits, dealing a quarter of the base damage and inflicting the Plague debuff.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Epidemic Shredder will cause the next disk to release plague seekers as it travels.
