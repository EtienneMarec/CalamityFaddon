'''Whiskey''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Whiskey''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces [[defense]], but boosts damage, critical strike chance and knockback.

While the effects of the Whiskey debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
