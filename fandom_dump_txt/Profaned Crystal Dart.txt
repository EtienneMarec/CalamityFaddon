'''Profaned Crystal Darts''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[darts]] that are an upgrade to [[tgc:Crystal Dart|Crystal Darts]]. It ricochets up 6 times from walls and enemies, piercing them. Every time the dart ricochets, it summons a small profaned rock that homes onto the closest enemy. The profaned rock deals 10% of the dart's base damage and inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 2 seconds. Profaned Crystal Darts serve as a counterpart to [[Profaned Fire Dart]]s.
