The '''Dialogue System''' is a mechanic that would be introduced by the Tranquility Mod. Some dialogues would appear during certain cutscenes, e.g the [[Brimstone Elemental]]'s finising cutscene. The dialogue would let the player choose the reply option. The dialogues would have their own UI: consisting of text field, a portrait of whoever's dialogue this is and the buttons with reply options.

== Trivia ==
