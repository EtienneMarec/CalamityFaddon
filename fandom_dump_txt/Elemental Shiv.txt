The '''Elemental Shiv''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[shortsword]] that autoswings. It fires a homing projectile that can pass through blocks but cannot pierce enemies. Once these projectiles impact an enemy, six magical shivs are summoned off-screen to the left and right-side of the player, flying towards the hit enemy. As with the initial projectile, the summoned shivs will pierce blocks. The initial projectile will fire horizontally to the right or left, until enemies are nearby, which will cause the shots to curve into the enemy.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, short travelling beams are shot out which split into 5 more beams which also create more summoned shivs and heal the player for 5 health per hit. Additionally, player melee speed, defense, and damage reduction are boosted by 15%.
