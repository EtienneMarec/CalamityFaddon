'''Breakthrough''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spears|spear]]. Right clicking will cause the spear to constantly spew forth icy winds which can give enemies the [[Frozen]] debuff. Attacking frozen enemies will remove the debuff from them, but will deal immense damage, and will cause a small ice explosion that can hurt nearby enemies.
