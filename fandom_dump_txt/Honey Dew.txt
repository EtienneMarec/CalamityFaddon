The '''Honey Dew''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It grants 5% [[Damage Reduction]], 9 [[Defense]] and 1 Life Regen while in the [[Jungle]] biome and provides immunity to the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuff]]s. it also gives the effect of the [[Honey (buff)|Honey]] [[buff]] anywhere. Additionally, the player receives 25% less damage from most bees, hornets, and their projectiles.
