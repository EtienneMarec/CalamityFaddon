The '''Supercharged Dart Blaster''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Dart guns|dart gun]] and an upgrade to the [[Charged Dart Blaster]]. It rapidly fires blue plasma darts that create small explosions upon enemy or tile hits. Right clicking unleashes an electric wave that splits into 9 more waves over time, covering a larger area.
