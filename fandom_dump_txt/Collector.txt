The '''Collector''' is an [[NPC]] vendor that is part of the Calamity Mod's crossover with [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cells Dead Cells].

The Collector will move into an available NPC house if the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty [[tgc:House|house]].
*[[Skeletron]] has been defeated.
*The player has entered the deeper dungeon to the depths where voided dungeon enemies can spawn.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will defend himself with a unique spinning attack. Additionally, he can heal himself to full health by drinking a flask.

==Turn in blueprint==
When the player selects the Collector's "Shop" option while possessing a [[Blueprint]] in their inventory, it will automatically be removed from the player's inventory and a new item will be unlocked to buy from him. Once this is done the respective blueprint will stop being able to drop. The Collector has two unique quotes for this interaction:

'''When delivering a blueprint:'''
*"Excellent! Bring me more of these and I'll let you in on some of my more... experimental items."

'''If no item is unavailable:'''
*"It seems you've found every unique blueprint. There's nothing left to collect..."

== Names ==
The Collector lacks a name prefix and will thus will always be referred to as "The Collector" in line with his naming in Dead Cells.

== Trivia ==
*The Collector is four blocks tall instead of the typical three of players and NPCs.
*The Collector is meant to be the same collector from Dead Cells.
**Many of the collector's quotes are based off his lines in Dead cells as well as a secret encounter with him at the end of the game.
***The Collector's spin attack is based off his unique skill, collector's syringe.
*The quote after defeating [[Polyphemalus]] is a reference to the boss Conjuctivius.
*The Collector can be killed despite his healing if attacked by a boss with very high damage or is hit very rapidly.
**If The Collector is killed, there is a chance the message in chat will instead display "Oh my god! They killed the Collector!" in reference to another rare line from Dead Cells.
