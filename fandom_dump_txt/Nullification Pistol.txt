The '''Nullification Pistol''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that has a chance of dropping from the [[Martian Saucer]] in the [[Martian Madness]] event. It does not use any ammo. Each shot will alter its target in some way, as long as it is not a [[boss]]. The following effects can happen at an equal chance:
*Grow 100% bigger
*Shrink 50% smaller
*Deal 10 more contact damage
*Deal 10 less contact damage
*Become completely resistant to [[knockback]]
*Remove all knockback resistance
*Increase [[defense]] by 5
*Decrease defense by 5
