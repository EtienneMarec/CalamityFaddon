'''Endothermic Energy''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] sold by the [[Starborn Princess]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. It is occasionally used as an alternative to [[Nightmare Fuel]], or in conjunction with [[Darksun Fragment]]s, Nightmare Fuel, and [[Phantoplasm]] to make [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]]s.

{{dei}} Endothermic Energy can also be placed.

== Trivia ==
*The item is a reference to the Endothermic Fire from ''[[wikipedia:Don't Starve|Don't Starve]]'', which cools the player. 
*Endothermic Energy originated as an item obtained from various [[Frost Moon]] enemies after [[The Devourer of Gods]] had been defeated, dropping from all enemies in small quantities, [[Everscream]] in a somewhat larger quantity, [[Santa-NK1]] in an even larger quantity, and [[Ice Queen]] in a colossal quantity.
