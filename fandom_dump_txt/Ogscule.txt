The '''Ogscule''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Classless weapons|classless weapon]] that has a 100% chance to be dropped by [[Demon Eyes]] when killed with a full set of  [[Ebonwood Chair]] coverings equipped, using the item will lock the weapon directly on the position of the player, and will move around with them.

The [[Wulfrum miner]] [[Boss]] requires there to be no Ogscule in the player's inventory while being summoned.
