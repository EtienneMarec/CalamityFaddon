'''Coruplent armor''' is a craftable Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set. It requires 7 [[Disgusting Meat]] to craft the entire set.

It consists of a '''Coruplent Mask''', '''Coruplent Chestplate''', '''Coruplent Pants''', and '''Corpulent Shoes'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*14 defense
*10 melee damage
*12% increased movement speed
*Attacks inflict the [[Burning Blood]] debuff.
*The removal of the melee speed cap.
