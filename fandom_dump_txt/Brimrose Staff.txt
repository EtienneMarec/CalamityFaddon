The '''Brimrose Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. When used, it fires a single short laser beam that instantly splits into two beams, with the resulting beams also splitting into two more beams. There are a total of four splits, resulting in an immediate flower of 16 beam-endings being created a short distance from the player in the direction fired. The beams inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] and do not pierce enemies. The beams will bounce off of tiles, causing subsequent splits to have skewed angles and be shorter in size.
