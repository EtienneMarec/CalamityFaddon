The '''Cursed Dagger''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[dagger]] that is dropped by [[Clinger]]s. It inflicts [[Cursed Inferno]] to any enemies hit and can pierce through 2 enemies or bounce twice off of tiles. It disappears upon its third impact. Like the [[Shadowflame Knife]], it is heavily affected by gravity. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Ichor Spear]] ({{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} [[Ichor Dagger]]).

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Cursed Dagger causes the resulting dagger to release gravity-affected sparks and balls of Cursed Flames as it travels.
