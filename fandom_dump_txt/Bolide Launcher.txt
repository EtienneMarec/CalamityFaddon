The '''Bolide Launcher''' is a Godseeker Mode [[bow]] that drops from [[The Starborn Moth]]. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as ammunition, they are converted into bright bolides that can pierce up to four targets and will phase through tiles. The weapon will additionally fire a spread of two Galactica Comets that inflict the [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]]. These comets deal 250% of the weapons damage and unlike Galactica Comets fired from the [[Starmada]], they do not phase through tiles.
