Aspect of the End is a craftable [[Hardmode]] sword. In addition to being a [[true melee]] weapon, right-clicking can also allow the player to teleport, at the cost of 50 mana. Teleporting does not inflict [[Chaos State]]. If the player has an Enderman pet then the sword gets a 50 damage boost after teleporting for 6 seconds.
