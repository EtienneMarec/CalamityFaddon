The '''Spirit Glyph''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. Whenever a [[minion]] hits an enemy, the player will gain one of these buffs: Spirit Power, Spirit Defense or Spirit Regen.

'''Spirit Regen''' grants +5 life regen.
'''Spirit Defense''' increases [[defense]] by 10 and damage reduction by 5%.
'''Spirit Power''' increases minion damage by 20%.

== Tips ==
*Although it is primarily a summoner covering, the good defensive boosts from '''Spirit Defense''' and '''Spirit Regen''' make it a decent option for players of other classes if they are willing to use minions.

== Trivia ==
*The shield in the '''Spirit Defense''' buff icon is the [[Shield of the Ocean]].
*The knife in the '''Spirit Power''' buff icon uses the same palette as the [[Warblade]].
*The heart in the '''Spirit Regen''' buff icon is a [[Heart]].
*This item was previously named the '''Spirit Generator'''.
