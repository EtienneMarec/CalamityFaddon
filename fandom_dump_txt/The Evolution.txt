'''The Evolution''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that drops from the [[Ceaseless Void]]. Any projectile that strikes the player will be reflected. When a projectile is reflected, it will deal 10 times its original damage to any enemy it touches, and the player will gain a life regeneration boost. This effect can only occur every 120 seconds. Additionally, if the next projectile to hit the player is of the same type, the projectile will deal 15% less damage. This effect will only trigger once per projectile type. So, if the player were to get hit by three projectiles of the same type - reflecting the first - only the second projectile would deal 15% less damage.
