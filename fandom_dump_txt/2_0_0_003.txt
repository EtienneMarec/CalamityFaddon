The '''2.0.0.003''' (or '''2.0.0.3''') update ported the mod to tModLoader v2022.6.96. This update fixed several bugs and issues from previous Calamity updates.

== Additions ==
*Added more NPC-based happiness quotes for the [[Drunk Princess]]. <!-- Loves: Stylist and Zoologist; Likes: Truffle and Party Girl (liking Princess already existed as of 2.0.0.001); Dislikes: Tavernkeep and Tax Collector; Hates: Goblin Tinkerer and Angler -->

== Changes ==
=== Balance and Reworks ===
*[[Bladecrest Oathsword]] and [[Old Lord Oathsword]] are no longer true melee weapons.
*Reworked the [[Chlorophyte Armor]] set bonus. The crystal no longer fires leaf projectiles at enemies. Instead, it releases a short-range pulse of energy every 3 seconds which deals 300 damage towards enemies and heals all nearby players including the wearer for 10 HP. The heal has a cooldown of 2.5 seconds. Both the damage and healing amount increase with the player’s strongest class damage.
*Nerfed the following [[modifiers]]:
**The '''Guarding''' modifier now provides {{color|+7|green}} defense once [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] has been defeated, instead of {{color|+8|green}} defense.
**The '''Armored''' modifier now provides {{color|+10|green}} defense once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated, instead of {{color|+11|green}} defense.
**The '''Warding''' modifier now provides {{color|+11|green}} defense once [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated, instead of {{color|+12|green}} defense. It also now provides {{color|+12|green}} defense once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated, instead of {{color|+15|green}} defense.

=== Recipes ===
*Added all vanilla [[tgc:Food and drink potions|Food and drink items]] to the "Any Food Item" recipe group. <!-- No, I will not write this all out, also :borgorpat: - Merkalto -->
*The following items now craft into each other while at an [[Anvil]] in [[Ecto Mist]]: <!-- or Ecto Mist, whatever you want, it doesn’t matter -->
**The [[Crimson Rod]] and [[Vilethorn]].
**The [[Ball O' Hurt]] and [[The Rotted Fork]].
**The [[Musket]] and [[The Undertaker]].
*The following items now craft into each other while at a [[Tinkerer's Workshop]] in Ecto Mist:
**The [[Bloody Worm Tooth]] and [[Rotten Brain]].
**The [[Brain of Confusion]] and [[Worm Scarf]].
**The [[Crimson Heart]] and [[Shadow Orb]].
*[[Bloody Edge]] now requires 5 [[Purified Gel]] in both of its recipes.
*[[Caustic Tear]] now requires being near [[water]] to craft, as intended.
*[[DPS Meter]] can now be crafted with 10 [[Wire]] and 5 of Any Gold Bar at an Anvil.
*[[Helium Flash]] now requires 5 [[Auric Bar]]s as intended instead of 1.
*[[Plasma Grenade]] now requires 25 [[Dubious Plating]], 15 [[Mysterious Circuitry]], 8 [[Cosmilite Bar]]s, and 2 [[Ascendant Spirit Essence]] in its recipe, instead of 5 Dubious Plating, 5 Mysterious Circuitry, 1 Cosmilite Bar, and 1 Ascendant Spirit Essence.
*Each set piece of the [[Shroomite armor]] set and [[Spectre armor]] set alternative recipes now require 1 [[Living Shard]] to be crafted.
*[[Terra Blade]]'s recipe which uses [[True Bloody Edge]] now requires a [[Broken Hero Sword]] to craft.
*Both [[Terra Edge]] recipes now require a [[Broken Hero Sword]] to craft.
*True Bloody Edge now requires 3 [[Soul of Fright|Souls of Fright]], [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]], [[Soul of Sight|Souls of Sight]] instead of a Broken Hero Sword in its recipe.

=== Renames ===

**Renamed Encrypted Schematic found in the Ice biome to [[Encrypted Schematic (Ice)]].
**Renamed Encrypted Schematic found in the Jungle to [[Encrypted Schematic (Jungle)]].
**Renamed Encrypted Schematic found in Space to [[Encrypted Schematic (Planetoid)]].
**Renamed Encrypted Schematic found in the Underworld to [[Encrypted Schematic (Underworld)]].
**Renamed Schematic to [[Schematic (Sunken Sea)]].
*Renamed Treasure Bag (Exo Mechs) to [[Treasure Box (Exo Mechs)]].

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Draconic Destruction]].

=== Additional Changes ===
*Updated the Calamity Mod Steam Workshop description to link to Music Mod and vice versa with the Calamity Mod Music Steam Workshop description.
*[[Adrenaline Meter]], [[Defense damage]], [[Rage Meter]], and [[Stealth]] sound effects are now only played client side in multiplayer.
*The [[Advanced Display]], [[Decryption Computer]], [[Long Ranged Sensor Array]], [[Voltage Regulation System]] tooltips now clearly mention which Schematic it allows the Codebreaker to decrypt.
**Additionally, Schematic-locked recipes now clearly mention which Schematic must be decrypted before their recipe is unlocked in their tooltips.
*Added a line to the [[Arid Artifact]]'s tooltip explaining that it will stop a sandstorm if used while one is ongoing.
*The [[Arms Dealer]], [[Dryad]], and [[Merchant]] Town NPCs no longer sell any Crimson or Corruption related items, excluding evil biome seeds.
*[[Astral Ore]], [[Eutrophic Sand]], [[Navystone]], [[Prism Shard]]s, [[Sea Prism]] can no longer be [[tgc:Block Swap|Block swapped]].
*Gave [[Azathoth]] the "Developer item" tooltip tag.
*[[Boss summoning items]] now clearly mention boss biome enrages, if applicable, on their tooltips.
**Additionally, capitalized all biomes listed on Boss summoning item tooltips.
**The [[Rune of Kos]]'s item tooltip now dynamically updates depending on which biome the player is in.
*[[The Community]]'s item tooltip now dynamically updates and clearly mentions what stats it increases when worn.
*Updated many of the Drunk Princess' happiness quotes.
*[[Enchanted Starfish]] now has auto-reuse.
*[[Golem]] now directly drops a [[Picksaw]] on all World Difficulties when defeated for the first time. <!-- 2 in 1 bug fix and auto picksaw moved out of Golem’s treasure bag. -->
*[[Guide Voodoo Doll]]s can now stack up to 999.
*Calamity and vanilla [[Ores]] that generate after defeating a boss, excluding [[Exodium Cluster]]s, now generate deeper in the world. <!-- On medium worlds, a few ores stop generating at the top of Hell, fun fact! Check source code for ore generation ranges. - Merkalto -->
*[[Pwnhammer]] now mentions that vanilla Hardmode ores spawn after defeating Mechanical bosses for the first time if the <code>Early Hardmode Progression Rework</code> configuration is enabled.
*The [[Roxcalibur]] tooltip now dynamically updates whether or not the world is in Hardmode.
*Decreased [[Wooden Boomerang]] sell price from {{sc|10}} to {{cc|20}}.
*Added the "New Shop Icon" support to the [[Golfer]], [[Princess]], and [[Zoologist]] Town NPCs.
*Added several Town NPC names:
**Added the name Danmaku for the [[Guide]] Town NPC.
**Added the name Cap'n Deek for the [[Pirate]] Town NPC.
**Added the name Catalyst for the [[Princess]] Town NPC.
