The '''1.1.7.704''' update added a few new [[items]], along with several nerfs to [[Grand Guardian]], and a resprite.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Arch Amaryllis]], [[Chicken Cannon]], and [[Malachite]].
*Added [[Decapodita Sprout]].
*Added [[Providence Trophy]].

== Changes ==
*Nerfed [[Grand Guardian]]'s damage from 320 to 300, made its explosions and bolts deal 75% of the weapon's damage, and its bolts now last for 2.5 seconds instead of 5.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Nano Purge]].
