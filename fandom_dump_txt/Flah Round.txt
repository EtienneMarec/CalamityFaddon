'''Flah Rounds''' are Godseeker Mode [[bullets]] crafted from [[Flahium Bar]]s. Upon contact with anything, it explodes and bounces. It can bounce 9 times before finally releasing a large explosion similar to [[Flash Round]]s. Small explosions deal 33% of the damage of the initial shot and the final explosion deals 150% of the damage of the initial shot.
