The '''1.2.4.205''' update added [[Shrine]]s and several buffs to some Godseeker Mode weapons. It also added [[Treasure Bag]]s for a few [[bosses]] missing them, and made some adjustments to [[The Slime God]] fight. This update was the final version in the 1.2 series of updates.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Treasure Bag (Astrum Deus)]], [[Treasure Bag (Bumblebirb)]], and [[Treasure Bag (Ravager)]].
*Added Cavern Shrine, Corruption Shrine, Crimson Shrine, Desert Shrine, Granite Shrine, Ice Shrine, Marble Shrine, Mushroom Shrine, Surface Shrine, and Underworld Shrine.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*[[Astrum Deus]], [[Bumblebirb]], and [[Ravager]] now drop their respective Treasure Bags.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] no longer drops the [[Murasama]].
*[[Slime God]] changes:
**Buffed the Ebonian Slime God's health from {{dv|4000|6400|8000|16,000}} to {{dv|5000|8000|9600|16,000}}.
**Buffed the Crimulan Slime God's health from {{dv|3000|4800|6400|12,800}} to {{dv|4000|6400|8000|12,800}}.
**Buffed the split Ebonian Slime God's health from {{dv|2000|3200|4000|8000}} to {{dv|2500|4000|4800|8000}}.
**Buffed the split Crimulan Slime God's health from {{dv|1500|2400|3200|6400}} to {{dv|2000|3200|4000|6400}}.
**Lowered the speed of the large slimes.
**Split large slimes now spawn further apart.
**Split large slimes now have a longer distance before they speed up towards the player.
**Tweaked split large slimes' speeds.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**Decreased hit cap in Revengeance Mode from {{revengeance|15}} to {{revengeance|10}}.
**Adjusted [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] resistance in Normal, Expert, and Revengeance Mode.
**Brimstone Hearts are now invincible for 10 seconds after spawning.

=== [[Items]] ===
*Buffed [[The Burning Sky]]'s damage from 165 to 210.
*Buffed [[Celestus]]' damage from 85 to 104.
*Buffed [[Chicken Cannon]]'s damage from 20 to 22.
*Buffed [[Dragon Rage]]'s damage from 450 to 683.
*Buffed [[Dragon's Breath]]'s damage from 205 to 236.
*Buffed [[Greatsword of Blah]]'s damage from 195 to 247.
*Buffed [[Infernal Spear]]'s damage from 720 to 1040.
*Murasama changes:
**Decreased localNPCHitCooldown from 2 to 0. 
**Changed tooltip to say "ID and power-level locked.  Prove your strength or have the correct user ID to wield this sword." instead of "Revengeance drop".
**Can now only be used if [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] has been defeated or if the player is named "Sam" or "Samuel Rodrigues". 
*Buffed [[Photoviscerator]]'s damage from 46 to 50.
*Nerfed [[Vivid Clarity]]'s damage from 160 to 136.
*[[Void of Calamity]] now provides a 15% increase to all damage instead of increasing summon damage by 10% and all other damage types by 15%.
*[[Void of Extinction]] changes:
**Increased damage boost from 10% to 15%.
**Now grants temporary immunity to [[Lava]] and 25% increased damage while submerged in it.
**Now requires a [[Magma Stone]] to craft.
*Buffed [[Voltaic Climax]]'s damage from 100 to 110.
*Buffed [[The Wand]]'s infernado damage from 280 to 390.
*Tweaked the coloring of [[Yharim's Crystal]]'s beam.
*Nerfed [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]]'s damage from 250 to 232.

=== Other ===
*Non-minion player projectiles now despawn after [[Adrenaline Mode]] wears off.
*Tweaked how fast players respawn in Expert Mode.
*Completely overhauled the generation of the [[Profaned Crag]]. 

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited The Wand and Yharim's Crystal.
*Resprited [[Rune of Kos]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Elysian Aegis]]' tooltip erroneously stating that the player must double tap down to activate [[Elysian Guard]] instead of using its dedicated hotkey.
