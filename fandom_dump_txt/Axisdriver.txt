The '''Axisdriver''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Guns|gun]]. When used, it fires a burst of 3 fast exo bullets that can go through tiles and pierce 10 times.  When used with its secondary fire (right-click), it fires a single homing exo bullet that deals 500% of the weapon's damage and explodes on contact with an enemy. Both types of exo bullets summon 5 homing elemental bolts on critical strikes and have a 50% to not consume bullets. Exo bullets, elemental bolts, and exo bullet explosions inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
Elemental bolts additionally inflict [[Brimstone Flames]] for 2 seconds.
