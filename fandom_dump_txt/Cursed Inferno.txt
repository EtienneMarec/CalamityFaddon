'''Cursed Inferno''' is a vanilla [[debuff]] that deals damage over time to anything it afflicts. The Calamity Mod further augments this by causing those afflicted to have their DR ([[Damage Reduction]]) stat multiplicatively reduced by 20%. This effect stacks with most other damage reduction reducing effects. Players will take 20% extra damage instead of having a DR penalty.
