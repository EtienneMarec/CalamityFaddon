The '''Squirrel Prince Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and attack enemies by shooting fireballs which create massive explosion on impact and split into more homing fireballs. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
