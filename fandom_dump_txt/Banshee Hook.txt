The '''Banshee Hook''' is a Godseeker Mode [[spear]] dropped by [[Polterghast]]. It is swung in front of the player at an odd angle, similarly to the [[Ghastly Glaive]], and releases a line of five projectiles at once. Hitting an [[enemy]] directly with the weapon causes a ghostly explosion, dealing rapid damage. Its projectiles go through blocks, ignore [[immunity frames]], pierce infinitely, and will slow to a stop some distance away if uninterrupted.
