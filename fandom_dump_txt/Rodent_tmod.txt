'''Rodent.tmod''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns during the [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. It attacks by charging after the player at high speeds. The enemy can fly, and can pass through walls.

== Trivia ==
*Rodent.tmod is a reference to the creepypasta Sonic.exe.
*A tmod file is the file type used for terraria mods.
*A rodent is is a group of mammal found in the rodentia order which contains such species as rats and guinea pigs.
*The amount of coins this enemy drops is a reference to the number 666, which is known as the devils number.
