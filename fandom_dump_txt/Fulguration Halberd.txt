The '''Fulguration Halberd''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that autoswings. The Fulguration Halberd can be swung both as a [[broadsword]] and a [[spear]] by pressing left and right click respectively. It will inflict [[Burning Blood]] on each successful hit.
