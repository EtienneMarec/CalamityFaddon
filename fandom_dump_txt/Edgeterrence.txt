'''Edgeterrence''' is a Post-[[Hydrabots Invasion]] [[Combat coverings|combat]] [[coverings|covering]]. It grants the following bonuses:

* 50 defence
* 100% more damage
* Doubled max health
* 40% increased max mana
* Abyssal Fireballs release on every hit taken.
* 30% increased damage reduction over 50% health and 75% increased damage reduction under 50% health
* healing potions heal 25% more health and have a 50% shorter cooldown
* reduces enemies contact damage by 25%
* 50% faster health regeneration
* +9 max minion slots
* your minions can confuse your enemies
* a 1 flesh demon is summoned to protect you
** The flesh demon punches your enemies, inflicting bleeding and dealing 500 summon damage per punch.
* a blood demon is summoned for every minion slot
** The blood demon works the same way as the flesh demon but deals 100 damage instead of 400
* summons fiery hands when damaged
** The hands fly towards your enemies and grab them, dealing 100 damage per second
* knockback immunity
* increased speed, damage and immunity time after getting damaged
* Rage bar is changed to now have three levels
** Activating Rage consumes one full bar and grants [[Rage Meter|Rage]]. Activating Rage a second time consumes a full bar and grants [[Bloody Fury]]
** A third activation causes a barrage of [[Gael's Greatsword|Gael Skulls]] to be launched at the cursor, and grants [[Violent Frenzy]]

== Notes == 
This covering cant be used at the same time as [[The Verboten One]]
