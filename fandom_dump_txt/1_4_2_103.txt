The '''1.4.2.103''' update made various optimizations to the mod in both its code and actual content. The update also adjusted the stats of several [[Revengeance Mode]] vanilla bosses and added a few new [[weapons]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Galaxy Smasher]], [[Helium Flash]], [[Nanoblack Reaper]], and [[Stellar Contempt]].

== Changes ==

*Added afterimages to the following projectiles:

*[[Aquatic Scourge]] and [[Desert Scourge]]'s sand blasts.
*[[Astrum Aureus]]' flame crystals and lasers.
*[[Astrum Deus]]' lasers.
*[[Brimstone Elemental]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]]' Brimstone Barrages and Hellblasts.
*[[Bumblebirb]]'s lightning feathers.
*[[Great Sand Shark]]'s sand blasts.

==== Aquatic Scourge ====
*Nerfed head's defense from 40 to 20, its body's defense from 60 to 35, and its tail's defense from 90 to 45.
*Now takes 20% damage from infinitely piercing non-minion projectiles and divides the damage of other piercing projectiles by the amount of times they pierce instead of just having a 50% resistance to [[Holy Arrow]] stars and [[Crystal Bullet]] shards.

==== Astrum Aureus ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|80,000|128,000|163,200|252,800}} to {{dv|96,000|153,600|195,200|299,200}}, and nerfed its defense from 120 to 70.
*Buffed post-Moon Lord health from {{revengeance|640,000}} to {{revengeance|704,000}}.
*Buffed Boss Rush health from {{dv|1,200,000|1,920,000|0|2,240,000}} to {{dv|1,400,000|2,240,000|0|2,560,000}}.
*Flame crystals are now easier to see.

==== Astrum Deus ====
*Increased the amount of celestial fragments dropped from {{dv|6-11|6-12}} to {{dv|12-20|20-32}}, and reworked its movement.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*Revengeance Mode changes:
**Buffed health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.4x|1.9x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.6x|2.3x}}.
**Creepers now have a health multiplier of {{dv|0|0|1.2x|1.4x}}.
**Creepers no longer respawn.
**Can no longer hit the player in phase 1.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Now drops [[Animosity]] in all difficulty modes.
*Nerfed [[Soul Seeker]] health from {{dv|3750|7500}} to {{dv|2500|5000}}.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Increased overall speed.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Increased the amount of [[Cosmilite Brick]]s dropped from 5-15 to {{dv|155-265|205-335}}.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Nerfed health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.8x|2.7x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.25x|1.6x}}, and reduced its Death Mode size modifier.
*Nerfed [[Probe]]'s health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.6x|1.9x}} to {{death|1.25x}}, and removed its Death Mode size modifier.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Revengeance Mode changes:
**Buffed health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.9x|2.9x}} to {{dv|0|0|2.3x|3.4x}}.
**Plantera's Tentacles no longer gain extra health.
**Now starts shooting seeds once all tentacles have been defeated.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Now only enrages when taken to the surface.

==== Providence, the Profaned Goddess ====
*Now drops her biome exclusive drops for all players in multiplayer.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Nerfed Revengeance Mode Skeletron Prime and his arms' health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.6x|2.9x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.15x|1.5x}}.

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Reworked how movement works.

==== [[The Twins]] ====
*Revengeance Mode changes:
**Nerfed Retinazer's health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.8x|3x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.25x|1.4x}}.
**Nerfed Spazmatism's health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.8x|3x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.3x|1.45x}}.
**Decreased speeds slightly.

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Colossal Squid]]s, [[Eidolon Wyrm]]s, and [[Reaper Shark]]s no longer block each others spawn rates.
*Hardmode [[Dungeon Guardian]]s now drop the [[Burden Breaker]].
*Nerfed [[Giant Clam]]'s pearl burst damage from {{dv|150|200}} to {{dv|70|112}}, and its pearl rain damage from {{dv|100|120}} to {{dv|70|112}}.

*Fixed Hadal Stew interacting strangely with quick buff and quick heal.
*Fixed [[King Slime]] only dropping Gel on its first kill.
*Fixed [[Hide of Astrum Deus]] being labeled as an Expert Mode item.
*Fixed [[Moon Lord]]'s core being vulnerable in phase 1.
*Fixed [[Riptide]] having a messed up animation.
*Fixed [[Siren's Heart]]'s Ice Shield buff's description saying the buff absorbs 20% damage instead of 15%.
*Fixed The Slime God only dropping Gel in Normal Mode.
*Fixed [[Spectre armor]] with the [[Spectre Mask]]'s souls not being affected by its 30% damage nerf.
*Fixed [[Statigel Wall]]s being unable to be crafted back into [[Statigel Block]]s.
*Fixed [[Svantechnical]] dropping twice as commonly from Supreme Calamitas as other weapons.
*Fixed [[Grenade Shells]], [[Explosive Shotgun Shell]]s, and [[Magnum Rounds]] only dropping from [[Spazmatism]].
*Fixed [[Victide armor]]'s set bonus activating when using [[Murasama]].
