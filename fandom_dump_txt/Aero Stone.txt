The '''Aero Stone''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. It provides buffs to movement, which makes it a great covering during [[Pre-Hardmode]]. It also causes the player to emit a dim blue light while it is equipped.
