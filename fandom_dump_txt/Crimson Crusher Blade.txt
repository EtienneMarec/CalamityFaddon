The '''Crimson Crusher Blade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it emits faint blood particles and inflicts the [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] [[debuff]] upon striking enemies. While no [[bosses]] are alive, critical strikes caused by this weapon reduce the target's base contact damage by 5.

== Notes ==
*The amount of contact damage reduction will scale with difficulty.
**For example, [[Great Sand Shark]] has a 60% scaling factor in [[Expert Mode]] and 15% scaling factor in [[Revengeance Mode]]. Therefore, the amount of damage decrease will equal to {{dv|5|8|9}}.
*Enemies with 5 or less contact damage can only be reduced up to 1 instead of nullifying damage completely.
*It is considered a good early game weapon due to its high damage and low crafting cost.
*It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Corrupted Crusher Blade]].

== Trivia ==
*Despite the Corrupted Crusher Blade emitting light while swung, the Crimson Crusher Blade does not.
