The '''Charred Relic''' is a [[pet]] summoning item that summons a Brimling. It is obtained by opening the Brimstone Elemental's [[Treasure Bag]] in [[Revengeance Mode]].

The Brimling will float behind the player and follow them very closely. If the player manages to outrun her for too long, she will teleport near them and create some red dust in the process.

The Brimling will also go in her shell form if the player is below 25% of their maximum health.

== Trivia ==
*The Charred Relic resembles a broken version of the [[Charred Idol]], which is used to summon the [[Brimstone Elemental]].
*The Brimling pet is identical in name and appearance to the [[Brimling]] [[enemies]] summoned by the Brimstone Elemental.
