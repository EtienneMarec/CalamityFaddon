'''Crush Depth''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by most [[Abyss]] [[enemies]] and [[weapons]], as well as the [[Depth Crusher]] and its upgrades, the [[Abyss Blade]] and [[Neptune's Bounty]]. It is also inflicted by [[Broken Biome Blade]] and its upgrades while the player is in the [[Ocean]]. If inflicted on an enemy, they lose 30 health per second in [[Pre-Hardmode]], and 90 health per second in [[Hardmode]]. If inflicted on the player, they instead lose 9 health per second. If the player has the [[Depths Charm]] equipped, they will only lose 4.5 health per second.

Additionally, if a player is inflicted with Crush Depth while in the Abyss, their [[tgc:Breath_meter|breath meter]] will drain more quickly.

Crush Depth has its damage reduced by the amount of defense its target has. For the player, 2.5% of their defense (rounded down to the nearest integer) is subtracted from the health lost per second. For enemies, 250% of their defense is subtracted from the health lost per second.

== Notes ==
*Although the debuff does more damage to targets with lower defense, players with 0 defense take no damage from Crush Depth.
*[[NPCs]] that have more than 12 and 36 defense don't take any damage from Depth Crush in Pre-Hardmode and Hardmode respectively, but still emit blue particles from the debuff.
*While in the [[Abyss]], Crush Depth causes afflicted enemies to lose 750 health per second. This does not apply to [[bosses]] or enemies with 0 contact damage.
**However, all enemies that spawn naturally in the Abyss are immune to Crush Depth.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Submarine_depth_ratings#Crush_Depth|Crush depth]] is the rated lowest depth a submarine can go before imploding.
