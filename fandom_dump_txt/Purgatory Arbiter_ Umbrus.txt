'''Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus''' (Japanese: 混沌の神、ノクサス ''Konton no kami, nokusasu'') is an Epilogue [[Superbosses|Superboss]] that can be fought after completing the [[Boss Rush]] [[Events|event]].

== Spawn==
Umbrus does not spawn on its own and requires the player to summon it with [[Genesis]]. While using it, Genesis will glow similarly to [[Terminus]] until Umbrus falls from above.

Umbrus can be fought in any biome at any time.

==Behavior==
Umbrus both deals and takes 25% more damage if a [[Rock]] is placed in the world.

Umbrus has 2 phases:

=== Phase 1 Attack Cycle ===
Umbrus uses attacks in a well defined cycle during Phase 1.

*Umbrus will randomly teleport around the player and leave behind{{item|NoxExplosion|nolink=yes|ext=gif|maxsize=42px|Nox Explosions}} each time.
*It will hover above the player, telegraphing its fall path and the paths of  {{item|NoxSpike|nolink=yes|ext=png|size=9px|Nox Spikes}} before going down.
*It flies directly at the player while shooting {{item|NoxComet|nolink=yes|ext=png|size=9px|Nox Comets}} that move in a wave formation.
*{{item|NoxRift|nolink=yes|ext=png|maxsize=105px|Nox Rifts}} open up near the player that shoot {{item|NoxBlast|nolink=yes|ext=PNG|maxsize=65px|Nox Blasts}} .

===Phase 2 Attack Cycle ===
Umbrus switches to simpler attacks, but Umbrus' stats are also increased.

*It teleports then charges at the player 3 times. After every charge, a trail of {{item|NoxExplosion|nolink=yes|ext=gif|maxsize=42px|Nox Explosions}} is left in its wake.
*It will hover at the player's side and shoot a wide spread of {{item|NoxSpike|nolink=yes|ext=png|size=9px|Nox Spikes}}.
*It flies back and forth above the player, raining down {{item|NoxComet|nolink=yes|ext=png|size=9px|Nox Comets}} that move in a wave formation.
*It will stay immobile while its hands move up and down from the corners of the screen and fire Nox Spikes along with {{item|NoxFireball|nolink=yes|ext=gif|maxsize=42px|Nox Fireballs}} at the player that leave behind a Nox Explosion on impact or after a certain distance.
* It revolves around the player while shooting homing {{item|NoxShard|nolink=yes|ext=gif|maxsize=42px|Nox Shards}}.

===Progression===
Umbrus will perform specific actions once, triggered by it reaching certain amounts of health.

* Upon '''spawning''', Umbrus summons a small ring of Nox Spikes directly on the player. The Nox Spikes are initially harmless and immobile, but they will fly outward and be able deal damage after a few seconds. This is done 5 times total.
*Upon reaching '''{{dv|85|'''90|95|100'''}}%''' health, Umbrus will become invincible and stationary while 3 Chaos Constructs are summoned. The cycle will continue once all Construct have been defeated. Chaos Constructs behave similarly to the Astrageldon Slime and have the following attacks:
**Periodically teleport into the air above the player in an attempt to crush them.
**Fire 9 Nox Spikes of varying speeds in large, asymmetrical shapes.
**Attempt to shoot Nox Spikes directly at the player, and it fires faster as its health decreases.
**Spawn damaging chaos mist platforms in midair to reach the player if they're barely out of reach.
**Teleport near the player if they got too far away.
*Upon reaching '''{{dv|75|'''80|85|90'''}}%''' health, Umbrus will randomly use two of its Phase 1 attacks simultaneously (e.g., charging while both shooting Nox Shards and leaving behind Nox Explosions).
*Upon reaching '''{{dv|60|'''65|70|80'''}}%''' health, Umbrus roars and transitions into its second phase. The background also becomes covered in a dark veil of smoke.
*Upon reaching '''{{dv|45|'''50|55|80'''}}%''' health, {{dv|1|1|1|2}} invincible Chaos Construct is summoned and will fight until the battle ends.
*Upon reaching '''{{dv|10|'''10|10|20'''}}%''' health, Umbrus gains a new attack:
**Umbrus will charge up a {{item|NoxRay|nolink=yes|ext=png|maxsize=42px|Nox Ray}} that shatters the screen, causing immense lag.

===In Revengeance Mode===
Massively increased speed and attack cycle is faster.
Transitions into phases faster.
Summons more projectiles.
Projectiles are 15% faster.

===In Death Mode===
The combat zone is smaller.
Summons 3 Chaos Constructs immediately upon spawning while attacking the player with Nox Spikes.
Transitions into phases even faster.
Damage is further increased by 33% on top of Death Mode's damage increase.
Invincible Chaos Constructs are summoned immediately upon reaching phase 2.

===In Malice Mode===
The combat zone is smaller.
Partially enrages Umbrus, increasing the speed of its projectiles by 60%.
Chaos Constructs move 25% faster.
Umbrus moves 33% faster.

===In Master Mode===
One additional Chaos Construct is summoned in every move that summons them.
Vulnerable Chaos Constructs have 33% more HP on top of Master Mode's health increase.
Umbrus and Chaos Constructs have 15% more damage reduction.
Invincible Chaos Constructs delete projectiles.
Chaos Constructs are 10% larger.
Chaos Constructs gain immunity to [[Demonshade armor]]'s Enrage mechanic, but take 25% more damage from the armour's Red Devil follower.
Enraging Umbrus instantly heals the player for -25% of their health.

===On the For the Worthy seed===
Umbrus is 45% smaller.
Chaos Constructs are 60% bigger.
All projectiles move 25% faster.
All projectiles are 15% bigger.
All attacks inflict [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] and [[God Slayer Inferno]], further increasing damage taken.

== Resistances==
Umbrus takes decreased damage from some items and projectiles. It takes:

*85% damage from  [[Halibut Cannon]], [[Temporal Umbrella]], and [[Vehemence]].
*65% damage from [[Axisdriver]] and [[Heavenly Gale]].
*50% damage from [[Vermillion Angel]]'s stealth strike.

==Notes==

*Umbrus only deals contact damage while not in an immobile state.
*[[Heart|Hearts]] (such as from Heart Statues) hurt for its healing amount instead of heals, making it difficult to recover health.
*The Adrenaline Meter in Revengeance and Death Mode will fill up at a slower rate. It will take 40 seconds to fill up during its fight rather than the typical 30 seconds.
*Umbrus ignores invincibility frames.

==Tips==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating Umbrus, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Umbrus strategies]].}}
*It is highly recommended to complete the Boss Rush a few times with different [[Boss Rush#Boss Rush Curses|curses]] before attempting Umbrus, as doing so will provide permanent buffs.
*Avoid touching Umbrus at all costs, as its contact damage ignores immunity frames and will result in a swift death.
== Trivia ==

*The theme heard during the battle against Umbrus is '''REALITY FAILURE''', which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, the '''Moon Lord's theme''' will play during the entire fight instead.
*Umbrus is considered a '''"Superboss"''' in this mod. In some RPG games, Superbosses are extremely powerful enemies that are very challenging even if the player has endgame equipment.
**It shares this trait with the following bosses: [[Adult Eidolon Wyrm]], [[Vision of the Tyrant]], [[Disgusting Creature: Dron]], [[Esserwyrm]], [[Reactive Guardian, Sodis]], [[Nihil, the Fathomless Seeker]] and [[Exodygen]].
*Umbrus is one of two bosses that drops no weapons and only a tool(s), the other being the [[Profaned Guardians]].
*Umbrus, as the compilation of many other dark gods, is a major Calamity lore figure that is indirectly responsible for the creation of Xeroc, Yharim's rise to power and ultimately the Terrarian's existence. 
*Some of Umbrus’ quotes start with "Hmm…", similar to the quotes said during the Boss Rush event.
**Additionally, Umbrus’ final quote is a callback to the quote said upon entering Tier 5 of the event.
*If the Boss Checklist Mod is installed, its unique despawn message will be "Hmm… you were not as useful as expected. Pathetic."
*If the Calamity Umbrus (Addon Version) Voiceover Mod is installed, Umbrus will audibly speak.
*Umbrus' concept sprite was later reused for [[The Godfather]]'s Critical Slime Core.
