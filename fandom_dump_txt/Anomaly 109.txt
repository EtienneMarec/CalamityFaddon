The '''Anomaly 109''' is a [[furniture]] item. When right-clicked with a [[Bloody Vein]], it is used to summon [[Monstro]].

== Notes ==
*The Anomaly 109 cannot be destroyed by [[explosive]]s.
*The Anomaly 109 can be highlighted by the [[Spelunker Potion]] effect.
*This item is a reference to [https://bindingofisaacrebirth.fandom.com/wiki/Dataminer Dataminer]  from the hit game ''The Binding of Isaac''
