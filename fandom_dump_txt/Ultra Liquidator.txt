The '''Ultra Liquidator''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] which is a direct upgrade to the [[Infernal Rift]]. It automatically fires a set of five rainbow tridents, which summon block-piercing blades that fly from the left and right on enemy hits. Both projectiles inflict [[Cursed Inferno]], [[Brimstone Flames]], and [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] [[debuffs]].

The Ultra Liquidator also boasts a very high base critical strike chance of 34%, greatly improving its damage.
