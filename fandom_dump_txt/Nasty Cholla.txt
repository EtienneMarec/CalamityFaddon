The '''Nasty Cholla''' is a craftable consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws a gravity affected cactus ball that explodes into three to four cactus needles after landing, each dealing 25% of the ball's damage. The cactus ball will stick to all blocks and enemies. Unlike most weapons, the Nasty Cholla can damage [[Town NPCs]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Nasty Chollas will cause five cactus balls to be thrown instead of one.
