The '''Inferna Cutter''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[axe]] that produces light particles when swung, and is the direct upgrade of the [[Axe of Purity]]. It inflicts the [[Brimstone Flames]] and [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] when striking an enemy and causes an explosion when landing critical hits. On swing, it releases several sparks similar to those fired by the [[Wand of Sparking]].
