The '''Hellkite''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that auto-swings and is the direct upgrade to the [[Fiery Greatsword]]. It is larger than most other swords of its tier, and upon hitting an enemy it inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] and summons a damaging geyser of flame on top of each target struck, dealing additional damage.

== Notes ==
*The explosion generated by sword hits deals exactly 100% of the weapon's base damage, can not critically strike, does not damage nearby targets, and cannot be modified by any means.
**This effect, along with its visuals, is identical to the [[Ultimus Cleaver]].

== Trivia ==
*The word "hellkite" refers to a fiercely cruel person.
**This weapon may be named after the [https://darksouls.fandom.com/wiki/Hellkite_Dragon Hellkite Dragon] miniboss from the game ''Dark Souls'', or the [https://mtg.gamepedia.com/Dragon#Hellkites Hellkite] breed of dragons from the game ''Magic: The Gathering''.
