The '''1.4.5.007''' (or '''1.4.5.7''') update is a hotfix of the prelude to the Draedon Update and is the final version in the 1.4 series of updates. This update fixed several tooltip and glowmask issues.

== Changes ==
*Decreased [[Romajeda Orchid]] price from {{pc|5}} to {{gc|40}} and sell price adjusted accordingly.
*The following [[enemies]] no longer have reduced life scaling in multiplayer:
**[[The Hungry]], [[The Hungry II]], [[Ice Queen]], [[Mothron]], [[Plantera's Tentacle]], [[Pumpking]].
*The following enemies now have reduced life scaling in multiplayer:
**[[Golem Fist]]s, [[Golem Head]] (Phase 2), [[Martian Probe]], [[Moon Lord]], [[Moon Lord Hand]], [[Nebula Pillar]], [[Solar Pillar]], [[Stardust Pillar]], [[Vortex Pillar]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed the [[Adrenaline Mode]] buff tooltip, it should now reflect the correct damage boost.
*Fixed [[Black Hawk Remote]]'s minion laser projectiles being invisible.
*Fixed a bug that caused [[Ethereal Subjugator]] to crash the game when it was dropped in the world.
*Fixed the [[God Slayer armor]] tooltip being outdated, it should now reflect that life regeneration is reduced by 50% instead of negated when the [[God Slayer Cooldown]] is procced.
*Fixed the [[Matter Modulator]] tooltip being outdated, it should now reflect its current functions.
*Fixed the [[True Caustic Edge]] tooltip mentioning an outdated function that was removed in Calamity version [[1.4.5.006]].
