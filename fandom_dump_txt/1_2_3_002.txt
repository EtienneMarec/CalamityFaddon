The '''1.2.3.002''' update completely changed [[Death Mode]], and made it rework and buff bosses instead of simply being a mode where the player dies in one hit.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Each worm now gets {{death|40,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Increased speed in Death Mode.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|2x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Now gets {{death|44,800}} health in Death Mode.
*Now switches through phases quicker in Death Mode.

==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*The main Bumblebirb now gets {{death|750,000}} health in Death Mode.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Calamitas' first phase now gets {{death|25,600}} health in Death Mode.
*Calamitas' second phase now gets {{death|40,000}} health in Death Mode.
*[[Cataclysm]] now gets {{death|14,400}} health in Death Mode.
*[[Catastrophe]] now gets {{death|12,800}} health in Death Mode.
*Now fires more aggressively in Death Mode.
*Now summons 2 pairs of brothers at 55% and 10% health in phase 2 in Death Mode.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Now spawns more beam portals in Death Mode.

==== [[Crabulon]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|3000|4800|7200}} to {{dv|5000|8000|9600}}, and it now gets {{death|13,600}} health in Death Mode.
*Now switches through attacks quicker in Death Mode.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Now gets {{death|32,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Now shoots more often in Death Mode.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*Now gets {{death|10,720}} health in Death Mode.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.6x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*The Devourer of Gods' first phase now gets {{death|1,200,000}} health in Death Mode.
*The Devourer of Gods' second phase now gets {{death|880,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Laser walls now appear faster in Death Mode.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|2.3x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now enters phase 3 upon reaching 50% health in Death Mode.

==== [[Eater of Worlds]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.5x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[Eye of Cthulhu]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|2x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now charges faster and more often in Death Mode.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|7x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now fires fireballs and lasers faster in Death Mode.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*The Hive Mind's first phase now gets {{death|5600}} health in Death Mode.
*The Hive Mind's second phase now gets {{death|7200}} health in Death Mode.
*Now fires vile clots less often.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Now gets {{death|4,640,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Homing fireballs now gain stronger homing properties in Death Mode.
*Now enters phases 2 and 3 earlier and stays in them for longer in Death Mode. 
*No longer does "slow" charges, and instead only does "fast" charges in Death Mode.

==== [[King Slime]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.8x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now faster and teleports more often in Death Mode.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Now rains projectiles much faster in Death Mode.
*[[Phantasm Dragon]] now gains a {{death|12x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Core now gains a {{death|1.6x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Eyes now gain a {{death|1.4x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now spawns 2 waves of 9 True Eyes of Cthulhu in Death Mode instead of 1.

==== [[The Perforators]] ====
*The Perforator Hive now gets {{death|10,400}} health in Death Mode.
*The Perforator Hive's blood geysers no longer collide with tiles in Death Mode.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Now gets {{death|104,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Now has a cap of 3 [[Plague Mine]]s instead of 4.
*Now has a cap of 4 large [[Plague Charger]]s.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.8x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now fires petals as fast as her enraged state in Death Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Now gets {{dv|0|0|0|672,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Laser arms now spawn in phase 2 instead of phase 3 in Death Mode.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Now gets {{death|424,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Now more aggressive in Death Mode.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.5x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now charges more often and fires more stingers in Death Mode.

==== [[Ravager]] ====
*The body now gets {{death|124,800}} health in Death Mode.
*Now spawns with two extra homing claws in Death Mode.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Now gets {{death|512,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Now switches through phases quicker in Death Mode.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*The Core now gets {{death|3520}} health in Death Mode.
*Ebonian and Crimulan Slime Gods, along with their split forms now shoot mines more often in Death Mode.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*Nerfed the Siren's health from {{dv|30,000|48,000|52,800}} to {{dv|30,000|48,000|51,200}}, and she now gets {{death|64,000}} health in Death Mode.
*The Leviathan now gets {{death|84,800}} health in Death Mode.
*Both are now more aggressive in Death Mode.

==== [[Skeletron]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.6x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Hands now gain a {{death|1.3x}} health modifier in Death Mode.
*Now fires skulls more often in Death Mode.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|1.3x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Buffed health from {{dv|1,400,000|2,240,000|2,400,000}} to {{dv|2,300,000|3,680,000|4,000,000}}, and she now gets {{death|4,800,000}} health in Death Mode.
*Buffed contact damage from {{dv|500}} to {{dv|650}}.
*Hitcap now lowers to {{death|15}} in Death Mode.
*Most projectiles are now faster.
*Brothers and Supreme [[Soul Seeker]]s now despawn if Supreme Calamitas isn't alive.
*Arena is now smaller in Death Mode.

==== [[The Twins]] ====
*Both eyes now gain a {{death|1.6x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

==== [[Wall of Flesh]] ====
*Now gains a {{death|2.5x}} health modifier in Death Mode.

=== Items ===
*Decreased the speed bonuses granted by [[Auric Tesla armor]] and [[Silva armor]].
*Buffed [[Bloody Edge]]'s damage from 42 to 47, and gave it auto swing.
*Lowered the speed and accelerations for [[Celestial Tracers]], [[Drew's Wings]], [[Elysian Tracers]], and [[Seraph Tracers]].
*Increased [[Celestus]]' range.
*[[Death]] changes:
**Changed tooltip's first line from "Makes all damage one-shot you." to "Makes bosses even more EXTREME", and added the lines "Effect will only work if revengeance mode is active" and "Using this while a boss is alive will instantly kill you and despawn the boss",
**Can now only be used if Revengeance Mode is active.
**Will now instantly kill the player if used while any boss is active.
**Removed the word "Instant" from Death's status messages.
*[[Galactus Blade]] now produces less dust.
*Galactus Blade now inflicts [[On Fire!]] and [[Frostburn]] for 10 seconds, instead of On Fire! for 20 seconds, and [[Cursed Inferno]] for 10 seconds.
*Galactus Blade's comets now explode on impact.

=== Other ===
*Death Mode changes:
**No longer makes the player instantly die when taking any damage.
**Now grants a global {{death|1.4x}} damage increase to all enemies.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Forsaken Saber]], Galactus Blade, [[Seraph Tracers]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed accessories which have an instakill feature (such as [[Elemental Gauntlet]]) instakilling Bumblebirb's clones instead of Yharon's Bumblebirbs.
*Fixed several multiplayer desync bugs with the mod's enemies.
