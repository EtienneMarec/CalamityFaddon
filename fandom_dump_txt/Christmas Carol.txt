The '''Christmas Carol''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[sentry]] sold by [[The General]]. It summons a Carol Bell sentry that floats in place. If the player strikes the bell with a whip, it unleashes a circular sound pulse that rapidly damages enemies caught within it. The pulse continuously grows until it reaches a radius of 300 blocks, then fades away.

*The weapon is directly taken from a similar weapon from the Starlight River mod.
