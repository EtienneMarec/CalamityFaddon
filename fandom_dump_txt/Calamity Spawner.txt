The '''Calamity Spawner''' was a [[Hardmode]] item used to generate the [[Brimstone Crag|Calamity Biome]] and all of the mod's [[ores]] into the world.

== Trivia ==
*In the mod's source files, the item was referred to as 'TestItemKYS'.
*It was known by the name 'Calamity Biome Spawner' prior to it gaining the ability to generate ores.
