'''The General''' (Mandarin: 天秤座 ''Tiānchèngzuò'') is a [[Hardmode]] [[NPC]] vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:

*There is an empty house.
*Any one of the following three conditions are met:
**It is either December, January, or February.
**[[Astrum Deus]] has been defeated in the [[snow biome]].
**[[The General (miniboss)]] has been defeated.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will defend himself with the [[Halberd of the Holidays]].

Talking to The General in [[Revengeance Mode]] while [[Stamina]] {{fantag|Catalyst}} is active, will fully replenish it. 

{{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} killing The General while under the effects of Fluorescent Vodka initiates the [[22 Bullet Theory]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} [[event]].

== Trivia ==
*It’s implied that the General’s potential names correspond to different fragments of his soul.
*The General can not be named Virgo, Aries, or Pisces despite otherwise having a Zodiac theme. The reason for this is unknown.
**The General’s seemingly irrational hatred for people named Aries may be related.
*The General is referred to as "YEENA" in the mod's source files, a reference to the beta tester reference in the [[Holiday Halberd]]'s flavor text, whose online username is "Yeena".
