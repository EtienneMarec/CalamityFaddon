The '''Q.C.S.T.T.''' is a valuable [[Tool]] in the Deimos, the Starborn Princess addon that is made from the [[Bygone Chassis]]. It is used to access the various [[Subworld]]s in the addon, as well as projecting various [[Q.C.S.T.T.#Blueprints|Blueprints]] when it is placed for building various important structures. 

The Q.C.S.T.T. can be upgraded as you progress through the mod through the upgrade slots found around the top of the UI. It is also able to hold all [[Lore items]] and [[Schematics|Texts from Encrypted Schematics]] within it.

If the Deimos, the Starborn Princess addon is enabled while in Calamity: Distorted Memories {{fantag|Calamity: Distorted Memories}}, the tool will also have all of the effects of the [[XO-Tell]].
