The '''Whipped Bouquet Blade''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung, it fires a volley of 4 cream pearls that explodes on enemy/solid tile impact, casting down 6-8 cream petals on the site where said pearl exploded.

Uniquely, it also has a graze mechanic where Cream points are attained from grazing enemy projectiles. These cream points are instantly diminished by switching out to another weapons, being out of combat for too long, or getting hit. Getting 120 cream points adds a Dessert charges, indicated by a glowing white feather orbiting the player. These dessert charges increases defense by 20 and damage by 5%. {{Key|open}} releases all the dessert charges at once, firing off gravity-affected bouquets that explodes on enemy contact, creating more petals in a flower-like pattern upon doing so. Up to 6 Dessert charges (720 cream points) can be stored at once, and for every dessert charges, an extra bouquet will be fired.

== Notes ==
*Successfully grazing an enemy attack is indicated by a sound effect and a pink (blue if its maxed out) sparkle.
*The graze has a radius of 12 tiles.
*Each graze adds +5 Cream points.
**This means 144 grazes are needed to reach the maximum.
