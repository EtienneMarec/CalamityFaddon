The '''Terriblade''' is a craftable [[broadsword]] that auto-swings.

On swing, it randomly performs one of the following effects:
*Swings normally.
*Inflicts [[On Fire!]] (3 seconds).
*throws a [[Cryon]] at an enemy and inflicts [[Glacial State]] (3 seconds).
*Summons a lightning bolt on screen in a random location and inflicts [[Electrified]] (3 seconds).
*inflicts [[Bleeding]] (3 seconds).
*Fires like a [[boomerang]] and lodges into the enemy for 5 seconds. The sword can be swung again.
*Explodes into shrapnel which deals 20% damage to everything around them.
*Gets thrown and, skipping on ground and water.
*swings up, Launching the player vertically into the air (similarly to [[Plagued Fuel Pack]]).
