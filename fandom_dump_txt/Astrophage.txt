The '''Astrophage''' is a [[pet]] summoning item obtained from [[Astral Crate]]s. It summons an astrophage to follow the player.

== Trivia ==
*The Astrophage is designed and named after [[wikipedia:Bacteriophage|bacteriophages]], which are real life viruses known for their uniquely insectoid and mechanical appearance.
*An astrophage-like enemy was originally developed by a community member named L0st_M1ner while Calamity 1.4's [[Astral Infection]] expansion was being planned. A pet version of that enemy was added to the [[Community modifications#Calamity's Vanities|Calamity's Vanities]] fan mod, prior to this redesigned Astrophage pet being added to Calamity proper.
*The name Astrophage roughly translates to "star eater".
