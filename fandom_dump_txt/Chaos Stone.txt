The '''Chaos Stone''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Calamitas]] with a 20% chance. While equipped, the [[Mana Sickness]] [[debuff]] is replaced by '''Mana Burn''', which deals damage over time to the player. The damage per second increases exponentially the more time is left on the debuff, peaking at 36 damage per second at the maximum duration.
