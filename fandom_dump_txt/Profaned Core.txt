The '''Profaned Core''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]. It can also be used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] in the [[Underworld]] or [[Hallow]] by having at least one Core in 40 different slots within your inventory, afterwhich 1 core from each slot will be consumed and Providence will awaken.
