The '''Pristine Annihilation''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that drops from [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it will rapidly fire a string of five flame helices that do not pierce but will create a small explosion and form a visual pillar of flame dust. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), it will rapidly fire a set of four fiery dust clouds that decelerate to a complete stop and linger for a short while before dissipating, attacking all enemies in its path. Dust clouds deal 120% damage.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The primary fire has a fixed use time of 15 ticks, and the secondary fire has a fixed use time of 20 ticks; as a result, no modifier can increase or decrease the firing rate of the weapon.
*[[Storm Weaver]] takes only 50% damage from the Pristine Annihilation.
