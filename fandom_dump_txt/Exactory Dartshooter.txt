The '''Exactory Dartshooter''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Dart guns|dart gun]]. It has 2 attacks:

Primary: normally fires darts and 10 homing fireballs that fly in curves and inflict:
<br/>[[On Fire!]]
<br/>[[tgc:Hellfire|Hellfire]]
<br/>[[Cursed Inferno]]
<br/>[[Brimstone Flames]]
<br/>[[Holy Flames]]
<br/>[[God Slayer Inferno]]
<br/>[[Dragon Fire]]

Secondary: fires a fast darts with 2 times more damage and 3 times slower firerate, it causes Exo Fireballs rain from the top of the screen, the fireballs inflict the debuffs above, pierce through everything and fly in a random curve-like path. Depending on the zoom from 8 to 16 Fireballs are summoned for every shot.
