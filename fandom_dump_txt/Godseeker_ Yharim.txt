'''Godseeker, Yharim''' (Japanese: 神を求める者、ヤハリム ''Kami o motomeru mono, yaharimu''), real name '''Jake''' (Japanese: ジェイク、じぇいく ''Jeiku''), is a Godseeker Mode [[boss]] fought atop the [[Dragon's Aerie]], and the final non-[[Superbosses|superboss]] of the Calamity Mod. He can be fought after both the [[The Fabricational Quartet|Fabricational Quartet]], [[Supreme Calamitas]] and the [[Yharim Worm]] have been defeated, though it is '''highly''' recommended that both [[XC-39 Oizys]] and [[Goozma]] are defeated beforehand, as he is the final foe most players will encounter, and the gear both drop will help immensely against him the one you have heard all about in the lore he throws 17 bananas at you
.

== Spawn ==
Yharim is encountered in the Dragon's Aerie on the final island standing alone at the Grave of Light. If approached, he will draw his blade, and the fight will begin.

== Behavior ==
While Yharim may be quite old, he is still very fast and hits very hard. Multiple attacks can overlap, as only Yharim's attack animation needs to finish for him to start another attack.

=== Attacks ===
* He throws out an infernal projection of Yharon that travels along the screen and lightly homes in on the player, inflicting Dragonfire on contact.
* Yharim leaps towards the player and swings his sword when close enough. The swing of the blade will cause a second, much larger but less dangerous slash to appear.
* He quickly maneuvers around and throws multiple Draconic Knives at a time at you from different angles.
** While it may seem overwhelming at first, keeping calm is important to dodging this one.
* Yharim stabs his sword into the ground and multiple Godblood spines will raise up from the ground all the way to the top of the arena.
* He throws his sword at the player, and the sword will circle back after it has passed the player.
** Constantly moving is key, as the sword travels quickly and may catch the player off guard.
*Yharim throws out Draconic Orbs in multiple directions, which after a delay will home in on the player from various angles.
*Yharim uses his middle finger (which is covered by a censor bar) to rapidly fire hundreds of Chlorophyte bullets at the player.

=== In [[Baby Ghost Bell|Classic Mode]] ===

* if Yharim stabs his sword into the ground at any moment in the entire fight and you have DELTARUNE installed, he will inexplicably create a large dark pillar where he stabs the ground before a funny looking short man spawns from said pillar and attempts to bludgeon you with a comically large GIF of a dollar sign. Getting hit by this attack for the first time will datamine your computer for your credit card information, and getting hit again will buy 400$ Apple Gift Cards shipped to an abandoned shed<ref>"Nobody has ever survived The Shed and come back with their gift cards. In my opinion, I think they all have horrendous skill issues and should just dodge." -The Calamity Mod Dev Team</ref> in the woods surrounded by bear traps and landmines and automatic sentry guns.

=== In [[Expert Mode]] ===

* Yharim has 5% more HP.
* Yharim now has a gun.

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===

* Yharim moves slightly faster.
* Yharim now has two guns.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===

* The game window is automatically locked out of fullscreen. If any of Yharim's projectiles collide with the top right corner of the screen, the window will be instantly closes as if it had hit the X button.
* Yharim now has three guns.
* Yharim is aided by his son Yharim Jr who is a complete copy of Yharim.

=== In [[Malice Mode]] ===

* Yharim will keylog the player's computer. If the player presses the "alt" key or the "f4" key, accidentally or not, Yharim will forcibly close Calamity, delete the mod from the player's computer, and corrupt the player's save file. Yharim will express surprise if the player manages to open the world to fight him again, as evidenced in his dialogue.
* Yharim now has four guns, wielding two in each hand. 
* In addition, if the player attacks Yharim in the back before the fight begins, he will reveal that he had a secret fifth gun under his cape. This gun will instantly kill the player. Then, it will kill the player in the next session of Terraria they play. Then, it will kill the player in the next game they play, even if it's not Terraria. 
* If the player performs this action twice, Yharim will astral project into [[Sulphurous Sea|Real Life]], attempting to bludgeon the player to death with a [[Hammers|hammer]].
