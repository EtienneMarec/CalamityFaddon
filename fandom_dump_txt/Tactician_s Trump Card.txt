The '''Tactician's Trump Card''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic weapon]] that behaves similarly to a [[broadsword]]. When swung, it fires an electric beam which pierces twice and spawns an electric explosion on each hit. Each explosion deals 50% of the original damage and inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]].

== Trivia ==
*The weapon resembles the [https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Levin_Sword Levin Sword] from the series ''[[wikipedia:Fire_Emblem|Fire Emblem]]'', which is most well known for being the sword used by the tactician Robin in their appearance as a playable character in the ''[[wikipedia:Super_Smash_Bros.|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series.
**The "Princess from the future" mentioned in the description refers to the character Lucina from ''Fire Emblem Awakening'', the game where Robin originates from.
