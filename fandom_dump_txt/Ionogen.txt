'''Ionogen''' (Japanese: イオノゲン ''Ionogen'') is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is fought in the [[Sulphurous Sea]], and part of the [[Elemental route]]. It is also fought during [[Genesis (event)|Genesis]].

Defeating Ionogen will drop the [[Soul of Ionogen]], which must be sold to the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest.

==Spawn==
Ionogen is summoned by [[fishing]] in the Sulphurous Sea without any bait, during a [[Thunderstorm]], after the [[Angler]] has been fed to the [[Trasher]].

==Behavior==
Ionogen has 3 main phases similar to [[Cryogen]], however they are based on a specific mechanic unique to this boss fight. It is unable to enrage and can be led out of the biome without any consequences.

Ionogen's gimmick is its "damage capacitance" mechanic, which upgrades its attacks based on how much damage Ionogen accumulates over every 2 seconds. Higher bursts of damage from the player increases Ionogen's damage reduction, as well as upgrades its attacks. The player should be wary of this mechanic and either space their attacks as much as possible, or rely on low-damage weapons to negate it entirely.

===Attacks===
====0% Damage Reduction====
*Ionogen hovers slowly towards the player.
*Electrifies and instantly kills any players or NPCs in contact with water for the entire fight.
*Attempts to hit the player by firing the batteries orbiting the main body at the player.
*Lightning strikes occasionally hit the ground.

====20% Damage Reduction====
*Occasionally bursts into battery acid, similar to [[Toxic Minnow]]s.
*Summons arcs of lightning which fire out in completely random directions.

====40% Damage Reduction====
*Ignites and starts chasing the player relentlessly, however it cannot slow down to turn around easily.
*Pulses of electromagnetic energy pull the player in occasionally.
*All lightning is fired much more rapidly.

====100% Damage Reduction====
*Eventually drops dead, ending the fight.
*Reaching amount this damage reduction is only reachable with late-game equipment.

===In [[Expert Mode]]===
*"Phase" now changes every 1.33 seconds.

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===
*Ionogen and its projectiles are slightly faster.
*Ionogen's shield will now aggressively ram the player during downtime after 25% health.
*Ionogen and its projectiles are even more aggressive and faster.

===In [[Death Mode]]===
*Now teleports.
