The Rusty Flamethrower is a Craftable Hardmode [[Ranged weapons|Ranged]] [[Flamethrowers|Flamethrower]], When used, It sprays out a barrage of fire that inflicts On Fire! for 1 second. Using the weapon for to long will cause it to overheat and deactivate for 15 seconds and deals 150 damage to the player. The weapon can cool down for at most 7 seconds if the weapon does not overheat.

The weapon consumes 0.025 units of charge and has a maximum charge of 90

The best modifier is Unreal
