'''Noctic Weaver''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that is either craftable or dropped from the [[Esserwyrm]]. It casts webs that stick to hit enemies, slowing then down and dealing 50% of weapons damage every second for every pile of webs that stuck onto the target.
