The '''Charm Gar''' (Japanese: チャームガー ''Chāmugā'') is a Godseeker Mode [[mini boss]] summoned by [[fishing]] during a [[Blood Moon]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated. It behaves similarly to [[Duke Fishron]], repeatedly lunging towards the player 4 times while predicting their movement. After these dashes, it will stop and summon a large Bloodflare Bomb that follows the player for 2 seconds before exploding into an even spread of {{dv|8||||12}} Bloodflare Fireballs.

The Charm Gar drops large amounts of [[Blood Orb]]s, [[Bloodstone]], and [[Bloodstone Bar]]s, as well as the [[Bloodflare Rod]].
