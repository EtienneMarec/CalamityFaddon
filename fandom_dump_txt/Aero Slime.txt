The '''Aero Slime''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[slime]] which spawns naturally in the [[Cavern]] and [[Space]] layers after the player has defeated [[The Hive Mind]] or [[The Perforators]].  They exist to drop [[Aerialite Ore]], so that the player cannot use up all the ore available to them. Unlike other ore slimes, they can fly around freely.

== Trivia ==
*The Aero Slime's sprite uses a recolored and slightly modified version of the [[Skyline Wings]] for part of its wing animation.
