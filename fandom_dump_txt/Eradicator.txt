The '''Eradicator''' is a Godseeker Mode [[boomerang]] dropped by [[The Devourer of Gods]]. It passes through walls and sporadically fires nebula lasers at any nearby enemies that deal 40% of the weapon's damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Eradicator causes the next boomerang thrown to stick to enemies, after which it will fire lasers in all directions.
