The '''Elemental Bow''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]] that rains arrows from the sky in groups of seven. It converts [[Wooden Arrow]]s into rainbow blasts that home in on [[enemies]]. Each set of bolts consists of a Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, and Violet bolt which all act identically to each other and are fired in a random order from the sky.
