The '''Penumbra Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] made from [[Solar Tablet Fragment]]s. Upon consumption it grants the '''Penumbra''' [[buff]], increasing the rate that [[Spy#Stealth|Spy stealth]] builds by 15% while moving and 15% while standing still at night. These bonuses are boosted to 20% during a [[Solar Eclipse]].
