The '''Perforator Cyst''' is a rare [[Pre-Hardmode]] stationary [[enemy]] that spawns in [[The Crimson]] biome. They exist to drop [[Crimtane Ore]], so that the player cannot use up all the ore available to them.

The Perforator Cyst can be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]] and its upgrades.
