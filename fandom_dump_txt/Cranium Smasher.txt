The '''Cranium Smasher''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that drops from the [[Ravager]]. When used it auto-fires a spinning disk that can pierce four enemies before disappearing on its next impact. Disks that are thrown will also roll along the ground, and have a chance to spawn as an explosive disk that will explode on contact with enemies and blocks.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Cranium Smasher causes it to deal 175% damage and fire a disk that will explode on enemy hits and can pierce nine enemies before disappearing on its next impact. This disk will explode on contact with blocks.
