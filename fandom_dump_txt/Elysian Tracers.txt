The '''Elysian Tracers''' are a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that count as [[Wings]] and are an upgraded version of the [[Seraph Tracers]]. Along with all the effects of the Seraph Tracers, wearing these boots boosts maximum movement speed and acceleration by 5%, allowing the player to reach up to 49 mph while running.
