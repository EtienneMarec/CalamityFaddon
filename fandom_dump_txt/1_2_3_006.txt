The '''1.2.3.006''' update made [[The Slime God]] fight harder, added three [[Music Boxes]], and added the [[Siren's Heart]] [[coverings]].

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Levi]], Siren's Heart (Blue), Siren's Heart (Green).
*Added [[Ice Shield (buff)]], [[Ice Shield Cooldown]], [[Levi (buff)]], [[Aquatic Heart|Siren (Blue)]], Siren (Green), Siren Speed.
*Added [[Music Box (Calamitas)]], [[Music Box (Cryogen)]], [[Music Box (DoG Phase 1)]].

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[???]] now has a 25% chance to drop either Siren's Heart in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*[[Angry Dog]]s and [[Ice Clasper]]s can no longer spawn if the player is in the [[Dungeon]].
*Increased the size of the [[Armored Digger]].
*[[Desert Scourge]] now uses [[Music#Guardian of the Former Seas|Guardian of the Former Seas]] as its theme.
*[[Irradiated Slime]]s now have a 10% chance to drop a [[Lead Core]] instead of a 20% chance.
*Phase 2 of [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] now despawns quicker if no players are alive.
*Yharon's infernadoes now last 8 seconds instead of 7.
*Lowered the distance required for the Ebonian and Crimulan [[Slime God]]s to fly towards the player.
*Ebonian and Crimulan Slime Gods are now more aggressive in Death Mode.
*Lowered Slime God Core's speed.

==== [[Items]] ====
*Renamed Gun to [[Soma Prime]].
*Renamed Viable? to [[Lacerator]], and added "Someone thought this was a viable weapon against DoG at one point lol" to its tooltip.
*[[Staff of the Mechworm]]'s damage scaling is now capped at 10, and added the line, "The damage scaling stops growing after 10 minion slots" to its tooltip.

=== Other ===
*[[Exo Freeze]] and [[Glacial State]] now also slow down vertical movement in addition to slowing horizontal movement.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Asgardian Aegis]], [[Astral Arcanum]], [[Enchanted Pearl]].
*Resprited [[Mechworm (buff)]].
*Resprited The Devourer of Gods' phase 1 map icon.
*Resprited [[Atlantis]]' spear projectile.

=== Fixes ===
*Fixed a bug where [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] wouldn't despawn once every player had been killed.
*Fixed a bug where the Slime God's large slimes wouldn't split up.
*Fixed a bug where Yharon's infernadoes could hurt the player immediately upon spawning.
