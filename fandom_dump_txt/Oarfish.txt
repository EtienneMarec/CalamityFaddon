The '''Oarfish''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the 2nd and 3rd layers of the [[Abyss]]. It is a long enemy with multiple segments. Like most [[Abyss]] enemies, it won't attack the player unless they get too close or they attack it. It attacks by using its very long body to trap the player.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Oarfish|Oarfish]] in real life live in deep sea away from turbulent, shallow water. The name likely refers to the discarded belief that the fish use their pelvic fins to "row" through water.
