The '''Profaned Desert''' is a [[biome]] added by the Calamity Mod. Upon losing against [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], the entire world will be converted into this biome. All natural blocks are converted into [[Torrefied Tephra]], while all non-natural blocks are destroyed. [[Profaned Crystal|Profaned Crystals]] will also generate, jutting out of the surface at random locations. The Profaned Desert covers the screen in a yellow haze, and is populated solely by profaned enemies. It is considered the game over state for failing to defeat Providence.

While in the Profaned Desert, the player is afflicted by the [[Punishment]] debuff, making long-term survival in this biome near impossible. The biome can also not be overwritten by any other, and the sun will not set while inside, making many of the games [[Challenges|Bosses]] unavailable as well.

== Notes ==
* It is impossible to create an artificial profaned desert, as the biome is world dependent, similar to [[The Underworld]].
* The profaned Desert can be an excellent place to farm for Unholy Essence, as the only enemies who can spawn in it drop the crafting material.
** This is unfeasible for hardcore characters, as they will die within minutes due to being under the constant effects of the Punishment debuff.

== Trivia ==
*The Profaned Desert's [[Music|theme]] is '''Empire of Ash''', which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Desert''' will play instead.
*The idea of the Profaned Desert likely came from long discussed plans for a biome themed around Providence and her minions.
