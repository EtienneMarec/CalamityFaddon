The '''Amalgamated Brain''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It grants an increase to all damage types, causes shade to rain down when invincibility frames are active, and has a chance to inflict [[Confused]] on nearby enemies when the player is hit. The chance to inflict Confused and its duration both scale with the damage the player took. This covering can be considered the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Bloody Worm Scarf]], even though they both can be crafted regardless of the world evil.

== Notes ==
*Shade rain deals magic damage and benefits from magic bonuses. However, its damage cannot be increased through any means.
*The shade rain has a base damage of 60.

== Tips ==
*If the player lives in a Corruption world, defeating the [[Eater of Worlds]] will allow the [[Dryad]] to sell the [[Brain of Confusion]]. Conversely, she will start selling the [[Rotten Brain]] if the player defeats [[The Perforators]] in a [[Crimson]] world.
*While the Bloody Worm Scarf is geared towards melee users, the Amalgamated Brain is geared towards all types of classes, as it increases damage for all classes.
