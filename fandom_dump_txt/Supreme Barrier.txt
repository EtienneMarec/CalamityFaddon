The '''Supreme Barrier''' is a craftable [[Godseeker Mode]] [[accessory]]. It grants 12 [[defense]], immunity to knockback and the majority of [[debuffs]], including [[God Slayer Inferno]].

When equipped, the player can double-tap a horizontal movement key to perform a cosmic dash with a base distance of 28 blocks. Striking enemies during the shield dash will inflict the God Slayer Inferno debuff and deal 1000 melee damage as well as creating an explosion for an additional 1000 typeless damage. The dash itself is affected by melee damage bonuses, while the explosion is affected by all damage stats. Every time an enemy is struck, the player is given 12 [[immunity frames]].
