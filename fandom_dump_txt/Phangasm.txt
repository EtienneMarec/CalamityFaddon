The '''Phangasm''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]] and a direct upgrade to the [[Phantasm]]. It shoots volleys of 5 arrows at an initially low rate of fire that increases as it is used continuously. When its arrows hit an enemy, Phangasm spawns an additional barrage of phantasm arrows that home in on that enemy. It can later be used to craft the [[Contagion]].
