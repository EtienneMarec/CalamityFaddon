The '''Bobbit Worm''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the lowest layer of the [[Abyss]]. It stays attached to the seafloor, waiting for the player to come close enough to it. When they do, it rapidly extends out towards the player, dealing significant damage if contact is made, before quickly returning to its previous location.

Upon hitting the player, they inflict [[Crush Depth]] for 5 seconds. 

== Notes ==
*While it is referred to as a worm, it does not act like a typical [[Worm AI]] enemy.

== Trivia ==
*Its behavior mimics that of real [[wikipedia:Eunice aphroditois|bobbit worms]], which also launch themselves from underground to attack nearby prey with their mandibles before receding again.
