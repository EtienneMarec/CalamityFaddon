The '''Dust Mite''' is a [[critter]] found typically within [[Cavern]]s and passively flies around [[tgc:Soils|Soil]] blocks. When killed, Dust Mites drop 1 [[tgc:Coins|Copper Coin]].

It can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].

{{-}}
==Trivia==
*Dust Mites are based off of a real life experience one of the Calamity developers had, where they accidentally inhaled a dust mite.
