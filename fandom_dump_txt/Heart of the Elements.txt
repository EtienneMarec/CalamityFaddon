The '''Heart of the Elements''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It will summon all of the Elementals to assist the player and attack enemies.

When equipped, the Heart of the Elements will summon a [[Rose Stone|Brimstone Elemental]], a [[Elemental in a Bottle|Sand Elemental]], a [[Eye of the Storm|Cloud Elemental]],a [[Prehistoric Tablet|Sulphur Elemental]], a [[Infected Remote|Plague Elemental]], and a [[Pearl of Enthrallment|Water Elemental]] to attack nearby enemies and a [[Rare Elemental in a Bottle| Rare Sand Elemental]] to heal the player. Elementals have a base damage of 90, and are affected by summon damage bonuses.

== Notes ==
*All of the elementals' attacks grant enemies 20 local [[immunity frames]], except for the Sand Elemental that grants 30 local immunity frames with contact damage.
*The Heart of the Elements will not work if used along with the [[Heart of the Machines]].

== Tips ==
*This covering  can be very helpful when performing less combat-oriented tasks such as [[Fishing|fishing]], as the elementals will keep most normal enemies at bay and allow the player's full attention to be focused on what they're doing.
*[[Potions]] such as [[Heartreach Potion]] and [[Cadance Potion|cadence]] [[Cadance Potion|potion]] effectively grant homing to the Rare Sand Elemental's healing orbs.

== Trivia ==
*This item has the {{rare|rainbow}} rarity despite not being an [[Expert Mode]]-exclusive item.
*Despite being an "elemental" covering similar to the [[Elemental Gauntlet]] or [[Elemental Quiver|Quiver]], it is craftable before defeating The Devourer of Gods.
*Elementals summoned by this covering formerly had different names:
**The Sand Elemental minion used to be called "Sand Waifu". After that, she was known as "Miss Sandy".
**The Rare Sand Elemental minion used to be called "Rare Sand Waifu". After that, she was known as "Madame Sandy".
**The Cloud Elemental minion used to be called "Cloudy Waifu". After that, she was known as "Miss Cloudy".
**The Brimstone Elemental minion used to be called "Brimmy Waifu". After that, she was known as "Miss Brimmy".
**The Sulphur Elemental minion used to be called "Acid Waifu". After that, she was known as "Miss Sophie".
**The Anahita minion used to be called "Siren Lure", which spawned during previous versions of the [[Leviathan and Anahita]] fight.
*Fittingly for the item's name and summons, most of the Elemental minions seem to correspond to one of the classical Greek elements:
**The Brimstone Elemental minion corresponds to fire.
**The Cloud Elemental minion corresponds to air.
**The two Sand Elemental minions correspond to earth.
**The Anahita minion and Sulphur Elemental both correspond to water.
**The Plague Elemental is the only exception to this, with her inclusion being a reference to the retroactive addition to the horseman of Pestilence to the [[wikipedia:Four_Horsemen_of_the_Apocalypse|Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse]].
*If the {{fantag|Tranquility Mod}} [[Tranquility Mod]] is enabled, this item becomes unobtainable, due to being replaced with [[Elemental Souls|Elemental Soul]].
