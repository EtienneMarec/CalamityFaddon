The '''1.2.4.204''' update rebalanced a large amount of the weapons obtained in the Godseeker Mode stage of the game along with rebalancing most of the mod's armor sets. It also added the [[Sepulcher]] [[boss servant]] to the [[Supreme Calamitas]] fight.
{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Brimrose]].
*Added [[Brimrose Mount]].
*Added [[Brimstone Heart]] and [[Sepulcher]].

== Changes ==

=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*[[Astrageldon Slime]] now drops {{dv|12-20|18-30}} [[Nebula Fragment|Nebula]], [[Solar Fragment|Solar]], [[Stardust Fragment|Stardust]], and [[Vortex Fragment]]s after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
*[[Brimstone Elemental]] now drops the Brimrose in [[Revengeance Mode]] after [[Providence, the Profaned God]] has been defeated.
*Buffed [[Bumblebirb]]'s health from {{dv|250,000|375,000|450,000|825,000}} to {{dv|380,000|570,000|750,000|1,125,000}}.
*[[Cosmic Lantern]]s are now immune to [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]].
*Renamed THE LORDE to [[SELF-INSERT BOSS UNCREATIVE AS FUCK BABY RAGE K U LOL BAI]].
*The large [[Slime God]]s now teleport above the player if they are too far away from them.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**Bullet hells now last half as long.
**Now has a new phase after her first bullet hell and at 8% life, which spawns 10 Brimstone Hearts based on arena size, along with the Sepulcher.
**Halved the amount of time it takes for her projectiles to despawn upon reaching 1% life.
**The fight must now last at least 75 seconds for her to drop loot instead of 150.
**Increased the speed of her Brimstone Monsters.
**Now directly spawns her brothers instead of using projectiles to spawn them.
**Brimstone Darts now collide with tiles.
**Brothers no longer despawn if the player is too far away.
**Adjusted the brothers' speeds.
**Reduced hitcap from {{dv|0|25|20|10}} to {{dv|0|20|15|5}}.
**Simplified enemy spawn reduction code.

=== Items ===
*[[Galaxia]] now fires the fireballs from [[Cursed Flames]] while in [[The Corruption]] instead of [[Vilethorn]]'s projectile.
*Tweaked [[Subsuming Vortex]]'s dusts.
*[[Suspicious Looking Jelly Bean]] now blocks extra jumps from coverings like [[Cloud in a Bottle]].

== Balance ==

=== [[Covering]] and [[coverings]] ===
*[[Aerospec armor]]'s set bonus no longer scales with the player's health and gives 5% critical strike chance and movement speed at all times.
*Revengeance Mode [[Affliction]] changes:
**Nerfed life regeneration boost from 4 to 3.
**Nerfed damage reduction boost from 8% to 7%.
**Nerfed defense boost from 45 to 40.
**Nerfed damage boost from 15% to 12%.
**Nerfed health boost from 20% to 15%.
*[[Astral armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides highly increased movement speed scaling with the player's health, and instead now grants 22% increased damage and critical strike chance along with an additional 25% increased movement speed.
**Astral Helmet no longer provides damage and critical strike chance boosts.
*[[Ataxia armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides increased damage, defense, and critical strike chance as the player's health lowers, and instead provides a 5% increased damage and critical strike chance boost at all times.
**Increased Ataxia Armor's damage boost from 5% to 8%.
**Increased Ataxia Subligar's critical strike chance boost from 5% to 7%.
**Chaos Spirit's chaos flares now last 2 seconds instead of 5.
*[[Auric Tesla armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides stat boosts as the player's health lowers.
**Removed the second "and" from the line “20% increased damage and critical strike chance and +7 max minions" in the Helm's tooltip and replaced it with a comma.
**Auric Tesla Body Armor now provides an 8% damage boost and a 5% critical strike chance boost.
**Auric Tesla Cuisses now provide a 12% damage boost and a 10% critical strike chance boost.
*[[Bloodflare armor]] changes:
**Bloodflare Mask now provide a 10% damage and critical strike chance boost.
**Increased Bloodflare Body Armor's defense from 33 to 35, and decreased its critical strike chance boost from 12% to 7%.
**Bloodflare Body Armor's tooltip now specifies the critical strike chance boost it gives.
**Bloodflare Cuisses now provide a 10% damage and critical strike chance boost.
**Set bonus now has a cooldown between hits for when it can spawn [[Heart]]s and [[Mana Star]]s.
*[[Daedalus armor]] changes:
**Decreased Daedalus Brestplate damage and critical strike chance boosts from 5% to 3%.
**Set bonus no longer provides increased damage, critical strike chance, and defense as health lowers, and instead provides a 5% damage and critical strike chance boost at all times.
*[[Demonshade armor]] changes:
**Rewrote Demonshade Helmet's tooltip to say "critical strike chance" instead of "crit. chance".
**Changed Demonshade Breastplate's tooltip to say "critical strike chance" instead of "crit".
*[[Elemental Quiver]] now splits projectiles 25% less often and split projectiles now deal 50% damage instead of 80%.
*[[God Slayer armor]] changes:
**Removed the second "and" from the line “14% increased damage and critical strike chance and +3 max minions" in the Helm's tooltip and replaced it with a comma.
**God Slayer Leggings now provide a 10% damage and critical strike chance boost.
*[[Reaver armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides increased damage, critical strike chance, defense, and speed as the player's health lowers, and instead provides a 10% damage and speed boost and a 5% critical strike chance boost at all times.
**Merged movement speed tooltip line with [[Lava]] and [[Cursed Inferno]] immunity tooltip line.
**All five helmets now have the word "and" between the damage and critical strike chance boosts in their tooltips.
**Reaver Cuisses now provide 9% increased damage and 4% increased critical strike chance instead of 5% increased damage and critical strike chance.
*[[Silva armor]] changes:
**Decreased Silva Helm's damage and critical strike chance boosts from 15% to 13%.
**Silva Armor now provides a 12% damage and 9% critical strike chance boost.
**Silva Leggings now provide a 12% damage and 10% critical strike chance boost.
*[[Statigel armor]] changes:
**Set bonus now provides a 10% damage and 7% critical strike chance boost instead of a 7% damage boost and 5% critical strike chance boost.
**Set bonus no longer provides increased stats as the player's health lowers.
*[[Tarragon armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides increased stats as the player's health lowers.
**Buffed Tarragon Helm's defense from 27 to 30.
**Tarragon Helm now provides a 10% damage and critical strike chance boost.
**Buffed Tarragon Breastplate's defense from 30 to 42, but lowered its critical strike chance boost from 10% to 5%.
**Tarragon Leggings now provide a 10% damage and critical strike chance boost.
*[[Victide armor]] changes:
**Lowered set bonus' damage boost from 15% to 10%.
**Victide Breastplate now provides a 5% critical strike chance boost.
*[[Xeroc armor]] changes:
**Set bonus no longer provides increased stats as the player's health lowers.
**Increased Xeroc Mask's damage and critical strike chance boosts from 10% to 12%.
**Xeroc Plate Mail now provides a 6% damage and critical strike chance boost.
**Decreased Xeroc Plate Mail's movement speed boost from 12% to 6%.
**Decreased Xeroc Cuisses' critical strike chance boost from 7% to 5%.
