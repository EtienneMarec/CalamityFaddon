{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.4.4.004 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.4.4.004''' (or '''1.4.4.4''') Music mod update added a music track for the Acid Rain tier 3 event.

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod version [[1.4.4.004]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Music Box (Sulphuric Monsoon)]].
*Added "raidoactive", the [[Acid Rain]] Tier 3 music track.

== Changes ==
*Added a check that disables the "Calamity" music track from playing on the Title Screen while the "Terraria Overhaul" mod is enabled. 
*Renamed the <code>TitleScreenOverride</code> [[Configuration option]] to <code>ReplaceTitleMusic</code>.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed an issue that caused custom music tracks to occasionally not play during boss fights.
