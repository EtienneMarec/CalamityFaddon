The '''Plaguecom Tablet''' is a [[Hardmode]] Magic Weapon dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon use, the player can draw a colored line that damages enemies contacting said line. These lines have different behaviors, nanite ink drain rate, and colors depending on which 2 modes is active. These can be switched with {{Key|open}}. The 2 modes are as follows:

*'''Offensive Mode''': Lines in this state are colored in lime and red, scrolling through a pattern of a green/red segments. Enemies hit by that line will be inflicted a [[Plague]] debuff, and will burst into chains of tiny explosions dealing 135% of the weapon's damage upon releasing the {{Key|use}} button, or running out of ink.

*'''Defensive Mode''': Lines in this state resembles a grey line with a repeating pattern of cyan and green. It can block projectiles for up to 10 times before shattering. Additionally, it has a 50% chance to inflict the [[Glacial State]] debuff. These lines remain, even after releasing the {{Key|use}} button or running out of ink, but will shatter regardless if 30 seconds has passed, indicated by the ends flashing red.

Drawing lines costs nanite ink, with the latter costing more at 12% per 10 tiles worth than the former's 6%. Refilling the ink by double-tapping {{Key|down}}, costs the player 100 Mana + 50% of the player's max mana. Mana regeneration is reduced by 75% while holding this weapon.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifiers]] is '''Zealous''', due to its unique way of attacking.

*The item's first name is a portmanteau of "Plague", and "Wacom", a Japanese company specializing on graphical tablets and related products.
*Its design is directly inspired by the [https://pixelgun.fandom.com/wiki/Digital_Drawing_Pad Digital Drawing Pad] from a first-person shooter game [[wikipedia:Pixel_Gun_3D|Pixel Gun 3D]].
*The defensive line's behavior mirrors that of the [https://wikirby.com/wiki/Rainbow_Line Rainbow Line] from a platforming spinoff game [https://wikirby.com/wiki/Kirby:_Canvas_Curse Kirby: Canvas Curse].
