The '''Norfleet''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ranged]] [[weapon]] that drops from [[The Devourer of Gods]]. When used, it fires ten large stars that explode upon contact with an enemy. Upon collision, each star will create a massive explosion similar to that of the Starmada. It uses [[Fallen Star]]s as ammo and consumes six fallen stars per shot fired.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==

*Similarly to the Starmada, this weapon fires faster the longer the player uses it, and emits a particle ring to indicate when the maximum fire rate has been reached. However, it still fires very slowly, even at the maximum fire rate.

== Trivia ==
*The projectiles from this weapon were intentionally prevented from splitting with the [[Elemental Quiver]], when the covering still had that effect.
*This weapon is a reference to a [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Norfleet weapon of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Starmada]].
