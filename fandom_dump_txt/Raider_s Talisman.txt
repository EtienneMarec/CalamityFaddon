The '''Raider's Talisman''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]]. It is craftable as soon as the player is able to obtain [[Obsidian]].

Every time the player lands a critical strike with a Spy weapon, the player's Spy damage increases by 0.1%. The damage bonus stacks up to 150 times at a cap of 15%.

== Notes ==
*If the player un-equips the covering then re-equips it, the bonus is not reset.
*If the player dies or quits the game, the bonus is lost and has to be rebuilt.
*Damage can only be stacked from hitting enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher, with the exception of enemies spawned from [[statues]].

== Trivia ==

* The Raider's Talisman is a reference to an item of the same name from ''Darkest Dungeon'', although the two are similar only in appearance and class application.
