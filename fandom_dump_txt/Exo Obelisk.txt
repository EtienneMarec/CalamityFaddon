The '''Exo Obelisk (Japanese: エクソオベリスク Ekusooberisuku)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. It is completely stationary, but will activate when approached, emitting an aura around it that will supercharge other [[Exo Enemies]]. It will also begin to occasionally charge up and fire a high damaging Prismatic Deathray.
