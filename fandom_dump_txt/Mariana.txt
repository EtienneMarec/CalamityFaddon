The '''Mariana''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When hitting an enemy, the enemy explodes into four or five bubble projectiles that travel for a long distance. These bubbles can bounce off of tiles infinitely as well as pierce twice, popping on the third enemy hit or after roughly two seconds have passed.

== Trivia ==
*The sword's name is a reference to the [[wikipedia:Mariana Trench|Mariana Trench]], the deepest known part of the world's oceans.
*Prior to the introduction of the Aquatic Dissolution, the Mariana was used to craft the [[Terratomere]]. When its successor was added to the mod, the sword was replaced with [[Floodtide]].
