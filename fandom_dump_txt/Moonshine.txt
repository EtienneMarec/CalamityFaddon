'''Moonshine''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Moonshine''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen but increases [[defense]] and damage reduction.

While the effects of the Moonshine debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
