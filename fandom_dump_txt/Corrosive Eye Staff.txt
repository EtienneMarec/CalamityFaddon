}}}}The '''Corrosive Eye Staff''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] summon weapon that is dropped by the [[Acidsighter]]. It summons corrosive eyes that circle around you and fire acid shots at nearby enemies.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Ruthless'''. The Mythical modifier provides the widest array of stat bonuses, but these primarily affect the initial summon rather than the resulting minion. Additionally, minions cannot deal [[Critical hit|critical hits]]. The only lasting advantage a Mythical Corrosive Eye Staff has over a Ruthless one is [[knockback]].{{summoned|Corrosive Eye|type=minion|image=[[File:Corrosive Eye (minion).png]]}}
