'''The Mutilator''' is a Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]]. It does not fire a projectile, but striking an enemy below 20% life will cause a "bloodsplosion" to occur that spawns multiple [[heart]]s. As with most [[Bloodstone]] weapons, it has very high damage for its tier and swings quickly compared to other similar swords.
