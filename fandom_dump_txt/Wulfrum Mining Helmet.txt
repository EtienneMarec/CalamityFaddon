The '''Wulfrum Mining Helmet''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] helmet that combines the effects of the [[Wulfrum Lenses]] and [[Wulfrum Hardhat]]. It creates a blue beam of light in front of the player that highlights treasure, and gives them full immunity to traps.
