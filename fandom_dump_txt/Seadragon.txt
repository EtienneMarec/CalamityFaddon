The '''Seadragon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Guns|gun]] that is an upgrade to the [[Megalodon]]. It rapidly fires bullets, with every other shots turning into a stream of water that does not consume ammo. Every 18 shots, it will instead fire a homing rocket that explodes into fire shards, which also does not consume ammo.
