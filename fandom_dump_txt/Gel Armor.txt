'''Gel armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set made from [[Unpurified Gel]]. It can be upgraded into [[Statigel armor]] once the Slime Gods have been defeated.

It consists of '''Gel Armor''', '''Gel Greaves''' and five different headpieces: '''Gel Helm''', '''Gel Headgear''', '''Gel Cap''',  '''Gel Hood''' and '''Gel Mask'''.

The '''Gel Armor''' gives 7 defense and +3% increased critical strike chance; and the '''Gel Greaves''' give 5 defense, 3% increased damage, and 5% increased movement speed.

*The '''Gel Helm''' gives 4 defense, 5% increased melee damage, 4% increased melee critical strike chance, and 5% increased melee speed.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 200 [[Aggro]].
*The '''Gel Headgear''' gives 3 defense, 5% increased ranged damage, and 4% increased ranged critical strike chance.
*The '''Gel Cap''' gives 1 defense, 5% increased magic damage, 4% increased magic critical strike chance, 5% decreased mana cost, and +30 max mana.
*The '''Gel Hood''' gives 1 defense, increased minion knockback<br/>Its set bonus gives an additional 6% minion damage and +1 max minion, and summons a Baby Slime that will attack enemies.
**The Baby Slime moves and attacks like any other slime minion, dealing 13 damage.
*The '''Gel Mask''' gives 2 defense, 5% increased Spy damage, 4% increased Spy critical strike chance, 3% increased movement speed and 15% chance to not consume thrown items.<br/>Its set bonus gives a maximum [[Spy stealth]] of 50.

Equipping a full set will always increase the player's jump height by 3 blocks and jump speed by 6%, along with adding an extra jump which emits blue gel-like particles below the player when performed. Additionally, taking over 100 damage in one hit grants the player 30 extra [[immunity frames]].

A full set will always provide a total of at least 13 defense, 3% increased damage, critical strike chance, and movement speed.
