The '''Mirage Jelly''' is an [[enemy]] which spawns in the third layer of the [[Abyss]]. It slowly swims upward, ignoring all blocks in its way, and will occasionally teleport somewhere else on the screen upon taking damage. It never moves to directly attack the player, serving more as a living hazard than an enemy.
