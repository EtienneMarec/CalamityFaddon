The '''Dreamy Belt''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]]. While worn, it grants the following effects:
*+15% increased Melee and Magic damage
*+20% increased True Melee damage
*+35% Max Life and Mana
*+15 Defense and Armor Penetration
*+0.1 Luck

Additionally, every 3 seconds, a butterfly spawns from the player, orbiting them and firing homing bullets at an enemy. Pressing the {{Key|Butterfly Release}} Key sends out all the active butterflies, aggressively homing in to the cursor's current location. While all butterflies are active, True Melee attacks fires out a crescent wave that pierces infinitely for some time and inflicts the [[Moon Burnt]] debuff. This has a cooldown of 5 seconds before they can fire out the waves again, indicated by a sound effect upon its cooldown ending.

== Notes ==
*The Butterflies' bullets deal 150 magic damage, and as such, can be boosted by any magic damage-increasing buffs.
**The Butterfly Release butterflies, on the other hand, deals 200% its usual damage, accounting in buffs too. Both attacks can not receive any critical strike buffs.
*The crescent wave inherits 50% of the currently swung true melee weapon's damage and size. Like its other attacks, it can not receive any critical strike buffs.
