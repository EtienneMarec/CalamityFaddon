'''Crystal Piercers''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[javelins]]. They are spear-like projectiles that pierce indefinitely and inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] on contact. They travel a short distance before being affected by gravity. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being destroyed.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Crystal Piercer causes the resulting javelin to fly through tiles, completely ignore gravity, and summon crystal shards alongside itself as it flies.
