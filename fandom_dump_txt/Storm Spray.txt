The '''Storm Spray''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] obtained from the [[Desert Scourge]]. When used, it fires an arc of water particles that damage [[enemies]]. From points across the arc, extra trails of water fire downward which damage enemies as well.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Notes ==
*The Storm Spray is very similar to the [[Aqua Scepter]], the main differences being the Storm Spray's dripping trails effect and that it can be obtained before fighting [[Skeletron]].
