The '''Cirnogenic Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon]] [[sentry]] hybrid. It is initially summoned as a traditional minion that rams enemies and occasionally starts rapidly spinning to fling out ice spikes in random directions. Right clicking causes the minion to switch to sentry mode, locking it in place and greatly increasing the fire rate of its flung ice spikes. In sentry mode it will also occasionally fire an endo beam in a random direction which pierces infinitely.
