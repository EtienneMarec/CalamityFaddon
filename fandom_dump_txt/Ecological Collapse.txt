The '''Ecological Collapse''' is a craftable Epilogue non-consumable [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws out up to 10 Lumenous Mines which decelerate until stopping. Once stationary, they will create a lightning aura with a 20 tiles radius that completely ignores [[immunity frames]] of enemies caught within. Once the mine has existed for 20 seconds, it will explode into 8-10 Lumenyl shards.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Ecological Collapse will throw out a mine regardless of how many currently exists, which will explode into 20-30 shards. However said mines will only last for 8 seconds.
