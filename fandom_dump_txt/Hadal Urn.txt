The '''Hadal Urn''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Black Anurian]]. When used, the player holds an urn which rapidly releases a spread of three random marine creatures.

{{modifier|magic}}

== Trivia ==
- This item may be a reference to the story "The Urn Of 100 Poisons" in which 100 poisonous snakes, scorpions and centipedes are placed into a jar and forced to kill each other, and the surviving animal would have a much more potent poison than any in the jar it was put in.
