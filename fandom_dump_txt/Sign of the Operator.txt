The '''Sign of the Operator''' is a unique Godseeker Mode [[whip]] that drops from the [[Tall Man]]. When used, it summons a floating page in front of the player that constantly releases several short ranged tentacle whips.

Its best modifier is '''Legendary'''.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon is a reference to the Operator from the webseries Marble Hornets.
**The symbol on the paper is one seen commonly in the series.
**The term Operator Sickness is used to describe the negative effects being within the presence of the Operator will incur.
