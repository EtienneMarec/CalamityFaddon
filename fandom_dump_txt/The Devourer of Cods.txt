'''The Devourer of Cods''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[fishing pole]]. When swung, ten fishing lines are deployed at once instead of one. In addition to this, fishing lines will not break in a similar manner to equipping the [[High Test Fishing Line]] and it can be used to fish in [[Lava]]. The color of the fishing line is randomized between blue and pink.

== Trivia ==
*This item's name is a play on [[The Devourer of Gods]], the boss this pole is based on, and [[wikipedia:Cod|Cod]], a type of fish.
*Its tooltip is a play on [[Eater of Souls]], the vanilla enemy, and [[wikipedia:Shoaling and schooling|shoal]], a general name for a group of fish.
*If sold to the Archmage, he will offer you [[Delicious Meat]] instead of money. This is most likely a reference to the Devourer of Gods' crippling Delicious Meat addiction.
**It is hinted at that DoG's debilitating addiction was the reason they and Providence split up, and has been speculated as to the reason to why the player was able to beat a god.
*Due to firing 10 lines at once, The Devourer of Cods can be used to summon multiple [[Duke Fishron]]s or [[Old Duke]]s at once. It will use one [[Truffle Worm]] per Duke Fishron spawned, and one [[Bloodworm]] per Old Duke spawned.
