'''Refined Aegisalt''' is a post-[[Fatal Circuit]] [[Bars|bar]] made with 2 [[Aegisalt Ore]] at any [[Hardmode Forges|Hardmode Forge]]. It is used to craft various weapons and armor, as well as [[Life Alloy]] when combined with [[Refined Violium]] and [[Refined Ferozium]] after [[Golem]] has been defeated.
