The '''Eternal Pillow''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that has a chance to be fished up in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated. It summons Xun'Bakyr to protect you by using her Phaerakh Aura to reduce enemy defense and defense reduction. She also gives knockback immunity and shoots orbs that heal the player over time.
