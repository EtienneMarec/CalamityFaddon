The '''Night Cloak''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[covering]] , That gives the following bonuses:<br>
200% increased max HP and Mana<br>
Immunity to Umbrus Fumes and Holy Flames<br>
200% increased damage<br>

If any Elemental is alive:<br>
The player gets the obstructed debuff<br>
400% increased damage<br>
all the player's attacks inflict Holy Flames

== Notes ==
The covering cant be equipped if the player has [[The Verboten One]], [[Zenith Arcanum]], [[Heart of the Elements]] or any of its ingredients equipped.
