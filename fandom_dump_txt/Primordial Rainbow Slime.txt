The Primordial Rainbow Slime is an Epilogue [[mini boss]] that can rarely spawn around [[Pitchblende]] planetoids found around the top of the [[Caelus]] [[subworld]]. It moves slowly around the player and attacks through releasing an even spread of {{dv|13||||20}} {{Item|Exothermal Scythe|size=30px|ext=gif|nolink=yes}}s in a burst of {{dv|3|||4}}, then immediately after this it will fire a {{item|Gauss Nuke|link=|ext=gif|size=22px}}. This attack will be repeated every {{dv|6||5}} seconds.

The Primordial Rainbow Slime drops an assortment of [[ores]], as well as [[Primordial Ore]] when it is killed with any weapon that is crafted with [[Miracle Matter]]. It can also be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].

== Trivia ==
*Primordial Rainbow Slimes appear to be wearing [[Celestial Tracers]]; however, they do not drop them.
