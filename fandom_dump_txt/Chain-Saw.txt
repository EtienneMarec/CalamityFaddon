The ''Chain-Saw'' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Melee weapon]] that autoswings. It is dropped by [[Polyphemalus]], when Exotrexia is defeated last. Despite being a chainsaw both visually and in name, it does not have the ability to cut down trees. When an enemy is struck with the blade, Chain-Saw applies a stacking darkness effect. As more stacks are gained, the player is applied the [[Darkness]], [[Blackout]], and [[Obstructed]] debuffs. Over time, when not hitting an enemy, darkness stacks will decay. A player can see the amount of darkness they have by looking at the item's name, which will have a darkness percentage attached to the end. The darkness effect provides boosts to critical strike, and all stacks can be consumed with a right-click to shoot homing eye projectiles.

{{modifier|meleealt}}

==Notes==
* On hit, 10 (1.6%) darkness charge is applied, and a one-second cooldown before darkness charge starts decaying is applied. If Chain-Saw gets a critical hit, 20 (3.3%) charge is applied instead. Chain-Saw's charge maxes out at 600 (100%). Once darkness charge starts decaying, one charge will be removed once every four frames, for a total of 15 (2.5%) per second. When a right-click attack is used, the darkness charge will be reset to 0.
* the [[Darkness]] debuff is applied if the character has any darkness charge. the [[Blackout]] debuff is applied if the character has more than 30% charge, and the [[Obstructed]] debuff is applied if the character has more than 60% charge.
* As darkness charge increases, so does the critical strike chance of the weapon. This increases linearly, with 0% critical strike with no darkness and 100% critical strike when at 90% darkness.
* When a right click is used, the amount of eyes shot depends on the darkness level. Unlike the critical strike chance, this is done in discreet increments instead of a smooth scaling. 
** If the player has any darkness charge, 3 eyes will be shot
** If they have more than 30% charge, 9 eyes will be shot
** If they have more than 60% charge, 18 eyes will be shot
** If they have more than 90% charge, 27 eyes will be shot
** If they have full charge, 32 eyes will be shot.

==Tips==
*The debuffs this weapon provides allows it to synergize really well with the [[Laudanum]], cancelling its obstructive effect while granting a grand total of +95 defense and +85% damage increase.
**While it also grants +45% critical chance boost, this boost is redundant due to it not benefitting from any critical chance boost aside from itself.
