The '''Rusty Medallion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that can be found in [[Rusty Chest]]s. While equipped, [[ranged weapons]] have a 20% chance to release 3 acidic droplets from the sky. The droplets deal 30% of the weapon's base damage and behave identically to the droplets from [[Flak Toxicannon]]. The droplets are softcapped at 30 damage, with any further damage increases being 90% less effective.
