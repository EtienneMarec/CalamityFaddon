The '''Silver Heart''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a 0.1% chance to be obtained whenever any type of [[Souls|soul]] or [[Essence (disambiguation)|essence]] is collected. It summons Eleanor to protect you, which absorbs the debuffs from an enemy every 15 seconds and gives boosts to the player depending on how many debuffs were removed.

Whenever Eleanor absorbs a debuff from an enemy, one of the following boosts are randomly given to the player as increased stats for 20 seconds:

*Life Regeneration by 6
*Defense Reduction by 3%
*Damage by 15
*Damage by 4%
*
*Critical Strike Chance by 2%
*Critical Damage by 10%
