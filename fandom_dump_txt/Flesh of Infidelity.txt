The '''Flesh of Infidelity''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart of the [[Eye of Night]]. It summons a flesh ball that jumps around erratically near the player. When an enemy is nearby, it jumps around near that enemy and fires a burst of 2 blood particles upwards every 3 jumps, which homes into enemies but does not pierce nor travel through tiles.
