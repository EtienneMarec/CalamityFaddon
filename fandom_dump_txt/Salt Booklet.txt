The '''Salt Booklet''' is a [[spell tome]] that fires a cube of salt. It can be obtained by using [[Sulphurous Sand]] at the [[Extractinator]]. The projectile travels a short distance before falling to the ground. It dissipates immediately after hitting a block or enemy.
