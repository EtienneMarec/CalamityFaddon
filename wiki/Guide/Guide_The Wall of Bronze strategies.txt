'''[[The Wall of Bronze]]''' is an optional boss fought after [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. Although it is initially spawned in the [[Brimstone Crag]]s, a majority of the fight will occur in [[The Underworld]]. The Wall of Bronze is summoned by placing a [[Bloody Vein]] into the [[Ancient Console]].

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
It is important that a large section of the Underworld is completely clear of obstructing terrain. Structures such as hills or hell buildings should be destroyed to allow for an easily navigatable path through the Underworld. Unlike the [[Wall of Flesh]], whom the Wall of Bronze is similar to, the player cannot build a long strip of blocks to avoid being hit by projectiles, as all of the bosses projectiles can pass through solid objects. It is still a very good idea to build a long, flat bridge spanning the length of your arena. The bridge should be made of platforms to allow for movement between the top and bottom half of the arena with ease. Another option may be to build the bridge out of [[Asphalt Block]]s, as the Wall of Bronze can move quite fast nearing the end of the fight.

While extra rails or platforms aren't entirely necessary for the fight, as there is not much vertical space to utilize in the Underworld, one or two rows of platforms placed above and below the main platform can be useful in dodging the walls deathrays. It is also recommended to place heart lanterns, campfires, and sunflowers across the arena, as they are cheap and can give the player useful [[Buffs]]. While the candles the [[Drunk Princess]] provides are useful, they are rather expensive, and thusly would be a large investment to cover an entire bridge across the Underworld. If the player has the money to spare however, the buffs from the candles are extremely helpful.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Auric Tesla armor]] is the preferred choice for all classes.

=== Coverings ===
==== Movement ====
*Seeing as how horizontal speed is of such importance in the fight, [[Celestial Tracers]] are far more desirable then any set of [[Wings]].
*A form of dash, such as from the [[Asgardian Aegis]] or [[Statis' Void Sash]] can be quite useful for avoiding the many projectiles the Wall of Bronze fires.
**Additionally, the Asgardian Aegis provides many defensive buffs that help immensely in such a crowded fight.

==== Defensive ====
*The [[Core of the Blood God]] is a powerful covering for both offense and defense.
*[[The Amalgam]] allows the player to dodge any attack once every 3 minutes, and increases the duration of buffs.
*The [[Rampart of Deities]] gives players many defensive benefits, such as decreased potion sickness, and increased immunity frames.


==== Offensive ====

**[[Elemental Gauntlet]] for Melee players.
**[[Elemental Quiver]] for Ranged players.
**[[Ethereal Talisman]] for Mage players.
**[[Nucleogenesis]] for Summoner players.
**[[Nanotech]] for Rogue players.

== The Fight ==
The Wall of Bronze spawns from the side of the screen furthest from the center of the world, and will move towards it, gaining speed as its health lowers. You will want to constantly be a safe distance away from the wall, as its many weapons will have a far easier time reaching you at close range. However, staying 100 or more blocks away from the wall for over 10 seconds will cause it to enrage. It is crucial the player never leave this zone unless they are in immediate danger of dying, as it will cost them time to defeat the boss before it reaches the edge of the world.

The attack pattern of each weapon individually are fairly easy to dodge
*'''Bronze Claws''' will perform a predictive lunge at the player, before retracting once they come in contact with a block or reach the end of their range. Try to only dodge after the claw begins moving. Dashes are especially helpful for avoiding claws. Also always be sure to watch out for the returning claw, as it can still damage the player.

*'''Bronze Turrets''' will fire 5 lasers at the player in rapid succession, readjusting their aim after each shot. They are somewhat harder to dodge, and can clutter the screen quickly if the player is moving too much, so try to move only short distances when being targeted. To compensate for their high rate of fire, turrets don't deal major amounts of damage, but they can inflict On Fire!, so they should still be avoided.

*'''Bronze Lasers''' will begin to charge up, signaled by a loud sound cue and their bulbs glowing yellow, for around 5 seconds, before firing a large death ray and sliding up or down from their current position towards the face. They will cease firing once they reach the face, and will not begin charging again for quite some time. If the player hears the signature sound of a laser charging, they should identify the direction the laser will move in, and try to make their way to the half of the screen that is safe. More then one laser cannot fire at the same time, so the player cannot become trapped.

*'''The Face''' is the main core of the wall. Only one can exist at a time, and it will always stay in the middle of the space between the floor and ceiling of the arena. It can fire 2 spreads of lasers
