'''[[The Derellect]]''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is usually fought alongside [[Calamitas]] and [[Plantera]]. The Derellect can be spawned by using a [[Mechanical Worm]] while the player has a [[Bloody Vein]] equipped.

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression. 

== Terrain Preparation ==

The Derellect requires a large vertical arena due to its lingering sparks. Wiring the ground to actuators to drop the sparks when the player leaves the area is recommended if you're experienced with wiring.
