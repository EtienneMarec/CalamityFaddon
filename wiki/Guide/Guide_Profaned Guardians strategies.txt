The '''[[Profaned Guardians]]''' are usually the first Post-[[Moon Lord]] bosses that you will face in your playthrough. The Profaned Guardians are summoned by using the [[Profaned Shard]] in [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]] biome during the day.

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
A long platform is generally enough for this boss. Ensure that you have plenty of room to run along. If you previously built a long platform to fight the [[Wall of Flesh]], that can be reused here.

A modestly sized square arena will also be an acceptable choice, especially in the underworld, where maintaining the Hallow biome is not a concern.

It is imperative that you remain within the Hallow or in the Underworld. [[Actuator]]s are a great tool in expanding the Hallow in an arena without getting in the way.

If you are doing the fight in [[Malice Mode]] you should make the platform out of [[Asphalt Block]]s as it makes the fight considerably easier.

Consider fighting underwater if you’re struggling. This will weaken all of the fire attacks sent at you.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*Lunar armors are the preferred choice for all classes.
**[[Solar Flare armor]] for Melee
**[[Vortex armor]] for Ranged
**[[Nebula armor]] for Mage
**[[Stardust armor]] for Summoner
**[[Empyrean armor]] for Rogue

=== Coverings ===
*[[Lunar Wings]] (with [[Angel Treads]]) or [[Seraph Tracers]] are the preferred wings.
*[[Asgard's Valor]] or [[Statis' Ninja Belt]] allow the player to make sharp turns and are very useful.
**Asgard's Valor additionally gives knockback immunity and many defensive boosts.
**Statis' Ninja Belt additionally provides the chance of a ninja dodge and increased jump acceleration.
*[[The Absorber]] is important for survivability by increasing damage reduction as well as giving increased jump acceleration.
*[[The Community]] is a great all-around covering that boosts offensive and defensive stats as well as boosting flight time.
*The [[Heart of the Elements]] provides many all-around boosts including damage, damage reduction, life regen, and jump acceleration.
*The [[Deific Amulet]] is an excellent option, providing additional [[immunity frames]] and defense penetration, as well as allowing 25% faster health potion use.
*Melee players should use the [[Bloody Worm Scarf]] for its strong melee boosts as well as increased damage reduction.
*Ranged players should use the [[Sniper Scope]] or [[Deadshot Brooch]], which both help in boosting damage output.
**Deadshot Brooch additionally gives some life regen and ammo conservation.
*Magic players should use the [[Sigil of Calamitas]] for its strong magic boosts.
*Summoners can boost their minion count and damage with [[Statis' Blessing]], the [[Star-Tainted Generator]] and [[Statis' Curse]].
*The [[Dark God's Sheath]] can be a good covering for Rogue players, provided they can gain stealth to perform stealth strikes.
**It also provides several passive Rogue bonuses.

=== Weapons ===
The weapons made from [[Luminite Bar]]s are typically the best options for the fight.
==== Melee ====
*The [[True Biome Blade]] is an excellent option with homing and high single target damage.
*The [[Ark of the Elements]] and the [[Plague Keeper]] have faster homing at the cost of lower DPS.
*The [[Stellar Striker]] is an extremely powerful [[true melee]] option that makes quick work of the guardians.
*The [[Elemental Disk]] is an excellent choice, being able to hit both the Rock and Crystal Guardians at the same time.
*[[Stellar Contempt]] performs similarly with high raw damage while being able to strike more than one guardian.
*The [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Terrarian?so=search Terrarian] can perform quite well when targeting one guardian at a time, although it requires close range.

==== Ranged ====
*The [[Elemental Blaster]] can deal high amounts of damage with homing projectiles, and is a good option for the latter 2 phases.
*The [[Onyx Chain Blaster]] is an excellent option that deals high damage when landing the onyx blasts.
*The [[Shredder]] can deal solid damage if all the bullets hit.
*The [[Disseminator]] is an incredibly powerful option, allowing for high DPS on all three guardians.
*The [[Golden Eagle]] can be used throughout the entire fight due to its high damage and tight spread.
*[[Planetary Annihilation]] with [[Wooden Arrow]]s is a solid pick with homing.
*[[Prideful Hunter's Planar Ripper]] with [[Musket Ball]]s is very effective for this battle. movement boost will help you evade attacks. 
*[[Holy Fire Bullet]]s should be used.


==== Mage ====
*Although DPS is lower, [[Nuclear Fury]] is a good homing option allowing the player to focus on dodging.
*The [[Apoctosis Array]] deals immense damage when high on mana. As this weapon requires attentive management of mana, the use of the [[Mana Flower]] is discouraged when using this weapon.
*The [[Augur of the Elements]] is an excellent choice, dealing high amounts of damage at the cost of limited range.
*The [[Last Prism]] is a good choice dealing high damage to the guardians.
*The [[Elemental Ray]] and [[Genisis]] are additional options with good DPS as well.
*The [[Ultra Liquidator]] is an excellent weapon for both dealing damage and applying [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] and [[Cursed Inferno]] for short periods of time.
*The [[Effervescence]] makes short work of the guardians due to its extreme damage and large area of effect.

==== Summoner ====
*The [[Elemental Axe]] is a good option being able to easily target and kill all the guardians.
*The [[Tactical Plague Engine]] is also a good option but requires a lot of ammo due to its high fire rate.
*The [[Energy Staff]] provides additional DPS to aid the player's minions.

==== Rogue ====
*Both the [[Elemental Disk]] and [[Stellar Contempt]] perform similarly to their Melee counterparts, dealing good DPS at the cost of smaller range.
*The [[Utensil Poker]] is an extremely viable option due to its massive damage output.
*[[Lunar Kunai]] are useful for this fight as they are able to home. In addition to this, they can deal immense damage to the guardians.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all around stat boost.
*Farm [[Blood Orb]]s to easily craft many helpful [[potions]] that will increase your survivability.

== The Fight ==
The Profaned Guardians will spawn offscreen, meaning you should be prepared to dodge them as they approach you.

For purposes of clarity, the Guardians will be referred to as their in game names, with the Main Guardian being the Commander Guardian, the Rock Guardian being the Defender Guardian, and the Crystal Guardian being the Healer Guardian.

When summoned, the Commander Guardian will be practically invincible. Until the Healing Guardian and the Defender Guardian are defeated, the Commander Guardian will have insane health regeneration and defense, respectively.When the fight begins, the Guardians will charge at you in a standard formation: The Commander Guardian will lead and fire two large, persisting flame projectiles at the player in a V shape, the Defender Guardian will follow shortly behind while firing slower and shorter range flame projectiles, and the Healer Guardian will attempt to stay as far away as possible, providing immense health regeneration to both other guardians. These have predictable gaps, so considerable attention should be given to ensure the player can pass through them safely. The Commander and Defender will operate within incredibly close proximity for the majority of the fight, with the Commander often speeding in front of the Defender and tanking the majority of the hits if fired at directly. Both guardians will now fire Profaned Flame projectiles which will linger, so be careful not to hit them while dodging the dashes or profaned spears. The Rod of Discord or Normality Relocator can be used effectively here to escape a dire situation in a pinch. Magic and Summon players, however, should maintain their distance, especially when using a penetrating weapon such as the Last Prism or the natural targeting of summons. Upon defeating the Defender Guardian, the Commander Guardian will enter its final phase, and an incredibly quick flurry of attacks will follow. Killing the Commander as fast as possible is imperative, although getting hit by its attacks will leave the player with very little HP remaining. The easiest way to defeat the first phase (Upon killing the Healer, the AI of the Commander and Defender will change) is to constantly move up and down, which will typically prevent a single flame from touching the player. When the Healer is defeated, the Commander and Defender will begin charging at the player, and with each charge, the Commander will release six Profaned Spears, while the Defender will release ten, both in a circular pattern. These have predictable gaps, so considerable attention should be given to ensure the player can pass through them safely. The Commander and Defender will operate within incredibly close proximity for the majority of the fight, with the Commander often speeding in front of the Defender and tanking the majority of the hits if fired at directly. Both guardians will now fire Profaned Flame projectiles which will linger, so be careful not to hit them while dodging the dashes or profaned spears. The Rod of Discord or Normality Relocator can be used effectively here to escape a dire situation in a pinch. Make sure to go underwater often to negate all of the flame attacks. For players in Revengeance+, a good strategy is to build Adrenaline during the Healer phase due to the ease of which you can dodge and kill the Healer, then activate Adrenaline and, if possible, Rage, which will increase your damage substantially. To deal with the Defender, it's a good idea to play aggressively, to separate the Commander and the Defender, leaving you able to damage the Defender as opposed to the Commander blocking him. Magic and Summon players, however, should maintain their distance, especially when using a penetrating weapon such as the Last Prism or the natural targeting of summons. Upon defeating the Defender Guardian, the Commander Guardian will enter its final phase, and an incredibly quick flurry of attacks will follow. Killing the Commander as fast as possible is imperative, although getting hit by its attacks will leave the player with very little HP remaining. This goes twofold for Revengeance+, where the Commander will now deal significantly higher damage from all of its attacks. Maintain distance, and use Adrenaline or just chip away as much as possible. Avoid running into the many traps it will set around the arena, such as its exploding flares.

The enrage can be dangerous, but is actually quite manageable. Consider fighting the Profaned Guardians in the ocean. This will make it easy to extinguish their fire attacks if you’re low on health.
== General Tips ==
*The [[Normality Relocator]] or [[Rod of Discord]] are very strong tools as they allow instantaneous teleporting.
**Be wary when teleporting with the Normality Relocator, as the player will fall at a much greater pace if unprepared in a similar manner to the [[Portal Gun]].
*The [[Ultra Liquidator]] can also be used to apply Ichor and Cursed Flames for other classes such as Ranger.
**The Ultra Liquidator activates the Summoner nerf. As such, the [[Golden Gun]] should be used to apply Ichor instead.
