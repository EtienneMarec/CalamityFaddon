'''The Inventors''' are a duo of [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[boss|bosses]]. They are intended to be the first boss the player fights, even before the [[King Slime]].

{{Fantag|Community Content Remix}} In the Community Content Remix Mod, the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] acts as an alternative boss fight.

This guide will mention items exclusive to Expert Mode with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression. Revengeance Mode exclusive items are not included, as the Rage and Adrenaline mechanics are locked behind defeating The Inventors.

==Terrain Preperation==
As the pair of bosses are limited in movement, especially Jane, building a platform above the ground can be very helpful in the battle. However, John does gain the ability to jump higher at 25% health, so be wary. Placing down [[Campfire]]s and [[Heart Lantern]]s will also be very helpful during the fight. 

==Gearing Up==
===Armor===
*[[Gold armor]] or [[Platinum armor]] are solid choices for the fight, especially for melee players, because of their comparatively high defense for the early game.
*[[Desert Prowler armor]] is a good armor for rangers.
*[[Snow Ruffian armor]] is a solid choice for Rogue players
*[[Jungle armor]] can be used for mages , but can be tricky to get due to the Underground Jungle's harsh environment.
*Ironically, [[Wulfrum armor]] is the best choice for summoners at this stage of the game. It may be advised to put on another armor as vanity to make it easier to tell the difference between you and the real John Wulfrum
===Coverings===
*[[Hermes Boots]] and any upgrades are extremely useful for this fight.
*[[Cloud in a Bottle]], [[Blizzard in a Bottle]], and [[Sandstorm in a Bottle]] are also useful for dodging attacks.
**[[Fledgling Wings]] are another option that also allows gliding, but is slightly slower.
*The [[Frog Leg]] may also be useful as a jump booster.
*[[Luxor's Gift]] is a powerful damage boosting covering that works well for all classes.
*The [[Worm Meal]] can prove to be an extremely useful pre-boss summoner covering.
