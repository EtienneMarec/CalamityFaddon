[[Guide:Pyrogen strategies|Pyrogen]] is a really simple boss, if you can't beat him, using good weapons like [[tgc:Wand_of_Sparking|Wand of sparking]] or [[tgc:Zenith|Zenith]] is reccomended.

Using good mobility accesories can be helpfull too, try using the [[tgc:Step_Stool|Terra Wings (click the link to see more)]] or [[tgc:Arctic_Diving_Gear|Arctic Diving Gear]].

Terra wings give great mobility, especially when the player has [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Searing_Lava Searing Lava] buff, Arctic Diving Gear is used in reverse psychology attempt.
