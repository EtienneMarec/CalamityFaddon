'''[[Astrum Deus]]''' is a late [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that can be fought after the [[Lunatic Cultist]] in order to gain access to [[Astral Ore]]. Astrum Deus is summoned by using a [[Titan Heart]] on an [[Astral Beacon]] at night. For future battles after the first defeat, Astrum Deus may be summoned using a [[Starcore]] on an Astral Beacon at night instead.

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
Astrum Deus is not bound to any biome and will not enrage due to biome restrictions. As Astrum Deus requires a lot of space, it is important to cover up holes in the ground to prevent yourself from getting caught by uneven terrain. Generally a large open space with several platforms is adequate.

For a dedicated arena, flatten out the surface of a biome (or an area that's about 800 to 1000 blocks long). It is suggested to line the floor of the arena with [[Ice Block]]s because you have significantly higher acceleration on it. You'll need 3,000+ platforms for this, so get those first. Place four layers of platforms, each spaced at least fifty blocks apart from the nearest block/platform. This will give you plenty of vertical and horizontal space to fight the boss.

Additionally, killing a few of the [[Celestial Pillars]] will minimize interruptions during the fight.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Hydrothermic armor]] is generally the preferred choice for almost all classes.
*Additional options include [[Beetle armor]] for Melee players, [[Spectre armor]] for Mage players, [[Plague Reaper armor]] for Ranger players at the cost of some defense, and [[Plaguebringer armor]] for Summoner players.

=== Accessories ===
*[[Wings]] are a near necessity. The preferred wings are the [[Fishron Wings]] or the [[MOAB]].
*[[Angel Treads]] are extremely useful for boosting flight time and keeping distance from Astrum Deus.
*The [[Heart of the Elements]] is a great option that gives a sizable jump boost as well as summoning the elementals for extra DPS.
*[[Asgard's Valor]]  provides a dash that helps increase horizontal mobility.
**Asgard's Valor provides defensive boosts such as knockback immunity.
*The [[Celestial Jewel]] and [[Ursa Sergeant]] help minimize the impact of the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] debuff.
**Alternatively, the [[Margarita]] drink will halve its duration at the cost of some defense and life regeneration.
*Melee players using yoyos should equip a [[Yoyo Bag]] to increase the range and effectiveness of the yoyo.
*Magic players can make use of the [[Sigil of Calamitas]] and/or [[Mana Flower]] along with [[Mana Potion]]s to recover their mana during the fight.
**[[Mana Regeneration Potion]]s can be vital as it allows mana to regen quickly even when the player is moving.
*Summoners can boost their minion count and damage with the [[Statis' Blessing]] and [[Statis' Curse]].
*The Vampiric Talisman for rogue players helps a ton in increasing survivability as well as giving some extra damage. 

=== Weapons ===
Weapons made from the [[Lunar Fragment]]s are typically your best options.

==== Melee ====
*The [[Aegis Blade]] is an extremely good [[true melee]] weapon that can decimate Astrum Deus. However, it can be very hard to obtain due to it being a [[legendary weapon]].
**The [[Ultimus Cleaver]] is a slightly weaker option that deals extreme damage to the boss and is significantly easier to obtain.
*The [[Grand Guardian]] performs phenomenally with large explosions on every hit.
*The [[Tenebreus Tides]] performs very well due to its high damage and piercing.
*The [[Solar Eruption]]'s piercing will do high damage against Astrum Deus, especially when hitting many worms at once. However, its low range can make it risky to use.
*The [[Brimlash Buster]] is a strong weapon that can hit many worm segments at once allowing for high damage.
*The [[Entropic Claymore]] is a powerful sword that can deal high piercing damage with its homing plasma balls.
*The [[True Forbidden Oathblade]], while being of a lower tier, has high damage that can mitigate Astrum Deus' defense.
*The [[Pwnagehammer]], a ''much'' lower tier weapon, has high explosive damage in a large AoE, making it incredibly powerful against both phases of Astrum Deus. The chance for much more powerful homing hammers also contributes greatly to ease of use and how powerful it can be, though even regular throws are incredibly strong. Typically, it will deal consistently high damage without [[Adrenaline]], outperforming even Lunar fragment weapons, but will benefit less from the buff than other choices. It should typically be used while [[Adrenaline]] is charging.
*The [[Hydraulic Volt Crasher]] can deal extreme damage to Astrum Deus, especially with adrenaline. It can bring the boss to its second phase with one cycle of adrenaline, and bring one of the worms to extremely low HP, or potentially even kill the boss, with a second cycle. However, its range is incredibly short.

==== Ranged ====
*The [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Impaler Impaler] is a surprisingly outstanding option due to it's ability to pierce tons of segments as well as occasionally triggering explosions. It can even do better than the Aerial bane and v
*The [[Aerial Bane]] is an phenomenal choice when fire above the boss, as it's barrages of explosive arrows would do
*The [[God's Bellows]] performs very well for the boss' first phase with its high piercing allowing it to deal significant damage to many segments at once. Though, this weapon falls a bit off at the boss' second phase due to the shorter and faster worms, especially when they're not often grouped together.
*The [[Charged Dart Blaster]] performs well, particularly with the alternate fire, enabling it to hit many targets at once.
**[[Crystal Dart]]s with the primary fire can also do reliable damage.
*The [[Phantasm]] with [[Holy Arrow]]s is an excellent choice due to their piercing.
*The [[Vortexpopper]] deals amazing damage, as its storm of bullets usually all hit the boss due to it's numerous segments.
**[[Icy Bullet]]s are the preferred ammunition for guns, as their piercing and shattering effects work amazing against worms like Astrum Deus himself. [[Hyperius Bullet|Hyperius Bullets]] and [[Acid Round|Acid Rounds]] are quite less effective but can be used. Chlorophyte Bullets have lackluster damage but can be used if the player really wants homing.
**[[Rocket III]]s should be utilized with [[Launchers]].
*The [[Gauss Rifle]] works very well together with Plague Reaper armor, plus [[Adrenaline]] if the player is playing on Revengeance Mode or higher.
*The Scorpio is a pretty straight forward homing option but it's DPS is outclassed by many other weapons on this list.
*The [[Hellborn]] can make light work of both phases whilst tanking inside the boss, and using Adrenaline and [[Purified Jam]] can up the damage even further, completely decimating the boss. However, it can be very difficult to obtain both the weapon and the jam, due to the Hellborn being a [[challenge item]] and the Purified Jam only being obtainable once per world.

==== Mage ====
*The [[Keelhaul]] does an absurd amount of damage to Astrum Deus.
*The [[Eidolon Staff]] is a powerful option that can hit many targets at once.
*The [[Tome of Fates]] is similar to the Solar Eruption. It has good damage at the cost of being quite dangerously close to the worms.
*The [[Razorblade Typhoon]] can tear Astrum Deus apart swiftly.
*The [[Lazhar]] is a high damage option with strong piercing allowing it to melt the boss.
*The [[Hematemesis]], although it can't pierce, is a good option as all of the projectiles from its wide attack will likely hit.
*The [[Cosmic Rainbow]] is a powerful option that can be awkward to use but does well as the worms are falling back to the ground.

==== Summoner ====
*The [[Stardust Dragon Staff]] is the best option for summoners, as it can pierce the many segments of Astrum Deus, especially when combined with the elementals from the [[Heart of the Elements]].
*The [[Spikecrag Staff]] is an excellent option with its wide area of effect being able to take advantage of Astrum Deus' large hitboxes.

==== Rogue ====
*[[Star of Destruction]] can pierce the boss if used properly, and should definitely be utilised.
*[[Cranium Smasher]] is a great option for Rogue users with good piercing and DPS.
*[[Plaguenade]]s are an amazing option for their high damage explosion and piercing, especially when paired with the [[Plague Hive]].
**[[Shock Grenade]]s may be a better option due to the fact that they do not deal reduced damage to the boss.

==== Classless ====
*[[Yanmei's Knife]] is an extremely powerful weapon which is made most effective right when Astrum Deus spawns, due to its debuff dealing 125 damage per second, so multiple segments can be inflicted with it. It should be treated as more than a side-weapon in this fight.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all around stat boost.
*Farming the [[Blood Moon]] for [[Blood Orb]]s is a great way to gather potions for the fight.

== The Fight ==
=== Phase 1 ===
Upon being summoned, Astrum Deus will take little damage until a few seconds have passed. Astrum Deus has 3 different attacks in this phase:

*Fires lasers from each segment of its body, one by one.
*Leaves behind astral mines where it initially traveled.
*Attempts to ram into the player.

The lasers can be dodged by constantly maneuvering around Astrum Deus, while the mines released should be kept at a distance or have their placements memorized to help with staying away from them. Astrum Deus's rams may be avoided by simply jumping or dashing out of the way, however it should be noted that the boss' projectiles may occasionally get in the way, whether it be left over mines or lasers directly fired afterwards.

=== Phase 2 ===
Once Astrum Deus is below 50% of its health, it will split in half and the fight will be composed of 2 worms. This is the most difficult section of the fight, as while each worm still goes with the original worm's AI, both of them act seperately and are considerably faster, meaning it is much more difficult to avoid attacks from one worm without being intercepted by the other. It should be noted however, that only one of the worms needs to be killed to end the fight entirely, so attacks should be focused on only one worm if possible. Phase 1 measures to avoid attacks still work in this phase, although dashing is notably riskier for avoiding rams from either worm.

== General Tips ==
*Disabling backgrounds can help players who have trouble focusing on all the different things that are going on during the fight.
*In Revengeance mode and higher, using [[Adrenaline]] or [[Rage]] can remove a large chunk of Astrum Deus' health when it is low to swiftly end the fight.
*Using the Overlay [[Minimap]] and changing its transparency with {{key|Page Up}} and {{key|Page Down}} keys can provide the player with icon of bosses that are too far to see without the Minimap, making it a useful tactic to aim at bosses.
*Using the [[Rod of Discord]] can allow a quick escape, especially if you are walled by several mines.
