'''[[XP-00 Hypnos]]''' is one of the final Post-[[Moon Lord]] bosses that you will face in your playthrough. Hypnos is summoned by using a [[Bloody Vein]] at the fully-upgraded form of [[The Codebreaker]].

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
You will benefit greatly from deleting an entire world and replacing it with hammered platforms. Should this be undoable, a smaller space (such as a singular biome) will work, though you may find yourself hurting for the extra space.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Auric Tesla armor]] is the preferred choice for all classes.

=== Accessories ===
==== Movement ====
*[[Drew's Wings]] or the [[Celestial Tracers]] are the preferred movement accessories for this fight. The former offers increased control and a higher flight time, while the latter provides increased speed and immunity frames.
*The [[Asgardian Aegis]] or [[Statis' Void Sash]] allow the player to make sharp turns, which can be useful for dodging fast attacks.
**The Asgardian Aegis additionally provides a ram and immunity to knockback. It's offensive and defensive capabilities are especially useful for the second phase of the fight, where all attacks get significantly harder.

==== Defensive ====
*[[The Sponge]], [[Core of the Blood God]], and the [[Rampart of Deities]] are all great defensive accessories that will substantially increase the player's survivability. 
*[[The Community]] is a great all-around accessory that boosts offensive and defensive stats as well as providing more movement speed.
*[[Affliction]] is a good accessory that provides both offensive and defensive buffs.

==== Offensive ====
*Melee players should use the [[Elemental Gauntlet]] for its strong melee boosts as well as increased immunity frames.
**[[Warbanner of the Sun]] is another good option as Hypnos will usually be close to the player.
*Ranged players should use the [[Elemental Quiver]], as it works well in boosting damage output.
*Magic players should use the [[Ethereal Talisman]] for its magic damage and crit chance boosts as well as helping maintain mana when combined with [[Supreme Mana Potion]]s.
*Summoners can boost their minion count and damage with the [[Nucleogenesis]].
**The [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] summons guardians that boost the player's survivability and offensive capabilities, including providing an extra minion slot.
**The [[Godly Soul Artifact]] will help boost the player's minion count if they are using the summon from [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]].
*Rogue users will find the [[Nanotech]] useful to boost their damage output.
**Stealth based Rogues will find the [[Eclipse Mirror]] to be more useful.
**The tornado from [[Dragon Scales]] can do heavy damage to all the plugs.
*The [[Occult Skull Crown]] proves incredibly useful for doing large amounts of burst damage to Hypnos itself.
*If [[Supreme Calamitas]] has been defeated, the [[Shattered Community]] can be used, providing a variety of stat boosts and greatly increasing the damage of [[Rage Mode]].

=== Weapons ===
The weapons made from [[Auric Bar]]s or dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] are typically the best options for the fight. Note that some of these weapons require defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]], who is on the same tier as the Exo Mechs. Thus, one boss' drops can greatly help with defeating the other.

==== Melee ====
*Anything with an 'on hit' effect will perform incredibly well against the Hypnos Plugs due to their large number.
*The [[Murasama]]'s range and high DPS make it an all-around good choice for this fight, but it is especially useful in Phase 1, as it can hit all plugs at once.
*The [[Dragon Rage]] is incredibly powerful against Hypnos' plugs, as it deals incredible damage and procs it's on hit effect near-constantly due to the amount of targets.
*The [[Dragon Pow]] is excellent at destroying the plugs in the first phase, but struggles to deal consistent damage in Phase 2.
*The [[The Enforcer|Enforcer]] is excellent, if risky, due to the frequency Hypnos enters melee range in both stages of the fight.
**Getting underneath the fan of neurons is incredibly easy and allows burst damage to all plugs.
*[[Violence]] performs very well regardless of the fight's stage.

==== Ranged ====
*The [[Minigun]] with [[God Slayer Slug]]s is a solid choice that can deal high damage in Phase 2.
**Chlorophyte may be prefered until the plugs are defeated, as they are very small targets, though the damage isn't desirable. It can hit them when other weapons can't, however.
*The [[Drataliornus]] is a powerful option for the second phase, but is too risky to use during the first phase of the fight.
*[[Tyranny's End]] is an excellent option for the second phase of the fight due to Hypnos' single-target nature.
*The [[Dragon's Breath]] performs adequately against the plugs, as Hypnos' laser fan allows it to hit all four of them, when positioned to the side.
*The [[Chicken Cannon]] is great against the Plugs, though it can't perform incredibly against Hypnos itself.
*The [[Pulse Rifle]] does decent damage to the plugs.

==== Mage ====
*The [[Aetherflux Cannon]] is an easily obtainable option that is sufficient for the fight.
*[[Yharim's Crystal]] is a decent damage-dealer for the plugs, but struggles to justify it's use over other options in Phase 2.
**[[Rancor]] is much more powerful, and does not have this problem.
*The [[Voltaic Climax]] is great against the plugs and useful against Hypnos while it rears up to charge.
*[[Gruesome Eminence]] is exceptionally good against Hypnos in all stages of the fight, doing incredible damage to both the plugs and the boss itself.

==== Summoner ====
*[[Ares' Exoskeleton]] is an exceptionally powerful option if you manage to defeat the [[Exo Mechs]] first.
*[[Yharon's Kindle Staff]] is one of the strongest options for this fight, providing very high DPS.
**Extra summoning slots can be filled with the [[Midnight Sun Beacon]], the [[Staff of the Mechworm]], or [[Saros Possession]].
*[[Vigilance]] is a very powerful option.
*[[Metastasis]] can deal quite a lot of DPS to the plugs, but requires the player to manage the hearts that spawn.
**Desummoning and resummoning the minion will despawn all hearts, allowing the player's summons to focus on the mechs.
*The [[Pole Warper]] has decent results against Hypnos' second phase.
*The [[Cinders of Lament]] is ill-advised in general, but can perform well against the plugs if the player is careful.

==== Rogue ====
*[[The Final Dawn]]'s stealth strike dash does immense damage, and provides the player with immunity frames.
*The [[Wrathwing]] is an alternative with lower DPS that is easier to use.
*[[Plasma Grenade]]s are a decent option for this fight, especially against the plugs.
*[[Sacrifice]] is a powerful option providing good DPS and lifesteal.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The [[Rod of Discord]] or the [[Normality Relocator]] are very strong tools as they allows instantaneous teleportation.
**If you've defeated [[Supreme Calamitas]], the Rod of Harmony is an amazing choice as well.
*The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all around stat boost.
**[[Yharim's Stimulants]] are also vital for any class due to its high buffs to both defense and offense.
*Enchantments can greatly improve DPS if the player has defeated Supreme Calamitas first. 

== The Fight ==
The XP-00 Hypnos fight consists of 2/3 phases total.

*'''Phase 1''': Hypnos will attack the player until all plugs are defeated.
*'''Phase 2''': Hypnos will begin to attack the player itself.
*'''Phase 3''' (in Revengeance Mode+): Hypnos will berserk at 25% life, gaining a new attack and increased speed/aggression.

In general, Hypnos will follow the exact attack order listed here- it will only deviate on higher difficulties if interrupted by a health transition, and even then, it will only reset it's cycle.

=== Phase 1 ===

During this phase, Hypnos has 4 attacks:

-The Aergia Neurons line up in an upwards-pointing fan above XP-00 Hypnos, then shoot Blue Exo Pulse Lasers outwards.

-XP-00 Hypnos moves to the bottom left of the player and positions its Aergia Neurons into a wall formation facing upwards. 

-XP-00 Hypnos will begin to move between the bottom left and bottom right of the player at a gradually increasing speed, firing lasers randomly upward from it's neurons as it travels.

-The Aergia Neurons form a circle around XP-00 Hypnos and begin spinning rapidly, as XP-00 Hypnos begins charging towards the player, firing a ring of lasers from it's neurons every second as it travels. Every third charge is predictive.

-XP-00 Hypnos extends their Aergia Neurons upwards significantly farther than normal, settling them to top left, top right, bottom left, or bottom right of the player, then periodically rams them into you.

The fan of lasers can be avoided by flying upward or underneath Hypnos when he pauses. This attack is incredibly exploitable, and should be used to deal much higher damage than average.

The wall formation may seem daunting at first due to the laser formations' inconsistency, however flying upward and away from the beams trivializes avoiding this attack. It's optimal to avoid returning to the place you avoided this attack for a time to give the lasers a chance to despawn, however it is still possible to slip through the gaps due to their very generous hitboxes (remember: only the tip of the beam deals damage).

The circular charges are best avoided by 'kiting' Hypnos in the wrong direction; slipping through the gaps in the beam and circling to Hypnos's other side, he can be sent charging in a direction opposite to you. This will keep him far enough away for the majority of the attack that falling diagonally can keep him far enough away until he moves onto the next in his cycle. This is incredibly difficult to perform, however it is doable with practice.

For the most part, the Neuron's ramming can be avoided by diagonally falling. If the neurons to the bottom left or right of the player rear up to attack, however, you need to react quickly; dashing in the other direction can prevent you from taking a hit.

The unplugged Aergia Neurons look incredibly scary at first glance, however their contact damage is greatly reduced when unplugged due to how much more freedom they have in movement. If you have to take a hit, choose to take one from the Neurons that have been unplugged.

=== Phase 2 ===

During this phase, Hypnos loses his previous attacks, but gains 4 more:

-The Aergia Neurons pick random areas on the screen and stick to them for a few seconds, then create a 'net' of lasers between each other. After this, XP-00 Hypnos will charge towards the player. 

-XP-00 Hypnos begins to circle around the player from a far distance, gradually building up speed. The Aergia Neurons begin spinning around the player in a number of rings depending on the difficulty (2 rings of 6 generally, and 4 rings of 3 in Revengeance or higher). Random rings of neurons will then fire lasers inward toward the player. At the end of this attack, Hypnos will stop circling, then charge at the player.

-XP-00 Hypnos rapidly charges towards the player in a predictive, if slow-turning, manner. It's neurons will orbit it in an electrified ring around Hypnos, increasing the area of each ram.

-The Aergia Neurons line up in a vertical wall formation to the left of the player, then begin accelerating toward the other side of the screen. When they reach it, they snap back to the other side and repeat this process. 

The laser cage is a tricky attack to avoid, especially on higher difficulties- the best strategy is to search for an open area, then do your best to sharply turn out of the way of all of Hypnos' dashes. A ram can work in a pinch, but remember not to get overreliant on them, as failing the timing will cause you to take large amounts of damage as Hypnos capitalizes on your mistake.

The laser rings are incredibly difficult to avoid, and are far and away the most difficult attack in the fight; it's important to keep in mind, however, that damage is not inevitable. Picking a direction and falling in it helps, though quick adjustments will be required when neurons inevitably fire straight at you. Taking damage from this is not the end of the world, though it's highly advised to keep your Healing Potions prepared for this attack just in case worst comes to worst.

The ram attack is not horrifically difficult to avoid, though the adrenaline from the prior attack may get to you. Kiting this attack much like the ram in Phase 1 works, but flying away from Hypnos is usually a much more successful tactic.

When the Neurons begin to line up on the edge of the screen, stay still- the neurons above and below will not harm you as long as you maintain a consistent height level. Once you've settled, perform short hops over the neuron you're level with as it passes you. Doing this will usually be enough to avoid damage.

=== Higher Difficulty-specific Tips ===

==== Expert ====
In Phase 1, Hypnos begins to follow up the Neuron ramming with a new, if similar attack: having them fire rings of lasers.

This is a troubling thing to avoid, however it is not altogether unreasonable; using the same diagonal fall trick used on the Neurons ramming the player works. The dash timing is much more sharp, however.

Additionally, Hypnos gains a Large Tesla Sphere which pursues the player constantly. This may seem like a constant threat at first, however the exponential increase in it's speed is incredibly small and slow. It is unlikely to actually impede the fight much.

==== Revengeance ====

The Berserk attack is incredibly dangerous. It is important to remain moving in one direction, as getting caught close enough to where the Neurons meet will cause you to be greeted by a Gauss Nuke, which deals immense amounts of damage.

Remain moving in one direction and jump or fall if a neuron is about to hit you.

=== General Tips ===
*Internalize Hypnos' cycles, as they are consistent throughout the entire fight. Doing so will ensure you always know what's coming next.
*The plugs are tiny targets; utilize homing or the laser fan whenever possible to deal as much burst damage as you can per cycle.
*The [[God Slayer armor]]'s dash ability, [[Rod of Discord]], or the [[Normality Relocator]] are very useful tools for escaping certain situations that can lead to getting hit from a deadly attack.
**Do not, however, use the Rod of Discord to escape the laser cage in Phase 2. This will enrage Hypnos for a while and make your life significantly harder.
*The Suspicious Looking Jelly Bean or Slimy Saddle can be used to accelerate fall speed and avoid charges.
