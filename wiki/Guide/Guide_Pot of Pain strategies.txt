The [[Pot of Pain]] is an optional [[subworld]] accessed inside of the [[Jungle Temple]]. It is hidden behind a breakable wall at the beginning of the first buzzsaw room on the second floor, above the atrium. It can be entered anytime after defeating [[Plantera]], though the twin [[Golem]] fight at the end makes the challenge difficult before defeating the [[Moon Lord]].

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Gearing Up ==

=== [[Armor]] ===
*{{item|Reaver armor|size=25px|image=Reaver Visage.png}} while wearing the reaver visage is probably the best armor set for the Pot of Pain if entered immediately because of its several movement benefits and it being classless.
*If the player chooses to wait until defeating the Moon Lord then {{item|Winter Soldier armor|size=25px|image=Winter Soldier's Visage.png}} would be more preferable with its high defensive stats.
*{{item|Astral armor|size=25px|image=Astral Helm.png}} is a cheaper but less effective armor set as it is obtained before defeating the Moon Lord, but can still prove effective.

=== Coverings ===
*A dashing covering is an absolute must in order to defeat the Pot of Pain.
**The best covering would be the {{item|Asgardian Aegis|size=25px}} because of its long dash length and high defensive stats however it is only obtainable after defeating the [[Sentinels of the Devourer]].
**The {{item|Elysian Aegis|size=25px}} or {{item|Asgard's Valor|size=25px}} can also work if the player wants to obtain a lesser version of the Asgardian Aegis earlier.
**{{item|Statis' Void Sash|size=25px}} is another later game dash covering that can prove valuable as it has one of the longest dash lenghts of any covering.
**If the player wants to access the Pot of Pain as soon as possible then the {{item|Tabi|size=25px}} or {{item|Master Ninja Gear|size=25px}} can be serviceable.
*As [[Wings]] are disabled, it is valuable to have ways to gain vertical movement.
**The {{item|Plagued Fuel Pack|size=20px}} can act as a quick dash upwards if the player farms stealth off of heart statues.
**Any {{item|Cloud in a Bottle|bottle|size=20px}} covering or their upgrades provide helpful vertical boosts.
***Since the {{item|MOAB|size=25px}} combines several bottles, it can be one of if not the most important coverings to bring to the Pot of Pain.
*Wall jumping is required for certain sections of the Pot of Pain, so player's should bring {{item|link=tgc:Climbing Claws|Climbing Claws|size=25px}} or any of their upgrades.
*A class offensive covering such as the {{item|Fire Gauntlet|size=25px}} should be brought to fight the Golems at the end of the gauntlet.

=== Weapons ===
==== Melee ====
*Long true melee weapons are preferred for the Pot of Pain in order to increase the amount of rebound gained from pogoing off of obstacles.
**The {{item|Biome Blade|size=25px}}'s desert attunement has a built in pogo function and can trivialize certain sections of the Pot of Pain such as the final tunnel.
**Some longer swords include {{item|Briny Baron|size=25px}}, {{item|Astral Blade|size=25px}}, and especially {{item|Omniblade|size=15px}}.

==== Ranged ====
*Any weapon with point blank is preferred for the Pot of Pain due to the extra movement gained from recoil.
**The weapon with the highest recoil is the {{item|Titanium Railgun|size=25px}}.

==== Mage ====
*The player's mana is limited inside of the Pot of Pain, so it is best to bring low mana weapons that can deal high amounts of damage quickly in order to kill the final Golems.
**The piercing of forbidden sun can knock out the Golems quickly.
**If the player chooses to wait until after defeating the [[Lunatic Cultist]], they can fill the arena with {{item|Nebula Arcanum|size=25px}} projectiles that will decimate the Golems with ease.
**{{item|Apoctosis Array|size=25px}} is also a viable candidate due to its mana mechanics.

==== Summoner ====
*Summons are generally discouraged in the Pot of Pain due to the player needing to focus on platforming, so active summon weapons such as the {{item|Borealis Bomber|size=25px}} or {{item|Slime Puppet Staff|size=25px}} are preferable for the twin Golem fight.

==== Rogue ====
*{{item|Quasar|size=25px}}'s pull ability can run the Golems into the spikes on the sides of the arena, and can also be used to pull the player in a certain direction during the platforming section.
*{{item|Star of Destruction|size=25px}} can pass through blocks potentially trivializing the final Golem fight.
*{{item|Skyfin Bombers|size=25px}} are infamously powerful and can kill the Golems in a few seconds, but are dangerous to have during the platforming section.

==== Classless ====
*{{item|Aestheticus|size=25px}} can be used for non-melee classes in order to pogo off of obstacles.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The {{item|Bounding Potion|size=20px}} can help prevent fall damage in certain sections.
*The {{item|Swiftness Potion|size=10px}} can be used to increase the players speed through certain sections, but players may have trouble stopping before certain traps.

== Walkthrough ==
The very first checkpoint of the Pot of Pain consists of a large vertical column above the player with [[Wooden Spikes]] aligning the left side while the players objective is to climb up the right side. A singular buzzsaw will patrol the wall and the player will need to jump over it once it moves downwards. Once the player scales the wall they will be met with a horizontal hallway of [[Spear Trap]]s that will need to be bounced off of. At the end of the hallway is a loop made of Wooden Spikes containing more Spear Traps to bounce off of. Finally, the player will have to fall through two rooms and bounce off of buzzsaws, then will reach the first [[Heart Statue]].

*For the first section, climb up the wall and jump once the buzzsaw appears.
*For the hallway of Spear Traps, find a rhythm bouncing off of each one. With a dash available, every other Spear Trap can be skipped.
*Do not panic upon seeing the loop, simply pogo off of each Spear Trap in order counter clockwise.
**Alternatively, if your dash covering allows, you can skip the loop and dash under it. This is more of a speedrun strategy though, and not recommended for most players, as the timing is very precise.
*For the final falling section, dash upon entering each room, then pogo off of the buzzsaw and continue moving right.


The second checkpoint is the shortest and consists of several sparsely placed Spear Traps floating above a pit of Wooden Spikes. Once the pit is crossed the player can exit the chamber to go to the next one. Upon entering there is a short section of wall jumping with Wooden Spikes with a checkpoint on top.

*This section requires bouncing off of each Spear Trap with a dash between each one.
*The wall climbing section requires simple jumping once the player touches each safe zone.


The third checkpoint consists of two hallways in both horizontal directions of the player with the right one being locked. The left hallway contains two pillars and an overhang made of spikes, with a buzzsaw on top of the second pillar and two buzzsaws over a pit of Wooden Spikes under the overhang. After the player passes this section, they are greeted with a similar room with several pillars with Wooden Spikes on them and a scrolling buzzsaw sifting through a pit of Wooden Spikes at the bottom of the hallway. The next section consists of a large scrolling buzzsaw with a floating platform of Spear Traps in the center of the room. Once the player traverses this section, they reach the fourth checkpoint.

*This checkpoint can be overwhelming, as it is the only checkpoint consisting of multiple sections with no checkpoints in between. Players should fully utilize the Heart Statue at the checkpoint before going into the section. 
*For the first hallway, fall underneath the first pillar, then dash into the second pillar, then wall jump back onto the first pillar, then over the second pillar. After that, pogo off of the rightmost buzzsaw then dash to reach the safe spot.
*For the second hallway, use the same strategy but make sure to time your falls with the scrolling buzzsaw in mind. 
**An easy way to do this is to wait for the buzzsaw to reach the rightmost end of the room then falling in after it has moved left for 1 second.
*For the third section, wait for the buzzsaw to reach the leftmost end of its cycle then start dashing once it starts leaving. Pogo off of the Spear Trap platform in the middle, then dash as much as possible to get to the checkpoint once the buzzsaw retreats.


The fourth checkpoint is trailed by a hallway with a floor and ceiling completely composed of buzzsaws with a slowly vertically scrolling buzzsaw at the very end that the player will need to pogo off of. Once the player reaches the top of the room by pogoing, they are met with a hallway of Wooden Spikes with two scrolling buzzsaws moving in opposite directions back and forth. At the end of this hallway is a lever which opens up the locked door from the beginning of the third checkpoint. 

*The first hallway of buzzsaws can be trivialized with the Plagued Fuel Pack's charge ability. However if the player does not possess the plagued fuel pack, they may pogo off of each buzzsaw manually.
*The second hallway can be beaten by waiting for the bottom buzzsaw to reach its maximum left position then following it with pogos. Once the top buzzsaw is about to horizontally intersect with the bottom one, jump over it and continue pogoing off the bottom buzzsaw until reaching the lever.


The fifth checkpoint lies behind the previously locked door and consists of a slightly long section of vertical platforming. The section starts with a vertically scrolling buzzsaw that must be bounced off of upwards. The player then finds themselves in a vertical climb with both walls covered in Wooden Spikes along with a two chunks of [[Lihzahrd Brick]]s with Wooden Spikes aligning the right and top of them. Upon scaling up to the top of the upper chunk, a scrolling buzzsaw appears to the right of the platform and an opening to the left of the chunk will need to be reached to continue the climb. At the top of the safe zone lies two buzzsaws going back and forth into each other that the player needs to narrowly jump through to get onto another chunk of bricks. Then the player reaches an open area where they will need to climb the right wall while dodging the upper buzzsaw. Finally, the player will need to pogo off of two buzzsaws in a Wooden Spike filled hallway to get to the sixth checkpoint.

*Wait for the initial buzzsaw to reach the bottom of its cycle then start moving up before attempting the section. 
*For the first two chunks of Lihzahrd Bricks, follow the same strategy by waiting for buzzsaws to start moving upwards before proceeding. This also applies to the part where the player will need to dash into the opening.
**Alternatively, a player with quick reaction time can pogo off of one of the scrolling buzzsaws to skip the opening.
*Wait for the two scrolling buzzsaws to start leaving before performing a dash between them.
*For the climb while dodging a buzzsaw, dash into the wall in order to save some time when the buzzsaw starts approaching.
*Pogoing off the final two buzzsaws should be easy.


The sixth checkpoint starts off with a tunnel of Wooden Spikes that need to be Plagued Fuel Packed through. The player is then met with a series of Spear Traps with buzzsaws hovering either below or above each one. The player will then need to traverse a narrow tunnel of Wooden Spikes by pogoing off buzzsaws before reaching the final checkpoint.

*The Plagued Fuel Pack is highly reccomended for the initial tunnel of Wooden Spikes, but carefully timed dashes from the Asgardian Aegis or Statis' Void Sash can work.
*For the series of Spear Traps and buzzsaws, pogo off of Spear Traps with buzzsaws below them as you would with any other spear trap, then wait for openings to deal with the Spear Traps with buzzsaws on top.
*For the second Wooden Spike tunnel, pogo off of each buzzsaw then immediately dash. The timing can be hard to get down, but this is the only way to complete this section.


The seventh checkpoint is by far the most mentally demanding and consists of a very long Wooden Spike tunnel filled with an assortment of different trap patterns. It begins with two spaced out Spear Trap platforms, then goes into several small floating chunks of Lihzahrd Bricks in a sine pattern. Afterwards is two slowly horizontally scrolling buzzsaws that need to be continuously pogoed off above, before reaching what could be considered the hardest part of the Pot of Pain. The final platforming sequence consists of constantly pogoing off a very small patch of Spear Traps as a buzzsaw horizontally scrolls through the floor underneath a gigantic overhang of Wooden Spikes, then pogoging off a stationary buzzsaw on the other side of the overhang and reaching the twin Golem fight.

*The Plagued Fuel Pack can be used to skip the Spear Trap platforms, though this is unnecessary and the platforms can be traversed with careful pogoing and dashing.
*The floating platforms are easy and just require the player to go to each one before going to the scrolling buzzsaws.
*The scrolling buzzsaws are also self explanatory as long as the player makes sure they dont move too fast considering the slower speed of them.
*For the final sequence, time your pogos for the Spear Trap platforms and do not move until you have a clear opening. Wait for the buzzsaw to go past the overhang of Wooden Spikes then jump down, quickly dash, use a bottle covering to jet upwards, then fall down and pogo off of the buzzsaw.
*After pogoing off of the buzzsaw, dash once, pogo off the final buzzsaw, then dash again and you will drop down into the arena.


The twin Golem fight plays out identically to the normal Golem fight, except for the fact that there are obviously two. Using the weapons from the weapons section of this guide are optimal, and they should be taken out as quickly and as safely as possible. Once they have been defeated, the door to the right unlocks and the player can view the cutscene to complete the area.

== General Tips ==
*Keep things slow. There is no time limit to beating the Pot of Pain.
*Remember to keep the final arena in mind when creating a build for the Pot of Pain. A build exclusively dedicated to the platforming section can leave the player vulnerable during the twin Golem fight.
*Take breaks. Exiting the world will make you lose progress, but simply walking away and taking a few seconds for your mind to clear is recomended.
**It is especially recommended to take a break before going through the final tunnel before the twin Golem fight.
***Of course, this is impossible in multiplayer.
