The '''[[Brimstone Elemental]]''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is usually fought after defeating two [[Mechanical Bosses]]. Additionally, after defeating [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], the Brimstone Elemental receives a buff and begins to drop [[Bloodstone]]. The Brimstone Elemental can be spawned by using a [[Charred Idol]] in the [[Brimstone Crag]] [[biome]].

This guide will mention items exclusive to [[Expert Mode|Expert]] and [[Revengeance Mode]] with no special formatting and will follow the intended progression.

== Terrain Preparation ==
This fight requires a lot of space to easily avoid the Brimstone Elemental’s attacks. Leaving the Brimstone Crag will cause the Brimstone Elemental to enrage, so it’s extremely important to stay within the biome. Since the Brimstone Crag isn’t just limited to the Underworld, you can have additional space by digging out the area above you, around 100 blocks up works. [[Brimstone Slag]] can be mined at this point if you have an [[Adamantite Pickaxe|Adamantite]] or [[Titanium Pickaxe]]. Alternatively, actuators can be used to pass though the slag. Three rows of platforms is usually enough. Have them be spaced out in-between the area that you’ve hollowed out. The Brimstone Crag in the underworld is home to various house structures, mining or actuating them works to clear them out. The width of your arena will vary based on world size, but having your arena extend the length of the Brimstone Crag is not a bad idea.

== Gearing Up ==
=== Armor ===
*[[Titanium armor]] is an all-around good option for Melee, Ranged, and Mages due to its high survivability from [[Shadow Dodge]].
**[[Hallowed armor]] can also be utilized for extra damage.
*[[Spider armor]] is generally preferred for Summoners due to the high damage bonuses for minions.
*[[Daedalus armor]] is a well-rounded armor set for all classes.

=== Accessories ===
*[[Flame Wings]], [[Frozen Wings]] and [[tgc:Red's_Wings|Developer Wings]] are the best wings available to the player at this point, and should be prioritized. However, the Developer Wings can be hard to get due to their low drop rate.
**[[Starlight Wings]] are an alternative option when paired with Daedalus armor.
**A [[Frog Leg]] can be utilized with wings to give an extra boost to movement speed and vertical motion.
*The [[MOAB]] can also be used but is generally more difficult to use effectively during this fight.
**The [[Aero Stone]] can be used in conjunction with the MOAB to boost the effectiveness of each jump.
*[[Frostspark Boots]] are a necessity for keeping up with the Brimstone Elemental’s speed.
*The [[Evasion Scarf]] and the [[Shield of Cthulhu]] can be used to make quick dashes to escape deadly situations.
*The [[Crown Jewel]] can be used to help reduce the effects of [[Brimstone Flames]].
**The [[Margarita]] drink can halve the duration of the debuffs the Brimstone Elemental inflicts at the cost of some defense and life regeneration.
*[[Lava Waders]] may work to your advantage in this fight if [[Lava]] isn’t completely covered.
**Note that [[Water Walking Boots]] and [[Obsidian Water Walking Boots]] ''do not'' allow you to walk on lava.
**[[Water Walking Potion]] is a cheap alternative to Lava Waders as well as saving an accessory slot.
*Melee players should use the [[Bloody Worm Scarf]] for its strong melee boosts as well as increased damage reduction.
**If the player is utilizing Yoyos, then the [[Yoyo Bag]] should be prioritized.
*Ranged players should use the [[Ranger Emblem]] to help in boosting damage output.
*Magic players should use the [[Mana Flower]] and the [[Celestial Cuffs]] to quickly recover mana.
*Summoners can boost their minion damage with the [[Summoner Emblem]] and the [[Spirit Glyph]].
*Rogue users will find the [[Rogue Emblem]] and the [[Raider's Talisman]] useful to boost their damage output.
*The [[Ornate Shield]] is a good accessory to use when paired with Daedalus armor.

=== Weapons ===
*It is sometimes preferable to fight the other bosses at this tier in progression to have a much wider array of options, some of which are listed below.

==== Melee ====
*The [[Ark of the Ancients]]' range and high DPS should make the fight fairly straight-forward for Melee users.
*The [[Submarine Shocker]] is an extremely powerful option that deals colossal amounts of damage. 
*[[Darklight Greatsword]] has decent DPS which can spike if the split projectiles connect, especially during Phase 2.
*Though [[Roxcalibur]] lacks the range to consistently hit during faster phases, it can deal massive amounts of damage during Phase 1, namely during her Brimstone Ray attack.
*[[Shimmerspark]] can also be utilized due to its decent range and falling stars.
*The [[Bonebreaker]] also does decent damage, especially when close range.

==== Ranged ====
*The [[Barinautical]] is an extremely powerful option that can deal massive damage to the Brimstone Elemental.
*The [[Daedalus Stormbow]] can deal good damage to the Brimstone Elemental.
*The [[Darkecho Greatbow]] is also a viable option for this fight.
*The [[Megashark]] and the [[Butcher (weapon)|Butcher]] can deal decent damage to the Brimstone Elemental when paired with [[Ichor Bullet]]s.
*The [[Sea's Searing]] ([[Legendary item]]) is an effective and powerful option for this fight. However, it may be difficult to obtain due to its rarity.
**[[Bows]] should be paired with [[Holy Arrow]]s.

==== Mage ====
*The [[Relic of Ruin]] is an excellent homing option that can garner massive DPS.
*[[Poseidon]] is another overall good choice dealing solid damage due to its homing and piercing typhoons.
*The [[Death Valley Duster]] can deal good damage to the Brimstone Elemental, but it requires the player getting extremely close to her.
*The [[Nimbus Rod]] can be used to gain extra DPS on the Brimstone Elemental.
*The [[Shadecrystal Barrage]] deals decent damage to the Brimstone Elemental due to its high fire rate.
*A [[Golden Shower]] can be used to inflict [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] onto the Brimstone Elemental.
*The [[Ion Blaster]] can decimate the boss's health, provided you can manage your mana effectively.

==== Summoner ====
*The [[Ancient Ice Chunk]], the [[Black Hawk Remote]], or the [[Forgotten Apex Wand]] are all extremely effective at dealing damage to the Brimstone Elemental and her summons.
*The [[Mounted Scanner]] tends to have the highest DPS, however it requires you to have defeated Skeletron Prime first.
*The [[Orthocera Shell]] can provide additional DPS to your minions.
*The [[Shellfish Staff]] can latch onto the Brimstone Elemental to deal powerful damage over time.
*The [[Caustic Staff]] can be used for debuffing the Brimstone Elemental with several different effects.
*The [[Cold Divinity]] is the best possible summon for maximum DPS. However, as this is a [[Legendary item]], it can be very difficult to obtain.

==== Rogue ====
*The [[Gacruxian Mollusk]] is another powerful option, dealing high amounts of damage.
*The [[Prismalline]] can deal good damage to the Brimstone Elemental due to its high damage.
*The [[Kelvin Catalyst]] has a very high damage output during the laser phase if you can get relatively close to the Brimstone Elemental.
*The [[Spear of Paleolith]] deals decent damage towards the Brimstone Elemental due to its Fossil Shards.
*[[Skyfin Bombers]] are extremely viable thanks to their high damage output.

==== Classless ====
*A [[Lunic Eye]] and a [[Golden Gun]] can make the fight easier to bear due to their powerful debuffs.

=== Miscellaneous ===
*The [[Well Fed]] buff provides a useful all-around stat boost.

== The Fight ==
The Brimstone Elemental spawns offscreen, flying towards the player. The Brimstone Elemental has 3 phases that cycle throughout the fight.

In the first phase, the Brimstone Elemental will teleport around the player, flying towards you. During this phase it’s extremely important to not let her overlap you, as that is sure to result in you taking massive damage. Staying roughly 20 blocks away from the Brimstone Elemental works well in avoiding her during this phase. Applying Ichor and other debuffs is extremely easy during this phase so use that to your advantage.

The second phase will have the Brimstone Elemental begin to rapidly fly above you, raining down Brimstone Fireballs as she passes. Running in a single direction can work in this case, changing your direction half way through to keep the Brimstone Elemental close to you in preparation for the third phase. Aiming slightly in front of the Brimstone Elemental as she flies should allow for your weapons to hit her.

The third phase will have the Brimstone Elemental come to a complete halt, as she will begin to launch barrages of Brimstone Hellblasts and Darts. Hellblasts will be aimed towards your position, while Darts will be launched in 6 directions around the Brimstone Elemental. The further you are away from her, the faster they travel. Getting hit during this phase will lead to a huge dent in your health due to the Abyssal Flames debuff that they inflict. Making a slow circle around the Brimstone Elemental while staying at least 10 blocks away from her should allow you to dodge most projectiles. Also during this phase, the Brimstone Elemental will receive a massive Defense boost. Due to this boost, prioritize dodging over damage in this phase.

As the Brimstone Elemental takes damage, the amount she teleports, her rate of fire, and movement speed will all increase, so it’s important to be prepared to increase your speed to compensate.

In Revengeance mode, up to two [[Brimling]]s will be spawned to harass the player by occasionally firing Brimstone Fireballs. Killing them has no penalty, but they do respawn extremely fast, making them a hassle to kill. Thus they should be avoided rather then being targeted. The Brimstone Elemental will also begin to fire a laser from her eye twice in phase 1 after going below 50% of her max health, this laser will attempt to fire directly where your predicted destination will be and is telegraphed with a tracking line that comes to a halt when she is about to fire.

In Death Mode, the Brimstone Elemental will always be able fire a laser from her eye in phase 1. However, she will only fire it once in contrast to the 2 fired in Revengeance Mode.

== General Tips ==
*If you get overwhelmed during the third phase, getting hit by the Brimstone Darts is more preferable then being hit by the Hellblast.
*The [[Rod of Discord]] (or the [[Normality Relocator]] for the Post-Providence fight) is a very useful tool as it allows instantaneous teleporting to avoid the numerous projectiles.
