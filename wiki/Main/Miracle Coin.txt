'''Miracle Coins''' are a [[Coins|currency item]] used to store excessive amounts of [[Klepticoin]]s. Its only use is to be fired by [[The Ones Striker]] as [[ammunition]], and they cannot actually be used to buy items from [[Town NPCs]].
