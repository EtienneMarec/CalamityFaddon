The '''Secretary''' is a worm-type [[Enemies|Enemy]] that appears rarely in [[The Void]]. It has the unique property of being attracted to vibrations, and will quickly move towards any blocks the player attempts to mine using a [[Pickaxes|Pickaxe]], staying mostly motionless otherwise, Secretary's can see you if there is a light source and there is no blocks between you and the head which will make it chase you even without mining anything so be cautious about that.

== Notes ==
* It can feel vibrations from any point on the map, regardless of distance.
* Destroying background [[Walls]] will not attract Secretaries.

== Tips ==
*Players should avoid mining in the Void as to not attract the attention of a Secretary.
*If one is blocking a tunnel the player must progress through, it can easily be lured away by mining a block elsewhere.

== Trivia ==
*Its design is likely a reference to the Wyrms from the game Hollow Knight.
**Both are massive, pale, cave dwelling worms with several mandibles and segmented bodies.
*The name Secretary could possibly be a reference to enemies of the same name from the game OFF.
**Both enemies exist to dissuade players from doing certain actions.
*The lore of [[The Void]] potentially suggests that the entire cavern exists within the corpse of one gigantic Secretary.
