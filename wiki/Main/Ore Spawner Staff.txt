The '''Ore Spawner Staff''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[sentry]] that can be crafted with [[Antlion Bar|Antlion Bars]]. It summons an Antlion burrower that sits stationary in the ground. Every 20 seconds, the antlion will dig underground, and emerge moments later, dropping a random ore item.
