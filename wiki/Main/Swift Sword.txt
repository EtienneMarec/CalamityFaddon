The '''Swift Sword''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that can be purchased after giving its [[Blueprint]] to the [[Collector]]. Upon killing an enemy, the player will receive the Swiftness buff for 10 seconds. Additionally, its damage can increase up to 80% and its critical strike chance up to 30%.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The [[Blueprint]] for this item is obtained rarely from killing [[Puff enemies]] with an [[Aspect of the End]].
*This weapon synergizes well with items that dramatically boost mobility such as the [[Angel Treads]].
**However, due to a bug on the current patch, the speed boost mechanic does not work at all and is always scaled as if the player were standing still.
