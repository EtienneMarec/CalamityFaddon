'''Tarragon Ore''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ore]] obtained by chopping down [[Petrified Trees]] growing in the [[Tarragon Remains]]. It is used to craft [[Tarragon Bar]]s at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or a [[Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least a [[Lunar Pickaxe]] or higher to mine. It can also be dropped by [[Bloom Slime]]s upon death or from [[Rear Gar]]s after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated.

==Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Tarragon|Tarragon]], also known as estragon, is a species of perennial herb in the sunflower family.
*Tarragon Ore was previously known as '''Uelibloom Ore'''.
**Uelibloom is a combination of the words "Ueli" and "Bloom", with bloom referring to the process of a flower fully maturing, and ueli being the name of a swiss mountain climber, known for their outdoor affinity.
