The '''Sparkling Empress''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] obtained by [[fishing]] in the [[Sunken Sea]] after defeating [[Desert Scourge]] with a 3% chance. When used, it briefly charges, then releases a concentrated light-blue laser that pierces infinitely. Upon killing an [[enemy]], they explode into two to three prism shards that shoot out in random directions, dealing 20% of the weapon's base damage.

{{fantag|Catalyst}} Like other beam weapons, 1 point of [[Stamina]] will be removed for every second the weapon is being channeled.
