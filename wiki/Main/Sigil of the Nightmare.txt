The '''Sigil of the Nightmare''' is an item crafted with no ingredients at an any [[tgc:Altars|altar]]. When used in the [[Death Mode]], it activates the [[Nightmare Mode]]. The mode cannot be deactivated once it has been activated in the current world. Attempting to use it in a world without the [[Death Mode]] activated will have no effect.
