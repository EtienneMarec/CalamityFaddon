The Pungent Bomber is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] broadsword that is dropped by the [[Acidsighter]]. When the weapon strikes an enemy, it creates a static acid bomb. The bomb explodes into four gravity affected acid shots on enemy hits or after 4 seconds. Each acid shot deals 50% of the weapon's damage.
