The '''Stratosoul''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the Lower Layer of the [[Sky Abyss]]. Normally it circles around Sky Lacians, but if it sees a player or a Skyline Shark, it starts orbiting them instead, giving them the '''Shark Mistake''' debuff, which causes other enemies to think the player is a Skyline Shark, and start attacking them. If it orbits a real shark, it informs other enemies to know theres a shark there, and either attacking it or running away

In [[Death Mode]], It also slows down the enemy, and gets additional health and defense, and instead of orbitting Sky Lacians, it randomly flows around the Lower Sky Abyss-Upper Sky Abyss border in teams of 5 souls
