'''Prepare to Die''' and '''Prepare to Cry''' were gamemodes that have been replaced by [[Revengeance Mode]] and [[Death Mode]]. '''Prepare to Die''' was accessable by activating either the [[Demon Trophy]] or [[Celestial Onion]], and '''Prepare to Cry''' were accessed by having both items active simultaneously.

== Boss changes ==
In Prepare to Die and Prepare to Cry:
*[[King Slime]], [[Queen Bee]], and [[Wall of Flesh|Wall of Flesh's]] Eyes had more difficult AIs.
*The [[Eye of Cthulhu]] and [[Plantera]] became more aggressive as their health lowered.
*Damage dealt to the [[Eater of Worlds]] was divided by the weapon's piercing, or divided by 5 if infinite.

In Prepare to Die and Prepare to Cry (Expert Mode):
*The maximum piercing that weapons could deal to [[The Destroyer]] was 3.
*[[Skeletron Prime]] became more aggressive as its health lowered.
**Prime Cannon's cannonballs did not have to hit the ground to hurt the player.
*[[Golem|Golem's]] fists did not take damage.
