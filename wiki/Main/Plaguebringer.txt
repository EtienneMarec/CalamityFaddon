The '''Plaguebringer''' (Japanese: 疫病をもたらす者 ''Ekibyō o motarasu mono'') is a powerful [[Hardmode]] [[mini boss]] that spawns uncommonly in the [[Jungle]] after the [[Golem]] has been defeated. Behaving similarly to [[Queen Bee]], it alternates between charging at the player horizontally and hovering above them while firing projectiles and spawning [[Plague Charger]]s. When firing projectiles, it has a 6.66% chance to fire a plague rocket instead of an exploding stinger. The Plaguebringer Goliath summons several Plaguebringers as her health decreases.

If a Plaguebringer fights while in honey for 5 seconds in a row, it will become “neutralized” and stop attacking. It will hover near the player while invulnerable for a few seconds, then fly away after dropping its loot.

== Notes ==
*It can often take unsuspecting players by surprise, due to its ability to fly through walls.
*The Plaguebringer can be forewarned by its generic Boss Roar effect and a [[Boss Health Bar|Boss health bar]] appearing.
*Fighting a Plaguebringer or even the Plaguebringer Goliath in a Bee Hive will make the fights immensely easier.

== Trivia ==
*The Plaguebringer uses a slightly edited version of The Plaguebringer Goliath's previous sprite.
*The Plaguebringer was formerly known as "Plaguebringer Shade", and is still referred to in the mod's files.
**This was due to the fact that The Plaguebringer Goliath used to spawn a duplicate of herself, referred to as a "Shade", which used a separate sprite.
**She now summons multiple Plaguebringers instead.
*In the Japanese translation, the name is "疫病をもたらす者 ''Ekibyō o motarasu mono''"
