'''Chrononimbus''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that drops from [[The Starborn Moth]]; it can be considered an indirect upgrade of the [[Ultra Liquidator]]. It fires a set of five celestial blades, which summon small flames similar to the [[Fate's Reveal]] from off-screen on enemy hits. Blades inflict [[Astral Infection (debuff)]] and [[Embers of Duality]] while flames inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]].
