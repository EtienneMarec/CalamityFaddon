The '''Aetherflux Cannon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Nano Purge]], and is obtainable after [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] has been defeated. When used, it spins up for roughly half of a second, then it rapidly fires pairs of incredibly fast yellow lasers which travel in a double helix pattern. Each laser can pierce through one target and can go through tiles.

== Notes ==
*Unlike the [[Laser Machinegun]] and the Nano Purge, this weapon does not increase in fire rate over time. After the initial non-firing spin-up, it fires its lasers at a constant high speed.
*This weapon does not consume mana while spinning up, it is only consumed once it has begun firing lasers.

== Trivia ==
*The movement pattern of the lasers is a reference to the [https://metroid.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_Beam Wave Beam] from the ''[[wikipedia:Metroid|Metroid]]'' game series.
*The weapon's former name, "T1000" was likely named after the [[wikipedia:T-1000|T-1000]], the robotic main antagonist of the film ''[[wikipedia:Terminator 2: Judgment Day|Terminator 2: Judgment Day]]''.
