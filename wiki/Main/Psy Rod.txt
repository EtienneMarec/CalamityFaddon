The '''Psy Rod''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[tool]] obtained by defeating the [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] while she's in her Mucormycotic state. It functions similarly to the Rod of Discord, instantly teleporting the player to the location of cursor when used. In addition to this, a large circle of [[Glowing Mushroom]]s bursts outward from the player upon usage, with each mushroom dealing 2% of any hit enemy's health.
