'''Palladium Javelins''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[javelins]] made from [[Palladium Bar]]s. They fire a javelin made from palladium, which pierces infinitely, travels a moderate distance before being affected by gravity. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with a Palladium Javelin will cause it to split twice after a short time, resulting in four javelins.
