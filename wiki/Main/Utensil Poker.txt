The '''Utensil Poker''' is a Godseeker Mode [[dagger]] that drops from the Moon Lord. It fires random utensils in sets of three that grant the player the [[Well Fed]] [[buff]] for three seconds on enemy hits.

{{Modifier|rogue}}
