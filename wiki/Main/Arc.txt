The '''Arc''' or '''Polter-Arc''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[NPC]] vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:

*There is an empty house.
*[[Polterghast]] has been defeated.
*A [[tgc:Town Cat|Town Cat]] is present.

When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself with phantom blasts.
