'''Portative Forging Factory''' is an unique piece of [[furniture]], obtained after the player finishes the [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] and fight with the [[Crazed Mechanic]] for the first time. It will open an UI when right-clicked on, that will allow the player to [[Item Upgrade System|upgrade]] some of their items. 

The Factory uses one [[Essence of the Upgrade]] to upgrade an item, with every upgrade the needed amount of essences is increased by 1, the essecnes are also used to level up the weapons. An upgrade takes a 5 seconds in order to be done, the waiting time increases by 5 seconds with each upgrade. The level-up of the weapon takes 1 second in order to be done, the waiting time increases by 1 second with each level.

== Trivia ==
*Unlike most of UIs, the upgrade/level-up UI can be moved.
