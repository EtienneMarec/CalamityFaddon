The '''Ungodly Serpent''' was a rare Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawned in the [[The Hallow]] and in [[The Underworld]]. It acted like other worm-type enemies and burrowed through blocks. Its large size made it easy to hit, however, it also made the player more vulnerable to damage.
