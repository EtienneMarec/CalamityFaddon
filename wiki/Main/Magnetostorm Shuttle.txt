The '''Magnetostorm Shuttle''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in [[Space]] as part of the [[Falconer]]'s 650 {{item|Essence of Babil}} quest, and is a variant of the [[Shockstorm Shuttle]]. It attacks by switching between firing electric orbs which zap nearby entities, firing several lightning bolts and laser beams, and flying through portals attempting to ram the player.

{{dei}} It can also spawn as a regular enemy after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated.

== Notes ==
*{{dei}} Magnetostorm Shuttles that spawn outside of the quest will not drop the [[Magnetic Meltdown]].

== Trivia ==
*This enemy is an homage to [[Magnetic Meltdown]]'s sprite.
