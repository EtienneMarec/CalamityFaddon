The '''Seafoam Bomb''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] non-consumable [[bombs|bomb]]. Upon use, it throws a bomb that travels a small distance before being affected by gravity. Upon hitting a tile or enemy, the bomb explodes into damaging bubbles which ignore enemy [[immunity frames]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Seafoam Bomb causes the next bomb thrown to move faster and explodes into five bubbles instead of one.
