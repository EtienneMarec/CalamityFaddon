The '''Blood Boiler''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It fires a long and continuous stream of three bloodfire. Each use has a 25% chance to subtract 1 health from the player. The fire returns to the player after 2.67 seconds and has a 33.33% chance to [[lifesteal|heal]] them for 1 health each. If the flame hits an enemy, the player has a 50% chance to gain 1-3 health.
