'''Terra Arrows''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[arrows]] that are made with one [[Living Shard]] and 250 [[Wooden Arrow]]s. They accelerate while airborne, exploding upon reaching a certain velocity or collision. After exploding, Terra Arrows split into two short-lasting homing arrows which deal 25% of the base arrow's damage.

== Notes ==
*Their effect mirrors that of [[Terra Bullet]]s.
*It takes roughly fifty blocks for Terra Arrows to accelerate enough to explode.
*When used with [[Daedalus Stormbow]], they will only explode upon collision, ignoring its velocity.
*It takes shorter time to explode if they pass through liquid.
