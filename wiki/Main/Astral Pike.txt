The '''Astral Pike''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spear]]. It emits dust when swung, which produces light. Upon critically hitting a target, three astral stars move in a diagonal pattern towards the player, homing into nearby enemies, and dealing 40% of the spear's damage. The stars can move and home through tiles.
