The '''Gamma Slime''' is a Godseeker Mode [[slime]] that spawns during tier 3 of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. It behaves like a normal slime but will shoot 5 Gamma Acids out in a star pattern while it is jumping. Occasionally, it will stop jumping and charge up a laser and fire it upwards.

== Notes ==
*It replaces the [[Irradiated Slime]] once [[Polterghast]] is defeated.
*Uniquely among slimes, they do not float in water, and instead sink to the bottom.
