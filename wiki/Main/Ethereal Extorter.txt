The '''Ethereal Extorter''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] sold by the [[Bandit]] for {{pc|1}} after the [[Golem]] has been defeated. While equipped, all [[Spy]] projectiles explode into 2 homing souls upon impact. These souls have 25 base damage and are affected by Spy damage bonuses. Additionally, Spy damage is increased by 10%, at the cost of -1 life regen.
