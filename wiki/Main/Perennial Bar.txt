'''Perennial Bars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bars]] made with 5 [[Perennial Ore]] at a [[Hardmode Forge]] and are additionally dropped from the [[Fleshy Geode|Fleshy]] and [[Necromantic Geode]]. They are used to craft various items themed around savage plant-life, and are also used to craft [[Life Alloy|Life Alloys]].
