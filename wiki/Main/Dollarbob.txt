'''Dollarbob''' is a pre-[https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Hardmode hardmode] enemy featured solely in the [[Jelly Monsoon|Jelly Monsoon ]] event.

== Trivia ==

'''Dollarbob''' is a previously existing meme among the Spongebob Squarepants community, often paired with similarly-named pals like 'Quidward', 'Poundtrick' and 'Yendy Cheeks'.
