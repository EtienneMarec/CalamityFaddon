The '''Frigidflash Bolt''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that is an upgrade of both [[Frost Bolt]] and [[Flare Bolt]]. It fires a slow-moving ball of flash-freezing magma that can be autofired. The projectile travels in a straight line, emits a bit of light, is unaffected by gravity, can pierce through 4 enemies and can bounce off 4 walls. It inflicts the [[On Fire!]] and the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] on hit [[enemies]].
