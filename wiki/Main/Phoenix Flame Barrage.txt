The '''Phoenix Flame Barrage''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that drops from [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. When used, it rains down a barrage of holy flames in a vertical column which explode after a short delay, dealing additional damage. The projectiles inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]].

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Notes ==
*The Phoenix Flame Barrage shoots two volleys of holy flames. It is possible to set up a chamber of holy flames by clicking and moving the mouse quickly across the screen this way.
*The Phoenix Flame Barrage only deals 90% damage to [[Supreme Calamitas]]' brothers.
