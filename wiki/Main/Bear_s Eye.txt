'''Bear's Eye''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[pet]] summoning item that can be purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{pc|5}}. It summons a guardian angel cat named Bear to follow the player. If the player moves too fast, Bear will use blue wings to fly towards them.

== Trivia ==
*This item is based on Lilac Vrt Olligoci's real pet cat, Bear, who was blind with cataracts. Bear sadly passed away on December 2nd, 2018, hence this item's depiction of him as a "guardian angel".
*In previous versions, the [[Archmage]] would sell this item.
