'''Calamity Eyes''' are [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemies]] that spawn in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. They act like [[Demon Eyes]], but will inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] and [[Weak]] [[debuffs]] on contact. When below half health, their speed and maneuverability increase.

In [[Death Mode]], Calamity Eyes are capable of becoming intangible and passing through blocks for a brief period of time, in order to reach the player.

After [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated, their stats receive a significant boost and they begin dropping [[Bloodstone]].

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions, Calamity Eyes would spawn on the [[Surface]] after [[Plantera]] had been defeated.
