'''Karasawa''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. It slowly fires a light blue energy pulse that explodes into a trefoil. Both the energy pulse and the trefoil inflict the [[On Fire!]], [[Confused]], and the [[Irradiated]] [[debuffs]]. Each time the gun fires, it consumes five bullets instead of one.
