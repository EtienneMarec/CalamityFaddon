'''Cinnamon Roll''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Cinnamon Roll''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces [[defense]], but boosts mana regeneration rate and the damage per second of fire-based debuffs by 50%.

While the effects of the Cinnamon Roll debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
