The '''Grand Gar''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] and is the direct upgrade to the [[Majestic Gar]]. It does not shoot a projectile and has a very large melee range, similar to its downgrade. Every time it strikes an enemy, its defense will be lowered by 1, and if the sword hits an enemy with 0 or less defense the player [[Lifesteal|heals]] 4 life. Striking enemies causes large explosions similar to the explosions created by the [[Inferno Fork]] which deal 50% of the weapon's base damage, decreasing by 10% for each hit. Hitting enemies that have under half life will release rainbow bolts. Enemies killed by the Grand Gar will spawn healing orbs on death which heal the player for 8 life.
