The Calamity Mod's '''initial release''' added a variety of [[weapons]], [[Covering]] and other [[items]], as well as three [[bosses]]: [[Desert Scourge]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], and [[The Slime God]].

== Additions ==
=== Basic Items ===
*Added [[Aerialite Bar]], [[Aerialite Ore]], [[Ancient Feather]], [[Bar of Life]], [[Chaos Serpent Shard]], [[Core of Calamity]], [[Core of Chaos]], [[Core of Cinder]], [[Core of Eleum]], [[Cosmic Worm]], [[Cruptix Bar]], [[Crystallite]], [[Desert Feather]], [[Desert Medallion]], [[Diamond Dust]], [[Draedon Bar]], [[Energy Orb]], [[Essence of Chaos]], [[Essence of Cinder]], [[Essence of Eleum]], [[Living Dew Droplet]], [[Living Shard]], [[Meld Blob]], [[Meldiate Bar]], [[Overloaded Sludge]], [[Purified Gel]], [[Spectral Feather]], [[Stardust]], [[Twisted Tendril]], [[Ueliace Bar]], [[Verstaltite Bar]], [[Victide Bar]], [[Victory Shard]], [[Wyvern Feather]].

=== Accessories ===
*Added [[Ancient Fossil]], [[Aureate Wings]], [[Badge of Bravery]], [[Chaos Amulet]], [[Discordian Wings]], [[Harpy Ring]], [[Nebulous Core]], [[Necklace of Vexation]], [[Ornate Shield]], [[Shield of the Ocean]], [[Skyline Wings]], [[Starlight Wings]], [[Tarragon Wings]], [[Xeroc Wings]].

=== Tools ===
*Added [[Abyssal Warhammer]], [[Aerial Hamaxe]], [[Beastial Pickaxe]], [[Berserker Waraxe]], [[Blossom Pickaxe]], [[Chaoswarped Slashaxe]], [[Flamebeak Hampick]], [[Gallant Pickaxe]], [[Greatbay Pickaxe]], [[Reefclaw Axe]], [[Shardlight Pickaxe]], [[Skyfringe Pickaxe]].

=== Armor ===
*Added [[Aerospec Breastplate]], [[Aerospec Helm]], [[Aerospec Leggings]], [[Ataxia Armor]], [[Ataxia Mask]], [[Ataxia Subligar]], [[Daedalus Breastplate]], [[Daedalus Helm]], [[Daedalus Leggings]], [[Reaver Cuisses]], [[Reaver Helm]], [[Reaver Scale Mail]], [[Statigel Armor]], [[Statigel Greaves]], [[Statigel Helm]], [[Tarragon Breastplate]], [[Tarragon Helm]], [[Tarragon Leggings]], [[Victide Breastplate]], [[Victide Helmet]], [[Victide Leggings]], [[Xeroc Cuisses]], [[Xeroc Mask]], [[Xeroc Plate Mail]].

=== Weapons ===
*Added [[Abyssal Tome]], [[Apocalyptic Expulsor]], [[Baleful Harvester]], [[Bladedge Greatbow]], [[Blade of Enmity]], [[Catastrophe Claymore]], [[Caustic Edge]], [[Darkecho Greatbow]], [[Darklight Greatsword]], [[Exsanguination Lance]], [[Feralthorn Claymore]], [[Galactica Blade]], [[Galeforce]], [[Gamma Fusillade]], [[Gel Dart]], [[Goldplume Spear]], [[Greatbow of Turmoil]], [[Hellfire Flamberge]], [[Hellion Flower Spear]], [[Hellkite]], [[Hyperius Bullet]], [[Lifefruit Scythe]], [[Mariana]], [[Megalodon]], [[Nettleline Greatbow]], [[Nuclear Fury]], [[Overloaded Blaster]], [[Redtide Sword]], [[Seabow]], [[Shredder]], [[Starnight Lance]], [[Temporal Floe Sword]], [[Terratomere]], [[Tradewinds]], [[True Caustic Edge]], [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]], [[Urchin Spear]], [[Verium Bullet]], [[Wind Blade]], [[Xeroc Greatsword]].

=== Placeables ===
*Added [[Particle Accelerator]].

=== Bosses ===
*Added [[Desert Scourge]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], [[The Slime God]].

=== Enemies ===
*Added [[Corrupt Slime Spawn]], [[Crimson Slime Spawn]], [[Dried Seeker]], [[Stasis Probe]].

=== Recipes ===
*Added recipes for [[Blizzard in a Bottle]], [[Cloud in a Bottle]], [[Ice Machine]], [[Sandstorm in a Bottle]], [[Shiny Red Balloon]], [[Sky Mill]], [[Starfury]].
