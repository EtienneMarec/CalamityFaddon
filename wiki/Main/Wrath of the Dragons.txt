The '''Wrath of the Dragons''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spell tomes|spell tome]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Wrath of the Cosmos]]. When used, it casts a draconic pulse at the mouse cursor, which fires 2 rings of 32 homing flare dusts that inflict [[Dragon Fire|Dragonfire]] on intervals for a significant amount of time.  

The galactic pulse will fire two Flare Tornadoes that home in towards the closest enemy every seven rounds of homing flare dust rings that deal 200% of the weapon's base damage and summon lingering Flarenadoes that deal 150% of the weapon's base damage. 

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Mythical'''.
