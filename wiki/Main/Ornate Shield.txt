The '''Ornate Shield''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that provides 8 [[defense]]. When equipped alongside the [[Daedalus armor]] set, it grants an additional 8% [[damage reduction]] and +20 maximum life. It also grants a dash with a base distance of 15 tiles. Enemies hit with the dash take 50 damage and are inflicted with the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] for 1 second. Every time an enemy is struck, the wearer is given 4 immunity frames.
