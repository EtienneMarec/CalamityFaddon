The '''Shadowdrop Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] that can also drop from [[The Hive Mind]]. When used, it causes groups of thin, grey projectiles to fall from the sky, dealing damage to any enemy they touch. The fired projectiles cannot pass through blocks and cannot pierce enemies. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Blood Bath]].
