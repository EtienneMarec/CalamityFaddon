The '''Dank Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that can also be dropped by [[The Hive Mind]]. It is statistically identical to its [[Crimson]] counterpart, the [[Blood Clot Staff]]. When used, it summons a small dank creeper minion, which deals contact damage and will stay on enemies for as long as possible.

== Tips ==
*The Dank Staff is very helpful for multiple enemies stacked in one location, so it can be good for [[tgc:Events|invasions]].
*Although its base damage is lower than other summons of the same tier, the dank creeper's tendency to hover on top of enemies causes it to deal damage rapidly.
