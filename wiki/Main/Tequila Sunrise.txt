'''Tequila Sunrise''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Tequila Sunrise''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen, but boosts damage, critical strike chance, damage reduction, knockback and [[defense]] during the daytime and [[Solar Eclipse]]s.

While the effects of the Tequila Sunrise debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
