'''Tarragon Cloak Cooldown''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted to the player for 25 seconds after using the [[Tarragon Immunity]] [[buff]] from the Spy variant of the [[Tarragon armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]]. While the debuff is active, the usage of Tarragon Immunity is disabled.
