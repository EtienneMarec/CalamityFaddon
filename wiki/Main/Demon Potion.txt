The '''Demon Potion''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] that drops commonly from [[Slimes]]. It grants the '''Evil''' [[buff]], which boosts all spawn rates and maximum spawn count by 1000%. It is also used in the crafting of the [[Death Candle]].
