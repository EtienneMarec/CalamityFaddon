The '''Frostbite Blaster''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC. It fires 3 rounds of 2 bullets in uneven spreads before pausing to reload for 0.9 seconds. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], bullets are replaced with icicles that inflict [[Frostburn]].
