The '''Famine Slime''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] after [[Acidsighter]] has been defeated. It attacks by jumping towards the player and shooting high-velocity sulphur darts whenever they get too close to it or travel above it.

The Famine Slime is one of the required slimes to summon [[Astrum Aureus]].
