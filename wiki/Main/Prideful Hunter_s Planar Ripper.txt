The '''Prideful Hunter's Planar Ripper''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. It automatically rapidly fires bullets. Musket balls turn into fast lightning bolts which do not pierce enemies or tiles, inflict the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]], and explode when enemies are killed. Every fourth lightning bolt deals 35% more damage and in addition, critical strikes from the lightning bolts will grant a minor boost to movement speed and acceleration up to a maximum of 20%. The movement speed boost is reset if the player holds a different item.
