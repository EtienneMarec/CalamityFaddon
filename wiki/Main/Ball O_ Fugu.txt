The '''Ball O' Fugu''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[flail]] obtained from [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]]. It throws out a pufferfish, which behaves similarly to the [[Flairon]], dispersing homing urchin spikes into its vicinity.

Like the Flairon, it closely resembles a thrown flail. However, it functions more closely similar to items like [[Chain Knife]] and [[Golem Fist]]. It is not affected by gravity, does not remain deployed when holding the fire button, and cannot be swung around. Rather, holding the fire button causes it to auto-fire repeatedly, returning and re-firing when it impacts or reaches its maximum range.
