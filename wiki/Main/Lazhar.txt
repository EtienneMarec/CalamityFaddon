The '''Lazhar''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] that is the upgraded version of [[Heat Ray]]. It rapidly fires explosive heat rays that bounce off of blocks and create small explosions when hitting an enemy. Explosions inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]].
