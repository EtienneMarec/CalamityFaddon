The '''1.1.7.703''' update added ability to the [[Demonshade Breastplate]], along with a couple of other tweaks to it.

== Changes ==
*Nerfed all of Demonshade Breastplate's health-based melee speed boosts by roughly 33%.
*Buffed Demonshade Breastplate's defense from 42 to 50.
*Demonshade Breastplate now has a new ability activated by pressing down twice that increases speed, damage, and critical strike chance by 15%, but also lowers damage reduction by 15%.
