The '''1.4.2.001''' (or '''1.4.2.1''') Music mod update added a music track for the [[Sunken Sea]].

This Music Mod version was compatible with all Main mod versions between [[1.4.2.001]] and [[1.4.2.104]]. 

== Additions ==
*Added "sanctuary", the music track for the Sunken Sea.
*Added [[Music Box (Sunken Sea)]].

== Changes ==
*The [[Brimstone Crag]] music track no longer overrides the Underworld music track.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed several audio quality issues with [[The Devourer of Gods]] and [[Sulphurous Sea]] music tracks.
