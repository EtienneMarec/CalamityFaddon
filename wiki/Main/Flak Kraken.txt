The '''Flak Kraken''' is a unique craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. It summons a yellow reticle at the cursor's location that deals constant damage to any enemy within it, piercing infinitely. Similarly to other weapons such as the [[Phantasm]], the Flak Kraken will "spin up" the longer the fire button is held, increasing its damage per second and area of effect.

== Notes ==
*It takes 8.33 seconds of firing in order to reach maximum power.
**Continued usage at this power will stop the reticle but the weapon can still be used afterwards.
*When at full power, this weapon deals noticeably higher damage per second than other weapons of its tier, reaching upwards of 1000 DPS without armor or accessories.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon's design may be based on the [[wikipedia:8.8 cm Flak 18/36/37/41|8.8 cm flak cannons]] used by Germany in WW2.
