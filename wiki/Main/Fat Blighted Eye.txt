'''Fat Blighted Eye''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Falconer]] quest [[enemy]] which spawns as part of her 10 {{item|Essence of Babil}} quest. It behaves similarly to [[Blighted Eye]]s and mainly exists as a tutorial NPC. It drops [[Acidsighter]]'s weapons as a convenience. It wields the following attacks:

*Divebombing the player without warning.
*Crying acid while dashing in a line above the player.
*Summoning [[Blighted Eye]]s.

When it reaches 50% health, it can do two moves at once at random intervals.

{{dei}} Fat Blighted Eyes can also spawn outside of the quest on the surface at night after [[Calamitas]] has been defeated.
