The '''Dragon Pow''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flail]]. It fires out a dragon head that releases red, orange, and yellow homing sparks that inflict several [[debuffs]] on hit, and each spark will deal one-eighth of the initial swing. When fired, the roar of the [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] can be heard.

Upon contact with an enemy, twelve petals are spawned and will travel in two sets to create an X pattern that will damage enemies, ignoring [[immunity frames]], and passing through blocks, with each petal doing one-eighth of the damage of the initial swing. Twelve aquatic jets are also spawned in that will travel downwards, dealing damage, ignoring immunity frames, and passing through blocks, with each aquatic jet dealing one-sixth of the damage of the initial swing.
