The '''Seared Shredder''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] dropped by [[Pyrogen]]. It works similarly to the [[Death Sickle]], as it shoots out spinning flame scythes, which stop after a certain time and spin in place, inflicting the [[Brimstone Flames]] and [[On Fire!]] [[Debuffs]] to [[enemies]]. This projectile can pierce through multiple enemies, goes through blocks and gives off light, making it useful in caves.
