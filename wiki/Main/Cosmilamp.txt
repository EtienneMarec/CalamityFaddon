The '''Cosmilamp''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]]. It summons a Cosmilamp which follows the player and attacks nearby enemies. Each Cosmilamp takes up 2 minion slots.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Trivia ==
*The Cosmilamp is based off of the [[Cosmic Lantern]] that is summoned by Signus, Envoy of the Devourer.
