The '''1.4.2.004''' update fixed a handful of issues and inconsistencies within the mod. 

== Additions ==
*Added a rare, functionless [[status message]] that has a 0.000001% chance of occurring every frame. <!---THE LORDE is approaching...--->
*Added a [[Configuration option]] to disable [[Lethal Lava Burn]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Aquatic Scourge]] now enrages if the player has the [[Gravitation Potion]]'s effects active.
*Slightly decreased the smaller [[Astrum Deus]]' speeds.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] no longer loses its [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] and [[Cursed Inferno]] immunities in Boss Rush.
*[[Lunatic Cultist]] no longer drops [[Meld Blob]]s.
*Decreased the speeds of [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s [[Plague Charger]]s.
*Decreased [[Probe]]'s health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.8x|2.7x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.6x|1.9x}}.
*Revengeance Mode [[Skeletron Prime]] now enrages if the player has the Gravitation Potion's effects active.
*[[The Twins]] now enrage in Revengeance Mode if the player has the Gravitation Potion's effects active.
*[[Wulfrum Drone]]s and [[Wulfrum Slime]]s can now spawn in Hardmode, but with a rarer chance instead of becoming unspawnable in Hardmode.

=== Other ===
*The [[Drunk Princess]] now has a chat option that displays how many times a player has died.
*The [[Sea King]] now requires [[Desert Scourge]] to be defeated to respawn.
*Decreased the [[Witch Doctor]]'s price for [[Birb Pheromones]] from {{pc|20}} to {{pc|5}}.
*The Wizard no longer sells Eyes of Desolation or Starcores.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed some tile drawing errors with placed [[Ashen Slab]], [[Astral Monolith]], [[Cosmilite Brick]], [[Otherworldly Stone]], [[Smooth Brimstone Slag]], [[Smooth Voidstone]], [[Plagued Plate]], [[Profaned Rock]], [[Profaned Slab]], [[Runic Profaned Brick]], [[Stratus Bricks]], [[Uelibloom Brick]], [[Voidstone]] and [[Voidstone Slab]] on certain lighting modes.
*Fixed [[Bear's Eye]]'s Bear pet and [[Trash Can (pet)]]'s Trash Man getting stuck in blocks.
*Fixed an oversight where [[Effulgent Manipulator]], [[Silva Chandelier]], [[Silva Chest]], [[Silva Clock]], and [[Silva Crystal]] could only be crafted with [[Gold Bar]]s and not [[Platinum Bar]]s.
*Fixed the [[Eidolon Staff]] being unobtainable in Expert Mode and higher.
*Fixed [[Eutrophic Platform]]s not being able to be crafted back into [[Navystone]].
*Fixed a typo in [[The Plaguebringer Goliath (Lore)]]'s flavor text where "contained" was spelt as "containted".
