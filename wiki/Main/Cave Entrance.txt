The '''Cave Entrance''' is a special tile that can be found hidden behind blocks underground. It can be detected by [[Potions|Dangersense Potions]]. Right clicking it will teleport the player to a Cave Entrance in [[The Void]], and vice versa. It cannot be destroyed.
