The '''Subduction Slicer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] that auto-fires. When the scythe impacts an enemy, it inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] and creates a small explosion. It can be continuously thrown regardless of whether the first scythe has returned.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Subduction Slicer will create a pillar of fire on enemy hits, dealing 150% of the weapon's damage.
