'''XB-∞ Hekate''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that auto-swings and is the direct upgrade to the [[Hellkite]]. It is larger than most other swords of its tier, and upon hitting an enemy it inflicts the [[Exo Freeze]] [[debuff]] and summons a damaging geyser of exo flame on top of each target struck, dealing additional damage.
