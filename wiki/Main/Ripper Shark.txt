The '''Ripper Shark''' is a rare [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[pickaxe]]. It can be obtained via [[Fishing]] in the lowest layer of the [[Abyss]]. It applies [[Crush Depth]] to enemies on hit.

== Notes ==
*Although it has relatively low pickaxe power, it is one of the fastest pickaxes in the game, and one of the strongest weapons obtainable in Pre-Hardmode.

== Trivia ==
*The Ripper Shark seems to be a direct upgrade to the [[Reaver Shark]], as both are pickaxes based on sharks that are gotten from fishing and have unusually high mining speed.
*Despite it being obtainable Pre-Hardmode, the Ripper Sharks rarity is Pure Green, which is typical of most Post-[[Polterghast]] items.
