The '''Cuboid Curse''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns during the [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. It attacks by teleporting around and firing redstone flame balls at the player. Additionally, when below 50% health, it will enrage and begin teleporting faster, as well as gaining the ability to summon four pillars of redstone flame around itself.

== Trivia ==
*The Cuboid Curse is a direct reference to the Herobrine creepy pasta from the game [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft Minecraft].
