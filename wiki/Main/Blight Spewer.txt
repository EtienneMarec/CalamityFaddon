The '''Blight Spewer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that has a chance of dropping from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It shoots green flames in a large radius that inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]].

Note that, unlike other flamethrowers, it lacks a chance to not consume gel meaning the player should make sure they do not run out of ammo at an inopportune time.
