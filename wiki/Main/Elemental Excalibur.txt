The '''Elemental Excalibur''' is a craftable Epilogue [[broadsword]] and the upgrade of the [[Greatsword of Blah]]. On each swing, it fires a different colored beam that has different properties depending on its color and inflicts several [[debuffs]]. The sword beam colors are fired in a fixed looping order:

*{{color|Red beams|#FF0000}} have no special effects
*{{color|Orange beams|#FF8000}} curve back to the player
*{{color|Yellow beams|#FFFF00}} split into 3 red beams after travelling a certain distance
*{{color|Lime beams|#80FF00}} home back in on the player
*{{color|Green beams|#00FF00}} home in on enemies
*{{color|Turquoise beams|#00FF80}} speed up over time and ignore tile collision
*{{color|Cyan beams|#00FFFF}} spawn 3 turquoise beams to the location where the projectile dies (enemy or tile impact)
*{{color|Light blue beams|#0080FF}} slow down on enemy hits
*{{color|Blue beams|#0000FF}} bounce infinitely off of tiles and enemies until projectile time runs out
*{{color|Purple beams|#8000FF}} speed up and slow down over and over, similarly to a [[Profaned Guardian]] spear attack
*{{color|Fuchsia beams|#FF00FF}} start slow but speed up over time
*{{color|Hot pink beams|#FF0080}} spawn fuchsia sword beams while travelling until projectile time runs out
*{{color|Gray beams|#a8a8a8}} pass through tiles and enemies, have a chance to drop Platinum coins on impact

Direct hits with the weapon will [[lifesteal|heal]] the player for anywhere between 10 and 19. The weapon can also be right-clicked to perform a [[True melee]] swing that deals doubled damage.

Attacks with the Elemental Excalibur inflict the following debuffs:

*{{item|Exo Freeze}} (1 second)
*{{item|Holy Flames}} (4 seconds)
*{{item|Frostburn}} (5 seconds)
*{{item|On Fire!}} (6 seconds)

== Trivia ==
*The Elemental Excalibur is a reference to a [https://terrariamods.fandom.com/wiki/Exxo_Avalon/Elemental_Excalibur weapon of the same name] from the [https://avalonmod.fandom.com/wiki/Terraria_Avalon_Mod_Wiki Avalon] and [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/released-wip-terraria-avalon.378/ Exxo Avalon] mods for Terraria 1.1.2 and 1.2.4 respectively. The [[Greatsword of Blah]], which is a part of the Elemental Excalibur's recipe, is also a reference to the creator of the Avalon mods, ''Blahblahbal''.
