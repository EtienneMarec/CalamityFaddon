The '''Reemergence''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[repeater]] that is dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. If [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], the arrow is replaced with a plague arrow that explode into bees upon tile or enemy contact, inflicting the [[Plague]] debuff onto any hit enemy.
