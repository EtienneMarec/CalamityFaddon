'''Char Matter''' is an energy source item obtainable at any point of progression. It is used as an upgrade from standard combustible fuel and can be used in crafting recipes.

==Burnable Items==
Char Matter can be obtained by burning items directly or from collecting fuel as residue from smelting/cooking
