The '''Chasm Beast''' is an [[Enemies|Enemy]] found in the Cliffs biome in [[The Void]]. It wanders about randomly until it either sees light or is attacked. When 
provoked, it will rapidly leap after the player in a similar manner to [[Scorn Eater|Scorn Eaters]]. 

== Notes ==
* If it deals contact damage to the player without first seeing them or getting attacked, it will leap around in random directions for a short time before returning to its passive behavior.

== Tips ==
*Its eyes are visible in the darkness, so be sure to hide any lights if you spot them.

== Trivia ==
*The Chasm Beast's sprite seems to be a reimagining of the scrapped "Void Soldier" enemy from the Void biomes original concept.
