The '''Wulfrum Gyrator''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime. It is also summoned by the [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Wulfrum_Excavator Wulfrum Excavator] [[boss]] in [[Expert Mode]] upon it transitioning to a new phase. Initially, it remains in one place, jumping periodically. When it detects a player, it attacks by rolling quickly and jumping towards them.

It can be supercharged by the [[Wulfrum Pylon]] enemy, causing its lights to turn from green to blue and increasing their speed and jump height.
