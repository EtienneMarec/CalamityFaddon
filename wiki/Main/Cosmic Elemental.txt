The '''Cosmic Elemental''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Cavern]]. They behave the same as [[Granite Elemental]]s, flying through walls and rolling into a ball when damaged in [[Expert Mode]]. Upon death, the Cosmic Elemental has a chance to drop a number of rare [[swords]] that are otherwise not obtainable through conventional means. The Cosmic Elemental always applies the [[Confused]] [[debuff]] on hit players for three seconds.
