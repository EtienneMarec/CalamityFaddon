The '''The First Fracturer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is auto-swung. It shoots out light blue sword beams which summon 3-5 extra beams from offscreen to attack struck enemies upon enemy hits.

Its best modifier is '''Legendary'''.
