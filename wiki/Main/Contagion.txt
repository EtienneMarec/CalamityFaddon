The '''Contagion''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Bows|bow]]. It fires arrows that leave behind a trail of orbs, exploding after a short delay. Holding left-click will cause the bow to "spin-up", increasing the fire rate appreciably. Both the explosions and projectiles inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] for varying amounts of time.

*The word "contagion" refers to the communication of a disease from one organism to another, either by close contact or transmission through the air.
