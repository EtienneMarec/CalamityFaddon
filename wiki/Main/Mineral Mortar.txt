The '''Mineral Mortar''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Launchers|launcher]] that fires projectiles shaped like [[Forbidden Fragment]]s. Upon collision with a block, this projectile splits into four flying [[Sand Shark]]-shaped projectiles that spread out and do additional damage. Neither the initial rocket nor the sharks can hit more than one enemy, though the initial blast has a small area of effect which ignores enemy [[immunity frames]].

== Tips ==
*Since the sharks released by the weapon slightly home towards the player, one can kite them into enemies for additional damage if desired.
*Despite the fact that rockets are normally post-[[Plantera]], players can craft rockets in Calamity by using 20 [[Empty Bullet]]s and an [[Explosive Powder]] at an [[Orichalcum Anvil|Orichalcum]] or [[Mythril Anvil]].

== Trivia ==
*Unlike many rocket launchers, it will not deal any damage to the player.
*In the mod's source files, the Sand Shark projectile is referred to as 'SandyWaifuShark'.
**This is likely a reference to the weapon's recipe requiring Forbidden Fragments, which are dropped from [[Sand Elemental]]s.
