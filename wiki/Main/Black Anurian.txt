The '''Black Anurian''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] obtained from [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]]. It shoots violet bubbles that travel in an upward arc, alongside two homing planktons which are fired in a random spread. All projectiles fired deal half damage instead of full.

With the Infernum Mod {{fantag|Infernum}} enabled, the Black Anurian is instead obtained by placing down a [[Frog]] in any layer of the Abyss.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon can also be obtained from the [[Abyssal Crate]] after either [[The Slime God]] or [[Wall of Flesh]] have been defeated.
**This does not apply with the Infernum Mod active {{fantag|Infernum}}.

== Trivia ==
*Its name is a reference to the [[wikipedia:Order (biology)|biological order]] which frogs belong to, known as "[[wikipedia:Anura (frog)|Anura]]". This is reflected in the weapon's frog-like appearance.
