The '''Supernova''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[Bombs|bomb]]. It throws a gravity-affected bomb that explodes upon contact with a tile or [[enemy]]. At the impact zone, the supernova bomb will create a huge explosion radius, spawn six homing Supernova energies, and three Supernova spikes that travel in random directions. The explosion ignores immunity frames, the spikes deal 60% of the weapon's damage, and the energies deal 50% of the weapon's damage. All projectiles inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Supernova will release Supernova energies along the trajectory of the bomb and continues until the bomb explodes.
