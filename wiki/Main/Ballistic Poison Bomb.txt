The '''Ballistic Poison Bomb''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]]. It throws bombs that are affected by gravity. Upon contact with an enemy, it explodes and launches several fragments that fly in random directions, while also creating a poison cloud similar to the one produced by the [[Toxic Flask]]. Collision with a tile will cause the bomb to stick onto the tile for a short period of time, after which, it launches the fragments. The shrapnel will stick to all tiles and enemies, and will explode after a short delay. The poison clouds and shrapnel deal 30% of the bomb's damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Ballistic Poison Bomb will cause three bombs to be thrown instead of one.

== Notes ==
*The poison clouds deal typeless damage rather than rogue damage. However, boosting the weapon's damage will still increase the damage of the poison clouds.
**As a result, they cannot inflict critical hits and are unaffected by the [[Adrenaline Mode]] and [[Rage Mode]] [[buffs]].
*Unlike [[Seafoam Bomb|its predecessor]], it only explodes on impact when hitting an enemy. When impacting a tile, it behaves similarly to a Sticky Bomb.
