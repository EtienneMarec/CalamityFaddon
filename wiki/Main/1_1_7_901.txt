The '''1.1.7.901''' update added the [[Astral Meteor]] minibiome, along with its own set of [[items]] and a [[boss]] known as [[Astrum Deus]]. It also removed the [[Prepare to Cry]] and Prepare to Die difficulty modes and replaced them with [[Revengeance Mode]]. The update also rebalanced most Calamity bosses, reworded and removed various item tooltips, and resprited [[Calamitas]] and [[Storm Weaver]].

== Additions ==
*Added the [[Astral Meteor]] minibiome.
*Added [[Astral Head]], [[Astral Probe]], [[Astrum Deus]], [[Astrum Deus Probe]], and [[Desert Scourge (small)]].
*Added [[Aerospec Hat]], [[Aerospec Headgear]], [[Aerospec Helmet]], [[Aerospec Hood]], [[Astral Breastplate]], [[Astral Helm]], [[Astral Leggings]], [[Statigel Hood]], [[Victide Headgear]], [[Victide Helmet]], [[Victide Mask]], and [[Victide Visage]].
*Added [[Astral Arcanum]], [[Astral Bulwark]], and [[The Sponge]].
*Added [[Astral Blade]], [[Astral Pike]], [[Astral Repeater]], [[Astral Staff]], [[Exoblade]], [[Subsuming Vortex]], and [[Vivid Clarity]].
*Added [[Astral Hamaxe]] and [[Astral Pickaxe]].
*Added [[Astral Bar]].
*Added [[Baby Slime God (buff)]], [[Exo Freeze]], [[Urchin (buff)]], and [[Valkyrie (buff)]].
*Added [[Revengeance]] and [[Starcore]].
*Added [[Draedon's Forge]].
*Added [[Astral Ore]].
*Added [[Revengeance Mode]].
