The '''Driftorcher''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung while the player has [[Torch]]es in their inventory, the torch is consumed and five homing gravity-affected sparks will fly out of the weapon, with each one piercing once and dealing one-tenth of the initial damage of the weapon. The five sparks are always thrown out at the same semi-equidistant angles, with the highest going straight upward and the lowest going straight forward, regardless of where the player is aiming. Each spark lasts for 1.5 seconds before dissipating.
