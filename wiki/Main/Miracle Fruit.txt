The '''Miracle Fruit''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[permanent power-up]] item that increases the player's maximum life, as an extension to [[Life Fruit]]s that can be found on Jungle [[Planetoid]]s. There are 3 other items that function identically to this: the [[Hemagrape]], the [[Sambucus Fruit]], and the [[Dragonfruit]]. It is the second of these to be obtainable following boss progression. It can only be used once, and after that the player must use one of the remaining fruit to increase their maximum life. With all of these, the player's maximum life is capped at 600.
