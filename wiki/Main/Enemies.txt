{{dablink|For a list of all enemies with stats, see [[Enemy list]]}}.
'''Enemies''' are characters that attempt to inflict damage on players. Enemies do not take fall damage whatsoever.

There are a total of 357 enemies: 131 pre-Hardmode, 60 Hardmode and 22 are Godseeker Mode. Out of these, 5 belong to events (1 pre-Hardmode, 2 Hardmode and 2 are Godseeker Mode), 25 are bosses (6 pre-Hardmode, 8 Hardmode, and 12 Godseeker Mode), 39 are boss servants (9 pre-Hardmode, 8 Hardmode and 15 are Godseeker Mode).

This page lists all enemies that can be encountered in the Calamity Mod.
