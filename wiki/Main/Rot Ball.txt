'''Rot Balls''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[rogue weapons]] that are also dropped by [[The Hive Mind]]. They can pierce two [[enemies]] before breaking. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown. They function almost identically to [[Tooth Ball]]s.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Rot Balls causes a Shaderain cloud to spawn above every enemy that is hit. The cloud's raindrops damage enemies and inflict [[Cursed Inferno]] for 1.5 seconds.
