The '''Soul Harvester''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]]. It works similarly to the [[Death Sickle]], as it shoots out spinning plague scythes, which stop after a certain time and spin in place, inflicting the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] to [[enemies]]. This projectile can pierce through multiple enemies, goes through blocks and gives off light, making it useful in caves. Enemies below 15% health hit by main swing itself will take an additional hit resulting from a dust explosion. The sickle additionally inflicts the Plague and [[Cursed Inferno]] debuffs.
