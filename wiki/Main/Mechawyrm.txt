The '''Mechawyrm''' (Japanese: メカワーム ''Mekawāmu'') is a Godseeker Mode [[mini boss]] that spawns in the post-[[The Starborn Moth|Starborn Moth]] [[Martian Madness]] event. It spawns on waves 8 of the event and later waves with reduced health or can be spawned in manually by using an [[Interplanetary Worm Food]].

== Behavior ==
*Mechawyrm has 75 segments in total.
*The Mechawyrm has 4 attacks that are as follows:
**The Boss behaves like an airborne worm enemy, quickly chasing down players and charging at players every 5 seconds. 
**The Boss begins chasing players slower, when it gets near a player it will open its mouth and create a large spread of Deimian Fire.
**The Boss stays above players, raining down {{item|HolySpear|nolink=yes|size=12px|Holy Spears}} and {{item|image=Essence Fireball.gif|10px|nolink=y|God Slayer Fireballs}} from its segments.
**The Boss starts circling around players, and begins firing Celestial Beams from its segments.

== Notes ==
*The [[Martian Madness]] event cannot progress while a Mechawyrm is alive during wave 8.
*[[Martian Madness]] enemies cannot spawn while the Boss is alive.
