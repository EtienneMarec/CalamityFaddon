The '''Pearl God''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]]. On its first and second uses, it fires a shockblast round that [[lifesteal|heal]]s the player, along with two of which ever [[ammo]] is being used in a spread. On its third, fourth, fifth, and sixth uses, shockblasts continue to be shot, with the normal bullet spread continuously increasing along with more being shot. On its seventh use, only a shockblast is shot which has double damage and knockback. Shockblasts create harmless dust explosions on impact, with each use increasing the size of it, and the final shot creating a colossal explosion. After the seventh use, the weapon resets.
