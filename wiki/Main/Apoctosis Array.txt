The '''Apoctosis Array''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Ion Blaster]]. Like its predecessor, it fires ion blasts that continuously accelerate with their damage depending on the user's current [[mana]] level. The projectile damage is multiplied by 0.2 added to 1.4 times of the player's mana ratio, dealing 20% damage at 0 mana and 160% damage at full mana. Additionally, the projectile size is multiplied by 0.75 added to 0.75 times of the player's mana ratio, being 75% size at 0 mana and 150% size at full mana. Having the [[Astral Injection]] [[buff]] caps the maximum damage multiplier at 111% and size at 123.75%.

== Trivia ==
*The ion blasts shrink in size as their damage decreases.
*The word apoctosis is likely a reference to the term [[wikipedia:Apoptosis|apoptosis]], or programmed cell death, which occurs as a normal, controlled part of an organism's growth.
**Apoptosis comes from the Ancient Greek word ἀπόπτωσις, which means falling off. This is likely where the effect or etymology of the weapon comes from.
