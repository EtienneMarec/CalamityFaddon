The '''Plagued Jungle''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[biome]] that spawns in patches within the Jungle after defeating [[Golem]]. It features several new [[enemies]], including a couple [[mini boss]]es. [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] [[boss]] is summoned and fought in this biome.

In [[Death Mode]], the player will be inflicted with the [[Plague]] debuff when touching water.
