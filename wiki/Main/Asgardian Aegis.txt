The '''Asgardian Aegis''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] with a wide array of defensive effects. It grants 28 defense, immunity to knockback and most debuffs, +40 max life, increases life regeneration by 1, and an additional 20 defense while submerged in liquid.

While equipped, the wearer can double tap a horizontal movement key to perform a supreme holy flame dash with a base distance of 28 blocks. Striking enemies during the shield dash will heavily damage them, inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]], cause holy explosions, and leave behind a lingering Holy Eruption which damages enemies that touch it. Every time an enemy is struck, the wearer's [[immunity frames]] are set to 6.

Tapping the {{key|Elysian Gar}} keybinding with this covering equipped grants the [[Elysian Gar]] buff.

This covering's max life and defense stack with its components. However, the Elysian Gar bonuses will not stack with the [[Elysian Aegis]].

== Notes ==
*Directly colliding with an enemy during the shield dash deals 300 melee damage. This direct hit is affected by melee damage bonuses and the wearer's melee critical strike chance.
*The explosion produced upon colliding with an enemy deals 135 damage. This is boosted by the average of all damage stats.
*The Holy Eruption left behind after colliding with an enemy deals 90 damage and can strike 6 times per second. This is boosted by the average of all damage stats. The Holy Eruption inflicts [[Holy Flames]] for 4 seconds to all enemies which touch it.
*The Asgardian Aegis provides immunity to [[On Fire!]], [[Bleeding]], [[Broken Armor]], [[Burning]], [[Chilled]], [[Confused]], [[Cursed]], [[Darkness]], [[Frostburn]], [[Poisoned]], [[Searing Lava]], [[Silenced]], [[Slow]], [[Weak]], [[Brimstone Flames]], [[Glacial State]], [[God Slayer Inferno]], and [[Holy Flames]].
* The Elysian Gar buff will be deactivated when using [[Mounts]].
