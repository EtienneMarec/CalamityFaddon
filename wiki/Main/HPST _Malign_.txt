The '''HPST "Malign"''' (Heat Powered Spell Tome) is a craftable post-[[Umbrus]] [[Spell tomes|spell tome]]. When held, it creates a ring around the player that doubles life regen and magic damage. When used, it creates a ring around the cursor that fires homing bolts that damage the enemy and inflict Eradication.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Mythical'''.
