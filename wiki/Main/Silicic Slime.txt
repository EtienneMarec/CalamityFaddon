'''Silicic Slimes''' are pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Slimes]] that spawn in water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] and [[Ocean]] biomes. They exist to drop [[Silicon Ore]]. 

== Notes ==
*Unlike other slimes, they do not float in water and will instead sink to the bottom.
