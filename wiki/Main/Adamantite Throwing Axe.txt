'''Adamantite Throwing Axes''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]]s made from [[Adamantite Bar]]s. It fires a consumable throwing axe which pierces infinitely and travels a short distance before being affected by gravity. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with an Adamantite Throwing Axe will cause the projectile to summon three homing lightning bolts from the bottom of the screen upon hitting an enemy.
