The '''Asteroid Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. It is a direct upgrade of the [[Meteor Staff]], raining 2 to 3 glowing meteors with green trails from the sky upon use. The meteors fall in the direction of the cursor and explode upon contact with anything, dealing damage to nearby enemies.
