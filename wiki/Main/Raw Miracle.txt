'''Raw Miracle''' '''(Japanese: 生の奇跡 <nowiki>''</nowiki>Nama no kiseki<nowiki>''</nowiki>)''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]. It is the condensed form of all Exo-weapons, and is used to craft various endgame weapons. When dropped or in the inventory, the item will pulse and glow. The item will also emit bright red dust when dropped.
