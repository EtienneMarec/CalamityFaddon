The '''Universe Splitter''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, a field appears at the player's cursor, and inflicts the player with the '''Universe Splitter Cooldown''' [[debuff]]. After two seconds, a red laser sweeps down from the sky towards the field every second for six seconds, slightly randomized in the angle shot. One second after the sixth shot, a huge white laser beam strikes down from the sky towards the center of the field. All laser beams inflict the [[Electrified]] debuff and pierce infinitely through targets. The main beam also inflicts the [[Moon Burnt]] debuff but cannot travel through solid tiles. If used while the cooldown is active, the weapon will drop a [[Martian Saucer]] scrap projectile which deals {{dv|10|20}} damage to the player.

Despite being a summoning weapon, the beams created by the Universe Splitter ''can'' deal critical hits. It is therefore down to the preference of the player whether the '''Ruthless''' or '''Mythical''' modifier is desired.
