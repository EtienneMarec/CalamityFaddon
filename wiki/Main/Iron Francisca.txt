'''Iron Franciscas''' are craftable consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy weapon]]s that are the direct counterpart to the [[Lead Tomahawk]]s. They throw gravity affected axes that travel a short distance before falling to the ground. Each axe's damage has a 2x damage increase for the first 0.33 seconds after being thrown. All axes thrown have a 75% chance of breaking upon contact with an [[enemy]] or [[block]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Iron Franciscas causes the resulting francisca to pierce enemies indefinitely.
