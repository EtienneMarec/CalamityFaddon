The '''Snowman''' is a Godseeker Mode [[NPC]] that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:

*There is an empty house.
*[[The Wall of Bronze]] has been defeated.

The Snowman will spawn randomly in [[Frozen Hell]] as a traveling NPC, and will not move in until he has been defeated in a game of [[Black Jack]]

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will defend himself with a tommygun.

== Black Jack ==

Black Jack is a minigame that can be played with the Snowman. The player can bet up to 999 [[Chip|Chips]] per game. Winning the game will double their bet, while losing will leave them with nothing in return. Chips can be used to purchase items in the Snowman's shop.

{| class="terraria" align=center style="width:50em"
! Blackjack UI / Cards
|-
| [[File:Blackjack UI.png|left]] [[File:Cards.png|right]]
|}

In Black Jack, the player will take control of the blue cards, while the Snowman uses red. They can choose to either {{key|Hit}}, which will add another card to their hand, or {{key|Stand}}, which will prevent them from taking any more cards. Once both players have stood, their cards are presented in the middle of the table. If one players cards total up to the number 22, they will win. If no players achieve a 22, the one with the highest number wins. If a player gets a number higher then 22, they will bust, and the other player will win by default. If both players tie, or both bust, the win will go to the Snowman.

Both players can only have four of a single type of number, and can only have five cards in total. Attempting to hit while having five cards in your hand will always cause you to bust.

== Trivia ==
*The Snowman is implied to be a former member of the Frost Legion, who died and was sent to Hell.
*Many of his names are jokes.
**Ice Creamcone is a reference to the famous gangster Al Capone.
**Icenberg is a portmanteau of iceberg and Heisenberg, the main character of the show Breaking Bad.
**Snowton is a reference to the boss Spamton from the game Deltarune.
*The symbols seen in the design of the black jack UI are those of Yharim for the card backs, Xeroc for the Draw Pile, and Noxus for the chip area.
