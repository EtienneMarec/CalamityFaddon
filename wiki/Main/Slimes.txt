The Calamity Mod adds numerous '''slime''' [[enemies]] that can be found in various places throughout the game. Most Calamity slimes spawn underground and correspond to [[ore]] added by the Calamity Mod; they generally start to spawn once the ore generates or the player can access the tools required to mine it. All slimes added by the mod, excluding the ones summoned by [[The Slime God]] and the [[Cragmaw Mire]], are pacified by the [[Royal Gel]] covering. Boss-summoned slimes are pacified by the [[Nucleate Gello]].
