'''Pets''' are creatures that follow the player around. They are completely invincible and have no set duration. When summoned, they will give the player a buff with the same name as the pet. A pet will follow the player until they die, summon a different pet, or cancel the associated buff. All pets are purely cosmetic. Pets do not count against the player's current minion limit.

The Calamity Mod currently adds 16 [[tgc:Pet|pets]] and 5 [[tgc:Light Pet|light pets]] into the game.

== Light Pets ==
'''Light Pets''' are summoned secondary characters that float alongside players to provide a traveling light source. They incur no mana nor any other cost, and remain indefinitely until the player cancels the buff, summons a replacement light pet or dies. Only one light pet can be present at once.

== Notes ==
*Light pets affect the light levels in the [[Abyss]].
**Crimson Heart, Shadow Orb, Magic Lantern, Radiator, or Sparks pet: +1 to light level

**Wisp, Suspicious Looking Eye, or [[Strange Orb|Ocean Spirit]] pet: +3 to light level
