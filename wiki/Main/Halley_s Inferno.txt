'''Halley's Inferno''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that uses [[gel]] as [[ammunition]]. It shoots a light blue comet that pierces [[enemies]] ten times and inflicts the [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuff]]. There is a 50% chance for gel not to be consumed when fired. Pressing right-click while holding this weapon will allow the player to zoom out a large distance.
