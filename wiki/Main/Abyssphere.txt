The '''Abyssphere''' is an aquatic [[biome]] added by the Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic addon that generates below the [[Exosphere]]. The biome barely has any enemies located in it. But the enemies that are there are extremely strong so the player should always be on high alert.

The Abyssphere is divided into three distinct layers, each made with a different purpose.
*Layer 1 is the entrance to the biome consisting of [[Exoterrestrial Dust]] . It can be accessed immediately after entering the Exosphere and its only real purpose is the [[Exoterrestrial Eel]] enemy that drops various [[coverings]]
*Layer 2 is made out of [[Overgrown Gravel]] and is intended to be accessed after the defeat of [[The Fabricational Quartet]], it has enemeis that drop [[Overgrown Rock]] which is an ingredient of [[Miracle Matter]] with the Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic addon {{tota}} enabled.
*Layer 3 is the lowest layer of the biome intended to be accessed after the defeat of the [[Esserwyrm]], its a very deep trench consisting of [[Sodis Shellstone]] and [[Stiriatic Ore]], the [[Reactive Guardian, Sodis|Sodis]] boss dashes into the player immediately as the player hits the bottom of the biome.

== Light Level ==
Similarly to the normal [[Abyss]] the Abyssphere also has a light level requirement
*In Layer 1, the player's vision will be reduced unless they have a light level of 1 or higher.
*In Layer 2, the player's vision will be blackened unless they have a light level of 2 or higher, and reduced unless they have a light level of 5 or higher.
*In Layer 3, the player's vision will be blackened unless they have a light level of 8 or higher, and reduced unless they have a light level of 12 or higher.


The following items increase the player's light level. These values are cumulative, and stacking several of them at the same time is required for maximum vision in the Abyssphere. In Death Mode the effectiveness of light level increasing coverings and buffs is decreased by 25%.

**[[Crimson Heart (item)|Crimson Heart]], [[Shadow Orb (item)|Shadow Orb]], [[Magic Lantern]], or [[Radiating Crystal|Radiator]] light pets
**[[Aquatic Emblem]]
**[[Arctic Diving Gear]], [[Abyssal Diving Gear]], or [[Abyssal Diving Suit]]
**[[The Camper]]
**[[Giant Pearl]]
**[[Jellyfish Necklace]], [[Jellyfish Diving Gear]], [[Deific Amulet]], or [[Rampart of Deities]]
**[[Mining Helmet]]
**[[Radiant Ooze]] during Nighttime or [[Ambrosial Ampoule]]
**[[Aquatic Heart]]
*Buffs and coverings that increase light level by +2 / {{death|+1.50}}:

== [[Breath meter|Breath]] ==
The Abyssphere gives the player infinite breath.


== Enemy Detection ==
All enemies can see you if you come atleast 10 tiles close. There is no way to decrease that amount


== [[Defense]] ==
Being in the Abyss decreases the player's defenseto a certain percentage, with more severe reductions occurring at the deeper levels. All of these defense reductions can be reduced by equipping the [[Anechoic Plating]] or [[Abyssal Diving Suit]]:
*Layer 1: 50% defense (90% if wearing appropriate covering)
*Layer 2: 30% defense (60% if wearing appropriate covering)
*Layer 3: -40% defense (0% if wearing appropriate covering)

Unlike many defense reducing effects, the Abyssphere can reduce the player's defense below zero, causing them to take increased damage.


== Trivia ==
