}}The '''Evil Smasher''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that is obtained by breaking 3 [[Demon Altar]]s or [[Crimson Altar]]s. Upon killing an enemy, the weapon gains 10% increased damage, attack speed and knockback. This stacks up to 10 times. Using another item will remove the stacks. Getting hit by an [[enemy]] will remove 1 stack.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Smasher weapon of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Ancient Crusher]].
