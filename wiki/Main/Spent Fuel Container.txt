The '''Spent Fuel Container''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that drops from the [[Cragmaw Mire]] [[mini boss]]. When used, a gravity-affected flask is thrown, which erupts into a nuclear explosion on impact and inflicting the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]]. The explosion does not occur if no tiles are below it.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Spent Fuel Container causes the resulting flask to erupt into a lingering Irradiated explosion that persists after the nuclear explosion disappears and rapidly hits enemies.
