The '''Celestial Nuke''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Bombs]] and a direct upgrade to the [[Skyfin Bombers]] and the [[Shock Grenade]]. It throws a celestial nukes that explodes upon impact, leaving behind a huge starlit aura that damages enemies for 55% of the weapon's damage, along with large amounts of lingering god-slayer embers and celestial bolts, both dealing 40% and 80% of the weapon's damage, respectively.

Performing a [[Spy|Stealth Strike]] with this weapons releases a big shockwave, dealing 150% of the weapon's damage, while flinging out mini-nukes that fires out several smaller bolts upon impact.
