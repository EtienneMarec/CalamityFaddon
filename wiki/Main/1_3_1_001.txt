The '''1.3.1.001''' update continued to polish the [[Abyss]] and [[Sulphurous Sea]]. It made a large amount of recipe changes to encourage going to the Abyss and buffed the Aquatic Scourge into a more proper boss fight. The update also came with some changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]], some new [[Music]], and resprites for [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] and the [[Aerialite Bar|Aerialite]] and [[Scoria Bar|Chaotic]] sets.

*Buffed health from {{dv|40,000|80,000|89,000|130,400}} to {{dv|79,000|158,000|185,000|290,400}}.

*Buffed rapid laser damage from {{dv|60|88|92|132}} to {{dv|74|104|109|156}}.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Stat changes:
**Buffed phase 1's head damage from {{dv|350|560}} to {{dv|400|640}}.
**Buffed phase 2's head damage from {{dv|450|720}} to {{dv|500|800}}.
**Buffed phase 2's body damage from {{dv|300|480}} to {{dv|325|520}}.
**Nerfed phase 2's tail damage from {{dv|250|400}} to {{dv|200|320}}.
*Resistance changes:
**Both phases' heads and tails now take 1.25x damage from all sources instead of 1.4x.
**Removed phase 1's head and tail's damage taken based on health scaling.
**Phase 1's tail no longer takes 50% damage from the [[Life Drain]].
**All of phase 2's segments now take 50% damage from hits which deal over 50,000 damage instead of taking 25% damage from hits which deal over 100,000 damage, the formula also now accounts for critical hits.
**Body segments in both phases now take 50% damage from ranged projectiles when [[Vortex armor]]'s stealth is active.
**Body segments in both phases now take 20% damage from infinitely piercing projectiles instead of 30.0000012%, and 70% damage from projectiles which pierce more than 3 times instead of 89.999997615814209%.
*Beam portal changes:
**No longer deals damage.
**Speed now scales with difficulty.
**No longer collides with tiles.
**Now follows the player.
**Decreased the speed multiplier for lasers spawned from beam portals.
*Behavior changes:
**Can now shoot short ranged flames which inflict several debuffs.
**Now remains invincible for slightly longer.
**Decreased speed for both phases during its blue phase, and increased it during its purple phase.
**Body segments in both phases can now be homed in on.
*Other:
**Moved ModifyHitByProjectile field in phase 2's tail's code below StrikeNPC.
**Now drops 1 Cosmic Plushie in Death Mode if using a [[Hardcore]] character.
