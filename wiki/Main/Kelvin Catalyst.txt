The '''Kelvin Catalyst''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[boomerang]] that does [[rogue weapons|rogue]] damage. It inflicts the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] and multiple boomerangs can be deployed at once, with the boomerang returning to the owner after a set distance, and breaking upon contact with a solid block. Upon contact with an enemy, the boomerangs spawn eight ice stars each, which do one sixth of the boomerang's base damage, sharply home-in onto enemies, and go through tiles. If no enemies are nearby, the ice stars will shatter. There can only be up to 32 ice stars at a time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Kelvin Catalyst will cause the next boomerang thrown to ram towards the nearest enemy instead of returning to the player, and will return to the player 1 second later.

== Notes ==
*The ice stars deal typeless damage and cannot deal critical hits.
*Almost all of this weapon's materials can be obtained from [[Cryogen]]. The only exceptions are the Ice Stars, which are bought from the [[Archmage]] NPC who spawns after Cryogen is defeated. This makes the weapon an appealing option for early-Hardmode rogue players, provided that one can consistently defeat Cryogen.
*This weapon is extremely effective in early to mid-Hardmode, as it can tear through most bosses at that stage of the game with its high DPS.
