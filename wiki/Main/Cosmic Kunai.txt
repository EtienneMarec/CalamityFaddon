The '''Cosmic Kunai''' is a Godseeker Mode [[dagger]] dropped by [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]]. It rapidly fires a stream of quick-moving, short-range kunai that pass through tiles and are not affected by gravity. The kunai turn transparent as they travel; however, this does not affect their damage and is only an aesthetic addition. The kunai also very slightly curves toward nearby enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Cosmic Kunai will deal 321% damage and cause five {{item|Cosmic Scythe|s|link=}} to release around the player alongside the kunai. The scythes will each deal 256.8% damage, linger if no enemies are nearby, sharply home onto enemies, and erupt into an explosion that inflicts the [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]].

== Tips ==
*The Cosmic Kunai's incredibly high single-target damage has limited usefulness against fast bosses like [[Storm Weaver]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]]; conversely, the Cosmic Kunai is quite effective versus [[Blood Moon]]s and other buffed Calamity events due to its strong single-target damage.

== Trivia ==
*In the real world, a [[wikipedia:Kunai|kunai]] is a 14th century Japanese farming tool. Since then, the knife-like shape of the kunai has led to its common depiction as a weapon used by ninjas and assassins.
*This weapon functions very similarly to the [[Mycoroot]].
