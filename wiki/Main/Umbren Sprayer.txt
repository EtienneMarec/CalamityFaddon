The '''Umbren Sprayer''' is a Godseeker Mode usable [[tool]] that is dropped by [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]]. When used, it shoots a thin stream of purple chaos mist that deals no damage, similar to the [[Clentaminator]]. The mist has a range of about 40 tiles and travels very fast. The mist has infinite pierce and can travel through tiles.

[[Enemies]], [[Critters]], [[Town NPCs]], and [[Bosses]] hit by the mist will be vaporized in a puff of purple smoke regardless of how much health they have, but anything despawned this way will not drop any loot and bosses will not be registered as defeated.

If used on the Nameless Deity, the mist will just be reflected, despawning the player. When that happens, the screen will become a empty menu with the logo with text "Do not." for some seconds. Thats why its tooltip is "Kills 99.9% of germs" instead of "Kills every germ/100% of germs

== Notes ==
*The mist does not deal any [[damage]]. The NPC is simply despawned when it touches the mist.
*The Umbren Sprayer does not consume any [[ammo]] or [[mana]] to fire, so it can be used infinitely.

== Trivia ==
*The tooltip quote is based on the phrase “Kills 99.9% of germs”, a famous marketing claim used to sell cleaning products.
*The use of the term Umbren in the name is a reference to how, in the [[Lore]], the mist it produces is described as having an extremely unpleasant (umbrus) odor.
*The item is highly sought after by [[Crabulon]], as he believes the marketing that comes from watering his mushrooms and [[Leaves of Crabulon|leaves]] with a weapon that could destroy the universe would be a "cool flex".
