The '''Gauss Pistol''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]]. When used, it fires a sharpened green blast that is initially immobilized. After half a second, the blast travels towards the direction of the cursor when the item is used.

The weapon consumes 0.05 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 85.
