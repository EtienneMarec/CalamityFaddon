The '''1.3.4.103''' update moved some boss attacks and servants to [[Revengeance Mode]], resprited [[Crabulon]] and [[Cryogen]], fixed a handful of bugs, and removed the [[Future bosses]]. This update was the final version in the 1.3 series of updates.


== Additions ==
*Added [[Crabulon Mask]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Several boss attacks and servants have been moved to Revengeance Mode:
**[[Astrageldon Slime]] now gains its modified laser pattern in Revengeance Mode.
**The [[Astrum Deus Probe]]s spawned by striking [[Astrum Deus]]' body now only spawn in Revengeance Mode and higher.
**[[Brimstone Elemental]] now only spawns [[Brimling]]s in Revengeance Mode and higher.
**[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] now only spawns [[Cosmic Mine]]s in Revengeance Mode and higher.
**[[The Siren]] now only spawns [[Siren Clone]]s in Revengeance Mode and higher.
*[[Calamitas]] now spawns more [[Life Seeker]]s in Revengeance Mode.
*Buffed [[Ceaseless Void]]'s health from {{dv|150|400}} to {{dv|200|400}}.
*Ceaseless Void now summons rings of [[Dark Energy|Dark Energies]] in Revengeance Mode.
*Crabulon now summons falling walls of evenly-spaced Crab Shrooms in Revengeance Mode.
*Crabulon can now drop 1 Crabulon Mask.
*Buffed [[Cryocore]] (buffed)'s defense from 0 to 10.
*Cryogen's sprite now changes in its final phase.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] now uses [[Music#Roar of the Jungle Dragon|Roar of the Jungle Dragon]] as his theme in phase 2 once he reaches 25% health.
*[[Lunatic Cultist]] now uses [[Music#You Shall Know No More|You Shall Know No More]] as its theme.

=== Items ===
*[[The Amalgam]] now makes the player immune to [[Lava]].
*Adjusted The Amalgam's animation speed.
*[[Draedon's Forge]] now also counts as an [[Anvil]], [[Furnace]], and [[Work Bench]].
*The soul artifacts have been reworked into accessories, no longer summon their respective [[Future bosses]] and now use the {{rare|16}} rarity.
**Dimensional Soul Artifact now boosts the player's damage and critical strike chances by 15%, but makes them take 25% more damage.
**Eldritch Soul Artifact now boosts the player's melee speed by 10%, increases their ranged and throwing projectile speeds by 25%, reduces their mana cost by 25%, increases their throwing damage by 15%, and increases max minions by 2.
**Godly Soul Artifact now summons 2 Sons of Yharon identical to the ones summoned by [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]].

=== Recipes ===
*[[Cosmilite Brick]]'s base recipe now yields 20 instead of 1.
*[[Profaned Crystal]]'s recipe now requires 10 [[Glass]] instead of 1.
*[[Profaned Rock]]'s recipe now yields 1 instead of 10.

=== Other ===
*Lowered the amount of extra damage Revengeance Mode causes.
*[[The Underworld]] now uses [[Music#The Step Below Hell|The Step Below Hell]] as its theme.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug with [[Astral Arcanum]]'s UI.
*Fixed [[Counter Scarf]]'s dodge triggering on friendly NPCs.
*Fixed a bug where both of [[Runic Profaned Brick]]'s recipes were combined.
*Fixed a bug where [[Static Refiner]] dropped a [[Void Condenser]] when broken.

== Removals ==
*Removed [[Ancient Doomsayer]], [[The Devourer of Universes]], and [[Dragon God, Yharon]], along with the stat buffs the player gained after defeating them.
