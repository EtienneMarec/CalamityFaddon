The '''Tenebris Tides''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When used, the weapon is held out and spins on top of the player at increasing speed. While spinning, a tenebris spear is summoned 100 blocks below the cursor every 20 frames (1 third of a second) which homes in on enemies. If the weapon itself hits an enemy, 3 spears are summoned from random directions which strike the hit enemy. This has no cooldown, and each spawned spear deals 33% of the weapon's damage.
