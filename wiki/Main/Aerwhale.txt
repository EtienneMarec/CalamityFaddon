The '''Aerwhale''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Critters|Critter]] that spawns in [[The Aether]] during the day. Aerwhales cannot be caught due to their large size, and exist simply as ambience for [[Aether|The Aether]] biome.

== Trivia ==
*The Calamity devs have hinted at making the Aerwhale ridable in a future update, although not much is currently known past that.
