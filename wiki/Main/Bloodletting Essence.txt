'''Bloodletting Essence''' was a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that dropped from [[Face Monster]]s and [[Herpling]]s in [[The Crimson]]. Additionally, it was possible to obtain 5-8 Bloodletting Essences from [[Crimson Crate]]s at a 25% drop chance.

It was used to craft the [[Blood Relic]], [[Crimson Flask]] and [[Guide Voodoo Doll]]. It was also the Crimson counterpart to the [[Fetid Essence]].
