'''Twisted Netherite Bars''' are a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] made using [[Cosmilite Slag]] and [[Twisting Nether]]s. It is used to craft [[Twisted Netherite armor]], the [[Twisted Netherite Sword]], and Twisted Netherite tool set.

A total of 35 bars are required to craft one of every item requiring it.
