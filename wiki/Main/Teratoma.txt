The '''Teratoma''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] item used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[The Hive Mind]] [[boss]] in [[The Corruption]]. Note that the boss can also be summoned by dropping a [[Shadow Scale]] on a patch of [[Grimesand]] which generate on [[Floating Island]]s after breaking [[Shadow Orb]]s.
