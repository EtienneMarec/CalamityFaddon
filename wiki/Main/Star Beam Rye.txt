'''Star Beam Rye''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Star Beam Rye''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but boosts magic damage, max mana and reduces mana usage.

While the effects of the Star Beam Rye debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
