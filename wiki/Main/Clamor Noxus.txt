'''Clamor Umbrus''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] which is an upgrade to the [[Clamor Noctus]] and the final upgrade of the [[Sky Glaze]]. It fires 3 projectiles from the sky that are either Noxlings or Stars. Stars deal 25% of the weapon's damage, inflicting the [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuff]], and sticking on enemies and tiles for 2 seconds before exploding for 135% damage. Noxlings aggressively charge at enemies after falling on them then explode into bolts of Exo energy similar to [[Vivid Clarity]]'s beams as well as a lingering black hole that sucks in enemies.
