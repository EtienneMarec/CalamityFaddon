The '''1.1.5.03''' update removed and reduced item drops from the mod's [[bosses]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Amber Crawler]] and [[Crystal Crawler]].
*Added [[Crystal Blade]].

== Changes ==
*[[Armored Shell]]s are now dropped by [[The Devourer of Gods]] instead of [[Armored Digger]]s.
*The Devourer of Gods now limits piercing to a maximum of four.
*[[Calamitas]] no longer drops gear made from [[Draedon Bar]]s.
*[[Desert Scourge]] no longer drops gear made from [[Victide Bar]]s.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] no longer drops gear made from [[Ueliace Bar]]s.
*[[Slime God|True Slime God]] no longer drops gear made from [[Verstaltite Bar]]s.
*Reduced the amount of weapons dropped by every boss.
*Armored Shells can now be used to craft The Devourer of Gods' drops.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed looping in [[The Perforators]]' code which caused them to cause extreme lag when spawned.
