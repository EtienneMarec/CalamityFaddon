'''Aerospec armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set made with [[Aerialite Bar]]s and other [[Floating Island]] materials. It requires 23 Aerialite Bars, 19 [[Cloud]]s, 9 [[Rain Cloud]]s and 5 [[Feather]]s to craft one full set. Making one of every piece requires 43 Aerialite Bars, 31 Clouds, 13 Rain Clouds and 9 Feathers.

It consists of an '''Aerospec Breastplate''', '''Aerospec Leggings''', '''Aerospec Boots''', and one of five different headpieces: '''Aerospec Helm''', '''Aerospec Hood''', '''Aerospec Hat''',  '''Aerospec Helmet''' or '''Aerospec Headgear'''. Each headpiece provides different class-specific stats and set bonuses.

The '''Aerospec Breastplate''' gives 7 defense and 3% increased critical strike chance. The '''Aerospec Leggings''' give 6 defense and 12% increased movement speed.

*The '''Aerospec Helm''' gives 7 defense and 8% increased melee damage.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 5% melee critical strike chance, and +300 to [[Aggro]].
*The '''Aerospec Hood''' gives 5 defense and 8% increased ranged damage.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 5% ranged critical strike chance.
*The '''Aerospec Hat''' gives 3 defense, 8% increased magic damage, and +20 max mana.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 5% magic critical strike chance.
*The '''Aerospec Helmet''' gives 2 defense and 5% increased movement speed and minion damage.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 11% minion damage, +1 max minion and also summons a Valkyrie to fight for the player.
**The Valkyrie minion attacks by dashing at the enemy, dealing 20 base damage.
*The '''Aerospec Headgear''' gives 4 defense, 8% increased Spy damage, and 5% increased movement speed.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 5% Spy critical strike chance and a maximum [[Spy#Stealth|Spy stealth]] of 100.

All helmets also provide the set bonus of 5% increased movement speed, faster-falling speed, and immunity to fall damage. Additionally, taking over 25 damage in a single hit will cause homing feathers to fall from the sky, dealing 3 damage on contact and sticking, then exploding a short time later, dealing 30 damage to all enemies in range.

A full set will always provide at least 15 defense, 3% increased critical strike chance, 17% increased movement speed, +50% maximum falling speed, immunity to fall damage, and for homing feathers to fall from the sky when the player is hit. Additionally, you get an after-image effect while moving.
