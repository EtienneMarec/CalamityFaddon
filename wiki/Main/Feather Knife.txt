'''Feather Knives''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[daggers]]. They throw gravity affected knives that periodically release homing explosive feathers. The feather and explosion deal 5% and 50% of the weapon’s damage respectively. Additionally, one extra feather is released on enemy hits which deals twice as much damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Feather Knives will cause five knives to be thrown instead of one.
