'''Purified Jam''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] dropped by [[The Slime God]]. It grants the user the '''Invincible''' [[buff]] when consumed, which grants them full invincibility to all damage and most debuffs for a short amount of time, but also inflicts the [[Potion Sickness]] [[debuff]] for 30 seconds. 

== Notes ==
*A character '''AND''' a world will only yield one drop of Purified Jam, which means the player must create '''both''' a new character and a new world in order to receive more from [[The Slime God]].
*Certain situations bypass the effects of the Invincible buff:
**The instant-death effect from the [[Armageddon]] item bypasses Invincible.
**Wielding the [[Coldheart Icicle]] will bypass the effect as well.
*If the player has the [[Philosopher's Stone]] or its upgrades, the Potion Sickness duration is reduced to 25 seconds.
