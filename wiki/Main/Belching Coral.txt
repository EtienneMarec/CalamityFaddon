The '''Belching Coral''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] after the [[Aquatic Scourge]] has been defeated. It is stationary, and will fire Sulphurous Spikes toward the player if they are nearby.

== Notes ==
*It is one of the only sources of [[Corroded Fossil]]s outside of the [[Acid Rain]] [[Event]].
