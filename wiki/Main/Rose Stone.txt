The '''Rose Stone''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a 20% chance to be dropped by the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. It summons a friendly Brimstone Elemental to protect you, which attacks nearby enemies by shooting brimstone fireballs or through contact damage.
