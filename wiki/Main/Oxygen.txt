'''Oxygen''' (Japanese: 酸素 ''Sanso'') is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is fought in [[Space]], and part of the [[Elemental route]]. It is also fought during [[Genesis (event)|Genesis]].

Defeating Oxygen will drop the [[Soul of Oxygen]], which must be sold to the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest.
==Spawn==
Oxygen's spawn mechanics are hinted at by the [[Diviner Valkyrie]]. Oxygen must be summoned by using any kind of [[Lab Seeking Mechanism]] around the [[Planetoid]]s on a [[Windy Day]]. The mechanism will fly towards Oxygen and get stuck in its orbit, initiating the battle. 

==Behavior==
Oxygen has 5 main phases similar to [[Cryogen]]. Leaving [[Space]] will enrage Oxygen.

==Tips==
If you hyperventilate, the boss will immediately die.
===Attacks===
====1st Phase====
*Oxygen's satellites revolve around the sphere, flying towards the player one-by-one and then returning to Oxygen's core. These satellites do not take damage.
*Cold winds (indicated by the in-game dust) sweep across the screen towards the player. If the player comes into contact with them, they will take damage and be inflicted with [[Chilled]].
*Oxygen drops ice blocks from the sky to crush the player from above.

====2nd Phase====
*Oxygen summons two [[Diviner Valkyrie]]s. These valkyries have slowed attacks and do not speak in chat.
*Oxygen also has a chance to summon the [[Aether Valkyrie]] and [[Sunskater]]s.

====3rd Phase====
*Oxygen uses sunlight to create illusions of itself, and starts to move similarly to [[Brain of Cthulhu]]. Attacking the illusions absorbs the player's projectiles but deals no damage.

====4th Phase====
*Oxygen repeats its previous phases while distorting the player's audio with blowing wind. Any music tracks playing are lowered significantly in volume.

====5th Phase====
*Blows you

===In [[Expert Mode]]===
*Attacks now inflict [[Frostburn]].

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===
*Oxygen and its projectiles are even more aggressive and faster.
*The player will suffocate outside a certain radius of Oyxgen.

===In [[Death Mode]]===
*Now teleports.

==Trivia ==
*Oxygen's [[Music|theme]] is '''Aerobic Reinforcement''', which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
