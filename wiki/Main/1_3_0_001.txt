The '''1.3.0.001''' update added the [[Abyss]] and [[Sulphurous Sea]] biomes, along with an array of new [[enemies]], [[weapon]]s, [[coverings]], and other items obtainable from said biomes. The update also introduced the [[Aquatic Scourge]] [[boss]], resprited the [[Desert Scourge]], and added a variety of new enemies found in various other biomes.
