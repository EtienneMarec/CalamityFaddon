The '''1.2.0.005''' update made several changes and adjustments to the [[Bumblebirb]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]] fights, among other bosses. It also buffed and nerfed a large amount of Godseeker Mode weapons, and resprited [[Ceaseless Void]].

== Additions ==
*Added a new [[Bumblebirb]] variant.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*[[Bumblebirb]] changes:
**Now has {{dv|0|0|600,000}} health in Revengeance Mode, instead of inheriting Expert health.
**Main boss now takes up 25 NPC slots instead of 5.
**Main boss velocity and attack damage adjusted.
**Mini Bumblebirb cap reduced from {{dv|10|0|30}} to {{dv|4|0|6}}.
**Nerfed mini Bumblebirb health from {{dv|70,000|105,000}} to {{dv|30,000|45,000}}, and reduced size from 75% to 66%.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] phase 2 is now slightly faster.
*The Devourer of Gods' phase 2's body segments now take 20% damage instead of 50%.
*[[Golem]] is now guaranteed to drop the [[Picksaw]] when killed for the first time, alongside its normal drops.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] now spawns unique mini Bumblebirbs instead of the same mini Bumblebirbs from the main Bumblebirb fight. These new mini Bumblebirbs have greater stats. Yharon will also only summon up to 3 of them instead of 6.
*Jungle Dragon, Yharon phase 2's gravity-affected Yharon Fireball launch speed now has an increased maximum possible speed.
*[[Phantom Turret]]s and [[Polterghast Hook]]s now use an i-frame cooldown slot of 1 instead of the default 0.
*[[Polterghast]] now uses the default i-frame cooldown slot of 0, instead of 1.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]] changes:
**Now has [[status message]] quotes, after reaching 1% health.
***Some new quotes make use of new internal counters tracking Supreme Calamitas kills and player deaths.
**Death quote has been changed from "oh... guess that's it then.. huh..." to "So... guess that's it then.. huh...".
**While she is alive, hearts (and their holiday equivalents) heal for half their normal amount.
**All projectile damage now scales with the player's defense.
**If the player is below the surface layer, they will be rapidly accelerated upwards until they reach the surface.
***Note that this does not allow the player to pass through blocks, they will need a path between their current position and the surface or they will be forced against a ceiling.
**If the player has over 1000 defense or over 100% DR, they will now be instantly killed.
