The Observer is a [[tgc:Hardmode|Hardmode]] NPC that spawns once the [[Ampersandi Scouts]] are defeated.

When threatened by enemies, the Observer defends himself with a vertically-swung scythe.

|Deification
|Dead. Weak. Fake. Thus are the titles of grotesque figures which blink in my life. They mangle the souls of giants like clay; discard the excrements with little care. A word etched with abandon is infinitesimally weighted. Parade your strengths as a testament to divinity. Survivors retain your name; your title. Graveyards of achievements splay across the lands in your signature. Your servants swear upon your name and sake with little consequence. I dare you to speak mine.
Your blood-thirsty usurper manifested upon your lands. The culmination of centuries of karma. I see its adapting mind defy the laws of nature itself in fury. It knows not of your form, yet it continues to learn of your influence. In my bisected sight—as organic life stares back to me with one eye—this being of pure sight yearns for revenge it has yet to acclimate itself to.

Your sins have manifested as the very God you claim yourself to be.

Terraria itself—felt beyond the veil—released a bated breath. We both knew the iconoclast came from extreme anger. The world released its shoulders, the final hurdle had been cleared.

|Leitmotif
|Flawless. Without resurrection, the iconoclast annihilated the threat of challenge presented by the tyrant's previous adversaries. The engineer's magnum opera battered the iconoclast, yet they failed to claim victory. The same fate fell upon the witch. I question the power of this vengeful spirit.
While these ex-crusaders hold a grudge against their former commander, I find it vexing to see a modicum of respect—at least—from non-divinity. I find it hard to interpret their means. I did not create them. However, their perspectives on the divine have changed. The usurper—of nature's allegiance—has claimed themselves a spot among the pantheon of past and present.

The divine, alike, have championed the iconoclast as their own. The support of Terraria and its multifaceted inhabitants lift it as the final battle needed to press the tyrant's war off the battlefields and into the books. Even a foreigner like myself can rally behind this cause. Even the deity of light—a relatable parallel—rewarded the iconoclast in his wealth.

|Ontological
|It is over.
In an ironic twist, the tyrant was quickly surmounted by an eclectic army led by the iconoclast's burning passion. The Aerie fell silent, a deafening hush reverberated through the halls of an icon now slain.

The commander's tactics were robust, yet they were no match for pure malice. With one hand to its whip, the iconoclast lifted the tyrant's body off the floor without effort. I could not see beyond the visor's glare. Yet, I could feel the vitriol for the Godseeker's broken corpse.

It returned to the cradle of Terraria in the town square it build for its inhabitants. With the world's eyes placed upon the victor, its gaze would remain fixed as the iconoclast gently set the tyrant's empty helmet on the paved road. With an unceremonious stomp, the iconoclast kicked the dented helmet across the floor and walked away, never to return.

Now, I tell this story to you in jest. My many travels have bountifully presented tales and tribulations which rival my own happenstances. Yet, I present this to you as a warning, dear Laque. I admonish you in both theme and action. Terraria is a world best left alone. You should allow it the gratitude to heal in peace. However, its tales are free to benefit from.

Rest now. May the iconoclast serve hope wherever it can.

== Notes ==

* The Observer will always spawn after the final Ampersandi Scout is defeated.

== Trivia ==

* The Observer's native tongue is Pig Latin.
* The Observer's sole name is a reference to the popular series Critical Role.
* The Iconoclast mentioned in the tales refers to the player.
* The tales are written in the perspective of Skymarcher.
