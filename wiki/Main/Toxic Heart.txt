The '''Toxic Heart''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. When equipped, it inflicts 50 damage every so often, as well as the [[Plague]] [[debuff]], to enemies within an 18.75-tile radius of the player. It also makes the player immune to the Plague debuff.
