The '''1.1.5.02''' update resprited some of the mod's enemies, most notably, [[The Devourer of Gods]].

== Changes ==
*The Devourer of Gods no longer drops gear made from [[Cruptix Bar]]s.
*[[Nebulous Core]] no longer makes the player immune to [[Distorted]].
*Resprited [[Catastrophe Claymore]], [[Cosmic Worm]], [[Deathhail Staff]], [[Deathwind]], [[Eradicator]], [[Nebulous Core]], [[Staff of the Mechworm]], and [[Waraxe]].
*Resprited [[Crawlers]], [[Desert Scourge]], The Devourer of Gods, [[Devourer Spawn]], [[Ice Clasper]], [[Stasis Probe]], and [[Stormlion]].
