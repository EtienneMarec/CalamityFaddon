The '''Umbrus Eclipse''' is a rare [[Hardmode]] event that starts occuring if the player doesn't visit either the fourth layer of the [[Abyss]] or [[Underworld]] once a day. The event starts with the sky slowly fading darker as a silhouette of [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]]' larval form appears and slowly approaches the sun. If Umbrus makes it to the center of the sun, while the player isn't in the fourth layer of the Abyss or Underworld, the world is '''permanently''' deleted. The event will no longer occur in the world ever again once [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] is defeated.
