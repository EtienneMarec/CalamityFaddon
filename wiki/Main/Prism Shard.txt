'''Prism Shards''' are [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[crafting material]]s that are naturally generated in the [[Sunken Sea]] biome. They are used to craft [[Sea Prism]]s, the [[Aquashard Shotgun]], and pieces of the [[Eutrophic Shelf|Eutrophic furniture set]].

*5-10 Prism Shards can additionally be obtained as a drop from [[Sunken Crate]]s after the Desert Scourge is defeated.
*Prism Shards counts as a light source in housing requirement.
