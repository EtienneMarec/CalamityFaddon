The '''Vulcanite Lance''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spear]] that creates explosions upon contact with enemies and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]. Upon critically hitting an enemy, two homing flares will fly in from off-screen and seek a target near them. The flares deal half of the spear's damage.
