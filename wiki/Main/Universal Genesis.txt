The '''Universal Genesis''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] which is an upgrade to the [[Disseminator]]. When used, fires a tight spread of six bullets from the gun and six additional stars from above which are aimed towards the cursor. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], bullets are replaced with Starcaller Shots which spawns 2 additional stars on hit. Initial stars deal 40% damage, while stars from the Starcaller Shots deal 50% damage.
