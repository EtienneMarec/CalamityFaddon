== Trivia ==
*The slab has four different possible glyphs that can appear on it that take the form of [[wikipedia:Alchemical symbol|alchemical symbol]]s. These include:
**🜏, the Leviathan Cross, the alchemical symbol for sulfur (brimstone).
**🜍, an alternate alchemical symbol for sulfur.
**Ω, upper-case Omega, the last letter of the Greek alphabet, and also the alchemical symbol for death.
**🜂, the alchemical symbol for fire.
