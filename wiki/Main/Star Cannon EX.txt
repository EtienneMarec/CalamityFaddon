The '''Star Cannon EX''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] and the upgrade to the [[Star Cannon]]. It consumes [[Fallen Star]]s to rapidly fire three different types of star projectiles: red, yellow, and pink. Pink stars pass through tiles with unlimited range, red stars pass through tiles with a limited range, and yellow stars do not pass through tiles and have unlimited range.
