The '''Depths Charm''' is an [[covering]] found in [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]]. It reduces the damage the player takes when out of breath in the Abyss by 30 damage per second. It also removes the bleed effect caused by the Abyss. It is part of the recipe for the [[Abyssal Diving Gear]]. Despite not being in the tooltip, it also reduces the damage the player takes from the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]] from 18 health per second to 9 health per second.
