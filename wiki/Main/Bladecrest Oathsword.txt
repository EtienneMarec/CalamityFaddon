The '''Bladecrest Oathsword''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] dropped by [[Demon]]s and [[Voodoo Demon]]s in [[The Underworld]]. When swung, it fires a scythe projectile that pierces once, emits a faint red light, and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] on hit.
