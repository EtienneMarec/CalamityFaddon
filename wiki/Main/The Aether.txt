The '''Aether (Japanese: エーテル Ēteru)''' [[biome]] is completely reworked from the ground up when the calamity mod is installed. Rather than generate underground, the Aether will generate in the sky similar to [https://rain-world07.fandom.com/wiki/Sky_Islands Sky Islands], abeit much smaller and plentiful. The Naturally Generating islands are primarily composed of [[Plants#Grass|Aether Grass]], [[Aether Dirt]], [[Holystone]], and [[Quicksoil]], with occasional deposites of [[Ambrosium]], [[Zanite]], and [[Gravitite]] ores. [[Skyroot]] is a new wood type dropped from the biome's trees (which, much like the forest biome, have 4 visual varients: Skyroot, Golden Oak, Crystal, and Holiday). The outer edges of the island are made up of a mixture of [[tgc:Cloud|Clouds]], [[Cold Aercloud]], [[Blue Aercloud]], and [[Golden Aercloud]].

Outside of ores, the Aether is also where the player can gather [[tgc:Shimmer|Shimmer]]. Unlike Vanilla, Calamity requires that the player perform a special ritual in the biome to create the Shimmer. The player must create a 2x3 outline made out of [[Astral Ore]], and place a bucket of water in the center. This will cause the Astral Ore and water to disappear, leaving behind a small puddle of shimmer. This ritual will not work if the world is still in pre-hardmode.

The Aether can be reliably located by submerging a [[Zombie]] in [[Water]] for 3 minutes, after which its soul will pop out and move in a straight line towards The Aether. The Zombie's full body must be submerged or else the timer will reset.
