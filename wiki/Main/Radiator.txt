The '''Radiator''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns during the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. It slowly crawls around the sides of tiles much like a [[Snail]], and emits an aura around it that inflicts [[Irradiated]] and [[Poisoned]] onto the player. After defeating [[Polterghast]], the aura will additionally inflict [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] and [[Venom]].

There's a 5% chance for a Radiator to be spawned with brighter green glow compared to normal ones. These Radiators will drop a [[Radiating Crystal]] when hit with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].
