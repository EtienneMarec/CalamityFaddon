The '''Pulse Turret Remote''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[sentry]]. It summons a stationary turret sentry that fires a purple homing pulse blast towards nearby enemies. Every third shot, a spread of three blasts is fired.

The weapon consumes [[charge]] on use and has a maximum charge of 135.
