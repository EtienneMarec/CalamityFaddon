'''Heat Exhaustion''' was a [[debuff]] caused being in [[The Underworld]] [[biome]] in [[Death Mode]] without any form of [[Death Mode#Temperature Protective Gear|Heat Immunity]]. It was initially harmless while active, but would increase in severity over time. The following debuffs were inflicted onto the player while this debuff was active and cumulatively built up:
*[[Weak]] after 6 seconds
*[[Slow]] after 12 seconds
*[[On Fire!]] after 18 seconds
*[[Confused]] after 24 seconds
*[[Burning]] after 30 seconds
The timer was raised twice as fast while the player contacts [[lava]]. The debuff was removed as soon as the player exits The Underworld or obtained Heat Immunity. However, the additional debuffs would slowly be removed over time rather than instantly.
