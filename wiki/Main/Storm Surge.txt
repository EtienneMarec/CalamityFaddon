The '''Storm Surge''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. It fires a glowing blue shockwave that can pass through solid blocks and pierce a single enemy, making it useful for dispatching enemies through walls and searching for nearby caves. However, the storm shockwave has a short range. Unlike many other early-game ranged weapons, it does not consume [[Ammunition|ammo]].
