The '''Arctic Bear Paw''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{gc|48}} after all three [[Mechanical Boss]]es have been defeated. It fires large ice claws with very high knockback that pass through walls and enemies. The projectiles slow down shortly after being fired and then disappear, inflicting [[Glacial State]] and [[Confused]] on hit enemies.
