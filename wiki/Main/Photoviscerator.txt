The '''Photoviscerator''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that  uses [[gel]] as [[ammunition]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it rapidly fires five sets of a semi-accurate stream of two purple, green, and rarely yellow exo flames that travel very far, pierce infinitely and ignore enemy [[immunity frames]]. Two explosive exo light bombs are also fired at a 12.5% chance per set that travel to, and circle around the player’s cursor and deal damage. Exo light bombs will explode on contact with blocks and enemies, and release several purple, green, or yellow homing sparks. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), it fires five non-explosive exo flares that sharply home into nearby enemies and pass through tiles. Once an enemy is hit, the flare is stuck to the target, continuously dealing 427% of the weapon's base damage. Exo flares disappear after three seconds.

When the Photoviscerator's flames, flares, and sparks hit an enemy, they will inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
