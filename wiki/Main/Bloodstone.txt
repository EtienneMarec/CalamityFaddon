'''Bloodstone''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] dropped by [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], and [[enemies]] in the [[Brimstone Crag]], only after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated. Additionally, it can be obtained from [[Necromantic Geode]]s dropped by the [[Ravager]] post-Providence.

It is used to craft [[Bloodstone Core]]s.

{{dei}} Bloodstone can also be placed.
