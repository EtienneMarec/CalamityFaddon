The '''1.1.1, 1.1.2, 1.1.3, and 1.1.4''' updates focused on fixing the generation of [[Aerialite Ore]] and reworking the mod's [[bosses]]. 1.1.1, 1.1.2, and 1.1.3's attempts to fix the generation of Aerialite Ore failed.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Devourer Spawn]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Calamitas]] ===
*Now fires faster in Expert mode.
*Now gains a significant amount of damage when her brothers are killed.
*Can no longer despawn.

=== [[Desert Scourge]] ===
*Buffed health from {{dv|1625|2762}} to {{dv|5225|8882}}.
*Buffed head damage from {{dv|25|37}} to {{dv|40|60}}.
*Buffed body damage from {{dv|10|15}} to {{dv|20|31}}.
*Buffed tail damage from {{dv|5|7}} to {{dv|9|13}}.

=== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ===
*Now spawns Devourer Spawns, which will spawn more frequently as its health gets lower.
*Speed greatly increased, which will increase even further as its health gets lower.
*Body can now fire nebula lasers.
*Body can now fire frost beams in Expert Mode.
*Can no longer despawn.
*Can now properly fly.
*Now has 150 segments.
*Head now deals {{expert|500}} damage in Expert Mode.

=== [[Slime God]] ===
*Fires projectiles more frequently.
*Can no longer despawn.

== Fixes ==
*[[Desert Scourge]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]] no longer become near invincible in Expert Mode.
*Aerialite Ore should no longer generate in [[tree]]s, and should only generate [[underground]].
*[[Verium Bullet]]'s projectile sprite is now properly invisible.
