'''Yharim's Stimulants''' was a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[buff potions]] made from various combat-related potions. It grants the user the '''Yharim's Power''' [[buff]] when consumed, granting the following effects:
*+6% all damage
*+2% all crit chance
*+5% increased mining speed
*+100% increased minion knockback
*+15% movement speed
*+6% increased [[damage reduction]]
*+8 [[defense]]

== Notes ==
*This potion can only be crafted at an [[Alchemy Table]].
*This stacks with the potions used to craft it.
*Yharim's stimulant's were removed in changelog 336, citing that the buffs it gave were overpowered

== Trivia ==
*This item is a reference to Steroids from Real Life
*The buff tooltip appears to be a reference to a line from Senator Armstrong in ''[[wikipedia:Metal_Gear_Rising:_Revengeance|Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance]]'': "I could break the president in two... with my ''bare hands''!" As well as the buff icon resembling a Nanomachine augmented arm which is synonymous with Armstrong.
