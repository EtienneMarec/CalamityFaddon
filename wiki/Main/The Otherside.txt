'''The Otherside''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]]. The weapon has a unique swinging animation that leaves behind a damaging tear in reality that lingers for around 10 seconds. 5 of these portals can be active at once. If two or more portals overlap, they will combine, becoming larger and dealing more damage. A single portal will deal 25% of the weapons damage, and any additional ones will increase that percentage by 15%, up to a maximum of 100%.
