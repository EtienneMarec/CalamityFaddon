The '''Li'l Mine''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Spiky Ball]] that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon being thrown, once 2 seconds have passed, it arms itself, indicated by a sound and a red flash. Upon being stepped on by an enemy, it explodes, dealing 250% of its damage.

Performing a [[Spy|Stealth Strike]] triples the amount of mines thrown, and its arming is instead indicated by a pink flash. Additionally, activating the explosion instead deals 1000% of its damage.
