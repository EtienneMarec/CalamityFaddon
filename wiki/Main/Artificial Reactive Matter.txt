The '''Artifitial Reactive Matter''' is a craftable post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] item used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[Reactive Guardian, Sodis]] [[boss]].

To summon the boss, the item needs to be dropped onto a finished [[Aquatic Hypercomputer]].
