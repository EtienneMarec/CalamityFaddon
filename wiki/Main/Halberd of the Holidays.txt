The '''Halberd of the Holidays''' is a Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that is sold by [[The General]] after defeating [[The Devourer of Gods]]. When swung, it creates green or red gift bags that bounce on the ground and create a colossal explosion after either bouncing or hitting an enemy twice, inflicting [[Cursed Inferno]] and [[On Fire!]] respectively. There is a 66.67% chance for the bag to be red and 33.33% chance for the bag to be green. These gift bags also create damaging spore clouds of their respective color along their path every 0.25 seconds, which deal 75% of the bag's damage. 

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Legendary'''.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon is a direct homage to the [[Holiday Halberd]].
*The weapon is what The General uses.
