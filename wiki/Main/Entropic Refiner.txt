The '''Entropic Refiner''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[crafting station]] used to craft several items that use [[Quark-Gluon Sludge]] and [[Omnicidal Sludge Bar]]s. It also functions as a [[Static Refiner]], [[Crystal Ball]], [[Water]] source, [[Lava]] source, [[Honey]] source, and a [[tgc:Shimmer|Shimmer]] source.
