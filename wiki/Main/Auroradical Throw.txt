The '''Auroradical Throw''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] that drops from [[Astrum Aureus]]. When used, it throws out an astral star that splits into five stars after traveling for a short distance. The split stars will deal 87% of the base damage and travel away from where the main star split for two seconds before homing in on nearby enemies. All stars inflict the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]] and can travel through blocks.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Auroradical Throw causes the main star to deal 120% damage. The five stars each will also deal 104.4% damage and rain one meteor on enemy hits, each dealing 52.2% damage.
