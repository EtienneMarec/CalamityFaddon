The '''Claret Cannon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that shoots bullets in bursts of 3. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], bullets are converted into bloody tears that have a 50% chance to [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 1 HP on a successful hit, making it a good weapon to heal health quickly with against hordes of enemies or large bosses. Each bullet has a use time of 3.

{{Modifier|ranged}} However, '''Rapid''' may be preferred for faster healing.
