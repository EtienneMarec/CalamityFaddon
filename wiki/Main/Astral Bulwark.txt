The '''Astral Bulwark''' was a [[Hardmode]], [[Expert Mode]]-exclusive [[covering]] obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped by [[Astrum Deus]], which caused five stars to drop from the sky when the player is hit. It also granted immunity to the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]], which is inflicted by enemies in the [[Astral Infection]] biome.
