The '''Bloody Corn Maze (Japanese: ブラッディコーン迷路 Buraddikōn meiro)''' is a [[biome]] that generates on the outer third of the [[Jungle]] side of the world after [[Golem]] has been defeated.

==Description==
The Bloody Corn Maze is a biome similar to the [[The Crimson|Crimson]], however no plants or trees are present other than stalks of [[Plants#Blood Corn Stalks|Blood Corn]]. It is mostly devoid of enemies aside from [[Province Pilferer]]s and [[Perforator Cyst]]s. The biome contains many Silo structures that are made up of iron bricks, with interiors filled with hay, [[Flesh Block|flesh]] furniture, and shadewood chests (which contain loot). The biome can also contain small abandoned barns that contain [[Flesh Block|flesh]] furniture, although much like desert pyramids, these barns aren't guaranteed.

Near the end of the biome there will always be a much larger silo that can be climed up from the inside. Attempting to enter this silo by mining blocks rather than through the proper entrance (or when using the rod of discord to enter) will cause the player to gradually turn red then explode into blood. If the player decides to descend down the large silo, they will eventually find the [[Judgement Hall]]. The Judgement Hall contains serveral chests and a deactivated [[Ravager|Meatmetal Sans]].
