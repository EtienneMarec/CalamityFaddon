The '''Flurrystorm Cannon''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. It fires gravity affected snowballs at a gradually increasing rate as it is held out, increasing its fire rate every second until reaching a maximum of eight seconds. It has a 20% chance to fire an additional ice chunk that splits into three ice shards. Ice chunks deal 1.5x damage and ice shards deal 0.45x damage. Both the ice chunk and the ice shards inflict the [[Frostburn]] and [[Glacial State]] [[debuffs]]. It also has a 50% chance not to consume [[Snowball]]s.
