The '''Unstable Granite Core''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] found in the Granite [[shrine]]. When equipped three blue sparks will be released from an enemy upon critically hitting them. The sparks are affected by gravity and will pierce indefinitely until they despawn. The sparks deal 15 typeless damage which is affected by an average of all damage bonuses.
