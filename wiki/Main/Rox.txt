'''Rox''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]]. When used, it fires a slightly uneven spread of rock shards. Rocks have a 33% chance to bounce five times before dissipating, and a 10% chance to split into 8 normal rocks. Right clicking causes the player to rapidly descend downwards with invincibility. Upon hitting an enemy or tile, a powerful screenshake occurs and a large amount of rocks are unleash from the position the player landed. Each one of these rocks deals 10% of the weapon's damage.
