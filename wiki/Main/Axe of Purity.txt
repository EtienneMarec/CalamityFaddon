The '''Axe of Purity''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[axe]] that is the direct upgrade of the [[Feller of Evergreens]]. When left clicked it could be used as an axe, while right clicking produces light particles when swung and fires damaging [[Purification Powder]] that spreads the [[Purity]] and hurts enemies for 85% of the axe's damage.

== Notes ==
*The Axe of Purity can purify a [[Tortured Soul]].
*Only the left-click attack receives [[true melee]] boosts.

== Tips ==
*The Axe of Purity is available to craft as soon as Hardmode starts and has a rather high axe power of 125%, being roughly as powerful as the [[Shroomite Digging Claw]]; as a result, its usefulness maintains itself well into Hardmode before being replaceable by stronger tools.
*The Axe of Purity can fire an additional projectile from [[Luxor's Gift]] with the right click ability.

== Trivia ==
*The Purification effect of the axe is a reference to the [https://tremormod.fandom.com/wiki/Gloriana_Wrath Gloriana Wrath] from the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/tremor-remastered.28695/ Tremor Mod].
