The '''Bad Apple''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] dropped by the [[Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It summons Utsuho Reiuji. 


The summon follows a set pattern, repeating after completing the final attack:

*Shoots 2 rounds of 16 orbs in that home in aggressively after half a second. They deal 400% of the weapon's damage.
*Utsuho summons 3 giant stars that explode into homing flares. The explosions deal 1500% of the weapon's damage, while the flares deal 500% of the weapon's damage.
*Utsuho dives towards the closest enemy, exploding upon contact with an enemy, dealing 2000% of the weapon's damage.
*Utsuho fires 6 rays of energy that home towards the closest enemy. The rays deal 500% of the weapon's damage.

If the player falls below half health, Utsuho enrages, which is acompanied with an alarm sound and replaces the attack pattern with a more powerful one:

*Utsuho summons two giant stars to orbit her for 10 seconds, dealing 250% of the weapon's damage and ignoring immunity frames. The giant stars leave a trail of homing flares that deal 200% of the weapon's damage.
*A massive star appears above Utsuho, which grows in size and fires six smaller stars that home towards the closest enemy and deal 700% of the weapon's damage, before momentarily creating a massive explosion dealing '''6000%''' of the weapon's damage. This attack is skipped for the next three rotations after it is performed.
*Utsuho dives towards the closest enemy three times, leaving a trail of homing flares that deal 300% of the weapon's damage, and exploding upon contact with an enemy, dealing 3000% of the weapon's damage.
*Utsuho throws three giant stars that fly around and fires 12 rays of energy that home towards the closest enemy. The rays deal 500% of the weapon's damage. The stars explode and deal 1200% of the weapon's damage when hit by the rays of energy.


All attacks inflict Plague and Daybroken. The Daybroken debuff cannot stack with anything that can inflict Daybroken, with the exception of the [[tgc:Solar_Eruption|Solar Eruption]] and [[tgc:Daybreak|Daybreak]].

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Mythical'''. While '''Ruthless''' may seem to provide a bigger damage by percentages, rounding reveals that Mythical and Ruthless boost the damage equally for this weapon.

==Trivia==

*The weapon's name and summon are a reference to the Japanese game series called ''[[wikipedia:Touhou_Project|Touhou Project]].''
**The damage value of 11 is a reference to [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Subterranean_Animism Subterranean Animism], the 11th official game in the series. This is the game where Utsuho Reiuji appears as the final boss.
