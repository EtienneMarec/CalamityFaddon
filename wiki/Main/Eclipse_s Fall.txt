The '''Eclipse's Fall''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[javelin]] which is an upgrade to the [[Night's Gaze]] and the final upgrade to the [[Daybreak]]. It throws a rapid spear that can pierce infinitely and goes through blocks. Each time the spear strikes a target, a barrage of three to five smaller spears fall from the sky which very briefly home in on enemies that are not hidden behind tiles. Smaller spears can deal from 32% to 56% damage and 70% to 100% knockback.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Eclipse's Fall will cause the next spear thrown to not pierce but latch onto enemies for ten seconds similarly to Daybreak, and constantly summon one to two spears from above every 1/12th of a second. The spear will not continuously damage after it has been stuck to the target. The smaller spears will do 22% damage and knockback.
