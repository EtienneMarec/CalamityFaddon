The '''Astral Scythe''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that is dropped from [[Mantis]]es after defeating [[Astrum Aureus]]. When swung, it will fire out a spinning ring projectile much like the ones fired by Mantises, which deals 60% of the scythe's base damage, can pierce up to seven targets, and disappears upon tile contact. It will also form a cosmetic dust explosion when the projectile is destroyed.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon can also be obtained from the [[Astral Crate]] after Astrum Aureus has been defeated.

== Trivia ==
*The projectile created resembles those created by the Mantis Lords boss and the Mantis Petra enemy in the game [[wikipedia:Hollow Knight|''Hollow Knight'']].
