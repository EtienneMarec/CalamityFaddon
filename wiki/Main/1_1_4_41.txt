The '''1.1.4.41''' update added several [[enemies]] which dropped materials need to craft boss summon items. It also introduced a few "Ultimate" endgame accessories for a few classes.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Armored Digger]], [[Blighted Eye]], [[Crimulan Blight Slime]], [[Ebonian Blight Slime]], [[Stormlion]].
*Added [[Coral Spout]], [[Crystal Piercer]], [[Death Valley Duster]], [[Feather Knife]], [[Forbidden Sun]], [[Seashell Boomerang]], [[Starfleet]], [[Tarragon Throwing Dart]], [[Xeroc Pitchfork]].
*Added [[Braelor's Shield]], [[Calamitas' Scroll]], [[Yharim's Insignia]].
*Added [[Armored Shell]], [[Blighted Lens]], [[Crimulan Gel]], [[Ebonian Gel]], [[Stormlion Mandible]].

== Changes ==
*The [[Particle Accelerator]] now functions as a [[Hardmode Forge]] and a [[Hardmode Anvil]].
*[[Starnight Lance]] now fires projectiles.
*[[Hellion Flower Spear]] now fires spikes which pierce up to 3 [[enemies]].
*[[Exsanguination Lance]] now fires flare spears which can pierce up to 10 enemies.
*Nerfed [[Skyline Wings]] flight time from 70 to 50.
*Nerfed [[Skyline Wings]] speed and acceleration.
*Projectile lighting improved.
*[[Xeroc armor]] now provides 10% increased damage to throwing and summoner weapons.
*Slight nerfs to [[The Devourer of Gods]] and [[Calamitas]]' laser projectiles.
*Certain weapons now make more audibly pleasing noises.
*[[Blighted Eyeball]] now uses 3 Blighted Lenses in its recipe.
*[[Cosmic Worm]] now uses 1 Armored Shell in its recipe.
*[[Desert Medallion]] now uses 1 Stormlion Mandible in its recipe.
*[[Overloaded Sludge]] now uses 50 Ebonian Gel or Crimulan Gel in its recipe.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Lifefruit Scythe]] and [[Particle Accelerator]].
