The '''Stream Gouge''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spear]]. This weapon ignores enemy [[immunity frames]], pierces infinitely, and shoots out a spear clone. After travelling fifteen blocks, the clone turns into a portal that summons more clones, which pierce up to 9 enemies and inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]].
