The '''Nuclear Rod''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Cragmaw Mire]] [[mini boss]]. While equipped, [[minions]] spawn shortly-lingering irradiated dust clouds on enemy hits with a one-second cooldown, dealing 20 summon damage, and inflicting the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]]. It also grants one additional minion slot.
