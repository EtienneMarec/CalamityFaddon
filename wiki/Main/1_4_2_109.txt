The '''1.4.2.109''' update added several items, especially equipment for the [[Spy]] class, along with a handful of changes and fixes.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Abyssal Crate]] and [[Cosmilite Basin]].
*Added [[Aestheticus]], [[Ashen Stalactite]], [[Bouncy Spiky Ball]], [[Deep Wounder]], [[Defective Sphere]], [[Desecrated Water]], [[Equanimity]], [[Frostcrush Valari]], [[Gilded Dagger]], [[Gleaming Dagger]], [[God's Paranoia]], [[Hardened Honeycomb]], [[Infernal Kris]], [[Jaws of Oblivion]], [[Leviathan Teeth]], [[Nasty Cholla]], [[Nychthemeron]], [[Phantasmal Ruin]], [[Phantom Lance]], [[Profaned Partisan]], [[Shinobi Blade]], [[Shroomerang]], [[Sticky Spiky Ball]], [[Sylvan Slasher]], [[Terra Disk]], [[Terror Talons]], [[Turbulance]], [[Web Ball]], and [[Wyvern's Call]].
*Added [[Dynamo Stem Cells]].
*Added the [[King Slime (Lore)|King Slime]] [[lore item]].
*Added [[Clamor Noctus]], [[Ethereal Extorter]], [[Exorcism]], [[Latcher Mine]], [[Plagued Fuel Pack]], [[Poison Pack]], [[Sand Cloak]], [[Spectral Veil]], [[Star of Destruction]], and [[The Microwave]] as [[unobtainable items]].
*[[Gael's Greatsword]], [[Totality Breakers]], [[Umbraphile Boots]], [[Umbraphile Hood]], and [[Umbraphile Regalia]] are now obtainable.

== Changes ==
=== Balance ===
*Nerfed [[The Ballista]]'s damage from 125 to 90 and use time from 20 to 22. Fossil shards now deal 20% base damage instead of 75%.
*[[Blast Barrel]]'s shrapnel, [[Kylie]]'s stealth strike, and [[Celestial Reaper]]'s afterimages no longer deal flat damage.
*Nerfed [[Consecrated Water]]'s damage from 55 to 48. Flame pillars no longer deal flat damage.
*Nerfed [[Corroslime Staff]] and [[Crimslime Staff]]'s damages from 30 to 24 and hit cooldowns from 10 to 23.
*Nerfed [[Fantasy Talisman]]'s damage from 90 to 80 and use time from 14 to 16.
*Nerfed [[Firestorm Cannon]]'s damage from 18 to 9, capped primary fire's pierce at three, and decreased secondary fire's damage boost from 60% to 40%.
*Nerfed [[Helium Flash]]'s damage from 1900 to 1280. The explosion damage is halved, but no longer deals flat damage.
*Nerfed [[Galaxy Smasher]]'s damage from 855 to 480 for the melee variant, and 390 for the Spy variant. The on-hit lasers no longer deal flat damage.
*Nerfed [[Nanoblack Reaper]]'s damage from 800 to 500 for the Spy variant. Nanoblades no longer deal flat damage.
*Nerfed [[Statigel armor]]'s set bonus slime minion's damage from 33 to 26, and hit cooldown from 10 to 23.
*Nerfed [[Stellar Contempt]]'s damage from 575 to 350 for the melee variant, and 325 for the Spy variant. The on-hit lunar flares no longer deal flat damage.
*Buffed [[Totality Breakers]], its projectiles now ignore [[immunity frames]].

=== Recipes ===
*Added [[Any Evil Flesh]], [[Any Evil Water]], [[Any Gold Bar]], and [[Any Silt]] recipe groups.
*[[Celestus]] now uses [[Phantasmal Ruin]] in its recipe instead of [[Luminous Striker]].
*[[Draedon's Heart]] now requires 250 [[Nanites]] to craft.
*[[Elemental Disk]] now uses [[Terra Disk]] instead of [[Seashell Boomerang]].
*Adjusted [[Fantasy Talisman]]'s recipe. Increased the amount of [[Solar Veil]]s and [[Ectoplasm]] needed from 1 of each to 2 and 5 respectively. Now requires 3 [[Silk]] to craft. Resulting amount increased from 5 to 100.
*[[Metal Detector]] can now be crafted.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Blazing Star]].
*Resprited [[Ceaseless Void]]'s map icon.

=== Additional Changes ===
*[[Aureus Cell]] now counts as a [[buff potion]] instead of a [[food item]].
*[[Blood Frenzy]], [[Elysian Guard]], [[Tarragon Cloak]], and [[Tarragon Immunity]] can no longer be manually cancelled.
*Buffed the life regen granted by [[Bloodfin]]s.
*Many [[debuffs]] added by the Calamity Mod no longer take debuff slots on NPCs.
*Bosses and other enemies in [[Boss Rush]] are now vulnerable to more debuffs, including [[Spite]].
*[[Deathhail Staff]], [[Fate's Reveal]], [[Soul Piercer]], and [[Vesuvius]] are no longer held at a different offset.
*Increased [[Depth Cells]]' rarity from {{rare|3}} to {{rare|6}}, and its sell price from {{sc|40}} to {{sc|80}}.
*Decreased [[Ectoblood]]'s rarity from {{rare|8}} to {{rare|7}}.
*Reduced the volume of [[Handheld Tank]] when fired.
*[[Anechoic Plating]], [[Depths Charm]], [[Iron Boots]], and [[Strange Orb]] can no longer be caught without any rare-item-boosting equipment.
*Increased [[Lumenyl]]'s rarity from {{rare|3}} to {{rare|6}}, and its sell price from {{sc|36}} to {{sc|80}}.
*Increased [[Monkey Darts]]' buy price from {{sc|1}} to {{sc|4}}.
*[[Pitbull]]s can now drop the [[Adhesive Bandage]] in [[Hardmode]].
*[[Polaris Boost]], [[Spirit Defense]], [[Spirit Power]], and [[Spirit Regen]] buffs no longer display a timer.
*Doubled the chance of acquiring the [[Radiating Crystal]] from [[Radiator]]s.
*Any variants of the [[Sand Shark]] will now count for summoning the [[Great Sand Shark]].
*Projectiles from [[Sea's Searing]] now change colors as they travel.
*The [[Skeleton Merchant]] now sells the [[Marrow]] in Hardmode.
*[[Slush Block]]s can now be converted to [[Novae Slag]] by the [[Astral Infection]].
*Increased the drop rate of [[Solar Veil]]s from {{dv|5%|10%}} to {{dv|50%|75%}}.
*Increased [[Solar Veil]]'s rarity from {{rare|3}} to {{rare|6}}, and its sell price from {{sc|40}} to {{sc|80}}.
*Decreased [[Throwing Brick]]'s rarity from {{rare|2}} to {{rare|0}}, and its sell price from {{cc|40}} to {{cc|10}}.
*Several projectiles and enemies that inflict cold damage are now affected by the [[Warmth Potion]].

=== Other ===
*Optimized multiplayer biome syncing code.
*Removed unnecessary netcode from Aureus Spawn.
*Added network packets for toggling Calamity's difficulties and changing the life regen of NPCs.
*Added a network packet for enabling the Demon Trophy.
*All Calamity [[yoyos]] now internally count as yoyos. <!---This has no major effects on gameplay, except Cross-Mod Compatibility-->

== Fixes ==
=== Gameplay ===
*[[Ancient Iron Helmet]] and [[Ancient Gold Helmet]] now provide the correct mining speed set bonuses.
*Fixed the Godseeker Mode [[Blood Moon]] buffing the [[Celestial Pillars]].
*Fixed multiplayer desyncing issues with [[Bumblebirb]], [[Golem]], [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], and [[Queen Bee]].
*Fixed a bug where [[Cryonic Ore]] couldn't generate in [[Astral Infection|Astral Ice]] biomes.
*[[Daedalus armor]], [[Elemental Gauntlet]], [[Elemental Quiver]], [[Ethereal Talisman]], [[Nanotech]], and [[Statis' Belt of Curses]] can no longer affect projectiles fired from [[Town NPCs]].
*Fixed a bug where [[Daggers]] were internally counted as [[Boomerangs]].
*Projectiles with zero damage no longer split with Elemental Quiver.
*Fireworks launched when the player levels up [[Proficiency]] are no longer valid ranged attacks.
*Fixed the [[Mechworm]] randomly attaching to other projectiles and scaling in size.
*Fixed a minor error in the loot table of [[Novae Slag]].
*[[Sand Dollar]]s can now throw two projectiles while the stack size is two.
*[[Soul of Cryogen]] now correctly blocks fall damage.
*Fixed a large amount of tile errors that potentially lead to crashes.

=== Visual ===
*Fixed placed [[Aerialite Brick]]s when merged with other tiles.
*Fixed a bug involving heart textures on the main menu.
*Fixed sheeting errors with the [[Hive (enemy)|Hive]] and its [[Hive Pod|sentry form]].
*Fixed a minor oversight in the way [[God Slayer armor]]'s darts handled their opacity.
*Fixed [[Radiator]]s appearing as a black rectangle.
*Fixed an error in the tooltip of Gael's Greatsword.
