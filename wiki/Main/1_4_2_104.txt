The '''1.4.2.104''' update added new patreon items along with reworks to [[Revengeance Mode]] [[Queen Bee]], [[The Twins]], [[The Destroyer]], and [[Skeletron Prime]]. The update also introduced the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] debuff for [[Astral Infection]]-related content, gave most of the remaining [[Rare item variants]] original sprites, and made several balance changes to some Godseeker Mode [[weapons]].

== Additions ==
=== [[Buffs]] and [[Debuffs]] ===
*Added Astral Infection (debuff), [[Shadowflame]], and [[Time Distortion]].
*Added [[Blood Bound]], [[Resurrection Butterfly (buff)]], and [[Third Sage (buff)]].

=== [[Items]] ===
*Added rogue variants for [[Galaxy Smasher]], [[Nanoblack Reaper]], and [[Stellar Contempt]].
*Added [[Angelic Shotgun]], [[Bloody Vein]], [[Corpus Avertor]], [[Deep Sea Dumbbell]], [[Devil's Sunrise]], [[Dodu's Handcannon]], [[Karasawa]], [[Kelvin Catalyst]], [[The Last Mourning]], [[Mad Alchemist's Cocktail Glove]], [[Nadir]], [[Resurrection Butterfly]], [[Roxcalibur]], [[Time Bolt]], and [[Yateveo Bloom]].
*Added [[Hermit's Box of One Hundred Medicines]].

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*Added [[Fearless Goldfish Warrior]].

=== Worldgen ===
*Added Roxcalibur background object.

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*No longer drops the [[Aquatic Emblem]] in Normal Mode.

==== [[Astrum Aureus]] ====
*Now inflicts Astral Infection for 4 seconds on contact instead of [[God Slayer Inferno]] for 3 seconds.
*Flame crystals now inflict Astral Infection for 3 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 2 seconds.
*Lasers now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 1 second.
*[[Aureus Spawn]]s now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Head segments for both the small and spectral worms now inflict Astral Infection for 4 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 3 seconds.
*Body segments for both the small and spectral worms now inflict Astral Infection for 3 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 2 seconds.
*Tail segments for both the small and spectral worms now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 1.5 seconds.
*Lasers now inflict Astral Infection for 3 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 2 seconds.
*Mines now inflict Astral Infection for 5 seconds instead of God Slayer Inferno for 4 seconds.
*Orbitting [[Astrum Deus Probe]]s now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds on contact instead of God Slayer Inferno for 1.5 seconds.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*Nerfed health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.6x|2.3x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.4x|1.9x}}
*Nerfed Creepers' health multiplier from {{dv|0|0|1.2x|1.4x}} to {{dv|0|0|1.1x|1.2x}}.
*Can no longer hit the player in phase 1.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Adjusted Brimstone Barrage attack's pattern.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Greatly increased speed while enraged, but lowered its maneuverability. 

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Increased speed in phases 3 and 5.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*No longer drops the [[Ocean Crest]] in Normal Mode.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*No longer has specialized resistances to certain projectiles, and now takes 20% damage from infinitely piercing non-minion projectiles, and divides the damage of other piercing projectiles by the amount of times they pierce.
*Reworked Revengeance Mode AI:
**As it loses health, its speed increases but it fires lasers less frequently.
**Uses only normal lasers above 66% health, only frost lasers between 33-66%, and only homing lasers below 33%.
**Homing lasers now start homing after 3 seconds instead of 2, but speed up after 4.5 seconds instead of 6.
**Body segments with their Probes still attached have extra defense.
**Body segments with their Probes detached have less defense. 
**Body segments that have lost their Probes will fire scraps instead of lasers.
**Now has a size scale factor of 1.5x in both Revengeance and Death Mode instead of having a 1.2x factor in Revengeance Mode, and 1.25x in Death Mode.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Phase 2's tail is now invincible for 12 seconds upon spawning instead of 6.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Fireballs no longer have increased visual effects if fast lighting is selected.

==== [[King Slime]] ====
*Decreased teleportation frequency in Revengeance and Death Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Improved despawn behavior.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] ====
*Can now be lifestealed off of while not in either of her cocoon phases.
*Can now drop the [[Elysian Aegis]] and [[Elysian Wings]] in Normal Mode.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Reworked Revengeance Mode AI:
**Charges less times in a row.
**Fires more stingers, but they no longer have a random spread. 
**Can now spawn [[Hornet]]s in addition to regular bees.

==== [[Ravager]] ====
*Missiles are now faster in Normal and Expert Mode and have afterimages.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Buffed Boss Rush health from 780,000 to 980,000.
**Buffed Prime Cannon's Boss Rush health from 350,000 to 450,000.
**Buffed Prime Vice's Boss Rush health from 440,000 to 540,000.
**Buffed Prime Laser's Boss Rush health from 280,000 to 380,000.
*Arms now have their own health multiplier of {{dv|0|0|1.15x|1.45x}} instead of sharing theirs with the head.
*Can now drop the [[Burden Breaker]] while enraged in Revengeance Mode.
*Reworked Revengeance Mode AI:
**Head is almost immune to damage until all four arms are killed, but the arms have reduced HP. 
**The head gains new abilities each time an arm is killed and goes berserk when all four arms are slain. 
**Actively attacks the player with two arms at once, and switches which arms it is using over time.
**Head and melee arms inflict [[Bleeding]] on contact.
**Removed random spread from Prime's Laser's lasers. 
**Changed the pattern of its laser ring. 
**Prime Cannon can now fire rockets. 
**Prime Vice moves less erratically.

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Increased phase 2's Death Mode and [[Boss Rush]] segment count from 32 to 42.
*Projectiles which can pierce more than 4 times and don't pierce infinitely will now have their damage divided by the amount of times they pierce on phase 2's body segments.
*Minions now deal full damage to Storm Weaver.
*Phase 1's tail now shares its damage resistance formula with phase 2's segments.
*No longer starts [[Rain]] during [[The Devourer of Gods]] fight.
*Decreased phase 2's movement speed.
*No longer enrages when brought out of Space during The Devourer of Gods fight.
*Increased enraged movement speed.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Arena on Normal and Expert Modes is now as small as her Revengeance Mode arena.
*Arena no longer deletes liquids.
*Can now drop [[Fabstaff]], [[Soma Prime]], and [[Staff of Blushie]].
*All weapon drop rates doubled except for melee/rogue hybrid weapons, to give them equal drop rates.
*No longer instantly kills the player if they took less than 100 damage from a hit.

==== [[The Twins]] ====
*Buffed Spazmatism's Boss Rush health from 1,000,000 to 1,300,000.
*Buffed Retinazer's Boss Rush health from 700,000 to 900,000.
*Reworked Revengeance Mode fight:
**Increased the length of most large dashes to make them less oppressive.
**Spazmatism uses Shadowflame instead of Cursed Flames.
**Spazmatism goes berserk and does super fast charges when at low health or when Retinazer is killed. 
**Removed random spread from Retinazer's lasers in phase 1. 
**Reduced the amount of lasers Retinazer fires in phase 2 until at low health or Spazmatism is killed. 
**Retinazer can now charge in phase 2 if Spazmatism is killed, which leave behind homing laser rockets.

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*Nerfed the damage of [[Armored Digger]]'s scraps from {{dv|64|128}} to {{dv|60|120}}, and its spawn rate is now able to be affected by spawn rate modifiers other than the [[Zen]] and [[Zerg Potion]]s.
*Various Astral Infection enemies now inflict the Astral Infection debuff:
**[[Aries]]es, [[Astralachnea]]s, [[Astral Slime]]s, [[Big Sightseer]]s and their spit, [[Fusion Feeder]]s, [[Hadarian]]s, [[Hive]]s, [[Mantis]]es, [[Nova]]s, and [[Stellar Culex]]es now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds on contact. 
**[[Astral Probe]]s now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds at all times instead of inflicting God Slayer Inferno for 2.5 seconds after Astrum Aureus has been defeated.
**[[Atlas]]es now inflict Astral Infection for 2 seconds.
**[[Hiveling]]s and [[Small Sightseer]]s now inflict Astral Infection for 1 second.
*Astral Slimes now drop {{dv|8-12|11-16}} [[Astral Ore]] after [[Astrum Deus]] has been defeated.
*Revengeance Mode [[Black Recluse]]s, [[Clinger]]s, [[Gastropod]]s, [[Giant Tortoise]]s, [[Ice Tortoise]]s, [[Plagueshell]]s, and [[Sand Tortoise]]s can no longer be stun locked upon reaching 25% health instead of at all times.
*Revengeance Mode [[Crimson Axe]]s, [[Cursed Hammer]]s, and [[Enchanted Sword (NPC)|Enchanted Sword]]s can no longer be stun locked upon reaching 50% health instead of at all times.
*Hardmode [[Goblin Sorcerer]]s and [[Shadowflame Apparation]]s now inflict Shadowflame for 3 seconds in Revengeance Mode.
*Decreased the drop chance of [[Gladiator armor]] pieces from [[Hoplite]]s from {{dv|14.29%|20%}} to {{dv|5%|6.67%}}.
*Revengeance Mode [[Paladin]]s can no longer be stunlocked upon reaching 15% health instead of at all times.
*Godseeker Mode [[Skeleton Commando]]'s rockets no longer deal increased damage before exploding.
