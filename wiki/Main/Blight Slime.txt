The '''Blight Slime''' is a decently uncommon [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Corruption]]/[[Crimson]] biome. It has two variants, which appear depending on the world's evil biome. It drops [[Blighted Gel]], a [[crafting material]] used in the crafting of the [[Overloaded Sludge]], which is the summoning item for [[The Slime God]], as well as various other recipes such as the [[Zen Potion]]. Throwing a Shuriken at it will turn it into the [[Ninja Slime]]
