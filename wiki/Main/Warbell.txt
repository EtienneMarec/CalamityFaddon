The '''Warbell''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] dropped by [[Goblin Peon]]s. It works similarly to a [[broadsword]], being swung in order to hit enemies that collide with the weapon. Upon striking an enemy, a bell sound effect plays and minions gain a 10% damage boost for 5 seconds as well as the target getting inflicted with the [[War Cleave]] debuff for 5 seconds.
