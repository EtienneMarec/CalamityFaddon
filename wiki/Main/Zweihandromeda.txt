It swings in a back and forth pattern and summons fallen stars from the sky on a sucessful attack plus inflicts the moon burn defuff for 3 seconds when it hits an enemy.

== Notes ==
- The sword alternates between a downward swing and the same swing upward, aimed at the cursor; each swing covers about 220 degrees and hits each enemy once.

- Each enemy hit makes 2 [[Stratus Star|Stratus Stars]] fall from the sky onto it. They deal half the sword's damage, go through blocks and inflict [[Moon Burnt]] for 3 seconds.

- Due to the swords massive size, it can easily outrange weapons such as the holy collider and murasama, making it a strong option to use against the exo-mechs in a true melee playthrough.

- This weapon has one of the largest sprites in the mod, even comparing to such weapons in the calamity overhaul addon. {{Stratus}}
