The '''Nightmare Burrower''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns during the [[Ogswarm]] in a [[The Corruption|Corrupt]] world. It tunnels through blocks to get to the player and makes a distinctive shuffling sound while tunnelling, similar to the [[tgc: Giant Worm|Giant Worm]]. While in the air, it will randomly shoot [[tgc:Vile Spit|Vile Spit]] which has a 25% chance to inflict the [[tgc:Weak|Weak]] [[debuff]] for 10 seconds.
