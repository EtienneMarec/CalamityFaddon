== Trivia ==

* These blocks are a reference to the [https://ftb.fandom.com/wiki/Compressed_Cobblestone_(Extra_Utilities) Compressed Cobblestone] from the Minecraft mod ''Extra Utlities''.

* The Octuple Compressed Stone Block consists of 43,046,721 (9<span style="font-size: 0"> to the power of </span><sup>5</sup>) Stone Blocks, but sells for {{Value|0|0|0|10000000}} as opposed to the expected {{Value|0|0|0|4782969}}.
