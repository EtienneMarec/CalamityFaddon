The '''Tumbleweed''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[flail]]. It functions like a normal flail, but it cannot be dragged by holding the mouse button. Once the flail contacts an enemy, the chain will break, releasing a rolling tumbleweed that can pierce many enemies before disappearing.
