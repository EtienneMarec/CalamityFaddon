'''Lab Turrets''' are tiles found in [[Arsenal Lab]]s which fire lasers at players who enter a 37.5 tile radius around them. While turrets can target players through solid tiles, the lasers can not pass through them. When broken, they will drop 8 [[Dubious Plating]] and [[Mysterious Circuitry]]. 

== Notes ==
*Lab Turrets cannot be destroyed by [[explosive]]s.
