The '''Dormant Brimseeker''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that has a 25% chance (33.33% in Expert Mode) to drop from the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. When used, it summons a Brimseeker that charges towards enemies in a straight line, dealing damage and inflicting the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] to hit enemies.

When used while all minion slots are exhausted, it instead launches a Dormant Brimseeker towards the cursor that emits a large aura of brimstone after stopping. This aura emits swirling brimstone fireballs that deal damage and inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] debuff to hit enemies, and Brimseekers that enter the aura become more powerful until the Dormant Brimseeker expires. Only one Dormant Brimseeker can be active at any time.

{{fantag|Catalyst}} If more than one Brimseeker is summoned, [[Stamina]] will start depleting by 1 point every minute, for each Brimseeker active. If the Brimseekers are empowered, Stamina is instead depleted by 2 points per minute.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Notes ==
*[[Aquatic Scourge]] takes only 50% damage from Brimseekers.
*Brimseekers are able to target and attack enemies through solid blocks.
*Rather than spawning immediately, (Dormant) Brimseekers are thrown out and gradually slow to a stop before completely spawning. If this thrown Brimseeker hits an enemy or solid block before coming to a stop, it will instead deal damage equal to the damage of the weapon and not spawn a Brimseeker.
*The Dormant Brimseeker aura lasts for 13.67 seconds and has a diameter of 50 tiles, and Dormant Brimseekers cannot be thrown while one is active.

== Trivia ==
*This item is a reference to the indie game ''[[wikipedia:Celeste (video game)|Celeste]]''. The item's appearance, minion's name, and minion's appearance are based on [https://celestegame.fandom.com/wiki/Objects#Seekers Seekers] from the game, and the item's tooltip refers to the game's [https://celestegame.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_5:_Mirror_Temple Mirror Temple].
*While idle, Brimseekers fly around the player in an infinity symbol.
