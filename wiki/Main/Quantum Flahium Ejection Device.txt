The '''Quantum Flahium Ejection Device''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It grants the effects of the [[Dragon Scales]] and [[Ethereal Extorter]]. When equipped, Spy projectiles release flahblades while active for every half a second, up to a maximum of seven blades at once. Flahblades deal 80 Spy damage and will aggressively home into enemies. It also grants a 50% increase to Spy damage, a 25% increase to Spy velocity, a 15% increase to Spy critical strike chance, +50 maximum stealth, a 25% increase to the rate at which stealth generates, gives stealth back on Spy weapon critical hits, all other effects of the [[Eclipse Mirror]] except for the dodge, and the booster dash of the [[Blunder Booster]].

If the player utilizes a stealth strike with this covering equipped, three flahium flares will rain down around the projectile. Flahium flares deal 300 Spy damage.
