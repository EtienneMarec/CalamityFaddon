The '''Galaxy Smasher''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[boomerang]]. It is the direct upgrade to the [[Stellar Contempt]]. It repeatedly throws high velocity hammers at a short range, which can pass through tiles. Upon contact with a target, the hammer will inflict the [[God Slayer Inferno]] and summon five Nebula lasers to strike at the target's position, inflicting the [[Frostburn]] and [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]s.
