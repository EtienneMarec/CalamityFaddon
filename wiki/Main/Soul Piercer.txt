The '''Soul Piercer''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. When used, it fires a long-range beam that, upon hitting any [[enemy]], summons 3 additional short-range beams from nearby which shoot through the same enemy. Both beam types travel instantly, pierce infinitely, cannot pass through tiles, and partially ignore enemy immunity frames. Because of their i-frame ignoring properties and the fact that short lasers are summoned every time the main laser lands a hit, an incredibly large amount of lasers may be summoned per shot depending on the size or quantity of enemies hit.
