The '''Devil's Sunrise''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]] that is a direct upgrade to the [https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Terragrim Terragrim]. While being swung, white blurred slashes will appear in the direction of the mouse, creating an illusion that the sword itself is being swung very quickly. Any enemies that are hit by the slashes will take damage and are inflicted with the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]]. The Devil’s Sunrise’s slashes will turn red the longer it is deployed, with its damage increasing immensely.

The Devil's Sunrise will also fire a small energy ball that shares similar properties to the slashes. The energy ball will follow the mouse cursor much like a yoyo, and will inflict the Brimstone Flames debuff onto enemies. The energy ball will also turn red the longer it is deployed, with its damage increasing. After nine seconds of being deployed, the energy ball will return to the player.
