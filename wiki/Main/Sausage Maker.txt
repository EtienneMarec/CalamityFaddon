The '''Sausage Maker''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spear]] dropped by [[The Perforators]]. It can also be crafted from [[Vertebrae]], [[Crimtane Bar]]s and [[Blood Sample]]s at an [[Altar]]. Upon contact with an enemy, a blood bolt will be fired out that homes in on enemies.
