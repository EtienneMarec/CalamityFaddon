The '''Nebulous Core''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[The Devourer of Gods]]. It grants the player 10% increased damage and summons floating nebula stars around the player, just like the [[Spore Sac]], that deal 1500 damage. It also grants the player a [[Death Prevention|revive]] when hit with a lethal attack, healing the player for 100 HP when the effect triggers, this effect has a 90 seconds cooldown.
