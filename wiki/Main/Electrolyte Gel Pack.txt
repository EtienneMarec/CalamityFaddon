The '''Electrolyte Gel Pack''' is a [[permanent power-up]] item obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped from [[The Slime God]]. It increases the damage of the [[Adrenaline Mode]] [[buff]] by +15% and increases how much damage reduction the player gains when hit while the meter is full by +5%. There are 2 other items that function identically to this: the [[Starlight Fuel Cell]] and the [[Ectoheart]]. It can only be used once.
