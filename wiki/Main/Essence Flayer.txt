The '''Essence Flayer''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When swung, it creates scythe projectiles at a medium range that travel through walls and deal continuous damage for a few seconds. Its projectiles have a weak homing ability. When enemies are killed by the projectiles, healing essence flames are created which [[Lifesteal|restore]] 8 health to the player on contact.
