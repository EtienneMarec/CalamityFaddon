'''Swords''' are the player's basic [[melee weapon]]. '''Broadswords''' are swung over the head when used, while [[shortswords]] are stabbed in front of the player. Some swords also release a projectile when swung, making them a good option when fighting at range. There are currently 135 swords in the Calamity Mod.
