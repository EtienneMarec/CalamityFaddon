The '''Spectre Rifle''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] that turns any [[Musket Ball]]s into high-velocity, homing Spectre projectiles that chase enemies. Its high damage and critical strike chance give it excellent one-off damage potential, but a relatively slow fire rate brings it down in fights against large crowds.

Holding right click will use its "zoom" ability, which scrolls the viewing area of the screen in the direction of the cursor.
