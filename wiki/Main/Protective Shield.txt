The '''Protective Shield''' is a [[Nightmare Mode]]-exclusive [[Tools|tool]]. It has a 0.1% chance of dropping from an any [[Enemies|enemy]]. Upon use of this shield it will absorb up to 300 damage, and you become completely immune to all damage and all [[Debuffs|debuffs]], however you will have your damage decreased by 33% and movement speed decreased by 25%. The shield has a 7 seconds lifespan and 20 seconds before it can be used again.
