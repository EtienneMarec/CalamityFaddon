'''Searing Lava''' is a [[debuff]] caused by touching [[lava]] in the [[Brimstone Crag]] [[biome]]. It causes the player's health to quickly drain, even if they possess lava immunity.

The debuff is removed as soon as the player exits lava or the Brimstone Crag.
