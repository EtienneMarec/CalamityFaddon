The '''Crimson Effigy''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[effigy]] found in the Crimson [[shrine]]. When placed down, all players nearby will receive a 15% damage boost as well as 10 [[defense]], but will have their maximum health decreased by 10%.

== Notes ==
*The Crimson Effigy is the counterpart to the [[Corruption Effigy]], trading a damage and defense boost with a health penalty for a movement speed and crit chance boost and a damage reduction penalty.
*The range of the Crimson Effigy is approximately 203 units east and west, while 154 units up and down. This makes a total area on 406 X 308 units (203 X 154 blocks).
