The '''1.4.1.001''' update rebalanced various aspects of the mod, with some focus on critical hits and [[debuffs]], along with some vanilla items. It also reworked the throwing class into a new custom class known as [[Spy]], added a new mechanic known as [[Proficiency]], and added new [[Music Boxes]] and [[Dedicated items]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Level Meter]]s for magic, melee, ranged, Spy, and summoner.
*Added [[Dark Spark]], [[Divine Retribution]], [[Light God's Brilliance]], and [[Plague Keeper]].
*Added [[Magic Scarf and Hat]], [[Profaned Soul Artifact]], and [[Spy Emblem]].
*Added [[Omega Blue Chestplate]], [[Omega Blue Helmet]], [[Omega Blue Leggings]].
*Added [[Chaotic Puffer]], and [[Devil Fish]].
*Added [[Bear's Eye]], and [[Rock]].
*Added [[Supreme Mana Potion]].
*Added [[Bear (buff)]], [[Burnt Out]], [[Defensive Guardian (buff)]], [[Healer Guardian (buff)]], [[Noseless Popo]], [[Offensive Guardian (buff)]], [[Popo (buff)]], and [[Silva Invulnerability]].
*Added [[Music Box (Astrum Deus)]], [[Music Box (Brimstone Crag)]], [[Music Box (Brimstone Elemental)]], [[Music Box (The Tale of a Cruel World)|Music Box (Calamity)]], [[Music Box (DoG Phase 2)]], and [[Music Box (Lower Abyss)]]. 
*Added Alcohol Poisoning and [[Proficiency]] mechanics.
*Added the {{rare|d}} rarity.
