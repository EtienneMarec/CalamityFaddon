The '''Void of Calamity''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Calamitas]]. It increases the player's damage by 15% and causes brimstone fire to fall from the sky while invincibility is active, such as when the player takes damage, enters the world, or uses a [[Magic Mirror]]; however, it also decreases the player's [[damage reduction]] by 10%, causing the player to take more damage.

== Notes ==
*The Void of Calamity cannot be equipped if the player is wearing the [[Void of Extinction]] covering, and vice versa.
*The brimstone fire deals typeless damage. It is boosted by the average of player's class bonuses which stacks additively with bonuses to 'all damage', such as the [[Avenger Emblem]].
**The brimstone fire has a base damage of 30.

== Tips ==
*This covering is outclassed in some respects by the [[tgc:Emblems|class emblems]], which have the same damage boost and no penalty.

== Trivia ==
*This item was originally known as the "Calamity Ring", and was what cursed Calamitas into her eye-like form she possessed in previous versions of the mod.
