The '''1.1''' update featured the introduction of [[ores]], a rebalance for [[The Devourer of Gods]], and the introduction of [[Calamitas]].

== Additions ==
=== [[Enemies]] and [[Bosses]] ===
*Added [[Calamitas]], [[Cataclysm]], [[Catastrophe]].

=== [[Items]] ===
*Added [[Blighted Eyeball]], [[Calamity Dust]], [[Calamity Ring]], [[Chaotic Ore]], [[Cryonic Ore]], [[Ocean Crest]], [[Perennial Ore]].

=== [[Weapons]] ===
*Added [[Adamantite Throwing Axe]], [[Calamitas' Inferno]], [[Cobalt Kunai]], [[Deathwind]], [[Mythril Knife]], [[Orichalcum Spiked Gemstone]], [[Palladium Javelin]], [[Titanium Shuriken]].

=== [[Buffs]] ===
*Added [[Brimstone Flames]].

== Changes ==

=== Bosses ===

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*Body segments now gain 5 defense in Expert Mode.
*Tail segment now gains 8 defense in Expert Mode.
*Now generates [[Aerialite Ore]] on defeat.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Nerfed life from {{dv|500,000|750,000}} to {{dv|200,000|300,000}}.
*Nerfed body damage from {{dv|80|136}} to {{dv|70|119}}.
*Nerfed body defense from 60 to 30.
*Body now gains 5 defense in Expert Mode.
*Nerfed tail damage from {{dv|60|102}} to {{dv|55|93}}.
*Nerfed tail defense from 85 to 65.
*Tail now gains 30 defense in Expert Mode.
*Lowered segment count from 80 to 30.
*Head now inflicts [[Chilled]] for {{dv|6.67|16.67}} seconds instead of {{dv|10|25}}.
*Changed [[Stasis Probe]] contact damage from {{dv|50|50}} to {{dv|35|52}}.
*Buffed Stasis Probe health from {{dv|500|600}} to {{dv|500|800}}.
*No longer drops [[Blade of Enmity]], [[Nuclear Fury]], [[Luminite]] and [[Nebula Fragment]]s.
*No longer drops the [[Nebulous Core]] in Normal Mode.
*Now drops [[Deathwind]], [[Greatbow of Turmoil]], and [[Hellfire Flamberge]].
*Increased the amount of [[Coins|money]] dropped on defeat.
*Now generates [[Chaotic Ore]] on defeat.

==== [[Slime God]] ====
*Nerfed True Slime God's contact damage from {{dv|110|176}} to {{dv|90|144}}.
*Can now inflict [[Mana Sickness]] in Normal Mode.
*Can now only inflict [[Cursed]] in Expert Mode.
*Now shoots clusters of high velocity abyss orbs occasionally.
*Significantly reduced the amount of Slime Spawns it can summon in both phases.
*No longer has the ability to summon [[Blue Slime]]s and [[Spiked Slime]]s.
*Now generates [[Cryonic Ore]] on defeat.

==== [[Enemies]] ====
*[[Wyvern]]s now drop more [[Wyvern Feather]]s.
*[[Essence of Eleum]], [[Essence of Chaos]], and [[Essence of Cinder]] drop amounts increased.

=== Items ===
*[[Aerialite Bar]]s now require 4 Aerialite Ore instead of 3.
*Aerialite Ore can no longer be crafted.
*[[Aerospec Helm]]'s tooltip now says "20% increase to all damage types" instead of "Minor increase to all damage types".
*[[Falcon Boots]] flight time slightly nerfed.
*[[Badge of Bravery]] rarity increased from {{rare|6}} to {{rare|8}}.
*[[Chaos Amulet]] now provides the [[Spelunker Potion]]'s effect and uses 7 Spelunker Potions in its recipe.
*[[Crystallite]] now only requires 2 [[Crystal Shard]]s instead of 5.
*[[Cosmic Worm]] rarity decreased from {{rare|9}} to {{rare|7}}.
*Cosmic Worm's recipe now requires 1 [[Mechanical Worm]], 5 [[Calamity Dust]], 5 [[Hallowed Bar]]s, 3 [[Ectoplasm]], and 7 [[Iron Bar]]s at a [[Particle Accelerator]] instead of 5 [[Luminite Bar]]s, 3 [[Solar Fragment]]s, 3 [[Nebula Fragment]]s, 3 [[Stardust Fragment]]s, and 3 [[Vortex Fragment]]s at an [[Ancient Manipulator]].
*[[Cruptix Bar]]s now require 12 Chaotic Ore, instead of 2 [[Chlorophyte Ore]], 2 [[Hellstone]], and 2 [[Obsidian]].
*Desert Scourge's [[Treasure Bag]] now drops an Ocean Crest.
*The Devourer of Gods' Treasure Bag can now drop any piece of [[Ataxia Armor]], [[Greatbow of Turmoil]], and [[Hellfire Flamberge]].
*The Devourer of Gods' Treasure Bag can no longer drop any piece of [[Nebula armor]], Blade of Enmity, [[Celebration]], [[Lunar Flare]], [[Nebula Arcanum]], [[Nebula Blaze]], Nuclear Fury, Luminite, or Nebula Fragments.
*[[Draedon Bar]]s now require 15 Perennial Ore, instead of 10 Hellstone.
*[[Lifefruit Scythe]] now heals the player on hits.
*[[Mana Overloader]] and Nebulous Core now have the {{rare|rainbow}} rarity.
*Nebulous Core now provides 50 max mana, 10% reduced mana usage, 10% increased magic damage and critical strike chance, and [[Star Cloak]]'s on hit effect.
*[[Necklace of Vexation]] rarity decreased from {{rare|8}} to {{rare|2}}.
*[[Reaver armor]]'s set bonus now boosts melee damage by 20% instead of 25%.
*[[Reaver Cuisses]] now boost melee critical strike chance by 15% instead of 5%.
*[[Reaver Scale Mail]]'s tooltip now mentions that the armor grants the player an additional 100 health.
*[[Slime God]]'s Treasure Bag now always drops the Mana Overloader instead of dropping it at a 20% chance.
*Slime God's Treasure Bag no longer drops [[Royal Gel]].
*[[Stardust]] now only requires 3 [[Pixie Dust]] instead of 5.
*[[Statigel Greaves]] now boost summon damage by 10% instead of adding 10 damage.
*[[Tarragon Breastplate]]'s tooltip now mentions that it increases the player's melee, ranged, and magic damage and critical strike chances by 10%.
*[[Tarragon Helm]]'s tooltip now mentions that the armor grants the player an additional 100 health.
*[[Tarragon Leggings]]' tooltip now says "25% increased movement speed" instead of "Provides multiple movement stat boosts".
*[[Gamma Fusillade]], [[Gel Dart]], and [[Terratomere]] projectiles no longer play a sound on tile collide.
*[[Verstaltite Bar]]s now require 12 Cryonic Ore instead of 9 Obsidian.
*[[Victide Helmet]]'s tooltip now says "10% increase to all damage" instead of "Slight increase to all damage".
*[[Victide Leggings]]' tooltip now specifies that it increases movement speed by 15%.
*Slightly nerfed the [[Endurance]] effect given by [[Xeroc armor]], but increased max health and movement speed to compensate.
*[[Xeroc Cuisses]] now provide a 30% movement speed boost instead of a 20%, and updated its tooltip to reflect this.
*[[Xeroc Mask]] now provides a 40% [[damage reduction]] buff instead of a 45%.
*[[Xeroc Plate Mail]] now increases the player's maximum health by 150.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Desert Scourge]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]].

=== Fixes ===
*[[Shredder]] is now able to properly shoot bullets.
*[[Dried Seeker]]s will now properly die if one segment is killed.
*Slime God no longer drops [[King Slime]]'s crown when teleporting.
*Fixed Nebulous Core being unequippable.
