The '''Gamma Heart''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Nuclear Terror]]s. It summons a Gamma Hydra that is attached to the player; summoning the item again will add a head in exchange for taking up another minion slot. The heads fire Gamma Canisters in an arc at nearby enemies which inflict the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]]. Each canister splits into 6 Gamma Bullets upon contacting an enemy or tile, each of which deals half the original weapon's damage and will home into enemies after exactly 1.1666 seconds.
