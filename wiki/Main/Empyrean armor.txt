'''Empyrean armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Meld Construct]]s, as well as [[Luminite Bar]]s. It serves as the Lunar-tier armor for the [[Spy]] class. It requires 52 Meld Constructs and 36 Luminite Bars to make the whole set. If all three pieces of the set are visible (whether equipped or in social slots), the player will have a pulsing aura and afterimages. Additionally, the armor's movement and item swinging animations will change to be more unique than each individual piece's animation.

It consists of a '''Empyrean Mask''', '''Empyrean Cloak''', and '''Empyrean Cuisses'''.

== Stats ==
The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*71 defense
*32% increased Spy damage
*23% increased Spy critical strike chance
*9% increased Spy velocity
*20% increased movement speed
*+20 max life
*Temporary immunity to lava
*Grants the '''Empyrean Rage''' and '''Empyrean Wrath''' [[buffs]] for 4 seconds upon taking damage, granting the player 10% increased Spy damage and 5% increased Spy critical strike chance respectively
*Grants the [[Wrath]] and [[Rage Potion|Rage]] buffs indefinitely while below half health, further granting the player 10% increased damage and critical strike chance respectively
*Gives a max [[stealth]] of 115

With the set bonus active, all [[Spy]] projectiles will be able to spawn a variety of different Empyrean projectiles upon striking enemies. After a projectile spawns, there is a short cooldown before a new one can spawn:

== Notes ==
*This armor set is considered to be the Spy equivalent of the [[Lunar Fragment]]s armors.
*The additional projectiles spawned by the set bonus do not have any damage types.
*While the full set is equipped, melee weapons release magenta dust when swung, which emit a decent amount of light.

== Trivia ==

* Empyrean Armor was formerly known as '''Xeroc Armor''', crafted with '''[[Meld Construct#Trivia|Meld Bars]].''' 
** Xeroc Armor also used to be a classless [[Covering]] set before it was a [[Spy]] armor set.
