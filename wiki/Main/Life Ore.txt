'''Life Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Ores|ore]] which generates in rare large patches in [[Stone Block]]s and their infected variants in the [[Cavern]] layer after [[Ravager]] has been defeated. It is used to craft [[Life Alloy]]s at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or a [[Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least a [[Picksaw]] to mine. Life Ore is typically guarded by [[Life Slime]]s, which deal significant amounts of damage, acting to make it harder for the player to simply smelt the ore into Life Alloy rather than farming all of Life Alloy's individual components.
