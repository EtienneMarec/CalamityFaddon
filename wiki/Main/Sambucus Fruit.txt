The '''Sambucus Fruit''' is a Godseeker Mode [[permanent power-up]] item obtained from the [[Dye Trader]]'s [[Strange Plant]] quests after [[Providence]] has been defeated. It increases the player's maximum life, as an extension to [[Life Fruit]]s. There are 3 other items that function identically to this: the [[Hemagrape]], the [[Miracle Fruit]], and the [[Dragonfruit]]. It is the third of these to be obtainable following boss progression. It can only be used once, and after that the player must use one of the remaining fruit to increase their maximum life. With all of these, the player's maximum life is capped at 600.

*[[wikipedia:Sambucus|Sambucus]] are a real life fruit known for their tangy, tart flavor.
