The '''Frostcrush Valari''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Kylie]]. It automatically throws a slow-moving boomerang that travels a long distance before returning to the player. When contacting an [[enemy]], several gravity-affected ice shards will be released from the boomerang that deal 33% of the boomerang's base damage. Each boomerang inflicts the [[Frostburn]], [[Crush Depth]] and [[Glacial State]] [[debuffs]] onto enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Frostcrush Valari will cause three boomerangs with much lower range to be thrown, alongside a spread of slow-moving icicles that deal 44% damage, are very lightly affected by gravity and shatter after about 3 seconds.
