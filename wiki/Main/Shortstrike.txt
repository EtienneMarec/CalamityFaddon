'''Shortstrike''' is a mechanic that is based on the player's cumulative time using any [[Shortsword]].

For every 10 damage dealt using a shortsword, and for every swing of a shortsword that hits an enemy, the player gains a shortstrike point. Up to 100 shortstrike points can be gained through these methods. When the player reaches the maximum amount of shortstrikes, a sound effect will play and the player will gain an orange pulsing aura around themselves; right clicking with the shortsword while in this state will perform a special attack called a '''Shortflurry'''. A Shortflurry typically consists of a rapid barrage of swings from the weapon, typically done through separate magic clones, which provide a large damage boost, and occasionally additional player buffs or extra projectiles.

As the player's Shortstrike counter increases they also gain 1% damage for every {{mode|5|10}} points, and 1 defense for every {{mode|3|6}}, totalling for a boost of {{mode|20%|10%}} damage and {{mode|33|16}} defense by the time the Shortstrike counter has been maxed out. The counter is shown as a small bar under the player while a shortsword is held.

Switching to any other weapon will not reduce the Shortstrike counter. However, the stat boosts gained from Shortstrike points will only apply if a shortsword is the player's currently held weapon.
