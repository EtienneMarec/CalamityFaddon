The '''Wave Pounder''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bombs|bomb]]. When used, a gravity-affected bomb is thrown, which is able to stick onto a solid floor for 3 seconds or until a target is struck before it explodes. When exploded, a large shockwave is formed along with cosmetic cloud particles, continuously dealing damage for a short time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Wave Pounder causes the resulting explosion to dramatically increase in size, allowing it to shake the screen.

The weapon consumes 0.5 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 190.
