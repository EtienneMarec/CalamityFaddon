The '''Flowers of Mortality''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] and the final upgrade to the [[Frost Blossom Staff]]. When used, it summons up to five rainbow flowers that orbit around the player, and fire homing bolts at nearby targets. The bolt does not pierce, but bursts into a tile-ignoring shrapnel. Each flower consumes half a minion slot.
