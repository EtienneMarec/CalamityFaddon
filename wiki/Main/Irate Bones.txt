'''Irate Bones''' are a [[Hardmode]] [[Enemy]] found in the [[Dungeon]].

== Notes ==
* Like the [[Cursed Scepter]], it can spawn in the [[Dungeon]] at any point once a world is in [[Hardmode]], as opposed to most Hardmode Dungeon enemies which spawn once [[Plantera]] has been defeated.

== Trivia ==
* The sprite is similar to the [[Armored Skeleton]] enemy, but with red armor akin to [[Angry Bones]] instead of blue armor.
