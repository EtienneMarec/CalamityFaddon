The '''1.2.2.005''' update made some changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]], adjusted [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s projectile damage, and resprited [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]].

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Mines now last shorter, and inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]] for 2 seconds instead of 5.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Now enrages outside of the [[Dungeon]].

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Buffed phase 1's head damage from {{dv|350|875|1093}} to {{dv|500|800|1000}}.
*Nerfed phase 1's body damage from {{dv|275|440|550}} to {{dv|250|400|500}}.
*Buffed phase 2's head damage from {{dv|500|1500|1875}} to {{dv|600|960|1200}}.
*Nerfed phase 2's body damage from {{dv|425|680|850}} to {{dv|300|480|600}}.
*Nerfed phase 2's tail damage from {{dv|400|640|800}} to {{dv|200|320|400}}.
*Buffed phase 2's laser damage in Normal Mode from 300 to 360.
*Laser portals now last shorter.
*Laser walls now last shorter.
*Lowered distance before speeding up.
*No longer resists [[Life Drain]].
*Will now switch to its ground phase if the player strays too far away from it.
*Renamed The Devourer of Thots to [[Another worm boss]].
*Nerfed Another worm boss' body damage from {{dv|200|320|400}} to {{dv|150|240|300}}.
*Nerfed Another worm boss' tail damage from {{dv|150|240|300}} to {{dv|100|160|200}}.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Buffed flare damage from {{dv|180|340|425}} to {{dv|320|400|500}}.
*Buffed flare bomb damage in Normal Mode from 328 to 420.
*Nerfed flarenado damage from {{dv|720|920|1150}} to {{dv|480|600|750}}.
*Buffed giga flare damage from {{dv|180|240|300}} to {{dv|340|400|500}}.
*Nerfed infernado damage from {{dv|1000|1240|1550}} to {{dv|600|800|1000}}.
*Nerfed sky flare damage from {{dv|140|260|325}} to {{dv|140|200|250}}.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Now only increases the player's stress by 20 instead of 30.

==== [[Ravager]] ====
*Nerfed head laser damage from {{dv|138|220|275}} to {{dv|124|180|225}}.
*Changed its phase 2 laser damage from {{dv|136|240|300}} to {{dv|140|200|250}}.
*Nerfed normal laser damage from {{dv|130|208|260}} to {{dv|120|168|210}}.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ==== 
*Player damage anti-cheat reduced from 100 to 50 if the player has [[Flesh Totem]] equipped.

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*All buffed event minibosses are now much more aggressive.
*[[Mothron]]s are now 25% larger once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Mothrons now drop 10-21 [[Darksun Fragment]]s instead of 5-9.

=== Items ===
*Toggling visability on the [[Heart of the Elements]] now disables the waifus.
*Replaced the [[True Night's Edge]]/[[True Bloody Edge]] in [[Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword]]'s recipe with 1 [[Broken Hero Sword]].

=== Balance ===
*Nerfed [[Blood Pact]]'s critical hit damage to the player from 400% to 300%.
*Nerfed [[Holy Fire Bullet]]'s damage from 30 to 25.
*Nerfed [[Nebulash]]'s damage from 80 to 60.
*Nerfed [[Plague Staff]]'s damage from 98 to 70.
*Nerfed [[Triactis' True Paladinian Mage-Hammer of Might]]'s explosion damage by 50%.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Plague]].
*Resprited [[Another worm boss]], [[Plaguebringer]], The Plaguebringer Goliath.
*Resprited [[Carnage Ray]], [[Night's Ray]], [[Staff of the Mechworm]], [[Terra Ray]].
*Resprited The [[Stress]] bar.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where The Devourer of Gods wouldn't spawn correctly in multiplayer.
