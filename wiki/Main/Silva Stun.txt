'''Silva Stun''' was a [[debuff]] that made the target completely unable to move for its duration. Almost all [[enemies]] were susceptible to this debuff, as well as some [[bosses]]. At the time of removal, there were no natural ways to inflict or be inflicted with the debuff.

== Trivia ==
*Prior to Update [[1.5.0.001]], it was inflicted by melee projectiles while wearing [[Silva armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]] with the melee helmet, but became unused once the [[Silva Helm]] became a vanity piece.
