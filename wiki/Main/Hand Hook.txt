The '''Hand Hook''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that can be bought from the [[Collector]] after its respective [[Blueprint]] has been turned in. When enemies are hit by it, they will be launched behind the player. If they hit a block during this time they will be dealt double the weapon's damage again.

{{modifier|melee}}


== Notes ==
*This weapon is less useful against enemies with contact damage, as the launch will damage the player, or enemies that can move through tiles.
