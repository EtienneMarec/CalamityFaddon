'''Titan Heart armor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[Covering]] set crafted from several materials from the [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]] at an anvil. It requires 3 [[Titan Heart]]s and 44 [[Astral Monolith]]s  to craft the entire set.

It consists of a '''Titan Heart Mask''', '''Titan Heart Mantle''', and '''Titan Heart Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*43 defense
*24% increased Spy damage
*10% increased Spy velocity
*30% increased Spy knockback
*15% decreased Spy firing rate
*45% increased chance to not consume Spy items
*Gives a max [[stealth]] of 100
*Grants the player immunity to knockback
*Spy projectiles inflict the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]]
*[[Stealth strike]]s deal double knockback and cause astral explosions that each deal 50 typeless damage which can be affected by Spy damage bonuses. This effect only triggers for the first five enemies hit and has a cooldown of 0.25 seconds.

== Tips ==
*Because this armor decreases attack speed, it is more useful when utilizing Spy [[stealth]] than when just using normal Spy attacks.
**This makes it synergize well with the [[Glove of Precision]], if one is willing to deal with the further decreased attack speed. Conversely, the [[Glove of Recklessness]] can help mitigate the attack speed decrease.

== Trivia ==
*The stats of this armor are based on [[Atlas]], the enemy who drops Titan Hearts. Like Atlas, this armor is tanky, hard-hitting, but slow.
*When wearing a full set of Titan Heart armor, the player damage sound effect will be replaced with Atlas' damage sound effect. This is effect is similar to the one when the player wears a full set of [[Necro armor]].
