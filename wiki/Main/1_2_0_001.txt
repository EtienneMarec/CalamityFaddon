The '''1.2.0.001''' update added the [[Polterghast]] [[boss]] and a new [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive mechanic known as [[Stress]]. The update also made various balance changes, reworked the [[Siren and Leviathan]] fight, and added a plethora of resprites.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Polterghast]].
*Added [[Angry Dog]], [[Phantom]], [[Phantom Turret|Phantom Turret (Blue)]], Phantom Turret (Red), Polterghast Hook, [[Siren Clone]].
*Added [[Stress]].
*Added [[Banshee Hook]], [[Butcher (weapon)|Butcher]], [[Daemon's Flame]], [[Ethereal Subjugator]], [[Fate's Reveal]], [[Ghastly Visage]], [[Ghoulish Gouger]], [[Lunic Eye]], [[Marked Magnum]], [[Primordial Ancient]], [[Staff of Blushie]], [[Terror Blade]], [[Ultra Liquidator]].
*Added [[Affliction]], [[The Community]], [[Elemental Quiver]], [[Laudanum]], [[MOAB]], [[Raider's Talisman]], [[Stress Pills]].
*Added [[Afflicted]], [[Fab]], [[Heart Attack]], [[Horror]], [[Marked for Death]], [[Phantom (buff)]], [[Zerg]].
*Added [[Necroplasmic Beacon]], [[Portable Bulb]], [[Ruinous Soul]], [[Treasure Bag (Polterghast)]].
*Added [[Cataclysm Trophy]], [[Catastrophe Trophy]], [[Ceaseless Void Trophy]], [[Crabulon Trophy]], [[Signus Trophy]], [[Storm Weaver Trophy]].
