The '''Metal Ball''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[flail]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It throws out a metal ball that is affected by gravity and can be flung around by moving. If the player is standing still, the thrown flail eventually stables out at a position directly below the player.

== Tips ==
*Moving in small, quick circles to build momentum in the ball can cause the ball to orbit around you at a fairly decent speed.

== Trivia ==
*The item's physics are based on Samson's Chains from ''[[wikipedia:The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth|The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth]]''.
**However, unlike Samson's Chains, the Metal Ball can't block projectiles.
