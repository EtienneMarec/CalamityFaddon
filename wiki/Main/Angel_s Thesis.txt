== Trivia ==
*The item's name is a reference to the ''[[wikipedia:Evangelion|Evangelion]]'' song, "Cruel Angel's Thesis".
*As well as it being a reference to Evangelion, the statue also looks like The 6th Angel.
*The buff description is a reference to the Calamity community's obsession with the phrase "perfectly balanced", originating from ''[[wikipedia:Avengers: Infinity War|Avengers: Infinity War]]''.
