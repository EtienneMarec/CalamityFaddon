'''Cutter's Gift''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|umbrus]] [[Broadsword]] that is either crafted or dropped from the [[Esserwyrm]]. Its main attack (left click) Swings the sword leaving 4-pointed stars that stay for 3 seconds, they deal 80% of normal damage but inflict the [[Evercold]] debuff. Its secondary attack (right click) has 2 times slower speed. It points the sword to the given direction and launches a shockwave. The shockwave deals 200% of the weapon's damage, the sword deals 130% of it, but inflicts the Evercold debuff.
