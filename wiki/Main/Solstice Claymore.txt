The '''Solstice Claymore''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that shoots a different colored sword based on the real-time season. It inflicts the [[Daybroken]] [[debuff]] during the day, causing targets to lose 100 health every second, and the [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuff]] during the night, causing targets to lose 100 health every second.
