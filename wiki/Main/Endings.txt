The Calamity Mod features several possible ''Endings'' to achieve, as well as other possible outcomes the player may receive depending on how they completed the game.

==Epilogue==
''Requirements: [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]].''</br>
This outcome can be considered a "soft" ending as it does not conclude the story for the entire run, but rather the player's actions up until defeating [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. After Yharon is defeated, [[Godseeker, Yharim]] will telepathically communicate with the player through the in-game chat. The monologue will consist of many different comments on the player's stats and other decisions, including the following: 
*Steps taken.
*Bosses defeated.
**Every single boss has a separate line of dialogue, including minibosses.
*[[Town NPCs]] that have moved in.
*Amount of money spent.
*Total damage taken.
*Amount of time spent standing still.
*Amount of time spent [[Fishing]].
*Amount of unique players which have joined the world.
*Amount of [[Delicious Meat]] owned, bought and consumed.
*Amount of items crafted.
*Total times tabbed out of the game window (referred to by Yharim as "times you had to look at the Wiki")
*Amount of [[Arsenal Labs]] discovered.
**Amount of Draedon's Arsenal items crafted.
*Amount of [[Blocks]] and tiles destroyed.
*Whether the player has a [[Pet]] or not.
*Deepest [[Abyss]] layer.
*Whether the player has [[Royal Gel]] equipped.

While the monologue may have clues on how to achieve different endings, this whole sequence is extremely long and it is highly recommended to do something else until it is completed in chat.

The [[music]] that plays during the Epilogue is [https://youtu.be/dx1CYkunXoE '''soliloquy of yharim'''] which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@gummyleeches GummyLeeches].

==Neutral Ending==
''Requirements: Defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]] and the [[The Fabricational Quartet]].''</br>

This ending can be achieved by simply defeating [[Godseeker, Yharim]]. Upon defeat, Yharim attempts to perform another speech lamenting the players before a dragon flies by and knocks him into the void.

The [[music]] that plays during the ending is '''[https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1021557465776672799/1107935399596281916/Introspective_Lament_of_an_Oppressor.wav Introspective Lament of an Oppressor]''' which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@theexiiedfeiiow4748 The ExiIed FeIIow].

== Good Ending ==
''Requirements: Defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]] and the [[The Fabricational Quartet]].''

This ending can be earned by defeating [[Godseeker, Yharim]], then killing the dragon about to knock him into the void. Upon doing this, Yharim will thank the player and reward them with a buffed version of the [[Ogscule]] alongside his regular drops. This will unlock Layer 5 of the [[Abyss]].

The [[music]] that plays during the ending is '''[https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1021557465776672799/1107935399596281916/Introspective_Lament_of_an_Oppressor.wav Introspective Lament of an Oppressor]''' which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/@theexiiedfeiiow4748 The ExiIed FeIIow].

==Dark God ending==
''Requirements: Completing [[Boss Rush]] and obtaining [[Genesis]].''</br>

[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] is the final boss of this route. No special actions need to be taken and bosses can be killed in any order, however completing this route permanently affects how NPCs behave toward the player.

==Taming ending==
''Requirements: [[Taming]] all previous bosses.''</br>

Refer to the [[Taming]] page for the full process. Both the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] and [[XP-00 Hypnos]] must have been tamed before fighting the final boss of this route, [[Supreme Calamitas]]. This can be considered the peaceful ending, however the steps to do so can be difficult to ascertain without a guide or reading Yharim's full dialogue.

==Elemental ending==
''Requirements: Selling the souls of [[Cryogen]], [[Pyrogen]], [[Ionogen]], [[Phytogen]], [[Pathogen]], [[Oxygen]], [[Carcinogen]],  [[Dihydrogen Monoxide]] (must be defeated multiple times for [[Essence of water|Essence of Water]]) and [[Hydrogen]] to the [[Archmage]], and then initiating [[Genesis (event)]].'' 

[[Supreme Cryogen]] is the optional final boss of this route.

==[[La Ruga]] Ending==
''Requirements: See list. ''</br>
The following must be completed:
*[[La Ruga]] cannot be encountered beforehand.
*[[The Exosphere]]'s monitor room must have been accessed.
*The player must have 2,222 [[22 Hornet Round]]s in their inventory
*The player must have [[Terminus]].
*The player must have exactly 50 [[Scoria Ore]].

[[Lav'e Rugamarius]] is the final boss of this route. Extensive knowledge of the game's exploits is recommended, and La Ruga's true identity will be revealed at the end of the fight.

==[[Delicious Meat Ending]]==
''Requirements: Owning 2,147,483,647 [[Delicious Meat]].''</br>

[[Crabulon]] is the final boss of this route.
After beating Crabulon, the [[Shroom Garden]] will become accessible. Going there and consuming [[Sacred Meat]] permanently teleports the player to [[Heavenly Limbo]]. It is not possible to leave this area, essentially locking the character in this dimension forever.

== [[Delicious Meat Ending|Delicious Meat Ending+]] ==
Requirements: Owning 2,147,483,648 ''[[Delicious Meat]],'' and then using the Sacred Meat on the Altar of The Accursed

Supreme Ultramage Permafrost is the final boss of this route. after following all the instructions of The Delicious Meat Ending up to the [[Shroom Garden]], obtain the [[Sacred Meat]] but do not eat it. Instead, using Sacred Meat on the Altar of The Accursed will summon a buffed Supreme Ultramage Permafrost. "Beat" him, and the ending is yours.

Unlike The Delicious Meat Ending, you can leave and enter [[Heavenly Limbo]] as you please. This means that The Delicious Meat Ending+ has a true post-game, unlike the normal [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Delicious_Meat_Ending Delicious Route].
