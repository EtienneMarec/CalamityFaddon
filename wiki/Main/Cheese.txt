Cheese is a consumable [[Challenges|Godseeker Mode]] [[Food and Drink Potions|food item]] that is sold by [[The Big Cheese]] and drops from [[tgc:Mouse|Mice]], [[tgc:Rat|Rats]], [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Puff_enemies Cheese Puffs] and [[tgc:Moon_Lord|The Moon Lord]]. It will only drop from Mice, Rats and Cheese Puffs when in Godseeker Mode, while it will drop from The Moon Lord regardless of other [[Challenges|bosses]] beaten. When consumed, it grants the [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Exquisitely_Stuffed Exquisitely Stuffed] buff for 30 minutes. Additionally, it can be placed as a [[Blocks|Block]] by pressing Right Click while holding it.

== Trivia ==

* The tooltip is a reference to the video [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u_LWodDWPGQ The Quest for Cheese] By Zach Fuller, in which a group of Wallaces from the series [[wikipedia:Wallace_and_Gromit|Wallace and Gromit]] search for cheese. The part that is specifically being referenced is when one of the Wallaces says "I wish I could fly?" 
* Cheese dropping from Moon Lord is a reference to "[[wikipedia:The_Moon_is_made_of_green_cheese|The Moon is made of Cheese]]", a pop culture reference.
