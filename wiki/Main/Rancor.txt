'''Rancor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[magic weapon]]. It is the [[exhumed]] form of the [[Burning Sea]]. It creates a magic circle a set distance away from the player. After 4 seconds, the magic circle fires a large laser beam that pierces infinitely. Upon contact with solid tiles, the laser spawns dust, fog, and lava at the impact location. Additionally, damaging arms emerge out which deal 66.67% of the laser's damage, along with large cinders which fly out and deal 33.33% of the laser's damage. Both arms and cinders can also damage the player (the arms do {{dv|200-250|400-500}} damage, while the cinders do {{dv|100-150|200-300}} damage). Enemies struck by the laser beam are inflicted by the '''Rancor Burn''' debuff, which cause them to disintegrate into ashes on death.

== Trivia ==
*The word "rancor" means bitterness or ruthlessness.
*The magic circle that the weapon summons is based on the Human Transmutation Circle from ''[[wikipedia:Fullmetal_Alchemist|Fullmetal Alchemist]].''
**The weapon's damage of 444 references the fact that repetition of the number 4 usually represents death in Eastern media.
