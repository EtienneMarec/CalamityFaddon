The '''Sanguine Flare''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. When used, it fires a random spread of 6 to 8 red orbs, which begin waving erratically after 3 seconds of travel. The orbs can pass through blocks and, upon contact with enemies, inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] onto them and each [[lifesteal|return]] 1 HP to the player.
