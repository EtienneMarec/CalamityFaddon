The '''Leviathan Ambergris''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Leviathan and Anahita]]. It leaves poisonous seawater as the player moves and grants a boosts to damage, [[defense]], and movement speed while submerged in water. If the player is not submerged in liquid, they will receive 5% increased damage and damage reduction. If the player is damaged while they are submerged in liquid, they gain an aura around themselves for a short time which damages nearby enemies.
