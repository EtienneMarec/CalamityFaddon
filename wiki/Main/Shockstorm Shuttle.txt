The '''Shockstorm Shuttle''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in [[Space]]. It has two attacks; a blue lightning bolt that deals moderate damage and inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]] on hit and a yellow laser that travels at a high velocity and deals higher damage.

== Trivia ==
*It seems to be a miniature version of the [[Martian Saucer]] in both design and attacks.
*In previous versions of the mod, it was also able to drop [[Essence of Eleum]].
