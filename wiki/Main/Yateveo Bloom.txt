The '''Yateveo Bloom''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spears|spear]]. It strikes the ground, creating damaging roses that occasionally release petals upwards. The roses inflict the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]]. The petals pierce indefinitely and are affected by gravity, slowly floating downward. If the player holds down left-click, the spear will continue swinging, dealing damage to any enemy it contacts. Pressing or holding right-click will throw the flower spear, bursting into 12 petals when it collides with an enemy or wall. Only the swung (left-click) spear attack will have [[True melee]] boosts applied to it.
