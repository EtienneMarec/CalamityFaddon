The '''Aquatic Discharge''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. When swung, it emits harmless particles of electricity. Upon killing an enemy with it, it will release small electric sparks which linger on the ground for a short time, dealing damage to enemies that touch them.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemies hit by it and its sparks are [[Electrified]] for 5 seconds.

Like every [[shortsword]], it has an incredibly short range, but unlike most other vanilla shortswords, it has autoswing.
