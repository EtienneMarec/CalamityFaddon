The '''1.1.5.05''' update changed the spawn rates of certain enemies and made several nerfs to [[The Hive Mind]].

== Changes ==
*Hive Mind nerfs:
**Phase 1 now deals less contact damage.
**Vile spit projectiles now deal less damage.
**[[Dank Creeper]] damage reduced.
**[[Dark Heart]]'s shaderain projectile damage reduced.
*Increased [[Stormlion]] spawn rate.
*Decreased [[Plague Charger]] spawn rate.

=== Fixes ===
*Fixed some strange projectile interactions with tiles.
