{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.4.1.003 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.4.1.003''' (or '''1.4.1.3''') Music mod update seperated the music tracks and [[Music Boxes]] from the Main mod. This update also added music boxes for the [[Supreme Calamitas]] music tracks.

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod version [[1.4.1.003]]. 

== Additions ==
*Added [[Music Box (Acceptance)]], [[Music Box (Epiphany)]], [[Music Box (Grief)]], [[Music Box (Lament)]], and [[Music Box (Void)]].

== Changes ==
*Moved all Music Boxes and music tracks to the Music mod.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed several audio quality issues the [[Hive Mind]], [[The Perforators]], and [[The Slime God]] music tracks.
