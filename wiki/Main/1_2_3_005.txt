The '''1.2.3.005''' update reworked some bosses in [[Death Mode]], renamed most of the mod's [[Bars]], and added extra [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist] support for when bosses were killed in Death Mode.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Wulfrum Pickaxe]].

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*The [[Astrum Deus]] worms are now much faster, shoot more lasers, and have faster lasers in Death Mode.
*[[Brain of Cthulhu]] now gains a {{death|1.25x}} damage modifier in Death Mode, and [[Creeper]]s now spawn every {{dv|0|0|3rd|1st}} time the Brain of Cthulhu teleports in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*[[Bumblebirb]] now charges more often, and spawns more clones midair in Death Mode.
*[[Crab Shroom]]s no longer produce dust idly.
*The Destroyer's [[Probe]]s now gain a {{death|1.25x}} health modifier, a {{death|1.25x}} damage modifier, and a {{death|1.2x}} size modifier in Death Mode.
*The Destroyer's lasers can now go through tiles if a tile is between it and the player in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] now switches phases twice as often in both phases in Death Mode.
*[[Eater of Worlds]]' head now gains a 1.85x damage modifier, and its body and tail segments gain a 1.5x damage modifier.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] can now spawn an extra infernado in Death Mode.
*[[The Perforator Hive]] now rapidly shoots blood geysers in Death Mode that go through tiles.
*[[Polterghast]]'s phantom shots now penetrate 4 times in Death Mode, but now only penetrate twice in every other mode instead of 4 times in all modes.
*[[Providence, the Profaned God]] now has a different order of attacks in Death Mode.
*Buffed [[Skeletron]]'s hands' health modifier in Death Mode from {{death|1.3x}} to {{death|1.4x}}, and their health modifier in every other mode from 1.15x to 1.2x.
*[[Skeletron Prime]]'s head now fires bursts of lasers going in random directions if the Prime Laser is killed in Death Mode.
*[[Storm Weaver]]'s second phase now lowers its segment count to 12 in Death Mode.
*Storm Weaver's second phase now always chases the player in Death Mode.
*Reduced the amount of brimstone skulls [[Supreme Calamitas]] creates in the final bullet hell.
*[[The Twins]] now enter their second phases much earlier in Death Mode.
*Nerfed [[Wulfrum Drone]]'s health from {{dv|60|84}} to {{dv|35|49}}, and its damage from {{dv|25|35|43|52}} to {{dv|20|28|35|42}}.
*Nerfed [[Wulfrum Slime]]'s health from {{dv|32|44}} to {{dv|20|28}}, and its damage from {{dv|16|22|27|33}} to {{dv|13|18|22|27}}.

=== Items ===
*Nerfed [[Blood Clot Staff]]'s damage from 9 to 7.
*Increased the speed of [[Cosmilamp]]'s cosmic lantern minions, and reduced the size of their dust.
*Renamed Cruptix Bar to [[Chaotic Bar]].
*Renamed Cryo Bar to [[Frigid Bar]].
*Nerfed [[Dank Staff]]'s damage from 9 to 7.
*Renamed Draedon Bar to [[Perennial Bar]].
*Buffed [[Flare Bolt]]'s damage from 20 to 23.
*The [[Frog Leg]] can now be crafted with 10 [[Frog]]s at a pre-Hardmode [[Anvil]].
*Nerfed the damage of [[Fungicide]]'s split projectiles by 50%.
*Renamed Meldiate Bar to [[Meld Bar]].
*Renamed Not Viable to [[Viable?]].
*Buffed Viable?'s damage from 570 to 700.
*The [[Reaver Shark]] can now be swung in Revengeance Mode after [[Eater of Worlds]] or [[Brain of Cthulhu]] has been defeated instead of [[Skeletron]].
*Renamed Ueliace Bar to [[Uelibloom Bar]].
*Renamed Verstaltite Bar to [[Cryonic Bar]].

=== Other ===
*Bosses now have a separate Death Mode check in the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist] mod's boss checklist.
*[[Extreme Gravity]] now sets wing time to 100 if the player's wing time is over 400.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Mechworm (buff)|Mechworm buff]].
*Resprited [[Soul Harvester]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a sheeting error on [[Cloud Elemental]].
*Fixed a multiplayer desync bug with [[Death]] and [[Revengeance]].
*Fixed a sheeting error on [[Wulfrum armor]].
*Fixed a sheeting error on Wulfrum Slime.
