The '''Lucrecia''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that auto-swings. When swung, it fires a straight double helix directly left or right in the direction of the cursor which passes through blocks. If an enemy is hit by either the projectile or the sword itself, the player will gain 5 [[immunity frames]].

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, the helix's range is boosted by 300%. The player's life regeneration is also increased by 1 for 5 seconds.
