The '''Staff of Necrosteocytes''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Dark Caster]]s. When used, it summons a small skeleton minion which runs along the ground to target nearby enemies. While it is moving, lingering bone cells are occasionally dropped from the skeleton.
