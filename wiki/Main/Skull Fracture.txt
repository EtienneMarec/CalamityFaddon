The '''Skull Fracture''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]]. When used, it throws out a javelin that starts off slowly then quickly accelerates to much higher speeds. The javelin ignores immunity frames, hitting more times than a usual weapon would. If the player has enough mana, they will additionally fire 3-6 light blades in an uneven spread.
