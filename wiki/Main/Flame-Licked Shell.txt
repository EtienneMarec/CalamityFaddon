The '''Flame-Licked Shell''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. It reduces movement speed and acceleration by 35%, but in return it gives a massive [[defense]] boost. Additionally, enemy damage is reflected back at them, dealing 25% of the original hit. Movement speed is increased by 90% and defense decreased by 18 for a short time after the player is harmed by any source. The covering also grants 4 seconds of immunity to [[lava]] damage.
