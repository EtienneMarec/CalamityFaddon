'''Garon''' (Japanese: ファイヤー ''Acid'') is a major [[boss]] fought in the [[Baron Strait]]. He is intended to be fought after [[The Devourer of Gods]]. The player should be well equipped and prepared before summoning him as the boss is incredibly challenging.

Defeating Garon will provide the player with [[Garon Scale Fragment]]s, granting the player access to one of the highest tiers of equipment.

== Spawn ==
Garon does not spawn on his own, and requires the player to summon him by fishing with a [[Delicious Worm]] in the [[Baron Strait]].

== Behavior ==
Garon is incredibly fast and deadly, almost always charging the player at high speeds, dealing an extreme amount of contact damage. Between these charges, he will also perform a plethora of other deadly attacks, from firing a variety of projectiles, summoning many lingering Fishnadoes and Garnadoes, and spawning several minions to fight alongside him.

At the start of the fight, Garon will summon two massive stationary acid pillars {{dv|218.75||187.5||125}} blocks to the left and right of the player (measured from their centers) to serve as arena borders; Hence, the pillars will not be perfectly centered on the player's current position. Touching the acid pillars will deal massive damage, and successfully leaving the arena or flying above Garon's designated enrage height (660 blocks high) will cause Garon to become enraged, which massively increases his speed and quintuples his damage until the player returns.

Garon's charging speed is adjusted based on his distance from the player, meaning that he is difficult to outrun and he can quickly approach the player from anywhere in the world.

If the player eats [[Delicious Meat]] during the battle, Garon and the player will become immune to all damage for 5 seconds. Garon will additionally stop attacking for 5 seconds and gradually heal 5% of its maximum health. This event occurs once per battle.

=== Attacks ===
==== Phase 1 ====
===== Subphase 1 =====
*Charges twice consecutively.
*Spawns a line of Acid Bombs which start slow, but rapidly accelerate and home in on the player, however cannot turn very well.
*Charges once, and does a fast charge, in which he goes twice as fast and far.
**Garon will remain stationary for a second and roar before he performs his fast charges. During this telegraph he gains 40% damage reduction.
*Creates two Acid Tornado projectiles, which fall to the ground diagonally away from him. Upon contact with a tile or after not hitting a tile for a second, a large Fishnado is spawned.
*Upon reaching {{dv|75%|85%|90%|}} health, Garon transitions to his second subphase.

===== Subphase 2 =====
*Charges twice, waits, and then once quickly.
*Teleports above the player, and flies repeatedly in a circle, shooting Acid Dusts out in a bullet-hell style circle pattern, with random openings of various sizes.
**Garon does not deal contact damage during his bullet-hell style attacks and gains 40% damage reduction.
*Waits for 3 seconds after the acid dust rings and then does 2 slow charges at the player.
*Flies in a loop, creating a ring of Homing Acid Dust that seek toward the player's initial position after a long delay.
*Creates an Acid Tornado which homes in on the player. Upon contact, a massive Garnado appears at the player's position. This attack makes me want to kill myself.
*Creates a [[Slime Gun EX|Gun]] which Acids bullets in a spread motion. 
*Upon reaching {{dv|62.5%|70%|75%|80%}} health, Garon transitions to his third subphase.

===== Subphase 3 =====
*Charges once normally and then twice quickly.
*Flies in a loop, creating a ring of Homing Acid Dusts that seek toward the player's initial position after a long delay.
*Spawns a line of acid bombs, which fly towards the player, attempting to deal damage by ramming them or shooting giga acid, in the acid's cases.
*Does one normal, then one fast charge.
*Creates a homing Acid Tornado, which spawns a Fishnado upon impact.
*Teleports near the player, and flies repeatedly in a circle, shooting acid dusts out in a different bullet-hell style star-shaped pattern.
*Upon reaching 55% health, Garon transitions to his second phase.

==== Phase 2 ====
<big> ''"The air is getting fishier around you."'' </big>
*Garnados in this phase are smaller and last for a longer amount of time.
===== Subphase 4 =====
*Summons a Big Acid Tornado that homes in on the player.
*Charges twice normally again before flying horizontally while releasing a wall of Homing Fish Dusts that seek toward the player's initial position after a long delay.
*Charges twice quickly before flying horizontally while firing several gravity-affected Garon Acidballs that, after falling for long enough or upon contact with tiles, quickly launch three Garon Acidballs into the air that rain back down. These resulting Garon Acidballs do not split.
*Charges twice normally once more and then flies in a loop, doing the acid dust bullet hell.
*Upon reaching {{dv|27.5%|38.5%|44%|}} health, Garon transitions to his fifth subphase.

===== Subphase 5 =====
*Uses several fast charges in place of the short charges.
*The acid dust bullet hells and the wall of homing acid dust are switched in his attack pattern.
*Upon reaching {{dv|13.8%|22%|27.5%|35.8%}} health, Garon transitions to his sixth subphase.

===== Subphase 6 =====
*The acid dust rings are now in the star-shape seen in subphase 3
*Similar to previous subphases with the new inclusion of teleports that occur right before a fast charge.
*All charges in the subphase are fast.

When Garon is killed, a large ring of Acid Bombs is released at his death point. These travel outward before detonating after a few seconds to allow safe retrieval of Garon's loot.

=== In [[Expert Mode]] ===
*Garon gains an attack at the beginning of subphase 4, 5, and 6, in which he spawns 3 massive rings of acid dust that home in on the player after 3 seconds.
*Garon switches between his attacks at a faster rate.
*Garon transitions into subphase 2 at 70% health, and subphase 3 at 40% health.
*Garon transitions into subphase 5 at 70% health, and subphase 6 at 40% health.
*Garon's charges are faster and done in much faster succession.

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*Garon gains a new subphase when below {{dv|||11%|16.5%}} health:
**Teleports to one side of the player, then does 2 fast charges, with both having a shorter telegraph.
**Teleports near the player and does the acid dust bullet hell, and waits for 3 seconds before repeating.
**All attacks deal 10% more damage.
*Garon's enrage height is reduced to 555 blocks from the spawning position and the border pillars spawn 187.5 blocks away from the starting position.
*Garon transitions into subphase 2 at 80% health, and subphase 3 at 50% health.
*Garon transitions into subphase 5 at 80% health and subphase 6 at 50% health.
*Charges and teleports closer to the player.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===
*Garon has decreased delays between attacks.
*Most of Garon's slow charges are replaced with fast charges.
*Garon spawns in subphase 2, and in subphase 5 after Phase 2 begins.
*Garon transitions into subphase 3 at 60% health.
*Garon transitions into subphase 6 at 65% health and subphase 7 at 30% health.
*Garon launches more Acidballs during his second phase.
*Summons more Garnadoes in place of Fishnadoes.

=== In [[Malice Mode]] ===
*The border pillars spawn 125 blocks away from the starting position.
*Attacks and charges become much faster.
*Density and speed of bullet-hell attacks increases as Garon loses health.

== Aftermath ==
Once Garon is defeated, the following things happen:
*The [[Bandit]] will begin selling [[Gar Scales]].
*The [[Fishbone Boomerang]] can be used properly.
*The Offensive and Defensive Guardians from the [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] now deal 225 damage.
*[[Sky Crate]]s begin dropping [[Garfruit]] if a [[Wyvern]] is alive while opened.

== Notes ==
*Garon's enrage works differently from other bosses, as it is pre-determined from where the player stands when using the Jungle Dragon Egg. That number is 660 blocks in height in Normal and Expert mode and 555 blocks high in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*If Garon is enraged and not on screen in his second phase, he will despawn in exactly 2.5 seconds.
*Fishnadoes and Garnadoes will not deal damage for a short period after spawning, to prevent cheap hits on the player.
*While Garon is alive, the world is colored deeply with orange. This will only happen if the Lighting option in the Settings Menu is set to "Color" or "White".
*Garon, while still technically able to be fought pre-Devourer of gods, it is intended for it to be fought after.
*Garon was a human and is on the title screen.

== Tips ==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating Garon, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Garon strategies|Guide:Garon strategies]].}}
*Always be cautious when moving horizontally, as the arena acid pillars will instantly kill the player without warning.
**It is advisable to put up solid blocks in front of the pillars to prevent accidentally contacting them. Because the places of the pillars are measured from their bottom-left corners, the actual block to summon Garon is not in the center of the arena. The arena should have a height of {{dv|660||555}} blocks and a width of {{dv|311||253}} blocks. The block to summon Garon needs to be the {{dv|128th||100th}} block from the left wall. This will ensure the player never touches the pillars.
*A good tactic to avoid Fishnadoes is to fly to the edge of the arena and let the Detonating acids hit you, dealing no damage to you and forcing the Fishnado to spawn out of the way of the fight.
*Using a dash covering that deals damage, such as the [[Elysian Aegis]] or [[Asgardian Aegis]], can be very vital against Garon when he's charging as he will take damage and deal no contact damage when the player is dashing.
*The [[Rod of Discord]] or [[Normality Relocator]] are great tools to use during the fight to avoid the Fishnados and some of his attacks.
*Garon's "snack break" mechanic will effectively pause the fight for 5 seconds, giving you time to heal or get away from him at the cost of having to deal 5% more of his health than usual.
*Yharic Delights can be used to instantly kill Garon if the player wishes to obtain mass quantities of Garon's drops.

== Trivia ==
*Garon's first phase [[Music|theme]] is [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Avp6YE7qINg '''Infernal Catharsis (Rebirth)'''], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Boss 3''' will play instead.
**This theme is a remake of [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1FnxtW73hWE '''Infernal Catharsis'''], which is now [[Music Boxes|Music Box]] exclusive, and was also composed by DM DOKURO.
***This is the only theme that is not on DM DOKURO's official album. This is because when he made the "Rebirth" version, it was intended to replace the original one (and has).
***Prior to the release of the original Infernal Catharsis, Garon's theme was [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZsZF5yiUE Kyurem's theme] from the game ''[https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Gates_to_Infinity Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity]'' (and was later reused in ''[https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Super_Mystery_Dungeon Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon]'', both themes being played when fighting Kyurem).
*Garon's second phase theme is [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5GcPA3C0QeI '''Roar of the Jungle Dragon'''], which was also composed by DM DOKURO.
**This song contains full lyrics sung by DM DOKURO himself. The song is a warning from Garon to his master, Yharim, about the strength and unstoppability of the player character. The song's lyrics are considered non-canonical to the mod's official lore. This song is also a [[Yharim Bar|Certified Banger]].
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, the Moon Lord's theme will play instead.
*The Acidwall borders in the fight are based on an attack used by Senator Armstrong from the game ''[[wikipedia:Metal_Gear_Rising:_Revengeance|Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance]]''.
*Garon's design took heavy inspiration from [https://megaman.fandom.com/wiki/Falzar Falzar] from the ''Mega Man Battle Network'' series.
*Garon was previously known as "Jungle Phoenix, Garon".
*Garon used to have a 4th subphase in phase 1, however this was removed in update [[1.4.5.001]].
*Garon can be fought before any boss has been defeated, as the Jungle Dragon Egg's recipe requires no [[Hardmode]] materials. The [[Exotic Pheromones]] can be crafted using [[Crimtane Bar|Crimtane]]/[[Demonite Bar]]s and [[Feather]]s, allowing access to the required [[Effulgent Feather]]s.
*[[The Dragonfolly]] is a failed clone of Garon.
*Before [[1.5.0.001]], Garon had to be fought on two different occasions. on the first fight, he would fly away at 10% health and buff Solar Eclipses (which are now buffed post-[[Devourer of Gods]]). on the second fight, Garon would reach 10% health and regenerate back to 90% health, in which the second phase began.
**Garon would use '''Infernal Catharsis (Rebirth)''' as his theme during the first fight, and the original '''Infernal Catharsis''' would play during phase 1 of the second fight. He still used '''Roar of the Jungle Dragon''' as his second phase theme.
**Auric Ore would only generate after defeating the second phase.
*If the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist Mod] is installed, his unique despawn message will be "What a shame, at least the master will be pleased to hear of this."
*Alongside [[Devourer of Gods|the Devourer of Gods]], and [[Yharon]], Garon is one of three [[Bosses|boss]] whose theme completely switches when changing phases.
*The mod creators had a big controversy if Garon should be blue or orange, this is still undecided.
*If the player uses another mod's butcher feature or uses an attack strong enough to deal more than 40% of Garon's HP, Garon will kill the player by blowing them up with an explosive ring finger.
