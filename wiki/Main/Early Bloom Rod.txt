The '''Early Bloom Rod''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[fishing rod]]. When swung, six fishing lines are deployed at once instead of one. In addition to this, fishing lines will not break in a similar manner to equipping the [[High Test Fishing Line]].

== Trivia ==
*This item's tooltip is a parody of the common English expression "The early bird catches the worm."
*The name "Early Bloom" may be a mispronunciation of [[Uelibloom Bar|Uelibloom]], the material that the rod is made out of.
*Due to firing 6 lines at once, the Early Bloom Rod can be used to summon multiple [[Duke Fishron|Duke Fishrons]] or [[Old Duke|Old Dukes]] at once. It will use one [[Truffle Worm]] per Duke Fishron spawned, and one [[Bloodworm]] per Old Duke spawned.
