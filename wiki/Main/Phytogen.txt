'''Phytogen''' (Japanese: フィトジェン ''Fitojen'') is a [[Hardmode]] [[boss]] that is fought in the [[Underground Jungle]], and part of the [[Elemental route]]. It is also fought during [[Genesis (event)|Genesis]].

Defeating Phytogen will drop the [[Soul of Phytogen]], which must be sold to the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest.

==Spawn==
Phytogen can be summoned manually and early by shaking [[tree]]s in the Underground Jungle, but can also spawn naturally when there are at least 3 [[Plague]]-themed enemies on screen. Phytogen will target these enemies and remain neutral towards the player, until it takes damage.

==Behavior==
Phytogen has 5 main phases similar to [[Cryogen]]. Leaving the [[Underground Jungle]] and moving to the surface will enrage Phytogen while it is mobile, however every 20 seconds a special interaction has a 0.1% chance of occurring where [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] will swoop down and eat Phytogen, ending the fight instantly.

===Attacks===
====1st Phase====
*Phytogen roots itself using vine arms which clutch on to the nearest blocks. These roots must be targeted to deal damage to Phytogen and also have their own contact damage.
*Petals will revolve around Phytogen, filling the area with an aromatic mist. This mist serves as a visual indicator of [[Jungle]] enemy spawn rates increasing heavily.
*Sap blobs will be fired at the player, inflicting [[Oozed]] for 5 seconds as well as [[Poisoned]].

====2nd Phase====
*Phytogen is freed and starts pursuing the player. The shield also forms completely around Phytogen, which must be defeated to damage Phytogen directly.
*While the shield is being attacked, bug-themed enemies such as [[Derpling]]s as well as [[Jungle Bat]]s will spawn.
*Directly attacking the core itself will have Phytogen fire more of the aforementioned Sap blobs.

====3rd Phase====
*Phytogen's shield is thinner, allowing it to burrow through the ground and gain movement similar to [[Giant Worm]]s. This movement only occurs while Phytogen is burrowed.

====4th Phase====
*The shield reassembles around the player and Phytogen this time, forming a small arena. It maintains the same effects from previous phases.
*Phytogen permanently burrows and attempts to heal health, all while enveloping itself in bees. If it heals enough, it will revert to a previous phase.

===In [[Expert Mode]]===
*Attacks now inflict [[Venom]] and [[Armor Crunch]].

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===
*Phytogen and its projectiles are slightly faster.
*Phytogen's shield will now aggressively ram the player during downtime after 25% health.
*Phytogen and its projectiles are even more aggressive and faster.

===In [[Death Mode]]===
*Now teleports.

==Trivia ==
*Phytogen's [[Music|theme]] is '''Botanic Reinforcement''', which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh].
