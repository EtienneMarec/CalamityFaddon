The Fleshly Horn is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] item used to [[Summoning items|summon]] the [[Destroyer of Sanity]] superboss. It can be used in [[The Crimson]]/[[The Corruption]], and is not consumed upon use.

After the [[Wall of Flesh]] is defeated, if the Destroyer of Sanity has not been defeated yet, its sprites begins rotting away and becomes unusable and uncraftable, therefore locking off the Destroyer of Sanity.
