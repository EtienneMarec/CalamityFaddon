The '''Infernal Rift''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] that is an upgrade to the [[Sky Fracture]]. It shoots out four red tridents, which, after striking a target, summon more red tridents from off-screen that fly straight towards the hit target. Upon contact with any of these projectiles, the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] is inflicted.

The Infernal Rift has a base critical strike chance of 29%, as opposed to the usual 4% of weapons.
