'''The Swarmer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] and is the upgrade to both the [[Bee Gun]] and the [[Wasp Gun]]. Like its predecessors, it rapidly fires homing bees and wasps that dissipate upon damaging enemies and touching tiles.
