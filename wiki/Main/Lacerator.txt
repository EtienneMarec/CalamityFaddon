The '''Lacerator''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Yoyos|yoyo]] made from [[Bloodstone Core]]s. It grants +2 life regeneration upon hitting an enemy which resets after being hit by an enemy, or after 60 seconds.

== Notes ==
*The Lacerator moves extremely quickly compared to other yoyos, allowing it to more easily track fast enemies.

== Trivia ==
*The Lacerator is the only yoyo added by the Calamity Mod that does not have projectiles.
*Previously, the Lacerator had been renamed to "Not Viable" after getting nerfed in [[1.2.2.001]]. However, after a buff in [[1.2.3.005]] it was changed to "Viable?", and it was changed back to "Lacerator" in [[1.2.3.006]].
**Additionally in previous versions of the mod, the Lacerator had an invisible aura which would rapidly damage enemies within it, similar to the current ability of [[The Microwave]].
*The second tooltip and the name change to "Not Viable" are both a reference to the yoyo being nerfed to be ineffective against [[The Devourer of Gods]].
