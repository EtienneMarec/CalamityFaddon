The '''Strange Meteorite''' is a background tile that can fall from the sky in the [[Astral Infection]] at night. It can be broken to spawn the [[Astro Slime]].

== Notes ==
*The Strange Meteorite will deal 9999 damage to any enemies that are hit by it during its descent.
*It will not spawn during bossfights.

== Trivia ==
*The Strange Meteorite is technically not a furniture item, as it does not fit onto the tile grid, and hovers slightly above the ground.
