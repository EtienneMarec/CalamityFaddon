The '''Alula Australis''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] that drops from [[Astrum Aureus]]. It fires an aurora beam that travels in a cosine pattern. As the beam travels, Astral stars trail the beam and rain down from the sky. Astral stars' damage and knockback are randomized from 25% to 75% of their base values. Both the aurora beam and the astral stars inflict the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]].

== Trivia ==
*Alula Australis is an alternate name given to the star system "[[wikipedia:Xi Ursae Majoris|Xi Ursae Majoris]]", which was the first [[wikipedia:binary star|binary star]] system ever discovered.
