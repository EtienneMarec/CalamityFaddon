The '''2.0.1.001''' (or '''2.0.1.1''') update reworked several early-game item sets, including Wulfrum, Desert Prowler, and Marnite, as well as overhauled how Calamity difficulties are toggled, and removed Malice Mode from the game. Additionally, this update added several [[Donor items]].

This update also rebalanced several boss AIs and ported the mod to tModLoader v2022.7.58.

*Added [[Ares' Exoskeleton]], [[Crackshot Colt]], [[Death's Ascension]], [[Dynamic Pursuer]], [[Grave Grimreaver]], [[Hadal Urn]], [[Midas Prime]] and [[The Sevens Striker]].
*Added [[Wulfrum Lure]].

*Added [[Wulfrum Acrobatics Pack]], [[Wulfrum Digging Turtle]], [[Wulfrum Scaffold Kit]], [[Wulfrum Treasure Pinger]].
*Added [[XP-00 Hypnos]], [[XP-00 Hypnos Aergia Neuron]], and [[XP-00 Hypnos Plug]].
*Added [[Ares' Cannons]] buff.
*Added three custom map backgrounds for the Abyss layers.

=== Reworks ===
*Reworked the [[Abandoned Wulfrum Helmet]], it no longer grants any bonuses when equipped. Instead, it now functions as a vanity covering that, while equipped, transforms the player into a Wulfrum Robot. Additionally, it can also be worn in the head slot, which will not cause the player to transform. It is no longer considered a part of the Wulfrum armor set.
*Overhauled the [[Difficulty Indicator]], it now serves as the only way to enable [[Revengeance Mode]] and [[Death Mode]] on a world. The Difficulty Indicator will glow until it is pressed the first time. When pressed, the indicator will create a dropdown menu with three options between the world's vanilla difficulty, Revengeance Mode, and Death Mode. Each option will have a corresponding icon, which can be hovered over with the mouse cursor for more information. Additionally, holding down the {{key|Left Shift}} key will expand the descriptions even further. Additionally, there is a fourth option, indicated with the now removed Malice Mode icon, that explains that Malice Mode was removed from the game. Selecting one of the three primary options will apply the difficulty and close the menu. The menu can also be closed by closing the inventory screen. Enabling or disabling Revengeance Mode and Death Mode is prohibited while a boss is alive or if the world is on Classic Mode.
*Reworked the [[Exoblade]], it no longer fires an exo beam and summons exo comets to protect the player. It now has a custom swing animation, and when swung, it now fires out three exo energy beams that home onto enemies after a short duration. When an exo energy beam damages a target, it will summon two additional light beams that slash the enemy once. It also now has a right-click function that causes the player to quickly lunge forward and fly through the air. The Exoblade does 175% more damage while lunging. Colliding with an enemy while lunging will cause the lunge to prematurely end, powering up the Exo Blade, which causes its next swing to increase the sword size by 230%, and if the sword damages an enemy, it will create a power explosion that deals damage to nearby enemies. Nerfed the Exoblade's base damage from 900 to 640. Exoblade now uses the Melee No Speed damage type and the sword itself no longer inflicts any debuffs, however, its projectiles still inflict the Exo Freeze, Frostburn, Holy Flames, and On Fire! debuffs.
*Reworked the [[Desert Prowler armor]] set bonus, it no longer causes ranged damage critical hits to occasionally summon a sandstorm. It now grants the player the ability to shroud themselves in a sand cloud. While the sand cloud is active, the player's movement speed is increased by 150%, and dealing damage to enemies will decrease the duration of the sand cloud by 1.5 seconds. Dealing damage within a second of activating the sand cloud will cause your next ranged weapon to automatically critically hit, and have a critical strike damage modifier of 200%. The sand cloak has a base duration of 5 seconds, and has a cooldown of 20 seconds.
*Reworked the [[Marnite Obliterator]], it no longer functions as a hybrid axe and drill. It now functions akin to the [[Laser Drill]], casting a diamond beam that destroys blocks at the mouse cursor. Additionally, the Marnite Obliterator now has a bonus +7 tile range. Nerfed the Oblitrator's damage from 11 to 7.
*Reworked the [[Momentum Capacitor]], it no longer consumes stealth when pressing {{key|Momentum Capacitor Effect}} to generate a magnetic field that amplifies Spy projectile velocities. Instead, it now rapidly increases the player's movement speed, bypassing all speed caps, so long as the Momentum Capacitor is being left-clicked by the player. <!-- maximum player speed is +580% -->
*Reworked the [[Rover Drive]], it no longer periodically generates an energy shield around the player that increases their defense by 15 for 10 seconds. It now generates a protective matrix that absorbs 50 damage and increases the player's defense by 10. The protective matrix lasts indefinitely unless it is un-equipped or until the matrix is broken by damage. The protective matrix has a 10 second cooldown, and taking direct damage while the matrix is on cooldown will reset the cooldown to 10 seconds. Additionally, it can now be used in the [[Extractinator]].
*Reworked [[Wulfrum armor]]:
**The Wulfrum Jacket now grants the player 5% damage reduction when worn instead of 3% increased critical strike chance.
**The Wulfrum armor set bonus no longer grants various effects when equipped with different Wulfrum head pieces. It now always grants +1 minion slots and allows the player to transform into a Wulfrum Bastion if the player presses {{key|Down}} twice and there is at least 1 Wulfrum Metal Scrap in the player's inventory. When transformed, the player can not swap armor, coverings, or use any other items except a Grapple Hook and any buff potions in the player's inventory with a keybind. During the transformation, the player can only fire a prosthetic fusion cannon that shoots pellet projectiles in a burst of three. The Wulfrum Bastion transformation also grants the player an additional 13 defense, and has a base 30 second duration. After the transformation has ended, it will go on cooldown for 20 seconds. If the player takes damage during the transformation, the duration of the transformation will be slightly decreased.
*Reworked the [[Wulfrum Battery]], in addition to increasing the player's summon damage by 7%, it now causes all minions summoned to emit light, and adds a 50% chance for wulfrum enemies to drop an extra Wulfrum Metal Scrap when killed. Additionally, it can now be used in the Extractinator.
*Reworked the [[Wulfrum Blunderbuss]], it no longer functions like a bow and now functions as a gun that uses [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]] or Silver Coins as ammunition. When used, it will consume one Wulfrum Metal Scrap or Silver Coin and gain 30 charges. Each time the blunderbuss is fired, it consumes one charge. When fired, it shoots a large spread of bullets; the bullet spread can be tightened by moving the cursor further away from the player, however, the damage of each shot will be decreased by up to 25% depending on the distance away the cursor is from the player. Increased its use time from 25 to 55, and removed its auto reuse.
*Reworked the [[Wulfrum Controller]], the Wulfrum Droid minion now recoils after firing its green projectile at enemies, and can no longer fire at enemies through walls. Additionally, it now has a right-click function that causes it to become supercharged. While supercharged, any Wulfrum Droids active return to the player and fire a beam at them, increasing the player's defense and life regeneration by 3. This bonus is additive with each Wulfrum Droid active. Buffed the Wulfrum Controller's base damage from 10 to 16.
*Reworked the [[Wulfrum Drill]], it now functions like a drill instead of a pickaxe. Decreased its pickaxe power from 40% to 35%.
*Reworked the [[Wulfrum Knife]], it now throws out knives in a burst of three when used. The second and third knives have spread out slightly as they travel. Its stealth strike now causes each knife to damage an enemy twice, for a total of six hits if all of the knives hit an enemy. It now also has a right-click function that pulls Wulfrum Knives that are not broken on hit back towards the player. <!-- still doesn’t pierce, use is clockwork: usetime of 4, animation of 10, delay of 24 -->
*Reworked [[Wulfrum Prosthesis]], it no longer functions as a wand that casts a slow green bolt towards enemies. It now replaces the player's arm with a prosthetic that fires out a fast moving homing wulfrum bolt that damages enemies and does not pierce. It now also has a right-click function that drains a nearby enemy of their energy, replenishing the player's mana at a rate of 35/s.
*Reworked [[Wulfrum Screwdriver]], it no longer functions as a broadsword. It now functions as a shortsword, causing the player to stab in the general direction of their cursor with a screwdriver. Each time an enemy is damaged with the screwdriver, there is a 20% chance that a wulfrum screw will become available above the player's head, which is also indicated by a sound cue. Using right-click with a screw available will cause the player to throw out the screw in the direction the player is looking, damaging an enemy before breaking. If the player hits the screw mid-air with the screwdriver, it will be launched in the direction of the player's cursor, septupling the screw's damage. Only one screw can be available at a time. Additionally, damaging an enemy with the Screwdriver will reset its animation, making repeated hits come out quicker.
