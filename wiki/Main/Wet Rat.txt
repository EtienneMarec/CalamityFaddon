'''Wet Rat''' is a Hardmode [[NPC]] vendor that can be found in the 4th layer of the [[Abyss]] after [[The Forbidden Lantern]] has been defeated. 
After being found, Wet Rat will settle in an empty house if there is one. Wet Rat can be housed with another NPC, similarly to a town pet.

When threatened by [[enemies]], it will defend himself with [[The Watered Pew Pew]].
