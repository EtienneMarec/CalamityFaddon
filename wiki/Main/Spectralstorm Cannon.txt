The '''Spectralstorm Cannon''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] and an upgrade of the [[Firestorm Cannon]]. It inaccurately and rapidly fires three sets of [[flare]]s and homing lost souls. It consumes flares as [[ammunition]] and has a 70% chance of not consuming flares.
