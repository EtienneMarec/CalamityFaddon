The '''Endentant Bow''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[Bows|bow]] that is crafted from [[Waterfreeze Reactive Essence]]s, Once the shot arrow hits a target, it summons 3 more arrows from behind the enemy, the cycle continues until none of the arrows hit the enemy. The cloned arrows dissapear once the player shoots a new arrow, to avoid lag.

== Tips ==
While the arrows clone themselves, it is better to also use another weapon, so the cloned arrows dont just dissapear.
