'''Snowgrave''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] sold by [[The General]] NPC for {{gc|80}}. When used it slowly charges up a snowflake sigil below the player's cursor. After 3 seconds, a colossal fountain of snow and ice blows upwards, instantly killing any normal enemies that touch it, and dealing devastating damage to bosses. The fountain lasts for 10 seconds and then goes on cooldown for a minute.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Trivia ==
*Snowgrave is a direct reference to a spell of the same name from ''[[wikipedia:Deltarune|Deltarune]]'', with the fountain having identical visuals to Deltarune's version.
**The spell costing 200 mana is a reference to how Snowgrave from Deltarune takes 200 TP.
*The cooldown is a reference to the scrolls from the AlchemistNPC Mod, which have a similar cooldown mechanic.
