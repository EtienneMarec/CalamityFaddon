The '''1.1.5.04''' update contained a few changes and fixes.

== Changes ==
*Nerfed the damage for weapons dropped by [[The Hive Mind]] and [[The Perforators]].
*[[Brain of Cthulhu]] no longer drops [[Blood Sample]]s.
*[[Eater of Worlds]] no longer drops [[True Shadow Scale]]s.

=== Fixes ===
*[[Galactica Singularity]] is no longer equippable.
*Fixed [[Dried Seeker]]s spawning tiles of [[Water]] when hit.
