'''Orichalcum Spiked Gemstones''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[daggers]] crafted from [[Orichalcum Bar]]s. They fire a pink colored knife, which travel a short distance before being affected by gravity. However, once it reaches the ground, it will roll further until it hits a wall or stops. The knives pierce up to 5 times before dissipating on the sixth hit. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with an Orichalcum Spiked Gemstone will cause the projectile to pierce infinitely, last longer and spawn fast moving petals to fly from the side of the screen on each hit.
