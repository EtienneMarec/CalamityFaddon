The '''Coldant Firestarter''' is a craftable post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]]. It shoots light blue flames that deal great damage due to the very high fire rate. Any enemy hit will be inflicted with the [[Evercold]] debuff. If the player is holding Right click the flames will begin to circle around the player, giving it 40 additional defence.
