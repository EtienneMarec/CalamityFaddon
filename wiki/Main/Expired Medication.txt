The [[Expired Medication]] is a [[Death Mode]]-exclusive [[Hardmode]] permanent power-up that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] that increases the player's life by 100, as an extension to [[Life Fruit|Life Fruits]]. Additionally, it reduces life regen by 3, and increases healing potion cooldown by 25%.

== Notes ==
*The border that it grants are present no matter the appearance, as it is overlayed on top of the hearts.
