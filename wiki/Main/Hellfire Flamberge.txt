The '''Hellfire Flamberge''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. On each swing, it fires out two small fireballs and one large fireball at slightly randomized angles and speeds. Small volcanic fireballs deal 50% of the weapon's damage and large volcanic fireballs deal 75% of the weapon's damage, both additionally inflicting the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]. The fireballs aren't affected by gravity, slow to a stop and disappear shortly after being fired, and cannot penetrate tiles nor enemies. The sword itself also inflicts On Fire! on enemy hits.
