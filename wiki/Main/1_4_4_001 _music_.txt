{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.4.4.001 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.4.4.001''' (or '''1.4.4.1''') Music mod update, also known as '''"Acid Rain"''', added music tracks for the [[Acid Rain]] event, Underground [[Astral Infection]] biome, and [[The Old Duke]].

This Music Mod version was compatible with Main mod versions [[1.4.4.001]] and [[1.4.4.002]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Music Box (Underground Astral Biome)]], [[Music Box (Acidic Downpour)]], and [[Music Box (Old Duke)]].
*Added "corrosion!", the Acid Rain music track.
*Added "Toxic Wisdom", The Old Duke music track.
*Added "Treasures Within The Abomination", the Underground Astral Infection music track.
*Added the <code>Title Screen - Toggling this requires a reload to take effect</code> [[Configuration option]].

== Changes ==
*All [[Music Boxes]] can now be toggled on and off with [[Wire]]s.
*Decreased the base volume of the biome music tracks.
*Resprited [[Music Box (The Dragonfolly)|Music Box (Bumblebirb)]], [[Music Box (Ceaseless Void)]], [[Music Box (Cryogen)]], [[Music Box (Hive Mind)]], and [[Music Box (Storm Weaver)]].
*The "Calamity" music track will now play on the Title Screen. This change can be disabled through a configuration option.
*The "The Heaven-Sent Abomination" music track now loops properly.
*The "Unholy Insurgency" music track now loops properly during the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] fight.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug that allowed Music Boxes to recieve covering modifiers.
*Fixed an issue that caused the game to crash when reloading the mod via tModLoader.
