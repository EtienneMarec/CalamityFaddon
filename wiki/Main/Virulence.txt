The '''Virulence''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings and is dropped from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. Upon swinging the sword, a large green cloud is fired in the direction of the player's mouse that deals 85% of the weapon's damage, pierces indefinitely, goes through tiles, and inflicts the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] on enemy hits.
