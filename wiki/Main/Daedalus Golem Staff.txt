The '''Daedalus Golem Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a grounded golem that follows the player. When an enemy is nearby, the golem alternates between attacking with purple-blue pellets and firing red lightning bolts that pierce once.
