The '''Twinkler''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[critter]] found within the [[Astral Infection]] that acts similarly to a [[Firefly]]. Twinklers can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]] and, unlike other critters added by the Calamity Mod, may be used as [[Fishing|fishing bait]], with a bait power of 40%.
