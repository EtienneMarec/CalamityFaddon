The '''Dualpoon''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Harpoon]]. It can be obtained either through crafting or by receiving it from the [[Agent]]. It fires two harpoons that are unaffected by gravity. The harpoons travel for a short range before returning to the player.
