The '''Little Light''' is a [[Pets|light pet]] summoning item that drops from [[Storm Weaver]]. It summons a light construct to follow behind the player. While summoned, the construct provides a large amount of light to the player's surroundings. 

== Trivia ==
*Its name, appearance, and tooltip are references to the [https://destiny.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost Ghost] from [[wikipedia:Destiny (video game)|Destiny]].
