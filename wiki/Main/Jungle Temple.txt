The '''Jungle Temple''' [[biome]] features one new [[enemy]]. The Calamity Mod changes the Jungle Temple's generation to guarantee a large arena to fight the [[Golem]] in. It also adds the challenging [[Pot of Pain]] sub-area.

In [[Death Mode]], [[Wooden Spikes]] inflict [[Venom]].
