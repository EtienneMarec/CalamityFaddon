The '''Crumbling Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Armor Crumbling''' [[buff]], causing all melee and [[Spy]] attacks to inflict the [[Armor Crunch]] [[debuff]] as well as increases melee and Spy critical strike chance by 5%.
