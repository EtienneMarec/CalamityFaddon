'''Lumenyl''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that grows on blocks or is dropped by the [[enemies]] in the [[Abyss]] after [[Calamitas]] has been defeated. It is used in crafting many aquatic items, typically in conjunction with [[Depth Cells]] and [[Tenebris]].

Similarly to [[Crystal Shard]]s, they can also be placed on blocks and walls.

== Notes ==
*Lumenyl may also be obtained from [[Abyssal Crate]]s after [[Calamitas]] has been defeated.
*After Calamitas has been defeated, Lumenyl will begin growing on voidstone in the lower portions of the abyss being an effective way to farm large amounts of it.

== Trivia ==
*Its name was initially proposed as "Lumenite", and is still referred to as such in the mod's code. It was likely changed in-game to avoid confusion with [[Luminite]].
