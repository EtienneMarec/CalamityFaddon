'''Cobalt Kunais''' are craftable consumable [[Hardmode]] [[daggers]] made from [[Cobalt Bar]]s. They can pierce twice and travel a short distance before being affected by gravity. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with a Cobalt Kunai causes three homing cobalt energy bolts to be released instead, that ignore gravity and can pierce twice. The bolts deal 140% damage each.
