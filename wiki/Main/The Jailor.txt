'''The Jailor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XF-09 Ares]] has been defeated last.  It fires an infinitely piercing electric mine that decelerates to a stop after as it travels. The mine detects other mines in a 75-tile radius and forms a damaging electric chain between them. On hit, the damage of all mines and electric chains that are connected are exponentially decreased, to a lower bound of 42.5% of its original damage.
