The '''Sand Sharknado Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Forgotten Apex Wand]]. Upon use, it summons a sandnado to fight for you by firing miniature high-velocity [[Sand Shark]]s at nearby enemies.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Trivia ==
*This item, like the vanilla [[Tempest Staff]], is a reference to the 2013 movie ''[[wikipedia:Sharknado|Sharknado]]''.
