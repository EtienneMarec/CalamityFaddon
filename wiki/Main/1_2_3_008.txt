The '''1.2.3.008''' update added a new boss known as [[THE LORDE]] and made some adjustments to the [[Siren and Leviathan]] fight.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added THE LORDE.
*Added [[NO]]
*Added [[NO U]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] ===
==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Buffed laser damage from {{dv|66|108|135|162}} to {{dv|94|140|175|210}}.
*Mines now last shorter.
*Buffed [[Astrum Deus Probe]]'s laser damage from {{dv|96|160|200|240}} to {{dv|130|200|250|300}}.

==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*Now spawns less mini [[Draconic Swarmer|Bumblebirb]]s.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Phase 1's body segments now reduce infinitely piercing projectile damage by 60% instead of 40%
*Phase 2's body segments now reduce infinitely piercing projectile damage by 55% instead of 50%.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Nerfed [[Hive Blob]]'s health from {{dv|100|170}} to {{dv|50|140}}.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Now drops {{dv|5-9|6-11}} [[Ruinous Soul]]s instead of {{dv|12-19|15-21}}.
*[[Phantom]]s now spawn phantom mines.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Buffed the defensive variant of A Dunkin' Donut's health from {{dv|20,000|28,000}} to {{dv|25,000|35,000}}
*Buffed A Dunkin' Donut's spear damage from {{dv|108|200|250|300}} to {{dv|160|260|325|390}}.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*Buffed The Leviathan's health from {{dv|42,000|67,200|72,000|84,800}} to {{dv|52,000|83,200|88,000|104,000}}, her contact damage from {{dv|80|128|160|192}} to {{dv|100|160|200|240}}, and her meteor damage from {{dv|110|140|175|210}} to {{dv|150|220|275|330}}.
*Nerfed The Siren's health from {{dv|30,000|48,000|51,200|64,000}} to {{dv|21,000|33,600|40,000|48,000}}, and buffed her contact damage from {{dv|60|96|120|144}} to {{dv|80|128|160|192}}.
*Nerfed [[Ice Shield]]'s health from {{dv|1500|1800}} to {{dv|1000|1200}}, and buffed its damage from 20 to 80.
*Buffed [[Siren Clone]]'s water spear damage from {{dv|68|112|140|168}} to {{dv|96|140|175|225}}.
*Buffed [[Aquatic Aberration]]'s damage from {{dv|80|112|140|168}} to {{dv|100|140|175|225}}.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Now inflicts NO U in [[Death Mode]].

=== [[Enemies]] ===
*[[BOHLDOHR]]s now spawn 2x more often and can now drop NO.
*Buffed [[Parasea]]'s health from {{dv|500|700}} to {{dv|1000|1400}}, and its damage from {{dv|60|84|105|126}} to {{dv|90|126|157|189}}.

=== Items ===
*[[Abombination]] now uses 5 [[Iron Bar]]/[[Lead Bar]]s and 3 [[Obsidian]] instead of 3 Iron or Lead Bars and 10 Obsidian.
*Reduced the size of [[Alluvion]] and [[Monsoon]]'s tornadoes.
*[[Draedon's Heart]] now uses 30 [[Luminite Bar]]s in its recipe.
*Reduced the amount of time [[Elemental Quiver]]'s split projectiles last for.
*Buffed [[Exoblade]]'s damage from 720 to 800.
*[[Magnetic Meltdown]]'s projectiles now last shorter.
*Nerfed [[Omniblade]]'s damage from 90 to 80.
*Nerfed [[Spear of Paleolith]]'s damage from 52 to 50.
*Removed [[Storm Dragoon]]'s purple and blue projectiles.
*[[Vanquisher Arrow]]s now pierce through tiles and spawn homing arrows instead of having one arrow that does both.
*Buffed Vanquisher Arrow damage from 24 to 32.
*[[Voltaic Climax]]'s projectiles now last shorter.
*The Sons of Yharon spawned from [[Yharon's Kindle Staff]] no longer shoot homing flares at enemies.

=== Other ===
*The [[Dryad]] now sells [[Bloody Worm Tooth]] after [[The Hive Mind]] has been defeated, and [[Rotten Brain]] after [[The Perforators]] have been defeated.
*[[Meteorite]] can no longer be blown up before [[Eater of Worlds]] or [[Brain of Cthulhu]] has been defeated.
*Reduced the dust created by many projectiles.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Cloud Elemental]].
*Resprited [[Flarewing Bow]], [[Hellwing Staff]], [[Magna Cannon]].

=== Fixes ===
*Fixed a sheeting error on the [[Aureate Booster]].
*Fixed a bug involving music conflicts with the [[Profaned Crags]] and the [[Brimstone Elemental]].
*Fixed several issues that would cause bosses to despawn while the player was still alive.
*Fixed a bug which caused the Sons of Yharon from Yharon's Kindle Staff to despawn randomly.
