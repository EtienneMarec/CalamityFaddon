The '''Brimstone Elemental''' is an [[NPC]] that can only appear by using the [[Brimstone Elemental's Soul]].

When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself with her Glaive.

== Functions ==
*The Brimstone Elemental has only one dialogue panel option (other than just the talking dialogues) - "Soul" after what she will turn into a soul that will appear in the player's inventory.

== Names ==
*The Brimstone Elemental will always be named Helia.
