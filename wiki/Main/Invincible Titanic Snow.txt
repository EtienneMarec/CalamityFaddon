'''Invincible Titanic Snow''' is an [[unobtainable]] [[block]] encountered during daytime in the [[Stained Mountains]] biome. It is completely indestructible. It generates on the 2 Entrances to the [[Stained Mountains Arsenal Lab]]

== Notes ==
*Invincible Titanic Snow has no inventory item, existing only as a physical tile.
