'''The Storm''' is a Godseeker Mode bow that drops from the [[Storm Weaver]]. It turns [[Wooden Arrow]]s into energy bolts that rain down from the sky near the cursor. The bolts that rain down from off-screen are able to go through walls, making this useful in underground or in enclosed spaces.

== Tips ==
*Use this bow against enemies that linger near the top of the screen such as [[The Devourer of Gods]] or very bulky enemies like [[Polterghast]], as either will mitigate the inaccuracy of the bow.
*Bow-oriented rangers may want to defeat [[Storm Weaver]] prior to [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], as this bow can completely decimate the latter, specially when paired with [[Luminite_Arrow|Luminite Arrows]].
*Using this bow along with [[Vanquisher Arrow]]s can deal a decent amount of DPS as long as the player doesn't directly aim at an enemy.
