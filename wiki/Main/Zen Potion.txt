The '''Zen Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. It grants the '''Zen''' [[buff]], which reduces enemy spawn rates by 60% and maximum spawn count by 70%. It is also used in the crafting of the [[Tranquility Candle]].
