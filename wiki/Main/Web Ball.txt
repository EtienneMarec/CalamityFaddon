The '''Web Ball''' is a craftable consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws a gravity affected web-covered ball that inflicts the [[Webbed]] [[debuff]] onto enemies for one second, slowing their movement.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Web Balls causes the next ball thrown to inflict three seconds of Webbed instead of one.
