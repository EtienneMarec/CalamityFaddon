The '''Sylvan Slasher''' is a Godseeker Mode [[rogue weapon]] that is bought from the [[Bandit]] for {{pc|5}} after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated. When used, it creates a small barrage of slashes at the cursor's position that are visually similar to the [[Arkhalis]]. [[Rogue stealth]] builds up with each slash that hits an enemy, at a rate of 50 stealth per second. When striking enemies, sword waves have a chance to be released, which travel opposite to the player's direction relative to the cursor.

The weapon does not consume stealth and does not have a [[stealth strike]] effect.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Notes ==
*Stealth can only be raised from hitting enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher or when hitting bosses, as such it does not work on the [[Target Dummy]] nor [[Super Dummy]]. It also will not generate stealth when attacking enemies spawned from [[Statues]].
*The amount of stealth built per strike is 5 units of stealth, and the weapon can strike 10 times per second, giving it a stealth gain rate of 50 stealth per second.
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].
