The '''Voltaic Climax''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. Upon use, it summons a circular spread of 8 tile-ignoring magnetic orbs around the cursor. The summoned orbs rapidly fire electric beams at up to two nearby [[enemies]]. The orbs cannot target enemies through blocks.
