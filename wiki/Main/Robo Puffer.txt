The '''Robo Puffer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[grab bag]]-type [[Fishing|fish]] that can be caught in the [[Ocean]]. When opened, the player will receive one random [[Mechanical bosses|Mechanical Boss summoning item]]. If opened on the [[Get fixed boi]] seed, the player will recieve [[Ocram's Razor]].
