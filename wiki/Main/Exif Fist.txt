The '''Exif Fist''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] that fires a high-velocity projectile which leaves a particle trail behind. The projectile is slightly affected by gravity, and explodes into 4-6 stars on contact with enemies or solid blocks. The explosion ignores [[enemy]] [[immunity frames]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Exif Fist will cause the next fist thrown to bounce between enemies up to five times while raining down stars on each bounce before exploding.
