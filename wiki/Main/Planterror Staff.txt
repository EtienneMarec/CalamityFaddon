The '''Planterror Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker mode [[summon weapon]]. It summons a miniature Guntera that swaps between 3 modes of attack every 10 seconds.

*Guntera will pursue its target, firing tight spreads of 8 bullets every second.
*It will circle its target, firing a constant stream of low damage, but fast moving, bullets inwards at them.
*It will hover above the players head, firing a high damage sniper shot every 2 seconds.

Gunteras bullets will use the players top ammo type as its own. It does not consume ammo, and will not change ammo types until it is despawned, even if the player switches ammo types while it is summoned.

== Tips ==
*Players can use Gunteras inability to switch ammo to their benefit, equipping it with one bullet type while they use a different one.
*It is recommended to use rare or expensive bullet types with this weapon, as Guntera does not consume ammo.
*Despite shooting bullets, the weapon still deals summon damage.

== Trivia ==
*Guntera is a reference to the Fargos Souls mod, where it is a secret boss.
*The item was implemented because many players thought the name "Plantation Staff" was dubious.
