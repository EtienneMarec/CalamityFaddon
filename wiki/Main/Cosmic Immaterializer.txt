The '''Cosmic Immaterializer''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It summons an entity of cosmic energy that fires homing energy bolts and exploding stars at enemies. It requires 10 minion slots to summon 1 energy, and only 1 energy can be active at once.

When the energy bolts or exploding stars hit an enemy, they will inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
