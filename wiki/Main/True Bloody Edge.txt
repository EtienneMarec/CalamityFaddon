The '''True Bloody Edge''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It is a direct upgrade to the [[Bloody Edge]] and can be used to craft the [[Terra Blade]] or [[Terra Edge]]. On each swing, it fires out a yellow sword projectile that can pierce 2 targets before dissipating on the third. If an enemy is hit by the sword itself, there is a 50% chance for the player to be [[Lifesteal|healed]] for 1 to 6 hp. Both the sword and the projectile inflict the [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] and [[Burning Blood]] [[debuff]]s for 1 second.

It is an alternative to the vanilla weapon [[True Night's Edge]], having less raw damage but granting Ichor infliction and the ability to recover health.
