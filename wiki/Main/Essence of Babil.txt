'''Essence of Babil''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that drops from certain enemies related to plant-life, such as those present in the [[Jungle]] [[biome]]. It is used to craft various air-themed items.

{{dei}} Essence of Babil can also be placed.

*5-10 Essences of Babil can additionally be obtained from [[Babilfish]], and 5-15 can be obtained from [[Jungle Crate]]s at a 50% drop chance.
*The [[Falconer]] will require different amounts of essence as part of her quests. Finishing the quests will return the essence to the player in processed form.

== Trivia ==
*Essence of Babil is based on the Aero spell from the ''Final Fantasy'' series, hence the [[Aero Bolt]] item. The namesake originates from the ''Tower of Babil'' from ''Final Fantasy IV'', however the two concepts are unrelated.
**The Tower of Babil gets its name from the biblical Tower of Babel.
