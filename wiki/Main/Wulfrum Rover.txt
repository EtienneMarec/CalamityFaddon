The '''Wulfrum Rover''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime. It is also summoned by the [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Wulfrum_Excavator Wulfrum Excavator] [[boss]] in [[Expert Mode]] upon it transitioning to a new phase. It is initially stationary, but when it detects a player, it attacks by sliding towards them in order to deal contact damage. It is incapable of moving up non-slope blocks.

It can be supercharged by the [[Wulfrum Pylon]] enemy, causing its lights to turn from green to blue and gaining a temporary energy shield which increases its defense.

== Trivia ==
*The name of the enemy, as well as the covering it drops, are partially in reference to mod developer and wiki editor [[User:RoverdriveX|RoverdriveX]].
