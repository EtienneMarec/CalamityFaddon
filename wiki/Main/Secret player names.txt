Secret player names are easter eggs which give different effects based on the name the player chose on character creation. Some may benefit the player with various items, while some may make the game harder or even impossible with effects such as an [[Auric Ore]] trail. They may also change the appearance of the player.
There are currently 4 (6 {{fantag|Add-on content}}) known secret player names.

==="Jungle Jyrant Jharim" / "Jharim"===
[[File:Jharim player name preview.png|thumb|222x222px|"Jungle Jyrant Jharim" / "Jharim" in game. Note the trail of Auric Ore.]]
This player name will cause there to be a permanent trail of [[Auric Ore]] underneath the player. This also affects furniture and chests, where chests are filled with stacks of [[Auric Bar|Auric Bars]], overwriting the contents of what was previously inside. The player will spawn in with a set of [[Auric Tesla armor]] equipped in their vanity slots, which cannot be removed.

Playing with this secret name can be extremely challenging, as you will be propelled by the [[Auric Ore]] up to the Space layer.

No-hitting is impossible with this player name, due to the effect of [[Auric Ore]].

==="Crab secret"===
[[File:Crab secret player name preview.png|thumb|"Crab secret" in game. A giant Barocrab is following the player.|244x244px]]
Upon spawning in the world, an evil and unkillable version of the [[Barocrab]] will spawn in the Ocean and slowly hover towards the player. As more bosses are defeated, the [[Barocrab]] will get faster, with it reaching double the speed of the Dungeon Guardian by the time Moon Lord is defeated. If the [[Barocrab]] touches the player, the player will be permanently killed, regardless of if Hardcore is enabled or not. The player will spawn in with a [[Fungal Clump]], various [[Crabulon]] drops, and 50 [[Delicious Meat]].

In Multiplayer, there will be 1 [[Barocrab]] spawned in for every player with this name.
=== "YuH"===
[[File:YuH player name preview.png|thumb|"YuH" in game.|246x246px]]
This player name will cause the player to become an [[Aero Slime]] which has infinite flight but low health. Most movement accessories won't work, such as Hermes Boots.

Despite the low health, there's a huge advantage with infinite flight, allowing you to easily dodge most [[Bosses|boss]] attacks.
=== "Dron" ===
[[File:Dron player name preview.png|thumb|"Dron" in game. The player is only 2 tiles wide.|250x250px]]
This player name will cause your character to be missing a torso and only be 2 tiles tall. This can be helpful for mining, but your health will always be divided by 2 and you will always move and jump at ludicrous speeds. You will spawn with a random [[Miracle Matter]] weapon which will be significantly nerfed, but will gain power over time as more [[bosses]] are killed.

The player will have a 10-tile radius glow constantly.
==Secret player names added by Addons==

==="Fanny" {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} ===
[[File:Fanny player name preview.png|thumb|"Fanny" in game. The character is in the bottom right of the screen and must help Fanny the Flame beat the game by typing.|250x250px]]
Your player will permanently be in the bottom right corner of the screen, and an AI player with the appearance of Fanny will play the entirety of the mod. You have a dialogue input box, and you need to guide Fanny to beat the game. You can give helpful hints such as attack strategies, but also bad tips such as going in the dungeon before Skeletron is defeated. If you sucessfully manage to guide Fanny to beat [[Supreme Calamitas]], you will unlock a hidden [[Endings|ending]].

=== "Xeroc" {{fantag|Wrath of the Gods}}===
[[File:Xeroc player name preview 1.png|thumb|247x247px|The menu screen after making a character with the name "Xeroc". ]][[File:Xeroc player name preview 2.png|thumb|247x247px|When loading into a world with the corrupted "Xeroc" character, the game will turn windowed and the wallpaper will change. Afterwards, the player will be struck by a beam of light.]]
As soon as your character is created, the menu music will mute, the logo will disappear, and the background will become grayscale. In the character select screen, your character have a black censor and the name will be gibberish. Upon entering the world, you will be struck by a beam of light. After dying, the game will close and your character's ".plr" file will be deleted from the files.

Your wallpaper will also change to an ominous image of the word "Do not." repeated multiple times.

==Trivia==  

*The name '''"Jungle Jyrant Jharim"''' is a reference to [[Godseeker, Yharim]], also known as "Jungle Tyrant, Yharim".
*The name '''"Crab secret"''' is a reference to popular internet streamer '''DougDoug''', where in one of his Pajama Sam AI streams, the topic of "the crab secret" was brought up multiple times.
*The name '''"YuH"''' is a reference to the Calamity developer GinYuH, who has worked on many Calamity-related [[Community modifications|addons]].
*The name '''"Dron"''' is a reference to the [[Disgusting Creature: Dron|boss of the same name]].
*The name '''"Fanny"''' is a reference to the character "Fanny the Flame" from the [[Community modifications|addon]] "Calamity: Community Remix".
*The name '''"Xeroc"''' is a reference to the Calamity [[lore]] figure, Xeroc. The effects are a reference to the [[Community modifications|addon]] "Wrath of the Gods" by Lucille K.
