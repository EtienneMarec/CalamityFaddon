The '''Aquarel Pistol''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] that is an upgrade to [[Shattered Pistol]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[Ammunition|ammo]], it fires an aquarel bullet that shatters into 8 smaller ones with a slight spread after travelling about 9 tiles. The smaller bullets deal 25% of the weapon's damage each. Both the aquarel and the smaller bullets do not pierce through enemies nor go through walls, but inflict the [[Frostbite]], [[Suffocation]] and [[Wet]] [[debuffs]] for 2 seconds.
