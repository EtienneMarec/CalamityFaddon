'''Sulphurous armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set. It requires 95 [[Urchin Stinger]]s and 45 [[Acidwood]], 45 [[Sulphuric Scale]]s, and 45 [[Sulphurous Sand]] to craft the entire set. The armor mainly grants bonuses to the [[Spy]] class.

It consists of a '''Sulphurous Helmet''', '''Sulphurous Breastplate''', and '''Sulphurous Leggings'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*16 defense
*12% increased Spy damage
*7% increased Spy critical strike chance
*10% increased movement speed
*35% increased movement speed when submerged in liquid
*Underwater breathing identical to the [[Gills]] buff
*The [[Poisoned]] [[debuff]] is inflicted onto any attacking enemy
*Striking an enemy will inflict the Poisoned debuff onto them
*Reduces the amount of damage taken from the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] debuff, as well as the damage taken by the water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]]
*Grants the ability to double jump, emitting a large amount of dust below the player. While performing the jump, a sulphurous bubble is spawned which floats upward and inflicts [[Venom]] on enemy hits. The bubble deals 20 Spy damage.
*A max [[stealth]] of 95

== Notes ==
*Underwater breathing granted by the armor set does not stack with the [[Gills Potion]].
*The set bonus' effect of reducing severity of the water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] stacks with the [[Sulphurskin Potion]] in the following manner:
**While the full set is equipped without the [[Sulphurskin]] [[buff]], the amount of minimum life regen lost is 3 instead of 8, and further reduced by 1 every 6.67 seconds instead of 4.17 seconds.
**With both the armor and the Sulphurskin buff, the amount of minimum life regen lost is 1, and further reduced by 1 every 8.33 seconds.
**Both reduce the amount of life regen lost from the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]] by 2 and can stack with each other.
*The double jump happens after all other jump accessories, including the rocket boots and its upgrades.
*The double jump also resets if you hit a block above you, allowing you to stay "airborne" indefinitely, albeit confined to a ceiling.

== Tips ==
*Due to the better damage stat, the double jump, and the ability to inflict debuffs, this set could be considered superior to the [[Aerospec armor|Aerospec]] Spy variant.
