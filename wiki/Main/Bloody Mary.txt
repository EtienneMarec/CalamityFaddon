'''Bloody Mary''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Bloody Mary''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but boosts damage, critical strike chance, movement speed and melee speed during a Blood Moon.

While the effects of the Bloody Mary debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
