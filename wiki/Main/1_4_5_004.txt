The '''1.4.5.004''' (or '''1.4.5.4''') update included many AI and balance changes, along with fixes to several major bugs.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}
== Additions ==
*Added a [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[Cactus]] variant.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Anahita and Leviathan]] ====
*No longer become invincible and instead enrage when leaving the [[Ocean]].
*Decreased the maximum amount of [[Parasea]]s that the Leviathan spawns from 2 to 1 when not enraged and from 4 to 3 when enraged.

==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*No longer despawns and instead enrages when leaving the [[Sulphurous Sea]].
*Sulphur clouds no longer have increased duration in Death Mode.

==== [[Astrum Aureus]] ====
*Nerfed Astrum Aureus health from {{dv|96,000|153,600|195,200}} to {{dv|84,000|134,400|171,200}}.
*Nerfed Post-Moon Lord health from {{revengeance|704,000}} to {{revengeance|624,000}}.
*Nerfed Boss Rush health from {{dv|8,400,000|13,440,000|13,440,000}} to {{dv|7,400,000|11,840,000|11,840,000}}.
*Adjusted [[Aureus Spawn]] stats:
**Buffed health from 1008 to 3000.
**Buffed Boss Rush health from 1002 to 30,000.
**Buffed defense from 0 to 10.
**No longer has 99.9999% damage reduction.
*Now less aggressive in [[Death Mode]], and the spread and amount of its lasers have been reduced.
*Now enrages during the day.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*All worms now accelerate much faster and have a higher top speed if the player is far away.
*Now enrages during the day.
*No longer takes reduced damage from the [[Forbidden Sun]]'s explosions.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*No longer despawns outside of the Crimson but instead enrages in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Now more aggressive when fought in the [[Underground]] [[Crimson]] in Revengeance Mode.
*Now transitions to phase 4 at {{revengeance|55%}} health instead of {{revengeance|50%}} and phase 5 at {{revengeance|40%}} health instead of {{revengeance|30%}}.
*Nerfed [[Creeper]]s' speed in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Now enrages when leaving the [[Brimstone Crag]].
*Her and her projectiles are now significantly slower and inflict [[Brimstone Flames]] instead of [[Abyssal Flames]].
*The laser now shoots rows of darts perpendicular to it.
*Now inflicts Brimstone Flames for 3 seconds on contact.
*Hellfireballs and their explosions now only inflict Brimstone Flames for 4 seconds instead of 10 and 6 respectively.
*Nerfed [[Brimling]]'s health pre-Providence from 4000 to 2000.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*No longer despawns and instead enrages when fought during the day.
*Brimstone Lasers now only inflict Brimstone Flames for 2 seconds.
*Hellfireballs and their explosions now only inflict Brimstone Flames for 4 seconds instead of 10 and 6 respectively.
*[[Catastrophe]]'s fireballs now inflict Brimstone Flames for 2 seconds instead of 3.
*Brothers move faster if they are still alive after Calamitas is defeated; they move slightly slower otherwise, to compensate.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Dark Energies now spread out further apart from each other.
*Decreased [[Distorted]] duration from Dark Energies from 1 second to 0.33.

==== [[Crabulon]] ====
*Now enrages when fought on the [[Surface]] or outside of the [[Glowing Mushroom biome]].
*Now always launches 2 Crab Shrooms in Normal Mode instead of randomly launching 1 or 2.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*Can now always be seen during its last phase and its projectiles are now blue during the day.
*Now enrages when outside of the [[Snow biome]].
*No longer transitions between phases 1-5 at different healths in each difficulty mode. Instead it transitions to phases 2, 3, 4 and 5 at 85%, 70%, 55% and 40% health respectively.
*Updated the health thresholds for transitioning to final phase(s):
**Phase 6 now begins at {{dv|15%|20%|25%|30%}} health, instead of {{dv|15%||27%|40%}}.
**Phase 7 now begins at {{dv|||10%|15%}} health, instead of {{dv|||15%|25%}}.
**The first increase to rotation speed now happens at {{dv|5%||7.5%}} health instead of {{dv|10%||22.5%}}.
**The second increase to rotation speed now happens at {{dv|2.5%||5%|30%}} health instead of {{dv|5%||20%|40%}}.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*No longer becomes invincible and instead enrages when leaving the [[Desert]].

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Segment lights now glow purple while it's flying in Revengeance Mode.
*Now accelerates much faster and has a higher top speed if the player is far away.
*No longer despawns and instead enrages when fought during the day in Revengeance Mode.
*Reduced lasers' maximum speed from 18 to 14.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Can now charge directly at the player during phase 1.
*Can no longer teleport immediately after laser walls have been spawned and will now teleport 3 seconds after in all world difficulties.
*Top speed has been decreased by 15% in phase 2 on all world difficulties. However, it now accelerates much faster and has a higher top speed if the player is far away.
*[[Cosmic Guardian]]s now accelerate much faster and have a higher top speed if the player is far away.
*The Devourer of Gods now has an npcSlot check prior to being summoned. If the check fails, The Devourer of Gods cannot spawn. <!--Reason for change: Players who use several content mods will often spawn in a DoG that doesn't have the slots it needs to be properly fought. NOTE: A special log error is made if it procs, so we'll know what the issue is straight away.-->

==== [[The Dragonfolly]] ====
*Now has a slightly longer delay before dashing.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Now gains new charging patterns as its health decreases during phase 3 in Revengeance Mode. Duke Fishron will also now continuously charge at the player when its health is below 20% in Revengeance Mode.
*Now transitions to phase 2 at {{revengeance|70%}} health instead of {{revengeance|66%}} and phase 3 at {{revengeance|40%}} instead of {{dv|||20%|33%}}.

==== [[Eater of Worlds]] ====
*No longer despawns outside of the Corruption but instead enrages in Revengeance Mode.
*Now more aggressive when fought in the Underground [[Corruption]] in Revengeance Mode.
*Now smaller in Death Mode.

==== [[Eye of Cthulhu]] ====
*No longer despawns and instead enrages when fought during the day.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Nerfed Revengeance Mode body health from {{revengeance|54,000}} to {{revengeance|47,250}}.
*Buffed phase 2 head health from {{revengeance|20,625}} to {{revengeance|28,050}}.
*Body, Fists and Head no longer inflict [[Broken Armor]] in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Now enrages outside of the Corruption. 
*Now less aggressive when fought in the Underground Corruption.
*Hive Blobs will now despawn and no longer spawn in phase 2.

==== [[King Slime]] ====
*[[Crown Jewel (enemy)|Crown Jewel]] now fires a spread of 3 projectiles in Death Mode.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Phantasmal Eye projectiles are now less aggressive in Revengeance Mode at the start of the fight and become more aggressive as Moon Lord's health decreases.

==== [[The Old Duke]] ====
*Now spends less time preparing to dash during phase 3 and now dashes more aggressively while enraged instead of becoming invulnerable.
*Significantly decreased pre-charge velocity and acceleration.
*Reduced resistance to Crescent Moon's flail from 80% to 45%.
*Sulphur clouds no longer have increased duration in Death Mode.

==== [[The Perforators]] ====
*[[The Perforator Hive]] now fires its projectiles less often while a Perforator worm is alive.
*Now enrage outside of the Crimson.
*Now less aggressive in the Underground Crimson.
*The Perforator worms now move slower but gain speed as their health decreases.
*Nerfed the medium Perforator worm's total health from {{dv|2000|3906|4912|5526}} to {{dv|1600|3122|4016|4518}}. Nerfed individual segment health from {{dv|200|279|307}} to {{dv|160|223|251}}. <!--Reason for change: WAY too bulky for a boss summon in pre-hm.-->
*The large Perforator worm will now lunge at the player less often in all world difficulties.

==== [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Can no longer spawn [[Plaguebringer]]s, and is now less aggressive in Death Mode.
*The Plaguebringer Goliath's Plague Mines now glow.
*Enrage has been adjusted to make the fight fairer and now only enrages outside of the [[Jungle]].

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Spore clouds in Revengeance Mode now glow and now constantly travel at a small speed to hinder the player.
*The number of tentacle hooks that can be summoned at once has been capped in both phases in Revengeance Mode.
*No longer gains a defense boost per tentacle alive in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Clone now turns invisible, except for a red glowmask for its heart and veins, to convey that it's not dealing contact damage while it lines up for a charge.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] ====
*Molten Globs now only last for 15 seconds on the ground instead of 20.
*Holy Spears can no longer slightly home in on the player.
*Death Mode no longer multiplies the rate at which holy spears are fired by 2.
*Screen shader now gradually grow brighter when the player gets within 20 tiles of her Holy Inferno debuff distance. The screen shader is at max brightness while the player is too far away from Providence and will be inflicted with Holy Inferno.
*Providence at nighttime now fires 4 lasers during her scissor laser attack.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Reduced enrage scale from 0.5 to 0.25.

==== [[Ravager]] ====
*Rock Pillars now break on contact with the player.
*Flame Pillars now spawn further apart in phase 2.
*Detached head now stays further away from the player in all difficulties to prevent cheap shots.

==== [[Skeletron]] ====
*Nerfed Skeletron Hand health in Death Mode from {{death|1170}} to {{death|1014}}.
*Increased Chaos Balls fired when teleporting from {{dv|||2|4}} to {{dv|||3|5}}.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Nerfed Head health in Revengeance Mode from {{revengeance|60,900}} to {{revengeance|50,400}}.
*Nerfed Head health when summoned by [[Draedon's Remote]] in Revengeance Mode from {{revengeance|79,800}} to {{revengeance|60,900}}.
*Decreased rocket velocity.
*Buffed defense boost for each limb alive from {{dv|||10|15}} to {{revengeance|20}}.
*Prime Saw, Prime Vice and Head no longer inflict [[Bleeding]] in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Nerfed Core health from {{dv|4000|6400|8000}} to {{dv|2000|3200|4000}}.
