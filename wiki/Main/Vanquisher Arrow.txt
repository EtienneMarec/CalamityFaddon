'''Vanquisher Arrows''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[arrows]] that are made from [[Cosmilite Bar]]s. Fired Vanquisher Arrows pierce through tiles and fade after 1.5 seconds, spawning a homing arrow clone after 0.75 seconds. The arrow clone behaves similarly to the main one but does not pierce through tiles. The main arrow will strike an enemy twice if they are struck at the end of the arrow's lifespan. Both arrows inflict the [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]] on enemy hits.
