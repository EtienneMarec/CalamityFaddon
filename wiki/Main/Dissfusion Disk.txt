The Dissfusion Disk is a craftable Epilogue [[boomerang]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Eradicator]]. It initially fires a singular fast projectile that slows rapidly, this later splits into two glaives that orbit the central core, which then explodes, dealing large amounts of damage.
