The '''Molten Piercer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[dagger]] dropped by [[Pyrogen]]. It quickly fires gravity affected knives that break into obsidian shards when they hit enemies or tiles. 

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Molten Piercer will deal 200% damage and cause the dagger to spin into a hellstorm, decelerating until coming to a stop, while firing gravity-affected obsidian shards in all directions.

== Tips ==
*The Molten Piercer's incredibly high multi-target damage can deal very high damage to worm-type enemies such as [[The Destroyer]] or [[Aquatic Scourge]] while the stealth strike can deal even more massive damage to worms or bosses that will stay in a set position, like [[Calamitas]] allowing many hits of the spinning dagger as well as the shards which will spawn directly on top of enemies.

== Trivia ==
*The spinning hellstorm stealth strike is based on one of Pyrogen's attacks.
