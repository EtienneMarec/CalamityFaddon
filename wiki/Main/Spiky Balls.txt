{{dablink|For the vanilla weapon, see [[Spiky Ball]].}}
'''Spiky balls''' are [[weapons]] belonging to the [[spy]] class. They are thrown outwards from the player and deal continuous contact damage towards enemies. Most spiky balls are stackable, and will not deploy if all balls within a stack are currently used.
