The '''Grand Gelatin''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is an upgrade of [[Life Jelly]], [[Mirage Jelly (item)|Mirage Jelly]], [[Mana Jelly]] and [[Vital Jelly]].  It gives the bonuses of all its ingredients, therefore saving space for more covering slots. It is later used in crafting [[The Absorber]].
