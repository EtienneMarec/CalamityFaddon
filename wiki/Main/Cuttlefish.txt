The '''Cuttlefish''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd layers of the [[Abyss]]. It stays invisible on the ground, waiting for the player to come near and attack them. Unlike most [[Abyss]] enemies, it does not become passive again even if the player didn't hit it.

== Trivia ==
*The behavior of the Cuttlefish shares many similarities with [[wikipedia:Cuttlefish|its real-life counterpart]]; both hide with camouflage in an attempt to sneak up on their prey.
