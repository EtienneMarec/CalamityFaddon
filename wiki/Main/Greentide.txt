The '''Greentide''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] with autoswing, which is dropped by the [[Leviathan and Anahita]]. Upon striking an enemy with it, teeth will rain from the sky towards the cursor's position. Each tooth will inflict the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]] for 5 seconds.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*Green tide is the term given to large accumulations of green seaweed which harbor large amounts of algae that can be harmful to marine wildlife.
