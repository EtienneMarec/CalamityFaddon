The '''Elysian Aegis''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] when she is summoned in [[The Underworld]]. It grants 18 defense, immunity to fire blocks, knockback and the Holy Flames debuff, +30 max life.

While equipped, the wearer can double-tap a horizontal movement key to perform a supreme holy flame dash with a base distance of 28 blocks. Striking enemies during the shield dash will heavily damage them, cause holy explosions, and leave behind a lingering Holy Eruption which damages enemies that touch it. Every time an enemy is struck, the wearer is given 6 [[immunity frames]].

Tapping the {{key|Elysian Gar}} keybinding with this covering equipped grants the [[Elysian Gar]] buff.

This covering's max life and defense stack with the [[Asgardian Aegis]]. However, the Elysian Gar bonuses will not stack if both shields are worn at the same time.

== Notes ==
*Directly colliding with an enemy during the shield dash deals 250 melee damage. This direct hit is affected by melee damage bonuses and the wearer's melee critical strike chance.
*The explosion produced upon colliding with an enemy deals 120 damage. This is boosted by the average of all damage stats.
*The Holy Eruption left behind after colliding with an enemy deals 80 damage and can strike 6 times per second. This is boosted by the average of all damage stats. The Holy Eruption inflicts [[Holy Flames]] for 4 seconds to all enemies which touch it.
*Activating Elysian Gar only decreases the player's acceleration, not their top speed. The slow acceleration can be countered by dashing to reach higher speeds immediately.
*The Elysian Gar buff will be deactivated when using [[Mounts]].
