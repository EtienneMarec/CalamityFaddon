The '''Deep Diver''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. It grants 8 defense. While the player is submerged in water or honey, damage, movement speed and acceleration are all increased by 15%, the player gains an extra 15 defense while submerged in water or honey, and the player gains the ability to dash a base distance of 36 blocks by double tapping the {{key|left}} or {{key|right}} key.

== Tips ==
*Using this covering alongside an item that eliminates water physics such as [[Victide armor]] or the [[Sea Shell]] greatly increases the distance and velocity of the dash.
*This covering has the longest base dash distance of any dashing item. This makes it well worth it even in the endgame, providing that the player uses an underwater arena.

== Trivia ==
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Shield of the Ocean]]
