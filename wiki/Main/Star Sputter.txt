The '''Star Sputter''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] dropped by [[Astrum Deus]]. It fires volleys of three Sputter Comets and fires one Large Sputter Comet on every fourth volley fired, consuming [[Fallen Star]]s as ammo.
