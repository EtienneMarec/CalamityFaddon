The '''Spy's Lootbox''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spy]] [[covering]]. When equipped:
*The player's [[spy stealth]] cap is increased by 30.
*The player's spy damage and critical strike chance are increased by 12% each.
*Enemy [[aggro]] is reduced by 700.
*Enemies in the [[Abyss]] have their detection radius multiplied by 0.3.
*The player can build spy stealth 50% faster while standing still.
*The player receives an increase to spy stealth regeneration rate while moving and not attacking.
*All [[stealth strike]]s only require half of the player's maximum stealth to perform, and are guaranteed to critically strike.
*When a weapon is used, a second copy rains down from the sky towards the cursor that deals 30% of the weapon's damage. When the copy collides with an enemy or dissipates, a burst of homing souls, fireballs, and bouncing jewels explode outwards.
*Stealth strikes summon darksun tornadoes on hit. A maximum of two tornadoes can exist at once and each last 10 seconds.
*Hitting the Spectral Veil's hotkey causes a darksun aura to engulf the player that makes them immune to all damage for 5 seconds with a 20 second cooldown. The aura also deals a flat 200 damage per tick to enemies caught inside.
*If the player is hit by an enemy, a blast of darksun light will be released, nullifying damage from the hit and massively damaging enemies in a wide area. This effect has a 90 second cooldown and will not trigger again until the cooldown is finished. Additionally, if this effect occurs, the player's stealth will be instantly maxed out.
