'''God's Paranoia''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It automatically fires up to 10 homing spiky balls, with the amount of balls thrown depending on stack size. The balls latch onto enemies and unleash god slayer kunai. Right-clicking with the weapon causes all existing God's Paranoia balls owned by the player to dissipate. Both the balls and the kunai go through blocks and inflict the [[God Slayer Inferno]] [[debuff]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with God's Paranoia causes the resulting spiky ball to home in faster and release kunai more often.
