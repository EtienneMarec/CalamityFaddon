'''Icicle Arrows''' are [[Hardmode]] [[arrows]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{cc|80}} each after defeating all three [[minibosses]] in the [[Frost Moon]] [[event]]. They shatter into shards upon impact with a tile or an enemy and inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] on hit enemies.

The [[Eternal Blizzard]] also fires Icicle Arrows in addition to [[Wooden Arrow]]s.

== Notes ==
*The ice shards will deal 40% less damage to everything. The damage penalty takes the bow's base damage into account.
