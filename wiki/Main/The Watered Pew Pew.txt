'''The Watered Pew Pew''' is a Hardmode [[magic gun]] sold by the [[Wet Rat]]. It rapidly fires small bubbles on a short distance, the bubbles deal damage to enemies and inflict the [[Crush Depth]] debuff.

{{Modifier|magic}}


== Tips ==
*The high amount of projectiles spawned by this weapon make it ideal for crowd control or damaging large enemies many times.
**Note that the high amount of projectiles spawn can cause drops in frame rate, especially on low-end computers.
