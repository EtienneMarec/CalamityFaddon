'''Flah''' is a [[hardmode]] type of [[Herb]] that grows naturally on [[Brimstone Slag]] after [[Calamitas]] and [[Plantera]] are defeated. It can be harvested with any [[weapon]] or [[tool]], and can be planted manually with [[Flah Seeds]], which can also be planted in [[Clay Pot]]s and [[Planter Box]]es.
Flah will bloom during a [[Blood Moon]] or [[Solar Eclipse]]. It will only drop seeds while blooming and like any other herb will drop 1–3. Flah will have a very bright red glow during this time. 

Flah can also be obtained from [[Flahr]]s.
