The '''Elemental Disk''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[boomerang]]. It rapidly throws disks that have a chance of splitting into eight more disks when near an enemy. The split disks will move outward from the point they split from before returning in the player's direction. The split disks have a much shorter duration before disappearing.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Elemental Disk causes the next disk thrown to travel slower but farther, pierce through enemies and spawn extra disks more frequently.
