The '''Ichor Dagger''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[dagger]] that is dropped by [[Ichor Sticker]]s. It inflicts [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] to any enemies hit and can pierce through 2 enemies or bounce twice off of tiles. It disappears upon its third impact. Like the [[Shadowflame Knife]], it is heavily affected by gravity. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Cursed Dagger]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Ichor Dagger causes the resulting dagger to release gravity-affected sparks and streams of Ichor as it travels.
