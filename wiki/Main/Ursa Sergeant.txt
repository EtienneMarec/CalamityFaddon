The '''Ursa Sergeant''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is rarely [[fishing|fished]] up while in the [[Astral Infection]] biome. When equipped, the player becomes immune to the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] and [[Feral Bite]] [[debuff]]s, and gains 20 [[defense]], but their movement speed is reduced by 15%.

In addition, the player’s life regeneration increases as their health lowers:
*If they are below 50% life, life regen is increased by 1.
*If they are below 25% life, life regen is increased by 2.
*If they are below 15% life, life regen is increased by 3.

== Trivia ==
*This "Ursa" in this item's name refers to the [[wikipedia:Ursa Major|Ursa Major]] and [[wikipedia:Ursa Minor|Ursa Minor]] constellations. Additionally, the item resembles a [[wikipedia:Tardigrade|tardigrade]], also known as a "water bear", and the Latin word "Ursa" means "bear". Tardigrades are known for their incredible resilience and slow speed, hence the item's functionality.
*The "Sergeant" in this item's name refers to [[wikipedia:Sergeant_major_(fish)|sergeant major]]s, a species of fish.
