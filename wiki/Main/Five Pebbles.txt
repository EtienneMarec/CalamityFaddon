'''Five Pebbles''' is an surface [[biome]] added by the Calamity Mod which spawns upon world creation. It is located on the side of the world opposite to the [[Dungeon]]. It is extremely large, dark, and perilous, filled with deadly [[enemies]], which are made harder by the lack of gravity. Because many enemies in Five Pebbles are initially passive, only becoming hostile when hit by the player, stealthiness should be greatly prioritized while exploring the biome.

Five pebbles is divided into five distinct parts, each with a different set of enemies that will spawn.
*The First part is the bottom entrance to Five Pebbles, mostly characterized by sterile bio-mechanical cells, humming transformer arrays, and dimly lit access shafts in a unique zero gravity environment.
*The Top left part is the Recursive Transform Arrays of Five Pebbles. Recursive Transform Array consists of a collection of large, dimly lit zero-gravity rooms containing high voltage flux condensers that kill Player and most other creatures on contact. This area is generally devoid of threats.
*The Right part (Unfortunate Developement) acts as a quick but dangerous shortcut to the Memory Conflux. Its difficulty comes from the large amount of Rot enemies that resides in the area. In addition to the rot, gravity fluctuates between an on and off state, requiring the Player to position themself carefully.
*The Top right part (Memory Conflux) is a grid of mostly sterile white uniform chambers, some containing arrangements of bio-mechanical tendrils, fronds, and red latticed matter being tended to by small white spiders. These arrangements can be disrupted by crashing into them, but they slowly knit back together over time and cannot be permanently damaged. The lower right of the subregion leads into Unfortunate Development, where similar rooms are infested with Rot and feature intermittent gravity.
*The Top part (General Systems Bus) is the smallest part of Five Pebbles and contains no threats. In this area, flickering symbols and patterns are projected across the background walls, [[Neuron flies]] dart across the rooms, and long red tendrils spark and wave in the zero-gravity environment. Faint music can be heard in this part which increases in volume until reaching the central chamber, where [[Five Pebbles (boss)]] can be located.

The walls of Five Pebbles are mostly composed of Iterator Plating. Unfortunate developement begins replacing Iterator Plating with [[Rotting matter]]. [[Neuron Flies]] inhabit the entirety of the biome, containing several useful drops.
== Notes ==
*Player-created Five Pebbles biomes are not possible.
*The player cannot place or break any blocks in the biome until [[Five Pebbles (boss)]] is defeated
*Most of the biome is devoid of gravity, so the player should take extra care when exploring.

== Tips ==

* You can avoid most of the biome by entering from the top instead.
