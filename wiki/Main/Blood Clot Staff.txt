The '''Blood Clot Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that can also be dropped by [[The Perforators]]. It is statistically identical to its [[Corruption]] equivalent, the [[Dank Staff]]. It summons a meaty blood clot minion, which follows the player and attacks [[enemies]]. It deals contact damage, and will stay on enemies for as long as possible.
