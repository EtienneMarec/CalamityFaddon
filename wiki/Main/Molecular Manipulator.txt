The '''Molecular Manipulator''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]]. It is the upgrade to the [[Nullification Pistol]]. Each shot will alter its target in some way, as long as it is not a [[boss]]. The following effects can happen at an equal chance:
*Grow 400% bigger
*Shrink 90% smaller
*Deal 20 more contact damage
*Deal 20 less contact damage
*Become completely resistant to [[knockback]]
*Remove all knockback resistance
*Increase [[defense]] by 10
*Decrease defense by 10
*Increase velocity upwards
*Increase velocity to the left or right

== Notes ==
*The amount of contact damage increase or decrease will scale with difficulty.
**For example, [[Great Sand Shark]] has a 60% scaling factor in [[Expert Mode]] and 15% scaling factor in [[Revengeance Mode]]. Therefore, the amount of damage increase will equal to {{dv|20|32|boost=0.15}}.
*The amount of velocity increased is 1.875 blocks per second. This effect is additive, and can therefore counteract opposite motion.
**For example, under the effect of increasing vertical velocity, an NPC that moves downwards at a rate of 3 blocks per second will still move downwards, but at a rate of 1.125 blocks per second.

== Trivia ==
*The Molecular Manipulator will work on the [[Guide]] and the [[Clothier]] with their respective [[Guide Voodoo Doll|voodoo]] [[Clothier Voodoo Doll|dolls]] equipped.
