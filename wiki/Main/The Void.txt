'''The Void''' is a [[subworld]] added by the Calamity Mod which can be accessed by entering the [[Cave Entrance]] found buried underground. The subworld consists of many sub-biomes, all of which connect together via small branching tunnels to create one enormous cave system. A large chamber exists at the bottom of the world, where the [[Void Queen]] can be encountered and fought.

There are four distinct biomes in the Void, each housing unique terrain and enemies.

*''The Overgrowth:'' A section of the Void distinguished by the presence of foliage such as moss, vines, and various glowing plants, which can prove helpful for navigating the darkness. This biome is always located the closest to the Cave Entrance.

*''The Rock Pools:'' A series of caves submerged under water, with small coves sprinkled about for catching your breath. They are host to many aquatic enemies, and contain small amounts of foliage.

*''The Cliffs:'' A treacherous ravine full of stalagmites and stalactites the player must maneuver around. The many walls blocking the players field of vision can prove dangerous when combined with the unique lighting in the depths.

*''[[The pure dark]]:'' the darkest area with broken buildings and completely infested with secretaries alongside the sightings of a few undeads.

*''The Nest:'' The final room found in the deepest trenches of the subworld. The Nest is a large, majorly empty room intended to serve as an arena for the [[Bosses|Boss]] of the Void.

All biomes are connected by winding tunnels which can occasionally branch off into other biomes or dead ends. There will only be one path that leads to The Nest.{{Infobox wrapper|{{float file box|[[File:TheVoidRareCreature.png]]|Rare Creatures informational displaying "RAREDETECTED" red text.}}}}

If [[Chasm Beast]] or [[Secretary]] is nearby, the Rare Creatures informational will display "RAREDETECTED" multiple times.

==Light==
All sources of light placed in the Void will be put out exactly 8 seconds after being placed. Torches, Campfires, Candles, Lamps, Lanterns, Candelabras, and Chandeliers will all be turned off, as if toggled by [[Wire|Wires]]. Light generating blocks, on the other hand, will be destroyed.

Light its self works differently in the subworld. Any non-transparent block with collision will block light from penetrating past it, leaving the area behind it shrouded in shadow. Any tile that is not directly lit by a light source will remain pitch black, even if the global brightness of the game is raised. Glowmasks will still be visible in the darkness, allowing for certain enemies to be identified.

==Notes==
*Although the Void can be accessed from the very beginning of the game, the difficulty of enemies found within encourages players to have at least close to endgame tier equipment before entering.
*Any lava or shimmer placed within the subworld will harden instantly.
*Using an [[Escape Rope]] will teleport the player to the Cave Entrance, while using a Magic Mirror, or any of its upgrades, will cause the player to exit the subworld entirely.
