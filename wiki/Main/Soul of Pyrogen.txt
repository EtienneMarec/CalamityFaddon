The '''Soul of Pyrogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Pyrogen]].

When equipped, all of the player's melee attacks deal 20% more damge, and true melee strikes create 5 spears that deal 50% of the held weapons damage and inflict the [[Dragon Fire]] debuff.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to continue the quest, along with receiving some new dialogue.

* This gentle sensation of magma is an unmistakable product of the [[Underworld]]. Their mastery of fire would have sustained them for many more generations, just as we continue to live under the sun.
* The monks may have profaned the element with their concepts and the Auric Soul, but I will use this aspect of fire to rejuvenate this world and save it from total annihilation.

After this, Permafrost will be able to shoot fireballs to ignite enemies.
