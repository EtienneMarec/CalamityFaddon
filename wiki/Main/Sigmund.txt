'''[https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Sigmund?veaction=edit Sigmund]''' ([[wikipedia:Japanese_language|Japanese]]: ジークムント ''Jīkumunto'') is [https://among-us.fandom.com/wiki/Among_Us_Wiki a] [https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/pre- Pre][[wikipedia:Hyphen|-]][[tgc:Hardmode|Hardmode]] [[boss]] and is [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]]'s [https://safebooru.donmai.us/posts?tags=yaoi+&z=5 gay] cousin. It drops [[Nether]] which is a [[crafting material]] required to craft the [[Meaty Worm]].

== Spawn ==
Sigmund is summoned by using the [[Kos Fragment]] in [[The Underworld]] at any time.

== Behavior ==
Sigmund will walk towards the player, dealing contact damage. Sigmund will jump to meet the player.

== Resistances ==
*Sigmund takes decreased damage from some items and projectiles. It takes:
**90% damage from [[Steel Kunai]].

== Tips ==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating Sigmund, including [[weapon]] and [[tgc:Guide:Arena|arena]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Sigmund strategies]].}}
*Building a large arena to dodge Sigmund' attacks is a good idea in order to give more room to move around.
