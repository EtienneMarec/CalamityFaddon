The '''Sulphurous Grabber''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Yoyos|yoyo]] that drops from [[Sulphurous Skater]]s. While it is thrown, the yoyo will occasionally release seven damaging bubbles that can be either green or purple. The bubbles deal 25% of the yoyo's damage. If the yoyo touches a green bubble, it will power up, gaining increased damage and releasing bubbles more often. Only the yoyo can inflict the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]].
