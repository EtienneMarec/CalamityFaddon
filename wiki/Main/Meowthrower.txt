[[File:Meowthrower (demo).gif|frame|right|The Meowthrower being used against [[Target Dummy|Target Dummies]], showing the aesthetic bursts of flame, random spread, infinite piercing, and debuffs.]]

The '''Meowthrower''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that uses [[gel]] as [[ammunition]] and drops from the [[Wall of Flesh]]. It shoots blue and pink flames at a short range and with a more random spread than most flamethrowers. The flames inflict the [[On Fire!]] and [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]s on contact and pierce infinitely. Upon hitting an enemy, an aesthetic burst of flame occurs, and it makes a meowing sound.
