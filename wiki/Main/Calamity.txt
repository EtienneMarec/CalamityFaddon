'''Calamity''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that drops from [[Supreme Calamitas]]. While equipped, a flaming aura spawns at the user's cursor. While an enemy is within range, the aura glows and continuously deals 212 typeless damage at a rate of 12 hits per second, inflicting the [[Vulnerability Hex]] [[debuff]]. Additionally, the cursor alternates between red and orange colors, regardless if the covering is equipped in the main or vanity slot.
