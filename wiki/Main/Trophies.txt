'''Trophies''' are rare drops (10%) from most [[bosses]] and are essentially the boss version of banners. Unlike banners, trophies do not provide any special effects. They are purely decorative background objects that can be placed on background walls. In total, there are 36 trophies.

==Trivia ==
*Just like the trophies in the vanilla game, each trophy resembles a part of the boss it is dropped from:

*[[Jane Wulfrum]]'s trophy, much like John Wulfrum's, is her outfit.
*The [[Wulfrum Excavator]]'s trophy it one of its segments.
*The [[Desert Scourge]]'s trophy seems to be its jaw or teeth.
*The [[Giant Clam]]'s trophy is the pearl it encases within itself.
*The [[Acidsighter]]'s trophy is a piece of the eye.
*[[Monstro]]'s trophy is one of its teeth.
*[[Crabulon]]'s trophy is one of its claws.
*[[The Hive Mind]]'s trophy is one of its shadow scales.
*[[The Perforators]]' trophy is a small mouth from the hive.
*[[The Slime God]]'s trophy is the Slime God Core.
*[[Cryogen]]'s trophy is one of its ice chunks.
*[[Pyrogen]]'s trophy, like Cryogen's, is a chunk of itself.
*[[Brimstone Elemental]]'s trophy is her brimrose.
*The [[Aquatic Scourge]]'s trophy is one of its teeth.
*The [[Cragmaw Mire]]'s trophy is its jaw.
*[[Calamitas]]'s trophy is her visor.
*[[Catastrophe]]'s trophy is one of his metal jaws.
*[[Cataclysm]]'s trophy is one of his fangs.
*{{fantag|Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic}}[[The Forbidden Lantern]]'s trophy is its core.
*{{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}[[The Derellect]]'s trophy seems to be a central component of itself.
*The [[Great Sand Shark]]'s trophy appears to be one of its scales.
*[[Anahita]]'s trophy is her head.
*[[The Leviathan]]’s trophy is one of her teeth.
*[[Astrum Aureus]]'s trophy is its front plate.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s trophy is her stinger.
*The [[Ravager]]'s trophy is its two skulls.
*[[Astrum Deus]]'s trophy is its tail.
*The [[Profaned Guardians]]' trophy is a chunk of the main guardian's body.
*[[The Dragonfolly]]'s trophy is its head.
*[[Providence]]'s trophy is her main crystal.
*The [[Ceaseless Void]]'s trophy is a piece of its plating.
*[[Storm Weaver]]'s trophy is its two fangs.
*[[Signus]]'s trophy is a part of its cloak.
*{{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}}[[The Starborn Moth]]'s trophy is a part of her robe.
*[[Polterghast]]'s trophy is a fragment of its outer shell.
*The [[Mauler]]'s trophy, much like the Great Sand Shark's, appears to be one of its scales.
*The [[Nuclear Terror]]'s trophy appears to be a part of its exoskeleton at its rear body.
*[[Profusion, the Viral God]]'s trophy is its eye and one of its tentacles.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]'s trophy is a body segment.
*{{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}}The [[Horseslimes of the Goopocalypse]] trophy is a broken piece of each Horseslime.
*[[Yharon]]'s trophy is one of his feet and claws.
*[[The Wall of Bronze]]'s trophy is its face.
*[[XS-01 Artemis]] and [[XS-03 Apollo]]'s trophies are their eye lenses.
*[[XM-05 Thanatos]]'s trophy is its faceplate.
*[[XF-09 Ares]]'s trophy is its exposed core.
*[[XP-00 Hypnos]]' trophy is its eye.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]]' trophy is the brooch and shoulder pieces from her robes.
*[[Supreme Cataclysm]]'s trophy is his belt.
*[[Supreme Catastrophe]]'s trophy is part of his sword's blade. 
*[[Godseeker, Yharim]]'s trophy is his robe.
