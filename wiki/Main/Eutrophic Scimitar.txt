The '''Eutrophic Scimitar''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that is dropped by the [[Blinded Angler]] in the [[Sunken Sea]]. Upon being swung, it fires two electric shock projectiles which briefly stuns normal enemies on contact. The projectiles do not pierce and cannot pass through tiles, and deal 70% of the sword's damage.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The stun effect will not apply if any [[bosses]] are alive or to certain rare creatures.
