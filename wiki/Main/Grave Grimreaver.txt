}}The '''Grave Grimreaver''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[rogue weapon]]. When used, it throws a homing scythe which passes through solid tiles. Every 0.33 seconds, the scythe releases a flaming skull in its path which homes in after a short duration and inflicts [[Cursed Inferno]]. On hit, the scythe explodes into a wave of four bats in random directions. Bats deal 25% of the weapon's damage, bounces off tiles up to four times, and pierces once.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Grave Grimreaver will cause the next scythe thrown to spawn 20 bats and rain down 10 homing skulls on hit.
