==Celesticus==
The Celesticus is A Craftable [[Classless weapons|Classless]] Godseeker Mode Weapon, It is an upgrade to the Aestheticus. It travels in a straight line and can travel through walls.  it explodes into 8 homing Vaporfied crystals upon hitting an enemy that deals 25% of the weapons base damage and inflict Vaporfied for 2.5 seconds

The Celesticus cannot be reforged, and therefore cannot have [[modifiers]]
