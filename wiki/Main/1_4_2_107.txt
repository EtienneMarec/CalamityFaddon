The '''1.4.2.107''' update expanded on the [[fishing]] mechanic, adding several new [[Fishing Poles]] and some balancing tweaks to existing fishing gear. The update also added a few new [[Spy]] [[weapons]], added a variety of new [[Town NPC]] dialogue lines, resprited the [[Cryonic Bar|Cryonic]] and [[Uelibloom Bar|Uelibloom]] sets, and generally polished the mod.

== Additions ==
=== [[Weapons]] ===
*Added [[Duke's Decapitator]], [[Epidemic Shredder]], [[Luminous Striker]], [[Lunar Kunai]], [[Sandslasher]], and [[Sky Stabber]].
*[[The Syringe]] is now obtainable.
*Added [[Bouncing Betty]], [[Hell's Sun]], and [[Septic Skewer]] as unobtainable items.

=== [[Fishing]] ===
*Added [[Rift Reeler|Chaotic Spread Rod]], [[The Devourer of Cods]], [[Early Bloom Rod]], [[Feral Double Rod]], [[Heron Rod]], [[Navy Fishing Rod]], [[Slurper Pole]], [[Verstaltite Fishing Rod]], and [[Wulfrum Fishing Pole]].
*Added [[Enchanted Starfish]], [[Fish of Flight]], [[Fish of Light]], and [[Fish of Night]].
*Added [[Enchanted Nightcrawler]].

=== Other ===
*Added [[Alcohol Poisoning]], [[Boss Zen]], and [[Revivify]] buffs.
*Added an item for the [[Abyss Gravel Wall]].
*Added safe variants for Abyss Gravel Wall, [[Astral Dirt Wall]], [[Astral Ice Wall]], [[Astral Sandstone Wall]], [[Astral Stone Wall]], and [[Hardened Astral Sand Wall]].
*Added [[Mod Calls]] for setting Calamity armor set bonuses, and getting and setting [[Spy]] damage, velocity, and critical strike chance.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*[[Angry Dog]]s now have a 33% chance to drop an [[Essence of Eleum]] after [[Cryogen]] has been defeated.
*[[Aquatic Scourge]] now always drops all of its items instead of only dropping them in Hardmode, or after [[Skeletron]] has been defeated in the [[Golden Bug Net]]'s case.
*[[Atlas]]es now spawn more commonly if the [[Lunatic Cultist]] has been defeated but [[Astrum Deus]] hasn't.
*The transition between [[The Devourer of Gods]]' phases now applies Boss Zen.
*[[Hive Blob]] Vile Clots now have actual sprites instead of dust, pierce through players, make light, and have sounds.
*[[Siren and Leviathan]] now always drop all of their items instead of only dropping them in Hardmode, and no longer drop the [[Enchanted Pearl]].
*Revengeance Mode Skeletron changes:
**Can no longer fire homing skulls while either of his hands are alive.
**Reduced charging speed, including in Boss Rush.
**Slightly reduced the exponential speed increases Skeletron gets while spinning.
**Can no longer fire homing skulls immediately after spinning.
**Decreased amount of homing skulls fired overall.
**Massively increased despawn range.

=== Items ===
*[[Absolute Zero]], [[Abyss Blade]], [[Ark of the Ancients]], [[Ark of the Cosmos]], [[Ark of the Elements]], [[Brimstone Sword]], [[The Burning Sky]], [[Draconic Destruction]], [[Elemental Excalibur]], [[Elemental Lance]], [[Elemental Shiv]], [[The Enforcer]], [[Exoblade]], [[Galactus Blade]], [[Gelitic Blade]], [[Hellion Flower Spear]], [[Mirror Blade]], [[Nadir]], [[Neptune's Bounty]], [[Solstice Claymore]], [[Soul Edge]], [[Starnight Lance]], [[Storm Ruler]], [[Storm Saber]], [[Temporal Floe Sword]], [[Terra Lance]], [[Terra Shiv]], [[Terror Blade]], [[True Ark of the Ancients]], [[True Caustic Edge]], [[True Excalibur Shortsword]], and [[True Night's Stabber]]'s projectiles are now briefly invisible upon spawning.
*[[Affliction]], [[Banshee Hook]], [[Daemon's Flame]], [[Ectoheart]], [[Ethereal Subjugator]], [[Fate's Reveal]], [[Ghastly Visage]], [[Ghoulish Gouger]], [[Necroplasmic Beacon]], Terror Blade, and [[Treasure Bag (Polterghast)]] now have glowmasks.
*[[Alluring Bait]] now increases the chances of catching potion ingredient fish.
*[[Armageddon]], [[Death]], [[Defiled Rune]], [[Iron Heart]], [[Revengeance]], and [[Rune of Kos]] can no longer be used during the countdown to The Devourer of Gods' second phase.
*[[Astral Beacon]]s and [[Astral Chunk]]s can now only be used in the [[Astral Infection]].
*Increased the use time of Astral Dirt Walls from 9 to 6.
*[[Auric Tesla armor]] and [[Silva armor]] now provide immunity to Sulphuric Poisoning.
*[[Celestial Claymore]]'s projectiles, [[Darklight Greatsword]]'s beams, and [[True Ark of the Ancients]]' green balls now use actual sprites instead of dust.
*Celestial Claymore's projectiles now hover towards the player.
*Replaced [[Spear of Paleolith]] with Luminous Striker in [[Celestus]]' recipe.
*Buffed [[Chaos Candle]]'s spawn rate multiplier.
*[[Cryonic Bar|Cryonic]] and [[Perennial Bar]]s can now be placed.
*Nerfed [[Dark God's Sheath]] and [[Eclipse Mirror]]'s stealth boost from 100 to 50.
*Darklight Greatsword changes:
**Projectiles now change based on the time of day, with them inflicting [[Frostburn]] at night and [[On Fire!]] at day instead of always inflicting Frostburn.
**Reduced how long the main projectile lasts from 10 seconds to 5.
**Reduced how long its split crystals last from 5 seconds to 1.
*[[Divine Retribution]] and [[Spark Spreader]] are now boosted by [[Cinnamon Roll]] and [[Fireball]].
*[[Dragon Scales]] and [[Moonstone Crown]]'s extra projectiles no longer infinitely combo off of each other.
*Dragon Scales' fireballs now have a localNPCHitCooldown value of -1.
*[[Enchanted Axe]]'s sub projectiles now last for 2.5 seconds instead of 10.
*[[Enchanted Pearl]] changes:
**Now uses the {{rare|3}} rarity instead of {{rare|7}}.
**Can now be crafted with 8 [[Crate Potion]]s, 5 Shadow Scales or Tissue Samples, 1 [[Fishing Potion]], 10 [[Sea Prism]]s, and 3 [[Victide Bar]]s.
**Now increases the chances to catch [[crates]].
*[[Frostspark Bullet]]s are now crafted at an [[Ice Machine]] instead of a [[Mythril Anvil]].
*[[Invisibility Potion]]s and [[The Twins (Lore)]] now grant specialized buffs to each Spy subclasses.
*[[Lead Tomahawk]]'s tooltip now says "damage" instead of "dmg".
*[[Lifehunt Scythe]]'s scythes no longer collide with tiles.
*[[Nanotech]]'s tooltip now mentions that it inherits [[Moonstone Crown]]'s effects.
*[[Ornate Shield]] now has an equipped sprite on the player.
*[[Potion of Omniscience]] now highlights enemy projectiles.
*[[Reaver armor]]'s spores now have a localNPCHitCooldown of 60.
*[[Revivify Potion]]s now heal the player for 6.67% of their damage taken instead of 5%.
*[[Scourge of the Seas]] is now able to be boosted by [[Evergreen Gin]].
*[[Slick Cane]] will now only steal money from enemies with more than 5 contact damage that aren't spawned from statues, and bosses.
*[[Starfury]]'s stars now always travel through tiles.
*[[Supreme Bait Tackle Box Fishing Station]] changes:
**Nerfed fishing power boost from 100 to 80.
**Crate Potion effect now stacks with Crate Potions.
**Now has 5 [[Soul of Light|Souls of Light]] and 5 [[Soul of Night|Souls of Night]] in its recipe.
*Buffed The Syringe's damage from 50 to 60.
*Calamity no longer alters [[Tavernkeep]] armor set stats.
*Buffed [[Terra-cotta]]'s knockback from 5.5 to 7.
*[[Void of Extinction]]'s fireballs now have a localNPCHitCooldown value of 60.
*Spy [[Wulfrum armor]] now grants stealth.
*Nerfed [[Zen Potion]]'s spawn rate reduction.

=== Other ===
*[[Abyssal Flames]] and [[Brimstone Flames]] can now stack with each other. 
*The [[Angler]] now drops a [[Golden Fishing Rod]] with a 8.3% chance in Hardmode. This chance is boosted to 100% if fed to a [[Trasher]].
*[[Astral Ore]], [[Chaotic Ore]], and [[Perennial Ore]] should now appear above [[Life Crystal]]s on the [[Metal Detector]].
*The [[Bandit]] now sells [[Bear's Eye]] while named "Laura", instead of the [[Archmage]].
*Greatly increased the effectiveness of Boss Zen.
*The [[Clothier]] now sells the [[Pumpkin Moon Medallion]] and [[Naughty Present]] instead of the [[Dryad]].
*Doubled the chance for the Dryad's Astral Infection dialogue to occur.
*Increased the chance for the [[Goblin Tinkerer]]'s dialogue about his pockets getting lighter to occur.
*[[Mighty Wind]] is now disabled if a boss is alive.
*Significantly decreased the mining resistance on [[Profaned Rock]], [[Profaned Slab]], and [[Runic Profaned Brick]].
*Decreased [[Smooth Voidstone]] and [[Voidstone]]'s pickaxe power from 190 to 180. <!---Only Titanium can break 190 while Adamantite is left in the dust--->
*Stealth's enemy aggro and speed effects now only take effect if the player is wearing a Spy armor.
*Renamed Vile Toxins to [[Sulphuric Poisoning]].
*The player can now be inflicted with Sulphuric Poisoning.
*[[Town NPC]]s now take 200% damage from projectiles in Normal Mode and 400% in Expert Mode to make them consistent with the player.
*Added various new dialogue lines to vanilla Town NPCs.
*[[Water]] in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] now inflicts Sulphuric Poisoning instead of [[Poisoned]].
