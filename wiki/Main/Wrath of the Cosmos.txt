The '''Wrath of the Cosmos''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spell tomes|spell tome]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Wrath of the Eldritch]]. When used, it casts a galactic pulse at the mouse cursor, which fires 2 rings of 24 homing god slayer fireballs that inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]] on intervals for a significant amount of time.

The galactic pulse will fire two Cosmic Guardians that charge towards the closest enemy every seven rounds of homing fireball rings that deal 50% of the weapon's base damage, but ignore immunity frames.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Mythical'''.
