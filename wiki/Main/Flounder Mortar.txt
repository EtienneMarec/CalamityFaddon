The '''Flounder Mortar''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]] dropped by [[Flounder]]s. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as ammo, bullets are replaced with a stream of fast acid that can pierce one enemy before dissipating upon hitting a second.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Unreal'''.
