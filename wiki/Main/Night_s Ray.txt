The '''Night's Ray''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]]. It creates a purple particle beam whichs creates 4 homing, non-piercing beams upon hitting an enemy. Hitting more than one enemy will not create more beams. It is made with [[Rotten Matter]] in [[Corruption]] worlds and is the Corruption counterpart to the [[Carnage Ray]].
