The '''Exo Box''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Mount]] summoning item that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], [[XP-00 Hypnos]], and [[XB-10 Losbaf]] or {{fantag|Catalyst}} crafted. When used, it summons a flying chair mount that behaves similar to the UFO mount summoned by the [[Cosmic Car Key]]. It has a high overall movement speed and takes very little time to accelerate to its maximum speed.

Draedon's Gamer Chair has a top speed of 61 mph both vertically and horizontally, for an overall max speed of 87 mph. Holding the {{key|Exo Chair Slow Down}} key will halve its speed, while holding the {{key|Exo Chair Speed Up}} key will double its speed.
