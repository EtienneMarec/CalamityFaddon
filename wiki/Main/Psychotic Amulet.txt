The '''Psychotic Amulet''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is rarely dropped by [[Possessed Armor]]s. It benefits both the [[Spy]] and [[Ranged weapons|Ranged]] classes, increasing the damage and critical strike chance of Spy and Ranged attacks by 5%. Standing still with this covering equipped will quickly turn the player invisible as if they were wearing [[Shroomite armor]] and give them much stronger boosts to Spy and ranged stats. These bonuses rapidly wear off if the player moves again.

Once completely invisible, the player's total bonuses include the following:
*+25% Spy damage
*+65% ranged damage
*+15% ranged and Spy critical strike chance
*+50% ranged and Spy knockback
*-750 [[Aggro]]

The ranged bonuses offered by this stealth do ''not'' stack with [[Shroomite armor]], and having this covering equipped will prevent the player from activating the set bonus of the [[Vortex armor]].

The Psychotic Amulet is used later in crafting the [[Auric Tesla armor]], which is a Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set.
