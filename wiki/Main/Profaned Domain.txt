The '''Profaned Domain''' is the only [[Subworlds|subworlds]] introduced by the Tranquility Mod. It can be accessed through the Profaned Gates which will generate in the [[Underworld]] at the [[Jungle]]-side of the map, on the far edge of the map after defeating the [[Moon Lord]] if it was defeated while the [[Nightmare Mode]] was activated in the current world. Attempting to pass through the gates without the [[Nightmare Mode]] enabled in the world will have no effect.

The subworld looks somehow similar to the [[Underworld]], but without the [[Hellstone]] and with [[Sacred Soil]] blocks instead of the [[Ash Block|ash blocks]], and the [[Holy Grass]] with the various flowers grow on the Sacred Soil blocks, the [[Lava]] also has a different color in here and contacting it inflicts the [[Holy Flames]] debuff on player. In the middle of the subworld is located a large temple, where the player should fight the [[Profaned Guardians]] and later [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]].

As soon as the player will approach the temple, the [[Profaned Guardians]] will block the player's way and the player will have to defeat them.

Inside of the temple are some rooms and a long hallway that leads to the main and the largest room of the temple. In the middle of the room, on a pedestal there's an altar, the player should put the [[Profaned Core]] onto it, in order to summon the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Sun Goddess]]. Upon placing the [[Profaned Core]], an unique spawn animation will be played, and [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Profaned Goddess]] will be summoned.

Upon defeating the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Providence]], she will fall onto the player's head. If the player will not move, the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Goddess]] will instakill them, and the player would need to fight her again.
