The '''Wrath of the Ancients''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that drops from [[Necromancer]]s in the post-[[Plantera]] [[Dungeon]]. When used, it casts an energy pulse at the mouse cursor, which fires homing spirits at intervals for a significant amount of time. It is later used to craft [[The Dance of Light]].
