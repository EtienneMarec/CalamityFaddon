The '''Flare Bolt''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that auto-fires a ball of fire that inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]]. The projectile travels in a straight line, is unaffected by gravity, emits a dim light, and can damage 3 enemies or bounce off of 3 walls before dissipating.
