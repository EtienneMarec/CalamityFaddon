The '''Brimstone Flameblaster''' is a unique [[Hardmode]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that has a 8.33% ({{expert|10%}}) chance to drop from [[Cataclysm]]. It fires a Brimstone Ball that bounces off of blocks, ricocheting up to five times before dissipating. This fireball inflicts the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] upon impacting an enemy.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The Brimstone Flameblaster differs from other flamethrowers in that it does not fire a defined stream of particles, instead it functions more like the [[Flower of Fire]]. Despite this, it is still considered a flamethrower because it consumes [[Gel]] when used and is buffed by the [[Shroomite Visage]].
