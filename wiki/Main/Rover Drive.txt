The '''Rover Drive''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Wulfrum Rover]]s. While equipped, it periodically generates a spherical energy shield around the player, which grants 15 defense while it is active. The shield lasts for 10 seconds before disappearing, and then after 20 seconds it will automatically reappear.

It is later used as an ingredient for [[The Absorber]].
