'''Essence of Rend''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that drops from certain enemies related to trauma and abrasion, such as those present in the [[Desert]] and [[Dungeon]] [[biomes]]. It is used to craft various items themed around the [[Stab (element)|Stab]] and [[Slash (element)|Slash]] [[Elemental System|elements]].
