}}The '''Gravitonomy Pike''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spears|spear]]. Each use fires an exo pike beam which passes through walls and weakly homes in on enemies. When near or touching enemies, the more pike beams are spawned and home in on nearby targets. The pike and pike beams all ignore NPC [[immunity frames]] and additionally inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
