The '''Cosmic Worm''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode item used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[The Devourer of Gods]] [[boss]]. It can be used at any time and is not consumed upon use.

Attempting to use the item while The Devourer of Gods is alive will cause [[The Devourer of Gods]] to become enraged for 60 seconds, getting 200% faster and making sharper turns.
