The '''Void Vortex''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] which is an upgrade to the [[Voltaic Climax]]. When used, it fires twelve magnetic orbs at the cursor that quickly spin out in a circular fashion away from the cursor. Each magnetic orb will rapidly fire electric beams at nearby [[enemies]], which ignore [[immunity frames]] and deal incredibly high amounts of damage per second. The electric beams will not pass through blocks, and the orbs will despawn after traveling for 3 seconds.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Mythical'''. However, the weapon's knockback will not be affected due to already being 0.
