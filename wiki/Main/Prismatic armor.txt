'''Prismatic armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set. It requires 9 [[Armored Shell]]s, 15 [[Exodium Cluster]]s, 18 [[Divine Geode]]s, and 900 [[Nanites]] to craft the entire set. If the entire set is visible (regardless of whether it is equipped or in social slots), the wearer will have a faint pulsing aura.

It consists of a '''Prismatic Helmet''', '''Prismatic Regalia''', and '''Prismatic Greaves'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*72 defense
*60% decreased non-magic damage
*40% increased magic damage
*39% increased magic critical strike chance
*+20 max life and +80 max mana
*15% decreased mana usage
*Increased mana regeneration by 9
*2% increased jump speed 
*10% increased flight time
*While no [[bosses]] are alive, any enemy with less than 500 max health deals no contact damage to the player.
*Magic attacks have a chance to fire a pair of homing explosive rockets which explode for 80% of the projectile's damage and are softcapped at 200 damage.
*Pressing {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} will cause lasers to rain down at the cursor for 5 seconds. The lasers behave identically to the [[Deathhail Staff]], dealing 30 magic damage along with inflicting the [[On Fire!]] and [[Frostburn]] [[debuffs]]. It has a 30-second cooldown after its usage.
