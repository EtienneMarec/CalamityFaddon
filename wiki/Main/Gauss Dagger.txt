The '''Gauss Dagger''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When used, the player swings a short-ranged dagger that spawns an energy flux on top of enemies when they are struck two times. The flux repeatedly damages enemies that come into contact with it for 3 seconds.

The weapon consumes 0.05 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 50.
