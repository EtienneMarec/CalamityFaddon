'''SYNTAX ERROR''' was a Godseeker Mode item that was originally used to [[Summoning items|summon]] [[THE LORDE]]. It was dropped by [[BOHLDOHR]]s in the [[Jungle Temple]] after [[Supreme Calamitas]] had been defeated.

THE LORDE was removed from the mod, and this item proceded to do nothing up until its own removal from the mod.

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions after THE LORDE was removed, the item crashed the game when used.
*It was known by the name 'NO' before the update in which THE LORDE was removed, only with a description saying 'U'. This item was later renamed as 'SYNTAX ERROR'.
*Despite being an item, this item looks similar to the graphic of a buff.
