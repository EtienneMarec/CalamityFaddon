The '''Space''' layer features three new [[enemies]], including a [[mini boss]]. Numerous [[Planetoid]]s spawn in this layer, containing an abundance of [[ore]], [[Life Crystal]]s, and other loot. The [[Storm Weaver]] boss is summoned and fought in this layer.

In [[Death Mode]], the player will be inflicted with [[Burning]] and [[Frostburn]] during the day and night respectively without [[Death Mode#Temperature Protective Gear|heat and cold immunity gear]], unless standing in front of any type of [[wall]].
