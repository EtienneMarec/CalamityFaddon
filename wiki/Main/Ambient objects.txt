'''Ambient objects''' are unobtainable, naturally-generated background objects that function primarily as visual ambience, and can be broken with any [[pickaxe]]. Most of them cause no effects, aside from Small Brain Coral which will generate bubbles which grant the player the [[Gills]] buff for half a second, Brain Coral which generate bubbles that grant players the Gills buff for 1.5 seconds, Table Coral which can be stood on, and Fan Coral which will produce light. The Calamity Mod adds various ambient objects throughout most of the Calamity [[biomes]].
