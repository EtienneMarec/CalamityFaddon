The '''Aorta''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Yoyos|yoyo]] that is also dropped from [[The Perforators]]. When held out it fires a homing blood clot towards nearby enemies that deals 25% of the weapon's base damage.

== Notes ==
*Much like the [[Air Spinner]], this yoyo is especially nimble and can move very quickly while in use.
*This weapon does not have a [[Corruption]] variant.

== Trivia ==
*It shares its name with the [[wikipedia:aorta|aorta]], which is the largest [[wikipedia:artery|artery]] in the body.
**Coincidentally, both '''Aorta''' and [[tgc:Artery|Artery]] are yoyos that are based off a part of the human body, specifically. the artery.
