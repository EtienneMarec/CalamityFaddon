'''Astral armor''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Astral Bar]]s and [[Meteorite Bar]]s. It requires 30 Astral Bars and 23 Meteorite Bars to craft the entire set. Astral armor is unique because it permanently grants the effects of [[Dangersense Potion|Dangersense]], [[Hunter Potion|Hunter]], and [[Spelunker Potion]]s while it is equipped, allowing its wearer to see all nearby creatures, treasure, ores, and traps. If all three pieces of Astral armor are visible (whether equipped or in vanity slots), the player will have afterimages following them.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*63 defense
*35% increased damage
*25% increased critical strike chance
*15% increased movement speed
*+80 max mana
*+20 max life
*+3 max minions
*Creature, treasure, ore, and danger detection
*With a 1-second cooldown, [[critical hit]]s will cause three stars - fallen, hallowed and astral - to rain down on the target that deal 120 damage.
**Fallen stars decrease their damage by 6.25% each time they strike an enemy.
**Astral stars inflict the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]] for 2 seconds.
**Hallowed stars go through blocks but disappear upon striking an enemy.

== Notes ==
*Stars are only generated from enemies that have a damage stat of 5 or higher, with the exception of enemies spawned from [[statues]].

== Tips ==
*This armor has significantly lower bonuses for the summoner class relative to other classes, as minions cannot benefit from critical strike chance increases. This results in the stars not spawning from minions.
**However, with the addition of whips in 1.4 versions of the mod and due to the high damage boost, minion slots and movement speed increase that this armor provides, it can be still be efficient, especially for hybrid builds.
