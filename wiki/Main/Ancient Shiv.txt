The '''Ancient Shiv''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is dropped by [[Dark Caster]]s. Upon killing an enemy with this sword, they will release a small blue aura cloud that has infinite pierce and lingers for 5 seconds. The cloud deals 50% damage.

While it has an incredibly short range like most [[shortsword]]s, it also has auto-swing, rendering it slightly more effective.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, the player gains 5% melee speed for 4 seconds.
