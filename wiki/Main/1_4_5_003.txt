The '''1.4.5.003''' (or '''1.4.5.3''') update overhauled how the [[Charging Station]] and [[Power Cell Factory]] function, as well as introduced several boss adjustments and bug fixes. This update also removed all previous Charging Stations, [[Lab Hologram Projector]]s, and Power Cell Factories predating this version.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Auralis]]. <!--Patreon Donar: Goliath-->
*Added [[Ball and Chain]].
*Added [[Bloodfire Bullet]]s.
*Added [[God Slayer Slug]]s as [[unobtainable items]].
*Added an event progress bar for the [[Boss Rush]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*Switched [[Aquatic Scourge]] and [[Brimstone Elemental]]'s progression places. The [[Acid Rain]] tier 2 event and [[Unholy Core]] set are still locked behind their corresponding bosses.

==== [[Anahita and Leviathan]] ====
*Anahita in [[Death Mode]] now has increased velocity and spends less time firing bubbles towards the player.
*The Leviathan now charges twice in a row in Death Mode and moves faster.
*Both can no longer charge at the player from the beginning of the fight in Death Mode.

==== [[Aquatic Scourge]] ====
*Now fires a wider spread of sand blasts while circling, and increases its velocity and acceleration depending on how far away it is from the player as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*[[Aquatic Parasite]]s and [[Aquatic Urchin]]s no longer have increased health during Boss Rush.

==== [[Astrum Aureus]] ====
*Now fires more lasers, moves quicker, and has decreased attack delay as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Changed Aureus Spawn health from {{dv|1012|1007}} to 1008 in all modes.

==== [[Astrum Deus]] ====
*Now gradually reaches max velocity as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Brain of Cthulhu]] ====
*Now gains increased movement velocity and decreased attack delay as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*[[Creeper]]s now have increased velocity in Death Mode.

==== [[Brimstone Elemental]] ====
*Now moves quicker as its health decreases, and fires more projectiles during its cocoon phase in Death Mode.

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Now summons more [[Soul Seeker]]s that have less health in Death Mode.
*Now increases her velocity and acceleration while decreasing attack delay as her health decreases.
*[[Cataclysm]] and [[Catastrophe]] now move quicker and have decreased attack delay as their health decreases.

==== [[Ceaseless Void]] ====
*Now spawns more [[Dark Energy|Dark Energies]] and moves quicker as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Reduced potency of the enrage based on nearby tiles.

==== [[Crabulon]] ====
*Now moves quicker, spawns more [[Crab Shroom]]s, and has decreased attack delay as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Cryogen]] ====
*No longer deals increased contact damage in its final phase.

==== [[Desert Scourge]] ====
*Can now dive and lunge towards the player while burrowing in all world difficulties. A new sound effect is played before this occurs.
*Now larger, has more body segments, burrows more often, and gains increased velocity and acceleration as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Can now fly during its uncoiling phase in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Now longer, and increases in velocity and acceleration as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Laser walls now use enums, and will now always be synced up properly.
*The player can no longer be cheaply shot by its fireballs.
*No longer fires a spread of lasers during phase 1, and will now fire a telegraphed barrage of lasers that travel towards the player from both ends of the screen in an X-shape.
**These are now occasionally present during the Phase 2 laser walls.
*Now creates a portal to telegraph where it will appear after teleporting.

==== [[The Dragonfolly]] ====
*Enrage scale in Death Mode is now a bit larger, which causes it to have increased velocity and overall aggression.

==== [[Duke Fishron]] ====
*Now has increased attack delay, no longer transitions to phase 2 at the start of the fight, and its map icon is now invisible for the entirety of phase 3 in Death Mode.

==== [[Eater of Worlds]] ====
*Can now fly at players out of reach more easily, and increases in velocity and acceleration as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Now splits during [[Boss Rush]].

==== [[Eye of Cthulhu]] ====
*No longer transitions into Phase 2 at the start of the fight, and now charges faster and more often as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Now increases in velocity as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Head can now fire lasers from the start of the fight, and fires more often as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Free Golem Head no longer deals contact damage.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Bullet hell teleports are now always above the player rather than random.
*Fire ring released on its death is now possible to dodge.
*Now has a decreased attack delay in Death Mode.

==== [[King Slime]] ====
*Now moves faster, only spawns Spiked Slimes, no longer summons its Crown Jewel at the start of the fight, and is much larger in Death Mode.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*No longer deals contact damage.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Moon Lord and [[True Eye of Cthulhu|True Eyes of Cthulhu]] are now more aggressive in Death Mode.
*True Eyes of Cthulhu no longer deal contact damage.
*True Eyes of Cthulhu no longer use their death ray attack in Revengeance Mode and higher.

==== [[The Old Duke]] ====
*Now teleports a bit further away from the player in phase 3.
*Now has increased attack delay and no longer transitions to phase 2 at the start of the fight in Death Mode.

==== [[The Perforators]] ====
*The Perforator Hive's projectiles now rain in a consistent spread instead of at random.
*All Perforator Worms no longer fire Blood clots, and all Perforator Worms now have increased size and body segments based on world difficulty.
**Significantly reduced the speed of the Small Perforator Worm in all difficulties, buffed health from {{dv|1250|1749|2099}} to {{dv|1250|2125|2550}}
**Medium Perforator Worm can now split similarly to Eater of Worlds, changed health from {{dv|2000|2799|3079}} total to {{dv|200|279|307}} per segment.
**Large Perforator Worm can now burrow and lunge towards the player, buffed health from {{dv|2500|3499|3779}} to {{dv|2500|5000|5400}}.

==== [[Plaguebringer Goliath]] ====
*Now has a buffed enrage scale in Death Mode, causing it to have increased velocity, servant spawn rate, and stinger/missile fire rate.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Can now fire a single thorn ball in phase 2.
*Velocity and acceleration, maximum hook reach, and the number of tentacles that can spawn increase as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Plantera's tentacles move quicker in Death Mode.
*Reduced potency of the enrage based on nearby tiles.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Adjusted tile enrage to affect its projectiles more.
*Now charges towards the player at certain intervals.
*Phantom blasts no longer home in and instead accelerate over time.
*Mines no longer deal any damage until they've reached max velocity, and mine velocity is now affected by Polterghast's tile enrage.
*How often Polterghast's hooks move is now based on how close the player is to them.
*Polterghast's hooks can fire projectiles again.
*Now transitions into Phase 3 later in the fight in Death Mode.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] ====
*Attack delay now decreases as her health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Queen Bee]] ====
*Now has a buffed enrage scale in Death Mode, causing it to have increased velocity, boss servant spawning rate, and stinger fire rate.
*No longer spawns [[Hornet]]s in Revengeance Mode, and will instead spawn large [[Bee]]s with 40% more health.

==== [[Ravager]] ====
*No longer deals contact damage while preparing a stomp during phase 2.
*Ravager Head AI has been reworked, it now fires three lasers in a barrage including a rocket while detached.
*Rock Pillars now always deal contact damage and are formed further away.
*Ravager Nukes can only home onto the player during the first 5 seconds of it being spawned, and no longer spawn during phase 2.
*Stomp is now much more lenient with its player targeting range.
*Greatly decreased speed in Death Mode.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Cosmic Scythes now sharply return to their fired position after traveling for five seconds. Cosmic Scythes are now fired in bursts up to 3 depending on how much health Signus has. 
*Now attacks {{dv|4|3|2|1}} time(s) per-phase and now cycles through its phases faster depending on how much health Signus has.
*Buffed Cosmic Mine health from 100 to 6000.

==== [[Skeletron]] ====
*Skeletron can now respawn all four of its hands at 33% health in Death Mode.
*Nerfed Skeletron Head health in Death Mode from {{death|11,000}} to {{death|4400}}.
*Two chaos balls are now spawned in addition to its white magic projectiles after each teleport, fires skulls more often with extra velocity and Head accelerates quicker as its health decreases in Death Mode.
*Its hands accelerate quicker in Death Mode.
*Revengeance Mode stat changes:
**Nerfed Skeletron Head health from {{revengeance|11,000}} to {{revengeance|6600}}.
**Buffed Skeletron Hand health from {{revengeance|1170}} to {{revengeance|11,232}}.

==== [[Skeletron Prime]] ====
*Nerfed Prime Saw and Vice health from {{revengeance|13,500}} to {{revengeance|8775}}, Prime Laser health from {{revengeance|9000}} to {{revengeance|5850}}, and Prime Cannon health from {{revengeance|10,500}} to {{revengeance|6825}} in Revengeance Mode.
**[[Draedon's Remote]] scaling for Skeletron Prime's limbs decreased from x1.05 to x0.8.
*Skeletron Prime and all of its limbs will increase in velocity and acceleration as its health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====
*Lightning portals now spawn much closer to the player.
*Now larger and longer in Death Mode.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
*Now plays a sound effect each time she fires a projectile.

==== [[The Twins]] ====
*No longer transition into Phase 2 at the start of the fight, transition into Phase 3 later in the fight, have reduced attack delay, and increase in velocity and acceleration as their health decreases in Death Mode.

==== [[Wall of Flesh]] ====
*No longer fires Death Lasers at the start of the fight, now enrages easier, and moves faster as its health decreases in Death Mode.

=== Recipes ===
*[[Bloodfire Arrow]]s are now crafted in sets of 333 instead of 250.
*[[Hallowed Rune]] and [[Tundra Flame Blossoms Staff]] now require a [[Hardmode Anvil]] to craft.
*[[System Bane]] now requires 20 [[Dubious Plating]], 10 [[Mysterious Circuitry]], 5 [[Life Alloy]]s and 5 [[Infected Armor Plating]] to craft.

=== Renames ===
*Renamed Draedon's Charging Station to Charging Station.
*Renamed Draedon's Fuel Factory to Power Cell Factory.
*Renamed Fabsol (item) to [[Princess Spirit in a Bottle]].
*Renamed Holographic Display Box to Lab Hologram Projector.
*Renamed Turret to [[Lab Turret]].
