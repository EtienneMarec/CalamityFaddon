The '''Bucket of Coal''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]]. It throws gravity affected buckets that spin as they travel. Every 20 frames or 1/3 of a second, a piece of coal will be flung out in the direction of the bucket's surface. Coal pieces deal 10% of the weapon's damage. Upon hitting an enemy, the bucket creates a small black damaging explosion.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Bucket of Coal will cause the flung chunks of coal to grow in size and damage as they travel. The damage increase is a flat 1 damage per tile traveled, and the size increase is by a factor of 1.05x.
