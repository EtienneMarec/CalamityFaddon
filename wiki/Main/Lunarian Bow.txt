The '''Lunarian Bow''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bow]] that auto-fires. It fires two arrows. If [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], it instead fires straight beams that are not affected by gravity and can bounce on a block once; however, the bolts will slide on the hit block rather than bounce off of it.
