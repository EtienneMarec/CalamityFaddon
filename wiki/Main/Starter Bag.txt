The '''Starter Bag''' is a [[grab bag]] item that is given to every newly spawned player. Right-clicking on the bag while it is in the inventory will give the player many items, all of which will help them progress through the beginning of the game more quickly and easily. 

If opening the bag in a world that is in [[Expert Mode]] or [[Master Mode]], the bag will also contain three difficulty-changing items called [[Revengeance]], [[Death]], and [[Tormented Mind of Malice|Malice]], which will enable [[Revengeance Mode]], [[Death Mode]], and [[Malice Mode]] respectively in increasing order of difficulty, and where each next difficulty can only be unlocked by enabling the previous mode.
