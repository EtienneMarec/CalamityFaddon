'''Angelinite Ore''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ore]] which generates anywhere in the Floating Islands upon defeating [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. It is used to craft [[Angelinite Bar]]s at a [[tgc:Adamantite Forge|Adamantite Forge]] or [[tgc:Titanium Forge|Titanium Forge]]. It requires at least [[Any Lunar Pickaxe]] to mine. It is also dropped by [[Angel Slime]]s.
