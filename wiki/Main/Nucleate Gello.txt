The '''Nucleate Gello''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Cragmaw Mire]]s. While worn it will summon a Nucleate to jump at enemies and stick to them, dealing a base of 40 damage, scaling with the player's summon damage stat and inflicting [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] and [[Irradiated]]. The covering also pacifies the minions spawned by [[King Slime]], [[Queen Slime]], [[The Slime God]], and [[Astrum Aureus]].
