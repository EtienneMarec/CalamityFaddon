'''Vigilance''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Supreme Calamitas]]. It summons a soul seeker which flies in place at a set distance from the player, forming a ring around the player if multiple seekers are present. When enemies are nearby, the seekers will rapidly fire Brimstone Darts similar to those fired by Supreme Calamitas, then charge back and forth at great speeds.
