The '''Golden Gun''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]] that shoots yellow ichor projectiles, and does not belong to any damage class. Its damage cannot be increased by any stat bonuses. Its projectile inflicts the [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] [[debuff]] to enemies, which makes it useful to weaken bosses regardless of one's choice of class. This weapon does not consume any ammo or mana.
