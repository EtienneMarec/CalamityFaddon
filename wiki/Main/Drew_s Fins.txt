'''Drew's Fins''' are a Godseeker Mode [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allow the player to fly and glide, dropped by [[Garon]]. They are used in crafting the [[Celestial Tracers]] and [[Holy Mantle]].

== Notes ==
*The flight stats of Drew's Fins are near-identical to that of Celestial Tracers, except that Drew's Fins have superior flight time by a value of 111 frames. However, Celestial Tracers has improved horizontal acceleration compared to Drew's Fins due to its inherited boot functionality, even in the air.

== Tips ==
*Some players may prefer using Drew's Fins against [[Supreme Calamitas]] instead of the Celestial Tracers, not only for the increased flight time but also the reduced acceleration giving players tighter control over their movement.

== Trivia ==
*Drew's Fins are a recolor of the [[Fishron Fins]].
