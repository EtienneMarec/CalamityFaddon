'''Abyssal Jewel''' (Japanese: 深淵の宝石 ''Shin'en no hōseki'') is a Post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Superbosses|Superboss]] that can be spawned in the [[Sky Abyss]] biome.

==Spawn==
Abyssal Jewel doesn't spawn on its own and requires the player to use [[Expensive Jewelry]] while in the Sky Abyss biome. 

==Behavior==
1st phase attack cycle

#The Boss will hover near the player, Summoning Lacelic Eels
#It will stay at one place, charge for a few seconds and start shooting {{dv|5|6|7|8}} slow Beams that will do a full circle around the boss, similar to the Ogscule
#it will teleport around, Creating slow spirals, similar to the Empress of Light
#Teleports below the player and dashes to the top


2nd phase attack cycle (50%)

#It will create 4 semicircle shields, that disappear over time
#It will teleport around and dash into the player, summoning Jewel Swords on contact
#Stays at one place and shoots faster beams


3rd phase attack cycle (25%)

#Teleports around, shooting {{dv|4|6|8|12}} Jewel Crystals in a circle
#Stays at one place, summoning  {{dv|3|6|9|12}} Lacelic Eels
#Its core starts spinning, Shooting {{dv|20|30|40|60}} fast moving projectiles in random direction
