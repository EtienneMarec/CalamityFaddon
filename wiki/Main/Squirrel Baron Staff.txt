The '''Squirrel Baron Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and attack enemies by throwing sharp [[Prism Shard]]s that pierce once then split into 2-3 more shards which don't pierce. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
