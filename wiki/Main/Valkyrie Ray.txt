The '''Valkyrie Ray''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. When used, it charges for half a second, then releases a straight magenta laser beam that fades out very shortly after. The beam pierces infinitely, cannot pass through tiles, and cannot be aimed after its release. The beam also partially ignores enemy [[immunity frames]], and will strike enemies 6 times per shot if the beam remains in contact with them.

== Notes ==
