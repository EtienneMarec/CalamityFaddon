'''Dark Statis''' is a hostile [[enemy]] version of [[Statis]].

Dark Statis spawns 20 to 40 seconds after a player reaches zero [[Stamina]] three times without dying, emerging from a crack in the ground and creating a circle of damaging plague flames around him in his wake.  

Dark Statis continuously pursues the player by floating towards them, speeding up when further away and slowing down to a near standstill when within a fixed distance. If the player remains within that fixed distance for a short period of time, Dark Statis will perform a fiery plague charge attack in which he will pause for a brief moment with a sound cue before rushing towards them, damaging any enemies in his path.

Dark Statis is damageable as long as the player's cursor is on him. Killing Dark Statis will cause a colossal explosion similar to [[XF-09 Ares]]' Gauss Nuke. If the player's cursor isn't on Dark Statis, he will still be able to block enemy projectiles without being damaged.

== Notes ==
*Dark Statis' charge also ignores reactive [[damage reduction]].
Dark Statis' position at all times is determined by his position relative to the world, meaning he persists in the same [[subworld]] even when the player teleports to another one. Entering a different subworld cancels Statis' charge if he was preparing.

== Trivia ==
*Dark Statis is a direct homage to Dark Esau from ''[[wikipedia:The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth|The Binding of Isaac: Repentance]]''.
