The '''Corvid Harbinger Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Raven Staff]]. It summons a dark raven minion that flies around and rapidly charges towards enemies, occasionally teleporting near them and leaving a ring of dust before continuing its charge.

== Trivia ==
*The word corvid is derived from [[wikipedia:Corvidae|Corvidae]], the scientific family containing birds such as crows and ravens.
*The item's tooltip is a direct reference to "[[wikipedia:The Raven|The Raven]]", a poem by [[wikipedia:Edgar Allan Poe|Edgar Allan Poe]].
*This weapon can be seen as a counterpart of the [[Endo Hydra Staff]], as the two summon weapon's recipes contain post-[[Plantera]] items and have similar [[Crafting materials]].
