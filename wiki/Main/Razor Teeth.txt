The '''Razor Teeth''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Daggers|dagger]] that is dropped by the [[Acidsighter]]. It throws a gravity affected tooth that pierces twice and explodes into acid shots when hitting enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] throws three homing teeth that stick onto enemies and inflict [[Venom|Acid Venom]].

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Flawless'''.
