The '''Seismic Hampick''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Pickaxe]] and [[Hammer]] combination capable of mining blocks up to {{item|Lihzahrd Brick}} and {{item|Scoria Ore}}. Left clicking allows it to be used as a pickaxe, while right clicking allows it to be used as a hammer. Both modes automatically swing, emit fiery particles, and inflict the [[On Fire!]] debuff on contact.
