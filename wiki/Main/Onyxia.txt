The '''Onyxia''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that is the direct upgrade of the [[Onyx Chain Blaster]] and the final upgrade to the [[tgc:Shotgun|Shotgun]]. It rapidly fires bursts of six bullets at a time alongside an onyx blast that deals 145% the damage of the fired [[bullets]]. The bullets from the Onyxia are fired in 3 symmetric pairs surrounding the onyx blast. It has a 50% chance to not consume ammo when fired.
