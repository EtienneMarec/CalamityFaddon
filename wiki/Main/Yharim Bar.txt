'''Yharim Bars''' are Godseeker Mode [[bars]] that are dropped by bosses, specifically [[The Fabricational Quartet|The The Fabricational Quartet]], [[XP-00 Hypnos]], [[Supreme Calamitas]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], and [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. They are used to craft various items themed around the Tyrant, as well as a myriad of other equipment. They can also be transmogrified by the [[Starborn Princess]] into several other important [[crafting materials]].

==Notes==
* Dropped Yharim Bars are 16 times their normal size, as shown above, and can be picked up from anywhere on them. They keep their normal size in the inventory.
* Perceptive players may notice that the Yharim Bars have 'subpixeling'. However, this was actually intended by the developer of the mod, Fabsol, on the mod's Patreon Skype group.
