The '''Gruesome Eminence''' is a Godseeker Mode [[magic weapon]]. It is the [[exhumed]] form of the [[Ghastly Visage]]. It fires a spirit monster that poorly follows the cursor and occasionally moves around randomly on its own. As time passes, it spawns uncontrollable spirits which deal damage (ranging from {{dv|70|140
}} to {{dv|185|370}} depending on size) to players and [[Town NPC]]s. After 14 seconds, it grows to full size and becomes fully controllable by the player, but also stop releasing spirits. The damage of the spirit gradually builds up from 1x to 3.3x as it builds up to full size, but will also deal from {{dv|125|250}} to {{dv|250|500}} damage to the player until fully grown.
