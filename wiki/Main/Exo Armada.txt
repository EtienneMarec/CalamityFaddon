The '''Exo Arnada (Japanese: エクソ・アルナダ Ekuso arunada)''' is an [[enemy]] found on the [[Surface]] during daytime, or in [[The Exosphere]]. It attacks by hovering above the ground and diving downward before activating an energy shield, and charging the player. During its charge, all player projectiles will be deflected.

It can be supercharged by the [[Exo Obelisk]] enemy, causing its lights to turn bright yellow, and giving a significant boost to its speed.
