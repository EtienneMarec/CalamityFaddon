The '''Craw Carapace''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Crawdad]]s in the [[Cavern]] layer. It increases [[defense]] by 3, damage reduction by 5%, and makes enemies receive 25% of the damage they deal to player, similar to the [[Thorns]] effect.

== Notes ==
*Some worlds may never spawn [[Crawdad]]s, due to the unique spawn mechanic they share with [[Salamander]]s and [[Giant Shelly|Giant Shellies]], making this item unobtainable there. However, one can still find the [[Giant Shell]] and craft [[The Absorber]] in these worlds.
*This item stacks with other Thorns effects.
