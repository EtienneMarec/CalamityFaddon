The '''Coral Cannon''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that can be purchased from the [[Sea King]] at any time for {{gc|2}}. It fires a small piece of coral when used, and does not consume any ammo. There is a 20% chance for the coral to be larger, with twice as much damage and knockback, a slightly slower velocity, and the ability to briefly stun normal enemies.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The stun effect will not apply if any [[bosses]] are alive or to certain rare creatures.
