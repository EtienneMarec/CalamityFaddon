{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.5.0.001 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.5.0.001''' (or '''1.5.0.1''') Music mod update added a music track for the [[adult Eidolon Wyrm]], a placeholder music track for the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], and unobtainable music boxes for the [[Boss Rush]] [[event]].

This Music Mod version was compatible with the Main mod version [[1.5.0.001]].

== Additions ==
*Added "Threat of The Ocean Floor", the music track for the adult Eidolon Wyrm.
*Added "Inferior Fabrications", the placeholder music track for the The Fabricational Quartet.
*Added three music tracks that are intended to play during the Boss Rush event. They can only be heard from their corresponding music boxes.
**"Ensemble of Fools", the music track for the Boss Rush tier 1.
**"Onslaught of Beasts", the music track for the Boss Rush tier 2.
**"Reign of Lords", the music track for the Boss Rush tier 3.
*Added [[Music Box (Adult Eidolon Wyrm)]].
*Added [[Music Box (The Fabricational Quartet)]].
*Added [[Music Box (Boss Rush - Tier 1)]], [[Music Box (Boss Rush - Tier 2)]], and [[Music Box (Boss Rush - Tier 3)]] as [[unobtainable items]].
*Added [[Music Box (Boss Rush - Tier 4)]] and [[Music Box (Boss Rush - Tier 5)]] as non-functioning unobtainable items. <!-- Boss Rush T4 and T5 music boxes are unfinished and do not work as their corresponding music does not exist - Merkalto -->

== Changes ==
*[[Music Box (Yharon, Rebirth)]] is now crafted at a [[Cosmic Anvil]] instead of at a [[Draedon's Forge]].
*Resprited the Mod icon.
