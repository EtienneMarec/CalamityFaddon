The '''Storm Saber''' is a Pre-hardmode [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It has a chance to be dropped from [[Aether Valkyrie]]s. When swung, it fires a slow and homing glowing sword-like projectile from the blade, and another from the sky towards the cursor. The blade projectile deals 80% of the weapon's base damage, whereas the sky projectile deals 60%.
