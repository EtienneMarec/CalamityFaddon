<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> (Japanese: '''大禍の''' ''Dai wazawai no'') is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] challenge required to be fought as the first challenge, even before the [[Wulfrum Excavator]]. Although it is the first challenge the player will encounter, it is likely a considerable challenge even to experienced players. 

==Spawn==
<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> can be spawned in any biome by right clicking the [[Awakening]] [[lore item]].
==Behavior==
<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font>'s battle is unique as <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> will be automatically defeated after all players have survived for 1 minute and 30 seconds after summoning <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font>. During the fight, <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> is immune to any form of damage and will remain stationary during both phases.

In its first phase, <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> summons 2 {{item|Dark_Vein|nolink=yes|ext=png|maxsize=42px|Dark Veins}} from random locations that fire a spread of death lasers at the player.

During its second phase, <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> utilizes more difficult attacks:

*<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''Calamity'''</font> Lasers replace Dark Veins, which telegraph their direction before shooting but are faster than death lasers.
*<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> will begin to shoot death lasers from its eye at the nearest player.

*Many {{item|Darkscule|ext=png|maxsize=42px|Darkscules}} float at random locations around the player and explode into <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''Calamity'''</font> Lasers. If the player gets too close, the [[Darkscule|Darkscules]] immediately explode into more <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''Calamity'''</font> Lasers.

==Aftermath==  

*All other challenges are able to be summoned.

==Secret Encounter==
By throwing the <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font> lore item into Shimmer upon defeating every challenge up to and including Yharim (mini-challenges are not required but standard challenges are), as well as the Challenge Rush, it will become <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The True Calamity'''</font>, and can be used to summon <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font>, a vastly upgraded version of <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''The Calamity'''</font>. It will take the player into the [[Unknown Space]] subworld and begin the encounter. Similar to its standard version, the "fight" is a survival challenge where the player must survive for ten minutes.

===Attacks===
<font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font> will constantly fire a deluge of <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font> Lasers at the player. These are unique for projectiles as they inflict no debuff directly, but will actually reverse time and temporarily trap the affected player, meaning that all attacks launched within the previous five seconds will return to the sender. In multiplayer, this means the other players must anticipate where the reversed attacks will come from, as getting hit could potentially result in a stun lock chaining from player to player. In addition, <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font> will occasionally spawn Echoes, which take the form of greyed out illusions of previous challenges and perform weakened and easier to dodge versions of their attacks. Echoes are scaled, meaning Echoes taking the form of early-game challenges (such as the Brain of Cthulhu) will have much higher attack, health, and attack pattern difficulty compared to Echoes of endgame challenges such as Supreme Calamitas. When an Echo is alive, the survival timer pauses but cannot be increased by <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font> Lasers. <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''<code>Finality</code>'''</font> will spawn new Echoes every 45 seconds.
