The '''Quantum Flahium Militia Device''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It grants all effects of the [[Phantomic Soul Artifact]] and [[Nucleogenesis]], with sparks dealing 120 summon damage. It provides +6 minion slots and +3 sentry slots, which does not stack with downgrades, 55% increased summon damage, +167% minion knockback, 50% increased whip range, 25% increased whip speed, and grants minions and sentries the ability to deal critical hits at a fixed 10% chance when any whip is used.
