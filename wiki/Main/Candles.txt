'''Candles''' are pieces of [[furniture]] that emit light. The Calamity Mod currently adds twenty-one types of candles to the game, with fifteen corresponding to specific [[Furniture sets|sets of furniture]], two acting as upgrades to the [[Water Candle]] and [[Peace Candle]], and the remaining four being craftable with a Peace Candle, and a large amount of souls and essences. For the purpose of housing [[Town NPCs]], Chaos and Tranquility candles and Stat candles do not count as a light source. Additionally, Stat candles cannot be toggled by wire, unlike all other candles.
