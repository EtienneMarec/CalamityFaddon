The '''Surface''' layer features a new [[critter]] & eight new [[enemies]], which spawn at varying points throughout the game. A [[shrine]] structure, which contains the [[Trinket of Chi]] [[covering]], can be found somewhere within this layer.

In [[Death Mode]], during [[Rain]], lightning will occasionally strike down and inflict [[Electrified]].

== Trivia ==
*The Surface Purity is one of the only biomes affected by the Calamity Mod that does not have a [[boss]] or [[mini boss]] associated with it. However, bosses that are not bound by biome requirements, such as [[The Slime God]] or the [[Ravager]], are typically fought within this biome.
