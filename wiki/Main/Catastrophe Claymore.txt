The '''Catastrophe Claymore''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that auto-swings. Upon swing, it fires out one of three damaging projectiles, which will inflict numerous [[debuffs]] on hit, depending on their color. Hitting an enemy with the blade itself has a chance to inflict multiple debuffs all at once, albeit with a shorter duration than the projectiles.
