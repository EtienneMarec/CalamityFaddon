The '''Cadaverous Carrion''' is a Godseeker Mode [[sentry]] that drops from tier 3 [[Flak Crab]]s. It summons a geyser sentry, the sentry rapidly fires homing sulphurous cross blasts at nearby enemies.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Trivia ==
* 'Cadaverous' means "resembling a corpse", and 'carrion' is "the decaying flesh of an animal".
