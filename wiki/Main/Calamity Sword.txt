The '''Calamity Sword''' is a direct upgrade to the [[Swordsplosion]]. It inflicts On Fire and Brimstone Flames. It fires sword beams, If the sword beam hits an enemy, it explodes into tiny sword fragments and home in to foes shortly after, the tiny sword beams deal 25% of the weapons damage. The weapon also gets a damage boost if you are in the [[Brimstone Crag|Brimstone Crags]] (Up to a 3x damage multiplier)
