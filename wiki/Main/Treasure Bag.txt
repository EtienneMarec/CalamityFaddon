'''Treasure Bags''' are consumable [[grab bag]]s obtained in [[Expert Mode]] as a reward for defeating [[boss]]es. Its contents depend on the boss it comes from. It is opened by either right-clicking it inside the inventory or using it while it is in your hand. In multiplayer, each player that is online at the end of the battle receives one Treasure Bag each. Treasure bags are client-side and will despawn if you exit the world.

Each Treasure Bag contains the boss's usual drops from normal mode, and some contain Expert Mode-exclusive items (<span style="padding:1px 5px; background:#285128">green-highlighted items</span> below), [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive items (<span style="padding:1px 5px; background:#551518">red-highlighted items</span> below) or [[Death Mode]]-exclusive items (<span style="padding:1px 5px; background:#540070">purple-highlighted items</span> below).
