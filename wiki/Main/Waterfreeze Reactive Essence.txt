'''Waterfreeze Reactive Essence''' is a Post-[[The Entropic God, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Crafting materials|crafting material]], it is dropped from every enemy in the [[Snow biome]], [[Ocean]], [[Space]] and [[Abyss]] with a 10% chance. It is used to craft various Post-Umbrus cold-themed ranged equipment.
