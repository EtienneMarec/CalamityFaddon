'''Exodium Cluster''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ore]] that generates in [[Planetoid]]s after the Moon Lord has been defeated. It requires at least one of the [[Luminite Pickaxe]]s to mine. It is also dropped by [[Side Mollusk]]s. It is used to craft several items, including Stratus equipment as well as Soul Artifact [[coverings]].
