The '''Scourge of the Seas''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[javelin]] dropped by the [[Aquatic Scourge]] that is an indirect upgrade to the [[Scourge of the Desert]]. When used it automatically throws a javelin that gains velocity while in flight. Thrown javelins are slightly affected by gravity and when contact is made with an enemy, they will explode into toxic clouds. The javelin and clouds inflict the [[Venom]] [[debuff]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Scourge of the Seas will cause the next javelin and its spawned clouds to inflict the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] debuff.
