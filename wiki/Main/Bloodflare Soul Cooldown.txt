'''Bloodflare Soul Cooldown''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted to the player after activating {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} while wearing the Ranged [[Bloodflare armor|Bloodflare]] or [[Auric Tesla armor|Auric Tesla]] armors. It prevents the player from using the [[Bloodflare Soul]] bonus again for 30 seconds.
