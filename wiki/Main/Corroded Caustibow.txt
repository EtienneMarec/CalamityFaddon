The '''Corroded Caustibow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]]. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], It fires slow gravity-affected toxic shells that emit a wet aura along its path which deals 15% of the weapon's damage and inflicts the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]]. The toxic shell itself pierces five enemies and breaks upon contact with tiles, inflicting both Irradiated and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] onto enemies.
