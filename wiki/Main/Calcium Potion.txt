The '''Calcium Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Calcium''' [[buff]], causing the player to become immune to all fall damage.

== Notes ==
*The Calcium Potion can be used to negate all fall damage when riding a mount such as the [[Slimy Saddle]].
*The Calcium Potion will also negate fall damage from the [[Stoned]] debuff.
