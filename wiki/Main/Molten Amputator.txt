The '''Molten Amputator''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] that drops from [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. Each time the boomerang strikes an enemy, a lava glob flies out from the impact. Lava globs will remain on the ground for quite some time and each glob can damage an enemy once before disappearing.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Molten Amputator will cause the next scythe thrown to occasionally release Molten Globs as it travels. Additionally, enemies will release more globs when they are hit.
