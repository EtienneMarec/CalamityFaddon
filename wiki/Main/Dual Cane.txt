The '''Dual Cane''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. Each use fires a black orb and a white orb which collectively move in a somewhat wide helix pattern.

== Notes ==
*Unlike the [[Laser Machinegun]] and the Nano Purge, this weapon does not increase in fire rate over time. After the initial non-firing spin-up, it fires its lasers at a constant high speed.
*This weapon does not consume mana while spinning up, it is only consumed once it has begun firing lasers.

== Trivia ==
*The movement pattern of the orbs is a reference to the [https://metroid.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_Beam Wave Beam] from the ''[[wikipedia:Metroid|Metroid]]'' game series.
