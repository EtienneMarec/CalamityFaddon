'''XC-39 Oizys''' (Japanese: XC-39 オイジーズ ''XC - 39 oijīzu'') is an armored [[The Fabricational Quartet|Exo]] version of [[Supreme Calamitas]], intended to be fought just after defeating [[XB-10 HuY]] but just before the awakened [[Goozma]].

==Spawn==
Oizys does not spawn on her own and requires the player to put the [[Brimstone Locus]] into the [[The Codebreaker|Codebreaker]] with an Auric Quantum Cooling Cell. She can also be summoned using a [[Stellar Locus]].

==Behavior==
At the start of the fight, two walls will generate with 300 tiles of space between them, creating a boxed arena from which the player must fight in, similar to Supreme Calamitas. The walls will move from left to right, so the player can't stay at one place. During that time, fast-paced projectiles will generate at the right wall. Every 5 seconds, the projectiles' movement and generation speed gets higher. After 30 seconds pass, Oizys herself will appear on the right side of the screen, The arena will enlarge and center at Oizys. After that the fight will begin.

=== 1st phase ===
In the first phase, Oizys will do all of these attacks in random order:

*Creates a wide laser beam that does a full 360' circle. The starting position of the laser is determined by a line indicator.
*Goes a few tiles away from the player and shoots 5 bursts of 10 small lasers, similar to Artemis Laser Shotgun Barrage attack.
*Does 3 rows of 3 dashes towards the player, or towards the players future place when the player is moving, similar to [[The Old Duke]].
*Shoots 10 homing nukes that explode on collision or after 3 seconds, 1 nuke per second. If a nuke explodes untouched, it will fire 4 projectiles in an X pattern (Revengeance Mode)
*Uses a bigger holographic version of [[Murasama]], that attacks all the screen, requiring the player to use a dash shield with i-frames. (Death Mode only)

During these attacks the player only deals 50% of the damage.

After every attack was played, Oizys overheats and creates gas around her that damages the player when they go near, but also becomes more vulnerable during that time, where the player can now deals 200% of their damage inflicted. After 10.3 seconds, Oizys cools down and performs the above attacks again, and the damage taken will drop down back to 50%.

=== 2nd phase (65% HP) ===
At 65% HP, Oizys will stop attacking and reach her arms into the air, summoning a dozen of lightning strikes, Her eyes glowing white and pulsing electricity from her body. Phase 2 will start and all her attacks will have their speed increased, while the projectiles will inflict [[Electrified]]. The attack speed increase lasts through the whole fight.

=== 3rd phase (55% HP) ===
After reaching 55% HP, Oizys will stop attacking and charge up, making the entire screen bright. When 2 seconds pass, the screen comes back to normal.

In the 3rd phase, Oizys gets a new attack cycle, with changed old attacks, and some new ones:

Revised old attacks:

*The laser now does a 540' circle instead of 360'.
* The Laser Shotgun attack now shoots bursts of 150 lasers instead of 10.
*Dash attack now has 5.2 dashes in a row instead of 3, the final dash in the row will always be into the arena wall, When the boss hits the wall, an explosion happens that shoots small lasers in a circle.
*The Nuke attack gets smaller nukes added, they have: higher movement speed, lower turning speed and explosion radius. (Revengeance Mode)
*The giant Murasama now shoots lasers after every attack that gets dodged. (Death Mode)

New attacks: 

* Summons 8 Ferum Seekers that circle around the player and shoot Exo Darts at it one by one. After some time, they shoot these darts at the same time, and then they self destruct. During this attack, Oizys will attempt ramming the player.

After every attack is played, the boss overheats for 5 seconds and becomes vulnerable, where the player deals 100% more damage as usual.

=== 4th phase (40.00009% HP) ===
After reaching 40.00009% HP, Oizys gets exhausted and floats on one spot, becoming invulnerable to all of the player's attacks. The arena resizes into a 150x150 box, and Oizys' two brothers, '''Exo Cataclysm,''' and '''Exo Catastophe,''' will be summoned both with 500,000 HP. Both brothers need to be defeated in order for the player to be able to attack Oizys again.
*Exo Cataclysm floats around the player and stomps his fists every second shooting out a circle of small shockwaves that damage the player.
*Exo Catastrophe slowly chases the player and tries to slash them with a weapon similar to the [[Phaseslayer]].
