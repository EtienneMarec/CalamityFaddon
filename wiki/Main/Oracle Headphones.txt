The '''Oracle Headphones''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapons|Summoner weapon]]. It drops at a rare chance from [[Shockstorm Shuttle]]s.
The Oracle Headphones summon a png of Futaba to fight for the player.

== Notes ==
*During May 25th, the drop rate increases from 5% to 11.11%.
*If the player is named "Amber" or "Mishiro", they can obtain the Oracle Headphones from the [[Starter Bag]].

== Trivia ==
*This weapon summons the character Futaba Sakura from the video game ''[[wikipedia:Persona 5|Persona 5]]''.
**The quote in the item's tooltip directly references one of her quotes heard in-game.
**The item's name also references her in-game codename, 'Oracle'.
