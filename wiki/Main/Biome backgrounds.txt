'''Biome backgrounds''' are images that appear behind objects and blocks in each [[biome]]. A few of the Calamity Mod's biomes have unique backgrounds that usually show a landscape of the biome. Backgrounds do not affect a biome in any way. Turning off backgrounds in Settings hides them, though [[ambient objects]] will still appear.
