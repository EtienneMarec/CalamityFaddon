'''The Underworld''' layer features three new [[enemies]] along with three new [[boss]]es. The [[Brimstone Crag]] [[biome]] generates in the left side of the Underworld. There is also a unique [[Underworld Bio-center Lab]] located on the far-right side of the Underworld, which contains the [[schematic]] used to craft the fourth tier of Draedon's arsenal and decrypt the final schematic.
