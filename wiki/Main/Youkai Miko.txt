''<big>"The creation of spirits and faith, born to serve as a shrine's Miko..."</big>''

The '''Youkai Miko''' is a [[Town NPCs|town NPC]] vendor that sells various items relating to the abyss.

THe Youkai Miko can spawn once the following conditions are met:

* There is an empty house. 
* Either ''Vessel of the Abyss, Devlyn'' or ''Dragon of Balance, Devlyn'' are defeated.

When threatened by [[Enemies]], she will defend herself by throwing a modified Fantasy Talisman.

=='''Trivia'''==

* The Youkai Miko is implied to be canonically 12 years old.
** This means that similar to the [[Angler]] and the [[Princess]], her sprite is shorter than the usual NPC sprite, and they all share the same death message.
* Many of the things revolving around the Youkai Miko are an obvious nod to the [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Touhou_Wiki Touhou Project] Series, even including the direct mention of "[https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Gensokyo Gensokyo]," within one of the Youkai Miko's line, the setting where Touhou Project is usually based.
* The Youkai Miko bares a few resemblances to Dragon of Balance, Devlyn, including the blue long hair, dirty white horn, and a tail, further concluding that Devlyn is her mother.
* The bestiary entry reads "A strange, yet jolly abyssal being born from phantom spirits and faith, not native from the world of Terraria. How these two elements formed her is unknown."
