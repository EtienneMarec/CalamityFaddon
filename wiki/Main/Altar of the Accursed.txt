The '''Altar of the Accursed''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[furniture]] item. When right-clicked, it is used to summon [[Supreme Calamitas]] while [[Ashes of Calamity]] or a [[Ceremonial Urn]] is in the player's inventory. Upon successfully summoning the boss, one Ashes of Calamity is removed from the player's inventory if the player does not have a Ceremonial Urn, and the sky darkens, summoning Supreme Calamitas shortly after.
