'''Catastrophe Clay Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Ores|ore]] which generates in the [[Brimstone Crag]] after defeating [[Calamitas]]. It can be mined with a [[Titanium Pickaxe]] or [[Adamantite Pickaxe]] and is used to craft Catastrophic soul-themed weaponry. It is also dropped by [[Catastrophe]] and [[Supreme Catastrophe]].
