The '''Frost Bolt''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that auto-fires a slow-moving ball of ice, similar to the [[Water Bolt]], inflicting the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]. The projectile travels in a straight line unaffected by gravity, emit a bit of light, which can hit 2 enemies or bounce off 2 walls before dissipating.
