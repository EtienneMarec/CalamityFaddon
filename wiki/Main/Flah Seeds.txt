'''Flah Seeds''' are a type of [[seed]] that can be obtained from harvesting [[Flah]] while it is blooming or using the Staff of Regrowth on naturally grown [[Flah]] while it is fully grown. A [[Flah]] plant only blooms during a [[Blood Moon]] or a [[Solar Eclipse]]. Its blooming state can be identified by the brimstone fire on its flower. Each plant will drop 1–3 Flah Seeds when harvested while blooming. 
[[Flah]] Seeds can be placed on [[Brimstone Slag]], empty [[Clay Pot]]s, or any types of [[Planter Box]]es, and will grow into harvestable [[Flah]]. They will grow anywhere, not just in the [[Brimstone Crag]], but will only yield seeds when blooming. 

Flah Seeds can also be obtained from [[Flahr]]s.
