The '''Fault Line''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Yoyos|yoyo]]. Every time it strikes an enemy, it creates a small fiery explosion which deals additional damage. When the yoyo returns to the player, it will be automatically re-thrown if they are still holding the fire button.
