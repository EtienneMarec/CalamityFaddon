The '''Dark Harvest Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] and upgrade to the [[Harvest Staff]]. It shoots dark pumpkins that bounce off of tiles and can pierce one enemy before breaking on the next. Upon breaking, the pumpkin will split into 2-4 gravity effected chunks in random directions that deal 20% of the pumpkin's damage.
