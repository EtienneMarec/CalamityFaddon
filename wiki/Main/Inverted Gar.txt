The '''Inverted Gar''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that can be caught while [[fishing]] in any layer of the [[Abyss]]. When the player takes damage from an enemy, the '''Gar Mana Boost''' [[buff]] is applied, preventing mana from being consumed for one second.

== Notes ==
*The Inverted Gar can only be caught in the abyss, and due to this, mining out an air pocket is recommended. It can be caught at any fishing power with a 5% chance, making it not too difficult to obtain for early players.
**The Inverted Gar can be caught in the first layer of the abyss, which is recommended due to the general lack of enemies in that layer.
*The buff will no longer trigger when taking damage from [[Spikes]], due to an exploit that allowed for infinite mana.

== Trivia ==
*This covering was formerly named the "Shadow Gar" and lacked its additional tooltip line.
