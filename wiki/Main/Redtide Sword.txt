The '''Redtide Sword''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it fires a small glowing cyclone that grows in size, pierces once, deals half the broadsword's damage and travels a short distance before popping, emitting glowing water projectiles in a large radius. Despite the projectile growing in size, its hitbox does not change. The projectile can also go through walls if close enough to it.
