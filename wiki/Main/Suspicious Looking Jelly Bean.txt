The '''Suspicious Looking Jelly Bean''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Mount]] summoning item that is dropped from [[Astrum Aureus]] after [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated. When used, it summons a Squishy Bean mount similar to the [[Slimy Saddle]] that can horizontally move at a speed of 40 mph and fall very fast with a terminal fall speed of 102 mph. However, it will not deal damage to enemies or bounce on them. Unlike the Slimy Saddle, this mount will negate fall damage as well as sink through liquids, allowing for fast vertical movement through water and lava.

== Trivia ==
*The mount's appearance is a reference to [https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pyukumuku_(Pok%C3%A9mon) Pyukumuku], a Pokémon from ''[[wikipedia:Pokémon Sun and Moon|Pokémon Sun and Moon]]''.
*This item was dropped by the [[Astrageldon Slime]] before it was replaced by Astrum Aureus, hence its similarity to the Slimy Saddle.
