The '''Darkscule''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns during the [[Ogswarm]]. It floats around the player attempting to get close, similar to [[tgc:Meteor Head|Meteor Heads]]. If it gets too close to the player, it explodes into a bunch of <font face="thecalamity" color="#ff0000"><span style="text-shadow: 0 0 20px black;">'''Calamity'''</font> Lasers. In order to prevent them from exploding, they can be killed with a ranged weapon before being allowed to get too close. '''Darkscules''' are also spawned by the [[The Calamity]] in its second phase.
