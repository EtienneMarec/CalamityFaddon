'''Reactive Guardian, Sodis''' (Japanese: リアクティブガーディアン、ソディス ''Riakutibugādian, sodisu'') is an Epilogue [[Superbosses|Superboss]] that takes the appearance of a mix between a Mantis and a Human.
==Spawn==
Sodis doesn't spawn on its own and requires the player to drop the [[Artificial Reactive Matter]] onto the fully completed [[Aquatic Hypercomputer]]. The computer will create a glowing circle above it, and Sodis will jump from below the ground and steal the circle.
==Behavior==
If the player is too far above Sodis, it will charge for 2 seconds and perform a large jump, casting several Reactive Flames around itself.

==== Phase 1  ====
({{dv|100%-60%||100-80%}} HP) 
*Sodis will try to run away from the player, all the while leaving behind Reactive Flames.
*It performs {{dv|3|5}} high jumps that create long shockwaves.
*It stands in one place and fires {{dv||8|11|14}} homing stars at the player. (Expert Mode only)
*It will create a big wheel that shoots lasers in random directions. (Death Mode only)

====Phase 2 ====
({{dv|60%-30%||80-55%}} HP)

* The boss will shoot a large circle upwards that explodes into {{dv|4|6}} small Reactive Minions.
* It runs at the player in an attempt to deal contact damage.
* It charges up for {{dv||7|5|3}} seconds before firing a giant projectile that leaves a trail behind it and shoots small projectiles up and down. (Expert Mode only)
* It will crawl very slowly and shoot Reactive Lasers out of its mouth. (Death Mode only)

==== Phase 3 ====
({{dv|30%-0%||55%-30%}}) HP

* The boss fires {{dv|2|4}} projectiles that fly in a wave-like pattern and, after 4 seconds, explode into small projectiles.
* It will teleport around, leaving behind small Reactive Minions.
* It will rain Reactive Flames from the sky. (Expert Mode only)
* It will create a wall of Reactive Flames that stays stationary for 5 seconds. After that, the Reactive Flames will disperse in random directions. (Death Mode only)

==== Phase 4 (Revengeance Mode only) ====
({{dv|||30%-0%}}) HP

The boss creates 4 clones of itself that are recolored versions of the boss colored after the Reactive Essences. The clones can't be damaged, but when Sodis himself is hurt he takes 2 times more damage and swaps places with a random clone after 1 second.

Sodis attacks: 
* Runs away from the player, leaving behind Reactive Flames on the ground.
* Casts a projectile that creates 4 Reactive Lasers. The projectile flies very slowly and cannot be destroyed. (Death Mode only)

Blood-red clone attacks:
* Runs towards the player, casting firing flames at them.
* Jumps onto the player and sticks to them, inflicting the Confused debuff. (Death mode only)

Acidated clone attacks:
* Creates smaller copies of itself that shoot projectiles towards the player and dissapear after 10 seconds.
* Creates smaller copies of itself that heal Sodis and disappear after 5 seconds. (Death mode only)

Noctic clone attacks:
* Randomly teleports around the screen every second and runs towards the player to deal contact damage. While teleporting, it has a 20% chance to appear upside down. If there are no blocks below it, it will create temporary blocks to stand on.
* Shoots insanely fast projectiles that change direction randomly every 0.4 seconds. (Death mode only)
* Teleports behind the player, and runs towards them, switching gravity and placing temporary blocks.

Waterfreeze clone attacks:
* Shoots darts toward the player that leave behind Reactive Flames that fall to the ground, similar to Cursed Darts.
* Casts a giant slow missile that flies towards the player and explodes on collision. (Death mode only)


After Sodis dies, all of its clones except Noctic disappear. The Noctic clone teleports around wildly for 5 seconds before teleporting behind the player and rushing at them. However, before it can reach the player, a big rhombus-shaped portal appears that sucks Noctic in, defeating the boss.
