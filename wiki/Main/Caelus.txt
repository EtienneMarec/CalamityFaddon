'''Caelus''' is a [[subworld]] introduced by the Deimos, the Starborn Princess addon that can be accessed through the [[Q.C.S.T.T.]]. The subworld consists of many structures within an empty world, containing many useful [[Crafting materials]] and [[Ores]]. 

The subworld generates once and will not regenerate. However, when the subworld is entered various structures will be spawned in empty spaces, with new structures being able to generate as you progress.

Liquids, items, and falling blocks will stay in place in the subworld and gravity is the same as the top of the space layer. Touching the bottom border of the subworld or using a [[Magic Mirror]] will bring you back to the main world.

==[[Breath meter|Breath]]==
Caelus will drain your breath meter as if you were in [[Water]]. The subworld ignores items that grant permanent underwater breathing through a gills effect, such as the [[Gills Potion]], [[Neptune's Shell]], [[Effigy of Decay]], [[Amidias' Blessing]], and the [[Aquatic Heart]].

===Breath loss rate===
The following items reduce how frequently Caelus takes breath away from the player:
*A full set of [[Victide armor]]: +5/{{death|3}}
*[[Amidias' Blessing]]: +7/{{death|2}}
*[[Diving Helmet]], [[Diving Gear]], or [[Jellyfish Diving Gear]]: +10
*[[Arctic Diving Gear]] or [[Abyssal Diving Gear]]: +20
*A full set of [[Reaver armor]]: +25/{{death|20}}
*[[Abzu]]: +14
*[[The Absorber]]: +15
*[[Abyssal Diving Suit]]: +39
*A full set of [[Tarragon armor]]: +40/{{death|30}}
*A full set of [[Bloodflare armor]]: +50/{{death|37}}
*[[Alien Carbon Fiber Fabric]]: +30
*A full set of [[God Slayer armor]] or [[Silva armor]]: +55/{{death|40}}
*[[The Sponge]]: +25
*A full set of [[Auric Tesla armor]]: +75/{{death|65}}

===Housing===
If the player is within a closed perimeter of solid [[Blocks]] that is completely filled with background [[Walls]], they will stop drowning and will begin to regain breath. The player can sleep and set their spawn in the subworld and various light sources including normal [[Torch]]es and most [[Lamp]]s, [[Lanterns]], and [[Candles]] will only work in breathable housing.
