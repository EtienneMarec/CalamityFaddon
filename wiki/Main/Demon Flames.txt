'''Demon Flames''' is a very powerful [[debuff]] that causes the target to lose 1,250 health per second. It is inflicted by all [[weapons]] while [[Demonshade armor]] is equipped. It is also inflicted by [[Apotheosis]] and [[Vehemence]]. 

If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they lose 1,562.5 health per second instead of 1,250.
