'''???''' (Japanese: ??? ''???'') is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] stationary [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Ocean]]. When it is killed, it summons [[Anahita]]. It can drop the powerful [[Aquatic Heart]] [[covering]] at any point in the game. It can also be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].

== Notes ==
*Due to its ability to spawn in [[Pre-Hardmode]], it is possible to kill it and summon the powerful boss(es) by accident. However, this is not recommended as this boss can easily one-hit the player in Pre-Hardmode with its massive damage.
*It spawns 4x as often in Hardmode.
**After defeating [[Calamitas]] or [[Plantera]], its spawn rate is additionally boosted by 4x, totaling 16x.
*** You can stop '''???''' from spawning by using [[Sirenproof Earmuffs]]
*It cannot spawn in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]].
*It cannot spawn while any Lunar Pillars are alive.

==Trivia==
*When '''???''' is nearby, the [[Music|song]] [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EpXNHYldIE0 '''Siren's Call'''], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO], will play unless the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled.
