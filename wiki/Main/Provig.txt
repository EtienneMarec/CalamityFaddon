'''Provig''' is a superboss able to be summoned in the Underworld or the Hallow. When summoned, she will screech in a very funny way. Throughout the fight, she will yell inaudible things. 
==Spawn==
Provig can be spawned with a profaned trumpet.
==Behavior==
.
===In all phases===
*She will spin around and then suddenly start to zoom at the player, dealing damage as she charges into you.
*During the fight, she is able to bring out a lawnmower and she tries to run over the player mid air with it
