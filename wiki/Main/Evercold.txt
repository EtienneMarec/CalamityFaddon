'''Evercold''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by various sources. It causes the player to lose 30 health per second, 20 defense, 20% damage reduction and 40% movement speed, or 200 health and 20% damage reduction and movement if the debuff is inflicted to an enemy instead. It also prevents natural life regeneration.
