The '''Tundra Flame Blossoms Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons up to three pink flowers that orbit around the player, and passively fire three Blossom Orbs which inflicts the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]. If a target is nearby, the flower fires 3.1x faster and the projectiles are sped up by 2.33x. Unlike most minions, only one set of Tundra Flame Blossoms can be summoned.
