The '''Sea Shell''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Sea Snail]]s in the [[Ocean]] biome. It increases [[defense]] by 3 and allows free movement in liquids. Standing in a liquid increases its bonus by 3 defense and additionally gives +5% damage reduction and +15% movement speed.

== Tips ==
*If the player is patient enough to travel to the Ocean and farm Sea Snails, the Sea Shell is a great covering to use for your first boss battle, as it can provide up to a maximum of 9 defense with the Warding prefix.
**Additionally, the Sea Shell's ability to mitigate water physics can help the player navigate large bodies of water before they obtain important mobility items.
