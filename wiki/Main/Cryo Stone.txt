The '''Cryo Stone''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a 20% chance to drop from [[Cryogen]]. It grants the player a rotating ice shield identical to the one protecting Cryogen, that damages and slows down enemies on contact. It was previously one of the several components required for the [[Kelvin Catalyst]].
