'''Floating Biomasses''' are [[Hardmode]] [[enemies]] that spawn in the [[Jungle]] surface. They act like [[Demon Eyes]], but will inflict the [[Weak]] [[debuff]] on contact. When below half health, their speed and maneuverability increase.

In [[Death Mode]], Floating Biomasses are capable of becoming intangible and passing through blocks for a brief period of time, in order to reach the player.

==Trivia==
*These enemies are based of of the Flying Bio-mass from the ''Thorium Mod'', implying that they migrated into the Calamity mod.
