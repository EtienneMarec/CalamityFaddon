'''lul''' is an [[unobtainable]] [[covering]]. If the player equips the covering they will take no damage whatsoever with this covering equipped. However, [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s border Infernadoes will still kill the player along with drowning.

== Trivia ==
*Its sprite is a reference to a commonly used [https://www.twitch.tv Twitch] emote of the same name.
**The person seen in the sprite is the late game critic [[wikipedia:TotalBiscuit|TotalBiscuit]].
***This is the second reference to TotalBiscuit in Calamity, the other being the [[Halibut Cannon]].
*If the player is killed by the covering, the death message "<PlayerName> isn't worthy." will display.
*Before the [[1.3.3.002]] update, this item was instead called "lol" and used a different sprite. Its original sprite was used as a removed [[crafting station]] called the [[Particle Accelerator]].
