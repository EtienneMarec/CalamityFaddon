The '''Seeking Scorcher''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee]] [[boomerang]] that is a direct upgrade of the [[Possessed Hatchet]]. It fires a holy hatchet that can pass through tiles, home in and hit up to four enemies before returning to the player.

Its best [[modifier]] is '''Godly'''.
