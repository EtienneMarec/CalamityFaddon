The '''Dynamo Stem Cells''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[The Dragonfolly]]. It grants the player 10% increased movement speed and a 20% chance for ranged weapons to release miniature swarmers when fired. The swarmers deal more damage the higher the weapon's use time, up to a maximum of 70% of the weapon's base damage for weapons with a use time of 10.5 or higher. The swarmers are softcapped at 1000 damage.
