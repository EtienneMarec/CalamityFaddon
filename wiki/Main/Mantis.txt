The '''Mantis''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns on the [[Surface]] [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. It chases the player on the ground, gaining speed if continuously running in one direction, slowing down when turning around, and hopping if it encounters a gap in the ground or a change in elevation. If the player is on the ground and close enough, it briefly stops and fires a short-ranged spinning scythe projectile at them.

== Trivia ==
*The Mantis' projectile is a reference to the projectiles fired by the Mantis Lords boss and Mantis Petra enemy in the game [[wikipedia:Hollow Knight|''Hollow Knight'']].
