'''Dyes''' are cosmetic items that can be placed in the Dye Slots of a player's inventory to alter the colors/textures of equipped [[Covering]], [[vanity items]], and [[coverings]]. The Calamity Mod currently adds 30 types of dyes to the game, most of them being crafted at a [[Dye Vat]].

== Notes ==
*Profaned Moonlight Dye changes color based on the time of day, with red and yellow during the day and blue and purple at night. As it turns from day to night and vice versa, it will be a mix of the two.
*Profaned Moonlight Dye's aurora effect grows more intense with each additional dye added, until up to twenty have been equipped in any slot.
