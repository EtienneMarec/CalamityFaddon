The '''Crystal Flare Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. It shoots two frost flames that gradually fade in, which explodes on contact with anything, producing a large, blue explosion. Any [[enemy]] hit by the projectile or resulting explosion will be inflicted with the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]].
