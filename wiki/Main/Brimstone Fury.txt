The '''Brimstone Fury''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]]. When used, it shoots 3 arrows in a slight spread. If [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], arrows are converted into three fast brimstone laser bolts with a slight spread. The bolts dissipate upon impact with tiles and do not pierce [[enemies]]. Upon hitting an enemy, the bolts inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]].
