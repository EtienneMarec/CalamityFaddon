The '''Infested Clawmerang''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[boomerang]] dropped by [[Crabulon]]. It fires a slow-moving boomerang that pierces infinitely before returning to the player.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Infested Clawmerang will cause the next boomerang thrown to release homing spores every so often as it travels and temporarily grant the [[Mushy]] [[buff]] when hitting an enemy.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Trivia ==
*It was originally known as the “Shroomerang”, but the title changed to avoid confusion with the vanilla item of the same name.
