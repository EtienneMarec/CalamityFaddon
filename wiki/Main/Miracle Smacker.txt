The '''Miracle Smacker''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[whip]]. When used, it fires 3 different whips which have different effects and deal different [[debuffs]].

The red whip will inflict [[On Fire!]] (3 seconds) and make minion hits cause explosions.

The green whip will inflict [[Holy Flames]] (3 seconds) and steal 4 life.

The blue whip will inflict [[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds) and [[Frostburn]] (3 seconds) and give the player 5 defense and 2% damage reduction.

Its best modifier is '''Legendary'''. The Legendary modifier provides the widest array of stat bonuses including whip range and speed.
