The '''Star Swallower Containment Unit''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. It summons a grounded bio-mechanical frog minion that jumps around the player. When an enemy is nearby, it fires gravity-affected acid droplets that inflict the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]]. It does not directly deal contact damage.

The weapon consumes 0.08 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 50.
