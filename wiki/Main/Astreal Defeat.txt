The '''Astreal Defeat''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]]. It autofires streams of Astreal Arrows at four semi-randomized paths, ranging from straight forward to varying levels of curving downward, with a tendency to curve to the right as well. The arrows will begin to spawn spirit flames shortly after they're fired, which home in on nearby enemies and deal 50% of the weapon's base damage. Astreal Arrows inflict the [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]] for 6 seconds upon striking an enemy.
