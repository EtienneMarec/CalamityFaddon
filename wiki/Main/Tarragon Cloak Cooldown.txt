'''Tarragon Cloak Cooldown''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted to the player for 30 seconds after using the [[Tarragon Cloak]] [[buff]] from the Melee variant of the [[Tarragon armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]]. While the debuff is active, the usage of Tarragon Cloak is disabled.
