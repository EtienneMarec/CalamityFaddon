The '''Crustalline''' is a craftable non-consumable [[dagger]] which automatically throws a dagger that orbits the player once then is affected by gravity. If the dagger hits a tile, it sticks in place, accompanied by a {{sound|crack|ProvidenceHurt.wav}} sound. 

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Crustalline will cause each of the daggers to shatter into harmfull fragments after 30 frames.
