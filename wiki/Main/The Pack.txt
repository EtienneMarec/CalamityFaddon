[[File:The Pack (demo).gif|frame|The Pack’s multiple rockets and explosions dealing damage to a [[Super Dummy]] and five [[Target Dummy|Target Dummies]]. Notice how the smaller rockets home in on the Super Dummy, and that the large rocket doesn’t deal damage, only its explosion does.]]
'''The Pack''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[launcher]] that fires a single large rocket upon use. The large rocket will explode within 10 tiles of an enemy, splitting into 5 smaller rockets that will home in on the nearest target. The explosions created upon impact have a large radius and also damage enemies. The large rocket can go through tiles.
