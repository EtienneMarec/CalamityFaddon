'''Embers of Duality''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by certain [[Enemies]] and [[Weapons]] that are related to [[The Starborn Moth]]. It causes the player to lose 7% defense and [[Damage Reduction]]. If inflicted onto enemies, 35 health is lost per second and 10% damage reduction is lost.

While afflicted with this debuff, Celestial Portals will spawn around targets every 2 seconds. If these portals are spawning around a player affected with the debuff, it will either fire a bolt or summon a Celestial Spirit then close. These bolts deal 65 damage. If these portals are spawning around an enemy affected with the debuff, it will fire a bolt every second three times before closing. These bolts deal 70 typeless damage. Targets also emit glowing, yellow/pink flames at day or cyan/pink flames at night.

If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they will lose 44 health per second instead of 35.
