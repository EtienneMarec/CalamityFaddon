The '''Desecrated Water''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]]. When used, a gravity affected flask is thrown that breaks upon contact with [[enemies]] and blocks. Upon breaking, the flask releases five to eleven bubbles that travel in multiple directions away from where the flask broke. Each bubble will aggressively home in onto enemies and will deal 50% of the flask's base damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Desecrated Water will cause the next flask thrown to release three to five more bubbles and all the bubbles inflict the [[Cursed Inferno]] and [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] [[debuffs]].
