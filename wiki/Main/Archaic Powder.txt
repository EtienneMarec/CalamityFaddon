The '''Archaic Powder''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that increases [[defense]] by 10, damage reduction by 5%, and mining speed by 20% while the owner is in the [[Underground]], [[Cavern]] or [[Underworld]] layers. 

There is also an easter egg when having it equiped, giving a 20% chance to summon the winter enemy during christmas. This is a reference to winter
