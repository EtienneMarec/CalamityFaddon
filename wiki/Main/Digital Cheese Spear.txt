The '''Digital Cheese Spear''' is a craftable epilogue [[Dedicated content|developer]] [[Javelins|javelin]]. It fires a high-velocity spear that upon hitting an enemy, summons 12 more mini spears in a barrage, dealing 65% of the weapon's damage.

[[Spy|Stealth Strike]]s additionally cast 12 identical spears in a barrage that aggressively home in on the targeted enemy upon hitting said enemy. These spears will also call in 6 more mini spears that don't home in and deal 56% of the weapon's damage.
