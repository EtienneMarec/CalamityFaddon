The '''Subsuming Vortex''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]] that, on use, creates a large rainbow vortex and 4 exo tentacles around the player. When an enemy comes within 20 tiles of the vortex, they are pulled inwards and have their contact damage removed. The exo tentacles extend outwards and whip enemies, dealing damage. The vortex disappears once the player runs out of mana or stops holding the weapon.
