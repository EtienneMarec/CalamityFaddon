The '''Mushroom Plasma Root''' is a [[permanent power-up]] item obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped from [[Crabulon]]. It increases the [[Rage Mode]] duration by one second. There are 2 other items that function identically to this: the [[Infernal Blood]] and the [[Red Lightning Container]]. It can only be used once.
