The '''Charred Slime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[slime]] which spawns naturally in the [[Brimstone Crag]] after the player has defeated the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. They exist to drop [[Charred Ore]], so that the player cannot use up all the ore available to them.

Its stats receive a significant boost and starts dropping [[Bloodstone]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated.
