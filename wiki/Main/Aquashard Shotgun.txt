The '''Aquashard Shotgun''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]]. Upon firing, it will shoot a spread of 1-3 bullets. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], the bullets are replaced with aquamarine shards that split into six smaller weakly homing shards upon contact with an [[enemy]], which deal reduced damage.
