The '''Dragon''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that appears across the Overworld. It tries to deal contact damage by walking through blocks towards the player. Its primary purpose is to drop [[Auric Soul|Auric Souls]].

== Trivia ==
* The Dragons sprite is very similar to that of the Wyvern, hinting at their connection in lore.
