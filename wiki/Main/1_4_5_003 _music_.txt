{{dablink|This is the page for the '''1.4.5.003 Music mod''' update.}}
The '''1.4.5.003''' (or '''1.4.5.3''') Music mod update replaced a music track for the Acid Rain event, resprited the [[Anahita]] music boxes, and fixed a few bugs.

This Music Mod version is compatible with all Main mod versions between [[1.4.5.003]] and [[1.4.5.007]].

== Changes ==
*Renamed Music Box (Siren) to [[Music Box (Anahita)]].
*Renamed Music Box (Siren Lure) to [[Music Box (Anahita Lure)]].
*Replaced "radioactive" with "nuclear monsoon" as the [[Acid Rain]] Tier 3 music track.
*Resprited Music Box (Anahita) and Music Box (Anahita Lure).

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug that prevented [[Music Boxes]] from being toggled with [[Wire]]s.
*Fixed several audio quality issues with the Acid Rain Tier 1 and 2 music track.
