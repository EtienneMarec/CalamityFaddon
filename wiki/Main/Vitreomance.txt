'''Vitreomance''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It uses five minion slots to summon a swarm of five Miracle Blighted Eyes minions which cycle between charging at enemies in a line, firing homing exo bolts, and rotating close to enemies while growing flower petals to expand their hitbox. Holding right click channels a sigil on an enemy on the cursor. If the cursor is kept on the enemy for 5 seconds, the eyes lock to an evenly spread star position around the enemy and fire large exo beams inwards.
