The '''Photon Ripper''' is a Godseeker Mode [[axes|chainsaw]] that drops from the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] after [[XF-09 Ares]] has been defeated last. It rapidly fires out rainbow teeth which circle back after passing through the cursor or past a set distance. The maximum set distance along with tooth damage increases as the weapon is used up to 3 seconds where it is fully charged. The tooth deals 6% damage initially and scales up to 16.67%, while the chainsaw itself deals full contact damage. Additionally, teeth projectiles will instantly remove trees.
