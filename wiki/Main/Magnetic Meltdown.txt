The '''Magnetic Meltdown''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. It fires 4 purple orbs which go through blocks; the orbs travel a short distance and stay in the air for a couple of seconds, continuously shooting [[enemies]] around them with shadowbolts. The orbs act similarly to the orbs fired by the [[Magnet Sphere]] with higher damage and a larger range; thus, the Magnetic Meltdown can be considered a direct upgrade of the Magnet Sphere.
