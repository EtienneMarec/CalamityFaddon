The '''Warlok's Moon Fist''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It provides a 25% increase to melee speed and damage and a 5% increase to melee critical strike chance, a 50% increase to [[true melee]] damage, increases melee knockback, and gives the player temporary immunity to lava. The covering also has a 10% chance to kill any non-boss [[enemy]], and inflicts the Moon Fist buff on the player upon true melee strikes which increase true melee damage by an additional 60%. Enemies struck while wearing the gauntlet are also inflicted with [[Nightwither]].
