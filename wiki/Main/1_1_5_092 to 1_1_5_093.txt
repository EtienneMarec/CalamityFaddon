The '''1.1.5.092''' update fixed the mod's version number on the mod browser. The '''1.1.5.093''' update made some balance changes, and added several [[weapons]] and resprites.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Angry Chicken Staff]], [[Bladecrest Oathsword]], [[Bloody Edge]], [[Brimstone Flameblaster]], [[Clockwork Bow]], [[Hell Burst]], [[Holiday Halberd]], [[Ion Blaster]], [[Lazinator]], [[Magna Cannon]], [[Mana Rose]], [[Old Lord Oathsword]], [[Opal Striker]], [[Plasma Rod]], [[Raider's Glory]], [[Terra Edge]], [[True Bloody Edge]].
*Added [[Yharon Mask]].
*Added [[Angry Chicken]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Lowered [[Armored Digger]] spawn rate by 75%.
*Made [[The Hive Mind]]'s hitbox smaller.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' projectiles now last shorter.
*Reduced chance for The Devourer of Gods to spawn a [[Devourer Spawn]] or a [[Stasis Probe]] on hit.
*Reverted Jungle Phoenix, Yharon's name to Jungle Dragon, Yharon.
*Jungle Dragon, Yharon can now drop the Angry Chicken Staff.

=== Items ===
*Lowered [[Sun God Staff]]'s knockback.
*Nerfed [[Terra Ray]].
*Nerfed [[Forbidden Sun]]'s damage from 85 to 72.
*Forbidden Sun no longer includes 1 [[Inferno Fork]] in its recipe, and now only requires 5 [[Cruptix Bar]]s instead of 6.
*[[Terra Blade]] can now be crafted with 1 True Bloody Edge in place of the [[True Night's Edge]] in its recipe.
*[[True Caustic Edge]]'s projectile now uses dust ID 74 instead of 44.
*Nerfed [[Xeroc Cuisses]]' defense from 18 to 17.
*Nerfed [[Xeroc Mask]]'s defense from 25 to 23.
*Nerfed [[Xeroc Plate Mail]]'s defense from 23 to 22.

=== Other ===
*All Calamity [[ores]] now appear on the map.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Essence of Chaos]], [[Essence of Cinder]], [[Essence of Eleum]], and [[Shadow Essence]].
*Resprited [[Ancient Fossil]], [[Badge of Bravery]], and [[Chaos Amulet]].
*Resprited [[Ark of the Elements]], [[Elemental Ray]], [[Night's Ray]], and [[True Ark of the Ancients]].
*Resprited [[Treasure Bag (The Perforators)]].
