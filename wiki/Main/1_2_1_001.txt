The '''1.2.1.001''' update overhauled the [[Supreme Calamitas]] fight and added a new phase to [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]'s fight. It also rebalanced most Godseeker Mode [[weapons]], removed boss DPS caps, and added several [[vanilla item recipes]].

*Added [[Plague Mine]].
*Added [[Holy Inferno]].
