'''Field Generators''' were tiles found in [[Arsenal Lab]]s which fired symmetrical 8 way patterned lasers in addition to singular lasers periodically at players who entered a 60 tile radius around them. The lasers dealt {{dv|54|108}} damage. They could be mined using a [[Deathbringer Pickaxe]], [[Nightmare Pickaxe]], or higher.
