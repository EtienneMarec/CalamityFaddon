The '''Shinobi Blade''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[dagger]] dropped by [[Dark Caster]]s. It automatically fires fast daggers that are unaffected by gravity, and do not pierce enemies or blocks. Enemies killed by the projectile spawn slow homing blue orbs that [[lifesteal|heal]] the player for 10 health.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Shinobi Blade causes three daggers to be thrown instead of one.
