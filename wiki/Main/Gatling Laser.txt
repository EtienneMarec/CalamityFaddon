The '''Gatling Laser''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]]. When used, the gun has a short load period before a barrage of fast but inaccurate red lasers are fired at a massively rapid rate. The laser creates a tiny explosion on hit, inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]].

The weapon consumes 0.0075 units of [[charge]] for each frame the weapon is held (totaling 0.015 units of charge per use) and has a maximum charge of 135.
