The '''1.3.3.011''' update added the [[Great Sand Shark]] [[mini boss]] along with its own dedicated weapon set. It also completely overhauled [[The Hive Mind]] [[boss]], granted [[Providence, the Profaned God]] two new attacks, added several new [[ore]] bricks, and added 10 new [[Music Boxes]].

{{toc limit|3}}

== Additions ==
*Added Great Sand Shark.
*Added [[Duststorm in a Bottle]], [[Sand Sharknado Staff]], [[Sandstorm (weapon)|Sandstorm]], [[Shifting Sands]], and [[Tumbleweed]],
*Added Grand Scale. 
*Added [[Sandstorm's Core]] and [[Super Dummy]].
*Added [[Aerialite Brick]], [[Astral Brick]], [[Chaotic Brick]], [[Cryonic Brick]], [[Perennial Brick]], and [[Uelibloom Brick]].
*Added [[Aerialite Brick Wall]], [[Astral Brick Wall]], [[Chaotic Brick Wall]], [[Cryonic Brick Wall]], [[Perennial Brick Wall]], and [[Uelibloom Brick Wall]].
*Added [[Music Box (Aquatic Scourge)]], [[Music Box (Astrageldon)]], [[Music Box (Astral Biome)]], [[Music Box (Ceaseless Void)]], [[Music Box (Higher Abyss)]], [[Music Box (Hive Mind)]], [[Music Box (Perforator)]], [[Music Box (Signus)]], [[Music Box (Storm Weaver)]], [[Music Box (Sulphurous Sea)]].
*Added [[Bumblebirb Trophy]] and [[Polterghast Trophy]].
*Added [[Sandnado (buff)]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
==== [[Bumblebirb]] ====
*Can now drop a Bumblebirb Trophy.

==== [[The Devourer of Gods]] ====
*Now takes 12.5% damage from [[Crystal Bullet]] crystal shards, [[Holy Arrow]] stars, split [[Terra Arrow]]s, and split [[Terra Bullet]]s.

==== [[Golem]] ====
*Now removes the early-Hardmode nerf to enemy health and damage when defeated.

==== [[The Hive Mind]] ====
*Phase 1 will now teleport to the player's position every 12 seconds.
*Phase 2 no longer occasionally fires clusters of vile shots at the player.
*Phase 2 will now charge backwards then perform one of three attacks after being damaged enough. These attacks include:
**Spinning around the player several times, then charging at them.
**Appearing below the player, then rotating to the top of them while spawning enemies.
**Appearing to the top left of the player, then charging right while spawning dark rain clouds.

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Flare dusts no longer deal damage until they explode.
*Flare dusts now last for 2 seconds instead of 2.5.
*Now takes 50% damage if the player has [[Vortex armor]]'s stealth active and 25% damage from Crystal Bullet crystal shards, Holy Arrow stars, split Terra Arrows, and split Terra Bullets.

==== [[Lunatic Cultist]] ====
*Lowered health modifier from {{dv|3x||6x}} to {{dv|1.5x||2x}}
*Lightning Orbs no longer deal damage.
*Lowered speed of Revengeance and Death Mode projectile rain.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*The initial three True Eyes of Cthulhu no longer become damageable in Revengeance Mode.

==== [[Polterghast]] ====
*Now takes 25% damage from Crystal Bullet crystal shards, Holy Arrow stars, split Terra Arrows, and split Terra Bullets.
*Can now drop a Polterghast Trophy.
*Polterghast's clone now takes 50% damage if the player has Vortex armor's stealth active and 25% damage from Crystal Bullet crystal shards, Holy Arrow stars, split Terra Arrows, and split Terra Bullets.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Now has a new phase which changes depending on what biome Providence is fought in. In [[The Hallow]], Providence will stop in place and fire a sweeping laser beam, and in [[The Underworld]], Providence will spawn a crystal above the player that shoots spreads of gravity-affected crystals.
*Changed the positions in which the Profaned Guardians spawn.
*Tweaked how fast she moves vertically when despawning.
*Now takes 25% damage from Crystal Bullet crystal shards, Holy Arrow stars, split Terra Arrows, and split Terra Bullets.
*Now takes 70% damage from [[Galactus Blade]]'s comets.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*The Siren's notes no longer produce dust.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====
*Buffed the core's health from {{dv|800|1280|1600|4000}} to {{dv|2400|3840|4800|8000}}.
*The core is now damageable from the start of the fight instead of only being damageable after all large slimes have been killed.
*The large slimes now charge at the player from a shorter distance if the core is alive.
*Split slime gods now drop {{gc|8}}.

==== [[Supreme Calamitas]] ====
**Increased the distance at which her [[Soul Seeker]]s orbit her.
**Decreased the rate at which she shoots Brimstone Hellblasts.
**Changed how her brimstone fireblasts split.
**Now takes 50% damage if the player has [[Vortex armor]]'s stealth active and 25% damage from Crystal Bullet crystal shards, Holy Arrow stars, split Terra Arrows, and split Terra Bullets.
**Now uses [[Music#Now Stop Asking Where|Now Stop Asking Where]] as her theme.

=== Enemies ===
*[[tgc:Devourer|Devourer]]s and [[World Feeder]]s now have a {{dv|33%|50%}} chance to drop [[Fetid Essence]] instead of {{dv|20%|25%}}.
*[[Eidolist]] changes:
**Buffed health from {{dv|5000|10,000||16,000}} to {{dv|10,000|20,000||32,000}}.
**No longer deals contact damage.
**No longer drops [[Eidolon Tablet]]s if the [[Lunar Events]] are currently active.
**Lightning Orbs no longer deal damage.
*[[Eidolon Wyrm]]s no longer produce dust and are no longer slightly transparent.
*Buffed adult Eidolon Wyrm's damage resistance from 50% to 99.9999%.
*[[Hive Cyst]]s can no longer spawn if The Hive Mind is active.
*[[Mauler]]'s explosions now move instead of making its hitbox larger.
*[[Perforator Cyst]]s can no longer spawn if [[The Perforator Hive]] is active.
*Killing 20 [[Sand Shark]]s will now spawn the Great Sand Shark.

=== Items ===
*Buffed [[Alpha Ray]]'s damage from 40 to 280.
*Increased Alpha Ray's primary fire particle beam's LocalNPCHitCooldown from 8 to 80, and its secondary fire particle beam's LocalNPCHitCooldown from 3 to 30.
*[[Angel Treads]] now inherit the [[Harpy Ring]]'s effects.
*Nerfed [[Archerfish]]'s damage from 28 to 26.
*Renamed Profaned Sword to [[Brimstone Sword]].
*Decreased the movement and run speeds for [[Celestial Tracers]], [[Elysian Tracers]], and [[Seraph Tracers]].
*Changed Celestial Tracers' flavor text to say "Taking speed EVEN FURTHER BEYOND!" instead of "Ludicrous speed!".
*Buffed [[Celestus]]' damage from 130 to 145.
*Celestus' main projectile now has a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 30 instead of 10, and its split projectiles now have a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 40 instead of 20.
*[[Counter Scarf]]'s [[Scarf Cooldown]] now lasts 15 seconds instead of 11.
*Changed the color of summoner [[Daedalus armor]]'s Daedalus Crystal.
*Buffed [[Eidolic Wail]]'s damage from 158 to 188.
*[[Eidolon Tablet]] can no longer be used if the [[Lunar Events]] are active or if the [[tgc:Mysterious Tablet|Mysterious Tablet]] is present.
*Eidolon Tablet now spawns the [[Lunatic Cultist]] further away from the player.
*Decreased [[Elysian Wings]]' movement speed and flight time.
*Buffed [[Genisis]]' damage from 24 to 26.
*Increased Genisis' particle beam's LocalNPCHitCooldown from 8 to 80.
*Nerfed [[Onyx Chain Blaster]]'s damage from 63 to 58.
*Using the [[Rod of Discord]] no longer instantly kills the player if [[Armageddon]] is active.
*Decreased Seraph Tracers' horizontal flight acceleration.
*[[Silva armor]]'s set bonus now keeps the player at at least 1 health instead of at least 0.
*[[Siren's Song]]'s notes no longer produce dust.
*Buffed [[Subsuming Vortex]]'s damage from 144 to 165.
*Nerfed summoner [[Tarragon armor]]'s damaging aura's damage from 1500 to 300.
*Increased the flight time, horizontal speed, and horizontal acceleration of [[Tarragon Wings]].
*Nerfed [[Telluric Glare]]'s damage from 100 to 85.
*Split Terra Arrows now deal 66% of the original arrow's damage instead of 33.000001311302185%.
*Terra Bullets now split into 3-4 bullets instead of 1-3.
*Split Terra Bullets no longer have their damage cut in half in Revengeance Mode.
*Buffed [[Vivid Clarity]]'s damage from 145 to 165.
*Vivid Clarity's main beams now have a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 2 instead of 1, and its split beams now have a LocalNPCHitCooldown of 6 instead of 3.
*[[Void of Calamity]] and [[Void of Extinction]] can no longer both be equipped at the same time.
*Nerfed the damage for several of [[Xeroc armor]]'s projectiles:
**Reduced orb damage from 65% to 40%.
**Reduced blast damage from 33% to 15%.
**Reduced fire damage from 33% to 10%.

=== Other ===
