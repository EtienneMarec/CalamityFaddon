The '''YashBrowns''' is a craftable [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive [[Permanent Power-ups|Permanent Power-up]] item that permanently increases the duration of [[Rage Meter|Rage Mode]] by 2 seconds, [[Adrenaline Meter|Adrenaline Mode]] damage and DR by 25% and 7% respectively, and [[Stamina Meter|Stamina]] by 250. It can only be used once.
