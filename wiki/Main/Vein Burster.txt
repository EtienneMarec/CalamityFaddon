The '''Vein Burster''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that also drops from [[The Perforators]]. When swung, it fires a small spherical projectile that deals damage to the target hit and sticks to the first surface it touches. After sticking onto a surface for 4 seconds, it explodes - emitting red projectiles and dealing damage to all enemies in a small radius. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Perfect Dark]].
