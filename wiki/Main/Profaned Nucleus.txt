The '''Profaned Nucleus''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Bombs|bomb]] that drops from [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. When used it auto-fires an infinitely piercing molten chunk of profaned rock that is affected by gravity and creates explosions upon enemy hits. After 10 seconds have passed or the rock hits a block, it explodes into homing crystal shards.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Profaned Nucleus causes it to deal 150% damage and leave behind a patch of magma once it dissipates.
