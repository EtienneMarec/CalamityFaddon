'''White Wine''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''White Wine''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but boosts magic damage. It also inflicts [[Mana Sickness]] for 5 seconds.

While the effects of the White Wine debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
