The '''Nuclear Fury''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Razorblade Typhoon]]. It shoots out 8 cosmic typhoons evenly distributed in a circle around the player. These projectiles can pass through walls and home in on any enemy in range, though they will not actively target enemies behind walls or when the projectile itself is in a wall.
