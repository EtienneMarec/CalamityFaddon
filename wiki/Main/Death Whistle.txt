The '''Death Whistle''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[weapon]]. When used it fires a string of music notes that inflict [[Temporal Sadness]] and [[Confused]] on hit enemies. This effect does '''not''' work on bosses.

== Trivia ==
*A death whistle was an instrument historically used by the Aztecs during wars, known for its shrill frightening sound.
**The sound of the Death Whistle item being used is an unused roar in vanilla Terraria's files. It was chosen due to its resemblance to the sound of a real death whistle.
*This item used to be known as the "Ancient Medallion".
