'''Meld Gunk''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[block]] which randomly replaces [[Astral Infection]] [[Cavern]] [[blocks]] that have existed for more than 20 minutes. Meld Gunk is completely unbreakable by any means once it has generated, meaning the player must make it an active task to seek out any Astral Infection strains before they are permanently converted.

== Notes ==
*The [[Crystyl Crusher]], all explosives, and even the paint tool are unable to remove Meld Gunk.
*An unobtainable item form of Meld Gunk exists.
