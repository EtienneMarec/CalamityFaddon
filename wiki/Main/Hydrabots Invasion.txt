The '''Hydrabots Invasion''' is a post-[[Reactive Guardian, Sodis]] event that can randomly occur any night after defeating Sodis with a 1/12 chance

The event consists of 11 waves of robot-like creatures, with the [[Ancient Cutter]] Boss appearing at the end of it.

The invasion stops once the player leaves or dies.
