'''Silicon Ore''' is a pre-[[Hardmode]] [[ore]] found naturally generated in the [[Ocean]] and [[Sulphurous Sea]] in small patches. It can be mined with any pickaxe and is used to make [[Mysterious Circuitry]]. It is also dropped from [[Silicic Slime]]s.
