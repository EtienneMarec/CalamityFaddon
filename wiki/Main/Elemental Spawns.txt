'''Elemental Spawns''' are a group of Godseeker Mode enemies that are found in various [[biome]]s. They come in five varieties:

*One for the Cold and Water elements, that create a cross-shaped burst of {{dv|4|||6}} {{item|Ice Blast|s|link=}} and spawn in the [[Ocean]], [[Snow biome]], [[Sunken Sea]], and [[Frozen Hell]].
*One for the Fire and Unholy elements, that fire {{item|BrimstoneHellblast|nolink=yes|ext=gif|size=22px|Brimstone Hellblasts}} at the player and spawn in [[The Underworld]], [[Crimson]], [[Corruption]], and the [[Brimstone Crag]]
*One for the Poison and Dark elements, that fire {{dv|2|||3}} Plague Nuke Missiles at the player and spawn in the [[Jungle]], [[Sulphurous Sea]], and the [[Abyss]].
*One for the Stab and Slash elements, that aggressively approach and charge at the player and spawn in the [[Dungeon]], [[Desert]], and the [[The Exosphere]].
*One for the Magic and Holy elements, that create homing {{item|HolyFire2|nolink=yes|ext=gif|size=16px|Holy Flames}} and spawn in the [[Hallow]], [[Space]], and the [[Astral Infection]].
