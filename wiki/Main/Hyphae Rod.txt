The '''Hyphae Rod''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] dropped by [[Crabulon]]. It creates three hovering mushroom spores near the player that damage enemies. These spores last for 8 seconds before dissipating.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Tips ==
*This weapon is extremely useful for bosses that can move directly into the spores, such as [[the Hive Mind]] and [[Perforators]].
**Additionally, the spores can be stacked before a boss fight to deal a large amount of damage.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Hypha|Hyphae]] are long, branching filamentous structures of fungi.
*The created mushrooms are the same as the ones created by the [[Truffle]] NPC, while defending himself from enemies.
