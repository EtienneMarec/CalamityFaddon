The '''Radiating Crystal''' is a [[light pet]] summoning item that summons a pet Radiator that will emit a faint glow. It can be caught from special "glowing" [[Radiator]]s when hit with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].

== Notes ==
*The Radiators which can be captured to receive this item will glow a much brighter green color, as compared to the normal ones. They have a 5% chance to spawn instead of a normal Radiator, and will also disappear once hit by a net.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Holothurin|Holothurin]], mentioned in the item's tooltip, is a toxin contained within [[wikipedia:Sea cucumber|sea cucumbers]].
