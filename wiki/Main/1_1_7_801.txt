The '''1.1.7.801''' update added the [[Ravager]] [[boss]] and various [[coverings]] that are ultimately used to craft a new covering known as [[The Absorber]]. The update also made a large amount of boss balance changes, and added three new [[weapons]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Ravager]].
*Added [[Cosmic Discharge]], [[Needler]], and [[Yharim's Crystal]].
*Added [[The Absorber]], [[Craw Carapace]], [[Fungal Carapace]], [[Giant Shell]], [[Giant Tortoise Shell]], [[Grand Gelatin]], [[Life Jelly]], [[Mana Jelly]], [[Sea Shell]], and [[Vital Jelly]].
*Added [[Ancient Medallion]] and [[Birb Pheromones]].
*Added [[Cosmic Freeze]] and [[Shell Speed Boost]].
*Added [[status messages]] that appear after defeating certain vanilla bosses that announce new enemy spawns.

**Buffed contact damage from {{dv|60|72}} to {{dv|65|78}}.
**Nerfed Dust Bomb damage from {{dv|110|140}} to {{dv|70|100}}, her Hellfireball damage from {{dv|110|140}} to {{dv|50|80}}, her Hellblast damage from {{dv|120|160}} to {{dv|50|80}}, and her Brimstone Barrage damage from {{dv|90|120}} to {{dv|50|80}}.

**Increased [[Oblivion]]'s drop chance from {{dv|6.67%|7.14%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.
**Increased [[Entropy's Vigil]]'s drop chance from {{dv|6.25%|6.67%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.

*[[Cryogen]] changes:
**Nerfed its health from {{dv|0|31,800}} to {{dv|0|29,150}}, and buffed its contact damage from {{dv|35|42}} to {{dv|40|44}}.
**Nerfed its base Ice Bomb damage from {{dv|56|112}} to {{dv|46|92}}, its base Frost Blast damage from {{dv|100|112}} to {{dv|44|76}}, and its base Frost Shard damage from {{dv|80|160}} to {{dv|70|140}}.

*Buffed [[Dark Energy]]'s health from {{dv|11,000|13,200}} to {{dv|28,000|33,600}}, its damage from {{dv|110|132}} to {{dv|190|228}}, its knockback resistance from 25% to 75%, and gave it an i-frame cooldown slot of 1 instead of the default 0.
*[[Desert Scourge]] now drops {{gc|2}} instead of {{gc|5}}.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Tail and projectiles now use an i-frame cooldown slot of 1 instead of the default 0.

**Now takes 50% damage from ranged projectiles when [[Vortex armor]]'s stealth is active.
**Increased weapon drop chance for each weapon from {{dv|8.33%|10%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.
*[[The Hive Mind]] now drops {{gc|5}} instead of {{gc|15}}.
*Decreased the size of [[Ice Clasper]]s and prevented them from spawning during the [[Old One's Army]] event.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] changes:

**No longer reduces the player's life regeneration.
**Reduced speed and aggression.
**Can now only spawn up to 15 [[Detonating Flame]]s at once.
**No longer gains increased speed based on the player's damage reduction and defense.
**Buffed Detonating Flame variant 1's health from {{dv|1000|1200}} to {{dv|1500|1500}}, nerfed its contact damage from {{dv|0|210}} to {{dv|0|180}}, buffed its defense from 35 to 100, and gave it the ability to fire projectiles.
**Nerfed Detonating Flame variant 2's health from {{dv|2500|3000}} to {{dv|2000|2000}}, buffed its damage from {{dv|200|280}} to {{dv|300|360}}, buffed its defense from 45 to 120, and increased its size.
**Detonating Flames can no longer be homed in on, fade in when spawning, and no longer take up half an NPC slot.
**Yharon, his projectiles, and his Detonating Flames now use an i-frame cooldown slot of 1 instead of the default 0.
**Increased drop rate for each weapon from {{dv|16.67%|25%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.
*[[The Perforators]] no longer drop any coins, only their Hive does.
*Made [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s weapon drop chances consistent:
**Increased [[Pandemic]] and [[Pestilent Defiler]]'s drop chances from {{dv|16.67%|20%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.

*[[Cosmic Worm]] can now be used at any time of day instead of just at night.
*[[Draconic Destruction]]'s beams now set target immunity frames to 1.
*[[Elemental Disk]] now sets target immunity frames to 5.
*Increased [[Elemental Lance]]'s shot speed.
*Nerfed [[God Slayer Chestplate]]'s [[Thorns]] effect by 90%.
*[[lul]] now instantly kills the player if worn unless their name is "Fabsol".

*[[Reaver armor]] no longer provides [[Reaver Rage]] at any time the player has immunity frames active.

*[[Xeroc armor]] no longer provides [[Xeroc Rage]] and [[Xeroc Wrath]] at any time the player has immunity frames active.
