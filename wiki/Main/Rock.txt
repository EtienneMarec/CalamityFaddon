The '''Rock''' is the player's reward for beating the [[Vision of the Tyrant]] along with [[Genesis]]. Despite the challenge required to obtain it, it has no real purpose other than being a trophy for completing the Boss Rush or using it to craft the [[Eschaton]] or [[Wrath of the Gods]].

==Trivia==
*It only sells for 1 copper coin. Because of the fact that the bosses do not drop any loot or money at all during the event, this makes the Boss Rush one of the hardest (and worst) ways of making money in the game. "The Price To Pay For A Piece Of Copper"
*The item's name, and the name of Xeroc, is a reference to celebrity wrestler and actor Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson.
