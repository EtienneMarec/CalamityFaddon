The '''Gullet Worm''' is a [[critter]] that has a 10% chance to be encountered by destroying [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Objects| Background Objects] during a [[Events|Blood Moon]]. They can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]]. 

Gullet Worms act identically to [[Worm|Regular Worms]], slowly crawling along the floor.

When used as bait, Gullet Worms have a higher chance to catch enemies during a Blood Moon.

== Notes ==
*Gullet Worms can be detected by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].
*They will only have a chance to spawn during a Blood Moon.
*They can only appear from Objects that have a chance to spawn critters.
*Objects placed by the [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Rubblemaker| Rubblemaker] cannot spawn Gullet Worms.
