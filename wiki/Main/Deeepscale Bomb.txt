The '''Deeepscale Bomb''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] consumable [[explosive]]. When thrown, it explodes following a 5-second delay, dealing a high amount of damage to any entities caught in the blast, including the player. Unlike other explosives, it can only destroy naturally occurring [[Abyss#Contents|Abyss]] tiles. Despite having a large damage radius, only a few tiles are broken at a time.
