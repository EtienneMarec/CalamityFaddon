Calamity’s '''elemental system''' assigns elements to monsters and weapons. Elements can be resistant against, neutral to, or more vulnerable to others. Additionally, weapons inflict effects characteristic of their element.

== Status effects ==
Every hit from an elemental weapon can inflict the lasting effect of each of its elements on the enemy, for 2 seconds. In '''Full Fandom''' every hit inflicts every effect; in '''Calamity++''' each effect has its own chance per hit (see [[Fandom Accuracy]]). Enemies resistant to an element are immune to its effect.

=== Rules ===
* A weapon with several elements multiplies their damage modifiers: a fire weapon against an enemy vulnerable to fire and resistant to poison deals normal damage if it is also a poison weapon. The total is kept between 25% and 400%.
* Projectiles carry the elements of the weapon (or projectile) that fired them.
* Attacks whose damage is changed by an affinity show '''Vulnerable!''' or '''Resisted''' above the enemy.
* Machine weapons also increase the player's damage and critical strike chance by 5% while they are being used.
* Enemies affected by elemental debuffs show particles of the element and take a slight tint of its color; no debuff icon is displayed.

== Elemental Attributes ==
* Resistant: Enemies that are resistant to a specific element take 50% damage from attacks of that element. They are also immune to its effect.
* Neutral: Enemies with neutral affinity do not have any special resistance or vulnerability to any specific element. They take standard damage from the element in question.
* Vulnerable: Enemies vulnerable to a specific element take 200% damage from attacks of that element.

Enemies can have varying elemental affinities. Some examples include:
*A fire-based enemy like the [[Impious Immolator]] may be resistant to fire but vulnerable to water.
*An aquatic creature like the [[Reaper Shark]] could be resistant to water and vulnerable to fire.
Understanding the elemental affinities of enemies is helpful in combat.

== Tips ==
* When choosing weapons, consider the elemental affinities of the enemies you want to fight.
* Create elemental combos for maximum damage.
* Take advantage of elemental status effects.
* Adapt your tactics based on you and your enemies’ strengths and weaknesses
