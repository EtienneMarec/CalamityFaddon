'''The Camper''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. While equipped, the player receives the [[Well Fed]], [[Heart Lamp (buff)|Heart Lamp]], and [[Campfire]] [[buffs]] at all times.

The player will only deal 10% of their damage whenever they are moving, however they will be able to [[Health regeneration|regenerate health]] as if they were standing still, which results in more than doubled life regen than is normally attainable while moving.

While standing still, the player gains a damage aura that deals roughly 200 damage per second and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] onto nearby enemies. Additionally, while still, the player will be granted the following buffs:
*All damage increased by 15%.
*Nearby enemies will be inflicted with the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] and take 20-40 typeless damage.
*While holding a [[Melee weapon]], the player is granted the [[Sharpened]] buff for one second and melee speed is increased by 10%.
*While holding a [[Ranged weapon]], the player is granted the [[Ammo Box]] buff for one second.
*While holding a [[Magic weapon]], the player is granted the [[Clairvoyance]] buff for one second.
*While holding a [[Summon weapon]], the player is granted the [[Bewitched]] buff for one second and minion knockback is increased by 10%.
*While holding a [[Spy weapon]], the player is granted 10% increased projectile velocity.

== Tips ==
*The Camper's damage aura scales off of an average of all the player's damage boosts.
*The damage aura has a radius of 200 pixels, or 12.5 blocks.
*The Camper's damage reduction also affects currently airborne projectiles. For example, shooting [[Chlorophyte Bullet]]s while at a standstill and moving before they hit their target will cause them to do reduced damage.

== Trivia ==
*The name of this item refers to the gaming term "camper," where a player remains in one place for an extended period of time.
*The item is a reference to the [https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Cozy_Camper Cozy Camper] from ''[[wikipedia:Team Fortress 2|Team Fortress 2]]'', where there is a stainless pot and a bottle of [https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Jarate Jarate] hung behind.
*This item is a reference to Reddit user ''u/Dzicozan'' as he managed to defeat [[The Devourer of Gods]] while standing in one place.
