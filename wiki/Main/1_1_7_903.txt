The '''1.1.7.903''' update reduced the chances and times for the [[Exo Freeze]] debuff for a couple of weapons.

== Changes ==
*[[Exoblade]]'s explosions now inflict [[Exo Freeze]] for 2 seconds instead of 5.
*Lowered the chance for Exoblade's comets to inflict Exo Freeze from 10% to 3.33%.
*Lowered the chance for [[Subsuming Vortex]]'s vortices to inflict Exo Freeze from 3.33% to 1.33%.
