The '''Hematemesis''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] dropped by the [[Ravager]]. It fires 10 wall-piercing bolts that move upwards at a high speed near the player's mouse cursor. These bolts appear off-screen and do not pierce enemies.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Trivia ==
*The word "Hematemesis" means someone vomiting their blood.
*If the player has the "Blood and Gore" [[tgc:Setting|setting]] turned off, the projectiles will look like clouds instead of blood.
