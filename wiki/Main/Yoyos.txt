'''Yoyos''' (also called '''yo-yos''' or '''throws''') are [[Flails|flail]]-like [[melee weapons]] that can be thrown and seek after the player's cursor when used. They can remain in the air for a set amount of time or deal a set amount of hits before returning, although some may remain in the air indefinitely regardless.

The Calamity Mod currently adds {{PAGESINCATEGORY:Yoyos}} new yoyos to the game.
