Frost Hog is a rare  [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Ice biome]]. They are extremely fast and charge at the player constantly within their range.

It can be detected with the [[Lifeform Analyzer]].

== Tips ==
*Due to their size, they can be extremely hard to notice. 
*Although deadly, they have very low health and knockback resistance which makes it easy to kill.
