The '''Svantechnical''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[gun]]. It fires a stream of bullets along with firing a large shotgun blast near the cursor. The bullets in the stream have a use time of 2.

Holding right click will use its "zoom" ability, which scrolls the viewing area of the screen in the direction of the cursor.
