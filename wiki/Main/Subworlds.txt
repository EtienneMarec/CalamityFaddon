The Calamity Mod adds one ({{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} two)/({{fantag|Tranquility Mod}} {{dei}} three) new subworlds to the game.

== Subworlds ==
=== [[Pot of Pain]] ===
[[File:BiomeBannerJungleTemple.png|300px|right]]
The Pot of Pain is an optional post-[[Plantera]] challenge found in the [[Jungle Temple]] by breaking wall at the beginning of the first buzzsaw room on the second floor, above the atrium. It contains a series of difficult platforming rooms and sections, punctuated by a twin [[Golem]] fight. The player is rewarded with a special [[tgc:Bestiary|Bestiary]] entry upon completing it.

=== [[The Exosphere]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} ===
[[File:BiomeBannerExosphere.png|300px|right]]
The Exosphere is a desolate dimension devoid of any life or blocks aside from several world-spanning rows of [[Exo Platform]]s. The [[The Fabricational Quartet|Exo Mech]] boss fight is automatically initiated upon entering the subworld.

=== [[Profaned Domain]] {{fantag|Tranquility Mod}} ===

The ''Profaned Domain'' is a dimension where [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is intended to be fought if the [[Nightmare Mode]] is activated in the current world.

=== [[Caelus]] {{dei}} ===
[[File:BiomeBannerCaelus.png|300px|right]]
''Caelus'' is a space dimension accessible after defeating [[The Hive Mind]] or [[The Perforators]]. The subworld contains various structures made out of many valuable [[Crafting materials]].

===[[The Void]]===
[[File:BiomeBannerTheVoid.png|300px|right]]
The Void is a dimension which can be accessed by entering the [[Cave Entrance]] found buried underground. The subworld consists of many sub-biomes, all of which connect together via small branching tunnels to create one enormous cave system. A large chamber exists at the bottom of the world, where the [[Void Queen]] can be encountered and fought.

===[[Dragon's Aerie]]===
Dragon's Aerie is a dimension which can be accessed by entering the gateway, which opens up after defeating [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon]]. It is a serene set of floating islands of quiet forests, yellow flora and the ruins of those who enhabited it. Upon entering you are located in a cave on a seperate island connect to the main island with a large decaying bridge. The first island is a place meant to be explored by the player post-[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon]] for [[Auric Ore]] and various other things useful for the two upcoming boss fights. The second island is sprinkled with small ruins where the player can learn more about the dragons. The third is a set of gigantic ruins that act as a prebuilt arena, you gain creative shock on it and you will be unable to exit the island if you are fighting [[Godseeker, Yharim|Yharim]].
