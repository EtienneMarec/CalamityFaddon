The '''True Night's Stabber''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that autoswings. On each swing, it fires a projectile that pierces twice before stopping on the third [[enemy]]. The projectile only moves horizontally to the left or right.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, beams are shot out which last for 15 tiles and shoot cursed fireballs outwards for every 5 tiles travelled. These beams also heal the player for 5 health per hit. Additionally, the player's melee speed is increased by 10%.
