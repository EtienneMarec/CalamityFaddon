'''Grimesand''' is a [[block]] that generates on [[Floating Island]]s after breaking [[Shadow Orb]]s or [[Crimson Heart]]s. If a [[Shadow Scale]] is dropped onto a patch of Grimesand, [[The Hive Mind]] is summoned. Alternatively, if a [[Tissue Sample]] is thrown into a patch of Grimesand, [[The Perforators]] will be summoned. Whenever non-[[boss]] enemies come into contact with Grimesand, they will violently explode into purple particles, with a 10% chance to drop a piece of [[Fearmonger armor]].

== Notes ==
*Unlike [[Sand]], Grimesand is not affected by gravity.
*10-30 Grimesand can additionally be obtained as a drop from [[Corrupt Crate]]s.

== Trivia ==
*The block's sprite is derived from an edit of one of The Hive Mind's former sprites, where the sprite was duplicated several hundred times and zoomed out, creating a blurry brown texture.
