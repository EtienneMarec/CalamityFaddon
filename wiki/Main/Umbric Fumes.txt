'''Umbric Fumes''' is an [[Unobtainable features|unobtainable]] [[debuff]]. It is also inflicted by [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] {{fantag|Apotheosis and Friends}}. It functions as a stronger version of the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] and [[God Slayer Inferno]] debuffs as it reduces the health, defense, and movement speed. The debuff also has the unique effect of darkening vision and causing harmless hallucinations to appear.
[[File:Noxus Fumes Effect.gif|thumb|420x420px|The darkening and hallucination effect of Umbric Fumes.]]

==Trivia==
*Umbric Fumes being a stronger variant of the Astral Infection is connected with Umbrus' existence being responsible for the Astral Infection.
*The debuff's name is a play on the terms "Umbrus" and "umbric fumes". Umbric fumes are harmful gases as a result of chemical reactions and Umbric is a powerful, reclusive figure in Calamity lore.
