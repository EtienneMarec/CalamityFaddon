The '''Potion of Triboluminescence''' is a craftable Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] that grants the '''Triboluminescence''' [[buff]] upon usage, which grants the effects of the [[Hunter Potion|Hunter]], [[Spelunker Potion|Spelunker]], [[Dangersense Potion|Dangersense]], and [[Potion of Omniscience|Omniscience]] potions simultaneously. This causes enemies, treasures, traps, and enemy projectiles onscreen to be highlighted. Additionally, the Triboluminescence buff highlights all friendly projectiles.
