'''Bloodfire Arrows''' are Godseeker Mode [[arrows]] made from a single [[Bloodstone Core]]. They inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] upon hitting an enemy, and have a 33.33% chance to [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 1 HP. They are the default arrows shot by the [[Arterial Assault]].
