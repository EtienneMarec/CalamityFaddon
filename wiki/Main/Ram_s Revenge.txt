'''Ram's Revenge''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]]. It shoots a beam that inflicts the [[Wither]] [[debuff]] to hit [[enemies]] which reduces enemy [[defense]] by 20. If it hits a tile instead, it will create a blood explosion that inflicts [[Burning Blood]].
