== Behavior ==
They behaved similarly to the [[Probe]]s spawned by [[The Destroyer]] but they moved much faster and had a higher rate of fire.

== Notes ==
*Up to 3 of each Stasis Probe variant could spawn at a time.

== Tips ==
*The Stasis Probes made the [[Storm Weaver]] boss fight harder, so it was wise to destroy them first.
*The naked variant of the Stasis Probe moved faster than the regular variant. However, it had much less defense than the regular variant.
