'''.22 Hornet Rounds''' are Godseeker Mode [[bullets]] made from [[Auric Bar]]s. Upon contact with an enemy, the bullet will lock onto the closest enemy and redirect towards them, and continue this until hitting the 22nd enemy. If it cannot find another enemy, it will simply keep moving in the direction it was moving when it hit the enemy.

== Trivia ==
*The ammo is based on a [[wikipedia:.22_Hornet|rifle cartridge of the same name]].
*The ricocheting effect and tooltip are based on the "[[22 Bullet Theory|22 bullet]]" theory, a conspiracy theory which claims that an arch-god fired a .22 hornet bullet which pierced through and killed 22 normal gods.
*The item is also partially inspired by the 22 meme within the Terraria modding community, centered around an early [[Queen Bee]] sprite with "22" captioned on it.
