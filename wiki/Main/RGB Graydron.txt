The '''RGB Graydron''' is a Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Graydron]]. It possesses a larger than normal [[tgc:Critical hit|critical strike]] chance.

While being swung, large electric slashes that rapidly change color will swing in the direction of the cursor, and any enemies that are hit by the slashes will take damage. The slashes appear in a set looping order: one large upward slash, one larger electrified upward slash, and then a slightly smaller downward slash. Each slash deals the same amount of damage. The Graydron emits bright colored particles outward as it swings.

== Notes ==
*The RGB Graydron cannot attack through walls. The exception to this rule is enemies that move through tiles.
*Despite not behaving like normal [[swords]] or [[spears]], it is considered a [[true melee]] weapon.
**As such, it also has the most range of all true melee weapons.
*The RGB Graydron's attack speed is slower than what is visually indicated when using it, at 25 use time (2.4 swings per second). Its swing range is also fixed, despite the slashes appearing at varying distances.
*The RGB Graydron only deals 50% damage to [[The Fabricational Quartet#Ares|Ares]] and its weapons and 35% damage to [[Supreme Calamitas]]' Supreme Soul Seekers.
*If the player is playing with the retro or trippy light settings, then the RGB Graydron behaves as an immense source of light. When this is paired with a night owl potion or a night vision helmet, it generates even more light.

== Trivia ==
*The RGB Graydron is a reference to a community joke comparing Draedon's technology and the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] to a gaming computer with RGB lighting.
