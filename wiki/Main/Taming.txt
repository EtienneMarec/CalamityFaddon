'''Taming''' is a new mechanic that allows players to "tame" every boss in the game. Taming a boss involves performing a series of different steps during the bossfight in order to please them. When a boss is tamed, they will appear happy, and will disappear, leaving behind their loot as well as a unique item to reward players for their pacifistic efforts.

The player cannot harm the boss during the fight if they wish to tame it. If, at any point, the boss does take damage, the player will have to allow it to despawn before trying again. Killing a boss will make it untamable, and they must pay the [[Zoologist]] to '''Soothe''' the boss before they can try again.

== Soothing ==
Soothing is a new option presented by the Zoologist. If a boss has become untamable, they will show up in the Zoologists soothing menu. The player can pay an amount of [[Coins]] for the Zoologist to soothe said boss, allowing them to once again be tamed. Only one boss can be soothed per day.
