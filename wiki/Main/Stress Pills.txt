The '''Stress Pills''' is a [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that boosts the player's [[defense]] by 4, and max speed and acceleration by 5%. While equipped, the player loses half of their [[Adrenaline Meter]] when they get hit instead of all of it.

Any [[Boss]] [[Treasure Bag]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] has a 1.67% chance of containing this covering.

*All boss bags have a 5% chance of containing any of the three universal Revengeance coverings, the other two being [[Laudanum]] and [[Heart of Darkness]], and then a 1/3 roll is cast for which covering is given. This results in a 1.67% chance to obtain any of the three coverings, however the player can never receive more than one at a time.
