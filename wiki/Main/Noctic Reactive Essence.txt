'''Noctic Reactive Essence''' is a post-[[The Entropic God, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Crafting materials|crafting material]]. It is dropped from every enemy in the [[Corruption]], [[Underworld]], [[Astral Infection]] and [[Dungeon]] with a 10% chance. It is used to craft various Post-Umbrus night-themed mage equipment and Stiriatic Bars.
