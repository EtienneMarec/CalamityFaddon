'''Pandora's Chest''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Summon weapons|Summon Weapon]] that is dropped by [[Pandora, the Hypnotized Witch]]. It summons up to only 1 Opened Chest that hovers on top of the player. It spews out cursed apparitions to attack nearby enemies. There is a 1/400 chance that said apparitions will be replaced with a blue bird that instead heals the player by 10% their max health. It requires 4 minion slots to be used.

Various apparitions can inflict a variety of debuffs, as listed below:
*The Malevolent Dolls inflicts [[Whispering Death]] for 2 seconds.
*The Resisted Hands inflicts [[Exo Freeze]] and [[Frostburn]] for 1 second.
*The Sickened Locusts inflicts [[Plague]], and [[Venom]] for 3 seconds.
*The Crying Ghosts inflicts [[Cursed Inferno]], [[Shadowflame]], and [[Temporal Sadness]] for 5 seconds.

==Trivia==
*The weapon itself is based on [[wikipedia:Pandora's_box|Pandora's Box]], a greek artifact linking to the myth of Pandora, the first human woman, and as Zeus' way of punishing mankind for accepting Prometheus' fire he had stolen from heaven. In said myth, she was instructed not to open the box (or originally, a jar). Unfortunately, cursed with curiosity, she opened the box anyway, releasing death, sickness, hatred, etc; all the bad things in the world. By the time she closed said box, only one remained within it: Hope. Its unique way of attacking, along with a chance of the blue bird spawning reflects this.
*Additionally, the apparitions it spews out references various cursed objects:
**The Malevolent Dolls directly resembles the [[wikipedia:Annabelle_(doll)|Annabelle Doll]], a Raggedy-Ann doll that was said to be possessed by "Annabelle", a deceased girl.
**The Resisted Hands directly references [[wikipedia:The_Hands_Resist_Him|The Hands Resists Him]], a painting made by Bill Stoneham in 1972.
**The Crying Ghosts references [[wikipedia:The_Crying_Boy|The Crying Boy]], a cursed painting said to have survived amidst the ruins of burned-down houses.
**The Sickened Locust is the only one not referencing cursed objects, instead being based on one of the [[wikipedia:Plagues_of_Egypt|10 Plagues of Egypt]].
