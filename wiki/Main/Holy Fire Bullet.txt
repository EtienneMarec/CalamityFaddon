'''Holy Fire Bullets''' are Godseeker Mode [[bullets]] made from [[Unholy Essence]]s and [[Exploding Bullet]]s. Upon contact with anything, it explodes, and upon contact with an enemy, inflicts the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]]. Explosions deal 33% of the damage of the initial shot and inflict [[On Fire!]].
