The '''Scorched Earth''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[launcher]] which is an upgrade to the [[Blissful Bombardier]]. When used, it consumes two rockets and fires a burst of four homing rockets which can travel through tiles. After five seconds or upon hitting an enemy, the rocket creates a large explosion which inflicts the [[Daybroken]] [[debuff]] and spawns five additional cluster bombs in random directions outward from the enemy. Each cluster bomb explodes into 2-3 gravity-affected flaming shards which inflict the [[On Fire!]] debuff. Both the cluster bomb and shrapnel deal 25% damage.
