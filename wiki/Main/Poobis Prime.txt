'''Poobis Prime''' is a NECESSARY OR DEATH [[Challenges|Godseeker]] [[Superbosses|Superboss]] intended to be fought Post-Nameless. It serves as the "True" form of Poobisthulu and so it do be like the same, but vast differences in others.

Defeating it rewards [[Dollarbob]], allowing the player to die a horrible death. Additionally, defeating it rewards [[tgc:Poobium_Bar|Poobium Bars]], allowing players to craft Poobium Armor for the Spy Class from TF2, and also the Thrower from niche game Terraria, Poobium adapts to foreign combat doctrines.

== Spawn ==
He will find you, at the end of a long hallway, once you turn left at the crossroads.  

== Behavior ==
When spawned, Poobis Prime will rise from the gut deep below into your mind, with the Poob smashing all around the Poob Arena. Poobis Prime will dash at the nearest player, but will back away every 5 seconds after being hit like that bitch ass Infernum Hive MInd. Poobis is capitalist and shoots 100 Poobis Coin projectiles (not to be confused with Poobis Coin the item), in rapid succession. Poobis also has Anti Damage Shield so he's immune to your attacks, you cant hurt him.

== Tips ==

* Don’t try to react-dodge every coin. Watch their bounce rhythm and move ''between'' trajectories. Coins are most dangerous right after rebounds.
* Many attacks (Coin Scatter, Coin Gatling, Dash patterns) bias toward your current position. Hovering a bit to the side gives you more escape vectors.
* Most coin patterns spread horizontally or diagonally. Short vertical bursts are safer than long lateral dashes.
* Coins usually become threatening after their first or second bounce. Learn when a wave is “spent” so you don’t over-dodge into fresh patterns.
* Don’t waste dashes on coin spam. Dash only when Poobis Prime commits to a dash or laser overlap.
* Sharp starts/stops make predictive coin fire deadlier. Smooth movement throws off tracking more consistently.
* Flowing alongside the firing angle reduces correction time compared to crossing it head-on.
* Mini-Poobis are pressure tools, not the main threat. Eliminate them opportunistically, not at the cost of positioning.
* The exposed Poob Core tempts greed. Surviving denial zones matters more than maximizing DPS windows.
* Most lethal overlaps are telegraphed by Poobis Prime’s posture (core glow, rotation speed, dash wind-up), not by incoming projectiles.
* Assume no attack is isolated. Always leave yourself an exit route after every dodge.
* Overcorrecting while slowed causes chain hits. Take a breath, reset spacing, then re-engage.
