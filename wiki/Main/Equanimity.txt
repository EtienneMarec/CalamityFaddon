'''Equanimity''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]]. It automatically throws a series of returning light/dark disks that pierce infinitely. When contacting an enemy, several light and dark shards are released from the disk that deal 10% of the disk's base damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Equanimity will cause the next boomerang thrown to release additional light and dark shards.
