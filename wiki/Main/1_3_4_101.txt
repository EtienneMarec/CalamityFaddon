The '''1.3.4.101''' update added [[Planetoids]], several [[furniture]] sets, and the [[Future bosses]], along with a large amount of enemy health and damage reduction changes.

=== [[Items]] ===
*Nerfed [[The Absorber]]'s life regeneration boost when standing still from 6 to 3.
*Lowered [[Abyss Chest]]'s value by 10%.
*[[Abyss Chest]] changes:
**Recipe:
***No longer uses 2 [[Depth Cells]], 8 [[Spooky Wood]], and 2 [[Tenebris]] at any [[Work Bench]].
***Now uses 2 [[Iron Bar]]s/[[Lead Bar]]s and 8 Smooth Abyss Gravel at a Void Condenser.
**Changed how the chest shines when placed.
**Changed map color from ({{color|#000046 ●|#000046}}) to ({{color|#C8C8C8 ●|#C8C8C8}}).
**Name now displays as "Abyss Chest" on the map instead of "Sunken Chest".
*[[Abyss Gravel]]'s name no longer shows up when hovered over on the map.
*Lowered [[Affliction]]'s life regeneration boost from {{dv|2|0|3}} to {{dv|1|0|2}}.
*[[Astral Arcanum]] now has a UI.
**No longer uses hotkeys for each location, and now simply just uses one key to open the UI.
**Updated tooltip to reflect this.
*Moved the [[Magic Carpet]] line in [[Auric Tesla Cuisses]]' tooltip to a new line.
*The [[Celestial Jewel]] and Astral Arcanum now only boost life regeneration by 2 instead of 4 when not under a damaging debuff.
*Increased the amount of frames it takes for the [[Core of the Blood God]] and [[Bloodflare Core]] to heal the player.
*[[Cosmic Worm]] now longer requires 5 [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]].
*Removed life regeneration nerfs given to [[Crimson armor]] and [[Campfire]]s.
*[[Earth]]'s explosive projectiles no longer change size upon exploding.
*[[Feralthorn Claymore]] now shoots [[Vilethorn]]'s projectile instead of its own custom projectile.
*[[Rare Waifu in a Bottle]]'s Rare Sand Elemental minion reworked:
**No longer casts sandstorms.
**Now occasionally spawns healing orbs which the player can collect to recover 15 health.

=== Other ===
*The Abyss now transitions from Abyss Gravel to Voidstone in deeper layers. This includes the houses within it.
*The [[Boss Health Bar]] now accounts for the [[Old One's Army]] event's progression bar.
*The Boss Health Bar now counts the Moon Lord's individual parts.
*Lowered [[Heart Attack]]'s life regeneration boost from 2 to 1.
*Starter items will now be granted to the player if they die in Mediumcore mode.
*Increased volume for all [[Music]] tracks.
*The world's height is now increased to account for Planetoids.
*Revengeance Mode now cuts the player's damage reduction by 66% instead of 70%.
*The [[Water]] in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] now takes 3x longer to lower the player's life regeneration.
