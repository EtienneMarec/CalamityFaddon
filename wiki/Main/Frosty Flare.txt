'''Frosty Flares''' are consumable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy weapon]]s sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{sc|8}} each after the defeating all [[Mechanical Boss]]es. When thrown, icicle shards rain down from the sky around the flare. The flares remain fixed on struck enemies, causing the icicles to fall on the marked enemy. A max of five flares can mark a single enemy at a time. Thrown flares that hit enemies will always inflict the [[Frostburn]] and [[Glacial State]] [[debuffs]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with a Frosty Flare will make the weapon deal 90% damage, cause phantom copies of the Frosty Flare to rain down instead of icicle shards, and gravity-affected snowflakes to follow the path of the Frosty Flare. The phantom copies can spawn ice shards, and count towards the maximum of five flares on an enemy.

{{Modifier|none}}

== Notes ==
*Contrary to their name and appearance, they cannot be used as [[ammo]] for the [[Flare Gun]]. This is referenced in its tooltip.
*Unlike ordinary flares, Frosty Flares disappear upon impacting blocks and do not stick to them.
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].
