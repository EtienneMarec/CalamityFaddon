The '''Scab Ripper''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a tiny [[Blood Crawler]] minion, which flies around and latches onto nearby enemies. When latched, it deals contact damage in addition to causing damaging blood particles to rain from the sky. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Vile Feeder]].
