<big> ''"And on the Seventh Day..."'' </big>

'''Genesis''' is an [[Challenges|event]] added by the Calamity Mod that activates when the [[Soul of Carcinogen]], [[Soul of Ionogen]], [[Soul of Hydrogen]], [[Soul of Phytogen]], [[Soul of Pathogen]], and [[Soul of Oxygen]] are sold to the [[Archmage]]. During the event, [[Carcinogen]], [[Ionogen]], [[Hydrogen]], [[Phytogen]], [[Pathogen]], [[Oxygen]], and [[Origen]] will be fought in succession respectively, similar to the [[Boss Rush]].

Defeating '''Genesis''' will unlock the [[Endings|Elemental Ending]].
