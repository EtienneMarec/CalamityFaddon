The '''Void of Extinction''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It grants the user a 15% increase to all damage types, occasionally drops brimstone fireballs from the sky, rains down brimstone fire while invincibility is active, and grants temporary immunity to lava, reduced lava damage, and a 25% damage boost while in lava. Both the brimstone fire and fireballs inflict [[Brimstone Flames]] on hit enemies. The brimstone fireballs act almost identical to the projectiles fired by the [[Inferno Fork]], leaving behind lingering red explosions that deal heavy damage.

== Notes ==
*The Void of Extinction cannot be equipped if the player is wearing the [[Void of Calamity]] covering, and vice versa.
*The brimstone fire and fireballs deal typeless damage. They are boosted by the average of player's class bonuses which stacks additively with bonuses to 'all damage', such as the [[Avenger Emblem]].
*The brimstone fire has a base damage of 40; the brimstone fireballs have a base damage of 70.
