The '''Wrath of the Machines''' is a craftable post-[[The Fabricational Quartet]] [[covering]]. It will summon all of the Machine Elementals to assist the player and attack enemies.

When equipped, the Wrath of the Machines will summon [[Oil Bottle|Cyn]] the solver elemental, [[Silver Heart|Eleanor]] the agony elemental, [[Nameless Rat|Ash]] the phase elemental, and [[Eternal Pillow|Xun'Bakyr]] the necron elemental to attack nearby enemies. Elementals have a base damage of 550, and are affected by summon damage bonuses.

Elementals inherent their abilities with added damage and gain new, powerful attacks:
*Cyn instantly transforms weak enemies into Solver Bioforms.

*Eleanor produces clusters of Shadow Remnants.

*Ash throws Arc Snares at bosses.

*Xun'Bakyr summons Mini Necrons minions to attack enemies.
