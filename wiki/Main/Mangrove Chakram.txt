The '''Mangrove Chakram''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]]. It inflicts [[Cursed Inferno]] and can be thrown multiple times, with Chakrams returning to the owner after a set distance or hitting an enemy or solid block. Chakrams still in flight will disappear if they don't return to the player after a short while.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Mangrove Chakram causes the next boomerang thrown to leave a trail of lingering flowers as it travels.
