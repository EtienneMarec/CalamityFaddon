}}{{summoned|Exo Elemental|type=minion}}The '''Exotic Beacon''' is an [[covering]] that can be crafted. It summons a Exo Elemental to fight for you, which will cycle between two attacks when close enough to an enemy.
*It will fire three homing Exo Crystals that split upon colliding with them.
*It will stab at the enemy with vines while moving toward them.
