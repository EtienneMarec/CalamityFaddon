The '''Cavern''' layer features thirteen new [[enemies]], including two minibosses, as well as a [[shrine]] structure which contains an [[Onyx Excavator Key]]. Several new [[ores]] spawn in this layer after certain [[bosses]] have been defeated.

In [[Death Mode]], the Underground, Cavern, and Underworld layers become darker the deeper the player gets. This can be mitigated with [[Death Mode#Underground Lighting Gear|light-generating equipment]].
