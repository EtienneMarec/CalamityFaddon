The '''Shroomerang''' is a [[boomerang]] dropped by [[Crabulon]]. It fires a slow-moving boomerang that pierces infinitely before returning to the player.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Shroomerang will cause the next boomerang thrown to release homing spores every so often as it travels and temporarily grant the [[Mushy]] [[buff]] when hitting an enemy.
