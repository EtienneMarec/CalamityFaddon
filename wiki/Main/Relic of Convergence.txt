The '''Relic of Convergence''' is a Godseeker Mode usable [[tool]] that is dropped by the Crystal [[Profaned Guardian]]. When held out, it slows the player's movement speed by 50% and charges up over the course of four seconds, creating an aura around the player. When fully charged, the aura will converge on to the player, healing them for 70 HP.

== Notes ==
*Unlike most healing items, this item can be used as many times as the player wants with no cooldown.
*This can be useful for class playthroughs, as items such as the [[Vampire Knives]] or the [[Pearl God]] can heal the player, but require using a weapon outside of your class. This item heals regardless.
