The '''1.1.5.09''' update added [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] to the mod.

== Additions ==
*Added Jungle Dragon, Yharon.
*Added [[Detonating Flame]].
*Added [[Dragon Egg (summoning)|Dragon Egg]].

== Changes ==
*Hellcaster Fragment changes:
**Renamed to [[Yharon Soul Fragment]].
**Tooltip changed from "The essence of brutal fire creatures" to "A shard of a godly soul".
**Now drops from Jungle Dragon, Yharon instead of [[Solar Pillar]] enemies.
*Renamed Holy Light to [[Holy Flames]].
*Resprited [[Desert Medallion]], [[Seabound Staff]], and [[Victide Bar]].
*Resprited [[Dried Seeker]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Overloaded Sludge]] not being able to be crafted with [[Crimstone Block]]s.
