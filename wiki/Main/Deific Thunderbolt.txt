The '''Deific Thunderbolt''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[dagger]] that is an upgrade to the [[Stormfront Razor]]. It automatically throws a gravity-defying lightning bolt that does not pierce and travels faster while raining. When it hits an enemy, lightning strikes vertically from the sky which pierces indefinitely.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Deific Thunderbolt will cause the next lightning bolt thrown to summon five strikes of lightning, in addition to the main bolt travelling much faster.
