The '''Spy,''' previously known as the "rogue" class, is a new class added by the Calamity Mod. The Spy class utilizes a unique Stealth mechanic granted by its [[Covering]]s, and makes use of thrown [[Spy weapons|Spy Weapons]], 32 coverings, 5 new full armor sets, 11 armor helmets, and 2 modified vanilla armor sets, and new [[modifiers]].<!--Test-->

==Mechanics==
===Spy Damage===
The Spy's throwing weapons hit with a specific damage type called '''Spy damage'''. Coverings and armor can be used to augment Spy damage output, and generic 'damage' buffs still increase a Spy's damage.

===Proficiency===
Spy [[proficiency]] boosts the class's damage and critical strike chance, while also uniquely boosting projectile speed. Like other classes, the player's current Spy proficiency level and the stats gained from it can be viewed by purchasing a [[Level Meters|Level Meter]] from the [[Goblin Tinkerer]], and the proficiency gain rate will be boosted if it is equipped.

Stealth is a Spy-exclusive stat that the player gains access to upon wearing almost any Spy-class armor set or respective helmet. Stealth increases whenever the player is not using any items or weapons. Stealth will be gained at a higher rate if the player stands still. Stealth decreases when the player uses items or weapons, regardless of whether or not they are moving. Depending on the armor worn, the maximum Stealth value can vary between 50 and 130, though it is able to be boosted further through the use of [[Spy#Coverings|coverings]]. It initially takes exactly 3 and 9 seconds of inactivity to achieve full Stealth while standing still and moving respectively, though this can be significantly shortened with certain coverings. Upon reaching full Stealth, a sound effect plays to indicate it. Additionally, the stealth charge can be monitored through the stealth Meter towards the upper left-hand side of the screen. Max stealth is indicated when the stealth meter turns yellow.

For every point of Stealth, the player becomes slightly more transparent (this will not affect the player if they are near a town NPC, and can be disabled via configuration options), and the following stats are granted.
*+0.5% Spy damage (+0.67% per point at max stealth)
*-4 aggro

The following stats are what are granted when at maximum Stealth, listing the lowest max of 10, a typical player's max of 150 ([[Auric Tesla armor]] and an [[Eclipse Mirror]]), and the highest possible max of 200 (Auric Tesla Armor, Eclipse Mirror, [[Dark God's Sheath]], [[Silencing Sheath]], and [[Ethereal Extorter]]).<br />Note that once at maximum, every point counts toward +0.67% Spy damage instead of +0.5%.
*+13.33% Spy damage / +100% Spy damage / +133.33% Spy damage
*-80 aggro / -600 aggro / -800 aggro

====Stealth Strike====
[[File:Stormfront Razor (demo).gif|frame|Example of normal strikes versus a stealth strike on [[Stormfront Razor]].]]
A stealth strike occurs when a Spy weapon is used while the player has full Spy stealth. Upon executing a stealth strike, the player's stealth will be consumed. Wearing the [[Ruin Medallion]], [[Dark God's Sheath]], or [[Eclipse Mirror]] will cause stealth strikes to only require the player's stealth to be at least halfway full to perform, and to only consume half of the player's maximum stealth when performed. Stealth strikes have a 1 second cooldown.

Most covering effects that trigger on stealth strike hits (like sparks created from stealth strikes while wearing [[Electrician's Glove]]) can only be triggered a maximum of 5 times per stealth strike. Lifesteal effect from [[Bloodstained Glove]] and its upgrades don't have this limit, however.

===Throwing Class Compatibility===
'''Spy damage''' is internally '''throwing damage''', because of this, the Spy and Throwing classes are semi-compatible with each other:
*Coverings and armor belonging to the Throwing class that boost throwing damage and critical strike chance will also boost Spy weapons.
**Spy coverings and armor will not boost throwing damage nor critical strike chance.
* Spy stealth will not boost throwing damage.
