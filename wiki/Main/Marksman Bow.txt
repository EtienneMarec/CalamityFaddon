The '''Marksman Bow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]]. When fired, it semi-inaccurately fires three arrows at a time, each with a varying velocity. It additionally converts normal [[Wooden Arrow]]s into unique Marksman Arrows. Fired Marksman Arrows are able to be bounced off the [[Midas Prime]]'s coins to automatically aim them and greatly increase their damage and velocity.
