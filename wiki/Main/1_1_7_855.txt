The '''1.1.7.855''' update added the [[Ambrosial Ampoule]] and a variety of [[crafting materials]] and [[coverings]] used to make it. It also split [[Ataxia armor|Ataxia]] and [[Statigel armor]] into multiple classes using different helmets and changed immunity mechanics for various projectiles.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Ancient Bone Dust]], [[Beetle Juice]], [[Bloodletting Essence]], [[Demonic Bone Ash]], [[Fetid Essence]], [[Gypsy Powder]], [[Maneater Bulb]], [[Murky Paste]], [[Murky Sludge]], and [[Trapper Bulb]].
*Added [[Ambrosial Ampoule]], [[Archaic Powder]], [[Corrupt Flask]], [[Crimson Flask]], [[Living Dew]], and [[Radiant Ooze]].
*Added [[Ataxia Headgear]], [[Ataxia Helm]], [[Ataxia Helmet]], [[Ataxia Hood]], [[Statigel Cap]], [[Statigel Headgear]], and [[Statigel Mask]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Beams no longer inflict [[Frozen]].
**Projectiles now have consistent hitboxes that line up with their sprites.
**Bolt, beam, and star projectiles no longer create any dust.

**Statigel Helm no longer grants an 8% summon damage boost and extra minion.

*[[Triactis' True Paladinian Mage-Hammer of Might]]'s explosions now hit every other frame.
*[[True Ark of the Ancients]]' blue beams now hit every 5 frames, and its pink beams hit every 4.
*True Tyrant's Ultisword's Hyper Blades now pierce 5 times instead of 3, now collide with tiles, and now hit every 4 frames.
*True Tyrant's Ultisword's Sunlight Blades now pierce 3 times instead of 2, now collide with tiles, are no longer affected by gravity, and now hit every 4 frames.
