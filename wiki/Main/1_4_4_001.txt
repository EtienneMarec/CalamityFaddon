The '''1.4.4.001''' (or '''1.4.4.1''') update, also known as '''"Acid Rain"''', added the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]] and [[The Old Duke]] [[boss]]. It also added numerous [[weapons]] and other equipment especially for the Summoner class, changed the [[Sulphurous Sea]] world generation, among other balance and boss changes.

== Changes ==

*Added glowmasks to several bosses.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====

==== [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] ====
*Flare bomb ring attack now spreads in all directions. Attack animation slightly changed.

==== [[Moon Lord]] ====
*Now drops an additional 50 [[Luminite]] in every difficulty.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*Reduced [[The Siren]]'s charge distance.

==== [[The Slime God]] ====

==== [[Storm Weaver]] ====

*[[Conference Call]] now drops from [[Storm Diver]]s instead of [[Tactical Skeleton]]s.
