The '''Baby Ghost Bell''' is a [[critter]] found in the [[Sunken Sea]], and remains absolutely still. It can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].

There is a 5% chance for a Baby Ghost Bell that spawns to sparkle; sparkling Baby Ghost Bells will drop the [[Rusted Jingle Bell]] when caught with a Bug Net or Golden Bug Net, a light pet that provide extra light while submerged.
