'''Plaguebringer armor''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set that is an upgrade to the [[Bee armor]]. It requires an entire Bee armor set, along with 2 [[Alchemical Flask]]s, [[Flower Boots]], 16 [[Plague Cell Canister]]s and 16 [[Infected Armor Plating]]s  to craft the entire set. While wearing the Plaguebringer Carapace, the wearer has a tank equipped behind them. While wearing all three set pieces, a motion blur afterimage is displayed while the player is walking, paired with additional trails of green dust while sprinting.

It consists of a '''Plaguebringer Visor''', '''Plaguebringer Carapace''', and '''Plaguebringer Pistons'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*32 defense
*40% increased summon damage
*+3 max minions
*+20 max life
*15% increased movement speed
*Halves the damage taken from the [[Plague]] [[debuff]]
*Friendly bees and wasps inflict the Plague for 5 seconds.
*Summons a small Plaguebringer that causes nearby summons to deal between 10 and 20 extra flat damage while active and deals 80 summon damage every second, additionally inflicting Plague for 2 seconds.
*If the player moves at a speed of over 24 mph, they will begin releasing bees and plague-infected bees which home into nearby enemies and deal 30 summon damage.
*Allows the player to dash a base distance of 23 blocks by double-tapping the {{key|left}} or {{key|right}} key. Any enemies contacting the plague clouds trailed from dashing will take 50 summon damage and will be inflicted with the Plague for 5 seconds. The player is granted 4 [[immunity frames]] when hitting an enemy with the dash.
*The player has a chance to spawn flowers and other plants on grass beneath them, alongside random dye plants on grassless dirt, similarly to the [[Bloom Stone]].
