'''Ores''' are primary [[crafting material]]s necessary for game progress. They usually must be crafted into [[bars]] at a [[Furnace]], after which they can craft several essential [[tools]], [[weapons]], [[Covering]], and other [[items]]. Some items require raw ores themselves, most commonly [[potions]]. Raw ores can also be used as plain blocks for construction.

The Calamity Mod adds 10 (25 {{fantag}}) new ore [[block]]s to the game, that are used to craft a variety of [[tools]], [[weapons]], and [[Covering]]. With the exception of [[Sea Prism]], [[Charred Ore]], and [[Scoria Ore]], which are generated upon world creation, all ores are spawned upon the defeat of specific [[bosses]].

==Notes== 
*All ores (excluding Auric, and {{fantag|Calamity Loot Swap}} Shadowspec) have corresponding [[enemies]] (usually [[Slime]]s) which drop their respective ore, in order to make the amount obtainable unlimited. 
**[[Prism Shard]]s grow naturally in the Sunken Sea and are dropped by [[Prism-Back]]s after the [[Desert Scourge]] has been defeated as well as [[Blinded Angler]]s, which can be used to craft Sea Prisms.
**Instead of a slime, Scoria Ore is dropped by [[Devil Fish]]es and [[Chaotic Puffer]]s in the Abyss after the [[Golem]] has been defeated.
**[[Auric Slime]]s {{fantag|Calamity Loot Swap}} drop [[Auric Ore]].
