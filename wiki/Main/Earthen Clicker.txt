The '''Earthen Clicker''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] clicker. Like all clickers, it deals damage at the cursor's position within range and proper line of sight.
It has a radius multiplier of 295% and a default of 10 clicks to activate its clicker effect Crunch. Embrittle inflicts the Armor Crunch debuff.
Its best modifier is Elite.
