The '''Infected Remote''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It summons a Plague Elemental, who floats by the player and attacks enemies with various attacks set in the following pattern:
*Distances herself away from an enemy and launches up to six plague nukes that create a small explosion on enemy hits.
*Summons several miniature [[Plaguebringer]]s and tiny plagued bees around her. Plagued bees home in towards the enemy similarly to normal friendly bees, while Mini-Plaguebringers perform up to five successful horizontal charges before dissipating and may idle when no enemies are nearby.
*Charges her enemy up to a total of nine times.
All of the Plague Elemental’s patterns are based on a timer that will run even if no enemies are nearby, meaning she can start attacking an enemy in the middle of her attack pattern.
