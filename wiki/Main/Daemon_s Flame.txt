The '''Daemon's Flame''' is a Godseeker Mode [[bow]] dropped by [[Polterghast]]. It fires arrows as well as a Daemon flame, which moves in an up and down squiggly pattern, moves through blocks and explodes after a certain amount of time. Holding left-click continuously will cause the shot speed to increase.
