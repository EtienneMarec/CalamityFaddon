The '''Astral Effigy''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[effigy]] found in the [[Astral Infection]] [[shrine]]. When placed down, all players nearby become immune to the low gravity effects of [[Space]].

== Notes ==
*The range of the Astral Effigy is approximately 203 units east and west, while 154 units up and down. This makes a total area on 406 X 308 units (203 X 154 blocks).
