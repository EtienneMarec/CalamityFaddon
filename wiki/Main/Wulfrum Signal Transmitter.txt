}}The '''Wulfrum Signal Transmitter''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[furniture]] item. When right-clicked while the player has an [[Energy Core]], a signal is emitted which spawns up to 4 enemies every 4 seconds for 30 seconds. The possible enemies that could spawn include [[Wulfrum Drone]], [[Wulfrum Hovercraft]] or [[Wulfrum Gyrator]]. 

If right-clicked while the player has a [[Draedon Power Cell]], a signal is emitted that instead summons [[The Inventors]].
