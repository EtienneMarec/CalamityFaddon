The '''Vile Feeder''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a tiny [[Eater of Souls]] minion, which flies around and latches onto nearby enemies. When latched, it does not deal contact damage to enemies but releases several homing tiny eaters similar to the [[Scourge of the Corruptor]]. It is the [[Corruption]] counterpart to the [[Scab Ripper]].
