The '''BFB''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] which grants a variety of abilities related to vertical movement. It allows the player to quadruple jump and, after the jumps are expended, fly for 5 seconds. It also negates fall damage, boosts jump speed and height, and allows auto-jump when worn. It is considered to be a [[Wings|wing]] covering due to its flight capability.

== Notes ==

*This covering is notable for its incredible distance, letting you reach space easily combined with boosts to jump speed.

== Trivia ==
*The BFB is a direct reference to the [http://bloons.fandom.com/wiki/Brutal_Floating_Behemoth_(BFB) Brutal Floating Behemoth] from the ''[[wikipedia:Bloons Tower Defense|Bloons Tower Defense]]'' game series, in which the objective is to defend against waves of balloons.
