[[File:Storm Ruler (demo).gif|frame|right|The Storm Ruler and its tornado attacking a column of [[Target Dummy|Target Dummies]].]]
The '''Storm Ruler''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung it poops out a projectile which, upon impact with an enemy or tile, spawns a small tornado similar to the one the [[Aether Valkyrie]] spawns. The tornado lingers for 25 seconds, hits 8 times per second with infinite piercing, dealing damage to enemies that come in contact with it. The tornado also pulls in enemies similarly to these nuts.
