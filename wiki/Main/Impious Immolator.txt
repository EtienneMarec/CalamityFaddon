The '''Impious Immolator''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Hallow]] and [[Underworld]] biomes. Initially, it flies peacefully in one direction and turns around when it hits a block, similarly to the [[Sunskater]]. After being attacked, it will fly quickly towards the player while shooting bursts of fire which home in weakly on the player. It drops [[Unholy Essence]], a useful post-Moon Lord [[crafting material]].
