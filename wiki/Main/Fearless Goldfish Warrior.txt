The '''Fearless Goldfish Warrior''' is a rare [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns naturally on the [[Surface]] during [[Rain]]. It resembles a walking [[Goldfish]] equipped with a [[Tin Helmet]] and [[Magic Dagger]], both of which it can drop when killed. It will slowly approach the player in an attempt to stab them with the dagger.

== Trivia ==
*The Fearless Goldfish Warrior is a reference to [https://www.youtube.com/user/rwm8558 rwm8558] whose avatar shares a very similar sprite to the enemy.
*The Fearless Goldfish Warrior will instantly kill players named "Metarex" or "LordMetarex" on contact, with the [[tgc:Death#Death_Messages|death message]] "Metarex/LordMetarex was once again impaled by Goldfish." LordMetarex is one of the ''Calamity Mod'''s developers, and this easter egg is a reference to a bugged death message he received during a playthrough which stated that he was killed by a Goldfish.
