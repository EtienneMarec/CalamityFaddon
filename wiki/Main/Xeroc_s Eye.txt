'''Xeroc's Eye''' buff is a buff that you get after using [[Elderstone Armor]] set bonus, using it will spawn bright Xeroc's Eye that floats above the player's heads and charges at nearest enemies with powerful yellow laser dealing 22.000 damage every 8 tick for 4 seconds and has a cooldown of 8 seconds  (this buff lasts for 45 seconds and has a cooldown of 50 seconds after buff ends)
