The '''Death Slime''' is a Godseeker Mode [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Dungeon]] after [[Polterghast]] has been defeated. It attacks by jumping towards the player and shooting high-velocity phantom darts whenever they get too close to it or travel above it.

The Death Slime is one of the required slimes to summon [[Astrum Aureus]].

== Notes ==
*{{dei}} In the Deimos, the Starborn Princess addon, Death Slimes can spawn in the [[Dungeon]] after defeating [[Plantera]] or [[Calamitas]].
