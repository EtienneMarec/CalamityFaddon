The '''Desert Medallion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] item dropped by [[Cnidrion]]s after the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} has been defeated. It is used to [[Summoning items|summon]] the [[Grand Thunder Cnidrion]] during the day and the [[Desert Scourge]] at night. It can only be used in the [[Desert]].
