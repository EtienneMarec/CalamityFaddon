The ceasing void must have the "calamity community remix" mod enabled as it does not spawn in base calamity mod

== Spawn ==
The Ceasing Void does not spawn on its own and requires the player to summon it by using the [[Kos Fragment]] in the [[Dungeon]].

== Behavior ==
The Ceasing Void will constantly roll towards the player, accelerating until it hits a wall. It will jump to meet the player's elevation.

In the For The Worthy seed, The Ceasing Void's placement in progression is changed to post-ML between Providence, The Profanity Goddess and The Devourer of Gods. In the aforementioned seed, Ceasing Void will spawn alongside each Rune of Kos boss with 123,472 horse power, 123,456,789 max life in classic mode and 8273x damage. each time you are hit by Ceasing Void in one of these fights, Ceasing Void gains 10x damage multiplicatively with no cap.

Each Rune of Kos boss defeated in the For The Worthy seed will make 2 more Ceasing Voids spawn, although this is tied to boss kills and not boss progression so if you kill Ceaseless Void 800 times you will have to fight 1601 Ceasing Voids alongside it. Of course, the enemy limit caps this at 200 Ceasing Voids, as Ceasing Void has spawn priority and will instantly de-spawn bosses that spawn it. This includes The Devourer of Cods itself.
