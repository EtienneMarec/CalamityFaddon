== Behavior ==
Both variants circle their respective boss, attacking by firing {{item|BrimstoneBarrage|nolink=yes|ext=gif|size=9px|Brimstone Dart projectiles}}. The normal Soul Seekers stay close to the Calamitas, acting as a shield to protect her while also providing additional firepower. The Supreme Soul Seekers surround Supreme Calamitas in a ring while firing at the player in sync, serving as an additional type of obstacle that must be passed to continue damaging the boss.

== Notes ==
*{{dv|5|||10}} normal Soul Seekers spawn when Calamitas reaches 50% health.
*20 Supreme Soul Seekers spawn when Supreme Calamitas reaches 20% health.
**Supreme Soul Seekers will provide invulnerability to Supreme Calamitas until all of them are defeated.
*Supreme Soul Seekers have the following resistances, taking:
**90% damage from [[Celestus]] and the stealth strike of the [[Executioner's Blade]].
**75% damage from [[Yharim's Crystal]].
**65% damage from the [[Murasama]].
**15% damage from the [[Chicken Cannon|Chicken Cannon's]] explosions.

== Tips ==
=== Normal variant ===
*Due to the AI of the Soul Seekers, most non-piercing attacks will not hit the boss so it is recommended to destroy them first.

=== Supreme variant ===
*The [[Chicken Cannon]] and [[Celestus]] are greatly effective in taking down the Supreme Soul Seekers due to their large areas of effect.
