'''Permafrost's Concoction''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is sold by the [[Archmage]] [[NPC]] for {{gc|36}}. It increases the wearer's maximum [[mana]] by 50, decreases mana cost by 15%, and grants life regen that increases as the wearer's health lowers, with a significant bonus if the player is afflicted with the [[Poisoned]], [[On Fire!]] or [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]]s. If the player takes fatal damage while this covering is equipped, they will be [[Death Prevention|revived]] for 30% of their max health instead of dying. However, they are also inflicted with '''Encased''' upon reviving, trapping them in a defensive block of ice for 3 seconds and potentially leaving themselves open to further attacks. The revive effect has a 3-minute cooldown.

== Notes ==
*The 30% [[Damage Reduction]] gained when Encased is calculated independently from normal DR and is not affected by Calamity's [[Defense#Vanilla changes|DR scaling system]].
*With the covering equipped, the player's life regeneration is increased as follows:
**+1 if the player is below 50% life
**+1 if the player is below 25% life ''(total of +2)''
**+2 if the player is below 10% life ''(total of +4)''
**+4 if the player is afflicted with any of the three debuffs mentioned above

== Tips ==
*The player can use the [[Normality Relocator]] or similar items to teleport while encased, reducing Permafrost's Concoction's biggest drawback - being unable to move after reviving.
