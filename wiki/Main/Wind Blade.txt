The '''Wind Blade''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it creates glowing blue dust particles and fires a wind cyclone that emits projectiles and deals half the broadsword's damage. The projectile drags non-[[boss]] enemies towards it and disappears after a short while. The projectile pierces once, yet it usually hits one enemy twice instead of piercing due to [[immunity frames]].
