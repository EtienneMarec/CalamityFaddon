The '''1.3.0.002''' update made a few adjustments to the [[Abyss]] and [[Sulphurous Sea]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Aquatic Scourge]]'s body and tail segments, and [[The Destroyer]]'s body segments now take 35% damage from [[Vilethorn]]-like projectiles.
*[[Frogfish]] no longer spawn in the Abyss.

=== Other ===
*[[Abyss Gravel]], [[Planty Mush]], and [[Sulphurous Sand]] no longer generate [[Water]] in [[The Underworld]].
*Sulphurous Sand now generates more Water.
*The tunnel between the Sulphurous Sea and the Abyss is now longer.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where sulphuric bubbles would crash the game if a player touched one and had some form of invincibility from another mod.
