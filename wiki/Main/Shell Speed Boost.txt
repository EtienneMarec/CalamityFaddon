'''Shell Speed Boost''' is a [[buff]] acquired by getting hit while either the [[Giant Shell]]  or [[Flame-Licked Shell]] [[coverings]] are equipped. Having the buff increases movement speed by 90%.

== Notes ==
*This buff cannot be applied if the player is wearing an covering which can grant the [[Panic Necklace|Panic!]] buff, such as the [[Panic Necklace]]. This is due to the effects and causes of the two buffs being similar.
