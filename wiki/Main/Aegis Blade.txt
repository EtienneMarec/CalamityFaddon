[[File:Aegis Blade (demo).gif|frame|The Aegis Blade's left and right-click attacks.]]

The '''Aegis Blade''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[Golem]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it will swing and deal contact damage in a regular manner. Upon striking an enemy, a medium-sized stationary flame explosion is produced, capable of damaging the target 6 more times, each dealing 5% less damage, before dissipating. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), the broadsword itself will not deal damage but will instead shoot a fireball that deals 85% of the weapon's damage. The fireball explodes into 3 flaming sparks on contact with a tile or enemy, dealing 75% of the fireball's damage. The sparks do not pierce and can linger on the ground for 2 seconds before disappearing. If a spark hits an enemy, it spawns a small flame explosion that deals 75% of the spark's damage.

{{modifier|melee}}

*Only the left-click attack receives [[true melee]] boosts.
