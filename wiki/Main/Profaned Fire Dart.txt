'''Profaned Fire Darts''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[darts]] that are an upgrade to both [[tgc:Cursed Dart|Cursed Darts]] and [[tgc:Ichor Dart|Ichor Darts]]. The dart pierces infinitely, but cannot home. Additionally, after travelling about 7 blocks, the dart splits into 2–4 non-piercing profaned fireballs. The fireballs deal 50% of the dart's base damage, aggressively home onto the closest enemy, piercing up to 3 times and leave behind profaned sparks every 0.33 seconds. The sparks only deal 5% of the dart's base damage and home slightly slower. Profaned Fire Darts serve as a counterpart to [[Profaned Crystal Dart]]s.
