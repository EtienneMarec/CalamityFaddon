The '''Pole Warper''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It summons a pair of Warper Magnet minions that orbit around the player. When an enemy is found, the magnets disperse, pause for a brief moment, then charge at the target. Whilst using the item itself, the magnets independently circle around themselves and will not charge if enemies are nearby.

The weapon consumes 1.25 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 250.

== Notes ==

* Unlike other minions, when you use any source of teleportation like from the [[Astral Arcanum]] or the [[Magic Mirror]], the Warper Magnets don't instantly teleport alongside you. Instead, they travel to where you went, kinda like Lad Shark from the [[Joyful Heart]] pet item. This can be really annoying, as it requires you to either spend the weapon's charge re-summoning the minions or to wait for them to catch up to you.
