The '''Aestheticus''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Classless weapons|classless weapon]] which automatically swings and deal damage in a similar manner to [[broadswords]] while firing homing crystals that get their damage multiplied by damage bonuses from all classes. When the crystal hits an enemy or after a few seconds, it splits into 4 crystals that damage and home into enemies after a short time, exploding on successful enemy hits. Both crystals inflict the [[Vaporfied]] [[debuff]].

== Notes ==

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is themed around [[wikipedia:Vaporwave|vaporwave]] imagery, including pastel blues and pinks and a pattern reminiscent of the classic [[wikipedia:Windows|Windows]] logo. The name of Vaporfied debuff makes reference to this.
**The name of the weapon is a reference to the common usage of the term "ＡＥＳＴＨＥＴＩＣ" when referring to vaporwave imagery.
**It is also based off an enemy named 'Aestheticus' from the game 'Cat-Girl Without Salad: Amuse-Bouche'.
