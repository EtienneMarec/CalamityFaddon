The '''1.1.6''' update added a few new [[weapons]], and increased the effectiveness of most of the mod's damaging [[debuffs]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Crystyl Crusher]].
*Added [[Drew's Wings]].
*Added [[Contagion]], [[Cursed Capper]], [[Meteor Fist]], [[Nano Purge]], [[P90]], [[Soma Prime]], [[Soul Harvester]], and [[The Swarmer]].
*Added [[Shred]].
*Added [[Cosmolight]], [[Daylight]], and [[Moonlight]].

== Changes ==
*[[Aorta]], [[Azathoth]], [[Chaotrix]], [[The Eye of Calamitas]], [[The God's Gambit]], [[Quagmire]], [[Shimmerspark]], and [[Verdant]] now have tooltips explaining their functions.
*Nerfed [[Aureate Wings]]' horizontal acceleration from 3.5 to 3.
*Increased the damage of [[Brimstone Flames]], [[Holy Flames]], and [[Plague]] on [[enemies]].
*[[The Hive]] is now smaller, and its Hive Bombs now inflict [[Plague]].
*Renamed Virulent Katana to [[Virulence]].

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Life Seeker]].
*Resprited [[Toxic Heart]].
*Resprited The Hive, [[Malevolence]], [[Plague Staff]], and Virulence.
*Resprited [[Lifefruit Scythe]]'s projectile.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed all Calamity [[yoyos]] not dealing melee damage.
