The '''Dead Star's Laser Rifle''' is a [[Legendary items|Legendary]] gun dropped by the [[Astrum Deus]] with a 100% chance in [[Death Mode]], which shoots an infinitely piercing laser that changes its color and on-hit effects every shot:
*Blue lasers stun enemies and pulverize their defense and DR, inflicting the [[Glacial State]] debuff.
*Orange lasers ignore immunity frames, lighten the enemies ablaze and make them generate the colossal explosions on death.
*Yellow lasers make the enemies explode on hit and generate a few lingering stellar sparks on each hit which deal 80% of the weapon's damage.
*Green lasers increase the player's life regen by 5 and make the enemies emit the healing orbs that heal you for 10 HP with a 33% chance.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon can't have the autoattack, since its lasers change their properties and color only when weapon is used again, not when holding the LMB.
*This weapon is a reference to the game [https://soul-knight.fandom.com/f Soul Knight].
