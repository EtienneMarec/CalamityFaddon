'''Exterminative Darts''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[darts]]. Upon hitting an enemy, the dart will cause an explosion that deals 30% of the dart's base damage and spawn 4 planetary darts from all sides of the target. The planetary darts pierce once, dealing 10% of the dart's base damage each and home on the closest enemy. Additionally, depending on the planetary dart's color, it will decrease the enemy's stats:

*Solar darts decrease the enemy's [[Damage Reduction|damage reduction]] by 1% each, up to 4% total.
*Vortex darts decrease the enemy's movement speed by 2% each, up to 8% total.
*Nebula darts decrease the enemy's damage by 1% each, up to 4% total.
*Stardust darts will inflict a stackable [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 2 seconds, dealing 50 damage per second for each stack the enemy has, up to 200 DPS.

Each effect will slowly deplet, at the rate of 1 stack per 2 seconds. After 1.5 seconds have passed, the planetary darts will disappear. Additionally, the dart itself has homing, goes through blocks and inflicts the [[God Slayer Inferno]] debuff.
