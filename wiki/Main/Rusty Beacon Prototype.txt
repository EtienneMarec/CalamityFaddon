The '''Rusty Beacon Prototype''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that can also be found in [[Rusty Chest]]s. When used, it summons a Rusty drone that follows the player's cursor slowly. It cannot move through blocks. It will inflicts the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]] on enemies within a 5 tile radius of the drone. This radius is increased to 20 tiles if the drone is within a 2 tile range of the player's cursor. The Rusty drone is also helpful for providing light, and will provide an [[Abyss]] light level of 2.
