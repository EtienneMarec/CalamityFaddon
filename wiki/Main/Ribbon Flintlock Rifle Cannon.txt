The '''Ribbon Flintlock Rifle Cannon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[gun]]. Left clicking shoots out gold-colored bullets. Right clicking causes all active bullets to stop in place, shoot a yellow tendril at the nearest valid enemy, then shoot towards said enemies. Ribbons deal 200% of the weapon's damage.
