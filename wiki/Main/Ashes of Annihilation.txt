'''Ashes of Annihilation''' are Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]]s that drop exclusively from [[Supreme Calamitas]] and [[XC-39 Oizys]]. They are mainly used to craft several [[coverings]] and the [[Ceremonial Urn]], a non-consumable summon for [[Supreme Calamitas]].

== Trivia ==
*Ashes of Annihilation used to be exclusive to [[Expert Mode]] and above.
*The item was previously named 'Calamitous Essence'.
**Before that, it was named 'Shadow Essence'.
*The item's sell price's repeated usage of the number 6 is a reference to the [[wikipedia:Number_of_the_beast|biblical number of the beast]], 666.
