{{addon|Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic}}
'''Sanguine Shimmer''' is a liquid added in ''Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic'', that is obtained by dropping [[Catarachedrae]] into [[Shimmer]]. However, after 10 in-game minutes, Sanguine Shimmer will turn back into Shimmer. Additionally, the player can turn Sanguiner Shimmer back normal prematurely, by throwing [[Drained Catarachedrae]] into it.

Sanguine Shimmer can sanguinate items and entities dropped into it. Most items will be sanguinated into a number of [[Blood Orb|Blood Orbs]], but some give unique items that provide buffs or accessories that can be used to enhance your capabilities.
