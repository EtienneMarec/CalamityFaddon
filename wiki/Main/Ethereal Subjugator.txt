The '''Ethereal Subjugator''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Summon weapons|summon weapon]] that is dropped by [[Polterghast]]. It summons a Phantom, which follows the player and attacks [[enemies]]. It deals damage via a laser beam which has a short range. Unlike most minions, the Phantom only takes up half a minion slot.

== Tips ==
*The Ethereal Subjugator is very good at crowd control due to the piercing laser beam.
*The Ethereal Subjugator pairs incredibly well with the [[Angelic Alliance]] due to it only taking up half a minion slot, making it extremely viable endgame.

== Trivia ==
*Unlike most minions, Phantoms will target and attack some passive creatures, such as [[Goldfish]].
