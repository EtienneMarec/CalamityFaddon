'''Ashes of Calamity''' are Godseeker Mode [[crafting materials]] that drop solely from [[Supreme Calamitas]]. They are used in the crafting of [[Calamitas' Brew]], [[Sigil of Calamitas]], [[Brimflame armor]], and several [[weapons]]. They can also be used to summon the [[Calamitas]] [[boss]] when interacting (right-click) with the [[Altar of the Accursed]].
