The '''Necroplasmic Beacon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode item used to [[Summoning items|summon]] the [[Polterghast]] [[boss]] in the [[Dungeon]]. It is one of the few [[Bosses]] spawning items that gets consumed upon use; therefore, it is recommended to craft several copies of [[Necroplasmic Beacon]] as the price significantly increases after each time the boss is summoned. Note that, if the Polterghast has not been defeated yet, the boss can also be summoned by slaying 30 [[Phantom Spirit]]s in the Dungeon.

Attempting to use the item while the player is not in the Dungeon, Polterghast is alive, or using it in a world that has no [[bosses]] defeated will cause it to do nothing.
