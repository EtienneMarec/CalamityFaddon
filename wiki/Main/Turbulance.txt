The '''Turbulance''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] non-consumable [[javelin]]. When used, it automatically throws a javelin that releases four wind slashes in random directions upon contact with enemies or blocks. If the player performs a critical strike on the javelin itself, the wind slashes home on nearby enemies. Wind slashes deal one-third of the weapon's damage and do not pierce enemies or blocks.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Turbulance will cause the next javelin thrown to periodically release trails of homing wind slashes as it travels.
