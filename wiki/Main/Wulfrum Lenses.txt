The '''Wulfrum Lenses''' are a [[Pre-Hardmode]] helmet [[Covering]] piece obtained by [[Taming]] the [[Wulfrum Excavator]]. Equipping the lenses will cause a small beam of blue light to illuminate the space in front of the player. Any treasures in a generous area in front of the player are also highlighted with a similar effect to the [[Wulfrum Treasure Pinger]].
