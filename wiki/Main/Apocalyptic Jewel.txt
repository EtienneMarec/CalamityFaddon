The '''Apocalyptic Jewel''' is an unique structure that generates in all newly created worlds. It features a large construction consisting of: a portal, a Jewel Stand and the Apocalyptic Jewel itself. The structure is always located at the middle top of the world, in the [[Space]], however, the portal always generates at the 'Center point of the world.

Right-clicking the Portal will teleport the player straight to the Jewel Stand. 

Right-clicking the Jewel while the [[Death Mode]] is activated will play a sound: [[File:DemonicLaughter.wav]] and will enable the [[Nightmare Mode]] for the current world.

== Notes ==
*The Jewel Stand and the Jewel itself are always located at the top of the world, but the portal is always located at the spawn point, so the player could immediately activate the [[Nightmare Mode]] after creating the character and the world.

== Trivia ==
*Due to the Portal always generating at the normal spawn point of the world, the mod changes the spawn point globally for all players to 50' East.
*There's a 1/100 chance when actiavting the [[Nightmare Mode]] that the Mysterious Figure will spawn and will say:
**Welcome to the suffer fields, mortal
**Brace yourself before the face of the nightmare...
**I wish you best of the luck. You will need it.
*After saying it, the Mysterious Figure will disappear.
*This can only happen only when activating the mode in a new world (activating and deactivating the mode only in one world will not make it happen).
