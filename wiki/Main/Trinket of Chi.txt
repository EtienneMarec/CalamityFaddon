The '''Trinket of Chi''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] found in the Surface/Underground [[shrine]]. When equipped and the player has not moved for one second, the player will receive a 50% damage boost to all weapons and will take 15% reduced damage from all sources. Once the player moves again, the bonuses are revoked. If the player is moving, the covering will have no effect on the player.

== Notes ==
*If the player un-equips the covering then all bonuses are lost.
*Even if staying still, being on a [[mount]] will not grant any bonuses and will revoke any bonuses the player has, just the same as when moving.

== Tips ==
*This covering could be extremely useful to summoners, as the damage boost and damage reduction could allow the player to attack whilst still having the bonuses.

== Trivia ==
*Chi or Qi (Traditional: 氣, Simplified: 气) in Chinese culture refers to the believed universal manifestation of vital energy in everything.
*The item's visual design was inspired by the [[wikipedia:prayer beads|prayer beads]] worn by [[wikipedia:Akuma_(Street_Fighter)|Akuma]] in the ''[[wikipedia:Street Fighter|Street Fighter]]'' game series.
**The symbol depicted on the item is the 天 (heaven) kanji, the signature symbol of the energy that Akuma draws from.
