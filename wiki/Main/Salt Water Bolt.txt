The '''Salt Water Bolt''' is a craftable [[spell tome]] that auto-fires a ball of salt water that inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]]. The projectile acts similarly to the [[Water Bolt]], except the projectile falls to the ground after 2 seconds and does not rebound.
