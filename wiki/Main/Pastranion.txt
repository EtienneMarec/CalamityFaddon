The '''Pastranion''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spy weapon]]. It throws a bowl made of Argentum which has several strips of energy attached. These strips lash out at enemies within a 20 tile radius of the projectile. 

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Pastranion greatly increases the range of the strips and makes them attack quicker.
