'''Tarra Life''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player at a 25% chance when they are damaged while wearing the Melee [[Tarragon armor|Tarragon]] or [[Auric Tesla armor|Auric Tesla]] armors. It grants an additional +3 life regenerated per second, helping the player recover from hits.
