The '''Azathoth''' is a craftable Epilogue [[yoyo]]. While in use, the Azathoth constantly releases pink cosmic orbs. Each cosmic orb can fire up to six hitscan lasers at nearby enemies before vanishing. The orbs, their lasers, and the yoyo itself ignore all NPC [[immunity frames]], allowing the Azathoth to damage enemies unbelievably rapidly.

== Notes ==

== Trivia ==
*The name ''Azathoth'' is a reference to a [[wikipedia:Azathoth|H.P. Lovecraft deity]], much like Cthulhu. In the Cthulhu Mythos, Azathoth is said to be the king of the Outer Gods, whose dream constitutes all of reality.
*The Azathoth's projectiles and effects are a reference to the [https://terrariamods.fandom.com/wiki/Exxo_Avalon/Berserker_Nightmare Berserker Nightmare] from the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/released-wip-terraria-avalon.378/ Avalon Mod].
