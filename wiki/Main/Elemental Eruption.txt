The '''Elemental Eruption''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that fires clusters of rainbow flames, which inflict numerous [[debuffs]] onto [[enemies]]. The flames can pierce one enemy, and as with all flamethrowers of its type, it auto-fires.


On enemies, these combined debuffs cause the target to be unable to regenerate health, and lose 18 health per second for 5 seconds. In [[Revengeance Mode|Revengeance]] and [[Death Mode]], they also have 20% reduced damage reduction. Additionally, for the first 2 seconds, it will lose an extra 88 health per second and have their defense reduced by 4.
