The '''Brimblade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] made from [[Unholy Core]]s at a [[Hardmode Anvil]]. When used, it auto-throws a series of brimstone blades. When a blade strikes an enemy, it will split into 3 blades, which return to the player shortly after being released. The split blades deal 30% less damage than the main blade. Every blade inflicts the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] and emits a burst of red particles on contact.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Brimblade causes four brimstone darts to be fired alongside the Brimblade, and also causes the Brimblade to split into 4 blades instead of 3. The brimstone darts each deal the same amount of damage as the Brimblades, but have halved knockback.
