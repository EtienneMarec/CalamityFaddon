The '''Fallen Paladin's Hammer''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[boomerang]] and an upgrade of the [[Paladin's Hammer]]. It throws hammers at a short range that inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] and create small explosions upon striking enemies that inflict [[On Fire!]]. Unlike the Paladin's Hammer, the Fallen Paladin's Hammer can pass through walls unhindered.
