The '''Astral Arcanum''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It provides 12 [[defense]] and immunity to the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]].

It also boosts life regeneration by 3, even while the player is under the effects of a damaging debuff. When worn while being damaged by a debuff, the player's defense will increase by 15 and life regen will increase by an additional 3 (for a total of 6).

Pressing the {{key|Astral Arcanum UI Toggle}} key opens up a small menu, allowing the player to select a destination to teleport to by hovering their cursor above them and clicking. The player can teleport to either the [[Jungle]], [[Underworld]], [[Dungeon]] or to a random location. The teleports have no cooldown. This doesn't work if a [[boss]] is alive

== Notes ==
*The teleporting effect of the Astral Arcanum is disabled if the world is on [[tgc:Multiplayer|Multiplayer]].
*The boost life regen while under the effects of a damaging debuff stat is powerful enough to bypass the disabled life regen stat given from [[Vulnerability Hex]].
*Due to the Jungle's imprecisely defined location, teleporting to the Jungle can place the player nearby it instead of within it. It also tends to place the player underground.
*If the player is under the effect of one of the alcohols sold by the [[Drunk Princess]], the covering's life regen bonuses will be reduced to 2 regardless of other debuffs.
