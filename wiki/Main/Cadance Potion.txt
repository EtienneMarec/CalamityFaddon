The '''Cadance Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. It grants the '''Cadance''' [[buff]] upon usage, which increases life regen by 5 and max life by 25%, and increases heart pickup range. Enemy spawn rates are decreased by 56%, and maximum spawn count is decreased by 44%. The player also becomes immune to the [[Regeneration]] and [[Lifeforce]] buffs.

== Notes ==
*This potion can only be crafted at an [[Alchemy Table]].
*Despite mentioning [[aggro]] in its tooltip, it actually reduces enemy spawnrates instead. This oddity is shared with the [[Calming Potion]].
*The reduced enemy spawn rates do not stack with [[Calming Potion]], [[Tarragon armor]] set bonus, or [[Auric Tesla armor]] set bonus.

== Trivia ==
*This item's name is a reference to the character ''Princess Cadance'' from the TV series ''[[wikipedia:My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic|My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic]]''.
*This item was formerly known as "Cad'''e'''nce Potion".
