The '''Twisted Netherite Sword''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode broadsword that autoswings. If the sword hits a non-[[boss]] enemy while a boss isn't alive, they will fade into ashes and die instantly, dropping loot.

Its best modifier is '''Legendary'''.

== Trivia ==
*The Twisted Netherite Sword is based on the Netherite Sword from ''[[wikipedia:Minecraft|Minecraft]]''.
**Its base damage and use times are identical.
**Its research value is the same as the Netherite Sword's durability.
*The weapon's vaporization effect is a reference to the Twisted Sword from ''[[wikipedia:Deltarune|Deltarune]]'' which has a common misconception of being able to instantly kill enemies and turn them into dust.
