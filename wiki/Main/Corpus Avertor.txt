The '''Corpus Avertor''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] exclusive to Expert Mode, that drops from the [[Ravager]]. When used, a translucent black knife is thrown that creates smaller homing red knives as it travels. The red knives pass through blocks, only deal 40% of the Corpus Avertor’s base damage, and [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 5% of their damage.

10% of the player's missing health is added to the weapon's base damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Corpus Avertor throws a translucent rainbow knife that doesn't create smaller knives and deals 350% damage but has a 1 in 7 chance to cut the player's health in half upon hitting an enemy.

{{modifier|rogue}}

== Tips ==
*This weapon has incredible synergy with the [[Blood Pact]], as the doubled max hp allows for much more hp to be missing, greatly empowering the weapon.

== Trivia ==
*If the player is killed by the the stealth strike's self-damage, the death message ''"<PlayerName> became the blood god's sacrifice."'' will display.
