'''Heavy Metal Trash Can''' is a [[pet]] summoning item dropped by [[The Wall of Bronze]]'s [[Treasure Bag]]. It summons a mini [https://bocchi-the-rock.fandom.com/wiki/Hitori_Gotoh?so=search Hitori] to follow the player. If the player moves too far away, Hitori will fly on top of a pink slime to catch up to them. 

==Trivia==
*The item is a direct reference to the character [https://bocchi-the-rock.fandom.com/wiki/Hitori_Gotoh?so=search Bocchi] from the anime [https://bocchi-the-rock.fandom.com/wiki/Bocchi_the_Rock!_Wiki Bocchi the Rock].
**The character often hides inside of a trash can in the show.
**The tooltip is a reference to the characters online persona, who is referred to as "the Guitar Hero".
***It might also be a reference to the [https://guitarhero.fandom.com/wiki/Guitar_Hero_(series) Guitar Hero] game series.
