The '''Phantom Debris''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Underground]] layer and resembles a [[tgc:Ambient objects|background rock]]. It initially moves very quickly, but slows down noticeably the closer it gets to the player. It constantly emits a magenta glow, allowing it to be easily seen in dark caves.

After [[Moon Lord]] is defeated, its stats are buffed and it begins dropping [[Phantoplasm]], a versatile [[crafting material]].

== Trivia ==
*It is one of two enemies which can drop Phantoplasm that spawn in the Cavern layer, the other being the [[Overloaded Soldier]]. This is unlike the other [[Phantom Spirit|phantoplasmic creatures]] which are found in the [[Dungeon]].
