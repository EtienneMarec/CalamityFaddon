The '''Phantomic Soul Artifact''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. While equipped, whenever a minion hits an enemy, the player will gain one of the following [[buffs]]:

*'''Phantomic Empowerment''', which increases minion damage by 10%. Additionally, while this buff is active, there is a 10% chance for a phantomic dagger to spawn. The dagger has 75 base summon damage and there can be a maximum of three knives active at once. 
*'''Phantomic Shield''', which increases [[defense]] by 10 and damage reduction by 5%. Additionally, a phantomic bulwark will spawn and absorb 20% damage from any attack taken while it is active. After damage is absorbed, the bulwark will disappear and has a 30 second cooldown.
*'''Phantomic Regen''', which grants +2 life regeneration. Additionally, a phantomic heart will spawn at a 5% chance and grant the player a further +2 life regen.
