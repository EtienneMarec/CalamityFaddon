The '''Tiny Planet''' is a [[spy weapon]] that drops from [[Goozma]]. When fired, it throws out a planet that orbits enemies it hits. If an enemy already has a planet orbiting them, further hits by the weapon will instead increase the orbiting speed of the planet by 50% up until the planet has reached 500% of its original speed. The planets orbit at a range where they are guaranteed to not hit the orbited enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Tiny Planet will cause two smaller planets to be thrown as well.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].

== Trivia ==
*It is the only weapon Goozma drops that doesn't scale with progression.
