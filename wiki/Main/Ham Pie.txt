The '''Ham Pie''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Permanent Power-ups|Permanent Power-up]] item that grants the player a permanent 8% and +8 increase in Damage Reduction and Defense respectively, in addition to halving defense damage. It can only be used once.
