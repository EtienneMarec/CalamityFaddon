The '''Mycoroot''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[dagger]] that is dropped by [[Crabulon]]. When used, it auto-fires a continuous stream of short-range fungal roots. The fungal roots cannot pierce enemies, nor can they pass through walls.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Mycoroot causes a large amount of fungi spores to burst out of the player. These fungi spores home into nearby enemies shortly after.

{{Modifier|Spy}}

== Tips ==
*Its insanely fast rate of fire gives it good synergy with the [[Shark Tooth Necklace]] and [[Luxor's Gift]].
