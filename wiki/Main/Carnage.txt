The '''Carnage''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from the [[Wall of Flesh]]. Whenever it deals a finishing blow, 6-8 homing blood bolts erupt out of the enemy. Each bolt deals damage and [[Lifesteal|heals]] the player for 2 HP upon enemy contact.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*It was formerly a [[Malice Mode]] exclusive item dropped by [[The Hive Mind]] and [[The Perforators]].
** Before that, it was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Bloody Edge]] and dropped by [[tgc:Blood Zombie|Blood Zombies]] and [[Blight Slime|Crimulan Blight Slimes]] after defeating [[Skeletron]].
