The '''Ichor Spear''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[javelin]] that is rarely dropped by [[Ichor Sticker]]s. It travels a long distance before being affected by gravity and can pierce one enemy. The Ichor Spear applies the [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]] [[debuff]] to all hit enemies. It is the [[Crimson]] variant of the [[Cursed Dagger]] ({{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} [[Cursed Spear]]).

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Ichor Spear causes the resulting javelin to release streams of ichor in random directions as it travels.
