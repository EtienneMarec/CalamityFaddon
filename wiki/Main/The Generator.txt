'''The Generator''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that is dropped by [[Pyrogen]]. It fires an infinitely piercing spiked purple sigil that draws in nearby enemies and deals continuous damage to them. The sigil travels in a straight line and travels through placed tiles while emitting light.
