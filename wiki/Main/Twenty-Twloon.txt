The '''Twenty-Twloon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that can also be obtained from the [[Agent]]. It is the direct upgrade to the [[Twentloon]] and the final upgrade to the [[Harpoon]]. It rapidly fires twenty two harpoons that are unaffected by gravity. The harpoons travel a short distance before returning to the player.
