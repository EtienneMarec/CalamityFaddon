The '''Turtle''' is an [[NPC]] vendor that sells various items.

The Turtle will move into an available NPC house if the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty [[tgc:House|house]].
*[[The Wall of Bronze]] has been defeated.
*The player is wearing a full set of [[tgc:Spectre_armor|Spectre Armor]].

When threatened by [[enemies]], they will defend themselves with a Rocket Launcher.

==Trivia==
*The Turtle is a reference to the video [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tF1fitWqXdM Short Film "The Cursed Resort"], by youtuber Maizen. 
**Mikey the Turtle was a prominent character in the short.
**The items Mikey sells, and their relation to spectre armor, are all items relevant to the plot of the short.
*Despite most of Mikeys wares being far outclassed by the point in the game when you meet them, buying the Artisan Loaf from them can be a far less rng dependent method of obtaining the item compared to the alternative.
*The Turtle's sprite seems to be a modified version of the [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Creeper_set?so=search Creeper Costume].
