The '''Planetarium''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Wand]] dropped by [[XP-00 Hypnos]]. When used, it rapidly fires a volley of lasers into the sky that travel vertically from the player's position, damaging and piercing any [[enemies]] above the player. After leaving through the top of the screen, each laser will individually seek out a random enemy and enter from the top of the screen at the same horizontal position as the enemy, travelling directly downward until it makes contact with a target and disappears, dealing [[Magic weapons|magic]] damage and creating an explosion with a radius of 3 tiles, damaging all enemies inside. If a [[Challenges|boss]] is currently on the screen as the weapon is being fired, all lasers will automatically target the boss.
