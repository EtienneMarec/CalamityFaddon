The '''Spear of Paleolith''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[javelin]]. It automatically throws high-velocity spears which travel extremely far before being affected by gravity. These spears do not pierce through enemies, but they rain down fossil shards while traveling similar to the [[North Pole]]. The fossil shards deal half the damage of the spear. Any enemy hit by the spear or the fossil shards will be inflicted with [[Armor Crunch]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Spear of Paleolith will cause the next spear thrown to travel slower but farther and release more fossil shards.
