The '''Strange Orb''' is a [[Pets|light pet]] summoning item that summons an ocean spirit to follow the player. It is obtained from the Shadow Chests in the [[Abyss]]. When equipped while the player is not submerged in a liquid, it sleeps while holding the orb and gives a small amount of light. When in liquid, it swims above the player and provides much more light.

== Notes ==
*The Ocean Spirit is a very effective way to increase your light level in the [[Abyss]].
*This item can also be obtained from the [[Abyssal Crate]] after either [[The Slime God]] or [[Wall of Flesh]] have been defeated.

== Trivia ==
*The pet draws visual inspiration from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysaora_quinquecirrha Sea Nettle jellyfish].
*Talking to the [[Sea King]] with the Ocean Spirit active will display unique quotes.
*The Ocean Spirit's buff tooltip is the same as the [[Levi|Levi's]].
