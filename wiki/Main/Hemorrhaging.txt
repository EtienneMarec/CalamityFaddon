'''Irradiated''' is a [[debuff]] that deals -1% of the player's max health and -0.3% of the enemy's max health. Its damage increases to -2.5% to the player while they are moving horizontally. It also reduces their movement speed and acceleration by -25%. Enemies that are immune to either [[Bleeding]] or [[Electrified]] will also be immune to this debuff.
