The '''Stellar Contempt''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[boomerang]]. It repeatedly throws high velocity hammers at a medium range, which can pass through tiles. Upon contact with a target, lunar flares will rain down onto the target. If it is nighttime, the hammer itself will inflict the [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuff]] on every enemy it strikes.
