The '''1.1.5.07''' update resprited a few items, and removed a few sources of the [[Burning Blood]] debuff from [[The Perforator]] fight.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Elemental Axe]].
*Added [[Elemental Axe (buff)]].

== Changes ==
*[[Perforator Mini]]'s tail no longer inflicts [[Burning Blood]].
*The tails of the small and medium [[Perforators]] no longer inflict Burning Blood.
*[[Calamitas' Scroll]] now uses [[Crystal Shard]]s and 1 [[Sorcerer Emblem]] in its recipe.
*Nerfed [[Crystal Blade]]'s damage.
*Renamed Accretion Disk to [[Elemental Disk]].
*Renamed Spatial Lance to [[Elemental Lance]].
*Resprited [[Blood Sample]], [[Bloody Worm Tooth]], [[Devastation]], [[Essence of Chaos]], [[Essence of Cinder]], and [[Essence of Eleum]].
