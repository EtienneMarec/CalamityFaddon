The '''Monstrous Knives''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that acts similarly to the [[Vampire Knives]], and is the counterpart to the [[Moonstone Knives]]. When used, the player throws a spread of 3-6 gravity-affected knives that have a 33.33% chance to [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 7.5% to 90% of the damage dealt. The knives fade away after a short amount of time, losing 10% of its damage and knockback every 0.5 seconds, then disappear into cosmetic red dust when fully transparent.
