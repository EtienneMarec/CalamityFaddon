The '''Elephant Killer''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Classless weapons|classless weapon]]. It is a direct upgrade of the [[Lightning Hawk]] and the final upgrade of the [[Magnum]]. When used, it fires a bullet similar to a [[High Velocity Bullet]] which cannot pierce. 1.33% of the target's health is added to the damage of each bullet.

Like the Magnum and Lightning Hawk, it uses [[Magnum Rounds]] as ammunition, which can be purchased from the [[Arms Dealer]] if the player has the Elephant Killer in their inventory.

Once a boss is spawned, the Elephant Killer is only fireable three times until the boss is defeated.
