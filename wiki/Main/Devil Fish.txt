The '''Devil Fish''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the 1st and 2nd layers of the [[Abyss]].

It remains passive, swimming back and forth, until the player reaches under 1/4 of their max health, after which it will become aggressive and attempt to attack the player.

Its mask also protects it from 5 hits, after which it will break and the Devil Fish will become permanently aggressive. When the mask is broken, it receives a speed boost.

== Notes ==
*Unlike other fish in the Abyss, summons currently do not target or damage the Devil Fish even when it is hit with another weapon.
*The Devil Fish can spawn with one of two different masks, featuring different horn shapes.

== Trivia ==
*The Devil Fish's masked state is tied with the [[Flak Crab]]'s disguised state for having the highest defense of any enemy in the mod.
*The sound that plays when the Devil Fish's mask breaks is an edited version of the sound made by [https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie Zombies] breaking [https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Door Doors] from the game ''[[wikipedia:Minecraft|Minecraft]]''.
