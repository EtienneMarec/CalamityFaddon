The '''Frost Blossom Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a frozen flower minion above the player’s head which shoots frost beams towards a nearby enemy. The beam creates a small explosion in the shape of a flower with three, four, or five petals in a randomized manner, all of which deals one-third of the weapon’s damage. Unlike most minions, only one Frost Blossom can be summoned.
