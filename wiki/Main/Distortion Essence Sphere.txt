The '''Distortion Essence Sphere''' (Japanese: ディストーションエッセンススフィア ''Disutōshon'essensusufia'') is a Godseeker Mode [[mini boss]] that spawns in the post-[[The Starborn Moth|Starborn Moth]] [[Martian Madness]] event. It spawns on waves 5 of the event and later waves with reduced health or can be spawned in manually by using a [[Cosmic Entangler]], It has 5 attacks that are as follows: 

*Spawns in a [[Cosmic Guardian]] and dashes toward the player, Cosmic Guardians will orbit around the Boss. Up to 2 Cosmic Guardians can be alive at the same time.
*Fires several spreads of {{dv|8|10}} evenly spaced Laser Beams in all directions while the Boss approaches the player.
*Dashes away from the player and creates a Black Hole, the Black Hole will chase down the player for 3 seconds before exploding into a spread of {{dv|12|16}} Laser Beams.
*The Boss starts slowly moving towards the player and creates {{dv|3|4}} evenly spaced lines, which turn into solide Deathbeams after {{dv|1.5|1}} seconds which spin clockwise.
