The '''Detonating Flame''' was a Godseeker Mode [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] which spawned during the [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] fight. There were 2 variants.

The fireball variant aggressively flew toward the player and occasionally shot a fireball that inflicted the [[On Fire!]] debuff. It was spawned in Yharon's subphases 1 and 3.

The melee variant flew toward the player more slowly than the fireball variant. It dealt more contact damage but did not shoot. It was spawned in Yharon's subphases 2 and 3.

Both variants were summoned in all subphases of phase 2 after Yharon flew in a loop and summons a ring of {{item|FlareDust2|nolink=yes|ext=gif|size=16px|Homing Flare Dusts}}.
