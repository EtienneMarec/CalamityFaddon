The '''Aether's Whisper''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]]. It shoots a laser beam that splits into smaller beams that travel through blocks on tile contact and inflicts [[shadowflame]] to enemies. The beams additively increase their damage by 0.5% of the weapon's damage for every frame they exist.

The projectiles can inflict [[Shadowflame]] for 10 seconds.
