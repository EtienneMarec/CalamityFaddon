'''Horseslime armor''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Statigel armor]]. 20 [[Quark-Gluon Sludge]], 20 [[Life Alloy]], 10 [[Omnicidal Sludge Bar]]s, and the corresponding pieces from [[Statigel armor]] are required to craft a set utilizing one headpiece.

It consists of the '''Horseslime Core Plate''' and '''Horseslime Legs''', as well as having five different headpieces suited to each class, being: the '''Goopocalypse War Helm''', '''Goopocalypse Death Helmet''', '''Goopocalypse Famine Hat''', '''Goopocalypse Phantasmal Collar''', and '''Goopocalypse Conquest Visor'''. All helmets share a set bonus, but also provide set bonuses specific to their respective class as well.

The '''Horseslime Core Plate''' gives 56 defense, 7% increased [[Damage Reduction]], 15 extra [[Immunity frames]], +5 life regen, and +15 armor penetration.
The '''Horseslime Legs''' give 44 defense and 20% increased movement speed.
*The '''Goopocalypse War Helm''' gives 44 defense, 20% increased melee damage, 30% increased [[True melee|true melee]] damage, and 12% increased melee critical strike chance.
*The '''Goopocalypse Death Helmet''' gives 33 defense and 20% increased ranged damage and 12% increased ranged critical strike chance.
*The '''Goopocalypse Famine Hat''' gives 24 defense, 20% increased magic damage and 15% increased magic critical strike chance, +80 mana, and 15% reduced mana cost.
*The '''Goopocalypse Phantasmal Collar''' gives 18 defense and 5% increased minion damage.
*The '''Goopocalypse Conquest Visor''' gives 38 defense, 25% increased Spy damage and 12% increased Spy critical strike chance.

== Set Bonuses ==
Regardless of the headpiece chosen, equipping a full set of Goopocalypse armor will provide the following set bonuses:
*Walking and running will leave a trail of dark slime.
**The slime deals 1000 typeless damage.
*Jump speed is increased by 20%.
*All non-summoner attacks have a 20% chance of shooting a fireball.
**These fireballs deal 1500 typeless damage.
*If the player is at 50% of their life or below, they will gain 35 defense and +8 life regen.
*Immunity to the [[Omnislime]] debuffs.
*Most effects from the [[Statigel armor]] set.

In addition to the above, various headpieces provide their own set bonuses:

'''Goopocalypse War Helm:'''
*+700 to [[Aggro]].
*Causes true melee strikes to [[Lifesteal|heal]] on hit.

'''Goopocalypse Famine Hat:'''
*Grants the effects of [[Magic Cuffs]].

'''Goopocalypse Phantasmal Collar:'''
*55% increased minion damage, +3 max minions, and +1 max sentries.
*Summons an Apocalyptic Apparition, which shoots fireballs at enemies.
**These fireballs deal 900 summon damage.

'''Goopocalypse Conquest Visor:'''
*Gives a maximum [[Spy stealth]] of 100.
