The '''Deck of Souls''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]]. When used, it throws a talisman that latches onto [[enemies]] and sticks there, periodically releasing homing souls that deal 50% of the talisman's base damage. Up to three talismans can be stuck to an enemy at once.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Deck of Souls causes the resulting talisman to deal 80% damage and release additional souls once latched on, as well as making that talisman release lingering souls as it travels that deal 80% damage each. Additionally, homing souls spawned from latching talisman deal 40% damage.
