The '''Magnum''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]]. When used, it fires a bullet similar to a [[High Velocity Bullet]] which cannot pierce. 1.33% of the target's health is added to the damage of each bullet.

It uses [[Magnum Rounds]] as ammo, which can be purchased from the [[Arms Dealer]] if the player has the Magnum in their inventory.

Once a boss is spawned, the Magnum is only fireable three times until the boss is defeated.
