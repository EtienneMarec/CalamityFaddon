The '''2.0.1.002''' (or '''2.0.1.2''') update fixed numerous issues from the previous updates, reworked several weapons and significantly rebalanced the [[Spy]] class and other items. The update also added dozens of new sounds, a couple new [[critters]] for the [[Arsenal Labs]], and a variety of loading menu tips. In addition, this update polished support for features from the [[tgc:1.4.0.1|Journey's End]] update including [[Bestiary]], [[Journey Mode]], and various miscellaneous items and features such as tree shaking.

This update also ported the mod to tModLoader v.2022.9.47, and implemented its newly supported custom game tips feature.
