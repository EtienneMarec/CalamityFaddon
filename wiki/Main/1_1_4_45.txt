The '''1.1.4.45''' update added a handful of weapons, among an assortment of resprites.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Flarewing Bow]], [[Gunk Shot]], [[Hellwing Staff]], [[Helstorm]], [[Megafleet]], [[Plague Staff]], and [[Stardust Staff]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Calamitas]]' spawn rate lowered.
*[[Lunatic Cultist]] now drops the Stardust Staff.
*[[Slime God]] and True Slime God now gain Biome Mimic movement once they reach 50% health.
*Slime God's projectile movements changed to be more stationary.
*[[Wall of Flesh]] no longer gets a health buff in Hardmode.

=== Items ===
*Nerfed [[Animus]]' use time from 5 to 11.
*[[Azathoth]] now has the developer item tooltip.
*Azathoth now requires 15 [[Shadowspec Bar]]s to craft.
*Buffed [[Corroslime Staff]] and [[Crimslime Staff]] damage from 23 to 30.
*[[Galactica Blade]] changes:
**Buffed damage from 148 to 188, and its shot speed from 10 to 18.
**Now rains a barrage of comets from the sky on swing.
**Now uses 1 [[Star Wrath]], 1 [[Dark Shard]], 10 [[Ectoplasm]], 1 [[Light Shard]], and 20 [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]] in its recipe instead of 1 [[Influx Waver]], 1 [[Starfury]], 30 [[Chlorophyte Bar]]s, 3 Dark Shards, 20 Ectoplasm, 3 Light Shards, and 50 Souls of Might.
*Nerfed [[Infinity Boots]]' speed.
*Infinity Boots can now reach top speed much quicker.
*[[Skyline Wings]] now only require 5 Feathers in its recipe instead of 15.
*[[Tradewinds]] now only requires 3 Feathers in its recipe instead of 15.
*[[Virulent Katana]]'s projectile now lasts three times longer.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Adamantite Throwing Axe]], [[Cnidarian]], [[Cobalt Kunai]], [[Coral Spout]], [[Flare Bolt]], [[Frigidflash Bolt]], [[Frost Bolt]], [[Galactica Blade]], [[Gelitic Blade]], [[Impaler]], [[Mythril Knife]], [[Orichalcum Spiked Gemstone]], [[Palladium Javelin]], [[Redtide Sword]], [[Seabow]], [[Seashell Boomerang]], [[Starnight Lance]], [[Tears of Heaven]], [[Temporal Floe Sword]], [[Titanium Shuriken]], and [[Urchin Spear]].
*Resprited [[Abyssal Warhammer]], [[Greatbay Pickaxe]], [[Reefclaw Hamaxe]], and [[Shardlight Pickaxe]].
*Resprited [[Shield of the Ocean]].
*Resprited [[Victide Bar]].

== Fixes ==
*[[Royal Knives]] no longer stack to 999.
*[[Crystal Piercer]] and [[Starnight Lance]] projectile images fixed.
*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] can no longer fly into space and despawn randomly.
*The Slime God is no longer buffed twice at the same time once the Moon Lord has been defeated or Hardmode has begun.
*[[Staff of the Mechworm]]'s Mechworm's tail should now properly connect to the worm's body.
