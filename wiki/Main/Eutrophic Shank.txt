The '''Eutrophic Shank''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is dropped by [[Eutrophic Ray]]s with a 1/3 (33.33%) chance after the [[Desert Scourge]] has been defeated. When used, it shoots electrical sparks which linger on the ground and deal 40% of the weapon's base damage.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, enemies hit by it and its sparks are inflicted with [[Eutrophication]] for 5 seconds.
