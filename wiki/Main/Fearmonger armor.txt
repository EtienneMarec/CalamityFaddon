'''Fearmonger armor''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set obtained by getting any enemy to step on [[Grimesand]], after which they will explode and have a chance to drop a piece of the set. While wearing all three set pieces, a motion blur afterimage is displayed while the player is walking. If the entire set is visible (regardless of whether it is equipped or in social slots), the wearer will have a faint pulsing aura along with a red streak trailing behind their eye.

It consists of a '''Fearmonger Greathelm''', '''Fearmonger Plate Mail''', and '''Fearmonger Greaves'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*16 defense
*8% increased damage reduction
*11% increased damage
*5% increased critical strike chance
*30% increased summon damage
*10% increased movement speed
*+1 max minions
*Life regeneration temporarily increased by 2 on every minion hit. The duration increases by 1/3 of a second per minion hit, up to a maximum of 1.5 seconds.
*The [[Vanilla changes#Minions|summon damage penalty]] is reduced from -50% damage to -25% damage.
