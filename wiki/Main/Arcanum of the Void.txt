The '''Arcanum of the Void''' is an [[Expert Mode]]-exclusive Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped by [[Ceaseless Void]]. It summons a Void Barrier around the player. Getting hit blocks that hit and creates an explosion and releases Homing Dark Energy Projectiles that last for 4 seconds and deals ~200 DPS. This effect can only occur every 90 seconds.{{-}}
