'''Acidwood''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[block]] obtained by cutting down [[Acidwood Tree]]s in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]]. It is used to craft several weapons themed around the Acid Rain, as well as the [[Sulphurous armor]]. Acidwood can also be found in [[Abyssal Crate]]s obtained by fishing in Sulphurous Sea biome.
