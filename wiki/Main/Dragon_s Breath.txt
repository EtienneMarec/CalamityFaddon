The '''Dragon's Breath''' is a Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that is dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. When left clicking with the weapon, it fires a spread of 6 bullets, followed by a spread of 6 fast exploding Dragon's Breath rounds, both in even spreads. Right clicking shoots two rounds of three bullets and three Dragon's Breath rounds in a more random spread.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is a reference to real life [[wikipedia:Dragon's_breath_(ammunition)|"dragon's breath"]] incendiary ammunition.
