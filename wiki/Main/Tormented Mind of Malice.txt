The '''Tormented Mind of Malice''' is an item found within the [[Starter Bag]] in [[Expert Mode]] represented as a brain with four nails stuck in it, two of which are electrically charged. When used in [[Death Mode]], it can activate or disable [[Malice Mode]], yet again further increasing difficulty. Attempting to use it while not in Death Mode or during a boss fight will have no effect.

It could be crafted at an [[Altar]] and does not have any ingredients.
