'''The everquartz''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] sold by the [[Starborn Princess]]. When equipped, it grants 50 defense, reduces incoming contact damage by 25%, nullifies all [[defense damage]], provides bonuses to defense and life regeneration while the player is standing still, and adds a Piezoelectricity meter. The Piezoelectricity meter will gradually charge over time, taking 30 seconds to fill up. When the meter is full, it can be activated, which will cause the player to heal for 500 HP over two seconds and gain a massive boost to their damage reduction.
