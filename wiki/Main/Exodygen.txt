'''Exodygen''' (Japanese: 外酸素源 ''Gai sanso-gen''​) is a major Epilogue [[Superboss]] fought anywhere at any time. It is Meant after the [[Boss Rush]].

Once defeated, Exodygen will drop powerful endgame items and turn into the [[Sloome]] NPC.

== Spawn ==
Exodygen does not spawn on its own and will spawn at anytime if the player uses the [[Eschaton]].
When spawned, the screen will violently shake, and a bright flash will appear with Exodygen. Once a short time has passed, the fight will begin and the world will appear tinted, along with the player having infinite flight and lightning flashes in the background much like the ones you see during the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] fight.

== Behavior == 
=== Phase 1 (100%) ===
Exodygen teleports in mutliple directions  while a spread of enlarged Exoblade beams accelerate at a spread.
Exodygen will then repeatedly teleport left and right and dash at the player. a spread of Exolight will spawn at the middle of the player.
Exodygen then teleports above the player and fire multiple spreads of Exolight at random directions.

=== Phase 2 (75%) ===
2 instances of a cross symbol will appear on the player. the cross indicates the direction of where multiple turrets will start firing at the player depending on the cross shape. Exodygen will hover over the player during this.
Exodygen then spawns 3 beams first horizontal and the second vertical. Exodygen will dash through these lines while more burst of Exolights spread on each dash.
Exodygen will once again teleport above the player. It will then fire A slow moving Gauss nuke while firing multiple fast ones.

=== Phase 3 (50%) ===
Exodygen now Spawns Omega Sighter
Omega sighter must be killed in order to move on to the next phase and further damage Exodygen.

Omega Sighter teleports at the top while Exodygen teleports to the right. Omega sighter will then teleport at the bottom while Exodygen teleports to the left
Omega Sighter fires an enlarged Exoblade beam for both of these teleports.
Both Exodygen and omega sighter will dash in a cross position at the player 4 times. Each dash will have another spread of ExoLight.
Exodygen and Omega Sighter will then teleport above the player and fire Exolight bombs that spread into smaller lights.

=== Phase 4 (50% after Omega Sighter ===
Exodygen teleports far below the player and spawns a massive star. the star will fire a spread of Massive Exolights about 5 times in each direction and an additional 5 smaller Exolights at the player.
Exodygen then teleports above the player and Exoblade beams will start closing in on the player. Exodygen will stop and emit a Gauss nuke like blast that once again has another spread of Exolights. Exodygen will teleport to the bottom and repeat the same attack.
Exodygen as always once again spawns above the player. A bubble will surround both the player and Exodygen. If the player leaves the bubble, The player will receive Exo Annihilate until the player returns inside. Exodygen Rapidly fires a stream of Exolight at the player until the bubble fades.

=== Phase 5 (25%)===

Exodygen's phase transition will be slightly different. now shockwaving multiple times and a bright light will appear. Exodygen will then have a spinning aura in the center.

Exodygen will teleport above and  fire Exo slayer swords in the opposite direction of the player. Exodygen will spawn a beam diagonally and dash towards the player 4 times. While dashing, Exodygen leaves a trail of Exo slayer swords that start off slow while homing at the player and non accelerating Exolights. it will then stop homing and rapidly accelerate in a straight line
Exodygen will gain extra DR and teleport 4 times around the player, Each teleport spawns a Neutron star that has a random chance to go clockwise or counter clockwise and additional Exolight bullets.
Exodygen will teleport above the player once again. this time, Exodygen becomes invincible and an Singularity begins vacuuming nearby Exolights. The singularity begins to get brighter until a small beam will go clockwise or counter clockwise. The small beam will then turn massive and deadly while spinning for a period of time. Slow Exolights may appear in the middle that go to any direction. the beams will then die out and so as the singularity. Exodygen will now become vulnerable again.

== Notes ==  
*If Exodygen or a player dies when a Neutron Star isn't ready to attack, the Neutron Star will despawn.
*If the player is over 150 tiles away form Exodygen, it will teleport above the player.
*Absolute Exodygen is not meant to be fought legitimately and serves as an impossible boss easter egg.

== Tips ==  
*It is recommended to have a lot of buff potions and an offensive mobility build. [[Demonshade armor|Demonshade's]] Enraged Debuff can significantly increase your damage.
*Sloome Readds Yharim's Stimulates for 2 auric bars, which can improve the player's stats
*If playing Spy, it is recommended to use the [[Nanoblack Reaper]].
*The more you fight Exodygen, the more predictable its attack patterns become as its not 100% randomized.
*You can use the [[Exo Box]] for the Neutron Stars.
