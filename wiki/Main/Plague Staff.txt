The '''Plague Staff''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It shoots out 6-8 plague fangs, which are colored green and are trailed by pink light. The player can tighten the spread of the projectiles by aiming the cursor further away. All the fangs can pierce an enemy at the same time, and they can pierce through 5 enemies. On hit, they inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]].
