The '''Ultimus Cleaver''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from the [[Ravager]]. When swung, five homing gravity-affected sparks will fly out of the weapon, with each one piercing once and dealing one-tenth of the initial damage of the Cleaver. The five sparks are always thrown out at the same semi-equidistant angles, with the highest going straight upward and the lowest going straight forward, regardless of where the player is aiming. Each spark lasts for 1.5 seconds before dissipating. Additionally, landing a [[true melee]] hit will cause explosions to erupt on the target, dealing additional damage.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The explosion generated by sword hits deals exactly 100% of the weapon's base damage, can not critically strike, does not damage nearby targets, and cannot be modified by any means.
**This effect, along with its visuals, is identical to the [[Hellkite]].

== Trivia ==
*When it was initially released, and for a few versions thereafter, the weapon was not actually swung, and instead was held out as it was used, with sparks flying out only when the user was on the ground.
