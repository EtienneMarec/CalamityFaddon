The '''Baroclaw''' is a craftable Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[weapon]] and [[covering]] that is also dropped by [[Barocrab]]s and [[Barocraw]]s {{fantag|Deimos, the Starborn Princess}}. While equipped, defense is increased by 10. Pressing the {{key|Baroclaw}} key or manually using the item causes two large claws to appear near the player's cursor. The claws will then grab a nearby enemy and pull them inwards to where the claws were originally summoned, holding them in place. After five seconds, the claws despawn and the enemy is freed.

== Notes ==
*The Baroclaw is an exceptionally powerful covering as it can render certain bosses such as the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] and [[Desert Scourge]] powerless as the grip prevents them from charging.
*Enemies that can't be targeted by minions cannot be grabbed by the claws.
*Enemies that can teleport will still be able to briefly teleport out of the claws' grip, but will usually be dragged in again almost immediately after.
