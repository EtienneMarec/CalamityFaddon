The '''1.1.7.904''' update changed several crafting recipes which had previously used [[Stardust]] following the rework of the item. 

== Changes ==
*Changed the amount of [[Astral Ore]] needed to craft [[Astral Bar]]s from 12 to 8, and [[Stardust]] needed from 10 to 12.
*[[Devastation]] now uses 10 Astral Bars in its recipe instead of 10 Stardust.
*[[Shadecrystal Barrage]] now uses 6 [[Verstaltite Bar]]s in its recipe instead of 20 Stardust and 3 Verstaltite Bars.
*Changed Verstaltite Bar's recipe:
**No longer uses 4 Stardust, and now requires 16 [[Cryonic Ore]] instead of 12.
**Recipe now yields 4 bars instead of 5.
