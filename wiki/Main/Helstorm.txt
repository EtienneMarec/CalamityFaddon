The '''Helstorm''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]]. When used, it rapidly auto-fires two bullets at once, with a slightly random spread. The gun itself deals contact damage to enemies, an uncommon property for a [[ranged weapon]] that it shares with weapons such as [[Marnite Bayonet]] and [[Hellborn]]. Enemies that take contact damage from the gun will be inflicted with the [[On Fire!]] debuff.

== Notes ==

== Trivia ==
*Despite being called the "Helstorm", it does not share a connection with [[The Underworld]].
**This is likely because [[Scoria Ore]] used to spawn in the Underworld, before being moved to the [[Abyss]].
