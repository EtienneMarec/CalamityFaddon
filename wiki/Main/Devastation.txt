The '''Devastation''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that autoswings and is the upgrade of the [[Catastrophe Claymore]]. Upon being swung, arching beams are fired from the blade, and beams are fired from the sky. Beams fired from the sky will do half as much damage.
