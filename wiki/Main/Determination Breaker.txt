The '''Determination Breaker''' was a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]]. When equipped, it removed player [[immunity frames]] and any contact to [[Lava]] would instantly kill the player.

== Notes ==

== Trivia ==
*This item references the video game ''[[wikipedia:Undertale|Undertale]]''. Its name references "Determination", an important concept in ''Undertale''. Additionally, its effect is a reference to the fight with ''Sans'', where the player also takes damage from attacks every single frame. This is further referenced in the tooltip, where "The bad time" references Sans' iconic line, "you're gonna have a bad time."
