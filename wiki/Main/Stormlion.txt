The '''Stormlion''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Underground Desert]]. It walks towards the player relatively slowly in an attempt to do damage, and inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]] on contact.

It can drop a [[Stormlion Mandible]], which is necessary to craft the [[Desert Medallion]], as well as the [[Storm Surge]].

== Notes ==
*Despite having improved stats compared to [[Antlion Charger]]s, it moves noticeably slower, lacking the chargers' potentially devastating acceleration.

== Trivia ==
*In the mod's source files, the Stormlion is referred to as "Stormlion Charger", a reference to Antlion Chargers which they are based on.
