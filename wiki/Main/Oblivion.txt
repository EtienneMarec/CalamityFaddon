The '''Oblivion''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Yoyos|yoyo]] dropped by [[Calamitas]]. While in use, it fires brimstone lasers at nearby enemies which deal half of the yoyo's damage. Both the lasers and the yoyo inflict [[Brimstone Flames]].

== Notes ==
*Brimstone lasers appear near the yoyo instead of emitting from it directly. If the yoyo is pressed to a wall or is in a tight space, the lasers may disappear immediately and not be able to hit enemies.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon was previously known as "The Eye of Calamitas", and is still referred to as such in the mod's source code.
**Its name and sprite at the time were a reference to [[The Eye of Cthulhu]], another yoyo based on an eye-like boss.
