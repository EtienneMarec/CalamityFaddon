The '''Lion Heart''' is a Godseeker Mode [[true melee]] [[broadsword]] that is sold by the [[Cyborg]] NPC for {{pc|1}} {{gc|40}} after [[Polterghast]] has been defeated. When swung, an energy explosion is created on every enemy hit, dealing an additional 100% of the weapon's damage and inflicting the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]]. When used with the right-click, an energy shell is created around the player that halves all damage for 5 seconds while the weapon is held, with a 45-second cooldown after the shield runs out.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*The weapon is based off the [https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Lion_Heart_(weapon) Lion Heart], a "Gunblade" from ''[[wikipedia:Final Fantasy VIII|Final Fantasy VIII]]'' and ''[[wikipedia:Final Fantasy XIV|Final Fantasy XIV]]''.
**The right click effect is based off a skill Gunbreakers can use in Final Fantasy XIV, called Superbolide, which negates all damage taken for a short time at the cost of reducing the user's HP to 1.
**The energy explosions are based off the fact that Gunblades can release a large explosion on hit with a pull of its trigger.
