The '''Burning Sea''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that, when used, automatically fires fast brimstone fireballs which are affected by gravity and bounce off of tiles. The fireballs can bounce 3 times and pierce 2 enemies, and each last 5 seconds before disappearing. Upon contact with water, the fireballs transform into 3 or 4 small homing flares which shoot upward at random angles, dealing 1.35x the weapon's damage and dissipate upon impact with tiles or enemies.

== Notes ==
*The Burning Sea cannot shoot projectiles if the player is underwater.

== Tips ==
*Placing [[Bubble]] blocks with water inside them can be a good idea for arenas, providing more locations for the Burning Sea's fireballs to turn into homing flames.
*Alternatively, fighting in the [[Ocean]] biome could be helpful, as the fireballs will have a much higher chance of becoming homing flares.
