The '''Toxicant Twister''' is a Godseeker Mode [[boomerang]] that drops from tier 3 [[Trilobite]]s. It throws a fast boomerang that homes in after a short time, piercing all tiles and enemies. It only deals one-third damage before it begins homing.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Toxicant Twister causes the resulting boomerang to release dust blasts in their path.
