The '''Trilobite''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns during tiers 2 and 3 of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. They are a shelled creature, rolling itself while submerged and flops on the surface. When hit by a projectile, they release a spike that deals damage.

== Trivia ==
*Trilobites are based off real-world [[wikipedia:Trilobite|Trilobites]].
