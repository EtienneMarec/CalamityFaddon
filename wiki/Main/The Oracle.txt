'''The Oracle''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Yoyos|yoyo]]. It builds charge each time it strikes an enemy directly, creating a growing aura of red lightning which shocks nearby enemies and ignores [[immunity frames]]. Once The Oracle is sufficiently charged, it fires three orbs in a triangular pattern on every sixth direct hit. Each orb deals triple the mouse damage.

The Oracle’s red lightning aura shrinks and eventually vanishes when not striking any foes. Its size and damage increase with every successive hit landed by the mouse; the damage continues to rise even after the lightning aura stops visibly growing. The lightning aura has a base damage of 160 and is boosted by the user's melee stats. At maximum charge, the lightning aura deals 105 damage per frame.
