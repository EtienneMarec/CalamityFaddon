The '''Urchin Flail''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[flail]] that is purchased from the [[Sea King]] at any time. It throws out an urchin ball that will shoot an urchin spike into an enemy, inflicting the [[Venom]] [[debuff]]. Once the urchin ball hits a block or an [[enemy]], it will retract back to the player. It is not affected by gravity and cannot be swung around or be redeployed while the urchin has already been thrown out.
