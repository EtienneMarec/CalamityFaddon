'''Everclear''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Everclear''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and [[defense]], but boosts damage by 25%.

While the effects of the Everclear debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
