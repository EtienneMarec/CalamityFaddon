The '''Bandit''' is an [[NPC]] vendor that provides a plethora of [[weapon]]s and [[coverings]] that tailor to the [[Spy]] class.

The Bandit will move into an available NPC house if the following conditions have been met:
*There is an empty [[tgc:House|house]].
*[[Skeletron]] has been defeated.
*The player has at least {{pc|1}} in their inventory or personal storage items (such as the Piggy Bank). (This condition will not be required if the Bandit has moved in before on that world)

When threatened by [[enemies]], she will defend herself with the [[Cinquedea]].

== Refund ==
When the player selects the Bandit's "Refund" option after [[tgc:Modifiers#Reforging|reforging]] at the [[Goblin Tinkerer]] any number of times, the Bandit will return 1/5 of the money spent on those reforges. The Bandit can only return the money spent on reforges, and only in that world. The Bandit has three unique quotes when using this option.

'''When refunding:'''
*"Want in on a little secret? Since [Name of Goblin Tinkerer] always gets so much cash from you, I’ve been stealing some of it as we go. I need you to keep quiet about it, so here."
*"Hey, if government officials can get tax, why can’t I? The heck do you mean that these two things are nothing alike?"

'''If no refund is available:'''
*"Sorry, I got nothing. Perhaps you could reforge something and come back later..."

== Notes ==
*If the player leaves and rejoins the world, the Refund's reforge counter will reset and the player will not be able to collect any money from the Bandit.
*The Bandit does not give a discount to players with the [[Discount Card]] or similar items.

== Trivia ==
*The Bandit’s name ''Marisa'' is a reference to the character [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Marisa_Kirisame Marisa Kirisame] from the ''[[wikipedia:Touhou Project|Touhou Project]]'' series.
**Her quote "I’m just borrowing it until I die!" refers to Marisa's habit of stealing from youkai with the justification that her human lifespan is shorter than theirs.
**The Bandit's names ''Maribel'' and ''Hearn'' are references to the character [https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Maribel_Hearn Maribel Hearn] from the same series.
*The Bandit’s name ''Indiana'', and her quote regarding snakes is a reference to the character [[wikipedia:Indiana Jones (character)|Indiana Jones]] from the ''[[wikipedia:Indiana Jones|Indiana Jones]]'' franchise.
*The Bandit's name ''Rowan'' is related to developer Lord Metarex. It is a nod to his real name if he were a girl.
*The quote "Don’t tell [Name of Merchant], but I took some of his stuff and replaced it with Angel Statues." is a continuing reference to the well-known Angel Statue being absolutely useless.
**The Merchant selling Angel Statues while the Bandit is present is also a reference to this line.
*The quote "the eyrs, the-th-their-... ANGER!!!1!" is a reference to [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Polyphemalus Polyphemalus]' [[Music|theme]], [https://youtu.be/eBliz_IvFDo| the eyes are anger], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/@gummyleeches GummyLeeches.]
*The Bandit's name-specific quotes are references to members of the Calamity dev team and community.
*During a Party, the Bandit will wear no hat.
*In the mod's source files, the Bandit is referred to as 'THIEF'.
**This is a reference to the game [[wikipedia:The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]], in which the player's name is permanently changed to 'THIEF' if they ever steal from a shop. <!-- Confirmed reference by devs. -->
