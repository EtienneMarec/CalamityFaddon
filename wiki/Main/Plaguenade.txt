'''Plaguenades''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs]]. They are explosive projectiles that release 20 plague-infected bees upon explosion. Both the explosion and the bees inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] on contact. The bees spawned by the Plaguenade fly much faster than normal bees and are unaffected by liquids.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Plaguenades causes the resulting grenade to emit a larger explosion with 3.75x the explosion radius which releases 8 more bees.
