'''Tarragon Throwing Darts''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[daggers]]. They fire a long, spear-like projectile which pierces twice. They travel a significantly further distance than most other thrown items. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Tarragon Throwing Darts causes a circle of eight thorns to encircle enemies on the resulting hit. Each thorn deals 25% of the dart's damage.
