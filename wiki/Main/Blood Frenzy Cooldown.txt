'''Blood Frenzy Cooldown''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted to the player for 30 seconds after using the [[Blood Frenzy]] [[buff]] from the Melee variant of the [[Bloodflare armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]]. While the debuff is active, the usage of Blood Frenzy is disabled.
