'''Anechoic Plating''' is a pre-hardmode [[covering]] found in [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]]. It reduces Abyss creatures' ability to detect you, and also reduces the defense reduction from the Abyss to one third.

== Notes ==
*This item can also be obtained from the [[Abyssal Crate]] after either [[The Slime God]] or [[Wall of Flesh]] have been defeated.
*When used in tandem with the [[Fathom Swarmer Breastplate]], defense loss in the Abyss is reduced by 80%, rather than 73.6% that would naturally arise from equipping both.

== Trivia ==
*In real life, anechoic plating is used to make rooms that completely block out sound.
