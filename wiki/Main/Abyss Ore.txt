'''Abyss Ore''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ore]] which generates in the [[Abyss]] [[biome]]. It can be found on the underside of the floating [[Abyss Gravel]] islands, dropping harmful lava particles into the water beneath itself. These lava particles have a base damage of 25.

It requires at least a [[Picksaw]] to mine, and is used to craft [[Abyss Bar]]s at an [[Adamantite Forge]] or a [[Titanium Forge]]. It is also dropped by [[Chaotic Puffer]]s and [[Devil Fish]] after the [[Golem]] has been defeated.

== Notes ==
*Abyss Ore cannot be destroyed by explosives, even after Golem has been defeated.
*Unlike most other ores, this ore does not require a boss to be defeated in order to be mined aside from the Picksaw requirement, meaning that it can be mined even in a Pre-Hardmode world.
*The lava particles that this ore drops can provide natural light to the normally pitch-black Abyss.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Abyss|Abyss]] is a type of igneous rock with similar properties to basalt.
*Abyss Ore used to be known as '''Chaotic Ore''', and is still referred to as such in the mod's files.
*This ore used to generate in [[The Underworld]], but it was later relocated to the Abyss.
**This was to give further incentive to explore the Abyss, and also because the ore had issues with generating into the Underworld's [[tgc:Ruined House|ruined houses]].
