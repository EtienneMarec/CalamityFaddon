The '''1.2.3.003''' update made small adjustments to [[Supreme Calamitas]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]], along with nerfing [[Hell Burst]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Enemies]] ===
*The Devourer of Gods' head now instantly kills the player in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Lowered the rate at which Supreme Calamitas switches between her attacks in Death Mode.

=== Items ===
*Nerfed Hell Burst's damage from 43 to 34.
