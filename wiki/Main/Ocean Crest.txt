The '''Ocean Crest''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. While equipped, it grants underwater breathing and prevents [[Crab]]s, [[Jellyfish|Pink Jellyfish]], [[Sea Snail]]s, [[Shark]]s and [[Squid]]s from doing damage and targeting the player.

== Notes ==
*This item will not stack with the [[Aquatic Emblem]] or [[Gills]] [[buff]].
*In the [[Abyss]], equipping this item will only slow the speed at which breath is lost, rather than stop it from draining entirely.
