The '''Zerg Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. It grants the '''Zerg''' [[buff]], which boosts all spawn rates and maximum spawn count by 400%. It is also used in the crafting of the [[Chaos Candle]].

== Notes ==
*This potion is the opposite of the [[Zen Potion]], which drastically ''decreases'' spawn rates.

== Tips ==
*Having the [[Bloodflare armor]] equipped alongside this buff during a Godseeker Mode [[Blood Moon]] is a great way to get [[Blood Orb]]s fast.
*Make sure you use this in a place where you know you can survive as enemies can quickly overwhelm the player.

== Trivia ==
*This potion was named after the "Zerg", a race from the ''[[wikipedia:StarCraft|StarCraft]]'' series, which are known for overwhelming the enemy with large numbers.
