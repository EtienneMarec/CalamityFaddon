The '''Waraxe''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[axe]] that drops from [[Skeleton]]s, [[Hoplite]]s, [[Armored Skeleton]]s and [[Skeleton Archer]]s. Critical strikes inflict the [[War Cleave]] [[debuff]], which reduces the hit enemy's defense by 15 and damage reduction by 25%, for 15 seconds.

{{modifier|harvest}}

== Tips ==
*Although it has axe power, it is excellent to acquire early on to use as a [[weapon]], due to its high base damage and powerful debuff.
*Since the Waraxe is technically a tool and not a weapon, it will not trigger the summoner nerf and is thus an excellent early game weapon for summoners.
