The '''Assortegelatin''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. While worn, an invincible slime is summoned based on the player's current biome which will rapidly jump at enemies and latch onto them, dealing a base of 400 damage boosted by the player's summoner damage. If the player gets near a wild non-boss slime, the slime will have its contact damage boosted by 600% and its health by 1200%, then start fighting for the player. Boss slimes such as [[King Slime]], [[Queen Slime]], [[The Slime God]], [[Astrum Aureus]], and the [[Cragmaw Mire]] stop attacking the player, but won't fight for them.
