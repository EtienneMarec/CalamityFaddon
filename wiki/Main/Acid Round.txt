'''Acid Rounds''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bullets]] made from [[Plague Cell Canister]]s and [[Musket Ball]]s. Upon contact with an enemy, it explodes into acid, which inflicts the [[Plague]] [[debuff]] on contact.

Additional damage is added to bullets when hitting a target based on target's defense, the damage of the gun & bullet, and the gun's use time. The final damage formula is as follows: <code>Damage = Damage + (EnemyDefense * 0.05 * (Damage / 50) * (WeaponUsetime / 20))</code>
