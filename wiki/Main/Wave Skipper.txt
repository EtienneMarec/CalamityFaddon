The '''Wave Skipper''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[javelin]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Scourge of the Desert]]. When used it will throw a Wave Skipper that will bounce five times before disappearing.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Wave Skipper will cause three javelins to be thrown instead of one. The three javelins have slightly higher velocity.
