The '''Wyvern's Call''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Sky Glaze]]. It automatically fires out a volley of 3 projectiles from the sky, which can be the same type or different. None of the projectiles pierce through enemies or tiles.

{{Modifier|magic}}
