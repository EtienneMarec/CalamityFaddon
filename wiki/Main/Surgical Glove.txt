The '''Surgical Glove''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[coverings|covering]] that drops from the [[The Plaguebringer Goliath|Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It provides a 12% increase to melee speed and damage and a 3% increase to melee critical strike chance, an 8% increase to [[true melee]] damage,  and gives the player immunity to the [[Plague]]. Any enemy struck by a melee attack are inflicted with the [[Plague]] [[Debuffs|debuff]].
