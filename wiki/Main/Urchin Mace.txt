The '''Urchin Mace''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[flail]]. When used, the player starts spinning the mace around, creating a whirlpool around themselves. If the player stops using the weapon after 1.83 or more seconds of spinning, they launch the whirlpool towards their mouse cursor, which pierces infinitely and travels a short distance before popping, emitting glowing water dust in a large radius. The projectile can also travel through walls.
