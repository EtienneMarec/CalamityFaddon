The '''Galactus Blade''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that auto-swings. It rains down Galactica Comets above the cursor on swing. It inflicts the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]] on hit. The projectile will not pierce, but it will ignore tiles and explode on impact.
