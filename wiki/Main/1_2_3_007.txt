The '''1.2.3.007''' update reworked [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s [[Profaned Guardians]] (Now named A Dunkin' Donut), made changes to [[Plantera]], and added a few items.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added a second variant of the [[Aquatic Aberration]] which spawns naturally.
*Added [[Ancient Ice Chunk]], [[Heavenly Gale]].
*Added [[Supreme Bait Tackle Box Fishing Station]].
*Added [[Ice Clasper (buff)]].

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===

==== [[Calamitas]] ====
*Nerfed [[Cataclysm]]'s health from {{dv|4500|7200|10,400}} to {{dv|3500|5600|8800}}.
*Nerfed [[Catastrophe]]'s health from {{dv|4000|6400|9280}} to {{dv|3000|4800|7680}}.
*Cataclysm and Catastrophe no longer classify as bosses, and thus no longer drop [[Greater Healing Potion]]s or have death messages.

==== [[The Destroyer]] ====
*Increased speed as its health lowers.
*Buffed [[Probe]]'s health in [[Death Mode]] from {{death|375}} to {{death|450}}, and their damage from {{death|100}} to {{death|120}}.

==== [[Plantera]] ====
*Nerfed [[Plantera]]'s speed when she is enraged to prevent her from despawning.
*Tentacles now fire Spores in Revengeance and Death Mode.
*Now fires thorn balls occasionally in phase 2 in Death Mode.
*Now has a cap of 25 tentacles at a time, and 20 Spores at a time.

==== [[Providence, the Profaned God]] ====
*Renamed each of [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s guardians from "A Profaned Guardian" to "A Dunkin' Donut".
*Now summons all 3 of her guardians at once for every 30% of her health lost.
*Providence now stops attacking as much when her guardians are alive.
*Now switches between phases twice as often in Death Mode.
*Now becomes invincible if any guardian is alive.
*Nerfed defensive variant of A Dunkin' Donut's health from {{dv|30,000|42,000}} to {{dv|20,000|28,000}}.
*Buffed offensive variant of A Dunkin' Donut's health from {{dv|40,000|56,000}} to {{dv|50,000|70,000}}.
*The defensive variant of A Dunkin' Donut can now fire a barrage of spears going in multiple directions.
*The healer variant of A Dunkin' Donut can now fire a cluster of homing orbs which negatively heal the player upon contact.

==== [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] ====
*Phase 3's charge is now more ruthless.
*Nerfed [[Cosmic Lantern]]'s health from {{dv|6000|8400}} to {{dv|4000|5600}}.

==== [[Siren and Leviathan]] ====
*The Siren's [[Ice Shield]] now inflicts [[Frostburn]] for 3.5 seconds instead of [[Frozen]] for 1 second.
*Buffed [[Aquatic Aberration]]'s health from {{dv|900|1260}} to {{dv|1500|2100|0|4200}}.
*[[Aquatic Aberration]]s are now completely immune to knockback. 
*[[Siren Clone]]s can now only fire water spears instead of all of the Siren's projectiles.
*Buffed Siren Clone's water spear damage from {{dv|46|80|100|120}} to {{dv|68|112|140|168}}.
*Siren Clones are now more aggressive.

==== [[Wall of Flesh]] ====
*[[The Hungry II]]'s can now pass through tiles in Death Mode.
*Buffed The Hungry II's health in Death Mode to {{death|392}}, and their damage to {{death|111}}.
*The Hungry IIs now gain a 2.5x health modifier and a 1.35x damage modifier in all other modes.
*Buffed [[Leech]]'s health in Death Mode to {{death|240}}, and it gains a {{death|1.5x}} damage modifier.
*Leeches now gain a 2.1x health modifier and a 1.25x damage modifier in all other modes.

==== Other ====

*[[Stress]] is now increased by several other Calamity bosses.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] phase 2's body segments now only resist 50% of damage from infinitely piercing projectiles instead of 60%.
*[[Ice Clasper]]s now only have a chance to inflict [[Glacial State]] for 0.5 seconds instead of always inflicting it for 0.83 seconds.
*[[Ice Queen]]s now drop 20-41 [[Endothermic Energy]] instead of 10-21.
*Buffed [[Parasea]]'s health from {{dv|300|420}} to {{dv|500|700}}.
*Paraseas can now spawn naturally in the [[Ocean]] after [[Plantera]] has been defeated.
*Nerfed [[Profaned Energy]]'s health from {{dv|28,000|56,000}} to {{dv|10,000|20,000}}.
*Only one Profaned Energy can exist at a time now.
*Nerfed [[Scorn Eater]]'s health from {{dv|23,000|46,000}} to {{dv|12,000|24,000}}.

=== Items ===
*[[Angel Treads]] and [[Seraph Tracers]] now have equipped boot sprites.
*Nerfed [[Animosity]]'s damage from 28 to 25.
*Nerfed [[Cosmilamp]]'s damage from 230 to 150, made the Cosmilamp minions deal damage 4 times less often, and made each one take 2 minion slots.
*Lowered [[Exoblade]]'s use time from 16 to 14.
*Buffed [[Flare Bolt]]'s damage from 23 to 25.
*[[Magnetic Meltdown]]'s projectiles now last shorter.
*[[Reaver Shark]] now has a tooltip specifying it can't be swung until [[Eater of Worlds]] or [[Brain of Cthulhu]] has been defeated.
*[[Siren's Heart]]s now grant extra life regeneration and defense in Hardmode and after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
*Made the notes from [[Siren's Song]] smaller.
*[[Yharon's Kindle Staff]]'s Sons of Yharon now move twice as fast, but spawn flares 3 times less often.

=== Other ===
*Reduced dust on certain projectiles.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Devourer of Gods Mask]].
*Resprited [[Victory Shard]].
*Resprited [[Exoblade]], [[Nebulash]], [[Vivid Clarity]].
*Resprited [[The Devourer of Gods]] phase 2's head, [[Plaguebringer]].
*Reverted [[Earth]]'s sprite.

=== Fixes ===
*Fixed a bug caused when using [[Atlantis]].
*Removed some gray pixels on [[Daedalus Hood]]'s item sprite.
*Fixed a bug which caused [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] to become near invincible in multiplayer.
