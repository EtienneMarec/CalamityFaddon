The '''Depressed Cephalopod''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns during the [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. It attacks by firing a spread of bullets at the player. When defeated, the enemy will play a short animation of it firing its own gun at its self, which will cause it to explode into bouncing gore projectiles.

== Trivia ==
*The Depressed Cephalopod is a direct reference to the creepypasta known as Squidward Suicide.
