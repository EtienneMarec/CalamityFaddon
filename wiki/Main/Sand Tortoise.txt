The '''Sand Tortoise''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] found in the [[Underground Desert]]. It follows the attack pattern of a regular [[Giant Tortoise]], walking a short distance before jumping up and spinning, heading towards the player. If it enters water, it will immediately jump and spin.

== Notes ==
*Sand Tortoises are twice the size of other tortoise enemies.
*Damaging the Sand Tortoise causes it to stop its attack animation.
**This does not occur in [[Revengeance Mode]] if the Sand Tortoise is under 25% health.
