The '''Leeching Dagger''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[shortsword]] that is also dropped by [[The Hive Mind]]. Upon hitting an enemy with it, it will release leech orbs that home in on other enemies, and upon impact, [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 2 life. It functions identically to the [[Bloody Rupture]].

Like every [[shortsword]], it has an incredibly short range, but unlike others, it swings automatically.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, the player is healed 5 health per each attack.
