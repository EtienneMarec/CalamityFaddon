The '''Art Attack''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]]. Upon use, it fires a star with a rainbow trail that follows the mouse cursor. It initially deals no damage until a full shape with the trail is completed. All enemies within the completed shape emit rainbow dust and get damaged for 1x initially, and up to 2.3x after 4.5 seconds.

== Trivia ==
*The Art Attack is a reference to a [https://www.mariowiki.com/Art_Attack special move of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', which lets the player draw circles around enemies to harm them. Its appearance is based on the Ruby Star, which is the Crystal Star required to perform the move.
