The '''Crystyl Crusher''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[pickaxe]]. When used, it fires a beam capable of destroying any block in a radius near the end of its tip that moves along with the mouse cursor. The beam takes about 1.67 seconds to charge up, and has a reach of 15 blocks. The color of the beam alternates between magenta and blue. Right-clicking with the Crystyl Crusher will cause it to function as a normal pickaxe without a swing animation.

Speed modifiers have no effect on this pickaxe, so its best [[modifier]] is '''Massive''' for harvesting purposes, and '''Godly''' for combat purposes.

== Notes ==
*The Crystyl Crusher is the only pickaxe in the mod capable of mining the [[Arena Block]]s spawned by Supreme Calamitas.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity appears as a dark purple ({{color|#811D95 ●|#811D95}}) color.
*This weapon is Graydee's developer item, serving as a reference to their mod, [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/crystilium-mod.44558/ Crystilium] (including the misspelled "crystal" in the name).
*If the mod of redemption is on, this pickaxe is able to mine corium, meaning you can craft the unobtainable weapons that require corium.
