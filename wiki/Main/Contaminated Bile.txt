The '''Contaminated Bile''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]]. It fires small toxic flasks that explode on contact with tiles or enemies, dealing 75% of the weapon’s damage and lingering for a short time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Contaminated Bile will cause the resulting flask to form a larger and longer-lasting explosion.
