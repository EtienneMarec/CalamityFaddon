
<big> ''"The night of horrors crawls with even more frights!"'' </big>

Calamity adds many new foes to the '''Solar Eclipse''' event, which can be encountered after defeating [[The Devourer of Gods]] (except for Green Demon, which is instead encountered after [[Leviathan and Anahita]] are defeated). These new enemies drop [[Darksun Fragment|Darksun Fragments]], which are required to craft a variety of late game items and equipment.

== Notes ==
*The event lasts from the beginning of the day to the very end.
*Pre Devourer of Gods solar eclipse enemies will spawn much less frequently during the event.

== Aftermath ==
*The [[Starborn Princess]] will begin selling '''Solar Fragments'''.

== Tips ==
*Hiding inside a structure is not a recommended strategy, as many of the events enemies can attack the player through walls.
*Tall Men should be prioritized above all other enemies, as their attack will instantly kill the player regardless of their health.

== Trivia ==
*The upgraded Solar Eclipse's [[Music|theme]] is '''[[Music|Spaghetti Carnival]]''', which was composed by composer '''[https://www.youtube.com/@Jteoh Jteoh]'''.
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, the [[Solar Eclipse]]'s theme will play instead.
*In keeping with the Solar Eclipses theme of horror, all enemies featured in the upgraded Solar Eclipse are direct references to popular internet scary stories.
**Evil Animatronic is a reference to Freddy Fazbear from '''Five Nights at Freddy's'''.
**Rodent.tmod is a reference to Sonic.exe from the creepypasta of the same name.
**Glitch is a reference to the MISSINGNO glitch from Pokemon Red and Blue.
**Tall Man is a reference to the famous Slender Man image.
**Depressed Cephalopod is a reference to the creepypasta Squidward Suicide.
**Cuboid Curse is a reference to the Minecraft urban legend known as Herobrine.
**Crimson Kaiju is a reference to Red from the creepypasta NES Godzilla.
**Green Demon is a reference to Jumbo Josh from '''Garten of Banban'''.
