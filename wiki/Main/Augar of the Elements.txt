The '''Augar of the Elements''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Eldritch Tome]], the [[Tome of Fates]] and the [[Shadowflame Hex Doll]]. It continuously shoots out multiple rainbow tentacles, which is a trait it shares with its ingredients. The tentacles can pass through tiles, but do not pierce enemies. It is later used in the crafting of the [[Subsuming Vortex]].
