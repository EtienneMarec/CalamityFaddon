'''Elysian Arrows''' are Godseeker Mode [[arrows]] that are crafted with 150 [[Holy Arrow]]s and an [[Unholy Essence]]. Upon hitting an enemy or a tile, Elysian meteors swarm the area of impact, dealing 50% of the used weapon's damage to any enemy they hit. All damage is halved before being applied to the target.
