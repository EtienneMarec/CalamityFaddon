The '''Fungicide''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Guns|gun]] that has a chance of dropping from [[Crabulon]]. When [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], it fires a fungus bullet that, upon impact with an [[enemy]] or tile, splits into smaller spores which are affected by gravity.
