The '''Squirrel King Staff''' is a craftable Epilogue [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and attack enemies by summoning beams of holy light above them. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
