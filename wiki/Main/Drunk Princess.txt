== Notes ==
*The amount of damage dealt by Drunk Princess's slap was at least 20.
*The Drunk Princess cannot legally have her home set within 500 blocks of the Angler or the Princess.
*The [[Discount Card]] and other items that granted a similar effect would not reduce the prices of any items sold by the Drunk Princess.
*The price of all items sold by The Drunk Princess were increased.
**The cost of all drinks were increased by 50%
**The cost of all candles were increased by 300%
**The cost of odd mushroom was increased by 200%
*The displayed sell value of all items sold by the Drunk Princess were false due to her increased sell prices.
**The sell price of all drinks were decreased from 20% of the buy price to 13.34%
**The sell price of all candles were decreased from 20% of the buy price to 5%
**The sell price of odd mushroom were decreased from 20% of the buy price to 6.67%
*Using the [[Princess Spirit in a Bottle]] while she was present will cause her to despawn, and she would respawn on the last player to dismount the mount.

== Trivia ==
*The Drunk Princess was a self-insertion of the former owner of the mod, also known as Cirrus, Fabsol or MountainDrew, who has been known to get drunk a lot on several occasions.
**The Drunk Princess was removed following a controversy over the inclusions of unfitting content within the mod.
**A functionally identical Town NPC is planned for the future.
*The Drunk Princess had only one possible name, sharing this trait with [[Santa Claus]], the [[Sea King]], the [[Brimstone Witch]], and the [[Archmage]].
*The quote about reprogramming the whole game while drunk was a fourth-wall-breaking quote, implying the creator of the mod getting drunk and sending a broken mod to the beta testers of the mod.
*The quote about the buggy mechworm was a reference to the bugs caused by [[Staff of the Mechworm]] being frequently reported by the community.
*Dying from the no moral compass quote would result in the death message being "<PlayerName> was slapped too hard".
*The "deals" quote was a reference to [https://deltarune.fandom.com/wiki/Spamton Spamton G. Spamton] from the second chapter of [[wikipedia:Deltarune|Deltarune]].
*The "blood is fuel" quote was a reference to the game, ''[[wikipedia:Ultrakill|Ultrakill]]''.
*The quote about the Hara-kiri rock and scissors as well as space invaders referenced to the lines spoken by the ''Colonel'' in ''[[wikipedia:Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty|Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty]]''.
*The Drunk Princess was most likely a lesbian, as evidenced by her attraction to the Stylist, the player if wearing an Aquatic Heart, and her post-Leviathan quote; or bisexual, considering her implied sexual encounter with the Archmage.
*After Fabsol stepped down and left the Calamity Discord, the image on the right was shared by one of the admins.
**[[File:FabsolDead.png|thumb]]
*The "Talking Worms" quote was a reference to [[The Devourer of Gods]], as during the boss fight, it repeatedly talks to the player.
*In the mod's source files, the Drunk Princess was referred to as 'FAP'.
*The Drunk Princess despawning when mounting the Alicorn was in reference to how both are canonically the same entity.
