La '''Baguette''' est [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Food_and_Drink_Potions un aliment]. Lorsqu'elle est consommée, elle confère le [[Buffs|buff]] Baguette pendant 5 minutes. Le buff Baguette augmente les effets du [[Red Wine|Vin Rouge]], augmentant ses soins de 200 à 250, mais renforçant également la réduction de la régénération de vie de 1 à 4.

== Trivia ==
*The tooltip text "je suis Monte" is a reference to a generalized term used to refer to the French player base of ''[[wikipedia:Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate|Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate]]''. The game lacked an auto-translate tool, so French text remained written in French. French players in-game were well known for using a weapon called the [https://monsterhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_Glaive Insect Glaive], which helped players mount monsters, with the French default callout for a mount being "je suis Monte". These all combined to result in the in-game chat being frequently filled with "je suis Monte".
**"je suis Monte"  translates to "I've mounted", or in the sense of that game, "I've mounted the monster".
