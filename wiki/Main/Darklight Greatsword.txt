The '''Darklight Greatsword''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. When swung, it shoots darklight blades that inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]. The darklight blades explodes after hitting an enemy or a block into 3-4 smaller orbs. It is capable of autoswing.
