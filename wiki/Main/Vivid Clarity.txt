The '''Vivid Clarity''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] and the final upgrade of the [[Night's Ray]] or [[Carnage Ray]]. It fires a set of 5 beams towards the player's cursor that breaks upon contact with an enemy or tile. Once it breaks, it does one of the following:
*Summons a beam from the sky that rebound off of walls and pierces [[enemies]] up to 9 times. The beam deals 70% of the base damage, increasing by 20% when bouncing off a tile and by 10% when piercing an enemy. Enemies that are within nearby range of the beam will be struck with additional smaller beams with less lifespan.
*Forms a massive short-lasting explosion that deals double of the weapon's base damage and ignores [[immunity frames]].
*Bursts into eight homing bolts.

When Vivid Clarity hits an enemy, it will inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
