The '''Acidsighter''' (Japanese: アシッドサイター ''Ashiddosaitā'') is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[boss]]. It has the appearance of a giant Demon Eye that has been poisoned by sulphuric waters.

Defeating it will unlock the [[Acid Rain]] [[Events|event]].

==Spawn==
The Acidsighter does not spawn on its own, and requires the player to summon it with the [[Poisoned Sclera]]. It must be summoned in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] during the [[Night]].

==Behavior==
The Acidsighter has three phases, each of which has its own attack pattern and AI. It will progress through these phases as it loses health.

It becomes enraged if the player leaves the Sulphurous Sea or [[Ocean]], or if it becomes daytime, increasing its movement speed and attack rate. The effects will stack if the boss is both outside of the Sulphurous Sea or Ocean and it is daytime.

===Attacks===

===Phase 1===
*Hovers above the player while spawning Mutated Eyes.
*Charges at the player three times.

*Fires Acid Bombs that split into six Acid Shots.
*When at {{Dv|65%|70%|75%}} health or lower, every 2-3 attacks, spins around the player before charging at them.
*Fires Teeth Bombs that split into three homing teeth.

===Phase 2===
Upon reaching {{Dv|50%|55%|60%|65%}} health, the boss will roar, gaining a bright, green glow, and spawning 4-5 Mutated Eyes. 

*Hovers above the player while firing Acid Bombs.
*Charges at the player three times. Explodes into eight Acid Shots when dashing.
*Hovers below the player while firing Teeth Bombs.
*Spins around the player before charging at them. Leaves a trail of Acid Bombs when charging. 
**Acid bombs spawned from this attack explode into two Acid Shots.
*Chases the player while spawning Mutated Eyes.

===Phase 3===
Upon reaching {{Dv|30%|40%|45%|50%}} health, the boss will roar again, the green glow is stronger, and it deals 25% more damage.

*Spins around the player while firing Acid Bombs.
*Charges at the player three times. Explodes into 12 Acid Shots when dashing.
*Performs a rapid chain dash attack that leaves a trail of Acid Bombs.
**Acid bombs spawned from this attack explode into four Acid Shots.
*Chases the player at a rapid pace while firing Teeth Bombs.
*Fires Mutated Clusters that slow down and explode into 2-3 Mutated Eyes.

===In [[Expert Mode]]===

*Gains a new attack.
**Charges at the player while breathing Acid Venom.
*Mutated Eyes are faster.

===In [[Revengeance Mode]]===

*Increased attack rate.
*Mutated Eyes will fire Acid Shots if the Acidsighter is below 55% health.

===In [[Death Mode]]===

*Increased acceleration.
*Mutated Eyes explode into a lingering Acid Cloud that rains Acid Shots when hitting a solid tile.

===On the For The Worthy seed===

*Is 50% smaller.
*Increased velocity.
*Leaves Acid Bombs when charging regardless of its phase.
*Mutated Eyes fire Acid Bombs that deal 50% damage.

==Tips==
[[Swiftness Potion|Swiftness Potions]], [[Sunflower|Sunflowers]], [[Hermes Boots]] and their upgrades, and a [[Cloud in a Bottle]] and its upgrades or a [[Hooks|Hook]] are all heavily recommended, as it will be near impossible to outmaneuver the Acidsighter's powerful dashes with just the player's base movement, especially on higher difficulties.
