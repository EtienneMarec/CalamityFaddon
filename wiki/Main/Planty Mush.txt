'''Planty Mush''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[block]] that can be found naturally generated inside the [[Abyss]]. [[Viper Vines]] automatically grow from underneath it. It can be mined with any [[pickaxe]]. It is used as a crafting material for [[Life Fruit]] and [[Fathom Swarmer armor]].

== Notes ==
*Planty Mush can also be obtained from [[Abyssal Crate]]s, as well as by fishing in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] or the Abyss with a [[fishing power]] between 0 and 80.
**Planty Mush's coded requirement is actually 40 to 80. However, as this item replaces [[Junk]] catches in the Sulphurous Sea and Abyss, it is functionally 0 to 80.
*It can be broken and collected using destructive [[Explosives (class)|explosives]], such as [[Bomb]]s or [[Dynamite]].
