The '''Duel Hit''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[whip]] that, upon striking an enemy, creates temporary snowflakes that chases nearby enemies, dealing 20 damage and inflicting debuffs to them on contact. The summoned snowflakes ignores 30 enemy [[defense]]. If a snowflake is already summoned, attacking with the whip will reset its timer instead of creating a new one.

== Notes ==
* Tagging an enemy with a different whip will ''not'' cause the snowflakes to disappear. This allows it to be used as a support weapon for crowd control. 
** Although the snowflakes does not disappear, they will remain stationary at the position of the last killed enemy. They will not move until another enemy gets into its range.
