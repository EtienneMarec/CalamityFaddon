The '''Cryon''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Snow biome]]. It jumps and charges towards the player in an attempt to deal damage. It will always inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] onto the player if they are hit and has a 33% chance to inflict [[Glacial State]]. It has a 50% chance to drop an [[Essence of Eleum]].
