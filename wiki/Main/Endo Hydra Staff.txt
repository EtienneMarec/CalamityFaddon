The '''Endo Hydra Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Staff of the Frost Hydra]]. It summons a single-headed Endo Hydra at the back of the player, with each resummon consuming one additional minion slot for an extra head. The Endo Hydra itself stays at the back of the player, while the heads fire beams at nearby enemies.
