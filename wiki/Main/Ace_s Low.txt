The '''Ace's Low''' is a craftable Pre-[[Hardmode]] [[gun]]. It automatically fires a line of 3 spade cards with a mild delay between each set which pierce through 3 enemies before dissipating on the fourth. Cards inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] upon contact with enemies.
