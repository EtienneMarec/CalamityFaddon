}}The '''Crackshot Colt''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[gun]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), it accurately fires a high velocity gold blast which cannot pierce tiles nor enemies. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), a [[Copper Coin]] is consumed and fired with an upwards curve. Coins are gravity-affected, move slowly, and cannot damage enemies. When the blast collides with a coin, it is able to ricochet to a nearby enemy within a 56.25 tile radius, and blast damage is multiplied by 2. Coins can be recoverable at a 100% chance if shot by the blast, and 25% if not.

==Notes==
*The gun noticeably recoils while used with either fire. However, this does not affect the player's velocity in any way.
*The secondary fire is 1.3 times as fast as the primary fire but does not auto-use.
*If no Copper Coin is available, higher valued coins are automatically consumed by breaking down into smaller valued coins on use.
*Coins are usable from the player's Piggy Bank, Safe, or Defender's Forge but not from the Void Vault.
