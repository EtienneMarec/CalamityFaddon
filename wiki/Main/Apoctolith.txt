The '''Apoctolith''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] that throws a gravity-affected abyssal brick that inflicts the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]]. Whenever the brick critically hits an enemy, the struck target loses 15 [[defense]], which cannot stack with itself.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Apoctolith will cause the next brick to stun enemies with the [[Eutrophication]] debuff. The brick will also deal 125% damage and shatter on impact, creating 5 shards that deal 62.5% damage each.
