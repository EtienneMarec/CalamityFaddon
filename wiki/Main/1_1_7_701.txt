The '''1.1.7.701''' update added a new set of weapons known as [[Legendary weapons]], along with a new boss known as [[Crabulon]]. The update also added several new custom [[rarity]] colors used by most Godseeker Mode items, and added several new [[weapons]] and [[coverings]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Cnidrion]], [[Crab Shroom]], and [[Crabulon]].
*Added [[Aero Stone]], [[Bloom Stone]], [[Chaos Stone]], [[Cryo Stone]], [[Fungal Clump]], [[Harpy Ring]], [[Heart of the Elements]], and [[Rose Stone]].
*Added [[Aegis Blade]], [[Blossom Flux]], [[Briny Baron]], [[Comet Quasher]], [[Cosmilamp]], [[The Dance of Light]], [[Fungicide]], [[Gleaming Magnolia]], [[Hyphae Rod]], [[Mycelial Claws]], [[Mycoroot]], [[Neptune's Bounty]], [[SHPC]], [[Stellar Striker]], [[Storm Dragoon]], [[Vesuvius]], and [[The Wand]].
*Added [[Treasure Bag (Brimstone Elemental)]] and [[Treasure Bag (Crabulon)]].
*Added [[Brimstone Waifu (buff)]], [[Fungal Clump (buff)]], [[Mushy]], and [[Siren Lure (buff)]].
*Added the {{rare|12}}, {{rare|13}}, {{rare|14}}, {{rare|15}}, and {{rare|16}} [[rarity]] colors.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*[[Brimstone Elemental]] no longer despawns if the player leaves [[The Underworld]], and her Brimstone Barrages no longer produce dust.
*[[Bumblebirb]] no longer despawns if the player leaves the [[Jungle]].
*[[Cryogen]] now becomes invincible instead of despawning if the player leaves the [[Snow biome]].
*[[Desert Scourge]] now becomes invincible instead of despawning if the player leaves the [[Desert]].
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' Beam Portals now last 5 seconds instead of 10, have had their speed decreased, and produce less dust.
*[[The Hive Mind]] phase 1 no longer despawns if the player leaves [[The Corruption]], and phase 2 now becomes invincible instead of despawning.
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] no longer despawns shortly if the player leaves the Jungle.
*[[The Perforator Hive]] now becomes invincible instead of despawning if the player leaves [[The Crimson]].
*[[Profaned Guardians]] changes:
**Nerfed main guardian's spear damage from {{dv|200|220}} to {{dv|140|180}}, and Giga Flare damage from {{dv|160|180}} to {{dv|110|140}}.
**Nerfed defense guardian's spear damage from {{dv|160|180}} to {{dv|120|140}}.
**The defense and healer guardians now take up 3 NPC slots instead of 10.
*[[Providence, the Profaned God]] changes:
**Buffed defense from 100 to 150.
**Nerfed her Holy Blast damage from {{dv|0|320}} to {{dv|0|260}}, her Holy Fire damage from {{dv|0|260}} to {{dv|0|200}}, her Molten Blast's damage from {{dv|0|320}} to {{dv|0|260}}, and her Holy Bomb damage from {{dv|0|280}} to {{dv|0|220}}.
**Nerfed the offense and defense guardians' spear damage from {{dv|160|260}} to {{dv|100|140}}.
**Nerfed the offensive guardian's Flare Dust damage from {{dv|160|240}} to {{dv|100|160}}.
**All three guardians now take up 1 NPC slot each instead of 10.
**No longer despawns if the player leaves [[The Hallow]] or the Underworld.
*[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] now becomes invincible instead of despawning if the player leaves the Underworld.
*[[Siren Lure and Leviathan]] changes:
**Siren Lure changes:
***Buffed [[Detonating Bubble]] damage from {{dv|40|80}} to {{dv|60|120}}.
***Decreased coin drop count from {{gc|30}} to {{gc|18}}.
***Decreased speed.
***Now becomes invincible if the player leaves the [[Ocean]].
***Projectile damage value changes while over 75% health:
****Nerfed Music Note damage from {{dv|100|120}} to {{dv|80|100}} while dry, and {{dv|80|100}} to {{dv|60|88}} while wet.
****Nerfed Frost Mist damage from {{dv|0|104}} to {{dv|0|92}} while dry, and {{dv|0|84}} to {{dv|0|80}} while wet.
****Changed Water Spear damage from {{dv|70|96}} to {{dv|72|84}} while dry, and changed damage from {{dv|50|76}} to {{dv|52|72}} while wet.
***Projectile damage value changes while under 75% health, but above 50%:
****Nerfed Music Note damage from {{dv|104|136}} to {{dv|84|108}} while dry, and {{dv|84|116}} to {{dv|64|96}} while wet.
****Nerfed Frost Mist damage from {{dv|0|112}} to {{dv|0|100}} while dry, and {{dv|0|92}} to {{dv|88}} while wet.
****Changed Water Spear damage from {{dv|74|104}} to {{dv|76|92}} while dry, and {{dv|54|84}} to {{dv|56|80}} while wet.
***Projectile damage value changes while under 50% health, but above 25%:
****Nerfed Music Note damage from {{dv|108|140}} to {{dv|88|116}} while dry, and {{dv|88|120}} to {{dv|68|104}} while wet.
****Nerfed Frost Mist damage from {{dv|0|120}} to {{dv|0|108}} while dry, and {{dv|0|100}} to {{dv|0|96}} while wet.
***Projectile damage value changes while under 25% health:
****Nerfed Music Note damage from {{dv|114|144}} to {{dv|92|124}} while dry, and {{dv|94|124}} to {{dv|72|112}} while wet.
****Nerfed Frost Mist damage from {{dv|90|128}} to {{dv|88|116}} while dry, and {{dv|70|108}} to {{dv|68|104}} while wet.
****Nerfed Water Spear damage from {{dv|0|120}} to {{dv|0|108}} while dry, and {{dv|0|100}} to {{dv|0|96}} while wet.
**Leviathan changes:
***Nerfed meteor damage from {{dv|150|180}} to {{dv|110|140}} while dry, and {{dv|130|160}} to {{dv|100|120}} while wet.
***Now fires meteors more often.
***Increased speed.
*[[Prepare to Die\Cry]] Modes no longer affect bosses from the Thorium mod.

*[[Alchemical Flask]] and [[Plague Hive]]'s Plague Seekers now produce less dust, and attacking while either is equipped now inflicts [[Plague]] for 2 seconds instead of 3.
*Renamed Calamity Biome Spawner to [[Calamity Spawner]], and it now spawns all Calamity ores.
*Reworked [[Eye of the Storm]], [[Pearl of Enthrallment]], [[Rare Waifu in a Bottle]], and [[Waifu in a Bottle]] into [[coverings]] that summon their respective elementals when equipped, instead of summon weapons.
*Using [[Celestial Onion]] or [[Demon Trophy]] while a boss is alive no longer causes the player to drop all their items.
*[[Overloaded Sludge]] no longer uses a [[Slime Crown]] in its recipes, it now uses 25 [[Blighted Gel]] instead of 50 in its Blighted Gel recipe, and it now uses 25 [[Purified Gel]] instead of 20 in its Purified Gel recipe.
*Renamed Lure of Enthrallment to Pearl of Enthrallment, and it now uses {{rare|5}} rarity instead of {{rare|7}}.
*Changed [[Yharim's Gift]]'s defense and damage boosts while in the [[Jungle]] from 25% each to 30% and 15% respectively, buffed its Dragon Dust damage from 150 to 350, and buffed its Sky Flare damage from 300 to 750.

==== Weapons ====
*Tweaked the lighting for [[Abyss Blade]]'s orbs.
*[[Adamantite Throwing Axe]] no longer bounces or inflicts [[Bleeding]].
*Buffed [[Ark of the Elements]]' damage from 70 to 84.
*[[Astral Blaster]] now produces less dust.
*[[Charged Dart Blaster]] changes:
**Now has a right click ability that fires bouncing energy blasts.
**Left click projectiles no longer bounce.
**Nerfed use time from 22 to 24.
*Buffed [[Cosmic Rainbow]]'s damage from 65 to 100.
*[[Diseased Pike]] and [[Pandemic]]'s Plague Seekers now produce less dust.
*Nerfed [[Dragon Rage]]'s shot speed from 20 to 14, and it now shoots a spread of five Draconic Wave projectiles instead of a large blast of flames.
*Nerfed [[Elemental Axe]]'s damage from 550 to 250, and they now set target immunity frames to 3.
*Nerfed [[Elemental Eruption]]'s damage from 59 to 50.
*Buffed [[Elemental Lance]]'s shot speed from 10 to 12.
*[[Galaxia]] now uses item105 instead of item1 for its use sound.
*Nerfed [[Genisis]]' damage from 129 to 120.
*Nerfed [[Golden Eagle]]'s damage from 65 to 58, and its use time from 4 to 6.
*Decreased the amount of dust [[Greatsword of Judgement]] produces.
*Nerfed [[Hellwing Staff]]'s damage from 27 to 23.
*[[The Hive]]'s Bee RPGs now produce less dust.
*Buffed [[Magna Striker]]'s damage from 52 to 63.
*Nerfed [[Meteor Fist]]'s damage from 18 to 15.
*[[Nano Purge]]'s lasers now ignore [[Water]].
*Nerfed [[Phoenix Blade]]'s damage from 43 to 30, and its use time from 26 to 28.
*Buffed [[Phoenix Flame Barrage]]'s shot speed from 15 to 20.
*[[Shredder]] changes:
**Now uses Charged Dart Blaster in its recipe.
**Now has a right click ability that fires bouncing, exploding blasts.
**Left click now fires bouncing blasts that split into dozens of smaller projectiles, instead of directly using the player's ammo type.
*Buffed [[Soul Piercer]]'s damage from 95 to 110.
*Decreased the size of [[Tears of Heaven]]'s dust.
*[[Terratomere]]'s beams no longer produce any dust idly, now use dust type 107 instead of 74, and have a green color filter.

=== Other ===
*All Calamity ores now spawn on world generation instead of after killing certain bosses.
