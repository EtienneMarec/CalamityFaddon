The '''Abyss Shocker''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] dropped by [[Box Jellyfish]] and [[Mirage Jelly|Mirage Jellies]] after [[Skeletron]] has been defeated. It fires an erratic lightning bolt that travels very quickly on a semi-randomized path, leaving an aesthetic lingering trail. The lightning homes in on enemies and bounces to another nearby enemy on contact, hitting enemies a maximum of 6 times, though each bolt is unable to bounce to an enemy it has already hit. It is unable to pass through tiles and has a 1/3 chance to inflict the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]].
