'''Wulfrum armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] summoner [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]]s and [[Energy Core]]s. It takes a total of 25 Wulfrum Metal Scraps and 3 Energy Cores to make the full set.

It consists of a '''Wulfrum Hat  & Goggles''', '''Wulfrum Jacket''', and '''Wulfrum Overalls'''.

The '''Wulfrum Hat & Goggles''' gives 0 defense and 10% increased minion damage. The '''Wulfrum Jacket''' gives 2 defense and 5% increased damage reduction. The '''Wulfrum Overalls''' give 1 defense and 5% increased movement speed.

Wulfrum armor's set bonus is +1 minion slot. When double tapping the downwards movement key, the armor  set transforms into power armor.

* Calling the power armor consumes one piece of [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]].
*While the power armor is active, you can only use the gun built into the armor, but your defense is increased by 13.
*The power armor can last up to 30 seconds before falling apart, and 20 seconds to recharge. The time wearing the armor is decreased by roughly 2 seconds per hit.


The full set totals to 3 defense.

==Trivia==
*The design of the old Wulfrum Mask is inspired by the [https://riskofrain.fandom.com/wiki/Bandit Bandit] character from the game ''[[wikipedia:Risk of Rain|Risk of Rain]]''.
*This armor set was originally made by the elusive John Wulfrum.
*Wulfrum as a metal is often referred to as very weak. This is shown with how the power armor falls apart after use.
* A useful way to get bulk wulfrum metal scraps for the power suit is by using a [[Wulfrum Lure]], as it, hence the name, lures [[Wulfrum Enemies|wulfrum automatons]].
