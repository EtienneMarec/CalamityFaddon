The '''1.4.2.001''' update introduced the [[Sunken Sea]] biome, along with various [[item]]s, [[enemies]], and [[blocks]] to go along with it including the [[Sea King]] [[Town NPC]], and the [[Giant Clam]] [[mini boss]]. The update also made a variety of balance changes, introduced [[banners]] for most of the mod's enemies, and reworked [[Lava]].
