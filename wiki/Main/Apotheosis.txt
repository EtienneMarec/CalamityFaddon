'''Apotheosis''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Developer items|developer]] [[spell tome]]. It summons a cosmic portal that released a mini [[Devourer of Gods]] that will swarm an enemy and fire lasers, before disappearing 5 seconds later.

The Devourer of Gods inflicts several debuffs:
*[[God Slayer Inferno]] (10 seconds)
*[[Demon Flames]] (10 seconds)
*[[Armor Crunch]] (10 seconds)
*[[Exo Freeze]] (3.33% chance for 2 seconds)

On enemies, these combined debuffs cause the target to lose 2750 health per second, have its defense reduced by 66.67%, have its defense further decreased by 20 for 10 seconds, and have an additional 3.33% chance to completely freeze the enemy for 2 seconds.

Its lasers always inflict God Slayer Inferno and Demon Flames, but not Armor Crunch or Exo Freeze.

== Trivia ==
*Apotheosis was programmed by Terry N. Muse, one of the mod's lore writers, and implemented by the developer as a reward for completing a speedrun race challenge.
**This speedrun race challenge had the participants race to kill the Devourer of Gods in hardcore mode with only spears.
**The damage value of 77 and use time of 177 are references to Terry's excessive usage of the number 7. 
*The word "apotheosis" refers to a culmination or climax, or an elevation to a god-like status.
*The knockback value of 6.9 is a reference to the internet meme of 69.
*The mana cost and velocities of 42 are references to the internet meme of 420.
