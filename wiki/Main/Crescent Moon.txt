The '''Crescent Moon''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flail]] that auto-swings. The flail passes through tiles. When swung, it leaves behind homing crescent moons that deal 40% of the weapon's base damage. It behaves similarly to the [[Solar Eruption]].
