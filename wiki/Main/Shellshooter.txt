The '''Shellshooter''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bow]] that is purchasable from the [[Sea King]] at any time. [[Wooden Arrow]]s are converted into shells that have a very low velocity and pierce up to four enemies, breaking on the fifth.
