The '''Bazooka''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]]. When used, it fires a rocket that is affected by gravity after half a second of travel, and explodes when hitting an enemy or a tile. 1% of the target's health is added to the damage of each rocket, and another 1% from the explosion.

It uses [[Grenade Shells]] as ammo, which can be purchased from the [[Arms Dealer]] if the player has the Bazooka in their inventory.

Once a boss is spawned, the Bazooka is only fireable twice until the boss is defeated.
