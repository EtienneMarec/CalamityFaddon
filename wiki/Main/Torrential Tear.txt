The '''Torrential Tear''' is a non-consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] item. Shortly after usage, it will begin to [[Rain]]. It can be found inside [[Shadow Chest]]s in the [[Abyss]] or in [[Abyssal Crate]]s. It cannot be used while Slime Rain is active.

== Trivia ==
*This item used to be a [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive drop from the [[Desert Scourge]]. Before that, it was dropped by the Desert Scourge in the now-removed [[Prepare to Die]] mode.
