The '''Vital Jelly''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Green Jellyfish]] and [[Mirage Jelly|Mirage Jellies]]. It increases movement speed and jump speed by 10%. It can be combined with [[Mana Jelly]] and [[Life Jelly]] in order to craft the [[Grand Gelatin]].
