The '''Potion of Omniscience''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] that grants the '''Omniscience''' [[buff]] upon usage, which grants the effects of the [[Hunter Potion|Hunter]], [[Spelunker Potion|Spelunker]], and [[Dangersense Potion|Dangersense]] potions simultaneously. This causes enemies, treasures, and traps onscreen to be highlighted. Additionally, the Omniscience buff highlights all enemy projectiles.
