The '''Skyfin Bombers''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]] that drops from [[Skyfin]]s after defeating the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. It throws Skyfin bombs that only seek towards enemies that are below them, erupting into three sulphuric clouds on impact.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Skyfin Bombers causes the nuke to home regardless of position and will cause a massive irradiated aura on hits.
