The '''Dirt Clump''' is a craftable consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws a gravity-affected ball of dirt that hits enemies for small damage.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Dirt Clumps causes the next clump thrown to deal 50% more damage and break into dirt fragments which hit nearby enemies for 4 damage.
