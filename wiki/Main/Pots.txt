'''Pots''' are common items generated at world creation in random areas. When broken with any [[weapon]] or [[tool]], they will drop [[coins]] and/or random common items. Pots cannot be picked up, never respawn, and cannot be crafted. The Calamity Mod adds two new pots that spawn in the [[Abyss]] and the [[Sulphurous Sea]]. Pots in the Abyss can only drop [[Abyssal Treasure]] while pots in the Sulphurous Sea can only drop [[Sulphuric Treasure]].
