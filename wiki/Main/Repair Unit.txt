The '''Repair Unit''' is a [[critter]] found in all five [[Bio-center Lab]]s. It passively walks back and forth, jumping over gaps and solid blocks in its path. Every 5 seconds, it will latch onto a nearby wall if available for up to 10.33 seconds or until it leaves a wall.

== Notes ==
*Unlike other critters, the Repair Unit is not able to be caught with a [[Bug Net]].

== Trivia ==
*Repair Units were inspired by [https://subnautica.fandom.com/wiki/Alien_Robot_(Subnautica) Alien Robots] from the game, ''[[wikipedia:Subnautica|Subnautica]]''.
