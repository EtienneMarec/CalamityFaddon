The '''Chromatic Orb''' is a [[Pets|light pet]] summoning item that drops from [[Astrum Deus]]. It summons an ancient dragon to follow the player. The player can control the location of the pet by using the movement keys. While the pet is active, the player gains the [[Hunter]] and [[Dangersense]] effects.

== Trivia ==
*Its name is a reference to a [https://dungeonsdragons.fandom.com/wiki/Chromatic_orb spell of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Dungeons & Dragons|Dungeons & Dragons]]''.
*The buff tooltip is supposed to sound like the phrase 'He believes in you', as the dedicatee pronounces 'Xi' as 'kee'.
