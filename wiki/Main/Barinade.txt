The '''Barinade''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[bow]] that is dropped by the [[Desert Scourge]]. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as [[ammo]], arrows are converted into electric bolt arrows that emit a small explosion upon contact with tiles or enemies.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Trivia ==
*The name Barinade and the bolts it fires are a reference to the [https://zelda.fandom.com/Barinade Barinade] boss in ''[[wikipedia:The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]''.
