The '''Vitriolic Viper''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that drops from tier 3 [[Acid Eel]]s. It rapidly fires a tight spread of nine venomous spits followed by five venomous fangs. The spits move faster but do not pierce, while the fangs move slower and pierce up to three enemies. Both projectiles are capable of inflicting the [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuff]].
