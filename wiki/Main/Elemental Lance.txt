The '''Elemental Lance''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spear]]. It fires one gravity-affected cyan beam that splits into more beams with similar properties.

Each usage of the Elemental Lance results in 162 orange beams at most, if the blue and pink beams do not prematurely dissipate on enemies. All beams can move through blocks.
