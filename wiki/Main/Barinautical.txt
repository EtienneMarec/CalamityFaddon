The '''Barinautical''' is a pre-Hardmode [[bow]] dropped by the [[Aquatic Scourge]]; it can be considered an indirect upgrade of the [[Barinade]]. It fires bursts of three arrows, with a one-third second of a delay. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as ammo, arrows are replaced with electric arrows that explode upon impact. Each electric arrow does damage twice, for a total of six hits.
