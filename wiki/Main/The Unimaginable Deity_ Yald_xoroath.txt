Yald'xoroath, the Unfathomable Deity, is a Unique Major Epilogue Superboss encountered after defeating [[The Fallen Ascended Master, Xeroc|The Fallen Ascended, Master Xeroc]], [[Future bosses|Dragon God Zeratros]], After completion it will grant the player assess to face off against the [[The Entropic God, Noxus|Avatar of emptiness Noxus]]. He is renowned as one of the most challenging superbosses in modded Terraria along with Oblivion (From Ancients Awakend Mod), and emerging victorious against him unlocks the opportunity to face the ultimate challenge - Avatar of Emptiness, Noxus, Supreme Deity of Emptiness, Oblivion (only available with the Ancients Awakened Mod installed), as well as the Nameless Deity. He can be summoned with The Holy Grail. 

== Spawn ==
When summoned, a colossal cosmic wall composed of dark cosmic dust will emerge 240 blocks from the player, extending to the edge of the game world. If the player tries to escape this zone, they will receive a total annihilation debuff, leading to a rapid loss of health and causing them to vanish completely until "YOU CANNOT ESCAPE" Message will show up on death-screen instead of the usual "You were slain" Message. If the player remains outside the cosmic wall for 20 seconds The sky will crack open, revealing a pitch-black sky filled with white stars. A deafening roar will echo as a giant, photoshopped eye appears and gazes at the player before disappearing in a blinding explosion of light. The screen will then fade to black for 10 seconds, displaying a title bar that reads, "How pitiful for such a weakling to challenge a being beyond my will!" Suddenly, the screen will shatter, and Yald'xoroath will emerge from a dark vortex amidst a white flash and the fight begins.

== Phase 1 ==
During phase 1 the skybox will change to a white-black like moving-skybox Yald'xoroath will fly around the area for a bit before summoning a barrage of blacklight bolts that will sometimes home in on the player or not.

Yald'xoroath Will perform the following the attacks in random order.

* Teleport around the player 3 times before vanishing and summoning a white moving summoning geometric Squares, Triangles or Circles, that will break apart shortly after depending on what shape was fired.
* Stays stationary and will summon a white-hole which will circle around the player before colliding into a black hole which will form A Large Pulsar explosion. (The explosion must be parried by A parry Covering/Weapon that can parry attacks)
* Yald'xoroath Will summon 4 light-wall-like blast these attacks are very-similar to nameless deity however less bigger and quick.
* Summons A slow moving Black Hole which will summon Slithering Bolts that travel in a straight line while Yald'xoroath follows behind.

== Phase 2 ==
Upon reaching 70% HP During this phase the skybox will increasingly move faster and continue to get more and more chaotic and darker as well. These attacks are done in a random order. 

* Yald'xoroath will fly very fast and Will summon a saturn like planet that explodes into blacklight blasts while also simultaneously shooting out Distorted Spheres that explode.
* Yald'xoroath will summon a much bigger white hole that will summon blacklight bolts which will circle around the player before exploding into a blinding flash and will deal massive damage is caught in its center, he will also occasionally summon a black hole which will generate a massive explosion if it impacts the white-hole. (These black holes can be knocked back by weapons that have the wind element associated with it but it will be very minor)
* Yald'xoroath will summon 4 light bursts from the direction the player is moving as well as the opposing side 6 times, These bursts explode shortly after they pass each other.
* Yald'xoroath Will vanish before summoning A glowing cube which will crack and explodes into a blacklight explosion along with the scattered parts orbiting the player before smashing into each other forming another light-burst attack as each piece smashes into others.
* Yald'xoroath will summon a giant Judgment-blade made of pure-light and black that can cut open the fabric of reality with each slash which will also summon blacklight bolts, This ripple is 3 blocks wide at the center of the slash and linger for 2 seconds, He will not teleport but instead slash 3 times before vanishing and summoning 3 light bursts

== Phase 3 ==
Upon reaching 40% HP the screen will blacken with a message "You Dare Defy me!?...YOU WILL PERISH FROM THIS WORLD FOR GOOD!"  

during this phase the skybox will turn into blue-burning flame along with light-particles rising up to the sky, thunder will start to appear.

* Yald'xoroath will summon white stationary Distorted Spheres that explode into 6 evenly spaced spiraling light bursts.
* Yald'xoroath will teleport randomly while summoning a bullet hell of merging projectiles before summoning a white-hole which will explode in 3 seconds.
* Yald'xoroath Will Stay stationary before summoning a massive Archonic Deathray that can teleport in the direction the player is going, This lasts 5 seconds.
* Yald'xoroath sometimes will summon 5 white Slithering Bolts instead of 3 during phase 1.
* Yald'xoroath randomly does an attack stated from phase 2 but faster than normal. (This can be done 2 times and can be done again once Yald'xoroath has done the rest of attacks stated in phase 3)

'''''At 10% HP he will summon a Archonic Deathray this time a bit-faster and will also summon Blacklight bolts and Distorted Spheres, the player must quickly defeat him.'''''

Before the fight ends, Yald'xoroath will begin to flicker for 4 seconds before exploding into a huge-blinding light of colors with light-blasts before vanishing from existence, the world's skybox around will turn into a beautiful aurora-like rainbow signaling the end of the fight.
