The '''Phosphorescent Gauntlet''' is a Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]] that drops from [[Nuclear Terror]]s. When used, the player lunges slightly towards the direction of the cursor and performs a close-ranged punch. Upon punching a target or a solid tile, the player recoils in the opposite direction and nearby blocks will emit particles as if they are broken.

{{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} killing [[The General]] {{fantag|Apotheosis and Friends}} while under the effects of [[Fluorescent Vodka]] initiates the [[22 Bullet Theory]] [[event]].

== Notes ==
*Despite not behaving like standard swords or spears, it can deal [[true melee]] damage, and will receive bonuses from gear that boosts true melee damage.

== Trivia ==
*Its appearance and functionality are similar to the ''[https://riskofrain2.fandom.com/wiki/Loader Loader]'' from ''[[wikipedia:Risk of Rain 2|Risk of Rain 2]]'', a high-mobility character equipped with robotic gauntlets.
*The flavor text is a reference to Thanos and the Infinity Gauntlet from the ''[[wikipedia:Marvel|Marvel]]'' franchise.
**The forsaken fishron refers to [[The Old Duke]], the boss of the [[Acid Rain]] event, and a generally divisive boss within the community.
