The '''Parasitic Scepter''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] that drops from [[Water Leech]]es during tier 1 of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. On use, it fires two to four leeches which latch onto [[enemies]] on contact. They inflict the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]], which stacks up to three times depending on how many leeches are currently latched onto an enemy.
