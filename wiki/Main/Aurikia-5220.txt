The '''Aurikia-5220''' is a craftable '''Godseeker''' Mode [[Tools|Tool]] that combines the effects of the [[tgc:Shellphone|Shellphone]], [[Asgardian Aegis]], [[Seeking Mechanisms]], [[XO-Tell]], the [[Q.C.S.T.T.]], and the [[Stat Meter]]. The player can either {{Key|use}} to show which area they can teleport to (Spawn, Home, Ocean, Underworld, , the 5 Bio-center labs, and the subworlds, in a clockwise formation), or {{Key|open}} to open up a message log, where they can access the various blueprints, read the lore items that its holding, see the messages either the player or the other NPCs sent, or even contact the NPCs you met.

Favoriting this item grants the player the ability to perform a Ram Dash, akin to the [[Asgardian Aegis]]. Additionally, while its in the player's inventory, it grants various defensive buffs and grants various immunities to knockback and most debuffs, identical to the former. This buff ''can'' stack with its components.
