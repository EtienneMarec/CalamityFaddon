The '''Chaotic Puffer''' is an [[enemy]] that spawns in the 2nd and 3rd layers of the [[Abyss]]. It slowly swims towards the player and inflicts the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] on contact, while periodically puffing up every 5 seconds. While it is unable to damage the player while not puffed, if it hits the player or dies while puffed up, it creates a large brimstone explosion, which also hurts other enemies. It receives increased knockback upon being hit.

== Notes ==
*Though the Chaotic Puffer technically has 100% knockback resistance, it has a special interaction when hit by a projectile, which causes its velocity to get set to the projectile's velocity at the moment of impact. It will slowly drift to a stop afterwards.
*The Chaotic Puffer slowly rotates clockwise when moving to the right, and counterclockwise when moving to the left. It spins when hit.

== Trivia ==
*In previous versions of the mod, Chaotic Puffers only spawned in the final layer of the Abyss.
