'''Polyp Launcher''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[sentry]] that is purchased from the [[Sea King]] at any time.  When used, it summons a grounded polyp sentry which inaccurately fires gravity-affected balls of coral at nearby enemies. On hit, the coral chunk bursts into 1-4 fragments which deals half as much damage and knockback.
