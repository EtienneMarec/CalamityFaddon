The '''World Breaker''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Pickaxe]] and [[Axe]] combination that is a direct upgrade to the [[Gelpick]]. It can mine blocks up to {{item|Pitchblende}} and {{item|Primordial Ore}}. Right clicking with the pickaxe will fire a slow moving Armageddon Ball that can travel through blocks and will slowly decelerate until stopping. This ball will instantly break any tile it touches.
