The '''Heart of Darkness''' is a [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that fills up the [[Rage Meter]] by 1% of the total meter every second. It also prevents the Rage Meter from emptying out of combat.

Any [[Boss]] [[Treasure Bag]] in [[Revengeance Mode]] has a 1.67% chance of containing this covering.

== Notes ==
*All boss bags have a 5% chance of containing any of the three universal Revengeance coverings, the other two being [[Stress Pills]] and [[Laudanum]], and then a 1/3 roll is cast for which covering is given. This results in a 1.67% chance to obtain any of the three coverings, however the player can never receive more than one at a time.
