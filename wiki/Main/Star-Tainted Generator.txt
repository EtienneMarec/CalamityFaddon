The '''Star-Tainted Generator''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that functions as a direct upgrade to the [[Jelly-Charged Battery]], [[Nuclear Rod]], and the [[Starbuster Core]]. While equipped the player gains +2 minion slots, which doesn't stack with downgrades, and 7% increased minion damage. In addition to this, all minion attacks spawn a small astral explosion on enemy hits with a one-second cooldown, dealing 40 summon damage, and all minions will inflict the [[Electrified]], [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]], and [[Irradiated]] [[debuffs]].
