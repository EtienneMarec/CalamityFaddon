The '''Animus''' was a craftable Epilogue [[broadsword]] that auto-swings. Hitting an enemy would apply the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] for a significantly long time of 100 seconds. Animus would change its base damage every time it strikes an enemy, choosing a weighted value at random from the following options:
*25% chance of 800 damage
*25% chance of 1,200 damage
*20% chance of 1,800 damage
*15% chance of 3,000 damage
*10% chance of 6,000 damage
*5% chance of 10,000 damage
