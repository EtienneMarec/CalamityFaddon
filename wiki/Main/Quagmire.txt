The '''Quagmire''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[yoyo]]. While in use, the yoyo randomly and continuously emits spore clouds which deal 50% to 70% of the yoyo's damage. Only the yoyo itself inflicts the [[Venom]] [[debuff]]. When the yoyo returns to the player, it will be automatically re-thrown if they are still holding the fire button.
