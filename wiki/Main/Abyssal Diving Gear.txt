The '''Abyssal Diving Gear''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] which combined and enhanced effects of the [[Arctic Diving Gear]] and the [[Depths Charm]]. It was part of the recipe for the [[Abyssal Diving Suit]].
