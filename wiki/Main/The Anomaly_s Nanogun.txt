'''The Anomaly's Nanogun''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[ranged weapon]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), a reticle appears at the cannon's muzzle for 0.67 seconds. Then, it fires a solid, infinitely piercing red beam towards the cursor over five-round bursts.

When used with its secondary fire, the weapon has an effective base damage of 320, base use time of 59, and base knockback of 3.6. It causes the player to recoil in the opposite direction the weapon is fired towards, and simultaneously fires three bombs with varying speeds towards the cursor. Each bomb creates a massive explosion on impact which constantly damages enemies within radius.

On primary fire, {{charge|0.8|250|140}} On secondary fire, it consumes 0.375 units of charge per use. As such, the secondary fire can be used continuously from full charge 666 times, or for 11 minutes and 17 seconds.
