The '''1.2.3.004''' update buffed several bosses in [[Death Mode]] and reworked a few armor set bonuses.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Draedon's Heart]], [[Spirit Generator]].

== Changes ==

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*[[Brain of Cthulhu]] now gains extra knockback resistance in Death Mode.
*[[Brimstone Elemental]] now cycles through phases quicker in Death Mode.
*[[Cryogen]]'s phases now happen earlier in Death Mode.
*[[Desert Scourge]] is now more aggressive in Death Mode.
*[[The Destroyer]] now lowers the piercing of projectiles which pierce more than 3 times to 3 instead of ones that pierce more than 2 times.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' body in both phases now resists the [[Stardust Dragon Staff]]'s stardust dragon.
*Nerfed [[Providence, the Profaned God]]'s defensive guardian's health from {{dv|40,000|56,000}} to {{dv|30,000|42,000}}.
*Nerfed Providence, the Profaned God's offensive guardian's health from {{dv|50,000|70,000}} to {{dv|40,000|65,000}}.
*[[True Eye of Cthulhu|True Eyes of Cthulhu]] now gain extra velocity in Death Mode.
*[[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]] now cycles through phases quicker in Death Mode.
*The Death Mode [[Siren and Leviathan]] fight now begins with both bosses at the same time, along with a [[Siren Clone]].
*[[Slime God Core]] is now more aggressive in Death Mode.
*Lowered the frequency at which [[Supreme Calamitas]] cycles through phases in Death Mode.

=== Items ===
*[[Aerospec armor]]'s set bonus now disables fall damage, increases fall speed, and spawns a spread of homing feathers if the player is hit for over 50 damage.
*[[Celestial Tracers]] now use {{rare|15}} rarity instead of the {{rare|rainbow}} rarity.
*Nerfed [[Celestus]]' damage from 95 to 90.
*[[Meteorite]] will no longer drop if mined before [[Eater of Worlds]] or [[Brain of Cthulhu]] has been defeated in Revengeance Mode.
*Buffed [[Photoviscerator]]'s damage from 40 to 46.
*Lowered the extra damage inflicted by the [[Raider's Talisman]] from 25 to 10.
*The [[Reaver Shark]] can no longer be swung until [[Skeletron]] has been defeated.
*[[Statigel armor]]'s set bonus now grants increased jump speed, an extra jump, and temporary immunity if the player is hit for over 100 damage.
*Nerfed [[Terra Flameburster]]'s damage from 70 to 56.
*Slightly nerfed [[Terratomere]]'s projectile damage.
*[[Victide armor]]'s set bonus now gives weapons a chance to fire a damaging shell which deals classless damage.
*[[Voltaic Climax]]'s projectiles now last for 4 seconds instead of 4.5.
*[[Wulfrum Armor]] now requires 12 [[Wulfrum Shard]]s to craft instead of 20.
*All [[Wulfrum Helmet]]s now require 5 Wulfrum Shards to craft instead of 10.
*[[Wulfrum Leggings]] now require 8 Wulfrum Shards to craft instead of 15.

=== Other ===
*Death Mode now boosts enemy spawn rates by 40%.
*Changed the "Underworld" message during world generation to "Final Cleanup".

== Fixes ==
*Fixed [[Chaotic Ore]] generating on blocks other than [[Ash Block]]s, such as [[Obsidian Brick]]s.
*Fixed a bug where [[Elemental Quiver]] boosted throwing critical strike chance instead of ranged critical strike chance.
*Fixed desync bugs with Providence's guardians and Supreme Calamitas in multiplayer.
