The ''Rod of Misery'' is a pre-Hardmode [[tool]] and equivalent to the [[Rod of Discord]]. When used, it teleports the player opposite to the cursor's x-coordinate, y-coordinate, or both, randomly. This means the player has three potential positions they could teleport in, making this tool much more riskier during a [[Boss]] fight.
