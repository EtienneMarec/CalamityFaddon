'''Eutrophication''' is a [[debuff]] that makes the target completely unable to move for its duration. Almost all [[enemies]] are susceptible to this debuff, excluding all [[bosses]]. It is inflicted by the [[Apoctolith]], [[Prismalline]], [[Coral Cannon]], [[Eutrophic Scimitar]], [[Clam Crusher]], and [[Abyssal Mirror]].
