'''Shatterspectre Tracers''' are [[tgc:Hardmode|Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] that is an upgrade to [[tgc:Fairy Boots|Fairy Boots]] and [[tgc:Hellfire Treads|Hellfire Treads]]. It is considered a side-grade to [[Angel Treads]]. While equipped, they provide the following effects:
*10 [[defense]]
*4 increased life regen (2 HP/s)
*10% increased movement speed
* The player's max horizontal movement speed is increased to 30 miles per hour (~22 tiles per second).
* A fairy minion is spawned, attacking the closest enemy by shooting a homing petal projectile every 1.5 seconds. 
**The projectile deals 40 classless damage, which scales with the strongest class and cannot critically hit.
*The player's attacks inflict the [[Crush Depth]] [[Debuffs|debuff]].
*All water and ice debuffs deal extra 50% damage.
*Immunity to fall damage

*The fairy's color will change depending on the time of the day: 
**From 4:30AM to 12PM, the fairy will be pink.
**From 12PM to 7:30PM, the fairy will be green.
**From 7:30PM to 4:30AM, the fairy will be blue.
