The '''Romajeda Orchid''' is a [[pet]] summoning item sold by the [[Dryad]] NPC. It summons a dog named Kendra to follow the player. When the player is idle, Kendra will either yawn or bark to alert the player. If the player moves too fast, Kendra will use black dragon wings to fly towards them.

== Trivia ==
*This item is based on LordMetarex' real pet dog, Kendra. Kendra sadly died on the 31st of December 2018, hence this item's depiction of her as a "never forgotten friend".
*The term "Romajeda" was used due to the dog breed of Kendra being called "the Romajeda". Thus Kendra's full name would be "Kendra the Romajeda"
**The white Orchid is used for Kendra's memorial, which stands for: "real love", "pure", "dignity","elegance" and "beauty".
*The buff icon resembles a pixelated photo of Kendra. With the normal photo being used as an emoticon over at the Calamity Discord.
*There were multiple barking sounds considered for Kendra, but the Dedicatee decided his mother would choose which one were to be used, considering she spends the most time with her.
*The reason Kendra has dragon wings is due to the dedicatee's fondness of dragons, while it was also used to differentiate Kendra from [[Bear]], which was using white angelic wings.
**Another potential reason for the wings is if you were to shuffle the name "Kendra" around you would get "draken" the Dutch translation of "dragons", with the dedicatee being from the Netherlands.
