The '''Wulfrum Treasure Pinger''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[furniture]] item. When right-clicked while the player has an [[Energy Orb]], radio waves are released every 5 seconds which cause any [[Chest]]s that intersect the wave to have a {{item|Wulfrum Popup|nolink=y}} appear above them. These popups last for as long as the Wulfrum Treasure Pinger is powered. The Wulfrum Treasure Pinger will run out of energy after 30 seconds.
