The '''Titan Scale Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[buff potion]] made from [[Titan Potion]]s, a [[Beetle Husk]], and a [[Coralskin Foolfish]]. Upon use, it grants the '''Titan Scale''' [[buff]], which gives increased knockback, 5 [[defense]], and 5% damage reduction. Additionally after damaging an enemy via [[true melee]], the defense and damage reduction boosts are increased to 25 and 10% respectively for 10 seconds.
