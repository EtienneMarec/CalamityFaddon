The '''Soul of Hydrogen''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] and [[quest item]] dropped by [[Hydrogen]].

When equipped, the player will turn [[lava]] and [[honey]] into [[water]] on contact.

==[[Endings|Elemental quest]]==
Having the Elemental quest active will allow this item to be sold the [[Archmage]] to finish the quest, with the following dialogue. The player can then initiate the final fight of the Elemental route by clicking the [[Genesis (event)|Genesis]] button.

*Thank you very much, your dedication and faith have been outstanding. You would have been an excellent student of mine, given better situations. It is disappointing seeing this soul used like this; wasted on reusing unexploded ordinances. In the end it seems that it couldn't save the sea kingdom.

After this, Permafrost will be able to summon damaging bubbles.
