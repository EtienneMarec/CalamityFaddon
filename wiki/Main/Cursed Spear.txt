The '''Cursed Spear''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[javelin]] that is rarely dropped by [[Clinger]]s. It travels a long distance before being affected by gravity and can pierce one enemy. The Cursed Spear applies the [[Cursed Inferno]] [[debuff]] to all hit enemies. It is the [[Corruption]] variant of the [[Ichor Spear]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Cursed Spear causes the resulting javelin to release cursed fireballs in random directions as it travels.
