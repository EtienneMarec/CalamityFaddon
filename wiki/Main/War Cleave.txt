'''War Cleave''' is a [[debuff]] that reduces enemy defense by 15 and lowers enemy damage reduction by 25%. It is inflicted by the [[Waraxe]], the [[Warblade]], [[Overloaded Soldier]]s, and [[Phantom Debris]], and [https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Angry_Bones Angry Bones] (in Hardmode). Any enemy that can receive the [[Armor Crunch]] debuff can also receive War Cleave.
