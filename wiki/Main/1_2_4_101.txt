The '''1.2.4.101''' update replaced [[Stress]] with [[Rage Meter|Rage]] and [[Adrenaline]], and also buffed the [[Death Mode]] health values of most bosses.

{{toc limit|3|right=yes}}

== Additions ==
*Added [[Adrenaline Meter]].
**Slowly builds up during boss fights if the player doesn't take damage. Once full, it can be activated with a hot key to grant the player the Adrenaline buff and a large damage increase (quadrupled in Death Mode). Getting hit with the buff cancels it.
*Added [[Rage Meter]].
**Slowly builds up when the player takes damage, hits enemies with true melee weapons, or wears certain accessories. Once full, it can be activated with a hot key to grant the player the Rage Mode buff temporarily for a large damage increase (quadrupled in Death Mode). Getting hit with the buff cancels it.
*Added [[Ectoheart]], [[Electrolyte Gel Pack]], [[Infernal Blood]], [[Mushroom Plasma Root]], [[Red Lightning Container]], [[Starlight Fuel Cell]].
*Added [[Fox Drive]].
*Added [[Adrenaline]], [[Fox Pet (buff)|Fox]], [[Rage Mode]].

=== Items ===
*[[Affliction]]'s stat boosts which were previously only granted when the player was at or above 6000 Stress are now granted at all times.
*[[Cosmic Immaterializer]] can now only summon 1 Cosmic Energy at a time, and now only requires 10 minion slots instead of 12.
*[[Draedon's Heart]] and [[Heart of Darkness]] no longer grant any benefits relating to Stress, and now let the player generate Rage over time instead of Stress.
*[[Laudanum]] now increases the player's damage and critical strike chance by 6% at all times.
*Holding the [[Lunic Eye]] no longer decreases Stress.
*Nerfed [[Magna Striker]]'s damage from 52 to 48.
*Lowered the time that [[Magnetic Meltdown]] and [[Voltaic Climax]]'s projectiles last.
*True melee weapons now let the player gain Rage on strikes.
*Altered [[Revengeance]]'s tooltip to reflect the change from Stress to Rage and Adrenaline.
*[[Stress Pills]] now increases the player's damage and critical strike chance by 4% at all times.

=== Other ===
*The [[Astral Meteor]] now uses [[Music#Heaven's Hell-Sent Gift|Heaven's Hell-Sent Gift]] as its theme.
*[[Heart Attack]] now provides the player with a 30% health boost instead of decreasing health by 30% during the day and hurting the player upon being inflicted.
*[[Horror]] now reduces the player's vision, cuts their defense in half, and reduces their movement speed by 25% instead of just decreasing their speed by 25%.
*Any damage or critical strike chance bonus that goes above the current 75% cap will now only be 25% as effective.
