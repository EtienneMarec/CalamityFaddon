The '''Caustic Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a black Caustic Dragon minion that flies behind the player and attacks nearby enemies by firing gravity-affected thorns that pierce through tiles and up to two enemies. While the player is holding a summon weapon or [[tool]], the thorns will cycle through and inflict one of the following [[debuffs]] for 1-3 seconds in this repeating order:
*[[Marked]]
*[[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]]
*[[Venom]]
*[[Cursed Inferno]]
*[[On Fire!]]
