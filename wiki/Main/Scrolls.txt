'''Scrolls''' are a set of craftable [[tool]]s that provide a toggled permanent effect on use. Up to two scrolls can be in use at a time. Scrolls are crafted with [[Blank Scroll]]s and [[Lore items]] at a [[Scroll Press]].

Two indicators appears beside the health bar, showing which scrolls are currently active. Having two scrolls active at once will reduce the positive effects of scrolls by 15% and will boost the negative effects of scrolls by 30%.
