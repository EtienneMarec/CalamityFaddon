The '''1.1.6.11''' update made some defense changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]].

== Changes ==
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Head and tail no longer have any defense, and now have a 4x damage taken multiplier.
***This multiplier is halved once The Devourer of Gods hits 50% health.
**Buffed body's defense from 35 to 9999.
**All segments no longer take 33% damage from certain piercing projectiles, and 50% damage from the [[Stardust Dragon Staff]]'s Stardust Dragon.
**Greatly increased speed.
*[[Statis' Blessing]] and [[Statis' Curse]] now cause minions to inflict [[Temporal Sadness]], but no longer boosts magic damage, summon damage, and minon knockback. <!---Latter two only apply under 50% health. Stats were 15%, 15%, and 100% respectively--->
*Resprited [[Xeroc Cuisses]], [[Xeroc Mask]], and [[Xeroc Plate Mail]].
*Resprited [[Devourer Spawn]], and [[Staff of the Mechworm]]'s Mechworm.
