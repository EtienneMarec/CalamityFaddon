The '''Sky Glaze''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] obtained rarely from [[Harpy|Harpies]] after defeating the [[Eye of Cthulhu]]. It shoots down three feathers from the sky that stick to objects and explode after a few seconds. Note that the feathers that fall directly on the enemy do contact damage.
