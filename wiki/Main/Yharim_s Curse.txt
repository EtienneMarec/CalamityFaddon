The '''Yharim's Curse''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It is the [[exhumed]] form of [[Yharim's Gift]]. It makes the player take 90% more damage, while also making them deal 20% more damage. The debuffs outweigh the buffs this covering gives, making it not a good choice in a normal playthrough.
