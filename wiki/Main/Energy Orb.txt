Energy Orb is a Pre-Hardmode item Dropped by The [[Wulfrum Excavator]], and can also drop from any enemy after defeating the Wulfrum Excavator  or [[The Inventors]] with a 1% chance. It is used to craft many Early-Game items when combined with [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap|Wulfrum Metal Scraps]], and is also consumed by some of them.{{infobox wrapper
