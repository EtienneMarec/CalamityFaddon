The '''Irradiated Slime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[slime]] that only spawns during Tier 2 of the [[Acid Rain]] [[event]]. Rather than spawning like normal NPCs, they gradually fall from the sky while the event is ongoing, and can fall through platforms while doing so until they are at a similar height to the player. It has a very low chance to drop the [[Lead Core]] upon death, which provides immunity to the [[Irradiated]] [[debuff]] and is used in the crafting of [[Silva armor]].

== Notes ==
*Irradiated Slimes' unique method of spawning is unaffected by things that affect NPC spawn rates, such as [[Water Candle]]s, [[Battle Potion]]s, and [[Calming Potion]]s.
*The Irradiated Slime will be replaced by the [[Gamma Slime]] after [[Polterghast]] has been defeated, making them impossible to encounter in that world after its defeat.
