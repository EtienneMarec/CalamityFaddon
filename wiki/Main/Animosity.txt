The '''Animosity''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] dropped by [[Calamitas]]. Its left click attack is slow but accurate and fires a [[High Velocity Bullet|high velocity]] shot that does heavy damage. Its right click attack is fast and fires a rapid burst of three shots.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*The high velocity shot fired by Animosity's left click attack deals 580% of the weapon's overall damage, including the damage being contributed by the ammunition used.
*The right click attack has a fixed use time of 12 ticks, with each bullet having a use time of 4, and the left click attack has a fixed use time of 35 ticks; as a result, no modifier can increase or decrease the firing rate of the gun.

== Tips ==
*Since the left click attack multiplies bullet damage by 580%, the Animosity massively amplifies the effect of high damage bullets such as [[Venom Bullet]]s or [[Acid Round]]s.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon used to be [[Revengeance Mode]]-exclusive.
*The word "Animosity" means strong hostility.
