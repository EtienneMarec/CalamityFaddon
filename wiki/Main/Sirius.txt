'''Sirius''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]] and the upgrade to the [[Sun God Staff]]. It summons a stratus light entity that hovers above the player's head and shoots beams towards nearby enemies. Additionally, it summons light explosions onto nearby enemies similarly to the [[Rainbow Crystal Staff]].

== Notes ==
*The multiplier for the minion's damage is the natural log of the number of slots it occupies, plus 1.
**Notably, when the minion occupies 3 slots, it has roughly double its initial damage. When the minion occupies 8 slots, it has roughly triple its initial damage. The minion requires 20 slots in order to have roughly quadruple its initial damage.
*The amount of enemies that the minion's beam can pierce through is equal to the number of slots it occupies.

== Trivia ==
*The weapon and minion are based off of [[wikipedia:Sirius|Sirius]], the brightest star in the night sky with a visual magnitude of -1.46.
*Sirius was implemented based on a community suggestion made by Boltlord on the mod's Discord server.
