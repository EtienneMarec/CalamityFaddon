The '''Primordial Ancient''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[spell tome]]. As a direct upgrade to the [[Primordial Earth]], it fires large blasts of dust at an incredibly high velocity and rate, and to a relatively far distance. The dust blasts can pass through tiles and will pierce five enemies before bursting upon hitting the sixth.
