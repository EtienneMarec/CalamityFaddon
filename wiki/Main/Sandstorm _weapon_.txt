The '''Sandstorm''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Sandgun]]. It uses [[Sand Block]]s as [[ammo]] and fires sand blasts which burst on impact. Unlike its predecessor, it does not place Sand Blocks where its projectiles land.
