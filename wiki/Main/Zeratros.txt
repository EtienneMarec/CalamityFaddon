The '''Zeratros''' or ''Albatross'' is a [[Hardmode]] [[NPC]] vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met:

*There is an empty house.
*[[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
*The player has an [[Ogscule]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}} in their inventory.

When threatened by [[enemies]], he will release sound waves towards enemies which pierce 3 times.
