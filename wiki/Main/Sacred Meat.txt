'''Sacred Meat''' is a [[Summoning items|consumable item]] found in the [[Shroom Garden]] floating atop a glowing mushroom pedestal. Consuming it grants the [[tgc:Well_Fed|Exquisitely Stuffed]] buff along with irreversibly teleporting the player into [[Heavenly Limbo]], completing the secret [[Delicious Meat Ending|Delicious Meat Ending route]].

To enter the Shroom Garden in order to obtain Sacred Meat, one must first best [[Crabulon|The Extraordinarily Buff and Feverishly Reverent Defender of Intergalactic Gourmet Partisanship, Crabulon]] in combat.

== Notes ==

*Using Sacred Meat on an [[Altar of the Accursed]] summons a more powerful version of Supreme Ultramage, Permafrost with additional dialogue:
**"Ah, I see you have found my finest delicacy! Withstanding its fine flavour is truly irresistible, is it not?" (when spawned)
**"Your prowess proves your immaculate taste of mine refections. Though, I shan't hold back no longer!" (during the second bullet hell)
**"Thou'st've slaint my pardner, the intellectual, charming Crabulon. How quaint! I must disappoint, for he returns from the ashes!" (when spawning [[Crabulon|Fireulon]])
**"Impossible! Thee checked at all of mineth owne attacks. Art thee verily the chosen who hath the might to intermit the temptation of mine meal?" (when at 1% health)
**"No, you shall never! Alas, my true final attack! Begone!" (after Permafrost blasters end)
*During the fight, Permafrosts projectiles insta-kill and a burning version of [[Crabulon|The Extraordinarily Buff and Feverishly Reverent Defender of Intergalactic Gourmet Partisanship, Crabulon]] is summoned instead of [[Pyrogen]].
*After defeating sacred Supreme Ultramage, Permafrost, the player is forced to consume Sacred Meat, essentially ending the fight.

== Trivia ==
*There has been a lot of drama surrounding the implementation of Sacred Meat. Since Exodus 20:4 highlights that "Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image", some heavily criticized the fact that Sacred Meat is a direct depiction of god and thus a heretic statement against christian faith.
**This drama sparked so much conflict that Fabsol decided to step down from his position as lead developer.
