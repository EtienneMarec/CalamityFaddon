The '''Belladonna Spirit Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a flying forest spirit minion. The spirit can pass through walls to reach its target, and rapidly throws a tight spread of three gravity-affected petals towards its targets, inflicting the [[Poisoned]] [[debuff]]. Every ninth attack, it additionally fires a large and even spread of five petals into the air.
