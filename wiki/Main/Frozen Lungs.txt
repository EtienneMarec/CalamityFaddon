'''Frozen Lungs''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted in [[Expert Mode]] by being submerged in [[water]] in the [[Snow biome|Snow]] or [[Ice biome]]s. It causes the player's [[tgc:Breath meter|breath]] to drain significantly faster than normal. The debuff will not be inflicted if the player has the [[Arctic Diving Gear]] or any of its upgrades equipped.

This debuff replaces the [[Chilled]] infliction that occurs when entering cold water in Expert Mode in the vanilla game, and the player can revert back to this feature via the [[Configuration options]].

== Notes ==
*Water breathing-themed items such as the [[Gills Potion]] and the [[Aquatic Heart]] are still effective against this debuff.

== Trivia ==
*This debuff is a reference to the [https://banjokazooiewiki.com/wiki/Icy_water icy water] hazard from the game ''[[wikipedia:Banjo-Kazooie (video game)|Banjo-Kazooie]]''.
