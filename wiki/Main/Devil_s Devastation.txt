The '''Devil's Devastation''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It is the upgraded combination of both the [[Devastation]] and the [[True Forbidden Oathblade]]. When swung, the Devil's Devastation rapidly fires spreads of three purple demon scythes in the cursor's direction and launches three pitchforks from below towards the cursor's location. Critical hits additionally summon purple explosions on the target. Both the sword and its projectiles inflict the [[On Fire!]] and [[Shadowflame]] [[debuffs]].
