It looks like a large slime made out of mud and filled with stones and broken trees. It is summoned when the player uses the [[Muddy Gel]] in the Swamp, and a message appears in the chat before The Sludge arrives:

"''You hear the trees crack and the mud rumble."''
==The Fight==
===Phase One:===
In phase one, The Sludge jumps around like the King Slime, and occasionally spawn [[Swamp Slime|Swamp Slimes]] and [[Spiked Swamp Slime|Spiked Swamp Slimes]] while the tree on their head shoots leaves at you.

Phase Two starts once they reach 900 (1500 - Mod) {{Hp}}.
===Phase Two===
In phase two, sharp rocks and sticks spike out of the sludge, shooting mud, stones, sticks and chunks of trees, the ladder of which explode into leaves at the player. they gain both defense and offense, and can overpower most Pre-Hard Mode players. they will also jump much higher in this stage, and gain immunity to poison. Additionaly the birdperson on top of the tree will try to attack you, he will try to dash at you similarly to the angry sun from super mario bros 3 leaving behind feathers that go in random directions.

==Aftermath==
* The [[Finch]] will begin to arrive if provided suitable housing.
* The [[Suspicious Looking Eye]] can be used. 
* The [[Blood Moon]] and [[Flood Moon]] can occur naturally.
