The '''Jaws of Oblivion''' are a craftable Godseeker Mode non-consumable [[dagger]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Leviathan Teeth]]. When used, it rapidly throws a tight spread of six gravity-ignoring reaper teeth that stick to enemies and inflict the [[Venom]], [[Marked]], and [[Armor Crunch]] [[debuff]] for 3 seconds. Up to 6 teeth can be stuck on an enemy at once, and the damage over time inflicted by the debuff stacks accordingly, up to 72 damage per second.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Jaws of Oblivion will cause the next set of teeth to create a large shockwave on enemy hits, which inflicts the [[Crush Depth]] debuff for 4 seconds.
