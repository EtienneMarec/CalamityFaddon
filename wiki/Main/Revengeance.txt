'''Revengeance''' is an item found within the [[Starter Bag]] in [[Expert Mode]]. When used in Expert Mode, it activates or disables [[Revengeance Mode]], increasing difficulty. Attempting to use it in Normal Mode or during a boss battle will have no effect.

It can be crafted at an [[Altar]] and does not have any ingredients.
