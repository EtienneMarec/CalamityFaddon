The '''Sand Shark Tooth Necklace''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from the [[Great Sand Shark]]. While equipped, all of the player's stats will increase by 10%, and their armor penetration is increased by 10. Additionally, a Sand Shark minion is summoned.
Uniquely among minions, this Sand Shark will follow the cursor when a summoning weapon is held, allowing the player to directly control it. 

Pressing the Shark Recall key will make the Sand Shark return to your side, even when a summoning weapon is held.
