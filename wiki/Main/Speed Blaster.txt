The '''Speed Blaster''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Ranged weapons|ranged weapon]] that acts as a direct upgrade to the vanilla [[Paintball Gun]]. It fires in rapid bursts of six paintballs, and like the Paintball Gun, it does not consume ammo, making it a very reliable weapon. Its high damage potential is somewhat offset by the gravity effect drop and relatively low projectile velocity.

== Notes ==
*The Calamity Mod allows the [[Painter]] to sell the Paintball Gun, allowing the player to obtain the weapon without having to kill the NPC.
*Having the cursor extremely close to the character will slightly slow down the velocity of the shots.

== Tips ==
*Though lacking any piercing ability, the extraordinary damage output makes the Speed Blaster very effective against individual enemies with very high health.
