The '''Chain Spear''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spears|spear]]. Left clicking with the weapon swings it like a normal spear, inflicting [[Abyssal Flames]] and [[Burning Blood]] on enemy hits. Right clicking causes the spear to launch outwards with a chain. IF the launched spear hits an enemy, the enemy is dragged into the player. If the player presses left click while a dragged enemy is within a 10 block radius of them, the spear is retracted, then immediately thrusted forward again, creating a bloody explosion and temporarily removing the enemy's contact damage.
