'''Rare Plantera Fruits''' are a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] dropped by [[Giant Plantera Bulb|Giant Plantera Bulbs]]. It can be used to craft the [[Planterror Staff]], and can be combined with [[Blood Orb|Blood Orbs]] and other materials to create any of the four [[Permanent Power-ups|Permanent Health Powerups]] from the mod.
