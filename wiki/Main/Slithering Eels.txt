The '''Slithering Eels''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that drops from [[Acid Eel]]s after defeating the [[Aquatic Scourge]]. It fires magical Acid Eels that roughly follow the cursor around, trailing acid droplets up to five times before it disappears. The eels can also freely move through blocks and pierce infinitely, with each enemy hit reducing the lifespan of the eel.
