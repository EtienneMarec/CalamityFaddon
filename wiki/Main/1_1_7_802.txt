The '''1.1.7.802''' update resprited the [[Ravager]] [[boss]]. It also added DPS caps to several Godseeker Mode bosses to lock the fights to last for a certain time, and rebalanced a few [[weapons]]. 

== Changes ==
*[[Barinade]], [[Ion Blaster]], and [[Meteor Fist]] no longer have knockback.
*[[Blade of Enmity]] now uses the {{rare|8}} rarity instead of {{rare|9}}.
*Buffed [[Conclave Crossfire]]'s damage from 34 to 36.
*Reduced the sheet spacing on [[Cryo Stone]]'s animation.
*Removed unused code from [[Darkecho Greatbow]] that would make it inflict [[Frostburn]] on melee hits.
*Buffed [[Devastation]]'s damage from 120 to 250.
*Buffed [[Devil's Devastation]]'s damage from 230 to 350.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]], [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]], [[Providence, the Profaned God]], and [[Supreme Calamitas]] now have DPS caps.
*Nerfed [[Elemental Axe]]'s damage from 250 to 225.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited Ravager.
