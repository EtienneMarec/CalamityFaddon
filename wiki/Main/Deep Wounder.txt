The '''Deep Wounder''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[rogue weapon]] that is sold by the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|60}} after [[Calamitas]] has been defeated. It automatically throws a fast spinning hatchet that can pierce infinitely, damaging all enemies that it comes into contact with, inflicting [[Armor Crunch]] and [[Marked]] for 2 seconds.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Deep Wounder will cause the next hatchet to travel at a much higher velocity and leave a trail of water behind it, inflicting [[Crush Depth]] in addition to the other debuffs that it inflicts.

This weapon deals 500% damage to [[Amazon]] and [[Amazon Prime]].
