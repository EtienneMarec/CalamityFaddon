The '''Tesla Cannon''' is a Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]]. When fired, a large light-blue beam is fired that creates a large dust explosion when contacting a block or enemy. The beam will arc towards other nearby enemies once contacting an enemy.

The weapon consumes 0.9 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 250.
