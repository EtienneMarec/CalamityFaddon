The '''Winter's Fury''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{gc|80}} after defeating all three [[minibosses]] in the [[Frost Moon]] [[event]]. It fires a stream of ice orbs along with a barrage of ice shards towards the player's cursor. These projectiles have a long range, are affected by gravity, and inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] upon hitting an enemy.
