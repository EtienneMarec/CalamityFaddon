The Calamity Mod adds new drops and mechanics to existing vanilla invasions, as well as new unique invasions.

==Blood Moon==
[[File:Blood Relic.png|right|link=Blood Relic]]
[[tgc:Blood Moon|Blood Moon]]s can be summoned using the [[Blood Relic]]. All enemies during blood moons have a chance to drop [[Blood Orb]]s, which are used as an alternative method to craft all vanilla [[potion]]s as well as most of Calamity's. A blood moon must be active in order for the [[Drunk Princess]] to sell the [[Bloody Mary]] and for it to grant its beneficial effects. Killing the [[Lunatic Cultist]] during this invasion causes it to drop a unique [[Lore|Lore item]].

==Acid Rain==
[[File:Caustic Tear.png|alt=|right]]
{{dablink|Main article: [[Acid Rain]]}}
The '''Acid Rain''' is a weather invasion that has a 1/360000 chance to start every tick (1/6000 every second) after the [[Acidsighter]] has been defeated.

Throughout the duration of the invasion, all players and enemies in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] during the Rain will be inflicted with the [[Irradiated]] debuff. Numerous new enemies will spawn in the biome at different points of progression, with the invasions enemies receiving increases to their stats to make them more suited for each point in progression. The first tier of the invasion is made available after the player has defeated the [[Acidsighter]], the Second Tier is made available after the [[Aquatic Scourge]] is defeated and the final tier is made available once the player defeats the [[Polterghast]].

[[File:Arid Artifact.png|right|link=Arid Artifact]]

The [[Mighty Wind]] debuff is disabled whenever a [[boss]] is alive. Several new drops have been added to the invasion, and [[Forbidden Fragment]]s are included in multiple new recipes as well. Furthermore, a new [[mini boss]], the [[Great Sand Shark]], can be summoned using the [[Sandstorm's Core]] after [[Plantera]] has been defeated.

==Old One's Army==
[[File:Eternia Crystal.png|right|link=tgc:Eternia Crystal]]

==Mechanical Horde {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}==
[[File:Draedon's Remote.png|right|link=Draedon's Remote]]
{{dablink|Main article: [[Mechanical Horde]]}}

The Mechanical Horde is an invasion activated by using [[Draedon's Remote]] while inside [[The Exosphere]]. During the invasion, a number of greatly buffed robotic enemies and bosses will spawn in 9 waves, with a 10th and final wave involving a fight against [[Mechdusa]]. Completing this invasion enables the player to fight the [[The Fabricational Quartet]] and [[XP-00 Hypnos]].
