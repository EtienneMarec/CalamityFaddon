The '''1.1.7.853''' update reduced dusts from several of the mod's [[bosses]], buffed [[Supreme Calamitas]]' [[Soul Seeker]]s, and added the [[Spyker]].

== Additions ==
*Added Spyker.

== Changes ==
*Most projectiles shot by [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], Supreme Calamitas, and Supreme Calamitas' brothers no longer spawn dust.
*Buffed Supreme Calamitas' Soul Seekers' health from {{dv|25,000|27,500}} to {{dv|100,000|110,000}}, and their contact damage from {{dv|100|110}} to {{dv|200|220}}.
*Supreme Calamitas' Soul Seekers now produce less dust and inflict [[Vulnerability Hex]] for 10 seconds.
