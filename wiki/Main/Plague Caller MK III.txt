The '''Plague Caller MK III''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It summons a Plague Emperor, who hovers above the player and idly summons projectiles when enemies are nearby.
Projectiles include:
*Hordes of homing plague seekers and plague bees that deal 10% of the weapon's damage.
*A burst of plague missiles and mines that seek enemies and explode.
*Summoning mini plaguebringers that dash into enemies.
*Firing plague stingers from its abdomen.
*A plague deathray from its chest that moves in a sweeping motion.
*Throwing [[Flask of Plague]]s which infect nearby tiles with plague.
