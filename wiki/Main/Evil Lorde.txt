== EVIL LORDE] ==
Evil Lorde ('''邪悪なロード in japanesse) is a superboss from godseeker mode, he's intended to be fought after all of the bosses (superbosses included) and even the Lorde. He's an alternative version of the Lorde after they called him cringe and easy, he's very angry, may you have luck.'''

== Boss fight ==
The bossfight is similar to the Lorde's, but he has 666.666.666.666.666 health, the health doesn't change between dificulties and always stays the same no matter what. All of his attacks are instakills, dealing infinite damage and ignoring all equipment, it's recomended to use a glass cannon build, much like daytime empress of light.

== AI changes                                                                                                                                                                     ==
The most hard part of the Evil Lorde and the Lorde's bossfight is the sudden change of AI's, but Evil lorde has some changes;

the AI's can be from any mod ever made in Terraria, this means that you must memorize a great amount of bossfights from all of the workshop, even with these precautions, the bossfight is still the hardest by a wide margin.

=== The Lorde AI ===
There's a secret AI in the game for the Lorde, it was scrapped because it was impossible. The AI consists from combining all of calamity's AI's and from some other mods (fortunatelly not all) into one, the result is all dificulties combined at the same time, shooting each one all of their projectiles and more, this includes desperation phases, like the one of Supreme Calamitas in Infernum. it's recomended to run away, but if you head way too far, Evil Lorde will teleport on top of you and 7 page muda's you no matter what. Because of this, it's recomended to stay within the zone and try to dodge.

== Interactions ==
Some interactions occur during the bossfight, some of them are decorative, and some of them are a gamechanger.

1-The nurse: If you try to heal with the nurse, a cutscene will play in wich the Evil Lorde pushes you and instead heals him. This brings him to max HP once again.

2-Background: The sky will turn red as if a presence was brewing, all enemies will run, including critters.

3-Rod of Discord and Rod of Harmony: If you use any of the listed items, Evil Lorde inmediatelly 7 page muda's you, along with a hour long death loop.

4-Calamity music addon: If you have the calamity music addon on, you will hear the song "decrease your electronic device's aural output" made by terry muse, here's a link for you to hear it: <nowiki>https://youtu.be/7KiIWC14vu0</nowiki>

== After the fight ==
When you deplete his health, he will say "YuH" and he will start spinning. After the cutscene he will transform into a smaller version of himself, this version is a NPC named "Lordy" he's given up on killing edgy tyrants after killing most of them. He talks a lot to the player and sometimes breaks the fourth wall. He sellls the listed:
[[File:LORDY.png|alt=(autistic screeching)|thumb|155x155px|The Lordy is a NPC, he's Evil Lorde after defeat. He's very calm and offers to sell things to the player for free]]
-Lorde Armor : This armor makes you into a smaller Evil Lorde, you can change between AI, you have the same Hp as the Evil Lorde, etc. Price: 0 copper coins. (to see more enter the link)

-Every item: He sells all items in the game, including some from other mods.
