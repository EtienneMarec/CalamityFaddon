The '''Holy Wrath Potion''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] made from [[Unholy Essence]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Holy Wrath''' [[buff]], boosting the player's damage by 12%. In addition to this, all weapons will inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]]. The player will also become immune to the [[Wrath]] buff. It can be considered the upgrade to the vanilla Wrath Potion.
