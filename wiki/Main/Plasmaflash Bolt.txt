The '''Plasmalash Bolt''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that is an upgrade of both [[Thunder Bolt]] and [[Aero Bolt]]. It fires a fast-moving ball of flash-freezing magma that can be autofired. The projectile travels in a straight line, emits a bit of light, is unaffected by gravity, can pierce through 10 enemies but cannot pierce. It inflicts the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]] on hit [[enemies]].
