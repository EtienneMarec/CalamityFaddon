The '''Jet Engine''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a large floating fist. When a target is nearby, the fist charges into its target and rebounds upon hitting. If the target is over 75 tiles away, the fists teleports towards its target.
