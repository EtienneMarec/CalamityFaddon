The '''Profaned Core Fragment''' is a Post [[Moon Lord|Moon Lord]] Consumable dropped directly by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. Using it will cause the fragment to fly into the air, glowing brightly, before summoning the [[Profaned Birb]] and allowing her to move in as a Town Pet.

Only one per world can be used and cannot be used before Providence is defeated.


==Trivia==
*The Profaned Core Fragment is a piece of Providence's core, similar to her trophy.
*If the Profaned Birb is spawned in via other means before using this item, then she will not be able to respawn until this item has been used.
