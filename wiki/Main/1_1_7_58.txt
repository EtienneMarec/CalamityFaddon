The '''1.1.7.58''' update made some balance changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]], along with a few other miscellaneous changes to his fight. It also resprited the [[Shadowspec Bar]].

== Changes ==
*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Buffed head's contact damage from 325 to 330.
**Nerfed body's contact damage from {{dv|250|325}} to {{dv|220|286}}.
**Nerfed Laser Portal damage from {{dv|200|260}} to {{dv|140|180}}, its Nebula Shot's damage from {{dv|160|260}} to {{dv|100|140}}, its bolt damage from {{dv|220|300}} to {{dv|180|240}}, its star damage from {{dv|180|280}} to {{dv|120|180}}, and its orb damage from {{dv|200|260}} to {{dv|120|160}}. Buffed its Gravity Bomb damage from 180 to 200.
**Nebula Shots now last for 2.5 seconds instead of 3.
**Tail now loses its damage taken multiplier at 25% health instead of 15%.
**Decreased tail's damage taken multiplier from 4x to 2x and its multiplier when under 50% health from 2x to 1.5x.
**Now forces all piercing projectiles except for minions to pierce once.
**Increased overall speed.
**Decreased projectile fire rate.
**Added a period to the end of its "Nothing personal, kid" status message.
*Resprited [[Shadowspec Bar]].
