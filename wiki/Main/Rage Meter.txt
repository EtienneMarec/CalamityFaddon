The '''Rage Meter''' is a mechanic in [[Revengeance Mode]], along with the [[Adrenaline Meter]] (and [[Stamina Meter]] {{fantag|Catalyst}}), that is unlocked by using the [[Metabolic Converter]]. Once the meter has filled up completely, the '''Rage Mode''' [[buff]] can be activated by pressing the {{key|Rage Mode}} key. The Rage Meter has a minimum value of 0 and a maximum value of 100.

== Gaining Rage ==
The Rage Meter is charged via proximity to enemies. The closer the player is to an enemy, the faster the Rage Meter will charge. [[Bosses]] triple the rate the player fills the Rage Meter. Only the heads of segmented enemies will count towards generating the Rage Meter.
*The rate that the Rage Meter charges is linear and scales with how close the player is to the nearest enemy. The Rage Meter will charge fastest while the player is within 10 Blocks of an enemy. The slowest the Rage Meter will charge is if the player is 50 Blocks away from an enemy.
*The [[Heart of Darkness]] will constantly generate 1% of the total Rage Meter every second while equipped.
*[[Gael's Greatsword]] will constantly generate 3% of the total Rage Meter every second while held.

== Losing Rage ==
The Rage Meter can empty over time. This happens when the player is considered "Out of Combat", which means they are not near any enemies nor have they dealt any damage to enemies for 10 seconds.
*The Rage Meter will empty at a rate of 3.33%/per second. It takes 30 seconds for the Rage Meter to completely empty from full.
*The Heart of Darkness and the [[Shattered Community]] prevent the Rage Meter from emptying over time while either is equipped.
*Switching off of Gael's Greatsword will cause the Rage Meter to reset to 0.

== Rage Mode ==
When the '''Rage Mode''' buff is activated, the player receives a damage boost of 35%. The player can boost it to 85% by using these [[Permanent Power-ups]]:
*{{item|Ha-pu Fruit}}, attained by shimmering the 3 Rage mode power-ups dropped by bosses.
*{{item|Potato Cat}}, crafted using various materials.

This total damage boost is multiplicative and applied after other damage boosts such as those from [[Potions]] or [[coverings]]. Rage Mode stacks additively with [[Adrenaline Mode]] for a total damage boost of 235%. This can further be increased up to 445% with the six Adrenaline Mode permanent power-ups and the 2 rage mode damage increasing power-ups.

The player can also increase the duration of Rage Mode by 1 second three times for a total duration of 20 seconds by using these [[Permanent Power-ups|permanent power-up]] items either dropped by defeated bosses or crafted:
*{{item|Mushroom Plasma Root}}, dropped by [[Crabulon]].
*{{item|Infernal Blood}}, dropped by [[Ravager]].
*{{item|Red Lightning Container}}, dropped by [[The Dragonfolly]].
*{{item|Rage of the Cosmos}}, crafted using the items above.
*{{item|Sunwitch}}, crafted using the item above and the other combined ripper power-ups.
*{{item|YashBrowns}}, crafted using various materials.

== Notes ==
*Once the Rage Meter reaches full capacity, a unique sound effect plays to indicate it.
*Activating Rage Mode while holding Gael's Greatsword will fire a barrage of skulls. This effect replaces the Rage Mode buff.
*Players can move the Rage Meter around by clicking and dragging it. This can be disabled in Configuration options.
*The damage bonus from Rage is cut in half if the player is wielding a [[True melee]] weapon.

== Trivia ==
*The Rage Meter was previously generated by taking damage or by dealing damage with [[True melee]] weapons.
*Rage and Adrenaline are a reference to [https://metalgear.fandom.com/wiki/Ripper_Mode Ripper Mode] from ''[[wikipedia:Metal_Gear_Rising:_Revengeance|Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance]]''. Because of this, Rage and Adrenaline are often collectively referred to as "Rippers" by the community.
*Rage and Adrenaline used to have boosted damage increases while in [[Death Mode]].
*Rage and Adrenaline are replacements for a previous mechanic called [[Stress]].
