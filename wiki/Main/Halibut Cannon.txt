The '''Halibut Cannon''' is a craftable Epilogue [[gun]]. It fires out between 25 and 35 bullets per shot that slowly spread out, while only consuming one unit of [[ammo]].

== Notes ==
*When using [[Acid Round]]s or [[Ichor Bullet]]s as ammo, its damage is reduced by 15%.
*With the exception of [[God Slayer Slug]]s and [[Luminite Bullet]]s, piercing bullets are not effective when using the Halibut Cannon as only one bullet will strike per salvo.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is a reference to the late game critic [[wikipedia:TotalBiscuit|TotalBiscuit]], previously known as TotalHalibut, as indicated by the presence of stereotypical British attire on the weapon's sprite.
*[[wikipedia:Halibut|Halibut]] is a typical name of fish from the family of right eyed flounders.
*Prior to update [[1.5.0.001]], this weapon could be obtained from any Abyss enemy with a {{revengeance|0.001%}} chance; from Abyss [[mini boss]]es with a {{revengeance|0.1%}} chance; and from the adult Eidolon Wyrm with a {{revengeance|100%}} chance.
*the weapon's tool tip seems to be the weapon itself talking to the player of its glory.
