The '''Triumph Potion''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Triumph''' [[buff]], which reduces enemy contact damage by a percentage equal to the percentage of the enemy's missing health multiplied by 0.15. (For example, an enemy with 30% health remaining would deal 10.5% reduced contact damage.)
