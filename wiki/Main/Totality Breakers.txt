The '''Totality Breakers''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]] that hurls explosive flasks. On impact with tiles or enemies, the flasks create shortly lingering explosions and shoot out three explosive tar projectiles that each release lingering flames. The tar projectiles inflict the [[Oiled]] and [[On Fire!]] debuffs onto enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Totality Breakers will cause the next flask thrown to release tar projectiles as it travels.
