'''God Slayer Cooldown''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted onto the player for 35 seconds after using the God Slayer dash effect from the [[God Slayer armor]] or [[Auric Tesla armor]]. While the debuff is active, the usage of the God Slayer dash is disabled.
