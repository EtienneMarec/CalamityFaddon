'''Seeking Mechanisms''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] items. They are used to locate different kinds of [[Bio-center Lab]]s, with each color corresponding to a specific lab type. When used, it flies into the air for a brief moment then accelerate towards the lab, trailing dusts of matching color.

These items have 100 max [[charge]] and consume 15 charge per use.
