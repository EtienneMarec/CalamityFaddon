The '''Acidic Rain Barrel''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]] that is an upgrade to the [[Blast Barrel]]. It throws a barrel that rolls across the ground that pierces through all enemies it comes into contact with until it either despawns or hits a wall. Hitting a wall causes the barrel to erupt into shrapnel and acidic raindrops.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Acidic Rain Barrel causes the resulting barrel to be able to bounce off walls up to a total of three times, and also causes more shrapnels and raindrops to erupt.
