'''Statigel armor''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Covering]] set made from [[Purified Gel]] and [[Aerialite Bar]]s, and is a direct upgrade to [[Gel armor]]. It requires 19 Purified Gel and 33 Aerialite Bars (33 [[Obsidian]] and 99 [[Hellstone|Aerialite]]) to make the whole set or 39 Purified Gel and 69 Aerialite Bars (69 Obsidian and 207 Aerialite) for a set with all five headpieces.

It consists of '''Statigel Armor''', '''Statigel Greaves''' and five different headpieces: '''Statigel Helm''', '''Statigel Headgear''', '''Statigel Cap''',  '''Statigel Hood''' and '''Statigel Mask'''.

The '''Statigel Armor''' gives 10 defense and +5% increased critical strike chance; and the '''Statigel Greaves''' give 8 defense, 5% increased damage, and 5% increased movement speed.

*The '''Statigel Helm''' gives 9 defense, 10% increased melee damage, 7% increased melee critical strike chance, and 10% increased melee speed.<br/>Its set bonus grants an additional 400 [[Aggro]].
*The '''Statigel Headgear''' gives 7 defense, 10% increased ranged damage, and 7% increased ranged critical strike chance.
*The '''Statigel Cap''' gives 5 defense, 10% increased magic damage, 7% increased magic critical strike chance, 10% decreased mana cost, and +30 max mana.
*The '''Statigel Hood''' gives 4 defense, increased minion knockback<br/>Its set bonus gives an additional 18% minion damage and +1 max minion, and summons a Baby Slime God that will attack enemies.
**The Baby Slime God moves and attacks like any other slime minion, dealing 26 damage. The color of the minion depends on the world's evil biome.
*The '''Statigel Mask''' gives 6 defense, 10% increased Spy damage, 7% increased Spy critical strike chance, 5% increased movement speed and 34% chance to not consume thrown items.<br/>Its set bonus gives a maximum [[Spy stealth]] of 100.

Equipping a full set will always increase the player's jump height by 5 blocks and jump speed by 30%, along with adding an extra jump which emits blue gel-like particles below the player when performed. Additionally, taking over 100 damage in one hit grants the player 30 extra [[immunity frames]].

A full set will always provide a total of at least 22 defense, 5% increased damage, critical strike chance, and movement speed.

== Tips ==
*By using the Statigel Mask with the breastplate and greaves from the [[Fossil armor]], the player gains Spy damage and critical chance boosts similar to the [[Empyrean armor]]. The mixed set gives 30% damage versus 32% from Empyrean, and 22% critical chance versus 23% from Empyrean. However, this mixed set abandons Spy [[Stealth]], defense, and the set bonuses of the Statigel armor to accomplish this.
