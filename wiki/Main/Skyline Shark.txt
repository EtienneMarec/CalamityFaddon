The '''Skyline Shark''' is a post-[[Umbrus]] [[Enemies|enemy]] that spawns in the Upper Layer of the [[Sky Abyss]]. It prioritises attacking small fish over the player. When attacking it dashes 3 times and inflicts the [[Evercold]] debuff, if it gets 5 tiles close to any Lower Sky Abyss enemy it gets the Shark Alert debuff that makes other enemies either run away or attack it.

In Revengeance mode it can also grab its enemies using its hands.

If it sees a [[Baby Skyline Shark]] it starts rapidly dashing at anyone near it.
