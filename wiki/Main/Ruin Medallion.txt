The '''Ruin Medallion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[covering]]. When equipped, [[stealth strike]]s only require and consume half of the player's maximum stealth to perform. It also grants the player a 6% increase to Spy damage and critical strike chance.
