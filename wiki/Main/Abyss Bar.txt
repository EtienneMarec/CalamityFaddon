'''Abyss Bars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[bars]] that are made with 5 [[Abyss Ore]] at a [[Hardmode Forge]] and are additionally dropped from the [[Fleshy Geode|Fleshy]] and [[Necromantic Geode]]. They are used to craft various abyss-themed items, as well as being used to craft [[Life Alloy|Life Alloys]].
