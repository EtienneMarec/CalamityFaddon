'''The Transformer''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from [[Shockstorm Shuttle]]s. Sparks are released when the player is damaged, dealing 8 damage. Additionally, it gives immunity to the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]], causes enemy bullets to be reflected back at the enemy for 800% of their original damage, and reduces damage from electrical projectiles and enemies and enemy bullets by 50%.

== Tips ==
*This covering is great for the post-[[Plantera]] or Godseeker Mode [[Dungeon]] for the resistance it gives to the projectiles of [[Skeleton Sniper]]s and [[Tactical Skeleton]]s as well as often killing them in the process with the reflected bullets.
*This covering also holds use in the [[Martian Invasion]] with many of the enemies dealing reduced damage.

== Trivia ==
*This covering is a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/The_Transformer Transformer] and [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Antagonist Antagonist] shields from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Amidias' Spark]].
