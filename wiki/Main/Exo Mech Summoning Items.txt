== Notes ==
*Fighting specific bosses in The Fabricational Quartet can be a much quicker way to obtain specific drops, compared to fighting all three at once. 
**Because of this, and because of the fact that XP-00 Hypnos will only spawn on its own anyways, it is usually preferred to fight it using its original summoning method.

== Trivia ==
*The Crystal Skull is likely a reference to [https://indianajones.fandom.com/wiki/Crystal_Skull_of_Akator an artifact of the same name] from the [[wikipedia:Indiana_Jones|Indiana Jones]] series.
*A [[wikipedia:Seismometer|seismometer]] is a real world device used to measure the magnitude of earthquakes.
*The Jet Rec Radio is a reference to game series [[wikipedia:Jet_Set_Radio|Jet Set Radio]].
*Neural Activation Flare might be a reference to the "[https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/neuron-activation Neuron Activation]" meme.
*Stellar Locus is based on the [[Brimstone Locus]].
*Shady Gazing Skull's crafting recipe is based on the Suspicious Looking Skull's crafting recipe.
