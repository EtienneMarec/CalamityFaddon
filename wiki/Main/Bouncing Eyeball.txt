The '''Bouncing Eyeball''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy weapon]] that drops from [[Drippler]]s. When used, it throws a bouncing eyeball that pierces 3 enemies, bounces 8 times, or lasts 5 seconds before dissipating. Its critical chance is doubled during a [[Blood Moon]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Bouncing Eyeball will cause the eyeball thrown to pierce and bounce an unlimited number of times within 7 seconds, makes it travel much faster and unaffected by gravity, and makes it bounce almost directly towards the direction it came from, gently reaching a 45 degree angle, no matter the angle at which it strikes a block. It can damage a single enemy up to 12 times per second.

{{Modifier|Spy}}
== Notes ==
*This weapon benefits greatly from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].

== Trivia ==
*On performing a stealth strike, the eyeball will have teeth, similar to the [[Eye of Cthulhu]]'s second form.

{{-}}

* The high damage and use time of this weapon, as well as the critical chance and bouncing/piercing capabilities, makes it viable up until the [[The Slime God|Slime God]].
