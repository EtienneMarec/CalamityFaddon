The '''Star Struck Water''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] consumable [[classless weapon]] that acts as the [[Astral Infection]] variant of the [[Thrown Water]]. When thrown, it converts a small, square-shaped area with a radius of five blocks from the center into the Astral Infection. Directly striking an enemy also deals a small amount of typeless damage.
