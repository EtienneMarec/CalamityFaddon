The '''Pestilent Slime''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[slime]] that spawns in the [[Jungle]] after [[Golem]] has been defeated. It attacks by jumping towards the player and shooting high-velocity plague darts whenever they get too close to it or travel above it.

The Pestilent Slime is one of the required slimes to summon [[Astrum Aureus]].

== Trivia ==
* The Pestilent Slime can be considered a [[Spiked Jungle Slime]] that was infected by the [[Plague]].
