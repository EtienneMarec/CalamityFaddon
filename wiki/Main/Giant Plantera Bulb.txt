'''Giant Plantera Bulbs''' are background objects found in the Jungle. These bulbs are initially green and indestructible in Pre-Hardmode, blooming and turning pink once the [[Wall of Flesh]] is defeated, and turning a golden yellow upon defeating the [[Moon Lord]]. It can be destroyed using a [[Pickaxes|Pickaxe]] or [[Explosive|Explosives]]. When broken while pink, it spawns Plantera alongside 10 Spores and 30 harmless spore clouds. If destroyed while gold, it will drop 10 [[Life Fruit|Life Fruits]] as well as a [[Rare Plantera Fruit]].

== Notes ==
*Giant Plantera Bulbs are finite, and will not respawn after being destroyed, regardless of what growth stage they were broken in.
*The Giant Plantera Bulb's map color will change to #F352AB ● in hardmode, and #F7B538 in godseeker mode.
