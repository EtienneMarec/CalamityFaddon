The '''Infernal Blood''' is a [[permanent power-up]] [[consumable]] item obtained from the [[Treasure Bag]] dropped from [[Ravager]]. It increases the [[Rage Mode]] duration by one second. There are 2 other items that function identically to this; the [[Mushroom Plasma Root]] and the [[Red Lightning Container]]. It can only be used once.
