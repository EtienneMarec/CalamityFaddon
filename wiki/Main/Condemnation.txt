'''Condemnation''' is a Godseeker Mode [[repeater]] that drops from [[Supreme Calamitas]].  When used with its primary fire (left-click), the player holds the crossbow and charges up, increasing the number of arrows which is also indicated by a ring of dust. The player can load up to a maximum of 8 arrows and stop holding to release arrows with a 50% velocity boost. Each arrow spawns two smaller homing arrows every 0.75 seconds for a total of 3 times. Homing arrows deal half as much damage and cannot travel through solid tiles unlike the main arrow. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), the arrows are fired out normally.
