The Void Hydra (Japanese: ヴォイド・ヒドラ Vu~oido hidora) is a [[Master Template Items/Godseeker Mode NPCs|Godseeker-mode]] Boss that is fought in the Abyss anytime, It is intended to be fought before the Adult Eidolon Wyrm. It is recommended to wear coverings that increase your performance in the Abyss, such as the Abyssal Diving Suit and Fishron Wings, as well as mining out most of the Abyss, since the boss will travel deeper in the Abyss the lower its HP is. The Void Hydra inflicts Evercold if the player touches the Void Hydra. All immunities that the player has will be deactivated during the fight (Except Evercold)  

== Spawn ==
The Void Hydra will spawn in the second layer in the Abyss and will teleport deeper the less hp it has. It requires the player to use the Hydra Scale in the second layer in the abyss. Once spawned the hydra will roar sending echos throughout the abyss and inflicts the player with [[Debuffs|Fish Alert]] Debuff For 5 Seconds. The Void Hydra will despawn if the player leaves the abyss. and becomes invulnerable if the player is 200 blocks away from it.

== Phase 1 ==
3 Hydra Heads will emerge from the Void Hydra that share the same hp as the boss, Each head can preform a different attack at random every 4 seconds but can repeat the same attack more than once.

The Void Hydra preforms the following attacks in A random order:

* Shoots Venom Bolts that travel through blocks and Inflicts Acid Venom for 5 seconds
* Shoots Poison Bolts toward the player that can travel through blocks and inflicts Poisoned for 10 seconds
* Shoots Ink B lobs that either inflicts the player with Darkness for 30 seconds.
* Shoots Sulfuric Clouds that expand outwards and disappears shortly after and inflict Sulphuric Poisoning for 10 seconds
* Shoots Sulfuric Spores that slowly move toward the player and inflict Sulphuric Poisoning For 5 Seconds
* Shoots gravity affected Sludge Balls Toward the player and inflicts Oozed for 5 seconds
* Lunges Its head and attempts to bite the player. (Can only be done 3 times in this phase)

== Phase 2 (75%) ==
The Void Hydra will teleport somewhere near the center of the 3rd layer of The Abyss at 75% HP and become invulnerable. The player must come in range (200 blocks) of The Void Hydra in 25 seconds or the boss will despawn.

The Void Hydra will preform the following attacks in A random order:

* Shoots Volcanic Fireballs toward the player and inflicts On Fire! for 60 seconds.
* Shoots Giant Ink Blobs and inflicts Blackout for 30 seconds.
* Shoots moonblasts that explode and sends projectiles in the opposite direction to the direction it was fired and inflict Nightwither for 10 seconds 
* Shoots Crushblasts that inflicts Crush Depth, Withered Armor, Or Withered Weapon For 10 seconds
* Charges toward the player and leaves Riptide Vortex's Behind that inflict riptide for 60 seconds (This can be done every 20 seconds
* Lunges its head and attempts to bite the player (Can only be done 5 time in this phase)

== Phase 3 (30%) ==
Will teleport to the bottom of the abyss at 30% HP and become invulnerable until the player is in range of the Void Hydra while it shoots abyssal bolts upward until the player hits it. The player has 20 seconds to do this or it will despawn. Abyss Mines will occasionally spawn and explode after 15 seconds if the player does not drop them in time.

The Void Hydra will jump above the player and slamming the ground every 7.5 seconds and sometimes dashes toward the player.
