'''Auric Bars''' are Godseeker Mode [[bars]] that are made with [[Auric Ore]] and [[Yharon Soul Fragment]]s or [[Quark-Gluon Sludge]] {{dei}}. They are used in creating some of the most powerful items the ''Calamity Mod'' has to offer, as well as [[Shadowspec Bar]]s, which are used in the crafting of developer-tier items.

Crafting all items (excluding all items crafted from Shadowspec bars which have Auric Bars in its recipe) requires 180 Auric Bars (2160 Auric Ore and 36 Yharon Soul Fragments; requires defeating [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] {{dv|2–3|2}} times).
