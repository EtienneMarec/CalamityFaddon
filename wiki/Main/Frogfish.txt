The '''Frogfish''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Ocean]]. It stays invisible on the sand, waiting for the player to come near or hit it to attack them.

== Trivia ==
*Just like their [[wikipedia:Frogfish|real-life counterparts]], Frogfish camouflage into the ocean floor and wait for prey until they lunge and attack.
