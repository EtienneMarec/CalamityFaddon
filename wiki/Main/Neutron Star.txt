The '''Neutron Star''' is a Godseeker Mode [[classless weapon]] that drops from [[Exodygen]]. It can cycle through 5 different attack styles when right click. The first attack fires an orb that goes into a straight line to then deaccelerate and create a massive explosion with massive amounts of damage. The second attack behaves similarly to the Last prism, It quickly charges up for half a second before forming a full beam that can go through tiles while having a slower turn rate then other prisms. Anything that touches the beam will spawn Exo Slashes. The third Attack will fire an orb that behaves like a boomerang, Anything it hits will pierce through and spawn multiple smaller orbs that spread out and return to the player. The fourth attack behaves similarly to attack 1 but instead of exploding, it turns into rotating beams that can randomly go clockwise or counter clockwise. The fifth attack is similar to attack 3 but instead rapidly shoots large beams at the mouse position.

== Notes ==
* Neutron Star's first attack can not be used again until the orb and explosion is no longer active.

== Trivia ==
* Neutron stars are extremely dense space objects that can be found all over different galaxies.
