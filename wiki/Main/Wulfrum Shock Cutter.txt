The '''Wulfrum Shock Cutter''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[dagger]] dropped by [[The Inventors]] and the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. When used with left-click, the player throws out a burst of three cutters in quick succession. The cutters are gravity-affected and cannot pierce. Holding right-click will bring back all flying Wulfrum Shock Cutters.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Wulfrum Shock Cutter causes the resulting cutters to glow up, travel at a higher speed, and damage an enemy twice on hit. Each cutter resulting from the stealth strike deals 80% damage.
