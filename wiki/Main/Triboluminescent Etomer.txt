The '''Triboluminescent Etomer''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[pet]] summoning item that summons an electric troublemaker to follow the player. The pet can change forms depending on the biome the player is in or any ongoing events.

== Trivia ==
*The pet is based directly on the [https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rotom_(Pok%C3%A9mon) Rotom] from ''[[wikipedia:Pokémon|Pokémon]]'', which has many different forms that possess various electrical appliances.
*The name of the item is in reference to the electrical phenomenon called [[wikipedia:Triboluminescence|Triboluminescence]].
*'Etomer' is the word 'remote' spelled backwards, mirroring how Rotom's name is 'motor' spelled backwards.
