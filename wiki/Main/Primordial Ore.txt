'''Primordial Ore''' is an Epilogue [[ore]] which generates within small planetoids towards the top of [[Caelus]] after defeating the [[Vision of the Tyrant]]. When mined, it can drop any ore in the mod except for [[Flahium Slag]] and [[Pitchblende]]. It requires at least a [[Heaven Piercer]] or [[World Breaker]] to mine. Mining the ore with a [[Braxaxe]] will drop it in its item form. The ore can also be dropped by [[Primordial Rainbow Slime]]s if they are killed with any weapon that is crafted with [[Miracle Matter]].
