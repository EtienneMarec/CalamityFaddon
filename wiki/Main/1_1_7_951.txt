The '''1.1.7.951''' update added 3 new [[coverings]], some changes to two of the mod's [[bosses]], and an oversight fix.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Ethereal Talisman]], [[Frost Barrier]], and [[Lead Core]].

== Changes ==
*[[Astrum Deus]] now shoots lasers faster if the player's life is lower.
*Astrum Deus' body and tail segments now internally count as bosses.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' body segments now take no damage from [[Life Drain]].
*The Devourer of Gods' body and tail segments now internally count as bosses.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed an oversight that made immortal and friendly NPCs affected by the post-Devourer of Gods seasonal moon stat boosts.
