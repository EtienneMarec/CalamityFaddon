The '''Stormjaw Staff''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that drops from [[Stormlion]]s. When used, it summons a miniature Stormlion minion. It runs on the ground and aggressively rams enemies back and forth, does not deal contact damage but creates damaging sparks in contact with enemies. Sparks linger for 2 seconds and partially ignore [[immunity frames]], in a similar manner to the [[Amidias' Spark]].
