The '''Cosmolight''' is a [https://calamitymod.fandom.com/wiki/Hardmode Hardmode] tool. Upon use, it instantly changes the time to Dawn, Noon, Dusk and Midnight. One at a time

== Notes ==
*It cannot be used when any [[boss]] is alive.
*It can be used to trigger [[events]] like the [[Solar Eclipse]], or [[Blood Moon]].
*It cannot be used to reset the cooldown of the [[Enchanted Sundial]].

== Tips ==
*The Cosmolight can be used to cycle through [[moon phase]]s.
*It can also be used for easier cultivation of [[Daybloom]], [[Moonglow]], and [[Deathweed]].
*It can also be used to respawn the cultists at the dungeon 's entrance.
