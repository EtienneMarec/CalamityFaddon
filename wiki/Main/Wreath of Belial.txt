The '''Wreath of Belial''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]] sold by [[The General]]. It fires a wreath that continuously grows in size as it travels until it touches a block or has lasted for 10 seconds, after which it will explode into 6-8 icicle shards. 

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Wreath of Belial will cause it to turn black and suck in nearby enemies, never to escape until the wreath explodes.
