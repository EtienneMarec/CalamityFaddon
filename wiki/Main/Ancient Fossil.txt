The '''Ancient Fossil''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]]. While equipped, it increases the wearer's pickaxe speed by 15% while in the [[Underground]] or [[Cavern]] layer.  This speed increase applies when the player is at least below surface level, but they must also be above [[The Underworld]].
