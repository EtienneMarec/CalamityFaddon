The '''Brimstone Sword''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. It inflicts the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] and summons a small brimstone geyser when an [[enemy]] is hit with the blade. When using alternate fire, the sword is thrown in a manner similar to that of the [[Ichor Spear]]. The thrown sword can travel a long distance before being affected by gravity and pierces infinitely. On hitting an enemy, it will create a short lingering explosion that deals 50% of the weapon's damage.

== Notes ==
*Only the left-click attack receives [[true melee]] boosts.
*Due to the relative ease of crafting this sword, as well as how early it is obtainable, this weapon will remain a very powerful choice well into Hardmode.
*[[Aquatic Scourge]] takes only 10% damage from the Brimstone Sword's explosions. Additionally, the explosions' pierce is set to 2 once they hit one of its segments.

== Trivia ==
*This item was previously known as the "Profaned Sword". This was likely changed in order to prevent any kind of unwanted connections with [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]].
