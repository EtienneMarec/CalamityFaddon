The '''Wither Blossoms Staff''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that is an upgrade to the [[Tundra Flame Blossoms Staff]]. When used, it summons up to four dark-colored flowers that orbit around the player, and fire homing bolts at nearby targets. The bolts pierce once and travel through terrain, splitting into two plague seekers when destroyed. Both bolts and seekers inflict the [[Plague]] [[debuff]]. Each flower consumes half a minion slot.
