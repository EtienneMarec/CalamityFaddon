The '''1.1.7.852''' update made some changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]], and added new gores for a few of the mod's [[bosses]].

== Changes ==
*[[Desert Scourge]] and [[Providence, the Profaned God]] now have gores.
*The Devourer of Gods changes:
**Buffed body segment damage from {{dv|220|286}} to {{dv|275|440}}.
**Buffed Nebula Shot damage from {{dv|100|140}} to {{dv|110|180}}.
**Nerfed bolt damage from 180 to 150.
**Buffed star damage from {{dv|120|180}} to {{dv|130|220}}.
**Changed Gravity Bomb damage from {{dv|200|280}} to {{dv|180|300}}.
**Changed orb damage from {{dv|120|160}} to {{dv|110|180}}.
**Reduced dust created when hit or killed.
**[[Frostburn]] from body segments now lasts for 3.33 seconds instead of 1.67.
**Increased speed.
**Resprited gores.
