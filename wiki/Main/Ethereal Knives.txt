The '''Ethereal Knives''' are a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Magic weapons|magic weapon]] that are a direct upgrade to the [[Moonstone Knives]]. When used, the player throws out a stream of 8 knives in rapid succession, which each restore 3 mana on hit. Each knife also creates a damaging explosion on hit.
