The '''1.1.7.905''' update added the [[Astrageldon Slime]] boss, resprited most of the Meld set, and made a couple of balance changes.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Astrageldon Slime]] and [[Astral Slime]].
*Added [[Astral Chunk]].

== Changes ==
*Buffed [[Crystal Blade]]'s use time from 27 to 24.
*[[Elemental Lance]]'s beams now deal 30% of the previous beam's damage instead of 40%.
*Reimplemented [[Supreme Calamitas]]' brother spawning status message.
*Resprited [[Meld Blob]] and [[Meldiate Bar]].
*Resprited [[The Empyrean]], [[Gallant Pickaxe]], [[Tome of Fates]], [[Xeroc Greatsword]], and [[Xeroc Pitchfork]].
