'''Bouncing Betties''' are consumable [[Spy]] [[bombs]] sold by the [[Bandit]] for {{value|0|0|4}} after defeating any [[Mechanical Boss]]. They throw an explosive grenade that bounces off of enemies twice, exploding with each bounce. The grenade will bounce off of blocks for a short time after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Bouncing Betties will cause the next grenade thrown to release 12 projectiles on explosion. These projectiles will be a mix of damaging pieces of shrapnel, and sparks of flame that inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.
