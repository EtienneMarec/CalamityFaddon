The '''Exo Mine (Japanese: エクソマイン Ekusomain)''' is an [[enemy]] found in the [[Underground]] layer or towards the bottom of [[The Exosphere]]. It floats in place while firing {{item|Volatile Plasma Blast|s|link=|ext=gif|size=12px}} in a cross pattern, then rotating its turrets, firing again and repeating. After reaching 50% health, it supercharges, firing and rotating its turrets more quickly. After several seconds it glows white and explodes, destroying tiles in a 39,916,800 block radius.
