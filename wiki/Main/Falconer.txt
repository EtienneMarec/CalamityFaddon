The '''Falconer''' is a non-hostile [[Hardmode]] [[NPC]] that initially spawns in the [[Ocean]], by unearthing the [[Weathered Obelisk]] and waiting one day. She is different from [[Town NPCs]] in that she will not move into empty houses, and instead will spawn in various locations depending on the progression of her quests. She will use [[Mariana]] to defend herself, however if she is close to death she will disappear into smoke.

She functions similarly to the [[Angler]] in that she has her own quest system, with a fixed progression of target enemies. These enemies can be dealt with in different ways, however the reward from the Falconer will always be the same.
