The '''Pot of Pain''' is a optional [[subworld]] located in the [[Jungle Temple]], only accessible after the [[Fatal Circuit]] has been defeated. It is hidden behind a breakable wall at the beginning of the first buzzsaw room on the second floor, above the atrium. It can be entered anytime after defeating the Fatal Circuit, though the twin [[Golem]] fight at the end makes the challenge difficult before defeating the [[Moon Lord]]. This area is easily the most difficult platforming challenge in the entirety of the Calamity Mod, due to the fact that many types of movement that players may be used to, such as flight, have been disabled.

If players wish to leave the Pot of Pain, it is possible by simply walking back through the wall, by exiting the world and rejoining, or death.

==Gameplay==
The Pot of Pain is a long and brutally difficult platforming challenge. The area draws a few visual similarities with the Jungle Temple, however, it's appearance in the subworld is much more overgrown and lush. Spear Traps and Buzzsaws can be found everywhere, and Wooden Spikes blanket almost every walkable surface. However, to prevent tank builds from simply running through the area in disregard of the challenge, taking any hit from these Spikes, Buzzsaws, or Spear Traps will instead quickly darken the screen before teleporting the player back to a preset checkpoint. These usually are around [[Heart Statue|Heart Statues]] that can be found scattered throughout the map, which allow for players to regenerate their health whenever necessary.

While the majority of the Pot of Pain is focused primarily on platforming, this is not the case for the entire subworld. Towards the end, players drop through a shaft of spikes, and enters an arena where the player must emerge victorious against two significantly buffed [[Golem]] bosses. The arena cannot be escaped. Fortunately, in this arena, movement options are much less restricted and players are free to dodge almost however they see fit. Tile editing and teleportation, however, is still disabled. Unlike in the platforming segment of the subworld, taking a hit here will not teleport you back to a checkpoint, making the only escape either victory or death.

==Aftermath==
After the two Golems are defeated, the arena can be exited to the right.

After exiting the arena, players enter a room in which they can watch a special cutscene depicting [[Godseeker, Yharim|Godseeker Yharim]] and a younger version of [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon|Yharon, Dragon of Rebirth]]. After the cutscene, the player is rewarded with a [[tgc:Bestiary|Bestiary]] entry for Yharim, and following the defeat of the [[Exo Mechs]], a new dialogue option in the [[The Codebreaker|Codebreaker's]] Contact function. 

When the cutscene is finished, the player is teleported to the entrance of the Jungle Temple. The subworld '''cannot''' be entered again after it's completion.

==Tips==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating the Pot of Pain, including [[weapon]] and [[covering]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Pot of Pain strategies]].}}
*If you are having difficulty locating the entrance to the subworld, a [[Dangersense Potion]] can reveal the breakable wall, similar to how it reveals Cracked Dungeon Bricks.
** If you continue to have difficulty locating the entrance even after that, there may have been a bug with world generation. Please consult the Discord or Subreddit and '''provide members with a link to this wiki page''' for possible explanations and help.

*It's a necessity to equip some sort of dash covering, such as the [[Asgardian Aegis]].
*The Pot of Pain is also impossible without some sort of wall-clinging or climbing covering, such as the Climbing Claws or it's upgrades.
**The [[Master Ninja Gear]] grants both climbing ability and a dash, making it a very good covering for this challenge specifically.
*While flight is disabled, other methods of gaining vertical height, like the double jump gained from [[Cloud in a Bottle]] or the upright charge from the [[Plagued Fuel Pack]], are enabled and expected to be used. 
**This makes the [[MOAB]] an excellent covering for this challenge.
* While most likely already accounted for, double check that players have some form of fall damage protection, as some drops can and will be lethal, especially to those attempting before the Moon Lord's defeat.
*While the Golems at the end are not meant to be extraordinarily difficult, many players will also not have their combat coverings equipped, in favor of mobility-based coverings. Players should be ready to equip their combat coverings as they fall down the shaft or prepare for a fight without them.

==Notes==
* Items that allow teleportation, such as the [[tgc:Rod_of_Discord|Rod of Discord]], are disabled and unusable within the Pot of Pain. 
**If the player is in [[tgc:Journey_Mode|Journey Mode]], the teleportation ban is lifted, and teleporting instead doesn't allow the player to watch the final cutscene.   
***This means that no rewards are given from such a completion, as the rewards are locked behind the cutscene. However, the Pot of Pain still can be retried until the player completes it legitimately.
*Dying anywhere in the Pot of Pain will override normal death messages, instead displaying "<PlayerName> failed the challenge at hand." 
**This is similar to the death message when dying to a boss in the now-removed [[Armageddon]] difficulty.
*Other than the final two Golems, no enemies or bosses can spawn - any that do are instantly despawned.
*The Golems at the end are disabled until the player enters the arena. 
**If the player dies or leaves the area, they will reset back into their disabled state until the player enters the arena again. This also removes any damage the player may have dealt to them previously.
*The Golems are also affected by AI changes set by the current selected difficulty.  
**However, if the Infernum {{fantag|Calamity: Infernum}} addon is installed, the twin Golem's AI will not be updated accordingly due to bugs and extraordinary difficulty. It instead will use the [[Malice Mode]] AI.

==[https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Get_fixed_boi GetFixedBoi]==
If the player enters the Pot of Pain from a [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Get_fixed_boi GetFixedBoi] seed, like the main world, the subworld too will have significant gameplay alterations. Most notably, the player begins at the end of the subworld and must backtrack to the beginning in order to exit. This, however, raises a few noteworthy changes.
*The cutscene is played upon entering the subworld. However, this will not grant the Bestiary Entry, nor will it force players out of the subworld as it does normally.
** The Bestiary Entry is now granted upon leaving through the normal entry wall.  
***Leaving the subarea is no longer as simple as walking through this wall, as said wall is now the end of the subarea.
*The Golems must be immediately fought before moving on to the platforming segment of the challenge. 
**Due to the Arena being placed down a chasm far below the final platforming segment, players must have sufficient wing time or some other vertical mobility in order to reach such a towering height.  
***Wings, mounts, and other forms of mobility will be disabled as normal the immediate instant this chasm is left, so it is ill-advised to plan to rely on them.


Additionally:
*The Heart Statues and checkpoints around them are removed, making the only checkpoint in the entire challenge the spawn point.
**The Golems will not respawn if the player is teleported back to the spawn point however, though they will if the subworld is left.
*Sawblades move significantly faster.
*If players enter the subworld with the Catalyst {{fantag|Calamity: Catalyst}} addon enabled, 50 invincible [[Primal Aspid|Primal Aspids]] will spawn and will relentlessly pursue the player following the cutscene. 
**Taking a hit from these Primal Aspids will not teleport players to the subworld's spawn point - they merely exist to be annoying and distracting.
***As such, taking a hit from them also ignores any and all [https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Knockback knockback immunity].

Intriguing. I cannot even begin to comprehend your naming conventions. Such a name transcends normality and reason beyond comprehension. My only theorem is that there is perhaps some sort of work or fiction that you reference that I simply have failed to stumble upon in my years. Regardless, as none during my service under Yharim could settle on a name for it, I shall indulge in your awful alias, for simplicity’s sake.

This “Pot of Pain” was originally first discovered in the third month of 1826. Reports told of a hollow, radiant rift containing a brilliant, bright, and lush dungeon within, not unlike the Lihzahrd Temple that surrounds its entrance. Such reports sounded unfounded at best, and absurd to any individual of science. I had to investigate.

The rift itself was trivial to locate, especially due to sheer quantities of light it radiated. However, its nature within was frustrating to work with. Perhaps expectedly, simply redirecting a reconnaissance drone into the rift led it to instantly lose connection. Clearly, this implied that this realm was beyond the boundaries of our planet, if not reality itself- an idea only solidified by my incapability of locating it despite multiple surveys of this entire planet in the years following.

Curious, I outfitted another drone with an old-fashioned spool of wire and brought it back into the light. What I saw surprisingly confirmed the reports- a beautiful, overgrown, Lihzahrd Temple; a brilliant sun casting long shadows upon the chiseled brick floors and walls. The whines of buzzsaws and clink of retracting spear traps filled the air. It was a wondrous sight unparalleled by any other place on our planet. Quite unfortunately, those are all the details that drone could gather. As you could likely predict from your experiences, my drone inexplicably lost its capability of flight upon entry.

Not to be dismayed, my final idea was to use a more ground-based reconnaissance body, allowing me to collect the deserted drones, explore more of this realm, and even perhaps collect samples. However, my exploration was cut short, as quickly after entry, I was met with a cliffside you no doubt must remember. I gazed upon a distant fall into a pit of thorns, spikes, and vines. In the distance, they ascended into a towering summit that obstructed my vision and analysis of more of this world.

Disappointed, I opted to collect samples of the organic matter, mud, and bricks that laid most every surface. You must only imagine my frustration to learn that the matter within this realm infallibly resisted every attempt I made at collection or harvest- a mysterious property that you, again, must remember vividly. I begrudgingly decided to finally attempt to collect samples of the very air; in a desperate attempt to analyze and understand something about this rift.

Strangely, upon attempting to analyze the vial of aforementioned air back in my laboratory, I was once again met with disappointment, as it was left entirely devoid of content. While this may make sense when considering this realm’s flight incapability, the copious quantities of biological matter combined with the fact that mortals have survived within seem to suggest otherwise. Perhaps any matter that originates from within simply disappears when exiting this realm, as if it never existed at all.

Regardless, due to my very limited information, any theory about this “Pot of Pain<nowiki>''</nowiki> ends up largely being speculation, at best. Because of this realm’s supernatural and inexplicable natures, my best comparison for it ends up being the “dreams” of mortals. You of course are familiar with them- fictional realms that manifest in mortals’ rest that are unbound by explanation and the laws of our reality. Perhaps this rift is merely the physical manifestation of one of these dreams or thoughts, though the mechanics of how and by who is entirely beyond me.

As of recently, I’ve largely given up research on this rift. This is for many reasons. The Exo Mechs being my priority is perhaps a reason, but the most significant reason is that society has simply forgotten about it. Following my analysis, the entrance was sealed up, and any mention of it today is merely legend from very few veterans of the Crusade. You are the first to stumble upon it in many years, and to conquer it is an achievement heard from none.

But I must admit. While my research regarding the “Pot of Pain” has been mostly concluded,  it would simply be a lie to pretend that I were not even a bit curious. Tell me, what was it that you found upon reaching the realm’s terminus?

Nothing…? Just another entry inexplicably added to your Bestiary? Then the rift disappeared?

I see. As it is gone now, no more analysis can be or needs to be done.

I thank you for your contribution and closure.
