-->'''Dedicated Items''' and '''Dedicated NPCs''' are items or NPCs which have been made for, or simply attributed to, certain people for any reason. Some reasons include being a developer for the mod, donating to the developers, or completing organized challenges. Items that were made for Patreon Donators are labeled with the {{color|- Donor Item -|#8b0000}} ({{color|#8b0000 ●|#8b0000}}) tag in their tooltips. Items that were made for Developers of the mod are labeled with the {{color|- Developer Item -|#ff00ff}} ({{color|#ff00ff ●|#ff00ff}}) tag in their tooltips.

There are {{#expr: {{#dpl:|namespace=|uses=Template:Dedicated|resultsheader=%PAGES%|format=,}} - {{#var: dedicatednpcs}}}} dedicated items in total (not including the individual armor pieces from each of the dedicated armors).

There are {{#var: dedicatednpcs}} dedicated NPCs in total.
