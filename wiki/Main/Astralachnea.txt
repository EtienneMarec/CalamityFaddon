The '''Astralachnea''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[enemy]] which spawns in the [[Underground]] [[Astral Infection]] [[biome]]. It is an Astral variation of [[Wall Creeper]]s and [[Black Recluse]]s, chasing the player and climbing on walls when possible.
