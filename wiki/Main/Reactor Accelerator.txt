The '''Reactor Accelerator''' is an Epilogue [[Bows|bow]] that drops from [[Reactive Guardian, Sodis|Sodis]]. It shoots a cyan Waterfreezing arrow from the centre that circles the normal arrow and leaves a Reactive Explosion when touches anything. And from the sides it shoots 2 stones that orbit the waterfreezing arrow and ricochet on collision.
