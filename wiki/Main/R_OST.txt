R.OST is an [[enemy]] that mostly spawns during Godseeker Mode at anytime on the [[Surface]]. It has a very rare chance to spawn in [[Pre-Hardmode]]. R.OST will charge at the player at moderately fast speed and rarely passes through walls. Each hit has a 10% chance to do a "Crit" which doubles it's damage, along with high defense damage and Inflict the Dazed  ''[[debuff]]''. Stats Double depending on the progression.
