The '''Hive Pod''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[sentry]] that is dropped from [[Hive_(enemy)|Hives]] after [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated. The Astral Hive will spawn Hivelings if there are any enemies nearby, which will home onto the closest enemy to them, even through walls.

== Notes ==
*This weapon can also be obtained from the [[Astral Crate]] after Astrum Aureus has been defeated at a 20% chance.
