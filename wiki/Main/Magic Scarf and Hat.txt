The '''Magic Scarf and Hat''' are a [[Hardmode]] covering sold by the [[Archmage]]. Equipping them grants the '''Popo''' [[buff]], turning the user into a snowman. If the player is damaged by a [[Demon]], [[Voodoo Demon]] or [[Red Devil]], the '''Noseless Popo ''' [[debuff]] is inflicted upon the player and they lose their nose for 10 minutes. The covering is considered a vanity item, as it provides no stats or bonuses.

== Notes ==
*Equipping the covering in the vanity slot will cause the player to appear as a snowman without the Popo buff. The player will also be unable to lose their nose.
