The '''1.1.7''' update added a variety of Godseeker Mode content, with a total of 6 new bosses including [[Providence, the Profaned God]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]]. The update also added the [[Calamity Biome]], and a variety of weapons and balancing.

== Additions ==
=== [[Covering]] and [[coverings]] ===
*Added [[God Slayer Chestplate]], [[God Slayer Helm]], [[God Slayer Leggings]], [[Providence Mask]], and [[Shroomite Visage]].
*Added [[Asgardian Aegis]], [[Bloody Worm Scarf]], [[Counter Scarf]], [[Elysian Aegis]], [[Elysian Tracers]], and [[Elysian Wings]].

=== [[Biomes]] ===
*Added [[Calamity Biome]].

=== [[Blocks]] ===
*Added [[Charred Ore]] item.
*Brimstone Slag and Charred Ore blocks can now be naturally found.

=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Added [[Ceaseless Void]], [[Profaned Guardians]], [[Providence, the Profaned God]], [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]], [[Storm Weaver]], [[Supreme Calamitas]].
*Added [[Despair Stone]], [[Scorn Eater]], [[Scryllar]], [[Soul Slurper]], [[Ungodly Serpent]].
*Added [[Cosmic Lantern]], [[Dark Energy]], [[Stasis Probe|Stasis Probe (naked)]], [[Supreme Cataclysm]], [[Supreme Catastrophe]].

=== [[Buffs]] ===
*Added [[God Slayer Cooldown]], [[God Slayer Inferno]], [[Icarus' Folly]], [[Scarf Boost]], [[Scarf Cooldown]], [[Tyrant's Fury]], [[Vulnerability Hex]].

=== [[Weapons]] ===
*Added [[Anarchy Blade]], [[Ancient Shiv]], [[Aquatic Discharge]], [[Blissful Bombardier]], [[Bloody Rupture]], [[Brimblade]], [[Brimrose Staff]], [[Brimstone Fury]], [[Burning Sea]], [[Burnt Sienna]], [[Cleansing Blaze]], [[Cosmic Kunai]], [[Diseased Pike]], [[Elemental Blaster]], [[Energy Staff]], [[The Enforcer]], [[Essence Flayer]], [[Executioner's Blade]], [[Flameburst Shortsword]], [[Grand Guardian]], [[Holy Collider]], [[Icebreaker]], [[Leeching Dagger]], [[Mirror Blade]], [[Murasama]], [[Phoenix Blade]], [[Profaned Sword]], [[Purge Guzzler]], [[Sausage Maker]], [[SDFMG]], [[The Storm]], [[Stream Gouge]], [[Telluric Glare]], [[Terra-cotta]], [[Wrath of the Ancients]], and [[YinYo]].
*Added [[Spore Knife]] as an unobtainable item.

=== Other ===
*Added [[Cosmilite Bar]], [[Dark Plasma]], [[Twisting Nether]], [[Unholy Core]], [[Unholy Essence]].
*Added [[Calamity Biome Spawner]].
*Added [[Treasure Bag (Providence)]].
*Added [[Eye of Extinction]], [[Profaned Core]], [[Profaned Shard]], [[Rune of Kos]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*Buffed [[Abyssion, The Forgotten One]] from the Thorium Mod's [[Prepare to Cry]] mode health boost from 16,000 to 26,000.

*[[Calamitas]] changes:
**Now immune to all debuffs.
**Phase 1's lasers no longer gain extra damage after the [[Moon Lord]] has been defeated.
**Now spawns 10 times less frequently.
**Death message now refers to her as "A Calamitas Doppelganger".
**[[Cataclysm]]'s death message now refers to him as "A Cataclysm Shade".
**[[Catastrophe]]'s death message now refers to him as "A Catastrophe Shade".
**Now drops {{dv|3-5|4-5}} [[Essence of Chaos|Essences of Chaos]].

*[[Cryogen]] changes:
**Nerfed phase 1's contact damage from {{dv|80|96}} to {{dv|70|84}}.
**Nerfed phase 2's contact damage from {{dv|90|108}} to {{dv|80|96}}.
**Nerfed phase 3 and 4's contact damages from {{dv|95|114}} to {{dv|85|102}}.
**Nerfed phase 5 and 6's contact damages from {{dv|100|120}} to {{dv|90|108}}.
**Nerfed Ice Bomb damage from 100 to 56.
**Now drops {{dv|3-5|4-5}} [[Essence of Eleum|Essences of Eleum]] instead of {{dv|2-4|3-5}}.
**Projectiles no longer gain extra damage after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**No longer gains speed in [[Prepare to Die]] Mode.
**Now spawns 25 times less frequently.
**Buffed [[Aurora Spirit]] damage from {{dv|46|73}} to {{dv|66|105}}.
**Buffed [[Cryocore]] variant 1 damage from {{dv|35|42}} to {{dv|45|54}}.
**Buffed Cryocore variant 2 damage from {{dv|45|54}} to {{dv|55|66}}.

*[[Desert Scourge]] changes:
**Now spawns 10 times less frequently.
**Now drops up to 3 [[Coral]], [[Starfish]], and [[Seashell]]s instead of 2.
**Decreased all weapon drop chances from {{dv|33%|50%}} to {{dv|25%|33%}}.

*Moved [[The Destroyer]]'s piercing resistance from Prepare to Die ([[Celestial Onion]]) to Expert Mode.

*[[The Devourer of Gods]] changes:
**Buffed health from {{dv|500,000|750,000}} to {{dv|650,000|975,000}}.
**Now immune to all debuffs.
**Buffed star projectile damage in Normal Mode from 50 to 65.
**Buffed Beam Portal damage from {{dv|0|220}} to {{dv|0|260}}.
**Buffed Nebula Shot damage from {{expert|180}} to {{expert|220}}, its bolt damage from {{expert|240}} to {{expert|260}}, its star damage from {{expert|200}} to {{expert|260}}, and its Gravity Bomb damage from {{dv|100|120}} to {{dv|180|280}}.
**Body segments now makes all non-minion projectiles pierce no more than once.
**Tail now gains 99,999 defense once the boss reaches 15% health.
**Tail no longer halves its damage taken multiplier upon reaching 50% health.
**Now drops {{dv|25-34|30-39}} Cosmilite Bars instead of {{dv|8-10|10-12}} [[Armored Shell]]s.
**Changed all weapon drop chances from 9.09% to {{dv|8.33%|10%}}.
**Buffed [[Devourer Spawn]] health from {{dv|2000|2400}} to {{dv|20,000|24,000}}, nerfed its head's contact damage from {{expert|153}} to {{expert|136}}, and decreased its speed.
**Buffed [[Stasis Probe]]'s health from 500 to {{dv|1100|1430}}, and its defense from 40 to 80.
**Stasis Probes now only inflict [[Frozen]] on contact in Expert Mode, and are no longer guaranteed to inflict it.

*[[The Hive Mind]] changes:
**Can now spawn naturally in the [[Corruption]].
**Buffed Vile Clot damage from {{dv|52|56}} to {{dv|66|72}}.
**Buffed radial Vile Clot damage from {{dv|60|68}} to {{dv|80|92}}.
**Vile Clot projectiles no longer deal any extra damage after the Moon Lord has been defeated.
**Now shoots less Vile Clot projectiles and in a tighter spread.
**No longer gains speed in Prepare to Die Mode.
**Now enrages when outside the Corruption.

*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] changes:
**Flare dust projectiles now spawn higher.
**Now uses [[Music#Infernal Catharsis|Infernal Catharsis]] as its theme instead of [[Music#Kyurem|Kyurem's theme]].
**Now immune to all debuffs.

*[[The Perforators]] changes:
**Buffed the small Perforator's head's damage from {{dv|40|52}} to {{dv|53|68}}, its body's damage from {{dv|23|29}} to {{dv|33|42}}, and its tail's damage from {{dv|9|11}} to {{dv|19|24}}.
**Buffed the medium Perforator's head's damage from {{dv|55|71}} to {{dv|65|84}}, its body's damage from {{dv|31|40}} to {{dv|40|52}}, and its tail's damage from {{dv|11|14}} to {{dv|25|32}}.
**Buffed the large Perforator's head's damage from {{dv|65|84}} to {{dv|80|104}}, its body's damage from {{dv|42|54}} to {{dv|52|67}}, and its tail's damage from {{dv|15|19}} to {{dv|35|45}}.
**All three Perforators now enrage outside of the [[Crimson]].
**Decreased all three Perforators' spawnrates.

*[[The Plaguebringer Goliath]] is now immune to all debuffs.

*[[Siren Lure and Leviathan]] are now immune to all debuffs.

*[[Crawlers]], [[Cultist Assassin]]s, [[Heat Spirit]]s, [[Ice Clasper]]s, [[Pitbull]]s, [[Sand Tortoise]]s, and [[Sun Bat]]s no longer have gores.
*Cultist Assassins now drop {{gc|2}} instead of {{gc|5}}.
*All Essence of Chaos, [[Essence of Cinder]], and Essence of Eleum drop rates increased by one.
*Shadow Essences no longer drop from [[Bone Lee]]s and [[Necromancer]]s. <!---Scal now--->
