The '''Statis' Void Sash''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It grants the ability to cling to and slide down vertical walls, quickly dash a base distance of 38 blocks by double-tapping the horizontal movement key, enables auto-jump by holding the {{key|jump}} key. Additionally, the player is a granted a guaranteed dodge that can only occur every 90 seconds. When dashing, three {{item|Cosmic Scythe|s|link=}} are released that home into nearby enemies and deal 375 base damage, which is boosted by all classes' damage boosts. It also grants immunity to fall damage.
