'''Tarragon Bars''' are Godseeker Mode [[bars]] made with 5 [[Tarragon Ore]] and a [[Clockwork Bar]] at a [[Furnace]]. They are used to craft various plant-themed items.

*1-3 Tarragon Bars can additionally be obtained from [[Jungle Crate]]s at a 10% drop chance after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] has been defeated.

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Tarragon|Tarragon]], also known as estragon, is a species of perennial herb in the sunflower family.
*Tarragon Bars were previously known as '''Uelibloom Bars''', and prior to that were known as '''Ueliace Bars'''.
**The word "Uelibloom" contains the word "bloom", as in blooming plants. The word "Ueliace", and therefore the "Ueli-" prefix of "Uelibloom", was made up and has no meaning.
