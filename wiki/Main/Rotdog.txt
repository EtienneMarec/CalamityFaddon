The '''Rotdog''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns on the [[Surface]] at [[Night]] after the [[Eye of Cthulhu]] has been defeated. It has the same AI as the [[Unicorn]], charging and ramming into the player at high speeds. This, along with their ability to inflict [[Bleeding]], makes them a dangerous enemy for unprepared players.

== Trivia ==
*The Rotdog's name is a play on the words, "rot" and "hotdog".
**This is because when Rotdogs are processed into meat they are especially delicious.
*The Rotdog was previously named ''Pitbull''.
