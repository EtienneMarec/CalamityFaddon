The '''Cinder Blossom Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, it summons a fiery flower minion above the player’s head which shoots a volley of three flaming cinders above a nearby enemy. The cinders are able to linger on the ground for a short time. Unlike most minions, only one Cinder Blossom can be summoned.
