The '''Frigid Bulwark''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]]. It is a somewhat direct upgrade to the [[Frozen Turtle Shell]] and the [[Paladin's Shield]], combining the effects of both coverings. When below 50% life, damage is reduced by 15%. When below 15% life, it gives 20% damage reduction instead.
