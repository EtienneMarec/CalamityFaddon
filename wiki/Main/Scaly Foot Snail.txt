The '''Scaly Foot Snail''' is a Godseeker Mode [[critter]] found within [[Frozen Hell]] that acts similarly to any other [[Snail]]. Scaly Foot Snails can be caught with a [[Lavaproof Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]].
