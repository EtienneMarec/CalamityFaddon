'''Frozen Hellstone''' is a Godseeker Mode [[ore]] which replaces Hellstone in [[Frozen Hell]]. It is used to craft [[Enchanted Metal]] at [[Draedon's Forge]]. It requires at least a [[Large Father]] or higher to mine. It can also be dropped by all [[Enemies]] in Frozen Hell.
