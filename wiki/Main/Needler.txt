The '''Needler''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[gun]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as [[ammo]], bullets are converted into needles that stick to enemies and tiles which explode after a delay. It has a 4% (1/25) chance to be dropped by [[Moss Hornet]]s.
