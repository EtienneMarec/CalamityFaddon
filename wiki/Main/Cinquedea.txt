The '''Cinquedea''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] non-consumable [[dagger]] sold by the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|9}}. When used, it automatically throws a dagger that is affected by gravity. The dagger can pierce one enemy.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Cinquedea will cause the next dagger thrown to home in on nearby enemies after striking an enemy.
