''''''Orb of Infinity'''

''Rarity'': Indescribable

''Type'': Covering

''Tooltip'': "The end, and nothing more..."

'''Description''':

The '''Orb of Infinity''' is an covering of immeasurable power—claimed to be the culmination of everything the mod has to offer. The moment you equip it, you’ll know you’re only scratching the surface of what’s possible. Though countless players have sought to uncover its full potential, the truth remains just beyond their grasp. Surely, if you continue your journey, you might be the one to finally discover what lies at the end.

Legend says that the '''Orb of Infinity''' is an item that holds powers beyond imagination, but all attempts to harness those powers have ended in ambiguous and inconclusive results. It’s said that with every major achievement you make, the Orb inches closer to revealing its ultimate purpose—until, of course, it doesn’t. The mysterious energy it gives off promises something grand, though it’s unclear what.

The Orb’s power scales with an undefined factor, which is rumored to be the "Infinity Variable." But what is the "Infinity Variable?" Some say it's the mod’s creator’s way of encouraging constant exploration. Others suspect it’s merely an elaborate joke. However, one thing is for certain: its potential is... infinite.
----'''Effects''':

* Grants an effect that is too powerful to be understood... but no one has figured out what that is yet.
* ''Imminent Potential'': Wearing the Orb makes you feel like you’re ''almost'' about to achieve something incredible
* Sometimes emits an unrecognizable aura that promises greatness

----'''Trivia''':

* '''Name Origin''': The '''Orb of Infinity''' gets its name from the fact that no one truly knows what it does. The idea of "infinity" is meant to represent endless possibilities to the power it withstands.
* '''Tooltip Explanation''': The tooltip, "The beginning... and nothing more," is said to represent the countless adventurers who embarked on a journey of infinite hope, only to discover they were still at the start.
* '''Special Boss Interaction''': When equipped during boss fights, bosses will sometimes react in peculiar ways—like pausing momentarily or emitting a strange laugh. Players have interpreted this as the Orb playing mind games, as if it’s mocking their quest for answers.
* '''Myths & Rumors''': According to some, equipping the Orb of Infinity while holding any item starting with the letter "I" might ''possibly'' increase its power, though no one has been able to verify this claim.
* '''The "Infinity Update"''': The '''Orb of Infinity''' was teased as part of the infamous "Infinity Update," which has yet to be released, despite several cryptic hints that it's coming "very soon."
