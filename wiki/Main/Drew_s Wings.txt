'''Drew's Wings''' were a Godseeker Mode [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allow the player to fly and glide, dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. They were used in crafting the [[Celestial Tracers]]. Replaced with [[Wings of Rebirth]] in the 2.0.5 update.

== Notes ==
*The flight stats of Drew's Wings are near-identical to that of Celestial Tracers, except that Drew's Wings have superior flight time by a value of 111 frames. However, Celestial Tracers has improved horizontal acceleration compared to Drew's Wings due to its inherited boot functionality, even in the air.

== Tips ==
*Some players may prefer using Drew's Wings against [[Supreme Calamitas]] instead of the Celestial Tracers, not only for the increased flight time but also the reduced acceleration giving players tighter control over their movement.

== Trivia ==
*Drew's Wings are a recolor of the [[Solar Wings]].
