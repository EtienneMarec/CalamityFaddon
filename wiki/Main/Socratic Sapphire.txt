The '''Socratic Sapphire''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[coverings|Covering]] that halves the player's total critical strike chance and damage reduction when equipped, in exchange for a 10% chance to replace a critical hit with a savage blow, which deals 5x the usual damage. That chance increases if the player has boosts to critical strike chance and damage reduction, maxing out at 75%. Additionally, it reduces almost all debuff durations by 70%.

==Note==
*Its critical chance reduction will neither affect nor be affected by certain items that makes use of critical strike chance differently (e.g, but not limited to: [[Daawnlight Spirit Origin]], [[Soma Prime]]).
*Its debuff duration reducing effect will not affect certain debuffs that are either related to Alcohol-related potions, or are tied to item cooldowns.
==Trivia==
*The covering's design is based on the same gem found on [https://battle-cats.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_Mind_Soractes_(Floating)_(Sage) Sage of Mind Soractes] from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Battle_Cats The Battle Cats]. In turn, it also references the ancient greek philosopher [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socrates Socrates].
**Its tooltip is inspired from Socrates' logically paradoxical saying "I know that I know nothing".
**Additionally, its debuff duration reduction effect mirrors that of the Sage subtrait and its debuff-resisting effect from the same game.
