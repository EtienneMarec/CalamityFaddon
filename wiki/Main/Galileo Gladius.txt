The '''Galileo Gladius''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[shortsword]]. It is held and thrust forward with a short glowing blade. When a stab hits nothing, the weapon fires a homing crescent moon that deals 33.33% of the weapon's base damage and passes through blocks. Hitting an enemy with a true melee hit instead causes a large blue planetoid to rain on the enemy (at most one every quarter of a second). These planetoids explode on contact with tiles and enemies.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, crescent moons will drop down planetoids on enemy hits. The player also gains 50% increased melee speed and immunity to [[Moon Burnt]] for 20 seconds.

{{mode||In Calamity++, the Galileo Gladius is a held sword that charges Stratus starbursts and can be thrown with right click, as in the current Calamity Mod.}}
