The '''Galileo Gladius''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[shortsword]]. When swung, the weapon auto-fires homing crescent moons that deal 33.33% of the weapon's base damage and pass through blocks. Hitting an enemy with a true melee hit will cause large blue planetoids to rain on the enemy. These planetoids explode on contact with tiles and enemies.

When the weapon's [[Shortstrike]] ability activates, crescent moons will drop down planetoids on enemy hits. The player also gains 50% increased melee speed and immunity to [[Moon Burnt]] for 20 seconds.
