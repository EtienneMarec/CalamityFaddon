'''Elemental Souls''' are the unique items, which can be obtained from a respective Elemental, but only if the world has the [[Nightmare Mode]] enabled. A respective soul summons a respective [[Allies|ally]], as well as boosting some of the player's stats while the corresponding item is in player's inventory. Pressing {{key|Interact}} will release a soul and spawn a respective [[Town NPCs|Town NPC]] version of an ally. Currently, there are 6 souls present in the addon.

== Trivia ==
*Despite [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Providence]] not being an elemental, her soul is still considered.
*The stat buffs, and ally summon will work as well if the soul is placed in some sorts of personal storages, like [[tgc:Piggy Bank|Piggy Bank]], however, they wouldn't work if placed in a [[tgc:Safe|Safe]] or [[tgc:Defender's Forge|Defender's Forge]].
*In the previous versions of the addon, prior to 1.1.0.001 update, the souls couldn't be transfered to an another world other than the one where it was obtained and there was a [[Soul Container]] for such purpose. However, the idea was proven inefficient, and therefore was abolished.
