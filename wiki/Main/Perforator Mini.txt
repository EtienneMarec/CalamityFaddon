The '''Perforator Mini''' was a [[Enemies#Boss Servants|boss servant]] [[enemy]] that was spawned exclusively by [[The Perforators]], but has since been removed from the mod. It behaved identically to the [[Giant Worm]], but it could also inflict the [[Burning Blood]] and [[Bleeding]] [[debuffs]].
