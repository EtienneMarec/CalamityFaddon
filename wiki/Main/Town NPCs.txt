==Notes==
{{float file box|[[File:Town NPC Shop Alert Icon.png]]|Shop Alert Icon}}

*All Town NPCs will have an exclamation mark icon above them when a new item is sold, which can only be removed by interacting with them. They can also be removed completely via [[configuration options]].
*All Calamity and Vanilla Town NPCs gain several changes to their stats.
**They are made immune to every [[debuff]].
**They take as much damage as the player does, instead of taking half of it.
**They gain bonuses to their defense and damage when certain bosses are defeated. [[Moon Lord]] and [[Supreme Calamitas]] will each increase their defense by 20 and damage by 60% when defeated. [[Providence]], [[Polterghast]], [[The Devourer of Gods]], and [[Yharon]] will each increase their defense by 12 and damage by 20% when defeated.
*When killed, the [[Angler]] has a 8.3% chance to drop a [[Golden Fishing Rod]] in [[Hardmode]].
**If fed to a [[Trasher]], this chance rises to 100%.

==Trivia==
*Some added NPC quotes include references to other media.
**The Guide's quote related to the Astral Infection is a reference to Obi-Wan Kenobi's "Disturbance in the Force" quote from the original ''[[wikipedia:Star Wars (film)|Star Wars]]'' film.
**The Merchant's quote involving lamps is a reference to the attraction of moths towards lamps as well as the "[https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/moth-lamp moth lamp]" meme.
**The Dye Trader's quote asking if the [[Archmage]] knows how to "let it go" is an obvious reference to the popular song "[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=moSFlvxnbgk Let It Go]".
**The Clothier's quote when Moon Lord is alive is a [[wikipedia:Houston, we have a problem|popular quote]] from the ''[[wikipedia:Apollo 13|Apollo 13]]'' mission.
**The Clothier's other quote is a reference to the theme of the show ''[[wikipedia:Ghostbusters (franchise)|Ghostbusters]]''.
**The Cyborg's quote is a line from a famous monologue from the 1982 film ''[[wikipedia:Blade Runner|Blade Runner]]''.
**The Painter's quote when in the Desert references a famous quote from the film ''[[wikipedia:Star_Wars:_Episode_II_–_Attack_of_the_Clones|Star Wars: Episode II - Attack of the Clones]]''.
* Some patreon-requested NPC names are references to other media.
**The Clothier's donator-requested name "Joeseph Jostar" is a reference to [https://jojo.fandom.com/wiki/Joseph_Joestar a character of a similar name] from ''[[wikipedia:JoJo's Bizarre Adventure|JoJo's Bizarre Adventure]]''.
**The Skeleton Merchant's donator-request names "Papyrus Undertale" and "Sans Undertale" are references to the characters, [https://undertale.fandom.com/wiki/Papyrus Papyrus] and [https://undertale.fandom.com/wiki/Sans Sans] respectively from the game ''[[wikipedia:Undertale|Undertale]]''.
**The Tavernkeep's donator-requested name "Tim Lockwood" is a reference to [https://cloudywithachanceofmeatballs.fandom.com/wiki/Tim_Lockwood a character of the same name] from the film adaptations of ''[[wikipedia:Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs (film)|Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs]]''.
**The Travelling Merchant's donator-requested name "Stan Pines" is a reference to [https://gravityfalls.fandom.com/wiki/Stan_Pines a character of the same name] from the television series, ''[[wikipedia:Gravity Falls|Gravity Falls]]''.
**The Wizard's donator-requested name "Merasmus" is a reference to [https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Merasmus a character of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Team Fortress 2|Team Fortress 2]]''.
**The Arms Dealer's donator-requested name "Barney Calhoun" is a reference to [https://combineoverwiki.net/wiki/Barney_Calhoun a character of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Half-Life (video game)|Half-Life]]''.
*Both the [[Sea King]] and the Archmage were both recurring characters in the mod's [[lore]] for a long time, even before they were added as Town NPCs.
**The way that the Archmage drops from [[Cryogen]] is a reference to how v1 from the game ULTRAKILL said "It's ultrakilling time" and ultrakilled everything in the room.
*The internal variable for finding the Merchant's name is called "angelstatue".
