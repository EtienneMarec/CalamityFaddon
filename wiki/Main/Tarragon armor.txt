'''Tarragon armor''' is a Godseeker Mode [[Covering]] set crafted from [[Uelibloom Bar]]s and [[Divine Geode]]s. It requires 33 Uelibloom Bars and 36 Divine Geodes to make a set utilizing one headpiece; it requires 61 Uelibloom Bars and 60 Divine Geodes to make a set including all of the headpieces. If creating a set utilizing only one headpiece, [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is required to be defeated at least thrice (twice in [[Expert Mode]]) in order to obtain the sufficient amount of Divine Geodes needed to craft the armor. If creating a set including all headpieces, Providence, the Profaned Goddess must be defeated at least four times (at least three times in Expert Mode). If the entire set is visible (regardless of whether it is equipped or in social slots), the wearer will have a faint pulsing aura.

It consists of the '''Tarragon Breastplate''' and '''Tarragon Leggings''', as well has having five different headpieces suited to each class, being: the '''Tarragon Helm''', '''Tarragon Visage''', '''Tarragon Mask''', '''Tarragon Horned Helm''', and '''Tarragon Helmet'''. All helmets share a set bonus, but also provide set bonuses specific to their respective class as well.

The '''Tarragon Breastplate''' provides 37 defense, 10% increased damage and 5% increased critical strike chance, +2 life regen, and +40 max life. The '''Tarragon Leggings''' provide 32 defense, 8% increased damage and critical strike chance, and 10% increased movement speed.

*The '''Tarragon Helm''' gives 33 defense, 5% damage reduction, and 10% increased melee damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''Tarragon Visage''' gives 21 defense, 5% damage reduction, and 10% increased ranged damage and critical strike chance.
*The '''Tarragon Mask''' gives 10 defense, 5% damage reduction, 20% increased magic damage, 10% critical strike chance, +100 max mana, and 15% reduced mana cost.
*The '''Tarragon Horned Helm''' gives 3 defense, and 5% damage reduction.
*The '''Tarragon Helmet''' gives 15 defense, 5% damage reduction, 10% increased Spy damage and critical strike chance, and 5% increased movement speed.

== Set Bonuses ==
Regardless of the headpiece chosen, equipping a full set of Tarragon armor provides the following set bonuses:
*An increased heart pickup range (akin to the [[Heartreach Potion]]).
*Enemies to have a chance to drop extra hearts upon death.
In addition to the above, each headpiece provides its own class-specific set bonus:

'''Tarragon Helm''':
*+800 to [[Aggro]].
*Upon taking damage, there is a 25% chance to gain the [[Tarra Life]] [[buff]] for 2 to 6 seconds, causing rapid life regeneration.
*Pressing {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} allows the player to cloak themselves in life energy, granting the [[Tarragon Cloak]] buff for 10 seconds.
**This effect has a 30-second [[Tarragon Cloak Cooldown|cooldown]].

'''Tarragon Visage''':
*Enemy spawn rates are decreased by 56%, and maximum spawn count is decreased by 44%.
*With a 1-second cooldown, ranged projectiles split into two life energies and leaves on impact.
**These deal 33% of the ranged attack's damage, capped at 65 damage.

'''Tarragon Mask''':
*Enemy spawn rates are decreased by 56%, and maximum spawn count is decreased by 44%.
*On every fifth critical strike dealt, the player fires a burst of nine to eleven leaf projectiles.
**The leaves deal 20% of the attack that summoned them and are softcapped at 50 damage.
*Magic projectiles [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player upon hitting an enemy every 1.5 seconds.
**The amount healed is 2% of the projectile damage.

'''Tarragon Horned Helm''':
*Enemy spawn rates are decreased by 56%, and maximum spawn count is decreased by 44%.
*+3 max minions and 50% increased minion damage.
*Summons a life aura around the player that damages nearby enemies.
**The aura deals 120 damage per 1.33 seconds, and is affected by summon damage bonuses.
**The aura does not affect enemies which do not deal contact damage.

'''Tarragon Helmet''':
*Enemy spawn rates are decreased by 56%, and maximum spawn count is decreased by 44%.
*While under the effects of a debuff, the player gains a 10% increase to Spy damage.
*After every twenty-fifth Spy critical strike, the player gains 3 seconds of the [[Tarragon Immunity]] buff.
**This effect has a 25-second [[Tarragon Immunity Cooldown|cooldown]].
*Gives a maximum [[stealth]] of 115.
