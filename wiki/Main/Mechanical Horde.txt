The '''Mechanical Horde''' is an event that occurs in [[The Exosphere]]. It does not occur naturally, and must be activated by using the [[Draedon's Remote]].

The event consists of 9 waves of buffed mechanical enemies from throughout the game's progression, with most waves requiring specific [[challenges]] to be defeated before progressing to the next wave. All enemies in the event have massively increased health and damage. After the 9th wave, the event ends with a fight against [[Mechdusa]], a combination of the three mechanical challenges usually exclusive to [[Get fixed boi]] worlds.

Upon reaching the end of the event, [[Draedon]] appears to give the player the {{item|Encrypted Schematic (Exosphere)}}, allowing them to craft the {{item|Auric Quantum Cooling Cell}} and complete [[The Codebreaker]]. After completion of the event, Draedon's Remote will become unusable in the Exosphere, preventing the event from being restarted. Completion also allows [[La Ruga]] to spawn in the Exosphere.

==Tips==
{{dablink|For more elaborate strategies on defeating the Mechanical Horde, including [[weapon]] and [[covering]] recommendations, see [[Guide:Mechanical Horde strategies]].}}
* The event is not time-sensitive. Players are able to run away if necessary; for example, if they need to drink a potion.
* None of the enemies in the event are immune to [[Cursed Inferno]] or [[Ichor]], making them potent debuffs.
* It is imperative to kill enemies as quickly as possible, particularly in later waves, to avoid being overwhelmed.
==Trivia==
* The [[Music|theme]] of the The Mechanical Horde is [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eFLdPMjWy8o '''Inferior Fabrications'''], which was composed by [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCfbpIwaXAPuE3kTFq1tEqDA PinpinNeon].
** However, in a similar fashion to the [[The Fabricational Quartet]], this theme is confirmed to be a placeholder.
* In the mod's source files, the Mechanical Horde is referred to as "RobotsFTW". This is believed to be a reference to [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EP3fDx644w a track from Portal 2].
* Originally, the Mechanical Horde was planned to last up to post-[[The Fabricational Quartet]] event, before it was reworked into a prerequisite due to the lack of robotic Godseeker Mode enemies.
