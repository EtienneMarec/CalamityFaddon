'''Charging Station''' is a [[furniture]] item used to [[charge]] certain [[weapons]]. It uses [[Draedon Power Cell]]s as fuel to grant weapons charge. To charge an item, right-click the station, put the arsenal weapon at the top, and the power cell at the bottom. It can be found inside [[Arsenal Lab]]s in the [[Cavern]] layer.
