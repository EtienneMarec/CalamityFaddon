The '''Onyx Gunblade''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that also deals ranged damage. When swung with bullets used as [[ammo]], it fires an onyx blast similarly to the [[Onyx Blaster]], along with a spread of 2-7 whatever bullet is being used. If the player has no bullets, the sword has no special effets.
