'''Nucleogenesis''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that is the direct upgrade to the [[Star-Tainted Generator]] and [[Statis' Curse]]. While equipped, the player gains +4 minion slots, which doesn't stack with downgrades, 15% increased minion damage, and all minion knockback is increased by 4. All minion attacks spawn a damaging spark on enemy hits with a cooldown of 1.67 seconds, dealing 60 summon damage.

While equipped, all minions will inflict the following [[debuffs]]:
*[[Electrified]] for 1 second.
*[[Temporal Sadness]] for 1 second.
*[[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] for 3 seconds.
*[[Irradiated]] for 3 seconds.
*[[Shadowflame]] for 3 seconds.
*[[Holy Flames]] for 3 seconds.
