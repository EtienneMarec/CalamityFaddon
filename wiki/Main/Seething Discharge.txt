The '''Seething Discharge''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] dropped by the [[Brimstone Elemental]]. It fires a large blast of brimstone and two smaller projectiles, similar to the attack performed by the Brimstone Elemental while in her rose. The attack has a mild spread and travels through blocks.
