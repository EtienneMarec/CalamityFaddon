'''Entropic armor''' is a craftable post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] multiclass [[Covering]] set that drops from the said boss.

It consists of an '''Entropic Skull''', '''Entropic Tunic''', and '''Entropic Boots'''.

The full set grants the following stats and bonuses (this includes the set bonus):
*80 defense
*140% increased movement speed
*140% increased damage
*200 max health and mana
*80% chance to not waste ammo/mana/consumable Spy weapons
*All attacks inflict Umbrus Fumes
