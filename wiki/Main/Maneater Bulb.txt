The '''Maneater Bulb''' was a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] dropped by [[Man Eater]]s. Additionally, it was possible to obtain 1-3 Maneater Bulbs from [[Jungle Crate]]s at a 20% chance. It was used to craft the [[Living Dew]] and [[Portabulb]].
