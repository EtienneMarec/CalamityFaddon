The '''Yharic Delight''' is a craftable consumable [[food item]], obtainable after defeating [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]]. It heals the player to full health when used.

Consuming a Yharic Delight while Yharon is alive will cause him to instantly explode. This counts as defeating Yharon, allowing the player to easily farm him for their desired drops.

== Notes ==
* Consuming the Yharic Delight while [[Godseeker, Yharim|Yharim]] is alive will cause him to enrage, increasing his damage and movement speed by 1%.
* Attempting to consume the Yharic Delight while [[Crabulon]] is alive will cause it to replace all Yharic Delights in your inventory with [[Delicious Meat]].

== Tips ==

* Since Yharon drops both Yharon Soul Fragments and Yharim Bars in [[Expert Mode]] or higher, this item can essentially be duplicated by instakilling Yharon with it.
* This item is best used with the [[Philosopher's Stone]] and its upgrades, as it allows for a full heal every 45 seconds while equipped.
*In the [[get fixed boi]] seed, Yharon Soul Fragments can be obtained after beating [[The Devourer of Gods]]. Since the Devourer of Gods can also drop Yharim Bars, Yharic Delights can be crafted after killing him, meaning a normal Yharon fight can be skipped entirely. It is currently unknown whether this is intended by the developers.

==Trivia==
*The Yharic Delight is the strongest healing item in the game.
** It also boasts the longest non-infinite buff duration of any buff item in the game.
*The Yharic Delight is implied to be a competitor to the Delicious Meat.
** This is supported by Crabulon, the creator of Delicious Meat according to the [[lore]], replacing Yharic Delights with Delicious Meat.
**This is further supported by [[Draedon]]'s dialogue during the Exo Mechs fight when you use the Yharic Delight, in which he claims that the Delicious Meat is superior.
*The item's sprite being the same as the [[Chicken Nugget]] is a reference to how Yharon is described as a chicken in the mod's lore.
