The '''Profaned Guardians''' (Japanese: 冒涜の守護者 ''Bōtoku no gādian'') are a group of Godseeker Mode [[Boss|bosses]] fought either in [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]]. They need to be killed in order to obtain the [[Profaned Core]], used to summon [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]], and each Guardian also drops a special respective "Relic" item.

Multiple weaker Guardians are also summoned during the fight against Providence.

== Spawn ==
The Profaned Guardians will not spawn on their own and must be summoned by using a [[Profaned Shard]] while in the [[The Hallow]] or [[The Underworld]]. The Profaned Guardians can only be summoned during the [[Day]] and will flee and despawn if it becomes nighttime.

== Behavior ==
All four Profaned Guardians spawn at the same time. The fiery main Guardian is immune to all damage until the wood Guardian, rock Guardian, and the crystal Guardian are defeated.

If the player exits the Underground for more than 5 seconds during the fight, the Rock and Crystal guardians will spin while rapidly converging on the player’s location, similar to how [[King Slime]] attacks during nighttime.

If the [[Archmage]] is present anywhere in the world, the Guardians will enrage and immediately fly at him, using various attacks until he dies.

=== Attacks ===
==== The Guardian Archer (Wood Guardian) ====
*Rapidly shoots fiery arrows horizontally for 3 seconds before going on cooldown for 5 seconds.
**Getting hit by the guardian directly causes the guardian to steal 20 ammo or coins from the player and instantly recharge this attack. 

==== The Guardian Defender (Rock Guardian) ====
*Shoots huge {{item|Flaming Boulders|image=Holy Meteor.gif|nolink=y|size=20px}} from its head every 0.33 seconds.
*Repeatedly fires a circular spread of 32 {{item|Holy Spears|image=Divine Spear.png|nolink=y|size=42px}}.
*All projectiles have a 90% chance to reflect, flying back at the player with twice the velocity.
**Attacks that cannot be reflected will instead cause the player’s armor to break instantly.

==== The Guardian Healer (Crystal Guardian) ====
*Stays far offscreen, firing healing orbs rapidly at the other Guardians.
*Periodically fires a lifesteal laser at the player, completely restoring all Guardians’ healths if it hits.

==== The Proflamed Guardian (Main Guardian) ====
*HIGHLY resistant to all forms of damage until the other three Guardians are defeated.
*Cycles through 5 different attack patterns:
**Uses a {{item|Holy Spear Rain|image=Divine Spear.png|nolink=y|size=42px}} attack, covering the screen in falling Holy Spears. Chases the player at a moderate pace.
**Uses a {{item|Proflamed Flame|image=HolyBlast.gif|nolink=y|size=42px}} attack, launching exploding flares with an infinite lifespan, precise homing, and accelerating speed. Travels in an outward spiral.
**Uses a {{item|Holy Spear Barrage|image=Divine Spear.png|nolink=y|size=42px}} attack, shooting many Holy Spears in a circular motion while keeping a vertical alignment to the player. The spears accelerate extremely fast, and do 7.25x damage at max acceleration.
**Uses a Fiery Zigzag attack, moving in a rapid zigzag motion around the player while leaving {{item|Lingering Flames|image=HolyBomb.gif|nolink=y|size=22px}} as it travels. Lingering Flames don’t move, however they never despawn, even in the case of the boss being defeated or reloading the world.
**Uses a Holy Incineration attack, burning everything in a 50,000 block radius. This deals immense damage and is capable of destroying every block.
*After reaching 75% health, it will attack 2x as fast.
*After reaching 50% health, it will summon [[Scorn Eater|Scorn Eaters]] every 0.4 seconds.
*After reaching 25% health, it will attack 12x as fast.
*After reaching 10% health, it will eat a dorrito before firing a death ray

=== In [[Revengeance Mode]] ===
*All Guardian attacks can partially damage blocks. If not managed in time, the damaged blocks will catch fire and burn their surroundings.
*The Proflamed Guardian additionally deals greatly increased damage after the other three have been killed.
*It is significantly more aggressive, attempting to ram into the player whenever possible.

=== In [[Death Mode]] ===
*The Guardian Defender has a 100% chance to deflect projectiles, making all classes except for [[Melee]] nearly useless.
*At 10% health, the Proflamed Guardian enters a “desperation” phase where it attacks 80% faster and constantly uses all 5 of its attacks at once.

=== In [[Malice Mode]] ===
* At 5% health, the Proflamed Guardian enters an “extreme desperation” phase where it flees the scene as fast as it possibly can.

=== On the [[For the Worthy]] seed ===
* Summons a weaker version of [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] when one Guardian dies.

== Trivia ==
*The Profaned Guardians' [[Music|theme]] is [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P5r9WCfA3y4 '''Unholy Ambush'''], which was composed by the artist [https://www.youtube.com/user/SUPEREMERALD123 DM DOKURO].
**Despite their theme being called Unholy Ambush, the fight could only be considered an ambush in Death Mode, where the Profaned Guardians can spawn naturally.
**Their theme has similarities with Providence's, due to the fact that their theme is a repurposed scrapped portion of Providence's theme.
**If the Calamity Music add-on mod is disabled, '''Boss 1''' will play instead.
*Despite being a boss, the Guardians don't drop a [[Treasure Bag]] in Expert Mode.
*In previous versions of the mod, each of the Profaned Guardians were named "A Dunkin' Donut" as their previous sprites were often compared to donuts. Their name was also a reference to [[wikipedia:Dunkin' Donuts|Dunkin' Donuts]] which is a famous coffee shop known for their donuts.
*One of the Proflamed Guardian’s dialogue lines reference the [[Profaned Rage Potion]].
**Another one of their lines reference [[Delicious Meat]], the dish served by the Archmage.
**Yet another one of their lines reference the song [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MT6HTpY_OyE&pp=ygUXc3RhaW5lZCBicnV0YWwgY2FsYW1pdHk%3D Stained, Brutal Calamity], more specifically, the part where the lyrics says "Your mental fortitude is fading."
* The reason for the Guardians attacking the Archmage is unknown, maybe they wanted delicious meat? they do mention it after all.
**According to the [[Lore]], however, it may be because they were sabotaged by the Archmage during [[Yharim (disambiguation)|Yharim]]’s cooking show, causing them to lose.
**This unique behavior has been confirmed to be intended.
*If the [https://forums.terraria.org/index.php?threads/boss-checklist-in-game-progression-checklist.50668/ Boss Checklist Mod] is installed, their unique despawn message will be "The Profaned Guardians continue to guard the Proflames.”
