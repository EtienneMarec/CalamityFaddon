'''Tarragon Cloak''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player upon activating {{key|Armor Set Bonus}} while wearing the Melee [[Tarragon armor|Tarragon]] or [[Auric Tesla armor|Auric Tesla]] armors. It reduces the player's contact damage by 50% for 10 seconds. This buff has a [[Tarragon Cloak Cooldown|cooldown]] of 30 seconds after it runs out.
