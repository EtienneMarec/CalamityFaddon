The '''Wrenching whip''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[whip]] that can be purchased after giving its [[Blueprint]] to the [[Collector]]. Upon striking a non [[Boss]] enemy, the enemy will be pulled closer to the player.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Notes ==
*The [[Blueprint]] for this item is obtained rarely from killing worm AI [[Enemies]] after [[Ravager]] has been defeated.
*This weapon can be dangerous to use against enemies with a high contact damage.
