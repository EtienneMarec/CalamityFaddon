The '''Illustrious Knives''' are a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] weapon. They are the final upgrade to the [[Monstrous Knives]] and the [[Moonstone Knives]]. When used, the weapon launches a spread of 4-8 slow moving green knives, similar to the [[Vampire Knives]]. The knives are destroyed upon contact with tiles, but will rapidly accelerate and home in on nearby enemies. All knives will inflict [[God Slayer Inferno]] upon striking an enemy.

When a knife hits an enemy, the player is [[Lifesteal|healed]] by 1.5% of the damage dealt, and a significant amount of mana is restored. This lifesteal and mana regeneration is far stronger than either of the weapons previous forms.

== Notes ==
*The Illustrious Knives have a slightly higher chance than Vampire Knives or Empyrean Knives to throw out greater amounts of knives per usage. While they all throw a minimum of 4 knives, the predecessors have 4 independent chances of 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, and 1/16 to increase the number of knives by 1, placing their average number of knives per throw at 4.94. The Illustrious Knives' 4 independent chances are 1/2, 1/4, 1/6, and 1/8, meaning its average number of knives is 5.04.
