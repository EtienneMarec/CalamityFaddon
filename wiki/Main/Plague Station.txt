The '''Plague Station''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Mount]] summoning item that drops from [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. When used, it summons a flying chair mount that behaves similar to the UFO mount summoned by the [[Cosmic Car Key]]. It has a high overall movement speed and takes very little time to accelerate to its maximum speed.

The Plague Throne has a top speed of 51 mph both vertically and horizontally, for an overall max speed of 62 mph. Holding the {{key|Exo Chair Slow Down}} key will multiply its speed by 75%, while holding the {{key|Exo Chair Speed Up}} key will multiply its speed by 150%.
