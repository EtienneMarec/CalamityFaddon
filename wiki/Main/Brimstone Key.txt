The '''Brimstone Key''' was an [[unobtainable]] item. It had no purpose when it was removed.

== Trivia ==
*The key previously allowed the player to access the since removed [[Locked Ashen Chest]]s when the key was their inventory. It was not consumed when opening the chest, nor was the lock removed on the chest when opened.
*The shape of the key is the alchemical symbol for brimstone or sulphur, also known as the Leviathan Cross.
