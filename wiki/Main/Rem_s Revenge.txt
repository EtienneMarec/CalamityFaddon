The '''Rem's Revenge''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[flail]]. It throws out a gravity-defying spike ball that travels through solid tiles. Upon travelling far enough or on contact with 4 targets, the flail returns to the player. On hit, the flail inflicts the '''Wither''' [[debuff]] which reduces enemy [[defense]] by 20. The first five hits spawn a blood explosion which deals 50% of the weapon's damage and inflicts the [[Burning Blood]] debuff.
