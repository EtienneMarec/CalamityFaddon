'''Ablaze''' is a [[debuff]] of the [[Elemental System]], inflicted on enemies by [[Fire (element)|Fire]] weapons. It lasts 2 seconds and is refreshed by every new hit that inflicts it.

== Effects ==
* Loses 6 / 20 / 60 life per second (pre-[[Hardmode]] / [[Hardmode]] / after the [[Moon Lord]])
* Defense reduced by 10%

== Causes ==
* Any hit from a weapon with the Fire element (or from its projectiles), with a {{mode|100%|25%}} chance per hit (Full Fandom / Calamity++, see [[Fandom Accuracy]]).
* Enemies [[Elemental System#Elemental Attributes|resistant]] to Fire are immune.

== Notes ==
* Enemies affected by elemental debuffs show particles of the element's color instead of a debuff icon.
* Effects of several elemental debuffs add up, capped at 50% for slowing, damage reduction, defense reduction and missing.
