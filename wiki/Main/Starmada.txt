The '''Starmada''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[ranged weapon]], the direct upgrade to the [[Starfleet]], and the final upgrade of the [[Star Cannon]]. It automatically fires a barrage of five random star-related projectiles. These projectiles do not pierce and have randomly varying movement speeds, even amongst others of the same type, although the plasma blasts are always the fastest. The Starmada initially fires rather slowly, but fires faster the longer the player uses the weapon. Upon reaching around 10 seconds of sustained fire, a ring of particles is emitted from the player to indicate that they've reached the maximum fire-rate. It uses [[Fallen Star]]s as ammo.
