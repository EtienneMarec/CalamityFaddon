The '''Comet Quasher''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. The sword does not fire any projectile when swung, but summons 3 falling meteors towards the mouse cursor whenever it strikes an enemy. The meteors resemble those summoned by the [[Meteor Staff]] and deal half the damage of the sword hit that spawned them.
