'''Clamor Noctus''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] which is an upgrade to the [[Wyvern's Call]] and the final upgrade of the [[Sky Glaze]]. It fires 3 projectiles from the sky that are either Night Wyverns or Stars. Stars deal 15% of the weapon's damage, inflicting the [[Moon Burnt]] [[debuff]], and sticking on enemies and tiles for 2 seconds before exploding for 135% damage. Night Wyverns do not have special effects, but they deal 375% of the weapon's damage.
