The '''Aeries''' was a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[gun]] that autofired and was a direct upgrade to the [[Cursed Capper]]. When [[Musket Ball]]s were used as [[ammo]], it fired a light blue shockblast round that emitted an aesthetic particle cloud on impact. It inflicted two hits of damage per bullet and [[Lifesteal|returned]] life to the wielder equivalent to 5% of the damage dealt to the enemy.

Its best [[modifier]] was '''Unreal'''.
