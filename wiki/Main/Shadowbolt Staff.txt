The '''Shadowbolt Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mod e[[wand]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Shadowbeam Staff]]. It shoots a beam of purple light, which bounces 8 times before disappearing. With every bounce, the damage is increased by 15%. With every enemy pierced, the damage is increased by 10%.
