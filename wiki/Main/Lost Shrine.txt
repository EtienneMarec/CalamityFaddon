The '''Lost Shrine''' is an unique structure that generates at the [[Jungle]]-side of [[Ocean]], at the bottom, close to the edges of the map. It features a stand with the interactable Orb inside of it.

Right-clicking the Orb while the [[Nightmare Mode]] is activated in the current world will play an unique animation and summon the [[Anahita]]. Right-clicking the Orb without the [[Nightmare Mode]] enabled will do nothing.

== Trivia ==
*The Lost Shrine always spawns on the [[Jungle]]-side of the [[Ocean]] to prevent it from spawning in the [[Sulphurous Sea]].
*This is the smallest structure added by the Tranquility Mod and the addon doesn't change the worldgen in order for it to have enough space at all.
