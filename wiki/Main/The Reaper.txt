'''The Reaper''' is a Godseeker Mode [[rogue weapon]] that drops from [[Reaper Shark]]s. When fired, it throws out a reaper scythe that homes in onto [[enemies]]. Each reaper scythe will hit an enemy twelve times before disappearing, inflicting the [[Crush Depth]] [[debuff]] onto enemies.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with The Reaper will cause four scythes to be thrown instead of one.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].

== Trivia ==
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Valediction]].
