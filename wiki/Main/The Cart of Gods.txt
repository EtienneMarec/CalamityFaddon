'''The Cart of Gods''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Mount]] summoning item. When used on [[Minecart Track]]s, it summons a rideable cart with a trail of 17 body segments and 1 tail segment.

The Devourer Cart has a top running speed of 112 mph with an acceleration of 36 tiles/second<sup>2</sup> (49 mph/second). Similar to other minecarts, the Devourer Cart is able to jump with a similar height and acceleration.
