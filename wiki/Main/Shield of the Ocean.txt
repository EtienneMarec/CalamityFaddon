The '''Shield of the Ocean''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that grants +2 [[defense]], and an additional 5 defense when submerged in a liquid. It also increases the player's life regen by 2 and movement speed by 10% when equipped alongside the [[Victide armor]] set.
