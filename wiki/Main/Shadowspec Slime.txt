The '''Shadowspec Slime''' is a post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[slime]] which spawns naturally in the [[Underworld]] after [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] has been defeated. They exist to drop [[Shadowspec Ore]], so that the player cannot use up all the ore available to them.
