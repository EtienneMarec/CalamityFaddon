The '''Bonebreaker''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]] that is an upgrade to [[Bone Javelin]]s. It fires javelins that stick to enemies for a long time, then bursting into damaging rocky shrapnels. Up to six javelin projectiles can be stuck on an enemy at once, totalling 120 damage per second from the [[Venom]] and [[Penetrated]] [[debuffs]].
