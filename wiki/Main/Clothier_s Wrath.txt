'''Clothier's Wrath''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[magic weapon]] that drops from the [[Clothier]]. When used it fires three shadowflame skulls that pierce four times and inflict the [[Shadowflame]] [[debuff]]. Each skull cannot pass through blocks and will explode after traveling a short distance.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Trivia ==
*The shadowflame skull projectiles fired have an identical sprite to the projectiles fired by the [[Clothier]].
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Book of Skulls]].
