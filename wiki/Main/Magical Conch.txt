The '''Magical Conch''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] that is purchased from the [[Sea King]] at any time. It will run on the ground, dealing contact damage to [[enemies]]. If the player is flying, the Hermit Crab will float alongside the player, but won't directly attack enemies.

{{Modifier|summon}}

== Notes ==
*Due to the Hermit Crab's low jump height, it is not too effective against flying enemies.
*Unlike other minions, Hermit Crabs cannot be re-summoned unless they are summoned directly on an enemy, or until the buff is removed.
*Due to the Hermit Crab’s lack of active flight and an inability to move through walls, it often fails to reach its target and gets constantly stuck in chasms.
