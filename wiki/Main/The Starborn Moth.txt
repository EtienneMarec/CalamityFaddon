'''The Starborn Moth''' (Japanese: スターボーン・フェイ ''Sutābōn fei'') is a Godseeker Mode [[boss]] that is intended to be fought after defeating all three [[Sentinels of the Devourer]].

Defeating her will cause the [[Starborn Princess]] to move in.

==Spawn==
The Starborn Moth can be spawned with a [[Celestial Geode]] in The Hallow during the daytime or in Space at any time. 


==Behavior==
The Starborn Moth has many varying attacks during both stages, and deals no contact damage to the player during her first phase. During her second phase, she will now deal contact damage to the player. She is a flying boss at best, and will consistently fly towards the player if ran away

=== In all phases ===

* The Starborn Moth will perk her ears up, this is when she is listening out for you. This also means she becomes more vulnerable as she briefly does not attack and her defense significantly decreased. 
* She will slightly hover over to the player only to then push herself back and create an influx of wind with her wings. This causes the player to be pushed back, only for her then to charge back at the player with her horns. This is the only time during phase one where she can actually deal contact damage and during phase two, the damage is significantly increased.
* Straying too far away from The Starborn Moth will cause her to stunt the player and slowly pull them in via gravity until she is in the player's reach again. 
* In some cases, she will go idle for a very short period of time, before suddenly lashing out and spreading her wings out as far as she can. She will summon Fae Dust from her wings as projectiles which are to be avoided. These projectiles will inflict  [[God Slayer Inferno]] on the player.

==Trivia==
*The Starborn's Moth theme is 'Starborn', which was made by the artist Evobyte. 
*At the end of the fight, considering the Starborn Moth is entirely made out of 'rock', she will shatter into pieces of crystal and rock and the [[Starborn Princess]] will drop out of her. Lore wise, this hints that the [[Starborn Princess]] is the same person as the Starborn Moth.
*The Starborn Moth is known to be half fae and half moth, hence why she drops fae dust.
*Her Japanese name translates to 'starborn fae' opposed to 'starborn moth'
