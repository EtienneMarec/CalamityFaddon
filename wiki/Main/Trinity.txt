The '''Trinity''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings. When swung, the sword launches four glowing projectiles in a small spread. The projectiles can be red, purple, or blue, and are unaffected by gravity. All projectiles cannot pierce nor pass through solid tiles.
