The '''Clockwork Bow''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]]. It rapidly fires out cogs with a large spread. Each hit with a cog increases the bow’s stacked damage value, up to a total of 50 times. After 50 hits with a bow, a Precision Blast will launch out at an incredibly fast speed, dealing high damage. The stacked damage value will reset to 0.

It uses Cogs as ammunition, which are purchased from the Steampunker.

Firing this bow near the Steampunker (in a radius of 100 blocks) will double this bow’s damage, knockback, and velocity. This is possibly a reference to how the Steampunker is adept at steampunking, such as with weapons like this bow.
