The '''Hellion Flower Spear''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spear]]. On each swing it fires out a projectile that pierces blocks and enemies twice, while also inflicting the [[Venom]] [[debuff]] to any hit enemy. Whenever the spear or the projectile inflicts a critical hit, a single petal, such as those from [[Orichalcum armor]], will fly in toward the target's position.
