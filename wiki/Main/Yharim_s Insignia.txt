The '''Yharim's Insignia''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] obtained by completing the [[Pot of Pain]] challenge within the [[Jungle Temple]] that grants the player the following effects:
*10% increased damage when under 50% life
*10% increased melee damage and speed
*10% additionally increased [[true melee]] damage
*Melee attacks inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]]
*Temporary immunity to [[lava]]
*Increased melee [[knockback]]

{{PAGENAME}} can also be crafted after completing the Pot of Pain.
