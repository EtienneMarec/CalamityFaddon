The '''Lifehunt Scythe''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Melee weapons|melee weapon]]. When swung, it fires a short-range projectile that damages enemies on contact, piercing twice before dissipating on the third hit or after 4 seconds. Attacks landed by the scythe itself [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 5 HP per hit.
