The '''Supreme Mana Potion''' is a Godseeker Mode [[potion]] crafted using a [[Super Mana Potion]] and [[Phantoplasm]]. Upon drinking, it restores 400 of the player's mana and inflicts the [[Mana Sickness]] [[debuff]].
