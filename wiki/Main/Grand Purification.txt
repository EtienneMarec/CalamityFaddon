The '''Grand Purification''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Dedicated content|Developer]] [[Wands|Wand]]. It casts a fast blue fireball that explodes into a lingering explosion of flames. This explosion ignores half of the [[Immunity frames]] and its damage increases by 150% for every frames said explosion lasted for. It also inflicts the [[Moon Burnt]] Debuff.

{{Key|Open}} with this weapon instead unleashes a holy supernova that flies slowly until touching either an enemy, a tile, or has lingered long enough. Upon expiry, it explodes into a gigantic explosion, damaging enemies caught in it. Said explosion ignores all NPC [[Immunity frames]], deals 500% of the weapon's damage, and multiplies by 2.5 per frames that the explosion lasted for. It also strips enemies of fire-based immunities, multiplies all fire-based debuff damage by 10x for 10 seconds, and makes them take 3 times more damage from all [[Fire (element)|Fire]] and [[Holy (element)|Holy]] elemental attacks for 15 seconds, regardless of their resistance. Additionally, after casting it, the player gains the ability to heal 50% their life after taking fatal damage for 1 minute, indicated by the halo that appears above the player.

This ability has a cooldown of 2 minutes, in which during that time, {{Key|Open}} summons a light pillar at the cursor's position that damages enemies caught in them. These pillars deal 75% its weapons' damage.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon actually has a unique [[Rarity]] but is referred here as Hot Pink rarity for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity alternates between Cerulean Frost ({{color|#6D9BC3 ●|#6D9BC3}}) and Kiwi ({{color|#8EE53F ●|#8EE53F}}) colors.
*The weapon itself, along with the quote in its tooltip and its elements directly references [https://epicbattlefantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Natalia Natalia], a boss enemy from the [[wikipedia:Epic_Battle_Fantasy|Epic Battle Fantasy]] series (More specifically, Epic Battle Fantasy 5).
**Said reference is made more blatant by the fact that it is identical to the [https://epicbattlefantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Sol_Spear Sol Spear] from that same series, barring the color, which is changed to match the dedicatee's profile picture.
**Its alternate attack further reinforces this reference, being a combination of both the [https://epicbattlefantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova Supernova] and [https://epicbattlefantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Genesis Genesis], both being Limit Breaks that Natalia (and by extension, [https://epicbattlefantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Natalie Natalie]) uses.
