The '''Empyrean Knives''' are a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee]] weapon that are a direct upgrade to the [[Vampire Knives]]. When used, the player throws a spread of 4 to 8 homing knives, each dealing damage and [[Lifesteal|healing]] the player for 0.5% of the damage dealt. The knives can bounce off of walls twice and travel much farther than the Vampire Knives before spinning and fading away.
