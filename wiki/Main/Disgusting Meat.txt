'''Disgusting Meat''' is a Pre-[[Hardmode]] crafting material. It is primarily obtained by killing [[Zombie]]s, but can also be obtained from [[bosses]] like the [[Eater of Worlds]], the [[Dragonfolly]], and [[Boar Cat]], along with the [[Party Girl]] NPC.

It is used to craft all the various [[Tainted Potions]], as well as just being a general nuisance to obtain.
