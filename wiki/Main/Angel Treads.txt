The '''Angel Treads''' are a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] which are a combination and upgrade to multiple movement-based coverings, namely the [[Frostspark Boots]], the [[Lava Waders]], and the [[Harpy Ring]].

When equipped, they grant the ability to fly, sprint up to 40 mph, walk on [[tgc:Liquids|liquids]], immunity to the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]], 4 seconds of immunity to [[lava]] damage, improved movement on [[tgc:Ice#Blocks|ice blocks]], increased movement speed by 12%, increased maximum acceleration by 36%, and boost maximum flight time by 10%.

== Notes ==
*When Angel Treads are equipped, equipping Rocket Boots or any of their upgrades will not stack their flight boost.
*The lava immunity stacks with the [[Lava Charm]] and [[Lava Waders]].

== Trivia ==
*The Angel Treads were originally known as the "Falcon Boots".
*The vanilla [[Terraspark Boots]] function similarly to the Angel Treads.
