The '''Yin-Yo''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[yoyo]]. If enemies are near, the yoyo will randomly fire Dark Shards towards the target or Light Shards away from the target. Both shards will travel back and forth five times before fading away, passing through walls and piercing any number of enemies. When the yoyo returns to the player, it will be automatically re-thrown if they are still holding the fire button.
