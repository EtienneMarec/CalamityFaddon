The '''Bloody Worm Tooth''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[The Perforators]]. It grants 5% increased damage reduction, 5% increased melee damage, and 5% increased melee speed when equipped in an covering slot. The bonuses are doubled when below 50% life. The damage reduction will stack with the damage reduction of the [[Worm Scarf]].

If the player lives in a [[Corruption]] world, defeating [[The Hive Mind]] for the first time will allow the [[Dryad]] to sell the Bloody Worm Tooth.
