The '''Insidious Impaler''' is a Godseeker Mode [[spear]] which drops from tier 3 [[Orthocera]]s. It throws a spear that is unaffected by gravity and sticks to blocks and [[enemies]], releasing toxic and sulphuric clouds that inflict the [[Venom]] and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuffs]].
