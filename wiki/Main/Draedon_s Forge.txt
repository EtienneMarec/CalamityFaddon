'''Draedon's Forge''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting station]] used to craft end-game items. It counts as a [[Work Bench]], [[Hardmode Forge]], a [[Beach Ball]]]], a [[Tinkerer's Workshop]], a [[Table]], a [[Chair]], a [[Demon Altar|Demon]]/[[Crimson Altar]], and an [[Ancient Manipulator]].

== Trivia ==

* Crafting Draedon's Forge requires 65536 Delicious Meat, which takes up 66 inventory slots on its own. This makes it impossible to craft without the use of a chest or similar containers that can hold crafting materials.
* The number 65536 is a reference made by one of the developers, 1965 being the year he was born in and 536 being his favourite number.
