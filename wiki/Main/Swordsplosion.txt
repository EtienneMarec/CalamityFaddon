The '''Swordsplosion''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that drops from [[The Dragonfolly]]. The sword fires several beam swords that converge on the cursor, and causes even more beam swords to rain from the sky when swung. The sword rotates between firing different colored beam swords.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Trivia ==
*This weapon is a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/SWORDSPLOSION!!! weapon of the same name] from ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]], a weapon which reappears in [[wikipedia:Tiny_Tina's_Wonderlands|Tiny Tina's Wonderlands.]]''
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Ark of the Elements]].
