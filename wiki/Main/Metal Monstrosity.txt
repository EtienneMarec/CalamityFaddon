The '''Metal Monstrosity''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] non-consumable [[spiky balls|spiky ball]]. It throws a heavy gravity-affected metal ball that breaks upon hitting a tile or enemy. Upon breaking it releases 3 spiky balls and 3 spikes that stick into enemies inflicting damage over the time and fading away shortly after.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Metal Monstrosity will make the metal ball shoot several spikes in random directions until the metal ball breaks.
