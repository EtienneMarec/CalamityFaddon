'''Cryophobia''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] sold by the [[Archmage]] NPC for {{gc|36}} after defeating all the [[Mechanical Boss]]es. When used it fires a light blue growing wave that splits into 3 twice, for 9 final waves, each pierces three times and inflicts the [[Glacial State]] and [[Frostburn]] [[debuffs]].
