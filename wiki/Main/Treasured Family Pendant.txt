The '''Treasured Family Pendant''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[coverings|covering]] that can be rarely found in Gold Chests found in the [[Underground]] and [[Cavern]] biomes. When equipped, it increases the player's acceleration and deceleration by 50%. This stacks with the [[tgc:Hermes Boot|Hermes Boot]] and its variants/upgrades. It also emits a faint light and increases Damage Reduction by 8%. This DR gain is calculated ''after'' everything else, meaning it is not affected by neither DR scaling nor debuffs that reduces DRs.

== Notes ==
*This has a 1/30 (3.23%) chance to appear in any underground gold chests.

== Trivia ==
*This item is directly based on the same pendant that [https://ongezellig.fandom.com/wiki/Coco Coco Schoppenboer-Potgieter] wears nearly all the time in the dutch webcartoon "Ongezellig".
**Its tooltip also directly references it containing her deceased family's photo.
