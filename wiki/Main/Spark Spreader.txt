The '''Spark Spreader''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[flamethrowers|flamethrower]] that uses [[gel]] as [[ammunition]], and is currently the earliest obtainable flamethrower. It shoots short-ranged orange flames when used. The flames inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] and pierce three enemies. There is a 70% chance for gel not to be consumed upon firing.
