The '''Cinders of Lament''' is a Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It is the [[exhumed]] form of the [[Entropy's Vigil]].  When used, a full-sized husk of [[Supreme Cataclysm]] or [[Supreme Catastrophe]] appears at the player's cursor. After 0.67 seconds, its damage increases by 45% and charges at the player. The brothers pierce infinitely, travel through solid tiles, and are capable of damaging players or [[Town NPCs]] along with enemies. Players and Town NPCs take {{dv|140|280}} damage regardless of the player's damage boosts.
