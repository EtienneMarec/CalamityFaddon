'''The Last Lightice''' is a [[Hardmode]] non-consumable [[Spy]] [[weapon]] that is dropped by [[Snow Balla]]s. It rapidly throws out chunks of magic ice which turn into solid blocks of [[Ice Rod|temporary ice]] upon impact with enemies or tiles. 

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with The Last Lightice shoots out a burst of 20 blocks in a 25 degree spread.
