The '''Wrath of the Gods''' is a craftable Epilogue [[Spell tomes|spell tome]] and is the final upgrade to the [[Wrath of the Ancients]]. When used, it casts a godly pulse at the mouse cursor, which fires a ring of 48 homing Traitorous Fireballs inflict numerous debuffs on intervals for a significant amount of time.

The godly pulse will explode into a hexagram of projectiles and fire a red deathray that homes in towards the closest enemy every ten rounds of Traitorous Fireball rings that deal 75% and 500% of the weapon's base damage respectively. The godly pulse does '''not''' despawn when it explodes.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Mythical'''.
