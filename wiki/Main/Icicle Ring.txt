The '''Icicle Ring''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[Frost Imp]]s. While equipped, the players health will drain at 15 health per second until it reaches exactly 1/3 of the players health rounded up. In return, the covering provides a 100% reduction on mana cost, and a 50% increase in magic damage.

== Trivia ==
*The item is a reference to the Thorn Ring from the game Deltarune.
**The tooltip is a slight alteration to that of the Thorn Rings.
**The items effects also mirror its Deltarune counterpart, draining 2/3rds of the users health and lowering the cost of magic attacks.
**The sell price of the Icicle Ring is the same as the purchase price of the Thorn Ring.
