The '''Astral Injection''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[Buff Potions|buff potion]] made from [[Aureus Cell]]s. Upon consumption it grants the player +180 mana regen per second at the cost of the player losing 5 health and being inflicted with the [[Mana Sickness]] [[debuff]].

== Notes ==
*Astral Injections may also be obtained from [[Astral Crate]]s once [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated.
*This item essentially acts as a mana potion that restores 900 Mana, over a time of 5 seconds, which is superior to [[Supreme Mana Potion]]s later in the game. However, they are not automatically consumed by the [[Mana Flower]] or [[Ethereal Talisman]] and are arguably harder to obtain en masse.
*The damage inflicted by using this item does not get affected by [[Defense]] or [[Damage Reduction]], and will not reset adrenaline while fighting a [[boss]].

== Trivia ==
*This item is a reference to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroin Heroin] from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_insurance Real Life]
*This item’s name is a pun based on the Astral Infection biome.
*If the player is killed by the damage inflicted upon consumption of the potion, the death message "<PlayerName>'s blood vessels burst from drug overdose." will display.
