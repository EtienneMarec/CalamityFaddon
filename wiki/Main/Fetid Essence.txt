'''Fetid Essence''' was a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[crafting material]] that dropped from [[tgc:Devourer|Devourer]]s and [[World Feeder]]s in [[The Corruption]]. Additionally, it was possible to obtain 5-8 Fetid Essences from [[Corrupt Crate]]s at a 25% drop chance. 

It was used to craft [[Blood Relic]]s, [[Guide Voodoo Doll]]s, and the [[Corrupt Flask]]. It was also the Corruption counterpart to the [[Bloodletting Essence]].
