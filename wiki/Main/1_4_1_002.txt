The '''1.4.1.002''' update made some bosses more fair, along with buffing the [[Revengeance Mode]] [[Golem]]. It also fixed several issues from Update [[1.4.1.001]], changed the recipes for the Ancient and Ashen [[Furniture sets]], and resprites for the [[Cosmilite Brick|Cosmilite]] furniture set.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Cosmilite Basin]] and [[Cosmilite Sconce]].

== Changes ==
=== Bosses and Enemies ===
*Nerfed [[Armored Digger]]'s health from {{dv|40,000|80,000}} to {{dv|30,000|60,000}}.
*Small [[Astrum Deus]] worms no longer have increased firerate based on how much health the player has left.
*Nerfed free [[Astrum Deus Probe]]'s health from {{dv|2000|4000}} to {{dv|600|1200}}.
*Decreased the frequency at which [[Brimstone Elemental]] fires her Hellfireballs.
*[[Calamitas]] changes:
**Decreased Calamitas and her brothers' speeds.
**Decreased the frequency at which phase 2 fires her lasers.
**Brothers can no longer instantly shoot their respective projectiles as soon as they spawn.
**Catastrophe longer shoots fireballs more frequently based on how much health the player has left.
*Decreased [[Revengeance Mode]] [[Duke Fishron]]'s charge speed.
*Revengeance Mode [[Golem]] now inflicts [[Armor Crunch]] and enrages outside the [[Jungle Temple]].
*[[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]] now summons his [[Bumblebirb]]s closer to himself.
*Decreased Revengeance Mode [[Plantera]]'s speed and projectile firerate.
*[[Siren and Leviathan]] changes:
**Decreased The Leviathan's speed.
**Increased the distance from the player at which The Leviathan starts her charge.
**Increased the distance from the player at which The Siren starts her charge.
**Decreased the speed of The Siren's Frost Mists.
*Decreased Revengeance Mode [[Skeletron Prime]]'s speed.
*Decreased Revengeance Mode [[Spazmatism]]'s speed.

=== Items ===
*[[Ancient Altar]]s, [[Ancient Basin]]s, [[Ancient Bathtub]]s, [[Ancient Bed]]s, [[Ancient Bookcase]]s, [[Ancient Chair]]s,  [[Ancient Chandelier]]s, [[Ancient Door]]s, [[Ancient Dresser]]s, [[Ancient Lamp]]s, [[Ancient Lantern]]s, [[Ancient Monolith]]s, [[Ancient Pipe Organ]]s, [[Ancient Sink]]s, [[Ancient Sofa]]s, [[Ancient Table]]s, and [[Ancient Work Bench]]es now use {{rare|3}} rarity instead of {{rare|0}}, and can no longer be crafted with their Ashen counterparts and [[Essence of Chaos|Essences of Chaos]] at an [[Ashen Altar]]. <!---Ancient Candelabra, Candle, and Chest never had these recipes--->
*Ancient Altar now uses 10 [[Brimstone Slag]] and 10 [[Charred Ore]] in its recipe instead of 25 of each.
*Ancient Basin now uses 10 Brimstone Slag and 5 Charred Ore in its recipe instead of 25 of each.
*Ancient Monolith now uses 9 Charred Ore in its recipe instead of 3 Charred Ore and 6 [[Unholy Core]]s.
*Renamed Ancient Piano to Ancient Pipe Organ.
*All Ashen furniture pieces now use {{rare|3}} rarity instead of {{rare|0}}.
*Ashen Altars, [[Ashen Basin]]s, [[Ashen Bathtub]]s, [[Ashen Bed]]s, [[Ashen Bookcase]]s, [[Ashen Chair]]s,  [[Ashen Chandelier]]s, [[Ashen Door]]s, [[Ashen Dresser]]s, [[Ashen Lamp]]s, [[Ashen Lantern]]s, [[Ashen Monolith]]s, [[Ashen Pipe Organ]]s, [[Ashen Sink]]s, [[Ashen Sofa]]s, [[Ashen Table]]s, and [[Ashen Work Bench]]es can no longer be crafted with their Ancient counterparts and Unholy Cores at an Ancient Altar. <!---Ashen Candelabra, Candle, and Chest never had these recipes--->
*Ashen Altar now uses 10 [[Smooth Brimstone Slag]] and 10 Unholy Cores in its recipe instead of 25 [[Cinderplate]] and 25 Brimstone Slag.
*Ashen Basin now uses 10 Smooth Brimstone Slag and 5 Unholy Cores in its recipe instead of 25 Cinderplate and 25 Smooth Brimstone Slag.
*[[Ashen Chest]]s can no longer be crafted with 1 [[Ashen Key]] and 1 [[Locked Ashen Chest]].
*Ashen Monolith now uses 9 Unholy Cores in its recipe instead of 3 Cinderplate and 6 Unholy Cores.
*Renamed Ashen Piano to Ashen Pipe Organ.
*Nerfed [[Aureate Booster]]'s flight time from 120 to 100.
*[[Divine Retribution]] now has a tooltip.
*Buffed [[Murasama]]'s damage from 666 to 999, and gave it a 30% critical strike chance.
*Nerfed the damages of the offensive and defensive guardians from the [[Profaned Soul Artifact]] from 400% of the player's summon damage to 150%.

=== Other ===
*Armor Crunch can now be inflicted on players.
*The [[Boss Health Bar]] no longer appears for any NPCs with under 400 health.
*Death Mode natural boss spawns are now indicated by a status message in chat shortly before they spawn.
*Reduced Death Mode natural boss spawnrates.
*The [[Drunk Princess]] can now mention "DevAesthetic" in her donor quote.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Cosmilite Brick]].
*Resprited [[Cosmilite Brick Wall]].
*Resprited [[Cosmilite Platform]].
*Resprited [[Cosmilite Bathtub]], [[Cosmilite Bed]], [[Cosmilite Bookcase]], [[Cosmilite Candelabra]], [[Cosmilite Candle]], [[Cosmilite Chair]], [[Cosmilite Chandelier]], [[Cosmilite Chest]], [[Cosmilite Clock]], [[Cosmilite Door]], [[Cosmilite Dresser]], [[Cosmilite Lamp]], [[Cosmilite Lantern]], [[Cosmilite Piano]], [[Cosmilite Sink]], [[Cosmilite Sofa]], [[Cosmilite Table]], and [[Cosmilite Work Bench]].

== Fixes ==
*Fixed a bug that prevented Armored Diggers from despawning.
*Fixed an issue that prevented damage from Sentries and Vanilla [[Minions]] from increasing the player's summoner [[Proficiency|proficiency]].
*Fixed an issue that prevented [[Boss Rush]] from progressing in multiplayer.
*Fixed an issue with the hitbox of [[Bear's Eye]]'s Bear pet.
*Fixed Juvenile [[Eidolon Wyrm]]s having inconsistent health values across its segments.
