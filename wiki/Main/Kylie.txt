'''Kylie''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Spy]] [[boomerang]] purchased from the [[Bandit]] for {{gc|9}}. When used, it throws a slow-moving boomerang that travels a medium distance before returning to the player. Up to three boomerangs can be thrown out at a time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Kylie will cause three short-range boomerangs with higher velocity to be thrown out.
