The '''Deathhail Staff''' is a Godseeker Mode [[wand]] that is dropped by [[The Devourer of Gods]]. It rains down a barrage of nebula lasers on the cursor's location upon use. The lasers will inflict the [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]] for 10 seconds and will reduce the number of [[Immunity frames]] given to an enemy by 80%. The lasers can pierce up to eight targets and will phase through blocks.

{{Modifier|magic}}

== Notes ==
*The projectiles do not disappear after reaching their pierce limit; they will continue to fly downwards and phase through enemies and tiles, but will no longer damage any NPCs in their path.
*Like [[Lunar Flare]], this weapon can be manipulated with the cursor's position to inflict damage over a wide area or focus it on a single target.
