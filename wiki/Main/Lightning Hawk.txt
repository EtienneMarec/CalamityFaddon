The '''Lightning Hawk''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]] that is a direct upgrade of the [[Magnum]]. When used, it fires a bullet similar to a [[High Velocity Bullet]] which cannot pierce. 1.33% of the target's health is added to the damage of each bullet.

Like the Magnum, it uses [[Magnum Rounds]] as ammo, which can be purchased from the [[Arms Dealer]] if the player has the Lightning Hawk in their inventory.

Once a boss is spawned, the Lightning Hawk is only fireable three times until the boss is defeated.
