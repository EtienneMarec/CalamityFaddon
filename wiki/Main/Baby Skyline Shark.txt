The '''Baby Skyline Shark''' is a post-[[Noxus]] [[item]] thats fished in the Upper Layer of [[Sky Abyss]]. It can be tossed inside Sky Abyss to summon a Baby Skyline shark Critter, all the enemies that attack Skyline Sharks can attack the babies too and they primarily target them over over normal Sharks or The Player, it can be used to get the [[Stratosoul]]s away from you in [[Death Mode]] as you cant move with them on you.

If a big Skyline Shark sees it it becomes enraged and does a series of dashes on the player.
