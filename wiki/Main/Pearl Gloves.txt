The '''Pearl Gloves''' are a [[magic weapon]] dropped by [[Goozma]]. They fire out spectral gloves which stick into enemies. Stuck fists do not deal damage. After defeating [[Supreme Calamitas]], the weapon fires out two gloves per shot instead of one, which hover near enemies and rapidly punch them. Right clicking causes all glove pairs to clap together, unleashing harmless soundwaves which stun enemies for 5 seconds. Bosses get stunned for 2 seconds.
