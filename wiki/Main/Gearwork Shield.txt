The '''Gearwork Shield''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spy]] [[boomerang]]. When hitting right click, all enemies that can be homed in on have their AI states recorded. Left clicking at any point after recording causes all enemies to reset back to when the snapshot had been taken. This ability has a 1 minute cooldown. During the cooldown, the shield can be thrown like a boomerang.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Gearwork Shield causes all enemies that get reset to take 20x the weapon's damage. If the cooldown is up, each hit of damage the shield does reduces the ability cooldown by 1 second.
