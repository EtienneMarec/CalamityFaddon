The '''Puff Shroom''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]] dropped by [[Crabulon]]. It summons a Puff Warrior which stays grounded and follows the player. When a target is nearby, it attempts to jump towards the target, spawning three damaging puff clouds which move towards the target when the minion is jumping if the target is within a 22.5-tile radius.
