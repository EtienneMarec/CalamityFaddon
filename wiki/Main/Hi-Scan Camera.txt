The '''Hi-Scan Camera''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Classless weapons|Classless Weapon]] dropped by [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]. It has 2 modes:
*Scan Mode: On use, creates a blinding flash that spans 10 tiles, taking any debuff the enemy inflicts, and then storing it unto itself. Only 3 debuffs could be stored at once. Additionally, it deals 200% its damage to any enemies tagged as "Organic", and can insta-kill [[Critters]], leaving behind a card as a purely visual effect.
*Snapshot Mode: When held, a viewfinder is brought up. Once released, all enemies within the viewfinder's area are inflicted said debuffs, and the debuffs stored are consumed. Additionally, it deals 300% its damage to any enemies tagged as "Spectral", and makes said enemies sweat after being captured as a purely visual effect.

The Hi-Scan Camera cannot be reforged, and therefore cannot have [[modifiers]].

**[https://cookierun.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Garlic_Cookie Black Garlic Cookie], an Epic Cookie from [[wikipedia:Cookie_Run|Cookie Run]], who screen-shot/records ghost sightings for her channel. The Snapshot Mode's ability to deal extra damage to "Spectral" enemies and its visual effect upon screenshotting them reflects that.
