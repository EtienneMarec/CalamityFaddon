The '''Pulse Rifle''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[ranged weapon]]. When fired, a large purple beam is fired that creates a small dust explosion when contacting a block or enemy. The beam will arc towards other nearby enemies once contacting an enemy.

The weapon consumes 0.24 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 250.
