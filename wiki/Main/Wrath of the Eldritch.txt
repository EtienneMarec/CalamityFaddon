The '''Wrath of the Eldritch''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Spell tomes|spell tome]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Wrath of the Ancients]]. When used, it casts an eldritch pulse at the mouse cursor, which fires 2 rings of 16 homing spirits at intervals for a significant amount of time.

The eldritch pulse will fire a deathray every three rounds of homing spirit rings that deals 150% of the weapon's base damage and points towards the closest enemy before arcing.

Its best [[Modifiers|modifier]] is '''Mythical'''.
