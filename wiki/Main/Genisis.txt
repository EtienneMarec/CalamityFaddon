The '''Genisis''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[magic gun]] that shoots a large concentrated beam. The main beam ignores damage frames, pierces enemies, and splits into two more beams when it reaches its maximum range. It also rapidly fires inaccurate smaller laser beams that cannot go through walls and do not pierce enemies. The smaller laser beams disappear after a short time.
