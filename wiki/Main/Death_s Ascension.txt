}}The '''Death's Ascension''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[melee weapon]]. When used with its primary fire (left-click), the scythe does a horizontal slash that causes 3-5 souls to fire out of the back of a hit enemy. These souls can come in either purple or blue colors which either heal the player for 2-5 health or deal 10% of the weapon's damage respectively. When used with its secondary fire (right-click), it fires an even spread of five slashes that create rip effects upon enemy hits similar to the [[Exoblade]] which deal 20% of the weapon's damage.
