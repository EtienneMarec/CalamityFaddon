'''Exoquartz (Japanese: エクソクォーツ Ekusoku~ōtsu)''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in [[The Exosphere]] after [[XM-05 Thanatos]] has been defeated. She charges and rams into the player at speeds of up to 300 mph and can jump approximately 49 tiles high and 400 tiles wide. When charging towards the player, she will create {{dv|6|8|9|10|12}} evenly spaced Exo Overload Beams which spin around clockwise or counterclockwise similarily to [[XF-09 Ares]] every 10 seconds.

== Trivia ==

* Exoquartz is based on the game Minecraft, which has a special material called Quartz (the material this enemy derives its name from)
