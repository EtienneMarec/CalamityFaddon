The '''Primordial Earth''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[spell tome]]. It is a direct upgrade to the [[Death Valley Duster]], firing dust blows with a greater area of effect and dealing higher DPS. The dust blows can pass through tiles and burst into explosions of dust upon hitting an enemy, which each last for about a second and deal damage on contact.
