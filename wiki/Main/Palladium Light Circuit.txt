The '''Palladium Light Circuit''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that turns the player into a light mech. The mech form increases the max hp by 100, defence by 30 and jump height by 25%, but it halves the movement speed. It is a counterpart to [[Cobalt Night Circuit]]
