The '''Continental Greatbow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]]. It fires 3 arrows at once, quickly followed by two more arrows that will always be either [[Cursed Arrow|Cursed]], [[Ichor Arrow|Ichor]], or [[Hellfire Arrow]]s. Each of the two additional arrows can be different or the same type. All [[Wooden Arrow]]s fired will convert into [[Flaming Arrow]]s.
