The Volcanic Reaper Shark is a buffed version of the [[Reaper Shark]] added by the Polyphemalus addon. It is spawned by right clicking an unprompted '''Reaper Shark''' while holding a stack of 22 [[Abyss Bar|Abyss Bars]]. The boss is a mildly stronger version of the reaper shark, inflicting [[La Ruga|Scorinfestation]] and [[Crush Depth]].

It's also faster and does more damage. It can be exclusively spawned after [[Golem]] has been defeated, and Attempting to spawn it before '''Golem''' has been defeated will result in you having your breath set to 0 and giving you the [[Stoned]] Debuff. This together forces you to die.

The '''Volcanic Reaper Shark''' acts similarly to the Reaper Shark. Uniquely, it cannot be picked up by the [[Lifeform Analyzer]], but rather, having the Lifeform Analyzer enabled enrages it. To avoid enraging the Volcanic Reaper Shark, you must not go higher than layer 3 of the [[Abyss]]. Upon reaching 60% HP, it gains an animation where it becomes invulnerable to all damage and takes a bite out of Molten Pyre Mantles or Abyss Ores. If above layer 3, this animation failing will make it despawn and remove all Abyss items from your inventory.
