The '''Mounted Scanner''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. It summons a floating machine that surrounds the player. When an enemy is nearby, the machine fires red laser beams at them. The beam's damage is reduced by 40% every hit.

The weapon consumes 0.85 units of [[charge]] per use and has a maximum charge of 85.
