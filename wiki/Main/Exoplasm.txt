'''Exoplasm''' is a Godseeker Mode [[crafting material]] obtained by using [[Bottle]]s on blocks that had recently been touched by [[XF-09 Ares]]' plasma fireball projectiles. It is used to craft [[Totality Tides]] {{fantag|Catalyst}} and its [[weapon]] ingredients.

== Tips ==
*It may be wise to go into the Exo Mechs fight with the goal of collecting Exoplasm as your primary goal and not caring about winning as it allows you to focus on collecting Exoplasm and actually fighting the boss respectively.

== Trivia ==
*The item is a reference to [https://minecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Breath Dragon's Breath] from the game, ''[[wikipedia:Minecraft|Minecraft]]'', which has a similar obtainment method.
