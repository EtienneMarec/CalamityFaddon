The '''Rift Reeler''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[fishing rod]]. When swung, three to five fishing lines are deployed at once instead of one. In addition to this, it can be used to fish in [[Lava]].

== Trivia ==
*This item's tooltip is a reference to a quote said by [[wikipedia:Napoleon Bonaparte|Napoleon Bonaparte]].
*This item was originally known as the "Chaotic Spread Rod".
*Due to firing 3-5 lines at once, the Rift Reeler can be used to summon multiple [[Duke Fishron|Duke Fishrons]] or [[Old Duke|Old Dukes]] at once. It will use one [[Truffle Worm]] per Duke Fishron spawned, and one [[Bloodworm]] per Old Duke spawned.
