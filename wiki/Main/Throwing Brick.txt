The '''Throwing Brick''' is a craftable consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]]. Additionally, the [[Starter Bag]] contains 150 of them. When used, it throws a gravity affected brick that travels a short distance before falling to the ground and breaking.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Throwing Brick will cause the next brick to shatter into 3 fragments upon impact, which each deal 30% of the damage of the main brick.
