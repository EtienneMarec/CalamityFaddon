The '''1.4.5.005''' (or '''1.4.5.5''') update fixed a few minor issues during the [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] fight and slightly rebalanced a few [[Melee weapons]].
__NOTOC__

== Changes ==
=== Balance ===
*Increased the sword size of [[Aegis Blade]], [[Aftershock]], [[Ancient Crusher]], [[Animus]], [[Astral Blade]], [[Avalanche]], [[Blade of Enmity]], [[Brimstone Sword]], [[Comet Quasher]], [[Earth]], [[The Enforcer]], [[Grax]], [[Greentide]], [[Holy Collider]], [[The Last Mourning]], [[Mariana]], [[The Mutilator]], [[Stellar Striker]], [[Terra-cotta]], [[Terra Edge]], and [[Titan Arm]] by 50%.
*Increased the sword size of [[Evil Smasher]] by 100%.
*Nerfed [[Amidias' Trident]]'s projectile homing range by 67%.
*Decreased [[Astrum Deus]]' resistance to [[Grand Guardian]] explosions from 80% to 50%.
*Nerfed Comet Quasher's meteor damage from 75% base damage to 50%.

=== Additional Changes ===
*Increased the distance the player needs to be before the screen shader becomes black during the Providence, the Profaned Goddess fight.

== Fixes ==
*Fixed the [[Adrenaline]] and [[Rage Meter]]s not charging during the Providence fight.
