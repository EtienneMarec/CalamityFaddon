The '''Frost Flare''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Cryogen]].

When equipped, all of the player's melee attacks and melee projectiles inflict the [[Frostburn]] debuff to enemies. It also grants immunity to [[Frostburn]], [[Chilled]], and [[Frozen]], as well as reducing damage from "cold attacks". Additionally, it provides a minor boost to offensive stats at high health and significant boosts to [[defense]] and mobility at low health.

Like the [[Warmth Potion]], this covering reduces damage from all enemies and projectiles that can inflict the [[Chilled]] [[debuff]] by 30%. This is calculated separately from and is applied before defense and damage reduction calculations. This makes the Frost Flare extremely effective for farming [[Cryogen]] after the first kill and has significant benefits for the [[Frost Moon]] event.
