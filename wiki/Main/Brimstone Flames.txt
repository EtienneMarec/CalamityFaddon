'''Brimstone Flames''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by many sources throughout the Calamity Mod. If inflicted on an enemy, they lose 20 health per second. If inflicted on the player, they instead lose 8 health per second. The inflicted target will always emit bright red particles.

If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they lose 25 health per second instead of 20.

The player can become immune to Brimstone Flames by equipping the [[Asgardian Aegis]] [[covering]] or consuming [[Purified Jam]]. Equipping the [[Abaddon]] or [[Asgard's Valor]] coverings will reduce the damage from Brimstone Flames.
