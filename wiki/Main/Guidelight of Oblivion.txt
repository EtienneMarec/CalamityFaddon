The '''Guidelight of Oblivion''' is a Godseeker Mode [[sentry]] dropped by [[Scryllar]]s after defeating [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. It summons a soul lantern that floats up and down near the location it was summoned. The lantern summons red flames around it which home in on enemies, travel through tiles, and explode upon contact, inflicting the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] for three seconds.
