The '''Temporal Umbrella''' is a craftable Epilogue [[summon weapon]] that is the direct upgrade of [[Saros Possession]] and the final upgrade to the [[Sun Spirit Staff]]. It summons up to only one Magic Hat minion which summons elemental sniper rifles, axes, bats, hammers, umbrellas, and birds to attack nearby enemies. It requires 8 minion slots to use.

The Temporal Umbrella's various projectiles can inflict a variety of debuffs. They are listed below:
*Elemental Axes inflict [[Brimstone Flames]], [[Frostburn]], and [[Holy Flames]] for 1.5 seconds.
*Bullets inflict [[Betsy's Curse]], [[Ichor (debuff)|Ichor]], [[Armor Crunch]], [[War Cleave]], and [[Whispering Death]] for 3 seconds.

The combined effects of the debuffs cause the target to lose 95 health per second, lose 154 defense, have their damage reduction reduced by 25%, and become unable to move.

== Trivia ==
*The word "Temporal" in the weapon's name, the phrase in the tooltip "Surprisingly sturdy, I reckon this could defeat the Mafia in a single blow", the buff tooltip "The hat carries your soul, bound by a contract", and the weapon's visuals are a reference to the game ''[[wikipedia:A Hat in Time|A Hat in Time]]''.
**The use of a Top Hat and Umbrella in the crafting recipe is also a reference to ''A Hat in Time''.
*In the mod's source files, this weapon is internally named "Ben's Umbrella".
*This weapon actually has a unique rarity, but is referred to here as Hot Pink for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity alternates between purple ({{color|#D200FF ●|#D200FF}}) and yellow ({{color|#FFF818 ●|#FFF818}}) colors.
