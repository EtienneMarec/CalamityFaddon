The '''Ocean''' [[biome]] features eight new [[enemies]], two of which spawn after either [[Plantera]] or [[Calamitas]] are defeated. The [[Leviathan and Anahita]] [[bosses]] are summoned and fought in this biome after killing the [[???]] enemy.

In [[Death Mode]], [[tgc:Shark|Sharknado]]es spawn on the surface occasionally and [[tgc:Sharkron|Cthulhunado]]es spawn during [[Rain]].
