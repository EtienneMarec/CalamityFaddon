'''Red Wine''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It inflicts the '''Red Wine''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen by 1, and heals 200 life.  It also inflicts the [[Potion Sickness]] debuff for 1 minute.

If the player is under the effect of the [[Baguette (buff)|Baguette]] [[buff]], it heals 250 life but reduces life regen by 4 instead.
