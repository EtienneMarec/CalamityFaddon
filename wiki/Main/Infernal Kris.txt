'''Infernal Krises''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[daggers]]. When used, a dagger is thrown that begins to spin after traveling a short distance. While spinning, each dagger does 75% more damage. The daggers can pierce one enemy before breaking on the second. When contacting an enemy they inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] for two seconds. They have a 50% chance of being recovered after being thrown.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Infernal Krises will cause the next dagger thrown to glow orange, create an explosion upon contact with a tile or an enemy and create several burning cinder projectiles when impacting.
