'''Yharim''', known as the Godseeker, Jungle Tyrant, or Tyrant King, is a prominent figure in the Calamity Mod's lore and is responsible for many of the events preceding the game. Most information regarding him can be found on the [[lore]] page.

On this wiki, "Yharim" may refer to:
*{{item|Godseeker, Yharim}}, the final boss of the Calamity mod.
*{{item|Yharim Bar}}, a Godseeker Mode [[Bars|bar]] dropped by the endgame [[boss|bosses]] of the Calamity mod that is utilized in the crafting of various items themed around Yharim.
*{{item|Yharim's Insignia}}, a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that enhances [[melee weapons]].
*{{item|Yharim's Gift}}, a post-Moon Lord covering dropped by [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]].
*{{fantag}}{{item|Yharim's Curse}}, a post-Moon Lord covering [[exhumed]] from [[Yharim's Gift]].
*{{item|Yharim's Crystal}}, a post-Moon Lord [[magic weapon]] dropped by Jungle Dragon, Yharon.
*{{item|Yharim's Stimulants}}, a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[buff potion]].
*{{item|Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword}}, a [[Hardmode]] [[sword]].
*{{item|True Tyrant's Ultisword}}, a post-Moon Lord sword that is an upgrade to Tyrant Yharim's Ultisword.
*{{item|Tyrant's Fury}}, a [[buff]] granted by the above swords.
*{{item|Tyrant Shield}} {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}, a [[classless weapon]] that acts as an active shield, and is a corpse of Yharim.
*{{item|The Codseeker}} {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}, a [[fishing pole]] that casts a corpse of Yharim as its bobber.

Not to be confused with:
*{{item|Jungle Dragon, Yharon|size=150px}}, a similarly named post-Moon Lord [[boss]] who is Yharim's loyal pet and friend.
