'''Eye of Architectory''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] covering. It lets you do three things:<br>
Tag your enemies and teleport them upside down , casting lunar fireballs at them (bosses, minibosses and flying enemies stay upside up)<br>
Right click anywhere not on an enemy to make a 3x3 space of temporary babilplate<br>
Left click to cast lunar fireballs and spinning hammers

== Notes ==

* Babilplate blocks dont replace other tiles
* The placed babilplate doesnt drop when mined
