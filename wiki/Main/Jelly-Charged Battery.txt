The '''Jelly-Charged Battery''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] that is the direct upgrade to both the [[Voltaic Jelly]] and [[Wulfrum Battery]]. While equipped, it grants +1 minion slots, increases [[minion]] damage by 7%, and gives a chance for minions to inflict the [[Electrified]] [[debuff]] for one second. All minions will also spawn energy orbs that deal 15 summon damage with a cooldown of one second.
