The '''Gnasher''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Sulphurous Sea]]. It walks towards the player in an attempt to attack them and can spawn in both the sea and the beach.

== Trivia ==
*The Gnasher is likely based off of a [[wikipedia:Macrochelys_temminckii|snapping turtle]], which both live in murky waters, though they aren't known for killing humans.
