The '''Despair Stone''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Brimstone Crag]]. It moves by rolling and bouncing similarly to an [[Angry Tumbler]]. Each bounce causes it to emit red particles and make an impact sound.

Its stats receive a significant boost and starts dropping [[Bloodstone]] after [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] is defeated.
