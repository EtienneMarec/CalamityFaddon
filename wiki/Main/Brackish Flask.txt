The '''Brackish Flask''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Bombs|bomb]] (Although can be obtained [[tgc:Pre-Hardmode|Pre-Hardmode]]), that is dropped by the [[Leviathan and Anahita]]. When used, it is thrown from the player at a medium velocity and with a heavy gravity-affected arc. Once it collides with an object, it shatters and releases 8 seawater blasts in all directions, which then explode into damaging clouds after traveling a short distance or impacting enemies or tiles. All of these projectiles deal 50% of the flask's damage, and inflict the [[Poisoned]] and [[Venom]] [[debuffs]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Brackish Flask causes it to deal 85% damage and release a large brackish spear spike upwards from the point of impact, that acts similarly to [[Atlantis]], going through walls and enemies dealing 85% damage. Additionally, other projectiles spawned by the weapon will deal 42.5% damage.

{{Modifier|rogue}}

== Notes ==
*Due to the fact that the mist projectiles are released in all directions, several of them will often immediately explode against the area that the flask initially impacted.

== Trivia ==
*Brackish is a term used to describe water that is a mix between fresh and salty.
