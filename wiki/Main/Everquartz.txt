'''Everquartz''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Hallow]] and [[Underworld]] biomes. She is a very fast enemy and she can jump approximately 7 tiles high and 20 tiles wide. When charging towards the player, she will charge rainbow-like magic at the player. She drops [[Unholy Essence]], a useful post-Moon Lord [[crafting material]].  

==Trivia==
*Everquartz is based upon on the infamous Calamity Mod fan artist: everquartz.
*Upon killing Everquartz, she has a 1% chance to spawn [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] and [[The Devourer of Gods]] at the same time.
