The '''Quadlens Staff''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[summon weapon]]. It uses four minion slots to summon an Elemental Eye Mass that attacks enemies by firing small lightning bolts, fireballs, stars, and pink lasers. The player can hold right click onto an enemy to channel a sigil on them. If the player successfully keeps their cursor on the enemy for 5 seconds, the mass will split into five eyes, with each elemental eye ramming into the target aggressively and the central eye firing a laser beam.
