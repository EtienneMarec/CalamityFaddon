The '''Abyssal Diving Suit''' was a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] which transformed the user into an armored diver when equipped. It provided water breathing, and it combined and enhanced the effects of the [[Abyssal Diving Gear]], [[Anechoic Plating]] and [[Iron Boots]], making it the ultimate tool for exploring the [[Abyss]]. Additionally, while the player was underwater, their movement speed and acceleration were boosted by 5%; otherwise, their movement speed and acceleration were reduced by 60%.

The suit possessed "armored plates" that [[Defense#Separate_Damage_Reduction_Boosts|reduced damage]] dealt to the player by 15%. However, the plates would be damaged if the player took more than 50 damage in one hit. The plates could sustain 3 of those hits before breaking. Once broken, they would take 3 minutes to regenerate. While the plates recharged, the player would not benefit from their damage reduction bonus.

== Notes ==
*The damage reduction provided by the suit's plates was calculated separately from typical damage reduction. As a result, it was not affected by the Calamity Mod's [[Vanilla_changes#Defense_and_DR|DR scaling system]].
*The check for how much damage was dealt to the player, for determining whether the plates will be damaged, occured ''after'' all defense and damage reduction calculations.

== Trivia ==
*When the player got hurt, the sound played is similar to the mechanical hurt sound.
