The '''Statis' Ninja Belt''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that combines the abilities of the [[Master Ninja Gear]] & [[Frog Leg]]. It grants the ability to cling to and slide down vertical walls, quickly dash with a base distance of 24 blocks by double-tapping the horizontal movement key, enables auto-jump by holding the {{key|jump}} key. Additionally, the player is a granted a guaranteed dodge that can only occur every 90 seconds. It also increases the player's jump speed by 6% and fall resistance by 35 blocks, allowing the player to fall 60 blocks before taking fall damage.
