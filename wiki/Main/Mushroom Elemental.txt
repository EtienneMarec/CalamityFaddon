The '''Mushroom Elemental''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in the [[Glowing Mushroom biome]] if the player has entered the [[Ocean|Ocean Biome]] previously in their world. They hover above players, raining down floating mushroom spores.

If a Mushroom Elemental and a [[Barocrab]] are within 50 blocks of one another, a small animation will play of the elemental entering the crab, turning it into [[Crabulon]].
