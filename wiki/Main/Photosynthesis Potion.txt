The '''Photosynthesis Potion''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[buff potion]]. Upon consumption it grants the '''Photosynthesis''' [[buff]], which provides 6 additional life regen when the player isn't moving. The regeneration increases to 34 during the daytime, regardless of where you are in the world. In addition, dropped [[heart]]s will heal an extra 5 HP.

== Notes ==
*The boosted life regeneration from the Photosynthesis buff is overridden by the effects of the [[Shiny Stone]], the [[Demonshade Breastplate]], the [[Cosmic Freeze]] buff, and [[Draedon's Heart]].
*Although the tooltip says the regeneration is 5 times stronger during the daytime, it provides up to 34 regeneration at its peak instead of the expected 30.
