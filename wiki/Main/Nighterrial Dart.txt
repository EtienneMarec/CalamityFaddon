'''Nighterrial Darts''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[darts]]. If an enemy is present in a 12 blocks radius, the dart will stop in its place and release a fast polterhook. The polterhook will try to grab the enemy in the radius, slowing it down by 30% if done successfully. Additionally, the polterhook will deal 150% of the dart's base damage every 2 seconds, inflicting the [[Moon Burnt]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 3 seconds. After 4 seconds have passed, the polterhook will disappear and the dart will fall down. There can only be 3 polterhooks at the same time on the same enemy and the dart itself can release only up to 3 polterhooks. Additionally, polterhooks from the same dart cannot grab the same enemy. If the polterhook limit was hit, the dart will act without any special effects, similarly to [[Poison Dart]].
