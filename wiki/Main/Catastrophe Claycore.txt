The '''Catastophe Claycore''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bombs|bomb]]. It throws claycore bombs that, upon impact, explode into 3-4 of one of three damaging projectiles, which will inflict numerous [[debuffs]] on hit, depending on their color.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] causes the bomb to explode into 4-8 bolts instead.
