The '''Squirrel Knight Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[sentry]]. When used, it summons a squirrel sentry that stands still and attack enemies by throwing knives that pierce once. If summoned mid-air, the squirrel will slowly fall down until it reaches stable ground. The squirrel can still attack while airborn.
