The '''Wingman''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[magic gun]] that auto-fires sets of three overlapping laser beams which do not pierce enemies. It is dropped by [[tgc:King_Slime|King Slime]] and two are required to craft the [[Alpha Ray]].

== Trivia ==
*A [[wikipedia:Wingman|wingman]] is a supporting pilot that protects the main aircraft. This is seen in the appearance of the [[Alpha Ray]], where two of it are situated alongside the [[Genisis]].
*The Wingman was implemented based on a community suggestion made by Gouvod on the mod's Terraria Forums post.
