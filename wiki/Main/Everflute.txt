The '''Everflute''' is a Godseeker Mode [[pet]] item that rarely drops from the [[Glitch]]. It summons a weird bird to follow the player. If the player moves too fast, it will start flying to catch up to them.

== Trivia ==
*The item is based on the Eon Flute from Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire.
*Bird Type is a reference to a glitched type from the first generation of Pokemon games.
**MISSINGNO, the Pokemon that inspired the [[Glitch]], is also bird type.
*The pet is a reference to the baby Opila bird from the Garten of Banban series of indie horror games.
**The developers of the games, the Euphoric Brothers, personally requested Fabsol, who is a fan of the series, to add the pet to the game.
**The buff tooltip is a reference to the children's book "Are You My Mother" by Doctor Seuss.
