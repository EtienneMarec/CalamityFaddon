The '''Handheld Tank''' is a Godseeker Mode [[launcher]] that fires a small, high velocity rocket upon use. The rocket will explode on contact, damaging [[enemies]] that are in the range of its blast.

*The [[Tiger Skin]] in the recipe is a reference to the German [[wikipedia:Tiger I|Tiger tank]].
