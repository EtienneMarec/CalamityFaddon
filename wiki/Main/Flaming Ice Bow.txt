The '''Flaming Ice Bow''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[bow]]. When used, it auto-fires arrows which inflict the [[Frostburn]] debuff for 3 seconds. For each frame the item is held, the player loses 1 health point due to their hands touching the fire. A typical player with 500 health will die within 9 seconds of continuously holding the bow. Heat resistant effects cannot stop this.
