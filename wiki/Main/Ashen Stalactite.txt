The '''Ashen Stalactite''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[dagger]] that is dropped by [[Fire Imp]]s. It automatically throws a gravity-affected stalactite that does not pierce and breaks into cosmetic dust shortly after.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Ashen Stalactite will cause the next stalactite thrown to be larger, travel slower, have a longer lifespan, and break into piercing, damaging dust.

{{Modifier|Spy}}

== Tips ==
*Due to its high damage and fast speed, this weapon is a very good early game choice for the [[Spy|spy]] class.
