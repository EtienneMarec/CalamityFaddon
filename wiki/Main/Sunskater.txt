The '''Sunskater''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[enemy]] that spawns in [[Space]]. It flies peacefully in one direction and turns around when it hits a block. Once attacked, it starts diving into the player at a fast speed in order to ram them.

== Trivia ==
*When the player is fully submerged in water, the Sunskater will stop attacking the player.
**Additionally, if the Sunskater is submerged in water, it will become stationary and sink to the bottom.
*It is the only Pre-Hardmode enemy able to inflict the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]].
*The [[Hadopelagic Echo]] has a small chance to play the Sunskater death sound instead of the normal use sound.
