The '''Holy Napalm Launcher''' is a Post-[[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus|Umbrus]] [[Launchers|launcher]]. It slowly fires napalm rockets that fly for 5 seconds and leave holy flames behind, the flames deal 25% of the weapon's damage. After the rocket flies for 5 seconds, It explodes in a giant 20x20 tile circle, dealing 3 times more damage.
