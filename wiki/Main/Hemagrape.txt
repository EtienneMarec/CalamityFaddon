The '''Hemagrape''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[permanent power-up]] item that increases the player's maximum life, as an extension to [[Life Fruit]]s. It is obtained by throwing a Grape into a pool of [[Water]] during a [[Blood Moon]] , which transforms the Apple into a Hemagrape, provided the [[mechanical bosses]] have been defeated. There are 3 other items that function identically to this: the [[Miracle Fruit]], the [[Sambucus Fruit]], and the [[Dragonfruit]]. It is the first of these to be obtainable following boss progression. It can only be used once, and after that the player must use one of the remaining fruit to increase their maximum life. With all of these, the player's maximum life is capped at 600.
