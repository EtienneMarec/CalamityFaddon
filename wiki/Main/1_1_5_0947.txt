The '''1.1.5.0947''' update added a sizeable amount of new [[weapons]], with a focus on the [[Sandstorm]] event and [[Forbidden Fragment]] items that had recently been added to the base game. 

== Additions ==
*Added [[Alpha Ray]], [[Corrupted Crusher Blade]], [[Crimson Crusher Blade]], [[Crystalline]], [[Cursed Dagger]], [[Dualpoon]], [[The Empyrean]], [[Excalibur Shortsword]], [[Genisis]], [[Greatsword of Judgement]], [[Ichor Spear]], [[Mineral Mortar]], [[Relic of Ruin]], [[True Excalibur Shortsword]], [[Waifu in a Bottle]], and [[Wingman]].
*Added [[Celestial Onion]].
*Added [[Burden Breaker]] and [[Determination Breaker]].

== Changes ==
*Renamed Mephetic Sprayer to [[Blight Spewer]].
*[[Death Valley Duster]] now uses 5 [[Feather]]s and 1 [[Forbidden Fragment]] in its recipe instead of 10 Feathers and 100 [[Sand Block]]s.
*Removed [[Plasma Rifle]] and [[Scorpio]]'s right click functions. <!---Due to bugs--->
*[[Primordial Earth]] now uses 5 [[Ectoplasm]], 1 Forbidden Fragment, and 5 [[Meteorite Bar]]s in its recipe instead of 100 [[Bone]]s, 6 [[Desert Feather]]s, 10 Ectoplasm, 20 [[Fallen Star]]s, 15 Meteorite Bars, 300 [[Sand Block]]s, 20 [[Soul of Might|Souls of Might]], and 45 [[Sturdy Fossil]]s.
*[[Rocket I]]s can now be crafted with 1 [[Empty Bullet]] and 3 [[Explosive Powder]] at a Hardmode Anvil.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Abyssal Tome]], [[Animus]], [[Blight Spewer]], and [[Floodtide]].
*Resprited [[Statis' Blessing]].
*Resprited [[Stormlion Mandible]], and [[Yharon Soul Fragment]].
