The '''1.1.4.3''' update focused on adding several [[Spell tomes]].

== Additions ==
*Added [[Everglade Spray]], [[Flare Bolt]], [[Frigidflash Bolt]], [[Frost Bolt]], [[Shadecrystal Tome]], [[Tears of Heaven]].
*Added [[Holy Light]].

== Changes ==
*After killing a boss three times, they will no longer generate [[ore]] on death.
*Given a fair amount of light to most projectiles.
*[[The Devourer of Gods]]' projectiles given more damage.
*[[Calamitas]]' projectiles given more damage.
