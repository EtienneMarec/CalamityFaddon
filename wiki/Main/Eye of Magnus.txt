'''Eye of Magnus''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]] that is an upgraded version of the [[Lunic Eye]]. When used it fires a fast, blue-and-white beam that homes in on enemies and will [[lifesteal|restore]] 25 mana and 1 HP upon contact with an enemy. The beam inflicts the [[Marked]] [[debuff]] to enemies, which lowers enemy damage reduction by 50%. Upon impact, each beam will create an explosive plume of blue-and-white particles which can deal additional damage. This weapon does not consume any ammo or mana.

The [[Eye of Magnus]] cannot be reforged, and therefore cannot have [[modifiers]].

== Notes ==
*This weapon does not have any critical strike chance and cannot gain any from [[coverings]], [[Modifier]]s, [[Buff]]s and [[Covering]] bonuses.
*As this weapon does not count as a specific weapon type, it does not provoke the penalty on minions' damage when holding a non-summoner weapon, making it an indispensable asset for [[Summon weapons|summon]]-based characters.
*The damage multiplication is equal to the sum of all the damage multiplication across each of the 5 classes (Melee, Ranged, Mage, Summoner, [[Spy]]).
**e.g. If the player has +10% melee damage, +30% ranged damage and +5% Spy damage, the Lunic Eye will deal 45% more damage.
*The additional hits from the impact explosion will not replenish HP or mana, only the initial enemy hit will.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon shares its effects and name with the [https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Staff_of_Magnus_(Skyrim) Staff of Magnus] and [https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Magnus Eye of Magnus], respectively, from ''[[wikipedia:The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim|The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim]]''.
*This weapon was originally obtained from [[Rune Wizard]]s, then changed to a drop from the [[Lunatic Cultist]], before becoming a craftable upgrade to the Lunic Eye.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Lunic Eye]].
