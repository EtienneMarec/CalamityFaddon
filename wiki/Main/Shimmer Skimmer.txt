The '''Shimmer Skimmer''' is a [[critter]]. It is found in the [[Aether]] and behaves similarly to a typical [[Water Strider]]. It can be caught with a [[Bug Net]] or [[Golden Bug Net]], and when released outside of [[Shimmer]] it will phase through the floor until it hits Shimmer or reaches the [[The Underworld|Underworld]].
