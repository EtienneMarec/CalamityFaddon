'''Elysian Wings''' are a Godseeker Mode [[Wings|wing]]-type [[covering]] that allow the player to fly and glide, dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] when summoned in the [[Hallow]]. It grants temporary immunity to [[lava]], 15% increased movement speed, and heat protection in [[Death Mode]].
