The '''Sulfuric Scale''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that drop from [[Nuclear Terror]]s. While equipped the player gains immunity to the [[Poisoned]], [[Venom]], and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] [[debuffs]]. Additionally, the player gains 10% increased damage and 5% increased critical strike chance while under the effects of a damaging debuff.

If the player has lost 25% of their health, they will gain 6% increased damage and 3% increased critical strike chance. This effect can occur three times, maxing out at 18% increased damage and 9% increased critical strike chance, occurring at 25% of the player’s health or below.
