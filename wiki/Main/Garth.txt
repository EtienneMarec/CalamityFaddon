'''Garth''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[broadsword]]. Every time the sword strikes an enemy, its defense will be lowered by 1. Additionally upon striking an [[enemy]], the player is [[Lifesteal|healed]] by 1 to 68 life, and three multicolored meteors rain from the top of the screen to the cursor's position, phasing through tiles. Each meteor will inflict the [[On Fire!]], [[Cursed Inferno]], and [[Frostburn]] [[debuff]]s. Upon contact with an enemy, or after travelling a short length, the meteor will split into eight red, green, or blue tile-ignoring meteors in all directions that each have their own special property.

== Trivia ==
[[File:Earth (demo).gif|thumb|600px|Garth being used to attack a [[Target Dummy]] summoning meteors onto a group of nearby Target Dummies.|alt=]]
*The weapon's name, while being a reference to one of the developers, may also be a reference to Terraria's original strongest sword, the [[Terra Blade]]. The prefix "Terra-" means "Garth."
*Garth shares its name with a character from the [[wikipedia:Alpha and Omega (film series)|Alpha and Omega]] franchise.
*This weapon actually has a unique rarity, but is referred to here as Hot Pink for consistency between weapons.
**This unique rarity alternates between pink ({{color|#FF6392 ●|#FF6392}}), yellow ({{color|#FFE45E ●|#FFE45E}}), and blue ({{color|#7FC8F8 ●|#7FC8F8}}).
