'''Barolegs''' are a consumable [[food item]] that drops from [[Abyss]] enemies. When consumed, it grants the [[Well Fed]] buff for 20 minutes. Additionally, after defeating [[Calamitas]], this is buffed to [[Plenty Satisfied]], and again after defeating [[Polterghast]] to [[Exquisitely Stuffed]].
