'''Noxcalibur''' is an upgraded version of the [[Roxcalibur]]. It can be obtained by defeating [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]] with nothing but the [[Unloxcalibur]].

When swung, it launches a gravity affected nox boulder towards the cursor. The boulder will bounce and roll on tiles in a similar manner to placeable boulders, and will despawn after 10 seconds.

While in the air, right-clicking with the Noxcalibur will cause the player to dive downwards and bounce off tiles and on top of enemies and nox boulders. Upon hitting a tile or a boulder, the player creates an entropic explosion and bounces considerably higher. The explosion does 100,000 base damage and has a cooldown of 30 seconds. When the explosion becomes available again, a sound plays to indicate it.
