'''The Microwave''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[yoyo]] dropped by [[Astrum Deus]]. While the yoyo is in the air, a small aura surrounds it which continuously damages nearby enemies, dealing 35% of the yoyo's base damage and inflicting the [[Ancient Astral Blight]] [[debuff]].

{{modifier|meleealt}}

== Trivia ==
*The Microwave emits a notable humming noise as it travels, and a [[wikipedia:Beep (sound)|beep]] sound when enemies are killed with it, mimicking that of [[wikipedia:microwave oven|a real-life microwave oven]]. Its tooltip makes reference to this, mentioning cooking.
*The Microwave could also be a reference to the [[wikipedia:Cosmic microwave background|cosmic microwave background]] radiation in space, supported by the weapon's related boss being themed around space.
