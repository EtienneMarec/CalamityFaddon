}}The '''Blood Bath''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]] that is also dropped by [[The Perforators]]. When used, it causes large red droplets to drop from the sky at a fast pace. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Shadowdrop Staff]].
