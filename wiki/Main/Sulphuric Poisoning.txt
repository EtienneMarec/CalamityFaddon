'''Sulphuric Poisoning''' is a [[debuff]] caused by being submerged in water in the [[Sulphurous Sea]] [[biome]], or inflicted by the [[Sea's Searing]] and [[Scourge of the Seas]]'s [[stealth strike]].

The debuff is removed as soon as the player exits sulphuric water. [[Sulphurskin Potion]]s and [[Sulphurous armor]] will reduce the damage taken from this debuff. The [[Effigy of Decay]] and [[Silva armor]] will make the player immune to this debuff.
