The '''Baleful Harvester''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]]. Upon striking enemies with the blade, homing pumpkins and fiery orange skulls will spawn off-screen and attack enemies in the same fashion as [[The Horseman's Blade]]. Both projectiles deal 150% of the weapon's base damage and skulls split into 2 homing fire orbs which each deal 50% of the weapon's base damage. The sword, the skulls and their fire orbs inflict the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]].
