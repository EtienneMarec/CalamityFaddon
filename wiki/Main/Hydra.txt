The '''Hydra''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[classless weapon]]. When used, it fires fifteen extremely fast bullets that create small explosions when hitting an enemy or a tile. 0.25% of the target's health is added to the damage of each bullet, and another 0.25% from the explosion. If all fifteen of the bullets hit, they can deal up to 7.5% of a target's max health prior to defense and damage reduction calculations.

It exclusively uses [[Explosive Shotgun Shell]]s as [[ammo]], which can be bought from the [[Arms Dealer]] if the player has the Hydra in their inventory.

Once a boss is spawned, the Hydra is only fireable once until the boss is defeated or until it despawns after the player is killed.

== Notes ==
*Despite resembling a [[gun]], the weapon has no damage type, making it equally effective for all classes.
*This weapon does not have any critical strike chance and cannot gain any from [[coverings]], [[Modifier]]s, [[Buff]]s and [[Covering]] bonuses.

*The weapon is a reference to the [https://residentevil.fandom.com/wiki/Hydra_(SG) gun of the same name] from the game ''[[wikipedia:Resident Evil 5|Resident Evil 5]]''.
