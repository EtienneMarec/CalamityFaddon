The '''Rainbow Party Cannon''' is a craftable Epilogue [[developer]] [[magic gun]]. When used, a cannon is locked in place at the player and firing at the direction of the cursor, unable to change angles until released, similarly to [[Charged Blaster Cannon]]. After one second of firing non-damaging confetti, the cannon starts firing comets at the rate of its use time. Comets have exceptional vertical acceleration and explode upon collision with tiles or targets, bursting into 18 infinitely-piercing decelerating rainbow sparkles along with seven rockets of varying colors. The rockets are followed by rainbow trails, move in a curve for one second before homing into enemies, and explode into a random number of rainbow sparkles. The rockets and their sparkles deal tripled damage, and more sparkles can spawn the longer the rocket lasts.

Each rocket has a different color and a different on-death visual effect. The order of the rockets are as follows (clockwise):

== Notes ==
*The phrase in the tooltip "Let the rainbow remind you that together we will always shine…" alternates between lavender ({{color|#9c6cfb ●|#9c6cfb}}) and pink ({{color|#ff6cfb ●|#ff6cfb}}) colors.

== Trivia ==
*The colors and explosion effects represent the different characters in ''[[wikipedia:My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic|My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic]]'' and their respective cutie marks.
**The characters include Pinkie Pie, Applejack, Fluttershy, Rarity, Rainbow Dash, Twilight Sparkle, and Starlight Glimmer respectively.
**The cannon itself also refers to Pinkie Pie's party cannon.
**The phrase in the tooltip is a direct lyric from a song in the show's fourth season finale.
