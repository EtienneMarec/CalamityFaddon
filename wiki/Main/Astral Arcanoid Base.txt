'''Astral Arcanoid Base''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Equipment|covering]] and a direct upgrade to the [[Arcanoid Orb]]. While equipped, it summons an '''Astral Arcanoid Base''' minion.

Like the Arcanoid Orb, the Astral Arcanoid Base is normally stationary, until the player presses the {{key|Arcanoid Orb}} key to move it. This action will cost 7 mana, and, when held down for 1.5 seconds, drains the player's mana at a rate of 21 per second to have the Base track the cursor.

The Astral Arcanoid Base deals 260 contact damage, which scales with summon damage bonuses, and grants a third of a second of static [[immunity frames]]. Additionally, the Orb fires out astral stars every 2 seconds, which deal 120 damage each and inflict the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[Debuffs|debuff]] for 5 seconds.
