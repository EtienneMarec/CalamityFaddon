The '''Skullmasher''' was a Godseeker Mode [[gun]] that dropped from [[The Devourer of Gods]] in [[Malice Mode]]. The gun fired four high velocity bullets. Upon collision with enemies, more high velocity bullets and which ever [[ammo]] type was being used were shot at the enemy from random positions near it.

Its best [[modifier]] was '''Legendary'''.

== Trivia ==
*This weapon was a reference to the [https://borderlands.fandom.com/wiki/Skullmasher_(Borderlands_2) sniper rifle of the same name] from the ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands (series)|Borderlands]]'' series.
*It was formerly the [[rare variant]] of the [[Anti-materiel Rifle]].
