The '''Deadshot Brooch''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that grants the player the following effects:
*10% increased ranged damage
*5% increased ranged critical strike chance
*20% reduced ammo usage
*[[Defense]] increased by 5
*Life regen increased by 2
*15% increased pick speed

== Tips ==
*The Deadshot Brooch stacks with the [[Elemental Quiver]], so with both equipped the player can have a total of 25% increased ranged damage, 10% increased ranged crit chance, 36% reduced ammo usage, 10 defense, life regen increased by 4, and 30% increased mining speed as well as all the other Elemental Quiver effects.

== Trivia ==
*This covering was previously known as the "Artemis Emblem", and before that, the "Daedalus Emblem".
