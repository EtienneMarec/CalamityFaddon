The '''Caeles Mod''' is a remade from the ground-up version of a '''Tranquility Mod''', which is currently in development. Currently it's rather a small-scale mod, adding a little of content, but more will be introduced in future. It has a lot of cross-mod content with Calamity Mod (and uses its story and some characters from it as a base), as well as with some other large content mods, like Spirit Mod or Mod of Redemption. The mod'd development is going in collaboration between Adeianos/dei and eq.

== Version History ==
== 1.2.5.001, "Back from Hell: ==

''Release Date: 7th January, 2024''
{{Stub}}

The '''1.2.5.001''' update "revived" the mod, also beginning its ground-up rework, effectively removing or reworking every aspect that previously existed there.

=== Changes Highlight ===
*Renamed the mod from "Tranquility Mod" to "Caeles Mod".
*Resprited the mod's icon.
*Removed the [[Nightmare Mode]] and all content associated with it, as well as almost all other content that previously existed in the mod.
*Added cross-mod support for Calamity Mod, Spirit Mod and Mod of Redemption.
*Reintroduced and reworked [[Allies]], now they are depedent on TerraGuardians mod's libraries.
*Added reputation system and its content.
*Added a ton of [[configuration options]], allowing the player to configure pretty much any aspect of the mod.
*Added some QoL features.
*Expanded the [[Death Mode]].
