The '''Eldritch Tome''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[spell tome]] that drops from [[The Slime God]]. When used, it fires three red tendrils which bend in random directions, similarly to the [[Shadowflame Hex Doll]]. It is the [[Crimson]] counterpart to the [[Abyssal Tome]].
