'''Fireball''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[potion]]. It grants the '''Fireball''' [[debuff]] when consumed, which reduces life regen and boosts the damage per second of fire-based debuffs by 50%.

While the effects of the Fireball debuff are mostly beneficial, it is still considered a debuff, and therefore cannot be removed by right-clicking the icon.
