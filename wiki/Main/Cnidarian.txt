The '''Cnidarian''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[summon weapon]]. When used, the user holds out a fishing rod and a jellyfish on a string attaches to the bottom of the user's cursor. Moving the cursor causes the string to swing proportional to the rate of its movement, with the cursor as the pivot point. While enemies are nearby, it will select up to three enemies at random and fire electric sparks which deals 50% of the jellyfish's damage. If the jellyfish swings fast enough, it will assign minions to target the struck enemy and play a wet slap sound.
