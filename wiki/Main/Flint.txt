The '''Aftershock''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that is bought from the [[Collector]]. When used it will spawn a flame at the player's foot that deals damage. The weapon can be held to use a charge attack, dealing more damage and spawning a wave of fire that travels on top of blocks.

{{modifier|melee}}

== Tips ==
*The Flint's blueprint can be obtained rarely by putting [[Silt Block]]s through an [[Extractinator]].
*Having a flint in your inventory is required to access [[The Exosphere]].
