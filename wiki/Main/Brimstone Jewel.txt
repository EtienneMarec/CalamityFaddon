The '''Brimstone Jewel''' is a [[pet]] summoning item dropped by [[Supreme Calamitas]] in [[Expert Mode]]. It summons a miniature Supreme Calamitas to follow the player.

== Trivia ==
*The pet's design is based on the eye-like appearance that Supreme Calamitas used in previous versions of the mod.
*Due to the extremely erratic movement of the pet, it was confirmed by the Mod's Official Developer Fabsol that the pet has [[wikipedia:ADHD|ADHD]].
