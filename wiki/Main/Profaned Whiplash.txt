The '''Profaned Whiplash''' is a Post-[[tgc:Moonlord|Moonlord]] [[Whips|whip]], dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess|Providence]]. When used, it launches a profaned whip that generates Holy Flame explosions on each enemy hit. After killing an enemy, they will spawn 3-5 homing [[File:Holy Crystal Shard.png]] Holy Crystal Shards that deal 110% of the weapon's damage.

Its best modifier is "Legendary", as it provides the widest array of stat bonuses including whip range and speed.
