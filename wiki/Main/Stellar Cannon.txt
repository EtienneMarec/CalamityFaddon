The '''Stellar Cannon''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] that is dropped from [[Nova]]s after [[Astrum Aureus]] has been defeated. It fires explosive astral crystals that explode on contact with enemies, inflicting the [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]] [[debuff]]. The astral crystal fired requires no ammo and will pass through blocks.

{{Modifier|ranged}}

== Notes ==
*This weapon can also be obtained from the [[Astral Crate]] after Astrum Aureus has been defeated.
