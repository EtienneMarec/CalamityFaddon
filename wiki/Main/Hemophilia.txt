'''Hemophilia''' is a [[debuff]] inflicted by various components of [[Edgeterrence]]. The debuff reduces the target's [[Defense]] by 1 point for each second on it's timer, which is then added to your own defense, and depletes 10 health per second plus however many seconds are on the timer.
