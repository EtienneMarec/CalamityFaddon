'''Bouncy Spiky Balls''' are craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] consumable [[spiky balls]]. They throw gravity-affected spiky balls that bounce off solid blocks. When a ball hits an enemy, it ricochets to other nearby enemies and gains a multiplicative boost of 1.1x per enemy hit.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Bouncy Spiky Balls causes the next spiky ball thrown to have its damage be increased by 1.5 times per bounce instead of 1.1.
