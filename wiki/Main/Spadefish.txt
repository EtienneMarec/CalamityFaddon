The '''Spadefish''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[pickaxe]] that can be [[Fishing|fished up]] in the [[Underground]] Layer with a 2.5% chance. It can mine up to {{item|Gold Ore}} and {{item|Platinum Ore}}. Though it has a very low pickaxe power, its [[mining speed]] is higher than most other pickaxes of its tier.

{{Modifier|harvest}}

== Trivia ==
*The Spadefish has the lowest pickaxe power of any pickaxe in either vanilla or Calamity, requiring four hits to break a [[Stone Block]].
