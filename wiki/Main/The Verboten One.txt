'''The Verboten One''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. While worn it has the following effects:
* Increased damage by 105%, and the damage of players on the same team by 17%.
*True melee damage is increased by 240% after being struck.
*Weapon speed increased by 50%.
* 50% increased life regeneration and critical strike chance.
*100% increased max life, jump speed, and movement speed.
*200% increased mining speed and mana.
*55% increased damage reduction.
*120 defense.
*100% reduced mana and ammo costs.
* 150 armor penetration.
*99.5% reduced stealth costs.
*20 max minions.
*50% increased Spy stealth.
*800 reduced enemy [[aggro]].
*Quadrupled immunity frames.
*Flight with infinite flight time.
* Immunity to [[Lava]].
*Complete invisibility to Abyss enemies.
*Complete immunity to the Abyss' unique effects.
*[[Healing Potion]]s heal 30% more health, and have their cooldowns reduced by 75%.
* Grants the ability to climb walls.
*Getting hit causes an endosplosion and enemies to take 33,000 flat damage.
*When invincibility is active (i.e. when the player is damaged, enters the world or uses the [[Magic Mirror]]), shade, draconic fireballs, stars and brimstone fire rains from the sky, dealing 600 typeless damage which can be affected by the average of all damage bonuses.
*Also when invincibility is active, the player is trailed by mushrooms and sends out sparks in multiple directions.
*Standing still causes a Pearl Aura to generate around the player which slows down enemies within it, inflicts [[Exo Freeze]] to enemies within it, and increases the player's defense by 50 and life regeneration by 5. Team mates receive 25 defense and 2 life regeneration.
*All yoyos are cloned four times, have 8 [[Counterweights]], and an 8 times longer string.
*Four void portals are summoned which rapidly orbit the player. Any projectile that intersects with them gains a 300% damage boost.
*Red targets appear on enemies randomly. Any projectile that intersects with these targets gains a 700% damage boost.
*Rainbow bolts spawn when a Mana Potion is used with a 10 second cooldown. These deal a flat 40,000 damage and rapidly home in on enemies.
* Summons a Brimstone Elemental, Sand Elemental, Cloud Elemental, Plague Elemental, Sulphur Elemental, Water Elemental, Young Duke, Gladiator Swords, Howl Trio, Cosmic Conflict Construct, and Critical Slime Core to attack enemies.
*Summons a buffed Rare Sand Elemental that rapidly fires homing healing orbs that heal 3 health each.
*Abyssal Shards appear when an enemy is killed which cause all nearby hostile projectiles to become passive and home in on it, damaging it. The shard has a total health of 33,000, and lasts for 10 seconds. Once the timer is up, the shard explodes for 100,000 damage. If the player touches the shard, they are healed 100 health.
*Any negative effects from other coverings are nullified.
*Light spikes are summoned on minion hits which deal 1060 damage each.
*Spy projectiles leave behind nanoblack blades with a maximum of 8 blades at once.
*Each critical strike with a Spy weapon increases Spy damage for up to 200%.
*Hitting enemies with Spy projectiles causes lightning sparks, nano shards, and a lightning tornado to fall out of enemies that deal 20% damage each.
*Hitting the Spectral Veil hotkey causes the player to be engulfed by nether matter which grants immunity to damage for 10 seconds with a 30 seconds cooldown. The nether matter also deals a flat 300 damage per tick to enemies caught within,
*If the player is hit by an enemy, a blast of wind is released, causing them to ignore damage and dealing 1,000,000 to any enemies caught within. This effect has a 90 second cooldown.
*Lights the cursor on fire with Demon Flames.
*[[Adrenaline]] additionally causes the player to heal 300 health over the course of 3 seconds.
*Adrenaline no longer disappears on hit.
*The Elysian Guard's stats are tripled.
*Immunity to all debuffs.
*Rage builds at all times.
*Using any weapon including true melee weapons will have a 10% chance to fire out a rapid homing Mini Birb, mushroom, sigil, or a miniature sun that pulls in enemies.
*Every 0.33 seconds, either a sulphuric cloud, a ball of toxic sea brine, dragon dust, a homing plague bee, or a spore is generated which deals 500 typeless damage to enemies that come into contact with them.
*As the player's life decreases, they gain 1% damage per every 5 health lost, usually resulting in a boost of up to 75% damage.
*At all times, enemies in a 22.5-34.75 tile radius are inflicted with [[Brimstone Flames]], [[Confused]], [[Glacial State]], [[God Slayer Inferno]], [[Irradiated]], and [[Sulphuric Poisoning]] for 5-8 seconds when the player is struck. The range and debuff duration both increase based on the damage taken.
*25% of enemy damage is turned into healing.
*The durations of all [[potion]] [[buffs]] are extended by 200%, and potion buffs remain active after dying.
*Dying revives the player inside of a shell that lasts for 3 seconds. This ability has a 30 second cooldown.
*Passively generates an energy shield that takes a single hit. It takes 30 seconds to generate.

==Trivia==
*The item is based on an item from the [[Community modifications|Apotheosis and Friends]] fan addon, which was known for its large crafting recipes, usually for coverings. The Verboten One was also a boss within the mod.
*The item's sprite is composed of every single covering in the mod excluding most [[Wings]], [[Enchanted Pearl]], [[Angelic Alliance]], [[Profaned Soul Artifact]], [[Profaned Soul Crystal]], [[Frost Barrier]], [[Psychotic Amulet]], [[Lead Core]], and the [[Supreme Bait Tackle Box Fishing Station]].
**These coverings are all excluded from the covering either due to being or being components of other endgame items, or being [[fishing]]-themed.
*The item has the most complex crafting tree in the mod.
*Verboten is another word for forbidden.
*This item originally dropped from THE LORDE before it was removed.
