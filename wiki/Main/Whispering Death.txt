'''Whispering Death''' is a [[debuff]] only inflicted by the [[Omniblade]], [[Temporal Umbrella]], [[Signus]], and [[The Devourer of Gods]]. If inflicted on an enemy, their defense is reduced by 50. If inflicted on a player, their defense is reduced by 20, damage is reduced by 10%, movement speed reduced by 10% and life regen is set to zero.
