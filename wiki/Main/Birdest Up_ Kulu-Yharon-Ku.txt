'''Birdest Up, Kulu-Yharon-Ku''' (Japanese: クルルイハロンク (プリックバード) ''Kururuiharonku (purikkubādo)'') is a major [[boss]] fought in the [[Desert]]. Despite being categorized as a pre-Hardmode boss, Kulu-Yharon-Ku is not meant to be fought until well into the Epilogue. The player should not summon the boss beforehand as they will die almost immediately without adequate protection.

Defeating Kulu-Yharon-Ku will provide the player with extreme guilt over killing an epic bird.

== Spawn ==
Kulu-Yharon-Ku does not spawn on his own, and requires the player to wait in a desert until Eric André's voice is heard. As soon as it happens, one has to throw [[Genesis]] on the floor, prompting Kulu-Yharon-Ku to spawn and approach the location of the player's residence at an alarming rate.

== Behavior ==
Kulu-Yharon-Ku will evade and ignore all attempts at hitting him, making all weapons but a large amount of [[Chlorophyte Bullets]] ineffective.

=== Attacks ===
Kulu-Ya-Ku runs at the player with [[Genesis]] in his arms, dealing an instant death upon touch through the sheer amount of [[Auric Tesla Bar|Auric Tesla Bars]] required to have a chance at obtaining [[Genesis]]. If the player successfully dodges any of his attacks, for example through the [[Hallowed set|Hallowed set's]] set bonus, they are instead sent to the Bird Dimension, where they contract hepatitis c
