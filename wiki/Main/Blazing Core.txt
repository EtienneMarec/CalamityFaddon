The '''Blazing Core''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] dropped by [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]]. While equipped, it increases the player's damage reduction by 10%. If the player is hit by an [[enemy]], a miniature sun is created at the player's location for ten seconds that sucks enemies towards it, deals damage, and inflicts the [[Holy Flames]] [[debuff]] for three seconds. Only one sun can be active at a time.
