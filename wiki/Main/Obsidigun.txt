The '''Obsidigun''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[Gun]] dropped by [[Pyrogen]]. It slowly fires high velocity bullets like the [[Sniper Rifle]]. If [[Musket Ball]]s are used as ammo, bullets will be replaced with unique obsidian bullets that act like a combination between a [[High Velocity Bullet]] and a [[Crystal Bullet]] which will travel at a high speed and split into multiple damaging shards on impact with an enemy or tile. While these bullets lose the inherent pierce of high velocity bullets, the shards can instead pierce up to three times, its damage decreasing by 15% for each target hit.
