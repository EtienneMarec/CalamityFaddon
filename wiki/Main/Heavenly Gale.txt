The '''Heavenly Gale''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[Bows|bow]]. It shoots down a storm of 20 arrows from above the cursor. When [[Wooden Arrow]]s are used as ammunition, arrows are converted into gilded exo arrows which accelerate downwards, then turn right upon hitting an enemy. Once the arrow starts moving, it creates a gale trail which summons pillars of heavenly lightning after 1.67 seconds.

All arrows fired from the bow inflict the following [[debuffs]] on hit enemies:
*[[Exo Freeze]] (0.5 seconds)
*[[Holy Flames]] (2 seconds)
*[[Frostburn]] (2.5 seconds)
*[[On Fire!]] (3 seconds)
