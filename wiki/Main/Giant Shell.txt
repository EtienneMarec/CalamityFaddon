The '''Giant Shell''' is a [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[covering]] dropped by [[Giant Shelly|Giant Shellies]] in the [[Cavern]] layer. It increases [[defense]] by 6 and [[Damage Reduction]] by 5% but decreases movement speed by 15%. When the player is harmed by any source, the player gains a '''Shell Speed Boost''' [[buff]] that increases movement speed by 90%.

== Notes ==
*Some worlds may never spawn [[Giant Shelly|Giant Shellies]], due to the unique spawn mechanic they share with [[Salamander]]s and [[Crawdad]]s, making this item unobtainable there. However, one can still find the [[Craw Carapace]] and craft [[The Absorber]] in these worlds.
*[[Shell Speed Boost]] will not be granted on hit if the player has the [[Panic Necklace]] or any of its upgrades equipped.
