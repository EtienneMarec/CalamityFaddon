'''Roxcalibur''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that can also be found in [[Pre-Hardmode]], though the sword cannot be swung until the [[Wall of Flesh]] is defeated.

When swung, it launches three gravity affected rock shards that do 40% of the Roxcalibur's base damage. The shards are always launched upward in an arc, though their horizontal velocity is based on the cursor's position, and they have a semi-random spread.

While in the air, right-clicking with the Roxcalibur will cause the player to dive downwards and bounce off tiles and on top of enemies in a similar manner to the [[Slimy Saddle]]. Upon hitting tiles, the player creates a shockwave and bounces considerably higher. The shockwave does 300 base damage and has a cooldown of 15 seconds; during that time, the player will still bounce off of blocks but will not create the shockwave, and a sound will play upon hitting a block. When the shockwave becomes available again, rock particles appear around the player and a sound plays to indicate it.

Roxcalibur can be found naturally in the world, in a random location within the [[Underground]] or [[Cavern]] Layers, and can be harvested with a [[Pickaxe]].  Only one Roxcalibur can be generated per world.

*During the generation of the Roxcalibur in world creation, the message reads "I Wanna Rock."
*The weapon is a reference to [https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Excalibur Excalibur] from [https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Castlevania:_Aria_of_Sorrow Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow].
