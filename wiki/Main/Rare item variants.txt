'''Rare item variants (RIVs)''' were unique [[weapons]] and [[coverings]] that were rarer versions of their common counterparts. Most rare item variants looked and functioned identically to their common counterpart but often had different sprites, altered stats, or additional effects. Each rare item variant was dropped from a certain enemy or boss (or the boss's respective [[Treasure Bag]]). This system was removed and replaced with the [[Challenge items]] system in the [[1.5.0.001]] update.

== Notes ==
*All Rare item variants had a custom rarity, [[File:Rarity color rare variant.png|link=]].
*Rare item variants were separate items and could not be used in any recipes.

== Trivia ==
*Rare item variants were largely inspired by a similar mechanic in ''[[wikipedia:Borderlands 2|Borderlands 2]]''.
**Many of the items also shared the names of different weapons from the series.
