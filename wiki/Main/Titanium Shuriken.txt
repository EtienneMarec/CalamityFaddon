'''Titanium Shurikens''' are craftable [[Hardmode]] [[rogue weapon]]s. They pierce infinitely and can be recovered after hitting a block about 50% of the time.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Titanium Shurikens causes the shuriken to behave similarly to a boomerang, bouncing off tiles and recovering the shuriken to the player 100% of the time. If the boomerang shuriken hits an enemy, 1-2 homing shurikens appear from the shuriken itself.
