The  '''Hypothermia''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[rogue weapon]]. It fires a constant barrage of black ice shards that ricochet off of tiles and inflict [[Frostburn]].

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Hypothermia causes it to fire four black ice chunks that deal 130% damage and shatter into additional ice shards.

== Notes ==
*This weapon has a strange synergy with the [[Venerated Locket]] and [[Dark God's Sheath]], as stealth strikes with Hypothermia cause multiple waves of homing knives to be released.
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].

== Trivia ==
*[[wikipedia:Hypothermia|Hypothermia]] is a condition where the body's core temperature lowers below 35.0 °C.
*The Hypothermia could be considered a counterpart to the [[Penumbra]] due to their similar recipes.
