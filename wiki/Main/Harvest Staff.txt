The '''Harvest Staff''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[wand]]. It shoots flaming pumpkins that bounce off of tiles and can pierce one enemy before breaking on the next. The pumpkins disappear after a few seconds.

Its projectiles emit a significant amount of light particles that fall down through blocks. This, combined with their physics, makes them ideal for exploring caves and potentially revealing new ones underneath.
