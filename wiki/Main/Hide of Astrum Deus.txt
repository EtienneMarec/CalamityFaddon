The '''Hide of Astrum Deus''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that drops from [[Astrum Deus]]. The wearer creates an astral blight explosion and twelve blight stars rain from the sky that damage nearby enemies upon being struck or dealing a [[true melee]] hit. True melee damage is boosted by 50% for up to fifteen seconds after being struck by an enemy. The Hide of Astrum Deus also provides immunity to [[Astral Infection (debuff)|Astral Infection]], [[Ancient Astral Blight]], [[Cursed Inferno]], [[On Fire!]], and the [[Frostburn]] [[debuffs]]. Any enemy that hits you will take 75% of the damage they dealt.
