The '''1.5.0.002''' (or '''1.5.0.2''') update slightly reworked [[Reactive Damage Reduction]], [[Defense damage]], and fixed several bugs and other issues from the [[Draedon Update]].

== Additions ==
*Added a [[Mod Calls|mod call]] that allows for making [[exhumed]] versions of items.

== Changes ==
=== Bosses ===
*[[Reactive Damage Reduction]] durations are now 33% shorter in Normal Mode and 15% shorter in Expert Mode.
*Reactive Damage Reduction is now disabled in tiers. If a boss from a tier is killed on a world, all bosses below its tier will have their Reactive Damage Reduction removed. This effect also includes [[Malice Mode]]. A full list of tiers can be found below:
**Tier 1 consists of all Pre Hardmode bosses, including [[King Slime]], [[Desert Scourge]], [[Eye of Cthulhu]], [[Crabulon]], [[Eater of Worlds]], [[Brain of Cthulhu]], [[Hive Mind]], [[The Perforators]], [[Queen Bee]], [[Skeletron]], [[The Slime God]], and the [[Wall of Flesh]].
**Tier 2 consist of all early Hardmode bosses, including [[Cryogen]], [[Mechanical Bosses]], [[Aquatic Scourge]], [[Brimstone Elemental]], [[Calamitas]], and [[Plantera]].
**Tier 3 consist of all late Hardmode bosses, including [[Anahita and Leviathan]], [[Astrum Aureus]], [[Golem]], [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]], [[Duke Fishron]], [[Ravager]], the [[Lunatic Cultist]], [[Astrum Deus]], and the [[Moon Lord]].
**Tier 4 consists of [[The Dragonfolly]], [[Profaned Guardians]], and [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]].
**Tier 5 consists of [[Ceaseless Void]], [[Storm Weaver]], [[Signus, Envoy of the Devourer]], [[Polterghast]], and [[The Old Duke]].
**[[The Devourer of Gods]], [[Jungle Dragon, Yharon]], [[The Fabricational Quartet]], and [[Supreme Calamitas]] have no tier, and must be individually defeated to have their Reactive Damage Reduction removed. However, upon being defeated they will still remove Reactive Damage Reduction on previous bosses.
*Most segmented bosses have had their Reactive Damage Reduction modifiers decreased from 5x to 3x.
**This excludes [[XM-05 Thanatos]], who now only receives a 2x modifier, including on Malice Mode.
**This modifier has been decreased from 5x to 4x in Malice Mode.
*Increased the amount of time needed to pass before [[Damage Reduction#Timed Aggression|Timed Aggression]] kicks in from 35% longer than the expected kill time to 50%.
*Decreased [[Desert Scourge]] velocity by 25% on all world difficulties.
**[[A Desert Nuisance]]s no longer drop Hearts.
*Significantly decreased how often [[The Perforator]] worms burrow and lunge at the player on all world difficulties.
*[[Polterghast]]'s map icons now rotate alongside the boss.
*The Rock and Crystal [[Profaned Guardians]] are no longer considered bosses.
*Decreased [[XS-03 Apollo]] and [[XF-09 Ares]] plasma bolt projectile damage from {{dv|400|600|632|648}} to {{dv|350|528|556|572}}.
*XF-09 Ares can no longer fire any projectiles for 10 frames after its Exo Overload Beam attack ends. Increased the distance the player must travel away from the lasers to proc its enrage by 5 blocks.

=== Enemies ===
*The following enemies can no longer inflict [[Defense damage]]:
**All [[Armed Zombie]] variants, all [[Crimera]] variants, [[Devourer]]s, all [[Eater of Souls]] variants, and [[Goblin Warrior]]s.
**Additionally, [[Antlion]]s and [[Tomb Crawler]]s can no longer inflict Defense damage until the Desert Scourge has been defeated.
*Nerfed [[Cnidrion]] HP from {{dv|400|800}} to {{dv|280|560}}.
*[[Eidolist]]s can no longer spawn in the [[Dungeon]].

=== Recipes ===
*[[Auric Tesla Body Armor]] and [[Auric Tesla Cuisses]] now require either the [[God Slayer Chestplate]] or [[Silva Armor]] / [[God Slayer Leggings]] or [[Silva Leggings]] to be crafted instead of requiring both.
*[[Auric Quantum Cooling Cell]] now requires 8 [[Mysterious Circuitry]], 8 [[Dubious Plating]], 40 [[Endothermic Energy]], and 6 [[Core of Eleum|Cores of Eleum]] to be crafted instead of 40 [[Wire]]s, 80 Endothermic Energy, and 12 Cores of Eleum.
*The [[Decryption Computer]] now requires 100 [[Wire]]s to be crafted instead of 300.
*Unsafe Eutrophic Sand Walls can now be crafted from [[Eutrophic Sand]].
*Unsafe Eutrophic Sand and Navystone Walls can now be crafted back into Eutrophic Sand and [[Navystone]] respectively.
*Silva Leggings are now crafted at a [[Cosmic Anvil]] instead of an [[Ancient Manipulator]].
*Removed the [[Rod of Discord]] crafting recipe.
*[[Unholy Core]]s are now crafted at a [[Hellforge]] instead of a Hardmode Forge.
*The [[Voltage Regulation System]] is now crafted at an [[Ancient Manipulator]].

=== Renames ===
*Renamed Eutrophic Sand Wall to [[Unsafe Eutrophic Sand Wall]].
*Renamed Eutrophic Sand Wall Safe to [[Eutrophic Sand Wall]].
*Renamed Glove Of Precision to [[Glove of Precision]].
*Renamed Glove Of Recklessness to [[Glove of Recklessness]].
*Renamed Navystone Wall to [[Unsafe Navystone Wall]].
*Renamed Navystone Wall Safe to [[Navystone Wall]].

=== Balance and Reworks ===
*Moved 5% summon damage from the [[Aerospec Helmet]] set bonus to the headpiece itself.
*Gave [[Bloody Edge]] a size modifier of 125%.
*Reworked the Defense damage formula:
**The ratio of damage to defense damage is now always 15%, regardless of difficulty.
**The player cannot take more than 25% of their maximum defense as defense damage.
**Increased the defense damage regeneration delay timer from 0.25 seconds to 0.5.
**Defense that is lost now regenerates back at a dynamic percentage every 3 frames once the regeneration delay timer has ended.
**If the player takes defense damage and is not hit again, all defense will have been regenerated back in 1 second.
**Consecutive hits after taking defense damage now slow the percentage of defense that is regenerated back every 3 frames. The total time for all of the player's defense to be regenerated back is capped at 15 seconds.
**Defense damage can now only be applied to the player once every 0.25 seconds instead of every 0.083.
*Nerfed Defense-increasing modifiers:
**The '''Hard''' modifier now increases defense by {{color|+1|green}} / {{color|+2|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+3|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+4|green}} once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated; instead of increasing defense by {{color|+1|green}} / {{color|+2|green}} once Golem or Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+3|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+4|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated.
**The '''Guarding''' modifier increases defense by {{color|+2|green}} / {{color|+3|green}} once the Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+4|green}} once Golem has been defeated / {{color|+5|green}} once Providence, the Profaned Goddess has been defeated / {{color|+6|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated / {{color|+8|green}} once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated; instead of increasing defense by {{color|+2|green}} / {{color|+3|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+4|green}} once Golem has been defeated / {{color|+5|green}} once Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+6|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+7|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+8|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated.
**The '''Armored''' modifier now increases defense by {{color|+3|green}} / {{color|+5|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+6|green}} once Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+7|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+8|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+9|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated \ {{color|+11|green}} once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated; instead of increasing defense by {{color|+3|green}} / {{color|+4|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+6|green}} once Golem has been defeated / {{color|+7|green}} once Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+9|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+10|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+12|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated.
**The '''Warding''' modifier now increases defense by {{color|+4|green}} / {{color|+6|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+7|green}} once Golem has been defeated / {{color|+8|green}} once Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+9|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+10|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+12|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated / {{color|+15|green}} once Jungle Dragon, Yharon has been defeated; instead of increasing defense by {{color|+4|green}} / {{color|+6|green}} once Wall of Flesh has been defeated / {{color|+8|green}} once Golem has been defeated / {{color|+10|green}} once Moon Lord has been defeated / {{color|+12|green}} once Providence has been defeated / {{color|+14|green}} once Polterghast has been defeated / {{color|+16|green}} once The Devourer of Gods has been defeated.
*Nerfed [[Endurance Potion]]'s damage reduction bonus from 10% to 5%.
*Nerfed [[Eye of Night]]'s base damage from 35 to 28, its minion cell projectile can now only pierce enemies once instead of infinitely, no longer explodes, and it grants enemies 90 local immunity frames instead of 19. Reduced the maximum amount of cells stuck on an enemy at once from 10 to 4. Additionally, the minion can no longer deal contact damage.
*Rebalanced [[Flesh of Infidelity]], nerfed its base damage from 26 to 23 and its minion now grants enemies 60 local immunity frames instead of standard immunity frames. Its Flesh Ball minion now fires 2 blood particles every 3 jumps instead of 1-4 every jump. Increased its Blood projectile's duration from 3 seconds to 5.
*Buffed [[Frostbite Blaster]]'s base damage from 31 to 55. It no longer fires more bullets than intended.
*The [[Heart of the Elements]] no longer grants the player 9 defense while equipped.
*[[Heart]]s can now drop from the [[King Slime]] boss servants.
*[[The First Shadowflame]] now lists that it grants minion slots in its tooltip. Decreased its minion slot bonus from +3 to +1.
**However, it now stacks with the minions slots granted by [[Statis' Blessing]].
*Decreased the [[Shattered Community]] maximum rage level from 60 to 25.
*[[Tavernkeep]] armor set bonuses buffed:
**[[Monk armor]] now grants 15% minion damage, 10% melee damage, 10% melee criticial strike chance and 10% melee speed.
**[[Squire armor]] now grants 3 life regen and 15% melee and minion damage.
**[[Huntress armor]] and [[Red Riding armor]] now grant 10% minion and ranged damage.
**[[Apprentice armor]] now grants 5% minion damage and 15% magic critical strike chance.
**[[Shinobi Infiltrator armor]] now grants 30% minion damage, 10% melee damage, 10% melee criticial strike chance and 10% melee speed.
**[[Valhalla Knight armor]] now grants 6 life regen and 10% melee and minion damage.
**[[Dark Artist armor]] now grants 10% minion damage and 15% magic critical strike chance.
*Buffed [[Virid Vanguard]]'s base damage from 156 to 280 and its alternative fire function now functions correctly.
