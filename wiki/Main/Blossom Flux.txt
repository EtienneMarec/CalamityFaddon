The '''Blossom Flux''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[bow]] that is dropped by [[Plantera]]. It has two modes of attack: its primary fire (left-click) will shoot leaf arrows at a very high rate, and its secondary fire (right-click) launches a spore orb that travels in a wave pattern and explodes into spore clouds upon impact.

== Notes ==
*Due to its incredible rate of fire and lack of inherent chance to not consume ammo, this weapon can drain arrow reserves extremely quickly.
**Conversely, the [[Endless Quiver]] is very useful when paired with the Blossom Flux.
*The primary fire has a fixed use time of 16 ticks, and the secondary fire has a fixed use time of 25 ticks; as a result, no modifier can increase or decrease the firing rate of the bow.
*The secondary fire's spore orbs can inflict critical hits upon striking an enemy, but the gas clouds emitted by the orbs cannot.

== Tips ==
*Use [[Cursed Arrow]]s or [[Venom Arrow]]s with this weapon, as it does not gain any benefit from ammo choice besides raw damage.
*The velocity of the leaf arrows fired by Blossom Flux is low, but can be improved with speed-increasing modifiers such as '''Hasty''' or '''Rapid''', with the [[Archery Potion]] buff, or by equipping the [[Magic Quiver]].
