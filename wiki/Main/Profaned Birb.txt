The '''Profaned Birb''' is a Godseeker Mode Town Pet.

The only requirements for the Profaned Birb to appear is to have defeated [[Providence, the Profaned Goddess]] and the presence of a valid house. The Profaned Birb's house can be changed, as with any other NPC, however the Profaned Birb can share the same house as another NPC. The Profaned Birb needs a 2-tile floor space to sleep on occasionally; it may not move into a house if that space is not available.

== Snacks ==
By clicking on the "Snacks" button when interacting with her, the Profaned Birb can be fed a Gummy Worm to grant the player [[Provi's Blessing]] for 20 minutes.

Feeding her another will reset the buff's timer.

==Names==
*The Profaned Birb will always be named Providence.

==Trivia== 
*The Profaned Birb is a creation of the artist and fanfiction writer Tehpootisman.
**Just like in the written work in which she appears, the Profaned Birb is the result of Providence's core being shattered, thus limiting her to the form of a small dove-like bird.
**The Profaned Birb is directly inspired by a brief trend of artists on the Calamity Art Discord drawing pictures of Providence as a large bird.
*Unlike the other Town Pets, the Profaned Birb is very talkative.
*Providence will occasionally make bird sounds.
*Selecting the "pet" option will cause her to blush.
*Upon taking damage and/or killed, the Profaned Birb will release a cloud of feathers. This is purely cosmetic.
*The Profaned Birb was originally intended to be a part of the Community Remix mod, however it was eventually decided to be a standalone mod.
