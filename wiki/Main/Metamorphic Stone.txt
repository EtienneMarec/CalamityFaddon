The '''Metamorphic Stone''' is a [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that has a {{dv|20%|33.33%}} chance to be dropped by [[Earth Elemental]]s. Equipping it summons a Earth Elemental to fight for you, which deals summon damage. The Earth Elemental cycles between dashing at enemies, shooting a stream of smaller rocks concluded with a large splitting rock, and swinging its arm into enemies to deal 600% damage and inflict [[War Cleave]] and [[Armor Crunch]].
