The '''Ambrosial Ampoule''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[covering]] that gives the user a wide array of benefits. These include immunity to certain [[debuffs]], increased life regeneration by 8, 14 [[defense]], 7% damage reduction, and making the user emit light.

== Trivia ==
*An ampoule is a sealed glass container containing a liquid.
*The term "ambrosial" refers to [[wikipedia:Ambrosia|ambrosia]], the food of the gods in Greek mythology.
*The Ambrosial Ampoule once also combined the effects of the [[Archaic Powder]] and [[Honey Dew]], but these are now standalone items.
