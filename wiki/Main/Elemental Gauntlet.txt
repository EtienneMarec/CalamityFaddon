The '''Elemental Gauntlet''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[covering]]. It provides a 15% increase to melee speed and damage and a 5% increase to melee critical strike chance, a 20% increase to [[true melee]] damage, increases melee knockback, and gives the player temporary immunity to lava.

While wearing the Elemental Gauntlet, any enemy struck by a melee attack will be inflicted with these [[debuffs]]:
*[[On Fire!]] for 5 seconds
*[[Frostburn]] for 5 seconds
*[[Holy Flames]] for 5 seconds
