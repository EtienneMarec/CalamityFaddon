'''Plasma Grenades''' are craftable Godseeker Mode [[bombs]]. When used, the bomb is affected by gravity after a short time. When it collides with a surface or enemy, it explodes, dealing damage within the area.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Plasma Grenade causes the resulting explosion to be larger and strike twice.

The weapon can also be used as [[ammunition]] for the [[SDOMG]].
