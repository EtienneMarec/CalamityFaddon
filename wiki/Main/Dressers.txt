'''Dressers''' are pieces of [[furniture]] that allow the player to change their character's appearance. They additionally can be used as storage, similar to [[Chest]]s. The Calamity Mod currently adds sixteen types of Dressers to the game, each corresponding to specific [[Furniture sets|sets of furniture]].
