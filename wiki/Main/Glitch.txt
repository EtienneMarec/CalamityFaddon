The '''Glitch''' is a Godseeker Mode [[enemy]] that spawns semi rarely during the [[Solar Eclipse]] after [[The Devourer of Gods]] has been defeated. The Glitch will summon corruptions, which will slowly home in on the player. Corruptions will disappear once the Glitch is defeated, and there can only be 7 corruptions alive at once.

== Trivia ==
*Glitch is a reference to the MISSINGNO exploit from Pokemon Red and Blue.
*Corruptions appear to be made of Terraria spritesheets.
