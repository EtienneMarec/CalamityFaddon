'''Work Benches''' are pieces of [[furniture]] that function as early game crafting stations. The Calamity: Tales of the Aquatic currently adds fifteen new types of Work Benches to the game, each corresponding to specific [[Furniture Sets (CTotA)|sets of furniture]].
