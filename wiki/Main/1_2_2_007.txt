The '''1.2.2.007''' update made a few changes to [[The Devourer of Gods]], and added an assortment of resprites.

== Additions ==
*Added [[Heart of Darkness]].

== Changes ==
=== [[Bosses]] and [[Enemies]] ===
*Any boss can now drop a Heart of Darkness in [[Revengeance Mode]].
*Nerfed [[Another worm boss]]' health from {{dv|150,000|240,000}} to {{dv|120,000|192,000}}.
*The Devourer of Gods now broadcasts a message to chat upon spawning: "You are no god...but I shall feast upon your essence regardless!"
*The Devourer of Gods now cuts infinitely penetrating projectile damage by 75%, and other piercing projectile damage by 90%.
*[[Polterghast]] now has a despawn timer, which will force the boss to flee if it's enraged for too long.
*[[Supreme Calamitas]]' minimum player damage limit lowered from 100 to 20 (10 with [[Flesh Totem]]).

=== Items ===
*Renamed Daedalus Hat to [[Daedalus Hood]].
*Reduced the dust on [[Grand Guardian]].
*Lowered the duration of [[Purge Guzzler]]'s beams.

=== Other ===
*Dying no longer rids players of [[Stress]], and instead gives them more.
*Stress is now easier to lower with natural methods.

=== Resprites ===
*Resprited [[Siren Lure]], [[Xeroc Wrath]].
*Resprited [[Ornate Shield]].
*Resprited [[Daedalus Breastplate]], [[Daedalus Headgear]], [[Daedalus Helm]], [[Daedalus Helmet]], Daedalus Hood, [[Daedalus Leggings]], [[Daedalus Visor]], [[Xeroc Cuisses]], [[Xeroc Mask]], [[Xeroc Plate Mail]].
*Resprited [[Draedon's Forge]].
*Resprited [[Crystal Piercer]], [[Darkecho Greatbow]], [[The Empyrean]], [[Shadecrystal Barrage]], [[Shimmerspark]], [[Siren's Song]], [[Starnight Lance]], [[Tome of Fates]], [[Trinity]], [[Verium Bullet]], [[Xeroc Greatsword]], [[Xeroc Pitchfork]].
*Resprited [[Abyssal Warhammer]], [[Gallant Pickaxe]], [[Shardlight Pickaxe]].
*Resprited [[Meld Blob]], [[Verstaltite Bar]].
*Resprited [[The Plaguebringer Goliath]]'s map icon.
*Resprited [[The Obliterator]]'s projectile.

=== Fixes ===
*Fixed [[MOAB]]'s equipped sprite having too many frames.
*Fixed a sheeting error with The Plaguebringer Goliath and [[Plaguebringer]]s.
