The Delicious Meat Ending is a [[Endings|secret ending]] of the Calamity Mod. The ending revolves around the common struggles and hardships of enduring perfect delicacy in spite of eternal confinement.

== Requirements ==
The Delicious Meat Ending can be achieved at every stage of the game, although it is very difficult to do so before beating [[Purgatory Arbiter, Umbrus]]. To initiate the ending, the player has to spawn [[Crabulon]] and tame it (see [[Taming]]) at least once by consuming a [[Yharic Delight]] while the boss is alive.

Next, one must summon the alternative version of [[Crabulon]] by having 2,147,483,647 [[Delicious Meat]] in their inventory/storage and bringing a [[Barocrab]] to a Mushroom Forest. Gathering the [[Delicious Meat]] is best achieved by repeatedly defeating [[The Fabricational Quartet]] and buying from the [[Archmage]] when the item is on sale.
The following battle against the more difficult version of Crabulon is both emotionally and physically challenging as the big crab launches a barrage of projectiles and very hurtful insults at the player. Therefore, it is important to proceed with utmost diligence and caution.

== The Delicious Meat Ending ==
Instead of burrowing away after defeating [[Crabulon]] like it usually does, it explodes into a multitude of [[Glowing Mushroom|Glowing Mushrooms]] and drops the [[Delicious Key]]. With this key, it is possible to enter the [[Shroom Garden]] that was previously locked by a door similarly to the [[Jungle Temple]]. While exploring, the player will be warned of "The Curse" by an unknown voice. Proceeding nonetheless leads to a chest with [[Sacred Meat]] which, if consumed, teleports them to the [[Heavenly Limbo]]. It is impossible to leave this dimension with the character, even when entering a different world. Uninstalling the Calamity Mod will also result in the corruption of that characters save file.

Eating [[Delicious Meat]] while in [[Heavenly Limbo]] causes one of the following audio messages to be played:
*''"You fool. Your ignorance and unsatiable hunger shall punish you for the longest time."'' (Guaranteed first message if willingly consumed)
*''"In five octillion years, you may have digested all this world has to offer, but you have (*inaudible*) your sheer stupidity."''
*''"Sometimes I wonder; What is going on inside your head? But then I realize that all there is is your microscopic brain smaller than a peanut."''
*''"Your/You're(?) (*inaudible*) so fat, no wonder you are this disgustingly greedy for bacon."''
*''"Is this really what you wanted? Is this really what you wanted? Is this really what you wanted? Is this really what you wanted?"'' (This goes on for five minutes)
*''"You should have never slain that crab. Do you (*inaudible*) close friends we were?''"
Note that the audio is extremely distorted and it is thus difficult to discern what is said in some passages.

If the character has only played in [[For the Worthy]] worlds, all messages are replaced by the popular hit song ''[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ Never Gonna Give You Up]'' by Rick Astley.

== The Delicious Meat Ending+ ==
To achieve this variant of the ending, you must first repeat all of the previous instructions, but do NOT instantly consume the Sacred Meat, no matter how tempting it may be.

Using the Sacred Meat on an [[Altar of the Accursed]] summons a buffed version of Supreme Ultramage, Permafrost. Aside from some minor differences such as dialogue, the key difference with this fight is that after almost winning, Permafrost will FORCE you to consume the Sacred Meat, in a desperate attempt not to lose his ego by losing the fight. This will take you to Heavenly Limbo, but it will also take Permafrost, forced to live with the consequences of his actions.

This version of The Delicious Meat Ending is not the end of your story, however, as you are put into a cutscene in which the voice that insulted you in the original Delicious Meat Ending attempts to decide who is more guilty and who can go free. Ultimately, The Curse decides that despite your heist and killing Crabulon, attempting to eternally imprison someone to save your ego is unforgivable, and decides that you can go free.

After this, a portal opens up in the center of Heavenly Limbo that lets you leave. You can return to Heavenly Limbo any time as a portal opens up above the original location of the Sacred Meat in the Shroom Garden, allowing you to return.

Additionally, Heavenly Limbo functions differently compared to the normal ending. Archmage will occasionally snack on a Delicious Meat, which will trigger the dialogue from the normal ending. Said dialogue will not trigger if you consume any delicious meat. Archmage will refuse to sell you anything out of spite and cannot move in as a town NPC. Archmage is immortal, yet can still be hit and is affected by knockback.

== The Escape ==
There are three simple methods to escape Heavenly Limbo, excluding The Delicious Meat Ending+.

The first method is to download a cheat mod to summon Crabulon, who monologues for a bit about how you've met with a terrible fate, your immense desperation to escape, and asks that you do not repeat the same mistake again. If for some reason you repeat the same mistake again, Crabulon will not spawn no matter what you do. You can technically get the same effect infinitely via summoning [[Crabulon|The Extraordinarily Buff and Feverishly Reverent Defender of Intergalactic Gourmet Partisanship, Crabulon]], however there have only been 7 people on record who have been able to survive his insults, and only one person who is not currently hospitalized.

The second method is to load up a multiplayer world. This will put you in whatever world you chose and allow you to roam free, although this is not permanent and will still put you back in your Delicious Prison if you go back to singleplayer. This also applies a debuff called "Free Trial Of Freedom" which gives you -35% damage and makes you take 15% more damage, so it would be advised against doing this if your comfortable installing a cheat mod to instead perform the first method.

The third method only works if you have any other mods installed that have subrealms. If you have any items that take you to a subrealm and said subrealm lets you leave, you'll be taken back to the overworld. This is an oversight on the calamity dev team, because if you disable calamity it will still corrupt your player's save file. This also confirms that the corruption of the player's save file is NOT because of reckless coding, but an intentional method of corrupting your character for the crimes you've committed.

All methods have their pros and cons. The first method requires a cheating mod and works only once, the second method gives you a debuff and forces you into multiplayer, and the third method will still corrupt your character upon disabling calamity and requires other major content mods. I would advise you do not go for this ending if you value your character and are not comfortable installing cheat mods.

== Lore ==
The Delicious Meat Ending has several heavy implications for the Calamity lore while also commenting on today's issues of obesity and societal pressures. It is confirmed by Fabsol that "The Curse" is the voice speaking in [[Heavenly Limbo]]. Some players theorized that The Curse, making excessively rude remarks about the player, represents the toxicity of the Terraria Calamity community and the issues the developers have to deal with.

== Trivia ==

* The voice actor for The Curse (the voice that can be heard in Heavenly Limbo) is Gianni Matragrano who voices Gabriel from ULTRAKILL among many other roles.
