The '''Defiled Feather''' was a craftable item that when favorited, would prevent [[Wings]] from working. It was obtained from opening a [[Starter Bag]] or by crafting it at a [[Demon Altar]] or [[Crimson Altar]].

== Trivia ==
*This item was formerly known as the "Defiled Rune".
*This item was a reference to the [https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Defiled+Chalice Defiled Chalice] from the game ''[[wikipedia:Bloodborne|Bloodborne]]'', and it had some similar functions to it previously.
*The sprite and tooltip were references to the game ''[[wikipedia:Celeste (video game)|Celeste]]''. The item's appearance was based on the [https://celestegame.fandom.com/wiki/Objects#Feathers Feathers] from the game.
*In previous versions of the mod, this item also made enemies have a chance to critically hit you for 1.5x damage, make certain rare items have a 5% or higher chance of dropping, and make enemies drop 50% more cash. 
**Before that, this item used to halve a player's max HP and increase enemy damage resistance by 50%.
*Prior to the [[1.5.0.001]] update, this item could only be used in [[Revengeance|Revengeance Mode]] or [[Death Mode]].
