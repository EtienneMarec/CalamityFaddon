<big> ''"Slime is Gushing from the Sky!"'' </big>

The '''Jelly Monsoon''' is a weather [[event]] added by the Calamity Mod. It has a 1/3,600 chance of occurring every frame (1/60 every second) once the [[King Slime]] has been defeated, or can be [[Summoning items|summoned]] manually through the use of an [[Overloaded Sludge]].

The event serves to act as an easier method of obtaining resources throughout progression by allowing the player to fight corresponding slimes.
