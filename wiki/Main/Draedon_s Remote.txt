'''Draedon's Remote''' is a non-consumable [[Pre-Hardmode]] item, which drops from [[Wulfrum Enemies]]. When used at [[night]] pre-plantera, it will [[Summoning items|summon]] all three [[Mechanical Bosses]] at once. The bosses are much stronger (and larger) than in their original fights.
When used in [[day]] in different parts of the playtrought, it will summon the [[Mechanical Horde]].

The summoned bosses have the following stats changed:
*Both of [[The Twins|The Twins']] health have been raised by x1.8.
*[[The Destroyer|The Destroyer's]] health is raised by x1.8 and its size by x1.5.
**Each [[Probe|Probe's]] health is raised by x1.6 and size by x1.2.
*[[Skeletron Prime|Skeletron Prime's]] head's health is raised by x1.45.
**Skeletron Prime's Cannon, Laser, Saw, and Vice's health are all lowered by x0.8.
*While at least one of the Mechanical Bosses summoned by the remote is alive, the player will take 20% more damage from all sources.
*All 3 Mechanical bosses will now drop 8-16 {{item|Mysterious Circuitry}} and {{item|Dubious Plating}} which can be used to craft various [[Template:Draedon's Arsenal|weapons]].

Attempting to use the item while any of the three Mechanical Bosses are still alive will do nothing.

{{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}Additionally, when using the item inside [[The Exosphere]], it will instead initiate the [[Mechanical Horde]] event provided the event has not already been completed. If used in the Exosphere after the Mechanical Horde has been defeated, the item will do nothing.
