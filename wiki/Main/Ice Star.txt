'''Ice Stars''' are [[Hardmode]] [[Spy weapon]]s sold by the [[Archmage]] [[NPC]]. They are large throwing stars made of ice, which pierce 3 times and will fall after traveling a short distance. They are stackable and cannot be recovered after impacting the ground. Ice Stars also have slight homing abilities.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Ice Stars will cause the next star thrown to pierce infinitely for four seconds before disappearing, and release rings of 8 smaller ice stars on enemy hits. The smaller ice stars will not be released if 10 or more stars are already present.

== Notes ==
*The homing ability of the weapon is extremely unreliable, as they will usually cause the projectile to fall quicker and not hit the [[enemy]] at all.
*The stars deal one-sixth of the weapon’s base damage, sharply home-in onto enemies, go through tiles, deal typeless damage, and cannot deal critical hits.
*This weapon does not benefit from the effects of the [[Invisibility Potion]] or the [[Shadow Potion]].

== Tips ==
*They can be used as an effective weapon against [[The Destroyer]] and the [[Aquatic Scourge]] as the slight homing can make them curve along the bosses' segments.
