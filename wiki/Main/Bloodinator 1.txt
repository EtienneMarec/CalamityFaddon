}}}}The '''Bloodinator 1''' is a craftable [[Pre-Hardmode]] [[Daggers|dagger]] dropped from the [[wikipedia:Destruction_under_the_Mongol_Empire|Bloodinating]] event. Upon dealing damage to an enemy, they inflict a special version of [[Bleeding]] (Called Bloodinated) where the weapon loses stats instead of the enemy losing health. After 5 stacks of [[Bloodinated]] is inflicted on an enemy, the Bloodinated stacks disappear, and the enemy gets a debuff called [[wikipedia:First_Turkic_Khaganate|Drenched in Blood]]. If an enemy is killed during this state, they will never spawn again, except during a a [[Bloodinated Moon|Bloodinated Moon.]]

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with Bloodinator 1 causes the player themselves to be inflicted with Drenched in Blood, and if they die in that state, no players can ever respawn.

== Tips ==

* The Bloodinator 1 should not be trashed even past [[Hardmode]], since it is one of the most reliable counters to the [[Bloodinator 7]].
* Upon being given to the [[Guide]], the Bloodinator 1 will reverse the scrolling direction during the Crafting Guides, this of course, counters the mouse wheel disabling ability of the [[Bloodinator Origins]]
