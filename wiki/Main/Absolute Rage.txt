'''Absolute Rage''' was a [[buff]] that could be inflicted randomly on the player when their [[Rage Meter]] was high. It increased their maximum life by 5%. If the player was equipped with either the [[Heart of Darkness]] or [[Draedon's Heart]], Absolute Rage would boost their damage by 10%.

== Notes ==
*Equipping either the Heart of Darkness or the Draedon’s Heart made the player five times as likely to receive Absolute Rage.

== Trivia ==
*This buff used to be known as "Heart Attack".
*Before the update [[1.2.4.101]], this buff was a debuff that was inflicted on the player when the [[Stress]] value was too high. For a long time, this was reflected in the  {{item|Absolute Rage (1.2.0.001)|mode=image|nolink=y}} buff's old icon, as its background is red, not blue.
