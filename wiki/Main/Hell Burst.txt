The '''Hell Burst''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[wand]] that is a direct upgrade to the [[Crystal Vile Shard]]. It shoots out a consecutive chain of large fire spikes, which lingers for a second and inflicts the [[On Fire!]] [[debuff]] to enemies on contact. The projectile can hit any enemy up to eight times before disappearing.
