}}The '''Wulfrum Blunderbuss''' is a [[pre-Hardmode]] [[ranged weapon]] dropped by [[The Inventors]] and the [[Wulfrum Excavator]] {{fantag|Calamity: Community Remix}}. It uses [[Wulfrum Metal Scrap]]s or [[Silver Coin]]s as [[ammunition]] and fires a spread of six scrap bullets. The farther the cursor is from the gun, the tighter the spread but the lower its damage. This linearly scales up to a maximum of 28.75 or more tiles away, in which the spread decreases by 66.67% but damage also decreases by 20%. The player is required to reload and consume ammo after every 30 shots. All bullets have a default armor penetration of 3.
