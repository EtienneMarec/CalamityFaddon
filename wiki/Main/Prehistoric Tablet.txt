The '''Prehistoric Tablet''' is a Godseeker Mode [[covering]] that has an 8% chance to be dropped by [[Mauler]]. It summons a Sulphur Elemental to fight for you. The elemental, upon spotting an enemy, will cycle between two attacks.

*It will cast 3 sulphuric rainclouds in the general area above the target, which last for 6 seconds.
*It will dash at the enemy 5 times.

== Trivia ==
*The Prehistoric Tablet, along with the [[Infected Remote]], are the only two elemental summoning items that do not drop from a boss or miniboss counterpart.
*The Sulphur Elemental's name in the mods files was previously "Acid Waifu".
*The Sulphur Elemental appears to be wearing the skull of a [[wikipedia:Dunkleosteus|dunkleosteus]], a species of extinct armored fish.
