The '''Shroomite Visage''' is a [[Hardmode]] helmet [[Covering]] piece, crafted with 12 [[Shroomite Bar]]s at a [[Hardmode Anvil]]. It grants 11 defense as well as 25% increased ranged damage for [[flamethrowers]].

When equipped with the [[Shroomite Breastplate]] and [[Shroomite Leggings]], the full set grants 51 armor, 38% increased ranged damage for flamethrowers, 13% ranged damage for all non-flamethrower [[ranged weapons]], 13% increased ranged critical strike chance, 12% increased movement speed, and 20% chance not to consume ammo. The set bonus grants the ability to enter stealth mode when standing still, reducing the chance that enemies will target the player and increasing damage by 60% and critical chance by 10%.
