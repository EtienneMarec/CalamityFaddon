The '''Forbidden Oathblade''' is a craftable [[Hardmode]] [[broadsword]] that autoswings and is the upgrade of both the [[Bladecrest Oathsword]] and the [[Old Lord Oathsword]]. When swung, it fires a demonic scythe that collides with terrain and pierces up to two targets. Both the sword and the fired projectiles inflict the [[On Fire!]] and [[Shadowflame]] [[debuffs]]. Critical hits additionally summon purple explosions on the target.
