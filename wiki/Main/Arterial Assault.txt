The '''Arterial Assault''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[bow]] made from [[Bloodstone Core]]s. It converts normal [[Wooden Arrow]]s into homing [[Bloodfire Arrow]]s upon use, which inflict the [[Brimstone Flames]] [[debuff]] and have a 33.33% chance to [[Lifesteal|heal]] the player for 1 HP upon hitting enemies. The bow rapidly fires chains of 5 arrows from the sky per use, consuming 5 arrows at once. Arrows fired from this bow pass through tiles. Each arrow shot has a use time of 3.
