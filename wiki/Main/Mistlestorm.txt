The '''Mistlestorm''' is a craftable Godseeker Mode [[wand]] and the direct upgrade of both the [[Razorpine]] and the [[Leaf Blower]]. It rapidly fires flurries of quick pine needles, which are slightly affected by gravity, and slower swarms of leaves, which are not affected by gravity.
