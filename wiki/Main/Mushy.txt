'''Mushy''' is a [[buff]] applied to the player when they are near [[Crabulon]] while it is passive, or hitting a [[stealth strike]] with the [[Shroomerang]], granting the Mushy buff for 12 seconds.

The buff gives the player an extra 5 [[defense]], increases their life regen by 1, and causes them to emit glowing blue particles.
