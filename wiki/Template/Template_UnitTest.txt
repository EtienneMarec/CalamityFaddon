A simple helper template for unit test cells in template documentation.

== Usage ==
<code><nowiki>{{unitTest|[expected result]|[actual result]}}</nowiki></code>

If the expected result and the actual result are equal, the output will be the result. If they are not equal, {{color|'''FAIL!'''|red}} will be displayed.
