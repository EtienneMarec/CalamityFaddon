== Usage ==
Add this to the tops of pages that describe characters that cannot spawn either manually or naturally without hacks. 

* <code>{{tl|unspawnable}}</code>
{{unspawnable|nocat=y}}

Enter "section" as a parameter if used at the top of a section alone:
* <code>{{tlx|unspawnable|section}}</code>
{{unspawnable|section}}
