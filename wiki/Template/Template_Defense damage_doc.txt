Places a captioned and linked symbol of broken armor. Used to indicate attacks that inflict [[defense damage]], placed after damage values and attack name in NPC infoboxes.

== Usage ==
<code><nowiki>{{defense damage}}</nowiki></code> gives:
{{defense damage}}

=== Example ===
<code><nowiki>{{dv|100|boost=1}} (Contact) {{defense damage}}</nowiki></code> gives:
{{dv|100|boost=1}} (Contact) {{defense damage}}
